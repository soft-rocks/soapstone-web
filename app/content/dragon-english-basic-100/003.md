# It is really pleasant to take a walk early in the morning, listening to birds sing.

## 1. 形式主詞的 it（anticipatory it）

句型公式：

**It + be + 形容詞 + `to` + 動詞原形**

句首的 `it` 是形式主詞。它只佔主詞的位置。真正的主詞是後面的 `to` 不定詞片段。原句真正的主詞是 `to take a walk early in the morning, listening to birds sing`。

* It is really pleasant to take a walk along the beach.
  沿著海邊散步真的很舒服。
* Is it safe to walk here at night?
  晚上走在這裡安全嗎？

## 2. 表示同時動作的分詞構句（participle clause）

句型公式：

**主要子句, + 動詞-ing + ...**

逗號後面的動詞-ing 是分詞構句。它表示做主要動作的人，同時在做的另一個動作。動詞-ing 的主詞是做主要動作的人。原句的主詞是形式主詞 `it`，散步的人沒有寫出來。`listening` 的主詞是散步的人，不是 `it`。

* My grandfather sat on the sofa, reading the newspaper.
  我爺爺坐在沙發上看報紙。
* She walked home, singing a song.
  她一路唱著歌走回家。

## 3. 感官動詞 + 受詞 + 動詞原形（verbs of perception）

句型公式：

**`hear` / `see` / `watch` / `listen to` + 受詞 + 動詞原形**

感官動詞後面先接受詞，再接動詞原形。動詞原形前面不放 `to`。受詞是做動作的人或東西。原句裡 `birds` 是受詞，`sing` 是鳥做的動作。

* I heard someone call my name.
  我聽到有人叫我的名字。
* We saw the sun go down behind the mountains.
  我們看到太陽落到山的後面。
* I like to listen to my father play the guitar.
  我喜歡聽我爸爸彈吉他。

## 4. 例句

1. It is really pleasant to take a walk early in the morning, listening to birds sing.
一大早去散步，一邊聽鳥叫，真的很舒服。
2. It is really pleasant to take a walk along the beach.
沿著海邊散步真的很舒服。
3. Is it safe to walk here at night?
晚上走在這裡安全嗎？
4. It is nice to have breakfast outside on a sunny morning.
晴朗的早上在外面吃早餐很舒服。
5. It was exciting to watch the players run onto the field.
看球員跑進球場真讓人興奮。
6. It is fun to watch the boats go by.
看船開過去很有趣。
7. My grandfather sat on the sofa, reading the newspaper.
我爺爺坐在沙發上看報紙。
8. She walked home, singing a song.
她一路唱著歌走回家。
9. The children ran into the park, laughing loudly.
孩子們大聲笑著跑進公園。
10. Tom came into the room, carrying a big box.
Tom 搬著一個大箱子走進房間。
11. He left the room, saying nothing.
他一句話也沒說就離開了房間。
12. She stood at the door, waving goodbye to us.
她站在門口跟我們揮手道別。
13. I often study in the library, listening to music.
我常在圖書館邊聽音樂邊讀書。
14. I heard someone call my name.
我聽到有人叫我的名字。
15. Did you hear the phone ring?
你有聽到電話響嗎？
16. I saw a man cross the street.
我看到一個男人穿越馬路。
17. We saw the sun go down behind the mountains.
我們看到太陽落到山的後面。
18. My mother watched me play soccer.
我媽媽看我踢足球。
19. We watched the children swim in the pool.
我們看孩子們在游泳池游泳。
20. I like to listen to my father play the guitar.
我喜歡聽我爸爸彈吉他。
21. She listened to the rain fall on the roof.
她聽著雨打在屋頂上的聲音。
22. I heard the birds sing early this morning.
今天一大早我聽到鳥在叫。
23. It was really nice to hear you sing at the party.
在派對上聽到你唱歌真的很棒。
24. We sat by the river, listening to the water flow.
我們坐在河邊，聽著河水流動的聲音。
25. Grandma sat in the garden, looking at the flowers.
奶奶坐在花園裡看花。
26. We lay on the beach, listening to the waves.
我們躺在海灘上聽海浪聲。
27. Ann waited for the bus, checking her phone.
Ann 一邊看手機一邊等公車。
28. We walked around the lake, talking about our school days.
我們繞著湖散步，聊著我們的學生時代。
29. They lay on the grass, looking up at the stars.
他們躺在草地上抬頭看星星。
30. It was fun to sit by the window, watching the snow fall.
坐在窗邊看雪落下來很有趣。
