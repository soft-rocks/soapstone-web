# Tom told us that we should rent a car to get around the city because the public transportation was inconvenient.

## 1. tell + 人 + that 子句（`tell` + person + `that`-clause）

句型公式：

**`tell` + 人 + (`that`) + 主詞 + 動詞**

**`say` + (`that`) + 主詞 + 動詞**

`tell` 後面先接聽話的人，再接 `that` 子句。`that` 子句是說話的內容。`that` 可以省略。`say` 後面不接人，直接接 `that` 子句。主要動詞是過去式，`that` 子句的動詞也改成過去式。原句的 `told` 是過去式，所以 Tom 說的理由用 `was inconvenient`。

* She told me that she was busy.
  她跟我說她很忙。
* My father told us he would be home late.
  我爸爸跟我們說他會晚回家。
* Ann said that she liked the movie.
  Ann 說她喜歡那部電影。

## 2. should 表示建議（`should` for advice）

句型公式：

**主詞 + `should` + 動詞原形**

`should` 表示建議或應該做的事。後面接動詞原形。否定是 `should not`，縮寫成 `shouldn't`。轉述過去的建議時，`should` 不改形式。

* You should go to bed early tonight.
  你今晚應該早點睡。
* We shouldn't eat too much before bed.
  我們睡前不應該吃太多。

## 3. 表示目的的 to 不定詞（infinitive of purpose）

句型公式：

**主詞 + 動詞 + ... + `to` + 動詞原形**

`to` 不定詞放在動作後面，說明做這個動作的目的。原句的 `to get around the city` 說明租車的目的。

* We rented bikes to ride around the island.
  我們租腳踏車在島上騎一圈。
* I took a taxi to save time.
  我為了省時間搭計程車。

## 4. 例句

1. Tom told us that we should rent a car to get around the city because the public transportation was inconvenient.
Tom 告訴我們應該租車在市區移動，因為大眾運輸不方便。
2. She told me that she was busy.
她跟我說她很忙。
3. My father told us he would be home late.
我爸爸跟我們說他會晚回家。
4. Ann said that she liked the movie.
Ann 說她喜歡那部電影。
5. The teacher told the students that the test was on Friday.
老師跟學生說考試在星期五。
6. Ken told me he had a cold.
Ken 跟我說他感冒了。
7. Tell your mother that you will come home late.
跟你媽媽說你會晚一點回家。
8. Did Tom tell you that he was moving to Osaka?
Tom 有跟你說他要搬去大阪嗎？
9. He said that the bus was full.
他說公車客滿了。
10. My friend said she wanted to visit Taiwan.
我朋友說她想去台灣玩。
11. You should go to bed early tonight.
你今晚應該早點睡。
12. We shouldn't eat too much before bed.
我們睡前不應該吃太多。
13. You should bring an umbrella today.
你今天應該帶把傘。
14. Should I buy a ticket before I get on the bus?
我應該在上公車之前買票嗎？
15. My teacher told me that I should read more English books.
我的老師跟我說我應該多讀英文書。
16. The doctor told my father that he shouldn't drink so much coffee.
醫生跟我爸爸說他不應該喝那麼多咖啡。
17. She said that we should leave early to avoid the traffic.
她說我們應該早點出發，避開車潮。
18. We rented bikes to ride around the island.
我們租腳踏車在島上騎一圈。
19. I took a taxi to save time.
我為了省時間搭計程車。
20. She went to the station to buy a ticket for Kyoto.
她去車站買去京都的票。
21. Tom used a map app to find the hotel.
Tom 用地圖 app 找飯店。
22. We should leave now to catch the last bus.
我們應該現在出發，才趕得上末班公車。
23. My uncle told me that the trains in Japan were always on time.
我叔叔跟我說日本的火車總是很準時。
24. The hotel staff told us that we should take a taxi because it was raining.
飯店員工跟我們說，因為在下雨，我們應該搭計程車。
25. Mary said that the public transportation in her town was very good.
Mary 說她住的小鎮大眾運輸很好。
26. Tom told me that I shouldn't worry about the exam.
Tom 叫我不用擔心考試。
27. Everyone told me that I should try the ramen in Fukuoka.
每個人都跟我說我應該吃吃看福岡的拉麵。
28. My sister said she would rent a car to get around Hokkaido.
我姊姊說她會租車在北海道到處玩。
29. You should buy a day pass if you want to get around the city.
如果你想在市區到處跑，你應該買一日券。
30. The guide told us that the museum was closed on Mondays.
導遊跟我們說博物館星期一休館。
