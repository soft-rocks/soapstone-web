# This dryer doesn’t work. Something seems to be wrong with it.

## 1. seem to be / seem + 形容詞（`seem`）

句型公式：

**主詞 + `seem` + `to be` + 形容詞**

**主詞 + `seem` + 形容詞**

**主詞 + `seem` + `to` + 動詞原形**

`seem` 表示看起來像是。說話的人不確定，只是從看到的情況這樣判斷。後面可以接 `to be` 加形容詞，也可以直接接形容詞，意思一樣。`seem to` 也可以接其他動詞原形。原句裡，說話的人看到烘乾機不動，判斷它可能有問題。

* She seems to be tired.
  她似乎很累。
* The test seemed easy.
  考試似乎很簡單。
* He seems to know the answer.
  他似乎知道答案。

## 2. something is wrong with + 名詞（`something is wrong with`）

句型公式：

**`Something` / `Nothing` + `be` + `wrong with` + 名詞**

**`What's wrong with` + 名詞?**

`wrong with` 表示某個東西或某個人有問題。`wrong` 是形容詞，放在 `be` 動詞後面。`Nothing is wrong with` 表示沒有問題。問有什麼問題，用 `What's wrong with` 加名詞。原句把 `seems to be` 放在 `something` 和 `wrong` 中間，表示好像有問題。

* Something is wrong with my car.
  我的車有點問題。
* What's wrong with your leg?
  你的腳怎麼了？

## 3. 例句

1. This dryer doesn’t work. Something seems to be wrong with it.
這台烘乾機不能用了，好像哪裡壞掉了。
2. She seems to be tired.
她似乎很累。
3. The test seemed easy.
考試似乎很簡單。
4. He seems to know the answer.
他似乎知道答案。
5. The curry seems a little salty.
這咖哩似乎有點鹹。
6. The new teacher seems to be kind.
新老師似乎很親切。
7. Tom seemed to like the present.
Tom 似乎很喜歡那份禮物。
8. The restaurant seems to be closed today.
那家餐廳今天似乎沒開。
9. The new student seems shy.
新同學似乎很害羞。
10. It seems to be cold outside.
外面似乎很冷。
11. You don't seem to be busy today.
你今天似乎不忙。
12. Ann seems to know everyone at the party.
Ann 似乎認識派對上的每個人。
13. The shop seemed empty when we walked in.
我們走進去的時候，店裡似乎沒有人。
14. Your idea seems good.
你的點子似乎不錯。
15. Something is wrong with my car.
我的車有點問題。
16. What's wrong with your leg?
你的腳怎麼了？
17. Nothing is wrong with your computer. You just need to restart it.
你的電腦沒有問題。你只需要重新開機。
18. What's wrong with Ken? He looks sad.
Ken 怎麼了？他看起來很難過。
19. Something was wrong with the TV, so we called the shop.
電視出了點問題，所以我們打電話給店家。
20. The doctor said nothing was wrong with my heart.
醫生說我的心臟沒有問題。
21. Something is wrong with my eyes. I can't read the board.
我的眼睛有點問題。我看不清楚黑板。
22. Is something wrong with the bus? It isn't moving.
公車是不是有問題？怎麼都不動？
23. What's wrong with this door? It won't open.
這扇門怎麼了？打不開。
24. Nothing is wrong with the milk. You can drink it.
牛奶沒有問題。你可以喝。
25. What's wrong with the light? It keeps going off.
燈怎麼了？一直熄掉。
26. Something seems to be wrong with the printer.
印表機好像有什麼問題。
27. Something seemed to be wrong with my bike, so I took it to the shop.
我的腳踏車好像有什麼問題，所以我把它牽去店裡。
28. Nothing seems to be wrong with the engine.
引擎似乎沒有問題。
29. Something seems to be wrong with my phone. It turns off by itself.
我的手機好像有問題。會自己關機。
30. Something seems to be wrong with Tom. He hasn't said a word all day.
Tom 好像有什麼不對勁。他一整天都沒說一句話。
