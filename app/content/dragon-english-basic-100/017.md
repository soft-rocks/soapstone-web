# The best thing about my stay with an American family was that the parents treated me just like their daughter.

## 1. the + 最高級 + thing about + 名詞（`the best thing about`）

句型公式：

**`The` + 最高級 + `thing about` + 名詞**

這個句型說出一件事最好、最差或最難的地方。最高級前面加 `the`。`about` 後面接要說的那件事。

* The best thing about summer is the long vacation.
  夏天最棒的是長長的假期。
* The worst thing about the trip was the weather.
  這趟旅行最糟的是天氣。

## 2. be 動詞 + that 子句（`that`-clause as a complement）

句型公式：

**主詞 + `be` + `that` + 主詞 + 動詞**

`that` 子句放在 `be` 動詞後面，說明主詞的內容。原句的主詞是 `the best thing`。`that` 子句說明最棒的那件事是什麼。

* The problem is that we don't have enough time.
  問題是我們沒有足夠的時間。
* The good news is that Tom passed the test.
  好消息是 Tom 通過了考試。

## 3. treat + 人 + like / as（`treat` + person + `like` / `as`）

句型公式：

**`treat` + 人 + `like` / `as` + 名詞**

**`treat` + 人 + 副詞**

`treat` 表示用某種態度對待人。`like` 或 `as` 後面接名詞，表示把那個人當成那樣來對待。`just` 放在 `like` 前面，表示完全一樣。`treat` 也可以接 `kindly`、`well` 這類副詞，表示對待的態度。

* My host family treated me like a family member.
  我的寄宿家庭把我當家人一樣對待。
* They treated me as a guest.
  他們把我當客人對待。
* The teacher treats every student kindly.
  老師親切地對待每個學生。

## 4. 例句

1. The best thing about my stay with an American family was that the parents treated me just like their daughter.
住在美國家庭最棒的一點，是那對父母把我當成他們自己的女兒一樣看待。
2. The best thing about summer is the long vacation.
夏天最棒的是長長的假期。
3. The worst thing about the trip was the weather.
這趟旅行最糟的是天氣。
4. The most interesting thing about Kyoto is its old temples.
京都最有趣的是那些老寺廟。
5. What is the best thing about your new school?
你的新學校最棒的是什麼？
6. The hardest thing about learning Japanese is reading kanji.
學日文最難的是讀漢字。
7. The problem is that we don't have enough time.
問題是我們沒有足夠的時間。
8. The good news is that Tom passed the test.
好消息是 Tom 通過了考試。
9. The truth is that I forgot her birthday.
事實是我忘了她的生日。
10. My only worry is that it might rain on Saturday.
我唯一擔心的是星期六可能會下雨。
11. The reason I'm late is that I missed the bus.
我遲到的原因是我錯過了公車。
12. The best thing about this hotel is that it is near the station.
這家飯店最棒的是它離車站很近。
13. The worst thing about my job was that I had to work on weekends.
我那份工作最糟的是我週末必須上班。
14. My host family treated me like a family member.
我的寄宿家庭把我當家人一樣對待。
15. Don't treat me like a child.
不要把我當小孩子看。
16. They treated me as a guest.
他們把我當客人對待。
17. The teacher treats every student kindly.
老師親切地對待每個學生。
18. My grandmother treats her cat like a baby.
我奶奶把她的貓當嬰兒一樣對待。
19. The hotel staff treated us very well.
飯店員工對我們很好。
20. My classmates treated the new student as a friend from the first day.
同學們從第一天就把新同學當朋友。
21. The hardest thing about moving was saying goodbye to my friends.
搬家最難的是跟朋友說再見。
22. The trouble is that my bike has a flat tire.
麻煩的是我的腳踏車爆胎了。
23. The worst thing about winter is that it gets dark so early.
冬天最糟的是天黑得那麼早。
24. My teacher treats us like adults.
我的老師把我們當大人看待。
25. The funniest thing about the movie was the dog.
那部電影最好笑的是那隻狗。
26. The best thing about my stay in London was that I made many friends.
我在倫敦那段時間最棒的是我交了很多朋友。
27. The best thing about my homestay was that the family treated me like their son.
我寄宿期間最棒的是那家人把我當成他們的兒子一樣對待。
28. The best thing about the party was the food.
那場派對最棒的是食物。
29. What's the worst thing about your job?
你的工作最糟的是什麼？
30. Thank you for treating me like your own daughter.
謝謝你們把我當成自己的女兒一樣對待。
31. Ann's grandmother treats me just like her own grandchild.
Ann 的奶奶完全把我當成她自己的孫子一樣對待。
