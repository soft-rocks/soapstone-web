# I lived in Canada for three years when I was in my teens because my father was transferred there.

## 1. 過去式 + for + 一段時間（past simple with `for`）

句型公式：

**主詞 + 過去式 + `for` + 一段時間**

**主詞 + `have` / `has` + 過去分詞 + `for` + 一段時間**

`for` 加一段時間表示動作持續多久。用過去式表示那段時間已經結束了。原句表示我現在不住在加拿大。到現在還在持續的事用現在完成式。

* My grandfather worked at the post office for forty years.
  我爺爺在郵局工作了四十年。
* I have lived in this town for ten years.
  我已經在這個小鎮住了十年。

## 2. 被動語態 be + 過去分詞（passive voice）

句型公式：

**主詞 + `be` + 過去分詞 + (`by` + 做動作的人)**

被動語態表示主詞接受動作。原句的父親是被公司調職的，所以用 `was transferred`。做動作的人不重要或大家都知道時，句子裡不寫出來。要寫出來時用 `by`。

* My father was transferred to Osaka last year.
  我爸爸去年被調到大阪。
* The window was broken by the wind.
  窗戶被風吹破了。

## 3. 例句

1. I lived in Canada for three years when I was in my teens because my father was transferred there.
我十幾歲的時候在加拿大住了三年，因為我爸爸被調到那裡工作。
2. My grandfather worked at the post office for forty years.
我爺爺在郵局工作了四十年。
3. We waited for the bus for twenty minutes.
我們等公車等了二十分鐘。
4. I studied in London for a year.
我在倫敦讀了一年書。
5. I have lived in this town for ten years.
我已經在這個小鎮住了十年。
6. They stayed in Hokkaido for a week.
他們在北海道待了一個星期。
7. My aunt lived in Taiwan for ten years before she came back to Japan.
我阿姨回日本之前在台灣住了十年。
8. My mother worked as a nurse for ten years.
我媽媽當了十年護理師。
9. He played soccer for six years.
他踢了六年足球。
10. My grandfather was in the hospital for two weeks last year.
我爺爺去年住院兩個星期。
11. Our school was built fifty years ago.
我們學校是五十年前蓋的。
12. My parents have worked at this school for twenty years.
我爸媽在這所學校工作二十年了。
13. The bridge was closed for a week after the typhoon.
颱風過後那座橋關閉了一個星期。
14. My father was transferred to Osaka last year.
我爸爸去年被調到大阪。
15. This temple was built in 1600.
這座寺廟建於 1600 年。
16. The window was broken by the wind.
窗戶被風吹破了。
17. English is spoken in many countries.
很多國家都說英語。
18. My bike was stolen yesterday.
我的腳踏車昨天被偷了。
19. The game was stopped because of the rain.
比賽因為下雨被暫停了。
20. This song was written by a high school student.
這首歌是一個高中生寫的。
21. Tom was transferred to the Tokyo office in April.
Tom 四月被調到東京分公司。
22. When my father was transferred to Taichung, we lived there for two years.
我爸爸被調到台中的時候，我們在那裡住了兩年。
23. I was invited to the party by Ann.
Ann 邀我去參加派對。
24. She worked in a bank for five years in her twenties.
她二十幾歲時在銀行工作了五年。
25. In her teens, my mother lived in Kobe for a few years.
我媽媽十幾歲時在神戶住了幾年。
26. We were told to wait outside.
有人叫我們在外面等。
27. My uncle was sent to New York by his company.
我叔叔被公司派到紐約。
28. I took piano lessons for six years when I was a child.
我小時候學了六年鋼琴。
29. The school was closed for three days because of the typhoon.
學校因為颱風關閉了三天。
30. My brother lived in Australia for two years in his twenties.
我哥哥二十幾歲時在澳洲住了兩年。
