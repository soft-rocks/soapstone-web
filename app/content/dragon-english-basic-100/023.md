# I’m not wearing glasses now, so I can’t make out what the sign says.

## 1. 現在進行式和現在式（present progressive / present simple）

句型公式：

**主詞 + `am` / `is` / `are` + (`not`) + 動詞-ing + (`now`)**

**主詞 + 現在式**

現在式講平常的習慣。現在進行式講說話這個時候的情況。原句說的人平常可能戴眼鏡，只是現在沒戴，所以用 `I'm not wearing`。

* I usually wear glasses, but I'm not wearing them now.
  我平常戴眼鏡，但現在沒戴。
* We usually eat at home, but tonight we are eating out.
  我們平常在家吃飯，但今晚我們在外面吃。

## 2. 間接問句當受詞（indirect question as an object）

句型公式：

**動詞 + 疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞**

疑問句放進另一個句子裡當受詞時，用直述句的語序，主詞放在動詞前面。What does the sign say? 放進句子裡變成 `what the sign says`。助動詞 `does` 拿掉，動詞照主詞變化。疑問詞本身是主詞時，後面直接接動詞，像 `who is standing over there`。原句的 `make out` 表示勉強看清楚或聽清楚。`what the sign says` 是 `make out` 的受詞。

* Can you make out what she is saying?
  你聽得清楚她在說什麼嗎？
* Do you know what time the store opens?
  你知道那家店幾點開門嗎？

## 3. 例句

1. I’m not wearing glasses now, so I can’t make out what the sign says.
我現在沒戴眼鏡，所以看不清楚那個牌子上寫什麼。
2. I usually wear glasses, but I'm not wearing them now.
我平常戴眼鏡，但現在沒戴。
3. We usually eat at home, but tonight we are eating out.
我們平常在家吃飯，但今晚我們在外面吃。
4. She is wearing a red dress today.
她今天穿著一件紅色洋裝。
5. Students in my school wear uniforms.
我們學校的學生穿制服。
6. My father always wears a tie to work.
我爸爸上班總是打領帶。
7. Ann isn't wearing a watch today.
Ann 今天沒戴手錶。
8. Are you wearing your new shoes today?
你今天穿了新鞋嗎？
9. My brother usually walks to school, but today he is taking the bus.
我弟弟平常走路上學，但今天他搭公車。
10. Look! It's snowing.
你看！下雪了。
11. My mother is talking on the phone now, so she can't come to the door.
我媽媽現在在講電話，所以她沒辦法來開門。
12. I'm not using my bike now, so you can borrow it.
我現在沒有在用腳踏車，所以你可以借。
13. Tom isn't wearing a jacket, so he looks cold.
Tom 沒穿外套，所以他看起來很冷。
14. I'm wearing my contact lenses today, so I can see the blackboard clearly.
我今天戴了隱形眼鏡，所以黑板看得很清楚。
15. Can you make out what she is saying?
你聽得清楚她在說什麼嗎？
16. Do you know what time the store opens?
你知道那家店幾點開門嗎？
17. I don't know where Ken lives.
我不知道 Ken 住在哪裡。
18. Please tell me what this word means.
請告訴我這個字是什麼意思。
19. I wonder why Tom is so quiet today.
我在想 Tom 今天為什麼這麼安靜。
20. I can't make out who is standing over there.
我看不清楚站在那裡的是誰。
21. I couldn't make out what the guide said because the music was so loud.
音樂太大聲了，我聽不清楚導遊說了什麼。
22. Without my glasses, I can't make out what the clock says.
沒戴眼鏡，我看不清楚時鐘上是幾點。
23. Grandpa put on his glasses to read what the sign said.
爺爺戴上眼鏡去看告示上寫什麼。
24. I wasn't wearing my glasses, so I couldn't make out who was waving at me.
我當時沒戴眼鏡，所以看不清楚是誰在對我揮手。
25. Put on your glasses and tell me what the paper says.
戴上你的眼鏡，告訴我那張紙上寫什麼。
26. Can you see what the menu says?
你看得到菜單上寫什麼嗎？
27. I asked the clerk where the shoes were.
我問店員鞋子在哪裡。
28. Nobody knows what happened to the old man.
沒有人知道那位老先生發生了什麼事。
29. Could you tell me what the sign at the entrance says?
可以請你告訴我入口的告示上寫什麼嗎？
30. I can't remember what the teacher told us yesterday.
我想不起來老師昨天跟我們說了什麼。
