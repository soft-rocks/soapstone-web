# Without fossil fuels such as oil and coal, the history of the 20th century would have been completely different.

## 1. without 代替 if 子句（`without` in conditionals）

句型公式：

**`Without` + 名詞, + 主詞 + `would have` + 過去分詞**

**`Without` + 名詞, + 主詞 + `would` / `could` + 動詞原形**

`without` 加名詞可以代替一個假設的 `if` 子句。講過去的事，主要子句用 `would have` 加過去分詞。原句的意思是如果過去沒有化石燃料。講現在的事，主要子句用 `would` 加動詞原形。講現在能不能做到，用 `could` 加動詞原形。

* Without your help, I would have failed the test.
  沒有你的幫忙，我考試就會不及格了。
* Without water, we couldn't live.
  沒有水，我們就活不下去。

## 2. such as、like 和 for example（`such as` / `like` / `for example`）

句型公式：

**名詞 + `such as` / `like` + 例子**

**句子。 + `For example`, + 句子。**

`such as` 放在一個名詞後面，舉出屬於這個名詞的例子。例子可以是一個，也可以是兩三個。原句的 `oil and coal` 是 `fossil fuels` 的例子。口語常用 `like`，用法一樣。`such as` 和 `like` 後面只接例子，不接句子。要用一整句舉例時，用 `for example`，常放在句首，後面加逗號。

* I like fruit such as apples and bananas.
  我喜歡蘋果和香蕉這類水果。
* I love sweet food like cake and chocolate.
  我很愛蛋糕和巧克力這類甜食。
* Japan has many festivals. For example, Kyoto has the Gion Festival in July.
  日本有很多慶典。例如，京都七月有祇園祭。

## 3. 例句

1. Without fossil fuels such as oil and coal, the history of the 20th century would have been completely different.
要是沒有石油和煤這類化石燃料，二十世紀的歷史就會完全不一樣。
2. Without your help, I would have failed the test.
沒有你的幫忙，我考試就會不及格了。
3. Without water, we couldn't live.
沒有水，我們就活不下去。
4. Without the map, we would have got lost.
沒有那張地圖，我們就會迷路了。
5. Without my glasses, I couldn't read the sign.
沒有眼鏡，我就看不清楚那個標誌上的字。
6. Without his advice, we would have made a big mistake.
沒有他的建議，我們就會犯一個大錯。
7. Without phones, our lives would be very different.
沒有電話，我們的生活就會很不一樣。
8. Without the bus, I would have had to walk for an hour.
沒有那班公車，我就得走一個小時了。
9. I like fruit such as apples and bananas.
我喜歡蘋果和香蕉這類水果。
10. We visited cities such as Kyoto and Nara.
我們去了京都和奈良這些城市。
11. My brother plays sports such as tennis and soccer.
我哥哥會打網球、踢足球這類的運動。
12. You can buy things such as bread and milk at that store.
你可以在那家店買麵包和牛奶這些東西。
13. Animals such as cats and dogs need a lot of love.
貓和狗這類動物需要很多愛。
14. Taiwan has many night markets, such as Shilin Night Market.
台灣有很多夜市，像是士林夜市。
15. Without rain, rice couldn't grow.
沒有雨，稻米就長不出來。
16. Without music, life would be boring.
沒有音樂，生活會很無聊。
17. Without your phone call, I would have missed the train.
要不是你那通電話，我就會錯過火車了。
18. Without a dictionary, I couldn't read this book.
沒有字典，我就看不懂這本書。
19. Small animals such as rabbits are popular as pets.
兔子這類小動物很受歡迎，常被當成寵物。
20. Without computers, our work would be completely different.
沒有電腦，我們的工作就會完全不一樣。
21. Without the internet, I couldn't talk with my friends in America.
沒有網路，我就不能跟在美國的朋友聊天。
22. Without electricity, we couldn't use our phones.
沒有電，我們就不能用手機。
23. Without trains such as the Shinkansen, travel in Japan would be much slower.
沒有新幹線這類火車，在日本旅行就會慢很多。
24. Without the teacher's help, the school festival would have been a mess.
沒有老師的幫忙，校慶就會一團糟。
25. I don't eat food such as fried chicken very often.
我不常吃炸雞這類食物。
26. My mother likes Japanese food such as sushi and tempura.
我媽媽喜歡壽司和天婦羅這類日本料理。
27. Without my friends, my high school life would have been boring.
沒有我的朋友，我的高中生活就會很無聊。
28. Bring things such as a hat and water when you go hiking.
去爬山的時候帶帽子和水這些東西。
29. Without cars, people in the 20th century would have traveled less.
沒有汽車，二十世紀的人就會比較少旅行。
30. Without the bridge, people would have to take a boat.
要是沒有那座橋，大家就得坐船。
31. I love sweet food like cake and chocolate.
我很愛蛋糕和巧克力這類甜食。
32. Japan has many festivals. For example, Kyoto has the Gion Festival in July.
日本有很多慶典。例如，京都七月有祇園祭。
33. My brother likes sports like baseball and basketball.
我弟弟喜歡棒球和籃球這類運動。
34. There are many ways to save money. For example, you can cook at home.
存錢的方法有很多。例如，你可以在家煮飯。
35. Big cities such as Tokyo and Osaka are always crowded.
東京和大阪這類大城市總是很擁擠。
