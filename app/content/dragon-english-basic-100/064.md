# Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

## 1. 獨立分詞片語（`frankly speaking`）

句型公式：

**`Frankly` / `Generally` / `Honestly` + `speaking`, + 主要子句**

這類片語放在句首，表示說話的人用什麼態度說後面的話。`speaking` 的主詞是說話的人，不一定是後面子句的主詞。`frankly speaking` 是坦白說，`generally speaking` 是一般來說。

* Frankly speaking, I don't like this movie.
  坦白說，我不喜歡這部電影。
* Generally speaking, Japanese trains are on time.
  一般來說，日本的火車很準時。

## 2. far + 比較級（`far` + comparative）

句型公式：

**`far` / `much` + 比較級 + `than` ...**

`far` 放在比較級前面，表示差很多。它的意思跟 `much` 一樣。

* This suitcase is far heavier than mine.
  這個行李箱比我的重很多。
* My brother's room is far bigger than mine.
  我哥哥的房間比我的大很多。

## 3. than + 主詞 + had expected（`than I had expected`）

句型公式：

**比較級 + `than` + 主詞 + `had expected` / `had thought` / `expected` / `thought`**

`than I had expected` 是比我原本預料的更怎樣。預料發生在結果之前，所以用過去完成式。口語裡也常用過去式，說成 `than I expected` 或 `than I thought`。

* The trip was more expensive than we had expected.
  這趟旅行比我們原本預料的還貴。
* The soup was hotter than I thought.
  湯比我想的還燙。

## 4. 例句

1. Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.
坦白說，我昨天考的入學考試比我預期的難得多。
2. Frankly speaking, I don't like this movie.
坦白說，我不喜歡這部電影。
3. Generally speaking, Japanese trains are on time.
一般來說，日本的火車很準時。
4. Honestly speaking, I forgot your birthday.
老實說，我忘了你的生日。
5. Generally speaking, children learn languages faster than adults.
一般來說，小孩學語言比大人快。
6. Frankly speaking, your plan won't work.
坦白說，你的計畫行不通。
7. This suitcase is far heavier than mine.
這個行李箱比我的重很多。
8. My brother's room is far bigger than mine.
我哥哥的房間比我的大很多。
9. Osaka is far warmer than Sapporo in winter.
冬天的大阪比札幌溫暖很多。
10. My sister is much taller than me.
我姊姊比我高很多。
11. This summer was far hotter than last summer.
今年夏天比去年夏天熱很多。
12. Tom speaks English far better than I do.
Tom 英文說得比我好很多。
13. The trip was more expensive than we had expected.
這趟旅行比我們原本預料的還貴。
14. The soup was hotter than I thought.
湯比我想的還燙。
15. The hotel was smaller than we had expected.
那家飯店比我們原本預料的小。
16. The movie was longer than I had thought.
那部電影比我原本想的長。
17. The new teacher is kinder than I expected.
新老師比我預料的親切。
18. The line was far longer than we had expected.
隊伍比我們原本預料的長很多。
19. Frankly speaking, the food at the party was far better than I had expected.
坦白說，派對上的食物比我原本預料的好很多。
20. The math test I took last week was much easier than I had thought.
我上星期考的數學考試比我原本想的簡單很多。
21. Generally speaking, life in a big city is far more expensive than life in the country.
一般來說，大城市的生活比鄉下的生活貴很多。
22. Kyoto was far more crowded than we had expected.
京都比我們原本預料的擁擠很多。
23. Frankly speaking, I'm far more tired than I look.
坦白說，我其實比看起來累得多。
24. The walk to the station took far longer than I had expected.
走到車站花的時間比我原本預料的久很多。
25. Honestly speaking, the game was more exciting than I had thought.
老實說，那場比賽比我原本想的刺激。
26. My new room is far quieter than the old one.
我的新房間比舊的安靜很多。
27. The homework took less time than I expected.
功課花的時間比我預料的少。
28. Generally speaking, people in this town go to bed early.
一般來說，這個小鎮的人很早睡。
29. Frankly speaking, the interview was far harder than I had expected.
坦白說，那場面試比我原本預料的難很多。
30. The tickets for the concert were much cheaper than we had thought.
演唱會的票比我們原本想的便宜很多。
31. The party ended earlier than we thought.
派對比我們想的早結束。
