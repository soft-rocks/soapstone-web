# In some countries people have too much food, while in others tens of thousands of children are starving.

## 1. some ... others（`some` ... `others`）

句型公式：

**`some` + 複數名詞 ..., + `others` ...**

`some` 指一群中的一部分。`others` 指另一部分。原句的 `others` 是 `other countries`，名詞省略了。`the others` 指剩下的全部。

* Some students walk to school, and others take the bus.
  有些學生走路上學，有些搭公車。
* Some people like summer, while others like winter.
  有些人喜歡夏天，有些人喜歡冬天。

## 2. 表示對比的 while（`while` for contrast）

句型公式：

**子句, + `while` + 子句**

`while` 在這裡不表示時間，表示兩件事的對比。兩個子句講的是不同的人或地方。`while` 前面常加逗號。

* My brother likes sports, while I like reading.
  我哥哥喜歡運動，而我喜歡看書。
* It is raining in Taipei, while it is snowing in Hokkaido.
  台北在下雨，而北海道在下雪。

## 3. tens of thousands of（數以萬計的）

句型公式：

**`hundreds` / `thousands` / `tens of thousands` + `of` + 複數名詞**

這些說法表示很大但不精確的數量。`hundreds of` 是好幾百，`thousands of` 是好幾千，`tens of thousands of` 是好幾萬。`hundred` 和 `thousand` 前面有確定的數目時，不加 `-s`，後面也不加 `of`。

* Thousands of people came to the festival.
  好幾千人來參加慶典。
* There are hundreds of books in my room.
  我房間裡有好幾百本書。

## 4. 例句

1. In some countries people have too much food, while in others tens of thousands of children are starving.
有些國家的人食物多到吃不完，另一些國家卻有好幾萬個小孩在挨餓。
2. Some students walk to school, and others take the bus.
有些學生走路上學，有些搭公車。
3. Some people like summer, while others like winter.
有些人喜歡夏天，有些人喜歡冬天。
4. Some of my friends went home, but the others stayed.
我有些朋友回家了，但其他的人都留下來。
5. In some cities it is easy to find a job, while in others it is hard.
在一些城市找工作很容易，而在其他城市很難。
6. Some dogs are friendly, and others are not.
有些狗很友善，有些則不是。
7. I bought three cakes. One is for you, and the others are for my sisters.
我買了三個蛋糕。一個給你，其他的給我的姊妹。
8. My brother likes sports, while I like reading.
我哥哥喜歡運動，而我喜歡看書。
9. It is raining in Taipei, while it is snowing in Hokkaido.
台北在下雨，而北海道在下雪。
10. Tom is very quiet, while his sister talks a lot.
Tom 很安靜，而他妹妹話很多。
11. Some people waste food, while others don't have enough to eat.
有些人浪費食物，而有些人吃不飽。
12. I like coffee, while my wife prefers tea.
我喜歡咖啡，而我太太比較喜歡茶。
13. My father gets up early, while my mother sleeps late.
我爸爸很早起，而我媽媽都睡到很晚。
14. Thousands of people came to the festival.
好幾千人來參加慶典。
15. There are hundreds of books in my room.
我房間裡有好幾百本書。
16. Tens of thousands of tourists visit Kyoto every week.
每個星期有好幾萬個遊客去京都。
17. Hundreds of birds were flying over the lake.
好幾百隻鳥在湖上飛。
18. About five thousand people came to the concert.
大約五千人來看那場演唱會。
19. Thousands of students take this test every year.
每年有好幾千個學生考這個考試。
20. In some countries children go to school for free, while in others they have to pay.
在一些國家，小孩上學不用錢，而在其他國家要付錢。
21. Tens of thousands of people lost their homes in the earthquake.
好幾萬人在地震中失去了家。
22. Some shops close at eight, while others stay open all night.
有些店八點關門，而其他店整晚營業。
23. Hundreds of volunteers helped after the flood.
洪水之後好幾百個志工來幫忙。
24. In some parts of Japan it snows a lot, while in others it hardly snows.
在日本的一些地方雪下得很多，而在其他地方幾乎不下雪。
25. Some of the children were laughing, while others were crying.
有些孩子在笑，而其他孩子在哭。
26. Thousands of fans waited outside the stadium.
好幾千個球迷在體育場外面等。
27. We have too much food at home, while many people around the world are hungry.
我們家有太多食物，而世界各地很多人在挨餓。
28. Some trains stop at every station, and others stop only at big ones.
有些火車每站都停，有些只停大站。
29. Tens of thousands of people ran in the Tokyo Marathon.
好幾萬人參加了東京馬拉松。
30. About three hundred students go to this school.
大約三百個學生在這所學校讀書。
