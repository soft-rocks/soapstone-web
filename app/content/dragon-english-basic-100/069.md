# There is a saying that a cold can cause a variety of diseases. Take care not to catch a cold.

## 1. 同位語 that 子句（appositive `that` clause）

句型公式：

**`a saying` / `the idea` / `the news` + `that` + 子句**

`that` 子句放在 `saying`、`idea`、`news` 這類名詞後面，說明這個名詞的內容。原句的 `that` 子句就是那句俗話的內容。這個 `that` 在子句裡不當主詞，也不當受詞。

* There is a saying that time is money.
  有一句話說時間就是金錢。
* I heard the news that Tom won the race.
  我聽到 Tom 贏了賽跑的消息。

## 2. take care not to + 動詞原形（negative infinitive）

句型公式：

**`take care` / `be careful` + `not to` + 動詞原形**

`not` 放在 `to` 前面，表示不要做那件事。`take care not to` 和 `be careful not to` 都是小心不要做某事。

* Be careful not to drop the cups.
  小心不要把杯子弄掉。
* Take care not to wake the baby.
  小心不要吵醒寶寶。

## 3. can 表示可能性（`can` for possibility）

句型公式：

**主詞 + `can` + 動詞原形**

`can` 除了表示能力，也表示一件事有時候會發生。原句的 `a cold can cause a variety of diseases` 表示感冒有可能引起各種疾病，不是每次都會。

* Too much sun can hurt your skin.
  曬太多太陽可能會傷害你的皮膚。
* A small mistake can cause a big problem.
  一個小錯誤可能造成大問題。

## 4. 例句

1. There is a saying that a cold can cause a variety of diseases. Take care not to catch a cold.
有句話說感冒是萬病之源。小心別感冒了。
2. There is a saying that time is money.
有一句話說時間就是金錢。
3. I heard the news that Tom won the race.
我聽到 Tom 贏了賽跑的消息。
4. There is an old saying that the early bird catches the worm.
有一句老話說早起的鳥兒有蟲吃。
5. I like the idea that everyone should help each other.
我喜歡每個人都應該互相幫忙這個想法。
6. The news that the school would close surprised everyone.
學校要關閉的消息讓每個人都很驚訝。
7. There is a saying in Japan that a smile brings good luck.
日本有一句話說，笑容會帶來好運。
8. Be careful not to drop the cups.
小心不要把杯子弄掉。
9. Take care not to wake the baby.
小心不要吵醒寶寶。
10. Be careful not to get lost in the old town.
在舊城區小心不要迷路。
11. Take care not to eat too much at the party.
在派對上小心不要吃太多。
12. Please be careful not to leave anything on the train.
請小心不要把任何東西忘在火車上。
13. She took care not to make any mistakes.
她很小心，不讓自己犯任何錯。
14. Too much sun can hurt your skin.
曬太多太陽可能會傷害你的皮膚。
15. This road can be dangerous in winter.
這條路冬天可能很危險。
16. Eating too fast can give you a stomachache.
吃太快可能會讓你肚子痛。
17. The weather in the mountains can change quickly.
山上的天氣可能變得很快。
18. A small mistake can cause a big problem.
一個小錯誤可能造成大問題。
19. Too much stress can cause a variety of problems.
壓力太大可能會引起各種問題。
20. Be careful not to catch a cold before the exam.
考試前小心不要感冒。
21. Take care not to get wet on the way home.
回家路上小心不要淋濕。
22. There is a saying that laughter is the best medicine.
有一句話說笑是最好的藥。
23. Trains can be very crowded in the morning.
早上火車可能很擠。
24. I was careful not to say anything about her new hairstyle.
我很小心，對她的新髮型什麼都沒說。
25. There is a saying that practice makes perfect.
有一句話說熟能生巧。
26. Be careful not to spend all your money on the first day of the trip.
小心不要在旅行的第一天就把錢花光。
27. In winter, my grandmother takes care not to catch a cold.
冬天的時候，我奶奶很小心不要感冒。
28. I don't agree with the idea that boys shouldn't cry.
我不同意男生不應該哭這個想法。
29. Children can catch a cold easily in winter.
冬天小孩很容易感冒。
30. There is a saying that a cold is the start of every disease, so take care not to catch one.
有一句話說，感冒是萬病之源，所以小心不要感冒。
