When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.

等電梯來或等紅綠燈變換的時候，大部分日本人只要三十秒就會不耐煩。

when 後⁠面省⁠略主⁠詞和 be 動⁠詞，直⁠接接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.

這是時間子句的省略形式。while 也可以這樣省略。

省⁠略⁠的主⁠詞跟主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞相⁠同

When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.

等公車的時候，我看了一本書。

原句省略的主詞是 most Japanese people。

wait for 後⁠面先接⁠受⁠詞，再⁠接 to 不⁠定⁠詞

When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.

我們等雨停。

受詞是等待的對象。to 不定詞說明那個對象要做的事。

兩組受⁠詞和 to 不⁠定⁠詞共⁠用 waiting for

When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞 become 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞，表⁠示進⁠入一⁠種狀⁠態

When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.

等電梯來或等紅綠燈變換的時候，大部分日本人只要三十秒就會不耐煩。

表⁠示人⁠的感⁠覺⁠的形⁠容⁠詞常⁠用 ⁠-⁠ed 結⁠尾

例句

  1. When waiting for the elevator to come or the traffic light to change, most Japanese people become irritated in only thirty seconds.
    等電梯來或等紅綠燈變換的時候，大部分日本人只要三十秒就會不耐煩。
  2. When crossing the street, look both ways.
    過馬路的時候要看左右兩邊。
  3. When using the microwave, don't put metal plates in it.
    用微波爐的時候，不要把金屬盤子放進去。
  4. Be careful when walking on ice.
    走在冰上的時候要小心。
  5. Tom often listens to music when studying.
    Tom 讀書的時候常常聽音樂。
  6. While waiting for the bus, I read a book.
    等公車的時候，我看了一本書。
  7. She hurt her leg while playing tennis.
    她打網球的時候傷到了腿。
  8. Please don't talk while eating.
    吃東西的時候請不要說話。
  9. I fell asleep while watching TV.
    我看電視的時候睡著了。
  10. We waited for the rain to stop.
    我們等雨停。
  11. I'm waiting for my friend to call me.
    我在等我朋友打電話給我。
  12. The students waited for the teacher to come back.
    學生們等老師回來。
  13. Wait for the train to stop before you get on.
    上車之前要等火車停下來。
  14. We are waiting for the water to boil.
    我們在等水燒開。
  15. Everyone waited for the movie to start.
    每個人都在等電影開始。
  16. She waited for her son to come home.
    她等她兒子回家。
  17. I became bored halfway through the movie.
    電影看到一半我就覺得無聊了。
  18. My mother became worried when I didn't come home.
    我沒回家的時候，我媽媽開始擔心。
  19. He became very quiet after the phone call.
    接完那通電話之後，他變得很安靜。
  20. The passengers became irritated when the train stopped again.
    火車又停下來的時候，乘客開始不耐煩。
  21. The teacher became angry because nobody did the homework.
    因為沒有人寫作業，老師生氣了。
  22. The children became excited when they saw the snow.
    孩子們看到雪的時候興奮起來。
  23. My grandfather became very sick last winter.
    我爺爺去年冬天病得很重。
  24. People become irritated when they wait in a long line.
    人排長隊的時候會不耐煩。
  25. When driving, you shouldn't use your phone.
    開車的時候不應該用手機。
  26. While walking to school, I met an old friend.
    走路去學校的時候，我遇到一個老朋友。
  27. When waiting for a friend, I usually read a book.
    等朋友的時候，我通常看書。
  28. I waited for the light to turn green.
    我等燈號變綠。
  29. We waited for the plane to land and the doors to open.
    我們等飛機降落，再等機門打開。
  30. Most students become bored when the class is too long.
    課太長的時候，大部分的學生會覺得無聊。
  31. While waiting for my turn, I became more and more nervous.
    在等著輪到我的時候，我越來越緊張。
