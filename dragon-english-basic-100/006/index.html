An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

火車上坐在我旁邊的一位老太太問我要去哪裡。

名⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 片⁠語修⁠飾這⁠個名⁠詞

火車上坐在我旁邊的一位老太太問我要去哪裡。

片語說明這個人正在做什麼。

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 片⁠語等⁠於省⁠略了 who 和 be 動⁠詞

An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

ask 後⁠面先接⁠人，再⁠接間⁠接⁠問⁠句

An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

她問我現在幾點。

間⁠接⁠問⁠句用直⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序，主⁠詞在動⁠詞前⁠面

An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

沒⁠有疑⁠問⁠詞⁠的問⁠題，用 if 或 whether 帶⁠出間⁠接⁠問⁠句

An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

if

The teacher asked us if we had any questions.

老師問我們有沒有問題。
whether

I asked Ann whether she wanted to come with us.

我問 Ann 她想不想跟我們一起來。

主⁠要動⁠詞是過⁠去⁠式，間⁠接⁠問⁠句⁠的動⁠詞也改⁠成過⁠去⁠式

An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

老太太當時直接問的話是 Where are you going?

knew、wondered 後⁠面⁠的間⁠接⁠問⁠句也用過⁠去⁠式

An elderly woman sitting next to me on the train asked me where I was going.

know

Nobody knew what time the store closed.

沒有人知道那家店幾點關門。
wonder

I wondered why the bus was so late.

我很納悶公車怎麼這麼晚。
