Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

每個人都應該能自由決定什麼時候結婚、要不要生小孩。

be free to 表⁠示主⁠詞可⁠以照自⁠己⁠的意⁠思做某⁠件⁠事

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

學生可以自由使用圖書館的電腦。

沒有人限制主詞。

feel free to 請對⁠方不⁠用客⁠氣，直⁠接做某⁠件⁠事

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

有任何問題都可以問我，不用客氣。

疑⁠問⁠詞加 to 不⁠定⁠詞⁠的作⁠用跟名⁠詞一⁠樣

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

我們還沒決定今年夏天要去哪裡。

它常放在 decide、know、ask、tell 後面當受詞。

疑⁠問⁠詞加 to 不⁠定⁠詞表⁠示應⁠該做⁠的事

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

what

I don't know what to say.

我不知道要說什麼。
how

Can you tell me how to get to the station?

你可以告訴我怎麼去車站嗎？
when

Please tell us when to start.

請告訴我們什麼時候開始。

原句的 when to get married 是應該什麼時候結婚。

whether to 表⁠示要不⁠要做某⁠件⁠事

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

or not

I can't decide whether to go or not.

我無法決定要不要去。
or + 另一個選擇

She is thinking about whether to study in Japan or in Korea.

她在考慮要去日本還是韓國讀書。

or not 可以省略。
if 不接 to 不定詞，這個位置只用 whether。

get married 是結⁠婚⁠的動⁠作，be married 是已⁠婚⁠的狀⁠態

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

get married
動作

My sister got married last year.

我姊姊去年結婚了。
be married
狀態

My parents have been married for twenty years.

我爸媽結婚二十年了。

get married 常跟過去式和時間一起用。
be married 常跟現在式或 for 加一段時間一起用。

get married 和 be married 用 to 接⁠人，marry 直⁠接接人

Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.

get married to

Ann got married to a Japanese man.

Ann 嫁給了一個日本人。
be married to

Ken is married to a nurse.

Ken 跟一位護理師結婚了。
marry

He married a girl from Osaka.

他跟一個大阪來的女孩結婚了。

跟誰結婚不用 with。

訂⁠婚和離⁠婚也分⁠成動⁠作和狀⁠態

get engaged
動作

My brother got engaged last month.

我哥哥上個月訂婚了。
be engaged
狀態

My best friend is engaged to a doctor.

我最好的朋友跟一位醫生訂婚了。
get divorced
動作

My aunt and uncle got divorced last year.

我阿姨和姨丈去年離婚了。
be divorced
狀態

Her parents are divorced.

她爸媽離婚了。

例句

  1. Everyone should be free to decide when to get married and whether to have children.
