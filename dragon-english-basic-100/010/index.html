Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

Tom 堅持說一切都會沒事，可是我還是忍不住擔心。

insist that 堅⁠持說某⁠件⁠事是真⁠的，insist on 堅⁠持要做某⁠件⁠事

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

insist that
堅持說

She insisted that she was right.

她堅持說她是對的。
insist on
堅持要做

My father insisted on paying for dinner.

我爸爸堅持要付晚餐的錢。

insist that 常用在別人不相信的時候。

insist on 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

Ann 堅持要自己走路回家。

主⁠要動⁠詞是過⁠去⁠式，之⁠後⁠的事用 would

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

would 是 will 的過去式。Tom 當時說的話是 Everything will be all right.

said、thought 後⁠面講之⁠後⁠的⁠事，也用 would

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

said

He said he would call me.

他說他會打電話給我。
thought

I thought it would rain.

我本來以為會下雨。

can't help 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 表⁠示忍⁠不⁠住做某⁠件⁠事

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

Tom 堅持說一切都會沒事，可是我還是忍不住擔心。

這裡的 help 的意思是避免。

現⁠在⁠式用 can't help，過⁠去⁠式用 couldn't help

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

現在式

She can't help eating sweets.

她忍不住會吃甜食。
過去式

When I saw the photo, I couldn't help crying.

我看到那張照片的時候，忍不住哭了。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞 feel 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞，表⁠示主⁠詞⁠的感⁠覺

Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

他演講前覺得很緊張。

表示人的感覺的形容詞常用 -⁠ed 結尾，像 worried。

例句

  1. Tom insisted that everything would be all right, but I couldn’t help feeling worried.

    她堅持說她是對的。

    Ann 堅持要自己走路回家。

    他堅持說那家餐廳是鎮上最好的。
  7. He said he would call me.
    他說他會打電話給我。
  8. I thought it would rain.
    我本來以為會下雨。

    天氣預報說明天會是晴天。

    她答應會很快回來。
  11. I knew you would like this book.
    我就知道你會喜歡這本書。
  12. My teacher told me that I would pass the test.
    我的老師跟我說我會通過考試。
  13. I couldn't help laughing.
    我忍不住笑出來。
  14. She can't help eating sweets.
    她忍不住會吃甜食。
  15. When I saw the photo, I couldn't help crying.
    我看到那張照片的時候，忍不住哭了。
  16. I can't help thinking about the exam.
    我忍不住一直想著考試的事。
  17. We couldn't help smiling at the baby.
    我們忍不住對著小嬰兒微笑。
  18. I feel tired today.
    我今天覺得很累。
  19. He felt nervous before the speech.
    他演講前覺得很緊張。
  20. Do you feel better now?
    你現在覺得好一點了嗎？

    我兒子沒回家的時候，我覺得很擔心。
  22. Everyone felt excited before the school trip.
    校外教學之前大家都覺得很興奮。
  23. Ken insisted that he had locked the door.
    Ken 堅持說他有鎖門。

    醫生說我過幾天就會覺得好一點。
  25. Ken insisted that he was fine, but he looked pale.
    Ken 堅持說他沒事，可是他臉色看起來很蒼白。
  26. I couldn't help feeling sorry for the lost child.
    我忍不住為那個走失的孩子感到難過。
  27. The movie was so sad that I couldn't help crying.
    那部電影太悲傷了，我忍不住哭了。
  28. She felt happy because everyone remembered her birthday.
    她覺得很開心，因為每個人都記得她的生日。
  29. My friends insisted on taking me to the hospital.
    我的朋友們堅持要帶我去醫院。
  30. My father said that everything would be all right, so I felt relieved.
    我爸爸說一切都會沒事，所以我覺得放心了。
