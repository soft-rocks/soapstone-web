“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

「Tom，晚餐好了。」「好，媽，我馬上來。」

come 往說⁠話⁠的人或聽⁠話⁠的人那⁠裡，go 往別⁠的地⁠方

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

come

Can you come to my party on Saturday?

你星期六可以來我的派對嗎？
go

I'm going to the library now.

我現在要去圖書館。

原句裡 Tom 要走到媽媽那裡，所以用 come。

bring 帶⁠到說⁠話⁠的人或聽⁠話⁠的人那⁠裡，take 帶⁠到別⁠的地⁠方

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

bring

Can I bring my sister to the party?

我可以帶我妹妹來派對嗎？
take

Take this letter to the post office.

把這封信拿去郵局。

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式可⁠以表⁠示馬⁠上就⁠要做⁠的事

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

原句的 I’m coming 表示我現在就過去。

come、go、leave 常⁠用現⁠在進⁠行⁠式表⁠示馬⁠上要移⁠動

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

come

Hurry up. The bus is coming.

快點，公車要來了。
go

My sister is going out now.

我姊姊現在要出門。
leave

I'm leaving now. See you tomorrow.

我現在要走了。明天見。

東⁠西當主⁠詞⁠時，be ready 表⁠示東⁠西可⁠以用或可⁠以吃⁠了

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

「Tom，晚餐好了。」「好，媽，我馬上來。」

ready 是形容詞，意思是準備好了。

人當主⁠詞⁠時，be ready 後⁠面⁠接 to 加動⁠詞⁠原⁠形或 for 加名⁠詞

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

to + 動詞原形

Are you ready to order?

你準備好點餐了嗎？
for + 名詞

We are ready for the trip.

我們準備好去旅行了。

be ready 是已⁠經準⁠備⁠好，get ready 是去做準⁠備

“Tom, dinner is ready.” “OK, Mom. I’m coming.”

be ready

The students are ready for the final exam.

學生們準備好期末考了。
get ready

The children are getting ready for school.

孩子們正在準備上學。
