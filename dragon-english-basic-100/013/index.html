I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.

我到成田機場的時候會打電話給你。

will 可⁠以表⁠示對別⁠人⁠的承⁠諾

我到成田機場的時候會打電話給你。

原句是對聽話的人承諾會打電話。口語常把 will 縮寫成 ’ll。

will 也⁠可⁠以表⁠示說⁠話當⁠下才做⁠的決⁠定

I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.

電話響了

“The phone is ringing.” “I'll get it.”

「電話在響。」「我來接。」
覺得冷

It's cold. I'll close the window.

好冷，我來關窗戶。

時⁠間子⁠句講未⁠來⁠的⁠事，也用現⁠在⁠式

I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.

主要子句常用 will 或祈使句。

when、before、after、as soon as 帶⁠出時⁠間子⁠句

I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.

when

I'll text you when I arrive.

我到的時候會傳訊息給你。
before

I'll clean my room before Mom comes home.

我會在媽媽回家之前把房間打掃好。
after

We'll go to the park after it stops raining.

雨停了之後我們會去公園。
as soon as

I'll call you as soon as I get to the hotel.

我一到飯店就會打電話給你。

時⁠間子⁠句放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，後⁠面加逗⁠號

I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.

雨停的時候，我們就出去。

時⁠間子⁠句⁠的動⁠作先做⁠完⁠時，可⁠以用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式

I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.

我看完這本書會借你。

先看完書，主要子句的動作才發生。

例句

  1. I’ll call you when I get to Narita Airport.
    我到成田機場的時候會打電話給你。
  2. I'll carry that bag for you.
    我來幫你提那個袋子。
  3. Don't worry. I'll be there on time.
    別擔心，我會準時到。
  4. It's cold. I'll close the window.
    好冷，我來關窗戶。
  5. I'll pay for lunch today.
    今天的午餐我來付。
  6. “The phone is ringing.” “I'll get it.”
    「電話在響。」「我來接。」
  7. We'll send you the photos tonight.
    我們今晚會把照片寄給你。
  8. I'll never forget your kindness.
    我永遠不會忘記你的好意。
  9. I'll text you when I arrive.
    我到的時候會傳訊息給你。
  10. I'll lend you the book when I have finished it.
    我看完這本書會借你。
  11. When you get home, please call your grandmother.
    你到家的時候，請打電話給你奶奶。
  12. We'll have dinner when Dad comes back.
    爸爸回來的時候我們就吃晚餐。
  13. You can watch TV when you have done your homework.
    你寫完功課就可以看電視。
  14. Let me know when you have decided.
    你決定好了就告訴我。
  15. I'll tell her the news when she wakes up.
    她醒來的時候我會告訴她這個消息。
  16. When the rain stops, we'll go out.
    雨停的時候，我們就出去。
  17. We'll open the presents when Grandma arrives.
    奶奶到了，我們就拆禮物。
  18. Will you call me when the package arrives?
    包裹到的時候，你會打電話給我嗎？
  19. When you're ready, we'll leave.
    你準備好我們就出發。
  20. I'll help you with your homework when I have washed the dishes.
    我洗完碗就教你寫功課。
  21. When I see Ann tomorrow, I'll give her the book.
    我明天見到 Ann 的時候，會把書給她。
  22. I'll call you as soon as I get to the hotel.
    我一到飯店就會打電話給你。
  23. Call me as soon as you hear any news.
    你一有消息就打電話給我。
  24. Please wait for me at the exit when you arrive at the airport.
    你到機場的時候，請在出口等我。
  25. We'll start the meeting when everyone gets here.
    大家到齊了，我們就開始開會。
  26. I'll lend you my bike.
    我把腳踏車借給你。
  27. I'll email you when I have checked the schedule.
    我確認完行程表會寄電子郵件給你。
  28. I'm tired. I think I'll go to bed.
    我累了。我想我要去睡了。
  29. I promise I'll come back next year.
    我保證我明年會回來。
  30. Ken will meet us when we get to Shinjuku Station.
    我們到新宿車站的時候，Ken 會跟我們碰面。
  31. I'll buy you a souvenir when I go to Hokkaido.
    我去北海道的時候會買紀念品給你。
  32. I'll clean my room before Mom comes home.
    我會在媽媽回家之前把房間打掃好。
  33. Before you go to bed, I'll read you a story.
    你睡覺之前，我會唸一個故事給你聽。
  34. We'll go to the park after it stops raining.
    雨停了之後我們會去公園。
  35. After I finish this, I'll help you.
    我做完這個之後就來幫你。
