If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

如果你從這裡搭火車去東京迪士尼樂園，要換三次車。

if 子⁠句⁠的條⁠件是未⁠來⁠的⁠事，也用現⁠在⁠式，不⁠用 will

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

如果你從這裡搭火車去東京迪士尼樂園，要換三次車。

if 句⁠的主⁠要⁠子⁠句可⁠以用 will、have to、can 或祈⁠使⁠句

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

will

If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.

如果明天下雨，我們就待在家。
have to

If you go by car, you have to pay for parking.

如果你開車去，就必須付停車費。
can

If we leave now, we can catch the eight o'clock bus.

如果我們現在出發，就趕得上八點的公車。
祈使句

Call me if you get lost.

如果你迷路了，打電話給我。

if 子⁠句可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首或句⁠尾，放⁠在句⁠首⁠時加逗⁠號

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

句首

If you are hungry, eat this sandwich.

如果你餓了，吃這個三明治吧。
句尾

You can use my bike if you need it.

如果你需要，可以用我的腳踏車。

have to 表⁠示因⁠為規⁠定或情⁠況，必⁠須做某⁠件⁠事

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

我明天必須六點起床。

has to 用在第⁠三⁠人⁠稱單⁠數，had to 是過⁠去⁠式

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

第三人稱單數

My brother has to wear a uniform at school.

我弟弟在學校必須穿制服。
過去式

She had to wait for an hour.

她只好等了一個小時。
不需要

You don't have to bring lunch.

你不用帶午餐。

don't have to 表示不需要做。

take 加交⁠通⁠工⁠具表⁠示搭那⁠個交⁠通⁠工⁠具

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

by 加交⁠通⁠工⁠具⁠時，名⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 the、a 或 my

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

take

I take the bus to school.

我搭公車上學。
by

爺爺搭飛機來東京。
走路

My sister goes to school on foot.

我妹妹走路上學。

走路用 on foot，不用 by foot。

在公⁠車、火⁠車、飛⁠機上用 on，在汽⁠車、計⁠程⁠車⁠裡用 in

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

on + 公車

in + 計程車

in + 汽車

換車說 change trains，名⁠詞用複⁠數

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

我在路上換了兩次公車。

換車會用到兩班車，所以 trains、buses、planes 用複數。

change 後⁠面省⁠略 trains，意⁠思不⁠變

If you take the train to Tokyo Disneyland from here, you have to change three times.

換車⁠的地⁠點用 at 加車⁠站，或用 in 加城⁠市
