I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

我已經決定大學畢業後要找國外的工作。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式表⁠示過⁠去⁠的動⁠作跟現⁠在有⁠關

我已經決定大學畢業後要找國外的工作。

決定已經做了，現在還是這個決定。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式是 have 或 has 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

have

I've lost my key.

我把鑰匙弄丟了。
has

She has finished her homework.

她已經寫完功課了。

decide 後⁠面⁠接 to 加動⁠詞⁠原⁠形

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

我們決定去露營。

決⁠定不⁠做某⁠件⁠事，not 放⁠在 to 前⁠面

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

他決定不買那輛車。

abroad 是副⁠詞，表⁠示在國⁠外或到國⁠外

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

在國外

My sister studies abroad.

我姊姊在國外讀書。
到國外

Many people go abroad in summer.

很多人夏天出國。

abroad 前面不加 to 或 in。

abroad 放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面修⁠飾名⁠詞

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

原句的 a job abroad 的意思是國外的工作。

時⁠間子⁠句講未⁠來⁠的⁠事，動⁠詞用現⁠在⁠式，不⁠用 will

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

時⁠間子⁠句可⁠以放⁠在主⁠要⁠子⁠句後⁠面，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首

I’ve decided to look for a job abroad when I graduate from college.

主要子句後面

What will you do after you graduate from college?

你大學畢業後要做什麼？
句首

When I graduate from high school, I'll be eighteen.

我高中畢業的時候就十八歲了。

放在句首時，後面加逗號。主要子句照常用 will。
