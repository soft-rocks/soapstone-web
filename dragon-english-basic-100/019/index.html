The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

網路被廣泛使用，所以個人電腦的銷售量過去幾年一直快速成長。

被⁠動⁠語⁠態⁠的副⁠詞放⁠在 be 動⁠詞和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

widely 表示範圍很廣。

well、often、highly 也放⁠在 be 動⁠詞和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

often

This bridge is often used by students.

學生常走這座橋。
well

The new teacher is well liked by the students.

新老師很受學生喜愛。
highly

This doctor is highly respected in the village.

這位醫生在村子裡很受尊敬。

現⁠在完⁠成進⁠行⁠式表⁠示動⁠作從過⁠去一⁠直持⁠續到現⁠在

The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

原句表示銷售量這幾年一直在增加。這個動作可能還會繼續。

現⁠在完⁠成進⁠行⁠式常跟 for 或 since 一⁠起用

The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

for

I have been studying English for six years.

我學英文已經學了六年。
since

It has been snowing since last night.

從昨晚開始一直在下雪。

over the last 加時⁠間，表⁠示從過⁠去到現⁠在⁠的這⁠段期⁠間

The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

last 和 past 在這裡意思一樣。

over the last 常跟現⁠在完⁠成⁠式一⁠起用

The internet is widely used, so sales of personal computers have been rapidly increasing over the last few years.

the last

Prices have gone up over the last two years.

過去兩年物價上漲了。
the past

The town has changed a lot over the past ten years.

過去十年這個小鎮改變了很多。

現在完成進行式也常跟 over the last 一起用。
