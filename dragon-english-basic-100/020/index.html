I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

我常聽 Tom 吹噓說他很會游泳，可是我從來沒有真的看過他游泳。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式表⁠示到現⁠在為⁠止⁠的經⁠驗，副⁠詞放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

often 表示經常，never 表示從來沒有。

經⁠驗⁠的問⁠句用 ever

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

never

I've never eaten natto.

我從來沒吃過納豆。
often

We have often talked about this.

我們常常談到這件事。
ever，問句

Has Tom ever cooked dinner for you?

Tom 有煮過晚餐給你吃嗎？

have been to 加地⁠方表⁠示去過那⁠個地⁠方

我爸爸去過韓國三次。

感⁠官⁠動⁠詞後⁠面先接⁠受⁠詞，再⁠接動⁠詞⁠原⁠形

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

完成式裡也一樣，過去分詞後面接受詞和動詞原形。

感⁠官⁠動⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞⁠原⁠形前⁠面不⁠加 to

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

我從來沒聽過她唱歌。

感⁠官⁠動⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞是代⁠名⁠詞⁠時用受格

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

be good at 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞或動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

名詞

My sister is good at math.

我妹妹數學很好。
動詞-ing

Ann is good at playing the violin.

Ann 很會拉小提琴。

be good at 表示很會做某件事。at 是介系詞。

不⁠擅⁠長用 be bad at 或 be not good at

I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.

be bad at

I'm bad at remembering names.

我很不會記名字。
be not good at

Tom is not good at speaking in front of people.

Tom 不擅長在別人面前說話。

例句

  1. I’ve often heard Tom boast that he is very good at swimming, but I’ve never actually seen him swim.
    我常聽 Tom 吹噓說他很會游泳，可是我從來沒有真的看過他游泳。
  2. I've never eaten natto.
    我從來沒吃過納豆。
  3. Have your parents ever been to Taipei 101?
    你爸媽去過台北 101 嗎？
  4. We have often talked about this.
    我們常常談到這件事。
  5. My father has been to Korea three times.
    我爸爸去過韓國三次。
  6. She has never ridden a horse.
    她從來沒騎過馬。
  7. I've often visited my grandparents in Tainan.
    我常去台南看我爺爺奶奶。
  8. Has Tom ever cooked dinner for you?
    Tom 有煮過晚餐給你吃嗎？
  9. I've never heard her sing.
    我從來沒聽過她唱歌。
  10. Have you ever seen a dolphin jump?
    你看過海豚跳起來嗎？
  11. I've often seen him play basketball in the park.
    我常看到他在公園打籃球。
  12. We have never heard the teacher shout.
    我們從來沒聽過老師大吼。
  13. I've often heard the neighbors talk late at night.
    我常聽到鄰居深夜說話。
  14. Has anyone ever seen Tom dance?
    有人看過 Tom 跳舞嗎？
  15. My sister is good at math.
    我妹妹數學很好。
  16. Tom is not good at speaking in front of people.
    Tom 不擅長在別人面前說話。
  17. Ann is good at playing the violin.
    Ann 很會拉小提琴。
  18. I'm bad at remembering names.
    我很不會記名字。
  19. Are you good at cooking?
    你很會做菜嗎？
  20. My brother has always been good at sports.
    我哥哥一直很擅長運動。
  21. I'm good at English but bad at science.
    我英文很好，但理科很差。
  22. I've heard my mother say that she was good at tennis in high school.
    我聽過我媽媽說她高中時很會打網球。
  23. Have you ever heard Ken speak Japanese?
    你聽過 Ken 說日文嗎？
  24. I have never seen my father cry.
    我從來沒看過我爸爸哭。
  25. Our coach is good at swimming, and I've often seen him swim in the sea.
    我們的教練很會游泳，我常看到他在海裡游泳。
  26. Have you ever tried Taiwanese bubble tea?
    你喝過台灣的珍珠奶茶嗎？
  27. I've never been to a baseball game.
    我從來沒去看過棒球比賽。
  28. He's not good at waking up early.
    他不擅長早起。
  29. Ken often boasts that he has been to more than twenty countries.
    Ken 常誇口說他去過二十多個國家。
  30. My grandmother has never seen snow fall.
    我奶奶從來沒看過下雪。
