Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

回到家鄉不到二十四小時，我就會不知不覺開始講起當地的方言。

一⁠段時⁠間放⁠在 after 前⁠面，表⁠示兩⁠件⁠事中間⁠隔了多⁠久

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

原句表示回到家鄉之後不到二十四小時。

一⁠段時⁠間也⁠可⁠以放⁠在 before 前⁠面

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

after

Two days after I arrived in Paris, I lost my wallet.

我到巴黎兩天之後，弄丟了錢包。
before

The bus left five minutes before we got to the stop.

公車在我們到站前五分鐘開走了。
only

Only a week after he started the job, he quit.

他才做那份工作一個星期就辭職了。

without 是介⁠系⁠詞，後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，表⁠示沒⁠有做某⁠件⁠事

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

without realizing it 表示自己沒有發覺。it 指後面講的說方言這件事。

start 後⁠面⁠接 to 不⁠定⁠詞或動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，意⁠思一⁠樣

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

to 不定詞

It started to rain.

開始下雨了。
動詞-ing

She started learning Korean last year.

她去年開始學韓文。

begin 也⁠可⁠以接 to 不⁠定⁠詞或動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

to 不定詞

Everyone began to laugh.

大家開始笑了。
動詞-ing

We began walking toward the station.

我們開始往車站走。

現⁠在⁠式表⁠示每⁠次都⁠會發⁠生⁠的事

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

每次回到家鄉都會發生這件事。

every time 和 when 子⁠句講每⁠次都發⁠生⁠的⁠事，也用現⁠在⁠式

Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.

every time

Every time I see this photo, I smile.

我每次看到這張照片都會微笑。
when

When I drink coffee at night, I can't sleep.

我晚上喝咖啡的話就睡不著。

例句

  1. Less than twenty-four hours after I return to my hometown, without realizing it, I start to talk in the local dialect.
    回到家鄉不到二十四小時，我就會不知不覺開始講起當地的方言。
  2. Two days after I arrived in Paris, I lost my wallet.
    我到巴黎兩天之後，弄丟了錢包。
  3. The bus left five minutes before we got to the stop.
    公車在我們到站前五分鐘開走了。
  4. Only a week after he started the job, he quit.
    他才做那份工作一個星期就辭職了。
  5. About an hour after we left home, it started to snow.
    我們離開家大約一小時之後，開始下雪了。
  6. Less than a month after they met, they got married.
    他們認識不到一個月就結婚了。
  7. I woke up about ten minutes before the alarm rang.
    我在鬧鐘響前大約十分鐘醒來。
  8. She left the party without telling anyone.
    她沒跟任何人說就離開了派對。
  9. Don't go swimming without warming up first.
    不要沒先暖身就去游泳。
  10. She went out without taking an umbrella.
    她沒帶傘就出門了。
  11. I finished the test without making any mistakes.
    我寫完考試，一個錯都沒有。
  12. You can't enter the museum without buying a ticket.
    你沒買票就不能進博物館。
  13. I fell asleep on the train without realizing it.
    我不知不覺在火車上睡著了。
  14. It started to rain.
    開始下雨了。
  15. She started learning Korean last year.
    她去年開始學韓文。
  16. The baby began crying when the dog barked.
    狗叫的時候，小嬰兒開始哭。
  17. When did you start playing the guitar?
    你什麼時候開始彈吉他的？
  18. I'll start to study for the exam tomorrow.
    我明天會開始準備考試。
  19. Everyone began to laugh.
    大家開始笑了。
  20. Every time I see this photo, I smile.
    我每次看到這張照片都會微笑。
  21. When I drink coffee at night, I can't sleep.
    我晚上喝咖啡的話就睡不著。
  22. My grandmother always gives me candy when I visit her.
    我去看奶奶的時候，她總是給我糖果。
  23. Whenever I go to Osaka, I eat takoyaki.
    我每次去大阪都會吃章魚燒。
  24. Only a few minutes after I get on the train, I usually fall asleep.
    我上火車才幾分鐘，通常就會睡著。
  25. As soon as I get home, my dog starts barking.
    我一到家，我的狗就開始叫。
  26. Less than an hour after I go to bed, my little brother starts to snore.
    我上床不到一個小時，我弟弟就開始打呼。
  27. My father starts talking in Taiwanese without realizing it when he meets his old friends.
    我爸爸跟老朋友見面的時候，會不知不覺開始說台語。
  28. Two weeks after I came to Japan, I started to dream in Japanese.
    我來日本兩個星期之後，開始用日文做夢。
  29. She often reads my messages without answering them.
    她常常讀了我的訊息卻不回覆。
  30. Every summer, a few days after school ends, we go to the beach.
    每年夏天，學校放暑假幾天後，我們就會去海邊。
  31. We began walking toward the station.
    我們開始往車站走。
  32. The leaves began to turn red in October.
    樹葉在十月開始變紅。
