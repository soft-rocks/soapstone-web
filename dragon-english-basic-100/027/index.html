Last summer I took two weeks off, and went on a trip to Europe with my wife.

去年夏天我請了兩個禮拜的假，跟我太太去歐洲旅行。

take 加時⁠間加 off 表⁠示請⁠假，時⁠間放⁠在 take 和 off 中⁠間

Last summer I took two weeks off, and went on a trip to Europe with my wife.

請假就是不去上班或上學。

take off 中⁠間⁠的時⁠間可⁠以是一⁠段長⁠度或特⁠定⁠的日⁠子

Last summer I took two weeks off, and went on a trip to Europe with my wife.

一段長度

My father took three days off to visit my grandmother.

我爸爸請了三天假去看我奶奶。
特定的日子

Can I take Friday off next week?

我下星期五可以請假嗎？
時段

She was sick, so she took the afternoon off.

她生病了，所以下午請了假。

同⁠一⁠個主⁠詞做兩⁠件⁠事⁠時，用 and 連⁠接兩⁠個動⁠詞

Last summer I took two weeks off, and went on a trip to Europe with my wife.

原句的逗號只表示停頓，可以省略。

and 連⁠接兩⁠個動⁠詞⁠時，前⁠面一⁠般不⁠加逗⁠號

Last summer I took two weeks off, and went on a trip to Europe with my wife.
