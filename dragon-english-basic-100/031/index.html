現⁠在完⁠成⁠式表⁠示動⁠作做完⁠了，結⁠果留⁠到現⁠在

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

我昨天跟圖書館借的小說已經看完了，所以現在沒事可做。

小說看完了，所以現在沒事做。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式常和 just、already、yet 一⁠起用

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

just

I've just finished my homework.

我剛寫完功課。
already

I've already paid for dinner.

晚餐的錢我已經付了。
yet

Has your sister found a new job yet?

你姊姊找到新工作了嗎？

finish 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

你寫完那封信了嗎？

finish 後面不接 to 不定詞。

當受⁠詞⁠的關⁠係⁠代⁠名⁠詞可⁠以省⁠略

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

novel 是 borrowed 的受詞。

名⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接主⁠詞和動⁠詞，修⁠飾這⁠個名⁠詞

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

book

Have you finished reading the book I lent you?

你看完我借你的書了嗎？
novel

The novel I'm reading now is very long.

我現在在看的小說很長。
umbrella

I returned the umbrella I borrowed last week.

我把上星期借的傘還回去了。

to 不⁠定⁠詞放⁠在 nothing 後⁠面，修⁠飾 nothing

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

nothing to do 是沒有事可以做。

something、anything 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接 to 不⁠定⁠詞

I’ve finished reading the novel I borrowed from the library yesterday, so now I have nothing to do.

anything

I'm hungry. Is there anything to eat?

我餓了。有什麼可以吃的嗎？
something

I have something to tell you.

我有事要跟你說。
