If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

如果你那時吃了這個藥並且躺在床上休息，大概兩三天就會好了。

講過⁠去沒⁠發⁠生⁠的⁠事，if 子⁠句用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

如果我早一點出門，我就會趕上火車了。

原句的人沒有吃藥，也沒有躺在床上。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以用 could have 或 might have

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

could have

If it hadn't rained, we could have played soccer.

如果沒有下雨，我們就可以踢足球了。
might have

If you had studied harder, you might have passed the test.

如果你更用功讀書，你可能就會通過考試了。

probably 放⁠在 would 和 have 中⁠間

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

probably 表示說話的人認為很可能。

if 子⁠句放⁠在主⁠要⁠子⁠句後⁠面⁠時，中⁠間不⁠加逗⁠號

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

如果她沒有生病，她就會來派對了。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞 get 接形⁠容⁠詞，表⁠示主⁠詞變⁠成什⁠麼狀⁠態

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

我希望你早日康復。

well 是形容詞，意思是健康的。

get well 是病好起⁠來，get sick 是生⁠病

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

get well

My grandmother got well after a week in the hospital.

我奶奶住院一個星期之後好起來了。
get sick

Many students got sick after the school trip.

校外教學之後很多學生生病了。

get 的過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，英⁠式英⁠文用 got，美⁠式英⁠文常⁠用 gotten

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

英式 got

If I had stayed in bed, I would probably have got well sooner.

如果我待在床上休息，我大概會好得比較快。
美式 gotten

It has gotten very cold this week.

這個星期變得很冷。

and 連⁠接兩⁠個過⁠去完⁠成⁠式⁠的動⁠作，第⁠二⁠個 had 可⁠以省⁠略

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

stayed 的意思是 had stayed。

一⁠般⁠的過⁠去完⁠成⁠式句⁠子也⁠可⁠以省⁠略第⁠二⁠個 had

If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.

cleaned = had cleaned

I had finished my homework and cleaned my room before dinner.

晚餐前我已經寫完功課、打掃好房間了。
gone = had gone

By the time we got to the party, everyone had eaten and gone home.

我們到派對的時候，大家都已經吃完回家了。

例句

  1. If you had taken this medicine and stayed in bed, you would probably have got well in two or three days.
    如果你那時吃了這個藥並且躺在床上休息，大概兩三天就會好了。
  2. If I had left home earlier, I would have caught the train.
    如果我早一點出門，我就會趕上火車了。
  3. If it hadn't rained, we could have played soccer.
    如果沒有下雨，我們就可以踢足球了。
  4. If you had studied harder, you might have passed the test.
    如果你更用功讀書，你可能就會通過考試了。
  5. If I had known your phone number, I would have called you.
    如果我知道你的電話號碼，我就會打給你了。
  6. She would have come to the party if she hadn't been sick.
    如果她沒有生病，她就會來派對了。
  7. If we had taken a taxi, we might have arrived on time.
    如果我們搭了計程車，我們可能就會準時到了。
  8. I hope you get well soon.
    我希望你早日康復。
  9. My grandmother got well after a week in the hospital.
    我奶奶住院一個星期之後好起來了。
  10. Drink a lot of water, and you will get well.
    多喝水，你就會好起來。
  11. The doctor said Tom would get well in a few days.
    醫生說 Tom 幾天後就會好。
  12. My cat was sick last month, but she got well quickly.
    我的貓上個月生病了，但很快就好了。
  13. If I had checked the weather and taken an umbrella, I wouldn't have got wet.
    如果我查了天氣、帶了傘，就不會淋濕了。
  14. By the time we got to the party, everyone had eaten and gone home.
    我們到派對的時候，大家都已經吃完回家了。
  15. She had washed the dishes and put them away before I woke up.
    我起床之前，她已經把碗盤洗好收起來了。
  16. When I called the office, Tom had already packed his bag and gone home.
    我打電話到辦公室的時候，Tom 已經收好包包回家了。
  17. I had finished my homework and cleaned my room before dinner.
    晚餐前我已經寫完功課、打掃好房間了。
  18. If you had gone to the doctor, you would have got well in a few days.
    如果你去看了醫生，你幾天就會好了。
  19. If I had stayed in bed, I would probably have got well sooner.
    如果我待在床上休息，我大概會好得比較快。
  20. If she had taken the medicine, she would have felt better in an hour.
    如果她吃了那個藥，她一個小時後就會覺得好一點。
  21. You would have got well faster if you hadn't gone to school.
    如果你沒去上學，你就會好得比較快。
  22. Take this medicine, and you'll get well in two or three days.
    吃這個藥，你兩三天就會好了。
  23. If Tom hadn't eaten so much, he wouldn't have got sick.
    如果 Tom 沒有吃那麼多，他就不會生病了。
  24. If we had brought a map, we wouldn't have got lost.
    如果我們帶了地圖，我們就不會迷路了。
  25. If I had seen you at the station, I would have said hello.
    如果我在車站看到你，我就會跟你打招呼了。
  26. My father got well in a week and went back to work.
    我爸爸一個星期就好了，回去上班了。
  27. If you had told me earlier, I could have helped you.
    如果你早一點告訴我，我就可以幫你了。
  28. If you had gone to bed early and eaten breakfast, you wouldn't have felt sick.
    如果你早點睡、吃了早餐，就不會覺得不舒服了。
  29. If it had been sunny, we would probably have gone to the beach.
    如果那天是晴天，我們大概就會去海邊了。
  30. The doctor told me to rest so that I could get well in time for the trip.
    醫生叫我休息，這樣我就可以在旅行之前康復。
  31. Many students got sick after the school trip.
    校外教學之後很多學生生病了。
  32. It has gotten very cold this week.
    這個星期變得很冷。
  33. My English has gotten better since I joined the club.
    我加入社團之後，英文變好了。
