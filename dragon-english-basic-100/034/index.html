People wish they could live forever and never become older. However, if this wish came true, there would be too many people on the earth.

人們希望能長生不老。可是如果這個願望成真，地球上的人就會太多了。

wish 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句講現⁠在做不⁠到⁠的⁠事，用 could 加動⁠詞⁠原⁠形

People wish they could live forever and never become older. However, if this wish came true, there would be too many people on the earth.

人們想永遠活著，但這件事做不到。

假⁠設句⁠的主⁠要⁠子⁠句可⁠以用 there would be 加名⁠詞

People wish they could live forever and never become older. However, if this wish came true, there would be too many people on the earth.

there would be 表示那時候會有某樣東西。

if 子⁠句⁠的過⁠去⁠式也⁠可⁠以講有⁠可⁠能發⁠生⁠的事

People wish they could live forever and never become older. However, if this wish came true, there would be too many people on the earth.

有可能發生

If everyone came, there would be no seats left.

如果每個人都來，就不會有空位了。
現在不是這樣

If there were no trains, there would be more cars on the road.

如果沒有火車，路上就會有更多車。

說話的人把這兩個情況都當成假設來說。

too many 接可⁠數⁠名⁠詞複⁠數，too much 接不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞

People wish they could live forever and never become older. However, if this wish came true, there would be too many people on the earth.

too many
可數名詞複數

There are too many people on this train.

這班火車上人太多了。
too much
不可數名詞

I drank too much coffee today.

我今天喝了太多咖啡。

數量超過需要，造成問題。原句的 people 是可數名詞複數，所以用 too many。
