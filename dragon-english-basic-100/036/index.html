例句

  1. If you have been to old European cities, you must have been impressed by the beautiful streets.
    如果你去過歐洲的古老城市，一定對那些美麗的街道印象深刻。
  2. Has anyone in your family ever been to Europe?
    你家有人去過歐洲嗎？
  3. I have been to Taipei twice.
    我去過台北兩次。
  4. My parents have never been to a foreign country.
    我爸媽從來沒去過國外。
  5. Tom has been to Kyoto twice.
    Tom 去過京都兩次。
  6. My father has gone to Osaka on business. He will be back on Friday.
    我爸爸去大阪出差了。他星期五會回來。
  7. She has been to the new museum once.
    她去過那間新的博物館一次。
  8. If you have seen this movie, you must have cried at the end.
    如果你看過這部電影，你在結尾一定哭了。
  9. If you have tried her cake, you must have liked it.
    如果你吃過她做的蛋糕，你一定很喜歡。
  10. If you have climbed Mt. Fuji, you must have been very tired at the top.
    如果你爬過富士山，你到山頂的時候一定很累。
  11. If you have lived in Tokyo, you must have taken a crowded train.
    如果你住過東京，你一定搭過擠滿人的火車。
  12. If Ann has read this book, she must have enjoyed it.
    如果 Ann 看過這本書，她一定很喜歡。
  13. I was impressed by his speech.
    他的演講讓我印象深刻。
  14. We were impressed with the clean streets of Tokyo.
    東京乾淨的街道讓我們印象深刻。
  15. The teacher was impressed by your answer.
    老師對你的答案印象深刻。
  16. Everyone was impressed by the view from the tower.
    每個人都對從塔上看出去的景色印象深刻。
  17. I'm always impressed by how kind people in Taiwan are.
    我總是對台灣人的親切印象深刻。
  18. My mother was impressed with the food at the hotel.
    我媽媽對飯店的食物印象深刻。
  19. If you have been to Hokkaido in winter, you must have been impressed by the snow.
    如果你冬天去過北海道，你一定對那裡的雪印象深刻。
  20. If you have been to Paris, you must have seen the Eiffel Tower.
    如果你去過巴黎，你一定看過艾菲爾鐵塔。
  21. If you have visited Nara, you must have been surprised by the deer.
    如果你去過奈良，那些鹿一定讓你很驚訝。
  22. Ann isn't here. She has gone to the library.
    Ann 不在這裡。她去圖書館了。
  23. I have never been to Europe, but I want to go someday.
    我從來沒去過歐洲，但我有一天想去。
  24. The visitors were impressed by the old temples in Kyoto.
    遊客們對京都的古老寺廟印象深刻。
  25. If you have been to his house, you must have been impressed by his garden.
    如果你去過他家，你一定對他的花園印象深刻。
  26. My sister has been to London once, and she was impressed by the old buildings.
    我姊姊去過倫敦一次，她對那些古老的建築印象深刻。
  27. If you have heard her sing, you must have been impressed.
    如果你聽過她唱歌，你一定印象深刻。
  28. If you have used this app, you must have found it useful.
    如果你用過這個 app，你一定覺得它很好用。
  29. The judges were impressed by the students' English.
    評審對那些學生的英文印象深刻。
  30. Has your brother ever been to Taiwan?
    你哥哥去過台灣嗎？
