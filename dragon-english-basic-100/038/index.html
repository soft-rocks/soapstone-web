Some students coming to the library act as if they are in a coffee shop.

有些來圖書館的學生，表現得好像他們在咖啡店一樣。

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 片⁠段放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面，修⁠飾這⁠個名⁠詞

Some students coming to the library act as if they are in a coffee shop.

這個片段等於 students who come to the library。

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 片⁠段說⁠明這⁠個人或東⁠西做⁠什⁠麼或正⁠在做⁠什⁠麼

Some students coming to the library act as if they are in a coffee shop.

例句

  1. Some students coming to the library act as if they are in a coffee shop.
    有些來圖書館的學生，表現得好像他們在咖啡店一樣。
  2. The boy sitting next to me is my cousin.
    坐在我旁邊的男生是我表弟。
  3. Who is the woman talking to your father?
    在跟你爸爸說話的那個女人是誰？
  4. The man standing at the door is our new teacher.
    站在門口的那個男人是我們的新老師。
  5. Students living far from school can take the school bus.
    住得離學校遠的學生可以搭校車。
  6. The cat sleeping on the roof belongs to my neighbor.
    在屋頂上睡覺的那隻貓是我鄰居的。
  7. Tourists visiting Kyoto often wear kimonos.
    來京都的遊客常常穿和服。
  8. He talks as if he knows everything.
    他說話的樣子好像什麼都知道。
  9. It looks as if it is going to rain.
    看起來好像要下雨了。
  10. She looked at me as if I were a stranger.
    她看著我，好像我是個陌生人。
  11. He talks to me as if I were a child.
    他跟我說話的樣子，好像我是小孩。
  12. Tom ate as if he hadn't eaten for days.
    Tom 吃東西的樣子好像好幾天沒吃飯了。
  13. It sounds as if the party is going to be fun.
    聽起來派對好像會很好玩。
  14. The man walking in front of us dropped his wallet.
    走在我們前面的男人掉了錢包。
  15. Do you know the girl singing on the stage?
    你認識在台上唱歌的那個女孩嗎？
  16. He acts as if he is the boss.
    他表現得好像他是老闆。
  17. Ann acted as if nothing had happened.
    Ann 表現得好像什麼事都沒發生過。
  18. Children swimming in the river should wear life jackets.
    在河裡游泳的孩子應該穿救生衣。
  19. Some people waiting for the bus act as if they are in their own homes.
    有些在等公車的人表現得好像他們在自己家裡一樣。
  20. The children playing in the park acted as if the park were theirs.
    在公園裡玩的孩子們表現得好像公園是他們的。
  21. Some passengers talking on the phone act as if the train is empty.
    有些在講電話的乘客表現得好像車上沒有別人。
  22. The girl reading a book by the window looks as if she is in another world.
    在窗邊看書的那個女生看起來好像在另一個世界。
  23. My brother acts as if he doesn't care, but he does.
    我弟弟表現得好像他不在乎，但他其實在乎。
  24. My dog looks at me as if he understands every word.
    我的狗看著我的樣子，好像聽得懂每個字。
  25. The students going on the school trip must come at eight.
    要去校外教學的學生必須八點到。
  26. Why are you acting as if you don't know me?
    你為什麼表現得好像不認識我？
  27. The man selling ice cream near the beach is very kind.
    在海邊附近賣冰淇淋的那個男人很親切。
  28. She speaks English as if she grew up in America.
    她說英文的樣子好像她是在美國長大的。
  29. The old man walking his dog over there is my neighbor.
    在那邊遛狗的那個老人是我的鄰居。
  30. Some customers coming to this café stay all day as if it were their office.
    有些來這家咖啡店的客人一待就是一整天，好像這裡是他們的辦公室。
  31. She looked as if she had seen a ghost.
    她看起來好像見到鬼一樣。
