At first, I thought Tom was joking, but later I realized he was serious.

一開始我以為 Tom 在開玩笑，後來才發現他是認真的。

主⁠要動⁠詞是過⁠去⁠式⁠時，子⁠句⁠的動⁠詞通⁠常也用過⁠去⁠式

At first, I thought Tom was joking, but later I realized he was serious.

一開始我以為 Tom 在開玩笑，後來才發現他是認真的。

thought、knew、said 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句也用過⁠去⁠式

At first, I thought Tom was joking, but later I realized he was serious.

thought

I thought you were at home.

我以為你在家。
knew

I knew that he was tired.

我知道他很累。
said

Tom said that he was hungry.

Tom 說他餓了。

子⁠句⁠的事比主⁠要動⁠詞更早發⁠生⁠時，子⁠句用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式

At first, I thought Tom was joking, but later I realized he was serious.

她發現她把手機忘在火車上了。

子⁠句前⁠面⁠的 that 常⁠常省⁠略

At first, I thought Tom was joking, but later I realized he was serious.

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式表⁠示過⁠去某⁠個時⁠間正⁠在進⁠行⁠的動⁠作

At first, I thought Tom was joking, but later I realized he was serious.

你打電話來的時候我正在洗澡。

原句的 was joking 表示 Tom 說話的時候正在開玩笑。
