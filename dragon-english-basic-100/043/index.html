Last Sunday I tried making some Italian food for the first time, and it was delicious.

上個禮拜天我第一次試著做義大利菜，結果很好吃。

try 接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，表⁠示試⁠著做一⁠件⁠事，看結⁠果怎⁠麼⁠樣

Last Sunday I tried making some Italian food for the first time, and it was delicious.

上個禮拜天我第一次試著做義大利菜，結果很好吃。

那件事本身做得到，重點在結果好不好。

try to 接動⁠詞⁠原⁠形，表⁠示努⁠力去做一⁠件不⁠容⁠易⁠的事

Last Sunday I tried making some Italian food for the first time, and it was delicious.

我試著打開窗戶，但窗戶卡住了。

那件事可能沒做到。

tried 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 看結⁠果，tried to 是努⁠力去做

Last Sunday I tried making some Italian food for the first time, and it was delicious.

否⁠定是 try not to，表⁠示盡⁠量不⁠要做

Last Sunday I tried making some Italian food for the first time, and it was delicious.

盡量不要發出聲音。

例句

  1. Last Sunday I tried making some Italian food for the first time, and it was delicious.
    上個禮拜天我第一次試著做義大利菜，結果很好吃。
  2. Try adding some salt to the soup.
    試試在湯裡加一點鹽。
  3. I tried to open the window, but it was stuck.
    我試著打開窗戶，但窗戶卡住了。
  4. Have you tried eating natto?
    你有試過吃納豆嗎？
  5. I tried using a new app to study English.
    我試著用一個新的 app 學英文。
  6. Try turning the computer off and on again.
    試試把電腦關掉再打開。
  7. She tried wearing a kimono at a shop in Kyoto.
    她在京都的一家店試穿了和服。
  8. I tried to call you, but you didn't answer.
    我試著打電話給你，但你沒接。
  9. Tom is trying to save money for a new bike.
    Tom 正在努力存錢買新腳踏車。
  10. Please try to come early tomorrow.
    明天請盡量早點來。
  11. I tried making curry for the first time, and it was too spicy.
    我第一次試著做咖哩，結果太辣了。
  12. Last week my sister tried cooking Thai food, and it was very good.
    上星期我妹妹試著做泰國菜，結果很好吃。
  13. I tried skiing for the first time in Nagano, and it was a lot of fun.
    我在長野第一次試著滑雪，很好玩。
  14. Have you ever tried making bread at home?
    你有沒有試過在家做麵包？
  15. I tried to finish the book in one day, but I couldn't.
    我想一天看完那本書，但沒辦到。
  16. Why don't you try taking the train instead of the bus?
    你何不試試搭火車，不要搭公車？
  17. Try drinking warm milk before bed. You'll sleep better.
    睡前試試喝溫牛奶。你會睡得比較好。
  18. We tried to climb the mountain, but the heavy snow stopped us.
    我們試著爬那座山，但大雪讓我們爬不上去。
  19. I'm trying to remember her name.
    我正在努力想起她的名字。
  20. The baby tried to stand up and fell down.
    小嬰兒試著站起來，結果跌倒了。
  21. My father tried changing the battery, but the clock still didn't work.
    我爸爸試著換電池，但時鐘還是不會動。
  22. She tried to stay awake, but she fell asleep in class.
    她努力保持清醒，但還是在課堂上睡著了。
  23. Try asking the station staff. They might know.
    試試問車站人員。他們可能知道。
  24. Ann tried sending him a message, but he didn't reply.
    Ann 試著傳訊息給他，但他沒回。
  25. Try not to make any noise. The baby is sleeping.
    盡量不要發出聲音。寶寶在睡覺。
  26. Don't try to carry all the boxes at once.
    不要想一次搬完所有的箱子。
  27. I tried using chopsticks for the first time in Taipei.
    我在台北第一次試著用筷子。
  28. We tried to find a cheap hotel, but all the hotels were full.
    我們想找便宜的旅館，但全都客滿了。
  29. I'll try not to be late again.
    我會盡量不要再遲到。
  30. Last month my grandmother tried singing karaoke, and she loved it.
    上個月我奶奶試著唱卡拉 OK，她很喜歡。
