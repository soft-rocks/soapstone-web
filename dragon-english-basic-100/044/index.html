例句

  1. Some people say that they don’t like summer because it is so hot that they don’t feel like doing anything. However, I like the heat of summer because I can swim in the sea.
    有些人說他們不喜歡夏天，因為太熱了，熱到什麼事都不想做。可是我喜歡夏天的炎熱，因為我可以去海裡游泳。
  2. The soup was so hot that I couldn't eat it.
    湯太燙了，我沒辦法喝。
  3. He spoke so fast that nobody understood him.
    他講得太快，沒有人聽得懂他。
  4. I was so tired that I fell asleep on the train.
    我累到在火車上睡著了。
  5. The bag is so heavy that my little sister can't carry it.
    這個包包重到我妹妹提不動。
  6. It rained so hard that we stayed home all day.
    雨下得很大，所以我們整天待在家裡。
  7. The movie was so funny that we laughed the whole time.
    那部電影好笑到我們從頭笑到尾。
  8. She sings so well that everyone loves her songs.
    她唱得很好，所以每個人都喜歡她的歌。
  9. I don't feel like going out tonight.
    我今晚不想出門。
  10. Do you feel like a cup of tea?
    你想來杯茶嗎？
  11. I feel like a cold drink.
    我想喝杯冷飲。
  12. My brother didn't feel like studying after the long trip.
    長途旅行之後，我哥哥不想讀書。
  13. Do you feel like taking a walk by the river?
    你想去河邊散步嗎？
  14. When I have a cold, I don't feel like eating anything.
    我感冒的時候什麼都不想吃。
  15. Many people like winter. However, I like summer better.
    很多人喜歡冬天。不過，我比較喜歡夏天。
  16. The test was hard. However, most of the students passed it.
    考試很難。不過，大部分的學生都通過了。
  17. Tokyo is a big city. However, it has many quiet parks.
    東京是個大城市。不過，那裡有很多安靜的公園。
  18. I wanted to go to the beach. However, it rained all weekend.
    我想去海邊。但是整個週末都在下雨。
  19. My father is very busy. However, he always has dinner with us.
    我爸爸很忙。不過，他總是跟我們一起吃晚餐。
  20. Taiwan is hot in summer. However, the mountains are cool.
    台灣夏天很熱。不過，山上很涼。
  21. It was so hot yesterday that I didn't feel like cooking.
    昨天熱到我不想煮飯。
  22. I was so sleepy that I didn't feel like doing my homework.
    我睏到不想寫作業。
  23. The room was so cold that nobody felt like taking off their coat.
    房間冷到沒有人想脫外套。
  24. The hotel was old. However, the rooms were very clean.
    那家旅館很舊。不過，房間很乾淨。
  25. Some people don't like rain. However, I like the sound of rain.
    有些人不喜歡下雨。不過，我喜歡雨聲。
  26. My grandmother is eighty. However, she still travels every year.
    我奶奶八十歲了。不過，她每年還是會去旅行。
  27. Kyoto was so beautiful that I took hundreds of pictures.
    京都美到我拍了好幾百張照片。
  28. Ann was so happy that she felt like singing.
    Ann 開心到想唱歌。
  29. We were so hungry that we ate all the pizza in ten minutes.
    我們餓到十分鐘就把披薩全部吃完了。
  30. I don't feel like swimming today. However, I will go to the pool with you.
    我今天不想游泳。不過，我會跟你一起去游泳池。
