例句

  1. The graph shows that in Japan the birth rate has been decreasing since 1985. This decrease is partly because it costs a lot of money to raise children.
    這張圖表顯示，日本的出生率從 1985 年開始一直在下降。下降的部分原因是養小孩要花很多錢。
  2. It has been raining since this morning.
    從今天早上開始一直在下雨。
  3. I have been waiting for you for an hour.
    我已經等你等了一個小時。
  4. My sister has been cleaning her room since breakfast.
    我妹妹從吃完早餐就一直在打掃她的房間。
  5. She has been studying Japanese for three years.
    她已經學日文學了三年。
  6. The number of foreign workers in Japan has been increasing since 2010.
    日本的外國勞工人數從 2010 年以來一直在增加。
  7. How long have you been living in Taipei?
    你住在台北多久了？
  8. We have been walking for two hours, and I'm tired.
    我們已經走了兩個小時，我累了。
  9. The price of rice has been going up since last year.
    米價從去年開始一直上漲。
  10. It costs a lot of money to live in Tokyo.
    住在東京要花很多錢。
  11. It cost me 300 yen to take the bus.
    搭公車花了我三百日圓。
  12. How much does it cost to send this letter to Taiwan?
    寄這封信到台灣要多少錢？
  13. It costs about 10,000 yen to stay at this hotel for one night.
    住這家旅館一晚大約要一萬日圓。
  14. It cost my father a lot of money to fix the car.
    修車花了我爸爸很多錢。
  15. It doesn't cost anything to visit this museum.
    參觀這間博物館不用花錢。
  16. I was late partly because there was a lot of traffic.
    我遲到的部分原因是路上很塞。
  17. The town is quiet mainly because many young people have left.
    這個小鎮很安靜，主要是因為很多年輕人離開了。
  18. I like this school partly because the teachers are kind.
    我喜歡這所學校，部分原因是老師很親切。
  19. Tom failed the test mainly because he didn't study.
    Tom 考試沒過，主要是因為他沒讀書。
  20. The shop closed partly because it had few customers.
    那家店關門的部分原因是客人很少。
  21. Our team lost mainly because two players were sick.
    我們隊輸了，主要是因為兩個隊員生病了。
  22. The birth rate has been falling since the 1980s partly because many people marry later.
    出生率從 1980 年代以來一直在下降，部分原因是很多人比較晚結婚。
  23. Prices have been rising since spring. This is partly because it costs more to bring food from abroad.
    物價從春天開始一直上漲。這有一部分是因為從國外運食物進來要花更多錢。
  24. The graph shows that the number of cars in Taipei has been decreasing since 2015.
    這張圖表顯示台北的汽車數量從 2015 年以來一直在減少。
  25. It costs a lot of money to go to college in Japan.
    在日本上大學要花很多錢。
  26. Ann has been learning the piano since she was five.
    Ann 從五歲開始一直在學鋼琴。
  27. It will cost us about 2,000 dollars to fly to Japan.
    搭飛機去日本會花我們大約兩千美元。
  28. More people ride bikes to work now, partly because it costs a lot to park in the city.
    現在比較多人騎腳踏車上班，部分原因是在市區停車要花很多錢。
  29. Many students are tired in class mainly because they stay up late.
    很多學生上課很累，主要是因為他們熬夜。
  30. I have been feeling sick since I ate that fish.
    我吃了那條魚之後一直覺得不舒服。
