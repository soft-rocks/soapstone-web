During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

數學課上我的手機響了起來，老師狠狠罵了我一頓。我很後悔沒有把手機關掉。

during 是介⁠系⁠詞，後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞，表⁠示在某段時⁠間⁠裡

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

數學課上我的手機響了起來。

接子⁠句⁠的時⁠候用連⁠接⁠詞 while，不⁠用 during

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

during + 名詞

I fell asleep during the movie.

我看電影的時候睡著了。
while + 主詞 + 動詞

My phone rang while the teacher was talking.

老師在講話的時候，我的手機響了。

regret 後⁠面⁠接 that 子⁠句或動⁠名⁠詞，表⁠示後⁠悔以⁠前⁠的事

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

that 子句

I regret that I didn't study harder.

我後悔我沒有更用功讀書。
動名詞

She regrets telling him the secret.

她後悔把秘密告訴他。

動⁠名⁠詞⁠的否⁠定把 not 放⁠在動⁠名⁠詞前⁠面

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

我後悔在京都沒有多拍一些照片。

過⁠去完⁠成⁠式表⁠示比過⁠去某⁠個時⁠間更早發⁠生⁠的事

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

我很後悔沒有把手機關掉。

沒有關手機發生在後悔之前。

turn off 的受⁠詞是名⁠詞⁠時，可⁠以放⁠在 off 前⁠面或後⁠面

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

睡覺前把電視關掉。

turn off 的受⁠詞是代⁠名⁠詞⁠時，只⁠能放⁠在 turn 和 off 中⁠間

During the math class, my cell phone began to ring, and the teacher severely scolded me. I regretted that I hadn’t turned it off.

你可以把它關掉嗎？

turn on 也一樣。
