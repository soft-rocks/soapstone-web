While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

在歐洲旅行的時候，不管我去哪裡都有很多日本觀光客，讓我很失望。

主⁠詞相⁠同⁠時，while 子⁠句省⁠略主⁠詞和 be 動⁠詞

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

while traveling 就是 while I was traveling。

when 子⁠句也⁠可⁠以省⁠略主⁠詞和 be 動⁠詞

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

while

Don't use your phone while driving.

開車的時候不要用手機。
when

Be careful when using a knife.

用刀子的時候要小心。

情⁠緒形⁠容⁠詞後⁠面⁠的 that 子⁠句說⁠明情⁠緒⁠的原⁠因

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

不管我去哪裡都有很多日本觀光客，讓我很失望。

surprised 和 happy 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接 that 子⁠句

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

surprised

We were surprised that the hotel was so cheap.

我們很驚訝那家旅館那麼便宜。
happy

My parents were happy that I passed the exam.

我爸媽很高興我通過了考試。

wherever 的意⁠思是不⁠管到哪⁠裡，帶⁠出副⁠詞子⁠句

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

不管我去哪裡都有很多日本觀光客。

wherever 子⁠句可⁠以放⁠在主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的前⁠面或後⁠面

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

前面

Wherever we went in Kyoto, there were many people.

我們在京都不管走到哪裡都有很多人。
後面

My dog follows me wherever I go.

不管我去哪裡，我的狗都跟著我。

whenever 的意⁠思是每⁠次，用⁠法和 wherever 相⁠同

While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.

前面

Whenever it rains, the trains are late.

每次下雨，火車都會誤點。
後面

My little brother cries whenever he sees a dog.

我弟弟每次看到狗都會哭。

例句

  1. While traveling in Europe, I was disappointed that wherever I went, there were many Japanese tourists.
    在歐洲旅行的時候，不管我去哪裡都有很多日本觀光客，讓我很失望。

    在市場買東西的時候，我把傘弄丟了。

    開車的時候不要用手機。

    待在大阪的時候，我們每天都吃章魚燒。

    她邊寫作業邊聽音樂。
  6. While cooking dinner, my mother listens to the radio.
    我媽媽煮晚餐的時候聽收音機。

    騎腳踏車的時候要戴安全帽。
  8. Be careful when using a knife.
    用刀子的時候要小心。
  9. I was disappointed that the museum was closed.
    我很失望博物館沒開。
  10. We were surprised that the hotel was so cheap.
    我們很驚訝那家旅館那麼便宜。
  11. Tom was disappointed that he didn't win the race.
    Tom 很失望自己沒有跑贏。
  12. I'm happy that you can come to my party.
    我很高興你能來我的派對。
  13. My parents were happy that I passed the exam.
    我爸媽很高興我通過了考試。
  14. She was sad that her best friend moved away.
    她很難過她最好的朋友搬走了。

    整個星期都在下雨，你很失望嗎？

    我們在京都不管走到哪裡都有很多人。

    不管我去哪裡，我的狗都跟著我。

    你想坐哪裡都可以。

    不管我去哪裡旅行，我都會買一張明信片。

    每次我去看我奶奶，她都會包餃子給我吃。

    每次下雨，火車都會誤點。

    在日本不管你去哪裡，都找得到便利商店。
  24. I was disappointed that the cherry blossoms had already fallen.
    我很失望櫻花已經謝了。

    他住在東京的時候學會了做日本菜。
  26. I'm surprised that you remember my birthday.
    我很驚訝你記得我的生日。
  27. While eating dinner, we talked about the trip.
    吃晚餐的時候，我們聊了那趟旅行。
  28. My little brother cries whenever he sees a dog.
    我弟弟每次看到狗都會哭。
  29. I was happy that wherever I went in Taiwan, the food was good.
    我很高興在台灣不管去哪裡，食物都很好吃。

    跟老師說話的時候要有禮貌。
