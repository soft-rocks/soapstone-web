Whether a college is good or bad depends not only on how many books its library has and how good they are. It also depends on how intelligent its teachers and students are.

一所大學好不好，不只看它的圖書館有多少書、那些書有多好，也要看它的老師和學生有多聰明。

whether 子⁠句是名⁠詞子⁠句，可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首⁠當主⁠詞

whether 子句表示兩種可能裡的哪一種。

whether 子⁠句算單⁠數，後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用單⁠數形

Whether a college is good or bad depends not only on how many books its library has and how good they are. It also depends on how intelligent its teachers and students are.

how 後⁠面可⁠以接 many 加名⁠詞、形⁠容⁠詞或副⁠詞

例句

  1. Whether a college is good or bad depends not only on how many books its library has and how good they are. It also depends on how intelligent its teachers and students are.
    一所大學好不好，不只看它的圖書館有多少書、那些書有多好，也要看它的老師和學生有多聰明。
  2. Whether we go or not depends on the weather.
    我們去不去要看天氣。
  3. Whether he comes or not doesn't matter to me.
    他來不來對我來說不重要。
  4. Whether the plan is good or bad is not clear yet.
    這個計畫是好是壞還不清楚。
  5. Whether you pass or fail depends on you.
    你會通過還是不及格取決於你自己。
  6. Whether we take the bus or the train is up to you.
    我們搭公車還是搭火車，由你決定。
  7. Whether a restaurant is good or not depends on its food and service.
    一家餐廳好不好取決於它的食物和服務。
  8. The price depends on the size.
    價格看尺寸而定。
  9. It depends on how much time we have.
    那要看我們有多少時間。
  10. Our trip depends on whether it rains.
    我們的旅行要看會不會下雨。
  11. Your score depends on how well you answer the last question.
    你的分數取決於你最後一題答得多好。
  12. Whether I can go depends on my parents.
    我能不能去要看我爸媽。
  13. Happiness doesn't depend on how much money you have.
    幸福不取決於你有多少錢。
  14. The trip to Okinawa depends on whether we can get cheap tickets.
    去沖繩的旅行要看我們買不買得到便宜的票。
  15. I don't know how many brothers he has.
    我不知道他有幾個兄弟。
  16. Tell me how old your sister is.
    告訴我你妹妹幾歲。
  17. Do you know how many languages Ann speaks?
    你知道 Ann 會說幾種語言嗎？
  18. I was surprised at how big the station was.
    我很驚訝那個車站那麼大。
  19. Nobody knows how deep this lake is.
    沒有人知道這座湖有多深。
  20. Count how many chairs we need.
    數一下我們需要幾張椅子。
  21. The teacher asked us how many books we read last month.
    老師問我們上個月讀了幾本書。
  22. You will see how beautiful Kyoto is in autumn.
    你會看到京都的秋天有多美。
  23. Whether the movie is interesting depends on who you watch it with.
    那部電影有沒有趣，要看你跟誰一起看。
  24. Whether this answer is right or wrong is not important now.
    這個答案是對是錯現在不重要。
  25. Our trip depends not only on the weather. It also depends on how much money we have.
    我們的旅行不只要看天氣，也要看我們有多少錢。
  26. The taste of the curry depends on how many spices you put in it.
    咖哩的味道取決於你放了多少種香料。
  27. I can't tell you how sorry I am.
    我真的說不出我有多抱歉。
  28. It depends on how hungry you are.
    那要看你有多餓。
  29. My mother asked me how many classes I had on Monday.
    我媽媽問我星期一有幾堂課。
  30. Whether we win or lose depends on how hard we practice.
    我們會贏還是會輸，要看我們練習得多努力。
  31. The price of a hotel room depends not only on its size. It also depends on how close it is to the station.
    飯店房間的價格不只看房間大小，也要看它離車站多近。
