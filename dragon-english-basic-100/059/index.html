The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

現代文明越進步，我們就被迫越晚睡，睡眠也越少。

兩⁠個 the 加比⁠較⁠級⁠的子⁠句表⁠示兩⁠件⁠事⁠的程⁠度一⁠起改⁠變

The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

第⁠三⁠個 the 加比⁠較⁠級⁠的子⁠句用 and 接上⁠去

The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

你越早睡，精神就越好，能做的事也越多。

比⁠較⁠級後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞跟比⁠較⁠級一⁠起放⁠在⁠前⁠面

The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

sleep 是 get 的受詞。

less 接不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞，fewer 接複⁠數名⁠詞

The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

less + 不可數名詞

I drink less coffee than before.

我喝的咖啡比以前少。
fewer + 複數名詞

There are fewer students in my class this year.

今年我班上的學生比較少。

sleep 是不可數名詞，所以用 less。

be forced to 加動⁠詞⁠原⁠形表⁠示被⁠迫做某⁠件⁠事

The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

因為颱風，我們被迫待在家裡。

主⁠動⁠的說⁠法是 force 加受⁠詞加 to 不⁠定⁠詞

The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.

這場雨讓我們不得不取消野餐。

例句

  1. The more modern civilization advances, the longer we are forced to stay up at night, and the less sleep we get.
    現代文明越進步，我們就被迫越晚睡，睡眠也越少。
  2. The more you practice, the better you will get.
    你練習得越多，就會變得越好。

    他年紀越大，就變得越安靜。
  4. The sooner we leave, the earlier we will arrive.
    我們越早出發，就會越早到。
  5. The harder you study, the higher your score will be.
    你讀書越用功，分數就會越高。
  6. The better I get to know him, the more I like him.
    我越了解他，就越喜歡他。
  7. The more money you make, the more you spend.
    你賺越多錢，就花越多錢。
  8. The longer I waited, the angrier I got.
    我等得越久，就越生氣。
  9. I drink less coffee than before.
    我喝的咖啡比以前少。
  10. There are fewer students in my class this year.
    今年我班上的學生比較少。
  11. You should eat less sugar.
    你應該少吃糖。
  12. We have less time than we thought.
    我們的時間比我們想的少。
  13. Fewer people smoke in Japan now.
    現在日本抽菸的人比較少了。
  14. I made fewer mistakes on this test.
    我這次考試犯的錯比較少。
  15. We were forced to stay at home because of the typhoon.
    因為颱風，我們被迫待在家裡。
  16. The rain forced us to cancel the picnic.
    這場雨讓我們不得不取消野餐。

    那家店被迫提早關門。
  18. I was forced to wait for an hour at the airport.
    我在機場被迫等了一個小時。
  19. The heavy snow forced the trains to stop.
    大雪讓火車不得不停駛。
  20. The teacher forced Tom to clean the classroom alone.
    老師逼 Tom 一個人打掃教室。

    你花在手機上的時間越多，睡眠就越少。
  22. The more people come, the fewer empty seats there will be.
    來的人越多，空位就越少。
  23. The less you eat at night, the better you will sleep.
    你晚上吃得越少，就會睡得越好。
  24. The busier I am, the less time I have for my family.
    我越忙，陪家人的時間就越少。
  25. The later it got, the fewer people there were on the street.
    天越晚，街上的人就越少。
  26. Many workers are forced to work late every night.
    很多上班族每天晚上都被迫工作到很晚。
  27. Because of the bad weather, we were forced to change our plans.

  28. The more she smiled, the happier everyone felt.
    她笑得越多，大家就覺得越開心。
  29. Modern people get less exercise than people in the past.
    現代人的運動量比以前的人少。
  30. The money problem forced my uncle to sell his car.
    錢的問題讓我舅舅不得不把車賣掉。
  31. The earlier you go to bed, the better you feel, and the more you can do.
    你越早睡，精神就越好，能做的事也越多。
