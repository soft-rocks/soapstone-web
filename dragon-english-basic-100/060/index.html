Doctors and teachers are alike in that both of them deal not with things but with people.

醫生和老師有一點很像，就是他們面對的都不是東西，而是人。

alike 放⁠在 be 或 look 後⁠面，表⁠示相⁠像

Doctors and teachers are alike in that both of them deal not with things but with people.

be

My sister and I are alike.

我和我姊姊很像。
look

The twins look alike, but they act very differently.

那對雙胞胎長得很像，但個性很不一樣。

alike 是形容詞。主詞是兩個以上的人或東西。

in that 子⁠句說⁠明前⁠面那⁠句⁠話在哪⁠一方⁠面成⁠立

Doctors and teachers are alike in that both of them deal not with things but with people.

醫生和老師有一點很像，就是他們面對的都不是東西，而是人。

not A but B 表⁠示不⁠是 A，而⁠是 B

Doctors and teachers are alike in that both of them deal not with things but with people.

A 和 B 都是 with 開頭的介系詞片語。

not A but B 的 A 和 B 用同⁠一⁠種詞⁠類或結⁠構

Doctors and teachers are alike in that both of them deal not with things but with people.

名詞

He is not my brother but my cousin.

他不是我哥哥，而是我表哥。
介系詞片語

I went there not by bus but by train.

我去那裡不是搭公車，而是搭火車。
to 不定詞

She went there not to shop but to see her friend.

她去那裡不是為了買東西，而是為了見她朋友。

例句

  1. Doctors and teachers are alike in that both of them deal not with things but with people.
    醫生和老師有一點很像，就是他們面對的都不是東西，而是人。
  2. My sister and I are alike.
    我和我姊姊很像。
  3. These two houses are alike.
    這兩棟房子很相似。
  4. Tom and his father are alike in many ways.
    Tom 和他爸爸在很多方面都很像。
  5. The two cities are alike, but the food is different.
    這兩個城市很像，但是食物不一樣。
  6. Cats and dogs are alike in that they both need love.
    貓和狗的相似之處在於牠們都需要愛。
  7. Tokyo and Taipei are alike in that both have good subways.
    東京和台北的相似之處在於兩個城市都有很好的地鐵。
  8. This job is hard in that you have to stand all day.
    這份工作辛苦的地方在於你必須整天站著。
  9. The trip was special in that it was my first time abroad.
    這趟旅行特別的地方在於那是我第一次出國。
  10. My brother and I are different in that he likes sports and I like books.
    我和我哥哥的不同之處在於他喜歡運動，我喜歡書。
  11. He is not my brother but my cousin.
    他不是我哥哥，而是我表哥。
  12. I went there not by bus but by train.
    我去那裡不是搭公車，而是搭火車。
  13. The meeting is not on Monday but on Tuesday.
    會議不是在星期一，而是在星期二。
  14. What matters is not money but health.
    重要的不是錢，而是健康。
  15. She went there not to shop but to see her friend.
    她去那裡不是為了買東西，而是為了見她朋友。
  16. I study English not for tests but for travel.
    我學英文不是為了考試，而是為了旅行。
  17. This museum is special in that you can touch everything.
    這間博物館特別的地方在於所有東西都可以摸。
  18. Ken and his brother are alike in that both of them love trains.
    Ken 和他弟弟的相似之處在於兩個人都很愛火車。
  19. The festival is not in July but in August.
    慶典不是在七月，而是在八月。
  20. The letter was not for me but for my sister.
    那封信不是給我的，而是給我妹妹的。
  21. My two dogs look alike, but they don't like each other.
    我的兩隻狗長得很像，但牠們彼此不喜歡。
  22. We met not at school but at the station.
    我們碰面的地方不是學校，而是車站。
  23. Shop clerks and nurses are alike in that they deal with people all day.
    店員和護理師的相似之處在於他們整天都在跟人打交道。
  24. The problem is not the price but the size.
    問題不是價格，而是尺寸。
  25. Life in Tokyo is hard in that everything is expensive.
    在東京生活辛苦的地方在於什麼都很貴。
  26. Japan and Taiwan are alike in that both are islands.
    日本和台灣的相似之處在於兩個都是島嶼。
  27. He came to Japan not to work but to study.
    他來日本不是為了工作，而是為了讀書。
  28. I'm lucky in that I have good friends.
    我幸運的地方在於我有好朋友。
  29. My grandparents are alike in that they both love cooking.
    我爺爺奶奶的相似之處在於他們都很愛做菜。
  30. The answer was not in the book but on the website.
    答案不是在書裡，而是在網站上。
  31. The twins look alike, but they act very differently.
    那對雙胞胎長得很像，但個性很不一樣。
