例句

  1. Professional baseball is far more exciting at a stadium than on TV.
    職棒在球場看比在電視上看刺激得多。
  2. This book is far more interesting than the movie.
    這本書比電影有趣得多。
  3. My new phone is much lighter than my old one.
    我的新手機比舊的輕很多。
  4. Today is much hotter than yesterday.
    今天比昨天熱很多。
  5. Ann sings far better than I do.
    Ann 唱歌比我好聽得多。
  6. The test was much easier than I expected.
    考試比我預期的簡單得多。
  7. This hotel is far cheaper than the one near the station.
    這家旅館比車站附近那家便宜得多。
  8. My sister is a little taller than my mother.
    我姊姊比我媽媽高一點。
  9. This bag is a little more expensive than that one.
    這個包包比那個貴一點。
  10. Food tastes better at home than at a restaurant.
    食物在家裡吃比在餐廳吃好吃。
  11. It is colder in Hokkaido than in Tokyo.
    北海道比東京冷。
  12. Fruit is cheaper at the market than at the supermarket.
    水果在市場買比在超市買便宜。
  13. I can study better in the library than at home.
    我在圖書館比在家裡讀得好。
  14. People drive more carefully in Japan than in my country.
    在日本，大家開車比在我的國家小心。
  15. The stars look brighter in the mountains than in the city.
    星星在山上看起來比在城市亮。
  16. Music sounds far better at a live concert than on a smartphone.
    音樂在現場演唱會聽比用智慧型手機聽好聽得多。
  17. Rent is much higher in Taipei than in Tainan.
    台北的房租比台南高很多。
  18. The sea is much warmer in Okinawa than in Hokkaido.
    沖繩的海比北海道的海溫暖很多。
  19. Movies are far more exciting in a theater than at home.
    電影在電影院看比在家裡看刺激得多。
  20. Ramen is a little cheaper in this shop than in that one.
    這家店的拉麵比那家便宜一點。
  21. The air is much cleaner in the country than in the city.
    鄉下的空氣比城市乾淨很多。
  22. I feel far more relaxed at my grandmother's house than at school.
    我在奶奶家比在學校放鬆得多。
  23. Soccer is more popular in Europe than in Japan.
    足球在歐洲比在日本受歡迎。
  24. My English is much better now than last year.
    我的英文現在比去年好很多。
  25. The train is far faster than the bus.
    火車比公車快得多。
  26. Sushi tastes much better in Japan than in other countries.
    壽司在日本吃比在其他國家吃好吃很多。
  27. Tom runs a little faster than his brother.
    Tom 跑得比他弟弟快一點。
  28. The view is far more beautiful from the top of the tower than from the ground.
    從塔頂看的景色比從地面看美得多。
  29. This question is much more difficult than the last one.
    這一題比上一題難很多。
  30. Prices are a little higher at the airport than in town.
    機場的物價比市區高一點。
