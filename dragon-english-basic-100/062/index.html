This motorbike is about twice as expensive in the U.K. as in Japan.

這台機車在英國的價錢大約是在日本的兩倍。

倍⁠數放⁠在第⁠一⁠個 as 的前⁠面

This motorbike is about twice as expensive in the U.K. as in Japan.

這台機車在英國的價錢大約是在日本的兩倍。

about 放在倍數前面。

兩⁠倍用 twice，三⁠倍以⁠上用 times，一⁠半用 half

This motorbike is about twice as expensive in the U.K. as in Japan.

twice

My room is twice as big as yours.

我的房間是你的兩倍大。
three times

This bridge is three times as long as that one.

這座橋是那座的三倍長。
half

This bag is half as heavy as my old one.

這個包包只有我舊包包的一半重。

比⁠較數⁠量⁠時，用 as much 或 as many 加名⁠詞

This motorbike is about twice as expensive in the U.K. as in Japan.

as much
不可數名詞

Tom earns twice as much money as his brother.

Tom 賺的錢是他弟弟的兩倍。
as many
複數名詞

Tom has three times as many comics as I do.

Tom 的漫畫是我的三倍多。

第⁠二⁠個 as 後⁠面⁠的介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語保⁠留介⁠系⁠詞

This motorbike is about twice as expensive in the U.K. as in Japan.

比較的是在英國的價錢和在日本的價錢。

沒⁠有倍⁠數⁠的 as ... as 也⁠可⁠以比⁠較兩⁠個介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

This motorbike is about twice as expensive in the U.K. as in Japan.

否定句

The beach isn't as crowded in winter as in summer.

海灘冬天沒有夏天那麼擠。
疑問句

Is it as hot in Kaohsiung as in Taipei?

高雄跟台北一樣熱嗎？
just as

This ramen shop is just as busy on weekdays as on weekends.

這家拉麵店平日跟週末一樣忙。

just as 表示兩邊完全一樣。
