例句

  1. Just because young people today read less than they used to, it doesn’t always follow that they are less eager to learn.
    現在的年輕人讀的書比以前少，不一定就表示他們比較不想學習。
  2. Just because he is quiet, it doesn't follow that he is unhappy.
    他很安靜，不代表他不開心。
  3. Just because a book is old, it doesn't follow that it is boring.
    一本書很舊，不代表它很無聊。
  4. Just because a restaurant is expensive, it doesn't always follow that the food is good.
    一家餐廳很貴，不一定代表食物好吃。
  5. Just because Tom failed once, it doesn't follow that he will fail again.
    Tom 失敗過一次，不代表他會再失敗。
  6. Just because you live in Japan, it doesn't follow that you can speak Japanese.
    你住在日本，不代表你會說日文。
  7. Just because it is sunny now, it doesn't follow that it won't rain later.
    現在是晴天，不代表等一下不會下雨。
  8. My father works less than he used to.
    我爸爸工作得比以前少。
  9. I eat more vegetables than I used to.
    我吃的蔬菜比以前多。
  10. Ann laughs more than she used to.
    Ann 比以前常笑。
  11. My grandmother walks more slowly than she used to.
    我奶奶走路比以前慢。
  12. We watch less TV than we used to.
    我們看電視看得比以前少。
  13. My uncle smokes less than he used to.
    我叔叔菸抽得比以前少了。
  14. The children were eager to open their presents.
    孩子們很想拆自己的禮物。
  15. She is eager to study abroad.
    她很想出國讀書。
  16. The new students are eager to make friends.
    新同學很想交朋友。
  17. Tom was eager to tell us about his trip to Taiwan.
    Tom 很想跟我們說他去台灣旅行的事。
  18. I'm eager to see the new movie.
    我很想看那部新電影。
  19. My son is less eager to practice the piano than he used to be.
    我兒子沒有以前那麼想練鋼琴。
  20. Just because I read less than I used to, it doesn't follow that I don't like books.
    我書讀得比以前少，不代表我不喜歡書。
  21. Just because she is eager to learn, it doesn't follow that she will learn quickly.
    她很想學，不代表她會學得很快。
  22. Just because Ken is tall, it doesn't follow that he is good at basketball.
    Ken 很高，不代表他很會打籃球。
  23. I sleep less than I used to because of my new job.
    因為我的新工作，我睡得比以前少。
  24. Young people today are eager to travel abroad.
    現在的年輕人很想出國旅行。
  25. Just because something is cheap, it doesn't always follow that it is bad.
    東西便宜，不一定代表不好。
  26. The students were eager to go on the school trip.
    學生們很想去校外教學。
  27. My mother drinks less coffee than she used to.
    我媽媽喝的咖啡比以前少。
  28. Just because you passed the test, it doesn't follow that you can stop studying.
    你通過了考試，不代表你可以不用再讀書了。
  29. Many visitors to Kyoto are eager to see the old temples.
    很多去京都的遊客很想看那些古老的寺廟。
  30. Just because my brother plays games a lot, it doesn't always follow that he is lazy.
    我弟弟常玩遊戲，不一定代表他很懶。
  31. The kids were eager for summer vacation.
    孩子們很期待暑假。
  32. The fans are eager for news about the band.
    歌迷很想知道那個樂團的消息。
