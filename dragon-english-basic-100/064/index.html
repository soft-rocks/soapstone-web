Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

坦白說，我昨天考的入學考試比我預期的難得多。

句⁠首⁠的 frankly speaking 表⁠示說⁠話⁠的人用什⁠麼態⁠度說⁠話

Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

坦白說，我昨天考的入學考試比我預期的難得多。

speaking 的主⁠詞是說⁠話⁠的⁠人，不⁠一⁠定是子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

一般來說，日本的火車很準時。

常⁠見⁠的 speaking 片⁠語

Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

Frankly speaking坦白說
Generally speaking一般來說
Honestly speaking老實說

far 放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面表⁠示差很⁠多，意⁠思跟 much 一⁠樣

Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

far

This suitcase is far heavier than mine.

這個行李箱比我的重很多。
much

My sister is much taller than me.

我姊姊比我高很多。

預⁠料發⁠生在結⁠果之⁠前，所⁠以 than 後⁠面用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式

Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

意思是比我原本預料的更難。

口⁠語也常⁠用過⁠去⁠式，說⁠成 than I expected 或 than I thought

Frankly speaking, the entrance exams I took yesterday were far more difficult than I had expected.

過去完成式

The trip was more expensive than we had expected.

過去式

The soup was hotter than I thought.

湯比我想的還燙。
過去式

新老師比我預料的親切。
