A lot of housewives complain that prices are too high.

很多家庭主婦抱怨物價太高。

不⁠滿⁠的內⁠容是完⁠整⁠的子⁠句⁠時，用 complain that

A lot of housewives complain that prices are too high.

很多家庭主婦抱怨物價太高。

complain 表示說出自己不滿意的事。

complain about 接名⁠詞，complain to 接抱⁠怨⁠的對⁠象

A lot of housewives complain that prices are too high.

that + 子句

例句

  1. A lot of housewives complain that prices are too high.
    很多家庭主婦抱怨物價太高。
  2. My brother always complains that he has too much homework.
    我弟弟總是抱怨他功課太多。
  3. The guests complained about the noise.
    客人抱怨噪音。
  4. Ann complained that the room was too cold.
    Ann 抱怨房間太冷。
  5. Students often complain about school lunches.
    學生常常抱怨學校的午餐。
  6. My father complained to the waiter about the soup.
    我爸爸跟服務生抱怨那碗湯。
  7. Don't complain about the weather. We can't change it.
    不要抱怨天氣。反正我們也改變不了。
  8. The neighbors complained about our dog.
    鄰居抱怨我們的狗。
  9. Stop complaining about your job and look for a new one.
    不要再抱怨你的工作了，去找新的吧。
  10. This room is too small for six people.
    這個房間對六個人來說太小了。
  11. The tea is too strong. Can I have some water?
    這茶太濃了。可以給我一點水嗎？
  12. Tom complained that nobody listened to him.
    Tom 抱怨沒有人聽他說話。
  13. My father complained about the long meeting.
    我爸爸抱怨那場很長的會議。
  14. This soup is too salty to eat.
    這碗湯太鹹了，沒辦法喝。
  15. The shoes are too small for me.
    這雙鞋對我來說太小了。
  16. It is too late to call him now.
    現在打給他太晚了。
  17. The music was too loud, so I couldn't sleep.
    音樂太大聲，所以我睡不著。
  18. My little brother is too young to go to school.
    我弟弟年紀太小，還不能上學。
  19. Many people complain that train tickets are too expensive.
    很多人抱怨火車票太貴。
  20. My mother complained that the price of vegetables was too high.
    我媽媽抱怨菜價太高。
  21. Some students complained that the test was too long.
    有些學生抱怨考試太長。
  22. The prices in Tokyo are too high for me.
    東京的物價對我來說太高了。
  23. Tourists sometimes complain about high prices at the airport.
    遊客有時候抱怨機場的價格太高。
  24. Ken complained that his new phone was too heavy.
    Ken 抱怨他的新手機太重。
  25. If the price is too high, don't buy it.
    如果價格太高，就不要買。
  26. My grandmother never complains about anything.
    我奶奶從來不抱怨任何事。
  27. The customers complained to the manager that the line was too long.
    客人跟經理抱怨隊伍太長。
  28. The test was too difficult, so many students complained.
    考試太難了，所以很多學生抱怨。
  29. This math problem is too hard for me to solve.
    這題數學對我來說太難了，我解不出來。
  30. Are you going to complain about the price?
    你要抱怨價格嗎？
  31. This book is too difficult for my son to read.
    這本書對我兒子來說太難了，他看不懂。
