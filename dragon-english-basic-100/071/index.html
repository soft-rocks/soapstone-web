However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

不管你做多少運動，不計算卡路里的話就不會瘦。

however 子⁠句⁠的條⁠件不⁠會改⁠變主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的結⁠果

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

不管你做多少運動，不計算卡路里的話就不會瘦。

however 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞、副⁠詞或 many，意⁠思是不⁠管多⁠麼

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

形容詞

However tired you are, you should brush your teeth.

不管你多累，你都應該刷牙。
副詞

However hard I try, I can't get up early.

不管我多努力，我都沒辦法早起。
many + 複數名詞

However many people come, there will be enough food.

不管來多少人，食物都會夠。

however much 後⁠面可⁠以接不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞，也⁠可⁠以不⁠接名⁠詞

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

不可數名詞

However much money you have, you can't buy happiness.

不管你有多少錢，你都買不到快樂。
不接名詞

However much I eat, I'm still hungry.

不管我吃多少，我還是餓。

no matter how 的意⁠思和用⁠法跟 however 一⁠樣

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

副詞

No matter how hard he works, his boss is never happy.

不管他多努力工作，他的老闆從來都不滿意。
many + 複數名詞

No matter how many times I call her, she doesn't answer.

不管我打給她幾次，她都不接。

unless 的意⁠思是除⁠非，等⁠於 if ... not

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

如果你不計算卡路里，就不會變瘦。

unless 子⁠句講未⁠來⁠的⁠事，動⁠詞也用現⁠在⁠式

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

句尾

I'll go to the beach unless it rains.

除非下雨，不然我會去海邊。
句首

Unless you hurry, you'll miss the train.

除非你快一點，不然你會趕不上火車。

unless 子句放在句首時，後面加逗號。

exercise 表⁠示運⁠動這⁠件⁠事⁠時是⁠不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞

However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.

get

You should get some exercise every day.

你應該每天做一些運動。
get

How much exercise do you get every week?

你每個星期做多少運動？
do

My sister does a lot of exercise, but she doesn't lose weight.

我姊姊做很多運動，但她沒有變瘦。

exercise 前面不加 an，後面不加 -⁠s。

例句

  1. However much exercise you get, you won’t lose weight unless you count your calories.
    不管你做多少運動，不計算卡路里的話就不會瘦。
  2. However hard I try, I can't get up early.
    不管我多努力，我都沒辦法早起。
  3. However tired you are, you should brush your teeth.
    不管你多累，你都應該刷牙。
  4. However much money you have, you can't buy happiness.
    不管你有多少錢，你都買不到快樂。
  5. However busy Tom is, he always calls his mother on Sundays.
    不管 Tom 多忙，他星期天總是打電話給他媽媽。
  6. However cold it is, my brother wears shorts.
    不管天氣多冷，我弟弟都穿短褲。
  7. However much I eat, I'm still hungry.
    不管我吃多少，我還是餓。
  8. You won't pass the test unless you study.
    除非你讀書，不然你不會通過考試。
  9. I'll go to the beach unless it rains.
    除非下雨，不然我會去海邊。
  10. Don't call me unless it is important.
    除非很重要，不然不要打給我。
  11. Unless you hurry, you'll miss the train.
    除非你快一點，不然你會趕不上火車。
  12. The shop won't open unless the owner comes back.
    除非老闆回來，不然店不會開。
  13. I can't help you unless you tell me the truth.
    除非你告訴我實話，不然我沒辦法幫你。
  14. You should get some exercise every day.
    你應該每天做一些運動。
  15. How much exercise do you get every week?
    你每個星期做多少運動？
  16. My father gets very little exercise.
    我爸爸很少運動。
  17. Walking is a good way to get exercise.
    走路是做運動的好方法。
  18. The doctor said I need more exercise.
    醫生說我需要多運動。
  19. Swimming is good exercise for your back.
    游泳對你的背來說是很好的運動。
  20. You won't lose weight unless you eat less.
    除非你吃少一點，不然你不會變瘦。
  21. However much I walk, I can't lose weight.
    不管我走多少路，我都瘦不下來。
  22. Unless you get enough sleep, you'll feel tired in class.
    除非你睡得夠，不然你上課會覺得很累。
  23. However late it is, my mother waits for me to come home.
    不管多晚，我媽媽都會等我回家。
  24. I don't do much exercise because I sit at a desk all day.
    我沒做什麼運動，因為我整天坐在辦公桌前。
  25. However many times I read this book, I still enjoy it.
    不管這本書我看了幾次，我還是很喜歡。
  26. My doctor says I won't lose weight unless I stop eating sweets.
    我的醫生說，除非我不吃甜食，不然我不會變瘦。
  27. However difficult the question is, Ken never gives up.
    不管題目多難，Ken 從來不放棄。
  28. We'll play soccer tomorrow unless it snows.
    除非下雪，不然我們明天會踢足球。
  29. My sister does a lot of exercise, but she doesn't lose weight.
    我姊姊做很多運動，但她沒有變瘦。
  30. However much you study, you won't remember new words unless you use them.
    不管你讀多少書，除非你去用新的字，不然你還是記不住。
  31. However many people come, there will be enough food.
    不管來多少人，食物都會夠。
  32. However carefully I write, my teacher always finds a mistake.
    不管我寫得多仔細，我的老師總是能找到錯誤。
  33. No matter how hard he works, his boss is never happy.
    不管他多努力工作，他的老闆從來都不滿意。
  34. No matter how many times I call her, she doesn't answer.
    不管我打給她幾次，她都不接。
