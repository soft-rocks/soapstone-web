Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.

就算你以為自己認識一個字，去查字典的話也可能學到新的東西。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句說⁠的事跟 although 子⁠句預⁠期⁠的結⁠果不⁠同

Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.

就算你以為自己認識一個字，去查字典的話也可能學到新的東西。

although 的意思是雖然。although 子句放在句首時，後面加逗號。

although 子⁠句也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠要⁠子⁠句後⁠面

Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.

句首

Although it was raining, we went for a walk.

雖然在下雨，我們還是去散步了。
句尾

We enjoyed the trip although it rained every day.

雖然每天都下雨，我們這趟旅行還是玩得很開心。
though

My brother went out though he had a cold.

我弟弟雖然感冒了，還是出門了。

though 的意思一樣，常用在口語。

look up 的受⁠詞是名⁠詞⁠時，可⁠以放⁠在 up 前⁠面或後⁠面

Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.

look up + 名詞

I looked up the word in the dictionary.

我在字典裡查了這個字。
look + 名詞 + up

I looked the address up on my phone.

我用手機查了那個地址。

look up 表示在字典、網路或書裡查一個字或資料。

look up 的受⁠詞是代⁠名⁠詞⁠時，放⁠在 look 和 up 中⁠間

Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.

may 表⁠示有⁠可⁠能，但不⁠確⁠定

Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.

may

It may snow tonight.

今晚可能會下雪。
might

穿件外套。等一下可能會變冷。
may not
可能不會

Tom 可能不會來派對。

例句

  1. Although you believe you know a word, you may learn something new if you look it up in the dictionary.
    就算你以為自己認識一個字，去查字典的話也可能學到新的東西。
  2. Although it was raining, we went for a walk.
    雖然在下雨，我們還是去散步了。
  3. Although Tom is young, he is very wise.
    雖然 Tom 很年輕，但他很有智慧。
  4. Although I was tired, I finished my homework.
    雖然我很累，我還是寫完了功課。
  5. Although the hotel was old, it was very clean.
    雖然那家飯店很舊，但很乾淨。
  6. Although she studied hard, she didn't pass the test.
    雖然她很用功，她還是沒通過考試。
  7. My brother went out though he had a cold.
    我弟弟雖然感冒了，還是出門了。
  8. I looked up the word in the dictionary.
    我在字典裡查了這個字。
  9. If you don't know the word, look it up.
    如果你不知道這個字，就去查。
  10. Let's look up the train times on the Internet.
    我們上網查火車時刻吧。
  11. I didn't know her address, so I looked it up.
    我不知道她的地址，所以我去查了。
  12. Look these words up before the next class.
    下堂課之前把這些字查一查。
  13. My father looks up new words in an old dictionary.
    我爸爸用一本舊字典查生字。
  14. It may snow tonight.
    今晚可能會下雪。
  15. Tom may not come to the party.
    Tom 可能不會來派對。
  16. My parents may visit us next month.
    我爸媽下個月可能會來看我們。
  17. You may find the answer in this book.
    你可能會在這本書裡找到答案。
  18. Ken may know the answer to this question.
    Ken 可能知道這個問題的答案。
  19. Wear a jacket. It might get cold later.
    穿件外套。等一下可能會變冷。
  20. Although I know this town well, I may get lost at night.
    雖然我很熟這個小鎮，晚上我還是可能會迷路。
  21. If you look it up, you may find a different meaning.
    如果你去查，你可能會找到不一樣的意思。
  22. Though the word looks easy, it has many meanings.
    雖然這個字看起來很簡單，卻有很多意思。
  23. I always look up words I don't know on my phone.
    我總是用手機查我不懂的字。
  24. Although we left early, we may not get there on time.
    雖然我們很早出發，我們還是可能不會準時到。
  25. You don't know his phone number? Look it up in the class list.
    你不知道他的電話號碼？在班上的名冊裡查。
  26. Although my grandmother is eighty, she still works in the garden every day.
    雖然我奶奶八十歲了，她每天還是在院子裡整理花草。
  27. The museum may be closed on Mondays. Let's look up its hours.
    博物館星期一可能沒開。我們查一下它的開放時間吧。
  28. Although I've lived in Japan for years, I still learn new words every day.
    雖然我在日本住了好幾年，我每天還是會學到新的字。
  29. If you look up this word, you may be surprised.
    如果你去查這個字，你可能會很驚訝。
  30. Although the test looks hard, you might get a high score.
    雖然考試看起來很難，你還是可能拿高分。
  31. We enjoyed the trip although it rained every day.
    雖然每天都下雨，我們這趟旅行還是玩得很開心。
  32. I looked the address up on my phone.
    我用手機查了那個地址。
