例句

  1. Ways of greeting vary from country to country. In Japan, bowing is more common than shaking hands.
    打招呼的方式每個國家都不一樣。在日本，鞠躬比握手更常見。
  2. Prices vary from shop to shop.
    價格每家店都不一樣。
  3. The weather varies from day to day.
    天氣每天都不一樣。
  4. School rules vary from school to school.
    校規每所學校都不一樣。
  5. The taste of ramen varies from city to city.
    拉麵的味道每個城市都不一樣。
  6. Wedding customs differ from country to country.
    婚禮習俗每個國家都不一樣。
  7. Hotel prices vary from season to season.
    飯店價格每個季節都不一樣。
  8. Swimming is good exercise.
    游泳是很好的運動。
  9. Learning a new language takes time.
    學一種新語言需要時間。
  10. Getting up early is hard for me.
    早起對我來說很難。
  11. Eating too much candy is bad for your teeth.
    吃太多糖果對你的牙齒不好。
  12. Taking pictures is not allowed in this museum.
    這間博物館不准拍照。
  13. Cooking with my mother makes me happy.
    跟我媽媽一起做菜讓我很開心。
  14. Walking is healthier than driving.
    走路比開車健康。
  15. Reading books is more interesting than watching TV.
    看書比看電視有趣。
  16. Taking the train is faster than taking the bus.
    搭火車比搭公車快。
  17. Speaking English is easier than writing it.
    說英文比寫英文容易。
  18. For me, staying home is more relaxing than going out.
    對我來說，待在家比出門輕鬆。
  19. In Taiwan, riding a scooter is more common than riding a bike.
    在台灣，騎機車比騎腳踏車常見。
  20. Table manners vary from culture to culture.
    餐桌禮儀每個文化都不一樣。
  21. In America, shaking hands is more common than bowing.
    在美國，握手比鞠躬常見。
  22. Bowing is an important part of Japanese culture.
    鞠躬是日本文化很重要的一部分。
  23. The way people celebrate New Year varies from family to family.
    人們過新年的方式每個家庭都不一樣。
  24. In Japan, paying in cash is more common than paying by card.
    在日本，付現金比刷卡常見。
  25. Saying hello to your neighbors is a good habit.
    跟鄰居打招呼是好習慣。
  26. Opening times vary from store to store.
    營業時間每家店都不一樣。
  27. Among young people, texting is more common than calling.
    在年輕人之間，傳訊息比打電話常見。
  28. Shaking hands with a stranger feels strange to some Japanese people.
    跟陌生人握手對有些日本人來說感覺很奇怪。
  29. How deeply people bow varies from person to person.
    鞠躬要彎多深，每個人都不一樣。
  30. Running in the morning is better than running at night.
    早上跑步比晚上跑步好。
  31. I see my old friends from time to time.
    我偶爾會跟老朋友見面。
  32. The shop is open from Monday to Saturday.
    這家店星期一到星期六營業。
  33. We moved from place to place when I was a child.
    我小時候，我們到處搬家。
  34. The students sold cookies from door to door.
    學生們挨家挨戶賣餅乾。
  35. My uncle calls us from time to time.
    我叔叔偶爾會打電話給我們。
  36. The band traveled from place to place by bus.
    這個樂團搭巴士到處跑。
  37. The man went from door to door looking for his lost dog.
    那個男人挨家挨戶找他走失的狗。
  38. Bus fares differ from city to city.
    公車票價每個城市都不一樣。
  39. It takes about an hour from Taipei to Taichung by high-speed rail.
    從台北到台中搭高鐵大約要一個小時。
