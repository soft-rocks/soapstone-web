be located 表示一個地方或建築在哪裡。主詞是城市、建築、店這類不會移動的東西。

說在某⁠個地⁠點⁠的哪⁠一⁠邊，用方⁠位詞加 of

Hamamatsu is located east of Lake Hamana, which is famous for its beautiful scenery and delicious fish.

方位詞前面不加 the。

逗⁠號後⁠面⁠的 which 子⁠句補⁠充說⁠明前⁠面⁠的名⁠詞

Hamamatsu is located east of Lake Hamana, which is famous for its beautiful scenery and delicious fish.

拿掉這個子句，句子的意思還是完整的。這種子句不用 that。

which 可⁠以當子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞或受⁠詞

Hamamatsu is located east of Lake Hamana, which is famous for its beautiful scenery and delicious fish.

主詞
which + 動詞

I visited Kyoto, which has many old temples.

我去了京都，那裡有很多古老的寺廟。
受詞
which + 主詞 + 動詞

I lost my umbrella, which I bought in Tokyo.

我弄丟了我的傘，那是我在東京買的。

子⁠句在句⁠子中⁠間⁠時，前⁠後都加逗⁠號

Hamamatsu is located east of Lake Hamana, which is famous for its beautiful scenery and delicious fish.
