When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

我在巴黎念書的時候，一位有名的畫家請我在一家一流的餐廳吃了一頓飯。

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式說正⁠在進⁠行⁠的動⁠作，過⁠去⁠式說其⁠間發⁠生⁠的一⁠件⁠事

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

我在巴黎念書的時候，一位有名的畫家請我在一家一流的餐廳吃了一頓飯。

when 和 while 都常帶⁠出過⁠去進⁠行⁠式

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

when

When I was walking home, it started to rain.

我走路回家的時候，開始下雨了。
while

While we were having dinner, the phone rang.

我們吃晚餐的時候，電話響了。

when 子⁠句放⁠在⁠後⁠面⁠時用過⁠去⁠式

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

地震發生的時候，我正在洗澡。

正在做一件事的時候，發生了另一件事。

buy 後⁠面⁠接兩⁠個受⁠詞，人在⁠前，東⁠西在⁠後

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

東⁠西放⁠在⁠前⁠面⁠時，人用 for 帶⁠出

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

我買了一個蛋糕給我奶奶。

make 和 cook 也⁠有這兩⁠種用⁠法

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

東西 + for + 人

I made a birthday card for my mom.

我做了一張生日卡片給我媽媽。
人 + 東西

My father cooked us breakfast this morning.

我爸爸今天早上煮早餐給我們吃。

用連⁠字⁠號連起⁠來⁠的詞放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面，當形⁠容⁠詞

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

first⁠-⁠class 表示最高等級的。

數⁠字和名⁠詞連起⁠來⁠時，名⁠詞用單⁠數

When I was studying in Paris, a famous painter bought me a meal at a first-class restaurant.

year

I have a ten-year-old brother.

我有一個十歲的弟弟。
hour

It was a two-hour movie.

那是一部兩小時的電影。
