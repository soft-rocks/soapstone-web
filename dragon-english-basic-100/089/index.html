Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

Tom 終於實現了當太空人的夢想，可是犧牲了很多其他的東西。

of 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面，說⁠明名⁠詞⁠的內⁠容

Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

Tom 的夢想是當太空人。

idea、chance、habit 也常接 of 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

idea

I like the idea of living in the country.

我喜歡住在鄉下這個主意。
chance

Is there any chance of winning the game?

有沒有贏得比賽的機會？
habit

She has a habit of biting her nails.

她有咬指甲的習慣。

dream、way、hope 也這樣用。

realize 接夢⁠想或目⁠標⁠時，意⁠思是實⁠現

Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

她實現了開咖啡店的夢想。

realize 接 that 子⁠句⁠時，意⁠思是意⁠識⁠到、發⁠現

Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

Tom 突然發現自己忘了帶錢包。

at the expense of 表⁠示得⁠到一⁠個結⁠果，但⁠是犧⁠牲了另⁠一⁠個東⁠西

Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

他日夜工作，犧牲了自己的健康。

at the expense of 常放⁠在句⁠尾，前⁠面可⁠以加 but

Tom finally realized his dream of becoming an astronaut, but at the expense of many other things.

Tom 終於實現了當太空人的夢想，可是犧牲了很多其他的東西。
