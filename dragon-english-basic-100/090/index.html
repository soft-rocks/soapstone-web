If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

如果城市生活讓你壓力大又疲倦，最好去山上或海邊放鬆，消除這些壓力和疲勞。

give 後⁠面⁠接兩⁠個受⁠詞，人在⁠前，東⁠西在⁠後

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

主詞 city life 是單數，所以動詞是 gives。

東⁠西放⁠在⁠前⁠面⁠時，人用 to 帶⁠出

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

請把這封信交給你爸爸。

主⁠詞不⁠是人⁠時，give 表⁠示帶⁠來某⁠種感⁠覺或結⁠果

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

這首音樂讓我很有精神。

it is best to 的 it 是形⁠式⁠主⁠詞，真⁠正⁠的主⁠詞是 to 不⁠定⁠詞

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

it is best to 表示最好的做法是什麼。

比⁠較兩⁠個做⁠法用 it is better to，另⁠一⁠個做⁠法用 than to

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

搭火車比開車好。

最⁠好不⁠要做⁠什⁠麼，用 it is best not to

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

睡前最好不要吃太多。

in order to 表⁠示目⁠的

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

意思跟表示目的的 to 不定詞一樣。

否⁠定⁠的目⁠的用 in order not to

if 子⁠句說一⁠般⁠的情⁠況或未⁠來可⁠能⁠的⁠事，用現⁠在⁠式

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

if 子句放在前面時，後面加逗號。

if 句⁠的主⁠要⁠子⁠句可⁠以用現⁠在⁠式、will、祈⁠使⁠句或 it is best to

If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.

  1. If city life gives you stress and fatigue, it is best to relax in the mountains or on beaches in order to relieve them.
