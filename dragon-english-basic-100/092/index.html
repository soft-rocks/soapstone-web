In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

在美國，父母去哪裡都開車載小孩，一直到小孩年紀夠大、可以考駕照為止。

drive 接人當受⁠詞，表⁠示開⁠車⁠載人⁠去某⁠個地⁠方

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

地⁠方是名⁠詞⁠時，前⁠面加 to

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

我媽媽每天早上開車送我上學。

everywhere、home、there 是副⁠詞，前⁠面不⁠加 to

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

everywhere

My uncle drove us everywhere when we visited him in Hokkaido.

我們去北海道找我叔叔的時候，他開車載我們到處去。
home

Can you drive me home?

你可以開車送我回家嗎？
there

The museum is far from here, so I'll drive you there.

博物館離這裡很遠，所以我開車送你去。

until 表⁠示動⁠作或狀⁠態持⁠續到某⁠個時⁠間點

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

我結婚以前都跟爸媽住。

until 子⁠句講未⁠來⁠的事也用現⁠在⁠式

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

好一點之前都待在床上。

原句講的是一般的情況，所以用現在式 are。

enough 放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞後⁠面，表⁠示程⁠度夠做某⁠件⁠事

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

not 加形⁠容⁠詞加 enough，表⁠示程⁠度不⁠夠，做不⁠到那⁠件⁠事

In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.

例句

  1. In the U.S., parents drive their children everywhere until they are old enough to get a driver’s license.
    在美國，父母去哪裡都開車載小孩，一直到小孩年紀夠大、可以考駕照為止。
  2. My mother drives me to school every morning.
    我媽媽每天早上開車送我上學。
  3. Can you drive me home?
    你可以開車送我回家嗎？
  4. My father drove us to the airport.
    我爸爸開車載我們去機場。
  5. I'll drive you to the station.
    我開車送你去車站。
  6. Tom drove his grandmother to the hospital.
    Tom 開車載他奶奶去醫院。
  7. My uncle drove us everywhere when we visited him in Hokkaido.
    我們去北海道找我叔叔的時候，他開車載我們到處去。
  8. It was late, so Ann's father drove me home.
    很晚了，所以 Ann 的爸爸開車送我回家。
  9. I lived with my parents until I got married.
    我結婚以前都跟爸媽住。
  10. Stay in bed until you feel better.
    好一點之前都待在床上。
  11. Please wait until I come back.
    請等到我回來。
  12. We played soccer until it got dark.
    我們踢足球踢到天黑。
  13. I didn't know about it until you told me.
    你告訴我之前，我都不知道這件事。
  14. You can't drive until you are eighteen.
    你滿十八歲之前不能開車。
  15. She is old enough to travel alone.
    她年紀夠大，可以自己旅行了。
  16. This box isn't big enough to hold all the books.
    這個箱子不夠大，裝不下所有的書。
  17. Is the lake deep enough to swim in?
    這座湖夠深，可以游泳嗎？
  18. My little brother is not tall enough to ride this roller coaster.
    我弟弟不夠高，不能坐這個雲霄飛車。
  19. He was kind enough to show me the way.
    他很好心，幫我指路。
  20. Are you strong enough to carry this bag?
    你力氣夠大，提得動這個袋子嗎？
  21. The room was large enough to hold fifty people.
    那個房間夠大，容納得下五十個人。
  22. In Japan, many students take trains everywhere until they are old enough to drive.
    在日本，很多學生搭火車到處去，直到他們年紀夠大可以開車。
  23. My parents drove me to the cram school until I got a bike.
    我爸媽開車送我去補習班，直到我有了腳踏車。
  24. I'm not old enough to get a driver's license yet.
    我年紀還不夠大，不能拿駕照。
  25. Keep walking until you see a big red building.
    一直走，走到你看到一棟紅色的大樓。
  26. My sister is old enough to drive me to school now.
    我姊姊現在年紀夠大，可以開車送我上學了。
  27. We waited at the bus stop until the last bus came.
    我們在公車站等到末班車來。
  28. This coat is warm enough to wear in Hokkaido.
    這件外套夠暖，可以在北海道穿。
  29. My grandfather drove us there in his old car.
    我爺爺開他的舊車載我們去那裡。
  30. Don't open your eyes until I say so.
    等我說可以再張開眼睛。
  31. The museum is far from here, so I'll drive you there.
    博物館離這裡很遠，所以我開車送你去。
