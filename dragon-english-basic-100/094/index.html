“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

「你覺得我們的新老師怎麼樣？」「她身上有某種吸引我的特質。」

that 在子⁠句⁠裡當主⁠詞，後⁠面直⁠接接動⁠詞

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

Tom 有一隻會游泳的狗。

子⁠句⁠的動⁠詞跟⁠著它說⁠明⁠的名⁠詞變⁠化

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

我想要一支拍照很好看的手機。

人也⁠可⁠以用 who，東⁠西也⁠可⁠以用 which

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

人
who

I have an aunt who works at the airport.

我有一個在機場工作的阿姨。
東西
which

This is the road which goes to the beach.

這是通往海邊的路。

人或東⁠西有說⁠不⁠出⁠來⁠的特⁠質，用 There is something about

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

his voice

There is something about his voice that makes people listen.

他的聲音有某種讓人想聽的地方。
the old man

There was something about the old man that reminded me of my grandfather.

那位老先生有某種讓我想起我爺爺的地方。

something about her 是她身上的某種特質。

that 子⁠句說⁠明 something，不⁠是 about 後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

動詞跟著單數的 something 用 attracts。

What do you think of 問對⁠方對人或東⁠西⁠的看⁠法

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

中文說怎麼樣，英文的疑問詞用 what。

of 可⁠以換⁠成 about，問過⁠去⁠的經⁠驗⁠時 do 改⁠成 did

“What do you think of our new teacher?” “There is something about her that attracts me.”

about

What do you think about my idea?

你覺得我的想法怎麼樣？
did

What did you think of the movie?

你覺得那部電影怎麼樣？
