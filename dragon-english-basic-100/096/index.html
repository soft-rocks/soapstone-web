Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

每個人生來都有一種天分。問題是他們能不能找到它。

出⁠生用被⁠動形⁠式 be born，born 不⁠能單⁠獨當動⁠詞

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

be born with 表⁠示一⁠出⁠生就⁠有某⁠樣東⁠西

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

有些人天生有一副好嗓子。

年⁠份、月⁠份和地⁠方用 in，日⁠期用 on，時⁠刻用 at

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

in
地方、年份

I was born in Taichung in 2008.

我 2008 年在台中出生。
on
日期

My son was born on May 5.

我兒子五月五日出生。
at
時刻

The baby was born at three in the morning.

寶寶在凌晨三點出生。

whether 子⁠句放⁠在 be 動⁠詞後⁠面，說⁠明主⁠詞⁠的內⁠容

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

whether 表示是不是。

or not 可⁠以放⁠在句⁠尾，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在 whether 後⁠面

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

句尾

The problem is whether he will come or not.

問題是他會不會來。
whether 後面

The question is whether or not the plan will work.

問題是這個計畫行不行得通。

everyone 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用單⁠數

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

everybody、someone 後面的動詞也用單數。

後⁠面再提⁠到 everyone 或 someone，常⁠用 they、their、them

Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.

their

Everyone has their own dream.

每個人都有自己的夢想。
them

If someone calls, tell them I'm out.

如果有人打電話來，跟對方說我出去了。

原句的 they 指 everyone。

例句

  1. Everyone is born with a talent. The question is whether they can find it or not.
    每個人生來都有一種天分。問題是他們能不能找到它。
  2. Some people are born with a good voice.
    有些人天生有一副好嗓子。
  3. I was born in Taichung in 2008.
    我 2008 年在台中出生。
  4. My son was born with a weak heart.
    我兒子天生心臟不好。
  5. Was your father born in Tokyo?
    你爸爸是在東京出生的嗎？
  6. Nobody is born with the ability to read.
    沒有人天生就有閱讀的能力。
  7. My grandmother was born in 1940.
    我奶奶 1940 年出生。
  8. Ann was born with a talent for music.
    Ann 天生有音樂的才能。
  9. The question is whether we have enough time.
    問題是我們有沒有足夠的時間。
  10. The problem is whether he will come or not.
    問題是他會不會來。
  11. The question is whether or not the plan will work.
    問題是這個計畫行不行得通。
  12. My worry is whether I can pass the entrance exam.
    我擔心的是我能不能通過入學考試。
  13. The important thing is whether you enjoy your work.
    重要的是你喜不喜歡你的工作。
  14. The question was whether the store was open on Sunday or not.
    問題是那家店星期天有沒有開。
  15. Everyone has their own dream.
    每個人都有自己的夢想。
  16. If someone calls, tell them I'm out.
    如果有人打電話來，跟對方說我出去了。
  17. Everybody in my class has their own phone.
    我們班上每個人都有自己的手機。
  18. Everybody wants to be happy.
    每個人都想要快樂。
  19. Everyone did their best in the game.
    每個人在比賽中都盡了全力。
  20. Someone left their umbrella in the classroom.
    有人把傘留在教室裡了。
  21. Everyone is good at something. The question is whether they know it.
    每個人都擅長某件事。問題是他們知不知道。
  22. No one is born with a perfect memory.
    沒有人天生有完美的記憶力。
  23. The question is not whether you win or lose.
    問題不在於你贏還是輸。
  24. Everyone has to bring their own lunch tomorrow.
    明天每個人都必須帶自己的午餐。
  25. Tom was born in Canada, but he grew up in Japan.
    Tom 在加拿大出生，但在日本長大。
  26. The problem was whether we could get tickets or not.
    問題是我們買不買得到票。
  27. Every child is born with something special.
    每個孩子生下來都有某種特別的地方。
  28. Does everyone have their passport?
    每個人都有帶護照嗎？
  29. The real question is whether or not you want to do it.
    真正的問題是你想不想做這件事。
  30. Everyone needs someone who believes in them.
    每個人都需要一個相信他的人。
  31. My son was born on May 5.
    我兒子五月五日出生。
  32. The baby was born at three in the morning.
    寶寶在凌晨三點出生。
  33. When were you born?
    你是什麼時候出生的？
  34. My father was born in a small village in Kyushu.
    我爸爸出生在九州的一個小村子。
  35. Ann was born on October 10, 2009.
    Ann 是 2009 年十月十日出生的。
  36. My sister was born in April.
    我妹妹四月出生。
  37. Both of my children were born in July.
    我的兩個孩子都是七月出生的。
  38. I was born at midnight.
    我是半夜十二點出生的。
