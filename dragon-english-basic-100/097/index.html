When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

我離開辦公室去吃午餐的時候，巧遇一個高中時代的老朋友。

兩⁠件⁠事都發⁠生在過⁠去，兩⁠個子⁠句都用過⁠去⁠式

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

when 子句放在句首時，後面加逗號。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以說那⁠個時⁠候⁠的狀⁠態

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

我看到考試分數的時候很驚訝。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句用過⁠去進⁠行⁠式，表⁠示當⁠時正⁠在進⁠行⁠的動⁠作

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

我離開電影院的時候，我的朋友們在外面等。

leave 直⁠接接地⁠方，中⁠間不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

我每天早上七點出門。

leave for 加目⁠的⁠地，表⁠示出⁠發前⁠往那⁠裡

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

我們明天出發去大阪。

離⁠開⁠的地⁠方放⁠在 leave 後⁠面，目⁠的⁠地放⁠在 for 後⁠面

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

我姊姊去年離開台灣去加拿大。

原句⁠的 for lunch 不⁠是目⁠的⁠地，它說⁠明離⁠開⁠的目⁠的

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

run into 表⁠示偶⁠然遇⁠到某⁠人

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

沒有約好，碰巧遇到老朋友。

bump into 的意⁠思和用⁠法跟 run into 一⁠樣

When I left the office for lunch, I ran into an old friend from high school.

run into

I ran into my English teacher at the supermarket.

我在超市偶然遇到我的英文老師。
bump into

I bumped into Ann at the station this morning.

我今天早上在車站碰到 Ann。
