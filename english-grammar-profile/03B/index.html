Were it not for that kind of toy, children would gain more team spirit, creativity and self-confidence.

要是沒有那種玩具，孩子會更有團隊精神、創造力和自信。

were it not for 是 if it were not for 的倒⁠裝

Were it not for that kind of toy, children would gain more team spirit, creativity and self-confidence.

要不是有這座燈塔，船會撞上這些礁石。

省略 if，把 were 放到 it 前面。it 不指任何東西。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句說現⁠在⁠的結⁠果，或過⁠去⁠的結⁠果

would + 原形動詞
現在

Were it not for her salary, the family would struggle to pay the bills.

要不是有她的薪水，這個家會付不出帳單。
would have + 過去分詞
過去

Were it not for the doctor's quick action, the patient would have died.

要不是醫生處理得快，病人早就死了。

were it not for 放⁠在句⁠尾

句尾

Laura would have accepted the job in London were it not for her family.

要不是因為家人，Laura 早就接受倫敦的那份工作了。
句尾

Many small theatres would close were it not for government grants.

要不是有政府補助，很多小劇場會關門。

were it not for 放在主要子句後面時，前面不加逗號。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以用 could 或 might

could
可以做到的事

Were it not for the cost, the council could build a new library.

要不是費用的問題，市議會可以蓋一座新圖書館。
might
可能發生的事

Were it not for the high rents, young artists might stay in the city centre.

要不是房租高，年輕藝術家可能會留在市中心。

原句：沒⁠有那⁠種玩⁠具⁠時，孩⁠子現⁠在會得⁠到⁠的東⁠西

Were it not for that kind of toy, children would gain more team spirit, creativity and self-confidence.

要是沒有那種玩具，孩子會更有團隊精神、創造力和自信。

寫的人認為這種玩具對孩子有壞的影響。

倒⁠裝⁠的句⁠型不⁠縮⁠寫

Were it not for that kind of toy, children would gain more team spirit, creativity and self-confidence.

Weren't it for the heater, we would freeze in here.

Were it not for the heater, we would freeze in here.

for 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞片⁠語，不⁠接子⁠句

Were it not for that kind of toy, children would gain more team spirit, creativity and self-confidence.

Were it not for she supported us, we would have closed the shop.

Were it not for her support, we would have closed the shop.

倒⁠裝已⁠經取⁠代 if

Were it not for that kind of toy, children would gain more team spirit, creativity and self-confidence.

If were it not for the snow, the roads would be open.

Were it not for the snow, the roads would be open.

保留 if 時，用 If it were not for。

If it weren't for 意⁠思一⁠樣，也用在口⁠語

If it weren't for

If it weren't for the wind, it would be a lovely day.

要不是有風，今天會是很舒服的一天。
If it weren't for

If it weren't for my brother, I wouldn't have a job.

要不是我哥哥，我就沒有工作。

Had it not been for 只⁠說過⁠去⁠的事

Had it not been for

Had it not been for the storm, the ship would have arrived on time.

要不是那場暴風雨，船就準時到了。
Had it not been for

Had it not been for his mistake, we would have won the final.

要不是他的失誤，我們就贏得決賽了。

Were it not for 的名詞片語到現在還是一樣。

例句

① 說現在的結果

  1. Were it not for tourism, the island would have very few jobs.
    要不是有觀光業，這座島的工作機會會非常少。
  2. Were it not for the river, the valley would be completely desiccated.
    要不是有這條河，這座山谷會完全乾涸。
  3. Were it not for the music, the film would be rather banal.
    要不是有配樂，這部電影會相當平淡。
  4. Were it not for his ostentatious lifestyle, few people would notice him.
    要不是他生活奢華招搖，很少人會注意到他。
  5. Were it not for public apathy, the government would face much more pressure.
    要不是民眾冷漠，政府會面臨大得多的壓力。
  6. Were it not for the new trains, commuters would spend two hours a day on the road.
    要不是有新的火車，通勤族每天會在路上花兩個小時。

② 說過去的結果

  1. Were it not for her courage, the truth would never have come out.
    要不是她有勇氣，真相永遠不會曝光。
  2. Were it not for his ambition, he would have stayed in his home town.
    要不是他有企圖心，他當初就會留在家鄉了。
  3. Were it not for the support of my family, I would have left university in the first year.
    要不是家人支持，我大一就離開大學了。
  4. Were it not for their peaceable nature, the two groups would have fought long ago.
    要不是兩邊的人個性溫和，兩群人早就打起來了。
  5. Were it not for the army's loyalty, the rebels would have overthrown the king.
    要不是軍隊忠誠，叛軍早就推翻國王了。

③ were it not for 放在句尾

  1. Many small theatres would close were it not for government grants.
    要不是有政府補助，很多小劇場會關門。
  2. The city would be unbearable in summer were it not for its parks.
    要不是有公園，這座城市夏天會讓人受不了。
  3. I would give up this course immediately were it not for my scholarship.
    要不是有獎學金，我會馬上放棄這門課。
  4. The report would be quite jejune were it not for the final chapter.
    要不是有最後一章，這份報告會相當空洞。

④ could 和 might 說可能的結果

  1. Were it not for the language barrier, the two companies could work together easily.
    要不是有語言障礙，兩家公司可以輕鬆合作。
  2. Were it not for the high rents, young artists might stay in the city centre.
    要不是房租高，年輕藝術家可能會留在市中心。
  3. Were it not for the murky water, divers could see the wreck clearly.
    要不是水很混濁，潛水員可以清楚看到沉船。
  4. Were it not for the strict rules, the event might become rather frivolous.
    要不是規定嚴格，這個活動可能會變得相當輕浮。

⑤ 論說文

  1. Were it not for advertising, children would want fewer expensive toys.
    要不是有廣告，孩子不會想要那麼多昂貴的玩具。
  2. Were it not for social media, many people would spend more time outdoors.
    要不是有社群媒體，很多人會花更多時間在戶外。
  3. Were it not for the beneficence of a few rich families, the arts would struggle to survive.
    要不是有幾個富有家族的善舉，藝術會很難生存。
  4. Were it not for strict laws, companies would infringe workers' rights far more often.
    要不是有嚴格的法律，公司會更常侵犯勞工的權利。
  5. Were it not for modern medicine, life expectancy would be much lower.
    要不是有現代醫學，平均壽命會低得多。
  6. Were it not for computer games, teenagers would be less unsociable.
    要不是有電腦遊戲，青少年不會那麼不愛交際。
  7. Were it not for vaccines, millions of children would die every year.
    要不是有疫苗，每年會有幾百萬個孩子死亡。

⑥ 正式信件和演講

  1. Were it not for your generous donation, this project would not exist.
    要不是您慷慨捐款，這個計畫就不會存在。
  2. Were it not for the hard work of our staff, we would not be celebrating today.
    要不是員工努力工作，我們今天就不會在這裡慶祝。
  3. Were it not for a prior engagement, I would gladly accept your invitation.
    要不是之前已經有約，我會很樂意接受您的邀請。
  4. Were it not for your letter, I would not have known about the problem.
    要不是您的來信，我不會知道這個問題。
  5. Were it not for Professor Hall's monograph, this research would have taken twice as long.
    要不是有 Hall 教授的專論，這項研究會多花一倍的時間。
  6. Were it not for your patience, the negotiations would have failed.
    要不是您有耐心，談判早就破局了。
