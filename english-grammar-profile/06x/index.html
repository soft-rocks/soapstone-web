I hate shopping via the internet.

我很討厭在網路上買東西。

via 說⁠明透⁠過什⁠麼方⁠式

我很討厭在網路上買東西。

via 和 through 說方⁠法和途⁠徑

I hate shopping via the internet.

via
經由、透過

We flew to London via Dubai.

我們經過杜拜飛到倫敦。
through
穿過、透過

I found this job through my cousin.

我是透過我表哥找到這份工作的。

within、throughout 和 beyond 說範⁠圍和時⁠間

I hate shopping via the internet.

within
在……之內within ten minutes, within the city
throughout
在整段時間或整個地方

The shop is open throughout the holidays.

那家店整個假期都有開。
beyond
超出、在……另一邊beyond the mountains

beside

兩⁠個之⁠間用 between，among 用在三⁠個以⁠上

within 接一⁠段時⁠間，時⁠間點用 by

例句

① via / through

  1. You can send the forms via email.
    你可以用電子郵件寄表格。
  2. We drove to Tainan via Taichung.
    我們經過台中開車到台南。
  3. Sunlight came in through the gap in the curtains.
    陽光從窗簾的縫隙照進來。
  4. We walked through the forest to the lake.
    我們穿過森林走到湖邊。
  5. I met my husband through a friend from work.
    我是透過一個同事認識我先生的。
  6. You can pay via the app.
    你可以用應用程式付款。

② within / throughout / beyond

  1. The pizza will arrive within thirty minutes.
    披薩會在三十分鐘內送到。
  2. Everything you need is within walking distance.
    你需要的東西走路都到得了。
  3. The baby cried throughout the flight.
    寶寶整趟飛行都在哭。
  4. The museum is popular with tourists throughout the year.
    那間博物館一年到頭都很受觀光客歡迎。
  5. There's a small village just beyond the hill.
    山丘的另一邊有一個小村子。
  6. Most of the guests left within an hour.
    大部分的客人一個小時內就走了。

③ towards / beside / among / against

  1. The dog ran towards me.
    那隻狗朝我跑過來。
  2. We had a picnic beside the river.
    我們在河邊野餐。
  3. I found my ring among the leaves.
    我在落葉堆裡找到了我的戒指。
  4. Don't lean against the door.
    別靠著門。
  5. My bike is parked beside the vending machine.
    我的腳踏車停在販賣機旁邊。
  6. The house stands among tall pine trees.
    那棟房子位在高大的松樹之間。

④ despite / unlike

  1. Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.
    儘管塞車，我們還是準時到了。
  2. She smiled despite the pain.
    儘管很痛，她還是笑了。
  3. Unlike his brother, Tom loves sports.
    跟他哥哥不一樣，Tom 很愛運動。
  4. Despite his age, my grandfather still swims every day.
    儘管年紀大了，我爺爺還是每天游泳。
  5. Unlike most cats, ours loves water.
    跟大部分的貓不一樣，我們家的貓很愛水。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Turn right towards the harbor at the next corner.
    在下一個轉角往港口的方向右轉。
  2. The children hid behind the curtains.
    孩子們躲在窗簾後面。
  3. I'll finish the report within the week.
    我會在這個禮拜內完成報告。
  4. The path goes past the church and over the bridge.
    那條小路經過教堂，再過橋。
  5. We're against the new parking rules.
    我們反對新的停車規定。
  6. She sent the photos via a messaging app.
    她用通訊軟體傳了照片。
  7. Despite the price, the concert sold out in an hour.
    儘管票價很高，演唱會一個小時就賣完了。
