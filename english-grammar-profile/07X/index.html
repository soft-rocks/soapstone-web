例句

① I / you

  1. I get up at six because I start work early.
    我六點起床，因為我很早就要上班。
  2. If you want, you can go inside.
    你想的話，可以進去。
  3. I live in a small flat with my sister.
    我和姊姊住在一間小公寓。
  4. You always know the right thing to say.
    你總是知道該說什麼。

② he / she

  1. My little brother doesn't like football. He prefers chess.
    我弟弟不喜歡足球。他比較喜歡下西洋棋。
  2. My friend's name is Julissa. She is from Ecuador, and she lives near my home.
    我朋友叫 Julissa。她來自厄瓜多，住在我家附近。
  3. My dad is a doctor. He works at the city hospital.
    我爸爸是醫生。他在市立醫院上班。
  4. My grandma is eighty, but she still rides a bike.
    我奶奶八十歲了，可是她還在騎腳踏車。

③ it

  1. I like my home because it is a beautiful home. It has a big garden and a good sitting room.
    我喜歡我家，因為它是一個漂亮的家。它有一個大花園和一間舒服的客廳。
  2. I like my front garden because it's very nice and warm.
    我喜歡我家前院，因為那裡很舒服又溫暖。
  3. I love this café. It makes the best coffee in town.
    我很喜歡這家咖啡店。它的咖啡是鎮上最好喝的。

④ we / they

  1. My neighbours help me a lot. They're nice people.
    我的鄰居常常幫我。他們人很好。
  2. My family and I love the sea. We go to the beach every summer.
    我和家人很喜歡海。我們每年夏天都去海邊。
  3. My cousins live in Canada. They have a big dog.
    我的表兄弟姊妹住在加拿大。他們養了一隻大狗。
  4. We start school at eight o'clock.
    我們八點開始上課。

⑤ 介紹自己和家人

  1. This is my sister, Maria. She is nineteen.
    這是我姊姊 Maria。她十九歲。
  2. I have two cats. They sleep on my bed.
    我養了兩隻貓。牠們睡在我床上。
  3. Our school is small. It has only two hundred students.
    我們學校很小。只有兩百個學生。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Where's Tom?" "He's in the kitchen."
    Tom 在哪裡？他在廚房。
  2. "Do you like the new teacher?" "Yes, she's really funny."
    你喜歡新老師嗎？喜歡，她很好笑。
  3. "Where are the kids?" "They're at the park."
    孩子們在哪裡？他們在公園。
