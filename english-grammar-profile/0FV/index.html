It may be argued that a child's academic success is crucial to his or her future.

也許可以說，孩子在學業上的成就對他的未來非常重要。

it may be argued that、it may be concluded that、it may be said that

例句

① It may be argued that + 子句：提出一方的看法

  1. It may be argued that children today spend too much time indoors.
    可以說，現在的孩子待在室內的時間太多了。
  2. Top athletes earn huge salaries. It may be argued that their careers are short, so the money is well deserved.
    頂尖運動員賺很高的薪水。可以說，他們的職業生涯很短，所以這些錢是他們應得的。
  3. It may be argued that homework takes away children's free time.
    可以說，功課佔用了孩子的自由時間。
  4. It may be argued that tourism damages small villages.
    可以說，觀光業傷害了小村莊。
  5. It may be argued that zoos help to protect rare animals.
    可以說，動物園有助於保護稀有動物。
  6. It may be argued that public transport should be free.
    可以說，大眾運輸應該免費。
  7. It may be argued that mobile phones have made people less patient.
    可以說，手機讓人變得比較沒耐心。

② On balance, it may be concluded that + 子句：論文的結論

  1. On balance, it may be concluded that the main aim of international sport is still to find the fairest winner.
    整體來說，可以得出結論：國際運動比賽的主要目的仍然是公平地找出贏家。
  2. On balance, it may be concluded that the advantages of city life are greater.
    整體來說，可以得出結論：城市生活的好處比較多。
  3. On balance, it may be concluded that the new law has helped young workers.
    整體來說，可以得出結論：新法律幫助了年輕的工作者。
  4. On balance, it may be concluded that online learning works best for adults.
    整體來說，可以得出結論：線上學習對成人最有效。
  5. On balance, it may be concluded that both sides have good points.
    整體來說，可以得出結論：雙方都有道理。
  6. On balance, it may be concluded that the project was a success.
    整體來說，可以得出結論：這個專案是成功的。

③ It may be said that + 子句：總結一般的看法

  1. It may be said that television has changed family life.
    可以說，電視改變了家庭生活。
  2. It may be said that the internet is the greatest invention of our time.
    可以說，網路是我們這個時代最偉大的發明。
  3. It may be said that young people today are more open to new ideas.
    可以說，現在的年輕人比較能接受新想法。
  4. It may be said that cities never sleep.
    可以說，城市從不休息。
  5. It may be said that good teachers change lives.
    可以說，好老師能改變人的一生。
  6. It may be said that the twentieth century was a century of great change.
    可以說，二十世紀是一個巨變的世紀。

④ It may be suggested / claimed that + 子句

  1. It may be suggested that the survey asked too few older people.
    可以這麼看：這份調查問到的年長者太少了。
  2. It may be claimed that advertising makes people buy things they don't need.
    有人可能會說，廣告讓人買不需要的東西。
  3. It may be suggested that the results were affected by the weather.
    可以這麼看：結果受到了天氣的影響。
  4. It may be claimed that sport teaches children to work in a team.
    有人可能會說，運動教孩子團隊合作。
  5. It may be suggested that more research is needed.
    可以這麼看：還需要更多的研究。
  6. It may be claimed that working from home saves time and money.
    有人可能會說，在家工作省時又省錢。

⑤ It may be argued that …, but / however …：先說一方，再反駁

  1. It may be argued that exams are unfair. However, they are the easiest way to compare students.
    可以說，考試不公平。可是考試是比較學生最簡單的方法。
  2. It may be argued that cars give us freedom, but they also pollute the air.
    可以說，汽車給我們自由，可是它們也汙染空氣。
  3. It may be argued that famous people deserve privacy. However, they often look for attention.
    可以說，名人應該有隱私。可是他們常常在尋求關注。
  4. It may be argued that video games are a waste of time, but some of them teach useful skills.
    可以說，電玩浪費時間，可是有些電玩教人有用的技能。
  5. It may be argued that cheap flights are good for travellers. However, they harm the environment.
    可以說，便宜機票對旅客有好處。可是它們傷害環境。
  6. It may be argued that children should not have phones, but phones help parents keep them safe.
    可以說，孩子不應該有手機，可是手機幫助父母保護孩子的安全。
