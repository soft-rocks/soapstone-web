Have you bought anything?

你有買什麼東西嗎？

Have 放⁠在 you 前⁠面，問到現⁠在做⁠了沒⁠有

Have you bought anything?

你有買什麼東西嗎？

朋友還在逛街，說話者打電話問。

回⁠答用 have 或 haven't

Have you bought anything?

例句

① Have you + 過去分詞

  1. It's Grandma's birthday on Friday. Have you bought a card for her?
    星期五是奶奶的生日。你買好要給她的卡片了嗎？
  2. Everyone is talking about Mr Green. Have you seen the new teacher in Room 4?
    大家都在聊格林老師。你見過四號教室的新老師了嗎？
  3. You look very pale. Have you eaten anything since this morning?
    你臉色很蒼白。你從早上到現在有吃東西嗎？
  4. There's an envelope on the table for you. Have you read the letter from the bank?
    桌上有一封你的信。銀行寄來的那封信你看了嗎？
  5. You're still holding your face. Have you phoned the dentist about your tooth?
    你還捂著臉。你打電話給牙醫說你牙齒的事了嗎？
  6. The guests arrive at seven. Have you put the chicken in the oven?
    客人七點到。你把雞放進烤箱了嗎？
  7. We're leaving for the airport now. Have you turned off the lights upstairs?
    我們現在要去機場了。樓上的燈你關了嗎？
  8. I can't see a thing in here. Have you fixed the light in the bathroom?
    這裡什麼都看不到。浴室的燈你修好了嗎？
  9. This is my cousin Lucy. Have you met her before?
    這是我表妹露西。你以前見過她嗎？
  10. You've lived in this town for a month. Have you visited the castle on the hill?
    你在這個小鎮住了一個月。你去過山上那座城堡嗎？
  11. There's some on the kitchen table. Have you tasted the cake I made?
    廚房桌上還有一些。我做的蛋糕你吃過了嗎？
  12. It's time for bed. Have you locked the back door?
    該睡覺了。後門你鎖了嗎？

② 簡答：Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

  1. The cat is sleeping on the sofa. Have you fed her? — Yes, I have.
    貓在沙發上睡覺。你餵她了嗎？餵了。
  2. Your friends are coming over at five. Have you tidied your bedroom? — No, I haven't.
    你朋友五點要來。你房間整理了嗎？還沒。
  3. We need to leave early on Sunday. Have you checked the train times? — Yes, I have.
    我們星期天要早點出發。火車時刻你查過了嗎？查過了。
  4. Dad doesn't know about our plans. Have you sent him a message about dinner? — No, I haven't.
    爸爸不知道我們的計畫。你傳訊息跟他說晚餐的事了嗎？還沒。

③ 疑問詞 + have + you + 過去分詞

  1. I called you five times. Where have you been all afternoon?
    我打了五次電話給你。你整個下午跑去哪裡了？
  2. Show me the bags. What have you bought for the party?
    給我看看袋子。你為了派對買了什麼？
  3. It's not in my drawer. What have you done with my blue jumper?
    它不在我的抽屜裡。你把我的藍色毛衣弄到哪裡去了？
  4. I need the car this morning. Where have you left the keys?
    我今天早上要用車。你把鑰匙放在哪裡了？
  5. You bought presents for everyone. How much money have you spent on this trip?
    你幫每個人都買了禮物。這趟旅行你花了多少錢？
  6. Your passport is full of stamps. Which countries have you travelled to?
    你的護照蓋滿了章。你去過哪些國家旅行？
  7. It's so hot in here. Why have you closed all the windows?
    這裡好熱。你為什麼把窗戶全部關起來？

④ 問近況

  1. Hi Anna, it's been months. How have you been since the wedding?
    嗨，安娜，好幾個月沒見了。婚禮以後你過得好嗎？
  2. You left the bank in the spring. Have you found a new job?
    你春天離開了銀行。你找到新工作了嗎？
  3. You got the keys last week. Have you moved into your new house?
    你上禮拜拿到鑰匙了。你搬進新家了嗎？
  4. You bought a guitar for your birthday. Have you started your lessons?
    你生日買了一把吉他。你的課開始上了嗎？

⑤ 在店裡和餐廳

  1. The waiter is coming back. Have you decided what to order?
    服務生又過來了。你決定要點什麼了嗎？
  2. Excuse me, sir. Have you paid for these shoes?
    先生，不好意思。這雙鞋你付過錢了嗎？
  3. That one looks a bit small on you. Have you tried this jacket in a bigger size?
    那件你穿起來有點小。這件外套你試過大一號的嗎？
  4. It's Friday, and the restaurant gets busy. Have you booked a table for tonight?
    今天是星期五，那家餐廳會很多人。你訂好今晚的位子了嗎？
