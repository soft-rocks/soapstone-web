I think it would be better to include a student's view of the college, not only a teacher's.

我覺得最好也納入學生對這所學院的看法，而不只是老師的看法。

前⁠面說⁠過⁠的名⁠詞，'s 後⁠面可⁠以省⁠略

我覺得最好也納入學生對這所學院的看法，而不只是老師的看法。

以 ⁠-⁠⁠s 結⁠尾⁠的複⁠數名⁠詞只⁠留⁠下撇號

例句

① 名詞片語 + not / not only + 名詞 + 's

  1. The report should include a student's view of the college, not only a teacher's.
    這份報告應該納入學生對學院的看法，而不只是老師的。
  2. This is my decision, not my parents'.
    這是我的決定，不是我爸媽的。
  3. We need the manager's signature on this form, not the assistant's.
    這張表格需要經理的簽名，不是助理的。
  4. It was Tom's idea, not Sarah's.
    那是 Tom 的主意，不是 Sarah 的。

② 名詞片語 + than + 名詞 + 's

  1. Our after-sales team is faster and friendlier than our competitors'.
    我們的售後服務團隊比競爭對手的更快、更友善。
  2. Your hour of peace might be very different from your neighbours'.
    你的安靜時段可能跟你鄰居的很不一樣。
  3. Her exam results were better than her brother's.
    她的考試成績比她哥哥的好。
  4. This year's sales are higher than last year's.
    今年的銷售額比去年的高。
  5. My flat is smaller than my sister's, but it's closer to work.
    我的公寓比我姊姊的小，可是離公司比較近。

③ 名詞片語 + as … as + 名詞 + 's

  1. Our prices are as low as any supermarket's.
    我們的價格跟任何一家超市的一樣低。
  2. His English is as good as a native speaker's.
    他的英文跟母語人士的一樣好。
  3. My salary isn't as high as my manager's.
    我的薪水沒有我經理的高。
  4. The new model's battery lasts as long as the old model's.
    新款的電池跟舊款的一樣耐用。

④ of 片語裡的名詞省略

  1. The size of my room is about the same as the size of my brother's.
    我房間的大小跟我哥哥房間的差不多。
  2. The rent of our flat is lower than the rent of our neighbours'.
    我們公寓的房租比我們鄰居公寓的低。
  3. The start of this year's festival was better than the start of last year's.
    今年音樂節的開場比去年的開場好。

⑤ 正式文章裡的省略

  1. The government's response was slower than other countries'.
    政府的反應比其他國家的慢。
  2. Our branch's sales figures are higher than the London office's.
    我們分店的銷售數字比倫敦辦公室的高。
  3. The committee's decision differs from the board's.
    委員會的決定和董事會的不同。
  4. The author's argument is weaker than her critics'.
    作者的論點比她的評論者的弱。
  5. The museum's collection is larger than the gallery's.
    博物館的館藏比美術館的多。

⑥ 日常對話裡的省略

  1. Whose coat is this? Is it yours or Jack's?
    這是誰的外套？是你的還是 Jack 的？
  2. My phone's dead. Can I borrow my brother's?
    我的手機沒電了，我可以借我哥哥的嗎？
  3. Your handwriting is much neater than your dad's.
    你的字比你爸爸的整齊多了。
  4. Mum's cooking is better than any restaurant's.
    媽媽煮的菜比任何一家餐廳的都好吃。
  5. Their garden looks nicer than the neighbours'.
    他們的花園看起來比鄰居的漂亮。

⑦ 介系詞後面的省略

  1. I compared my answers with my classmate's.
    我把我的答案跟我同學的對了一下。
  2. We put our bags next to the other passengers'.
    我們把包包放在其他乘客的旁邊。
  3. He mixed up his keys with his flatmate's.
    他把他的鑰匙跟他室友的搞混了。
  4. She checked her notes against her teacher's.
    她拿她的筆記跟老師的對照。
  5. You can leave your bike beside the children's.
    你可以把腳踏車放在孩子們的旁邊。
