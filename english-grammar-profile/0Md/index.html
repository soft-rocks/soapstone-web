例句

① 主詞 + ought not to + forget / ignore：提醒重要的事

  1. Food may look safe, but we ought not to forget that the food industry has increased the chemicals in its products.
    食物看起來可能很安全，可是我們不應該忘記，食品業增加了產品裡的化學物質。
  2. The mayor spoke at the town's two-hundredth birthday. We ought not to forget the people who built this town.
    市長在鎮上兩百週年的慶祝會上致詞。我們不應該忘記建設這個鎮的人。
  3. Summers are getting hotter every year. We ought not to ignore the warnings of scientists.
    夏天一年比一年熱。我們不應該忽視科學家的警告。
  4. The new MP hasn't visited our town once. Politicians ought not to forget who voted for them.
    新的國會議員一次都沒來過我們鎮上。政客不應該忘記是誰投票給他們。
  5. Everyone at the council meeting loves the plan. But we ought not to ignore the cost of the new stadium.
    議會上大家都很喜歡這個計畫。可是我們不應該忽視新體育場的費用。
  6. Many old people in our town live alone. Young people ought not to forget their grandparents.
    我們鎮上很多老人一個人住。年輕人不應該忘記他們的祖父母。

② There ought not to be + 名詞：不應該存在的情況

  1. In a fair society, there ought not to be such a big division between rich and poor.
    在一個公平的社會裡，貧富之間不應該有這麼大的差距。
  2. Our class cleaned the beach last Saturday. There ought not to be any rubbish on it.
    我們班上星期六去清理海灘。海灘上不應該有任何垃圾。
  3. The air in the centre is terrible. There ought not to be so many cars there.
    市中心的空氣很糟。那裡不應該有這麼多車。
  4. My essay argues that children need time to play. There ought not to be any homework at weekends.
    我的作文主張孩子需要時間玩。週末不應該有任何功課。
  5. The station asked for fifty pence to use the toilet. There ought not to be a charge for that.
    車站上廁所要收五十便士。那不應該收費。
  6. My sister earns less than the men in her team. There ought not to be any difference between men's and women's pay.
    我姊姊賺得比她團隊裡的男生少。男女的薪水不應該有任何差別。

③ 主詞 + ought not to + 原形動詞：議論文的意見

  1. In my essay on technology, I argue that children ought not to spend more than two hours a day in front of a screen.
    我在科技的作文裡主張，孩子一天不應該在螢幕前超過兩個小時。
  2. Many shampoos are still tested on rabbits. Companies ought not to test their products on animals.
    很多洗髮精還在用兔子測試。公司不應該用動物測試產品。
  3. Families are already struggling to pay for bread and milk. The government ought not to raise taxes on food.
    家庭已經很難負擔麵包和牛奶了。政府不應該提高食物的稅。
  4. A third of the children in our town are overweight. Schools ought not to sell sugary drinks.
    我們鎮上三分之一的孩子過重。學校不應該賣含糖飲料。
  5. The singer couldn't even go to the shops in peace. Famous people ought not to be followed by photographers everywhere.
    那位歌手連去商店都不得安寧。名人不應該到哪裡都被攝影師跟著。（被動）
  6. Ships full of old plastic leave Europe every week. Rich countries ought not to send their waste to poor countries.
    每週都有載滿舊塑膠的船離開歐洲。富裕的國家不應該把垃圾送到貧窮的國家。

④ 主詞 + ought not to + 原形動詞：規範和行為

  1. My cousin is a young doctor, and he often works all night. Doctors ought not to work thirty hours without sleep.
    我表哥是年輕醫生，常常整晚工作。醫生不應該不睡覺連續工作三十個小時。
  2. The car in front didn't move when the light went green. Drivers ought not to use their phones at red lights.
    綠燈亮了，前面那台車都沒動。駕駛停紅燈的時候不應該用手機。
  3. The hotel lost two hundred towels last summer. Guests ought not to take them home.
    飯店去年夏天少了兩百條毛巾。客人不應該把毛巾帶回家。
  4. Half the essays were the same as a website. Students ought not to copy from the internet.
    一半的作文跟某個網站一模一樣。學生不應該從網路上抄東西。
  5. In my interview, they asked if I planned to have children. Employers ought not to ask about your private life.
    面試時他們問我打不打算生小孩。雇主不應該問你的私生活。
  6. I couldn't sleep until three because of the flat upstairs. Neighbours ought not to play loud music after midnight.
    因為樓上的公寓，我到三點都睡不著。鄰居半夜以後不應該放很大聲的音樂。

⑤ 主詞 + ought not to + 原形動詞, because + 子句

  1. Our town had no rain in July. We ought not to waste water, because the lakes are very low.
    我們鎮上七月都沒下雨。我們不應該浪費水，因為湖水很低。
  2. My mum always asked why I wasn't like my sister. Parents ought not to compare their children, because every child is different.
    我媽媽總是問我為什麼不像我姊姊。父母不應該比較孩子，因為每個孩子都不一樣。
  3. You always feel weak by eleven. You ought not to skip breakfast, because it gives you energy.
    你總是到十一點就沒力氣。你不應該不吃早餐，因為早餐給你能量。
  4. The council wants to build a road through Hill Wood. We ought not to cut down the old forest, because many animals live there.
    議會想在 Hill 森林裡蓋一條路。我們不應該砍掉那片老森林，因為很多動物住在那裡。
  5. Most car trips in our town are under a mile. People ought not to drive short distances, because walking is healthier.
    我們鎮上大部分的開車行程都不到一英里。人們不應該開車去近的地方，因為走路比較健康。
  6. The supermarket now opens on Christmas Day. Shops ought not to open on every holiday, because staff need rest too.
    超市現在聖誕節也營業。商店不應該每個假日都開，因為員工也需要休息。
  7. The man in the old coat was the company's owner. We ought not to judge people by their clothes, because appearances can be wrong.
    穿舊外套的那個人是公司的老闆。我們不應該以衣著判斷人，因為外表可能會誤導。
