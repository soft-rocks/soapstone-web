I really love walking.

我真的很愛散步。

really 放⁠在主⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

I really love walking.

我真的很愛散步。

有助⁠動⁠詞⁠時，really 放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

我真的很想泡個熱水澡。

疑⁠問⁠句⁠的 really 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

你真的住在這裡嗎？

don't really 是不⁠太，really don't 是真⁠的不

don't really

I don't really read the local paper.

我不太看地方報紙。
really don't

I really don't eat spicy food.

我真的不吃辣。

really 不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

She misses really her friends in Spain.

She really misses her friends in Spain.

修⁠飾動⁠詞用 really，不⁠用 real

I real need a break from work.

I really need a break from work.

very 不⁠修⁠飾動⁠詞

My parents very miss the old neighbourhood.

My parents really miss the old neighbourhood.

第⁠三⁠人⁠稱單⁠數⁠的動⁠詞照⁠樣加 ⁠-⁠s

My cousin really hate crowded trains.

My cousin really hates crowded trains.

例句

① really + like、love、enjoy、hate

  1. My little brother really loves dinosaurs. He has twenty toy ones.
    我弟弟超愛恐龍。他有二十隻玩具恐龍。
  2. The weather was perfect. We really enjoyed our walk along the river.
    天氣好極了。我們沿著河邊散步，走得很開心。
  3. The weekend always ends too soon. I really hate getting up early on Mondays.
    週末總是結束得太快。我真的很討厭星期一早起。
  4. Our students really like the new library. It has comfortable chairs and big windows.
    我們的學生很喜歡新的圖書館。那裡有舒服的椅子和大窗戶。
  5. Grandpa really enjoys his garden in spring. He plants new flowers every year.
    爺爺春天很喜歡待在他的花園裡。他每年都種新的花。

② really + want、need、hope

  1. The screen is broken again. I really need a new phone.
    螢幕又破了。我真的需要一支新手機。
  2. The kids really want a puppy for Christmas. They've asked every day since summer.
    孩子們很想要一隻小狗當聖誕禮物。他們從夏天開始每天都在問。
  3. You're my oldest friend. We really hope you can come to the wedding.
    你是我最老的朋友。我們很希望你能來參加婚禮。
  4. My dad hasn't had a day off for months. He really needs a holiday.
    我爸好幾個月沒休假了。他真的需要放個假。
  5. All my friends can swim. I really want to learn to swim this summer.
    我的朋友都會游泳。我今年夏天很想學游泳。

③ 助動詞 + really + 動詞

  1. I love Japanese food. I would really like to visit Japan one day.
    我很愛日本菜。我很想有一天去日本玩。
  2. You should really try the fish here. It comes straight from the harbour.
    你真的該試試這裡的魚。魚是從港口直接送來的。
  3. I've cleaned one window. I can really see the difference now.
    我擦了一扇窗戶。我現在真的看得出差別了。
  4. Helen is retiring after twenty years. Everyone at the office will really miss her.
    Helen 做了二十年要退休了。辦公室的大家都會很想念她。
  5. My sister is much more confident now. She has really changed since last year.
    我妹妹現在自信多了。她從去年到現在變了很多。

④ don't really + 動詞

  1. Turn the TV off if you like. I don't really care about the match.
    想關電視就關吧。我不太在乎那場比賽。
  2. My parents don't really understand computers. I set up their email for them.
    我爸媽不太懂電腦。我幫他們設定了 email。
  3. Ken is a bit shy. He doesn't really talk much in class.
    Ken 有點害羞。他上課不太說話。
  4. It rained every day. We didn't really enjoy the trip.
    每天都下雨。那趟旅行我們玩得不太開心。
  5. We've only met once. I don't really know your cousin.
    我們只見過一次。我跟你表哥不太熟。

⑤ really don't + 動詞

  1. I'm so tired after work. I really don't feel like going out tonight.
    下班後我好累。我今晚真的很不想出門。
  2. My mum left the supermarket without buying anything. She really doesn't like waiting in queues.
    我媽什麼都沒買就離開超市了。她真的很不喜歡排隊。
  3. We have plenty of food. You really don't need to bring anything.
    我們的食物很多。你真的什麼都不用帶。
  4. We have three meetings this afternoon. We really don't have time for lunch today.
    我們今天下午有三個會。我們今天真的沒時間吃午餐。
  5. Our cat really doesn't like car journeys. He hides under the bed when we take out his box.
    我們的貓真的很討厭坐車。我們拿出牠的籠子，牠就躲到床底下。

⑥ 疑問句

  1. You can hear the trains from your bedroom? Do you really live next to the station?
    你在臥室聽得到火車？你真的住在車站旁邊嗎？
  2. Tom is only twenty. Does he really speak four languages?
    Tom 才二十歲。他真的會說四種語言嗎？
  3. The pizza box is empty. Did Amy really eat the whole pizza?
    披薩盒空了。Amy 真的把整個披薩吃完了嗎？
  4. The sky is clear tonight. Do you really think it will snow tomorrow?
    今晚天空很晴朗。你真的覺得明天會下雪嗎？
  5. Your parents love the city. Do they really want to move to the countryside?
    你爸媽很愛城市。他們真的想搬到鄉下嗎？
