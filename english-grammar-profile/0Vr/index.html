If somebody wants to be a journalist, he or she has to have a wide knowledge of the world and people.

如果有人想當記者，他就必須對世界和人都有廣泛的了解。

he or she 回指性⁠別不⁠明⁠的人

If somebody wants to be a journalist, he or she has to have a wide knowledge of the world and people.

如果有人想當記者，他就必須……

If somebody wants to be a journalist, he or she has to have a wide knowledge of the world and people.

例句

① 單數名詞 + he or she

  1. If a child feels tired, he or she should go to bed early.
    孩子如果覺得累，就應該早點上床睡覺。
  2. A good teacher knows when he or she has made a mistake.
    好老師知道自己什麼時候犯了錯。
  3. When a tourist gets lost, he or she can ask at the information desk.
    遊客迷路的時候，可以到服務台詢問。
  4. A nurse has to be calm, even when he or she is very busy.
    護理師就算很忙，也要保持冷靜。
  5. If a member forgets the password, he or she can reset it online.
    會員如果忘了密碼，可以在網路上重設。
  6. Before a driver starts the car, he or she must fasten the seat belt.
    駕駛發動車子之前，一定要繫好安全帶。

② 不定代名詞 + he or she

  1. If anybody calls while I'm out, tell him or her to call back later.
    我出去的時候如果有人打電話來，請對方晚點再打。
  2. If someone wants to join the club, he or she must pay a small fee.
    有人想加入社團的話，要繳一點會費。
  3. Everyone thinks that he or she is a good driver.
    每個人都覺得自己很會開車。
  4. Nobody should have to work when he or she is ill.
    誰都不應該在生病的時候還得工作。
  5. If anyone has a problem with the room, he or she should tell the reception.
    任何人對房間有問題，都應該告訴櫃檯。

③ he/she 用在書面

  1. The patient must tell the doctor if he/she is taking any medicine.
    病人如果正在吃任何藥，必須告訴醫生。
  2. Each candidate must write his/her name on the answer sheet.
    每位考生都必須在答案卷上寫姓名。
  3. If the buyer is not satisfied, he/she may return the item within 14 days.
    買方如果不滿意，可以在十四天內退貨。
  4. A student who misses an exam must explain why he/she was absent.
    錯過考試的學生必須說明缺考的原因。
  5. The user can change his/her settings at any time.
    使用者隨時可以更改自己的設定。

④ him or her 和 his or her

  1. If a colleague helps you, you should thank him or her.
    同事如果幫了你，你應該謝謝對方。
  2. Every child needs someone to read to him or her.
    每個孩子都需要有人唸書給他聽。
  3. Each guest will find his or her name on a card at the table.
    每位賓客都會在餐桌的名牌上找到自己的名字。
  4. A parent should always know where his or her children are.
    父母應該隨時知道孩子在哪裡。
  5. Somebody left his or her umbrella in the classroom.
    有人把傘忘在教室裡了。

⑤ they 回指單數名詞

  1. If a friend is upset, you should listen to them.
    朋友心情不好的時候，你應該聽他說。
  2. When a customer pays by card, they need to enter a PIN.
    客人刷卡付款時，需要輸入密碼。
  3. A teenager often thinks that their parents don't understand them.
    青少年常覺得爸媽不了解自己。
  4. If your partner snores, they should see a doctor.
    你的另一半如果會打呼，應該去看醫生。
  5. Every athlete hopes they will win a medal one day.
    每個運動員都希望自己有一天能拿到獎牌。

⑥ 知道是男是女，直接用 he 或 she

  1. My sister is a journalist, and she travels a lot.
    我姊姊是記者，常常出差。
  2. Our new neighbour is a pilot, and he is often away.
    我們的新鄰居是飛行員，常常不在家。
  3. The doctor said she would call me tomorrow.
    醫生說她明天會打電話給我。
  4. When my son was a baby, he cried every night.
    我兒子還是小嬰兒的時候，每天晚上都哭。
  5. Ask the waiter if he can bring us some water.
    問問服務生能不能幫我們拿點水。
