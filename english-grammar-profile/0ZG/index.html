Let's meet at half past four.

我們四點半見吧。

Let's 邀對⁠方跟自⁠己一⁠起做

Let's meet at half past four.

我們去公園吃午餐吧。

不⁠想做⁠時用 Let's not

Let's meet at half past four.

Let's not take the car. It's hard to park there.

我們別開車了，那裡很難停車。

同⁠意⁠時說 Yes, let's

Let's meet at half past four.

A: Let's order pizza. B: Yes, let's.

A：我們叫披薩吧。B：好啊。

let's 後⁠面不⁠加 to

Let's meet at half past four.

Let's to go home before it gets dark.

Let's go home before it gets dark.

let's 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠ing

Let's meet at half past four.

Let's going swimming at the sports centre.

Let's go swimming at the sports centre.

否⁠定用 Let's not，不⁠用 Let's don't

Let's meet at half past four.

Let's don't stay at the party after midnight.

Let's not stay at the party after midnight.

例句

① Let's + 原形動詞（活動）

  1. The shops open at ten tomorrow. Let's meet outside the bank at ten.
    商店明天十點開門。我們十點在銀行外面碰面吧。
  2. The festival starts on Friday. Let's go!
    節慶星期五開始，我們去吧！
  3. We have one free afternoon in Prague. Let's go to the old town. It's really pretty.
    我們在布拉格有一個空閒的下午。我們去舊城區吧，那裡很漂亮。
  4. The weather will be sunny this weekend. Let's have a picnic by the lake on Saturday.
    這個週末天氣會很好。星期六我們去湖邊野餐吧。
  5. It's the first day of the holidays, kids. Let's make pancakes for breakfast tomorrow.
    孩子們，明天是放假第一天。我們明天早餐做鬆餅吧。
  6. I've booked a court at the sports centre. Let's play tennis on Sunday.
    我在運動中心訂了一個場地。我們星期天去打網球吧。

② Let's + 原形動詞（安排和計畫）

  1. Let's take the train. It's faster.
    我們搭火車吧，比較快。
  2. Everyone from the team is coming to dinner. Let's book a table for eight people.
    團隊每個人都會來吃晚餐。我們訂一張八人桌吧。
  3. Ann loves the mountains. Let's ask her to come too.
    Ann 很愛山。我們也找她一起來吧。
  4. Everyone is here, and it's ten o'clock. Let's start the meeting now.
    大家都到了，已經十點了。我們現在開始開會吧。
  5. Mum already has lots of books. Let's buy her flowers for her birthday.
    媽媽已經有很多書了。我們買花送她當生日禮物吧。

③ Let's + 原形動詞（解決問題）

  1. It's pouring outside. Let's play a board game instead.
    外面下大雨，我們改玩桌遊吧。
  2. I'm hungry. Let's get something to eat.
    我餓了，我們去買點東西吃吧。
  3. We're lost. Let's ask someone.
    我們迷路了，找個人問問吧。
  4. The bus is late. Let's walk.
    公車誤點了，我們用走的吧。
  5. It's too hot. Let's go for a swim.
    太熱了，我們去游泳吧。

④ Let's not + 原形動詞

  1. Let's not drive. The traffic is terrible at this hour.
    我們別開車了，這個時間塞車很嚴重。
  2. We have to get up early tomorrow. Let's not stay too long.
    我們明天要早起。我們別待太久。
  3. It's our anniversary. Let's not talk about work tonight.
    今天是我們的紀念日。今晚我們別談工作了。
  4. They'll be too excited to sleep. Let's not tell the kids about the trip yet.
    他們會興奮到睡不著。我們先別跟孩子們說旅行的事。
  5. It's only a film. Let's not argue about it.
    那只是一部電影。我們別為這件事吵了。

⑤ 回應和不同的句型

  1. A: Let's go for a walk after dinner. B: Good idea!
    A：我們晚餐後去散步吧。B：好主意！
  2. A: Let's order pizza. B: Yes, let's.
    A：我們叫披薩吧。B：好啊。
  3. It's getting late. Let's head back to the hotel, shall we?
    時間晚了，我們回飯店吧，好嗎？
  4. We're too tired to go out. Let's see what's on TV.
    我們累到不想出門。我們看看電視上在演什麼吧。
  5. I'm bored of our usual place. Let's try the new café on the corner.
    我吃膩了我們常去的那家。我們去試試轉角那家新咖啡店吧。
  6. The doctor will have the results on Friday. Let's wait and see.
    醫生星期五會拿到結果。我們等著看吧。
  7. We didn't finish the plan today. Let's meet again next week.
    我們今天沒做完計畫。我們下禮拜再見面吧。
  8. Let's sit at the back so we can leave early.
    我們坐後面吧，這樣可以早點走。
  9. We've painted three rooms, and it's getting dark. Let's call it a day.
    我們漆了三個房間，天快黑了。今天就到這裡吧。
