At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

我在演唱會上遇到一個朋友，他的工作就是籌辦那類的演出。

whose 加名⁠詞，給前⁠面⁠的人補⁠充資⁠料

我在演唱會上遇到一個朋友，他的工作就是籌辦那類的演出。

逗號表示後面是補充資料。

whose 加名⁠詞可⁠以當子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞或受⁠詞

At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

主詞

I called Mrs. Tsai, whose son is in my class.

我打電話給蔡太太，她兒子跟我同班。
受詞

We met Paula Ríos, whose novels I've read many times.

我們見到了 Paula Ríos，她的小說我讀過很多遍。

whose 也⁠可⁠以指地⁠方，子⁠句在中⁠間⁠時前⁠後都加逗⁠號

At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

地方

We stayed in Lukang, whose old streets are full of temples.

我們住在鹿港，那裡的老街到處都是廟。
句子中間

My neighbor, whose dog barks all night, has finally moved.

我的鄰居終於搬走了，他的狗整晚都在叫。

說誰⁠的東⁠西用 whose，不⁠用 who's

At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

I met Anna, who's brother is a pilot.

I met Anna, whose brother is a pilot.

whose 已⁠經表⁠示他⁠的，不⁠再⁠加 his

At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

I talked to Mr. Lin, who his daughter is a doctor.

I talked to Mr. Lin, whose daughter is a doctor.

whose 後⁠面直⁠接接名⁠詞，不⁠加 the

At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

We visited Lisa, whose the house is by the lake.

We visited Lisa, whose house is by the lake.

子⁠句是補⁠充資⁠料，前⁠面要加逗⁠號

At the concert, I met a friend of mine, whose job is to organize shows like that.

I phoned my sister whose car had broken down.

I phoned my sister, whose car had broken down.

說話的人只有一個姊姊。

例句

① 子句在句尾：whose + 名詞當主詞

  1. I had coffee with Jenny, whose husband works in my office.
    我跟 Jenny 喝了咖啡，她先生在我辦公室上班。
  2. We had dinner with the Wangs, whose garden is the best on the street.
    我們跟王家吃了晚餐，他們的花園是這條街上最漂亮的。
  3. I sat next to an old man, whose stories kept me awake all the way to Tainan.
    我坐在一位老先生旁邊，他講的故事讓我一路清醒到台南。
  4. She's dating Leo, whose parents own the bakery on the corner.
    她在跟 Leo 交往，他的爸媽開了轉角那家麵包店。
  5. I borrowed a dress from Mia, whose taste in clothes is excellent.
    我跟 Mia 借了一件洋裝，她對衣服很有品味。
  6. We talked to the farmer, whose family has grown tea here for a hundred years.
    我們跟那位農夫聊天，他家在這裡種茶種了一百年。

② 子句在句子中間

  1. My cousin, whose wedding I missed, still hasn't forgiven me.
    我錯過了我表姊的婚禮，她到現在還不原諒我。
  2. Mr. Okada, whose English is perfect, will translate for us.
    岡田先生會幫我們翻譯，他的英文很好。
  3. The old library, whose roof leaks every winter, needs repairs.
    那間老圖書館需要整修，它的屋頂每年冬天都漏水。
  4. Our teacher, whose patience seemed endless, never raised her voice.
    我們的老師好像有用不完的耐心，從來不大聲說話。
  5. My best friend, whose parents are both doctors, wants to be an artist.
    我最好的朋友想當藝術家，他的爸媽都是醫生。
  6. The restaurant, whose chef trained in France, is always full.
    那家餐廳總是客滿，它的主廚在法國受過訓練。

③ whose + 名詞當受詞

  1. I finally met Professor Hall, whose books I studied at university.
    我終於見到了 Hall 教授，我大學時讀過他的書。
  2. We went to see Amy, whose new baby we hadn't met yet.
    我們去看 Amy，我們還沒見過她剛出生的寶寶。
  3. That's Mr. Chou, whose car I accidentally scratched.
    那位是周先生，我不小心刮到他的車。
  4. I'm reading a novel by Kazuo Ishiguro, whose work I really admire.
    我在讀石黑一雄的小說，我很欣賞他的作品。
  5. Our neighbors, whose cat we feed when they're away, have gone to Japan.
    我們的鄰居去日本了，他們不在的時候我們幫他們餵貓。

④ 指東西和地方

  1. We drove through Tainan, whose temples are famous across Taiwan.
    我們開車經過台南，那裡的廟在全台灣都很有名。
  2. I bought this phone, whose battery lasts two whole days.
    我買了這支手機，它的電池可以撐兩整天。
  3. They live in a cottage, whose windows look out over the sea.
    他們住在一間小屋，窗戶看出去就是海。
  4. The museum, whose collection includes over ten thousand paintings, opens at nine.
    那間博物館九點開門，館藏有一萬多幅畫。
  5. We hiked up Hehuan Mountain, whose peak was covered in snow.
    我們爬了合歡山，山頂覆蓋著雪。

⑤ 不同的時態和情境

  1. I'll introduce you to Kevin, whose company is looking for designers.
    我會介紹你認識 Kevin，他的公司在找設計師。
  2. She thanked her neighbor, whose quick thinking had saved the house.
    她向鄰居道謝，鄰居反應很快，救了那棟房子。
  3. We hired Sara, whose experience in marketing is impressive.
    我們雇用了 Sara，她的行銷經驗很豐富。
  4. I'm worried about Tom, whose exams start tomorrow.
    我很擔心 Tom，他明天開始考試。
  5. My grandmother, whose memory is getting worse, still remembers every song from her childhood.
    我奶奶的記性越來越差，但還記得小時候的每一首歌。
  6. The singer, whose voice everyone recognizes, rarely gives interviews.
    那位歌手很少接受訪問，大家都認得她的聲音。
  7. I asked my uncle, whose advice is always practical, what I should do.
    我問了我叔叔該怎麼做，他的建議總是很實際。
  8. We stayed with Ken, whose love of hiking got us up at five every morning.
    我們住在 Ken 家，他超愛爬山，每天早上五點就把我們叫起來。
