Don't tell Lucy, please.

拜託不要跟 Lucy 說。

Don't + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，叫別⁠人不⁠要做

Don't tell Lucy, please.

別碰熨斗，我剛用過。

Don't 用⁠來勸⁠告、指⁠示和禁⁠止

Don't tell Lucy, please.

勸告

Stop at the next service station for a coffee. Don't drive when you're sleepy.

在下一個休息站停下來喝杯咖啡。想睡的時候別開車。
指示

Here's a present from Grandma. Don't open the box until your birthday.

這是奶奶送的禮物。生日以前別打開這個盒子。
禁止

The floor is wet, children. Don't run in the hall.

孩子們，地板是濕的。走廊上不要跑。

Don't be + 形⁠容⁠詞

Don't tell Lucy, please.

Don't be shy. Come and say hello.

別害羞，過來打招呼。

否⁠定祈⁠使⁠句用 Don't 開⁠頭

Don't tell Lucy, please.

Not leave your bike by the gate.

Don't leave your bike by the gate.

Don't 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Don't tell Lucy, please.

Don't crying in front of the children.

Don't cry in front of the children.

祈⁠使⁠句⁠的否⁠定一⁠律用 Don't

Don't tell Lucy, please.

Doesn't be late for the train tomorrow.

Don't be late for the train tomorrow.

例句

① 勸告

  1. Don't worry about the bill. I'll pay.
    帳單別擔心，我來付。
  2. Your maths exam is at nine tomorrow. Don't stay up too late before the exam.
    你的數學考試明天九點。考試前別熬夜熬太晚。
  3. Don't buy that phone. It breaks easily.
    別買那支手機，很容易壞。
  4. Have a light lunch today. Don't eat too much before you swim.
    今天午餐吃清淡一點。游泳前別吃太多。
  5. Stop at the next service station for a coffee. Don't drive when you're sleepy.
    在下一個休息站停下來喝杯咖啡。想睡的時候別開車。
  6. Don't trust every review you see on that website.
    別相信那個網站上的每一則評論。

② 拜託和請求

  1. Don't tell Mom about the broken vase, please.
    拜託別跟媽媽說花瓶破了的事。
  2. The guard spoke to two boys on the train. Please don't put your feet on the seats.
    列車員跟火車上兩個男孩說話。請不要把腳放在座位上。
  3. I want to hear about your first day. Don't forget to call me tonight.
    我想聽聽你第一天的情況。別忘了今晚打給我。
  4. I know you have an early flight. Please don't leave without saying goodbye.
    我知道你要搭早班飛機。請不要沒說再見就走。
  5. Grandma is cooking tonight, so don't come home late.
    奶奶今晚要煮飯，所以別太晚回家。

③ 指示

  1. Here's a present from Grandma. Don't open the box until your birthday.
    這是奶奶送的禮物。生日以前別打開這個盒子。
  2. The tomatoes are already sweet. Don't add sugar to the sauce.
    番茄已經很甜了。醬汁裡別加糖。
  3. The bank needs a clear copy. Don't use a pencil on the form.
    銀行需要清楚的副本。填表格時別用鉛筆。
  4. That's the emergency stop for the lift. Don't press the red button.
    那是電梯的緊急停止按鈕。別按紅色的按鈕。
  5. Don't turn left here. Go straight.
    這裡別左轉，直走。

④ 禁止和警告

  1. Don't touch the iron. I've just used it.
    別碰熨斗，我剛用過。
  2. The floor is wet, children. Don't run in the hall.
    孩子們，地板是濕的。走廊上不要跑。
  3. Don't swim here. The water is deep.
    別在這裡游泳，水很深。
  4. Bread makes ducks ill. Don't feed the ducks bread.
    麵包會讓鴨子生病。別餵鴨子吃麵包。
  5. We heard an announcement on the platform. Do not leave bags unattended in the station.
    我們在月台上聽到廣播。請勿在車站內放置無人看管的行李。

⑤ Don't be + 形容詞 和不同的句型

  1. Don't be shy. Come and say hello.
    別害羞，過來打招呼。
  2. Don't be sad. You'll see her again soon.
    別難過，你很快就會再見到她。
  3. Don't be nervous. The interviewer is very friendly.
    別緊張，面試官人很好。
  4. Don't be cross with your sister. She didn't break it on purpose.
    別對你妹妹生氣，她不是故意弄壞的。
  5. Don't panic! I've got the tickets.
    別慌！票在我這裡。
  6. Don't give up. You're nearly there.
    別放棄，你快成功了。
  7. I asked for a little off the top, but look at it. Don't laugh at my new haircut!
    我只說頭頂剪一點，可是你看。別笑我的新髮型！
  8. Don't wait for me. Start eating.
    別等我，你們先吃。
  9. Don't make so much noise. The baby's asleep.
    別那麼吵，寶寶睡著了。
