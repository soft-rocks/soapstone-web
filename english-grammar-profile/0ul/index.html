these 和現⁠在有⁠關，those 是過⁠去

these

例句

① these + things / clothes

  1. I bought a swimsuit, shorts, T-shirts and sunglasses. I'll need these things because it's summertime.
    我買了泳衣、短褲、T 恤和太陽眼鏡。我會需要這些東西，因為現在是夏天。
  2. I've packed my passport, my tickets and some money. I can't travel without these things.
    我帶了護照、機票和一些錢。沒有這些東西我沒辦法旅行。
  3. My mum gave me two jumpers and a scarf. I'm wearing these clothes today.
    我媽給了我兩件毛衣和一條圍巾。我今天就穿著這些衣服。
  4. You need flour, eggs and sugar. Mix these ingredients in a bowl.
    你需要麵粉、蛋和糖。把這些材料放進碗裡攪拌。

② these + places

  1. In the town you can visit the Old Museum, the Green Park, the old bridge and the old church. These places are very old.
    在鎮上你可以去老博物館、綠園、古橋和老教堂。這些地方都很古老。
  2. We'll see the castle, the market and the cathedral today. These places are all in the old town.
    我們今天會去城堡、市集和大教堂。這些地方都在老城區。
  3. I've marked three cafés on the map. These places have free Wi-Fi.
    我在地圖上標了三家咖啡店。這些地方有免費 Wi-Fi。

③ 比較：these + 名詞

  1. The first jeans I wanted were cheaper, but too small. These jeans are better, and they fit me well.
    我一開始想要的牛仔褲比較便宜，可是太小了。這件牛仔褲比較好，很合身。
  2. The old chairs were very uncomfortable. These chairs are much better.
    舊椅子很不舒服。這些椅子好多了。
  3. My last headphones broke easily. I hope these headphones last longer.
    我之前的耳機很容易壞。希望這副耳機可以用久一點。

④ these + 人

  1. I'd like you to meet Anna and Lucy. These girls are in my class.
    我想介紹 Anna 和 Lucy 給你認識。這兩個女孩是我班上的同學。
  2. Three new teachers started today. These teachers will help with the exams.
    今天來了三位新老師。這幾位老師會幫忙考試的事。
  3. Two boys found my wallet and gave it back. These boys were so honest.
    兩個男生撿到我的錢包還給我。這兩個男生好誠實。

⑤ 說明和指示

  1. Read pages ten to twenty. These pages will be in the test.
    讀第十頁到第二十頁。考試會考這幾頁。
  2. Press the red and green buttons. These buttons turn the machine on.
    按紅色和綠色的按鈕。這兩個按鈕會打開機器。
  3. Here are the rules for the trip. Please follow these rules.
    這是校外教學的規則。請遵守這些規則。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Do you like the photos from the wedding?" "Yes, these pictures are lovely."
    你喜歡婚禮的照片嗎？喜歡，這些照片好漂亮。
  2. "I've made some sandwiches and a salad." "These sandwiches look great."
    我做了一些三明治和一份沙拉。這些三明治看起來很棒。
  3. "We need apples, bananas and grapes." "These fruits are all on sale today."
    我們需要蘋果、香蕉和葡萄。這些水果今天都特價。
