Although he was famous, I didn't like him.

雖然他很有名，我還是不喜歡他。

Although 子⁠句說一⁠件⁠事，主⁠要⁠子⁠句說出⁠乎⁠意⁠料⁠的結⁠果

雖然他很有名，我還是不喜歡他。

子⁠句在⁠前⁠面加逗⁠號，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠要⁠子⁠句後⁠面

Although he was famous, I didn't like him.

前面

Although it was raining, we went for a walk.

雖然在下雨，我們還是去散步了。
後面

I still enjoy my job, although it's very tiring.

雖然工作很累，我還是很喜歡。

although 最常⁠用，though 比⁠較口⁠語，even though 語⁠氣比⁠較強

Although he was famous, I didn't like him.

although口語和書面語都可以
though比較口語
even though

Even though she was exhausted, she finished the race.

雖然她累壞了，還是跑完了比賽。

一⁠句⁠話只⁠用 although 或 but 其⁠中一⁠個

Although the hotel was cheap, but it was clean.

Although the hotel was cheap, it was clean.

although 後⁠面⁠接子⁠句，只接名⁠詞用 despite

Although he was famous, I didn't like him.

Although the rain, we played tennis.

Although it was raining, we played tennis.

只接名詞要寫 despite the rain。

連⁠接子⁠句要⁠用 even though，不⁠能只⁠用 even

Even it was late, he kept working.

Even though it was late, he kept working.

例句

① Although + 子句放在前面

  1. Although the restaurant was full, they found us a table.
    雖然餐廳客滿了，他們還是幫我們找到一張桌子。
  2. Although I studied hard, I didn't pass the test.
    雖然我很用功，還是沒考過。
  3. Although she's only twelve, she speaks three languages.
    雖然她只有十二歲，她會說三種語言。
  4. Although the film was long, nobody got bored.
    雖然電影很長，沒有人覺得無聊。
  5. Although we live in the same street, we rarely see each other.
    雖然我們住在同一條街，我們很少見面。
  6. Although the flat is small, it's very bright.
    雖然公寓很小，採光很好。

② although + 子句放在後面

  1. I bought the jacket, although it was quite expensive.
    我買了那件夾克，雖然它滿貴的。
  2. He came to work, although he had a bad cold.
    他來上班了，雖然他感冒得很嚴重。
  3. I enjoyed the book, although the ending was sad.
    我很喜歡那本書，雖然結局很悲傷。
  4. We had a great time, although the weather was terrible.
    我們玩得很開心，雖然天氣很糟。
  5. She said yes, although she didn't really want to go.
    她答應了，雖然她其實不太想去。
  6. The plan worked, although nobody expected it to.
    計畫成功了，雖然沒人料到。

③ though

  1. Though my cousin lives in Canada, we talk every week.
    雖然我表哥住在加拿大，我們每個禮拜都會聊天。
  2. Though the shop is small, it sells everything.
    雖然那家店很小，卻什麼都賣。
  3. I'll come to the party, though I can't stay long.
    我會去派對，不過我不能待太久。
  4. Though he's rich, he lives very simply.
    雖然他很有錢，生活卻很簡樸。
  5. The soup was nice, though it was a bit salty.
    湯很好喝，不過有點鹹。

④ even though

  1. Even though I was tired, I couldn't fall asleep.
    雖然我很累，還是睡不著。
  2. He kept running even though his leg hurt.
    雖然他的腳很痛，他還是一直跑。
  3. Even though it was my birthday, I had to work.
    雖然那天是我生日，我還是得上班。
  4. She didn't call me even though she promised.
    她答應過，卻沒有打給我。
  5. Even though the tickets were expensive, the show sold out.
    雖然票很貴，那場表演還是賣完了。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. My dad still drives to work, even though the office is a short walk away.
    我爸爸還是開車上班，雖然公司走路一下就到。
  2. Although my grandmother is ninety, she still goes swimming.
    雖然我奶奶九十歲了，她還是會去游泳。
  3. I couldn't reconcile with him, although I tried many times.
    雖然我試了很多次，還是沒辦法跟他和好。
  4. Although the bus was late, we arrived on time.
    雖然公車誤點，我們還是準時到了。
  5. He's very gregarious, even though his brother is quite shy.
    他很愛交朋友，雖然他哥哥滿害羞的。
  6. Though the exam was hard, most students passed.
    雖然考試很難，大部分的學生都過了。
  7. We went to the beach even though it was cloudy.
    雖然是陰天，我們還是去了海邊。
  8. Although I've lived here for years, I still get lost.
    雖然我在這裡住了好幾年，還是會迷路。
