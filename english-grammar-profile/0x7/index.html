It was obvious that I would be late for work.

很明顯，我上班要遲到了。

It + is / was + obvious / likely / certain + that + 子⁠句

obvious明顯的。
likely很可能的。
certain確定的。
sure確定。主詞常用人，像 I am sure。

時⁠態要一⁠致

It was obvious that I would be late for work.

現在

It is obvious that we will be late.

很明顯我們會遲到。
過去

It was obvious that we would be late.

那時很明顯我們會遲到。

主要子句用過去式時，that 子句說以後的事用 would。

句⁠子要⁠有主⁠詞，這⁠裡用形⁠式⁠主⁠詞 it

It was obvious that I would be late for work.

Was obvious that the bus wasn't coming.

It was obvious that the bus wasn't coming.

主⁠要⁠子⁠句是過⁠去⁠式，子⁠句⁠裡用 would

It was obvious that I would be late for work.

It was obvious that I will be late.

It was obvious that I would be late.

sure 是形⁠容⁠詞，前⁠面要⁠有 be 動⁠詞

It was obvious that I would be late for work.

I sure that you will like it.

I am sure that you will like it.

例句

① It + is / was + obvious + that + 子句

  1. It was obvious that the waiter had made a mistake with the bill.
    很明顯，服務生把帳單算錯了。
  2. It was obvious that the old man needed help with his bags.
    很明顯，那個老人需要人幫他提袋子。
  3. It is obvious that the children love their new teacher.
    很明顯，孩子們很喜歡他們的新老師。
  4. It was obvious that nobody had cleaned the kitchen for weeks.
    很明顯，已經好幾個星期沒人打掃廚房了。
  5. It is obvious that this house needs a new roof.
    很明顯，這間房子需要新屋頂。
  6. It was obvious that she had been crying.
    很明顯，她剛剛哭過。
  7. It's obvious that the team is tired after the long season.
    很明顯，球隊經過漫長的球季已經累了。

② It + is / was + likely + that + 子句

  1. It is likely that the new café will be busy at weekends.
    新的咖啡店週末很可能會很忙。
  2. It was likely that the river would flood after so much rain.
    下了這麼多雨，河水很可能會氾濫。
  3. It is likely that we'll need more volunteers next year.
    我們明年很可能需要更多志工。
  4. It's likely that the trains will be late because of the snow.
    因為下雪，火車很可能會誤點。
  5. It was likely that the factory would close by the end of the year.
    工廠很可能在年底前關閉。
  6. It is likely that most people will agree with the plan.
    大部分的人很可能會同意這個計畫。

③ It + is / was + certain + that + 子句

  1. It is certain that our new screen will sell well.
    我們的新螢幕一定會賣得很好。
  2. It was certain that the president would win again.
    那時很確定總統會再次勝選。
  3. It is certain that prices will go up in the new year.
    新的一年物價一定會上漲。
  4. It's certain that the museum will need more space.
    博物館一定會需要更多空間。
  5. It was certain that the match would go ahead, rain or shine.
    不管晴天還是下雨，比賽那時確定會照常舉行。
  6. It is almost certain that the road will close for repairs.
    那條路幾乎確定會封閉整修。

④ 人 + am / is / was + sure + that + 子句

  1. The castle is about a thousand years old, so I am sure that you will find it fascinating.
    那座城堡大約有一千年歷史，所以我確定你會覺得很有意思。
  2. I was sure that I had locked the door.
    我那時確定我鎖了門。
  3. She is sure that her brother will get the job.
    她確定她哥會得到那份工作。
  4. We were sure that the shop would still be open.
    我們那時確定那家店還開著。
  5. I'm sure that your grandmother will love the scarf.
    我確定你奶奶會喜歡那條圍巾。
  6. The coach is sure that his team can win the cup.
    教練確定他的隊伍能贏得獎盃。

⑤ 否定：It is not + 形容詞 + that / I'm not sure + that

  1. It isn't certain that the concert will go ahead.
    演唱會不確定會不會照常舉行。
  2. It was not obvious that he was unhappy.
    他不開心這件事並不明顯。
  3. It isn't likely that we'll get a refund.
    我們不太可能拿到退款。
  4. I'm not sure that the hotel has a car park.
    我不確定那家飯店有沒有停車場。
  5. It wasn't obvious that the two women were sisters.
    看不太出來那兩個女人是姊妹。
  6. I'm not sure that the bus stops here.
    我不確定公車有沒有停這裡。
