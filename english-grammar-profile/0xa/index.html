Sometimes we need a person to look up to.

有時候我們需要一個可以尊敬的人。

Sometimes we need a person to look up to.

一般的語序是 look up to a person。

介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞沒⁠有小⁠品⁠詞，介⁠系⁠詞一⁠樣可⁠以懸⁠空

介系詞懸空

This is the book I was looking for.

這就是我在找的書。
介系詞 + which
正式

There is one difficulty with which we must deal.

有一個難題是我們必須處理的。

例句

① 名詞 + to 不定詞

  1. "Why did you become a nurse?" "My mum was a nurse. I had someone to look up to."
    你為什麼當護士？我媽媽是護士。我有一個可以尊敬的榜樣。
  2. Young players need a good captain to look up to.
    年輕球員需要一個可以尊敬的好隊長。
  3. When I moved to London, I had no friends to hang out with and no one to get on with at work.
    我搬到倫敦的時候，沒有朋友可以一起出去，工作上也沒有處得來的人。
  4. After the exam I'll have nothing to look forward to.
    考完試以後我就沒有什麼可以期待的了。
  5. The new flat has a lot of problems to put up with, but it's cheap.
    新公寓有很多要忍受的問題，可是很便宜。
  6. On a long walk, there are always things to look out for, like snakes and loose rocks.
    走長路的時候，總是有要留意的東西，像蛇和鬆動的石頭。

② 關係子句

  1. My boss is a person I really look up to.
    我的老闆是我很尊敬的人。
  2. Rude customers are something every waiter has to put up with.
    沒禮貌的客人是每個服務生都得忍受的事。
  3. The plan that we came up with was simple and cheap.
    我們想出來的計畫又簡單又便宜。
  4. "Is your new boss nice?" "Yes, she's someone I get on with."
    你的新老闆人好嗎？好啊，她是我處得來的人。
  5. The trip to Japan is the thing I'm looking forward to most this year.
    去日本旅行是我今年最期待的事。
  6. These are the signs which drivers should look out for.
    這些是開車的人要留意的標誌。
  7. The paper we ran out of was the expensive kind.
    我們用完的是比較貴的那種紙。

③ wh 問句

  1. "What are you looking forward to this summer?" "A week at the beach."
    你這個夏天在期待什麼？去海邊玩一個星期。
  2. "Who did you look up to as a child?" "My uncle. He was a pilot."
    你小時候崇拜誰？我舅舅。他是飛行員。
  3. "What did you come up with?" "Nothing yet. I need more time."
    你想出什麼了？還沒有。我需要更多時間。
  4. "How much noise do you have to put up with?" "Every night the music goes on until two."
    你得忍受多少噪音？每天晚上音樂都放到兩點。
  5. "Which colleague do you get on with best?" "Probably Sam."
    你和哪個同事最處得來？大概是 Sam 吧。
  6. "What have we run out of?" "Eggs and milk."
    我們什麼用完了？雞蛋和牛奶。

④ wh 子句

  1. Let me tell you what ideas I came up with for the party.
    我跟你說我想到哪些派對的點子。
  2. I can't believe what she has to put up with at home.
    真不敢相信她在家裡得忍受那些事。
  3. The guide told us what to look out for in the forest.
    導遊告訴我們在森林裡要留意什麼。
  4. Ask the kids what they're looking forward to on the trip.
    問問孩子們這趟旅行他們在期待什麼。

⑤ 被動

  1. Dr Lee was looked up to by all her students.
    李博士的學生都很尊敬她。
  2. Bullying in this school will not be put up with.
    這所學校不會容忍霸凌。
  3. The old school rules were done away with last year.
    學校的舊規定去年廢除了。

⑥ 受詞放在句首

  1. This you will have to look out for: the last train leaves at eleven.
    這一點你得注意：最後一班火車十一點開。
  2. That kind of rude behaviour I won't put up with.
    那種沒禮貌的行為我不會忍受。

⑦ 其他片語介系詞動詞

  1. "Is that your cousin Ben?" "Yes. He's the one I never got on with as a kid."
    那是你表哥 Ben 嗎？對。他就是我小時候一直處不來的那個。
  2. The old printer is something we've learnt to put up with.
    那台舊印表機是我們已經學會忍受的東西。
  3. It was a challenge I hadn't come up against before.
    那是我以前沒遇過的挑戰。
