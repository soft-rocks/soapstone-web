例句

① 名詞 + which + is / are + 過去分詞 + by

  1. I like comfortable clothes. I also like wearing clothes which are manufactured by famous brands.
    我喜歡舒服的衣服。我也喜歡穿名牌廠商做的衣服。
  2. On our school trip, we climbed a hill which is surrounded by small villages.
    校外教學的時候，我們爬了一座四周都是小村莊的山。
  3. My dad is careful about what he buys. He only buys coffee which is grown by small farmers.
    我爸爸買東西很講究。他只買小農種的咖啡。
  4. For my history project, I chose a building which is used by the whole town.
    我的歷史報告選了一棟全鎮的人都在用的建築。
  5. My cousin has an interesting job. She works for a charity which is supported by a lot of famous people.
    我表姊的工作很有意思。她在一個很多名人支持的慈善機構工作。
  6. My sister is getting married in May, so I'm learning a dance which is performed by women at weddings.
    我姊姊五月要結婚，所以我在學一支婚禮上女人跳的舞。

② 名詞 + which + was / were + 過去分詞 + by

  1. The film club meets on Fridays. Last week we watched a film which was directed by a sixteen-year-old girl.
    電影社每個星期五聚會。上星期我們看了一部十六歲女生導的電影。
  2. In music class, our teacher played us a song which was written by her brother.
    音樂課的時候，老師放了一首她哥哥寫的歌給我們聽。
  3. I was cleaning the attic and found some letters which were sent by my grandmother during the war.
    我在打掃閣樓，找到幾封我外婆在戰時寄的信。
  4. Our holiday was lovely. We stayed in a hotel which was designed by a famous architect.
    我們的假期很棒。我們住在一間有名建築師設計的旅館。
  5. At the open day, they showed us some pictures which were painted by children from the school.
    開放日那天，他們給我們看了幾幅那所學校的孩子畫的畫。
  6. It's the oldest street in the city. The shops are in houses which were built by rich traders in the 1700s.
    那是這個城市最古老的街。店都開在十八世紀有錢商人蓋的房子裡。

③ 名詞 + that + be + 過去分詞 + by

  1. Is this the cake that was made by your sister? It's delicious.
    這是你姊姊做的那個蛋糕嗎？很好吃。
  2. My friend Sam gave me a watch for my birthday. It's a watch that was owned by his grandfather.
    我朋友 Sam 送我一支手錶當生日禮物。那是他爺爺的手錶。
  3. In English class, we read a story that was written by a girl in my class.
    英文課的時候，我們讀了一篇我們班一個女生寫的故事。
  4. My mum always tells me to be careful online. She doesn't trust news that is shared by strangers.
    我媽媽總是叫我上網要小心。她不相信陌生人分享的新聞。

④ 名詞是人 + who + be + 過去分詞 + by

  1. We're all very proud. There's a girl in my class who was chosen by the coach for the national team.
    我們都很驕傲。我們班有一個女生被教練選進國家隊。
  2. On holiday, I talked to a man who was rescued by a fisherman after his boat sank.
    度假的時候，我跟一個男人聊過，他的船沉了以後被一個漁夫救起來。
  3. Mrs Lee is the best teacher in the school. The children who are taught by her always do well in exams.
    李老師是學校裡最好的老師。她教的學生考試都考得很好。
  4. The book fair was great. We met a writer who is loved by teenagers all over the world.
    書展很棒。我們見到一位全世界的青少年都很喜歡的作家。

⑤ 關係子句在句中，說明主詞

  1. Our family has lived in this village for a long time. The house which was built by my grandfather is still standing.
    我們家在這個村子住很久了。我爺爺蓋的那棟房子還在。
  2. Go to the bakery on the corner. The bread which is baked by the owner every morning is always fresh.
    去轉角那家麵包店。老闆每天早上烤的麵包一直都很新鮮。
  3. My uncle loves photography. The photos which were taken by him are on the wall in our living room.
    我叔叔很喜歡攝影。他拍的照片掛在我們家客廳的牆上。
  4. My dad is a football coach. The team which is coached by him won every game this year.
    我爸爸是足球教練。他帶的那支隊伍今年每場都贏。
  5. Our class made its own rules this year. The rules which were written by the students are on the classroom door.
    我們班今年自己訂了規則。學生寫的規則貼在教室門上。

⑥ 不同的情境

  1. Your place on the trip isn't confirmed yet. Please send us the form which was signed by your parents.
    你參加旅行的名額還沒確定。請把你爸媽簽過名的表格寄給我們。
  2. I'm reading a book which was recommended by my teacher. It's about a family in Ireland.
    我在看一本老師推薦的書。內容是愛爾蘭的一個家庭。
  3. "Which bag is yours?" "The one which was given to me by my aunt."
    哪一個包包是你的？我阿姨送我的那個。
  4. Our printer is very slow. We need a printer which is used by a lot of offices and is easy to repair.
    我們的印表機很慢。我們需要一台很多辦公室都在用、而且容易修的印表機。
  5. Look, this is the bridge which was damaged by the flood last year. They've just repaired it.
    你看，這就是去年被洪水沖壞的那座橋。他們剛修好。
  6. The restaurant has a new chef. Have you tried the soup which is made by him?
    那家餐廳來了一個新廚師。你喝過他做的湯了嗎？
