The best time for me to visit Scotland would be July, because I will not have finished my exams by June.

我最適合去蘇格蘭的時間應該是七月，因為到六月我的考試還沒考完。

主⁠詞 + will not have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

will not / won't、have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The best time for me to visit Scotland would be July, because I will not have finished my exams by June.

will not / won't否定。not 放在 will 後面、have 前面。
have + 過去分詞到那個時間點還沒完成的事。

還⁠沒⁠完⁠成多⁠少

例句

We won't have done more than forty kilometres by lunch.

到午餐時間，我們騎的路還不會超過四十公里。

否定句也可以說到那個時間點，數量還不到多少。常接 more than。

not 放⁠在 will 後⁠面、have 前⁠面

The best time for me to visit Scotland would be July, because I will not have finished my exams by June.

I will have not finished by June.

I will not have finished by June.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The best time for me to visit Scotland would be July, because I will not have finished my exams by June.

They won't have went home by then.

They won't have gone home by then.

未⁠來完⁠成⁠式⁠的否⁠定用 won't have，不⁠用 don't have

By Friday I don't have finished the report.

By Friday I won't have finished the report.

例句

① 主詞 + will not have + 過去分詞

  1. The architects will not have finished the plans by the deadline.
    到截止日，建築師還沒畫完設計圖。
  2. The new hospital will not have opened by the end of the year.
    到年底，新醫院還不會開幕。
  3. By the time the meeting starts, the report will not have arrived.
    等會議開始的時候，報告還不會送到。
  4. The workers will not have repaired the road by the holiday.
    到假期的時候，工人還沒把路修好。
  5. I will not have saved enough money by the summer.
    到夏天，我還存不夠錢。
  6. The paint will not have dried by tonight.
    到今晚，油漆還不會乾。
  7. By next week, the committee will not have made a decision.
    到下星期，委員會還不會做出決定。
  8. The professor will not have read all the dissertations by Friday.
    到星期五，教授還沒把論文全部看完。
  9. The company will not have paid the workers by the end of the month.
    到月底，公司還不會付工資給工人。
  10. By the time you land, the storm will not have passed.
    等你降落的時候，暴風雨還不會過去。
  11. The students will not have learned all the words by the test.
    到考試的時候，學生們還沒把單字全部學會。
  12. By noon the shop will not have sold half its bread.
    到中午，這家店賣出的麵包還不到一半。
  13. The doctors will not have received the test results by Monday.
    到星期一，醫生還不會收到檢查結果。
  14. By then, the government will not have changed the law.
    到那時候，政府還不會修改法律。
  15. The river will not have gone down by morning.
    到早上，河水還不會退。

② 主詞 + won't have + 過去分詞

  1. Once we stop for lunch, we won't have done more than forty kilometres.
    我們停下來吃午餐的時候，騎的路還不會超過四十公里。
  2. Don't call at six. I won't have got home yet.
    六點別打來，那時候我還沒到家。
  3. She won't have finished her homework by dinner.
    到晚餐時間，她還沒寫完功課。
  4. They won't have heard the news by the time we arrive.
    等我們到的時候，他們還不會聽到這個消息。
  5. By Christmas, we won't have paid off the car.
    到聖誕節，我們還沒付清車貸。
  6. He won't have woken up by eight.
    到八點他還不會醒。
  7. You won't have seen the film by Saturday, so don't read the review.
    到星期六你還沒看那部電影，所以別看影評。
  8. I won't have read the book by the next class.
    到下一堂課，我還沒讀完那本書。
  9. The guests won't have left by eleven.
    到十一點，客人還不會走。
  10. By then, the kids won't have eaten anything for six hours.
    到那時候，孩子們就已經六個小時沒吃東西了。
  11. We won't have sold the house by the time the baby comes.
    等寶寶出生的時候，我們還沒把房子賣掉。
  12. My brother won't have passed his driving test by the summer.
    到夏天，我弟還沒考過駕照。
  13. The band won't have recorded the album by the end of the year.
    到年底，樂團還不會錄完專輯。
  14. She won't have spent all the money by next week.
    到下星期，她還不會把錢花光。
  15. By the time the bus comes, I won't have waited long.
    等公車來的時候，我應該還沒等很久。
  16. You won't have forgotten me by next year, will you?
    到明年你還不會忘了我吧？
