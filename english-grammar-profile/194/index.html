例句

① one can

  1. One can learn a lot about a country from its food.
    從一個國家的食物可以了解很多關於這個國家的事。
  2. In this city, one can find restaurants from all over the world.
    在這座城市，可以找到來自世界各地的餐廳。
  3. One can easily spend a whole day in this museum.
    在這間博物館很容易就能待上一整天。
  4. From the tower, one can see three countries on a clear day.
    天氣晴朗的時候，從塔上可以看到三個國家。
  5. One cannot always get what one wants.
    人不能總是得到自己想要的。

② one should / must

  1. One should never judge people by appearances.
    不應該以貌取人。
  2. One must show a passport to enter the country.
    入境這個國家必須出示護照。
  3. One should drink plenty of water in hot weather.
    天氣熱的時候要多喝水。
  4. One must remember that not everyone has the same opportunities.
    要記得不是每個人都有一樣的機會。
  5. One should try to save a little money every month.
    每個月都應該試著存一點錢。

③ if one …, one …

  1. If one wants to stay healthy, one needs to exercise regularly.
    想保持健康，就要規律運動。
  2. If one travels by train, one can work during the journey.
    搭火車旅行的話，可以在路上工作。
  3. If one lives in the countryside, one usually needs a car.
    住在鄉下的話，通常需要一輛車。
  4. If one is lucky, one may see dolphins from the boat.
    運氣好的話，可以從船上看到海豚。
  5. If one has a problem, one should talk to someone about it.
    有問題的話，應該找人談一談。

④ one's

  1. One ought to keep one's word.
    人應該說話算話。
  2. It is important to look after one's health.
    照顧自己的健康很重要。
  3. One can't choose one's family.
    人沒辦法選擇自己的家人。
  4. One's childhood memories often stay for ever.
    童年的記憶常常會一輩子留著。
  5. One needs to plan one's time carefully at university.
    上大學要好好規劃自己的時間。

⑤ 說出一般的看法

  1. One never knows what life will bring.
    誰也不知道人生會發生什麼。
  2. One would think the trains would be on time by now.
    照理說火車到現在應該會準時了。
  3. One often forgets how lucky one is.
    人常常忘了自己有多幸運。
  4. One rarely sees such beautiful weather in March.
    三月很少有這麼好的天氣。
  5. One has to admit that the plan has some good points.
    不得不承認這個計畫有一些優點。

⑥ 作文和正式的文章

  1. If one works from home, one saves time and money on travel.
    在家工作的話，可以省下通勤的時間和錢。
  2. One might argue that cars are more convenient than buses.
    有人可能會說汽車比公車方便。
  3. When one considers the cost of living, one understands why young people leave the city.
    想想生活費，就能明白為什麼年輕人要離開城市。
  4. One cannot deny that the internet has changed our lives.
    不能否認網路改變了我們的生活。
  5. If one cycles to work, one gets exercise and helps the environment.
    騎腳踏車上班的話，既能運動又能幫助環境。
  6. In conclusion, one can see that there are many ways to get to work.
    總結來說，可以看出上班的方式有很多種。
