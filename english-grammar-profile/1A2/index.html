Who are those people across the street?

對街那些人是誰？

those 指遠⁠處⁠的人

對街那些人是誰？

these 近，those 遠

Who are those people across the street?

these

These shoes are too small.

這雙鞋太小了。
those

Can I try those shoes in the window?

我可以試穿櫥窗裡那雙鞋嗎？

those 也指過⁠去

Who are those people across the street?

those days

In those days, we didn't have phones.

那個時候我們沒有手機。
those years

I was very happy in those years.

那幾年我很快樂。

單⁠數名⁠詞用 that

Who are those people across the street?

Who is those man over there?

Who is that man over there?

those 前⁠面不⁠加 the

Who are those people across the street?

Look at the those birds on the roof.

Look at those birds on the roof.

those 動⁠詞用複⁠數

Who are those people across the street?

Those flowers is beautiful.

Those flowers are beautiful.

例句

① those + 複數名詞：遠處的人

  1. There's a film crew outside. Who are those people across the street?
    外面有一組拍片的人。對街那些人是誰？
  2. Do you know those boys at the bus stop?
    你認識公車站那幾個男生嗎？
  3. Those children over there are in my sister's class.
    那邊那幾個小孩是我妹妹班上的。
  4. Can you see those men on the roof? They're fixing it.
    你看得到屋頂上那幾個男人嗎？他們在修屋頂。

② those + 複數名詞：遠處的東西

  1. Excuse me, how much are those bags in the window?
    請問櫥窗裡那些包包多少錢？
  2. Look at those balloons in the sky! There are hundreds of them.
    你看天上那些氣球！有好幾百個。
  3. Can you pass me those books on the top shelf?
    你可以把書架最上面那幾本書拿給我嗎？
  4. Those mountains are covered in snow all year.
    那些山一整年都積著雪。
  5. I'd like two of those cakes, please.
    請給我兩個那種蛋糕。

③ those days / those years：過去的時間

  1. My grandmother grew up on a farm. In those days, there was no electricity.
    我奶奶在農場長大。那個時候沒有電。
  2. I remember those summers at the beach with my cousins.
    我記得那幾個夏天跟表兄弟姊妹在海邊度過。
  3. Life was simpler in those days.
    那個時候生活比較簡單。
  4. We were students then. Those years were the best of my life.
    那時候我們是學生。那幾年是我人生中最好的時光。

④ 購物和指東西

  1. I don't like these shoes. Can I try those ones?
    我不喜歡這雙鞋。我可以試穿那雙嗎？
  2. Those flowers by the door are for you.
    門邊那些花是給你的。
  3. Are those your keys on the table?
    桌上那串是你的鑰匙嗎？

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Those houses on the hill have a beautiful view.
    山坡上那些房子的景色很美。
  2. Don't eat those berries. They might be poisonous.
    別吃那些莓果。可能有毒。
  3. Can you hear those birds? They sing every morning.
    你聽得到那些鳥叫嗎？牠們每天早上都在唱歌。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Whose are those coats?" "They're ours. We'll take them."
    那些外套是誰的？是我們的。我們會拿走。
  2. "Are those your parents?" "Yes, the ones by the car."
    那兩個是你爸媽嗎？對，車子旁邊那兩個。
  3. "Which glasses do you want?" "Those ones on the left, please."
    你要哪副眼鏡？左邊那副，謝謝。
