No sooner had I gone to bed than someone broke the door down.

我才剛上床睡覺，就有人把門撞破了。

No sooner + had + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 + than + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠式

no sooner、had + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞、than

no sooner一……就……。放在句首。
had + 主詞 + 過去分詞先發生的事，倒裝。
than後面接馬上接著發生的事，用過去式。

一⁠般⁠的說⁠法

例句

As soon as I got there, my classmates told me the news.

我一到那裡，同學們就告訴我那個消息。

日常口語常用 as soon as 代替。

no sooner 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，had 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

No sooner had I gone to bed than someone broke the door down.

No sooner I had gone to bed than the phone rang.

No sooner had I gone to bed than the phone rang.

no sooner 和 than 一⁠起用

No sooner had I gone to bed than someone broke the door down.

No sooner had she left when it started to rain.

No sooner had she left than it started to rain.

had 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 sat

No sooner had I gone to bed than someone broke the door down.

No sooner had we sit down than the lights went out.

No sooner had we sat down than the lights went out.

例句

① No sooner had I + 過去分詞 + than + 句子：故事的轉折

  1. I was exhausted after the long climb. No sooner had I gone to bed than someone broke the door down.
    爬了很久的山，我累壞了。我才剛上床，就有人把門撞破了。
  2. No sooner had I closed my eyes than the baby started to cry.
    我才剛閉上眼睛，寶寶就開始哭了。
  3. No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang.
    我才剛坐下，電話就響了。
  4. No sooner had I left the house than it began to pour.
    我才剛出門，就開始下大雨了。
  5. No sooner had I paid for the dress than I saw it cheaper in another shop.
    我才剛付完洋裝的錢，就在另一家店看到更便宜的。
  6. No sooner had I started my new job than the company closed.
    我才剛開始新工作，公司就倒了。

② No sooner had I got + 地方 + than + 句子：一到就發生

  1. I was worried about my exam. But no sooner had I got to school than my classmates told me that I had passed.
    我很擔心我的考試。可是我一到學校，同學們就告訴我我通過了。
  2. No sooner had we got to the beach than the sun disappeared.
    我們一到海灘，太陽就不見了。
  3. No sooner had she got home than her friends arrived.
    她一到家，她的朋友就來了。
  4. No sooner had they got to the station than the train left.
    他們一到車站，火車就開走了。
  5. No sooner had he got into the car than he realised he had lost his keys.
    他一上車，就發現他的鑰匙不見了。
  6. No sooner had we got off the plane than we felt the heat.
    我們一下飛機，就感覺到熱氣。

③ No sooner had + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + than + 天氣或意外

  1. No sooner had we started the picnic than it began to rain.
    我們才剛開始野餐，就開始下雨了。
  2. No sooner had the match begun than the lights went out.
    比賽才剛開始，燈就熄了。
  3. No sooner had the plane taken off than the storm hit.
    飛機才剛起飛，暴風雨就來了。
  4. No sooner had she painted the fence than the dog jumped on it.
    她才剛油漆好圍籬，狗就跳到上面了。
  5. No sooner had they built the bridge than the river flooded.
    他們才剛蓋好橋，河就氾濫了。
  6. No sooner had the farmer cut the hay than a terrible storm swept across the fields.
    農夫才剛割完乾草，一場可怕的暴風雨就橫掃了田野。

④ No sooner had + 主詞 + said + than + 句子：話剛說完

  1. No sooner had I said his name than he walked through the door.
    我才剛說到他的名字，他就走進門了。
  2. No sooner had she promised to be quiet than she started talking again.
    她才剛答應要安靜，就又開始說話了。
  3. No sooner had the teacher explained the rules than someone broke them.
    老師才剛解釋完規定，就有人違反了。
  4. No sooner had he said he wasn't hungry than he ate three sandwiches.
    他才剛說他不餓，就吃了三個三明治。
  5. No sooner had we decided to go than the tickets sold out.
    我們才剛決定要去，票就賣完了。
  6. No sooner had she told me the secret than she asked me to forget it.
    她才剛把秘密告訴我，就叫我忘掉它。

⑤ As soon as + 主詞 + 過去式, + 主詞 + 過去式：一般的說法

  1. As soon as I went to bed, someone knocked on the door.
    我一上床，就有人敲門。
  2. As soon as we arrived, the party started.
    我們一到，派對就開始了。
  3. As soon as she opened the window, the cat jumped out.
    她一打開窗戶，貓就跳出去了。
  4. As soon as the bell rang, the children ran outside.
    鐘一響，孩子們就跑到外面。
  5. As soon as he heard the news, he called his mother.
    他一聽到消息，就打電話給他媽媽。
  6. As soon as I sat down, the waiter brought the menu.
    我一坐下，服務生就拿菜單來了。
  7. As soon as the sun came out, the beach filled with people.
    太陽一出來，海灘就擠滿了人。
