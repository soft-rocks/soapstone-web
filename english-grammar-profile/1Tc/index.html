例句

① 請人做事：過去

  1. After I left you on Monday, I had my car serviced and then visited Washington.
    星期一跟你分開之後，我把車送去保養，然後去參觀華盛頓。
  2. It was very hot last month, so she had her hair cut short.
    上個月很熱，所以她把頭髮剪短了。
  3. We had the whole house painted before we moved in.
    我們搬進去之前，請人把整間房子都油漆了。
  4. I had my eyes tested last week. I need glasses.
    我上星期去檢查眼睛。我需要戴眼鏡。
  5. Their old kitchen was falling apart, so they had a new one fitted in the spring.
    他們的舊廚房快壞了，所以春天請人裝了新的。

② 請人做事：現在和未來

  1. It's a shame I'm having my room redecorated, but the garden will be perfect for the party.
    很可惜我的房間正在重新裝潢，可是花園拿來開派對很完美。
  2. My hair grows really fast, so I have it cut every six weeks.
    我的頭髮長得很快，所以每六個星期剪一次。
  3. We're having a new fence put up next week.
    我們下星期要請人裝新的圍籬。
  4. You should have that cough looked at by a doctor.
    你應該讓醫生看看你的咳嗽。
  5. I'll have the documents sent to your office tomorrow.
    我明天會請人把文件送到你的辦公室。

③ 遇到不好的事

  1. My plans fell through because I had my purse stolen and I had no money to continue my journey.
    我的計畫泡湯了，因為我的錢包被偷了，我沒錢繼續旅行。
  2. I played for the best team in São Paulo, but I had to stop because I had my leg broken in a car crash.
    我曾經在聖保羅最好的球隊踢球，可是因為在車禍中斷了腿，只好停下來。
  3. She had her bag taken while she was asleep on the train.
    她在火車上睡著的時候，包包被拿走了。
  4. We had our windows broken by some kids playing football.
    我們家的窗戶被一些踢足球的孩子打破了。
  5. He had his flight cancelled and had to stay another night.
    他的班機被取消了，只好再住一晚。
  6. My neighbour had his car damaged in the storm.
    我鄰居的車在暴風雨中受損了。

④ having + 受詞 + 過去分詞

  1. In a big city, the risk of having your bike stolen is much bigger.
    在大城市，腳踏車被偷的風險大很多。
  2. I hate having my photo taken.
    我討厭被拍照。
  3. She's afraid of having her phone stolen on the bus.
    她怕在公車上手機被偷。
  4. Learn to fix small problems yourself. Having your car repaired can be very expensive.
    小問題要學著自己修。請人修車可能很貴。

⑤ 生活中常見的服務

  1. Where do you have your shirts cleaned?
    你都去哪裡洗襯衫？
  2. I'm going to have my teeth checked tomorrow.
    我明天要去檢查牙齒。
  3. We had the piano tuned before the concert.
    音樂會之前我們請人調了鋼琴。
  4. You can have your shopping delivered to your door.
    你可以讓人把買的東西送到家門口。
  5. My mum loves pretty hands. She has her nails done every Saturday.
    我媽媽很愛漂亮的手。她每個星期六去做指甲。

⑥ 否定句

  1. I didn't have my hair cut. I just washed it!
    我沒去剪頭髮。我只是洗了頭！
  2. We haven't had the heating checked this year.
    我們今年還沒請人檢查暖氣。
  3. Luckily, we didn't have anything taken during the break-in.
    幸好小偷闖進來的時候，我們沒有東西被拿走。

⑦ 問句

  1. "Your hair looks great! Did you have it coloured?" "Yes, yesterday."
    你的頭髮好好看！你去染了嗎？對，昨天。
  2. "Have you had your car checked before the long drive?" "Not yet."
    長途開車前你有去檢查車子嗎？還沒。
  3. "When are you having your kitchen done?" "In March, I hope."
    你的廚房什麼時候要整修？希望是三月。
