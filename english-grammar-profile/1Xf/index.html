I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

我相信你已經看過第一集了，不過第二集更好看！

even 表⁠示比已⁠經很怎⁠麼⁠樣⁠的還⁠要更怎⁠麼⁠樣

I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

星期一已經很熱，今天更熱。

much 只⁠說差很⁠多，even 表⁠示前⁠面那⁠個已⁠經很怎⁠麼⁠樣

I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

much

My brother is much taller than me.

我哥哥比我高很多。
even

My brother is tall, but my sister is even taller.

我哥哥很高，但是我姊姊更高。

get even + 比⁠較⁠級，表⁠示變⁠得更怎⁠麼⁠樣

I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

到了下午，雨下得更大了。

even 後⁠面要⁠用比⁠較⁠級

I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

The second test was even hard.

The second test was even harder.

very 不⁠修⁠飾比⁠較⁠級

I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

The soup was good, but the dessert was very better.

The soup was good, but the dessert was even better.

even 放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面

I'm sure you've seen part one already, but part two was even better!

This year the festival was bigger even.

This year the festival was even bigger.

例句

① be + even + 形容詞-er

  1. The first hotel was nice, but the second one was even nicer.
    第一家旅館很好，第二家更好。
  2. My brother is tall, but my sister is even taller.
    我哥哥很高，但是我姊姊更高。
  3. The new stadium is even bigger than the old one.
    新的體育館比舊的還要大。
  4. Tokyo was busy, but Osaka was even busier.
    東京很熱鬧，大阪更熱鬧。
  5. The second box is even heavier.
    第二個箱子更重。
  6. Her new flat is even smaller than her old one.
    她的新公寓比舊的還要小。
  7. Is the beach even quieter in winter?
    海灘冬天更安靜嗎？

② be + even + more + 形容詞

  1. The ending of the film was even more exciting.
    電影的結局更刺激。
  2. The view from the top was even more beautiful.
    從山頂看出去的景色更美。
  3. This phone is even more expensive than mine.
    這支手機比我的還要貴。
  4. My grandfather is quiet, but my grandmother is even more reticent.
    我爺爺很安靜，我奶奶話更少。
  5. Is the final exam even more difficult?
    期末考更難嗎？
  6. Your idea is even more interesting than his.
    你的點子比他的還要有趣。

③ even better / even worse

  1. The sequel was even better than the original.
    續集比第一集還要好看。
  2. The food was good, and the service was even better.
    食物很好吃，服務更好。
  3. The traffic is bad today, but tomorrow will be even worse.
    今天交通很糟，明天會更糟。
  4. Her second song was even better than her first.
    她的第二首歌比第一首還要好聽。
  5. That would be even worse.
    那樣會更糟。

④ get even + 比較級

  1. The rain got even heavier in the afternoon.
    到了下午，雨下得更大了。
  2. Her smile got even bigger when she saw the cake.
    她看到蛋糕，笑得更開了。
  3. The roads will get even busier at five.
    五點的時候路上會更塞。
  4. Ann's English got even better after a year in London.
    在倫敦待了一年以後，Ann 的英文變得更好了。
  5. The room got even colder at night.
    到了晚上，房間變得更冷。
  6. The questions got even harder after lunch.
    午餐後題目變得更難。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Next week will be even hotter.
    下禮拜會更熱。
  2. Ken looks even happier than before.
    Ken 看起來比以前更開心。
  3. The second day of the trip felt even longer.
    旅行的第二天感覺更長。
  4. Why is this bag even more expensive?
    為什麼這個包包更貴？
  5. I feel even more tired now.
    我現在覺得更累了。
  6. The second floor seems even quieter.
    二樓好像更安靜。
