First of all, I must tell you that it always depends on how badly you want to do something.

首先，我必須告訴你，這始終要看你有多想做一件事。

言談標⁠記替要說⁠的幾⁠件⁠事排順⁠序

First of all, I must tell you that it always depends on how badly you want to do something.

首先，我們需要一位好老師。第二，我們需要好的教材。

言談標記放在句首時，後面常加逗號。

第一⁠件⁠事用 first of all、firstly 或 first

First of all, I must tell you that it always depends on how badly you want to do something.

first of all

First of all, I'd like to thank you for coming.

首先，我想謝謝大家來參加。
firstly

Firstly, the flat is too far from the city centre.

第一，這間公寓離市中心太遠了。
first

First, wash your hands.

首先，洗手。

中⁠間⁠的論⁠點用 secondly 和 thirdly

secondly

Secondly, the rent is far too high.

第二，房租實在太高了。
thirdly

Thirdly, there's no parking.

第三，那裡沒有停車位。

中⁠間⁠的步⁠驟用 next、then 和 after that

next

Next, add the eggs.

接著，加入蛋。
then

Then cook it for ten minutes.

然後，煮十分鐘。
after that

After that, turn off the heat.

之後，把火關掉。

then 後面常不加逗號。

最⁠後一⁠件⁠事用 finally 或 lastly

finally

Finally, I would be grateful if you could send me a timetable.

最後，如果您能寄一份時刻表給我，我會很感激。
lastly

Lastly, please don't forget your passport.

最後，請別忘了帶護照。

排論⁠點和排步⁠驟用不⁠同⁠的言談標⁠記

論點和理由
論說文、正式信件firstly、secondly、thirdly、lastly
步驟
食譜、使用說明first、next、then、after that
兩種都可以first of all、finally

原句：First of all 引⁠出要說⁠的第一⁠件⁠事

First of all, I must tell you that it always depends on how badly you want to do something.

首先，我必須告訴你，這始終要看你有多想做一件事。

at first 的意⁠思是一開⁠始，不⁠替幾⁠件⁠事排順⁠序

At first, you need a passport. Secondly, you need a visa.

First, you need a passport. Secondly, you need a visa.

at last 的意⁠思是等了很⁠久終⁠於，不⁠排最⁠後一⁠件⁠事

At last, I'd like to thank my parents for their support.

Finally, I'd like to thank my parents for their support.

固⁠定說⁠法是 first of all，first 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠ly

First of all, I must tell you that it always depends on how badly you want to do something.

Firstly of all, let me introduce myself.

First of all, let me introduce myself.

at first、at last 和 in the end 不⁠替幾⁠件⁠事排順⁠序

at first
一開始

At first, I didn't like my new school. Now I love it.

一開始，我不喜歡新學校。現在我很愛它。
at last
終於

We waited for two hours. At last, the bus came.

我們等了兩個小時。公車終於來了。
in the end
結果

We argued for hours. In the end, we stayed at home.

我們吵了好幾個小時。最後，我們待在家裡。

例句

① first of all

  1. First of all, I must apologise for my late reply.
    首先，我必須為這麼晚回信道歉。
  2. First of all, let's look at the costs.
    首先，我們來看看成本。
  3. We had an awful holiday. First of all, the hotel was fetid and dirty.
    我們的假期糟透了。首先，那家飯店又臭又髒。
  4. First of all, you need to decide what you really want.
    首先，你得決定你真正想要什麼。

② firstly、secondly 和 thirdly

  1. Firstly, the plan isn't plausible. Secondly, it would cost too much.
    第一，這個計畫不可信。第二，它花費太大。
  2. I'm sending the bookshelf back. Firstly, the instructions weren't intelligible. Secondly, two parts were missing.
    我要把書架退回去。第一，說明書看不懂。第二，少了兩個零件。
  3. Firstly, cars cause pollution. Secondly, they make cities noisy. Thirdly, they're expensive to run.
    第一，汽車造成污染。第二，它們讓城市變吵。第三，養車很貴。
  4. Firstly, I'd like to know the price. Secondly, could you tell me when the course starts?
    第一，我想知道價格。第二，可以告訴我課程什麼時候開始嗎？

③ first、next、then 和 after that

  1. First, open the box carefully. Next, take out the batteries.
    首先，小心打開盒子。接著，取出電池。
  2. First, we drove to the coast. Then we took a boat to the island.
    首先，我們開車到海邊。然後我們搭船去島上。
  3. Mix the flour and sugar. Next, add the milk.
    把麵粉和糖拌在一起。接著，加入牛奶。
  4. We had lunch in the square. After that, we visited the castle.
    我們在廣場吃午餐。之後，我們去參觀城堡。
  5. Fill in the form. After that, take it to the front desk.
    填好表格。之後，把它拿到櫃台。
  6. She checked her email. Then she called the client.
    她看了電子郵件。然後打電話給客戶。

④ finally 和 lastly

  1. Finally, I would like to thank everyone who helped.
    最後，我要謝謝每一位幫忙的人。
  2. Pour the mixture into a tin. Finally, put the cake in the oven for 40 minutes.
    把麵糊倒進烤模。最後，把蛋糕放進烤箱烤四十分鐘。
  3. Turn off all the lights. Lastly, remember to lock the door when you leave.
    把燈全部關掉。最後，離開時記得鎖門。
  4. Lastly, I want to assert that nobody here acted dishonestly.
    最後，我要明確表示，這裡沒有人做出不誠實的事。

⑤ 寫給朋友的信

  1. First of all, thanks for the lovely present.
    首先，謝謝你送的可愛禮物。
  2. Firstly, try to get more sleep. Secondly, eat more vegetables.
    第一，盡量多睡一點。第二，多吃蔬菜。
  3. Our trip to Italy was perfect. First of all, the weather was great. Secondly, the food was amazing.
    我們的義大利之旅很完美。首先，天氣很好。第二，食物很棒。
  4. Eat well and get some exercise. And lastly, please believe in yourself.
    吃好一點，多運動。最後，請相信自己。

⑥ 正式信件和論說文

  1. I am writing to complain about my order. Firstly, it arrived two weeks late.
    我寫信是要投訴我的訂單。第一，它晚了兩個星期才送到。
  2. Firstly, there is no evidence. Secondly, the witness's testimony is deceptive.
    第一，沒有證據。第二，那位證人的證詞有誤導性。
  3. Finally, I would be grateful if you could arrange a car from the airport.
    最後，如果您能安排一輛車從機場接我，我會很感激。
  4. Firstly, homework helps students review. Secondly, it teaches them to work alone. Thirdly, it shows teachers who needs help.
    第一，作業幫助學生複習。第二，它教學生自己做事。第三，它讓老師知道誰需要幫忙。

⑦ 說明和指示

  1. First, switch off the power. Next, remove the cover.
    首先，關掉電源。接著，拆下外殼。
  2. First of all, read the questions carefully. Then write your answers.
    首先，仔細讀題目。然後寫下你的答案。
  3. Boil the water. Then pour it over the tea leaves.
    把水煮開。然後把水倒在茶葉上。
  4. Download the app. After that, create an account.
    下載這個應用程式。之後，建立一個帳號。

⑧ 說過去的事

  1. First, we checked into the hotel. After that, we went for a walk.
    首先，我們辦了飯店入住。之後，我們去散步。
  2. He packed his bags. Next, he called a taxi. Finally, he locked the house.
    他收拾好行李。接著，他叫了計程車。最後，他把房子鎖上。
