例句

① loads of

  1. Christmas is coming! I'm going to make loads of cookies this year and give them to my family and friends.
    聖誕節快到了！我今年要做一大堆餅乾，送給家人和朋友。
  2. Don't rush. We've got loads of time before the train leaves.
    別急。火車開之前我們還有很多時間。
  3. The party was great. There were loads of people I didn't know.
    派對很棒。有好多我不認識的人。
  4. You should try the café on the corner. They've got loads of different cakes.
    你應該去轉角那家咖啡店。他們有好多種蛋糕。
  5. I took loads of photos on holiday. Do you want to see them?
    我度假時拍了一大堆照片。你要看嗎？

② tons of / heaps of

  1. When I was a kid, I got tons of plastic ducks every birthday.
    我小時候每次生日都收到一大堆塑膠鴨子。
  2. Sorry, I can't play football on Saturday. My teacher gave us tons of homework.
    抱歉，我星期六不能踢足球。老師出了一大堆功課。
  3. Don't buy any more food. There's heaps of pasta in the cupboard.
    別再買吃的了。櫃子裡有一大堆義大利麵。
  4. She's got tons of energy. She runs every morning before work.
    她精力超多。她每天上班前都去跑步。
  5. We've got heaps of chairs in the garage if you need some.
    如果你需要，我們車庫裡有一大堆椅子。

③ millions of / thousands of / hundreds of

  1. My kitchen is tiny, but I managed to fit in a table and millions of cookbooks.
    我的廚房很小，可是我還是塞進了一張桌子和數不清的食譜。
  2. I've told you hundreds of times to take your shoes off!
    我跟你說過幾百次要脫鞋了！
  3. There were thousands of people at the station this morning.
    今天早上車站有好幾千人。
  4. My sister has millions of friends online, but she never goes out.
    我姊姊網路上有一大堆朋友，可是她從來不出門。
  5. I've been to that shop hundreds of times, and I've never seen it so busy.
    我去過那家店幾百次了，從來沒看過它這麼忙。

④ a million

  1. Sorry I forgot to call. I've had a million things to do today.
    抱歉我忘了打電話。我今天有一百萬件事要做。
  2. The kids asked me a million questions about the trip.
    孩子們問了我一大堆關於旅行的問題。
  3. I never use most of them, but my phone has a million apps on it.
    大部分我都沒在用，可是我的手機裡有一百萬個應用程式。
  4. I've got a million ideas for the party.
    我對派對有一大堆點子。

⑤ 不可數名詞

  1. Take your time. There's loads of food left.
    慢慢來。還剩很多食物。
  2. My grandparents have tons of money, but they never spend it.
    我爺爺奶奶有很多錢，可是從來不花。
  3. Be careful, there's heaps of snow on the road.
    小心，路上有一大堆雪。
  4. You need loads of patience to teach young children.
    教小小孩需要很多耐心。

⑥ 抱怨

  1. I've asked the landlord hundreds of times to fix the light, and nothing happens.
    我已經請房東修燈修了幾百次了，什麼都沒發生。
  2. There are tons of mistakes in this report. Who wrote it?
    這份報告有一大堆錯誤。是誰寫的？
  3. My inbox has thousands of emails I haven't read.
    我的收件匣有好幾千封還沒讀的信。
  4. I've spent loads of money on this car, and it still doesn't work.
    我在這台車上花了好多錢，它還是不能動。

⑦ 對話

  1. "Do you need help with the shopping?" "No, thanks. I've got loads of bags."
    買東西要幫忙嗎？不用，謝謝。我有很多袋子。
  2. "Have you got any books about Spain?" "Tons! Come and look."
    你有講西班牙的書嗎？一大堆！來看。
  3. "Was the concert busy?" "Yes, there were thousands of people."
    演唱會人多嗎？多，有好幾千人。
  4. "How many times have you seen this film?" "Hundreds of times!"
    這部電影你看過幾次？幾百次了！
