She could hear a man talking.

她聽得到有個男人在說話。

could 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，說主⁠詞在過⁠去做得⁠到⁠的事

She could hear a man talking.

她聽得到有個男人在說話。

故事接著寫，那個男人在對她下命令。

could 常接 see、smell、feel 這⁠類感⁠官⁠動⁠詞

She could hear a man talking.

could see

We could see the mountains from our room.

我們從房間看得到山。
could smell

I could smell smoke in the hall.

我在走廊上聞得到煙味。
could feel

He could feel the floor shaking.

他感覺得到地板在搖。

受⁠詞後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠⁠ing，表⁠示當⁠時正⁠在做⁠的動⁠作

She could hear a man talking.

她聽得到有個男人在說話。

could 也說過⁠去一⁠段時⁠間會⁠的技⁠能

She could hear a man talking.

我妹妹上學之前就會讀書了。

could 已⁠經表⁠示過⁠去，後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She could hear a man talking.

She could heard a noise.

She could hear a noise.

could 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

She could hear a man talking.

I could to see the lake.

I could see the lake.

某一⁠次做⁠成⁠的⁠事，用 was able to

She could hear a man talking.

The fire was big, but everyone could escape.

The fire was big, but everyone was able to escape.

感官動詞不受這個限制，could see、could hear 可以說一次的情況。

can 說現⁠在⁠的能⁠力，could 說過⁠去⁠的能⁠力

She could hear a man talking.

說未⁠來⁠的 could 是可⁠能，不⁠是能⁠力

She could hear a man talking.

今晚可能會下雨。

was able to 說某一⁠次做⁠成⁠的事

She could hear a man talking.

三個小時後，他們終於打開了門。

例句

① could + see / hear / smell / feel

  1. I could see the sea from my bedroom window.
    我從臥室窗戶看得到海。
  2. She could hear the rain on the roof.
    她聽得到雨打在屋頂上的聲音。
  3. We could smell fresh bread from the bakery.
    我們聞得到麵包店飄來的新鮮麵包香。
  4. He could feel the sun on his face.
    他感覺得到臉上的陽光。
  5. They could see the lights of the city below.
    他們看得到下面城市的燈光。
  6. I could hear my neighbours arguing.
    我聽得到鄰居在吵架。
  7. The children could see a bird building a nest.
    孩子們看得到一隻鳥在築巢。
  8. She could smell something burning in the kitchen.
    她聞得到廚房裡有東西燒焦了。
  9. I could feel my heart beating fast.
    我感覺得到自己的心跳得很快。
  10. We could hear music coming from the house next door.
    我們聽得到隔壁房子傳來的音樂。
  11. From the top, you could see three countries.
    從山頂上看得到三個國家。
  12. He could taste the salt in the air.
    他嚐得到空氣中的鹹味。

② could + understand / remember

  1. I could understand most of the film without subtitles.
    那部電影不看字幕，我大部分也聽得懂。
  2. She could remember every word of the song.
    那首歌的每個字她都記得。
  3. He could understand a little Japanese.
    他聽得懂一點日文。
  4. My grandfather could remember the day the war ended.
    我爺爺記得戰爭結束那天的事。

③ could + 原形動詞，過去會的技能

  1. My son could count to a hundred at four.
    我兒子四歲就會數到一百了。
  2. By the age of ten, he could play the piano well.
    他十歲的時候，鋼琴就彈得很好了。
  3. My grandmother could sew her own clothes.
    我奶奶會自己縫衣服。
  4. When I lived in Spain, I could speak Spanish quite well.
    我住在西班牙的時候，西班牙文說得還不錯。
  5. Our dog could open the gate with its nose.
    我們家的狗會用鼻子把柵門頂開。
  6. She could run very fast when she was young.
    她年輕的時候跑得很快。
  7. As a child, I could climb any tree in the park.
    我小時候，公園裡的每一棵樹我都爬得上去。
  8. He could cook only one dish, but he cooked it very well.
    他只會做一道菜，可是做得很好。

④ could + 原形動詞，當時的情況讓人做得到

  1. The hotel was close, so we could walk everywhere.
    飯店很近，所以我們去哪裡都可以走路。
  2. In my old job, I could work from home on Fridays.
    我以前那份工作，星期五可以在家上班。
  3. With the new glasses, she could read the small print.
    戴了新眼鏡，她看得清小字了。
  4. The library was open late, so students could study there at night.
    圖書館開到很晚，所以學生晚上可以在那裡讀書。
  5. Back then, you could buy a bus ticket for ten cents.
    那個時候，十分錢就買得到一張公車票。
  6. On a clear day, we could see the island from the beach.
    天氣晴朗的時候，我們從海灘看得到那座島。
