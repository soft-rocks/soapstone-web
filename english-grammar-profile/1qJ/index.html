I believe there are a few things that could be improved for next year's festival.

我認為明年的節慶活動有幾個地方可以改進。

情⁠態動⁠詞加 be，再⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

可以改進的地方

可⁠以、應⁠該、一⁠定要

例句

① could be + 過去分詞

  1. I had a marvellous time, but I believe there are a few things that could be improved for next year's festival.
    我玩得很開心，可是我認為明年的節慶活動有幾個地方可以改進。
  2. The food was good, but it could be served a bit faster.
    食物很好吃，可是上菜可以快一點。
  3. With more computers, students could be taught in a more interesting way.
    有更多電腦的話，學生可以用更有趣的方式學習。
  4. Nowadays it could be said that nearly everyone is living in a digital world.
    現在可以說幾乎每個人都活在數位世界裡。
  5. That old building is empty. It could be turned into a youth club.
    那棟舊建築是空的。可以改成青年活動中心。

② should be + 過去分詞

  1. Our town needs more green space. Architects should be hired to design parks where people can have a picnic.
    我們鎮需要更多綠地。應該請建築師設計公園，讓大家可以去野餐。
  2. Students should be given more time to finish the test.
    應該給學生多一點時間寫完考試。
  3. In my opinion, mobile phones should not be used in class.
    我認為上課時不應該用手機。
  4. This letter is important. It should be sent today.
    這封信很重要。應該今天寄出。
  5. The rubbish on the beach is terrible. Something should be done about it.
    海灘上的垃圾很嚴重。應該想辦法處理。

③ must be + 過去分詞

  1. Please read the rules. All bags must be checked before you go in.
    請看規定。所有包包進去之前一定要檢查。
  2. There's a form for the school trip. It must be signed by a parent.
    校外教學有一張表格。一定要家長簽名。
  3. Animals must not be fed by visitors.
    遊客不准餵動物。
  4. Don't forget, Tom. Your homework must be handed in by Friday.
    Tom，別忘了。你的作業一定要在星期五前交。

④ can / can't be + 過去分詞

  1. You don't need to call. The tickets can be bought online.
    你不用打電話。票可以在網路上買。
  2. This sofa can be turned into a bed when we have guests.
    有客人來的時候，這張沙發可以變成床。
  3. Sorry, this coat can't be washed. It has to be dry-cleaned.
    抱歉，這件大衣不能水洗。要乾洗。
  4. From the top of the tower, the whole city can be seen.
    從塔頂可以看到整座城市。

⑤ may / might be + 過去分詞

  1. Check the weather before you go. The match might be cancelled because of the rain.
    出門前看一下天氣。比賽可能會因為下雨取消。
  2. Be careful what you write online. It may be read by anyone.
    在網路上寫東西要小心。任何人都可能看到。
  3. If you're late again, you might be asked to leave the team.
    你要是再遲到，可能會被要求離開球隊。
  4. The road may be closed tomorrow, so leave early.
    那條路明天可能會封，所以早點出門。

⑥ will / won't be + 過去分詞

  1. Don't worry about getting to the hotel. You will be met at the airport.
    別擔心怎麼去旅館。會有人在機場接你。
  2. We all entered the photo competition. The winners will be announced on Friday.
    我們都參加了攝影比賽。得獎名單星期五公布。
  3. Your money won't be returned if you cancel after Monday.
    你要是星期一之後取消，錢不會退。
  4. The new bridge will be opened by the mayor next spring.
    新橋明年春天由市長主持通車。

⑦ would be + 過去分詞

  1. If we had more money, the library would be opened on Sundays too.
    如果我們有更多錢，圖書館星期天也會開。
  2. Our town would be improved by a new sports centre.
    有新的運動中心，我們鎮會變得更好。

⑧ 問句

  1. "Can this be done by tomorrow?" "I'll try my best."
    這個明天前可以做好嗎？我盡量。
  2. "Should the windows be left open at night?" "No, close them, please."
    晚上窗戶要開著嗎？不用，請關起來。
  3. "When will the results be sent out?" "Next Monday, I think."
    結果什麼時候寄出？我想是下星期一。
