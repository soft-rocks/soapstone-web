Not only do I speak fluent English, but I also speak French, Spanish and Portuguese.

我不只英文說得很流利，還會說法語、西班牙語和葡萄牙語。

Not only + do + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Not only do I speak fluent English, but I also speak French, Spanish and Portuguese.

我不只英文說得很流利，還會說法語。

do、does、did 的選⁠擇

Not only do I speak fluent English, but I also speak French, Spanish and Portuguese.

do現在式，I、you、we、they、複數名詞
does現在式，he、she、it、單數名詞
did過去式，主詞不分單複數

第⁠二⁠個子⁠句不⁠倒裝

Not only do I speak fluent English, but I also speak French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Not only did they teach us useful knowledge, but they also organised practical exercises.

他們不只教我們實用的知識，還安排了實作練習。

前⁠面加連⁠接副⁠詞

IndeedIndeed, not only did the trip help my English, …
FurthermoreFurthermore, not only does the hotel offer free breakfast, …
For meFor me, not only do team sports keep me fit, …

Not only 後⁠面加助⁠動⁠詞

Not only they cooked dinner, but they also cleaned the kitchen.

Not only did they cook dinner, but they also cleaned the kitchen.

did 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Not only did he passed the test, but he also got the highest score.

Not only did he pass the test, but he also got the highest score.

單⁠數主⁠詞用 does

Not only do the museum open late, but it also has free entry on Sundays.

Not only does the museum open late, but it also has free entry on Sundays.

but 後⁠面用一⁠般語⁠序

Not only do I speak fluent English, but I also speak French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Not only did she sing, but did she also play the piano.

Not only did she sing, but she also played the piano.

例句

① Not only + do + 主詞

  1. Not only do I enjoy cooking, but I also love trying new restaurants.
    我不只喜歡做菜，也很喜歡嘗試新餐廳。
  2. Not only do our students learn the language, but they also live with local families.
    我們的學生不只學語言，還住在當地人家裡。
  3. Not only do extreme sports provide a feeling of achievement, but they also help you express yourself.
    極限運動不只帶來成就感，也幫你表達自己。
  4. Not only do these plants look nice, but they also clean the air in the room.
    這些植物不只好看，還能淨化房間裡的空氣。
  5. Not only do we offer free delivery, but we also accept returns for thirty days.
    我們不只提供免運，還接受三十天內退貨。

② Not only + does + 主詞

  1. Not only does the new system save time, but it also reduces mistakes.
    新系統不只省時間，也減少錯誤。
  2. Not only does my sister play the violin, but she also writes her own music.
    我姊姊不只會拉小提琴，還自己作曲。
  3. Not only does the city have excellent museums, but it also hosts a famous film festival.
    這座城市不只有很棒的博物館，還舉辦一個有名的電影節。
  4. Not only does this jacket keep you warm, but it also folds into a small bag.
    這件外套不只保暖，還能摺成一個小袋子。
  5. Not only does Mark work full-time, but he also studies at night.
    Mark 不只做全職工作，晚上還去上課。

③ Not only + did + 主詞

  1. Not only did they teach us useful knowledge, but they also organised practical exercises to check our progress.
    他們不只教我們實用的知識，還安排了實作練習來檢查我們的進度。
  2. Not only did the hotel lose our booking, but it also charged us twice.
    飯店不只弄丟了我們的訂房，還收了我們兩次錢。
  3. Not only did we miss the train, but we also left our tickets at home.
    我們不只錯過了火車，還把車票忘在家裡。
  4. Not only did the team win the cup, but they also scored a record number of goals.
    這支球隊不只拿下獎盃，進球數還破了紀錄。
  5. Not only did my neighbour fix the fence, but he also painted it for free.
    我鄰居不只修好了籬笆，還免費幫忙上漆。

④ 前面有 Indeed、Furthermore

  1. Indeed, not only did the course improve my writing, but it also gave me more confidence.
    確實，這門課不只讓我的寫作進步，也讓我更有自信。
  2. Furthermore, not only do I speak English at work, but I also use it with my friends online.
    此外，我不只在工作上說英文，也在網路上和朋友用英文聊天。
  3. Furthermore, not only does the flat have a balcony, but it also comes with a parking space.
    此外，這間公寓不只有陽台，還附一個停車位。
  4. Indeed, not only did the volunteers clean the park, but they also collected money for new benches.
    確實，志工不只打掃了公園，還募款買新長椅。

⑤ 書信和作文裡的句子

  1. For me, not only do long walks help me relax, but they also give me time to think.
    對我來說，長距離散步不只讓我放鬆，也給我時間思考。
  2. In my opinion, not only does social media waste time, but it also affects our sleep.
    我認為社群媒體不只浪費時間，也影響我們的睡眠。
  3. Not only did I enjoy the conference, but I also met several useful contacts there.
    我不只很喜歡這場研討會，還在會上認識了好幾個有用的人脈。
  4. As you know, not only do I have experience in sales, but I also speak three languages.
    您也知道，我不只有業務經驗，還會說三種語言。
  5. Not only did your letter arrive on time, but it also answered all my questions.
    您的信不只準時寄到，還回答了我所有的問題。

⑥ but 後面省略 also

  1. Not only did the bus arrive late, but it was full when it came.
    公車不只晚到，來的時候還客滿。
  2. Not only does she run the business, but she looks after three children.
    她不只經營公司，還照顧三個孩子。
  3. Not only do they make good coffee, but their cakes are excellent too.
    他們不只咖啡煮得好，蛋糕也很棒。

⑦ 和一般語序比較

  1. I not only speak Italian, but I also teach it.
    我不只會說義大利文，還教義大利文。
  2. Not only do I speak Italian, but I also teach it at a local school.
    我不只會說義大利文，還在當地一所學校教義大利文。
  3. The festival not only attracted tourists but also helped local shops.
    這個節慶不只吸引了觀光客，也幫助了當地的商店。
  4. Not only did the festival attract tourists, but it also helped many small local shops.
    這個節慶不只吸引了觀光客，也幫了很多當地的小店。
