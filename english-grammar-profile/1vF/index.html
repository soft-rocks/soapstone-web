Too much work can cause stress and heart problems.

工作過量可能會造成壓力和心臟問題。

主⁠詞 + can + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

主⁠詞、can、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Too much work can cause stress and heart problems.

主詞一類事情或一類人，像 too much work、young people、some medicines。
can一般來說可能會。
原形動詞常見的結果或反應，像 cause、make、feel、lead to、damage。

說造⁠成⁠的結⁠果

Too much work can cause stress and heart problems.

例句

Loud music can damage your hearing.

很大聲的音樂可能會損害聽力。

常用 cause、lead to、make、damage、help。

說人⁠的反⁠應

例句

Some young people can feel bored in museums.

有些年輕人在博物館裡可能會覺得無聊。

常用 feel、get、become。

can 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Too much work can cause stress and heart problems.

Too much sugar can causes problems.

Too much sugar can cause problems.

can 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Too much work can cause stress and heart problems.

Stress can to make you ill.

Stress can make you ill.

lead to 的 to 是介⁠系⁠詞，後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞

Lack of sleep can lead to make mistakes.

Lack of sleep can lead to mistakes.

例句

① 主詞 + can + cause / lead to + 名詞

  1. Too much stress can cause headaches.
    壓力太大可能會造成頭痛。
  2. Smoking can cause serious illness.
    抽菸可能會導致嚴重的疾病。
  3. Lack of sleep can lead to mistakes at work.
    睡眠不足可能會導致工作出錯。
  4. Heavy rain can cause floods in this part of the country.
    大雨可能會讓這個地區淹水。
  5. A small argument can lead to a big fight.
    小小的爭執可能會演變成大吵一架。
  6. Eating too much salt can cause high blood pressure.
    吃太多鹽可能會造成高血壓。
  7. Poor food in childhood can lead to health problems later.
    小時候飲食不好，以後可能會有健康問題。
  8. Ice on the roads can cause accidents.
    路上結冰可能會造成車禍。

② 主詞 + can + make / damage / help + 受詞

  1. Cold weather can damage old pipes.
    寒冷的天氣可能會損壞老舊的水管。
  2. A good night's sleep can make a big difference.
    睡個好覺可能會有很大的差別。
  3. Regular exercise can help you sleep better.
    規律運動可能會讓你睡得比較好。
  4. The sun can damage your skin, even in winter.
    就算是冬天，陽光也可能傷害皮膚。
  5. Strong coffee can make some people feel nervous.
    濃咖啡可能會讓有些人覺得緊張。
  6. A kind word can make someone's day.
    一句好話可能會讓人開心一整天。
  7. Too much screen time can hurt children's eyes.
    看螢幕太久可能會傷害孩子的眼睛。
  8. Learning music can help children with maths.
    學音樂可能會幫助孩子學數學。

③ 人 + can + feel / get / become + 形容詞

  1. New students can feel shy on their first day.
    新生第一天可能會覺得害羞。
  2. Older people can feel lonely when their children move away.
    孩子搬走以後，老人家可能會覺得寂寞。
  3. Students can get nervous before exams.
    學生考試前可能會很緊張。
  4. Babies can become tired very quickly.
    寶寶可能很快就會累。
  5. Tourists can get lost in the old part of the city.
    觀光客在老城區可能會迷路。
  6. Some dogs can become aggressive when they're scared.
    有些狗害怕的時候可能會變得兇。
  7. Teenagers can feel embarrassed by their parents.
    青少年可能會覺得爸媽讓他們丟臉。
  8. People can get angry very quickly when they're hungry.
    人餓的時候可能很快就會生氣。

④ 主詞 + can + 其他動詞：一般的情況

  1. In the mountains, the temperature can drop very quickly.
    山上的氣溫可能會降得很快。
  2. Children can learn a second language very fast.
    小孩學第二種語言可能會很快。
  3. Prices can change from one week to the next.
    價格可能每週都不一樣。
  4. A cold can last for two weeks.
    感冒可能會持續兩個星期。
  5. Plants can grow without soil if they have water and light.
    只要有水和光，植物不用土也能長。
  6. The journey can take up to six hours in winter.
    冬天這段路程可能要花到六個小時。
  7. Friendships can end for very small reasons.
    友誼可能會因為很小的原因結束。
