否⁠定疑⁠問⁠句要⁠有主⁠詞 that 或 it

They're filming part of Harry Potter at our school. Isn't that great?

My sister is having twins. Isn't amazing?

My sister is having twins. Isn't that amazing?

例句

① 說一件事 + Isn't that great / amazing?

  1. They're filming part of Harry Potter at our school. Isn't that great?
    他們正在我們學校拍《哈利波特》的部分場景。是不是很棒？
  2. My sister is having twins! Isn't that amazing?
    我姊姊要生雙胞胎了！是不是很驚人？
  3. Our team won the cup for the first time in fifty years. Isn't that fantastic?
    我們隊五十年來第一次贏得冠軍。是不是很棒？
  4. I got the job at the zoo. Isn't that exciting?
    我拿到動物園的工作了。是不是很令人興奮？
  5. My grandad is learning to use a smartphone at eighty-five. Isn't that wonderful?
    我爺爺八十五歲在學用智慧型手機。是不是很棒？

② 說一件事 + Isn't it / Isn't that funny / strange?

  1. Finally, two days later, I found the key in my pocket! It's funny, isn't it?
    兩天後，我終於在口袋裡找到鑰匙！很好笑，對吧？
  2. We both have the same birthday. Isn't that strange?
    我們兩個同一天生日。是不是很奇怪？
  3. The cat sleeps in the dog's bed every night. Isn't that funny?
    那隻貓每天晚上都睡在狗的床上。是不是很好笑？
  4. He lived next door for ten years, and we never met. Isn't that odd?
    他住在我隔壁十年了，我們從來沒見過面。是不是很怪？

③ 說過去的事 + Wasn't that ...?

  1. There was a concert, and I got to see Rihanna! Wasn't that awesome?
    那裡有一場演唱會，我看到 Rihanna 了！是不是超棒？
  2. We swam with dolphins on the last day. Wasn't that lucky?
    我們最後一天和海豚一起游泳。是不是很幸運？
  3. The whole class passed the exam. Wasn't that brilliant?
    全班都考過了。是不是很厲害？
  4. The bus came just as we reached the stop. Wasn't that perfect timing?
    我們一到站公車就來了。是不是時間剛剛好？

④ 想像的事 + Wouldn't that be ...?

  1. You can relax and sunbathe while you drink a cold lemonade. Wouldn't that be amazing?
    你可以一邊喝冰檸檬汁一邊放鬆曬太陽。那不是很棒嗎？
  2. We could all go camping together this summer. Wouldn't that be fun?
    我們今年夏天可以一起去露營。那不是很好玩嗎？
  3. Imagine having a pool in the garden. Wouldn't that be lovely?
    想像一下院子裡有個游泳池。那不是很好嗎？
  4. You could visit us at Christmas. Wouldn't that be nice?
    你可以聖誕節來看我們。那不是很好嗎？
  5. We could open our own café one day. Wouldn't that be a dream?
    我們有一天可以開自己的咖啡店。那不是夢想成真嗎？

⑤ 看法 + Don't you think?

  1. Cooking with friends is more fun than eating out. Don't you think?
    跟朋友一起煮飯比去外面吃好玩。你不覺得嗎？
  2. This town is more beautiful in winter. Don't you think?
    這個鎮冬天更美。你不覺得嗎？
  3. Her new song is even better than the last one, don't you think?
    她的新歌比上一首還好聽，你不覺得嗎？
  4. It's a bit expensive for a sandwich, don't you think?
    一個三明治這樣有點貴，你不覺得嗎？
  5. Our teacher looks happier this term. Don't you think?
    我們老師這學期看起來比較開心。你不覺得嗎？

⑥ Don't you think + 子句?

  1. The walls are grey and the paint is coming off. Don't you think it's time we painted the kitchen?
    牆都灰了，漆也在剝落。你不覺得我們該漆廚房了嗎？
  2. Look at this photo of Sam and his dad. Don't you think he looks just like his father?
    你看這張 Sam 和他爸爸的照片。你不覺得他長得跟他爸爸一模一樣嗎？
  3. We're having a barbecue on Sunday. Don't you think we should invite the new neighbours?
    我們星期天要烤肉。你不覺得我們應該邀請新鄰居嗎？

⑦ Isn't it / Doesn't it + 動詞：請對方同意

  1. Smell the bread. Doesn't it smell wonderful?
    聞聞看麵包。是不是很香？
  2. Listen to the rain on the roof. Isn't it relaxing?
    聽屋頂上的雨聲。是不是很放鬆？
  3. Look at the stars tonight. Aren't they beautiful?
    你看今晚的星星。是不是很美？
  4. Taste this soup. Doesn't it taste like Grandma's?
    喝喝看這湯。是不是很像奶奶煮的味道？
  5. Feel this blanket. Isn't it soft?
    摸摸看這條毯子。是不是很軟？
