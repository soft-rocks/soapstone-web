these 指剛說⁠過⁠的事⁠物

He is very clever and generous, and these are the things that I like most about him.

他很聰明又很大方，這些就是我最喜歡他的地方。

these 指什⁠麼

He is very clever and generous, and these are the things that I like most about him.

列出的事

She sings and plays the guitar. These are her hobbies.

她會唱歌又會彈吉他。這些是她的嗜好。
複數名詞

I watch The Simpsons and Friends. These are my favourites.

我看《辛普森家庭》和《六人行》。這些是我最喜歡的。
things like these

Old films and jazz? I love things like these.

老電影和爵士樂？我很喜歡這類東西。

these 和 those

「我很喜歡你以前的畫。」「謝謝，不過我已經不畫那樣的畫了。」

these + 名⁠詞

這雙鞋子穿得我腳很痛。

the following：指後⁠面⁠的事⁠物

你會需要以下的東西：一支筆、一把尺和一台計算機。

例句

① these 做總結

  1. My mother is patient and kind. These are the qualities I admire in her.
    我媽媽很有耐心又很和善。這些是我欣賞她的特質。
  2. Good food, fresh air and enough sleep. These are the secrets of a long life.
    好的食物、新鮮空氣和充足的睡眠。這些就是長壽的祕訣。
  3. The hotel was cheap and near the beach. These were the main reasons we chose it.
    那家旅館很便宜，又靠近海灘。這些是我們選它的主要原因。
  4. Tea, coffee and cola all contain caffeine. These can keep you awake at night.
    茶、咖啡和可樂都含有咖啡因。這些會讓你晚上睡不著。
  5. Rice, beans and vegetables. These are the main foods in my country.
    米飯、豆子和蔬菜。這些是我們國家的主食。

② these 指剛說過的複數名詞

  1. There are a few funny programmes like The Simpsons. These are the only programmes I like watching.
    有幾個好笑的節目，像《辛普森家庭》。我只喜歡看這些節目。
  2. I've brought some sandwiches. These are for lunch.
    我帶了一些三明治，這些是午餐要吃的。
  3. I've picked some flowers from the garden. These are for you.
    我從花園摘了一些花，這些是要給你的。
  4. I took some photos of the wedding. These are the best.
    我拍了一些婚禮的照片，這幾張拍得最好。
  5. Here are some photos of my trip. These were taken in Rome.
    這是我旅行的照片，這些是在羅馬拍的。

③ things like these

  1. I love camping and fishing. Do you enjoy things like these?
    我很喜歡露營和釣魚。你喜歡這類活動嗎？
  2. My brother collects stamps and old coins. He loves things like these.
    我哥哥收集郵票和舊硬幣。他很喜歡這類東西。
  3. Lying and cheating are wrong. I never do things like these.
    說謊和作弊是不對的。我從來不做這類事情。
  4. She worries about exams and money. Problems like these are common for students.
    她擔心考試和錢的事。這類問題在學生之間很常見。
  5. We saw lions and elephants. You don't see animals like these every day.
    我們看到了獅子和大象。這種動物不是每天都看得到。

④ these 當受詞

  1. I've written down some new words. Please learn these for tomorrow.
    我寫下了幾個新單字，請在明天以前把這些背起來。
  2. Here are the tickets. Don't lose these.
    票在這裡，這些別弄丟了。
  3. Here are some old letters. Read these when you have time.
    這裡有一些舊信，你有空的時候讀讀這些。
  4. We found some shells on the beach. Let's take these home.
    我們在海灘上撿到一些貝殼，把這些帶回家吧。
  5. I've made a list of questions. Can you answer these?
    我列了一些問題，你可以回答這些嗎？

⑤ 回答和說明

  1. "What are your hobbies?" "Swimming and painting. I do these every weekend."
    你的嗜好是什麼？游泳和畫畫。我每個週末都做這些。
  2. Read the instructions carefully. These will tell you what to do.
    仔細讀說明，這些會告訴你該怎麼做。
  3. Our teacher gave us three tips. These helped me a lot in the exam.
    老師給了我們三個訣竅，這些在考試時幫了我很多。
  4. Wash your hands and wear a mask. These are simple ways to stay healthy.
    洗手和戴口罩。這些都是保持健康的簡單方法。
  5. Check the oil and the tyres. These are the first things to look at.
    檢查機油和輪胎。這些是最先要看的東西。

⑥ 寫信和短文

  1. My best friend is funny and loyal. These are the reasons we get on so well.
    我最好的朋友很幽默又很講義氣。這些就是我們處得這麼好的原因。
  2. The town has a castle, a market and a lake. These make it a great place to visit.
    鎮上有城堡、市場和湖。這些讓它成為很適合參觀的地方。
  3. I want to travel, learn Spanish and start a band. These are my goals for next year.
    我想去旅行、學西班牙文、組樂團。這些是我明年的目標。
  4. Many people feel lonely or stressed at work. These are serious problems.
    很多人在工作上覺得孤單或壓力大。這些都是嚴重的問題。
  5. He is never late and he always smiles. These are the things I remember about him.
    他從來不遲到，又總是面帶笑容。這些是我記得他的地方。
  6. The rooms are small and the walls are thin. These are the only bad points.
    房間很小，牆壁很薄。這些是唯一的缺點。
