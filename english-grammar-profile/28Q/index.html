例句

① turn off / turn on

  1. Please turn the lights off when you leave the room.
    離開房間的時候，請把燈關掉。
  2. "It's freezing in here." "I'll turn the heating on."
    這裡好冷。我來開暖氣。
  3. The teacher asked us to turn our phones off during the test.
    老師叫我們考試時把手機關機。
  4. Can you turn the oven on? I want to bake some bread.
    你可以打開烤箱嗎？我想烤麵包。

② call up / pick up

  1. The lights stopped working, so we had to call the electrician up.
    燈不亮了，所以我們得打電話叫電工。
  2. "Have you told Grandma?" "Not yet. I'll call Grandma up tonight."
    你跟奶奶說了嗎？還沒。我今晚打電話給奶奶。
  3. Can you pick the kids up from school today? I'm working late.
    你今天可以去學校接孩子嗎？我要加班。
  4. I'll pick your sister up at the station on my way home.
    我回家路上會去車站接你妹妹。

③ work out

  1. If we can make these changes, we might be able to work this problem out.
    如果我們能做這些改變，也許就能解決這個問題。
  2. "The maths homework is so hard." "Let's work the answers out together."
    數學作業好難。我們一起把答案算出來吧。
  3. They talked for hours and finally worked their differences out.
    他們談了好幾個小時，終於化解了分歧。

④ take time off

  1. I'm glad that you have decided to take some time off. You need a rest.
    我很高興你決定休假一陣子。你需要休息。
  2. "Are you going away this summer?" "Yes, I'm taking two weeks off in August."
    你今年夏天要出去玩嗎？要，我八月請兩個星期的假。
  3. My dad never takes a day off, even when he's ill.
    我爸從來不請假，生病也一樣。
  4. She took a year off before university and travelled around Asia.
    她上大學前休了一年，到亞洲各地旅行。

⑤ give back / bring back

  1. "Is that my jacket?" "Sorry, I'll give your jacket back tomorrow."
    那是我的外套嗎？抱歉，我明天把外套還你。
  2. You have to give the keys back when you leave the hotel.
    離開飯店時要把鑰匙還回去。
  3. Can you bring the books back to the library? They're due today.
    你可以把書拿回圖書館還嗎？今天到期。
  4. My parents brought some sweets back from Japan.
    我爸媽從日本帶了一些糖果回來。

⑥ put down / let in / let out

  1. Put the knife down! You'll hurt yourself.
    把刀放下！你會受傷的。
  2. He put the phone down and started to cry.
    他放下電話，哭了起來。
  3. "Someone's at the door." "I'm in the shower! Can you let the plumber in?"
    有人在門口。我在洗澡！你可以讓水電工進來嗎？
  4. Can you let the dog out? He needs a walk.
    你可以把狗放出去嗎？牠需要散步。

⑦ 名詞受詞放在小品詞後面

  1. Don't forget to turn off the TV before you go to bed.
    睡覺前別忘了關電視。
  2. I need to call up the bank about my card.
    我得打電話給銀行問我的卡的事。
  3. We're trying to work out a plan for the weekend.
    我們在想週末的計畫。
  4. Please give back the books by Friday.
    請在星期五前把書還回來。

⑧ 受詞很長，放在小品詞後面

  1. The company has turned off all the machines in the old factory.
    公司關掉了舊工廠裡所有的機器。
  2. He picked up the two boys who were waiting at the bus stop.
    他接了那兩個在公車站等的男孩。
  3. The council wants to bring back the old trams that ran through the town.
    市議會想把以前在城裡跑的舊電車找回來。
  4. She called up all the friends she hadn't seen since school.
    她打電話給所有畢業後就沒見過的朋友。
