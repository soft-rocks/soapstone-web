I have to admit that it is a very difficult choice.

我得承認，這是個非常困難的選擇。

I + have to + admit + (that) + 子⁠句

I have to admit that it is a very difficult choice.

I have to admit我必須承認、老實說。
that 子句承認的事。that 可以省略。

承⁠認自⁠己⁠的缺⁠點

例句

I have to admit that I watch too much TV.

我必須承認，我看太多電視了。

這個說法常用來承認自己的缺點或不太好意思說的事。

承⁠認別⁠人說⁠得對

例句

I have to admit, the film was better than I expected.

我必須承認，那部電影比我想的好看。

也常用來承認別人比自己想的好。

admit 後⁠面直⁠接接 that 子⁠句

I have to admit that it is a very difficult choice.

I have to admit to that I was wrong.

I have to admit that I was wrong.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I have to admit that it is a very difficult choice.

I have to admitting that I was late.

I have to admit that I was late.

這⁠個說⁠法是 have to admit

I have to admit that it is a very difficult choice.

I have admit that I forgot.

I have to admit that I forgot.

例句

① I have to admit that + 子句：承認自己的習慣

  1. I have to admit that I watch a lot of TV, especially films and soap operas. I love them.
    我必須承認，我看很多電視，特別是電影和肥皂劇。我很喜歡。
  2. I have to admit that I eat too much chocolate.
    我必須承認，我吃太多巧克力了。
  3. I have to admit that I don't read the news very often.
    我必須承認，我不常看新聞。
  4. I have to admit that I spend hours on my phone every night.
    我必須承認，我每天晚上花好幾個小時在手機上。
  5. I have to admit that I hardly ever cook at home.
    我必須承認，我幾乎不在家煮飯。
  6. I have to admit that I'm not very good at saving money.
    我必須承認，我不太會存錢。

② I have to admit that + 子句：說事情很難

  1. I have to admit that choosing a new flat is harder than I expected.
    我必須承認，挑新公寓比我想的難。
  2. I have to admit that the exam was harder than I thought.
    我必須承認，考試比我想的難。
  3. I have to admit that I found the first week at my new job very tiring.
    我必須承認，新工作的第一個星期我覺得非常累。
  4. I have to admit that I didn't understand the end of the film.
    我必須承認，我看不懂那部電影的結局。
  5. I have to admit that moving to a new city was frightening.
    我必須承認，搬到新的城市很可怕。
  6. I have to admit that the instructions were very confusing.
    我必須承認，那份說明非常難懂。

③ I have to admit + 子句：承認自己錯了

  1. I have to admit I was wrong about your friend. She's really nice.
    我必須承認，我看錯你的朋友了。她人真的很好。
  2. I have to admit I forgot to post your letter.
    我必須承認，我忘了幫你寄信。
  3. I have to admit I didn't do my homework last night.
    我必須承認，我昨天晚上沒寫功課。
  4. I have to admit I broke the vase. I'm really sorry.
    我必須承認，花瓶是我打破的。真的很抱歉。
  5. I have to admit I wasn't listening when you explained it.
    我必須承認，你解釋的時候我沒在聽。
  6. I have to admit I lied about my age.
    我必須承認，我謊報了年齡。

④ I have to admit, + 子句：承認別人或事情比想的好

  1. I have to admit, the film was better than I expected.
    我必須承認，那部電影比我想的好看。
  2. I have to admit, your cake is better than mine.
    我必須承認，你的蛋糕比我的好吃。
  3. I didn't want to go camping, but I have to admit, it was great fun.
    我本來不想去露營，可是我必須承認，非常好玩。
  4. I have to admit, the new teacher explains things really well.
    我必須承認，新老師解釋得真的很好。
  5. I have to admit, the small hotel was more comfortable than the big one.
    我必須承認，那家小飯店比大飯店舒服。
  6. I have to admit, you were right to bring an umbrella.
    我必須承認，你帶傘是對的。

⑤ I have to admit + 名詞

  1. I have to admit my mistake. I put the wrong date on the poster.
    我必須承認我的錯。我在海報上寫錯了日期。
  2. I have to admit the truth. I've never been to London.
    我必須承認事實。我從來沒去過倫敦。
  3. I have to admit my fear of flying.
    我必須承認我怕坐飛機。
  4. I have to admit defeat. You're a much better chess player than me.
    我必須認輸。你下西洋棋比我厲害多了。
  5. I have to admit my part in the argument.
    我必須承認我在那場爭吵裡也有錯。
  6. I have to admit my ignorance. I've never heard of that singer.
    我必須承認我不懂。我從來沒聽過那個歌手。
  7. I have to admit one thing. I've lost your book.
    我必須承認一件事。我弄丟了你的書。
