get 和 have

Our minibus broke down and it took hours to get it repaired.

get

It took hours to get it repaired.

花了好幾個小時才修好。
have

I had my car repaired.

我請人修了車。

get things done：把事⁠情做⁠完

Our minibus broke down and it took hours to get it repaired.

get things done

She's always the one who gets things done.

她總是那個把事情做完的人。

受⁠詞放⁠在 get 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

Our minibus broke down and it took hours to get it repaired.

It took hours to get repaired it.

It took hours to get it repaired.

受⁠詞後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Our minibus broke down and it took hours to get it repaired.

I need to get my hair cutting.

I need to get my hair cut.

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 to

Our minibus broke down and it took hours to get it repaired.

I got the car to repaired.

I got the car repaired.

例句

① 修理和服務

  1. On the way to the coast, our minibus broke down and it took hours to get it repaired.
    去海邊的路上，我們的小巴拋錨了，花了好幾個小時才修好。
  2. My phone screen is cracked. I need to get it fixed before the weekend.
    我的手機螢幕裂了。週末前我得拿去修。
  3. We finally got the heating checked after three cold weeks.
    冷了三個星期之後，我們終於找人來檢查暖氣。
  4. Where can I get my watch repaired around here?
    這附近哪裡可以修手錶？
  5. I got my bike serviced, so it's running well now.
    我把腳踏車拿去保養了，所以現在騎起來很順。

② get things done

  1. Caroline and I are good together. I'm a bit lazy, but she's always the one who gets things done.
    我和 Caroline 很合得來。我有點懶，可是她總是那個把事情做完的人。
  2. If you want to get things done, make a list every morning.
    如果你想把事情做完，每天早上列一張清單。
  3. Our new manager doesn't talk much, but she gets things done.
    我們的新經理話不多，可是她做事很有效率。
  4. I couldn't get anything done today. The phone kept ringing.
    我今天什麼事都做不完。電話一直響。

③ 訂單和送貨

  1. We've had complaints from customers about not getting their orders delivered on time.
    我們接到客人的抱怨，說訂單沒有準時送到。
  2. Can you get these letters posted today? They're important.
    你今天可以把這些信寄出去嗎？很重要。
  3. I need to get this parcel sent to Spain by Friday.
    我得在星期五前把這個包裹寄到西班牙。
  4. We got the new furniture delivered on Saturday morning.
    我們星期六早上讓人把新家具送來了。

④ 頭髮和外表

  1. I'm getting my hair cut tomorrow. It's far too long.
    我明天要去剪頭髮。太長了。
  2. She got her ears pierced for her sixteenth birthday.
    她十六歲生日時去穿了耳洞。
  3. You should get that suit cleaned before the wedding.
    婚禮前你應該把那套西裝拿去洗。
  4. He got his photo taken for his new passport.
    他為了辦新護照去拍了照片。

⑤ 文件和工作

  1. I need to get this report finished by five.
    我得在五點前把這份報告寫完。
  2. We got the contract signed just before the deadline.
    我們在截止日前讓合約簽好了。
  3. Let's get the boring jobs done first, and then we can relax.
    我們先把無聊的工作做完，然後就可以放鬆了。
  4. It took weeks to get my visa approved.
    花了好幾個星期才讓我的簽證核准。
  5. Can you get the room cleaned before the guests arrive?
    客人來之前，你可以讓人把房間打掃好嗎？

⑥ 遇到困難

  1. It's almost impossible to get anything fixed in this building.
    在這棟大樓裡，要找人修任何東西都幾乎不可能。
  2. We tried for months to get the noise stopped, but nothing changed.
    我們試了好幾個月想讓噪音停下來，可是什麼都沒改變。
  3. I can't get my money returned. The shop says it's too late.
    我沒辦法讓店家退錢。店家說太晚了。
  4. Getting a new road built in this town takes years.
    在這個鎮上要蓋一條新路，要花好幾年。

⑦ 命令和建議

  1. Get that cut looked at. It might be infected.
    那個傷口去給人看一下。可能感染了。
  2. Get your homework done before you go out.
    出去之前先把功課做完。
  3. You'd better get your passport renewed before the trip.
    你最好在旅行前去換新護照。

⑧ 問句

  1. "Did you get the window fixed?" "Yes, a man came this morning."
    你有找人修窗戶嗎？有，今天早上有人來修了。
  2. "How long did it take to get your car repaired?" "Three days."
    你的車修了多久？三天。
