Could you meet me at my home at 7 p.m.?

你可以晚上 7 點到我家跟我碰面嗎？

Could you 是客⁠氣⁠的請⁠求

你可以晚上 7 點到我家跟我碰面嗎？

不⁠同⁠的情⁠態動⁠詞用在邀⁠請、提⁠議和問建⁠議

Could you meet me at my home at 7 p.m.?

Would you like to
邀請

Would you like to go to the concert with me?

你想跟我去聽演唱會嗎？
Shall I
提議

Shall I open the window?

要我開窗嗎？
Should I
問建議

Should I bring anything?

我應該帶什麼東西嗎？

Would you like 後⁠面⁠接 to

Could you meet me at my home at 7 p.m.?

Would you like go to the cinema?

Would you like to go to the cinema?

情⁠態動⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Could you meet me at my home at 7 p.m.?

Could she helps me?

Could she help me?

有情⁠態動⁠詞⁠的疑⁠問⁠句不⁠加 do

Could you meet me at my home at 7 p.m.?

Do you would like some tea?

Would you like some tea?

例句

① Could you + 原形動詞：客氣的請求

  1. We need to finish our project. Could you meet me at my home at 7 p.m.?
    我們得完成報告。你可以晚上 7 點到我家跟我碰面嗎？
  2. I've lost my ring somewhere in your house. Could you look for it?
    我把戒指掉在你家某個地方了。你可以找找看嗎？
  3. Could you turn the music down a little? I'm trying to study.
    你可以把音樂關小聲一點嗎？我在念書。
  4. I deleted your message by mistake. Could you text me the address again?
    我不小心刪了你的訊息。你可以再傳一次地址給我嗎？
  5. My meeting will finish late. Could you collect Emma from her dance class?
    我的會議會開到很晚。你可以去舞蹈課接 Emma 嗎？
  6. My English isn't perfect. Could you read through my cover letter for mistakes?
    我的英文不完美。你可以幫我看看求職信有沒有錯嗎？

② Would you like + to + 原形動詞：邀請

  1. I've got two tickets. Would you like to go to the U2 concert with me?
    我有兩張票。你想跟我去聽 U2 的演唱會嗎？
  2. We're having a barbecue on Saturday. Would you like to bring your kids along?
    我們星期六要烤肉。你想帶孩子一起來嗎？
  3. You read so many novels. Would you like to join our book club?
    你讀那麼多小說。你想加入我們的讀書會嗎？
  4. There's plenty left. Would you like another slice of pie?
    還剩很多。你想再吃一片派嗎？

③ Would you + 原形動詞：客氣的請求

  1. My hands are full. Would you hold the door for me, please?
    我兩手都拿滿了東西。你可以幫我扶一下門嗎？
  2. There's a cold wind coming in. Would you mind closing the window?
    有冷風吹進來。你介意把窗戶關上嗎？
  3. Sorry, I missed the last part. Would you explain that again, please?
    抱歉，我沒聽到最後那部分。你可以再解釋一次嗎？

④ Will you + 原形動詞

  1. I don't want to go alone. Will you come with me?
    我不想一個人去。你要跟我一起來嗎？
  2. He knelt down and opened a small box. "Will you marry me, Anna?"
    他單膝跪下，打開一個小盒子。Anna，你願意嫁給我嗎？
  3. I want to bring back your drill. Will you be at home this evening?
    我想把電鑽還你。你今天晚上會在家嗎？
  4. I can't do these maths problems alone. Will you sit with me after dinner?
    這些數學題我自己做不來。晚餐後你會陪我一起做嗎？

⑤ Shall I / Shall we + 原形動詞：提議

  1. You look cold. Shall I turn the heating up?
    你看起來很冷。要我把暖氣開大一點嗎？
  2. The train leaves at quarter past nine. Shall we meet at the station at nine?
    火車九點十五分開。我們九點在車站見面好嗎？
  3. Your bags look heavy. Shall I carry one?
    你的袋子看起來很重。要我幫你拿一個嗎？
  4. It's raining and the last bus has gone. Shall we walk home together?
    下雨了，最後一班公車也走了。我們一起走路回家好嗎？

⑥ Should I / Should we + 原形動詞：問建議

  1. I'm going to their party. Should I bring anything?
    我要去他們的派對。我應該帶什麼東西嗎？
  2. Should we book a table? The restaurant is popular.
    我們應該訂位嗎？那家餐廳很熱門。
  3. Nobody was hurt, but the car is damaged. Should I tell my parents about the accident?
    沒有人受傷，可是車子撞壞了。我應該把車禍的事告訴我爸媽嗎？
  4. It's a small design company. Should I dress smartly for the interview, or is that too much?
    那是一家小設計公司。我面試應該穿得正式一點嗎？還是那樣太誇張？

⑦ Could I / May I + 原形動詞：客氣地問可不可以

  1. I'm sorry to ask, but could I leave my bike in your garden tonight?
    不好意思問一下，我今晚可以把腳踏車放在你家院子嗎？
  2. She knocked on the manager's door. "May I come in for a moment?"
    她敲了經理的門。我可以進來一下嗎？
  3. Excuse me, my phone is nearly dead. Could I charge it at your desk?
    不好意思，我的手機快沒電了。我可以在你桌上充電嗎？

⑧ 回答

  1. "This box is too heavy for me. Could you help me?" "Yes, of course."
    這個箱子對我來說太重了。你可以幫我嗎？當然可以。
  2. "We're going to the cinema tonight. Would you like to come?" "I'd love to."
    我們今晚要去看電影。你想來嗎？我很樂意。
  3. "You sound busy. Shall I call you later?" "Yes, please."
    你聽起來很忙。要我晚點打給你嗎？好，麻煩你。
