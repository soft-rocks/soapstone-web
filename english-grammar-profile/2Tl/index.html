I like it because it is an interesting place.

我喜歡那裡，因為那是一個很有意思的地方。

an + 形⁠容⁠詞 + 名⁠詞

I like it because it is an interesting place.

那是一個很有意思的地方。

a + 形⁠容⁠詞 + 名⁠詞

We have a big garden and a small swimming pool.

我們有一個大花園和一個小游泳池。

a 還⁠是 an 看形⁠容⁠詞

I like it because it is an interesting place.

anan old house、an easy test、an interesting place
aa quick snack、a new car、a useful book

加⁠了形⁠容⁠詞，冠⁠詞跟⁠著換

an applea red apple
a housean old house

冠⁠詞看後⁠面⁠的形⁠容⁠詞

It's a old house.

It's an old house.

冠⁠詞放⁠在最⁠前⁠面

I like it because it is an interesting place.

It is interesting a place.

It is an interesting place.

形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

We have a garden big.

We have a big garden.

有形⁠容⁠詞也⁠要加冠⁠詞

She is good student.

She is a good student.

例句

① a + 形容詞 + 名詞：家和房間

  1. We live in a quiet street near the park.
    我們住在公園附近一條安靜的街上。
  2. My bedroom has a large window.
    我的臥室有一扇大窗戶。
  3. They bought a new sofa for the living room.
    他們幫客廳買了一張新沙發。
  4. There is a little shop at the end of our road.
    我們這條路的盡頭有一間小店。

② an + 形容詞 + 名詞：家和房間

  1. It's an old house, about fifty years old.
    那是一棟老房子，大概五十年了。
  2. We have an empty room upstairs.
    我們樓上有一間空房間。
  3. My grandmother has an amazing garden.
    我奶奶有一座很棒的花園。
  4. He lives in an ugly building next to the station.
    他住在車站旁邊一棟很醜的大樓。

③ 介紹人

  1. Sam was a shy child at primary school.
    Sam 讀小學時是個很害羞的孩子。
  2. The man next door is a friendly neighbour.
    隔壁那位先生是個很友善的鄰居。
  3. She is an excellent cook.
    她很會做菜。
  4. He is an honest man.
    他是個誠實的人。
  5. My dad is a busy doctor.
    我爸爸是個很忙的醫生。

④ 說吃的東西

  1. After school I usually eat a quick snack.
    放學後我通常會很快吃個點心。
  2. I had a big breakfast this morning.
    我今天早餐吃很多。
  3. She ordered an ice cream with chocolate sauce.
    她點了一份淋巧克力醬的冰淇淋。
  4. We had a delicious lunch at the new restaurant.
    我們在那家新餐廳吃了一頓很好吃的午餐。
  5. This is an easy recipe for soup.
    這是一個很簡單的湯的食譜。

⑤ 說地方

  1. In Kyoto we visited a famous temple.
    我們在京都參觀了一間有名的寺廟。
  2. We went to an old castle on Sunday.
    我們星期天去了一座古堡。
  3. It's a popular beach in summer.
    那是夏天很熱門的海灘。
  4. There's an Italian café near my office.
    我公司附近有一家義大利咖啡館。
  5. They live in a small village in the mountains.
    他們住在山裡的一個小村莊。

⑥ 說東西

  1. I want a red bike for my birthday.
    我生日想要一台紅色的腳踏車。
  2. He has an expensive watch.
    他有一支很貴的手錶。
  3. This is a useful book for beginners.
    這是一本適合初學者的實用書。
  4. My aunt sent me an orange scarf from Spain.
    我阿姨從西班牙寄給我一條橘色圍巾。
  5. Dad keeps a sharp knife in the top drawer.
    爸爸把一把很利的刀放在最上面的抽屜。

⑦ 說時間和活動

  1. Yesterday was a terrible day for our team.
    昨天對我們隊來說是很糟的一天。
  2. It was a long day at work.
    今天上班的時間好長。
  3. Tomorrow we must catch an early train to Leeds.
    明天我們得搭一早的火車去里茲。
  4. Tennis is an exciting sport.
    網球是一種很刺激的運動。
