My house is very clean.

我家很乾淨。

be 連⁠接主⁠詞和補⁠語

我家很乾淨。

be 的形⁠式跟⁠著主⁠詞

My house is very clean.

Iam, was
he / she / itis, was
you / we / theyare, were

補⁠語可⁠以是形⁠容⁠詞、名⁠詞片⁠語、介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語或日⁠期

My house is very clean.

形容詞

The tea is too sweet.

茶太甜了。
名詞片語

Ms Lee is our new teacher.

李老師是我們的新老師。
介系詞片語

Our teacher is from Ireland.

我們老師是愛爾蘭人。
日期

The test is on Monday.

考試在星期一。

主⁠詞和形⁠容⁠詞補⁠語中⁠間要⁠有 be

My house is very clean.

The new swimming pool very popular.

The new swimming pool is very popular.

主⁠詞是複⁠數，用 are

My house is very clean.

The tickets for the concert is expensive.

The tickets for the concert are expensive.

agree 是動⁠詞，前⁠面不⁠加 be

My grandparents are own a small boat.

My grandparents own a small boat.

例句

① be + 形容詞

  1. Come in! The kitchen is a mess because we're painting it.
    進來吧！廚房很亂，因為我們在油漆。
  2. The soup is too hot. Let it cool down first.
    湯太燙了，先放涼一下。
  3. Grandpa is tired after the long drive, so he's having a nap.
    爺爺開了很久的車很累，所以在睡午覺。
  4. Your new shoes are really nice.
    你的新鞋好好看。
  5. The shops were very busy on Saturday.
    星期六商店人很多。

② be + 名詞片語

  1. Martin is my brother, and Anna is his wife.
    Martin 是我哥哥，Anna 是他太太。
  2. My cousin is a firefighter in Glasgow.
    我表哥在格拉斯哥當消防員。
  3. These are my new friends from college.
    這些是我在學院認識的新朋友。
  4. It was a lovely day at the beach.
    那天在海邊過得很愉快。

③ be + from + 地方

  1. Martin is from Poland, but he lives in London now.
    Martin 是波蘭人，不過他現在住在倫敦。
  2. Your English is very good. Are you from Canada?
    你的英文很好，你是加拿大人嗎？
  3. My grandparents are from a small village in Wales.
    我爺爺奶奶來自威爾斯的一個小村子。

④ be + 地點

  1. The spare blankets are in the cupboard under the stairs.
    備用的毯子在樓梯下的櫃子裡。
  2. Sorry, I'm still on the train. I'll be ten minutes late.
    抱歉，我還在火車上，會晚十分鐘到。
  3. Mum isn't here. She's at work.
    媽媽不在，她在上班。
  4. Your passport is in the top drawer of my desk.
    你的護照在我書桌最上面的抽屜裡。

⑤ be + 時間或日期

  1. I can't come tonight because today is my birthday.
    我今晚不能去，因為今天是我生日。
  2. The school concert is on the last Friday of term.
    學校音樂會在學期最後一個星期五。
  3. My appointment is at half past ten.
    我的預約是十點半。

⑥ be + 年齡和天氣

  1. Our cat is nearly twenty, so she sleeps most of the day.
    我們的貓快二十歲了，所以她一天大部分時間都在睡。
  2. It's cold and windy today. Wear a hat.
    今天又冷又颳風，戴頂帽子。
  3. It was sunny all week in Spain.
    西班牙整個禮拜都是晴天。

⑦ 否定句

  1. I'm not tired. I want to watch the film.
    我不累，我想看電影。
  2. The water isn't warm enough for swimming yet.
    水還不夠暖，不能游泳。
  3. The children weren't very happy about the rain.
    孩子們對下雨不太開心。

⑧ 疑問句

  1. Is your flat near the university?
    你的公寓在大學附近嗎？
  2. Are you OK? You look pale.
    你還好嗎？你臉色好蒼白。
  3. Was the concert good last night? I couldn't go.
    昨晚的音樂會好聽嗎？我沒辦法去。
  4. How old is your brother?
    你哥哥幾歲？
  5. Who's that girl with your sister?
    跟你妹妹在一起的那個女生是誰？
  6. What's the name of that new café near the station?
    車站附近那家新咖啡店叫什麼名字？
