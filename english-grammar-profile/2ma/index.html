Wherever you go, you find shops, hotels and pubs which are full of tourists.

不管你走到哪裡，都會看到擠滿觀光客的商店、旅館和酒吧。

Wh⁠-⁠ever + 子⁠句, + 主⁠要⁠子⁠句

Wherever you go, you find shops, hotels and pubs which are full of tourists.

不管你走到哪裡，都會看到擠滿觀光客的商店、旅館和酒吧。

However + 形⁠容⁠詞或副⁠詞

However hard I try, I can't remember his name.

不管我多努力想，就是想不起他的名字。

每⁠個字⁠的意⁠思

Wherever you go, you find shops, hotels and pubs which are full of tourists.

whatever不管什麼
wherever不管哪裡
whoever不管誰
whenever不管何時
however不管多麼

whatever + 名⁠詞

有 be 動詞

Whatever the reason is, we must solve the problem.

不管原因是什麼，我們都得解決這個問題。
省略 be 動詞

Whatever the cause of the delay, we apologise.

不管延誤的原因是什麼，我們都很抱歉。

子⁠句⁠裡不⁠用 will

Whatever you will decide, I'll respect it.

Whatever you decide, I'll respect it.

no matter 和 whatever 不⁠混⁠用

No matter whatever you say, I won't change my mind.

Whatever you say, I won't change my mind.

however 後⁠面直⁠接接副⁠詞

However I try hard, I can't open this jar.

However hard I try, I can't open this jar.

用逗⁠號接主⁠要⁠子⁠句

Wherever you go, you find shops, hotels and pubs which are full of tourists.

Wherever I go. I take my camera with me.

Wherever I go, I take my camera with me.

例句

① whatever

  1. Whatever you decide, your parents will support you.
    不管你怎麼決定，你爸媽都會支持你。
  2. Whatever happens tomorrow, stay calm.
    不管明天發生什麼事，都要保持冷靜。
  3. Whatever she cooks, it always tastes good.
    不管她煮什麼，都很好吃。
  4. Whatever you buy, keep the receipt.
    不管買什麼，都要留著收據。
  5. Whatever the problem is, we can talk about it.
    不管有什麼問題，我們都可以談。

② wherever

  1. Wherever I travel, I always try the local food.
    不管我去哪裡旅行，都會嘗試當地的食物。
  2. Wherever you sit, you'll have a good view of the stage.
    不管你坐哪裡，都看得清楚舞台。
  3. Wherever we went in the city, there were crowds of tourists.
    我們在城裡不管走到哪裡，都是一群群的觀光客。
  4. Wherever she lives, she makes friends quickly.
    不管她住在哪裡，都很快交到朋友。

③ whoever

  1. Whoever wins the election, taxes will probably go up.
    不管誰贏得選舉，稅大概都會漲。
  2. Whoever we choose, they will need training.
    不管我們選誰，他們都需要受訓。
  3. Whoever finishes first, please wait for the others outside.
    不管誰先寫完，都請在外面等其他人。
  4. Whoever made this cake, it's delicious.
    不管這個蛋糕是誰做的，都很好吃。

④ whenever

  1. Whenever you're ready, we can start the meeting.
    你什麼時候準備好，我們就什麼時候開會。
  2. Whenever I hear this song, I think of my first holiday abroad.
    每次聽到這首歌，我都會想起第一次出國度假。
  3. Whenever he visits, he brings flowers for my mother.
    他每次來，都會帶花給我媽媽。
  4. Whenever the phone rings at night, I get worried.
    每次晚上電話響，我都會擔心。

⑤ however + 形容詞或副詞

  1. However hard the exam is, don't give up.
    不管考試多難，都不要放棄。
  2. However late you get home, send me a message.
    不管你多晚回到家，都傳個訊息給我。
  3. However much money he earns, he never seems happy.
    不管他賺多少錢，他好像都不快樂。
  4. However carefully I explain it, she still doesn't understand.
    不管我解釋得多仔細，她還是聽不懂。
  5. However cold it is, my grandfather swims in the sea every morning.
    不管天氣多冷，我爺爺每天早上都去海裡游泳。

⑥ whatever + 名詞

  1. Whatever the cause of the problem, we will fix it this week.
    不管問題的原因是什麼，我們這星期都會修好。
  2. Whatever the weather, the race will go ahead.
    不管天氣如何，比賽都會照常舉行。
  3. Whatever the result, I'm proud of you.
    不管結果如何，我都以你為榮。
  4. Whatever your age, it's never too late to learn a language.
    不管你幾歲，學語言都不嫌晚。

⑦ 正式信件和作文裡的句子

  1. Whatever the reason for the poor service, we recommend that you take action immediately.
    不管服務不好的原因是什麼，我們建議你們馬上採取行動。
  2. Whoever we appoint, he or she should be well qualified and flexible.
    不管我們聘用誰，那個人都應該條件好、能變通。
  3. Wherever you look in the old town, you see beautiful buildings.
    在舊城區不管往哪裡看，都看得到漂亮的建築。
  4. However difficult the situation becomes, we will continue to help our customers.
    不管情況變得多困難，我們都會繼續幫助顧客。
  5. Whenever you have a question, please contact our office.
    你有任何問題，隨時都可以聯絡我們辦公室。
