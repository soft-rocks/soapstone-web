句中副⁠詞放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

I've always loved seafood.

我一直都很愛吃海鮮。

說話者從小到現在都愛吃海鮮。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式常⁠用⁠的句中副⁠詞

I've always loved seafood.

頻率

I've never tried sushi.

我從來沒吃過壽司。
時間

We've looked at twenty houses. We've finally found one we like.

我們看了二十間房子。我們終於找到一間喜歡的了。
程度

I've nearly finished the report.

報告我快寫完了。
確定的程度

Look at the man in the red coat. I've definitely seen him before.

你看那個穿紅外套的男人。我確定以前見過他。
附加

I've also invited your sister.

我也邀了你妹妹。

否⁠定⁠句：probably、certainly 放⁠在 hasn't 前⁠面

I've always loved seafood.

hasn't 後面

My aunt moved to this town ten years ago. She hasn't always lived here.

我阿姨十年前搬來這個小鎮。她不是一直都住在這裡。
hasn't 前面

Don't mention the wedding to Tom yet. He probably hasn't heard the news.

先別跟湯姆提婚禮的事。他可能還沒聽到消息。

問⁠句⁠的副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

I've always loved seafood.

我表姊要在里約結婚。你去過巴西嗎？

副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在 have 前⁠面

I've always loved seafood.

I always have loved seafood.

I've always loved seafood.

nearly 放⁠在 has 和 finished 中⁠間

I've always loved seafood.

My daughter has finished nearly the Harry Potter book.

My daughter has nearly finished the Harry Potter book.

否⁠定⁠句⁠的 probably 放⁠在 hasn't 前⁠面

I've always loved seafood.

The manager hasn't probably seen your report yet.

The manager probably hasn't seen your report yet.

already 可⁠以放⁠在句⁠尾，always 不⁠行

I've always loved seafood.

我現在可以出去玩嗎？我功課已經寫完了。

現⁠在簡⁠單式⁠的句中副⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

I've always loved seafood.

我們去魚市場吧。我星期五都吃魚。

be 動⁠詞⁠的句中副⁠詞放⁠在 be 後⁠面

I've always loved seafood.

不用等露西。她總是遲到。

例句

① 頻率：always / never / often / sometimes

  1. They don't want to move to the city. My grandparents have always lived by the sea.
    他們不想搬到城市。我爺爺奶奶一直都住在海邊。
  2. I'd like to order the salmon roll. I've never tried sushi.
    我想點鮭魚捲。我從來沒吃過壽司。
  3. We're tired of the noise in the city. We've often talked about moving to the country.
    我們受夠了城市的噪音。我們常常聊到要搬去鄉下。
  4. You asked why the sky is blue. I've sometimes wondered about that too.
    你問天空為什麼是藍的。我有時候也想過這件事。
  5. Let's get my sister a puppy for her birthday. She's always wanted a dog.
    我們送我妹妹一隻小狗當生日禮物吧。她一直都想養狗。
  6. My uncle and aunt are big football fans. They've never missed a match.
    我叔叔和嬸嬸是超級足球迷。他們從來沒錯過一場比賽。

② 時間：already / just / recently / finally

  1. You're too late for the party. The guests have already left.
    你來參加派對太晚了。客人已經走了。
  2. Don't park under the tree. Dad has just washed the car.
    不要停在樹下。爸爸剛洗好車。
  3. I'm trying to get fit. I've recently joined a gym.
    我正在努力鍛鍊身體。我最近加入了一家健身房。
  4. We've looked at twenty houses. We've finally found one we like.
    我們看了二十間房子。我們終於找到一間喜歡的了。
  5. You don't need to cook. The kids have already eaten their dinner.
    你不用煮了。孩子們已經吃過晚餐了。
  6. My brother can drive us to the beach now. He has finally passed his driving test.
    我哥哥現在可以載我們去海邊了。他終於考過駕照了。

③ 程度：nearly / almost / hardly / really

  1. Give me ten more minutes. I've nearly finished the report.
    再給我十分鐘。報告我快寫完了。
  2. Can you go to the shop? We've almost run out of rice.
    你可以去店裡一趟嗎？我們的米快吃完了。
  3. My daughter has a cold. She's hardly eaten anything all day.
    我女兒感冒了。她一整天幾乎沒吃東西。
  4. Thank you for showing us around Taipei. I've really enjoyed this holiday.
    謝謝你帶我們逛台北。這次假期我真的玩得很開心。
  5. The same shops are still on the main street. The town has hardly changed since I was a child.
    大街上還是那幾家店。這個小鎮跟我小時候比幾乎沒變。

④ 確定的程度：probably / certainly / definitely

  1. Nobody has asked about the old plan. They've probably forgotten about it.
    沒有人問起舊的計畫。他們大概已經忘了這件事。
  2. My daughter got top marks in every subject. She has certainly worked hard this year.
    我女兒每一科都拿最高分。她今年的確很努力。
  3. Look at the man in the red coat. I've definitely seen him before.
    你看那個穿紅外套的男人。我確定以前見過他。
  4. The shop sent the parcel on Monday. It has probably arrived at the office.
    店家星期一寄出包裹。包裹大概已經送到辦公室了。

⑤ 附加：also

  1. There are twelve people coming to dinner now. I've also invited your sister.
    現在有十二個人要來吃晚餐。我也邀了你妹妹。
  2. The company has shops in London. It has also opened an office in Berlin.
    這家公司在倫敦有店。它也在柏林開了辦公室。

⑥ 否定句

  1. My boss and I work well together, but I haven't always agreed with him.
    我跟老闆合作得很好，可是我不是一直都同意他的看法。
  2. Our son wants to study abroad. We haven't really talked about it yet.
    我們兒子想出國念書。我們還沒有真正談過這件事。
  3. Kate hasn't replied to my text. She probably hasn't got the message.
    凱特還沒回我的簡訊。她大概還沒收到訊息。
  4. Ben left the race halfway. He certainly hasn't done his best.
    班比賽跑到一半就退出了。他確實沒有盡全力。

⑦ 問句

  1. You've lived in Los Angeles for years. Have you ever met a famous person?
    你在洛杉磯住了好幾年。你見過名人嗎？
  2. Our trip is next week. Has your mum already booked the hotel?
    我們的旅行在下禮拜。你媽媽已經訂好飯店了嗎？
  3. The house is quiet now. Have the builders finally gone home?
    屋子現在很安靜。工人終於回家了嗎？
  4. You got full marks again. Have you always been so good at maths?
    你又考了滿分。你數學一直都這麼好嗎？
