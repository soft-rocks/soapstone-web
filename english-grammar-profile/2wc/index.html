例句

① 集合名詞 + 單數動詞（一個整體）

  1. We loved the hotel. The staff is friendly and polite to guests.
    我們很喜歡那家飯店，員工對客人很友善，也很有禮貌。
  2. A TV company has decided to make a film at my high school.
    一家電視公司決定在我的高中拍一部片。
  3. Our team is top of the league this year.
    我們的球隊今年在聯賽排第一。
  4. The government is planning a new tax.
    政府在計畫一項新稅。
  5. The committee meets once a month.
    委員會每個月開一次會。
  6. My family is very big. I have ten cousins.
    我家族很大，我有十個表兄弟姊妹。

② 集合名詞 + 複數動詞（每一個人）

  1. After the spring break the whole staff have to start work at 7.15 am.
    春假過後，全體員工都得早上七點十五分開始上班。
  2. My family are all coming for Christmas.
    我的家人全都要來過聖誕節。
  3. The team are getting changed after the match.
    球隊的隊員比賽後在換衣服。
  4. The class are working on their projects.
    班上的同學在做各自的專題。
  5. The audience were clapping and cheering.
    觀眾在拍手歡呼。

③ police + 複數動詞

  1. The police are looking for a tall man in a black coat.
    警方在找一個穿黑色大衣的高個子男人。
  2. The police were very helpful when someone stole my bag.
    有人偷了我的包包時，警察幫了很多忙。
  3. Call the police! They know what to do.
    報警！他們知道該怎麼做。

④ 集合名詞 + 複數動詞 + they

  1. My family are great. They always help me.
    我的家人很棒，他們總是幫我。
  2. The crowd were singing, and they didn't want to go home.
    群眾在唱歌，他們都不想回家。
  3. The staff are very friendly, and they speak good English.
    員工都很友善，而且英文說得很好。

⑤ 集合名詞 + 單數動詞 + it

  1. The company is small, but it's growing fast.
    這家公司不大，但成長得很快。
  2. The team is new, so it needs time.
    這支隊伍是新組的，所以需要時間。
  3. Our class is the biggest in the school, and it has thirty-five students.
    我們班是全校最大的一班，有三十五個學生。

⑥ 集合名詞當受詞

  1. I'd like to thank the staff for their hard work.
    我想感謝員工的辛勞。
  2. She joined the team last year.
    她去年加入這支隊伍。
  3. The singer waved to the crowd.
    歌手向群眾揮手。
  4. We met his family at the wedding.
    我們在婚禮上見到他的家人。

⑦ 集合名詞在介系詞後面

  1. There are twenty students in my class.
    我們班有二十個學生。
  2. He works for a big company in the city.
    他在城裡一家大公司上班。
  3. Everyone in the audience stood up.
    所有觀眾都站了起來。
  4. I'm the youngest in my family.
    我是我家最小的。

⑧ 一個句子裡前後一致

  1. The jury is ready, and it will give its decision tomorrow.
    陪審團準備好了，明天會做出決定。
  2. The jury are still talking, and they can't agree.
    陪審員還在討論，他們無法達成共識。
  3. The orchestra is playing its last concert tonight.
    樂團今晚要舉行最後一場演出。
