限⁠定詞 + 修⁠飾⁠語 + one

I love blue, so I bought the blue one.

我很愛藍色，所以我買了藍色那一個。

例句

① a / an + 修飾語 + one

  1. My watch stopped working, so I bought a new one.
    我的手錶不走了，所以我買了一支新的。
  2. This chair is too hard. Have you got a softer one?
    這張椅子太硬了，有軟一點的嗎？
  3. My laptop is very slow. I need a faster one.
    我的筆電很慢，我需要快一點的。
  4. We don't need an expensive camera. A cheap one is fine.
    我們不需要很貴的相機，便宜的就可以了。
  5. The old fridge was too small, so we got a bigger one.
    舊冰箱太小了，所以我們換了一台大一點的。

② the + 修飾語 + one

  1. There are two jackets. I like the red one.
    有兩件外套，我喜歡紅色那件。
  2. Which cake do you want? The chocolate one?
    你要哪一個蛋糕？巧克力那個嗎？
  3. I prefer the second plan to the first one.
    比起第一個方案，我比較喜歡第二個。
  4. I tried both hats and bought the cheaper one.
    兩頂帽子我都試戴了，買了比較便宜的那頂。
  5. The first song was good, but the last one was even better.
    第一首歌不錯，最後一首更好聽。
  6. We looked at three flats and took the cheapest one.
    我們看了三間公寓，租了最便宜的那間。

③ 所有格 + 修飾語 + one

  1. I lost my old phone, so I'm using my new one.
    我的舊手機弄丟了，所以我在用新的。
  2. She sold her small car and bought a bigger one.
    她把小車賣了，買了一台大一點的。
  3. Can I borrow your black pen? My blue one has run out.
    我可以借你的黑筆嗎？我的藍筆沒水了。
  4. Our new teacher is nice, but I miss our old one.
    我們的新老師人很好，可是我想念以前那位。
  5. He forgot his big umbrella and took his little one.
    他忘了帶大傘，帶了那把小的。

④ the other one

  1. This key doesn't open the door. Try the other one.
    這把鑰匙開不了門，試試另一把。
  2. One shoe is under the bed. Where's the other one?
    一隻鞋在床底下，另一隻呢？
  3. I don't like this restaurant. Let's go to the other one.
    我不喜歡這家餐廳，我們去另一家吧。
  4. This printer is broken, but the other one works.
    這台印表機壞了，可是另一台可以用。
  5. I've read the first book. Can I borrow the other one?
    第一本我讀完了，可以借我另一本嗎？

⑤ favourite one

  1. I've got lots of T-shirts, but this is my favourite one.
    我有很多 T 恤，這件是我最喜歡的。
  2. Which film did you like? My favourite one was the funny one.
    你喜歡哪一部電影？我最喜歡那部好笑的。
  3. She has three cats, and the grey one is her favourite.
    她有三隻貓，灰色那隻是她最愛的。
  4. I've been to many beaches, but my favourite one is near my home.
    我去過很多海灘，我最喜歡的是家附近那一個。
  5. Of all his songs, his first one is still my favourite.
    他所有的歌裡，我最喜歡的還是第一首。

⑥ this / that + 修飾語 + one

  1. Do you want this round one or that square one?
    你要這個圓的還是那個方的？
  2. That wooden one by the door was my grandfather's.
    門邊那張木頭的是我爺爺的。
  3. Whose is this purple one with the stars on it?
    這個上面有星星的紫色的是誰的？
  4. Can I see that silver one, please?
    可以給我看看那個銀色的嗎？
  5. This little one is perfect for travelling.
    這個小的很適合旅行帶。
