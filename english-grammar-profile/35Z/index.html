What have you been doing?

你最近都在做什麼？

疑⁠問⁠詞加 have，再⁠接主⁠詞和 been 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

What have you been doing?

你剛剛都在做什麼？

室友回家，頭髮濕了，鞋子上都是泥巴。

是⁠非問⁠句用 Have / Has 開⁠頭，簡答用 have

What have you been doing?

問

I haven't seen you in this office before. Have you been working here long?

我以前沒在這間辦公室見過你。你在這裡工作很久了嗎？
答

No, I haven't.

沒有。

Who 當主⁠詞，不⁠移⁠動 has

What have you been doing?

電池沒電了。誰一直在用我的筆電？

不⁠用 do 造問⁠句

What have you been doing?

What do you have been doing in the garage?

What have you been doing in the garage?

have 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

What have you been doing?

How long your parents have been living in Spain?

How long have your parents been living in Spain?

been 不⁠能漏⁠掉

What have you been doing?

Have you waiting for me outside the cinema?

Have you been waiting for me outside the cinema?

簡答只⁠用 have

What have you been doing?

Have you been waiting long? — Yes, I have been waiting.

Have you been waiting long? — Yes, I have.

現⁠在完⁠成簡⁠單式問做⁠完⁠的事

What have you been doing?

一段時間的活動

You're covered in mud. What have you been doing?

你全身都是泥巴。你剛剛都在做什麼？
做完的事

The vase is broken. What have you done?

花瓶破了。你做了什麼好事？

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式問過⁠去⁠的時⁠間點

What have you been doing?

我打了三次電話給你。你昨天晚上十點在做什麼？

例句

① What 問句

  1. The house is still a mess. What have you been doing all day?
    家裡還是一團亂。你一整天都在做什麼？
  2. Mum and Dad look upset. What have they been talking about?
    爸媽看起來很不高興。他們剛剛在聊什麼？
  3. What has your sister been eating? She looks really ill.
    你妹妹最近都吃了什麼？她看起來很不舒服。
  4. What have you been doing to your hands? They're covered in paint.
    你的手怎麼了？上面都是油漆。
  5. Your brother looks at me strangely now. What has he been telling you about me?
    你哥哥現在看我的眼神很奇怪。他跟你說了我什麼？

② How long 問句

  1. You play the violin beautifully. How long have you been playing in the orchestra?
    你小提琴拉得真好。你在管弦樂團演奏多久了？
  2. The car is under a white blanket. How long has it been snowing?
    車子蓋上了一層白雪。雪下多久了？
  3. Your face is very pale. How long have you been feeling like this?
    你的臉色很蒼白。你這樣不舒服多久了？
  4. Your sister speaks good French now. How long has she been living in Canada?
    你姊姊現在法文說得很好。她住在加拿大多久了？
  5. How long have we been driving? I'm getting hungry.
    我們開多久了？我開始餓了。

③ Where / Who / Why 問句

  1. Where have you been hiding? I haven't seen you for days.
    你躲到哪裡去了？我好幾天沒看到你了。
  2. Our son left home in May. Where has he been staying since then?
    我們兒子五月離開家。他從那時候起都住在哪裡？
  3. You were laughing a lot. Who have you been chatting to on the phone?
    你剛剛笑得好開心。你剛剛在跟誰講電話？
  4. You never answer my texts. Why have you been avoiding me?
    你從來不回我的簡訊。你為什麼一直躲著我？
  5. Nobody slept last night. Why has the dog been barking all night?
    昨晚大家都沒睡。狗為什麼整個晚上都在叫？

④ Who 當主詞

  1. My screen has someone else's photos on it. Who has been using my computer?
    我的螢幕上有別人的照片。誰一直在用我的電腦？
  2. The box is half empty. Who's been eating my biscuits?
    盒子空了一半。是誰一直在吃我的餅乾？
  3. You were away for work. Who has been looking after the kids this week?
    你出差去了。這個禮拜是誰在照顧小孩？

⑤ 是非問句：Have + 主詞

  1. You seem very quiet since the interview. Have you been thinking about it a lot?
    面試以後你好像很安靜。你常常在想這件事嗎？
  2. You look so tired. Have you been getting enough sleep?
    你看起來好累。你最近睡得夠嗎？
  3. Have you been crying? Your eyes are red.
    你哭過嗎？你的眼睛紅紅的。
  4. The team looks tired. Have they been working on the project all week?
    團隊看起來很累。他們整個禮拜都在做這個專案嗎？
  5. Your cough is better. Have you been taking the tablets the doctor gave you?
    你的咳嗽好多了。醫生給你的藥你有在吃嗎？

⑥ 是非問句：Has + 主詞

  1. Your daughter's test is on Monday. Has she been studying for it?
    你女兒星期一要考試。她有在準備嗎？
  2. Has it been raining here? The ground is wet.
    這裡剛剛有下雨嗎？地上濕濕的。
  3. You're walking very slowly. Has your back been hurting again?
    你走得好慢。你的背又在痛了嗎？
  4. How is your grandfather? Has he been sleeping better since the operation?
    你爺爺還好嗎？他開完刀以後睡得比較好嗎？

⑦ 簡答

  1. You're out of breath. Have you been running? — Yes, I have.
    你喘不過氣。你剛剛去跑步了嗎？——對。
  2. Is your wife OK? Has she been working late? — No, she hasn't.
    你太太還好嗎？她最近常加班嗎？——沒有。
  3. Thanks for babysitting. Have the children been behaving? — Yes, they have.
    謝謝你幫忙帶小孩。孩子們有乖嗎？——有。
  4. It's very cold in your flat. Has the heating been working properly? — No, it hasn't.
    你的公寓好冷。暖氣一直都正常嗎？——不正常。
