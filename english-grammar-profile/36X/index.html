才兩個小時而已。

only 放⁠在 be 後⁠面，指⁠出數⁠量

才兩個小時而已。

only 和 just 放⁠在一⁠般動⁠詞前⁠面

It's only two hours.

just

I just want a glass of water.

我只想要一杯水。
only

She only drinks tea.

她只喝茶。

only 放⁠在哪⁠個部⁠分前⁠面，就指⁠出哪⁠個部⁠分

It's only two hours.

指出主詞

Only I saw her.

只有我看到她。
指出受詞

I saw only her.

我只看到她。

指⁠出主⁠詞用 only，不⁠用 alone

Alone my sister knows the answer.

Only my sister knows the answer.

有助⁠動⁠詞⁠時，only 放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

It's only two hours.

I only can stay a few minutes.

I can only stay a few minutes.

just 放⁠在數⁠量前⁠面

The tickets were £8 just.

The tickets were just £8.

only 和 just 意⁠思相⁠同，一⁠句只⁠用一⁠個

It's only two hours.

It's just only a short walk.

It's just a short walk.

just 放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間，表⁠示剛⁠剛

我剛吃過午餐。

例句

① be + only + 數量或名詞片語

  1. The station is only five minutes away.
    車站只有五分鐘的路程。
  2. The tickets were only ten pounds each.
    票一張才十英鎊。
  3. She's only six, so she can't stay up late.
    她才六歲，所以不能熬夜。
  4. Don't worry, it's only a small cut.
    別擔心，只是個小傷口。
  5. The button is only a little loose.
    釦子只是有點鬆。
  6. Was the film only an hour long?
    那部電影只有一個小時嗎？

② be + just + 數量或名詞片語

  1. The jacket was just £20 in the sale.
    那件夾克特價才二十英鎊。
  2. It's just a short walk to the beach.
    走到海灘只有一小段路。
  3. We're just friends.
    我們只是朋友。
  4. It was just a joke!
    只是開玩笑啦！
  5. Is it just me, or is it cold in here?
    是我的錯覺，還是這裡真的很冷？
  6. It wasn't just the money. I didn't like the job.
    不只是錢的問題。我不喜歡那份工作。

③ only / just + 一般動詞

  1. I only drink coffee in the morning.
    我只有早上喝咖啡。
  2. He only reads the news on his phone.
    他只用手機看新聞。
  3. She only speaks a little Japanese.
    她只會說一點日文。
  4. It only took ten minutes to mend the chair.
    修那張椅子只花了十分鐘。
  5. We just want to talk to you.
    我們只是想跟你談談。
  6. I just need one more minute.
    我只需要再一分鐘。
  7. They just wanted to help.
    他們只是想幫忙。
  8. I just want a plain T-shirt.
    我只想要一件素面的T恤。

④ 助動詞 + only / just + 一般動詞

  1. I can only stay for an hour.
    我只能待一個小時。
  2. We could only see a small part of the lake from our room.
    從我們的房間只看得到湖的一小部分。
  3. The shop will only take cash.
    這家店只收現金。
  4. You can just leave the keys on the table.
    你把鑰匙放在桌上就好。
  5. Can I just ask one question?
    我問一個問題就好，可以嗎？

⑤ only / just + 主詞

  1. Only my mother knows the password.
    只有我媽媽知道密碼。
  2. Only two students passed the test.
    只有兩個學生考試及格。
  3. Only children under twelve can play here.
    只有十二歲以下的兒童可以在這裡玩。
  4. Just one person called me back.
    只有一個人回我電話。
  5. Just a few people came to the meeting.
    只有幾個人來開會。

⑥ only / just + 受詞

  1. I had just one cup of tea.
    我只喝了一杯茶。
  2. He invited only a few friends to his party.
    他只請了幾個朋友來他的派對。
  3. We have only one bathroom.
    我們只有一間浴室。
  4. She packed just one small bag for the trip.
    她這趟旅行只帶了一個小包包。
