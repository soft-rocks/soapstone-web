Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

我們公司已經跟幾家德國公司簽了合約。

肯⁠定⁠句是主⁠詞加 has，再⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

我們公司已經跟幾家德國公司簽了合約。

說話者在年度會議上報告今年的成果。

代⁠名⁠詞後⁠面⁠的 have / has 常縮⁠寫

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

I have → I've

I can't get into the house. I've lost my keys.

我進不了家門。我把鑰匙弄丟了。
she has → she's

My sister won't be at Christmas dinner. She's moved to Italy.

我姊姊不會來吃聖誕晚餐。她搬去義大利了。
it has → it's

I got a new job and found a flat. It's been an amazing week.

我找到新工作，也找到了公寓。這個禮拜太棒了。

不⁠規⁠則動⁠詞⁠的過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

和過去式一樣buy – bought – bought
加 -n 或 -entake – taken、write – written
母音改變swim – swum、begin – begun

has 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

Mum has took the train to Manchester.

Mum has taken the train to Manchester.

複⁠數名⁠詞主⁠詞用 have

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

The nurses has asked for more pay.

The nurses have asked for more pay.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

I have finish the sales report for June.

I have finished the sales report for June.

說⁠出過⁠去⁠的時⁠間，用過⁠去簡⁠單式

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

今年生意很好。我們公司三月跟一家德國公司簽了合約。

she's 加形⁠容⁠詞⁠時，'s 是 is

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

讓你媽媽睡吧。她累了。

現⁠在完⁠成進⁠行⁠式說持⁠續到現⁠在⁠的動⁠作

Our company has signed deals with a few German companies.

我們很了解柏林的團隊。我們跟他們合作兩年了。

例句

① 代名詞主詞：I / we / you / they + have

  1. Good news about the move. I've found a cheaper flat.
    搬家有好消息。我找到一間比較便宜的公寓了。
  2. Come and see the house. We've painted the living room green.
    來看看房子。我們把客廳漆成綠色了。
  3. You sent the email to the whole office. You've made a big mistake.
    你把那封信寄給了全辦公室。你犯了一個大錯。
  4. The bakery is doing well. They've opened a new branch in Leeds.
    那家麵包店生意很好。他們在里茲開了一家分店。
  5. The noise from the building site is terrible. I've written to the manager.
    工地的噪音很可怕。我寫信給經理了。
  6. Thank you all for your help. We've raised enough money for a new church roof.
    謝謝大家的幫忙。我們募到夠買教堂新屋頂的錢了。

② 代名詞主詞：he / she / it + has

  1. You won't know my brother. He's grown a beard.
    你會認不出我哥哥。他留了鬍子。
  2. Listen to my daughter. She's learned to play the guitar.
    你聽我女兒彈。她學會彈吉他了。
  3. I need a new printer. It's stopped working again.
    我需要一台新的印表機。這台又不動了。
  4. I went to get a drink, and now he's taken my seat.
    我去拿飲料，結果他坐了我的位子。
  5. My aunt is a strong swimmer. She has swum across the lake.
    我阿姨很會游泳。她游過了那座湖。

③ 名詞主詞：單數 + has

  1. Mum is very proud. My sister has started a new job at a bank.
    媽媽很驕傲。我姊姊開始在一家銀行做新工作了。
  2. Driving to work is expensive now. The price of petrol has gone up.
    現在開車上班很貴。汽油的價格漲了。
  3. Get your tickets now. Our team has reached the final.
    現在就去買票。我們隊打進決賽了。
  4. Let's go to the cinema tonight. The new Paddington film has won three awards.
    我們今晚去看電影吧。新的柏靈頓熊電影得了三個獎。
  5. Come and look at the fridge. My daughter has drawn a picture of our cat.
    來看看冰箱。我女兒畫了一張我們家貓的畫。

④ 名詞主詞：複數 + have

  1. The teacher can mark the work now. The students have handed in their projects.
    老師現在可以改作業了。學生們交了他們的作業。
  2. The dog can't get into our garden now. My neighbours have built a new fence.
    狗現在進不了我們的花園了。我的鄰居蓋了一道新的圍籬。
  3. Autumn is here. The leaves have turned yellow.
    秋天到了。葉子都變黃了。
  4. Our shop is doing well online. Our customers have given us good reviews.
    我們的店在網路上做得很好。我們的客戶給了我們很好的評價。
  5. Drive carefully on Hill Street. The workers have dug a hole in the road.
    在山丘街開車要小心。工人在路上挖了一個洞。

⑤ 更多的不規則動詞

  1. Sorry, there's none left for your coffee. I've drunk all the milk.
    抱歉，沒有牛奶可以加你的咖啡了。我把牛奶都喝光了。
  2. Please turn off your phone. The concert has begun.
    請把手機關機。音樂會開始了。
  3. I can't log in to my email. I've forgotten my password.
    我登不進電子郵件。我忘了密碼。
  4. My friend takes the bus now. She's sold her old bike.
    我朋友現在搭公車。她把舊腳踏車賣掉了。
  5. My son and I can't stop sneezing. We've both caught a cold.
    我和我兒子一直打噴嚏。我們兩個都感冒了。
  6. The players had a vote this morning. They've chosen a new captain.
    球員今天早上投票。他們選出了新隊長。

⑥ 結果到現在還在

  1. You can visit them in the summer. My parents have bought a flat by the sea.
    你夏天可以去找他們。我爸媽在海邊買了一間公寓。
  2. I can't pay with my card. The bank has closed my account.
    我不能刷卡。銀行把我的帳戶關了。
  3. The jar is empty. The kids have eaten all the biscuits.
    罐子空了。孩子們把餅乾都吃光了。
