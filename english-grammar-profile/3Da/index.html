That's something that makes me angry!

這就是會讓我生氣的事！

形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面，說受⁠詞變⁠成什⁠麼樣

That's something that makes me angry!

很吵的音樂讓我覺得很累。

受⁠詞也⁠可⁠以是東⁠西

That's something that makes me angry!

雨讓路面變濕了。

make 後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

That's something that makes me angry!

Your phone call made I very happy.

Your phone call made me very happy.

說⁠明受⁠詞⁠的狀⁠態用形⁠容⁠詞

That's something that makes me angry!

Her smile makes me happily.

Her smile makes me happy.

make 加受⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接形⁠容⁠詞

That's something that makes me angry!

The long walk made us to be tired.

The long walk made us tired.

例句

① make + 人 + 感覺

  1. Waiting in long lines makes me angry.
    排長隊讓我很生氣。
  2. Long car trips make me sleepy.
    長途坐車讓我想睡。
  3. Our old neighbour died last week. The news made my parents very sad.
    我們以前的鄰居上星期過世了。這個消息讓我爸媽很難過。
  4. Exams always make me nervous.
    考試總是讓我很緊張。
  5. Her funny emails make me happy.
    她寫的好笑的電子郵件讓我很開心。
  6. The smell of bread makes me hungry.
    麵包的味道讓我肚子餓。
  7. You always sleep with the light on. Does the dark make you afraid?
    你睡覺總是開著燈。黑暗會讓你害怕嗎？

② make + 人 + 狀態

  1. The hike up the hill made us hungry.
    爬上山讓我們肚子都餓了。
  2. My dad only drinks one cup a day. Too much coffee makes him sick.
    我爸一天只喝一杯。喝太多咖啡會讓他不舒服。
  3. Nobody knew the writer before 2019. Winning the prize made her famous.
    二〇一九年以前沒有人認識那位作家。得獎讓她出了名。
  4. We played football at noon in July. The heat made everyone thirsty.
    我們七月中午踢足球。天氣熱得大家都很口渴。
  5. Don't drive after you take this. The medicine will make you sleepy.
    吃了這個以後別開車。這個藥會讓你想睡。
  6. My grandfather started with one small shop. Hard work made him rich.
    我爺爺一開始只有一間小店。努力工作讓他變得很有錢。

③ make + 東西 + 形容詞

  1. We couldn't swim the next day. The storm made the river brown.
    隔天我們不能游泳。暴風雨讓河水變成褐色。
  2. A little sugar makes the sauce sweet.
    加一點糖會讓醬汁變甜。
  3. We painted my bedroom yellow. The new paint made the room bright.
    我們把我的臥室漆成黃色。新的油漆讓房間變得很明亮。
  4. Too much salt will make the soup salty.
    放太多鹽會讓湯變得很鹹。
  5. We parked on the street all afternoon. The sun made the car really hot.
    我們整個下午把車停在街上。太陽把車子曬得很燙。
  6. Our manager bought some plants for the office. They make it more pleasant.
    我們經理買了一些植物放在辦公室。它們讓辦公室舒服多了。

④ try to make / want to make

  1. I'm moving to another city, and I don't want to make you sad.
    我要搬到別的城市了，我不想讓你難過。
  2. I just want to make my customers happy.
    我只想讓我的客人開心。
  3. Grandma came to stay with us. We tried to make the spare room cozy for her.
    奶奶來我們家住。我們想把客房弄得溫馨一點給她住。
  4. Dad is tired after work. Try not to make him angry.
    爸爸下班以後很累。盡量別惹他生氣。
  5. My cousin studies every night. She wants to make her parents proud.
    我表妹每天晚上都在讀書。她想讓她爸媽以她為榮。

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. You're singing in the kitchen. What makes you so happy today?
    你在廚房裡唱歌。今天什麼事讓你這麼開心？
  2. The ending of the film made me really emotional.
    電影的結局讓我很感動。
  3. There was a loud bang outside last night. Did the noise make you nervous?
    昨天晚上外面有一聲巨響。那個聲音讓你緊張嗎？
  4. My brother and I travelled around Japan for a month. The trip has made us closer.
    我和我哥哥在日本旅行了一個月。這趟旅行讓我們更親近了。
  5. That song always makes me sleepy.
    那首歌總是讓我想睡。
  6. They're building a road from the city to our town. Will the new road make the town busier?
    他們在蓋一條從市區到我們鎮上的路。新的道路會讓鎮上更熱鬧嗎？
