I had always wanted to visit European cities, and Paris was one of the easiest to get to.

我剛放學回到家，就看到那封信。

主⁠詞 + had + 副⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

never、ever、just

never從來沒有。
ever曾經。常用在最高級和問句。
just剛剛。
always一直。

the + 最⁠高⁠級 + I had ever + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

例句

It was the most beautiful one I had ever seen.

那是我看過最美的一個。

說過去某個時候看過或做過最好的東西。

had just + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

例句

I had just arrived home when I saw the letter.

我剛到家，就看到那封信。

had just 說主要的事發生之前，剛剛才做完的事。

說過⁠去某⁠時之⁠前⁠的經⁠驗，用 had never 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Before that winter, I did never see snow.

Before that winter, I had never seen snow.

had 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 seen

I had always wanted to visit European cities, and Paris was one of the easiest to get to.

It was the best film I had ever saw.

It was the best film I had ever seen.

already 只⁠用一⁠次

They had already left already.

They had already left.

例句

① 主詞 + had always + 過去分詞：一直想要

  1. During my year in England, I visited Paris. I had always wanted to visit European cities, and Paris was one of the easiest to get to.
    我在英國的那一年去了巴黎。我一直很想去歐洲的城市，而巴黎是最容易去的城市之一。
  2. She had always dreamed of living by the sea.
    她一直夢想著住在海邊。
  3. He had always loved music, so he became a teacher.
    他一直很喜歡音樂，所以他當了老師。
  4. We had always hoped to visit Japan one day.
    我們一直希望有一天能去日本。
  5. My grandfather had always kept a diary.
    我爺爺一直有寫日記的習慣。
  6. They had always lived in the same village.
    他們一直住在同一個村子。

② the + 最高級 + 主詞 + had ever + 過去分詞

  1. My aunt brought me a dress from Paris. It was the most beautiful one I had ever seen.
    我阿姨從巴黎帶了一件洋裝給我。那是我看過最漂亮的一件。
  2. It was the best meal we had ever eaten.
    那是我們吃過最好吃的一餐。
  3. That was the funniest film he had ever watched.
    那是他看過最好笑的電影。
  4. It was the hardest exam I had ever taken.
    那是我考過最難的試。
  5. She was the kindest person I had ever met.
    她是我遇過最善良的人。
  6. It was the coldest winter they had ever known.
    那是他們經歷過最冷的冬天。

③ 主詞 + had never + 過去分詞 + before：以前從來沒有

  1. She came to America to see a relative she had never met before.
    她來美國看一個她以前從來沒見過的親戚。
  2. I had never tried Indian food before that evening.
    那天晚上之前，我從來沒吃過印度菜。
  3. We had never seen such a big crowd before.
    我們以前從來沒看過那麼多人。
  4. He had never been on a plane before the school trip.
    那次校外教學之前，他從來沒坐過飛機。
  5. They had never heard that song before.
    他們以前從來沒聽過那首歌。
  6. My dog had never seen snow before that winter.
    那年冬天之前，我的狗從來沒看過雪。

④ 主詞 + had just + 過去分詞 + when + 主詞 + 過去式

  1. I had just arrived home from school when I saw the letter on the table.
    我剛放學回到家，就看到桌上的那封信。
  2. We had just sat down to dinner when the phone rang.
    我們剛坐下來吃晚餐，電話就響了。
  3. She had just finished her essay when the computer crashed.
    她剛寫完作文，電腦就當機了。
  4. They had just left the house when it started to rain.
    他們剛出門，就開始下雨了。
  5. He had just fallen asleep when the baby woke up.
    他剛睡著，寶寶就醒了。
  6. I had just washed the car when a bird landed on it.
    我剛洗完車，就有一隻鳥停在上面。

⑤ 主詞 + had already + 過去分詞 + when / by the time + 子句

  1. The film had already started when we found our seats.
    我們找到位子的時候，電影已經開始了。
  2. She had already eaten when I invited her to lunch.
    我邀她吃午餐的時候，她已經吃過了。
  3. The shop had already closed by the time we got there.
    我們到的時候，那家店已經關了。
  4. I had already bought a ticket when I heard about the strike.
    我聽說有罷工的時候，已經買了票。
  5. They had already sold the house when we called.
    我們打電話的時候，他們已經把房子賣掉了。
  6. He had already left for work when the parcel arrived.
    包裹到的時候，他已經去上班了。
  7. We had already decided to go when the weather changed.
    天氣變了的時候，我們已經決定要去了。
