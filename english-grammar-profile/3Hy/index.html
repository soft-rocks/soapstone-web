例句

① 數字 + 名詞s

  1. You have to walk just five minutes down the road.
    你只要沿著這條路走五分鐘就到了。
  2. I have two brothers and three sisters.
    我有兩個哥哥和三個姊姊。
  3. We need four chairs for the kitchen.
    我們廚房需要四張椅子。
  4. The bus comes every ten minutes.
    公車每十分鐘來一班。
  5. My dad works six days a week.
    我爸爸一個禮拜工作六天。

② some / a lot of / many + 名詞s

  1. My favourite season is summer, because there are a lot of flowers and nice trees.
    我最喜歡的季節是夏天，因為有很多花和漂亮的樹。
  2. I've got some friends in London.
    我在倫敦有幾個朋友。
  3. There are many shops near the station.
    車站附近有很多商店。
  4. Grandma brought some biscuits for the children.
    奶奶帶了一些餅乾給孩子們。

③ these / those + 名詞s

  1. Who left these bags in the hall? I nearly fell over them.
    是誰把這些袋子放在走廊的？我差點被絆倒。
  2. Are those books yours?
    那些書是你的嗎？
  3. These cakes are delicious. Did you make them?
    這些蛋糕好好吃，是你做的嗎？

④ 說一般的人或東西

  1. I like cats, but my husband likes dogs.
    我喜歡貓，可是我先生喜歡狗。
  2. Doctors and nurses often work at weekends.
    醫生和護理師常常週末也要上班。
  3. We play computer games every Friday.
    我們每個星期五都玩電腦遊戲。
  4. Horses are beautiful animals, but I'm scared of them.
    馬是很漂亮的動物，可是我很怕牠們。

⑤ 複數名詞當主詞

  1. My friends are from Poland, Hungary and Morocco.
    我的朋友來自波蘭、匈牙利和摩洛哥。
  2. In my town the banks close at four, so I go at lunchtime.
    我們鎮上的銀行四點就關門，所以我午休時間去。
  3. My parents live in a small village.
    我爸媽住在一個小村子。
  4. The students are in the library.
    學生們在圖書館。

⑥ 名詞s 當受詞

  1. I bought two shirts and some socks for my trip.
    我為了旅行買了兩件襯衫和幾雙襪子。
  2. She writes letters to her grandma.
    她寫信給她奶奶。
  3. Do you eat eggs for breakfast?
    你早餐吃蛋嗎？
  4. We visited three museums in one day.
    我們一天逛了三間博物館。

⑦ 時間和數量

  1. I'm going on holiday for two weeks.
    我要去度假兩個禮拜。
  2. The film is two hours long.
    這部電影長兩個小時。
  3. My grandma is eighty years old.
    我奶奶八十歲了。
  4. It's three kilometres to the beach.
    到海灘有三公里。

⑧ a 和數字一後面用單數

  1. I have one sister and two brothers.
    我有一個姊姊和兩個哥哥。
  2. There's a cat in the garden and two birds on the roof.
    院子裡有一隻貓，屋頂上有兩隻鳥。
  3. Can I have one coffee and two teas, please?
    請給我一杯咖啡和兩杯茶。
