Ought we not to learn from the mistakes of the past?

我們難道不該從過去的錯誤中學習嗎？

Ought + 主⁠詞 + not to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞？

Ought … not to、Oughtn't … to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Ought … not to難道不應該……嗎？很正式。
Oughtn't … to縮寫的形式，比較口語。
原形動詞說話者認為應該做的事。

反⁠問⁠的意⁠思

例句

Half of the old forest has gone in twenty years. Ought we not to protect the rest?

老森林二十年內消失了一半。我們難道不應該保護剩下的部分嗎？（意思是：我們應該保護）

這種問句通常不需要回答。說話者用問句提出自己的看法。

not 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面、to 前⁠面

Ought we not to learn from the mistakes of the past?

The same bridge has failed twice. Ought we to not learn from this?

The same bridge has failed twice. Ought we not to learn from this?

ought 的問⁠句把 ought 放⁠在句⁠首，不⁠用 do

Ought we not to learn from the mistakes of the past?

Our neighbours lost everything in the fire. Do we not ought to help them?

Our neighbours lost everything in the fire. Ought we not to help them?

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Ought we not to learn from the mistakes of the past?

The experts warned us about the floods. Ought we not to listening to them?

The experts warned us about the floods. Ought we not to listen to them?

例句

① Ought we not to + 原形動詞？：演講和議論文

  1. The river flooded this area fifty years ago. Ought we not to learn from the mistakes of the past?
    這條河五十年前淹過這一區。我們難道不應該從過去的錯誤中學習嗎？
  2. Only a few old woods are left in this country. Ought we not to protect the last of our forests?
    這個國家只剩幾片老樹林了。我們難道不應該保護我們最後的森林嗎？
  3. The storm destroyed two hundred houses on the coast. Ought we not to help the people who have lost their homes?
    暴風雨毀了海邊兩百棟房子。我們難道不應該幫助失去家園的人嗎？
  4. Thousands of students marched through the city today. Ought we not to listen to what young people are saying?
    今天有好幾千名學生在市區遊行。我們難道不應該聽聽年輕人在說什麼嗎？
  5. Our schools have leaking roofs and old books. Ought we not to spend more on schools than on roads?
    我們的學校屋頂漏水，書本又舊。我們難道不應該在學校上比在道路上花更多錢嗎？
  6. The new factory would pollute the river for years. Ought we not to think about our children's future?
    新工廠會污染河流好多年。我們難道不應該想想我們孩子的未來嗎？

② Ought + 名詞 + not to + 原形動詞？：質疑別人的做法

  1. Many pensioners can't heat their homes this winter. Ought the government not to do more for old people?
    很多領退休金的人這個冬天付不起暖氣。政府難道不應該為老人做更多事嗎？
  2. The workers only heard about the factory closing on the news. Ought the company not to tell its workers the truth first?
    工人是從新聞上才知道工廠要關。公司難道不應該先告訴員工真相嗎？
  3. Some countries have plenty of food while others are starving. Ought rich countries not to help poorer ones?
    有些國家食物很多，有些國家卻在挨餓。富裕的國家難道不應該幫助比較窮的國家嗎？
  4. The council has decided to close our park without a meeting. Ought the council not to ask local people first?
    議會沒開會就決定關閉我們的公園。市議會難道不應該先問當地人嗎？
  5. Many young people get into debt in their first year of work. Ought schools not to teach children how to manage money?
    很多年輕人工作第一年就欠債。學校難道不應該教孩子怎麼管錢嗎？
  6. My father had an operation and nobody told him about the side effects. Ought doctors not to explain the risks to patients?
    我爸爸開了刀，卻沒人告訴他有什麼副作用。醫生難道不應該向病人解釋風險嗎？

③ Ought I not to + 原形動詞？：很正式地問自己的責任

  1. I saw the man who broke the shop window. Ought I not to tell the police what I saw?
    我看到打破商店櫥窗的人了。我難道不應該把我看到的告訴警察嗎？
  2. We both said unkind things in the argument. Ought I not to apologise to her first?
    我們吵架時都說了難聽的話。我難道不應該先跟她道歉嗎？
  3. The client wants a new deadline from me today. Ought I not to ask my manager before I agree?
    客戶今天要我給新的截止日期。我答應之前，難道不應該先問我的經理嗎？
  4. My uncle has been in hospital for two weeks. Ought I not to visit him while he's there?
    我叔叔住院兩個星期了。他住院的時候，我難道不應該去看他嗎？
  5. I found a wallet with two hundred pounds in it. Ought I not to return the money?
    我撿到一個裝了兩百英鎊的皮夾。我難道不應該把錢還回去嗎？
  6. My editor wants the story by tonight. Ought I not to check the facts before I write the article?
    我的編輯今晚就要稿子。我寫文章之前，難道不應該先查證事實嗎？

④ Oughtn't + 主詞 + to + 原形動詞？：比較口語

  1. Oughtn't we to leave soon? It's getting dark.
    我們難道不該快點走嗎？天快黑了。
  2. It's Monday morning. Oughtn't you to be at the office by now?
    今天是星期一早上。你現在難道不該在辦公室了嗎？
  3. Their train got in at two, and it's now four. Oughtn't they to have arrived by now?
    他們的火車兩點到站，現在已經四點了。他們現在難道不該已經到了嗎？
  4. Grandma wants to go back to work next week. Oughtn't she to rest after the operation?
    奶奶下星期就想回去上班。她手術後難道不該休息嗎？
  5. We're going to the lake with Leo. Oughtn't we to tell Mum where we're going?
    我們要跟 Leo 去湖邊。我們難道不該告訴媽媽我們要去哪裡嗎？
  6. It's half past nine on a school night. Oughtn't the children to be in bed by now?
    明天要上學，現在已經九點半了。孩子們現在難道不該在床上了嗎？

⑤ If …, ought we not to + 原形動詞？：先說條件，再反問

  1. If the plan failed before, ought we not to try something new?
    如果這個計畫以前失敗過，我們難道不應該試試新的方法嗎？
  2. If prices keep rising, ought we not to look for a cheaper supplier?
    如果價格一直上漲，我們難道不應該找便宜一點的供應商嗎？
  3. If the bridge is unsafe, ought we not to close it?
    如果那座橋不安全，我們難道不應該封閉它嗎？
  4. If people are unhappy, ought we not to ask them why?
    如果大家不快樂，我們難道不應該問問他們為什麼嗎？
  5. If the climate is changing, ought we not to change our habits?
    如果氣候在改變，我們難道不應該改變我們的習慣嗎？
  6. If nobody uses the old library, ought we not to find a new use for it?
    如果沒有人用那間舊圖書館，我們難道不應該為它找新的用途嗎？
  7. If the children are bored, ought we not to change the lessons?
    如果孩子們覺得無聊，我們難道不應該改變課程嗎？
