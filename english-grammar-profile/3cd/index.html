例句

① apologise

  1. I was ill yesterday. I apologize for missing your class.
    我昨天生病了。很抱歉我沒去上你的課。
  2. The train is ten minutes late. We apologise for the delay.
    火車誤點十分鐘。很抱歉造成延誤。
  3. I apologise for the mess. We're painting the kitchen.
    抱歉家裡很亂。我們在油漆廚房。
  4. I was tired and angry. I apologise for my rude words last night.
    我那時候又累又生氣。我為我昨晚無禮的話道歉。
  5. The road is closed until Friday. We apologise for any trouble this may cause.
    這條路封到星期五。如果造成任何不便，我們深感抱歉。

② suggest

  1. If you travel, I suggest that you pay for your own trip.
    如果你要旅行，我建議你自己出旅費。
  2. The roads are busy. I suggest taking the train.
    路上車很多。我建議搭火車。
  3. The concert starts at ten. I suggest we meet at the station at nine.
    音樂會十點開始。我建議我們九點在車站碰面。
  4. It's late. I suggest that we finish this tomorrow.
    很晚了。我建議我們明天再做完。
  5. Keep a bottle on your desk. Doctors suggest drinking at least two litres of water a day.
    在桌上放一瓶水。醫生建議每天至少喝兩公升的水。
  6. What do you suggest? Should we stay or go?
    你有什麼建議？我們該留下還是走？

③ recommend

  1. For these reasons, I definitely recommend staying in a city.
    因為這些原因，我非常推薦住在城市裡。
  2. Have you been to Kyoto? I recommend the old temples.
    你去過京都嗎？我推薦那些古老的寺廟。
  3. I recommend that you book a table. It gets very busy.
    我建議你先訂位。那裡人很多。
  4. Don't skip the evening. My dentist recommends brushing twice a day.
    晚上別偷懶。我的牙醫建議一天刷兩次牙。
  5. It's our first time here. Which dish do you recommend?
    我們第一次來。你推薦哪一道菜？

④ promise

  1. I promise I'll call you as soon as I land.
    我保證飛機一落地就打給你。
  2. Your secret is safe with me. I promise not to tell anyone.
    你的祕密我會守住。我保證不告訴任何人。
  3. Thank you for shopping with us. We promise to send your order within three days.
    感謝你在本店購物。我們保證三天內寄出你的訂單。
  4. Sorry I'm late again. I promise this is the last time.
    抱歉我又遲到了。我保證這是最後一次。

⑤ thank

  1. Welcome to the Sea View. We thank you for choosing our hotel.
    歡迎光臨海景飯店。我們感謝您選擇我們的飯店。
  2. Before we eat, I thank everyone who helped with the party.
    吃飯之前，我感謝每一個幫忙辦派對的人。
  3. Dear Mrs Brown, I thank you for your kind letter.
    親愛的 Brown 太太，我感謝您親切的來信。

⑥ 其他言語行為動詞

  1. It looks like rain. I suggest you take an umbrella.
    看起來要下雨了。我建議你帶把傘。
  2. Their pizza is the best. I recommend this place to all my friends.
    他們的披薩最好吃。我跟所有朋友推薦這家店。
  3. You were right about the road. I admit I was wrong.
    你對那條路的判斷是對的。我承認我錯了。
  4. You said sorry, and I forgive you. Let's forget about it.
    你道歉了，我原諒你。這件事就算了吧。
  5. You helped me so much. I insist. Please take the money.
    你幫了我那麼多。我堅持。請收下這筆錢。
  6. I have some news, everyone. I invite you all to my wedding in June.
    大家，我有個消息。我邀請大家來參加我六月的婚禮。
  7. Don't jump in yet. I warn you, the water is very cold.
    先別跳下去。我先警告你，水很冷。
