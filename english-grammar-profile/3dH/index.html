I miss you a lot.

我很想你。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面

我很想你。

各⁠個程⁠度副⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

I miss you a lot.

a lot、very much很，非常
so much好，非常，說話者的感覺很強
too much太多，超過適當的程度
a bit、a little一點點
at all一點也不，只用在否定句和疑問句

too much 是太⁠多，句⁠尾⁠的 too 是也

too much

You work too much.

你工作太多了。
too

You work too.

你也有工作。

否⁠定⁠句和疑⁠問⁠句⁠的 at all

否定句

I didn't sleep at all last night.

我昨晚完全沒睡。
疑問句

Did you enjoy the film at all?

你到底喜不喜歡那部電影？

程⁠度副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I miss you a lot.

I changed a bit my plans.

I changed my plans a bit.

at all 放⁠在句⁠尾

I don't at all understand this question.

I don't understand this question at all.

肯⁠定⁠句不⁠用 at all

I enjoyed the trip at all.

I enjoyed the trip a lot.

表⁠示太⁠多用 too much

You worry too. Everything will be fine.

You worry too much. Everything will be fine.

例句

① 動詞 + a lot

  1. It rains a lot in Scotland.
    蘇格蘭常下雨。
  2. My little brother talks a lot at dinner.
    我弟弟吃晚餐時話很多。
  3. I travelled a lot when I was younger.
    我年輕的時候常常旅行。
  4. My cousins and I argued a lot when we were children.
    我和表兄弟姊妹小時候常吵架。

② 動詞 + 受詞 + a lot、very much

  1. My parents help me a lot with my homework.
    我爸媽幫我做功課幫很多。
  2. Thank you very much for looking after our cat in August.
    非常謝謝你八月幫我們照顧貓。
  3. She likes her new teacher very much.
    她很喜歡她的新老師。
  4. I enjoyed your party a lot.
    我在你的派對上玩得很開心。
  5. The whole class respected Mr Hill very much.
    全班都很敬重 Hill 老師。

③ so much

  1. My daughter loves her swimming lessons so much!
    我女兒好愛她的游泳課！
  2. Thanks so much for driving me to the airport.
    非常感謝你開車送我去機場。
  3. The kids missed their dad so much while he was away.
    爸爸不在的時候，孩子們好想他。
  4. My knee hurt so much that I couldn't walk.
    我的膝蓋痛到不能走路。

④ too much

  1. I ate too much at lunch, and now I feel sick.
    我午餐吃太多了，現在覺得不舒服。
  2. Our neighbour's dog barks too much at night.
    鄰居的狗晚上叫得太兇了。
  3. My son plays computer games too much.
    我兒子打電動打太多了。
  4. Don't think about it too much.
    別想太多。

⑤ a bit、a little

  1. Can you move your chair a bit? I can't see.
    你可以把椅子挪一點嗎？我看不到。
  2. Let's rest a little before we walk up the hill.
    我們爬山坡之前先休息一下吧。
  3. I think you should cut your fringe a bit before the photo.
    我覺得你拍照前應該把瀏海剪短一點。
  4. I only know her a little.
    我跟她只是有一點認識。
  5. Could you turn the radio down a bit, please?
    可以請你把收音機轉小聲一點嗎？

⑥ 否定句 + at all

  1. My grandmother doesn't like cats at all.
    我奶奶一點都不喜歡貓。
  2. It didn't snow at all last winter.
    去年冬天完全沒下雪。
  3. I can't hear you at all. Can you speak up?
    我完全聽不到你，你可以大聲一點嗎？
  4. We weren't tired at all after the walk.
    走完路以後我們一點也不累。
  5. He hasn't changed at all since school.
    他從畢業以後一點都沒變。

⑦ 疑問句 + at all

  1. Do you speak any Italian at all?
    你到底會不會說一點義大利文？
  2. Has the new medicine helped your back at all?
    新的藥到底對你的背有沒有幫助？
  3. Is there any milk left at all?
    到底還有沒有牛奶？
  4. Can you help me at all this weekend?
    你這個週末有辦法幫我一下嗎？
