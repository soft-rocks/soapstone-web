You needn't worry about where we can stay.

你不用擔心我們要住哪裡。

主⁠詞 + needn't + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

needn't / need not、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

needn't / need not不必。
原形動詞不必做的事。

needn't 後⁠面不⁠加 to

You needn't worry about where we can stay.

例句

Anna's arm is fine now. She needn't come back to the clinic.

Anna 的手臂好了。她不必再回診所。

needn't 是情態動詞的用法。後面直接接原形動詞，主詞是 he 或 she 時也不加 -⁠s。

和 don't need to 比⁠較

You needn't worry about where we can stay.

needn't

I'll be home by six. You needn't call.

我六點前會到家。你不必打電話。
don't need to

I'll be home by six. You don't need to call.

我六點前會到家。你不用打電話。

needn't 和 don't need to 意思一樣。needn't 比較常用在英式英文。

needn't 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You needn't worry about where we can stay.

You needn't to bring anything.

You needn't bring anything.

needn't 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

You needn't worry about where we can stay.

He needsn't wait for us.

He needn't wait for us.

needn't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You needn't worry about where we can stay.

You needn't worrying about tickets for the match. I've got two.

You needn't worry about tickets for the match. I've got two.

例句

① You needn't worry about + 名詞：讓對方放心

  1. My uncle has a cabin by the lake, so you needn't worry about where we can stay.
    我叔叔在湖邊有一間小木屋，所以你不必擔心我們住哪裡。
  2. Come with us to Italy this summer. You needn't worry about the cost. My parents are paying.
    今年夏天跟我們去義大利吧。你不必擔心費用。我爸媽會付。
  3. Come in, it's all right. You needn't worry about the dog. He's very friendly.
    進來吧，沒關係。你不必擔心那隻狗。牠很友善。
  4. You needn't worry about tomorrow's exam. You've studied hard all term.
    你不必擔心明天的考試。你整個學期都很用功。
  5. Enjoy your evening at the theatre. You needn't worry about the children. Grandma is looking after them.
    好好享受在劇院的晚上。你不必擔心孩子們。奶奶在照顧他們。
  6. Our farm is hard to find, but you needn't worry about getting lost. I'll send you a map.
    我們的農場很難找，可是你不必擔心迷路。我會寄地圖給你。

② I need not + 原形動詞：說自己不必做的事

  1. My sister lives in New York, but when I send an email, I need not worry about the time difference.
    我姊姊住在紐約，可是我寄電子郵件的時候，不必擔心時差。
  2. I need not get up early tomorrow. It's Sunday.
    我明天不必早起。是星期天。
  3. My old coat is still warm and looks fine. I needn't buy a new one this winter.
    我的舊外套還很保暖，看起來也不錯。今年冬天我不必買新的。
  4. We moved to a flat near my work, so I need not take the bus any more.
    我們搬到離我公司很近的公寓，所以我不必再搭公車了。
  5. It's my birthday, and Dad has booked a table. I needn't cook tonight.
    今天是我生日，爸爸訂了位。我今天晚上不必煮飯。
  6. Every shop in this city takes cards. With this one, I need not carry any cash.
    這個城市每家店都收卡。有了這張卡，我不必帶任何現金。

③ You needn't + 原形動詞, because + 子句

  1. You needn't go jogging this evening because you've already had your exercise today.
    你今天傍晚不必去慢跑，因為你今天已經運動過了。
  2. Pack light for the trip. You needn't bring a towel because the hotel has them.
    這趟旅行東西帶少一點。你不必帶毛巾，因為飯店有。
  3. It's only half past seven. You needn't hurry because the film starts at nine.
    現在才七點半。你不必趕，因為電影九點才開始。
  4. It's late, and it's raining. You needn't call a taxi because I can drive you home.
    很晚了，又在下雨。你不必叫計程車，因為我可以載你回家。
  5. Your son is five, isn't he? You needn't buy a ticket for him because children under six travel free.
    你兒子五歲，對嗎？你不必幫他買票，因為六歲以下的小孩搭車免費。
  6. Thanks for dinner. Can I help? No, you needn't wash the dishes because we have a dishwasher.
    謝謝你的晚餐。我可以幫忙嗎？不用，你不必洗碗，因為我們有洗碗機。

④ 主詞 + needn't + 原形動詞：別人不必做的事

  1. Tell Sarah the meeting is only about the summer party. She needn't come if she's busy.
    跟 Sarah 說那個會只是討論夏日派對。如果她很忙，她不必來。
  2. The party at the office is very casual. Your husband needn't wear a tie.
    公司的派對很隨性。你先生不必打領帶。
  3. The guests at the wedding needn't pay for the drinks. They're free all night.
    婚禮的客人不必付飲料的錢。整晚都是免費的。
  4. Monday is a bank holiday. The children needn't go to school.
    星期一是國定假日。孩子們不必上學。
  5. Your brother needn't bring anything to the barbecue. We've got plenty of food.
    你哥哥不必帶任何東西來烤肉。我們有很多食物。
  6. The museum website says: "Visitors need not book in advance."
    博物館網站上寫著：參觀者不必事先預約。

⑤ needn't + 原形動詞 + if + 子句

  1. It's only a small party at my flat. You needn't come if you don't want to.
    只是在我公寓的小派對。如果你不想來，你不必來。
  2. The report is due on Friday. You needn't finish it today if you're tired.
    報告星期五才要交。如果你累了，你今天不必做完。
  3. Let me check the board first. We needn't leave the café yet if the train is delayed.
    我先看一下看板。如果火車誤點，我們還不必離開咖啡店。
  4. It's just a quiz for fun. You needn't answer if you don't know.
    這只是好玩的小測驗。如果你不知道，你不必回答。
  5. Tell Mia she needn't stay at work if she feels ill.
    跟 Mia 說，如果她覺得不舒服，她不必留在公司。
  6. Your essay is good. You needn't write it all again if there are only small mistakes.
    你的作文很好。如果只有小錯，你不必全部重寫。
  7. The beach is only a mile away. We needn't take the car if the weather is nice.
    海灘只有一英里遠。如果天氣好，我們不必開車。
