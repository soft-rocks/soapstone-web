例句

① could be + 形容詞或名詞

  1. Your phone could be in your other bag.
    你的手機可能在你另一個包包裡。
  2. The weather could be better tomorrow.
    明天的天氣可能會好一點。
  3. The new job could be a great chance for you.
    這份新工作對你來說可能是個好機會。
  4. Moving to a big city could be stressful at first.
    搬到大城市，一開始可能會壓力很大。
  5. The man at the door could be the plumber.
    門口那個男人可能是水電工。
  6. Learning to drive could be useful one day.
    學開車有一天可能會派上用場。
  7. This old coin could be worth a lot of money.
    這枚舊硬幣可能很值錢。
  8. Your idea could be the answer to our problem.
    你的點子可能就是解決我們問題的辦法。
  9. It could be fun to go camping with the whole class.
    跟全班一起去露營可能會很好玩。

② could + 原形動詞

  1. It could snow this weekend.
    這個週末可能會下雪。
  2. The road could flood if the rain doesn't stop.
    雨要是不停，那條路可能會淹水。
  3. This project could take months.
    這個案子可能要花好幾個月。
  4. The meeting could finish early today.
    今天的會議可能會提早結束。
  5. Too much sugar could harm your teeth.
    吃太多糖可能會傷牙齒。
  6. The shop could close before we get there.
    我們還沒到，那家店可能就關門了。
  7. A small mistake could cause big problems.
    一個小錯誤可能會造成大問題。
  8. Our team could win the cup this year.
    我們隊今年有可能拿到冠軍。

③ could be + 動詞-ing

  1. She could be waiting for us outside.
    她可能在外面等我們。
  2. They could be having lunch at the moment.
    他們這個時候可能在吃午餐。
  3. My brother could be driving home now.
    我哥現在可能正在開車回家。
  4. The kids could be playing in the garden.
    孩子們可能在院子裡玩。
  5. Someone could be watching us.
    可能有人在看著我們。

④ Could + 主詞 + 原形動詞？

  1. Could this really happen?
    這真的有可能發生嗎？
  2. Could he really be there, in that dark café?
    他真的可能在那間昏暗的咖啡館裡嗎？
  3. Could the story be true?
    那個故事有可能是真的嗎？
  4. Could it be a mistake?
    會不會是弄錯了？
  5. Could she be the new manager?
    她會不會就是新來的經理？
  6. Could the noise be coming from next door?
    聲音會不會是隔壁傳來的？
  7. Could the medicine make me sleepy?
    這個藥會不會讓我想睡覺？
  8. Could the parcel arrive tomorrow?
    包裹有可能明天到嗎？
