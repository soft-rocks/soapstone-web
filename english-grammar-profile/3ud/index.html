Need I say more?

還需要我多說嗎？

Need + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

Need放在句首，不加 -⁠s，也不用 do。
原形動詞問是不是必要做的事。

Need 開⁠頭⁠的問⁠句，need 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Need I say more?

You've been late three times this week. Need I to tell you again?

You've been late three times this week. Need I tell you again?

情⁠態動⁠詞⁠的 need 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Need I say more?

It's only a short meeting. Needs he come?

It's only a short meeting. Need he come?

情⁠態動⁠詞⁠的 need 放⁠在句⁠首，不⁠用 do

Need I say more?

Do need I bring a pen?

Need I bring a pen?

例句

① Need I say more?：問題已經很清楚

  1. The floors are dirty, the showers are cold and the bins are always full. Need I say more?
    地板很髒，淋浴是冷水，垃圾桶總是滿的。我還需要多說嗎？
  2. My review of the restaurant is short. The food was cold and the waiter was rude. Need I say more?
    我對那家餐廳的評論很短。食物是冷的，服務生很沒禮貌。我還需要多說嗎？
  3. Why do I love this bike? It's cheap, it's fast and it never breaks. Need I say more?
    我為什麼愛這台腳踏車？它很便宜，很快，而且從來不壞。我還需要多說嗎？
  4. Come to Crete with us this summer. Sun, sea and great food. Need I say more?
    今年夏天跟我們去克里特島吧。陽光、海洋和美食。我還需要多說嗎？
  5. Dear Sir, I am writing about the airport bus. It was an hour late and then it broke down. Need I say more?
    先生您好，我寫信是為了機場巴士的事。它晚了一個小時，然後還拋錨。我還需要多說嗎？
  6. You ask why I want a new assistant. He's late every day and never does his work. Need I say more?
    你問我為什麼要換助理。他每天遲到，從來不做事。我還需要多說嗎？

② Need you ask?：答案很明顯

  1. "Do you want to come to the beach?" "Need you ask?"
    你想去海灘嗎？你還需要問嗎？
  2. "Would you like some chocolate cake?" "Need you ask?"
    你想吃巧克力蛋糕嗎？還用問嗎？
  3. "Are you coming to my party?" "Need you ask? Of course!"
    你會來我的派對嗎？還用問嗎？當然！
  4. "You've been in Canada for a year. Do you miss your family?" "Need you ask?"
    你在加拿大一年了。你想念你的家人嗎？還用問嗎？
  5. "Shall we order pizza?" "Need you ask?"
    我們要叫披薩嗎？還用問嗎？
  6. "Are you tired after the marathon?" "Need you ask?"
    跑完馬拉松你累嗎？還用問嗎？

③ Need I + 原形動詞？：正式地問是不是必要

  1. Thank you for inviting me for an interview. Need I bring my passport with me?
    謝謝您邀請我面試。我需要帶護照嗎？
  2. I sent you the application form last week. Need I fill it in again?
    我上星期寄了申請表給您。我需要再填一次嗎？
  3. I've just joined your gym. Need I show my membership card every time I come?
    我剛加入你們的健身房。我每次來都需要出示會員卡嗎？
  4. The course costs six hundred pounds. Need I pay the whole amount today?
    課程要六百英鎊。我今天需要付全額嗎？
  5. I live two hours from your office. Need I come in person to sign the papers?
    我住的地方離你們公司兩個小時。我需要親自去簽文件嗎？
  6. I missed two lessons because of the flu. Need I explain why I was absent?
    我因為流感缺了兩堂課。我需要解釋我為什麼缺席嗎？

④ Need we / he / she + 原形動詞？

  1. The trip to Rome is in term time. Need we take the children out of school for it?
    去羅馬的旅行在學期中。我們需要為了這趟旅行讓孩子們請假嗎？
  2. Dad would only worry. Need he know about this?
    爸爸只會擔心。他需要知道這件事嗎？
  3. Mia is only a part-time worker. Need she come to every meeting?
    Mia 只是兼職員工。她需要每次會議都來嗎？
  4. We broke the window by accident and paid for it. Need we tell the others?
    我們不小心打破窗戶，也賠了錢。我們需要告訴其他人嗎？
  5. The builders have worked since seven. Need they finish the wall tonight?
    工人從七點就開始工作了。他們需要今天晚上把牆做完嗎？
  6. The wedding will cost thirty thousand pounds. Need we really spend so much?
    婚禮要花三萬英鎊。我們真的需要花這麼多錢嗎？

⑤ Need + 主詞 + 原形動詞 …？：委婉地表示不必

  1. The forecast says light showers in the afternoon. Need we worry about a little rain?
    天氣預報說下午有小陣雨。一點點雨我們需要擔心嗎？
  2. Need you shout? I'm right here.
    你需要大吼嗎？我就在這裡。
  3. Need I remind you that the rent is due tomorrow?
    我需要提醒你明天要交房租嗎？
  4. We talked about the holiday for two hours last night. Need we discuss it again?
    我們昨晚討論假期討論了兩個小時。我們需要再討論一次嗎？
  5. He only brought the wrong drink. Need you be so rude to the waiter?
    他只是送錯飲料。你需要對服務生這麼沒禮貌嗎？
  6. Your report still isn't on my desk. Need I point out that the deadline was yesterday?
    你的報告還沒放在我桌上。我需要指出截止日期是昨天嗎？
  7. We only have two things to decide. Need the meeting last three hours?
    我們只有兩件事要決定。這個會議需要開三個小時嗎？
