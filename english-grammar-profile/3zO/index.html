"We have come together," the man said, "to celebrate our annual meeting."

「我們齊聚一堂，」那個男人說，「是為了慶祝我們的年會。」

引⁠述子⁠句插⁠在引⁠號⁠裡⁠的話中⁠間

「我們齊聚一堂，」那個男人說，「是為了慶祝我們的年會。」

同⁠一⁠句用逗⁠號，後⁠半句小⁠寫；新⁠的一⁠句用句⁠號，後⁠半句大⁠寫

"We have come together," the man said, "to celebrate our annual meeting."

同一句

"If you need anything," she said, "just call me."

「如果你需要什麼，」她說，「打電話給我就好。」
新的一句

"Forgive me," he said. "I will never leave you alone again."

「原諒我，」他說。「我再也不會丟下你一個人了。」

thought 轉⁠述心⁠裡⁠的⁠話，標⁠點一⁠樣

「沒問題，」我心想。「排隊的只有一個人。」

主⁠詞是名⁠詞⁠時可⁠以倒⁠裝

「問題是，」Tom 說，「根本沒有人問我。」

後⁠半句接⁠著前⁠半⁠句，第⁠一⁠個字小⁠寫

"We have come together," the man said, "to celebrate our annual meeting."

"If you need anything," she said, "Just call me."

"If you need anything," she said, "just call me."

引⁠述子⁠句放⁠在引⁠號外⁠面

"We have come together," the man said, "to celebrate our annual meeting."

"I think, she said, we should leave now."

"I think," she said, "we should leave now."

兩⁠個完⁠整⁠的句⁠子，引⁠述動⁠詞後⁠面用句⁠號

"I'm tired," he said, "I'm going to bed."

"I'm tired," he said. "I'm going to bed."

前⁠半句和引⁠述動⁠詞後⁠面都加逗⁠號

"Well" she said "that's a surprise."

"Well," she said, "that's a surprise."

引⁠號⁠裡⁠的話一⁠次說⁠完，引⁠述子⁠句放⁠在⁠後⁠面

「我們齊聚一堂，是為了慶祝我們的年會。」那個男人說。

引⁠號⁠裡⁠的 I think 是說⁠出⁠的⁠字，不⁠是引⁠述子⁠句

「這個包包，我想，是你的。」

例句

① 後半句接同一句

  1. "If you're free tomorrow," Mia said, "we could go shopping."
    如果你明天有空，Mia 說，我們可以去逛街。
  2. "The best thing about this job," he said, "is the people."
    這份工作最好的地方，他說，是這裡的人。
  3. "When I was your age," my grandfather said, "I walked to school every day."
    我在你這個年紀的時候，我爺爺說，每天都走路上學。
  4. "Before you go," the teacher said, "please hand in your homework."
    走之前，老師說，請把作業交出來。
  5. "Actually," she said, "I've never been to Spain."
    其實，她說，我從來沒去過西班牙。
  6. "The problem with this phone," Dad said, "is the battery."
    這支手機的問題，爸爸說，是電池。

② 後半句是新的一句

  1. "Thank you," she said. "That's very kind of you."
    謝謝，她說。你真好。
  2. "I'm sorry," he said. "I didn't mean to hurt you."
    對不起，他說。我不是故意要傷害你的。
  3. "Come in," the doctor said. "Take a seat."
    請進，醫生說。請坐。
  4. "It's late," Mum said. "You should go to bed."
    很晚了，媽媽說。你該去睡了。
  5. "Don't worry," my friend said. "Everything will be fine."
    別擔心，我朋友說。一切都會沒事的。

③ 轉述想法

  1. "This can't be right," I thought. "I must have made a mistake."
    這不可能是對的，我心想。我一定是哪裡算錯了。
  2. "If I hurry," she thought, "I can still catch the train."
    如果我快一點，她心想，還趕得上火車。
  3. "Maybe," he thought, "she didn't get my message."
    也許，他心想，她沒收到我的訊息。
  4. "What a lovely day," I thought. "I'll go to the beach."
    天氣真好，我心想。我要去海邊。
  5. "Either I talk to her now," I thought, "or I'll never get the chance."
    我要不是現在跟她說，我心想，就再也沒機會了。

④ 主詞是名詞時倒裝

  1. "In my opinion," said the manager, "we need more staff."
    我認為，經理說，我們需要更多人手。
  2. "Ladies and gentlemen," said the pilot, "we are about to land."
    各位先生女士，機長說，我們即將降落。
  3. "The thing is," said Jack, "I don't have any money."
    問題是，Jack 說，我沒錢。
  4. "Please," said the little girl, "can I keep the puppy?"
    拜託，小女孩說，我可以養這隻小狗嗎？
  5. "Of course," said Mrs Lee, "you can stay with us."
    當然，Lee 太太說，你可以跟我們住。

⑤ asked / replied / shouted

  1. "Excuse me," she asked, "is this seat taken?"
    不好意思，她問，這個位子有人坐嗎？
  2. "If it rains," he asked, "what shall we do?"
    如果下雨，他問，我們要怎麼辦？
  3. "Well," she replied, "I'm not really sure where the station is."
    嗯，她回答，我不太確定車站在哪裡。
  4. "Hurry up," he shouted, "or we'll miss the bus!"
    快點，他大喊，不然我們會錯過公車！
  5. "Can I use your laptop?" "Yes," I replied, "but only for a few minutes."
    我可以用你的筆電嗎？好，我回答，但只能用幾分鐘。

⑥ 故事裡的長句

  1. "We have to leave," the guide said, "before it gets dark."
    我們得離開，導遊說，在天黑之前。
  2. "I found this wallet," the boy said, "on the floor of the bus."
    我撿到這個錢包，男孩說，在公車的地板上。
  3. "This house," the old man said, "has been in my family for a hundred years."
    這棟房子，老先生說，已經在我們家傳了一百年。
  4. "When the bell rings," the teacher said, "put your pens down."
    鈴聲一響，老師說，就把筆放下。
  5. "My sister," Ana said, "is the one in the blue dress."
    我姊姊，Ana 說，是穿藍色洋裝的那個。
