Do you still remember my best friend?

你還記得我最好的朋友嗎？

副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞和主⁠要動⁠詞中⁠間

Do you still remember my best friend?

你通常騎腳踏車去辦公室嗎？

有疑⁠問⁠詞⁠時，副⁠詞⁠的位⁠置不⁠變

Do you still remember my best friend?

What kind of music do you usually listen to in the car?

你在車上通常聽哪一類的音樂？

例句

① Have you ever + 過去分詞?

  1. You've lived in Taipei for a month. Have you ever been to the night market in Shilin?
    你在台北住了一個月。你去過士林夜市嗎？
  2. I bought a big fish at the market. Have you ever cooked a whole fish by yourself?
    我在市場買了一條大魚。你自己煮過一整條魚嗎？
  3. My uncle needs help with the apples this autumn. Has your brother ever worked on a farm?
    我叔叔今年秋天需要人手採蘋果。你哥哥在農場工作過嗎？
  4. You work at the concert hall. Have you ever shaken hands with a famous singer?
    你在音樂廳工作。你跟有名的歌手握過手嗎？
  5. The manager asked about the two new cleaners. Have they ever been late for work?
    經理問起那兩個新來的清潔人員。他們上班遲到過嗎？
  6. We're going camping by the sea. Have you ever slept in a tent on the beach?
    我們要去海邊露營。你在海灘上睡過帳篷嗎？

② Do you usually / often + 原形動詞?

  1. You always have a book in your hand. What kind of books do you usually read?
    你手上總是拿著一本書。你通常讀哪一類的書？
  2. Your office is only ten minutes away. Do you usually go there on foot?
    你的辦公室只要十分鐘。你通常走路去嗎？
  3. You look very fit. How often do you usually go to the gym?
    你身材看起來很好。你通常多久去一次健身房？
  4. You never cook at the end of the week. Do you often eat out on Fridays?
    你週末前從來不煮飯。你星期五常出去吃嗎？
  5. I saw her jogging at six this morning. Does your sister always get up so early?
    我今天早上六點看到她在慢跑。你姊姊總是這麼早起嗎？
  6. You look tired every morning. What time do you normally go to bed?
    你每天早上看起來都很累。你平常幾點睡？

③ Do you still + 原形動詞?

  1. I found this old photo of us in the sand. Do you still remember our first trip to the beach?
    我找到這張我們在沙灘上的舊照片。你還記得我們第一次去海邊嗎？
  2. I want to send you a wedding invitation. Do you still live on Park Road?
    我想寄喜帖給你。你還住在公園路嗎？
  3. I need some advice about a loan. Does Ken still work at the bank?
    我需要一些貸款的建議。Ken 還在銀行上班嗎？
  4. I used to buy sweets at the corner shop as a child. Do they still sell those old sweets?
    我小時候常在轉角的店買糖果。他們還有賣那種懷舊糖果嗎？
  5. I saw you at the stop an hour ago. Are you still waiting for the bus?
    我一個小時前看到你在公車站。你還在等公車嗎？

④ Do you really / Have you already ...?

  1. The party has only just started. Do you really want to leave now?
    派對才剛開始。你真的現在就想走嗎？
  2. Our teacher said the exam is cancelled? Did she really say that?
    我們老師說考試取消了？她真的那樣說嗎？
  3. It's Mum's birthday dinner. Have you already booked the restaurant for Friday?
    那是媽媽的生日晚餐。你已經訂好星期五的餐廳了嗎？
  4. Has the film already started? I can hear music.
    電影已經開始了嗎？我聽到音樂了。
  5. Tom says we should sell the car. Do you really think he's right?
    湯姆說我們應該把車賣了。你真的覺得他是對的嗎？

⑤ 不同的助動詞和句型

  1. Your flat is next to the railway. Can you always hear the trains from it?
    你的公寓在鐵路旁邊。你在公寓裡總是聽得到火車的聲音嗎？
  2. I'm so sorry I missed your wedding. Will you ever forgive me?
    很抱歉我錯過了你的婚禮。你會原諒我嗎？
  3. The meeting started twenty minutes ago. Why do you always arrive late?
    會議二十分鐘前就開始了。你為什麼總是遲到？
  4. You sent back the broken phone in March. Did you ever get your money back from that shop?
    你三月把壞掉的手機寄回去。你後來有從那家店拿回你的錢嗎？
  5. There's no car park at your office. Where do you usually park?
    你辦公室沒有停車場。你通常把車停在哪裡？
  6. You look busy on Saturdays. Do you sometimes work at weekends?
    你星期六看起來很忙。你週末有時候要上班嗎？
  7. You love Italian food and the language. Have you ever thought about moving abroad?
    你很愛義大利菜和義大利文。你想過搬到國外嗎？
  8. We're thinking of buying a house by the water. Does the river often flood after heavy rain?
    我們在考慮買一間水邊的房子。大雨過後那條河常淹水嗎？
