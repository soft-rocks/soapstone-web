You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

你要我跟你介紹我的一個朋友。

受⁠詞是被請⁠求⁠的⁠人，不⁠定⁠詞是那⁠個⁠人要做⁠的事

你要我跟你介紹我的一個朋友。

tell、would like 也接⁠受⁠詞和不⁠定⁠詞

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

tell

The coach told us to run three laps.

教練叫我們跑三圈。
would like

I would like you to meet my parents.

我希望你見見我爸媽。

not 放⁠在 to 前⁠面

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

我媽叫我不要遲到。

被⁠動句把受⁠詞移⁠到主⁠詞⁠的位⁠置

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

我們被要求在外面等。

主動句是 They asked us to wait outside.

want 後⁠面接⁠受⁠詞加不⁠定⁠詞，不⁠接 that 子⁠句

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

I want that you come with me.

I want you to come with me.

受⁠詞後⁠面要加 to，再⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

She asked me help her.

She asked me to help her.

動⁠詞後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

The doctor told he to rest.

The doctor told him to rest.

叫某⁠人做⁠事用 tell，say 後⁠面不⁠能直⁠接接人

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

My boss said me to finish the report.

My boss told me to finish the report.

沒⁠有受⁠詞⁠時，做⁠事⁠的人是主⁠詞

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

沒有受詞
I 要見經理

I asked to see the manager.

我要求見經理。
有受詞
助理打電話

I asked my assistant to call the manager.

我請我的助理打電話給經理。

make 和 let 的受⁠詞後⁠面不⁠加 to

You asked me to tell you about one of my friends.

我老闆讓我午休久一點。

例句

① ask + 受詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. Thanks for your letter. You asked me to describe my village.
    謝謝你的來信。你要我描述一下我的村子。
  2. "Why are you still here?" "Mr Brown asked me to wait for him."
    你怎麼還在這裡？Brown 先生叫我等他。
  3. My neighbour asked us to keep the noise down after ten.
    我的鄰居請我們十點以後小聲一點。
  4. The room was very cold, so I asked the hotel to send up another blanket.
    房間很冷，所以我請飯店再送一條毯子上來。

② tell + 受詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. The teacher told us to open our books at page twenty.
    老師叫我們把課本翻到第二十頁。
  2. "Why are you wearing a coat?" "Mum told me to take one. It might rain."
    你為什麼穿外套？媽媽叫我帶的。可能會下雨。
  3. The police told everyone to leave the building.
    警察叫大家離開大樓。
  4. My knee hurts, so the physio told me to stop running for a month.
    我的膝蓋痛，所以物理治療師叫我一個月不要跑步。

③ want / would like + 受詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. Your parents want you to go on holiday with them this summer.
    你爸媽要你今年夏天跟他們去度假。
  2. I would like you to come to a picnic on Saturday.
    我希望你星期六來參加野餐。
  3. "Do you want me to drive?" "Yes, please. I'm really tired."
    要我來開車嗎？好，拜託。我好累。
  4. We would like you to come to our office on Monday to discuss the contract.
    我們希望您星期一來我們辦公室討論合約。
  5. My boss wants me to work on Saturday again.
    我老闆又要我星期六上班。

④ invite / remind / advise + 受詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. They invited us to stay with them in Italy.
    他們邀請我們去義大利住他們家。
  2. Please remind me to call the dentist tomorrow.
    明天請提醒我打電話給牙醫。
  3. "Did you buy the milk?" "No! Why didn't you remind me to get it?"
    你買牛奶了嗎？沒有！你怎麼沒提醒我買？
  4. The bank advised us to keep our cards in a safe place.
    銀行勸我們把卡放在安全的地方。
  5. My friend invited me to join her band.
    我朋友邀請我加入她的樂團。

⑤ order / warn + 受詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. The officer ordered the soldiers to stop.
    軍官命令士兵們停下來。
  2. The captain ordered everyone to put on a life jacket.
    船長命令大家穿上救生衣。
  3. The guide warned us to stay on the path.
    導遊警告我們要走在步道上。

⑥ 動詞 + 受詞 + not to + 原形動詞

  1. I have to go now. My dad told me not to be late for dinner.
    我得走了。我爸叫我吃晚飯不要遲到。
  2. The guide warned us not to swim in the river.
    導遊警告我們不要在河裡游泳。
  3. I asked my brother not to touch my computer.
    我叫我弟弟不要碰我的電腦。
  4. The doctor advised him not to eat so much salt.
    醫生勸他不要吃那麼多鹽。

⑦ 被動：be + 過去分詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. When we arrived, we were asked to wait outside.
    我們到的時候，被要求在外面等。
  2. All passengers are reminded to keep their bags with them.
    提醒所有乘客隨身攜帶行李。
  3. "Why did you leave the party early?" "I was told to be home by eleven."
    你為什麼那麼早離開派對？家裡叫我十一點前到家。
  4. The students were told not to use their phones during the exam.
    學生們被告知考試時不准用手機。

⑧ 問句

  1. "What did the manager ask you to do?" "She asked me to check the stock."
    經理叫你做什麼？她叫我清點庫存。
  2. This room is for staff only. Who told you to come in here?
    這間是員工專用的房間。誰叫你進來的？
  3. Would you like me to carry your bag?
    要我幫你提包包嗎？
