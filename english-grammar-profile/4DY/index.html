例句

① as for / in terms of

  1. The food was great. As for the service, it was a bit slow.
    食物很好吃。至於服務，有點慢。
  2. I'm ready to go. As for my sister, she's still getting dressed.
    我準備好了。至於我姊姊，她還在換衣服。
  3. In terms of price, this phone is the best choice.
    就價格來說，這支手機是最好的選擇。
  4. The town is small in terms of population.
    就人口來說，這個小鎮很小。
  5. As for the weekend, I haven't made any plans yet.
    至於週末，我還沒有任何計畫。

② in spite of

  1. In spite of the storm, the plane landed safely.
    儘管有暴風雨，飛機還是安全降落了。
  2. She went to work in spite of her cold.
    儘管感冒了，她還是去上班了。
  3. In spite of being tired, he finished the race.
    儘管很累，他還是跑完了比賽。
  4. The old car still runs well in spite of its age.
    儘管那輛舊車很老了，還是很好開。
  5. In spite of the high price, the tickets sold out.
    儘管票價很高，票還是賣完了。

③ in addition to / apart from

  1. In addition to English, students learn two other languages.
    除了英文，學生還要學另外兩種語言。
  2. In addition to cooking dinner, he did the washing up.
    除了煮晚餐，他還洗了碗。
  3. Apart from the beach, the island has some beautiful forests.
    除了海灘，那座島還有一些美麗的森林。
  4. Apart from a sandwich, I haven't eaten anything today.
    除了一個三明治，我今天什麼都沒吃。
  5. In addition to the fee, you'll need to pay for the books.
    除了學費，你還要付書錢。

④ except for / apart from（排除）

  1. The museum is open every day except for Monday.
    博物館除了星期一，每天都開。
  2. I've finished all my homework except for the maths.
    除了數學，我的功課都寫完了。
  3. Apart from one small mistake, your essay is perfect.
    除了一個小錯，你的作文很完美。
  4. The streets were quiet except for a few taxis.
    除了幾輛計程車，街上很安靜。
  5. The room was empty except for an old piano.
    房間裡除了一台舊鋼琴，什麼都沒有。

⑤ on behalf of 和不同的情境

  1. On behalf of the whole team, I'd like to thank you.
    我代表整個團隊向您致謝。
  2. She accepted the prize on behalf of her father.
    她代表她父親領了獎。
  3. As for dessert, I'll just have some fruit.
    至於甜點，我吃點水果就好。
  4. In terms of size, the new office is perfect.
    就大小來說，新辦公室很完美。
  5. In spite of his age, my grandfather is very active.
    儘管年紀大了，我爺爺還是很活躍。
  6. In addition to a salary, the job offers free lunch.
    除了薪水，這份工作還提供免費午餐。
  7. We invited everyone except for the new neighbors.
    除了新鄰居，我們每個人都邀請了。
  8. As for the noise, I'll speak to the neighbors tomorrow.
    至於噪音，我明天會去跟鄰居談。
  9. Apart from being a doctor, she's also a talented painter.
    除了是醫生，她也是一位很有天分的畫家。
  10. I'm writing on behalf of my company.
    我代表我的公司寫這封信。
