例句

① is called：說名字

  1. My favourite band has four singers. The group is called Playmo.
    我最喜歡的樂團有四個歌手。這個團體叫做 Playmo。
  2. I've got a new cat. She's called Lulu.
    我養了一隻新的貓。她叫 Lulu。
  3. We stayed in a small town near the coast. It's called Whitby.
    我們住在海邊附近的一個小鎮。那裡叫 Whitby。
  4. There's a great café on our street. It's called The Blue Cup.
    我們這條街有一家很棒的咖啡店。叫 The Blue Cup。
  5. My best friend lives next door. He's called Ben.
    我最好的朋友住在隔壁。他叫 Ben。
  6. I'm reading a book about a dog. It's called Lost in the Snow.
    我在看一本講一隻狗的書。書名叫《雪中迷路》。

② is made of / from / in

  1. I bought a T-shirt for £42 because it is made of cotton.
    我買了一件 42 英鎊的 T 恤，因為它是棉做的。
  2. My grandad gave me this box. It's made of wood.
    我爺爺給了我這個盒子。它是木頭做的。
  3. Try this cheese. It's made from goat's milk.
    吃吃看這個起司。它是羊奶做的。
  4. This bread is really good. It's made from rice flour.
    這個麵包很好吃。它是用米磨的粉做的。
  5. My new bike is light and fast. It's made in Taiwan.
    我的新腳踏車又輕又快。它是台灣製造的。
  6. Don't put that cup in the microwave. It's made of metal.
    那個杯子不要放進微波爐。它是金屬做的。

③ is used / spoken / sold

  1. We don't sleep in the small room. It's used as my mum's office.
    我們不睡那個小房間。那裡拿來當我媽媽的辦公室。
  2. Our town is near the border. French is spoken in a lot of shops.
    我們鎮靠近邊界。很多店都說法語。
  3. You can't buy this tea in the supermarket. It's only sold at the market on Saturday.
    你在超市買不到這種茶。它只在星期六的市集賣。
  4. This app is great for students. It's used in a lot of schools.
    這個應用程式很適合學生。很多學校都在用。

④ is + 過去分詞：常常發生的事

  1. Don't worry about the mess. Our classroom is cleaned every evening after school.
    別擔心這麼亂。我們的教室每天放學後都會打掃。
  2. Your letter should arrive soon. The post is delivered at about ten every morning.
    你的信應該快到了。郵件每天早上大約十點送來。
  3. Welcome to the summer camp. Lunch is served in the hall at twelve o'clock.
    歡迎來到夏令營。午餐十二點在禮堂供應。
  4. The museum is closed on Mondays, so let's go on Tuesday.
    博物館星期一休館，我們星期二去吧。
  5. My mum starts work very early. My little sister is taken to school by my dad.
    我媽媽很早就要上班。我妹妹是我爸爸送去學校的。

⑤ is + 過去分詞：說人或地方

  1. My uncle is a doctor. He's paid very well.
    我叔叔是醫生。他的薪水很高。
  2. Our village is surrounded by hills, so it's very quiet.
    我們村子四周都是山，所以很安靜。
  3. This beach is visited by a lot of tourists in summer.
    這個海灘夏天有很多遊客來。
  4. My brother is good at football. He's known as the fastest player in the team.
    我哥哥很會踢足球。大家都知道他是隊上跑最快的。
  5. The castle is open again. It's looked after by a group of volunteers.
    城堡又開放了。它是一群志工在照顧的。

⑥ 問答

  1. "What's your dog called?" "He's called Max."
    你的狗叫什麼名字？他叫 Max。
  2. "What's this bag made of?" "It's made of leather."
    這個包包是什麼做的？是皮做的。
  3. "Where's this coffee from?" "It's grown in Brazil."
    這個咖啡是哪裡來的？是巴西種的。
  4. "Can I park here?" "No, sorry. This space is kept for the doctor."
    我可以停這裡嗎？不行，抱歉。這個車位是留給醫生的。
  5. "Is breakfast extra?" "No, it's included in the price of the room."
    早餐要另外付錢嗎？不用，含在房價裡。
