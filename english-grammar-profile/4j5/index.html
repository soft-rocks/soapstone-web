The thing that I love most is going to the shops.

我最喜歡的事就是去逛街。

The thing that 加子⁠句當主⁠詞，重⁠點放⁠在 is 後⁠面

我最喜歡的事就是去逛街。

說人用 the person who，說事⁠情用 the thing that，the one 都⁠可⁠以

The thing that I love most is going to the shops.

the person who

The person who helped me most was my sister.

幫我最多的人是我姊姊。
the one who

The one who always remembers my birthday is my grandmother.

總是記得我生日的人是我奶奶。
the thing that

The thing that surprised me was the price.

讓我驚訝的是價錢。

加⁠上 only，強⁠調只⁠有這⁠一⁠個

The thing that I love most is going to the shops.

the only one

You're the only one who understands me.

你是唯一懂我的人。

重⁠點可⁠以是名⁠詞片⁠語、動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing、to 不⁠定⁠詞或 that 子⁠句

The thing that I love most is going to the shops.

that 子句

The thing that worries me is that nobody has called.

讓我擔心的是一直沒人打來。

說人用 the person who，不⁠用 thing

The thing that cooked dinner was my brother.

The person who cooked dinner was my brother.

that 已⁠經是受⁠詞，子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

The thing that I love most is going to the shops.

The thing that I hate it is waiting.

The thing that I hate is waiting.

關⁠係子⁠句後⁠面要接主⁠要動⁠詞

The thing that I love most is going to the shops.

The person who called me my best friend.

The person who called me was my best friend.

例句

① The thing that ... is ...

  1. The thing that I miss most about home is my mother's cooking.
    我最想念家裡的是我媽媽煮的菜。
  2. The thing that annoys me most is people talking in the cinema.
    最讓我煩的是有人在電影院講話。
  3. The thing that I liked best about the hotel was the breakfast.
    那家飯店我最喜歡的是早餐。
  4. The thing that impressed everyone was her calm voice.
    讓大家印象深刻的是她冷靜的聲音。
  5. The thing that I don't understand is why he lied.
    我不懂的是他為什麼說謊。
  6. The thing that makes this city special is its night markets.
    讓這個城市特別的是它的夜市。

② The person who ... is ...

  1. The person who taught me to cook was my grandfather.
    教我做菜的人是我爺爺。
  2. The person who sits behind me in class is my best friend.
    上課坐我後面的那個人是我最好的朋友。
  3. The person who found my wallet was a young student.
    撿到我皮夾的人是一個年輕學生。
  4. The person that I admire most is my grandfather.
    我最欣賞的人是我爺爺。
  5. The person who should apologize is you, not me.
    該道歉的是你，不是我。

③ The one who / that ... is ...

  1. The one who broke the window was the boy next door.
    打破窗戶的是隔壁的男孩。
  2. The one that I want is the blue one.
    我要的是藍色那個。
  3. The one who always makes us laugh is Uncle Joe.
    總是逗我們笑的是 Joe 叔叔。
  4. The one that tasted best was the chocolate cake.
    最好吃的是巧克力蛋糕。
  5. The one who suffers most is the dog.
    最可憐的是那隻狗。

④ the only one who / that

  1. He's the only one who remembers where we parked.
    他是唯一記得我們車停在哪裡的人。
  2. This is the only one that still works.
    這是唯一還能用的一個。
  3. I was the only one who laughed at his joke.
    只有我笑了他的笑話。
  4. She's the only person who can make my dad laugh.
    她是唯一能讓我爸笑的人。
  5. That's the only thing that matters to me.
    那是我唯一在乎的事。

⑤ 重點的不同形式

  1. The thing that I hate most is getting up early.
    我最討厭的是早起。
  2. The thing that you need to do is to call her now.
    你要做的是現在就打給她。
  3. The thing that surprised us was that the shop was still open.
    讓我們驚訝的是那家店還開著。
  4. The person who annoyed me most was the man with the loud phone.
    最讓我煩的人是那個手機很吵的男人。
  5. The thing that helped me most was a good night's sleep.
    對我最有幫助的是好好睡一覺。
  6. The one thing that I can't stand is rudeness.
    我唯一受不了的是沒禮貌。
  7. The person who deserves the prize is Mrs. Chen.
    該得獎的是陳太太。
  8. The thing that worried my parents was that I was always alone.
    讓我爸媽擔心的是我總是一個人。
  9. The thing that I'd like to do this weekend is nothing at all.
    這個週末我想做的就是什麼都不做。
