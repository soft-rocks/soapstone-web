你從你家出發，一直往前走，然後右轉。

逗⁠號隔⁠開動⁠作，最⁠後一⁠個前⁠面加 then

You start at your house, go straight ahead, then turn right.

出車站，過馬路，然後左轉。

用 first、then、and then 排⁠出步⁠驟

You start at your house, go straight ahead, then turn right.

The receptionist at the clinic gave me a form. First fill in the form, then sign it, and then give it to the nurse.

診所的櫃台人員給我一張表格。先填表格，然後簽名，再交給護理師。

用 you 開⁠頭⁠時，後⁠面⁠的動⁠作省⁠略 you

You start at your house, go straight ahead, then turn right.

You start at the bank, walk to the corner, then turn right.

你從銀行出發，走到轉角，然後右轉。

指⁠示用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞開⁠頭

You start at your house, go straight ahead, then turn right.

Going straight, turning left, then stop.

Go straight, turn left, then stop.

接⁠下⁠來用 then，比⁠較才用 than

You start at your house, go straight ahead, then turn right.

Go straight, than turn left.

Go straight, then turn left.

主⁠詞是 you，動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

You start at your house, go straight ahead, then turn right.

You go to the corner and turns left.

You go to the corner and turn left.

例句

① 指路

  1. The receptionist told us how to find the beach. Leave the hotel, walk down to the harbor, then follow the coast path.
    櫃台人員告訴我們怎麼去海灘。離開飯店，走到港口，然後沿著海岸步道走。
  2. Come to my party after school. Walk to the end of the street, turn right, and my house is on the left.
    放學後來我的派對。走到街底，右轉，我家就在左邊。
  3. A tourist asked me for the post office. Take the third street, go straight, then turn left at the bakery.
    一個觀光客問我郵局在哪裡。走第三條街，直走，然後在麵包店左轉。
  4. My grandmother lives by the river. Get off the bus at the school, walk back a little, then cross the bridge.
    我奶奶住在河邊。在學校那站下公車，往回走一點，然後過橋。
  5. Let's meet at Rosa's Café at three. Go past the park, take the second left, and the café is next to the post office.
    我們三點在羅莎咖啡館見。經過公園，第二個路口左轉，咖啡店就在郵局旁邊。
  6. The new dentist is easy to find. Turn right at the lights, go up the hill, then look for the red door.
    新的牙醫診所很好找。在紅綠燈右轉，上坡，然後找紅色的門。

② You + 動詞, + 動詞, + then + 動詞

  1. The wedding is at the old hall. You leave the car park, go past the church, then take the first right.
    婚禮在舊的禮堂。你出停車場，經過教堂，然後在第一個路口右轉。
  2. A guard at the airport helped me find the trains. You go down these stairs, follow the signs, then take the exit.
    機場的警衛幫我找火車。你走下這些樓梯，跟著指標走，然後從出口出去。
  3. My uncle explained the way to his farm. You leave the highway, drive for two kilometers, then stop at the gas station.
    我叔叔說明去他農場的路。你下高速公路，開兩公里，然後在加油站停下來。
  4. Here's how to get to my school from the airport. You get on the train, change at Taipei, then get off at the third stop.
    這是從機場到我學校的方法。你上火車，在台北轉車，然後在第三站下車。

③ 做菜和做事的步驟

  1. Making these noodles is easy. Boil the water, add the noodles, then cook for three minutes.
    煮這種麵很簡單。把水煮滾，放入麵條，然後煮三分鐘。
  2. Mum left me a note about dinner. Wash the rice, put it in the pot, and turn on the rice cooker.
    媽媽留了一張晚餐的紙條給我。洗米，放進鍋子，然後打開電鍋。
  3. Can you make the sandwiches for the picnic? Cut the bread, add some butter, then put the cheese on top.
    你可以做野餐的三明治嗎？切麵包，抹點奶油，然後把起司放上去。
  4. The new bookshelf arrived today. Open the box, take out the parts, then read the instructions.
    新書架今天送到了。打開箱子，拿出零件，然後看說明書。
  5. The IT man answered my call about the frozen screen. Turn off the computer, wait ten seconds, then turn it on again.
    電腦人員接了我打去問當機的電話。關掉電腦，等十秒，然後再打開。

④ first ... then ... and then

  1. The receptionist at the clinic gave me a form. First fill in the form, then sign it, and then give it to the nurse.
    診所的櫃台人員給我一張表格。先填表格，然後簽名，再交給護理師。
  2. Lunch is ready, children. First wash your hands, then take a plate, and then help yourself.
    孩子們，午餐好了。先洗手，然後拿盤子，再自己取用。
  3. My son is taking the train alone for the first time. First buy a ticket, then go through the gate, and then wait on the platform.
    我兒子第一次自己搭火車。先買票，然後過閘門，再到月台等。
  4. The bank sent me an email about my account. First log in, then click on "Settings", and then change your password.
    銀行寄了一封關於我帳戶的電子郵件。先登入，然後點設定，再改密碼。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. The sports centre is near my house. When you reach the school, turn left, then go up the hill.
    運動中心在我家附近。到學校的時候左轉，然後上坡。
  2. Many people get lost on the way to our flat. Don't turn at the first corner, go to the second one, then turn right.
    很多人來我們公寓的路上會迷路。第一個轉角不要轉，走到第二個，然後右轉。
  3. Welcome to our home! Come in, take off your shoes and sit down.
    歡迎來我們家！進來，脫鞋，坐下。
  4. You can call the front desk from your room. Pick up the phone, press 1, then wait for an answer.
    你可以從房間打電話到櫃台。拿起電話，按 1，然後等人接聽。
  5. The taxi is here. Put on your coat, get your bag, and meet me at the door.
    計程車到了。穿上外套，拿好包包，到門口跟我會合。
  6. The art teacher showed the class how to make a snowflake. Fold the paper in half, cut along the line, then open it.
    美術老師教全班怎麼做雪花。把紙對折，沿著線剪，然後打開。
  7. The hotel is near the station. Walk for five minutes, cross the river, and you'll see it.
    飯店在車站附近。走五分鐘，過河，你就會看到它了。
  8. Grandma taught me her pancake recipe. Mix the flour and eggs, add milk, then stir well.
    奶奶教我做她的鬆餅。把麵粉和蛋混在一起，加牛奶，然後攪拌均勻。
  9. You're the last one in the office tonight. Close the windows, lock the door, then switch off the lights.
    你今天晚上是辦公室最後一個人。關窗，鎖門，然後關燈。
  10. The picnic is by the lake. Take this road, drive past the farm, then park near the water.
    野餐在湖邊。走這條路，開過農場，然後把車停在水邊。
  11. The teacher gave advice before the exam. Read the question, think for a moment, then write your answer.
    老師在考試前給了建議。讀題目，想一下，然後寫答案。
