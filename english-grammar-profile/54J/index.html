Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

我做夢也沒想到，我爸媽竟然會這樣對我。

never 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，助⁠動⁠詞移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

我做夢也沒想到，我爸媽竟然會這樣對我。

一般語序是 I had never thought。

一⁠般語⁠序沒⁠有助⁠動⁠詞⁠時，倒⁠裝加 did

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

一般語序

I never answered her letter.

我從來沒回她的信。
倒裝

Never did I answer her letter.

我從來都沒回她的信。

動⁠詞是 be 時，be 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

我從來沒有像那場比賽之後那麼累。

倒⁠裝和一⁠般語⁠序⁠的意⁠思一⁠樣，倒⁠裝⁠的語⁠氣比⁠較強

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

現在完成式I have never seen → Never have I seen
過去完成式I had never thought → Never had I thought
willI will never forget → Never will I forget
過去簡單式I never saw → Never did I see

never 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時要倒⁠裝

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

Never I have seen the river so high.

Never have I seen the river so high.

主⁠要動⁠詞不⁠移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，要加 did

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

Never saw I such a beautiful sunset.

Never did I see such a beautiful sunset.

did 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

Never did I thought the test would be so long.

Never did I think the test would be so long.

never 本⁠身是否⁠定，助⁠動⁠詞不⁠加 not

Never had I thought that my parents would do this to me.

Never hasn't my sister missed a match.

Never has my sister missed a match.

例句

① Never + have / has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. The special effects were amazing. Never have I seen such a realistic film.
    特效太厲害了。我從來沒看過這麼逼真的電影。
  2. Never have I been so happy to see the sea after a long winter.
    漫長的冬天過後，我從來沒有這麼高興看到海。
  3. Never have I met anyone as kind as my grandmother.
    我從來沒遇過像我奶奶這麼善良的人。
  4. Never has the town looked so beautiful as it does this spring.
    這個鎮從來沒有像今年春天這麼美。
  5. Never have we had so many visitors at the museum.
    博物館從來沒有過這麼多參觀者。
  6. Never have I eaten such good pasta, not even in Italy.
    我從來沒吃過這麼好吃的義大利麵，在義大利也沒有。

② Never + had + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. Never had I thought that a small dog could eat so much.
    我從來沒想過一隻小狗可以吃這麼多。
  2. Never had she felt so alone as on her first night in the city.
    她從來沒有像到這座城市的第一個晚上那麼孤單。
  3. Never had we seen so much snow. The whole village was white.
    我們從來沒看過這麼多雪。整個村子都白了。
  4. Never had my brother cooked a meal before he left home.
    我哥哥離家之前從來沒煮過一頓飯。
  5. Never had the team played so badly in front of their own fans.
    這支球隊從來沒有在自己的球迷面前打得這麼差。

③ Never + will / would / can + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Thank you for coming to help us after the fire. Never will we forget your kindness.
    謝謝你們在火災後來幫忙。我們永遠不會忘記你們的好意。
  2. Never will I fly with that airline again. They lost my bags twice.
    我再也不會搭那家航空公司的飛機了。他們弄丟我的行李兩次。
  3. Never would I have guessed that he was a famous singer.
    我怎麼也猜不到他是有名的歌手。
  4. Never could she understand why her brother left home.
    她怎麼也想不通她哥哥為什麼離家。
  5. We're very sorry about the mistake with your order. Never will we let this happen again.
    您的訂單出了錯，我們非常抱歉。我們絕對不會再讓這種事發生。

④ Never + did + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Never did I see him again after that summer.
    那個夏天以後，我就再也沒見過他。
  2. Never did I imagine that I would become a teacher.
    我從來沒想過自己會當老師。
  3. Never did she complain, even when she was very ill.
    就算病得很重，她也從來沒抱怨過。
  4. Never did we expect so many people to come to the concert.
    我們從來沒料到會有這麼多人來聽演唱會。
  5. Never did the old man tell anyone where he hid the money.
    那個老人從來沒告訴任何人他把錢藏在哪裡。

⑤ Never + do / does + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Never do I feel as relaxed as when I'm in the mountains.
    我從來沒有像在山上的時候那麼放鬆。
  2. Never does my father leave the house without his hat.
    我爸爸出門從來不會不戴帽子。

⑥ Never before / Never again + 倒裝

  1. Never before had the school won the national prize.
    這所學校以前從來沒得過全國獎。
  2. Never before have so many people used the internet to shop.
    以前從來沒有這麼多人用網路購物。
  3. Never again will I leave my homework until the last minute.
    我再也不會把功課拖到最後一刻了。
  4. Never again did they go camping in the rain.
    從那以後，他們就再也沒在雨中露營過了。

⑦ Never + was / were + 主詞

  1. We waited in the rain for an hour. Never was I so glad to see a taxi.
    我們在雨中等了一個小時。我從來沒有這麼高興看到計程車。
  2. Never were the streets so quiet as on the night of the snowstorm.
    街上從來沒有像暴風雪那天晚上那麼安靜。
  3. My grandparents loved visitors. Never was their house empty at weekends.
    我爺爺奶奶很喜歡有客人來。他們家週末從來沒有空過。
  4. Never was a holiday so badly planned. We missed every train.
    從來沒有一趟旅行計畫得這麼糟。每班火車我們都沒趕上。
