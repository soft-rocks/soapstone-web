He had promised that he would go to his grandparents' house that afternoon.

他之前答應過，那天下午要去他爺爺奶奶家。

以 ⁠-⁠⁠s 結⁠尾⁠的複⁠數名⁠詞只⁠加撇號

他之前答應過，那天下午要去他爺爺奶奶家。

撇號⁠的位⁠置說⁠明是一⁠個⁠人還⁠是好⁠幾⁠個人

He had promised that he would go to his grandparents' house that afternoon.

例句

① my / his / our + 複數名詞 + ' + 名詞

  1. Sorry, I can't play. I promised to go to my grandparents' house this afternoon.
    抱歉我不能去玩，我答應今天下午要去我爺爺奶奶家。
  2. Can you water the plants at my parents' flat while they're away?
    我爸媽不在的時候，你可以去他們公寓幫忙澆花嗎？
  3. Our neighbours' kids play football against our garage door.
    我們鄰居的小孩對著我們的車庫門踢足球。
  4. My brothers' bedroom is always a mess.
    我哥哥們的臥室總是亂七八糟。
  5. Their sons' school is just around the corner.
    他們兒子們的學校就在轉角。

② the + 複數名詞 + ' + 名詞

  1. It's essential to get customers' attention with special offers.
    用特價吸引顧客的注意非常重要。
  2. The students' exam results come out on Friday.
    學生的考試成績星期五公布。
  3. You can't go in there. That's the teachers' staff room.
    你不能進去，那是老師的辦公室。
  4. The players' families sat behind the goal.
    球員的家人坐在球門後面。
  5. The girls' changing room is on the left.
    女生更衣室在左邊。

③ 不以 -s 結尾的複數名詞 + 's

  1. The children's toys are all over the floor.
    孩子們的玩具丟得滿地都是。
  2. Where's the men's toilet, please?
    請問男廁在哪裡？
  3. She writes for a women's magazine.
    她替一本女性雜誌寫稿。
  4. People's opinions about the plan are very different.
    大家對這個計畫的看法差很多。

④ 時間 + ' + 名詞

  1. Our company is a digital company with 15 years' experience.
    我們是一家有十五年經驗的數位公司。
  2. The doctor told me to take two weeks' rest.
    醫生叫我休息兩個禮拜。
  3. It's about three hours' drive from here.
    從這裡開車大約三個小時。
  4. I get four weeks' holiday a year.
    我一年有四週的假。
  5. You need to give the landlord two months' notice.
    你要提前兩個月通知房東。

⑤ 單數 's 和複數 ' 比較

  1. My sister's room is next to mine.
    我姊姊的房間在我隔壁。
  2. My sisters' room is upstairs. They share it.
    我姊姊們的房間在樓上，她們共用一間。
  3. Someone stole the boy's bike outside the library.
    有人在圖書館外面偷了那個男孩的腳踏車。
  4. The boys' bikes were all in the garden.
    男孩們的腳踏車都在院子裡。

⑥ 複數名詞 + ' + 名詞當主詞

  1. The workers' new contracts start in May.
    工人們的新合約五月開始。
  2. My parents' car is older than me.
    我爸媽的車比我還老。
  3. The guests' rooms are ready.
    客人的房間準備好了。
  4. Our friends' wedding was in a castle.
    我們朋友的婚禮在一座城堡裡舉行。

⑦ 複數名詞 + ' + 名詞在介系詞後面

  1. I left my coat at the Smiths' house.
    我把外套留在 Smith 家了。
  2. We talked about the visitors' comments.
    我們討論了訪客的意見。
  3. She works in the nurses' office.
    她在護理師辦公室工作。
  4. Put your shoes by the kids' boots.
    把你的鞋子放在孩子們的靴子旁邊。
