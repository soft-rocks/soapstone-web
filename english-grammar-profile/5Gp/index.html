It was one of the best days of my life.

那是我這輩子最美好的日子之一。

one of the + 最⁠高⁠級後⁠面⁠接複⁠數名⁠詞

It was one of the best days of my life.

澀谷十字路口是東京最有名的景點之一。

one of the ... 當主⁠詞，動⁠詞用單⁠數

It was one of the best days of my life.

最好的球員之一今天生病了。

所⁠有⁠格可⁠以代⁠替 the

It was one of the best days of my life.

Ken is one of my oldest friends.

Ken 是我最老的朋友之一。

one of the 後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞用複⁠數

It was one of the best days of my life.

She's one of the best singer in Korea.

She's one of the best singers in Korea.

主⁠詞是 one，動⁠詞用單⁠數

It was one of the best days of my life.

One of my oldest friends live in Paris.

One of my oldest friends lives in Paris.

最⁠高⁠級前⁠面要加 the

It was one of the best days of my life.

It's one of best restaurants in town.

It's one of the best restaurants in town.

例句

① one of the best + 複數名詞

  1. Our trip to Kenting was one of the best holidays we've had.
    我們去墾丁的那趟是我們最棒的假期之一。
  2. This café makes one of the best cakes in town.
    這家咖啡店做的蛋糕是鎮上最好吃的之一。
  3. She's one of the best students in the school.
    她是學校裡最好的學生之一。
  4. That was one of the best films of the year.
    那是今年最好看的電影之一。
  5. He is one of the best cooks I know.
    他是我認識最會煮菜的人之一。

② one of the most + 形容詞 + 複數名詞

  1. Shibuya Crossing is one of the most famous sights in Tokyo.
    澀谷十字路口是東京最有名的景點之一。
  2. Rome is one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
    羅馬是世界上最美的城市之一。
  3. It's one of the most popular songs on the radio.
    那是電台上最受歡迎的歌之一。
  4. English is one of the most useful languages to learn.
    英文是最值得學的語言之一。
  5. This is one of the most difficult questions on the test.
    這是考試裡最難的題目之一。

③ one of the + 形容詞-est + 複數名詞

  1. It was one of the coldest winters in years.
    那是好幾年來最冷的冬天之一。
  2. Jade Mountain is one of the highest mountains in Asia.
    玉山是亞洲最高的山之一。
  3. He's one of the tallest boys in his class.
    他是班上最高的男生之一。
  4. It was one of the longest meetings I've been to.
    那是我開過最長的會議之一。
  5. It's one of the oldest temples in Tainan.
    那是台南最古老的廟之一。

④ one of my / our + 最高級 + 複數名詞

  1. Ken is one of my oldest friends.
    Ken 是我最老的朋友之一。
  2. That was one of our best years.
    那是我們最好的年頭之一。
  3. This is one of my warmest jackets.
    這是我最保暖的外套之一。
  4. It's one of her biggest dreams.
    那是她最大的夢想之一。
  5. This is one of their most popular dishes.
    這是他們最受歡迎的菜之一。

⑤ one of the ... 當主詞

  1. One of the best players is ill today.
    最好的球員之一今天生病了。
  2. One of my oldest friends lives in Paris.
    我最老的朋友之一住在巴黎。
  3. One of the biggest problems is the cost.
    最大的問題之一是費用。
  4. One of the nicest things about this town is the people.
    這個鎮最棒的地方之一是這裡的人。
  5. One of the most interesting rooms in the museum was closed.
    博物館裡最有趣的展間之一關閉了。
  6. Was the wedding one of the happiest days for your parents?
    婚禮是你爸媽最快樂的日子之一嗎？
  7. Is this one of the cheapest flights to Bangkok?
    這是飛曼谷最便宜的班機之一嗎？
  8. One of the hardest things was saying goodbye.
    最難的事情之一是說再見。
  9. One of the youngest players scored the goal.
    最年輕的球員之一進了那一球。
  10. One of the busiest streets in the city is near my office.
    這座城市最熱鬧的街之一在我辦公室附近。
