yet 放⁠在句⁠尾，說⁠到現⁠在還⁠沒⁠發⁠生

I haven't seen this film yet.

這部電影我還沒看過。

說話者打算看，只是到現在還沒看。

問⁠句也用 yet，回⁠答可⁠以只⁠說 Not yet.

I haven't seen this film yet.

問句

We're leaving for the cinema at six. Have you finished your homework yet?

我們六點要出發去電影院。你功課寫完了沒？
簡答

We've waited for twenty minutes. Has the bus come? — Not yet.

我們等了二十分鐘了。公車來了嗎？還沒。

yet 不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I haven't seen this film yet.

My brother hasn't cleaned yet his room.

My brother hasn't cleaned his room yet.

肯⁠定⁠句用 already，不⁠用 yet

I haven't seen this film yet.

I have written my essay for history class yet.

I have already written my essay for history class.

否⁠定⁠句用 yet，不⁠用 already

I haven't seen this film yet.

We haven't already chosen a name for the puppy.

We haven't chosen a name for the puppy yet.

still haven't 強⁠調等太久了

I haven't seen this film yet.

這部電影三月就上映了，我到現在還沒看。

already 用在肯⁠定⁠句

I haven't seen this film yet.

我們選別部吧。這部電影我已經看過了。

美⁠式口⁠語用過⁠去簡⁠單式加 yet

I haven't seen this film yet.

不要告訴我結局。這部電影我還沒看。

例句

① I haven't + 過去分詞 + yet

  1. Can I keep it until Friday? I haven't finished the book you lent me yet.
    我可以留到星期五嗎？你借我的書我還沒看完。
  2. Everyone says it's amazing, but I haven't seen Tom's new phone yet.
    大家都說它很厲害，可是我還沒看過湯姆的新手機。
  3. The wedding is next month, and I haven't bought a dress yet.
    婚禮在下個月，我還沒買洋裝。
  4. It's already three o'clock. I haven't had anything to eat yet today.
    已經三點了。我今天到現在還沒吃東西。
  5. The forms are due in January. I haven't decided which university to apply to yet.
    申請表一月要交。我還沒決定要申請哪一所大學。
  6. My birthday is in two weeks. I haven't sent out the invitations to the party yet.
    我的生日在兩個禮拜後。派對的邀請函我還沒寄出去。

② we / you / they + haven't + 過去分詞 + yet

  1. Our train gets in late. We haven't found a hotel near the station yet.
    我們的火車很晚才到。我們還沒找到車站附近的飯店。
  2. Their plane was late. Our friends from Canada haven't arrived yet.
    他們的飛機誤點了。我們從加拿大來的朋友還沒到。
  3. You're leaving school next month. You haven't told me your plans yet.
    你下個月就要畢業了。你還沒跟我說你的計畫。
  4. It's already the middle of December. We haven't put up the Christmas tree yet.
    已經十二月中了。我們還沒把聖誕樹擺出來。
  5. I've been out with Jack for a month. My parents haven't met him yet.
    我跟傑克交往一個月了。我爸媽還沒見過他。
  6. It's ten o'clock, and the children haven't gone to bed yet.
    已經十點了，孩子們還沒睡。

③ he / she / it + hasn't + 過去分詞 + yet

  1. I sent my boss the report this morning. She hasn't replied yet.
    我今天早上把報告寄給老闆。她還沒回覆。
  2. The concert is on Saturday. Ben hasn't given me the money for the tickets yet.
    演唱會在星期六。班還沒給我票錢。
  3. Don't worry, the film hasn't started yet. They're still showing adverts.
    別擔心，電影還沒開始。他們還在播廣告。
  4. My sister takes the bus to work. She hasn't passed her driving test yet.
    我妹妹搭公車上班。她還沒考過駕照。
  5. My aunt posted it two weeks ago, but the parcel hasn't come yet.
    我阿姨兩個禮拜前就寄了，可是包裹還沒到。
  6. Take your umbrella. It hasn't stopped raining yet.
    帶著你的傘。雨還沒停。

④ 問句：Have / Has + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + yet?

  1. You can't play outside now. Have you done your maths homework yet?
    你現在不能出去玩。你的數學作業寫了沒？
  2. Our clothes are piling up. Has anyone come to fix the washing machine yet?
    我們的衣服越堆越多。有人來修洗衣機了沒？
  3. The house next door was sold in May. Have the new neighbours introduced themselves yet?
    隔壁的房子五月賣掉了。新鄰居來打過招呼了沒？
  4. Tom said he'd call about the party. Has he called you yet?
    湯姆說他會打來說派對的事。他打給你了沒？
  5. There's a trip next week. Have you read the email from the school yet?
    下禮拜有校外教學。學校寄來的電子郵件你看了沒？

⑤ Not yet.

  1. Grandma is coming for dinner. Have you set the table? — Not yet.
    奶奶要來吃晚餐。你擺好餐具了嗎？還沒。
  2. We're leaving at six tomorrow. Have you packed your suitcase? — Not yet.
    我們明天六點出發。你的行李箱收好了嗎？還沒。
  3. You had a blood test last week. Has the hospital sent your results? — Not yet.
    你上禮拜驗了血。醫院把你的檢查結果寄來了嗎？還沒。

⑥ 說原因

  1. I can't tell you the ending of the book because I haven't finished it yet.
    我沒辦法跟你說這本書的結局，因為我還沒看完。
  2. We can't start the meeting because Sam hasn't arrived yet.
    我們還不能開會，因為山姆還沒到。
  3. Don't tell me the score. I haven't watched the match yet.
    不要跟我說比數，那場比賽我還沒看。
  4. I can't pay you back because I haven't received my wages yet.
    我還沒辦法還你錢，因為我還沒領到薪水。
  5. Please wait. The manager hasn't signed the form yet.
    請稍等，經理還沒簽那張表格。
