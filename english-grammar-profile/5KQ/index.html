I hope you haven't been working too hard!

希望你最近工作沒有太累！

haven't 加 been 和動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

希望你最近工作沒有太累！

朋友到新公司上班三個月了。

正⁠式⁠的文⁠字用 has not

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

Dear Sir, I am writing about your repair work. The heating system has not been working properly since your staff completed it.

敬啟者，我寫信是關於你們的維修工作。從你們的員工完工以後，暖氣系統就一直沒有正常運作。

否⁠定問⁠句把 haven't 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

你看起來好瘦。你一直沒吃東西嗎？

been 不⁠能漏⁠掉

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

My grandfather hasn't sleeping well since the operation.

My grandfather hasn't been sleeping well since the operation.

been 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

Our teacher hasn't been feel well lately.

Our teacher hasn't been feeling well lately.

not 加在 have 後⁠面，不⁠用 don't

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

We don't have been using the car much this month.

We haven't been using the car much this month.

簡⁠單式說還⁠沒⁠做⁠完，進⁠行⁠式說沒⁠有一⁠直⁠在做

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

現在完成簡單式

Can I keep it for another week? I haven't finished the book.

我可以再留一個禮拜嗎？那本書我還沒看完。
現在完成進行式

I've been so busy at work. I haven't been reading much lately.

我工作一直很忙。我最近都沒怎麼看書。

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式說過⁠去某⁠個時⁠間點

I hope you haven't been working too hard!

我那時候在花園。你打來的時候我沒在工作。

例句

① 主詞 + haven't been + 動詞-ing

  1. My neighbours have a new dog. I haven't been sleeping well.
    我的鄰居養了一隻新的狗。我最近都睡不好。
  2. We're saving for a house. We haven't been going out much.
    我們在存錢買房子。我們最近不太出門。
  3. You're looking at your phone again. You haven't been listening to a word I've said.
    你又在看手機了。我說的話你一個字都沒在聽。
  4. My two brothers had a fight at Christmas. They haven't been talking to each other since then.
    我兩個哥哥聖誕節打了一架。他們從那時候起就一直沒跟對方說話。
  5. The doctor says I need more vitamins. I haven't been eating enough vegetables.
    醫生說我需要更多維他命。我最近青菜吃得不夠。
  6. I'm worried about home. My parents haven't been getting on well recently.
    我很擔心家裡。我爸媽最近處得不太好。
  7. I'll stay at home tonight. I haven't been feeling very well this week.
    我今晚要待在家。我這個禮拜一直不太舒服。

② 主詞 + hasn't been + 動詞-ing

  1. I'm worried about Grandma. She hasn't been answering her phone.
    我很擔心奶奶。她一直沒接電話。
  2. I'm taking it to the garage. My car hasn't been running well since the winter.
    我要把它送去修車廠。我的車從冬天以後就一直跑得不太順。
  3. The teacher called Leo's parents. He hasn't been coming to class.
    老師打電話給李歐的爸媽。他一直沒來上課。
  4. We have to take the stairs. The lift hasn't been working for a week.
    我們得走樓梯。電梯壞了一個禮拜了。
  5. The farmers are worried. It hasn't been raining much this summer.
    農夫們很擔心。今年夏天沒怎麼下雨。
  6. We're taking her to the vet. Our cat hasn't been eating properly.
    我們要帶她去看獸醫。我們家的貓最近都吃得不好。

③ 正式的完整形式：have not / has not been + 動詞-ing

  1. Dear Sir, I am writing about our new office. The air conditioning has not been working since June.
    敬啟者，我寫信是關於我們的新辦公室。空調從六月起就一直沒有運作。
  2. Thank you for meeting me. I hope that I have not been wasting your time.
    謝謝您跟我見面。希望我沒有浪費您的時間。
  3. The report shows some problems. The company has not been paying its suppliers on time.
    報告顯示了一些問題。這家公司一直沒有準時付款給供應商。
  4. Dear Manager, residents have not been receiving their post regularly.
    經理您好，住戶一直沒有按時收到郵件。
  5. Dear Councillor, there is rubbish everywhere. The council has not been cleaning the streets.
    議員您好，到處都是垃圾。議會一直沒有清掃街道。

④ hope + 否定句

  1. Sorry the train was so late. I hope you haven't been waiting long.
    抱歉火車誤點那麼久。希望你沒有等很久。
  2. Thanks for babysitting tonight. I hope the kids haven't been giving you any trouble.
    謝謝你今晚幫忙帶小孩。希望孩子們沒給你添麻煩。
  3. I didn't call Mum all week. I hope she hasn't been worrying about me.
    我整個禮拜都沒打給媽媽。希望她沒有一直在擔心我。
  4. You've been very quiet in class. I hope you haven't been feeling ill.
    你上課的時候一直很安靜。希望你沒有不舒服。

⑤ 解釋原因

  1. I'm so tired. The baby hasn't been sleeping at night.
    我好累，寶寶晚上一直不睡。
  2. I've put on weight because I haven't been going to the gym.
    我變胖了，因為我一直沒去健身房。
  3. The plants are dying because I haven't been watering them.
    植物快死了，因為我一直沒澆水。
  4. He's going to fail the test because he hasn't been studying.
    他考試會不及格，因為他一直沒在念書。
  5. My English is getting worse. I haven't been practising.
    我的英文越來越差了，我一直沒在練習。

⑥ 問句：Haven't / Hasn't + 主詞 + been + 動詞-ing?

  1. Your cough is getting worse. Haven't you been taking your medicine?
    你咳得越來越嚴重了。你一直沒吃藥嗎？
  2. The landlord called about your flatmate. Hasn't he been paying the rent?
    房東打電話來說你室友的事。他一直沒付房租嗎？
  3. The teacher sent me a note about the twins. Haven't they been doing their homework?
    老師寫了張紙條給我說雙胞胎的事。他們一直沒寫功課嗎？
  4. I've written to you three times. Why haven't you been answering my emails?
    我寫信給你三次了。你為什麼一直沒回我的電子郵件？
