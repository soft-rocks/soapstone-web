What companies care about is money.

企業在乎的是錢。

What 子⁠句當主⁠詞，be 後⁠面放重⁠點

企業在乎的是錢。

分⁠裂句把重⁠點移⁠到句⁠尾

What companies care about is money.

一般的句子

My son wants a bike.

我兒子想要一台腳踏車。
分裂句

What my son wants is a bike.

我兒子想要的是一台腳踏車。

be 後⁠面可⁠以接名⁠詞片⁠語、不⁠定⁠詞、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞或 that 子⁠句

What companies care about is money.

名詞片語

What I need is a long holiday.

我需要的是一個長假。
to + 原形動詞

What tourists want is to see the typical sights.

遊客想要的是看到最有代表性的景點。
原形動詞

What I did was call the police.

我做的是打電話報警。
that 子句

What worries me is that nobody called.

讓我擔心的是沒有人打電話來。

be 的單複⁠數可⁠以跟⁠著重⁠點，時⁠態跟⁠著 What 子⁠句

What companies care about is money.

大家喜歡的是特效。

What 子句用過去式，be 通常也用過去式。

What 已⁠經是受⁠詞，子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

What companies care about is money.

What I need it is a coffee.

What I need is a coffee.

分⁠裂句⁠的主⁠詞用 What 開⁠頭

What companies care about is money.

That I love about summer is the long evenings.

What I love about summer is the long evenings.

What 子⁠句後⁠面要⁠有 be

What companies care about is money.

What I want a new phone.

What I want is a new phone.

What 子⁠句不⁠是問⁠句，不⁠加 do

What companies care about is money.

What do companies care about is money.

What companies care about is money.

例句

① What + 子句 + is + 名詞片語

  1. Footballers bring in money, and what companies care about is money.
    足球員能帶來錢，而企業在乎的是錢。
  2. What I need right now is a hot shower.
    我現在需要的是洗個熱水澡。
  3. Forget the new car park. What our village needs is a doctor's surgery.
    別管新停車場了，我們村子需要的是一家診所。
  4. What I enjoy about working from home is the quiet.
    在家工作我喜歡的是安靜。
  5. What she wants for her birthday is a puppy.
    她生日想要的是一隻小狗。

② What + 子句 + was + 名詞片語

  1. What everyone loved were the special effects.
    大家都喜歡的是特效。
  2. What surprised me was the price.
    讓我驚訝的是價錢。
  3. What I remember most is the smell of the sea.
    我記得最清楚的是海的味道。
  4. What we needed was more time.
    我們需要的是更多時間。
  5. We got to the airport and found that what he forgot was his passport.
    我們到了機場才發現，他忘了帶的是護照。

③ What + 子句 + be + to + 原形動詞

  1. What tourists want is to see the most typical things in England.
    遊客想要的是看到英格蘭最有代表性的東西。
  2. What I'd like to do is travel around Asia.
    我想做的是到亞洲各地旅行。
  3. What we want is to finish the project by Friday.
    我們想要的是星期五前把專案做完。
  4. My sister is saving hard. What she wants is to open her own café.
    我姊姊很努力存錢，她想要的是開一家自己的咖啡店。

④ What + 子句（有 do）+ be + 原形動詞

  1. What I did was call the police.
    我做的是打電話報警。
  2. If your neighbour is too loud, what you should do is write to the landlord.
    如果你鄰居太吵，你應該做的是寫信給房東。
  3. What we did next was walk to the beach.
    我們接下來做的是走去海邊。
  4. What he does every morning is go for a run.
    他每天早上做的是去跑步。

⑤ What + 子句 + be + that 子句

  1. What worries me is that nobody called.
    讓我擔心的是沒有人打電話來。
  2. What I don't understand is that she never told us.
    我不懂的是她從來沒跟我們說。
  3. What matters is that everyone is safe.
    重要的是大家都平安。
  4. What's strange is that the door was open.
    奇怪的是門是開著的。

⑥ What + 子句 + be + wh- 子句

  1. What I want to know is why he left.
    我想知道的是他為什麼離開。
  2. What nobody told me was how cold it would be.
    沒有人跟我說的是那裡會有多冷。
  3. What we need to decide is where to stay.
    我們要決定的是住哪裡。

⑦ 對話裡的 What 分裂句

  1. I don't need advice. What I need is a hug.
    我不需要建議，我需要的是一個擁抱。
  2. It's not the money. What bothers me is the way he spoke to me.
    重點不是錢，讓我不舒服的是他跟我說話的樣子。
  3. Don't buy me flowers. What I'd really like is a book.
    別買花給我，我真正想要的是一本書。
  4. What I love about this café is the music.
    我喜歡這家咖啡店的地方是音樂。
  5. What you need is a good night's sleep.
    你需要的是好好睡一覺。
  6. What the kids really want is someone to play with.
    孩子們真正想要的是有人陪他們玩。
