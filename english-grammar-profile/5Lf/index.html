I'm so happy because you can come!

你可以來，我真的好開心！

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

你可以來，我真的好開心！

really、extremely、pretty 加形⁠容⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

I'm so happy because you can come!

這本書對我很重要。

程⁠度從高到低：extremely、really、pretty、a bit

extremely

The new phones are extremely expensive.

新的手機貴得要命。
really、so

Your little girl is so clever.

你的小女兒好聰明。
pretty

Her French is pretty good.

她的法文滿好的。
a bit、a little

The test was a bit difficult.

考試有點難。

好⁠的性⁠質不⁠用 a bit，用 quite 或 pretty

食物還滿好吃的。

a bit 和形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間不⁠加 of

I'm a bit of tired today.

I'm a bit tired today.

a 後⁠面用 really，不⁠用 so

I'm so happy because you can come!

She's a so kind person.

She's a really kind person.

fantastic 前⁠面用 really，不⁠用 extremely

The idea was extremely fantastic.

The idea was really fantastic.

單⁠數可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 a

He's really tall man.

He's a really tall man.

a little 放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面表⁠示程⁠度，放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面表⁠示數⁠量

形容詞前面

The soup is a little salty.

湯有一點鹹。
名詞前面

Can I have a little salt?

可以給我一點鹽嗎？

pretty 放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面表⁠示相⁠當，放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面表⁠示漂⁠亮

程度副詞

The test was pretty easy.

考試滿簡單的。
形容詞

She's a pretty girl.

她是個漂亮的女孩。

例句

① really + 形容詞

  1. The chicken at that market is really good.
    那個市場的雞肉真的很好吃。
  2. My new boss is really nice.
    我的新老闆人真的很好。
  3. The roads were really icy this morning.
    今天早上路面結冰結得很厲害。
  4. You look really tired. Go to bed.
    你看起來真的很累。去睡覺吧。

② a + really / extremely / pretty + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. We had a really good time at the wedding.
    我們在婚禮上玩得很開心。
  2. That's a really interesting idea.
    那是個很有意思的想法。
  3. It was an extremely long journey.
    那是一趟非常漫長的旅程。
  4. My brother is a pretty good swimmer.
    我哥哥游泳游得滿好的。

③ extremely + 形容詞

  1. The new phones are extremely expensive.
    新的手機貴得要命。
  2. The teacher was extremely patient with us.
    老師對我們非常有耐心。
  3. It's extremely important to drink enough water.
    喝足夠的水非常重要。
  4. The path up the hill is extremely narrow.
    上山的小路非常窄。

④ so + 形容詞

  1. I'm so sorry I'm late.
    真的很抱歉我遲到了。
  2. Your little girl is so clever.
    你的小女兒好聰明。
  3. Why is the house so cold?
    房子怎麼這麼冷？
  4. We were so excited about the trip.
    我們對這趟旅行好興奮。

⑤ pretty + 形容詞

  1. The bus driver was pretty friendly to the tourists.
    公車司機對觀光客還滿友善的。
  2. I'm pretty sure he's at home.
    我滿確定他在家。
  3. Her French is pretty good.
    她的法文滿好的。
  4. The match was pretty boring.
    那場比賽滿無聊的。

⑥ a bit + 形容詞

  1. I'm a bit hungry. Let's eat.
    我有點餓。我們吃飯吧。
  2. This coat is a bit small for me.
    這件外套我穿有點小。
  3. The film was a bit long.
    那部電影有點長。
  4. He seemed a bit sad on the phone.
    他在電話裡聽起來有點難過。

⑦ a little + 形容詞

  1. I feel a little sick.
    我覺得有點不舒服。
  2. The instructions are a little confusing.
    說明有點難懂。
  3. My grandmother is a little deaf.
    我奶奶耳朵有點背。
  4. The water was a little cold, but we swam anyway.
    水有點冷，可是我們還是下去游了。

⑧ 否定句和疑問句

  1. The towels in the bathroom weren't really clean.
    浴室裡的毛巾不太乾淨。
  2. Are you a bit nervous about the exam?
    你對考試有點緊張嗎？
  3. Were the tickets extremely expensive?
    票是不是貴得要命？
  4. Is the museum really far from here?
    博物館離這裡真的很遠嗎？
