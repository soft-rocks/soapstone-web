I go to college every day.

我每天都去學校。

介⁠系⁠詞 + 名⁠詞，不⁠加冠⁠詞

I go to college every day.

我每天都去學校。

常⁠見⁠的固⁠定說⁠法

I go to college every day.

學校go to school、at school、go to college、at university
工作go to work、at work、from work
家at home、go home
床go to bed、in bed
交通工具by car、by bus、by train、on foot

加⁠了 the，說⁠的⁠是建⁠築

go to school

My son goes to school by bus.

我兒子搭公車上學。
go to the school

I went to the school to talk to his teacher.

我去學校跟他的老師談話。

go to bed 不⁠加 the

I go to the bed at eleven.

I go to bed at eleven.

by + 交⁠通⁠工⁠具不⁠加冠⁠詞

We went to Paris by the train.

We went to Paris by train.

go home 不⁠加 to

I go to home after school.

I go home after school.

走⁠路說 on foot

I go to work by foot.

I go to work on foot.

例句

① to school、to college

  1. My little brother started going to school last year.
    我弟弟去年開始上學。
  2. She goes to college in the evening after work.
    她下班後晚上去上課。
  3. My daughter is at school until three.
    我女兒在學校上課到三點。
  4. I want to go to university in Canada.
    我想去加拿大讀大學。

② at work、to work

  1. My mum is at work until six.
    我媽媽上班到六點。
  2. I usually walk to work in the summer.
    我夏天通常走路上班。
  3. Dad gets home from work at seven.
    爸爸七點下班回到家。
  4. Please don't phone me at work.
    請不要在我上班時打電話給我。

③ at home、go home

  1. I stay at home on rainy days.
    下雨天我都待在家裡。
  2. Is your sister at home now?
    你姊姊現在在家嗎？
  3. After the party we went home in a taxi.
    派對結束後我們搭計程車回家。
  4. My grandfather works from home three days a week.
    我爺爺一星期有三天在家工作。
  5. Ana left home at eighteen.
    Ana 十八歲就離家了。

④ to bed、in bed

  1. The children go to bed at eight.
    孩子們八點上床睡覺。
  2. I was ill and stayed in bed all day.
    我生病了，整天躺在床上。
  3. Mia reads in bed every night before she sleeps.
    Mia 每天晚上睡前在床上看書。
  4. What time did you go to bed last night?
    你昨晚幾點睡？

⑤ by + 交通工具

  1. I go to the city centre by bus.
    我搭公車去市中心。
  2. We travelled from London to Rome by plane.
    我們從倫敦搭飛機到羅馬。
  3. It takes two hours by train.
    搭火車要兩個小時。
  4. My uncle goes everywhere by bike.
    我叔叔去哪裡都騎腳踏車。
  5. We can get there by taxi in ten minutes.
    搭計程車十分鐘就到了。

⑥ on foot

  1. The beach is ten minutes from here on foot.
    從這裡走路到海灘要十分鐘。
  2. We explored the old town on foot.
    我們走路逛老城區。
  3. Most students come to school on foot.
    大部分學生走路上學。

⑦ 加 the 時意思不同

  1. Mum went to the school to pick up my lunch box.
    媽媽去學校拿我的便當盒。
  2. Please put the clean sheets on the bed.
    請把乾淨的床單鋪在床上。
  3. I sat on the bed and opened the letter.
    我坐在床上，打開那封信。
  4. Dad went to the college to see the new library.
    爸爸去那所學院看新的圖書館。
  5. There's a big tree behind the school.
    學校後面有一棵大樹。
