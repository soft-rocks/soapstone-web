例句

① going to be + 過去分詞

  1. They filmed our school for a TV programme. It is going to be shown this Friday.
    他們為一個電視節目來我們學校拍片。它這個星期五會播出。
  2. Take a photo of it now. Our old school building is going to be knocked down next year.
    現在拍張照吧。我們學校的舊大樓明年要拆了。
  3. Don't eat all the cake. It's going to be served at the party tonight.
    蛋糕不要吃光。今晚派對要端出來的。
  4. We finished the exams today. The results are going to be announced on Monday morning.
    我們今天考完了。結果星期一早上會公布。
  5. Grandma's birthday is next week. She's going to be given a surprise party.
    奶奶下星期生日。會有人幫她辦一個驚喜派對。
  6. The match has been moved. It's going to be played on Sunday instead.
    比賽改時間了。改到星期天比。

② have to be + 過去分詞

  1. You can come on the trip, but this form has to be signed by your parents.
    你可以參加旅行，可是這張表格必須讓你爸媽簽名。
  2. After that, they printed a form which had to be signed by my parents because I'm under eighteen.
    接著他們印了一張表格，因為我未滿十八歲，必須讓我爸媽簽名。
  3. Our teacher was very clear. All essays have to be handed in by Friday.
    我們老師說得很清楚。所有的作文都必須在星期五前交。
  4. I work at a hotel on Saturdays. The rooms have to be cleaned before the next guests arrive.
    我星期六在旅館打工。房間必須在下一批客人到之前打掃好。
  5. I was busy on the last day of term. The library books had to be returned before the holidays.
    學期最後一天我很忙。圖書館的書放假前必須還。
  6. Don't leave it on the table. This medicine has to be kept in the fridge.
    別放在桌上。這種藥必須放冰箱。

③ need to be + 過去分詞

  1. Look at the grass. It needs to be cut.
    你看那片草。該割了。
  2. My bike is making a strange noise. It needs to be repaired.
    我的腳踏車發出奇怪的聲音。需要修了。
  3. We leave for camp on Saturday. These shirts need to be washed before the trip.
    我們星期六要去露營。這些襯衫出發前要洗。
  4. Thanks for looking after our flat. The plants need to be watered every two days.
    謝謝你幫我們顧公寓。這些植物每兩天要澆一次水。
  5. Nobody had lived in the house for years. The windows were so dirty they needed to be cleaned twice.
    那棟房子好幾年沒人住。窗戶髒到要擦兩次。
  6. Welcome to the airport hotel, sir. Your passport needs to be checked at the desk.
    先生，歡迎來到機場旅館。您的護照要在櫃台檢查。

④ want to be + 過去分詞

  1. Please send me the numbers soon. My manager wants to be informed about sales by the end of November.
    請盡快把數字寄給我。我的主管希望十一月底前有人告訴他銷售的情況。
  2. I don't want to be disturbed while I'm studying, so I turned my phone off.
    我讀書的時候不想被打擾，所以把手機關了。
  3. Nobody wants to be left out, so we invited the whole class.
    沒有人想被排擠，所以我們邀了全班。
  4. My little brother wants to be taken to the zoo for his birthday.
    我弟弟生日想要有人帶他去動物園。
  5. My sister changed her clothes before the party. She didn't want to be seen in her school uniform.
    我姊姊去派對前換了衣服。她不想被人看到她穿學校制服。

⑤ 其他說法 + to be + 過去分詞

  1. Tom and Anna are getting married. I'd like to be invited to their wedding.
    Tom 和 Anna 要結婚了。我希望他們的婚禮有邀請我。
  2. Our kids always ask for a story. They love to be read to at bedtime.
    我們家的孩子都會要聽故事。他們很喜歡睡前有人唸書給他們聽。
  3. The trials for the school team are tomorrow. I hope to be chosen this year.
    校隊明天選拔。我希望今年能被選上。

⑥ 問答

  1. "When are the photos going to be printed?" "Tomorrow, I think."
    照片什麼時候會印出來？我想明天吧。
  2. "Does this have to be done today?" "Yes, before five."
    這件事一定要今天做完嗎？對，五點前。
  3. "Why are you taking the car to the garage?" "The brakes need to be checked."
    你為什麼要把車開去修車廠？煞車要檢查了。
  4. "Do you want to be picked up from the station?" "Yes, please. My train gets in at six."
    你要不要人去車站接你？好，麻煩了。我的火車六點到。
  5. "Is your room going to be painted?" "Yes, my dad is doing it this weekend."
    你的房間要粉刷嗎？對，我爸爸這個週末要刷。
