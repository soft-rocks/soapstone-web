例句

① has been being + 過去分詞 + for

  1. I'm late again. The bridge has been being repaired for three months, so there's always traffic.
    我又遲到了。這座橋已經修了三個月了，所以一直塞車。
  2. Our kitchen has been being redone for weeks. We're eating takeaway every night.
    我們的廚房已經重新裝修好幾個星期了。我們每天晚上都吃外帶。
  3. That new hotel has been being built for two years, and it still isn't finished.
    那間新旅館已經蓋了兩年，還是沒蓋好。
  4. The plan has been being discussed for months, but nobody has made a decision.
    這個計畫已經討論好幾個月了，可是還沒有人做決定。
  5. My car has been being fixed for a week. I'm tired of taking the bus.
    我的車已經修了一個星期。我搭公車搭膩了。
  6. The case has been being investigated for over a year.
    這個案子已經調查了一年多。

② have been being + 過去分詞

  1. The old trees along the river have been being cut down for weeks.
    河邊的老樹已經砍了好幾個星期了。
  2. The exhibition opens next week. These paintings have been being cleaned for months.
    展覽下星期開幕。這些畫已經清理了好幾個月。
  3. The roads in the city centre have been being dug up all summer.
    市中心的路整個夏天都在挖。
  4. Their emails have been being read by the company for years.
    他們的電子郵件多年來一直被公司偷看。

③ since + 時間

  1. The museum has been being renovated since last spring.
    博物館從去年春天就開始翻修了。
  2. The road outside our house has been being widened since January.
    我們家外面的路從一月就開始拓寬了。
  3. The new law has been being debated since the election.
    新法律從選舉後就一直在辯論。
  4. The station has been being rebuilt since 2022.
    車站從 2022 年就開始改建了。

④ 人當主詞

  1. I think I've been being followed all week. I keep seeing the same car.
    我覺得我整個星期都被跟蹤。我一直看到同一輛車。
  2. She's been being treated for back pain since the accident.
    她從那次意外以後就一直在治療背痛。
  3. The new players have been being trained by the coach since the start of the season.
    新球員從球季開始就一直由教練訓練。
  4. He's been being bullied at school for months, and nobody noticed.
    他在學校被欺負好幾個月了，可是沒人發現。

⑤ 抱怨

  1. The lift has been being repaired for ages. When will it work again?
    電梯已經修了好久了。什麼時候才會好？
  2. My complaint has been being looked at for six weeks. That's far too long.
    我的投訴已經處理了六個星期。實在太久了。
  3. The noise is terrible. The building next door has been being knocked down since Monday.
    噪音很可怕。隔壁的大樓從星期一就在拆了。
  4. This report has been being written for a month. Can we finish it this week?
    這份報告已經寫了一個月了。我們這星期可以寫完嗎？

⑥ 和其他說法比較

  1. The bridge has been being repaired for months. They've been repairing it since June.
    這座橋已經修了好幾個月了。他們從六月就開始修。
  2. The school has been being painted all week, but the classrooms are still not ready.
    學校整個星期都在油漆，可是教室還沒準備好。
  3. The house has been being cleaned since nine, and it's nearly done.
    房子從九點就開始打掃，快打掃完了。
  4. The documents have been being checked for days. They're under review.
    文件已經檢查好幾天了。還在審查中。

⑦ 新聞和報告

  1. The missing climbers have been being searched for since Tuesday.
    從星期二開始，搜救人員就在找失蹤的登山客。
  2. The problem has been being studied by scientists for many years.
    科學家已經研究這個問題很多年了。
  3. Prices have been being pushed up by the cost of energy.
    物價一直被能源成本推高。

⑧ 問句

  1. "How long has the road been being repaired?" "Since the spring, I think."
    這條路已經修多久了？我想是從春天開始。
  2. "Has the machine been being used all day?" "Yes, it's very hot now."
    這台機器整天都在用嗎？對，現在很燙。
