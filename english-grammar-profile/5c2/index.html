Thank you for the present you sent me. You shouldn't have!

謝謝你的錢，不過你真的不用寄給我的。

You shouldn't have!

Thank you for the present you sent me. You shouldn't have!

You shouldn't have!你太客氣了！動詞省略。
You shouldn't have + 過去分詞說出具體的事，像 bought、sent、gone to all this trouble。

先說謝⁠謝

Thank you for the present you sent me. You shouldn't have!

例句

Thank you so much. You shouldn't have!

非常謝謝你。你太客氣了！

這個說法常跟 Thank you 一起用。

send 的過⁠去⁠分⁠詞是 sent

Thank you for the present you sent me. You shouldn't have!

You shouldn't have sended it.

You shouldn't have sent it.

這⁠個說⁠法是 shouldn't have 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Thank you for the present you sent me. You shouldn't have!

You didn't should buy me flowers.

You shouldn't have bought me flowers.

禮⁠物已⁠經買⁠了，要⁠用 shouldn't have 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Thank you for the present you sent me. You shouldn't have!

Oh, chocolates! You shouldn't buy them!

Oh, chocolates! You shouldn't have bought them!

例句

① Thank you …, you shouldn't have!

  1. Thank you for the lovely scarf you sent me for my birthday. You shouldn't have!
    謝謝你寄給我的那條漂亮圍巾當生日禮物。你太客氣了！
  2. Flowers for me? Oh, thank you, you shouldn't have!
    送我的花？噢，謝謝你，你太客氣了！
  3. A box of chocolates! Thank you so much, you shouldn't have.
    一盒巧克力！非常謝謝你，你太客氣了。
  4. Thank you for the cake. You shouldn't have, but it looks delicious.
    謝謝你的蛋糕。你太客氣了，可是看起來很好吃。
  5. Tickets for the concert? Thank you, you really shouldn't have!
    演唱會的票？謝謝你，你真的太客氣了！
  6. Thank you for the book you left on my desk. You shouldn't have!
    謝謝你放在我桌上的書。你太客氣了！

② You shouldn't have + sent / bought + 名詞

  1. Thank you for the money, but you shouldn't have sent so much.
    謝謝你給的錢，可是你不必寄那麼多。
  2. You shouldn't have bought me such an expensive present.
    你不必買這麼貴的禮物給我的。
  3. You shouldn't have sent flowers all the way from Spain.
    你不必從西班牙寄花來的。
  4. You shouldn't have bought tickets for all of us.
    你不必幫我們所有人買票的。
  5. You shouldn't have brought wine. We have plenty.
    你不必帶酒來的。我們有很多。
  6. You shouldn't have paid for the meal. It was my turn.
    你不必請這頓飯的。這次輪到我。

③ You shouldn't have gone to all this trouble：對方花了很多心思

  1. What a wonderful dinner! You shouldn't have gone to all this trouble.
    好棒的晚餐！你不必這麼費心的。
  2. You shouldn't have cleaned the whole house for us.
    你不必為了我們打掃整間房子的。
  3. You shouldn't have driven all this way just to bring me a present.
    你不必只為了送我禮物開這麼遠的車來的。
  4. You shouldn't have made a cake. A card would have been enough.
    你不必做蛋糕的。一張卡片就夠了。
  5. You shouldn't have waited for me in the rain.
    你不必在雨中等我的。
  6. You shouldn't have taken a day off work to help me move.
    你不必為了幫我搬家請一天假的。

④ Oh, you shouldn't have! It's + 形容詞

  1. Oh, you shouldn't have! It's beautiful.
    噢，你太客氣了！好漂亮。
  2. A new watch? You shouldn't have! It's perfect.
    一支新手錶？你太客氣了！太完美了。
  3. Oh, you shouldn't have! It's exactly what I wanted.
    噢，你太客氣了！這正是我想要的。
  4. You shouldn't have! It's far too generous.
    你太客氣了！這太大方了。
  5. Oh, you shouldn't have! It's my favourite colour.
    噢，你太客氣了！這是我最喜歡的顏色。
  6. A handmade card? You shouldn't have! It's lovely.
    手作卡片？你太客氣了！好可愛。

⑤ You shouldn't have, but + 句子：收下禮物

  1. You shouldn't have, but I'm so glad you did.
    你太客氣了，可是我很高興你這麼做。
  2. You shouldn't have, but thank you. I'll wear it tonight.
    你太客氣了，可是謝謝你。我今天晚上就會穿。
  3. You shouldn't have, but the children will love it.
    你太客氣了，可是孩子們會很喜歡。
  4. You shouldn't have, but it's very kind of you.
    你太客氣了，可是你真好心。
  5. You shouldn't have, but I'll put it on the wall right away.
    你太客氣了，可是我馬上把它掛到牆上。
  6. You shouldn't have, but it will be very useful in my new flat.
    你太客氣了，可是它在我的新公寓會很有用。
  7. You shouldn't have, but I'll think of you every time I use it.
    你太客氣了，可是我每次用的時候都會想到你。
