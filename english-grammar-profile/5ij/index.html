It was the cheapest in this shop!

那是這家店裡最便宜的！

單⁠音⁠節形⁠容⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠est 是最⁠高⁠級

It was the cheapest in this shop!

old → oldest

My grandfather is the oldest man in the village.

我爺爺是村子裡年紀最大的人。
tall → tallest

That's the tallest building in the city.

那是這座城市最高的大樓。
long → longest

The Nile is the longest river in Africa.

尼羅河是非洲最長的河。

前⁠面說⁠過是什⁠麼，名⁠詞可⁠以不⁠寫

It was the cheapest in this shop!

I bought the shirt because it was the cheapest.

我買那件襯衫，因為它最便宜。

加⁠了 ⁠-⁠est，前⁠面不⁠加 most

This is the cheapest bag.

最⁠高⁠級前⁠面要加 the

It was the cheapest in this shop!

單⁠音⁠節形⁠容⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠est，不⁠用 most

It was the cheapest in this shop!

That's the most fast car in the race.

That's the fastest car in the race.

例句

① cheapest / oldest

  1. I only have fifty dollars. Which is the cheapest phone you sell?
    我只有五十塊，你們賣的最便宜的手機是哪一支？
  2. I bought the shirt because it was the cheapest.
    我買那件襯衫，因為它最便宜。
  3. What's the cheapest way to get to the airport?
    到機場最便宜的方法是什麼？
  4. My grandfather is the oldest man in the village.
    我爺爺是村子裡年紀最大的人。
  5. People say the oak by the gate is the oldest tree in the park.
    大家說門邊那棵橡樹是公園裡最老的樹。
  6. Who's the oldest in your class?
    你們班上誰年紀最大？

② tallest / smallest

  1. My brother is the tallest in our family.
    我哥哥是我們家最高的。
  2. That's the tallest building in the city.
    那是這座城市最高的大樓。
  3. I'd like the smallest size, please.
    請給我最小的尺寸。
  4. Our flat is the smallest one on the floor.
    我們家是這層樓最小的一戶。
  5. The smallest dog in the park is the loudest.
    公園裡最小的那隻狗叫得最大聲。

③ longest / fastest

  1. The Nile is the longest river in Africa.
    尼羅河是非洲最長的河。
  2. Monday felt like the longest day of the week.
    星期一感覺是一週裡最漫長的一天。
  3. That's the fastest train in the country.
    那是全國最快的火車。
  4. Who is the fastest swimmer in your class?
    你們班誰游得最快？
  5. Take the fastest road, not the prettiest.
    走最快的路，不要走最漂亮的那條。

④ youngest / 其他

  1. Ann was the youngest at the party, so she went home first.
    Ann 是派對上年紀最小的，所以她最先回家。
  2. The youngest player on the team is fifteen.
    隊上最年輕的球員十五歲。
  3. Put the milk in the garage. It's the coldest place we have.
    把牛奶放到車庫，那是我們家最冷的地方。
  4. That was the hardest test this year.
    那是今年最難的考試。
  5. We saw the most stars on the darkest night of the trip.
    我們在旅行中最黑的那一晚看到最多星星。
  6. He's the kindest man I know.
    他是我認識最好心的人。

⑤ the + 形容詞-est，省略名詞

  1. Look at all these melons. Which one is the sweetest?
    你看這些瓜，哪一顆最甜？
  2. We tried all the cafés, and this one is the cheapest.
    我們每家咖啡店都試過了，這家最便宜。
  3. Of all the roads, the main road is the quickest.
    所有路裡面，大馬路最快。
  4. Of our three cats, the grey one is the oldest and the laziest.
    我們家三隻貓裡，灰色那隻年紀最大，也最懶。
  5. I washed all the towels. The blue one is the softest.
    我洗了所有毛巾，藍色那條最柔軟。
  6. We looked at three beds, and this one is the longest.
    我們看了三張床，這張最長。
  7. I tried on five pairs of boots. These are the warmest.
    我試穿了五雙靴子，這雙最保暖。
  8. All the boys are strong, but Bob is the strongest.
    男生們都很強壯，不過 Bob 最強壯。
