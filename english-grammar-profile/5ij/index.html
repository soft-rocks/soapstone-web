It was the cheapest in this shop!

那是這家店裡最便宜的！

cheap 加 ⁠-⁠est 是最⁠高⁠級，前⁠面加 the

那是這家店裡最便宜的！

前⁠文說⁠過是什⁠麼，最⁠高⁠級後⁠面可⁠以不⁠接名⁠詞

It was the cheapest in this shop!

We tried all the cafés near the office, and this one is the cheapest.

我們把辦公室附近的咖啡店都試過了，這家最便宜。

My brother plays basketball. He's the tallest in our family.

我哥哥打籃球。他是我們家最高的。

一⁠般⁠的單⁠音⁠節形⁠容⁠詞直⁠接加 ⁠-⁠est

It was the cheapest in this shop!

old → oldest

People say the oak by the gate is the oldest tree in the park.

大家說門邊那棵橡樹是公園裡最老的樹。
tall → tallest

That's the tallest building in the city.

那是這座城市最高的大樓。
slow → slowest

We took the slowest train, so the trip took six hours.

我們搭了最慢的火車，所以這趟路花了六個小時。

字尾是 e，只⁠加 ⁠-⁠st

nice → nicest

She was the nicest person on the tour.

她是這趟旅行裡人最好的。
large → largest

Blue whales are the largest animals in the world.

藍鯨是世界上最大的動物。
safe → safest

That's the safest place for it.

那是放它最安全的地方。

字尾是一⁠個母⁠音加一⁠個子⁠音，重⁠複子⁠音再⁠加 ⁠-⁠est

big → biggest

Can we book the biggest room?

我們可以訂最大的那間嗎？
hot → hottest

July is usually the hottest month here, so we go to the beach then.

這裡七月通常最熱，所以我們那時候去海邊。
sad → saddest

It was the saddest part.

那是最悲傷的一段。

字尾是子⁠音加 y，y 改⁠成 i 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠est

dry → driest

It hardly ever rains in March. That's the driest month of the year.

三月幾乎不下雨。那是一年裡最乾的月份。

good 和 bad 的最⁠高⁠級不⁠加 ⁠-⁠est

good → best

It's the best present I got this year.

這是我今年收到最好的禮物。
bad → worst

It was the worst day of my holiday.

那是我假期裡最糟的一天。

所⁠有⁠格後⁠面⁠的最⁠高⁠級不⁠加 the

My oldest friend lives in Canada now, so we only talk on the phone.

我最老的朋友現在住在加拿大，所以我們只講電話。

Our youngest daughter starts school in September.

我們的小女兒九月開始上學。

加⁠了 ⁠-⁠est，前⁠面不⁠加 most

It was the cheapest in this shop!

Lily chose the most cheapest dress in the shop.

Lily chose the cheapest dress in the shop.

最⁠高⁠級前⁠面要加 the

It was the cheapest in this shop!

She is youngest person in the office.

She is the youngest person in the office.

單⁠音⁠節形⁠容⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠est，不⁠用 most

It was the cheapest in this shop!

That's the most fast car in the race.

That's the fastest car in the race.

big 的 g 要重⁠複，再⁠加 ⁠-⁠est

Tokyo is the bigest city in Japan.

Tokyo is the biggest city in Japan.

good 的最⁠高⁠級是 best

Mia makes the goodest cakes in our street.

Mia makes the best cakes in our street.

例句

① 加 -est

  1. My phone broke, and I only have fifty dollars. Which is the cheapest phone you sell?
    我的手機壞了，我只有五十塊。你們賣的最便宜的手機是哪一支？
  2. I don't want to pay for a taxi. What's the cheapest way to get to the airport?
    我不想付計程車錢。到機場最便宜的方法是什麼？
  3. My grandfather was born in 1925. He's the oldest man in the village.
    我爺爺 1925 年出生。他是村子裡年紀最大的人。
  4. People say the oak by the gate is the oldest tree in the park.
    大家說門邊那棵橡樹是公園裡最老的樹。
  5. That's the tallest building in the city. You can see it from everywhere.
    那是這座城市最高的大樓。你在哪裡都看得到它。
  6. These gloves are too big. I'd like the smallest size, please.
    這雙手套太大了。請給我最小的尺寸。
  7. We sailed down the Nile last winter. The Nile is the longest river in Africa.
    我們去年冬天沿著尼羅河航行。尼羅河是非洲最長的河。
  8. I had four meetings and a test. Monday felt like the longest day of the week.
    我開了四個會，還有一場考試。星期一感覺是一週裡最漫長的一天。
  9. The Shinkansen goes three hundred kilometres an hour. That's the fastest train in Japan.
    新幹線一小時開三百公里。那是日本最快的火車。
  10. Our team has players from fifteen to thirty-five. The youngest player is fifteen.
    我們隊的球員從十五歲到三十五歲。最年輕的球員十五歲。
  11. Put the milk in the garage. It's the coldest place we have.
    把牛奶放到車庫，那是我們家最冷的地方。
  12. Mr Ford drives the old lady next door to the doctor every week. He's the kindest man I know.
    Ford 先生每個星期都開車載隔壁的老太太去看醫生。他是我認識最好心的人。
  13. We took the slowest train, so the trip took six hours.
    我們搭了最慢的火車，所以這趟路花了六個小時。
  14. Have you seen my dad's phone? It's the newest model, and it was very expensive.
    你看過我爸的手機嗎？那是最新的型號，很貴。

② 字尾是 e，加 -st

  1. The guide was very friendly. She was the nicest person on the tour.
    導遊很友善。她是這趟旅行裡人最好的。
  2. Blue whales are the largest animals in the world.
    藍鯨是世界上最大的動物。
  3. Keep your passport in the hotel. That's the safest place for it.
    把護照放在飯店裡。那是放它最安全的地方。

③ 重複字尾的子音，再加 -est

  1. We need a lot of space for the party. Can we book the biggest room?
    派對需要很大的空間。我們可以訂最大的那間嗎？
  2. July is usually the hottest month here, so we go to the beach then.
    這裡七月通常最熱，所以我們那時候去海邊。
  3. The dog died at the end of the film. It was the saddest part.
    電影最後那隻狗死了。那是最悲傷的一段。

④ 把 y 改成 i，再加 -est

  1. It hardly ever rains in March. That's the driest month of the year.
    三月幾乎不下雨。那是一年裡最乾的月份。
  2. Keep the flour in the cupboard above the oven. It's the driest place in the kitchen.
    把麵粉放在烤箱上面的櫃子裡。那是廚房裡最乾燥的地方。

⑤ the + 形容詞-est，不接名詞

  1. Look at all these melons. Which one is the sweetest?
    你看這些瓜，哪一顆最甜？
  2. We tried all the cafés near the office, and this one is the cheapest.
    我們把辦公室附近的咖啡店都試過了，這家最便宜。
  3. My brother plays basketball. He's the tallest in our family.
    我哥哥打籃球。他是我們家最高的。
  4. Of our three cats, the grey one is the oldest and the fattest.
    我們家三隻貓裡，灰色那隻年紀最大，也最胖。
  5. I'm going to Finland in January. I tried on five pairs of boots, and these are the warmest.
    我一月要去芬蘭。我試穿了五雙靴子，這雙最保暖。
  6. The boys lifted the boat out of the water. They're all strong, but Bob is the strongest.
    男生們把船抬出水面。他們都很強壯，不過 Bob 最強壯。

⑥ 所有格加最高級

  1. My oldest friend lives in Canada now, so we only talk on the phone.
    我最老的朋友現在住在加拿大，所以我們只講電話。
  2. Our youngest daughter starts school in September.
    我們的小女兒九月開始上學。

⑦ best 和 worst

  1. Thanks for the scarf. It's the best present I got this year.
    謝謝你送的圍巾。這是我今年收到最好的禮物。
  2. Which is the best way to the station from here?
    從這裡去車站，哪條路最好走？
  3. I got lost and missed the train. It was the worst day of my holiday.
    我迷路了，還錯過火車。那是我假期裡最糟的一天。
  4. Don't go in August. That's the worst time to visit, because it's so crowded.
    不要八月去。那是最不適合去的時候，因為人太多了。
