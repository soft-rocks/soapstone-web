It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.

這個故事講的是有一個老人，他做了一個會活過來的木偶。

說故⁠事用 this 介⁠紹新⁠的人

有一個老人，他做了一個木偶

a 和 this

It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.

一般的說法

There's an old man who makes a puppet.

有一個老人做了一個木偶。
說故事

There's this old man who makes a puppet.

有這麼一個老人，他做了一個木偶。

用在哪⁠裡

It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.

講笑話

So this man walks into a bar.

有一個男人走進一家酒吧。
說情節

There's this girl who can talk to animals.

有一個女孩會跟動物說話。
說自己的事

I met this guy on the train.

我在火車上認識一個人。

複⁠數名⁠詞用 these

It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.

There were this two boys who lived in a forest.

There were these two boys who lived in a forest.

this 後⁠面要接名⁠詞

It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.

There's this who can fly.

There's this boy who can fly.

this 和 a 只⁠能選一⁠個

It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.

There's this a girl in my class.

There's this girl in my class.

例句

① there's / there was + this + 名詞

  1. It's a story where there's this old man who makes a marionette that comes to life.
    這個故事講的是有一個老人，他做了一個會活過來的木偶。
  2. In our group there was this girl, tiny and fragile and very sensitive; she had a special way with words.
    我們那一組有個女孩，個子小小的，看起來很柔弱，也很敏感；她說話很有一套。
  3. There's this café near my office that does amazing cakes.
    我公司附近有家咖啡店，蛋糕超好吃。
  4. There was this strange noise coming from the kitchen.
    廚房傳來一個奇怪的聲音。
  5. In the film, there's this robot that wants to be human.
    那部電影裡有個機器人，它想變成人。

② 說故事書和電影的情節

  1. The two characters fall in love, and there's this flame of passion between them.
    那兩個角色墜入愛河，彼此之間燃起一股激情。
  2. The book is about this boy who finds a door to another world.
    那本書講一個男孩找到一扇通往另一個世界的門。
  3. So there's this detective, and he has to solve a murder on a train.
    故事裡有個偵探，他得破解火車上的一樁謀殺案。
  4. It starts with this family moving into an old house.
    故事從一家人搬進一棟老房子開始。

③ 說自己遇到的事

  1. I was waiting for the bus, and this man came up and asked me for directions.
    我在等公車，有個男人走過來問我路。
  2. I met this guy at the party who knows your brother.
    我在派對上認識一個人，他認識你哥哥。
  3. Yesterday this woman in the supermarket started shouting at the cashier.
    昨天超市裡有個女人開始對收銀員大吼。
  4. We were walking home when we saw this huge dog in the street.
    我們走路回家的時候，在街上看到一隻超大的狗。

④ these + 複數名詞

  1. There were these two old ladies on the train who talked all the way to London.
    火車上有兩個老太太，一路聊到倫敦。
  2. Then these men in suits walked in and everyone went quiet.
    接著有幾個穿西裝的男人走進來，大家都安靜了。
  3. In the story, there are these children who live alone on an island.
    故事裡有幾個小孩，他們自己住在一座島上。

⑤ 講笑話

  1. So this man walks into a restaurant with a penguin.
    有一個男人帶著一隻企鵝走進一家餐廳。
  2. There's this teacher who asks her class a question.
    有一個老師問她的班上一個問題。

⑥ 對話

  1. "What's the film about?" "There's this woman who wakes up and can't remember anything."
    那部電影在演什麼？有一個女人醒來以後什麼都不記得了。
  2. "Why are you late?" "There was this accident on the motorway, and we waited for an hour."
    你怎麼遲到了？高速公路上有車禍，我們等了一個小時。
  3. "How was your holiday?" "Great. We found this little beach where nobody goes."
    你的假期怎麼樣？很棒。我們找到一個沒人去的小海灘。
