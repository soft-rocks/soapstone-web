例句

① nearly / almost every

  1. My best friend is called Richard. Since we were little kids, we've spent nearly every day together.
    我最好的朋友叫 Richard。我們從小到大幾乎每天都待在一起。
  2. I love this café. I come here almost every morning.
    我很喜歡這家咖啡店。我幾乎每天早上都來。
  3. Nearly every house in our street has a garden.
    我們這條街幾乎每一間房子都有花園。
  4. Almost every student in my class has a part-time job.
    我們班幾乎每個學生都有打工。
  5. It rained nearly every day of our holiday.
    我們度假的時候幾乎每天都下雨。

② nearly / almost all

  1. I don't watch TV any more, because almost all of the programmes are stupid.
    我不再看電視了，因為幾乎所有節目都很蠢。
  2. Nearly all my friends live in the city now.
    我幾乎所有朋友現在都住在城市裡。
  3. Almost all the food was gone by nine o'clock.
    九點的時候，食物幾乎都沒了。
  4. We've sold nearly all the tickets for Saturday's show.
    星期六演出的票我們幾乎都賣完了。
  5. Read to your son every night. Almost all children love stories.
    每天晚上唸書給你兒子聽。幾乎所有孩子都喜歡聽故事。

③ not every / not all

  1. Not every hotel has free Wi-Fi, so check before you book.
    不是每間旅館都有免費 Wi-Fi，所以訂房前要查一下。
  2. Not all my teachers are strict. Some are really friendly.
    不是所有老師都很嚴格。有些很友善。
  3. Not every child enjoys sport, and that's OK.
    不是每個孩子都喜歡運動，這沒關係。
  4. The exam wasn't too bad. Not all the questions were difficult.
    考試沒那麼糟。不是所有題目都很難。

④ hardly any

  1. There's hardly any milk left. Can you buy some?
    牛奶幾乎沒了。你可以去買一些嗎？
  2. Hardly any people came to the meeting because of the snow.
    因為下雪，幾乎沒人來開會。
  3. I've got hardly any money until payday.
    發薪日之前我幾乎沒錢了。
  4. We saw hardly any animals on the safari. It was too hot.
    我們去野生動物園幾乎沒看到動物。天氣太熱了。

⑤ quite a few / quite a lot of

  1. The office is very quiet this week. Quite a few people at work have the flu.
    這星期辦公室很安靜。公司裡不少人得了流感。
  2. I've read quite a lot of books this summer.
    這個夏天我讀了不少書。
  3. My teacher wasn't happy. There were quite a few mistakes in my essay.
    老師不太滿意。我的作文裡有不少錯誤。
  4. She's got quite a lot of friends in London.
    她在倫敦有不少朋友。

⑥ only / just + 限定詞

  1. The test was too long. Only a few students finished it on time.
    考試太長了。只有幾個學生準時寫完。
  2. There's only a little coffee left, so let's share it.
    咖啡只剩一點點，我們分著喝吧。
  3. It was a surprise for Mum. Just a few people knew about the party.
    那是給媽媽的驚喜。只有幾個人知道派對的事。
  4. Only some of the rooms have a sea view.
    只有一部分房間看得到海。

⑦ 不同的情境

  1. My grandma calls me almost every Sunday.
    我奶奶幾乎每個星期天都打給我。
  2. Do your shopping early. Nearly all the shops close at six here.
    早點去買東西。這裡幾乎所有商店六點就關門。
  3. Hardly any of my friends have a car.
    我的朋友幾乎都沒有車。

⑧ 對話

  1. "Do you go to the gym often?" "Almost every day."
    你常去健身房嗎？幾乎每天。
  2. "Did everyone like the film?" "Not everyone. Quite a few people left early."
    每個人都喜歡那部電影嗎？不是每個人。不少人提早走了。
