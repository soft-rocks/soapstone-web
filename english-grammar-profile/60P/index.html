However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris.

你得承認，不是所有現成的餐點都很好吃。

I + must admit + (that) + 子⁠句

However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris.

I must admit我必須承認。承認和自己原本想法不同的事。
you must admit你必須承認。請對方接受一件事。
that 子句承認的事。that 可以省略。

議⁠論文⁠的讓⁠步

例句

You must admit that ready-made meals are not always healthy.

你必須承認，即食餐點不一定健康。

議論文常用 you must admit 提出反方的論點。

前⁠面常加連⁠接⁠詞

However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris.

例句

On the other hand, I must admit it was fun.

另一方面，我必須承認那很好玩。

這個說法常跟 however、on the other hand、but 一起用。

admit 後⁠面直⁠接接 that 子⁠句

However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris.

I must admit to that I was wrong.

I must admit that I was wrong.

must 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris.

You must admitting that it's cheap.

You must admit that it's cheap.

must 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris.

You've checked the map twice. I must admit that your way is shorter.

例句

① I must admit (that) + 子句：承認改變了想法

  1. I wanted to take the mountain road. However, I must admit that I completely agree with Chris. It's too dangerous.
    我本來想走山路。不過，我必須承認，我完全同意 Chris。那太危險了。
  2. Thanks for bringing me to the party. I didn't want to come, but I must admit I'm enjoying myself.
    謝謝你帶我來派對。我本來不想來，可是我必須承認，我玩得很開心。
  3. After my first week at the new school, I must admit that my maths teacher is better than I expected.
    在新學校上了一週課以後，我必須承認，數學老師比我想的好。
  4. The meal tonight was delicious. I must admit I was wrong about this restaurant.
    今晚的餐點很好吃。我必須承認，我對這家餐廳的看法錯了。
  5. I hated the book at first, but I must admit the ending is brilliant.
    我一開始很討厭那本書，可是我必須承認，結局很精彩。
  6. We've talked about both trips for an hour. I must admit that your plan is better than mine.
    我們討論這兩個旅行計畫一個小時了。我必須承認，你的計畫比我的好。

② I must admit (that) + 子句：承認自己的缺點

  1. Let's order a pizza tonight. I must admit that I'm not very good at cooking.
    我們今晚叫披薩吧。我必須承認，我不太會煮飯。
  2. Sorry, I only got the report this morning. I must admit I haven't read the whole thing.
    抱歉，我今天早上才收到報告。我必須承認，我沒讀完整份。
  3. Everyone said my talk went well. I must admit that I was a little nervous before it.
    大家都說我的演講很順利。我必須承認，演講前我有點緊張。
  4. My English friends love watching cricket. I must admit I don't understand the rules.
    我的英國朋友很愛看板球。我必須承認，我不懂它的規則。
  5. You phoned me on Tuesday, didn't you? I must admit I forgot to call you back.
    你星期二打給我，對吧？我必須承認，我忘了回你電話。
  6. My wardrobe is full again. I must admit that I spend too much on clothes.
    我的衣櫃又滿了。我必須承認，我花太多錢買衣服。

③ You must admit (that) + 子句：請對方接受

  1. Ready-made meals are quick. On the other hand, you must admit that they are often not as healthy as fresh food.
    即食餐點很快。另一方面，你必須承認，它們常常不如新鮮食物健康。
  2. I know the climb was hard. But you must admit that the view from up here is amazing.
    我知道這段路爬得很辛苦。可是你必須承認，從這上面看出去的風景很棒。
  3. You don't like your cousin very much, but you must admit she plays the piano beautifully.
    你不太喜歡你表妹，可是你必須承認，她鋼琴彈得很好。
  4. We got the last two seats on the train. You must admit that it was a good idea to leave early.
    我們搶到火車上最後兩個位子。你必須承認，提早出發是個好主意。
  5. This bike is almost new, and I'm only asking fifty pounds. You must admit the price is very fair.
    這台腳踏車幾乎是新的，我只賣五十英鎊。你必須承認，這個價錢很公道。
  6. You said Lisbon was too hilly. But you must admit that the city looks lovely at night.
    你說里斯本坡太多。可是你必須承認，這個城市晚上看起來很美。

④ However / But + I must admit + 子句：議論文的讓步

  1. Living in the country is peaceful. However, I must admit that it can be lonely.
    住在鄉下很寧靜。不過，我必須承認，有時候會很寂寞。
  2. Mobile phones are useful, but I must admit they take up too much of our time.
    手機很有用，可是我必須承認，它們佔用了我們太多時間。
  3. Thanks for asking about the music festival. It was great. However, I must admit the food was expensive.
    謝謝你問起音樂節。它很棒。不過，我必須承認，食物很貴。
  4. Online shopping is easy, but I must admit I miss real shops.
    網購很方便，可是我必須承認，我懷念實體商店。
  5. Our teacher wants us to join a team. Team sports are fun. However, I must admit I prefer swimming alone.
    老師要我們參加球隊。團體運動很好玩。不過，我必須承認，我比較喜歡一個人游泳。
  6. You asked about the first aid course. It was useful, but I must admit some lessons were boring.
    你問起急救課程。它很有用，可是我必須承認，有些課很無聊。

⑤ I must admit, + 句子：放在句首當插入語

  1. I asked Emma to dance, and I must admit, I was surprised when she said yes.
    我邀請 Emma 跳舞。我必須承認，她答應的時候我很驚訝。
  2. Thanks for taking me to this little café. I must admit, this is the best coffee I've ever had.
    謝謝你帶我來這家小咖啡店。我必須承認，這是我喝過最好喝的咖啡。
  3. Congratulations on the race! I must admit, I didn't expect you to win.
    恭喜你贏了比賽！我必須承認，我沒想到你會贏。
  4. We just got back from Rome. I must admit, the hotel was much nicer than the photos.
    我們剛從羅馬回來。我必須承認，那家飯店比照片好多了。
  5. My brother got a new bike for his birthday. I must admit, I was a bit jealous of him.
    我哥哥生日收到一台新腳踏車。我必須承認，我有點嫉妒他。
  6. Thanks for taking the kids to the zoo. I must admit, they behaved very well today.
    謝謝你帶孩子們去動物園。我必須承認，他們今天表現得很好。
  7. Your train leaves in five minutes. I must admit, I've never been good at saying goodbye.
    你的火車五分鐘後就要開了。我必須承認，我一直都不擅長道別。
