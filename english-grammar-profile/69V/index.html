例句

① I wondered if + 主詞 + could

  1. I wondered if you could send me some information about your courses.
    不知道您能不能寄一些課程的資料給我。
  2. I wondered if you could tell me what time the museum opens.
    不知道您能不能告訴我博物館幾點開門。
  3. I wondered if you could recommend a good hotel near the station.
    不知道您能不能推薦車站附近一家好的飯店。
  4. I wondered if we could change the date of the meeting.
    不知道我們能不能改開會的日期。
  5. I wondered if I could borrow your car this weekend.
    不知道這個週末我能不能借你的車。

② I wondered if + 主詞 + would

  1. I wondered if you would like to join us for dinner.
    不知道你要不要跟我們一起吃晚餐。
  2. I wondered if you would be interested in giving a talk at our school.
    不知道您有沒有興趣來我們學校演講。
  3. I wondered whether you would mind checking my essay.
    不知道你介不介意幫我看一下我的文章。
  4. I wondered whether it would be possible to pay in two parts.
    不知道能不能分兩次付款。

③ I was wondering if

  1. I was wondering if you could help me move on Saturday.
    不知道你星期六能不能幫我搬家。
  2. I was wondering if you had any rooms available for next week.
    不知道你們下禮拜還有沒有空房。
  3. I was wondering whether you'd thought about my offer.
    不知道你考慮過我的提議了沒有。
  4. We were wondering if you could look after our cat while we're away.
    不知道我們不在的時候你能不能幫忙照顧貓。

④ I wanted to know / ask

  1. I wanted to know if the rooms are single or double.
    我想請問房間是單人房還是雙人房。
  2. I also wanted to know if there is a gym in the hotel.
    我也想請問飯店有沒有健身房。
  3. I wanted to ask whether breakfast is included in the price.
    我想請問房價有沒有含早餐。
  4. I wanted to ask about the job you advertised in the newspaper.
    我想詢問你們在報紙上刊登的那份工作。
  5. I just wanted to check that you received my application.
    我只是想確認您有收到我的申請。

⑤ I wanted to thank you

  1. First of all, I wanted to thank you for giving me the first prize in your competition.
    首先，我想謝謝你們在比賽中給我頭獎。
  2. I wanted to thank you for your help last week.
    我想謝謝你上禮拜幫忙。
  3. We wanted to thank everyone who came to the party.
    我們想謝謝每一位來參加派對的人。
  4. I wanted to thank you for being so patient with me.
    我想謝謝你對我這麼有耐心。
  5. I just wanted to say thank you for the lovely flowers.
    我只是想謝謝你送的漂亮花束。

⑥ I wanted to let you know / tell you

  1. I wanted to let you know that I'll be late tomorrow.
    我想跟你說一聲，我明天會晚到。
  2. I wanted to let you know that your parcel has arrived.
    我想通知您，您的包裹到了。
  3. I wanted to tell you how much I enjoyed your talk.
    我想跟你說我很喜歡你的演講。
  4. We wanted to remind you that the office will be closed on Friday.
    我們想提醒您，辦公室星期五不上班。

⑦ 跟 I wondered 一起用的其他客氣說法

  1. I wondered if you might be free for a quick chat.
    不知道你有沒有空簡單聊一下。
  2. I wondered if there was any chance of getting a refund.
    不知道有沒有可能退款。
  3. I wanted to apologise for missing your call yesterday.
    昨天沒接到你的電話，我想跟你說聲抱歉。
  4. I wanted to invite you to our new restaurant's opening night.
    我想邀請你來參加我們新餐廳的開幕晚會。
