be going to 說已⁠經決⁠定好⁠的打⁠算

I'm going to use pink paint.

我要用粉紅色的油漆。

用粉紅色是 Ann 說話前就決定好的事。

說自⁠己和別⁠人⁠的打⁠算

I'm going to use pink paint.

我媽媽要開車載我們去。

把 be 動⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面，問別⁠人⁠的打⁠算

I'm going to use pink paint.

你打算幾點來？

說⁠話前就決⁠定用 be going to，說⁠話⁠時才決⁠定用 will

I'm going to use pink paint.

be going to

I'm going to call Tom tonight.

我今晚要打給 Tom。說話前就決定了。
will

The phone's ringing. I'll answer it.

電話響了，我來接。說話的時候才決定。

going 後⁠面要⁠有 to

I'm going to use pink paint.

I'm going paint my room on Saturday.

I'm going to paint my room on Saturday.

going to 後⁠面要接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I'm going to use pink paint.

I'm going to use pink paint.

例句

① 說打算：主詞 + be going to + 原形動詞

  1. I'm going to paint the bedroom pink.
    我要把臥室漆成粉紅色。
  2. My mum is going to drive us to the beach.
    我媽媽要開車載我們去海邊。
  3. We're going to start a band.
    我們要組一個樂團。
  4. I'm going to save money for a new bike.
    我要存錢買新腳踏車。
  5. She's going to apply for a job at the bank.
    她要去應徵銀行的工作。
  6. They're going to open a café next to the station.
    他們要在車站旁邊開一家咖啡店。
  7. I'm going to read three books this summer.
    我今年夏天要讀三本書。
  8. He's going to give up eating sugar.
    他要戒掉吃糖。
  9. We're going to plant tomatoes in the garden.
    我們要在花園裡種番茄。
  10. I'm going to ask Ann to the dance.
    我要邀 Ann 去舞會。
  11. My brother is going to join the army.
    我哥要去從軍。
  12. We're going to celebrate Dad's birthday at a restaurant.
    我們要去餐廳慶祝爸爸的生日。
  13. I'm going to practise the piano every day.
    我要每天練鋼琴。
  14. She's going to take her driving test in June.
    她六月要考駕照。
  15. They're going to build a treehouse for the kids.
    他們要幫孩子們蓋一間樹屋。
  16. I'm going to make a cake for the party.
    我要為派對做一個蛋糕。

② 問打算：(wh- 疑問詞) + be + 主詞 + going to + 原形動詞 ...?

  1. What time are you going to leave for the concert?
    你打算幾點出發去音樂會？
  2. What kind of food are you going to bring to the picnic?
    你要帶哪一種食物去野餐？
  3. Are you going to invite Tom and his girlfriend to the barbecue?
    你要邀請 Tom 和他女朋友來烤肉嗎？
  4. Where are you going to put the new bookcase?
    新書櫃你要放在哪裡？
  5. Is she going to change schools?
    她要轉學嗎？
  6. What are you going to buy with your birthday money?
    你要拿生日收到的錢買什麼？
  7. Are they going to come with us?
    他們要跟我們一起去嗎？
  8. How are you going to tell your parents?
    你要怎麼跟你爸媽說？
  9. When are you going to finish your homework?
    你什麼時候要寫完功課？
  10. Is your dad going to fix the roof himself?
    你爸要自己修屋頂嗎？
  11. What are you going to study at university?
    你上大學要念什麼？
  12. Are we going to walk or take the bus?
    我們要走路還是搭公車？
  13. Who are you going to sit next to?
    你要坐在誰旁邊？
  14. Is Ann going to sing at the wedding?
    Ann 要在婚禮上唱歌嗎？
  15. Which colour are you going to choose?
    你要選哪個顏色？
