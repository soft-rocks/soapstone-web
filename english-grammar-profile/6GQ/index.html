I go to India every year to see my friends and family.

我每年都會去印度看我的朋友和家人。

every 加單⁠數名⁠詞

我每年都去印度

a 說一⁠個，every 說全⁠部

I go to India every year to see my friends and family.

a

I have a big kitchen and a small toilet.

有一間大廚房和一間小廁所。
every

Every bedroom has a desk.

每間臥室都有一張書桌。

every 加時⁠間說多⁠久一⁠次

I go to India every year to see my friends and family.

every Tuesday

We have English lessons every Tuesday.

我們每個星期二都有英文課。
every summer

My cousins come to stay with us every summer.

我的表兄弟姊妹每年夏天都來我們家住。

every 後⁠面用 morning，不⁠用 mornings

I go to India every year to see my friends and family.

My grandfather reads the newspaper every mornings.

My grandfather reads the newspaper every morning.

單⁠數名⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 a

I go to India every year to see my friends and family.

Our new flat has big balcony.

Our new flat has a big balcony.

every 前⁠面不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

I go to India every year to see my friends and family.

We play football in every Saturday.

We play football every Saturday.

例句

① a + 單數名詞

  1. The house is perfect for us. The first floor has two bedrooms and a big bathroom with a bath.
    這房子很適合我們。二樓有兩間臥室和一間有浴缸的大浴室。
  2. My brother has a dog and a cat. He lives in a house with a garden.
    我哥哥養了一隻狗和一隻貓。他住在一棟有花園的房子。
  3. We can swim and read at lunchtime. Our school has a swimming pool and a library.
    我們午餐時間可以游泳和看書。我們學校有一座游泳池和一間圖書館。
  4. I can walk to work. I live in a small flat in the city centre.
    我可以走路上班。我住在市中心的一間小公寓。
  5. It's very cold here in winter. I need a warm coat.
    這裡冬天很冷。我需要一件保暖的外套。

② every + 時間

  1. My mum phones her sister every evening. They talk for hours.
    我媽媽每天晚上都打電話給她姊姊。她們一聊就好幾個小時。
  2. Our teacher is from London. We have English lessons every Tuesday.
    我們的老師是倫敦人。我們每個星期二都有英文課。
  3. My dad goes swimming every morning. He's very fit for his age.
    我爸爸每天早上都去游泳。以他的年紀來說，他身體很好。
  4. My cousins come to stay with us every summer. We go to the beach together.
    我的表兄弟姊妹每年夏天都來我們家住。我們會一起去海邊。
  5. The market is very busy. It opens every Friday.
    市集很熱鬧。它每個星期五開。

③ every + 人或東西

  1. You can leave your books at school. Every student has a locker.
    你可以把書留在學校。每個學生都有一個置物櫃。
  2. The house is very bright. Every room in our house has a window.
    房子很明亮。我們家每個房間都有窗戶。
  3. Our village school is very small. The teacher knows every child's name.
    我們村子的學校很小。老師知道每個孩子的名字。
  4. It was a public holiday. Every shop in the street was closed.
    那天是國定假日。那條街上每家店都關門了。
  5. Mum cleaned the whole house. Every window is shining now.
    媽媽把整間房子打掃乾淨了。每扇窗戶現在都亮晶晶的。

④ 介紹自己

  1. Hi! My name is Paulo. I have a sister and a brother.
    嗨！我叫 Paulo。我有一個姊姊和一個弟弟。
  2. My name's Ana and I'm new in London. I'm from Brazil, and I go to a language school every afternoon.
    我叫 Ana，剛來倫敦。我來自巴西，每天下午都去語言學校。
  3. I'm a student, but I have a part-time job in a café. I work there at weekends.
    我是學生，可是我在一家咖啡店打工。我週末在那裡工作。
  4. I love sport. I go running every weekend.
    我很愛運動。我每個週末都去跑步。

⑤ 日常生活

  1. I'm hungry. Can I have an egg sandwich and a cup of tea, please?
    我餓了。請給我一份蛋三明治和一杯茶。
  2. I'm saving for a bike. I wash my dad's car every weekend for some pocket money.
    我在存錢買腳踏車。我每個週末幫爸爸洗車，賺點零用錢。
  3. You don't need a taxi. There's a bus to the airport every hour.
    你不需要計程車。每個小時有一班公車到機場。
  4. My grandmother is ninety. We visit her every Sunday.
    我奶奶九十歲了。我們每個星期天都去看她。

⑥ 對話

  1. "How often do you play tennis?" "Every Sunday."
    你多久打一次網球？每個星期天。
  2. "Have you got a pet?" "Yes, a rabbit."
    你有養寵物嗎？有，一隻兔子。
  3. "Do you walk to school?" "Yes, every day."
    你走路上學嗎？對，每天都走。
  4. "What did you buy?" "A new phone. My old one broke."
    你買了什麼？一支新手機。我的舊手機壞了。

⑦ 寫信給朋友

  1. My new job is great. I have an office with a view of the sea.
    我的新工作很棒。我有一間看得到海的辦公室。
  2. My flatmates love films. We go to the cinema every Friday night.
    我的室友很愛電影。我們每個星期五晚上都去看電影。
  3. There's a lovely park near my flat. I walk there every evening.
    我公寓附近有一座很漂亮的公園。我每天晚上都去那裡散步。
  4. Don't worry about me. Every person in my new class is friendly.
    別擔心我。我新班上的每個人都很友善。
