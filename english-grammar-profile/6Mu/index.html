例句

① few + 動詞：指一群人裡沒幾個人

  1. The workers were offered English lessons, but few had any time to go.
    工人可以上英文課，可是很少人有時間去。（被動）
  2. Many people apply for the course, but few get a place.
    很多人申請這門課，可是拿到名額的沒幾個。
  3. Many people start learning the piano as children, but few continue as adults.
    很多人小時候開始學鋼琴，可是長大後還繼續的沒幾個。
  4. Thousands of people visit the city every year, but few see the old town.
    每年有成千上萬的人來這座城市，可是去看舊城區的沒幾個。
  5. The company offered free training, but few took it.
    公司提供免費的訓練，可是參加的人沒幾個。
  6. Many people promise to exercise more, but few do.
    很多人都說要多運動，可是真的做到的沒幾個。

② very few + 動詞

  1. Most people can speak a bit of English, but very few speak it well.
    大部分人都會說一點英文，可是說得好的很少。
  2. The survey went to five hundred homes, and very few replied.
    調查寄到五百戶人家，回覆的很少。
  3. Many teenagers have a bank account, but very few save money regularly.
    很多青少年有銀行帳戶，可是很少人定期存錢。
  4. The village was once busy, but very few live there now.
    那個村子以前很熱鬧，可是現在住在那裡的人很少。

③ few / very few + of + 代名詞或名詞片語

  1. Few of the people we asked had heard of the new law.
    我們問的人裡，很少人聽說過新的法律。
  2. Very few of my classmates read newspapers.
    我的同學裡很少人看報紙。
  3. Few of them knew what to do when the fire alarm rang.
    火災警報響的時候，他們裡面很少人知道該怎麼做。
  4. Very few of the workers were happy with the new hours.
    員工裡很少人滿意新的上班時間。
  5. Few of the visitors stayed longer than one night.
    遊客裡很少人待超過一晚。

④ few + 複數名詞（指人）

  1. Few people in the town know about the museum.
    鎮上很少人知道那間博物館。
  2. Very few students chose the evening classes.
    很少學生選晚上的課。
  3. Few young people want to work on farms these days.
    現在很少年輕人想在農場工作。
  4. Very few tourists come here in winter.
    冬天很少觀光客來這裡。
  5. Few doctors agree with the new plan.
    很少醫生贊成新的計畫。

⑤ 報告和調查

  1. The report found that few employees took all their holiday.
    報告發現很少員工會把假休完。
  2. According to the survey, very few customers read the instructions.
    根據調查，很少顧客會讀使用說明。
  3. Few of the people in the study exercised more than once a week.
    研究裡很少人一星期運動超過一次。
  4. The results show that very few children walk to school now.
    結果顯示，現在走路上學的孩子很少。
  5. Few of those questioned felt safe at night.
    受訪者裡很少人晚上覺得安全。
  6. The study shows that very few drivers obey the speed limit on this road.
    研究顯示，在這條路上很少駕駛人遵守速限。

⑥ few + any

  1. Few of the guests had any idea who the speaker was.
    來賓裡很少人知道講者是誰。
  2. Very few people had any money left at the end of the month.
    月底還有錢剩的人很少。
  3. We interviewed forty nurses for the job. Few had any experience of living abroad.
    我們為這份工作面試了四十個護理師。很少人有在國外生活的經驗。

⑦ few + 動詞，後面說結果

  1. Few customers used the self-service machines, so the shop removed them.
    很少顧客用自助結帳機，所以那家店把機器撤掉了。
  2. Few people came to the meeting, so we cancelled it.
    沒幾個人來開會，所以我們取消了。
