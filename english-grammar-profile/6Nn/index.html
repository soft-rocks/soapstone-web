They have three wonderful children who I love to play with.

他們有三個很棒的孩子，我很喜歡跟他們一起玩。

who 是介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞，介⁠系⁠詞留⁠在子⁠句最⁠後⁠面

他們有三個很棒的孩子，我很喜歡跟他們一起玩。

限⁠定子⁠句可⁠以省⁠略關⁠係⁠代⁠名⁠詞，非限⁠定子⁠句用 who 或 which

They have three wonderful children who I love to play with.

限定

Is this the charger you were looking for?

這是你在找的充電器嗎？
非限定

I've started doing yoga, which I'm not very good at.

我開始做瑜伽了，不過我做得不太好。

介⁠系⁠詞跟⁠著子⁠句⁠裡⁠的動⁠詞或形⁠容⁠詞

They have three wonderful children who I love to play with.

depend on

My brother is someone who you can always depend on.

我哥哥是你隨時都可以依靠的人。
其他組合good at, interested in, listen to

正⁠式⁠的書⁠面語把介⁠系⁠詞放⁠在 whom 或 which 前⁠面

They have three wonderful children who I love to play with.

正式

The committee to which I sent my application has not replied.

我寄申請書去的委員會還沒回覆。

who 已⁠經是介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞，不⁠再⁠加 her

They have three wonderful children who I love to play with.

The girl who I went to the cinema with her is my cousin.

The girl who I went to the cinema with is my cousin.

listen 後⁠面⁠的 to 要留⁠在句⁠尾

That's the song which I was listening.

That's the song which I was listening to.

介⁠系⁠詞放⁠在⁠前⁠面⁠時不⁠能用 that

The man with that I spoke was very kind.

The man that I spoke with was very kind.

介⁠系⁠詞只出⁠現一⁠次

They have three wonderful children who I love to play with.

The house in which we lived in was old.

The house which we lived in was old.

例句

① 限定：who / that + ... + 介系詞

  1. She's the colleague who I always have lunch with.
    她是我都會一起吃午餐的同事。
  2. The chair that you're sitting on belonged to my great-grandfather.
    你坐的那張椅子是我曾祖父的。
  3. The topic that we argued about all night was politics.
    我們吵了一整晚的話題是政治。
  4. Is that the guy who you went to the concert with?
    那就是跟你一起去演唱會的人嗎？
  5. The box that I keep my letters in is under the bed.
    我放信的盒子在床底下。
  6. He's a friend who I can talk to about anything.
    他是我什麼都可以聊的朋友。

② 限定：省略關係代名詞

  1. The pen I'm writing with is running out of ink.
    我正在用的筆快沒水了。
  2. The town she comes from is famous for its tea.
    她的家鄉以茶聞名。
  3. These are the people I grew up with.
    這些是跟我一起長大的人。
  4. Is this the bag you were asking about?
    這是你剛剛問的那個包包嗎？
  5. The thing I'm most worried about is the cost.
    我最擔心的是費用。
  6. The company he works for is opening a branch in Taichung.
    他工作的公司要在台中開分公司。

③ 非限定：, which / who ... + 介系詞

  1. I've taken up painting, which I'm actually quite good at.
    我開始畫畫了，其實我畫得還不錯。
  2. We visited Mr. Liu, who my father used to work with.
    我們去看劉先生，我爸爸以前跟他一起工作。
  3. They gave us a long report, which nobody could make sense of.
    他們給我們一份很長的報告，沒人看得懂。
  4. My aunt, who I stayed with last summer, has a farm.
    我阿姨有一座農場，我去年夏天住在她家。
  5. The new policy, which many staff complained about, ends next month.
    很多員工抱怨的新政策下個月結束。
  6. Her latest book, which I'm really looking forward to, comes out in May.
    她的新書五月出版，我真的很期待。

④ 形容詞 + 介系詞

  1. Chemistry is the subject that I'm most interested in.
    化學是我最有興趣的科目。
  2. Spiders are the only thing I'm really afraid of.
    蜘蛛是我唯一真的很怕的東西。
  3. This is a result which we can all be proud of.
    這是我們都可以感到驕傲的成果。
  4. The dog, which my sister is allergic to, has to stay outside.
    那隻狗要待在外面，我姊姊對牠過敏。
  5. He showed me the scar that he's so embarrassed about.
    他給我看他很不好意思的那道疤。

⑤ 正式的寫法：介系詞 + whom / which

  1. The manager to whom I addressed my complaint never answered.
    我寄申訴的那位經理一直沒回覆。
  2. The hotel in which the conference took place was very modern.
    舉辦研討會的飯店很現代。
  3. Mr. Huang, with whom I worked for ten years, is retiring.
    黃先生要退休了，我跟他共事了十年。
  4. The project on which we spent two years finally ended last week.
    我們花了兩年的那個計畫上禮拜終於結束了。
  5. The charity to which she gave her savings helps children in need.
    她把積蓄捐給的那個慈善機構幫助有需要的孩子。
  6. The colleagues with whom she shared an office threw her a farewell party.
    跟她共用辦公室的同事幫她辦了歡送會。
  7. The principles on which the founders built the school still matter today.
    創辦人建校的理念到今天還是很重要。
