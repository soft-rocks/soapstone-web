My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

我家就在火車站對面，所以你很容易就能找到。

情⁠狀副⁠詞放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面

My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

你很容易就能找到。

情⁠狀副⁠詞、地⁠點、時⁠間⁠的順⁠序

My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

她昨晚在音樂會上唱得很好聽。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在情⁠狀副⁠詞前⁠面

My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

他英文講得很清楚。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞後⁠面用形⁠容⁠詞

形容詞說明主詞

You look happy.

你看起來很開心。
情狀副詞說明動作

You sing happily.

你唱得很開心。

情⁠狀副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

Please read carefully the instructions.

Please read the instructions carefully.

情⁠狀副⁠詞放⁠在地⁠點前⁠面

The children played in the garden happily.

The children played happily in the garden.

look 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞

You look beautiful in that dress.

例句

① 動詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. Please walk slowly. My feet hurt.
    請走慢一點，我的腳好痛。
  2. My dad snores loudly every night.
    我爸每天晚上打鼾都很大聲。
  3. The old train moves slowly through the mountains.
    那列老火車慢慢地穿過山區。
  4. Everyone worked hard before the exams.
    考試前大家都很用功。
  5. Speak quietly, please. Grandma is resting.
    說話小聲一點，奶奶在休息。

② 動詞 + 受詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. Please put the glasses down gently on the shelf.
    請把杯子輕輕放在架子上。
  2. Our guide answered all our questions patiently.
    我們的導遊很有耐心地回答了我們所有的問題。
  3. My grandmother makes soup really well.
    我奶奶湯煮得很好。
  4. He opened the present slowly.
    他慢慢地打開禮物。
  5. Hold the cup carefully. It's very hot.
    小心拿杯子，很燙。
  6. We finished the project quickly.
    我們很快就做完了那個專題。

③ 助動詞 + 動詞 + 受詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. You can find the hotel easily from the station.
    你從車站很容易就能找到那間旅館。
  2. I can't read your writing clearly.
    我看不清楚你寫的字。
  3. You should check your answers carefully.
    你應該仔細檢查你的答案。
  4. Could you explain the homework slowly, please?
    可以請你慢慢講解作業嗎？

④ 情狀副詞 + 地點 + 時間

  1. The children played happily in the park all afternoon.
    孩子們整個下午在公園玩得很開心。
  2. He drove carefully through the town.
    他小心地開車穿過市區。
  3. We waited patiently at the bus stop for an hour.
    我們在公車站耐心地等了一個小時。
  4. She swam well in the race yesterday.
    她昨天比賽游得很好。
  5. My cousin did really well at school last year.
    我表弟去年在學校表現很好。

⑤ 程度副詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. The doctor spoke very kindly to my son.
    醫生對我兒子說話很溫柔。
  2. You did that exercise really quickly.
    你那個練習做得真快。
  3. Our team played quite badly on Saturday.
    我們隊星期六打得滿差的。
  4. My sister dances so beautifully.
    我姊姊跳舞跳得好美。
  5. The old man walked very slowly up the hill.
    那位老先生很慢地走上山坡。

⑥ 連綴動詞 + 形容詞

  1. This pizza smells delicious.
    這個披薩聞起來好香。
  2. You sound tired. Are you OK?
    你聽起來很累，你還好嗎？
  3. The milk tastes a bit strange. Is it old?
    牛奶喝起來怪怪的，是放太久了嗎？
  4. The new sofa looks comfortable.
    新沙發看起來很舒服。

⑦ 疑問句和否定句

  1. Did the new waiter treat you politely at dinner?
    晚餐時那個新來的服務生對你們有禮貌嗎？
  2. He doesn't drive very carefully.
    他開車不太小心。
  3. Can you write your name clearly at the top, please?
    可以請你在最上面把名字寫清楚嗎？
