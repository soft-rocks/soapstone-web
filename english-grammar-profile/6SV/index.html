My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

我家就在火車站對面，所以你很容易就能找到。

情⁠狀副⁠詞放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面

My house is opposite the train station, so you can find it easily.

你很容易就能找到。

情⁠狀副⁠詞、地⁠點、時⁠間⁠的順⁠序

Lily 昨晚在音樂會上唱得很好聽。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在情⁠狀副⁠詞前⁠面

他英文講得很清楚。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞後⁠面用形⁠容⁠詞

形容詞說明主詞

Your grandad sounds cheerful on the phone.

你爺爺在電話裡聽起來心情很好。
情狀副詞說明動作

The kids are singing happily in the car.

孩子們在車上開心地唱歌。

情⁠狀副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

He packed carefully the plates.

He packed the plates carefully.

說⁠明動⁠作怎⁠麼做用情⁠狀副⁠詞，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

My uncle drives very careful.

My uncle drives very carefully.

look 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞

You look beautifully in that dress.

You look beautiful in that dress.

very 放⁠在情⁠狀副⁠詞前⁠面

He finished the race quickly very.

He finished the race very quickly.

例句

① 動詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. These new shoes are too tight. Please walk slowly. My feet hurt.
    這雙新鞋太緊了。請走慢一點，我的腳好痛。
  2. Mum sleeps in the spare room now. My dad snores loudly every night.
    媽媽現在睡在客房。我爸每天晚上打鼾都很大聲。
  3. Sit by the window and enjoy the view. The old train moves slowly through the mountains.
    坐在窗邊欣賞風景吧。那列老火車慢慢地穿過山區。
  4. The library was full every evening. Everyone worked hard before the exams.
    圖書館每天晚上都坐滿了人。考試前大家都很用功。
  5. Speak quietly, please. Grandma is resting.
    說話小聲一點，奶奶在休息。

② 動詞 + 受詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. These glasses were my mother's. Please put them down gently on the shelf.
    這些杯子是我媽媽的。請把它們輕輕放在架子上。
  2. We asked about everything in the castle. Our guide answered all our questions patiently.
    我們問了城堡裡的每樣東西。我們的導遊很有耐心地回答了我們所有的問題。
  3. Come for lunch on Sunday. My grandmother makes soup really well.
    星期天來吃午餐吧。我奶奶湯煮得很好。
  4. My grandson wanted to keep the paper. He opened the present slowly.
    我孫子想留著包裝紙。他慢慢地打開禮物。
  5. I've filled your cup to the top. Hold it carefully.
    我把你的杯子倒得很滿。小心拿著。
  6. We all knew our jobs. We finished the project quickly.
    我們每個人都知道自己要做什麼。我們很快就做完了那個專題。

③ 助動詞 + 動詞 + 受詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. Our hotel is a tall white building. You can see it easily from the station.
    我們的旅館是一棟高高的白色建築。你從車站很容易就能看到。
  2. I can't read your writing clearly. Can you type the letter for me?
    我看不清楚你寫的字。你可以幫我把信打字嗎？
  3. You still have ten minutes. You should check your answers carefully.
    你還有十分鐘。你應該仔細檢查你的答案。
  4. Some of us are new. Could you explain the homework slowly, please?
    我們有些人是新來的。可以請你慢慢講解作業嗎？

④ 情狀副詞 + 地點 + 時間

  1. The sun came out after lunch. The children played happily in the park all afternoon.
    午餐後太陽出來了。孩子們整個下午在公園玩得很開心。
  2. It was market day. Dad drove carefully through the town.
    那天是市集日。爸爸小心地開車穿過市區。
  3. The buses were on strike, but we didn't know. We waited patiently at the bus stop for an hour.
    公車在罷工，可是我們不知道。我們在公車站耐心地等了一個小時。
  4. Amy came second. She swam well in the race yesterday.
    Amy 拿了第二名。她昨天比賽游得很好。
  5. My cousin won a prize for science. He did really well at school last year.
    我表弟得了科學獎。他去年在學校表現很好。

⑤ 程度副詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. My son was scared of the needle. The doctor spoke very kindly to him.
    我兒子很怕打針。醫生對他說話很溫柔。
  2. You did that exercise really quickly. Did you check it?
    你那個練習做得真快。你有檢查嗎？
  3. We lost four to one. Our team played quite badly on Saturday.
    我們一比四輸了。我們隊星期六打得滿差的。
  4. My sister has had ballet lessons since she was five. She dances so beautifully.
    我姊姊五歲就開始上芭蕾課。她跳舞跳得好美。
  5. The old man walked very slowly up the hill. He stopped to rest three times.
    那位老先生很慢地走上山坡。他停下來休息了三次。

⑥ 連綴動詞 + 形容詞

  1. This pizza smells delicious. Is it ready yet?
    這個披薩聞起來好香。好了嗎？
  2. You sound tired. Are you OK?
    你聽起來很累，你還好嗎？
  3. The milk tastes a bit strange. Is it old?
    牛奶喝起來怪怪的，是放太久了嗎？
  4. Let's sit down. The new sofa looks comfortable.
    我們坐下吧。新沙發看起來很舒服。

⑦ 疑問句和否定句

  1. You were the only people in the restaurant. Did the new waiter treat you politely?
    只有你們在餐廳裡。那個新來的服務生對你們有禮貌嗎？
  2. I don't let my brother take my car. He doesn't drive very carefully.
    我不讓我哥哥開我的車。他開車不太小心。
  3. The teacher can't read some of the forms. Can you write your name clearly at the top, please?
    老師有幾張表格看不懂。可以請你在最上面把名字寫清楚嗎？
