例句

① 論說文：一般人的生活

  1. Living in Greece, I have had a chance to realize how much tourism can affect one's life.
    住在希臘讓我有機會了解到，觀光對一個人的生活影響有多大。
  2. In my opinion, learning a language depends on one's ability to communicate in other ways, apart from one's mother tongue.
    我認為學語言要看一個人能不能用母語以外的方式溝通。
  3. Moving to a new country can change one's view of the world.
    搬到一個新國家，可能改變一個人的世界觀。
  4. Regular exercise is good for one's health and mood.
    規律運動對一個人的健康和心情都有好處。
  5. Travel broadens one's mind.
    旅行讓人眼界開闊。

② one's + 家人

  1. After one has spent enough time away from one's parents, it is possible to move back in with them.
    一個人離開父母夠久以後，再搬回去和他們住是可能的。
  2. It is natural to want to make one's parents proud.
    想讓父母感到驕傲是很自然的。
  3. One's family can be one's greatest support in difficult times.
    在困難的時候，家人可能是一個人最大的支柱。

③ of one's own

  1. Once one has a personality of one's own, living with one's parents is less difficult.
    一個人有了自己的個性以後，和父母同住就沒那麼難。
  2. As a teenager, one longs for a room of one's own.
    青少年時期，一個人會渴望有自己的房間。
  3. It takes time to develop a style of one's own.
    發展出自己的風格需要時間。

④ 建議和規則

  1. One should never judge others by one's own standards.
    一個人不該用自己的標準評斷別人。
  2. One must always try to do one's best.
    一個人應該永遠盡力而為。
  3. It is important to know one's own limits.
    了解自己的極限很重要。
  4. One should keep one's opinions to oneself at a job interview.
    面試的時候，一個人應該把自己的意見放在心裡。

⑤ 演講和正式的寫作

  1. Losing one's job can be a very stressful experience.
    失業可能是一段壓力很大的經驗。
  2. One's freedom should never be taken for granted.
    一個人的自由永遠不該被視為理所當然。
  3. One's first year at university is often the hardest.
    一個人上大學的第一年往往最辛苦。
  4. Reading widely improves one's writing.
    廣泛閱讀能讓一個人的寫作進步。

⑥ 固定說法

  1. It's not easy to change one's mind once a decision is made.
    決定一旦做了，要改變心意並不容易。
  2. One needs to have a little faith in one's own abilities.
    一個人需要對自己的能力有一點信心。
  3. Sometimes it is wise to hold one's tongue.
    有時候閉嘴是明智的。
