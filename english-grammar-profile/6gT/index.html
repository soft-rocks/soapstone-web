You just have to send me your design and I'll do the rest.

你只要把你的設計寄給我，剩下的我來處理。

前⁠面有限⁠定⁠詞，design 和 rest 就⁠是名⁠詞

例句

① 和動詞同形的名詞

  1. I love the design of your new kitchen. Who did it?
    我好喜歡你新廚房的設計，是誰做的？
  2. The storm caused a lot of damage to the roof.
    暴風雨對屋頂造成很多損害。
  3. Thanks for your visit. It was lovely to see you.
    謝謝你來訪，見到你真開心。
  4. After ten years in the city, we needed a change.
    在城市住了十年後，我們需要改變一下。
  5. Can you give me a hand with this box?
    你可以幫我搬一下這個箱子嗎？

② the rest

  1. I'll pay for the tickets if you pay for the rest.
    如果你付其他的錢，票錢我來付。
  2. We ate half the pizza and saved the rest for later.
    我們吃了半個披薩，剩下的留著晚點吃。
  3. Two of us went by car, and the rest went by train.
    我們有兩個人開車去，其他人搭火車。
  4. You take the big bags, and I'll carry the rest.
    你拿大袋子，剩下的我來拿。

③ 抽象名詞

  1. Living in another country is a great experience.
    住在國外是很棒的經驗。
  2. This job is a good opportunity for you.
    這份工作對你來說是個好機會。
  3. One advantage of the flat is the big kitchen.
    這間公寓的一個優點是廚房很大。
  4. Plastic bags are bad for the environment, so bring your own bag.
    塑膠袋對環境不好，所以自己帶袋子。
  5. They have a very good relationship.
    他們的關係很好。

④ 不可數的抽象名詞

  1. My teacher gave me some good advice about the exam.
    我的老師給了我一些關於考試的好建議。
  2. She broke my heart, and nobody can repair the damage.
    她讓我心碎，沒有人能修補這個傷害。
  3. You can find more information on our website.
    你可以在我們的網站上找到更多資訊。
  4. When you're swimming, you exercise your whole body. It's great for your health.
    游泳的時候全身都在運動，對健康很好。

⑤ 工作和學校的名詞

  1. I've got an interview for a job in a bank.
    我在一家銀行有一個工作面試。
  2. My boss wants the report by Friday.
    我老闆要我星期五前交報告。
  3. Our project is about recycling.
    我們的專題是關於回收。
  4. The course starts next month.
    課程下個月開始。

⑥ 日常生活的名詞

  1. Excuse me, we'd like to pay. Could you bring us the bill?
    不好意思，我們要結帳，可以給我們帳單嗎？
  2. I've got an appointment at the dentist's.
    我在牙醫診所有預約。
  3. There's a problem with my phone. The screen won't work.
    我的手機有問題，螢幕不能用。
  4. What's the price of this jacket?
    這件夾克多少錢？

⑦ 名詞片語當主詞

  1. The weather was terrible all week.
    整個星期天氣都很糟。
  2. The result of the match surprised everyone.
    比賽的結果讓大家都很驚訝。
  3. My favourite subject at school is history.
    我在學校最喜歡的科目是歷史。
  4. The noise from the street keeps me awake.
    街上的噪音讓我睡不著。
  5. Their decision was a big mistake.
    他們的決定是一個很大的錯誤。
