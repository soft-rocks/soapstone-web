A person who acts in that way is sure to be successful.

用那種方式做事的人一定會成功。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are + sure to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

be sure to一定會。
be certain to一定會。比較正式。
原形動詞確定會發生的事。

說好⁠處和結⁠果

例句

By bicycle, you are sure to arrive at work on time.

騎腳踏車，你一定能準時上班。

寫作文和商業信件時，常用來說某個做法一定會帶來的好處。

be sure 後⁠面⁠接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，或接完⁠整⁠的 that 子⁠句

A person who acts in that way is sure to be successful.

He is sure that win the race.

He is sure to win the race.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

A person who acts in that way is sure to be successful.

We are certain to attracting more customers.

We are certain to attract more customers.

sure 是形⁠容⁠詞，前⁠面要⁠有 be 動⁠詞

A person who acts in that way is sure to be successful.

This plan sure to work.

This plan is sure to work.

例句

① 主詞 + be + sure to + 原形動詞

  1. A dog like that is sure to need a lot of exercise.
    那樣的狗一定需要很多運動。
  2. By bicycle, you don't get stuck in traffic, and you're sure to arrive at work on time.
    騎腳踏車不會塞車，你一定能準時上班。
  3. This recipe is sure to impress your guests.
    這道食譜一定會讓你的客人印象深刻。
  4. The new swimming pool is sure to attract families.
    新的游泳池一定會吸引很多家庭。
  5. If you water the seeds every day, they're sure to grow.
    如果你每天幫種子澆水，它們一定會長出來。
  6. A walk in the fresh air is sure to make you feel better.
    在新鮮空氣裡散個步一定會讓你覺得好一點。
  7. With such a good cast, the play is sure to do well.
    有這麼好的演員陣容，這齣戲一定會很成功。
  8. The question is sure to come up in the exam.
    這個問題一定會出現在考試裡。
  9. A present like that is sure to make her smile.
    那樣的禮物一定會讓她笑。
  10. Your story is sure to make everyone laugh.
    你的故事一定會讓大家笑。

② 主詞 + be + certain to + 原形動詞

  1. The market is still small, but we are certain to attract more and more students.
    市場還很小，可是我們一定會吸引越來越多學生。
  2. The new law is certain to cause some problems.
    新法律一定會造成一些問題。
  3. The company is certain to make a profit this year.
    那家公司今年一定會獲利。
  4. The decision is certain to upset the fans.
    這個決定一定會讓球迷不高興。
  5. Prices are certain to rise after the tax change.
    稅制改變後，物價一定會上漲。
  6. The minister is certain to face difficult questions.
    部長一定會面對很難回答的問題。
  7. Building a second runway is certain to divide the local community.
    蓋第二條跑道一定會讓當地居民意見分歧。
  8. The festival is certain to bring money into the town.
    這個節慶活動一定會為鎮上帶來收入。

③ 主詞 + be + not certain + to + 原形動詞

  1. The new medicine is not certain to work for everyone.
    新藥不一定對每個人都有效。
  2. The weather isn't certain to stay fine all weekend.
    天氣不一定整個週末都會很好。
  3. A higher price isn't certain to mean better quality.
    價錢比較高不一定代表品質比較好。
  4. The new manager isn't certain to stay for long.
    新經理不一定會待很久。
  5. Hard work isn't certain to bring success, but it helps.
    努力不一定會帶來成功，可是有幫助。

④ 主詞 + be + almost / fairly + sure / certain + to + 原形動詞

  1. She is almost certain to win the gold medal.
    她幾乎一定會拿到金牌。
  2. The train is almost sure to be late in this snow.
    這種雪，火車幾乎一定會誤點。
  3. The shop is fairly certain to close next year.
    那家店明年相當確定會關門。
  4. The tickets are almost certain to sell out on the first day.
    票幾乎一定會在第一天賣完。
  5. Our neighbours are almost sure to complain about the music.
    我們的鄰居幾乎一定會抱怨音樂太吵。
  6. The vote is almost certain to go in our favour.
    投票結果幾乎一定會對我們有利。
  7. The bridge is almost sure to need repairs after the storm.
    暴風雨過後，那座橋幾乎一定需要修理。
  8. He is almost certain to be chosen for the team.
    他幾乎一定會被選進隊伍。（被動）
