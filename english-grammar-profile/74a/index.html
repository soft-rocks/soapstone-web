How dare + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞!

How dare、主⁠詞、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

How dare you say that this event was a complete failure!

How dare你怎麼敢、竟敢。
主詞常用 you，也可以用 he、she、they、people。
原形動詞讓說話者生氣的事。前面不加 to，也不加 -⁠s。

說第⁠三⁠人⁠稱

How dare you say that this event was a complete failure!

例句

How dare she read my diary!

她竟敢看我的日記！

主詞是 he、she 時，dare 也不加 -⁠s。

單⁠獨說 How dare you!

例句

"You're lying." "How dare you!"

How dare you! 可以單獨說，表示非常生氣。

How dare you take my bike!

例句

① How dare you + 原形動詞!

  1. How dare you make fun of my accent!
    你竟敢取笑我的口音！
  2. How dare you read my messages without asking!
    你竟敢沒問我就看我的訊息！
  3. How dare you speak to your grandmother like that!
    你竟敢那樣跟你奶奶說話！
  4. How dare you drive my new car to the beach and fill it with sand!
    你竟敢把我的新車開去海邊，還弄得滿車都是沙！
  5. How dare you call me a liar!
    你竟敢說我是騙子！
  6. How dare you come into my room!
    你竟敢進我的房間！
  7. How dare you laugh at my cooking!
    你竟敢笑我煮的菜！
  8. How dare you tell my secret to everyone!
    你竟敢把我的祕密告訴大家！
  9. How dare you blame me for your mistake!
    你竟敢把你的錯怪到我身上！
  10. How dare you park in front of my gate!
    你竟敢把車停在我家大門前面！

② How dare + people / they + 原形動詞!

  1. How dare people say that we don't do any sport!
    人們竟敢說我們都不運動！
  2. How dare they raise the rent again!
    他們竟敢又漲房租！
  3. How dare they cut down the trees in our park!
    他們竟敢砍掉我們公園裡的樹！
  4. How dare people leave rubbish on the beach!
    人們竟敢把垃圾丟在海灘上！
  5. How dare they cancel the concert at the last minute!
    他們竟敢在最後一刻取消演唱會！
  6. How dare they treat their workers like that!
    他們竟敢那樣對待員工！

③ How dare + 第三人稱單數主詞 + 原形動詞!

  1. How dare she go through my bag while I was out!
    她竟敢趁我出去的時候翻我的包包！
  2. How dare he say I'm lazy!
    他竟敢說我懶！
  3. How dare she use my name without asking!
    她竟敢沒問就用我的名字！
  4. How dare he arrive two hours late and say nothing!
    他竟敢遲到兩個小時還什麼都不說！
  5. How dare the manager shout at the new staff!
    那個經理竟敢對新員工大吼！
  6. How dare he eat the last piece of cake!
    他竟敢吃掉最後一塊蛋糕！
  7. How dare that newspaper print lies about our school!
    那家報紙竟敢刊登關於我們學校的謊言！

④ How dare you!：單獨使用

  1. "You stole my wallet." "How dare you!"
    你偷了我的錢包。你好大的膽子！
  2. "Your work is terrible." "How dare you! I worked all night."
    你做得很糟。你怎麼敢這樣說！我做了一整晚。
  3. How dare you! I've never been so insulted.
    你好大的膽子！我從來沒被這樣侮辱過。（被動）
  4. "You only care about money." "How dare you!"
    你只在乎錢。你怎麼敢這樣說！
  5. How dare you! Get out of my house.
    你好大的膽子！滾出我家。
  6. "I think you cheated." "How dare you! I studied for weeks."
    我覺得你作弊。你怎麼敢這樣說！我讀了好幾個星期。
  7. How dare you! After everything I've done for you!
    你怎麼敢這樣！我為你做了那麼多！
  8. "That's not your real hair." "How dare you!"
    那不是你真的頭髮。你好大的膽子！
