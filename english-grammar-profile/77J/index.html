I'm going to get up early on Saturday.

我星期六要早起。

不⁠及物片⁠語動⁠詞後⁠面不⁠接⁠受⁠詞

I'm going to get up early on Saturday.

我星期六要早起。

get up 的意思是起床。

片⁠語動⁠詞後⁠面可⁠以接時⁠間、地⁠方或 to + 地⁠方

I'm going to get up early on Saturday.

時間

We can meet up at 2 pm.

我們可以下午兩點碰面。
地方

Come in and sit down on the sofa.

進來坐在沙發上吧。
to + 地方

Shall we go out to a concert this weekend?

我們這個週末要不要出去聽演唱會？

同⁠一⁠個動⁠詞，小⁠品⁠詞不⁠同，意⁠思不⁠同

I get up at seven every day.

here 是地⁠方副⁠詞，不⁠是小⁠品⁠詞

例句

① get up / wake up

  1. "Why are you so tired?" "I got up at five to catch the train."
    你怎麼這麼累？我五點就起床趕火車。
  2. My little brother wakes up at six every morning, even on Sundays.
    我弟弟每天早上六點就醒了，連星期天也一樣。
  3. On holiday we don't get up until ten.
    放假的時候，我們十點才起床。
  4. I woke up in the night because of the storm.
    暴風雨讓我半夜醒了過來。
  5. Every day I can see the sea when I wake up.
    每天我醒來都能看到海。

② go out / come back

  1. "Shall we go out tonight?" "Good idea. There's a new film on."
    我們今晚要不要出去？好主意。有部新電影上映了。
  2. My parents go out for dinner every Friday.
    我爸媽每個星期五都出去吃晚餐。
  3. "When is Dad coming back?" "He'll be back at six."
    爸爸什麼時候回來？他六點會回來。
  4. She went to Spain last year, and she wants to go back.
    她去年去了西班牙，還想再回去。

③ come in / come over

  1. "Hello? Can I come in?" "Yes, come in! The door's open."
    哈囉？我可以進來嗎？可以，進來！門沒鎖。
  2. If you have time, you can come over and I'll introduce you to my friends.
    你有時間的話可以過來，我介紹我的朋友給你認識。
  3. "Whose bag is this?" "Anna's. She came over for lunch."
    這是誰的包包？Anna 的。她過來吃午餐。
  4. It's raining. Come in and have a cup of tea.
    下雨了。進來喝杯茶吧。

④ sit down / stand up

  1. "Please sit down," said the doctor.
    請坐，醫生說。
  2. The students stood up when the teacher came in.
    老師進來的時候，學生們站了起來。
  3. There were no chairs, so we sat down on the floor.
    沒有椅子，所以我們坐在地上。
  4. Stand up, please. We're going to sing the song.
    請站起來。我們要唱這首歌了。

⑤ meet up

  1. "Are you free on Saturday?" "Yes. Let's meet up at the café at two."
    你星期六有空嗎？有。我們兩點在咖啡店碰面吧。
  2. My old classmates meet up once a year.
    我的老同學一年聚一次。
  3. We met up after work and went to the cinema.
    我們下班後碰面，去看了電影。

⑥ hurry up / go away

  1. "Hurry up! The bus is coming!"
    快一點！公車來了！
  2. My sister was in the bathroom for an hour, so I told her to hurry up.
    我姊姊在浴室待了一個小時，所以我叫她快一點。
  3. "Go away! I'm busy," my brother shouted.
    走開！我在忙，我弟弟大叫。
  4. We're going away for the weekend.
    我們這個週末要出去玩。

⑦ 未來的計畫

  1. I'm going to get up early tomorrow and go running.
    我明天要早起去跑步。
  2. "Can you come over on Sunday?" "Sure, I'll come over after lunch."
    你星期天可以過來嗎？好啊，我吃完午餐就過去。
  3. We're going out for pizza tonight. Do you want to come?
    我們今晚要出去吃披薩。你要來嗎？

⑧ 問句和否定

  1. "What time did you wake up this morning?" "Around nine. I was so tired."
    你今天早上幾點醒來的？九點左右。我好累。
  2. "Did you go out last night?" "No, I stayed at home."
    你昨晚有出去嗎？沒有，我待在家。
  3. The baby didn't wake up all night.
    寶寶整晚都沒醒。
  4. Don't stand up in the boat!
    在船上不要站起來！
