It may well be that after leaving school they become our employees.

我認為他們很可能會在這些付款問題上達成協議。

主⁠詞 + may well + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

may well、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

It may well be that after leaving school they become our employees.

may well很有可能。比 may 更有把握。
原形動詞很可能發生的事。

It may well be that

It may well be that after leaving school they become our employees.

例句

It may well be that the old method is better.

很有可能舊的方法比較好。

It may well be that 加子句，常用在報告和議論文，提出一個對方沒想到的可能。

well 放⁠在 may 後⁠面

It may well be that after leaving school they become our employees.

They well may come.

They may well come.

may well 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

It may well be that after leaving school they become our employees.

It may well to rain.

It may well rain.

這⁠個說⁠法用副⁠詞 well，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞 good

It may well be that after leaving school they become our employees.

She may good be right.

She may well be right.

例句

① 主詞 + may well + 原形動詞：很有可能達成

  1. The two companies have talked for weeks. I think they may well come to an agreement on the payment problems.
    兩家公司談了好幾個星期。我想他們很有可能在付款的問題上達成協議。
  2. If we keep working at this speed, we may well finish the project early.
    如果我們保持這個速度，很有可能提早完成這個專案。
  3. With this new coach, the team may well reach the final.
    有了這個新教練，球隊很有可能打進決賽。
  4. If she keeps practising, she may well become a professional singer.
    如果她繼續練習，她很有可能成為職業歌手。
  5. The book may well become a film one day.
    這本書很有可能有一天會拍成電影。
  6. Our little shop may well grow into a big business.
    我們的小店很有可能發展成大企業。

② 主詞 + may well + be：很有可能是

  1. You may well be right about the date. Let me check.
    你對日期的說法很有可能是對的。我查一下。
  2. That restaurant may well be the best in town.
    那家餐廳很有可能是城裡最好的。
  3. The old painting in the attic may well be worth a lot of money.
    閣樓裡那幅舊畫很有可能很值錢。
  4. He may well be the oldest man in the village.
    他很有可能是村子裡年紀最大的人。
  5. This may well be the hottest summer for fifty years.
    這很有可能是五十年來最熱的夏天。
  6. Her idea may well be the answer to our problem.
    她的想法很有可能就是我們問題的答案。

③ It may well be that + 子句：提出沒想到的可能

  1. It may well be that the new road brings more customers to the town.
    很有可能新路會為鎮上帶來更多客人。
  2. It may well be that the problem is in the software, not the machine.
    問題很有可能出在軟體，不在機器。
  3. It may well be that children learn more outside the classroom.
    孩子很有可能在教室外學到更多。
  4. It may well be that she never got your letter.
    她很有可能根本沒收到你的信。
  5. It may well be that the cheaper hotel is better.
    很有可能比較便宜的飯店比較好。
  6. It may well be that people are happier with less.
    很有可能人擁有少一點反而比較快樂。

④ 主詞 + may well + 原形動詞：提醒可能的問題

  1. Book early. The tickets may well sell out in a day.
    早點訂。票很有可能一天就賣完。
  2. Take a coat. The wind may well get stronger in the afternoon.
    帶件外套。下午風很有可能變大。
  3. The new rules may well cause problems for small shops.
    新規定很有可能為小店帶來問題。
  4. If you don't water the garden, the plants may well die.
    如果你不幫花園澆水，植物很有可能會死。
  5. The road may well be closed if it snows.
    如果下雪，那條路很有可能會封閉。
  6. Without a map, we may well get lost in the forest.
    沒有地圖，我們很有可能在森林裡迷路。

⑤ may well + 原形動詞, but + 句子：承認，再提出另一面

  1. You may well be tired, but we still have to finish this.
    你很有可能累了，可是我們還是要把這個做完。
  2. The plan may well save money, but it will take a long time.
    這個計畫很有可能省錢，可是要花很長的時間。
  3. He may well be clever, but he doesn't work hard.
    他很有可能很聰明，可是他不認真。
  4. The flat may well be cheap, but it's very far from the centre.
    那間公寓很有可能很便宜，可是離市中心很遠。
  5. The test may well be difficult, but you're ready for it.
    考試很有可能很難，可是你已經準備好了。
  6. Prices may well go down, but nobody knows when.
    價格很有可能會下降，可是沒有人知道什麼時候。
  7. The car may well be fast, but is it safe?
    這台車很有可能很快，可是它安全嗎？
