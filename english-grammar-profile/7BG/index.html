例句

① it has been found / shown that

  1. We asked 200 customers about our shop. It has been found that some products were difficult to find.
    我們訪問了兩百位顧客。調查發現，有些產品很難找到。
  2. It has been shown that children who read at home do better at school.
    研究顯示，在家閱讀的孩子在學校表現比較好。
  3. In our study, it was found that most people check their phones before breakfast.
    在我們的研究中發現，大部分人吃早餐前會先看手機。
  4. It has been shown many times that exercise helps people sleep better.
    已經有很多次研究顯示，運動可以幫人睡得更好。

② it can be concluded that

  1. To sum up, it can be concluded that this report has tried to summarise the main points about the Spanish school system.
    總之，可以得出結論：這份報告試著總結了西班牙教育制度的重點。
  2. From the results, it can be concluded that the new timetable has reduced delays.
    從結果可以得出結論：新的時刻表減少了誤點。
  3. It may be concluded that the course was too short for most students.
    可以得出結論：這門課對大部分學生來說太短了。

③ it is believed / thought / said that

  1. Our guide told us about the castle on the hill. It is believed that the castle was built in the twelfth century.
    導遊跟我們介紹山上的城堡。一般認為這座城堡建於十二世紀。
  2. Nobody has counted them for years, but it is thought that around five hundred people live on the island.
    好幾年沒人算過了，不過一般認為島上住了大約五百人。
  3. Nobody swims there at night. It is said that the lake is haunted, but I don't believe it.
    晚上沒人去那裡游泳。據說那座湖鬧鬼，可是我不相信。
  4. It is widely known that sugar is bad for your teeth.
    大家都知道糖對牙齒不好。
  5. It was once thought that the earth was flat.
    以前大家認為地球是平的。

④ it is expected that

  1. Food is already expensive, and it is expected that prices will rise again next year.
    食物已經很貴了，預計明年物價還會再上漲。
  2. It is expected that the new road will be finished by May.
    預計新路五月會完工。
  3. It was expected that more people would come to the opening.
    原本預計會有更多人來參加開幕。

⑤ it was decided / agreed that

  1. At the meeting, it was decided that the office would close at four on Fridays.
    在會議上決定，辦公室星期五四點下班。
  2. After a long discussion, it was agreed that the trip would go ahead.
    討論了很久之後，大家同意照計畫去旅行。
  3. It has been decided that all students will wear the new uniform from September.
    已經決定九月起所有學生都要穿新制服。
  4. It was agreed that the money should be spent on new books.
    大家同意這筆錢應該用來買新書。

⑥ it is recommended / suggested that

  1. Based on these results, it is recommended that staff take more breaks.
    根據這些結果，建議員工多休息。
  2. It is suggested that the café should open earlier in the morning.
    建議咖啡店早上早一點開。
  3. It is strongly recommended that visitors book their tickets online.
    強烈建議遊客在網路上訂票。
  4. It has been suggested that the park could be used for concerts.
    有人建議公園可以用來辦音樂會。

⑦ it should be noted / pointed out that

  1. The results look good. However, it should be noted that only fifty people answered the survey.
    結果看起來不錯。不過，要注意的是，只有五十個人回答了問卷。
  2. It must be pointed out that the figures are from last year.
    必須指出，這些數字是去年的。
  3. It should be remembered that the town had no hospital before 1990.
    要記得，鎮上在 1990 年以前沒有醫院。
  4. The hotel looks cheap online, but it should be noted that the prices do not include tax.
    網路上那間旅館看起來很便宜，可是要注意的是，價格不含稅。

⑧ 報告的結論

  1. In conclusion, it has been shown that the new system saves both time and money.
    總之，結果顯示新系統既省時又省錢。
  2. Overall, it can be said that the event was well organised.
    整體來說，可以說這次活動辦得很好。
  3. It is hoped that this report will help the manager make a decision.
    希望這份報告能幫經理做決定。
  4. Finally, it is generally accepted that the building needs to be repaired.
    最後，大家普遍認為這棟建築需要修理。
