It's the biggest room in the house.

這是整間房子最大的房間。

最⁠高⁠級後⁠面⁠接 in 加地⁠方

It's the biggest room in the house.

這是我們鎮上最安靜的街。

一⁠般名⁠詞前⁠面加 the，地⁠名不⁠加

It's the biggest room in the house.

in + the + 名詞

It's the oldest café in the city.

那是這座城市裡最老的咖啡店。
in + 地名

It's the tallest building in Taipei.

那是台北最高的大樓。

範⁠圍是地⁠方⁠時用 in，不⁠用 of

It's the biggest room in the house.

It's the oldest café of the city.

It's the oldest café in the city.

國⁠家和城⁠市⁠的名⁠稱前⁠面不⁠加 the

It's the biggest room in the house.

It's the highest mountain in the Taiwan.

It's the highest mountain in Taiwan.

短⁠的形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 most

It's the biggest room in the house.

These are the most cheapest jeans in the shop.

These are the cheapest jeans in the shop.

例句

① in the house / room / building

  1. My bedroom is the smallest room in the house.
    我的臥室是家裡最小的房間。
  2. In winter we all sit by the stove. The kitchen is the warmest place in the house.
    冬天我們都坐在爐子旁邊。廚房是家裡最溫暖的地方。
  3. Don't take Dad's armchair. That's the most comfortable seat in the room.
    別坐爸爸的扶手椅。那是房間裡最舒服的位子。
  4. We live on the twentieth floor. Our flat is the highest one in the building.
    我們住在二十樓。我們家是這棟樓裡最高的一戶。
  5. Everyone wants to sit by the window. It's the brightest spot in the office.
    大家都想坐在窗邊。那是辦公室裡最亮的地方。
  6. The guide showed us the oldest painting in the museum.
    導覽員帶我們看博物館裡最古老的畫。

② in the shop / restaurant / hotel

  1. I chose these jeans because they were the cheapest ones in the shop.
    我選了這條牛仔褲，因為它是店裡最便宜的。
  2. It's our first time here. What's the most popular dish in this restaurant?
    我們第一次來這裡。這家餐廳最受歡迎的菜是什麼？
  3. The hotel was nearly empty, so we got the biggest room in the hotel.
    飯店幾乎沒有客人，所以我們拿到飯店裡最大的房間。
  4. Don't touch that one. This is the most expensive watch in the store.
    別碰那支。這是店裡最貴的手錶。
  5. My son always asks for the chocolate cake. It's the sweetest thing in the bakery.
    我兒子每次都要巧克力蛋糕。那是麵包店裡最甜的東西。

③ in the city / town / street

  1. Elm Road is the widest street in our neighborhood.
    Elm Road 是我們這一區最寬的街。
  2. People queue outside my cousins' shop every morning. Their bakery sells the best bread in the city.
    每天早上都有人在我表親的店外面排隊。他們的麵包店賣全城最好吃的麵包。
  3. Our neighbours have roses all over the front wall. Their house is the prettiest one in the street.
    我們的鄰居前面整面牆都是玫瑰。他們家是這條街上最漂亮的房子。
  4. Let's open the new shop near the station. It's the busiest place in town.
    我們把新店開在車站附近吧。那是鎮上最熱鬧的地方。
  5. I eat my lunch here on sunny days. This park is the greenest area in the city center.
    天氣好的時候我在這裡吃午餐。這座公園是市中心綠地最多的地方。

④ in + 地名

  1. You can see the whole harbour from our room. This hotel has the best view in Kaohsiung.
    從我們的房間可以看到整個港口。這家飯店有高雄最棒的景觀。
  2. Jade Mountain is the highest mountain in Taiwan.
    玉山是台灣最高的山。
  3. Is it Toronto or Montreal? Which is the biggest city in Canada?
    是多倫多還是蒙特婁？加拿大最大的城市是哪一個？
  4. Look at the gold walls in the photo. This is the most famous temple in Kyoto.
    你看照片裡金色的牆。這是京都最有名的寺廟。
  5. We went up the Tokyo Skytree on our trip. It's the tallest tower in Japan.
    我們旅行的時候上了東京晴空塔。它是日本最高的塔。
  6. Go south for your summer holiday. Kenting has the best beaches in Taiwan.
    暑假往南部去。墾丁有台灣最好的海灘。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. We only have fifty dollars a night. Is this the cheapest hotel in the area?
    我們一個晚上只有五十塊。這是這一帶最便宜的旅館嗎？
  2. Excuse me, I need some cash. Where's the nearest bank in this neighborhood?
    不好意思，我需要一些現金。這附近最近的銀行在哪裡？
  3. My mother needs an operation on her heart. Who's the best doctor in the hospital?
    我媽媽心臟需要開刀。醫院裡最好的醫生是誰？
  4. I sat behind a big pillar all night. That was the worst seat in the theater.
    我整個晚上坐在一根大柱子後面。那是劇院裡最差的位子。
  5. Leo always remembers my order. He's the friendliest waiter in the café.
    里歐總是記得我點什麼。他是咖啡店裡最友善的服務生。
  6. I study there before exams. The library is the quietest building in the school.
    考試前我都在那裡讀書。圖書館是學校裡最安靜的建築。
  7. Line 2 goes through the centre. It's the most crowded underground line in the city.
    二號線經過市中心。它是城裡最擠的地鐵線。
  8. Nobody wants to sleep in the back bedroom. It's the coldest room in the flat.
    沒有人想睡後面那間臥室。那是公寓裡最冷的房間。
