This is the information about the new art class: it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.

這是新美術課的資訊：下午 4 點開始，6 點結束。

this 指接⁠下⁠來⁠的內⁠容

This is the information about the new art class: it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.

這是新美術課的資訊：下午 4 點開始，6 點結束。

this 常帶⁠出⁠的內⁠容

This is the information about the new art class: it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.

號碼和地址

This is my number: 0754 567 78 51.

這是我的電話號碼：0754 567 78 51。
計畫和時間

This is the plan: we meet at ten and take the bus.

計畫是這樣：我們十點碰面，然後搭公車。
要對方聽的話

Listen to this: our teacher is getting married!

你聽我說：我們老師要結婚了！

this 指後⁠面，that 指前⁠面

This is the information about the new art class: it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.

這是我的地址：公園路 12 號。你記下來了嗎？

帶⁠出後⁠面⁠的內⁠容用 this

This is the information about the new art class: it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.

That is my email address: tom.green@mail.com.

This is my email address: tom.green@mail.com.

this 接 is

This is the information about the new art class: it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.

This are the plan: we leave at nine and come back at five.

This is the plan: we leave at nine and come back at five.

細⁠節前⁠面加冒⁠號

This is my address: 25 Hill Street.

this 指前⁠面⁠的事

我們沒趕上火車，所以參加婚禮遲到了。

Here is 帶⁠出內⁠容

這是我的電話號碼：0712 345 678。

例句

① 號碼和地址

  1. This is my new address: 14 Green Lane, Oxford.
    這是我的新地址：牛津綠巷 14 號。
  2. If you need help, this is the number to call: 0800 123 456.
    如果你需要幫忙，要打的號碼是這個：0800 123 456。
  3. This is the name of the hotel: the Sea View.
    旅館的名字是這個：海景飯店。
  4. This is the website: www.artclass.com.
    網址是這個：www.artclass.com。
  5. This is the code for the door: 4 7 2 9.
    門的密碼是這個：4 7 2 9。

② 計畫和時間

  1. This is the plan for Saturday: lunch at one, then the cinema.
    星期六的計畫是這樣：一點吃午餐，然後去看電影。
  2. This is the timetable: the bus leaves at 8.15 and arrives at 9.
    時刻表是這樣：公車八點十五分出發，九點到。
  3. This is what we'll do: you buy the food and I'll cook.
    我們這樣做：你去買菜，我來煮。
  4. This is the programme for the day: a tour in the morning and free time in the afternoon.
    今天的行程是這樣：早上導覽，下午自由活動。
  5. This is the menu for the party: pizza, salad and ice cream.
    派對的菜單是這樣：披薩、沙拉和冰淇淋。

③ 說明做法

  1. This is how you make tea: boil the water, then add the tea bag.
    泡茶是這樣泡的：先把水煮開，再放茶包。
  2. This is the way to the station: turn left, then go straight on.
    去車站要這樣走：左轉，然後直走。
  3. This is what you do: press the green button and wait.
    你要這樣做：按綠色的按鈕，然後等著。
  4. This is the best way to learn words: write them on cards.
    學單字最好的方法是這個：把單字寫在卡片上。
  5. This is my advice: get a good night's sleep before the exam.
    我的建議是這樣：考試前好好睡一覺。

④ 要對方注意聽

  1. Listen to this: the school is closed tomorrow!
    你聽我說：學校明天停課！
  2. Look at this: a T-shirt for only two pounds.
    你看這個：一件 T 恤只要兩英鎊。
  3. Guess what? This is the big news: I'm going to Japan.
    你猜怎麼樣？大消息是：我要去日本了。
  4. Read this: "No swimming after 6 p.m."
    你看這個：下午六點後禁止游泳。
  5. This is the funny part: the dog ate his homework!
    好笑的地方是這個：狗吃了他的作業！

⑤ 通知和公告

  1. This is the information about the new art class: the class is on Fridays, and it starts at 4 p.m. and finishes at 6 p.m.
    這是新美術課的資訊：課在星期五，下午四點開始，六點結束。
  2. This is an important message for all students: the library will close at 3 p.m. today.
    這是給全體學生的重要通知：圖書館今天下午三點關門。
  3. This is the price list: adults £8, children £4.
    價目表是這樣：成人八英鎊，兒童四英鎊。
  4. This is a change to the timetable: there is no class on Monday.
    課表有異動：星期一沒有課。
  5. This is the room for the meeting: Room 12, second floor.
    開會的房間是這間：二樓十二號房。

⑥ 電子郵件和簡訊

  1. This is my email address: anna.lee@mail.com. Write to me soon!
    這是我的電子郵件地址：anna.lee@mail.com，快寫信給我！
  2. This is the list of things to bring: a towel, a hat and some water.
    要帶的東西是這些：一條毛巾、一頂帽子和一些水。
  3. This is where we're meeting: outside the cinema at seven.
    我們在這裡碰面：七點，電影院外面。
  4. This is my question: can you come to my party on Saturday?
    我的問題是這個：你星期六可以來我的派對嗎？
  5. This is the address of the restaurant: 8 King Street.
    餐廳的地址是這個：國王街 8 號。
  6. This is the only thing I need for my birthday: a new bike!
    我生日只想要這個：一台新腳踏車！
