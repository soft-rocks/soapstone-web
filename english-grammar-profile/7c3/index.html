I'm living with my friend in Wolverhampton.

我現在跟我朋友一起住在伍爾弗漢普頓。

I'm living 表⁠示住⁠在這⁠裡是暫⁠時⁠的

我現在跟我朋友一起住在伍爾弗漢普頓。

現⁠在簡⁠單式說長⁠久⁠的情⁠況，進⁠行⁠式說暫⁠時⁠的情⁠況

I'm living with my friend in Wolverhampton.

長久

I live in Taipei.

我住在台北。
暫時

I'm living in Taipei this year.

我今年住在台北。
長久

My mum works at the hospital.

我媽在醫院工作。
暫時

My mum is working today.

我媽今天要上班。

只維⁠持一⁠段時⁠間⁠的情⁠況，用現⁠在進⁠行⁠式

I'm living with my friend in Wolverhampton.

This month I stay with my grandparents.

This month I'm staying with my grandparents.

一⁠輩⁠子⁠的事不⁠是暫⁠時⁠的情⁠況

I'm living with my friend in Wolverhampton.

My parents are living in Canada all their lives.

My parents have lived in Canada all their lives.

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式要⁠有 am、is 或 are

I'm living with my friend in Wolverhampton.

I studying at a language school this year.

I'm studying at a language school this year.

例句

① be living + 地點

  1. Dear Mr Hill, I'm living with my friend in Wolverhampton this year.
    親愛的 Hill 老師，我今年跟我朋友一起住在伍爾弗漢普頓。
  2. The builders are fixing our roof, so we're living with my grandparents for now.
    工人在修我們家的屋頂，所以我們暫時住在我爺爺奶奶家。
  3. We only see my brother on video calls. He's living in Australia for a year.
    我們只能跟我哥哥視訊。他這一年住在澳洲。
  4. We're living in a small flat until our new house is ready.
    新房子好之前，我們先住在一間小公寓。
  5. She's living with her parents while she looks for a job.
    她在找工作，這段時間先跟爸媽住。

② be staying + 地點

  1. I can't meet you this week. My cousins are staying with us.
    我這個星期沒辦法跟你見面。我的表兄弟姊妹住在我們家。
  2. I hear you're on holiday. Where are you staying in Paris?
    聽說你在度假。你在巴黎住哪裡？
  3. Mum's in hospital, so I'm staying at home to look after my little sister.
    媽媽住院了，所以我待在家照顧我妹妹。
  4. Greetings from Spain! We're staying at a lovely hotel near the beach.
    從西班牙向你問好！我們住在海邊一家很棒的飯店。
  5. He's staying in bed today because he has a fever.
    他發燒，所以今天躺在床上休息。

③ be working

  1. My mother is working today, so my older brother can take us to the sports centre.
    我媽今天要上班，所以我哥哥可以帶我們去運動中心。
  2. Dad is working from home this week because the office is closed.
    辦公室關門，所以爸爸這個星期在家上班。
  3. I need money for my trip, so I'm working in a café during the summer holidays.
    我需要旅費，所以暑假在一家咖啡店打工。
  4. She's working nights at the moment, so she sleeps in the day.
    她現在上夜班，所以白天睡覺。
  5. Tom is working at the front desk until the new receptionist starts.
    新櫃檯人員來之前，Tom 先在櫃檯幫忙。

④ be studying

  1. I'm studying at Wolverhampton College for six months.
    我在伍爾弗漢普頓學院念六個月。
  2. My sister can't go out much. She is studying for her driving test this month.
    我姊姊沒什麼時間出門。她這個月在準備駕照考試。
  3. We're studying Shakespeare in English class this term.
    我們這學期的英文課在讀莎士比亞。
  4. He's studying in Japan for a year as an exchange student.
    他以交換學生的身分在日本念一年。
  5. I'm studying hard at the moment because my exams are next week.
    我現在很用功，因為下星期就考試了。

⑤ 其他常用動詞

  1. I'm using my dad's car this week because mine is at the garage.
    我的車在修車廠，所以我這個星期開我爸的車。
  2. We're having lunch in the garden every day while the weather is nice.
    天氣好的這段時間，我們每天在花園吃午餐。
  3. The kids are sleeping in the living room while we fix their bedroom.
    我們在修孩子們的房間，這段時間他們睡客廳。
  4. I'm wearing glasses this week. My eyes are too sore for contact lenses.
    我這個星期戴眼鏡。我的眼睛太痛，戴不了隱形眼鏡。
  5. My aunt is looking after our dog while we're on holiday.
    我們去度假的時候，我阿姨幫我們照顧狗。
  6. He's taking the bus to school until his bike is fixed.
    他的腳踏車修好之前，他搭公車上學。

⑥ 疑問句和否定句

  1. I'd love to visit you. Are you still living in that flat near the station?
    我很想去看你。你還住在車站附近那間公寓嗎？
  2. I'm not working this week. I'm on holiday.
    我這個星期不用上班。我在休假。
  3. Who's staying in your room while you're away?
    你不在的時候誰住你的房間？
  4. She isn't driving at the moment because she broke her arm.
    她手臂斷了，所以現在不開車。
