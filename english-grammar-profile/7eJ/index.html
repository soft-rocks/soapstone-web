Should you fail to do so, we would have no choice but to take further action.

如果您沒有這麼做，我們將不得不採取進一步的行動。

should 放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，取⁠代 if

Should you fail to do so, we would have no choice but to take further action.

如果天氣變壞，我們會把典禮延期。

這個句型說未來可能發生的事，用在客氣或正式的場合。

否⁠定和被⁠動

not + 原形動詞
否定

Should the payment not reach us by Friday, we would have to cancel your order.

如果款項星期五前沒有匯到，我們就得取消您的訂單。
be + 過去分詞
被動

Should the flight be cancelled, the airline would book you on the next one.

如果航班取消，航空公司會幫您訂下一班。

not 放在主詞後面。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句用 would like 或 would be happy

would like
想做的事

Should we win the contract, we would like to hire ten new engineers.

如果我們拿到合約，我們想再請十位工程師。
would be happy
客氣的提議

Should you require a larger room, we would be happy to upgrade you.

如果您需要大一點的房間，我們很樂意幫您升等。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以用 could 或 might

could
可以做到的事

Should you change your plans, we could still reserve a seat for you.

如果您改變計畫，我們還是可以幫您保留一個位子。
might
可能發生的事

Should the river rise any higher, the bridge might collapse.

如果河水再漲高，那座橋可能會垮。

原句：正⁠式⁠的警⁠告信

Should you fail to do so, we would have no choice but to take further action.

如果您沒有這麼做，我們將不得不採取進一步的行動。

fail to do so 是沒有做到前面要求的事。would have no choice but to 說那時只能做的事。

should 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Should you fail to do so, we would have no choice but to take further action.

Should the customer requires a refund, we would process it within a week.

Should the customer require a refund, we would process it within a week.

倒⁠裝已⁠經取⁠代 if

Should you fail to do so, we would have no choice but to take further action.

If should you change your mind, we would be happy to hear from you.

Should you change your mind, we would be happy to hear from you.

保留 if 時，用 If you should change your mind。

倒⁠裝⁠的句⁠型不⁠縮⁠寫

Shouldn't the parcel arrive, we would send another one.

Should the parcel not arrive, we would send another one.

If + 主⁠詞 + should 意⁠思一⁠樣，也用在口⁠語

祈使句

If you should see Tom, tell him to call me.

如果你碰到 Tom，叫他打給我。
would

If the printer should break down, we would call the technician.

如果印表機壞了，我們會找技術人員來修。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以用 will 或祈⁠使⁠句

祈使句
提出要求

Should you need directions to the venue, please call our office.

如果您需要會場的路線說明，請打電話到我們的辦公室。
will
一定會做的事

Should the meeting run late, I will call you.

如果會議拖晚了，我會打電話給你。

主要子句用 would 說可能的結果。

例句

① 正式信件

  1. Should you wish to cancel your booking, we would need written notice.
    如果您想取消預訂，我們需要書面通知。
  2. Should you decide to accept our offer, we would arrange a start date in June.
    如果您決定接受我們的條件，我們會把到職日安排在六月。
  3. Should you require a larger room, we would be happy to upgrade you.
    如果您需要大一點的房間，我們很樂意幫您升等。
  4. Should you visit London next month, I would gladly show you around.
    如果您下個月來倫敦，我很樂意帶您四處看看。
  5. Should you find any errors in the report, we would correct them at once.
    如果您在報告裡發現任何錯誤，我們會馬上改正。
  6. Should you need a reference, I would write one for you.
    如果您需要推薦信，我會幫您寫一封。

② would like 和 would be grateful

  1. Should you have time next week, I would like to discuss the budget with you.
    如果您下週有空，我想跟您討論預算。
  2. Should the item be out of stock, we would be grateful if you could tell us.
    如果那件商品缺貨，麻煩您通知我們。
  3. Should our proposal interest you, we would welcome a meeting.
    如果您對我們的提案有興趣，我們歡迎約時間見面。
  4. Should the board approve the plan, we would like to start building in spring.
    如果董事會核准這個計畫，我們想在春天開工。
  5. Should you join us for dinner, my wife and I would be delighted.
    如果您來跟我們吃晚餐，我和我太太會很高興。

③ 否定

  1. Should the medicine not work, the doctor would try a different treatment.
    如果這種藥沒效，醫生會改用別的療法。
  2. Should you not receive the parcel within a week, we would send a replacement.
    如果您一週內沒收到包裹，我們會寄一個新的給您。
  3. Should the tenants not leave by the end of the month, the landlord would take legal action.
    如果房客月底前沒搬走，房東會採取法律行動。
  4. Should the weather not improve, we would hold the wedding indoors.
    如果天氣沒有好轉，我們會在室內辦婚禮。
  5. Should the supplier not deliver on time, we would look for another one.
    如果供應商沒有準時交貨，我們會另外找一家。

④ 被動

  1. Should the documents be lost, we would have to start again.
    如果文件弄丟了，我們就得從頭來過。（被動）
  2. Should your application be rejected, you would have the right to appeal.
    如果您的申請被駁回，您有權提出上訴。（被動）
  3. Should the road be closed, the bus would take a different route.
    如果道路封閉，公車會改走別的路線。（被動）
  4. Should the match be postponed, ticket holders would get a full refund.
    如果比賽延期，持票人會拿到全額退款。（被動）
  5. Should the data be stolen, the bank would inform all its customers.
    如果資料被偷，銀行會通知所有的客戶。（被動）

⑤ could 和 might

  1. Should prices keep rising, many families might struggle to pay their bills.
    如果物價繼續上漲，很多家庭可能付不出帳單。
  2. Should you change your plans, we could still reserve a seat for you.
    如果您改變計畫，我們還是可以幫您保留一個位子。
  3. Should the storm reach the coast, thousands of people might lose power.
    如果暴風雨到達海岸，成千上萬的人可能會停電。
  4. Should the company expand, it could open an office in Singapore.
    如果公司擴張，它可以在新加坡開一間辦公室。
  5. Should the river rise any higher, the bridge might collapse.
    如果河水再漲高，那座橋可能會垮。

⑥ 警告和規定

  1. Should any employee conceal a mistake, the company would take disciplinary action.
    如果有員工隱瞞錯誤，公司會採取懲處行動。
  2. Should the article contain defamation, the newspaper would face a lawsuit.
    如果那篇文章有誹謗的內容，報社會面臨訴訟。
  3. Should the contract stipulate a later date, we would follow that date.
    如果合約規定更晚的日期，我們會照那個日期做。
  4. Should you fail to pay the fine, the court would issue a warrant.
    如果您沒繳罰款，法院會發出拘票。
  5. Should the guests behave badly, the hotel would ask them to leave.
    如果客人行為不當，飯店會請他們離開。
  6. Should the club break the rules again, the league would deduct points.
    如果那個俱樂部再違規，聯盟會扣分。
