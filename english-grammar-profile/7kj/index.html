They chose our school because it's very modern and there are a lot of students.

他們選了我們學校，因為學校很現代化，而且學生很多。

第⁠二⁠個 because 子⁠句可⁠以省⁠略 because

They chose our school because it's very modern and there are a lot of students.

我喜歡這家咖啡店，因為很安靜，而且咖啡很好喝。

and 也⁠可⁠以連⁠接兩⁠個動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 子⁠句

They chose our school because it's very modern and there are a lot of students.

我們要去山上，住在小木屋。

and 連⁠接⁠的兩⁠個子⁠句要同⁠類

They chose our school because it's very modern and there are a lot of students.

My sister loves dancing and to sing in the choir.

My sister loves dancing and singing in the choir.

兩⁠個 because 子⁠句用 and 連在同⁠一⁠個句⁠子⁠裡

They chose our school because it's very modern and there are a lot of students.

We left early because it was late. And because we were tired.

We left early because it was late and we were tired.

if 子⁠句⁠裡不⁠用 will

They chose our school because it's very modern and there are a lot of students.

If you want to relax and you will see your friends, go to the country.

If you want to relax and see your friends, go to the country.

例句

① because + 子句 + and + 子句

  1. My friend asked why I don't go to the new sports centre. I go to that gym because it's cheap and the trainers are friendly.
    我朋友問我為什麼不去新的運動中心。我去那家健身房，因為很便宜，而且教練很友善。
  2. We're travelling with three children. We chose this hotel because it's near the beach and the rooms are big.
    我們帶著三個孩子旅行。我們選了這家飯店，因為離海灘近，而且房間很大。
  3. My sister worked at the restaurant for a year. She left her job because the pay was low and the hours were long.
    我姊姊在那家餐廳工作了一年。她辭掉工作，因為薪水低，而且工時長。
  4. I've lived in Hualien for ten years. I love this town because the people are kind and the food is great.
    我在花蓮住了十年。我很喜歡這個小鎮，因為這裡的人很親切，而且食物很好吃。
  5. Tom missed the start of the meeting. He was late because the bus broke down and his phone died.
    湯姆錯過了會議的開頭。他遲到了，因為公車拋錨，而且他的手機沒電了。

② if / when + 子句 + and / or + 子句

  1. You spend too much on takeaways. If you want to save money and eat well, cook at home.
    你花太多錢買外帶了。如果你想省錢又想吃得好，就在家煮飯。
  2. I'll pick you up from the hotel. When you arrive and you're ready, call me.
    我會去飯店接你。你到了而且準備好的時候，打給我。
  3. The wedding starts at two. If it rains or the bus is late, we'll take a taxi.
    婚禮兩點開始。如果下雨或是公車誤點，我們就搭計程車。
  4. The hike is long, so listen to your body. If you're hungry or you feel tired, take a break.
    這段健行很長，所以要注意身體的感覺。如果你餓了或是覺得累，就休息一下。
  5. My evenings start late. When the kids are asleep and the house is quiet, I read.
    我的晚上很晚才開始。孩子們睡了、家裡安靜的時候，我會看書。

③ 動詞-ing + and + 動詞-ing

  1. We can't afford a hotel this year. This summer we're visiting Japan and staying with friends.
    我們今年住不起飯店。今年夏天我們要去日本，住在朋友家。
  2. My parents are visiting on Monday. I spent the weekend cleaning the house and washing the car.
    我爸媽星期一要來。我週末都在打掃房子和洗車。
  3. My grandmother has lots of free time now. She enjoys painting and playing the piano.
    我奶奶現在有很多空閒時間。她喜歡畫畫和彈鋼琴。
  4. We live in the city centre now. We're thinking of selling the car and buying a bike.
    我們現在住在市中心。我們在考慮把車賣掉，買一台腳踏車。
  5. Ask my uncle about your broken bike. He's good at fixing things and solving problems.
    你的腳踏車壞了就問我叔叔。他很會修東西和解決問題。

④ to 不定詞 + and + 不定詞

  1. I've been working abroad for a year. This Christmas I want to rest and see my family.
    我在國外工作了一年。今年聖誕節我想休息，也想見我的家人。
  2. My cousin finishes university in June. She hopes to travel and learn new languages.
    我表姊六月大學畢業。她希望能去旅行，學新的語言。
  3. It was raining hard on Saturday night. We decided to stay home and watch a film.
    星期六晚上雨下得很大。我們決定待在家看電影。
  4. My brother and his wife got married last spring. They plan to buy a house and start a family.
    我哥哥和他太太去年春天結婚。他們打算買房子，成家生小孩。

⑤ either ... or 和不同的句型

  1. You could spend the summer either working in a café or helping on your uncle's farm.
    你暑假可以去咖啡店打工，或是去你叔叔的農場幫忙。
  2. A company in Singapore offered me a job. I can't decide whether to take the job or to stay here.
    新加坡一家公司給了我一份工作。我沒辦法決定要接那份工作，還是留在這裡。
  3. My wife fell asleep on the sofa. She was tired because she'd worked all day and hadn't eaten.
    我太太在沙發上睡著了。她很累，因為她工作了一整天，而且還沒吃東西。
  4. We didn't go out on New Year's Eve. We stayed in because it was cold and we had no money.
    我們跨年夜沒出門。我們待在家裡，因為很冷，而且我們沒錢。
  5. My dad goes to the countryside every summer. He likes walking in the hills and swimming in the lake.
    我爸爸每年夏天都去鄉下。他喜歡在山裡散步和在湖裡游泳。
  6. The manager spoke to the new waiter. If you finish early and nobody needs you, go home.
    經理跟新來的服務生說話。如果你提早做完而且沒人需要你，就回家吧。
  7. We're asking students about their habits. Do you prefer to cook or to eat out?
    我們在調查學生的習慣。你比較喜歡自己煮，還是外食？
  8. The other students asked why I moved to London. I came here to study and to make new friends.
    其他學生問我為什麼搬來倫敦。我來這裡是為了讀書和交新朋友。
  9. My sister always listens. When I'm sad or I feel lonely, I call her.
    我姊姊總是會聽我說。當我難過或是覺得孤單的時候，我就打給她。
  10. We left the big city last year. We moved here because the schools are good and the air is clean.
    我們去年離開了大城市。我們搬來這裡，因為學校很好，而且空氣乾淨。
  11. My great-aunt doesn't have a TV. She spends her evenings reading books or writing letters.
    我的姑婆沒有電視。她晚上都在看書或寫信。
