It is probably in the sitting room on the table.

這一定會弄得亂七八糟。

主⁠詞 + be + 副⁠詞 + …

probably大概、可能。讓說法委婉。
definitely / certainly一定。讓說法肯定。
位置be 動詞後面，或情態動詞和主要動詞中間。

和 be going to 一⁠起用

例句

It's definitely going to get messy.

一定會弄得很髒亂。

副詞放在 be 後面、going to 前面。

委⁠婉⁠的建⁠議

例句

You should probably wear a T-shirt and shorts.

你大概應該穿T恤和短褲。

should probably 讓建議聽起來比較客氣。

放⁠在 be 後⁠面修⁠飾整⁠句⁠話，要⁠用副⁠詞 probably

It is probably in the sitting room on the table.

It is probable in the kitchen.

It is probably in the kitchen.

副⁠詞放⁠在情⁠態動⁠詞和主⁠要動⁠詞中⁠間

You should wear probably shorts.

You should probably wear shorts.

副⁠詞放⁠在 be 後⁠面、going to 前⁠面

It's going definitely to rain.

It's definitely going to rain.

例句

① 主詞 + be + probably + 地方：猜東西在哪裡

  1. You played games in the sitting room last night. Your phone is probably in there on the table.
    你昨天晚上在客廳打電動。你的手機大概在那裡的桌子上。
  2. You wore your blue jacket yesterday, so your keys are probably still in its pocket.
    你昨天穿了藍色夾克，所以你的鑰匙大概還在它的口袋裡。
  3. The cat is probably asleep under the bed.
    貓大概在床底下睡覺。
  4. Dad is probably still at the office.
    爸爸大概還在辦公室。
  5. The scissors are probably in the kitchen drawer.
    剪刀大概在廚房的抽屜裡。
  6. Your football boots are probably in the car.
    你的足球鞋大概在車上。

② 主詞 + be + definitely / certainly + going to + 原形動詞

  1. We're painting the kitchen today. It's definitely going to get messy, so wear old clothes.
    我們今天要油漆廚房。一定會弄得很髒亂，所以穿舊衣服。
  2. The sky over the hills has gone black. It's definitely going to pour soon.
    山丘上的天空變黑了。一定很快就會下大雨。
  3. The party is certainly going to be fun.
    派對一定會很好玩。
  4. That old tree is definitely going to fall in the next storm.
    那棵老樹下次暴風雨一定會倒。
  5. The exam is definitely going to be hard this year.
    今年的考試一定會很難。
  6. The shop is certainly going to be busy on Saturday.
    那家店星期六一定會很多人。

③ 主詞 + should probably + 原形動詞：委婉的建議

  1. It's going to be very hot. You should probably wear a T-shirt and shorts, with trainers.
    天氣會很熱。你大概應該穿T恤和短褲，配運動鞋。
  2. You should probably call your mum. She's worried.
    你大概應該打給你媽媽。她很擔心。
  3. We should probably leave now. It's getting late.
    我們大概應該現在走了。很晚了。
  4. You should probably take an umbrella.
    你大概應該帶把傘。
  5. I should probably go to bed. I have school tomorrow.
    我大概應該去睡了。我明天要上學。
  6. You should probably ask the teacher first.
    你大概應該先問老師。

④ 主詞 + will + probably / definitely + 原形動詞

  1. I'll probably be home by six.
    我大概六點前會到家。
  2. Mum collects old teapots, so she'll definitely love this one.
    媽媽收集舊茶壺，所以她一定會很喜歡這一個。
  3. The bus will probably be late again.
    公車大概又會誤點。
  4. We'll definitely come to your party.
    我們一定會去你的派對。
  5. It'll probably snow in the mountains tonight.
    今天晚上山上大概會下雪。
  6. Our neighbours will certainly complain about the noise from the party.
    我們的鄰居一定會抱怨派對的噪音。

⑤ 主詞 + be + probably / definitely + 形容詞

  1. The lights in the bakery are off, so it is probably shut for the holiday.
    麵包店的燈都關了，所以大概放假沒開。
  2. That restaurant is definitely the best in town.
    那家餐廳一定是城裡最好的。
  3. It's only April, so the lake is probably still freezing.
    現在才四月，所以湖水大概還冰得要命。
  4. She is certainly the fastest runner in our class.
    她一定是我們班跑得最快的。
  5. The film is probably too long for the children.
    那部電影對孩子們來說大概太長了。
  6. This is definitely the right road.
    這一定是對的路。
  7. Grandpa has just arrived from Australia, so he is probably very sleepy.
    爺爺剛從澳洲回來，所以他大概很睏。
