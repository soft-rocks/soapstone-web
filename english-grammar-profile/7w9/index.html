例句

① What a + 形容詞 + 名詞!

  1. You booked the tickets and the hotel already? What a brilliant plan!
    你已經訂好票跟飯店了？好棒的計畫！
  2. Look at the mountains from this window. What a beautiful view!
    從這扇窗看那些山，好美的景色！
  3. You made that dress yourself? What a lovely piece of work!
    那件洋裝是你自己做的？做得真好！
  4. Three meetings and two flights. What a long day it's been!
    三場會議加兩趟飛機，今天真漫長！
  5. Is that your new puppy? What a cute little thing!
    那是你的新小狗嗎？好可愛的小傢伙！
  6. Why do you want to know my shoe size? What a strange question!
    你為什麼想知道我穿幾號鞋？好奇怪的問題！

② What an + 形容詞 + 名詞!

  1. We saw dolphins and a rainbow. What an amazing trip!
    我們看到海豚和彩虹，這趟旅行太棒了！
  2. Your grandfather sailed around the world? What an interesting life!
    你爺爺航行過全世界？他的人生真有趣！
  3. Three hundred dollars for a T-shirt? What an expensive shop!
    一件 T 恤三百塊？這家店好貴！
  4. Close the fridge. What an awful smell!
    把冰箱關上，好難聞的味道！
  5. Everyone got full marks. What an easy quiz that was!
    每個人都滿分，那次小考真簡單！

③ What a + 名詞!（沒有形容詞）

  1. Toys everywhere and paint on the wall. What a mess!
    到處都是玩具，牆上還有顏料，好亂！
  2. You came all the way from Canada? What a surprise!
    你大老遠從加拿大過來？真是驚喜！
  3. The concert is sold out? What a pity!
    演唱會賣完了？真可惜！
  4. I missed the bus and lost my wallet. What a day!
    我錯過公車又弄丟皮夾，真是倒楣的一天！
  5. The test results are fine? What a relief!
    檢查結果沒問題？總算鬆了一口氣！

④ What a + 名詞片語 + 子句!

  1. What a nice room they gave us!
    他們給我們的房間真好！
  2. What a great time we had at the lake!
    我們在湖邊玩得真開心！
  3. What a beautiful house you live in!
    你住的房子真漂亮！
  4. What a good friend you are!
    你真是個好朋友！
  5. What a terrible mistake I made at work today!
    我今天在工作上犯了好嚴重的錯！
  6. What a lucky boy he is!
    他真是個幸運的男孩！

⑤ What + 複數 / 不可數名詞!

  1. Thank you for the roses. What lovely flowers they are!
    謝謝你送的玫瑰，這些花好漂亮！
  2. Rain again, and it's July. What terrible weather we're having!
    又下雨了，現在可是七月，天氣真糟！
  3. What beautiful eyes your daughter has!
    你女兒的眼睛真漂亮！
  4. Your mother cooked all this? What delicious food!
    這些都是你媽媽煮的？好好吃！
  5. They helped us carry everything. What kind people they are!
    他們幫我們搬了所有東西，他們真是好心的人！
  6. A flat tire on the way to the airport? What bad luck!
    去機場的路上爆胎？運氣真差！
  7. What nice shoes you're wearing!
    你穿的鞋真好看！
  8. What strange ideas he has!
    他的想法真奇怪！
