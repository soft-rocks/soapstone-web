例句

① 依序列出過去的事

  1. I got up, had a quick shower and caught the bus.
    我起床，快速沖了澡，然後搭上公車。
  2. She finished school, moved to London and found a job in a bank.
    她畢業後搬到倫敦，在銀行找到了工作。
  3. We met at nine and drove to the lake.
    我們九點碰面，開車去湖邊。
  4. He opened the letter, read it and put it back in the envelope.
    他打開信，讀完後又放回信封。
  5. They bought an old farmhouse, repaired the roof and moved in that summer.
    他們買了一棟老農舍，修好屋頂，那年夏天就搬進去了。

② and then / then

  1. We waited for an hour, and then we gave up and went home.
    我們等了一個小時，後來就放棄回家了。
  2. I tried calling her, and then I sent her a message.
    我試著打給她，然後傳了訊息給她。
  3. She paid the bill and then left without a word.
    她付了帳，然後一句話也沒說就走了。
  4. The lights went out. Then we heard a loud bang.
    燈熄了。接著我們聽到一聲巨響。

③ first、next、after that

  1. First, we checked into the hotel. After that, we went for a walk along the river.
    我們先辦了入住，之後沿著河邊散步。
  2. First we visited the castle, then we had lunch in the old town.
    我們先參觀城堡，然後在老城區吃午餐。
  3. We packed the car. Next, we locked up the house.
    我們把東西裝上車，接著把房子鎖好。
  4. Next, she showed us the kitchen.
    接著她帶我們看廚房。
  5. After that, nobody mentioned the subject again.
    那之後就沒有人再提起這件事了。

④ later、afterwards

  1. Three days later, my test results came back.
    三天後，我的檢查結果出來了。
  2. A week later, she called to apologise.
    一個禮拜後，她打電話來道歉。
  3. We went to a concert and had dinner afterwards.
    我們去聽演唱會，之後吃了晚餐。
  4. Afterwards, we walked back to the hotel.
    之後我們走回飯店。

⑤ finally、in the end

  1. Finally, after three hours, the plane took off.
    等了三個小時，飛機終於起飛了。
  2. We finally reached the top just before sunset.
    我們終於在日落前登上了山頂。
  3. We argued about the name for weeks. In the end, we called the dog Max.
    我們為了名字吵了好幾個禮拜。最後我們把狗取名叫 Max。
  4. In the end, the company acquiesced and gave us a refund.
    最後公司讓步了，退了錢給我們。

⑥ when / after 的子句

  1. When the band came on stage, everyone stood up.
    樂團上台的時候，大家都站了起來。
  2. When the rain stopped, we continued our walk.
    雨停了以後，我們繼續散步。
  3. After we had dinner, we played board games.
    吃完晚餐後，我們玩了桌遊。
  4. After the guide left, we explored the ruins on our own.
    導遊走了之後，我們自己去探索遺跡。

⑦ 說一段經過

  1. I missed the last train, so I walked to my sister's flat and slept on her sofa.
    我錯過了末班車，就走到我姊姊的公寓，睡在她的沙發上。
  2. She saw the advert, applied for the job and started the following week.
    她看到廣告，應徵了那份工作，隔週就開始上班。
  3. We got lost, asked a farmer for directions and arrived two hours late.
    我們迷路了，跟一個農夫問路，結果晚了兩個小時才到。
  4. He fell off his bike, broke his arm and spent a week in hospital.
    他從腳踏車上摔下來，摔斷了手臂，在醫院住了一個禮拜。
  5. The next morning, we took a boat to the island.
    隔天早上，我們搭船去那座島。
  6. On the last day, we went shopping and bought presents for everyone.
    最後一天，我們去逛街，幫每個人買了禮物。
