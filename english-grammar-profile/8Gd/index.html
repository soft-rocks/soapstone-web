Might it not be a good idea to change the travel company next time?

你們換一家合作的旅行社，會不會比較好呢？

Might + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

Might … ？會不會……？有沒有可能……？
Might … not … ？……難道不是……嗎？用來委婉地提出建議。
原形動詞問的那件事。

用 or 列⁠出兩⁠個可⁠能

Might it not be a good idea to change the travel company next time?

Did you send the wrong bill, or might it be the bill for the last tenant?

例句

① Might it not be + 名詞或形容詞？：委婉的建議

  1. Might it not be safer to travel in a group?
    結伴旅行難道不是比較安全嗎？
  2. Might it not be better to wait until the spring?
    等到春天難道不是比較好嗎？
  3. Might it not be cheaper to go by train?
    搭火車難道不是比較便宜嗎？
  4. Might it not be wise to ask a lawyer first?
    先問律師難道不是比較明智嗎？
  5. Might it not be easier to meet online?
    在網路上開會難道不是比較方便嗎？
  6. Might it not be fairer to let everyone vote?
    讓每個人都投票難道不是比較公平嗎？

② …, or might it be + 名詞？：列出另一個可能

  1. Is it possible that your company sent me the wrong bill, or might it be the bill for the last person who lived in my house?
    有沒有可能是貴公司寄錯帳單給我，還是那可能是上一個住在我家的人的帳單？
  2. Was the parcel lost, or might it be at the wrong address?
    包裹是不是遺失了，還是可能寄到錯的地址？
  3. Is the machine broken, or might it be the plug?
    是機器壞了，還是可能是插頭的問題？
  4. Did I misunderstand, or might there be a mistake in the timetable?
    是我誤會了，還是時刻表可能有錯？
  5. Is the shop closed, or might it have moved?
    那家店是關了，還是可能搬家了？
  6. Was it a mistake, or might someone have changed the figures?
    這是個錯誤，還是可能有人改了數字？

③ Might + 主詞 + 原形動詞？：正式地問可能性

  1. Might the delay have been caused by the weather?
    延誤有沒有可能是天氣造成的？（被動）
  2. Might there be another way to solve the problem?
    有沒有可能有另一個方法解決這個問題？
  3. Might the price go down before Christmas?
    價格有沒有可能在聖誕節前下降？
  4. Might the meeting be moved to next week?
    會議有沒有可能改到下星期？（被動）
  5. Might the hotel have a quieter room for us?
    飯店有沒有可能給我們一間安靜一點的房間？
  6. Might the new rules affect our business?
    新規定有沒有可能影響我們的生意？

④ Might I + 原形動詞？：很正式的請求

  1. Might I ask how long you have worked for the company?
    我可以請問您在公司工作多久了嗎？
  2. Might I suggest that the interview takes place in the afternoon instead?
    我可以建議面試改在下午進行嗎？
  3. Might I have a few more days to finish the report?
    我可以多幾天時間完成報告嗎？
  4. Might I see the room before I pay?
    我付錢之前可以先看房間嗎？
  5. Might I make a small comment on your plan?
    我可以對您的計畫提一點小意見嗎？
  6. Might I borrow your notes from yesterday's lecture?
    我可以借您昨天上課的筆記嗎？

⑤ Might + 主詞 + not + 原形動詞？：提出另一種看法

  1. Might the children not enjoy a day at the farm more?
    孩子們難道不會比較喜歡在農場玩一天嗎？
  2. Might you not regret it later?
    你以後難道不會後悔嗎？
  3. Might we not save money by buying in bulk?
    我們大量購買難道不能省錢嗎？
  4. Might they not prefer a quieter hotel?
    他們難道不會比較喜歡安靜一點的飯店嗎？
  5. Might the staff not work better with more breaks?
    員工休息多一點，難道不會工作得比較好嗎？
  6. Might a short holiday not help you relax?
    短短的假期難道不能幫你放鬆嗎？
  7. Might the students not learn more outside the classroom?
    學生在教室外面難道不會學到更多嗎？
