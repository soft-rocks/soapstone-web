例句

① good → better

  1. Homemade bread is better than store bread.
    自己做的麵包比店裡買的好。
  2. A walk is better than a nap after lunch.
    午餐後散個步比睡午覺好。
  3. I feel much better today.
    我今天覺得好多了。
  4. Which song do you think is better?
    你覺得哪一首歌比較好？
  5. We had better luck in the afternoon.
    我們下午運氣比較好。
  6. My grandmother's memory is better than mine.
    我奶奶的記性比我好。
  7. Do you think the sequel is better?
    你覺得續集比較好看嗎？
  8. This hotel has better rooms.
    這家旅館的房間比較好。
  9. My grades were better this year.
    我今年的成績比較好。
  10. Fresh fruit is better for you than juice.
    新鮮水果比果汁對你更好。

② bad → worse

  1. The pain in my knee is worse in winter.
    我膝蓋的痛冬天比較嚴重。
  2. My cold is worse than yesterday.
    我的感冒比昨天嚴重。
  3. The food was worse than I expected.
    食物比我想的還難吃。
  4. Nothing is worse than waiting in the rain.
    沒有什麼比在雨中等待更糟。
  5. His second song was worse.
    他的第二首歌比較差。
  6. The storm got worse at night.
    暴風雨到了晚上變得更嚴重。
  7. Is your back pain worse?
    你的背痛有比較嚴重嗎？

③ far → farther / further

  1. The lake is farther from the road than the river.
    湖離馬路比河還遠。
  2. My school is farther from home than yours.
    我的學校離家比你的遠。
  3. Which city is farther from Taipei?
    哪個城市離台北比較遠？
  4. For further information, call our office.
    想知道更多資訊，請打電話到我們辦公室。
  5. We need further help with the project.
    這個專案我們需要更多的協助。
  6. The beach was further away than the map showed.
    海灘比地圖上看起來的還遠。

④ 不同的句子

  1. Ann's cooking is getting better.
    Ann 的廚藝越來越好。
  2. With more practice, you'll get better at it.
    多練習的話，你會越來越拿手。
  3. Don't worry, it could be worse.
    別擔心，事情還可能更糟。
  4. Is the north farther than the south?
    北邊比南邊遠嗎？
  5. Ken found a better job last month.
    Ken 上個月找到一份比較好的工作。
  6. My handwriting is worse than my brother's.
    我的字比我哥哥的醜。
  7. The second half of the game was better.
    比賽的下半場比較精彩。
