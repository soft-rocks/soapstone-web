例句

① I think ...

  1. I left my bag on a chair before dinner. I think my bag is in your kitchen.
    我吃飯前把包包放在椅子上。我想我的包包在你家廚房。
  2. I think it's going to rain. Take an umbrella.
    我覺得會下雨。帶把傘。
  3. I think you're right. Let's take the train.
    我覺得你說得對。我們搭火車吧。
  4. This shop is expensive. I think we can find it cheaper online.
    這家店好貴。我覺得我們在網路上可以買到比較便宜的。
  5. He talks about her every day. My brother thinks the new teacher is great.
    他每天都在講她。我哥哥覺得新老師很棒。
  6. What do you think of my new haircut?
    你覺得我的新髮型怎麼樣？

② I don't think ...

  1. I don't think he's coming. It's already nine.
    我覺得他不會來了。已經九點了。
  2. We're going the wrong way. I don't think this is the right bus.
    我們方向不對。我覺得這不是對的公車。
  3. Ask Mum first. She doesn't think it's a good idea.
    先問媽媽。她覺得這不是個好主意。
  4. Can you go to the shop? I don't think we have enough milk for breakfast.
    你可以去一趟店裡嗎？我覺得我們的牛奶不夠吃早餐。

③ I hope ...

  1. Sorry to hear you're ill. I hope that you feel better soon.
    聽說你生病了。我希望你早日康復。
  2. It's my birthday next week. I hope all my friends can come.
    下星期是我生日。我希望我的朋友都能來。
  3. Thanks for the lovely flowers. We hope they last a long time.
    謝謝你送的漂亮花束。我們希望花能開得久一點。
  4. I hope it doesn't rain tomorrow. We're going to the beach.
    我希望明天不要下雨。我們要去海邊。
  5. I study every night. My parents hope I pass my exams.
    我每天晚上都讀書。我爸媽希望我考試及格。
  6. The film starts at eight. I hope he isn't late again.
    電影八點開始。我希望他不要又遲到。

④ I know ...

  1. I know it's late, but can I call you?
    我知道很晚了，可是我可以打給你嗎？
  2. Do you know that we're going to have a concert at school?
    你知道我們學校要辦音樂會嗎？
  3. I know the answer. It's Paris.
    我知道答案。是巴黎。
  4. Ask my aunt about it. She knows a lot about old cars.
    去問我阿姨。她很懂老爺車。
  5. Can you open the door for me? I don't know where my keys are.
    你可以幫我開門嗎？我不知道我的鑰匙在哪裡。
  6. We know you're busy, so we'll be quick.
    我們知道你很忙，所以我們會很快。

⑤ 疑問句

  1. The sun is out, but it's windy. Do you think it's too cold for a picnic?
    出太陽了，可是有風。你覺得這種天氣野餐會太冷嗎？
  2. It's a surprise. Does your sister know about the party?
    這是個驚喜。你姊姊知道派對的事嗎？
  3. My phone is dead. Do you know what time it is?
    我的手機沒電了。你知道現在幾點嗎？
  4. She smiled at me again today. Do you think she likes me?
    她今天又對我笑了。你覺得她喜歡我嗎？

⑥ I think so / I hope so

  1. "Is the shop open today?" "I think so."
    那家店今天有開嗎？我想有吧。
  2. "Will you be at the meeting?" "I hope so."
    你會來開會嗎？希望可以。
  3. "Is it going to rain?" "I hope not."
    會下雨嗎？希望不會。
  4. "Is Sam coming tonight?" "I don't think so."
    Sam 今晚會來嗎？我覺得不會。
