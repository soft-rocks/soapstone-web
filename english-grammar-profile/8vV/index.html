Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

以下列出幾個支持我這個選擇的重要理由。

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞和地⁠點放⁠在句⁠首，be 和主⁠詞倒⁠裝

以下列出幾個支持我這個選擇的重要理由。

一般語序是 Some essential reasons are outlined below.

be 跟⁠著句⁠尾⁠的主⁠詞變⁠化

Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

單數主詞

Attached is a copy of my CV.

隨信附上我的履歷一份。
複數主詞

Enclosed are two copies of the contract.

隨信附上合約兩份。

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞就⁠是主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

這座車站建於 1890 年，是鎮上最古老的建築。

車站是被建造的，所以用過去分詞。

Compared with、Based on、Given 是固⁠定說⁠法

Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

Compared with
比較

Compared with last year, sales have gone up.

跟去年比起來，銷售量增加了。
Based on
根據

Based on these results, we recommend a new system.

根據這些結果，我們建議換一套新系統。
Given
考慮到

Given the cost, the plan is not realistic.

考慮到費用，這個計畫不切實際。

這些固定說法的主詞可以不是主要子句的主詞。

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞⁠的主⁠詞要⁠是主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

Built in 1890, I visited the old station.

Built in 1890, the old station is worth a visit.

I 不是被建造的。

主⁠詞是複⁠數，be 用 are

Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

Attached is two copies of the invoice.

Attached are two copies of the invoice.

固⁠定說⁠法是 based on，用過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Basing on the survey, most staff want flexible hours.

Based on the survey, most staff want flexible hours.

倒⁠裝⁠的句⁠子要⁠有 be

Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

Enclosed a copy of my certificate.

Enclosed is a copy of my certificate.

現⁠在⁠分⁠詞是主⁠詞做⁠的動⁠作，過⁠去⁠分⁠詞是主⁠詞被做⁠的動⁠作

Outlined below are some essential reasons which justify my choice.

現在分詞

Walking home, I saw an old friend.

走路回家的時候，我遇到一個老朋友。
過去分詞

Asked about the delay, the manager refused to comment.

被問到延誤的事，經理拒絕回應。

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞子⁠句拿⁠掉連⁠接⁠詞、主⁠詞和 be

跟其他首都比起來，柏林名列第一。

例句

① 過去分詞 + 地點 + be + 主詞

  1. Listed below are the dates of this year's meetings.
    以下列出今年的會議日期。
  2. Shown above is the plan for the new office.
    上面是新辦公室的平面圖。
  3. Included below is a list of useful phone numbers.
    下面附上一份常用電話號碼表。
  4. Outlined in this report are three ways to cut costs.
    這份報告列出三種降低成本的方法。
  5. Printed on the back is a map of the campus.
    背面印著一張校園地圖。
  6. Hidden under the floor was a box of old letters.
    地板底下藏著一盒舊信。
  7. Listed on the next page are the names of all the winners.
    下一頁列出所有得獎者的名字。

② Attached / Enclosed + be + 主詞

  1. Attached is my CV for your consideration.
    隨信附上我的履歷，請您參考。
  2. Enclosed is a cheque for the full amount.
    隨信附上全額的支票。
  3. Attached are the minutes of yesterday's meeting.
    附件是昨天的會議記錄。
  4. Enclosed are the tickets you ordered.
    隨信附上您訂的票。
  5. Attached is the invoice for March.
    附件是三月的發票。

③ Compared with / to + 主要子句

  1. Compared with other cities, rent here is quite cheap.
    跟其他城市比起來，這裡的房租相當便宜。
  2. Compared to last year, fewer students applied this term.
    跟去年比起來，這學期申請的學生比較少。
  3. Compared with my old job, this one is far less stressful.
    跟我以前的工作比起來，這份工作的壓力小很多。
  4. Compared to the first book, the sequel was a disappointment.
    跟第一本書比起來，續集讓人失望。

④ Based on / Given + 主要子句

  1. Based on the feedback, we have changed the menu.
    根據顧客的意見，我們改了菜單。
  2. Based on her experience, she was offered the manager's job.
    根據她的經歷，公司請她當經理。
  3. Given the weather, the match will probably be postponed.
    考慮到天氣，比賽可能會延期。
  4. Given his age, he did remarkably well in the race.
    以他的年紀來說，他在比賽中的表現非常好。
  5. Given the circumstances, I think we made the right decision.
    考慮到當時的情況，我覺得我們的決定是對的。

⑤ 一般動詞的過去分詞 + 主要子句

  1. Built in the 1960s, the bridge now needs major repairs.
    這座橋建於 1960 年代，現在需要大修。
  2. Asked to wait outside, the visitors sat down on the steps.
    訪客被請到外面等，就在台階上坐了下來。
  3. Written in simple English, the book is ideal for beginners.
    這本書用簡單的英文寫成，很適合初學者。
  4. Shocked by the news, she sat down and said nothing.
    聽到這個消息，她嚇得坐下來，一句話都沒說。
  5. Surrounded by mountains, the village is hard to reach in winter.
    那個村子被群山環繞，冬天很難到達。
  6. Made from recycled paper, these bags are good for the environment.
    這些袋子用回收紙做成，對環境很好。
  7. Founded in 1985, the company now employs over 500 people.
    這家公司成立於 1985 年，現在有五百多名員工。
  8. Left alone in the house, the dog chewed the sofa.
    被單獨留在家裡，那隻狗咬壞了沙發。
  9. Exhausted after the long flight, we went straight to bed.
    長途飛行後我們累壞了，一到就直接上床睡覺。
  10. Delighted with the results, the board approved the project.
    董事會對結果很滿意，批准了這個計畫。
