I would prefer to pay in cash.

我比較希望用現金付款。

would prefer 後⁠面⁠接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

我比較希望用現金付款。

would prefer to 比 want to 客氣。

口⁠語常把 would 縮⁠寫成 'd

I would prefer to pay in cash.

我比較想坐出口附近。

would prefer not to 是客⁠氣⁠的拒⁠絕

I would prefer to pay in cash.

我不太想談這件事。

說話的人不想做那件事，可是不直接說 no。

rather than 後⁠面⁠接另⁠一⁠個選⁠擇，用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I would prefer to pay in cash.

我比較想騎腳踏車上班，不想開車。

問⁠句把 would 放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

I would prefer to pay in cash.

你比較想星期一見面嗎？

would 後⁠面⁠的 prefer 用原⁠形

I would prefer to pay in cash.

She would prefers to travel by boat.

She would prefer to travel by boat.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I would prefer to pay in cash.

I would prefer to staying in a hotel.

I would prefer to stay in a hotel.

這個 to 是不定詞的 to。

would prefer 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時，動⁠詞前⁠面加 to

I would prefer to pay in cash.

We'd prefer go by train.

We'd prefer to go by train.

prefer 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 說平⁠常⁠的喜⁠好

I would prefer to pay in cash.

I prefer swimming in the sea to swimming in a pool.

比起在游泳池游泳，我比較喜歡在海裡游。

would rather 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

I would prefer to pay in cash.

I'd rather pay in cash.

我寧願付現金。

例句

① 跟店員、服務生說話：would prefer to

  1. "Shall I gift-wrap it?" "No, thanks. I'd prefer to wrap it myself."
    要幫您包裝嗎？不用，謝謝。我比較想自己包。
  2. "Can I take your coat?" "That's kind, but I'd prefer to keep it on. I'm a bit cold."
    需要幫您掛外套嗎？謝謝，不過我想穿著。我有點冷。
  3. "We have a table near the door." "I'd prefer to sit by the window, if possible."
    我們門口附近有一張桌子。可以的話，我比較想坐窗邊。
  4. The hotel offered us a room on the ground floor, but we said we would prefer to be higher up.
    飯店給我們一樓的房間，可是我們說比較想住高一點。

② 約時間：would prefer to

  1. Thanks for your email. I would prefer to meet at six instead of four.
    謝謝你的來信。我比較希望六點見面，不要四點。
  2. "Shall we have the meeting online?" "I'd prefer to meet in person."
    我們線上開會好嗎？我比較想當面見。
  3. My boss would prefer to discuss the plan on Monday morning.
    我老闆比較想星期一早上討論這個計畫。
  4. "Is Saturday OK for the party?" "We'd prefer to have it on Sunday."
    派對星期六可以嗎？我們比較想星期天辦。

③ 說自己的選擇：would prefer to

  1. "Do you want to come with us?" "Thanks, but I'd prefer to stay at home tonight."
    你要跟我們去嗎？謝謝，不過今晚我比較想待在家。
  2. My grandmother would prefer to live in the countryside.
    我奶奶比較想住在鄉下。
  3. I'd prefer to go on holiday with my friends this year.
    今年我比較想跟朋友去度假。
  4. "Should I call you?" "I'd prefer to get a text message, actually."
    我該打電話給你嗎？其實我比較想收到簡訊。

④ 客氣地拒絕：would prefer not to

  1. "What happened with your ex?" "I'd prefer not to talk about it."
    你跟前任怎麼了？我不太想談這件事。
  2. The singer would prefer not to answer questions about her family.
    那位歌手不太想回答關於她家人的問題。
  3. "Do you want some wine?" "I'd prefer not to drink. I'm driving."
    你要喝點酒嗎？我還是不喝了。我要開車。
  4. Our neighbours would prefer not to have a party in the garden.
    我們的鄰居不太希望在花園辦派對。
  5. "Can I put your photo in the article?" "Sorry, I'd prefer not to have my photo in it."
    我可以把你的照片放進文章嗎？抱歉，我不太希望文章裡有我的照片。

⑤ 比較兩個選擇：would prefer to ... rather than

  1. I'd prefer to walk rather than wait for the bus in the rain.
    我寧可走路，也不想在雨中等公車。
  2. We would prefer to cook at home rather than eat out every night.
    我們比較想在家煮飯，不想每天晚上都在外面吃。
  3. Most students would prefer to study in the morning rather than stay up late.
    大部分學生比較想早上讀書，不想熬夜。
  4. "Train or plane?" "I'd prefer to take the train rather than fly."
    搭火車還是飛機？我比較想搭火車，不想坐飛機。

⑥ 問句：Would you prefer to ...?

  1. "Would you prefer to sit inside or outside?" "Inside, please. It's windy."
    您比較想坐裡面還是外面？裡面，謝謝。風很大。
  2. Would you prefer to start now or after lunch?
    你比較想現在開始，還是吃完午餐再開始？
  3. "Would your son prefer to share a room?" "No, he'd like his own."
    你兒子會比較想跟人同房嗎？不會，他想要自己一間。
  4. Would you prefer to pay now or when you collect the shoes?
    您比較想現在付錢，還是拿鞋子的時候再付？

⑦ 寫信和電子郵件：would prefer to

  1. Dear Sir, I would prefer to receive my tickets by post.
    您好，我比較希望用郵寄的方式收到票。
  2. If possible, I would prefer to start work in September.
    可以的話，我比較希望九月開始上班。
  3. My client would prefer to settle the matter without going to court.
    我的客戶比較希望不上法院就解決這件事。
  4. We would prefer to receive the payment before the end of the month.
    我們比較希望在月底前收到款項。

⑧ 第三人稱和過去的情境

  1. "Where is Ben?" "He said he would prefer to eat in his room."
    Ben 在哪裡？他說他比較想在房間吃。
  2. She told the doctor she would prefer to have the operation in the spring.
    她跟醫生說她比較想春天動手術。
  3. The children would prefer to play outside, but it's raining.
    孩子們比較想在外面玩，可是在下雨。
