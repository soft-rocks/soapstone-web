It will be a bit more expensive than the bus, but it's safer and quicker.

這會比搭公車貴一點，不過比較安全，也比較快。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面，說⁠明差多⁠少

這會比搭公車貴一點。

各⁠個程⁠度副⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

It will be a bit more expensive than the bus, but it's safer and quicker.

slightly、a bit、a little差一點
much、far、a lot差很多
considerably、significantly差很多，用在正式的場合
so much差很多，表達說話的人的感受
even更，兩邊都有這個性質

例句

① slightly、a bit、a little + 比較級

  1. The bus is a bit slower, but it's cheaper.
    公車慢一點，可是比較便宜。
  2. The second photo is a little clearer than the first.
    第二張照片比第一張清楚一點。
  3. Profits were slightly higher than last year.
    利潤比去年高了一點。
  4. I'd prefer a slightly smaller table.
    我比較想要一張小一點的桌子。
  5. The second interview was a little less stressful.
    第二次面試的壓力小了一點。
  6. Our new neighbours seem a bit more tolerant of noise.
    我們的新鄰居對噪音好像比較能容忍一點。

② much、far、a lot + 比較級

  1. The hotel was much nicer than the photos suggested.
    那間飯店比照片上看起來好得多。
  2. The city centre is far busier at weekends.
    市中心週末的時候熱鬧得多。
  3. This route is a lot shorter.
    走這條路近很多。
  4. She's much less shy than she used to be.
    她現在遠不像以前那麼害羞了。
  5. Living in the countryside is far cheaper than living in London.
    住鄉下比住倫敦便宜得多。
  6. You'll feel a lot more relaxed after a holiday.
    放個假之後你會輕鬆很多。

③ considerably、significantly + 比較級

  1. The new model is considerably lighter than the old one.
    新款比舊款輕了不少。
  2. Prices in the north are significantly lower.
    北部的物價低了很多。
  3. The second edition is considerably longer than the first.
    第二版比第一版長了不少。
  4. Patients who exercise recover significantly faster.
    有運動的病人復原得快很多。

④ even + 比較級

  1. The film was good, but the book is even better.
    電影不錯，可是書更好看。
  2. It was cold yesterday, and today it's even colder.
    昨天很冷，今天更冷。
  3. Clearing the garage turned out to be an even bigger chore than cleaning the kitchen.
    清車庫結果比打掃廚房還要累人。
  4. If you add salt, the soup will taste even worse.
    你再加鹽，湯會更難喝。

⑤ 程度副詞 + 比較級副詞

  1. Can you walk a bit faster? We're late.
    你可以走快一點嗎？我們遲到了。
  2. He drives much more carefully since the accident.
    出了那次車禍以後，他開車小心多了。
  3. The children behaved a lot better at their grandmother's.
    孩子們在奶奶家乖多了。
  4. This printer works slightly more quietly than the old one.
    這台印表機運作的聲音比舊的那台小一點。
  5. My mother cooks far better than I do.
    我媽煮菜比我好得多。
  6. Please answer a little more slowly so I can write it down.
    請你回答得慢一點，我好記下來。

⑥ so much + 比較級

  1. I feel so much better after that sleep.
    睡了那一覺，我覺得好多了。
  2. The house looks so much bigger without all that furniture.
    沒有那些家具，房子看起來大好多。
  3. Travelling by train is so much more comfortable.
    搭火車舒服太多了。
  4. Life got so much easier once we moved closer to work.
    我們搬到離公司近一點的地方以後，日子輕鬆多了。
