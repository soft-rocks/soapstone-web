I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.

我很久沒吃義大利菜了。

haven't + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，表⁠示到現⁠在還⁠沒⁠做

I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.

我畢業以後就沒回過母校。

haven't got 是沒⁠有，hadn't 說過⁠去以⁠前⁠的事

I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.

haven't got

I haven't got any cash. Can I pay by card?

我身上沒有現金，可以刷卡嗎？
hadn't

We hadn't eaten all day, so we were very hungry.

我們一整天沒吃東西，所以很餓。

haven't 後⁠面用過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.

We haven't saw the new film yet.

We haven't seen the new film yet.

單⁠數名⁠詞當主⁠詞，用 hasn't

I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.

The baby haven't slept all afternoon.

The baby hasn't slept all afternoon.

haven't got 後⁠面用 any，不⁠用 no

I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.

We haven't got no bread for breakfast.

We haven't got any bread for breakfast.

例句

① haven't + 過去分詞

  1. I haven't driven a car since my accident.
    我出車禍之後就沒開過車。
  2. We've visited Korea twice. We haven't been to Japan yet.
    我們去過韓國兩次。我們還沒去過日本。
  3. I haven't finished my homework, so I can't go out.
    我還沒寫完作業，所以不能出去。
  4. We asked the hotel about a late check-out. They haven't called us back.
    我們問飯店能不能晚一點退房。他們還沒回我們電話。
  5. Yes, let's order a pizza! I haven't had pizza for months.
    好啊，我們叫披薩吧！我好幾個月沒吃披薩了。
  6. I want to make an album for my parents. You haven't sent me the photos from the wedding.
    我想幫我爸媽做一本相簿。你還沒把婚禮的照片傳給我。

② hasn't + 過去分詞

  1. My sister hasn't paid me back for the concert ticket.
    我姊姊還沒把演唱會的票錢還我。
  2. He finished university in June. My brother hasn't found a job yet.
    他六月大學畢業。我哥哥還沒找到工作。
  3. The garden is so dry. It hasn't rained since June.
    花園好乾，從六月到現在都沒下雨。
  4. I wrote to Ken about the trip on Monday. He hasn't answered my email.
    我星期一寫信跟 Ken 說旅行的事。他還沒回我的電子郵件。
  5. We're waiting for Grandma to arrive. My son hasn't opened his presents yet.
    我們在等奶奶到。我兒子還沒拆禮物。

③ haven't got / hasn't got

  1. Can you go to the shop? We haven't got any milk left for the tea.
    你可以去一下商店嗎？我們沒有牛奶可以加在茶裡了。
  2. We haven't got a garage, so we park on the street.
    我們沒有車庫，所以把車停在街上。
  3. My girlfriend has three sisters. She hasn't got any brothers.
    我女朋友有三個姊妹。她沒有兄弟。
  4. Sorry, my bus is here. I haven't got time to talk now.
    抱歉，我的公車來了。我現在沒時間講話。
  5. I grow my tomatoes on the balcony. My flat hasn't got a garden.
    我在陽台種番茄。我的公寓沒有花園。

④ hadn't + 過去分詞 / hadn't got

  1. My grandparents hadn't got a phone when I was a child.
    我小時候，我爺爺奶奶家沒有電話。
  2. We hadn't eaten all day, so we were very hungry.
    我們一整天沒吃東西，所以很餓。
  3. My husband's family lives in Canada. I hadn't met his parents until the wedding.
    我先生的家人住在加拿大。婚禮之前我沒見過他爸媽。
  4. Thanks for telling me about Grandpa. I hadn't heard the news when you called.
    謝謝你告訴我爺爺的事。你打來的時候，我還沒聽說那個消息。
  5. Many people stood at the meeting. The school hadn't got enough chairs for everyone.
    很多人站著開會。學校那時的椅子不夠大家坐。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. I've worked late every night. I haven't been to the gym this week.
    我每天晚上都加班。我這個禮拜沒去健身房。
  2. We've only been together for a month. My parents haven't met my new boyfriend.
    我們才在一起一個月。我爸媽還沒見過我的新男朋友。
  3. No TV yet! The kids haven't cleaned their room.
    還不能看電視！孩子們還沒打掃房間。
  4. My dad has two jobs now. He hasn't got much free time these days.
    我爸爸現在有兩份工作。他最近沒什麼空。
  5. The party is tomorrow, and I haven't bought a present yet.
    派對就在明天，我還沒買禮物。
  6. Ann moved to Berlin in the spring. We haven't heard from her since May.
    Ann 春天搬去柏林。我們從五月以後就沒有她的消息了。
  7. The bus hasn't come, and we've waited for twenty minutes.
    公車還沒來，我們已經等了二十分鐘了。
  8. Tom left the party an hour ago. I haven't got a clue where he is.
    湯姆一個小時前離開派對。我完全不知道他在哪裡。
  9. We're taking him to the doctor. Grandpa hasn't felt like eating since Monday.
    我們要帶他去看醫生。爺爺從星期一起就沒胃口。
