I think they are rather jealous because they daren't give up their cars.

我覺得他們其實有點嫉妒，因為他們不敢放棄自己的車。

主⁠詞 + dare / daren't / dare not + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

dare有膽量去做。
daren't / dare not沒有膽量去做。
原形動詞需要勇氣的事。

說需⁠要勇⁠氣⁠的事

例句

She didn't dare say no to her boss.

她不敢拒絕她的老闆。

dare 常用在有風險、會被別人反對、或讓人害怕的事。

說某⁠人很勇⁠敢

例句

He was the only one who dared to jump.

他是唯一敢跳下去的人。

肯定句常用一般動詞 dare to，強調某人有膽量。

一⁠般動⁠詞⁠的 dare 說過⁠去⁠的事要⁠用 dared

Yesterday he dare to ask her out.

Yesterday he dared to ask her out.

daren't 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I think they are rather jealous because they daren't give up their cars.

They daren't to give up their cars.

They daren't give up their cars.

didn't dare 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，或接 to 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I didn't dare told anyone.

I didn't dare tell anyone.

例句

① daren't / dare not + 原形動詞：沒有膽量

  1. My neighbours daren't leave their windows open at night.
    我的鄰居晚上不敢開著窗戶。
  2. Most of my friends daren't leave their safe jobs.
    我大部分的朋友都不敢離開穩定的工作。
  3. He daren't jump from the high board.
    他不敢從高跳台跳下去。
  4. I daren't try skiing again after last year's accident.
    發生去年那次意外之後，我不敢再去滑雪了。
  5. Many workers dare not speak up about the problems in the factory.
    很多工人不敢說出工廠裡的問題。
  6. She daren't ride a motorbike in the city.
    她不敢在市區騎機車。
  7. Some parents dare not let their children walk to school alone.
    有些父母不敢讓孩子自己走路上學。
  8. I daren't go on the big rollercoaster.
    我不敢坐那個大雲霄飛車。

② didn't dare + (to) + 原形動詞：過去沒有膽量

  1. I didn't dare tell anyone about my plan.
    我不敢跟任何人說我的計畫。
  2. She didn't dare say no to her boss.
    她不敢拒絕她的老闆。
  3. We didn't dare to go into the cave.
    我們不敢進那個山洞。
  4. He didn't dare look his father in the eye.
    他不敢直視他爸爸的眼睛。
  5. The boys didn't dare climb over the wall.
    那些男孩不敢爬過那道牆。
  6. I didn't dare to ask the famous writer for a photo.
    我不敢請那位有名的作家跟我合照。
  7. On her first day, the new student didn't dare answer any questions.
    新同學第一天不敢回答任何問題。
  8. She didn't dare to quit university, although she hated her course.
    雖然她很討厭自己的科系，她還是不敢休學。

③ dare / dared + to + 原形動詞：有膽量

  1. He was the only one who dared to jump into the river.
    他是唯一敢跳進河裡的人。
  2. She finally dared to tell her parents she wanted to be an actor.
    她終於敢告訴爸媽她想當演員。
  3. Not many people dare to start their own business.
    敢自己創業的人不多。
  4. The young reporter dared to ask the president a difficult question.
    那個年輕記者敢問總統一個很難的問題。
  5. My grandmother dared to travel around the world alone at seventy.
    我奶奶七十歲還敢一個人環遊世界。
  6. Only a few climbers dare to try this mountain in winter.
    只有少數登山客敢在冬天挑戰這座山。
  7. He dared to sing in front of the whole school.
    他敢在全校面前唱歌。
  8. I've never dared to try the spicy noodles at that restaurant.
    我從來不敢吃那家餐廳的辣麵。

④ Would you dare + 原形動詞 ...?：問對方有沒有膽量

  1. Would you dare go skydiving?
    你敢去跳傘嗎？
  2. Would you dare sleep in a tent in the forest?
    你敢在森林裡的帳篷睡覺嗎？
  3. Would you dare tell your teacher she's wrong?
    你敢跟老師說她錯了嗎？
  4. Would you dare give up your phone for a month?
    你敢一個月不用手機嗎？
  5. Would you dare swim with sharks?
    你敢跟鯊魚一起游泳嗎？
  6. Would you dare move to another country without a job?
    你敢沒找到工作就搬去另一個國家嗎？
  7. Would you dare try the fried insects at the night market?
    你敢吃夜市的炸昆蟲嗎？
