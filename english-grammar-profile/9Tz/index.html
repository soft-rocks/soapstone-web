Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

我的衣服大多是檸檬黃、綠色或橘色，那些是我最喜歡的顏色！

those + 複⁠數動⁠詞

Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

我的衣服大多是檸檬黃、綠色或橘色，那些是我最喜歡的顏色！

that 和 those

Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

單數

I've got a new idea. That is my plan for the weekend.

我有一個新點子，那是我週末的計畫。
複數

Well, those are my ideas, but you can choose.

嗯，那些是我的想法，不過你可以自己選。

those 在句⁠子⁠裡⁠的位⁠置

Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

主詞

Those are my brother's shoes.

那些是我哥哥的鞋子。
受詞

Can you give me those, please?

可以把那些給我嗎？
介系詞後面

I'll put the new books next to those.

我會把新書放在那些旁邊。

複⁠數用 those

Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

I like red and blue. That are my favourite colours.

I like red and blue. Those are my favourite colours.

後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞用複⁠數

Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

Look at those birds! Those are a swan.

Look at those birds! Those are swans.

主⁠詞不⁠用 them

Most of my clothes are lemon, green or orange; those are my favorite colors!

Look at the cakes in the window. Them look delicious.

Look at the cakes in the window. Those look delicious.

those + 名⁠詞

那些顏色很適合你。

they：只指前⁠面說⁠過⁠的

我找到你的鑰匙了，在沙發底下。

例句

① those + are

  1. Listen to those birds singing outside. Those are blackbirds.
    你聽外面那些鳥在唱歌，那些是黑鶇。
  2. Those are the keys to the garage.
    那些是車庫的鑰匙。
  3. I like jazz and blues. Those are my favorite kinds of music.
    我喜歡爵士樂和藍調。那些是我最喜歡的音樂類型。
  4. Those are not my glasses. Mine are in my bag.
    那些不是我的眼鏡，我的在包包裡。
  5. Are those your children over there?
    在那邊的那些是你的孩子嗎？

② those + were

  1. Those were the best days of my life.
    那些是我這輩子最美好的日子。
  2. My grandparents lived on a farm. Those were happy times.
    我的祖父母以前住在農場，那段時光很快樂。
  3. Those were my old school friends at the door.
    在門口的那些人是我以前的同學。
  4. Those were my grandmother's earrings.
    那些是我奶奶的耳環。
  5. Those were the rules when I was a child.
    我小時候的規定就是那些。

③ those 當受詞

  1. Can I try those on, please?
    那些我可以試穿嗎？
  2. I don't want these apples. I'll take those.
    我不要這些蘋果，我要那些。
  3. Please put those in the box.
    請把那些放進箱子裡。
  4. Where did you find those? They're beautiful.
    那些你在哪裡找到的？好漂亮。
  5. My mum made those for my birthday.
    那些是我媽媽為我生日做的。

④ 介系詞 + those

  1. Put the cups next to those on the shelf.
    把杯子放在架子上那些旁邊。
  2. I'm not sure about the red shoes. What about those?
    那雙紅鞋我不確定，那雙呢？
  3. These plants grow faster than those.
    這些植物長得比那些快。
  4. We can't play with these balls. Can we play with those?
    我們不能玩這些球，可以玩那些嗎？
  5. Don't sit on these chairs. Sit on those.
    別坐這些椅子，坐那些。

⑤ 寫信介紹自己

  1. I love cats and horses. Those are my favorite animals.
    我很喜歡貓和馬。那些是我最喜歡的動物。
  2. I play tennis and basketball. Those are the sports I'm best at.
    我打網球和籃球。那些是我最擅長的運動。
  3. Maths and science are hard for me. Those are my worst subjects.
    數學和自然對我來說很難。那些是我最差的科目。
  4. My hobbies are reading and cooking. Those take up most of my free time.
    我的嗜好是閱讀和做菜，我大部分的空閒時間都花在那些上面。
  5. I wear jeans and trainers a lot. Those are the most comfortable clothes.
    我常穿牛仔褲和運動鞋，那些是最舒服的衣服。

⑥ 提出想法和計畫

  1. We could eat at home or go out. Those are our choices.
    我們可以在家吃或出去吃，就這兩個選擇。
  2. We could go to the beach or the zoo. Those are my suggestions.
    我們可以去海灘或動物園。那些是我的建議。
  3. Clean the kitchen and walk the dog. Those are your jobs today.
    打掃廚房、遛狗。那些是你今天的工作。
  4. Bring a coat and an umbrella. Those are the most important things.
    帶外套和雨傘。那些是最重要的東西。
  5. A map, some water and good shoes. Those are all you need.
    一張地圖、一些水和一雙好鞋。你只需要那些。
  6. Those are just my thoughts. What do you think?
    那些只是我的想法，你覺得呢？
