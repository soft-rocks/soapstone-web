In the morning, I go to the beach.

早上我會去海邊。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語放⁠在句⁠首，主⁠詞和動⁠詞⁠的順⁠序不⁠變

早上我會去海邊。

句⁠首⁠的介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語說⁠出時⁠間、地⁠點、起⁠點或看⁠法⁠的來⁠源

In the morning, I go to the beach.

時間

After lunch, we usually go for a walk.

吃完午飯，我們通常會去散步。
地點

In my country, people eat dinner very late.

在我的國家，大家晚餐都吃得很晚。
起點

From the station, take the number 12 bus.

從車站搭 12 號公車。
看法的來源

In my opinion, the first film was better.

我覺得第一集比較好看。

放⁠在句⁠首⁠先說⁠出地⁠點，放⁠在句⁠尾先說⁠出發⁠生⁠的事

句首

In my country, rice is the most popular food.

在我的國家，米飯是最受歡迎的食物。
句尾

Rice is the most popular food in my country.

米飯是我的國家最受歡迎的食物。

比⁠較兩⁠個時⁠間⁠時，介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語都放⁠在句⁠首

夏天

In the summer, we go to the beach.

夏天我們去海邊。
冬天

In the winter, we go skiing.

冬天我們去滑雪。

名⁠詞片⁠語說⁠出時⁠間⁠時，前⁠面要⁠有介⁠系⁠詞

In the morning, I go to the beach.

The morning, I go to the beach.

In the morning, I go to the beach.

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語不⁠是主⁠詞，後⁠面要⁠有主⁠詞

In my country is very hot in August.

In my country, it is very hot in August.

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語放⁠在句⁠首⁠時不⁠倒裝

In the morning, I go to the beach.

After school go we to the park.

After school, we go to the park.

一⁠天⁠裡⁠的⁠時⁠段用 in

In the morning, I go to the beach.

On the evening, we have dinner together.

In the evening, we have dinner together.

位⁠置⁠的動⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面，用在描⁠寫和故⁠事

山丘上矗立著一座古堡。

Suddenly 是副⁠詞，不⁠是介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

突然，門開了。

例句

① 時間

  1. In the evening, my grandparents sit in the garden.
    晚上我祖父母會坐在花園裡。
  2. On Saturdays, I play football with my brother.
    每個星期六我都跟我哥哥踢足球。
  3. After dinner, my dad always washes the dishes.
    吃完晚餐，我爸都會洗碗。
  4. At the weekend, the city centre is very busy.
    週末時市中心人很多。
  5. In 2019, my sister started university.
    2019 年，我姊姊上了大學。
  6. During the holidays, I worked in a café.
    放假期間，我在一家咖啡館打工。

② 地點

  1. In my town, there are two cinemas.
    我住的鎮上有兩家電影院。
  2. At school, we have to wear a uniform.
    在學校我們得穿制服。
  3. On the left, you can see the old church.
    左邊可以看到那座老教堂。
  4. In the kitchen, there's a big table by the window.
    廚房裡窗邊有一張大桌子。
  5. At the party, I met a lot of interesting people.
    在派對上，我認識了很多有趣的人。
  6. In Japan, people take off their shoes at home.
    在日本，大家在家裡會脫鞋。

③ 起點

  1. From your house, take the first turning on the left.
    從你家出來，在第一個路口左轉。
  2. From the airport, it's about an hour by train.
    從機場搭火車大約一個小時。
  3. From my window, I can see the sea.
    從我的窗戶可以看到海。
  4. From here, the road goes up the hill.
    從這裡開始，路就往山上去。

④ 看法的來源

  1. In my opinion, the book is better than the film.
    我覺得書比電影好看。
  2. For me, the best part was the music.
    對我來說，最棒的是音樂。
  3. In my experience, small hotels are friendlier.
    就我的經驗，小旅館比較親切。
  4. For most people, the price is too high.
    對大部分的人來說，價格太高了。

⑤ 比較兩個時間或地點

  1. In the summer, it's hot. In the winter, it snows.
    夏天很熱。冬天會下雪。
  2. On weekdays, I get up at six. On Sundays, I sleep until ten.
    平日我六點起床。星期天我睡到十點。
  3. In the city, life is fast. In the country, it's much slower.
    城市的生活步調很快。鄉下慢多了。
  4. At home, I speak Spanish. At work, I speak English.
    在家我說西班牙文。上班時我說英文。
  5. Before the trip, I was nervous. After it, I felt great.
    出發前我很緊張。回來後我覺得很棒。

⑥ 不加逗號

  1. In April we're going to visit my aunt.
    四月我們要去看我阿姨。
  2. At night the streets are very quiet.
    晚上街上很安靜。
  3. On Mondays I have a piano lesson.
    每個星期一我都有鋼琴課。
  4. After the film we went for a pizza.
    看完電影我們去吃了披薩。
  5. In this photo you can see my grandparents.
    這張照片裡可以看到我的祖父母。
  6. Near the station there's a good bakery.
    車站附近有一家不錯的麵包店。
