What have you brought for me?

你帶了什麼東西給我？

完⁠成⁠式⁠的 wh⁠-⁠ 疑⁠問⁠句把 have 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

主⁠詞是 he、she、it 時用 has，過⁠去完⁠成⁠式用 had

例句

① What + have / has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. Uncle Mark, you're back! What have you brought for me?
    Mark 叔叔，你回來了！你帶了什麼東西給我？
  2. The kitchen is a mess. What have you done?
    廚房一團亂。你做了什麼？
  3. What has happened to your arm? Did you fall?
    你的手臂怎麼了？你跌倒了嗎？
  4. They want an answer by Friday. What have you decided about the job in Paris?
    他們星期五前要答覆。巴黎那份工作你決定得怎麼樣了？
  5. What has she said to make you so angry?
    她說了什麼讓你這麼生氣？

② Where + have / has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. You're three hours late. Where have you been?
    你遲到了三個小時。你去哪裡了？
  2. Where have I put my glasses? I had them a minute ago.
    我把眼鏡放到哪裡去了？我剛剛還戴著。
  3. Where has Dad gone? His car isn't here.
    爸爸去哪裡了？他的車不在這裡。
  4. Where have you travelled in Europe?
    你在歐洲去過哪些地方？

③ How + have / has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. I haven't seen you for ages. How have you been?
    好久不見。你最近好嗎？
  2. How has your new job been so far?
    你的新工作到目前為止怎麼樣？
  3. You know every street here. How long have you lived in this town?
    你對這裡每條街都很熟。你在這個鎮住多久了？
  4. Your passport is full of stamps. How many countries have you been to?
    你的護照蓋滿了章。你去過幾個國家？
  5. How much have you spent on the wedding?
    你在婚禮上花了多少錢？

④ Who / Which / Why + have / has

  1. Who has eaten my sandwich? It was on the table.
    誰吃了我的三明治？它本來在桌上。
  2. I need to know how much food to buy. Who have you invited to the party?
    我得知道要買多少食物。你邀請了誰來派對？
  3. Which books have you read this year?
    你今年讀了哪些書？
  4. Why have you turned off the heating? It's freezing.
    你為什麼把暖氣關了？冷死了。
  5. It's nearly midnight. Why haven't the children gone to bed?
    都快半夜了。孩子們為什麼還沒去睡覺？

⑤ 疑問詞 + had + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. You speak French so well. Where had you lived before you came to London?
    你法文說得真好。你來倫敦之前住過哪裡？
  2. What had he done to make her so upset?
    他之前做了什麼讓她那麼難過？
  3. How long had you waited when the bus finally came?
    公車終於來的時候，你已經等了多久？
  4. Why had nobody told her about the meeting?
    為什麼之前都沒有人告訴她開會的事？
  5. Which countries had you visited before that trip?
    那趟旅行之前，你去過哪些國家？

⑥ 縮寫的 has

  1. Where's he gone? He was here a minute ago.
    他去哪裡了？他剛剛還在這裡。
  2. What's she done to her hair? It looks great.
    她的頭髮做了什麼？看起來好漂亮。
  3. My charger isn't on my desk. Who's borrowed it?
    我的充電器不在桌上。誰借走了？

⑦ 問經驗和改變

  1. How have things changed since you moved to the city?
    你搬到城市以後，生活有什麼改變？
  2. What have you learned from this experience?
    你從這次經驗學到了什麼？
  3. How much has the town changed since you were a child?
    跟你小時候比，這個鎮變了多少？
  4. What have you enjoyed most about the course?
    這門課你最喜歡哪個部分？
