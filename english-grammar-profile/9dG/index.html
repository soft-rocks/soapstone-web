例句

① Does + he / she / it

  1. A man in the hall just said hello to me. How does he know my name?
    走廊上有個男人剛剛跟我打招呼。他怎麼知道我的名字？
  2. Your brother is fifteen. Does he go on holiday with your parents?
    你哥哥十五歲了。他還跟你爸媽一起去度假嗎？
  3. I saw her in a nurse's uniform. Does your mum work at the hospital?
    我看到她穿護士服。你媽媽在醫院上班嗎？
  4. This machine looks old. Does it still work?
    這台機器看起來好舊。它還能用嗎？
  5. I need some bread tomorrow. Does the bakery open on Sundays?
    我明天要買麵包。那家麵包店星期天有開嗎？
  6. The time has changed. Does Anna know about the meeting tomorrow?
    時間改了。Anna 知道明天開會的事嗎？

② Do + we / they / 複數名詞

  1. You visit them every week. Do your parents live near you?
    你每個星期都去看他們。你爸媽住在你附近嗎？
  2. I'm packing a lunch box. Do the children have lunch at school?
    我在準備便當。孩子們在學校吃午餐嗎？
  3. I'm going to the shop now. Do we need to bring anything to the party?
    我現在要去店裡。我們需要帶什麼東西去派對嗎？
  4. Their kids speak English so well. Do they speak it at home?
    他們的孩子英文講得好好。他們在家說英文嗎？
  5. I'm flying out at six. Do these trains stop at the airport?
    我六點的飛機。這些火車有停機場嗎？

③ 疑問詞 + does + 單數主詞

  1. Your uncle always wears a suit. What does he do for a living?
    你叔叔總是穿西裝。他是做什麼工作的？
  2. I'm new in town. Where does this bus go?
    我剛搬來這裡。這班公車開到哪裡？
  3. He has so many fishing rods. How often does your dad go fishing?
    他有好多釣竿。你爸爸多久去釣一次魚？
  4. We need to buy popcorn first. What time does the film start?
    我們得先買爆米花。電影幾點開始？
  5. We're so tired. Why does the baby cry every night?
    我們好累。寶寶為什麼每天晚上都哭？
  6. I'm looking for a school for my daughter. Which school does your son go to?
    我在幫我女兒找學校。你兒子念哪一所學校？

④ 疑問詞 + do + 複數主詞

  1. Let's plan our TV evening. Which programmes do we all like to watch?
    我們來安排看電視的晚上吧。我們大家都喜歡看哪些節目？
  2. Your cousins are asking about the summer. Where do they want to spend their holidays?
    你表兄弟姊妹在問暑假的事。他們想去哪裡度假？
  3. You're always at home. What do your friends usually do at the weekend?
    你總是在家。你朋友週末通常都做什麼？
  4. The roads look empty. How do people get to work in your city?
    路上看起來好空。你們城市的人都怎麼去上班？
  5. I want to buy a gift tonight. When do the shops close here?
    我今晚想買個禮物。這裡的店幾點關門？

⑤ who / what 當主詞

  1. The lights are always on. Who lives in that big house on the corner?
    那裡的燈總是亮著。誰住在轉角那間大房子？
  2. This remote is new to me. What happens if I press this button?
    我沒用過這個遙控器。我按這個按鈕會怎麼樣？
  3. Your English is getting better. Who teaches you this year?
    你的英文越來越好了。今年誰教你？
  4. You're smiling a lot. What makes you so happy today?
    你一直在笑。今天什麼事讓你這麼開心？

⑥ 疑問詞當受詞

  1. She moved to London last year. Who does your sister live with there?
    她去年搬去倫敦了。你姊姊在那裡跟誰住？
  2. You've been dating him for a month. What do your parents think of your new boyfriend?
    你跟他交往一個月了。你爸媽覺得你的新男朋友怎麼樣？
  3. There are no kids on our street. Who do your children play with after school?
    我們這條街沒有小孩。你的孩子放學後跟誰玩？
  4. I keep seeing it in this book. What does this word mean?
    我在這本書裡一直看到它。這個字是什麼意思？
