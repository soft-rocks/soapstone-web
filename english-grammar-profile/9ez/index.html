May I borrow your bike?

我可以借你的腳踏車嗎？

May + I + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 …？

May I我可以……嗎？
原形動詞想做的事。

may 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

May I borrow your bike?

May I to use your phone?

May I use your phone?

may 放⁠在句⁠首，不⁠用 do

May I borrow your bike?

Do I may sit here?

May I sit here?

問⁠句把 may 放⁠在 I 前⁠面

May I borrow your bike?

I may open the window?

May I open the window?

例句

① May I + borrow / use：借東西

  1. May I borrow your umbrella? It's raining hard.
    我可以借你的傘嗎？雨下得很大。
  2. My phone is dead. May I use yours for a minute?
    我的手機沒電了。我可以借用一下你的嗎？
  3. May I borrow your pen? I've left mine at home.
    我可以借你的筆嗎？我的忘在家裡了。
  4. May I use your kitchen to make a cup of tea for my mother?
    我可以借用你的廚房幫我媽媽泡杯茶嗎？
  5. May I borrow this book until next week?
    我可以借這本書到下星期嗎？
  6. May I use the computer in the library?
    我可以用圖書館的電腦嗎？

② May I + 動詞：在別人家裡或辦公室

  1. May I sit here? Is this seat free?
    我可以坐這裡嗎？這個位子有人坐嗎？
  2. The room smells of paint. May I open the window behind your desk?
    房間裡有油漆味。我可以打開你桌子後面的窗戶嗎？
  3. May I come in and wait for her?
    我可以進來等她嗎？
  4. May I hang my wet umbrella by the door?
    我可以把濕雨傘掛在門邊嗎？
  5. May I have some more bread, please?
    請問我可以再要一點麵包嗎？
  6. May I close the door? There's a lot of noise outside.
    我可以關門嗎？外面很吵。

③ May I + 動詞：主動提供幫忙

  1. You're new in town. May I be your guide while you're staying here?
    你剛到這個城市。你在這裡的時候，我可以當你的導遊嗎？
  2. Those bags look heavy. May I help you?
    那些袋子看起來很重。我可以幫你嗎？
  3. May I carry your suitcase to your room?
    我可以幫你把行李箱提到房間嗎？
  4. May I drive you home after the dinner tonight?
    今晚吃完飯後，我可以開車送你回家嗎？
  5. May I show you the way to the station?
    我可以帶你去車站嗎？
  6. May I get you something to drink?
    我可以幫你拿點喝的嗎？

④ May I + 動詞：在商店和餐廳

  1. May I try on the grey jacket in the window display?
    我可以試穿櫥窗裡那件灰色外套嗎？
  2. May I sit at the table by the fireplace instead?
    我可以改坐壁爐旁邊那桌嗎？
  3. May I pay for the flowers by card? I haven't got any cash.
    我可以刷卡買這些花嗎？我身上沒有現金。
  4. May I taste a little of the cheese before I buy it?
    我買之前可以先嚐一點起司嗎？
  5. May I look at that watch in the window?
    我可以看看櫥窗裡那支手錶嗎？
  6. May I change this shirt for a bigger size?
    我可以把這件襯衫換大一號嗎？

⑤ May I + 動詞：在學校和工作

  1. May I leave early today? I have a dentist's appointment.
    我今天可以提早走嗎？我跟牙醫有約。
  2. May I go to the toilet, please?
    請問我可以去上廁所嗎？
  3. May I hand in my essay tomorrow?
    我可以明天交作文嗎？
  4. May I take a photo of the board?
    我可以拍黑板的照片嗎？
  5. May I bring my little brother to the drama club on Thursday?
    星期四我可以帶我弟弟去戲劇社嗎？
  6. May I have a day off next Friday?
    下星期五我可以請一天假嗎？
  7. May I record the lesson on my phone?
    我可以用手機錄下這堂課嗎？
