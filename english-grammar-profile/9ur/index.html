There + ought to be + 名⁠詞

there ought to be、名⁠詞

there ought to be應該有。
名詞應該有、可是現在沒有的東西。

投⁠訴信

There ought to be a lift, since the rooms were on the fourth floor.

例句

There was no hot water. There ought to be a notice about it.

沒有熱水。應該要有告示說明。

投訴信常先說現在的問題，再用 there ought to be 說應該有的東西。

建⁠議

例句

There ought to be more buses in the evening.

晚上應該要有更多公車。

例句

① There ought to be + 名詞：投訴信

  1. The rooms were dirty and there was no room service. There ought to be a lift, since the rooms were on the fourth floor.
    房間很髒，也沒有客房服務。應該要有電梯，因為房間在四樓。
  2. There ought to be a sign at the entrance. We couldn't find the reception.
    入口應該要有指示牌。我們找不到櫃台。
  3. Dear Sir, I travelled on the four-hour train to Leeds yesterday. There ought to be a toilet on the train.
    先生您好，我昨天搭四個小時的火車去 Leeds。火車上應該要有廁所。
  4. Someone took my camera from our hotel room. There ought to be a safe in every room.
    有人從我們飯店房間拿走了我的相機。每個房間都應該要有保險箱。
  5. We arrived at the hotel at midnight and waited an hour outside. There ought to be someone at the desk at night.
    我們半夜到飯店，在外面等了一個小時。晚上櫃台應該要有人。
  6. We paid full price for our two little boys. There ought to be a discount for families.
    我們兩個小兒子都付了全價。應該要有家庭折扣。

② There ought to be some changes / some + 名詞：會議的結論

  1. After the meeting, we came to the conclusion that there ought to be some changes to the club's publicity.
    開完會以後，我們得出結論：社團的宣傳應該要有一些改變。
  2. People leave dirty plates in the office kitchen every day. There ought to be some rules about using it.
    每天都有人把髒盤子留在公司廚房。使用廚房應該要有一些規定。
  3. Parents at the meeting had one complaint. There ought to be some way of contacting the teachers at weekends.
    家長在會議上有一個意見。週末應該要有聯絡老師的方法。
  4. Many first-year students feel lost in the first week. There ought to be some help for them.
    很多一年級新生第一週都很茫然。應該要給他們一些協助。
  5. The talk went on for two hours without a break. There ought to be some time for questions at the end.
    演講講了兩個小時都沒休息。最後應該要有一些發問的時間。
  6. The village hall roof is leaking again. There ought to be some money for repairs.
    村民會館的屋頂又漏水了。應該要有一些修理的經費。

③ There ought to be more + 名詞：建議增加

  1. I enjoyed the course, but I think there ought to be more excursions and guided tours.
    我很喜歡這門課，可是我想應該要有更多的校外活動和導覽。
  2. I waited fifty minutes for a bus last night. There ought to be more of them after eight.
    我昨晚等公車等了五十分鐘。八點以後應該要有更多班次。
  3. The park is covered in litter every Sunday. There ought to be more bins.
    公園每個星期天都滿地垃圾。應該要有更多垃圾桶。
  4. Half the voters are women, so there ought to be more women in parliament.
    一半的選民是女性，所以國會裡應該要有更多女性。
  5. Cycling in the city centre is dangerous. There ought to be more cycle paths.
    在市中心騎腳踏車很危險。應該要有更多腳踏車道。
  6. The café only has two kinds of sandwiches. There ought to be more choice on the menu.
    那家咖啡店只有兩種三明治。菜單上應該要有更多選擇。

④ There ought to be + a law / a rule + against + 名詞

  1. My neighbours had a party until four this morning. There ought to be a law against loud music after midnight.
    我鄰居的派對開到今天早上四點。應該要有法律禁止半夜以後放很大聲的音樂。
  2. Nobody talked at dinner. They all looked at their screens. There ought to be a rule against using phones at the table.
    晚餐時沒有人說話。大家都在看螢幕。應該要有規定禁止在餐桌上用手機。
  3. The shop across from the school sells sweets to children every afternoon. There ought to be a law against it.
    學校對面的店每天下午都賣糖果給孩子。應該要有法律禁止。
  4. I had to push the pram into the road because of a van. There ought to be a rule against parking on the pavement.
    因為一台廂型車，我只好把嬰兒車推到馬路上。應該要有規定禁止在人行道上停車。
  5. They cut down a three-hundred-year-old oak in our street. There ought to be a law against cutting down old trees.
    他們砍了我們街上一棵三百年的橡樹。應該要有法律禁止砍老樹。
  6. Two people chatted through the whole film last night. There ought to be a rule against talking in the cinema.
    昨晚有兩個人整部電影都在聊天。應該要有規定禁止在電影院裡說話。

⑤ There ought to be + 名詞 + for + 名詞

  1. The new estate has three hundred homes. There ought to be a playground for the kids who live there.
    新社區有三百戶。應該要有給住在那裡的孩子的遊樂場。
  2. The school library is noisy at lunchtime. There ought to be a quiet room for students who want to study.
    學校圖書館午餐時間很吵。應該要有一間安靜的房間給想讀書的學生。
  3. My dad uses a wheelchair, and the car park was full. There ought to be a parking space for disabled drivers.
    我爸爸坐輪椅，停車場又滿了。應該要有給身障駕駛的停車位。
  4. The wedding menu was all meat and fish. There ought to be a vegetarian dish for guests who don't eat meat.
    婚宴的菜單全是肉和魚。應該要有一道素食給不吃肉的客人。
  5. We stood in the rain for twenty minutes. There ought to be a shelter for people waiting at the bus stop.
    我們在雨中站了二十分鐘。公車站應該要有給等車的人的遮雨棚。
  6. The young people in our village have nowhere to go. There ought to be a place for teenagers to meet in the evening.
    我們村子的年輕人沒地方去。應該要有一個地方讓青少年晚上聚會。
  7. The quiz night is next Friday. There ought to be a prize for the best team.
    問答之夜在下星期五。應該要有給最佳隊伍的獎品。
