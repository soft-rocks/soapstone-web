In the evening, you can entertain yourselves by dancing or listening to music.

晚上你們可以跳跳舞或聽聽音樂，自己找點樂子。

例句

① ourselves

  1. Let's introduce ourselves before the meeting starts.
    會議開始前我們先自我介紹吧。
  2. We taught ourselves to cook during lockdown.
    封城期間我們自己學會了做菜。
  3. We kept asking ourselves what we had done wrong.
    我們一直問自己做錯了什麼。
  4. We dried ourselves with the hotel towels after the swim.
    游完泳後，我們用飯店的毛巾把自己擦乾。
  5. We shouldn't blame ourselves for the accident.
    我們不應該為這場意外怪自己。
  6. My husband and I treated ourselves to a weekend in Paris.
    我先生和我犒賞自己去巴黎玩了一個週末。
  7. Oh no, we've locked ourselves out of the car again!
    糟了，我們又把自己鎖在車外了！
  8. As a team, we pushed ourselves hard this season.
    我們整個團隊這一季都把自己逼得很緊。
  9. The music was so loud that we could barely hear ourselves.
    音樂太大聲了，我們幾乎聽不到自己說話。
  10. We need to prepare ourselves for some bad news.
    我們得讓自己做好心理準備，可能會有壞消息。
  11. We'll protect ourselves with good insurance before we start the business.
    開公司之前，我們會先買好保險保護自己。

② yourselves

  1. Kids, please behave yourselves while Grandma is here.
    孩子們，奶奶在的時候你們要守規矩。
  2. Could you all introduce yourselves, please?
    可以請大家自我介紹嗎？
  3. Be careful with those knives, or you'll cut yourselves.
    那些刀小心點，不然你們會割到自己。
  4. Make yourselves at home while I get dinner ready.
    我準備晚餐的時候，你們就當自己家一樣。
  5. You two should ask yourselves whether you really need a bigger house.
    你們兩個應該問問自己，是不是真的需要更大的房子。
  6. Remember to protect yourselves from the sun on the boat trip.
    搭船出遊時記得防曬，保護好自己。
  7. Please seat yourselves. The show starts in five minutes.
    請各位就座，表演五分鐘後開始。
  8. Don't wear yourselves out before the race.
    比賽前別把自己累壞了。
  9. Get yourselves ready, everyone. The bus leaves in ten minutes.
    大家準備好，巴士十分鐘後出發。
  10. Did you hurt yourselves when the boat tipped over?
    船翻的時候你們有沒有受傷？

③ themselves

  1. The children entertained themselves with a box of old toys.
    孩子們拿一箱舊玩具自己玩。
  2. My parents bought themselves a new car for their anniversary.
    我爸媽在結婚紀念日買了一台新車給自己。
  3. Several workers have hurt themselves on this machine.
    好幾個工人被這台機器弄傷過。
  4. The twins can dress themselves now.
    那對雙胞胎現在會自己穿衣服了。
  5. Many adults ask themselves whether zoos are cruel.
    很多成年人會問自己，動物園是不是很殘忍。
  6. The guests helped themselves to cake and coffee.
    客人們自己拿蛋糕和咖啡。
  7. The thieves gave themselves away by posting photos online.
    小偷在網路上貼照片，暴露了自己的身分。
  8. During the storm, they locked themselves in the bathroom.
    暴風雨的時候，他們把自己鎖在浴室裡。
  9. Cats spend hours washing themselves.
    貓會花好幾個小時把自己舔乾淨。
  10. The new employees introduced themselves at the morning meeting.
    新員工在晨會上自我介紹。
  11. Some players blamed themselves for losing the final.
    有些球員為輸掉決賽怪自己。
