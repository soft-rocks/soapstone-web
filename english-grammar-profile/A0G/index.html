I was scared and closed the window, but I couldn't sleep, so I opened it again.

我很害怕，就把窗戶關上了，可是我睡不著，所以又把它打開。

and + but + so 串起子⁠句

I was scared and closed the window, but I couldn't sleep, so I opened it again.

我很害怕，就把窗戶關上了，可是我睡不著，所以又把它打開。

then 接⁠著發⁠生⁠的事

We went to his studio, then he started singing, and I joined in.

我們去了他的錄音室，接著他開始唱歌，我也跟著唱。

各⁠個連⁠接⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

I was scared and closed the window, but I couldn't sleep, so I opened it again.

and補充另一件事，或接著發生的事
but和前面相反，或出乎意料
or兩件事選一件
so前面那件事的結果
then接著發生，常寫成 and then

先⁠後發⁠生⁠的事用 then 分開

We went to the shop and we bought bread and we went home and we made sandwiches.

We went to the shop and bought bread, then we went home and made sandwiches.

because 和 so 只⁠用一⁠個

I was scared and closed the window, but I couldn't sleep, so I opened it again.

Because I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.

I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.

結⁠果用 so

I was scared and closed the window, but I couldn't sleep, so I opened it again.

The shop was closed, but I went home.

The shop was closed, so I went home.

相⁠反⁠的事用 but

I was scared and closed the window, but I couldn't sleep, so I opened it again.

The film was very long, so we enjoyed every minute.

The film was very long, but we enjoyed every minute.

例句

① and + but + so

  1. I was cold and put on a jumper, but I was still shivering, so I made some tea.
    我覺得冷，就穿上毛衣，可是還是一直發抖，所以泡了一杯茶。
  2. I called Sam and left a message, but he didn't call back, so I went to his house.
    我打給 Sam 並留了言，可是他沒回電，所以我去他家找他。
  3. We got up early and drove to the beach, but it was raining, so we went to a café.
    我們很早起床開車去海邊，可是在下雨，所以我們去了一家咖啡館。
  4. Maria saw the dress and loved it, but it was too expensive, so she didn't buy it.
    Maria 看到那件洋裝很喜歡，可是太貴了，所以她沒買。
  5. The bus was late and the traffic was terrible, but we got there in time.
    公車誤點，交通又很糟，可是我們還是準時到了。

② and then

  1. I finished my homework and then I watched a film.
    我寫完功課，然後看了一部電影。
  2. We had dinner at a little restaurant and then we walked along the river.
    我們在一家小餐廳吃晚餐，然後沿著河邊散步。
  3. She opened the letter, read it twice and then started to cry.
    她打開信讀了兩遍，然後就哭了。
  4. We visited my grandparents and then drove home late at night.
    我們去看了爺爺奶奶，然後深夜才開車回家。

③ then + and

  1. We went to his studio, then he started singing, and I joined in.
    我們去了他的錄音室，接著他開始唱歌，我也跟著唱。
  2. I heard a strange noise, then the lights went out, and everyone screamed.
    我聽到一個奇怪的聲音，接著燈熄了，大家都尖叫起來。
  3. I woke up, then I looked at the clock, and it was already ten.
    我醒來，接著看了一下時鐘，已經十點了。
  4. The plane landed, then we waited for our bags, and finally we took a taxi.
    飛機降落，接著我們等行李，最後搭計程車。

④ but + so

  1. I wanted to go to the concert, but I had no money, so I stayed at home.
    我想去聽演唱會，可是沒錢，所以待在家。
  2. The tickets were sold out, but my friend had two, so we went together.
    票賣完了，可是我朋友有兩張，所以我們一起去了。
  3. It was a long walk, but the weather was lovely, so we didn't mind.
    路很遠，可是天氣很好，所以我們不介意。
  4. I forgot my keys, but my neighbour had a spare one, so I got in.
    我忘了帶鑰匙，可是鄰居有備用的，所以我進得了門。

⑤ and + so

  1. It started to snow and the roads were icy, so we stayed in the hotel.
    開始下雪，路面又結冰，所以我們待在飯店。
  2. I was hungry and there was nothing in the fridge, so I ordered a pizza.
    我很餓，冰箱裡又什麼都沒有，所以我叫了披薩。
  3. The baby was crying and nobody could sleep, so Dad took her for a drive.
    寶寶一直哭，大家都睡不著，所以爸爸開車載她出去兜風。
  4. The film was boring and very long, so we left early.
    那部電影很無聊又很長，所以我們提早離開了。

⑥ or + so

  1. You can take the train or the bus, but the train is faster, so I'd take that.
    你可以搭火車或公車，可是火車比較快，所以我會搭火車。
  2. We could stay in or go out, but I'm tired, so let's stay in.
    我們可以待在家或出去，可是我很累，所以我們待在家吧。
  3. You can pay now or later, but the price goes up next week.
    你可以現在付或之後付，可是下週價錢會漲。
  4. Should we call a taxi, or should we walk? It's not raining, so let's walk.
    我們要叫計程車，還是走路？沒在下雨，所以我們走路吧。

⑦ 說故事的長句

  1. I looked out of the window and saw two men in the garden, but when I called my dad, they were gone.
    我往窗外看，看到花園裡有兩個男人，可是等我叫爸爸過來，他們已經不見了。
  2. We arrived at the hotel, but our room wasn't ready, so we left our bags and went for lunch.
    我們到了飯店，可是房間還沒準備好，所以我們放下行李去吃午餐。
  3. I heard a strange voice, I woke up, and there was my mum next to my bed.
    我聽到一個奇怪的聲音，醒了過來，發現我媽就在我床邊。
  4. My phone died, so I borrowed a stranger's phone, and then I called my sister.
    我的手機沒電了，所以我跟一位陌生人借手機，然後打給我姊姊。
  5. The dog ran out of the house, then it crossed the road, and a kind lady caught it.
    狗跑出家門，接著穿過馬路，有一位好心的女士抓住了牠。
