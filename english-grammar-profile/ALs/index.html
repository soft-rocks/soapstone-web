例句

① What ... for / about / at

  1. What is this button for?
    這個按鈕是做什麼用的？
  2. What did you two argue about?
    你們兩個在吵什麼？
  3. What are all those people staring at?
    那些人都在盯著什麼看？
  4. What is your new book about?
    你的新書在講什麼？
  5. What did the restaurant charge us extra for?
    餐廳多收了我們什麼錢？
  6. What is your boss so angry about?
    你老闆在氣什麼？

② Who ... with / to / for

  1. Who are you going on holiday with?
    你要跟誰去度假？
  2. Who did you send the letter to?
    你把信寄給誰了？
  3. Who are these roses on the table from?
    桌上這些玫瑰是誰送的？
  4. Who does this coat belong to?
    這件外套是誰的？
  5. Who did your daughter sit next to on the school trip?
    你女兒校外教學的時候坐在誰旁邊？
  6. Who do you usually share a taxi with after work?
    你下班後通常跟誰共乘計程車？

③ Where ... from / to

  1. Where is your new flatmate from?
    你的新室友是哪裡人？
  2. Where did you get that hat from?
    你那頂帽子是在哪裡買的？
  3. Where is this train going to?
    這班火車開往哪裡？
  4. Where did your parents move to?
    你爸媽搬到哪裡去了？
  5. Where does this road lead to?
    這條路通往哪裡？

④ Which ... in / on

  1. Which room is the meeting in?
    會議在哪一間會議室？
  2. Which shelf did you find this old diary on?
    你在哪一層架子上找到這本舊日記的？
  3. Which bus did you come on?
    你搭哪一班公車來的？
  4. Which subject are you most interested in?
    你對哪一科最有興趣？
  5. Which drawer did you put the scissors in?
    你把剪刀放在哪一個抽屜？

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. What kind of music do you listen to?
    你聽哪一類的音樂？
  2. What kind of games is your son into these days?
    你兒子最近迷什麼遊戲？
  3. Who did you get this present from?
    這份禮物是誰送你的？
  4. What are you looking for? Maybe I can help.
    你在找什麼？也許我可以幫忙。
  5. Which team do you play for?
    你是哪一隊的球員？
  6. What do you need the money for?
    你要那筆錢做什麼？
  7. Who is your sister getting married to?
    你姊姊要嫁給誰？
  8. What are your parents arguing about this time?
    你爸媽這次在吵什麼？
