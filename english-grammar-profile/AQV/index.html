例句

① So + wh- 疑問句 + 回答：提出自己的看法

  1. Many people give presents out of habit. So what makes a good present? From my point of view, the best presents are personal.
    很多人送禮物只是出於習慣。那什麼樣的禮物才是好禮物呢？依我看，最好的禮物是有個人心意的禮物。
  2. Every student wants good marks. So what makes a good teacher? I think it's someone who listens.
    每個學生都想拿好成績。那怎樣才算是好老師？我認為是願意聽學生說話的人。
  3. Some people use apps, and others take classes. So what is the best way to learn a language? In my opinion, it's to live in the country.
    有些人用應用程式，有些人去上課。那學一種語言最好的方法是什麼？我認為是住在那個國家。
  4. So why do so many young people move to the city? The answer is simple: jobs.
    那為什麼有那麼多年輕人搬到城市？答案很簡單：工作。
  5. Many families never talk at meals. So what can parents do? First, they can turn off the TV at dinner.
    很多家庭吃飯時從來不聊天。那父母可以做什麼？首先，他們可以在晚餐時關掉電視。
  6. Electricity bills are going up. So how can we save energy at home? There are three easy steps.
    電費一直漲。那我們在家可以怎麼省電？有三個簡單的步驟。

② But + wh- 疑問句 + 回答：轉到另一個方向

  1. Cars cause a lot of pollution. But what are the alternatives? Berlin, for example, has an excellent underground.
    汽車造成很多污染。可是有什麼其他的選擇？舉例來說，柏林有很好的地鐵。
  2. Everyone agrees that we need more parks. But where will the money come from? The council must raise taxes.
    大家都同意我們需要更多公園。可是錢從哪裡來？市議會必須加稅。
  3. Exams are stressful. But how else can teachers check what students know? I believe projects are a good answer.
    考試讓人壓力很大。可是老師還能用什麼方法知道學生懂多少？我相信做專題是個好方法。
  4. Tourism brings money. But who really gets the money? Usually it's the big hotels, not local people.
    觀光業帶來收入。可是誰真的拿到這些錢？通常是大飯店，不是當地人。

③ What else ... ?：在信裡轉到下一個話題

  1. Use the coin phones, because the others need a card. What else can I tell you? There are cafés at the airport if you're hungry.
    用投幣式的電話，因為其他的要用卡。我還能告訴你什麼呢？如果你餓了，機場有咖啡店。
  2. The hotel is near the beach. What else should you know? Breakfast is from seven to ten.
    飯店在海灘附近。你還應該知道什麼呢？早餐是七點到十點。
  3. Bring warm clothes. What else? Oh yes, don't forget your passport.
    帶保暖的衣服。還有什麼？對了，別忘了帶護照。

④ Why is this important? / Why does this matter?

  1. Most teenagers sleep less than seven hours. Why is this important? Sleep affects how well we learn.
    大部分的青少年睡不到七個小時。這為什麼重要？睡眠會影響我們的學習。
  2. The river is getting dirtier every year. Why does this matter? Thousands of people drink its water.
    這條河一年比一年髒。這為什麼重要？有成千上萬的人喝這條河的水。
  3. Fewer children walk to school now. Why should we worry? Walking keeps them healthy.
    現在走路上學的孩子變少了。我們為什麼要擔心？走路讓他們保持健康。

⑤ How can we ... ?：引出解決的方法

  1. Traffic in the city centre is terrible. How can we solve this problem? We could make public transport free.
    市中心的交通很糟。我們要怎麼解決這個問題？我們可以讓大眾運輸免費。
  2. Some classes have forty students. How can schools help students who are struggling? One answer is smaller classes.
    有些班級有四十個學生。學校要怎麼幫助跟不上的學生？其中一個答案是小班制。
  3. Every year, more old houses in our town disappear. How do we protect old buildings? The first step is to list them.
    我們鎮上每年都有更多老房子消失。我們要怎麼保護老建築？第一步是把它們列冊。

⑥ What is the reason? / What happened next?：說故事

  1. Then the lights went out. What happened next? Nobody knows, because no one saw anything.
    接著燈熄了。接下來發生了什麼事？沒有人知道，因為沒有人看到任何東西。
  2. He never came back to the village. What was the reason? Some people say he was afraid.
    他再也沒回到那個村子。原因是什麼？有些人說他是害怕。
  3. She opened the box. And what did she find inside? A letter from her grandmother.
    她打開了盒子。她在裡面找到什麼？一封她奶奶寫的信。
  4. We waited for hours. And who arrived in the end? The same bus driver as before.
    我們等了好幾個小時。最後到的是誰？還是之前那個公車司機。

⑦ 演講和報告

  1. What do young people really want from a job? Our survey gives a clear answer: freedom.
    年輕人真正想從工作中得到什麼？我們的調查給了明確的答案：自由。
  2. So what did we learn from this project? Above all, we learned to work as a team.
    那我們從這個專案學到了什麼？最重要的是，我們學會了團隊合作。
  3. Why did the company fail? I see two main reasons.
    這家公司為什麼會失敗？我認為有兩個主要原因。
  4. What is the biggest danger for our planet? Many scientists say it is climate change.
    我們的星球最大的危險是什麼？很多科學家說是氣候變遷。
  5. So who should pay for the new hospital? I believe the government should.
    那新醫院應該由誰出錢？我認為應該由政府出。

⑧ 回答前面的疑問

  1. The bike costs a thousand pounds. Why is it worth the money? Because it will last for twenty years.
    這台腳踏車要一千英鎊。為什麼值得花這筆錢？因為它可以騎二十年。
  2. The old plan failed, and the money is almost gone. So where do we go from here? We need a new plan.
    舊計畫失敗了，錢也快用完了。那我們接下來該怎麼走？我們需要一個新的計畫。
  3. Solar panels can power a whole house. And what about the cost? It is lower than most people think.
    太陽能板可以供應整間房子的電。那費用呢？比大部分人想的還低。
