What a pity that you didn't come.

你沒來真是太可惜了。

What a pity 後⁠面⁠接子⁠句，說可⁠惜⁠的事

What a pity that you didn't come.

你結婚那天下雨，真可惜。

that 可⁠以省⁠略

What a pity that you didn't come.

你把相機忘在家裡，真可惜。

What a shame 意⁠思一⁠樣

What a pity that you didn't come.

What a shame the shop has closed.

那家店關了，真可惜。

pity 前⁠面要加 a

What a pity that you didn't come.

What pity that you can't stay.

What a pity that you can't stay.

that 後⁠面⁠接完⁠整⁠的子⁠句

What a pity that you didn't come.

What a pity that missing the train.

What a pity that we missed the train.

子⁠句不⁠倒⁠裝，主⁠詞在動⁠詞前⁠面

What a pity that you didn't come.

What a pity didn't you come.

What a pity you didn't come.

例句

① What a pity that + 過去式

  1. What a pity that you missed the party. It was great.
    你錯過了派對真可惜，派對很好玩。
  2. What a pity that it rained on your wedding day.
    你結婚那天下雨，真可惜。
  3. What a pity that the museum was closed.
    博物館沒開，真可惜。
  4. What a pity that we missed the train.
    我們錯過了火車，真可惜。
  5. What a pity that your brother couldn't join us.
    你哥哥不能跟我們一起來，真可惜。
  6. What a pity that they cancelled the concert.
    他們取消了演唱會，真可惜。

② What a pity + 子句（省略 that）

  1. What a pity you lost the map of the old town.
    你把舊城區的地圖弄丟了，真可惜。
  2. What a pity you left your camera at home.
    你把相機忘在家裡，真可惜。
  3. What a pity you didn't tell me earlier.
    你沒早點告訴我，真可惜。
  4. What a pity we live so far apart.
    我們住得那麼遠，真可惜。
  5. What a pity she didn't get the job.
    她沒得到那份工作，真可惜。

③ What a pity + 現在式 / can't / won't

  1. What a pity that you can't stay longer.
    你不能多待一陣子，真可惜。
  2. What a pity the beach is so crowded today.
    今天海邊人這麼多，真可惜。
  3. What a pity that he won't be here for my birthday.
    他不能來參加我的生日，真可惜。
  4. What a pity you have to work this weekend.
    你這個週末要上班，真可惜。
  5. What a pity the weather isn't better.
    天氣不好一點，真可惜。

④ What a shame (that) ...

  1. What a shame the shop has closed.
    那家店關了，真可惜。
  2. What a shame that you can't come to the wedding.
    你不能來婚禮，真可惜。
  3. What a shame we didn't take more photos.
    我們沒多拍一些照片，真可惜。
  4. What a shame that the holiday is over.
    假期結束了，真可惜。
  5. What a shame he forgot his lines in the play.
    他在話劇裡忘詞了，真可惜。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. What a pity! I really wanted to see you.
    真可惜！我很想見你。
  2. It's a pity that the library closes so early.
    圖書館這麼早關門，真可惜。
  3. What a pity that nobody told us about the sale.
    沒人跟我們說特賣的事，真可惜。
  4. What a pity the cake didn't turn out well.
    蛋糕沒做好，真可惜。
  5. What a pity that they're pulling down the old cinema.
    他們要拆掉那間老電影院，真可惜。
  6. What a pity we only have one day in Rome.
    我們在羅馬只有一天，真可惜。
  7. What a pity that your parents can't meet her.
    你爸媽不能見她，真可惜。
  8. What a pity you weren't there to see it.
    你不在那裡看到，真可惜。
  9. What a shame that the restaurant is full tonight.
    那家餐廳今晚客滿了，真可惜。
