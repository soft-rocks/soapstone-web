There shouldn't be any rubbish dumps; all the rubbish ought to be recycled.

所有的垃圾都應該回收。

主⁠詞 + ought to + be + 形⁠容⁠詞 / 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

ought to、as … as … ought to be

ought to應該。說理想的狀態。
as … as … ought to be跟應該的一樣……。常用在否定句，說現實不夠好。

說現⁠實不⁠夠好

例句

There were three accidents near our school this year. The roads are not as safe as they ought to be.

今年我們學校附近發生了三起車禍。路沒有應該的那麼安全。

not as … as … ought to be 說現實比理想差。

被⁠動⁠的理⁠想

There shouldn't be any rubbish dumps; all the rubbish ought to be recycled.

例句

The dump outside our town is getting bigger. All rubbish ought to be recycled.

我們鎮外的垃圾場越來越大。所有的垃圾都應該被回收。

說東西應該被怎麼處理，用 ought to be 加過去分詞。

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 be

There shouldn't be any rubbish dumps; all the rubbish ought to be recycled.

There are bottles on every bench. Our parks ought to cleaner.

There are bottles on every bench. Our parks ought to be cleaner.

被⁠動用 be 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

There shouldn't be any rubbish dumps; all the rubbish ought to be recycled.

Old phones end up in the bin. They ought to be recycle.

Old phones end up in the bin. They ought to be recycled.

句⁠尾用 ought to be，形⁠容⁠詞不⁠重⁠複

There shouldn't be any rubbish dumps; all the rubbish ought to be recycled.

A single ticket costs four pounds. The bus isn't as cheap as it ought to cheap.

A single ticket costs four pounds. The bus isn't as cheap as it ought to be.

例句

① 主詞 + ought to be + 過去分詞：理想的做法

  1. In my ideal city, there shouldn't be any rubbish dumps. All the rubbish ought to be recycled.
    在我理想的城市裡，不應該有任何垃圾場。所有的垃圾都應該回收。（被動）
  2. Children learn a lot from growing vegetables. Every school ought to be given a garden.
    孩子從種菜裡學到很多。每所學校都應該有一座花園。（被動）
  3. Our town has lost half its old houses in ten years. Old buildings ought to be protected.
    我們鎮上十年內拆掉了一半的老房子。老建築應該受到保護。（被動）
  4. The main square is full of traffic and noise. Cars ought to be banned from the town centre.
    主廣場上都是車子和噪音。市中心應該禁止汽車進入。（被動）
  5. My sister works sixty hours a week as a teacher. Teachers ought to be paid more.
    我姊姊當老師，一個星期工作六十個小時。老師的薪水應該更高。（被動）
  6. We live on an island, but many kids here can't swim. Children ought to be taught how to swim at school.
    我們住在島上，可是這裡很多孩子不會游泳。學校應該教孩子游泳。（被動）

② 主詞 + be + not as + 形容詞 + as + 主詞 + ought to be

  1. Not all zoos are as good as they ought to be, but we know much more about animals today.
    不是所有的動物園都像它們應該的那麼好，可是我們現在對動物了解多得多。
  2. I was late for work three times this week. The trains are not as reliable as they ought to be.
    我這個星期上班遲到了三次。火車沒有應該的那麼準時可靠。
  3. My dad caught an infection after his operation. Our hospital isn't as clean as it ought to be.
    我爸爸開刀後受到感染。我們的醫院沒有應該的那麼乾淨。
  4. Half the street lights in our area are broken. The streets aren't as safe at night as they ought to be.
    我們這區有一半的路燈壞了。晚上街道沒有應該的那麼安全。
  5. Students chat and play music on their phones. The library isn't as quiet as it ought to be.
    學生在圖書館聊天，還用手機放音樂。圖書館沒有應該的那麼安靜。
  6. In many old towns, the water from the tap isn't as fresh as it ought to be.
    在很多老城鎮，自來水沒有應該的那麼新鮮。

③ we ought to + 原形動詞：大家應該追求的事

  1. We ought to take care of our health by going to clean, unpolluted places as often as possible.
    我們應該盡量常去乾淨、沒有污染的地方，照顧自己的健康。
  2. Most parents work long hours and children are alone a lot. We ought to spend more time with our families.
    大部分父母工作時間很長，孩子常常一個人。我們應該多花時間陪家人。
  3. My grandparents lived through two wars. We ought to live in a world without war.
    我爺爺奶奶經歷過兩場戰爭。我們應該活在一個沒有戰爭的世界。
  4. Some farms keep animals in tiny cages. We ought to treat animals with respect.
    有些農場把動物關在很小的籠子裡。我們應該尊重動物。
  5. Most of our fruit comes from the other side of the world. We ought to grow more of our own food.
    我們大部分的水果都來自地球另一邊。我們應該自己種更多食物。
  6. The beaches near us are covered in bottles. We ought to use less plastic.
    我們附近的海灘滿是瓶子。我們應該少用塑膠。

④ 主詞 + ought to + 原形動詞：理想的社會

  1. Hundreds of people sleep on the streets of our city. Everyone ought to have a home.
    我們城市有好幾百人睡在街上。每個人都應該有一個家。
  2. A salad costs more than a burger here. Healthy food ought to cost less than junk food.
    這裡一份沙拉比一個漢堡還貴。健康的食物應該比垃圾食物便宜。
  3. Many bright students can't pay the fees. Every child ought to have the chance to go to university.
    很多聰明的學生付不起學費。每個孩子都應該有機會上大學。
  4. Nurses and cleaners finish work at three in the morning. Public transport ought to run all night.
    護理師和清潔工凌晨三點才下班。大眾運輸應該整夜都有。
  5. My doctor only had five minutes for me. Doctors ought to have more time for each patient.
    我的醫生只有五分鐘給我。醫生應該有更多時間看每一個病人。
  6. Our city is hot and grey in summer. Cities ought to have more trees and parks.
    我們的城市夏天又熱又灰暗。城市應該有更多樹和公園。

⑤ 主詞 + ought to be + 形容詞：說理想的樣子

  1. In my essay about family, I wrote that a home ought to be a place where you feel safe.
    我在關於家庭的作文裡寫道，家應該是一個讓你覺得安全的地方。
  2. We spent our whole trip in queues and traffic. Holidays ought to be relaxing, not stressful.
    我們整趟旅行都在排隊和塞車。假期應該是放鬆的，不是有壓力的。
  3. I left my bank job because I was bored. Work ought to be interesting as well as well paid.
    我辭掉銀行的工作，因為很無聊。工作應該有趣，薪水也要好。
  4. My son's football coach shouts at the kids all the time. Sport ought to be fun for children.
    我兒子的足球教練一直對孩子大吼。運動對孩子來說應該是好玩的。
  5. My daughter never eats the school pizza. School lunches ought to be healthy and tasty.
    我女兒從來不吃學校的披薩。學校午餐應該健康又好吃。
  6. Many children in our city have asthma. The air in our cities ought to be clean.
    我們城市很多孩子有氣喘。我們城市的空氣應該是乾淨的。
  7. My grandfather can't afford his heart medicine. Good medicine ought to be cheap enough for everyone.
    我爺爺買不起他的心臟藥。好的藥應該便宜到每個人都買得起。
