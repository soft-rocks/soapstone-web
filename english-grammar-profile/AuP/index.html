By the time he gets home, he will just want to go straight to bed.

等他到家的時候，他只會想直接上床睡覺。

By the time + 主⁠詞 + 現⁠在簡⁠單式, 主⁠詞 + will + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

By the time he gets home, he will just want to go straight to bed.

by the time到……的時候。後面接一個未來的時間點。
by the time 子句動詞用現在簡單式，不用 will。
will + 原形動詞到了那個時間點的狀態。
will have + 過去分詞到那個時間點之前已經完成的事。

那⁠個時⁠間點⁠的狀⁠態

例句

By the time we arrive, the shops will be closed.

等我們到的時候，店都關了。

那⁠個時⁠間點之⁠前已⁠經完⁠成⁠的事

例句

By the time you read this, I will have left.

等你讀到這封信的時候，我已經走了。

By the time we get there, the film will have started.

例句

① By the time + 現在簡單式, 主詞 + will + 原形動詞

  1. By the time you arrive, the information office at the airport will be closed.
    等你到的時候，機場的服務台已經關了。
  2. By the time the guests come, the food will be cold.
    等客人來的時候，菜都冷了。
  3. By the time she finishes her degree, she'll be thirty.
    等她讀完學位，她就三十歲了。
  4. By the time the bus comes, we'll be soaking wet.
    等公車來的時候，我們已經全身濕透了。
  5. By the time the doctor sees you, you'll probably feel better.
    等醫生看到你的時候，你大概已經覺得好一點了。
  6. By the time we reach the top, it'll be dark.
    等我們爬到山頂，天就黑了。
  7. By the time Dad finds a parking space, the match will be over.
    等爸爸找到停車位，比賽就結束了。
  8. By the time the children grow up, the world will be very different.
    等孩子們長大，世界會變得很不一樣。
  9. By the time I retire, I'll be too old to travel.
    等我退休，我就老得沒辦法旅行了。

② 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + by the time + 現在簡單式

  1. The kids will be asleep by the time we get back.
    等我們回來的時候，孩子們已經睡了。
  2. You'll be exhausted by the time you finish the marathon.
    等你跑完馬拉松，你會累壞了。
  3. The concert will be nearly over by the time we find our seats.
    等我們找到座位，音樂會差不多結束了。
  4. He'll be very hungry by the time lunch is ready.
    等午餐好的時候，他會很餓。
  5. The tickets will be gone by the time the shop opens.
    等店開門的時候，票就沒了。
  6. I'll be a different person by the time I come home from India.
    等我從印度回來，我會變成另一個人。

③ By the time + 現在簡單式, 主詞 + will have + 過去分詞

  1. By the time you read this letter, I will have left the country.
    等你讀到這封信的時候，我已經離開這個國家了。
  2. By the time we get to the cinema, the film will have started.
    等我們到電影院，電影已經開演了。
  3. By the time the plumber turns up, the kitchen floor will have flooded.
    等水電工來的時候，廚房地板已經淹水了。
  4. By the time she gets the job, she'll have spent a year looking.
    等她找到工作，她已經找了一年了。
  5. By the time the builders finish, we'll have paid twice the price.
    等工人完工，我們已經付了兩倍的錢。
  6. By the time I see him again, he'll have forgotten all about it.
    等我再見到他，他已經把這件事忘光了。
  7. By the time spring comes, the birds will have built their nests.
    等春天到的時候，鳥已經築好巢了。
  8. By the time the strike starts at eleven, most flights will have left.
    等罷工十一點開始的時候，大部分的班機已經起飛了。

④ 主詞 + will have + 過去分詞 + by the time + 現在簡單式

  1. The ice cream will have melted by the time we get home.
    等我們到家，冰淇淋已經融化了。
  2. Most of the guests will have gone by the time you arrive.
    等你到的時候，大部分的客人已經走了。
  3. I'll have finished the report by the time you come back from lunch.
    等你吃完午餐回來，我已經寫完報告了。
  4. The shop will have sold all the bread by the time we get there.
    等我們到那裡，那家店的麵包已經賣完了。
  5. She'll have read the whole book by the time the film comes out.
    等電影上映，她已經把整本書讀完了。
  6. We'll have saved enough money by the time the baby is born.
    等寶寶出生，我們已經存夠錢了。
  7. They'll have repaired the road by the time the tourists come back in summer.
    等觀光客夏天回來，他們已經修好路了。
  8. The team will have played forty games by the time the season ends.
    等球季結束，這支隊伍已經打了四十場比賽了。
