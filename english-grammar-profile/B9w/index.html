could 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞提⁠出建⁠議，讓對⁠方決⁠定

We could meet in the nice cafe next to your house and have a coffee.

我們可以約在你家旁邊那間不錯的咖啡館，喝杯咖啡。

對方可以說好，也可以提別的地方。

例句

① We could + 原形動詞

  1. We could book a table at the new Thai restaurant.
    我們可以在那間新開的泰式餐廳訂位。
  2. We could share a taxi to the airport.
    我們可以一起搭計程車去機場。
  3. We could invite your cousins for lunch on Sunday.
    我們星期天可以請你的表哥表姊來吃午餐。
  4. We could rent bikes and ride along the river.
    我們可以租腳踏車，沿著河邊騎。
  5. We could split the bill.
    我們可以平分帳單。
  6. We could practise our presentation together tonight.
    我們今天晚上可以一起練習簡報。
  7. We could paint the kitchen yellow.
    我們可以把廚房漆成黃色。
  8. We could celebrate your birthday at the beach.
    我們可以在海邊幫你慶生。
  9. We could meet halfway, at the station.
    我們可以約在中間的地方，在車站見。
  10. We could order pizza and stay in tonight.
    我們今天晚上可以叫披薩，待在家裡。

② You could + 原形動詞

  1. You could become a member of a gym.
    你可以去健身房辦個會員。
  2. You could ask your teacher for some extra exercises.
    你可以請老師多給你一些練習題。
  3. You could borrow my camera for the trip.
    你旅行的時候可以借我的相機。
  4. You could volunteer at the animal shelter.
    你可以去動物收容所當志工。
  5. You could try the soup. It's really good here.
    你可以試試看湯。這裡的湯很好喝。
  6. You could text her and say sorry.
    你可以傳訊息跟她道歉。
  7. You could sell your old books online.
    你可以把舊書放到網路上賣。
  8. You could learn to swim this summer.
    你今年夏天可以去學游泳。

③ Could + 主詞 + be + 時間或地點？

  1. I need to change the time of our meeting. Could it be at 5.30 pm?
    我得改一下我們開會的時間。可以改到下午五點半嗎？
  2. Could the party be on Saturday instead?
    派對可以改到星期六嗎？
  3. Could the interview be on the phone?
    面試可以改成用電話嗎？
  4. Could our lesson be a bit later today?
    我們今天的課可以晚一點上嗎？
  5. Could the meeting be in my office?
    會議可以改在我的辦公室開嗎？

④ Maybe / Perhaps + 主詞 + could + 原形動詞

  1. Maybe we could go for a walk after lunch.
    也許我們午餐後可以去散個步。
  2. Perhaps you could call the hotel and check.
    也許你可以打電話去飯店確認一下。
  3. Maybe you could take a few days off.
    也許你可以請幾天假。
  4. Perhaps we could discuss this tomorrow.
    也許我們明天可以再談這件事。
  5. Maybe we could leave a little earlier to avoid the traffic.
    也許我們可以早一點出發，避開塞車。
  6. Perhaps you could try a different route.
    也許你可以換條路走。
  7. Maybe we could cook dinner together this weekend.
    也許我們這個週末可以一起煮晚餐。
