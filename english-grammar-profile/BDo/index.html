子⁠句 + , but + 子⁠句

It is very small, but it is very easy to clean.

它很小，但打掃起來很容易。

形⁠容⁠詞 + but + 形⁠容⁠詞

It is very small, but it is very easy to clean.

My home is small but so comfortable.

我家很小，但是非常舒服。

出⁠乎⁠意⁠料⁠的對⁠比和一⁠般⁠的對⁠比

It is very small, but it is very easy to clean.

出乎意料

He is very rich, but he is not happy.

有錢通常讓人以為他很快樂。
一般的對比

I like tea, but my sister likes coffee.

兩件事不一樣，沒有預期。

沒⁠有對⁠比用 and

Our hotel was near the beach, but it had a lovely pool.

Our hotel was near the beach, and it had a lovely pool.

although 和 but 只⁠用一⁠個

Although the flat is small, it is very comfortable.

but 後⁠面是相⁠反⁠的結⁠果

It is very small, but it is very easy to clean.

It was raining hard, but we went for a walk.

but 放⁠在兩⁠個子⁠句中⁠間

The car is old, but it still runs well.

例句

① 缺點 + but + 優點

  1. I really like my home. It is very small, but it is very easy to clean.
    我真的很喜歡我家。它很小，但打掃起來很容易。
  2. The village is far from the city, but the air is really clean.
    那個村子離城市很遠，但是空氣很乾淨。
  3. The kitchen is tiny, but we cook big meals in it every weekend.
    廚房很小，但是我們每個週末都在裡面煮一大桌菜。
  4. The old bike is heavy, but it never breaks down.
    那台舊腳踏車很重，但是從來沒壞過。
  5. Our flat has no garden, but the park is just across the road.
    我們的公寓沒有花園，但是公園就在對面。

② 優點 + but + 缺點

  1. The restaurant is very popular, but the food is not very good.
    那家餐廳很受歡迎，但是東西不太好吃。
  2. The new phone looks great, but the battery doesn't last long.
    新手機看起來很棒，但是電池不耐用。
  3. My neighbours are friendly, but they are very noisy at night.
    我的鄰居很友善，但是晚上很吵。
  4. The city is beautiful, but it is very expensive to live there.
    那個城市很漂亮，但是住在那裡很貴。

③ 形容詞 + but + 形容詞

  1. My home is small but so comfortable.
    我家很小，但是非常舒服。
  2. The test was short but really difficult.
    考試很短，但是很難。
  3. My grandfather is old but very active.
    我爺爺年紀大了，但是很有活力。
  4. The path was narrow but safe.
    那條小路很窄，但是很安全。

④ 努力 + but + 沒有預期的結果

  1. I studied all night, but I still failed the exam.
    我念了一整晚，但是考試還是沒過。
  2. We ran to the station, but we missed the train.
    我們跑去車站，但是還是錯過了火車。
  3. She looked everywhere, but she couldn't find her keys.
    她到處都找了，但是找不到鑰匙。
  4. I followed the recipe carefully, but the cake didn't rise.
    我很仔細地照著食譜做，但是蛋糕沒有發起來。

⑤ 情況 + but + 相反的行動

  1. It was very cold, but the children played outside all day.
    天氣很冷，但是孩子們在外面玩了一整天。
  2. He was very tired, but he finished his homework.
    他很累，但是他還是寫完了功課。
  3. The tickets were expensive, but we bought them anyway.
    票很貴，但是我們還是買了。
  4. My brother hates vegetables, but he ate all his salad.
    我弟弟討厭蔬菜，但是他把沙拉全吃完了。

⑥ 身分 + but + 出乎意料的事

  1. He is very rich, but he is not happy.
    他很有錢，但是不快樂。
  2. My aunt is a chef, but she never cooks at home.
    我阿姨是廚師，但是她在家從來不煮飯。
  3. Tom lives next to the sea, but he can't swim.
    Tom 住在海邊，但是他不會游泳。
  4. My cousin is only twelve, but he speaks four languages.
    我表弟才十二歲，但是他會說四種語言。

⑦ but + still、also

  1. The hotel was cheap, but it was still very clean.
    飯店很便宜，但是還是很乾淨。
  2. I've lived here for ten years, but I still get lost.
    我住在這裡十年了，但是還是會迷路。
  3. The town is quiet, but it also has some good bars.
    那個小鎮很安靜，但是也有幾家不錯的酒吧。
  4. The exercise looks easy, but it is actually quite hard.
    這個運動看起來很簡單，但是其實滿難的。
  5. My car is twenty years old, but it still runs perfectly.
    我的車二十年了，但是還是跑得很好。
