There is always something you can improve, isn't there?

總是有可以改進的地方，不是嗎？

there 開⁠頭⁠的直⁠述⁠句，附⁠加問⁠句也用 there

否⁠定⁠的直⁠述⁠句接肯⁠定⁠的 is there

There is always something you can improve, isn't there?

例句

① There is / There are + 否定的附加問句

  1. Don't give up after one bad game. There is always something you can improve, isn't there?
    別因為一場比賽打不好就放棄。總是有可以改進的地方，不是嗎？
  2. I need some cash. There's a bank near here, isn't there?
    我需要一點現金。這附近有銀行，對吧？
  3. Let's eat out tonight. There are lots of good restaurants in this street, aren't there?
    我們今晚去外面吃吧。這條街上有很多好餐廳，對吧？
  4. There's a train at six, isn't there? We can still catch it.
    六點有一班火車，對吧？我們還趕得上。
  5. I can't go to the gym at three. There's a meeting this afternoon, isn't there?
    我三點不能去健身房。今天下午要開會，對吧？
  6. My brother could stay with us. There are two bedrooms in the flat, aren't there?
    我哥哥可以跟我們住。那間公寓有兩個房間，對吧？

② There isn't / There aren't + 肯定的附加問句

  1. Our time zones are close. There isn't much of a difference, is there?
    我們的時區很接近。沒有什麼差別，對吧？
  2. The sign says the show is sold out. There aren't any tickets left, are there?
    告示上寫表演賣完了。票一張都不剩了，對吧？
  3. We'll need to bring food. There isn't a supermarket in the village, is there?
    我們得自己帶吃的。村子裡沒有超市，對吧？
  4. There isn't time for coffee, is there? The film starts at seven.
    沒時間喝咖啡了，對吧？電影七點開始。
  5. We can sit anywhere. There aren't many people here tonight, are there?
    我們坐哪裡都行。今晚這裡人不多，對吧？

③ There was / There were

  1. The reports didn't come out. There was a problem with the printer, wasn't there?
    報告沒印出來。印表機有點問題，對吧？
  2. We couldn't see the stage at all. There were a lot of people at the concert, weren't there?
    我們完全看不到舞台。演唱會有很多人，對吧？
  3. You came back with the parcel. There wasn't anyone at home, was there?
    你把包裹帶回來了。家裡沒有人，對吧？
  4. You walked home last night. There weren't any buses after midnight, were there?
    你昨晚走路回家。半夜以後沒有公車，對吧？
  5. There was a fire in the old school last year, wasn't there?
    去年那所舊學校發生過火災，對吧？

④ There will be / There won't be

  1. There'll be time for lunch before the meeting, won't there?
    開會前會有時間吃午餐，對吧？
  2. I won't eat before I go. There'll be food at the party, won't there?
    我出門前不吃了。派對上會有吃的，對吧？
  3. It's six in the morning. There won't be any traffic at this time, will there?
    現在是早上六點。這個時間不會塞車，對吧？
  4. I've sent all the forms. There won't be a problem with the visa, will there?
    表格我都寄了。簽證不會有問題，對吧？

⑤ There has been / There have been

  1. There's been an accident on the motorway, hasn't there? The traffic isn't moving.
    高速公路上發生了車禍，對吧？車都動不了。
  2. There have been a lot of changes at work, haven't there?
    公司最近有很多改變，對吧？
  3. The garden looks so dry. There hasn't been any rain for weeks, has there?
    院子看起來好乾。好幾個星期沒下雨了，對吧？

⑥ no / nothing / little + 肯定的附加問句

  1. The last train has gone. There's nothing we can do now, is there?
    最後一班火車已經開走了。我們現在什麼也做不了，對吧？
  2. There's no milk left, is there? I'll go and buy some.
    牛奶沒了，對吧？我去買一點。
  3. There's no need to hurry, is there? We've got plenty of time.
    不用急，對吧？我們時間還很多。
  4. There's little chance of rain today, is there?
    今天不太可能下雨，對吧？
  5. Nobody answered when you rang. There was nobody in the office, was there?
    你打去的時候沒人接。辦公室裡沒有人，對吧？

⑦ 請對方同意

  1. There's nothing better than a hot bath after a long day, is there?
    忙了一整天之後，沒有什麼比泡熱水澡更好的了，對吧？
  2. Look at the house on the corner. There's something odd about it, isn't there?
    你看轉角那間房子。它有點怪怪的，對吧？
  3. There are worse places to live, aren't there?
    比這裡更糟的地方多得是，對吧？
