例句

① 環境和氣候

  1. Our planet is in danger. The global temperature is rising, the ice is melting, and miles and miles of coast are being submerged.
    我們的地球有危險。全球氣溫不斷上升，冰正在融化，綿延好幾英里的海岸正被海水淹沒。
  2. Every year, huge areas of rainforest are being destroyed to make room for farms.
    每年都有大片雨林被毀掉，用來開闢農地。
  3. Our rivers are being polluted by waste from factories.
    我們的河流正被工廠的廢水汙染。
  4. Look at the trees on the hill. They're being cut down for a new road.
    你看山上的樹。它們被砍掉要蓋新路。
  5. In many countries, old cars are being replaced by electric ones.
    在很多國家，舊車正在被電動車取代。

② 公司和工作

  1. This year's topic is the online training for employees, which is being relaunched.
    今年的主題是員工的線上訓練，這個訓練正在重新推出。
  2. Our computer system is being updated this week, so please save your work.
    我們的電腦系統這個星期在更新，所以請存好你的檔案。
  3. The company is doing well. New staff are being hired every month.
    公司經營得不錯。每個月都在招新員工。
  4. The new plan is being discussed at the moment. We'll know the result on Friday.
    新計畫現在正在討論。我們星期五會知道結果。
  5. Thank you for your email. Your question is being dealt with by our manager.
    謝謝你的來信。你的問題我們經理正在處理。

③ 科技和社會

  1. First, academic knowledge was linked through computers. Now people are being linked through billions of personal computers.
    一開始是學術知識透過電腦連在一起。現在人們正透過幾十億台個人電腦連在一起。
  2. These days, more and more letters are being replaced by emails.
    現在有愈來愈多信件被電子郵件取代。
  3. Small shops in our town are being closed because everyone buys online.
    我們鎮上的小店一間一間關門，因為大家都在網路上買東西。
  4. Children are being given phones at a younger and younger age.
    孩子拿到手機的年紀愈來愈小。
  5. A lot of new homes are being built outside the city.
    城外正在蓋很多新房子。

④ 正在處理的事

  1. Don't worry about your car. It's being looked at by our best mechanic.
    別擔心你的車。我們最好的技師正在看。
  2. The accident is being investigated by the police.
    警察正在調查這場車禍。
  3. Please wait here. Your room is being prepared.
    請在這裡等。你的房間正在準備。
  4. My grandmother is in hospital. She's being treated for a bad cough.
    我奶奶在住院。她因為咳得很厲害在接受治療。
  5. "Where's your bike?" "It's being fixed at the shop."
    你的腳踏車呢？在店裡修。

⑤ 學校

  1. Our school is being extended. There will be a new science building next year.
    我們學校正在擴建。明年會有一棟新的科學大樓。
  2. The exam papers are being marked now, so the results will be out soon.
    考卷現在正在改，所以成績很快就會出來。
  3. The children are being taught to swim this term.
    孩子們這學期在學游泳。
  4. A new kind of maths is being tried in some schools.
    有些學校正在試一種新的數學教法。

⑥ 人當主詞

  1. I can't talk now. I'm being called into a meeting.
    我現在不能講。有人叫我去開會。
  2. I think I'm being followed. There's a man behind me everywhere I go.
    我覺得有人在跟蹤我。我走到哪裡都有一個男人在我後面。
  3. Turn on the TV! The players are being interviewed after the match.
    快開電視！球員賽後正在接受訪問。
  4. You're being asked to help because you know the area well.
    請你幫忙是因為你很熟這一帶。

⑦ 問句

  1. "What's happening to the old cinema?" "It's being turned into flats."
    那間舊電影院怎麼了？正在改成公寓。
  2. "Is anything being done about the noise?" "Yes, the council is looking into it."
    噪音的事有人在處理嗎？有，市議會在調查。
  3. "Why is the road closed?" "A new pipe is being put in."
    路為什麼封起來？正在埋新的水管。
