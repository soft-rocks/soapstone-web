I learned to use my phone very quickly and easily.

我很快、很輕鬆就學會用我的手機了。

and 連⁠接兩⁠個情⁠狀副⁠詞，兩⁠個都修⁠飾同⁠一⁠個動⁠詞

我很快、很輕鬆就學會用我的手機了。

⁠-⁠ly 的拼⁠法看形⁠容⁠詞⁠的結⁠尾

I learned to use my phone very quickly and easily.

一般形容詞slow → slowly、careful → carefully
icautomatic → automatically

easy 的 y 改⁠成 i 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ly

I learned to use my phone very quickly and easily.

She passed the test easyly.

She passed the test easily.

gentle 去⁠掉 e，加 y

Hold the baby gentlely.

Hold the baby gently.

兩⁠個情⁠狀副⁠詞中⁠間加 and

I learned to use my phone very quickly and easily.

He spoke slowly clearly.

He spoke slowly and clearly.

and 後⁠面⁠的字也⁠要用副⁠詞

I learned to use my phone very quickly and easily.

The children played happily and quiet.

The children played happily and quietly.

friendly 是形⁠容⁠詞，修⁠飾動⁠詞用 in a friendly way

她友善地笑了笑。

lovely、lonely 也是形容詞。

例句

① 一般形容詞 + -ly

  1. The old man walked slowly to the bus stop.
    那個老先生慢慢走到公車站。
  2. The students left the library quietly.
    學生們安靜地離開圖書館。
  3. She checked her answers carefully.
    她仔細檢查了她的答案。
  4. The baby is sleeping peacefully.
    寶寶睡得很安穩。
  5. The receptionist greeted every guest politely.
    櫃檯人員很有禮貌地招呼每位客人。

② 子音 + y 結尾 → -ily

  1. We found the hotel easily.
    我們很輕鬆就找到飯店了。
  2. The kids laughed happily.
    孩子們開心地笑了。
  3. She shouted angrily at the driver.
    她生氣地對司機大吼。
  4. He got dressed hurriedly and ran out.
    他匆匆忙忙穿好衣服就跑出去了。
  5. The dog wagged its tail lazily.
    那隻狗懶洋洋地搖著尾巴。

③ 子音 + le 結尾 → -ly

  1. Put the plates down gently.
    把盤子輕輕放下。
  2. She explained the rules simply.
    她簡單地說明了規則。
  3. I played terribly in the last game.
    我上一場打得很爛。
  4. The cat jumped nimbly onto the wall.
    那隻貓輕巧地跳上牆。

④ ic 結尾 → -ally

  1. The doors open automatically.
    門會自動打開。
  2. He spoke enthusiastically about his new job.
    他熱情地談起他的新工作。
  3. The lights turn off automatically at midnight.
    燈到午夜會自動關掉。

⑤ very + 情狀副詞

  1. My grandmother crosses the road very carefully.
    我奶奶過馬路很小心。
  2. The ice cream melted very quickly in the sun.
    冰淇淋在太陽底下融化得很快。
  3. She plays the guitar very nicely.
    她吉他彈得很好聽。
  4. He talks very loudly on the phone.
    他講電話很大聲。

⑥ 情狀副詞 + and + 情狀副詞

  1. Read the questions slowly and carefully.
    慢慢地、仔細地讀題目。
  2. They worked quickly and quietly.
    他們做得又快又安靜。
  3. She answered calmly and politely.
    她冷靜又有禮貌地回答。
  4. The team played well and fairly.
    這支球隊打得好，也打得公平。
  5. He fixed my bike quickly and cheaply.
    他很快就修好我的腳踏車，也收得很便宜。

⑦ 疑問句和否定句

  1. Did you sleep peacefully last night?
    你昨晚睡得安穩嗎？
  2. He didn't explain it very clearly.
    他沒有解釋得很清楚。
  3. Can you write a bit more neatly?
    你可以寫整齊一點嗎？
  4. Why did she leave so suddenly?
    她為什麼突然就走了？
  5. They didn't treat us fairly.
    他們對我們不公平。

⑧ in a friendly way

  1. The waiter talked to us in a friendly way.
    服務生很友善地跟我們說話。
  2. The nurse spoke to my mum in a kind and friendly way.
    護士親切又友善地跟我媽媽說話。
