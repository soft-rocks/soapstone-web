例句

① Do you know where ...

  1. Excuse me, do you know where the station is?
    不好意思，你知道車站在哪裡嗎？
  2. I can't find my glasses. Do you know where I put them?
    我找不到我的眼鏡。你知道我放在哪裡嗎？
  3. Do you know where Tom lives? I have his birthday card.
    你知道 Tom 住在哪裡嗎？我有他的生日卡。
  4. We're lost. Do you know where we are on this map?
    我們迷路了。你知道我們在地圖上的哪裡嗎？
  5. I need some cash. Do you know where the nearest bank is?
    我需要現金。你知道最近的銀行在哪裡嗎？

② Do you know what / which ...

  1. I've got lots of presents this year. Do you know which present I like best?
    我今年收到很多禮物。你知道我最喜歡哪一個禮物嗎？
  2. We need to eat first. Do you know what time the film starts?
    我們得先吃飯。你知道電影幾點開始嗎？
  3. Do you know what this word means? I can't find it in my dictionary.
    你知道這個字是什麼意思嗎？我在字典裡找不到。
  4. A taxi is too expensive. Do you know which bus goes to the airport?
    計程車太貴了。你知道哪一班公車到機場嗎？
  5. I have no ideas for a gift. Do you know what Anna wants for her birthday?
    我想不到要送什麼。你知道 Anna 生日想要什麼嗎？
  6. There are two keys here. Do you know which one opens the back door?
    這裡有兩把鑰匙。你知道哪一把是開後門的嗎？

③ Do you know how ...

  1. Do you know how much this jacket costs? There's no price on it.
    你知道這件外套多少錢嗎？上面沒有標價。
  2. She looks so young. Do you know how old your grandmother is?
    她看起來好年輕。你知道你奶奶幾歲嗎？
  3. I'm ordering the T-shirts. Do you know how many students are in your class?
    我在訂 T 恤。你知道你們班有幾個學生嗎？
  4. My phone is new. Do you know how this camera works?
    我的手機是新的。你知道這個相機怎麼用嗎？
  5. I want to arrive before lunch. Do you know how long the journey takes?
    我想在午餐前到。你知道這趟路要多久嗎？

④ Do you know why / when / who ...

  1. The shop is closed. Do you know why it's closed today?
    店關著。你知道它今天為什麼沒開嗎？
  2. We just missed it. Do you know when the next train leaves?
    我們剛好錯過了。你知道下一班火車什麼時候開嗎？
  3. Do you know who that man is? He keeps looking at us.
    你知道那個男人是誰嗎？他一直看我們。
  4. He slammed the door. Do you know why Mark is so angry?
    他甩門了。你知道 Mark 為什麼那麼生氣嗎？
  5. I need to talk to them. Do you know when your parents get back?
    我得跟他們談談。你知道你爸媽什麼時候回來嗎？
  6. Someone left this on the bus. Do you know whose bag this is?
    有人把這個留在公車上。你知道這是誰的包包嗎？

⑤ Do you know + 疑問詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. I'll send you a map. Do you know how to get to my house?
    我會寄地圖給你。你知道怎麼去我家嗎？
  2. The kids want breakfast. Do you know how to make pancakes?
    孩子們要吃早餐。你會做鬆餅嗎？
  3. I've got a free afternoon. Do you know what to do in this town?
    我下午有空。你知道這個小鎮有什麼好玩的嗎？
  4. I really want to go. Do you know where to buy tickets for the concert?
    我好想去。你知道演唱會的票要去哪裡買嗎？
  5. I left my bag on the train. Do you know who to ask about it?
    我把包包忘在火車上了。你知道要去問誰嗎？

⑥ 問陌生人

  1. Excuse me, do you know which platform the next train leaves from?
    不好意思，你知道下一班火車從哪個月台開嗎？
  2. Sorry to bother you. Do you know where the toilets are?
    不好意思打擾了。你知道廁所在哪裡嗎？
  3. Hello, do you know what time the museum closes?
    你好，你知道博物館幾點關門嗎？
