I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

我得跟你說，我還沒決定。

still 加否⁠定⁠句，強⁠調到現⁠在還⁠沒⁠發⁠生

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

我得跟你說，我還沒決定。

朋友等答案已經等了一個月。

正⁠式⁠的文⁠字寫完⁠整形⁠式，still 的位⁠置不⁠變

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

那家公司到現在還沒付錢給我們。

疑⁠問⁠句⁠的 still not 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

你到現在還沒決定嗎？

still 放⁠在 haven't 前⁠面

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

I haven't still decided.

I still haven't decided.

做完⁠了用 already，不⁠用 still

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

I have still decided.

I have already decided.

hasn't 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

My brother still hasn't wrote back.

My brother still hasn't written back.

yet 只⁠說還⁠沒⁠發⁠生，still 強⁠調等太久

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

我還沒決定。

still 加現⁠在簡⁠單⁠式，說狀⁠態還⁠在持⁠續

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

我還跟爸媽住在一起。

already 說比預⁠期早做⁠完

I have to tell you that I still haven't decided.

我已經訂好票了。

例句

① 主詞 + still + haven't + 過去分詞

  1. I still haven't finished my essay.
    我的報告到現在還沒寫完。
  2. We still haven't found a flat.
    我們到現在還沒找到房子。
  3. They still haven't fixed the road outside our house.
    我們家外面的路他們到現在還沒修好。
  4. You still haven't answered my question.
    你到現在還沒回答我的問題。
  5. I still haven't read the book you lent me.
    你借我的書我到現在還沒看。
  6. My parents still haven't met my girlfriend.
    我爸媽到現在還沒見過我女朋友。
  7. We still haven't heard from the bank.
    銀行到現在還沒有消息。

② 主詞 + still + hasn't + 過去分詞

  1. My order still hasn't arrived.
    我訂的東西到現在還沒到。
  2. He still hasn't paid me back.
    他到現在還沒還我錢。
  3. The bus still hasn't come.
    公車到現在還沒來。
  4. She still hasn't replied to my message.
    她到現在還沒回我訊息。
  5. The landlord still hasn't fixed the shower.
    房東到現在還沒修好淋浴間。
  6. My brother still hasn't learned to drive.
    我哥到現在還不會開車。
  7. The plumber still hasn't turned up.
    水電工到現在還沒出現。

③ 正式的完整形式：still + have not / has not

  1. The council still has not repaired the bridge.
    議會到現在還沒修好那座橋。
  2. We still have not received your reply.
    我們到現在還沒收到您的回覆。
  3. The airline still has not refunded my ticket.
    航空公司到現在還沒退我的機票錢。
  4. The police still have not found the missing boy.
    警方到現在還沒找到那個失蹤的男孩。
  5. The school still has not explained the decision.
    學校到現在還沒解釋這個決定。

④ 等了一段時間

  1. It's been three weeks and I still haven't got my results.
    已經三個禮拜了，我到現在還沒拿到成績。
  2. We ordered an hour ago, and our food still hasn't come.
    我們一個小時前就點了，菜到現在還沒來。
  3. I sent the form in May, and they still haven't answered.
    我五月就把表格寄出去了，他們到現在還沒回覆。
  4. She moved here two years ago, but she still hasn't made any friends.
    她兩年前搬來這裡，可是到現在還沒交到朋友。
  5. He promised to call, but he still hasn't.
    他答應要打電話，可是到現在還沒打。

⑤ 疑問句：Have / Has + 主詞 + still not + 過去分詞

  1. Have they still not sent the invoice?
    他們到現在還沒寄發票嗎？
  2. Has he still not called you?
    他到現在還沒打給你嗎？
  3. Have the builders still not finished?
    工人到現在還沒做完嗎？

⑥ 說話者覺得太久了

  1. Honestly, I still haven't got used to the cold here.
    說真的，我到現在還不習慣這裡的冷。
  2. My son still hasn't tidied his room, even after I asked twice.
    我叫了兩次，我兒子到現在還沒整理房間。
  3. Sorry, I still haven't had time to look at your photos.
    抱歉，我到現在還沒空看你的照片。
  4. My boss still hasn't signed the contract.
    我老闆到現在還沒簽合約。
