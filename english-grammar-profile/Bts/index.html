I asked when it would be on TV.

我問它什麼時候會在電視上播。

asked + wh 詞 + 子⁠句，轉⁠述 wh 問⁠句

我問它什麼時候會在電視上播。

問⁠句⁠的語⁠序改⁠成陳⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序，will 改⁠成 would

I asked when it would be on TV.

原本的問題

"When will it be on TV?"

「什麼時候會在電視上播？」
轉述

I asked when it would be on TV.

我問它什麼時候會在電視上播。

主⁠詞放⁠回動⁠詞前⁠面

I asked when it would be on TV.

be 動詞

He asked where the station was.

他問車站在哪裡。
助動詞

I asked what we could play.

我問我們可以玩什麼。
do / does / did

She asked what time the film started.

她問電影幾點開始。

時⁠態後⁠移，代⁠名⁠詞以轉⁠述⁠的人⁠為準

I asked when it would be on TV.

you → I

She asked me where I lived.

她問我住在哪裡。
you → she

I asked her why she was here.

我問她為什麼在這裡。

wh 詞是主⁠詞，語⁠序不⁠變，只換時⁠態

who

He asked who had sent it.

他問這是誰寄的。
what

They asked what had happened.

他們問發生了什麼事。

wanted to know 也⁠可⁠以轉⁠述問⁠題

I asked when it would be on TV.

那位客人想知道多少錢。

時⁠態不⁠後移：引⁠述動⁠詞是現⁠在⁠式，或問⁠的事還成⁠立

現在式

She's asking when the shop opens.

她在問那家店什麼時候開。
還成立

He asked me what my plans are for next year.

他問我明年有什麼計畫。

子⁠句用陳⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序

I asked when it would be on TV.

He asked me where was the bank.

He asked me where the bank was.

子⁠句不⁠用 did，動⁠詞改⁠成過⁠去⁠式

My mum asked what did I want for dinner.

My mum asked what I wanted for dinner.

間⁠接引⁠述⁠的句⁠尾用句⁠號

I asked when it would be on TV.

The teacher asked how old I was?

The teacher asked how old I was.

原話⁠的 your 指⁠的⁠是⁠我，換⁠成 my

The man asked me what your name was.

The man asked me what my name was.

被問⁠的人直⁠接放⁠在 asked 後⁠面

I asked when it would be on TV.

She asked to me what time it was.

She asked me what time it was.

直⁠接引⁠述保⁠留問⁠句⁠的語⁠序和問⁠號

「什麼時候會在電視上播？」我問。

wh 詞加子⁠句是問⁠題，to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞是請⁠求

問題

I asked him where he worked.

我問他在哪裡工作。
請求

I asked him to work late.

我請他加班。

例句

① what

  1. She asked me what I did for a living.
    她問我是做什麼工作的。
  2. I called the gym and asked what classes they offered.
    我打電話到健身房，問他們有哪些課。
  3. The doctor asked what I had eaten that morning.
    醫生問我那天早上吃了什麼。
  4. My friend asked what I was going to wear to the party.
    我朋友問我要穿什麼去派對。

② where

  1. A tourist asked us where the nearest cash machine was.
    一個觀光客問我們最近的提款機在哪裡。
  2. He asked a man where the changing rooms were.
    他問一個男人更衣室在哪裡。
  3. The police asked where we had been that night.
    警察問我們那天晚上去了哪裡。
  4. My grandmother asked where I had bought my shoes.
    我奶奶問我鞋子在哪裡買的。

③ when / what time

  1. I asked the receptionist what time breakfast was served.
    我問櫃台人員早餐幾點供應。
  2. They asked when the next bus would come.
    他們問下一班公車什麼時候來。
  3. She asked me when I had started learning the guitar.
    她問我什麼時候開始學吉他的。
  4. Our neighbour asked when we were moving out.
    我們的鄰居問我們什麼時候搬走。

④ why

  1. We asked the film crew why they were filming our school.
    我們問攝影團隊為什麼在拍我們學校。
  2. The teacher asked why I hadn't done my homework.
    老師問我為什麼沒寫作業。
  3. He asked me why I looked so worried.
    他問我為什麼看起來那麼擔心。
  4. Mum asked why the kitchen was such a mess.
    媽媽問廚房為什麼那麼亂。

⑤ how / how much / how long

  1. I asked the shop assistant how much the jacket was.
    我問店員那件外套多少錢。
  2. She asked how long the journey would take.
    她問路程要多久。
  3. My cousin asked how I had found the flat.
    我表哥問我是怎麼找到那間公寓的。
  4. The interviewer asked how many languages I spoke.
    面試官問我會說幾種語言。

⑥ who / which

  1. Dad asked who had left the gate open.
    爸爸問是誰沒關大門。
  2. I asked her who she was waiting for.
    我問她在等誰。
  3. The waiter asked which wine we would like.
    服務生問我們想要哪一種酒。
  4. She asked which bus went to the airport.
    她問哪一班公車到機場。
  5. My boss asked who would be in charge while she was away.
    我老闆問她不在的時候誰負責。

⑦ wanted to know

  1. The police officer wanted to know where I was going.
    警察想知道我要去哪裡。
  2. My parents wanted to know who I had been talking to.
    我爸媽想知道我在跟誰講話。
  3. The customer wanted to know why the price had gone up.
    那位客人想知道價錢為什麼漲了。

⑧ 時態不後移

  1. The kids keep asking when we're going to arrive.
    孩子們一直問我們什麼時候會到。
  2. My boss asked me yesterday what my plans are for the summer.
    我老闆昨天問我今年夏天有什麼計畫。
  3. I've just asked how long the delay will be.
    我剛剛問了會延誤多久。
