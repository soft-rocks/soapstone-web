Jack, though disappointed, respects the result.

Jack 雖然很失望，還是尊重這個結果。

though 後⁠面直⁠接接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，省⁠略了主⁠詞和 be

Jack 雖然很失望，還是尊重這個結果。

though disappointed 等於 though he is disappointed。

子⁠句可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

Jack, though disappointed, respects the result.

句首

Although tired, she kept working until midnight.

雖然很累，她還是工作到半夜。
主詞後面

My grandfather, though ninety, still walks to the market every day.

我爺爺雖然九十歲了，還是每天走路去市場。

although 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Jack, though disappointed, respects the result.

動詞-ing

Although living abroad, he follows the news from home every day.

雖然住在國外，他每天都關注家鄉的新聞。

省⁠略主⁠詞和 be 的 although 子⁠句不⁠直⁠接接名⁠詞

Jack, though disappointed, respects the result.

Although a busy week, I finished everything.

Although busy all week, I finished everything.

省⁠略⁠的主⁠詞要⁠是主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

Jack, though disappointed, respects the result.

Though very expensive, I bought the coat.

Though very expensive, the coat was worth it.

很貴的是外套，不是 I。

一⁠句⁠話只⁠用 though 或 but 其⁠中一⁠個

Though nervous, but he gave a great speech.

Though nervous, he gave a great speech.

例句

① Although + 形容詞

  1. Although small, the flat has everything we need.
    這間公寓雖然小，卻有我們需要的一切。
  2. Although nervous, she answered every question clearly.
    雖然很緊張，她每個問題都回答得很清楚。
  3. Although expensive, the course was worth every penny.
    這門課雖然很貴，但每一分錢都花得值得。
  4. Although busy with her own work, she always helps her colleagues.
    雖然自己的工作很忙，她總是會幫同事。
  5. Although popular with tourists, the island is still very quiet.
    這座島雖然很受觀光客歡迎，還是很安靜。
  6. Although useful, the app uses a lot of battery.
    這個應用程式雖然好用，卻很耗電。

② Although + 過去分詞

  1. Although married, they live in different cities.
    雖然結婚了，他們住在不同的城市。
  2. Although committed to his studies, he plays football every weekend.
    他雖然很專心念書，每個週末還是會去踢足球。
  3. Although injured, the player finished the match.
    雖然受了傷，那位球員還是打完了比賽。
  4. Although surprised by the news, he stayed calm.
    雖然對那個消息很驚訝，他還是保持冷靜。
  5. Although trained as a lawyer, she works as a chef.
    她雖然受過律師的訓練，卻在當廚師。

③ 主詞, + though + 形容詞 / 過去分詞

  1. The hotel, though old, was very comfortable.
    那家飯店雖然舊，卻很舒服。
  2. My brother, though annoyed, agreed to help.
    我哥哥雖然很不爽，還是答應幫忙。
  3. The film, though long, never got boring.
    那部電影雖然很長，卻從不無聊。
  4. Her answer, though short, was very clear.
    她的回答雖然很短，卻很清楚。
  5. The town, though tiny, has three good restaurants.
    那個小鎮雖然很小，卻有三家好餐廳。
  6. The kids, though exhausted, didn't want to go to bed.
    孩子們雖然累壞了，卻不想上床睡覺。

④ although / though + 動詞-ing 或介系詞片語

  1. Although living in the city, she grows her own vegetables.
    雖然住在城市，她自己種菜。
  2. Though working full-time, he still finds time to paint.
    雖然全職工作，他還是有時間畫畫。
  3. Although in pain, the runner kept going.
    雖然很痛，那位跑者還是繼續跑。
  4. Though knowing the risks, they decided to go ahead.
    雖然知道風險，他們還是決定繼續。
  5. Although under pressure, the team stayed calm.
    雖然壓力很大，團隊還是保持冷靜。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Though thrifty, my aunt always buys good shoes.
    我阿姨雖然節儉，卻總是買好鞋子。
  2. Although tired after the flight, we went straight to the beach.
    雖然搭完飛機很累，我們還是直接去了海邊。
  3. The plan, though simple, worked perfectly.
    那個計畫雖然簡單，卻非常成功。
  4. Although unsure of the way, he refused to ask for directions.
    雖然不確定怎麼走，他還是不肯問路。
  5. The room, though tidy, smelled of smoke.
    那個房間雖然很整齊，卻有菸味。
  6. Although retired, my father still goes to the office twice a week.
    雖然退休了，我爸爸每個禮拜還是會去辦公室兩次。
  7. Though hungry, she waited for everyone to sit down.
    雖然很餓，她還是等大家都坐下才吃。
  8. Although disappointed with the result, the coach praised his players.
    雖然對結果很失望，教練還是稱讚了他的球員。
