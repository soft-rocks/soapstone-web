We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.

我們的業務總監星期四早上會來拜訪我們。

are being 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，再⁠加未⁠來⁠的時⁠間

我們的業務總監星期四早上會來拜訪我們。

時⁠間詞決⁠定說現⁠在還⁠是未⁠來

We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.

現在

The kitchen is being painted at the moment.

廚房現在正在油漆。
未來

The kitchen is being painted next week.

廚房下星期要油漆。

常⁠用⁠的動⁠詞：hold、deliver、visit

We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.

hold

The meeting is being held in Room 5 tomorrow.

會議明天在五號教室開。
deliver

The new sofa is being delivered on Friday.

新沙發星期五會送來。
visit

We are being visited by the director tomorrow.

總監明天要來拜訪我們。

用 is being，不⁠用 is been

We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.

The meeting is been held in Room 5 tomorrow.

The meeting is being held in Room 5 tomorrow.

being 前⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.

The new sofa being delivered on Friday.

The new sofa is being delivered on Friday.

being 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.

We are being visiting by the director tomorrow.

We are being visited by the director tomorrow.

例句

① 會議和活動

  1. Please tidy your desks. We are being visited by our sales director on Thursday morning.
    請把桌子整理好。我們的業務總監星期四早上要來。
  2. Don't go to the main hall tomorrow. The meeting is being held in Room 5.
    明天別去大禮堂。會議改在五號教室開。
  3. The school concert is being held in the town hall this year, because our hall is too small.
    今年的學校音樂會在市政廳舉行，因為我們的禮堂太小了。
  4. The seminar is being held in Chennai next week, so I need to book a flight.
    研討會下星期在清奈舉行，所以我得訂機票。
  5. I can't come on Saturday. My cousin's wedding is being held in Scotland that weekend.
    我星期六不能來。那個週末我表哥的婚禮在蘇格蘭舉行。

② 送貨和收東西

  1. Can you stay at home on Friday? The new sofa is being delivered between ten and twelve.
    你星期五可以待在家嗎？新沙發十點到十二點之間會送來。
  2. Put the bins out tonight. They're being emptied early tomorrow morning.
    今晚把垃圾桶拿出去。明天一大早要清。
  3. I ordered your present online. It's being sent next week, so it should arrive before your birthday.
    我在網路上訂了你的禮物。下星期寄出，應該會在你生日前到。
  4. Don't throw that box away. It's being collected by the post office tomorrow.
    別把那個箱子丟掉。郵局明天要來收。
  5. Can you come in early on Monday? The new books are being delivered at eight.
    你星期一可以早點來嗎？新書八點會送到。

③ 接人和拜訪

  1. You don't need a taxi. You're being met at the airport by our driver.
    你不用叫計程車。我們的司機會在機場接你。
  2. Grandma can't drive any more, so she's being picked up by my dad on Sunday.
    奶奶已經不能開車了，所以星期天我爸爸會去接她。
  3. I'm a bit nervous. I'm being interviewed for the job tomorrow afternoon.
    我有點緊張。我明天下午要去面試那份工作。
  4. Remember to pack a lunch for Ben. The children are being taken to the zoo next Friday.
    記得幫 Ben 準備午餐。下星期五老師要帶孩子們去動物園。
  5. We're going on holiday next week. Our cat is being looked after by the neighbours.
    我們下星期要去度假。鄰居會幫我們照顧貓。

④ 修理和工程

  1. We can't use the kitchen next week. It's being painted.
    我們下星期不能用廚房。廚房要油漆。
  2. Take the bus on Monday. The bridge is being repaired, so it'll be closed all day.
    星期一搭公車吧。橋要修，整天都會封起來。
  3. Our old windows are being replaced in March. The new ones will keep the house warmer.
    我們的舊窗戶三月要換。新的會讓房子比較暖。
  4. Don't park in front of the house tomorrow. The tree is being cut down.
    明天別把車停在房子前面。那棵樹要砍掉。

⑤ 開幕、播出和公布

  1. Have you heard? The new shopping centre is being opened by a famous footballer on Saturday.
    你聽說了嗎？新購物中心星期六由一個有名的足球員主持開幕。
  2. Don't miss it! Our school play is being shown on local TV next Sunday.
    別錯過！我們學校的話劇下星期天會在地方電視台播出。
  3. I'm so nervous. The exam results are being sent out on Thursday.
    我好緊張。考試成績星期四會寄出。
  4. The winners are being announced at the end of the evening, so please stay until then.
    得獎名單在晚會最後公布，所以請待到那時候。

⑥ 否定句

  1. You can relax. The test isn't being marked until next week.
    你可以放輕鬆。考卷下星期才會改。
  2. The shop isn't being closed after all. They found a new owner.
    那家店最後不會關了。他們找到新老闆了。
  3. Don't worry. You aren't being moved to another class next term.
    別擔心。下學期不會把你換到別班。

⑦ 問句

  1. "When is the new fridge being delivered?" "On Wednesday morning, I think."
    新冰箱什麼時候送來？我想是星期三早上。
  2. "Where's the party being held?" "At Lucy's house. Her parents are away."
    派對在哪裡辦？在 Lucy 家。她爸媽不在。
  3. "Are you being collected after school?" "Yes, my mum's picking me up."
    放學有人來接你嗎？有，我媽媽會來接我。
  4. "Is the match being shown on TV tonight?" "No, only on the radio."
    今晚的比賽有電視轉播嗎？沒有，只有廣播。
  5. "Who is the prize being given to tonight?" "To the girl who wrote the best story."
    今晚的獎要頒給誰？頒給寫出最好故事的那個女孩。
