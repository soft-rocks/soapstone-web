例句

① like / love / enjoy + 動詞-ing

  1. On holiday I practiced windsurfing and scuba diving every day.
    我度假的時候每天都練習風帆衝浪和水肺潛水。
  2. My grandad spends hours in the garden because he loves gardening.
    我爺爺常在花園待好幾個小時，因為他很愛園藝。
  3. Do you enjoy cooking?
    你喜歡做菜嗎？
  4. My son likes drawing animals.
    我兒子喜歡畫動物。
  5. We really enjoyed sailing around the islands.
    我們很喜歡在島嶼之間航行。

② prefer / hate + 動詞-ing

  1. I prefer dancing, but on Saturday I'd like to go to a jazz concert.
    我比較喜歡跳舞，不過星期六我想去聽爵士音樂會。
  2. I'll do the cooking if you do the ironing. I hate ironing!
    你燙衣服的話我就煮飯，我最討厭燙衣服了！
  3. Do you prefer walking or cycling to work?
    你上班比較喜歡走路還是騎腳踏車？
  4. My husband hates waiting in queues.
    我先生討厭排隊。

③ start / stop / finish + 動詞-ing

  1. My mum started learning Italian when she retired.
    我媽媽退休後開始學義大利文。
  2. It stopped raining, so we went outside.
    雨停了，所以我們去外面。
  3. Have you finished packing your suitcase?
    你行李收好了嗎？
  4. Our new puppy started barking as soon as we left the house.
    我們一出門，新養的小狗就開始叫。

④ go + 動詞-ing

  1. We went shopping in town on Saturday.
    我們星期六去城裡逛街。
  2. Let's go swimming after work.
    下班後我們去游泳吧。
  3. They go skiing in the Alps every winter.
    他們每年冬天都去阿爾卑斯山滑雪。
  4. My parents went dancing on their anniversary.
    我爸媽結婚紀念日那天去跳舞。

⑤ 介系詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. There we did a lot of things, like swimming from the beach and going shopping.
    我們在那裡做了很多事，像是從海灘下水游泳和逛街。
  2. My little brother is brilliant at telling jokes.
    我弟弟超會講笑話。
  3. I'm interested in learning Spanish.
    我對學西班牙文有興趣。
  4. Thanks for helping me with my bags.
    謝謝你幫我提包包。
  5. My uncle always takes the train because he's afraid of flying.
    我舅舅都搭火車，因為他怕搭飛機。

⑥ 動詞-ing + 受詞

  1. My daughter enjoys playing the piano.
    我女兒喜歡彈鋼琴。
  2. I love watching old films on Sunday afternoons.
    我喜歡在星期天下午看老電影。
  3. Tell me when you've finished using the laptop.
    你用完筆電的時候跟我說一聲。
  4. He started taking the bus to work.
    他開始搭公車上班。

⑦ 兩個動詞-ing

  1. In my free time I like reading and listening to music.
    我有空的時候喜歡看書和聽音樂。
  2. I practice speaking and writing every day.
    我每天練習口說和寫作。
  3. The kids love running and jumping in the garden.
    孩子們很愛在院子裡跑跑跳跳。
  4. My mum enjoys knitting and baking.
    我媽媽喜歡織毛線和烤東西。
  5. We love walking and cycling in the hills.
    我們很喜歡在山丘上散步和騎腳踏車。
