例句

① understand

  1. I totally understand that you want to go somewhere with your friends. But you're only sixteen.
    我完全了解你想跟朋友一起去哪裡走走。可是你才十六歲。
  2. I understand why you're angry. I'm sorry I forgot.
    我了解你為什麼生氣。很抱歉我忘了。
  3. Can you say that again? I don't understand the question.
    你可以再說一次嗎？我聽不懂這個問題。
  4. My grandma understands English, but she doesn't speak it.
    我奶奶聽得懂英文，可是不會說。
  5. We understand that the trains are late. Please wait for more news.
    我們知道火車誤點了。請等候進一步的消息。

② remember / forget

  1. I'm reading an adventure book. I don't remember the title, but it's very boring!
    我在看一本冒險小說。我不記得書名，可是很無聊！
  2. It's ten years today. Do you remember the first time we met?
    今天滿十年了。你記得我們第一次見面的時候嗎？
  3. I remember her face, but I forget her name.
    我記得她的臉，可是忘了她的名字。
  4. They're on his head again. My dad always forgets where he puts his glasses.
    眼鏡又在他頭上了。我爸總是忘記他把眼鏡放在哪裡。
  5. Your face looks familiar. I remember seeing you at Sam's wedding.
    你看起來很面熟。我記得在 Sam 的婚禮上見過你。
  6. She never forgets a birthday. She writes them all in a book.
    她從來不會忘記別人的生日。她把每個人的生日都寫在本子上。

③ believe / doubt

  1. I believe that he's telling the truth. He has no reason to lie.
    我相信他說的是實話。他沒有理由說謊。
  2. You always wear that shirt for matches. Do you believe in luck?
    你比賽都穿那件襯衫。你相信運氣嗎？
  3. He says the dog ate it. Nobody believes his story about the lost homework.
    他說狗把功課吃了。沒有人相信他功課不見的說法。
  4. It's getting late. I doubt she'll come now.
    很晚了。我懷疑她現在還會來。
  5. There's so much work left. I doubt that we can finish this by Friday.
    還有好多工作。我懷疑我們能在星期五前做完。

④ realise / imagine

  1. I realise this is difficult for you, but we need an answer today.
    我知道這對你來說很難，可是我們今天就需要答案。
  2. It's after midnight. Do you realise what time it is?
    已經過半夜了。你知道現在幾點了嗎？
  3. Mum gets back from Australia tonight. I imagine she's very tired after such a long trip.
    媽媽今晚從澳洲回來。我想她旅行那麼久，一定很累。
  4. I use it for everything. I can't imagine life without my phone.
    我什麼都用它。我無法想像沒有手機的生活。

⑤ agree / disagree

  1. The roads are terrible at rush hour. I totally agree with you, so let's take the train.
    尖峰時間路上很糟。我完全同意你，所以我們搭火車吧。
  2. They want me to be a doctor. My parents don't agree with me about my future.
    他們要我當醫生。我爸媽對我的未來跟我意見不同。
  3. Some schools give no homework at all. Do you agree that homework is important?
    有些學校完全不出功課。你同意功課很重要嗎？

⑥ mean / prefer / recognise

  1. No coffee for me, thanks. I prefer tea in the morning.
    我不用咖啡，謝謝。我早上比較喜歡喝茶。
  2. Sorry, I don't recognise you. Have we met?
    抱歉，我認不出你。我們見過嗎？
  3. What do you mean? I don't follow.
    你是什麼意思？我聽不懂。
  4. Don't put her in a group. She prefers to work alone.
    別把她分到小組裡。她比較喜歡一個人工作。

⑦ 加強的副詞

  1. Two kids and one bathroom? I completely agree. We need a bigger flat.
    兩個孩子只有一間浴室？我完全同意。我們需要大一點的公寓。
  2. You've trained so hard. I really believe you can win this race.
    你練得那麼辛苦。我真的相信你能贏這場比賽。
  3. I fully understand your worries, but the trip is safe.
    我完全了解你的擔心，可是這趟旅行很安全。
