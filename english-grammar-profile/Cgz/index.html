She sometimes goes to the cinema.

她有時候會去看電影。

頻⁠率副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

她有時候會去看電影。

be 和助⁠動⁠詞放⁠在頻⁠率副⁠詞前⁠面

She sometimes goes to the cinema.

be 後面

My grandparents are always happy to see us.

我爺爺奶奶看到我們總是很高興。
助動詞後面

I can never find my keys.

我老是找不到鑰匙。

各⁠個頻⁠率副⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

always每一次都這樣
usually大部分的時候，也可以放在句首
often常常，也可以放在句首
sometimes有時候，也可以放在句首
never從來不

一⁠個頻⁠率副⁠詞修⁠飾後⁠面⁠的幾⁠個動⁠詞

有空的時候，我都會聽音樂、看電視、整理房間。

疑⁠問⁠句和否⁠定⁠句⁠的頻⁠率副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠要動⁠詞前⁠面

She sometimes goes to the cinema.

疑問句

Do you usually walk to school?

你通常走路上學嗎？
否定句

We don't often eat out.

我們不常外食。

頻⁠率副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠要動⁠詞前⁠面

She sometimes goes to the cinema.

I listen always to music in the car.

I always listen to music in the car.

頻⁠率副⁠詞放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

I never can sleep on planes.

I can never sleep on planes.

疑⁠問⁠句⁠的頻⁠率副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

Do always you take the bus?

Do you always take the bus?

表⁠示從⁠來⁠不⁠用 never

I always don't eat breakfast.

I never eat breakfast.

例句

① 主詞 + 頻率副詞 + 動詞

  1. My father always walks the dog before work.
    我爸爸上班前都會去遛狗。
  2. We usually have dinner at seven o'clock.
    我們通常七點吃晚餐。
  3. Ken often plays football with his cousins on Sundays.
    Ken 星期天常常和他的表兄弟踢足球。
  4. I sometimes forget my umbrella on the train.
    我有時候會把雨傘忘在火車上。
  5. My little sister never eats vegetables.
    我妹妹從來不吃青菜。
  6. Our teacher usually gives us homework on Fridays.
    我們老師通常星期五會出作業。
  7. My grandmother never uses her mobile phone.
    我奶奶從來不用手機。
  8. Jack sometimes stays up very late.
    Jack 有時候很晚才睡。

② 主詞 + be + 頻率副詞

  1. The trains are often late in winter.
    冬天火車常常誤點。
  2. My boss is never angry with us.
    我老闆從來不對我們生氣。
  3. The swimming pool is usually empty in the morning.
    游泳池早上通常沒什麼人。
  4. I'm sometimes late for my piano lesson.
    我上鋼琴課有時候會遲到。
  5. Mr Brown is always polite to his customers.
    Brown 先生對客人總是很有禮貌。

③ 主詞 + 助動詞 + 頻率副詞 + 動詞

  1. You can always borrow my bike on weekdays.
    平日你隨時都可以借我的腳踏車。
  2. I will never forget my first day at school.
    我永遠不會忘記第一天上學的情景。
  3. She has often visited her aunt in Canada.
    她常常去加拿大看她阿姨。
  4. My brother can sometimes be very lazy.
    我哥哥有時候很懶。
  5. We have always lived in this town.
    我們一直都住在這個小鎮。

④ 一個頻率副詞修飾幾個動詞

  1. After school, I usually do my homework and play computer games.
    放學以後，我通常寫作業、玩電腦遊戲。
  2. On Saturdays my mum often goes shopping and cooks a big lunch.
    星期六我媽常常去買東西，再煮一頓豐盛的午餐。
  3. My friends and I sometimes go to the park and ride our bikes.
    我和朋友有時候會去公園騎腳踏車。

⑤ 疑問句

  1. Do your children always go to bed early?
    你的孩子都很早睡嗎？
  2. Does Anna often come to your house?
    Anna 常常來你家嗎？
  3. Are you usually at home on Monday evenings?
    你星期一晚上通常在家嗎？
  4. Do you often drink tea in the afternoon?
    你下午常喝茶嗎？

⑥ 否定句

  1. My husband doesn't usually work at weekends.
    我先生週末通常不上班。
  2. I don't often watch the news on TV.
    我不常看電視新聞。
  3. Teenagers don't always listen to their parents.
    青少年不一定都聽父母的話。
  4. The bus doesn't usually stop here.
    公車通常不停這一站。

⑦ sometimes 放在句首

  1. Sometimes we walk to the beach after dinner.
    我們有時候吃完晚餐會走路去海邊。
  2. Sometimes my dad picks me up from school.
    我爸爸有時候會來學校接我。
  3. Sometimes the corner shop closes early on Sundays.
    轉角那家店星期天有時候會提早關門。
