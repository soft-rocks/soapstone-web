說就⁠在那⁠個位⁠置用 right，不⁠用 very

I'm going to put it right in front of the window so I have enough light.

表⁠示就⁠在、正⁠好用 right，不⁠用 rightly

The shop is rightly next to my school.

The shop is right next to my school.

例句

① right + 位置

  1. The bus stopped right outside my house.
    公車就停在我家外面。
  2. I'm standing right behind you.
    我就站在你後面。
  3. The ball landed right in the middle of the pond.
    球正好掉在池塘的中間。
  4. Put the lamp right next to the bed.
    把檯燈放在床的正旁邊。
  5. There's a coffee shop right across the street.
    對街就有一家咖啡店。
  6. The answer was right under my nose.
    答案就在我眼前。

② right / straight + 時間

  1. Call me right after the meeting.
    會議一結束就打給我。
  2. The kids went straight to bed after the trip.
    旅行回來後，孩子們直接去睡了。
  3. We left right before the storm.
    我們就在暴風雨來之前離開了。
  4. My dad drove straight to the hospital after the call.
    我爸爸接到電話後直接開車去醫院。
  5. The baby fell asleep right in the middle of the film.
    寶寶就在電影演到一半時睡著了。

③ quite / very + 位置

  1. The beach is quite near our hotel.
    海灘離我們的飯店相當近。
  2. My school is very close to the station.
    我的學校離車站很近。
  3. They live quite far from the city center.
    他們住得離市中心相當遠。
  4. The airport is quite close to the city.
    機場離市區相當近。
  5. The hospital is very near the motorway.
    醫院離高速公路很近。

④ really / especially + 介系詞片語

  1. Our new flat is really near the river.
    我們的新公寓真的離河很近。
  2. I love shopping, especially with my sister.
    我很喜歡逛街，特別是跟我姊姊一起。
  3. It's cold here, especially in the mornings.
    這裡很冷，特別是早上。
  4. The town is busy, especially at the weekend.
    這個小鎮很熱鬧，特別是週末。
  5. Their farm is really in the middle of nowhere.
    他們的農場真的在荒郊野外。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. The keys were right in my pocket the whole time.
    鑰匙一直都在我的口袋裡。
  2. He walked straight into the glass door.
    他直接撞上了玻璃門。
  3. Our seats were right at the front.
    我們的座位就在最前面。
  4. The fire started right above the kitchen.
    火是從廚房正上方燒起來的。
  5. I'll be there straight after school.
    我放學後會直接過去。
  6. The shop is just round the corner, quite close to the bank.
    那家店就在轉角，離銀行相當近。
  7. She sat right between her two best friends.
    她坐在她兩個最好的朋友正中間。
  8. The light switch is right by the door.
    電燈開關就在門邊。
  9. The stars look amazing, especially from the top of the mountain.
    星星看起來好美，特別是從山頂看。
