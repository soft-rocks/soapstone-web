Is it cheap or expensive for you?

這對你來說算便宜還是貴？

選⁠擇疑⁠問⁠句用 or 連⁠接同⁠一⁠種詞⁠性⁠的字

這對你來說算便宜還是貴？

A 和 B 可⁠以是形⁠容⁠詞、名⁠詞、動⁠詞或副⁠詞

Is it cheap or expensive for you?

名詞

Do you want tea or coffee?

你要茶還是咖啡？
動詞

Shall we walk or drive?

我們要走路還是開車？
副詞

Do you want to leave now or later?

你要現在走還是晚一點？

語⁠調在 A 上⁠揚、在 B 下⁠降，對⁠方選一⁠個回⁠答

Is it cheap or expensive for you?

「茶還是咖啡？」「咖啡，謝謝。」

B 的語⁠調也上⁠揚，就變⁠成一⁠般⁠的疑⁠問⁠句

Is it cheap or expensive for you?

「你要喝點茶或咖啡嗎？」「好，謝謝。咖啡。」

or 前⁠後用同⁠一⁠種字

Is it cheap or expensive for you?

Do you want tea or drinking?

Do you want tea or coffee?

選⁠擇疑⁠問⁠句用 or，不⁠用 and

Is it cheap or expensive for you?

Is it cheap and expensive?

Is it cheap or expensive?

選⁠擇疑⁠問⁠句不⁠用 yes 或 no 回⁠答

"Is your new flat big or small?" "Yes."

"Is your new flat big or small?" "It's quite small."

例句

① 形容詞 + or + 形容詞

  1. You said the language course was £800. Is it cheap or expensive for you?
    你說語言課程要八百英鎊。這對你來說算便宜還是貴？
  2. I'm making tea. Do you like it strong or weak?
    我在泡茶。你喜歡濃的還是淡的？
  3. You started at the new school today. Is your teacher strict or friendly?
    你今天開始在新學校上課。你的老師是嚴格還是和善？
  4. I can't see the sky from here. Is it sunny or cloudy?
    我從這裡看不到天空。現在是晴天還是陰天？
  5. Was the film good or bad? I'm thinking of seeing it.
    那部電影好看還是不好看？我在考慮要不要去看。
  6. Is the water hot or cold? I want to have a shower.
    水是熱的還是冷的？我想洗澡。
  7. I want to study at the café. Is it quiet or busy at lunchtime?
    我想去那家咖啡店念書。那裡中午安靜還是很多人？

② 名詞 + or + 名詞

  1. Can you bring some food or drinks for the dinner?
    晚餐你可以帶一些吃的或喝的來嗎？
  2. I'm putting the kettle on. Do you want tea or coffee after dinner?
    我在燒水。晚餐後你要茶還是咖啡？
  3. Is the meeting on Monday or Tuesday? I've forgotten.
    會議是星期一還是星期二？我忘了。
  4. Would you like rice or chips with your fish?
    你的魚要配飯還是薯條？
  5. Congratulations! Is your sister's baby a boy or a girl?
    恭喜！你姊姊的寶寶是男生還是女生？
  6. I'm going to the market. Do you want apples or oranges?
    我要去市場。你要蘋果還是柳橙？
  7. You live quite far away. Do you go to work by bus or train?
    你住得滿遠的。你搭公車還是火車上班？
  8. A phone is ringing on the table. Is that your phone or mine?
    桌上有支手機在響。那是你的手機還是我的？

③ 動詞 + or + 動詞

  1. We're free tonight. Shall we chat or watch a film?
    我們今晚有空。我們要聊天還是看電影？
  2. Shall we walk or drive to the restaurant?
    我們要走路還是開車去餐廳？
  3. The party is nearly over. Do you want to stay or leave?
    派對快結束了。你要留下來還是離開？
  4. You look great. Did you buy the dress or borrow it?
    你好好看。那件洋裝是你買的還是借的？
  5. Are you going to cook or order a pizza tonight?
    你今晚要煮飯還是叫披薩？
  6. Do you want to swim or sunbathe this afternoon?
    你今天下午要游泳還是曬太陽？

④ 副詞或時間 + or + 副詞或時間

  1. The rain has stopped. Do you want to leave now or later?
    雨停了。你要現在走還是晚一點？
  2. Should I call you today or tomorrow?
    我該今天打給你還是明天？
  3. Is the party this Saturday or next Saturday?
    派對是這個星期六還是下個星期六？
  4. You look tired. Did you get up early or late this morning?
    你看起來很累。你今天早上很早起還是很晚起？

⑤ 代名詞 + or + 代名詞

  1. Who's driving tonight, you or me?
    今晚誰開車，你還是我？
  2. There are two names on the card. Is this present for him or her?
    卡片上有兩個名字。這個禮物是給他的還是給她的？
  3. Which do you want, this or that?
    你要哪一個，這個還是那個？

⑥ 省略的選擇疑問句

  1. "Tea or coffee?" "Coffee, please. Black."
    茶還是咖啡？咖啡，謝謝。黑咖啡。
  2. "Cash or card?" "Card, please."
    現金還是刷卡？刷卡，謝謝。
  3. "Window or aisle?" "Window, please. I like to look out."
    靠窗還是靠走道？靠窗，謝謝。我喜歡看外面。
