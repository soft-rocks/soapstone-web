Sir, may I come in?

請問我可以跟 Wright 小姐談一下嗎？

(稱⁠呼,) + may I + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

Sir, may I come in?

稱呼Sir、Madam、Mr Smith 這類稱呼常放在前面。
may I我可以……嗎？
please放在句尾，讓請求更有禮貌。

打⁠電⁠話

例句

May I speak to Miss Wright, please?

請問懷特小姐在嗎？

打電話找人時，常用 May I speak to。

問服⁠務和優⁠惠

例句

May I ask if the offer is still available?

請問這個優惠還有效嗎？

寫信給商店或公司時，常用 May I ask 問問題。

may 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Sir, may I come in?

May I coming in?

May I come in?

找⁠人說⁠話用 speak to

May I speak Miss Wright, please?

May I speak to Miss Wright, please?

主⁠詞用 I

Sir, may I come in?

May me leave the room?

May I leave the room?

例句

① (稱呼,) + may I + 原形動詞？：對老師和上司

  1. Excuse me, sir, may I close the window? It's very cold.
    不好意思，先生，我可以關窗嗎？好冷。
  2. Excuse me, Mrs Hall, may I ask a question about the homework?
    不好意思，霍爾老師，我可以問一個功課的問題嗎？
  3. Madam, may I take a seat while I wait?
    女士，我等的時候可以坐一下嗎？
  4. Mr Jones, may I leave the meeting a few minutes early?
    瓊斯先生，我可以提早幾分鐘離開會議嗎？
  5. Professor, may I send you my essay by email?
    教授，我可以用電子郵件寄作文給您嗎？
  6. Doctor, may I see the results of my blood test?
    醫生，我可以看我的驗血結果嗎？

② May I speak to + 人, please？：打電話

  1. Good afternoon. May I speak to Miss Wright, please?
    午安。請問懷特小姐在嗎？
  2. Hello, may I speak to the person in charge of bookings?
    你好，請問負責訂位的人在嗎？
  3. May I speak to someone in the sales department?
    我可以找業務部的人說話嗎？
  4. Good morning. May I speak to Dr Brown about my appointment?
    早安。我可以跟布朗醫生談我的預約嗎？
  5. May I speak to the head teacher, please? It's about my son.
    請問校長在嗎？是關於我兒子的事。
  6. Sorry to call so late. May I speak to your father?
    抱歉這麼晚打來。我可以跟你爸爸說話嗎？

③ May I ask (you) if + 子句？：寫信問問題

  1. I'm planning to buy the sofa later. May I ask you if your special offer will still be available in September?
    我打算晚一點買沙發。請問你們的特價到九月還有嗎？
  2. May I ask if the room has a view of the sea?
    請問那間房間看得到海嗎？
  3. May I ask if you deliver to other countries?
    請問你們有寄送到其他國家嗎？
  4. May I ask if there are any jobs available this summer?
    請問今年夏天有工作機會嗎？
  5. May I ask if the price includes a guide?
    請問價格有包含導遊嗎？
  6. May I ask you if I can pay in two parts?
    請問我可以分兩次付款嗎？

④ May I + 原形動詞？：在店裡和旅館

  1. May I keep my bags here until this afternoon?
    我可以把行李寄放在這裡到下午嗎？
  2. May I check out a little later tomorrow?
    我明天可以晚一點退房嗎？
  3. May I order a taxi for six o'clock?
    我可以叫一輛六點的計程車嗎？
  4. May I ask for some extra towels?
    我可以多要幾條毛巾嗎？
  5. May I return these shoes if they don't fit?
    如果這雙鞋不合腳，我可以退貨嗎？
  6. May I pay for the room when I arrive?
    我可以到了再付房錢嗎？

⑤ May I + 原形動詞 + , please？：在學校和辦公室

  1. May I have a copy of the timetable, please?
    請問我可以拿一份課表嗎？
  2. May I leave the room for a moment, please?
    請問我可以離開教室一下嗎？
  3. May I borrow the stapler from your desk, please?
    請問我可以借你桌上的釘書機嗎？
  4. May I work from home on Friday, please?
    請問我星期五可以在家工作嗎？
  5. May I take tomorrow afternoon off, please?
    請問我明天下午可以請假嗎？
  6. May I sit in on your class next week, please?
    請問我下星期可以旁聽您的課嗎？
  7. May I use the meeting room for an hour, please?
    請問我可以用會議室一個小時嗎？
