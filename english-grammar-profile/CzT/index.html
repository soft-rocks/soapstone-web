之後我會去海邊。

I / we + shall + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I / we、shall、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

After that, I shall go to the beach.

I / we這個用法的 shall 只接這兩個主詞。
shall表示主詞打算做的事。意思和 will 相同，是英式英語比較正式的說法。
原形動詞打算做的事。

答⁠應別⁠人⁠的事

After that, I shall go to the beach.

例句

I promise I shall finish the work and give it to you tomorrow.

我保證會把工作做完，明天交給你。

說話者答應別人要做某件事時，也可以用 I shall。

正⁠式場⁠合⁠的安⁠排

例句

We shall have a meeting about this next week.

我們下星期會就這件事開會。

公司、學校的正式通知裡，主詞常是 we。

shall 和 will 意⁠思相⁠同，只⁠用一⁠個

After that, I shall go to the beach.

I shall will go to the beach after lunch.

I shall go to the beach after lunch.

shall 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

After that, I shall go to the beach.

I shall to visit my aunt tomorrow.

I shall visit my aunt tomorrow.

shall 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

After that, I shall go to the beach.

We shall went to the museum.

We shall go to the museum.

例句

① I + shall + 原形動詞

  1. After lunch, I shall go for a swim in the lake.
    午餐後我會去湖裡游泳。
  2. I promise I shall cover all the work you missed.
    我保證會幫你補上你錯過的所有功課。
  3. I shall call the hotel this afternoon.
    我今天下午會打電話給飯店。
  4. I shall spend the summer with my grandparents.
    我暑假會跟我祖父母一起過。
  5. I shall take the early train tomorrow.
    我明天會搭早班火車。
  6. I shall buy the tickets online tonight.
    我今晚會在網路上買票。
  7. Tomorrow I shall start my new diet.
    明天我就開始新的飲食計畫。
  8. I shall ask my boss for a day off.
    我會跟老闆請一天假。
  9. I shall send you the photos when I get home.
    我到家後會把照片寄給你。
  10. First I shall have a shower, and then I shall cook dinner.
    我先洗個澡，然後煮晚餐。
  11. I shall look for a new flat next month.
    我下個月會找新公寓。
  12. I shall answer your letter at the weekend.
    我週末會回你的信。
  13. This evening I shall finish my essay.
    今天晚上我會寫完作文。
  14. I shall return the books to the library on Monday.
    我星期一會把書還給圖書館。
  15. I shall join a gym in the new year.
    新年我會加入健身房。

② We + shall + 原形動詞

  1. We shall have a meeting about the new rules next week.
    我們下星期會就新規定開會。
  2. We shall leave for the airport at six.
    我們六點會出發去機場。
  3. We shall decorate the classroom on Friday afternoon.
    我們星期五下午會布置教室。
  4. We shall contact you as soon as we have the results.
    我們一有結果就會聯絡你。
  5. We shall spend two nights in Edinburgh.
    我們會在 Edinburgh 住兩晚。
  6. We shall paint the fence next weekend.
    我們下個週末會油漆籬笆。
  7. We shall send the invitations in March.
    我們三月會寄出邀請函。
  8. After the concert, we shall have dinner at a small restaurant.
    音樂會結束後，我們會去一家小餐廳吃晚餐。
  9. We shall discuss your plan at the next meeting.
    我們會在下次會議討論你的計畫。
  10. We shall open a new branch in the city centre.
    我們會在市中心開一家新分店。
  11. We shall visit the old castle in the morning.
    我們早上會去參觀那座古堡。
  12. We shall reply to all emails within two days.
    我們會在兩天內回覆所有電子郵件。
  13. We shall plant some trees in the garden this spring.
    今年春天我們會在花園裡種幾棵樹。
  14. This year we shall celebrate Christmas at home.
    今年我們會在家過聖誕節。
  15. We shall stop at a café on the way.
    我們會在路上的一家咖啡店停一下。
  16. We shall move the meeting to Thursday.
    我們會把會議改到星期四。
