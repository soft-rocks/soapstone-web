Thinking of Christmas? Not necessarily.

在想聖誕節的事嗎？那可不一定。

"…？" "Not necessarily."

Not necessarily不一定。簡短的回答。
理由常接著說為什麼不一定。

接⁠著說理⁠由

Thinking of Christmas? Not necessarily.

"Big cities are dangerous." "Not necessarily. Mine is very safe."

「大城市很危險。」「不一定。我住的城市很安全。」

Not necessarily. 後面常接著說例外的情況。

簡⁠短⁠的回⁠答用副⁠詞 necessarily

例句

① "…？" "Not necessarily." + 理由：回答問題

  1. Presents, ribbons, wrapping paper. Thinking of Christmas? Not necessarily. People give presents all year round.
    禮物、緞帶、包裝紙。想到聖誕節了嗎？不一定喔。人們一整年都會送禮物。
  2. "Do I need a car to live in the countryside?" "Not necessarily. There's a good bus service."
    住在鄉下需要車嗎？不一定。那裡的公車很方便。
  3. "Is the most expensive phone the best?" "Not necessarily. Read the reviews first."
    最貴的手機是最好的嗎？不一定。先看評論。
  4. "Will the meeting take all morning?" "Not necessarily. It depends on the questions."
    會議會開一整個早上嗎？不一定。要看有多少問題。
  5. "Do vegetarians eat fish?" "Not necessarily. Most of them don't."
    素食者吃魚嗎？不一定。大部分不吃。
  6. "Should I bring a coat?" "Not necessarily. It's warm during the day."
    我應該帶外套嗎？不一定。白天很暖和。

② "肯定句" "Not necessarily." + 理由：對方的看法有例外

  1. "Big cities are always dangerous." "Not necessarily. Mine feels very safe at night."
    大城市都很危險。不一定。我住的城市晚上感覺很安全。
  2. "Older people don't like new music." "Not necessarily. My grandma loves pop music."
    老人不喜歡新的音樂。不一定。我奶奶很喜歡流行音樂。
  3. "Homework helps you learn." "Not necessarily. Too much can make you tired."
    功課能幫你學習。不一定。太多會讓你很累。
  4. "Cheap hotels are dirty." "Not necessarily. We stayed in a lovely one last year."
    便宜的飯店都很髒。不一定。我們去年住了一家很棒的。
  5. "Tall people are good at basketball." "Not necessarily. My brother is tall and terrible at it."
    高的人很會打籃球。不一定。我哥哥很高，可是打得很爛。
  6. "Cats don't like water." "Not necessarily. My cat jumps in the bath."
    貓不喜歡水。不一定。我的貓會跳進浴缸。

③ "Not necessarily," + 主詞 + said：故事裡的對話

  1. "So you'll be rich one day," said my uncle. "Not necessarily," I said with a smile.
    所以你有一天會很有錢，我叔叔說。不一定，我笑著說。
  2. "You'll need to work late," said the manager. "Not necessarily," she replied.
    你會需要加班，經理說。不一定，她回答。
  3. "The train will be full," said Dad. "Not necessarily," said Mum. "It's a holiday."
    火車會客滿，爸爸說。不一定，媽媽說。今天放假。
  4. "He must be angry with you," said Tom. "Not necessarily," said Anna.
    他一定在生你的氣，湯姆說。不一定，安娜說。
  5. "That means we've lost," said the captain. "Not necessarily," said the coach.
    那表示我們輸了，隊長說。不一定，教練說。
  6. "You'll have to sell the house," said the lawyer. "Not necessarily," said the old man.
    你必須賣掉房子，律師說。不一定，那位老人說。

④ "…？" "Not necessarily, but …"：部分同意

  1. "Is it better to study in the morning?" "Not necessarily, but it works for me."
    早上讀書比較好嗎？不一定，可是對我有用。
  2. "Do I have to wear a suit?" "Not necessarily, but you should look smart."
    我一定要穿西裝嗎？不一定，可是你應該穿得體面一點。
  3. "Will we need to book?" "Not necessarily, but it's safer."
    我們需要預約嗎？不一定，可是比較保險。
  4. "Is the new teacher strict?" "Not necessarily, but she likes quiet classes."
    新老師很嚴格嗎？不一定，可是她喜歡安靜的課堂。
  5. "Should we take the motorway?" "Not necessarily, but it's usually faster."
    我們應該走高速公路嗎？不一定，可是通常比較快。
  6. "Do you need a degree for this job?" "Not necessarily, but it helps."
    做這份工作需要學位嗎？不一定，可是有幫助。

⑤ Well, not necessarily. + 理由：委婉地不同意

  1. "Everyone at the party will know each other." "Well, not necessarily. Some are new."
    派對上每個人都會互相認識。嗯，不一定。有些人是新來的。
  2. "The best players always win." "Well, not necessarily. Luck matters too."
    最好的球員總是會贏。嗯，不一定。運氣也很重要。
  3. "Your parents will say no." "Well, not necessarily. Let me ask them."
    你爸媽會拒絕。嗯，不一定。讓我問問他們。
  4. "Rain means the picnic is off." "Well, not necessarily. We could eat under the trees."
    下雨表示野餐取消了。嗯，不一定。我們可以在樹下吃。
  5. "Learning a language takes years." "Well, not necessarily. You can learn the basics quickly."
    學一種語言要好幾年。嗯，不一定。基礎可以學得很快。
  6. "A late start means a late finish." "Well, not necessarily. We can skip the break."
    晚開始就表示晚結束。嗯，不一定。我們可以不休息。
  7. "Old cars break down all the time." "Well, not necessarily. Mine has never let me down."
    舊車常常拋錨。嗯，不一定。我的車從來沒讓我失望過。
