I think it may be dirty.

我覺得它可能髒了。

主⁠詞 + may + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

may、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I think it may be dirty.

may可能。所有主詞都用 may，不加 -⁠s。
原形動詞可能發生的事。

may 後⁠面不⁠加 to

I think it may be dirty.

例句

We may need it.

我們可能會需要它。

may 是情態動詞。後面直接接原形動詞。

主⁠詞不⁠改⁠變 may

例句

I may go.

我可能會去。
例句

She may go.

她可能會去。

may 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I think it may be dirty.

It may to rain tomorrow.

It may rain tomorrow.

may 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

I think it may be dirty.

She mays come later.

She may come later.

may 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I think it may be dirty.

He may comes with us.

He may come with us.

例句

① I / we + may + 原形動詞

  1. Can I take my winter clothes on the trip? I may need them in the mountains.
    我可以帶冬天的衣服去旅行嗎？在山上我可能會需要它們。
  2. Bring some money with you. We may need it for the bus.
    帶一點錢在身上。我們搭公車可能會需要。
  3. I may be a little late tonight. The traffic is bad.
    我今天晚上可能會晚一點到。交通很糟。
  4. We may go to the beach on Sunday if it's sunny.
    如果星期天天氣晴朗，我們可能會去海灘。
  5. I may stop at the bakery on my way home.
    我回家的路上可能會去麵包店。
  6. We may paint the kitchen yellow this spring.
    我們今年春天可能會把廚房漆成黃色。

② he / she / it + may + 原形動詞

  1. Check the bread first. It may be past its date.
    先看一下麵包，它可能過期了。
  2. Ask Tom. He may know the answer.
    問湯姆。他可能知道答案。
  3. My sister may join us for lunch after her swimming lesson.
    我妹妹上完游泳課可能會來跟我們吃午餐。
  4. Take an umbrella. It may rain later.
    帶把傘。等一下可能會下雨。
  5. The shop may be closed now. It's after six.
    那家店現在可能關了。已經過六點了。
  6. My brother may visit us at Christmas.
    我哥哥聖誕節可能會來看我們。

③ you / they + may + 原形動詞

  1. Try this cake. You may like it.
    吃吃看這個蛋糕。你可能會喜歡。
  2. They may be at home. Let's phone them.
    他們可能在家。我們打電話給他們吧。
  3. You may find the book in the library.
    你可能會在圖書館找到那本書。
  4. They may buy a new car this year.
    他們今年可能會買新車。
  5. You may need a jacket. It's cold in the evenings.
    你可能會需要外套。傍晚很冷。
  6. The children may be hungry after the walk.
    走完路孩子們可能會餓。

④ may be + 名詞或形容詞

  1. That woman may be our new teacher.
    那位女士可能是我們的新老師。
  2. The answer may be on page ten.
    答案可能在第十頁。
  3. The film may be too long for the kids.
    這部電影對孩子們來說可能太長了。
  4. This may be the best day of the holiday.
    這可能是假期裡最棒的一天。
  5. He may be tired after the long flight.
    長途飛行後他可能會很累。
  6. The water may be cold in the morning.
    早上水可能會很冷。

⑤ may + 原形動詞 + 理由

  1. I may stay at home tonight because I've got a cold.
    我今天晚上可能會待在家，因為我感冒了。
  2. We may eat outside because it's such a nice day.
    天氣這麼好，我們可能會在外面吃。
  3. She may take the train because the car is broken.
    車子壞了，她可能會搭火車。
  4. They may cancel the match because of the snow.
    因為下雪，他們可能會取消比賽。
  5. You may get lost, so take a map.
    你可能會迷路，所以帶一張地圖。
  6. I may buy those shoes because they're on sale.
    那雙鞋在特價，我可能會買。
  7. The baby may wake up, so please be quiet.
    寶寶可能會醒來，所以請安靜一點。
