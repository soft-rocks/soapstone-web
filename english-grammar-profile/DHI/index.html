例句

① 是非問句：Did + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Did the hotel refund your deposit?
    飯店有退你訂金嗎？
  2. Did you recognise anyone at the reunion?
    你在同學會上有認出誰嗎？
  3. Did the apology placate the customer?
    道歉有讓那位客人消氣嗎？
  4. Did the medicine help at all?
    藥有一點效果嗎？
  5. Did your parents approve of the plan?
    你爸媽贊成這個計畫嗎？

② 疑問詞問句：疑問詞 + did + 主詞

  1. Where did you learn to swim?
    你在哪裡學會游泳的？
  2. How did you persuade him to come?
    你是怎麼說服他來的？
  3. What did the doctor say about your knee?
    醫生怎麼說你的膝蓋？
  4. Why did they decide to extend the deadline?
    他們為什麼決定延長期限？
  5. Which hotel did you choose in the end?
    你最後選了哪家飯店？

③ Who / What 當主詞

  1. What caused the delay?
    延誤是什麼造成的？
  2. Who recommended this restaurant?
    這家餐廳是誰推薦的？
  3. What woke you up so early?
    是什麼把你這麼早吵醒的？

④ 附加問句

  1. We certainly had a terrific time together, didn't we?
    我們一起玩得超開心的，對吧？
  2. You remembered to feed the cat, didn't you?
    你有記得餵貓吧？
  3. She didn't recognise you, did she?
    她沒認出你吧？
  4. The train arrived on time, didn't it?
    火車準時到了，對吧？
  5. They didn't apologise, did they?
    他們沒道歉吧？

⑤ 否定問句：Didn't + 主詞

  1. Didn't you say you were allergic to cats?
    你不是說你對貓過敏嗎？
  2. Didn't he work in a bank before?
    他以前不是在銀行上班嗎？
  3. Didn't we meet at Anna's wedding?
    我們不是在 Anna 的婚禮上見過嗎？
  4. Didn't the shop open at nine?
    那家店不是九點開門嗎？
  5. Didn't you enjoy the concert?
    你不喜歡那場音樂會嗎？

⑥ Why didn't + 主詞

  1. Why didn't you say something earlier?
    你為什麼不早點說？
  2. Why didn't they invite us?
    他們為什麼沒邀請我們？
  3. Why didn't the alarm go off?
    鬧鐘為什麼沒響？

⑦ 回答否定問句

  1. "Didn't she call you?" "Yes, she did. She called this morning."
    她沒打給你嗎？有啊，她今天早上打了。
  2. "Didn't you post the letter?" "No, I didn't. I forgot."
    你沒寄那封信嗎？沒有，我忘了。

⑧ 用在書信和電子郵件

  1. Did you receive the documents I sent last week?
    我上禮拜寄的文件您收到了嗎？
  2. When did you first notice the leak?
    您是什麼時候第一次發現漏水的？
  3. Why didn't anyone inform us about the change?
    為什麼沒有人通知我們這項變更？
  4. You didn't receive my previous email, did you?
    您沒收到我上一封電子郵件吧？
