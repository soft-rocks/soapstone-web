例句

① 名詞 + and

  1. In my bag I have a book, a pen and an apple.
    我的包包裡有一本書、一支筆和一顆蘋果。
  2. I really like the students from Italy, Portugal, China and India.
    我很喜歡那些從義大利、葡萄牙、中國和印度來的同學。
  3. For the picnic we need sandwiches, fruit, juice and a blanket.
    野餐要帶三明治、水果、果汁和一條毯子。
  4. My aunt has two dogs, three cats and a parrot.
    我阿姨養了兩隻狗、三隻貓和一隻鸚鵡。
  5. We visited Rome, Florence and Venice on our holiday.
    我們度假時去了羅馬、佛羅倫斯和威尼斯。

② 名詞 + or

  1. I like to eat steak, vegetables, potatoes or rice.
    我喜歡吃牛排、蔬菜、馬鈴薯或米飯。
  2. You can choose chocolate, vanilla or strawberry ice cream.
    你可以選巧克力、香草或草莓冰淇淋。
  3. We can meet on Monday, Wednesday or Friday.
    我們可以星期一、星期三或星期五見面。
  4. Bring a jacket, a coat or a warm jumper.
    帶一件夾克、一件大衣或一件保暖的毛衣。

③ 時間 + and

  1. I go to college every Tuesday, Thursday and Friday morning.
    我每個星期二、星期四和星期五早上去學院上課。
  2. The museum is open on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.
    博物館星期六、星期天和國定假日有開。
  3. We have English in June, July and August.
    我們六月、七月和八月有英文課。
  4. My sister swims on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays.
    我妹妹每個星期一、星期三和星期六游泳。

④ 形容詞 + and

  1. My new neighbours are young, friendly and very helpful.
    我的新鄰居年輕、友善，而且很熱心。
  2. The weather was cold, wet and windy all week.
    整個星期天氣又冷又濕又颳風。
  3. Her flat is small, bright and very clean.
    她的公寓小小的，很明亮，也很乾淨。
  4. The film was long, slow and a bit boring.
    那部電影很長、節奏很慢，還有點無聊。

⑤ 動詞片語 + and、or

  1. In the evening I cook dinner, wash the dishes and watch TV.
    晚上我煮晚餐、洗碗，然後看電視。
  2. At the weekend we go swimming, play tennis or visit friends.
    週末我們去游泳、打網球或去拜訪朋友。
  3. Every morning she gets up, has a shower and drinks a coffee.
    她每天早上起床、洗澡，然後喝一杯咖啡。
  4. You can read a book, draw a picture or play outside.
    你可以看書、畫畫或去外面玩。

⑥ 冒號 + 列舉

  1. I have three rooms: a kitchen, a living room and a bathroom.
    我有三個房間：廚房、客廳和浴室。
  2. I speak four languages: English, French, German and Spanish.
    我會說四種語言：英語、法語、德語和西班牙語。
  3. We need three things: eggs, flour and sugar.
    我們需要三樣東西：蛋、麵粉和糖。
  4. You can pay in three ways: by cash, by card or by phone.
    你可以用三種方式付款：付現、刷卡或用手機。

⑦ 否定句 + or

  1. I don't like carrots, onions or peppers.
    我不喜歡胡蘿蔔、洋蔥，也不喜歡甜椒。
  2. There is no bread, milk or butter in the fridge.
    冰箱裡沒有麵包、牛奶，也沒有奶油。
  3. My little brother can't read, write or count yet.
    我弟弟還不會讀、不會寫，也不會數數。
  4. We didn't see any lions, tigers or elephants at the zoo.
    我們在動物園沒看到獅子、老虎，也沒看到大象。
  5. She never eats sweets, cakes or biscuits.
    她從來不吃糖果、蛋糕或餅乾。
