a friend of mine 說我⁠的朋⁠友之⁠中⁠的一⁠個

我的一個朋友送了我生日禮物。

聽⁠的人不⁠知⁠道是⁠誰用 a friend of mine，已⁠經知⁠道用 my friend

A friend of mine gave me a present for my birthday.

a friend of mine

A friend of mine is a nurse.

我有一個朋友是護理師。
my friend

My friend Lisa is a nurse.

我朋友 Lisa 是護理師。

說幾⁠個人用 some 或數⁠字，名⁠詞用複⁠數

A friend of mine gave me a present for my birthday.

複數

Some friends of mine are coming for dinner.

我的幾個朋友要來吃晚餐。

of 後⁠面用 mine，不⁠用 me

A friend of mine gave me a present for my birthday.

A friend of me works in a bank.

A friend of mine works in a bank.

a 和 my 不⁠能放在⁠一⁠起

A friend of mine gave me a present for my birthday.

A my friend called me yesterday.

A friend of mine called me yesterday.

說兩⁠個以⁠上，名⁠詞用複⁠數

Two friend of mine are in my class.

Two friends of mine are in my class.

例句

① a friend of mine 當主詞

  1. A friend of mine is getting married next month.
    我的一個朋友下個月要結婚了。
  2. A friend of mine works at that café.
    我有一個朋友在那家咖啡店上班。
  3. A friend of mine lent me this book.
    這本書是我的一個朋友借我的。
  4. A cousin of mine runs a small hotel by the sea.
    我的一個表哥在海邊開了一家小旅館。
  5. A neighbor of mine has three dogs.
    我有一個鄰居養了三隻狗。
  6. A colleague of mine is from Brazil.
    我有一個同事是巴西人。

② a friend of mine 當受詞

  1. I went to the cinema with a friend of mine.
    我跟我的一個朋友去看電影。
  2. I'm staying with a friend of mine in London.
    我住在倫敦的一個朋友家。
  3. Mr. Lin is an old colleague of mine.
    林先生是我以前的一個同事。
  4. I met an old classmate of mine at the station.
    我在車站遇到我以前的一個同學。
  5. I bought this bike from a neighbor of mine.
    這台腳踏車是我跟一個鄰居買的。
  6. This is a teacher of mine from primary school.
    這是我小學的一位老師。

③ some / two + 複數名詞 + of mine

  1. Some friends of mine run a hiking club.
    我的幾個朋友經營一個登山社。
  2. Two friends of mine are coming to stay.
    我的兩個朋友要來住。
  3. Some colleagues of mine went to Japan last year.
    我的幾個同事去年去了日本。
  4. A few cousins of mine live in Canada.
    我有幾個表兄弟姊妹住在加拿大。
  5. Some old friends of mine are visiting this weekend.
    我的幾個老朋友這個週末要來看我。

④ 東西：of mine

  1. That's an old shirt of mine.
    那是我的一件舊襯衫。
  2. A favorite song of mine came on the radio.
    廣播播了一首我最愛的歌。
  3. This is a photo of mine from the trip.
    這是我那趟旅行拍的一張照片。
  4. Some books of mine are still at my parents' house.
    我的一些書還在我爸媽家。
  5. An idea of mine won the competition.
    我的一個點子贏了比賽。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. A friend of mine drove me to the airport.
    我的一個朋友開車送我去機場。
  2. Is he a friend of yours? No, he's a cousin of mine.
    他是你的朋友嗎？不，他是我的表哥。
  3. I'm going on holiday with some friends of mine.
    我要跟我的幾個朋友去度假。
  4. A friend of mine taught me how to swim.
    我的一個朋友教我游泳。
  5. A student of mine won a prize in the competition.
    我的一個學生在比賽中得了獎。
  6. Two neighbors of mine helped me move the sofa.
    我的兩個鄰居幫我搬了沙發。
  7. A friend of mine knows a good dentist.
    我有一個朋友認識一位很好的牙醫。
  8. Some friends of mine are learning Korean too.
    我的幾個朋友也在學韓文。
