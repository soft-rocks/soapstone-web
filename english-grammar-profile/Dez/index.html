You wouldn't believe how much they cost!

你一定不敢相信它們有多貴！

主⁠詞 + wouldn't + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

wouldn't、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

wouldn'twould not 的縮寫。不會。
原形動詞想像中不會發生的事。

You wouldn't believe how much they cost!

例句

You wouldn't believe how big their house is!

You wouldn't believe 加疑問詞子句，強調一件很讓人驚訝的事。

例句

① You wouldn't believe + 疑問詞 + 子句：強調讓人驚訝的事

  1. We went into that designer shop. You wouldn't believe how much the clothes cost!
    我們走進那家名牌店。你一定不敢相信那些衣服有多貴！
  2. You wouldn't believe how big their new house is.
    你一定不敢相信他們的新房子有多大。
  3. You wouldn't believe what my little brother said to the teacher.
    你一定不敢相信我弟弟跟老師說了什麼。
  4. You wouldn't believe how cold it was on the mountain.
    你一定不敢相信山上有多冷。
  5. You wouldn't believe who I met at the airport!
    你一定不敢相信我在機場遇到誰！
  6. You wouldn't believe how long we waited for the bus.
    你一定不敢相信我們等公車等了多久。

② I think + 主詞 + would not + 原形動詞：想像的情況

  1. We can stay with my aunt and cook our own meals. I think we would not need much money.
    我們可以住我阿姨家，自己煮飯。我想我們不會需要很多錢。
  2. I think the children would not enjoy such a long museum visit.
    我想孩子們不會喜歡那麼長的博物館參觀。
  3. I think my parents would not let me go alone.
    我想我爸媽不會讓我一個人去。
  4. I think a small car would not be big enough for all of us.
    我想一台小車不夠大，坐不下我們所有人。
  5. I think she would not like a surprise party.
    我想她不會喜歡驚喜派對。
  6. I think the shop would not open on a holiday.
    我想那家店放假不會開。

③ 主詞 + wouldn't + 原形動詞：想像中不會做的事

  1. I wouldn't eat that fish. It smells strange.
    我不會吃那條魚。它聞起來很怪。
  2. My dog wouldn't hurt anyone. He's very gentle.
    我的狗不會傷害任何人。牠很溫和。
  3. I wouldn't wear that hat. It's too big.
    我不會戴那頂帽子。它太大了。
  4. She wouldn't leave her children alone at home.
    她不會讓她的孩子單獨在家。
  5. I wouldn't take a small boat out on a windy day like this.
    在今天這種大風天，我不會開小船出海。
  6. He wouldn't lie to his best friend.
    他不會對他最好的朋友說謊。

④ I wouldn't + 原形動詞, if I were you：給建議

  1. I wouldn't buy that car if I were you. It's too old.
    如果我是你，我不會買那台車。它太舊了。
  2. I wouldn't tell her the truth yet, if I were you.
    如果我是你，我還不會告訴她真相。
  3. I wouldn't swim there if I were you. The water is dirty.
    如果我是你，我不會在那裡游泳。水很髒。
  4. I wouldn't call him now if I were you. He's very angry.
    如果我是你，我現在不會打給他。他很生氣。
  5. I wouldn't wear white to the farm if I were you.
    如果我是你，我不會穿白色的衣服去農場。
  6. I wouldn't spend all your money on the first day if I were you.
    如果我是你，我不會第一天就把錢花光。

⑤ 主詞 + wouldn't + 原形動詞：過去不肯做

  1. The baby wouldn't stop crying all night.
    寶寶整晚哭個不停。
  2. It was so cold that the old van wouldn't start until Dad pushed it.
    天氣太冷了，那台舊貨車發不動，直到爸爸推了它。
  3. My brother wouldn't share his sweets with me.
    我哥哥不肯跟我分他的糖果。
  4. The door wouldn't open because of the snow.
    因為下雪，門打不開。
  5. She wouldn't tell me where she had been.
    她不肯告訴我她去了哪裡。
  6. We called and called, but the kitten wouldn't come out from under the shed.
    我們叫了又叫，可是小貓就是不肯從棚子底下出來。
  7. My grandfather wouldn't take any money for the work.
    我爺爺做了那些工作卻不肯收錢。
