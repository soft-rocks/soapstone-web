例句

① What + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. You've been shopping all morning. What have you bought for your mother's birthday?
    你逛了一整個早上。你買了什麼給你媽媽當生日禮物？
  2. Christmas is in two weeks. What does your daughter want this year?
    再兩個星期就是聖誕節。你女兒今年想要什麼？
  3. I signed up for the cooking course yesterday. What must I bring to the first class?
    我昨天報名了烹飪課。第一堂課我得帶什麼？
  4. You took Max to the clinic this morning. What did the vet say about his leg?
    你今天早上帶小麥去診所。獸醫怎麼說牠的腿？
  5. You always have a book in your bag. What are you reading at the moment?
    你包包裡總是有一本書。你最近在看什麼書？
  6. Eight people are coming to dinner. What will you cook for the guests on Saturday?
    有八個人要來吃晚餐。星期六你要煮什麼給客人吃？

② Where + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. Your story won first prize. Where did you get the idea for it?
    你的故事得了第一名。你故事的點子是從哪裡來的？
  2. We're new in the area. Where do your kids go to school?
    我們剛搬來這一區。你的孩子在哪裡上學？
  3. The kids will be home soon. Where have you hidden the birthday presents?
    孩子們快回家了。你把生日禮物藏到哪裡去了？
  4. My flight is delayed, and my battery is low. Where can I recharge my phone in the airport?
    我的班機誤點，手機又快沒電了。機場裡哪裡可以幫手機充電？
  5. School finishes next week. Where are you spending the summer holidays?
    學校下星期就放假了。你暑假要去哪裡過？

③ When + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. I miss you already. When are you coming back from Japan?
    我已經在想你了。你什麼時候從日本回來？
  2. The client wants a plan by Friday. When can we meet to talk about the project?
    客戶星期五前要一份計畫。我們什麼時候可以見面討論專案？
  3. Your English is excellent. When did you start learning it?
    你的英文很棒。你什麼時候開始學的？
  4. I want to swim before breakfast on Saturday. When does the pool open on weekends?
    我星期六想在早餐前游泳。游泳池週末幾點開？
  5. You finished your last exam today. When will you know your results?
    你今天考完最後一科。你什麼時候會知道成績？

④ How + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. You had a high fever yesterday. How do you feel today?
    你昨天發高燒。你今天覺得怎麼樣？
  2. There's glass all over the floor. How did the kids break the kitchen window?
    地板上到處都是玻璃。孩子們是怎麼把廚房窗戶打破的？
  3. I asked the man at reception for directions. How can I get to the museum from the hotel?
    我問櫃台的人怎麼走。我要怎麼從飯店去博物館？
  4. I'm booking the table for you. How do you spell your surname?
    我在幫你訂位。你的姓怎麼拼？
  5. Your husband was away on business. How have you managed with the baby this week?
    你先生出差了。這個禮拜你帶寶寶還應付得來嗎？

⑤ Who / Why / Which + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. The tickets were all for different seats. Who did you sit next to at the concert?
    票都是不同的位子。你在演唱會上坐在誰旁邊？
  2. You're at the post office again. Who are you sending that parcel to?
    你又在郵局了。你那個包裹要寄給誰？
  3. You loved playing music. Why did you sell your old piano?
    你很愛彈琴。你為什麼把舊鋼琴賣了？
  4. The interviewer smiled and asked the first question. Why do you want this job?
    面試官笑著問了第一個問題。你為什麼想要這份工作？
  5. The station is very big. Which platform should we wait on for the Tainan train?
    車站很大。我們應該在哪個月台等去台南的火車？
  6. You went to the cinema last night. Which film did you watch?
    你昨天晚上去看電影。你看了哪一部電影？
  7. You're away for two weeks. Who will you ask to look after your cat?
    你要出門兩個星期。你會找誰幫你照顧貓？
  8. I'll drive you to the station. What time does the train leave?
    我開車送你去車站。火車幾點開？
  9. It looks very expensive. How much did you pay for that jacket?
    它看起來很貴。那件夾克你付了多少錢？
