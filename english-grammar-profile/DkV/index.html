I don't like trousers as much as you do.

我沒有像你那麼喜歡長褲。

第⁠二⁠個 as 後⁠面⁠接子⁠句

I don't like trousers as much as you do.

考試沒有我想的那麼難。

子⁠句⁠的動⁠詞常⁠用助⁠動⁠詞代⁠替

I don't like trousers as much as you do.

我沒有像以前讀書時那麼常玩電玩了。

did 代替 played video games。

as ... as I can 表⁠示盡我所⁠能

I don't like trousers as much as you do.

I'll help as much as I can.

我會盡量幫忙。

as ... as 中⁠間用原⁠級

I don't like trousers as much as you do.

It's not as colder as I expected.

It's not as cold as I expected.

as ... as 前⁠後都⁠是 as

I don't like trousers as much as you do.

She doesn't run as fast than I do.

She doesn't run as fast as I do.

代⁠替一⁠般動⁠詞用 did，不⁠用 was

I don't like trousers as much as you do.

I don't read as much as I was at school.

I don't read as much as I did at school.

例句

① as + 形容詞 + as + 子句

  1. We finally found a place with three bedrooms. The flat is as big as we wanted.
    我們終於找到一間有三個臥室的地方。那間公寓跟我們想要的一樣大。
  2. I finally saw the new Bond film. It was as good as everyone said.
    我終於看了新的 007 電影。它跟大家說的一樣好看。
  3. I went back to Crete after twenty years. The beach was as beautiful as I remembered.
    我隔了二十年又回到克里特島。那片海灘跟我記憶中的一樣美。
  4. You started at the bank last week. Is the new job as hard as you thought?
    你上星期開始在銀行上班。新工作跟你想的一樣難嗎？
  5. We sat on it for an hour last night. The new sofa is as comfortable as we hoped.
    我們昨天晚上在上面坐了一個小時。新沙發跟我們希望的一樣舒服。

② not as + 形容詞 + as + 子句

  1. I swam for half an hour in Scotland. The sea was not as cold as I expected.
    我在蘇格蘭游了半個小時。海水沒有我預期的那麼冷。
  2. The managers smiled and asked easy questions. The interview wasn't as scary as I feared.
    經理們面帶微笑，問了簡單的問題。面試沒有我擔心的那麼可怕。
  3. Our room is dark and small. The hotel isn't as nice as it looks online.
    我們的房間又暗又小。那家飯店沒有網路上看起來那麼好。
  4. My uncle has two grandchildren. He's not as young as he looks.
    我叔叔有兩個孫子了。他沒有看起來那麼年輕。
  5. The factory closed, and many families left. The town isn't as busy as it used to be.
    工廠關了，很多家庭都搬走了。這個小鎮沒有以前那麼熱鬧了。

③ as + 副詞 + as + 主詞 + 助動詞

  1. Tom doesn't enjoy shopping as much as his wife does.
    Tom 沒有他太太那麼喜歡逛街。
  2. They play outside after school now. My kids don't watch TV as much as they did last year.
    他們現在放學後都在外面玩。我的孩子沒有像去年那麼常看電視了。
  3. My niece came second in the race. She can't swim as fast as her brother can.
    我姪女比賽拿第二名。她游得沒有她哥哥那麼快。
  4. Dad made dinner tonight, but he doesn't cook as well as Mum does.
    爸爸今天晚上煮晚餐，可是他煮得沒有媽媽好。
  5. We're always tired these days. We don't go out as much as we did before the baby.
    我們最近總是很累。有了寶寶以後，我們沒有像以前那麼常出門。
  6. I lived in Paris twenty years ago. I can't speak French as well as I could then.
    我二十年前住在巴黎。我的法文沒有那時候說得那麼好了。

④ as much as I can / as soon as you can

  1. I'll practice as often as I can before the concert.
    演唱會前我會盡量多練習。
  2. Your father has fallen in the garden. Please come as soon as you can.
    你爸爸在花園裡跌倒了。請盡快過來。
  3. I'll answer your emails as quickly as I can while you're away.
    你不在的時候，我會盡快幫你回電子郵件。
  4. There's lots of food left from the party. Eat as much as you want.
    派對剩了很多食物。想吃多少就吃多少。
  5. The bus is leaving! Run as fast as you can!
    公車要開了！盡你所能地快跑！

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. My daughter got sick, so we came home early. The trip wasn't as long as we had planned.
    我女兒生病了，所以我們提早回家。這趟旅行沒有我們原本計畫的那麼長。
  2. The advert says you can learn Spanish in a month. Is it as easy as it sounds?
    廣告說一個月就可以學會西班牙文。它有聽起來那麼容易嗎？
  3. She goes to the market on foot every day. My grandmother still walks as much as she did ten years ago.
    她每天走路去市場。我奶奶現在走的路跟十年前一樣多。
  4. It was on sale for half price. The new phone wasn't as expensive as I feared.
    它在打對折。新手機沒有我擔心的那麼貴。
  5. Come on, only one more kilometre. You're not as tired as you think you are.
    加油，只剩一公里了。你沒有你以為的那麼累。
  6. He's eighty-five now. My grandfather doesn't drive as fast as he used to.
    他現在八十五歲了。我爺爺開車沒有以前那麼快了。
  7. We agreed on everything quickly. The meeting didn't last as long as we expected.
    我們很快就什麼都同意了。會議沒有我們預期的那麼久。
  8. Our children live abroad now. They don't visit us as often as they did.
    我們的孩子現在住在國外。他們沒有像以前那麼常來看我們了。
  9. You waited a year for the new comedy. Was the film as funny as you'd hoped?
    你等了一年那部新喜劇。那部電影有你希望的那麼好笑嗎？
