例句

① look as if / as though

  1. The windows are empty, and there's a sign on the door. It looks as if the shop has closed for good.
    櫥窗是空的，門上還貼了一張告示。那家店看起來好像永遠關門了。
  2. It's two in the afternoon, and you're still in your pyjamas. You look as if you've just woken up.
    現在下午兩點了，你還穿著睡衣。你看起來好像剛睡醒。
  3. From your email, it looks as if you need some help.
    從你的電子郵件看來，你好像需要幫忙。
  4. The garden is full of weeds, and the curtains are always closed. The house looks as though nobody lives there.
    花園長滿了雜草，窗簾總是拉著。那棟房子看起來好像沒人住。
  5. That man keeps checking his pockets. He looks as if he's lost something.
    那個男人一直摸他的口袋。他看起來好像丟了什麼東西。
  6. Listen to all that laughing in the garden. The kids look as though they're having fun.
    你聽花園裡那些笑聲。孩子們看起來好像玩得很開心。

② feel as if / as though

  1. On the swing, I felt as if I was flying.
    盪鞦韆的時候，我感覺自己好像在飛。
  2. When you walk into the café, you feel as if you're in Paris.
    一走進那家咖啡店，你會感覺好像到了巴黎。
  3. The old street made me feel as though I was back in the past.
    那條老街讓我感覺好像回到了過去。
  4. We only met last week, but I feel as if I've known you for years.
    我們上星期才認識，可是我感覺好像認識你好多年了。
  5. My sister walked into the party alone. She felt as though everyone was looking at her.
    我姊姊一個人走進派對。她覺得好像每個人都在看她。
  6. We climbed for six hours today. My legs feel as if they're made of stone.
    我們今天爬了六個小時。我的腿感覺好像是石頭做的。

③ sound as if / as though

  1. You danced until three in the morning? It sounds as if the party was a lot of fun.
    你跳舞跳到凌晨三點？聽起來派對好像很好玩。
  2. Your voice is very different on the phone today. You sound as if you have a cold.
    你今天在電話裡聲音很不一樣。你聽起來好像感冒了。
  3. Turn the TV down for a minute. That sounds as though someone is at the door.
    把電視關小聲一下。聽起來好像有人在門口。
  4. Listen to that loud noise. The car sounds as if it needs a new engine.
    你聽那個很大的聲音。這輛車聽起來好像需要換引擎了。
  5. My friend called me late last night. She sounded as though she was crying.
    我朋友昨天深夜打給我。她聽起來好像在哭。

④ seem as if / as though

  1. There are thousands of people at the summer fair. It seems as if the whole town is here.
    夏季市集有好幾千人。好像整個鎮的人都在這裡。
  2. It rained every day of our holiday in Wales. It seemed as though the rain would never stop.
    我們在威爾斯度假的每一天都在下雨。雨好像永遠不會停。
  3. The delivery man didn't even say hello. He seems as if he's in a hurry.
    送貨員連招呼都沒打。他好像在趕時間。
  4. Their car isn't outside, and the lights are off. It seems as though they've already left.
    他們的車不在外面，燈也關了。他們好像已經走了。
  5. We got lost in the forest. It seemed as if we'd walked for hours.
    我們在森林裡迷路了。我們好像走了好幾個小時。

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. I'm driving to the mountains tomorrow. Does it look as if it'll snow?
    我明天要開車去山上。看起來會下雪嗎？
  2. It's nine o'clock, and our guests haven't called. It doesn't look as if they're coming.
    已經九點了，我們的客人都沒打電話來。看起來他們好像不會來了。
  3. I came home from school and opened the door. The kitchen smelled as if someone had been baking.
    我放學回家打開門。廚房聞起來好像有人在烤東西。
  4. Quick, check the toaster! The bread smells as if it's burning.
    快，看一下烤麵包機！麵包聞起來好像在燒焦了。
  5. Check the date on the bottle. This milk tastes as though it's gone bad.
    看一下瓶子上的日期。這牛奶喝起來好像壞了。
  6. It's cold and very bitter. This coffee tastes as if it's been sitting there all day.
    它又冷又很苦。這咖啡喝起來好像放了一整天。
  7. Your face is white. Why do you look as if you've seen a ghost?
    你的臉好白。你為什麼一副見到鬼的樣子？
  8. The kids had a pillow fight while we were out. The room looked as if a storm had passed through it.
    我們出門的時候，孩子們在玩枕頭大戰。房間看起來好像被暴風吹過一樣。
