Not only did I find high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers and plane manufacturers.

我不但找到了高科技公司，也找到了做鞋子和造飛機的廠商。

Not only 放⁠在句⁠首，did 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Not only did I find high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers and plane manufacturers.

我不但找到了高科技公司，

說話者參觀商展後寫報告給經理。

but ... also 接上第⁠二件⁠事，不⁠倒裝

Not only did I find high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers and plane manufacturers.

也找到了做鞋子和造飛機的廠商。

but also 後⁠面可⁠以只接名⁠詞

Not only did I find high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers and plane manufacturers.

Not only did we visit Rome, but also Florence and Venice.

我們不但去了羅馬，還去了佛羅倫斯和威尼斯。

not only 不⁠在句⁠首，不⁠倒裝

Not only did I find high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers and plane manufacturers.

動詞前面

I not only found high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers.

連接名詞

I found not only high-technology companies but also shoemakers.

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式用 have 倒⁠裝

Not only did I find high-technology companies, but I also found shoemakers and plane manufacturers.

Not only have I visited the factory, but I have also met the staff.

我不但參觀過工廠，也見過員工了。

例句

① Not only did + 主詞 + 原形動詞, but + 主詞 + also + 過去式

  1. Not only did she win the race, but she also broke the school record.
    她不但贏了比賽，還打破了學校的紀錄。
  2. Not only did they cancel the flight, but they also refused to give us a refund.
    他們不但取消了班機，還拒絕退錢給我們。
  3. Not only did he forget my birthday, but he also forgot our anniversary.
    他不但忘了我的生日，還忘了我們的紀念日。
  4. Not only did we enjoy the food, but we also loved the music.
    我們不但喜歡那裡的菜，也很喜歡那裡的音樂。
  5. Not only did the storm damage the roof, but it also knocked down two trees.
    暴風雨不但吹壞了屋頂，還吹倒了兩棵樹。
  6. Not only did I meet the author, but I also got her autograph.
    我不但見到了作者，還拿到了她的簽名。

② but also 後面只接名詞

  1. Not only did I find high-technology companies, but also shoemakers and plane manufacturers.
    我不但找到了高科技公司，還找到了做鞋子和造飛機的廠商。
  2. Not only did we visit Rome, but also Florence and Venice.
    我們不但去了羅馬，還去了佛羅倫斯和威尼斯。
  3. Not only did she speak to the manager, but also to the owner.
    她不但跟經理談過，還跟老闆談過。
  4. Not only did they bring flowers, but also a cake.
    他們不但帶了花，還帶了一個蛋糕。

③ Not only didn't + 主詞

  1. Not only didn't we travel 5,000 miles, as you had advertised, but we also stayed eight days in one place.
    我們不但沒有像你們廣告說的走了五千英里，還在一個地方待了八天。
  2. Not only didn't I learn any new grammar, but I also couldn't improve my vocabulary.
    我不但沒學到新的文法，詞彙也沒有進步。
  3. Not only didn't he apologise, but he also blamed me.
    他不但沒道歉，還怪我。
  4. Not only didn't the guide turn up, but nobody told us why.
    導遊不但沒出現，也沒有人告訴我們原因。

④ Not only was / were + 主詞

  1. Not only was the hotel dirty, but it was also noisy.
    那家飯店不但髒，還很吵。
  2. Not only were the tickets expensive, but the seats were also terrible.
    票不但很貴，座位還很差。
  3. Not only was he late, but he also forgot the documents.
    他不但遲到，還忘了帶文件。
  4. Not only was the course useful, but it was also fun.
    那門課不但有用，也很有趣。

⑤ 正式的書信和報告

  1. Not only did the trip fail to match your description, but it also cost more than we had agreed.
    這趟旅行不但跟你們的描述不符，費用也比我們說好的還高。
  2. Not only did the delivery arrive late, but several items were also missing.
    貨不但晚到，還少了好幾樣東西。
  3. Not only did the event raise money for the hospital, but it also brought the community together.
    這次活動不但幫醫院募到了錢，也讓社區的人聚在一起。
  4. Not only did our sales increase, but we also opened two new branches.
    我們的業績不但成長了，還開了兩家新分店。
  5. Not only did the staff ignore my complaint, but they also lost my booking.
    員工不但不理我的投訴，還把我的訂位弄丟了。

⑥ 第二件事不用 also

  1. Not only did he pass the exam, but he got the highest mark in the class.
    他不但考過了，還拿到全班最高分。
  2. Not only did she cook dinner, but she even washed the dishes.
    她不但煮了晚餐，甚至還洗了碗。
  3. Not only did they lose the match, but two players were injured as well.
    他們不但輸了比賽，還有兩個球員受傷。
  4. Not only did we miss the train, but we had to wait three hours for the next one.
    我們不但錯過了火車，還得等三個小時才有下一班。

⑦ 口語和故事

  1. Not only did he eat all the pizza, but he also drank my juice!
    他不但把披薩吃光，還喝了我的果汁！
  2. Not only did it rain all week, but the heating also broke down.
    不但整個禮拜都在下雨，暖氣還壞了。
  3. Not only did the kids clean their rooms, but they also made us breakfast.
    孩子們不但打掃了房間，還幫我們做了早餐。
  4. Not only did I get lost, but my phone also ran out of battery.
    我不但迷路了，手機還沒電了。
