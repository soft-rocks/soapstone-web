there + be + 不⁠定代⁠名⁠詞

When I arrived at his house, there wasn't anyone there.

我到他家的時候，那裡一個人也沒有。

動⁠詞後⁠面、介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

動詞的受詞

I didn't know anyone at the party.

派對上我一個人都不認識。
介系詞的受詞

She talked to everyone in the room.

她跟房間裡每個人都說了話。

there + be 後⁠面、補⁠語

there + be

There was nothing on the table.

桌上什麼都沒有。
補語

That's everything for today.

今天就這些了。

not + any⁠-⁠ 等⁠於 no⁠-⁠

When I arrived at his house, there wasn't anyone there.

not + any-

There wasn't anyone in the car.

車上沒有人。
no-

There was no one in the car.

車上一個人也沒有。

一⁠句只⁠用一⁠個否⁠定

When I arrived at his house, there wasn't anyone there.

We didn't eat nothing on the long flight.

We didn't eat anything on the long flight.

肯⁠定⁠句說沒⁠有⁠人，用 no one

There was anyone at home.

There was no one at home.

everywhere 前⁠面不⁠加 to

He has been to everywhere in Europe.

He has been everywhere in Europe.

any 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞

路上我一輛車都沒看到。

none of + 名⁠詞

我的表兄弟姊妹沒有一個能來參加婚禮。

例句

① 當動詞的受詞

  1. I need something for my headache.
    我需要吃點治頭痛的藥。
  2. She invited everyone from work.
    她邀請了公司的每個人。
  3. We didn't hear anything during the night.
    我們晚上什麼聲音都沒聽到。
  4. He told nobody about the accident.
    他沒跟任何人說那場意外。
  5. Did you bring anything to read on the train?
    你有帶什麼在火車上看的東西嗎？
  6. I want to show you something in the garden.
    我想給你看花園裡的一樣東西。

② 當介系詞的受詞

  1. Don't tell this to anyone.
    這件事別跟任何人說。
  2. He's afraid of nothing.
    他什麼都不怕。
  3. She's kind to everybody.
    她對每個人都很好。
  4. I got a letter from someone in Canada.
    我收到一封加拿大的人寄來的信。
  5. We talked about everything and nothing.
    我們天南地北什麼都聊。

③ there + be + 不定代名詞

  1. There's someone at the door.
    門口有人。
  2. There was nothing in the post today.
    今天沒有收到任何信。
  3. Is there anything I can do?
    有什麼我可以幫忙的嗎？
  4. There wasn't anybody in the waiting room.
    候診室裡一個人都沒有。
  5. There's something strange about that man.
    那個男人有點怪怪的。

④ 否定句和問句

  1. My handbag is so full that I can't find anything in it.
    我的手提包塞得太滿，裡面的東西我什麼都找不到。
  2. Have you met anyone new at school?
    你在學校有認識新朋友嗎？
  3. It was raining, so we didn't meet anybody on the mountain path.
    那時在下雨，所以我們在山路上一個人都沒遇到。
  4. Do you know anyone who can fix a bike?
    你認識會修腳踏車的人嗎？
  5. The baby won't drink anything today.
    寶寶今天什麼都不肯喝。

⑤ 地方

  1. We travelled everywhere by train.
    我們到哪裡都是搭火車。
  2. Let's go somewhere warm this winter.
    今年冬天我們去個溫暖的地方吧。
  3. We couldn't find a hotel room anywhere in town.
    城裡哪裡都訂不到飯店房間。
  4. Park anywhere you like in the car park behind the hotel.
    飯店後面的停車場，你想停哪裡都可以。
  5. There's nowhere to hide in this room.
    這個房間沒有地方可以躲。

⑥ 當補語

  1. "Thanks for your help." "It was nothing."
    謝謝你幫忙。小事一樁。
  2. That's everything I know about him.
    我對他的了解就這些。
  3. Before the film, he was nobody.
    演那部電影之前，他只是個無名小卒。
  4. His new job is something to do with computers.
    他的新工作跟電腦有關。
  5. This isn't anything new.
    這沒什麼新鮮的。
