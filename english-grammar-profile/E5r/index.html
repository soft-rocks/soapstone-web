May I give you a short presentation on 17th December?

我可以問你一下我住宿的事嗎？

May + I + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

May I give you a short presentation on 17th December?

May放在句首。
IMay 的問句主詞通常是 I，有時候是 we。
原形動詞想做的事。

May I ask

例句

May I ask if the price includes tax?

請問價格有包含稅嗎？

May I ask 用來有禮貌地問問題。後面可以接名詞、if 子句或疑問詞子句。

子⁠句用直⁠述⁠句⁠的順⁠序

例句

May I ask when the train leaves?

請問火車什麼時候開？

if 或疑問詞後面的子句用直述句的順序，主詞在動詞前面。

ask 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句用直⁠述⁠句⁠的順⁠序

May I ask where is the office?

May I ask where the office is?

may 後⁠面⁠的主⁠詞用 I

May I give you a short presentation on 17th December?

May me ask a question?

May I ask a question?

是⁠非問⁠題要⁠用 if 或 whether 帶⁠出子⁠句

May I ask you we will get a discount?

May I ask you if we will get a discount?

例句

① May I + 原形動詞：正式的請求

  1. May I arrange a meeting with your sales team next month?
    我可以安排下個月跟貴公司的業務團隊開會嗎？
  2. May I call you next week to discuss the details?
    我可以下星期打電話給您討論細節嗎？
  3. May I send you a copy of our new catalogue?
    我可以寄一份我們的新目錄給您嗎？
  4. May I visit your office on Monday morning?
    我可以星期一早上去您的辦公室拜訪嗎？
  5. May I introduce myself? My name is Anna Clark.
    我可以自我介紹嗎？我叫安娜．克拉克。
  6. May I make a suggestion about the timetable?
    我可以對時間表提一個建議嗎？
  7. May I change my booking to the following week?
    我可以把預約改到下一個星期嗎？

② May I ask you about + 名詞

  1. I'm starting the course in September. May I ask you about my accommodation?
    我九月要開始上課。我可以請問您住宿的事嗎？
  2. May I ask you about the parking at the hotel?
    我可以請問您飯店停車的事嗎？
  3. May I ask you about the job you advertised?
    我可以請問您刊登的那份工作嗎？
  4. May I ask you about the cost of the trip?
    我可以請問您這趟旅行的費用嗎？
  5. May I ask you about your experience in sales?
    我可以請問您在銷售方面的經驗嗎？
  6. May I ask you about the meals on the course?
    我可以請問您課程期間的餐點嗎？

③ May I ask (you) if / whether + 子句

  1. We've ordered from you for many years. May I ask you if our company will receive the usual ten per cent discount?
    我們跟貴公司訂貨很多年了。請問我們公司還會拿到平常的九折優惠嗎？
  2. May I ask if there's a lift in the building?
    請問大樓裡有電梯嗎？
  3. May I ask whether breakfast is included?
    請問有包含早餐嗎？
  4. May I ask if you have any rooms free in July?
    請問七月有空房嗎？
  5. May I ask you whether the tickets can be changed?
    請問門票可以更改嗎？（被動）
  6. May I ask if students get a cheaper price?
    請問學生有比較便宜的價錢嗎？

④ May I ask + 疑問詞 + 子句

  1. May I ask when the course starts?
    請問課程什麼時候開始？
  2. May I ask how long the delivery will take?
    請問送貨要多久？
  3. May I ask who is in charge of the project?
    請問誰負責這個專案？
  4. May I ask what time the museum closes?
    請問博物館幾點關門？
  5. May I ask why my order hasn't arrived yet?
    請問為什麼我的訂單還沒到？
  6. May I ask where I can leave my luggage?
    請問我可以把行李放在哪裡？

⑤ May I + have / see / speak to：電話和櫃台

  1. Good morning. May I speak to Mr Davis, please?
    早安。請問我可以找戴維斯先生嗎？
  2. May I have your name and the number of your booking, please?
    請問您的大名和訂位編號是？
  3. May I see your passport and the letter from your school?
    我可以看一下您的護照和學校的信嗎？
  4. May I have a receipt for the payment?
    我可以拿一張付款的收據嗎？
  5. May I leave a message for the manager?
    我可以留言給經理嗎？
  6. May I have a copy of the contract before I sign?
    簽約前我可以拿一份合約影本嗎？
