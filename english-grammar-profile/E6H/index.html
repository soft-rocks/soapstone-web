Perhaps I left it in your living room.

也許我把它忘在你家客廳了。

副⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面照⁠樣接主⁠詞和動⁠詞

Perhaps I left it in your living room.

也許我把它忘在你家客廳了。

However 連⁠接前一⁠句

Perhaps I left it in your living room.

我很想來。不過我只有星期一能上課。

各⁠個副⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

Perhaps I left it in your living room.

perhaps也許，比 maybe 正式
however不過，說出和前一句相反的事
suddenly突然
luckily幸好
usually、often通常、常常，也可以放在句中

同⁠一⁠句用 but，換一⁠句用 However

but

It was a lovely hotel, but it was too expensive.

那是一間很棒的旅館，不過太貴了。
However

It was a lovely hotel. However, it was too expensive.

那是一間很棒的旅館。不過，太貴了。

however 前⁠面⁠的句⁠子用句⁠號結⁠束

I like my job, however I want more money.

I like my job. However, I want more money.

but 和 however 只⁠用一⁠個

The museum was interesting. But however, it was very crowded.

The museum was interesting. However, it was very crowded.

句⁠首用副⁠詞 suddenly，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞 sudden

Sudden the dog started barking.

Suddenly the dog started barking.

lucky 把 y 改⁠成 i 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ly

Luckyly, nobody was hurt.

Luckily, nobody was hurt.

例句

① perhaps

  1. Perhaps the bakery on the corner sells birthday cakes.
    轉角那家麵包店也許有賣生日蛋糕。
  2. Perhaps we should take a taxi. It's getting late.
    也許我們該搭計程車，時間不早了。
  3. Perhaps you can ask your teacher for help.
    也許你可以請老師幫忙。
  4. Perhaps the train is late because of the snow.
    火車也許是因為下雪才誤點。
  5. Perhaps, your sister would like to come with us to the lake.
    也許你妹妹會想跟我們一起去湖邊。

② however

  1. The flat is very small. However, it's close to the city centre.
    那間公寓很小，不過離市中心很近。
  2. I wanted to go to the concert. However, all the tickets were sold.
    我本來想去那場音樂會，不過票全賣光了。
  3. My brother is good at maths. However, he hates science.
    我弟弟數學很好，不過他很討厭自然科學。
  4. The food at the hotel was great. However, the rooms were dirty.
    那間旅館的餐點很好，不過房間很髒。
  5. We planned to have a picnic. However, it rained all day.
    我們本來打算去野餐，不過下了一整天的雨。

③ suddenly

  1. Suddenly someone knocked on the door.
    突然有人敲門。
  2. We were watching TV. Suddenly the screen went black.
    我們正在看電視，螢幕突然黑掉了。
  3. Suddenly I remembered my keys were in the car.
    我突然想起鑰匙在車上。
  4. I was walking home. Suddenly it started to rain very hard.
    我在走路回家，突然下起了大雨。
  5. Suddenly, a cat jumped onto the table.
    一隻貓突然跳上了桌子。

④ luckily

  1. I missed the bus. Luckily, my friend gave me a lift.
    我錯過了公車，幸好我朋友載了我一程。
  2. Luckily, the weather was perfect for the wedding.
    幸好婚禮那天天氣好極了。
  3. I dropped my phone. Luckily it didn't break.
    我把手機摔到地上，幸好沒摔壞。
  4. Luckily, there was a chemist's near the hotel.
    幸好旅館附近有一家藥局。
  5. Luckily, my mum found my passport in the kitchen.
    幸好我媽在廚房找到了我的護照。

⑤ usually、often

  1. Usually I walk to work, but today I drove.
    我通常走路上班，不過今天開車。
  2. Often we eat dinner in front of the TV.
    我們常常邊看電視邊吃晚餐。
  3. Usually, the library is quiet in the mornings.
    圖書館早上通常很安靜。
  4. Often my grandad tells us stories about the war.
    我爺爺常常跟我們講戰爭時的故事。
  5. Usually the post arrives before lunch.
    郵件通常午餐前就送到了。

⑥ 句首的副詞 + 否定句

  1. Perhaps they don't know about the party.
    也許他們不知道有派對。
  2. Luckily, we didn't miss the start of the film.
    幸好我們沒錯過電影的開頭。
  3. The car is old. However, it doesn't use much petrol.
    那輛車很舊，不過不太耗油。
  4. Usually my cat doesn't go outside at night.
    我的貓晚上通常不出門。
  5. Suddenly I couldn't remember the name of my own street.
    我突然想不起自己住的那條街叫什麼。
  6. Often I don't have time for breakfast.
    我常常沒時間吃早餐。
