I've just received your email.

我剛收到你的電子郵件。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式加 just，說剛發⁠生⁠的事

I've just received your email.

我剛收到你的電子郵件。

說話者正在讀這封信，也正在回覆。

不⁠加 just 也⁠可⁠以，結⁠果到現⁠在還⁠在

I've just received your email.

我不能去派對。我媽媽右腳骨折了。

媽媽的腳現在還打著石膏，不能走路。

just 放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

I've just received your email.

I just have received your email.

I've just received your email.

有過⁠去⁠的時⁠間⁠點，用過⁠去簡⁠單式

I've just received your email.

I've just received your email two days ago.

I received your email two days ago.

has just 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I've just received your email.

Your mum has just leave for the station.

Your mum has just left for the station.

美⁠式英⁠語常⁠用 just 加過⁠去簡⁠單式

I've just received your email.

我可以晚點回電給你嗎？我剛到家。

just now 放⁠在句⁠尾，接過⁠去簡⁠單式

I've just received your email.

你姊姊剛剛打過電話來。她想跟你說話。

說經⁠驗⁠的現⁠在完⁠成⁠式，說發⁠生過幾⁠次

I've just received your email.

我在滑雪道上很小心。我的腳斷過兩次。

例句

① I / we / you / they + have just + 過去分詞

  1. Don't touch the walls. I've just finished painting the kitchen.
    不要碰牆壁。我剛把廚房漆完。
  2. We're celebrating tonight. We've just signed the contract for our new flat.
    我們今晚要慶祝。我們剛簽了新公寓的租約。
  3. Mr Lee left the office two minutes ago. You've just missed him.
    李先生兩分鐘前離開辦公室了。你剛好跟他錯過了。
  4. Good news for the concert. I've just booked our tickets.
    演唱會有好消息。我剛訂好我們的票。
  5. I got a message from my parents. They have just landed in Rome.
    我收到爸媽的訊息。他們剛在羅馬降落。
  6. I've just made some tea. Would you like some?
    我剛泡了茶，你要喝一點嗎？
  7. Turn on the radio. They've just announced the winners of the singing contest.
    把收音機打開。他們剛宣布歌唱比賽的得獎名單。

② he / she / it + has just + 過去分詞

  1. Hurry up and sit down. The match has just kicked off.
    快點坐下。比賽剛開打。
  2. Sorry, Maria isn't at her desk. She's just gone out for lunch.
    抱歉，瑪麗亞不在座位上。她剛出去吃午餐。
  3. Come and see the driveway. My dad has just bought a new car.
    來看看車道。我爸剛買了一輛新車。
  4. We're having a big party on Saturday. My grandmother has just turned ninety.
    我們星期六要辦一個大派對。我奶奶剛滿九十歲。
  5. Please talk quietly. The baby has just fallen asleep.
    請小聲說話。寶寶剛睡著。
  6. We can go for a walk now. It's just stopped raining.
    我們現在可以去散步了。雨剛停。

③ 不加 just：結果還在現在

  1. Stop worrying about money. I've found a job for you at my uncle's shop.
    別再擔心錢了。我幫你在我叔叔的店找到一份工作了。
  2. I'll have to take the bus today. Someone has stolen my bike.
    我今天得搭公車了。有人把我的腳踏車偷走了。
  3. I can't open the front door. Oh no, I've forgotten my keys.
    我打不開前門。糟了，我忘了帶鑰匙。
  4. Can you bring a plaster? Tom has cut his finger.
    你可以拿一片 OK 繃來嗎？湯姆割到手指了。
  5. We'll have to buy milk somewhere else. The shop has run out.
    我們得去別的地方買牛奶了。這家店賣完了。
  6. Look out of the window. Everything is white. It's snowed!
    你看窗外。一片白。下雪了！
  7. I can't pay for lunch. I've lost my wallet.
    我沒辦法付午餐錢。我的錢包不見了。

④ 問句和回答

  1. The glass is so clear. Have you just cleaned the windows?
    玻璃好乾淨。你剛擦過窗戶嗎？
  2. I want to wash the dishes. Has the plumber fixed the sink?
    我想洗碗。水電工修好水槽了嗎？
  3. Has Anna just got home? — Yes, she has. She's in her room.
    安娜剛到家嗎？對，剛到。她在房間裡。
  4. Can we start the dinner? Have the guests arrived? — Yes, they've just come in.
    我們可以開始晚餐了嗎？客人到了嗎？到了，他們剛進來。

⑤ 先說剛發生的事，再說現在的情況

  1. I've just washed the floor, so don't walk on it.
    我剛拖完地，不要踩上去。
  2. Have you seen Amy today? She's just had her hair cut, and it looks great.
    你今天看到艾美了嗎？她剛剪了頭髮，看起來很好看。
  3. Thanks for the sandwich, but I've just eaten, so I'm not hungry.
    謝謝你的三明治，不過我剛吃過，所以不餓。
  4. Be careful with the front door. Dad has just painted it, and it's still wet.
    小心前門。爸爸剛漆好，還沒乾。
  5. We've just heard about your new baby, and we're so happy for you.
    我們剛聽到你生寶寶的消息，真替你高興。
  6. The kids have just gone to bed, so please be quiet.
    孩子們剛去睡，請小聲一點。
