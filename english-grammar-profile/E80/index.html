Both are very good films.

這兩部電影都很好看。

當主⁠詞和當受⁠詞

Both are very good films.

當主詞

I have two brothers. Both live in London.

我有兩個哥哥，兩個都住在倫敦。
當受詞

I think you'll enjoy both.

我想兩個你都會喜歡。

both 後⁠面用複⁠數動⁠詞

Both are very good films.

I bought two plants, and both is growing well.

I bought two plants, and both are growing well.

三⁠個以⁠上用 all

Both are very good films.

I have three sisters, and both are teachers.

I have three sisters, and all are teachers.

another 後⁠面不⁠加 ones

These biscuits are nice. Can I have another ones?

These biscuits are nice. Can I have another?

幾⁠個用 a few

I don't need many stamps. Few will do.

I don't need many stamps. A few will do.

兩⁠個⁠裡⁠的另⁠一⁠個：the other

一輛是紅的，另一輛是黑的。

both of + 受格代⁠名⁠詞

兩部都是很好看的電影。

例句

① Both 當主詞

  1. I've got two cousins in Spain. Both speak English.
    我在西班牙有兩個表哥，兩個都會說英文。
  2. Swimming and cycling are great exercise. Both keep you fit.
    游泳和騎腳踏車都是很好的運動，兩個都能讓你保持健康。
  3. We looked at two houses, and both had big gardens.
    我們看了兩間房子，兩間都有大花園。
  4. My parents are retired. Both enjoy gardening.
    我爸媽都退休了，兩個都喜歡園藝。
  5. I tried the two pizzas, and both were delicious.
    我吃了那兩種披薩，兩種都很好吃。

② Both 當受詞

  1. Tea or coffee? I'll have both, please.
    茶還是咖啡？兩個我都要，謝謝。
  2. I couldn't choose between the shirts, so I bought both.
    我挑不出要哪一件襯衫，所以兩件都買了。
  3. She has two dogs and walks both every morning.
    她養了兩隻狗，每天早上帶兩隻去散步。
  4. The beach or the mountains? I love both.
    海邊還是山上？兩個我都喜歡。
  5. He invited his two best friends, and I know both.
    他邀了兩個最好的朋友，兩個我都認識。

③ A few 當主詞

  1. Many people came to the meeting, but only a few asked questions.
    很多人來開會，可是只有幾個人提問。
  2. Most of the shops were closed, but a few were still open.
    大部分的店都關了，可是還有幾家開著。
  3. I wrote to several companies, and a few replied.
    我寫信給好幾家公司，有幾家回覆了。
  4. Lots of students live far away, but a few walk to school.
    很多學生住得很遠，可是有幾個走路上學。
  5. The apples were mostly bad, but a few were fine.
    蘋果大部分都壞了，可是有幾顆還好。

④ A few 當受詞

  1. Would you like some grapes? Just a few, thanks.
    要吃葡萄嗎？只要幾顆就好，謝謝。
  2. I didn't take many photos, just a few.
    我沒拍很多照片，只拍了幾張。
  3. There are lots of books on the list. I'm happy to select a few.
    清單上的書很多，我很樂意挑幾本。
  4. We need more chairs. Can you bring a few?
    我們需要更多椅子，你可以搬幾張過來嗎？
  5. I know most of the songs, but I've forgotten a few.
    大部分的歌我都會，可是有幾首忘了。

⑤ another

  1. That cake was lovely. Can I have another?
    那塊蛋糕很好吃，可以再來一塊嗎？
  2. I've lost my umbrella, so I need to buy another.
    我的雨傘弄丟了，所以要再買一把。
  3. One student finished early, and then another did.
    一個學生先寫完，接著又一個寫完了。
  4. This pen doesn't work. Have you got another?
    這支筆寫不出來，你還有別支嗎？
  5. Moving from one area to another is not easy.
    從一個地區搬到另一個地區並不容易。

⑥ 在對話和信裡

  1. You asked about the two films. Both are worth seeing.
    你問到那兩部電影，兩部都值得看。
  2. I've got lots of magazines. Take a few if you like.
    我有很多雜誌，你想要就拿幾本。
  3. The first bus was full, so we waited for another.
    第一班公車客滿了，所以我們等下一班。
  4. There are many TV programmes in my country, but only a few are any good.
    我們國家的電視節目很多，可是好看的沒幾個。
  5. I've read two of her books, and I enjoyed both.
    她的書我讀過兩本，兩本都很喜歡。
  6. One problem was solved, but then another appeared.
    一個問題解決了，接著又出現另一個。
