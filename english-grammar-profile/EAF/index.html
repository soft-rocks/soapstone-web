The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

唯一的問題是，他們晚上有點太吵了。

主⁠詞先說⁠出性⁠質，that 子⁠句再⁠說出那⁠件⁠事

唯一的問題是，他們晚上有點太吵了。

名⁠詞說⁠出那⁠件⁠事是什⁠麼：事⁠實、重⁠點、麻⁠煩或原⁠因

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

fact
事實

The simple fact is that we can't afford it.

事實很簡單，我們負擔不起。
point
重點

The important point is that the test is free.

重點是這個檢查是免費的。
problem
麻煩

The biggest problem is that the bus only comes once an hour.

最大的問題是公車一小時才來一班。
reason
原因

The main reason is that the rent is cheaper there.

主要的原因是那裡的房租比較便宜。

thing 最常用，可以接任何事。

口⁠語可⁠以省⁠略 that，is 後⁠面常加逗⁠號

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

有 that

The funny thing is that he doesn't even like coffee.

好笑的是，他根本不喜歡咖啡。
省略 that

The funny thing is, he doesn't even drink tea.

好笑的是，他連茶都不喝。

事⁠情發⁠生在過⁠去，is 改⁠成 was

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

奇怪的是，門已經開著了。

名⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 The

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

Only problem is that the car is too small.

The only problem is that the car is too small.

主⁠詞後⁠面要⁠有動⁠詞 is

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

The main reason that the flat is closer to work.

The main reason is that the flat is closer to work.

is 後⁠面⁠的完⁠整子⁠句用 that 帶⁠出⁠來

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

The strange thing is what nobody noticed the noise.

The strange thing is that nobody noticed the noise.

what 子句裡要少一個成分，像 What I need is time。

後⁠面只⁠說一⁠件⁠事，名⁠詞用單⁠數

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

The good things is that the hotel has a pool.

The good thing is that the hotel has a pool.

The thing is 沒⁠有修⁠飾⁠語，用來⁠說出麻⁠煩或藉⁠口

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

問題是，我時間不夠。

What 分⁠裂句用 What 子⁠句當主⁠詞

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

The + 修飾語 + 名詞

The only problem is that they are a little too noisy at night.

唯一的問題是，他們晚上有點太吵了。
What 子句

What worries me is the cost.

讓我擔心的是費用。

例句

① thing

  1. The best thing is that the course is completely free.
    最棒的是，這門課完全免費。
  2. The worst thing was that nobody told us about the delay.
    最糟的是，根本沒人告訴我們會延誤。
  3. The strange thing is that the dog never barks at him.
    奇怪的是，那隻狗從來不對他叫。
  4. The nice thing about this town is that everyone says hello.
    這個小鎮好的地方是大家都會打招呼。
  5. The sad thing was that she never saw her son again.
    令人難過的是，她再也沒見到她兒子。
  6. The annoying thing is, the printer works fine when the technician is here.
    氣人的是，技師在的時候印表機都好好的。
  7. The only thing is that I'll need a lift home.
    只是我需要有人載我回家。

② fact

  1. The sad fact is that many children here never finish school.
    令人難過的事實是，這裡很多孩子沒把書念完。
  2. The simple fact is that the bridge is not safe.
    事實很簡單，這座橋不安全。
  3. The plain fact is that we spent too much last month.
    說白了，我們上個月花太多錢了。
  4. The surprising fact is that most of the visitors are local.
    令人意外的是，大部分的遊客都是本地人。

③ point

  1. The main point is that we need to finish by Friday.
    重點是我們得在星期五之前做完。
  2. The key point is that the medicine must be taken with food.
    關鍵在於這個藥要跟食物一起吃。
  3. The whole point of the trip was that we could spend time together.
    這趟旅行的重點就是我們能在一起。
  4. The important point is, nobody blamed you.
    重要的是，沒有人怪你。

④ problem

  1. The only problem is that the kitchen is tiny.
    唯一的問題是廚房很小。
  2. The biggest problem was that we didn't speak the language.
    最大的問題是我們不會說當地的語言。
  3. The real problem is that the tickets sell out in minutes.
    真正的問題是票幾分鐘就賣光了。
  4. The main problem with the plan is that it costs too much.
    這個計畫的主要問題是太花錢了。
  5. The only problem was, the shop closed at five.
    唯一的問題是，那家店五點就關了。

⑤ reason

  1. The main reason is that I want to be closer to my parents.
    主要的原因是我想離我爸媽近一點。
  2. The real reason was that he didn't want to go.
    真正的原因是他不想去。
  3. The only reason I came is that you asked me.
    我來的唯一原因是你叫我來。
  4. The other reason is that the school has a great music department.
    另一個原因是那所學校的音樂科很好。

⑥ 省略 that

  1. The funny thing is, I've never been to Paris.
    好笑的是，我從來沒去過巴黎。
  2. The best thing is, you can bring your dog.
    最棒的是，你可以帶狗去。
  3. The odd thing was, nobody else heard the alarm.
    奇怪的是，其他人都沒聽到警報聲。
  4. The main thing is, you're safe now.
    最重要的是，你現在安全了。

⑦ 過去式

  1. The strange thing was that the lights were on.
    奇怪的是，燈是亮著的。
  2. The hardest thing was that we had to wait outside in the cold.
    最難熬的是，我們得在外面挨冷等著。
  3. The good thing was that the rain stopped before the match.
    好在比賽前雨就停了。
  4. The obvious problem was that nobody had a key.
    很明顯的問題是，沒有人有鑰匙。
