Write to me soon.

要早點寫信給我喔。

結⁠尾⁠的祈⁠使⁠句是友⁠善⁠的請⁠求或祝⁠福

Write to me soon.

請對方聯絡

It was great to meet you. Keep in touch!

很高興認識你，保持聯絡！
請對方來

Our new house has a spare room. Come and see us next summer.

我們的新家有一間客房。明年夏天來看我們。
轉達問候

I heard she's back from hospital. Give my love to your mother.

我聽說她出院了。代我向你媽媽問好。

幾⁠句祈⁠使⁠句可⁠以接在⁠一⁠起

Write to me soon.

Good luck tomorrow. Let me know how it goes.

明天祝你好運，結果如何要告訴我。

祈⁠使⁠句⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Write to me soon.

Phones me when the plane lands.

Phone me when the plane lands.

一⁠趟旅⁠程用 trip

Write to me soon.

Have a good travel to Japan!

Have a good trip to Japan!

轉⁠達問⁠候用 say hello to

Write to me soon.

Say hello for your grandparents in Tainan.

Say hello to your grandparents in Tainan.

例句

① 請對方聯絡

  1. Write to me when you're settled in.
    你安頓好以後寫信給我。
  2. You're flying to Canada tomorrow? Email or phone me when you get there.
    你明天要飛去加拿大？你到了以後寄電子郵件或打電話給我。
  3. I'm in meetings all morning. Call me tomorrow after lunch, OK?
    我整個早上都在開會。明天午餐後打給我，好嗎？
  4. It was great to meet you. Keep in touch!
    很高興認識你，保持聯絡！
  5. Have a great time on your trip! Send me a postcard from Paris.
    旅行玩得開心！從巴黎寄張明信片給我。
  6. Good luck on Monday. Let me know how the interview goes.
    星期一祝你好運。讓我知道面試的結果。

② 請對方來

  1. Come soon. We miss you.
    早點來喔，我們很想你。
  2. Our new house has a spare room. Come and see us next summer.
    我們的新家有一間客房。明年夏天來看我們。
  3. We live near the old fort. Visit us when you're in Tainan.
    我們住在古堡附近。你來台南的時候來找我們。
  4. We'll miss you at work. Come back soon!
    公司的大家會想你的，早點回來喔！
  5. We live just round the corner now. Drop by anytime.
    我們現在就住在轉角。隨時過來坐坐。

③ 關心和祝福

  1. Bye, Grandma. Look after yourself until we visit again.
    奶奶再見，好好照顧自己，等我們再來看你。
  2. Have a safe flight home, and text me when you land.
    回程一路平安，降落後傳簡訊給我。
  3. Enjoy your week in Bali, and don't forget the sunscreen.
    在峇里島的這個禮拜玩得開心，別忘了擦防曬。
  4. Rest as much as you can, and get better soon.
    盡量多休息，早點好起來。
  5. Your bags are already in the car? Have a lovely weekend at the lake.
    你的行李已經在車上了？在湖邊週末愉快。
  6. Good night, kids. Sleep well and sweet dreams.
    晚安，孩子們，好好睡，做個好夢。

④ 轉達問候

  1. Say hello to your family for me, and see you in May.
    幫我跟你的家人問好，五月見。
  2. I heard she's back from hospital. Give my love to your mother.
    我聽說她出院了。代我向你媽媽問好。
  3. You're seeing Ken at the party tonight? Say hi to him for me.
    你今天晚上會在派對上見到 Ken？幫我跟他打聲招呼。
  4. Thank you for the lovely visit. Give my best wishes to your grandparents.
    謝謝你們熱情招待。代我向你爺爺奶奶問候。
  5. I haven't seen Ann since school. Tell her I said hello.
    我畢業以後就沒見過 Ann 了。跟她說我向她問好。

⑤ 結合在一起的結尾

  1. I know you're busy with exams. Write back when you can. Take care.
    我知道你在忙考試。有空就回信。保重。
  2. Enjoy the camping trip, kids. See you next week. Have fun!
    孩子們，露營玩得開心。下禮拜見，玩得開心！
  3. My train is coming. Bye for now. Call me later.
    我的火車來了。先這樣，晚點打給我。
  4. Good luck tomorrow. Let me know how it goes.
    明天祝你好運，結果如何要告訴我。
  5. That's all my news for now. Write back when you have time.
    我的近況就先寫到這裡，有空再回信喔。
  6. Thanks again. Look after yourself.
    再次謝謝你，好好照顧自己。
  7. Hope to see you soon. Give my love to everyone.
    希望很快見到你，代我向大家問好。
  8. I have to go now. Give the baby a hug from me.
    我得走了，幫我抱抱寶寶。
