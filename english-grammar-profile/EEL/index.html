How differently we were brought up by our mother!

媽媽養我們的方式真是差好多啊！

How 加副⁠詞，後⁠面⁠接主⁠詞和動⁠詞

How differently we were brought up by our mother!

她唱得多好聽啊！

副⁠詞說動⁠作，形⁠容⁠詞說人或東⁠西

How differently we were brought up by our mother!

副詞

How quickly he runs!

他跑得好快！
形容詞

How quick he is!

他好快！

修⁠飾動⁠詞要⁠用副⁠詞

How differently we were brought up by our mother!

How beautiful she sings!

How beautifully she sings!

不⁠加 do，主⁠詞在動⁠詞前⁠面

How differently we were brought up by our mother!

How quickly does time go!

How quickly time goes!

副⁠詞緊⁠接在 How 後⁠面

How differently we were brought up by our mother!

How she speaks clearly!

How clearly she speaks!

例句

① How quickly / fast ...!

  1. How quickly the waiter brought our food!
    服務生上菜上得好快！
  2. How fast the children grow up!
    孩子們長得好快！
  3. How quickly she learned to swim!
    她學游泳學得好快！
  4. How fast the news spread!
    消息傳得好快！
  5. How quickly the snow melted this morning!
    今天早上雪融得好快！

② How beautifully / well ...!

  1. How beautifully she sings!
    她唱得多好聽啊！
  2. How well you speak English!
    你英文說得真好！
  3. How beautifully you've decorated the room!
    你把房間布置得真漂亮！
  4. How well the kids behaved today!
    孩子們今天表現得真好！
  5. How nicely you've painted the fence!
    你籬笆漆得真好！

③ How differently / strangely ...!

  1. How differently my brother and I see the world!
    我跟我哥哥看世界的方式真是差好多！
  2. How differently people live in the city!
    城市裡的人生活方式真不一樣！
  3. How strangely he acted at the party!
    他在派對上的舉止真奇怪！
  4. How differently things turned out!
    事情的結果真是完全不一樣！
  5. How oddly the cat is behaving today!
    貓今天的舉動真奇怪！

④ How hard / badly ...!

  1. How hard they worked to finish on time!
    他們為了準時完成，工作得多辛苦啊！
  2. How badly I wanted to win!
    我多想贏啊！
  3. How hard it rained last night!
    昨晚雨下得好大！
  4. How badly the team played today!
    這支隊伍今天踢得真差！
  5. How hard she tried to stay calm!
    她多努力想保持冷靜啊！

⑤ 不同的副詞和句型

  1. How kindly they treated us!
    他們對我們真好！
  2. How patiently he waited for her!
    他等她等得多有耐心啊！
  3. How easily you made friends!
    你交朋友交得真容易！
  4. How slowly the hours pass at work!
    上班時時間過得好慢！
  5. How carefully she wrapped the present!
    她包禮物包得好仔細！
  6. How loudly the baby cried!
    寶寶哭得好大聲！
  7. How clearly I can picture our old kitchen!
    我們以前的廚房，我到現在都還看得好清楚！
  8. How gently he held the baby bird!
    他抱著那隻小鳥的動作好溫柔！
  9. How easily the old man climbed the hill!
    那個老人爬山爬得好輕鬆！
  10. How happily the children played in the snow!
    孩子們在雪地裡玩得好開心！
