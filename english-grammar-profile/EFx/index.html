例句

① there will be

  1. In twenty years there will be flying cars and buses, and big houses.
    二十年後會有會飛的車子和公車，還有大房子。
  2. Don't worry, there will be plenty of food at the wedding.
    別擔心，婚禮上會有很多吃的。
  3. There will be a short break after the first hour.
    第一個小時結束後會有短暫的休息。
  4. Next year there'll be a new swimming pool in our town.
    明年我們鎮上會有一座新游泳池。

② there might / may / could be

  1. Take an umbrella. There might be some rain later.
    帶把傘吧，晚一點可能會下雨。
  2. There may be a delay because of the snow.
    因為下雪，可能會延誤。
  3. Ask at the desk. There could be some tickets left.
    去櫃台問問看，可能還有票。
  4. There might be a lot of traffic on Friday evening.
    星期五晚上可能會塞車。

③ there should be

  1. There should be a bus every ten minutes, but it's been twenty.
    應該每十分鐘就有一班公車，可是已經過了二十分鐘了。
  2. There should be some milk in the fridge. I bought some yesterday.
    冰箱裡應該有牛奶，我昨天買了一些。
  3. There should be more bike lanes in this city.
    這座城市應該要有更多自行車道。

④ there would be

  1. In the countryside there wouldn't be so many people, and there's nothing to do.
    在鄉下不會有那麼多人，而且沒什麼事可以做。
  2. If we lived by the sea, there would be no need for a car.
    如果我們住在海邊，就不需要車子了。
  3. Without the music festival, there would be no tourists here in summer.
    要是沒有音樂節，夏天這裡就不會有觀光客。

⑤ there must be / there can't be

  1. The door is locked. There must be someone inside.
    門鎖著，裡面一定有人。
  2. My bill is too high. There must be a mistake.
    我的帳單太高了，一定是哪裡弄錯了。
  3. There can't be another train tonight. It's after midnight.
    今晚不可能還有火車，已經過半夜了。

⑥ 否定句

  1. There won't be any classes on Monday because it's a holiday.
    星期一放假，所以不會上課。
  2. There might not be enough chairs for everyone.
    椅子可能不夠大家坐。
  3. There shouldn't be any problems with your visa.
    你的簽證應該不會有問題。
  4. There won't be time to eat before the film.
    電影開始前不會有時間吃東西。

⑦ 疑問句

  1. Will there be food at the party, or should I eat first?
    派對上會有吃的嗎？還是我應該先吃？
  2. Could there be a problem with the battery?
    會不會是電池有問題？
  3. Will there be a teacher on the trip?
    校外教學會有老師跟去嗎？

⑧ there + 情態動詞 + be + 名詞片語 + 修飾語

  1. In a big city, there might be more jobs that young people can do.
    在大城市裡，可能會有更多年輕人可以做的工作。
  2. There will be someone at the station to meet you.
    車站會有人去接你。
  3. There should be a key under the mat for you.
    腳踏墊下面應該有一把要給你的鑰匙。
  4. There could be a café near the museum where we can eat.
    博物館附近可能有一家我們可以吃飯的咖啡店。
  5. There must be somebody who can fix this.
    一定有人能修好這個。
  6. There may be students who need extra help.
    可能有些學生需要額外的幫助。
  7. There'll be a lot of things to do at the summer camp.
    夏令營會有很多活動可以參加。
