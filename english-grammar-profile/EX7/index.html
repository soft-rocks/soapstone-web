Can you believe they invented a robot that plays football?

你敢相信他們發明了一個會踢足球的機器人嗎？

Can you believe + (that) + 子⁠句?

Can you believe they invented a robot that plays football?

Can you believe表示驚訝，不是真的在問。
子句讓人驚訝的事。that 可以省略。
it / this / that指前面剛說的事。

放⁠在句⁠首或句⁠尾

句首

Can you believe he's only fifteen?

你敢相信他才十五歲嗎？
句尾

It cost me fifty pounds! Can you believe that?

這花了我五十英鎊！你相信嗎？

書寫時，句尾常用驚嘆號代替問號。

加⁠上 I can't believe

例句

Can you believe it? I can't believe it!

你相信嗎？我真不敢相信！

說話者自己也常接著說 I can't believe it。

can 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 believe

Can you believe they invented a robot that plays football?

Can you believed it?

Can you believe it?

問⁠句⁠裡，can 放⁠在 you 前⁠面

Can you believe they invented a robot that plays football?

You can believe he won?

Can you believe he won?

believe 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句用一⁠般⁠的語⁠序

Can you believe they invented a robot that plays football?

Can you believe how much did it cost?

Can you believe how much it cost?

例句

① Can you believe + (that) + 子句?

  1. Can you believe he's only fifteen and already plays for the national team?
    你敢相信他才十五歲就已經進國家隊了嗎？
  2. Can you believe it's snowing in April?
    你敢相信四月在下雪嗎？
  3. Can you believe that Tom passed his driving test on the first try?
    你敢相信 Tom 第一次就考過駕照嗎？
  4. Can you believe they're getting married after only three months?
    你敢相信他們才交往三個月就要結婚了嗎？
  5. Can you believe our teacher gave us no homework this weekend?
    你敢相信老師這個週末沒出功課嗎？
  6. Can you believe the shop charged me twice for the same shoes?
    你敢相信那家店同一雙鞋跟我收了兩次錢嗎？
  7. Can you believe that my grandma has her own YouTube channel?
    你敢相信我奶奶有自己的 YouTube 頻道嗎？
  8. Can you believe we've known each other for twenty years?
    你敢相信我們認識二十年了嗎？
  9. Can you believe she ran the whole marathon with a broken toe?
    你敢相信她腳趾骨折還跑完整場馬拉松嗎？
  10. Can you believe the train was three hours late?
    你敢相信火車誤點了三個小時嗎？
  11. Can you believe our team actually won the cup?
    你敢相信我們隊真的贏得了獎盃嗎？

② Can you believe + how / what + 子句?

  1. Can you believe how much they paid for that house?
    你敢相信他們花多少錢買那間房子嗎？
  2. Can you believe what he said to the teacher?
    你敢相信他跟老師說了什麼嗎？
  3. Can you believe how fast the children are growing?
    你敢相信孩子們長得多快嗎？
  4. Can you believe how cold it is today?
    你敢相信今天有多冷嗎？
  5. Can you believe what happened at the party last night?
    你敢相信昨晚派對上發生了什麼事嗎？
  6. Can you believe how many people came to the concert?
    你敢相信有多少人來聽演唱會嗎？

③ 子句. Can you believe it / this / that?

  1. The museum let us in for free. Can you believe this?
    博物館讓我們免費進去，你相信嗎？
  2. The repair cost more than the car! Can you believe that!
    修理費比車子還貴！你相信嗎！
  3. My brother cleaned the whole house without anyone asking him. Can you believe it?
    我哥沒人叫他就把整間房子打掃乾淨了，你相信嗎？
  4. We waited two hours for a table. Can you believe that?
    我們等了兩個小時才有位子，你相信嗎？
  5. The cat opened the fridge by itself. Can you believe it?
    那隻貓自己開了冰箱，你相信嗎？
  6. I found fifty pounds in my old jacket. Can you believe it?
    我在舊外套裡找到五十英鎊，你相信嗎？
  7. He forgot his own wedding anniversary. Can you believe that?
    他忘了自己的結婚紀念日，你相信嗎？
  8. The flight to Rome was cheaper than the train. Can you believe it?
    飛羅馬的機票比火車票還便宜，你相信嗎？
  9. My little sister read the whole book in one day. Can you believe this?
    我妹一天就讀完了整本書，你相信嗎？

④ Can you believe it? + I can't believe it!

  1. We've won a holiday in Spain! Can you believe it? I can't believe it!
    我們贏得了西班牙的假期！你相信嗎？我真不敢相信！
  2. Can you believe it? The concert is sold out already. I can't believe it!
    你相信嗎？演唱會的票已經賣完了，我真不敢相信！
  3. She said yes! Can you believe it? I still can't believe it.
    她答應了！你相信嗎？我還是不敢相信。
  4. Can you believe it? Our old teacher remembered my name after ten years.
    你相信嗎？我們以前的老師過了十年還記得我的名字。
  5. Can you believe it? The house we wanted is for sale again.
    你相信嗎？我們想要的那間房子又在賣了。
