I am due to start work in August.

我預定八月開始上班。

be due to 說已⁠經排⁠定時⁠間⁠的事

I am due to start work in August.

我預定八月開始上班。

上班的日期是公司排好的。

主⁠詞可⁠以是⁠人，也⁠可⁠以是事⁠情

I am due to start work in August.

人

She's due to give a talk on Monday.

她預定星期一演講。
事情

The new bridge is due to open next spring.

新橋預定明年春天通車。

被⁠動是 due to be 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I am due to start work in August.

結果預定明天公布。

否⁠定⁠的 not 放⁠在 be 後⁠面

I am due to start work in August.

下一班火車還要一個小時才會到。

due to 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞⁠時，意⁠思是因⁠為

I am due to start work in August.

due to + 原形動詞
預定

The game is due to start at three.

比賽預定三點開始。
due to + 名詞
因為

The game was cancelled due to rain.

比賽因為下雨取消了。

due to 前⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

I am due to start work in August.

The meeting due to start at nine.

The meeting is due to start at nine.

due to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I am due to start work in August.

The flight is due to leaving at noon.

The flight is due to leave at noon.

這裡的 to 是不定詞的 to。

被⁠動不⁠能少⁠了 be

I am due to start work in August.

The report is due to published next week.

The report is due to be published next week.

例句

① 主詞是人

  1. I'm due to start work in August, so July is the only month I can travel.
    我預定八月開始上班，所以七月是我唯一能旅行的月份。
  2. She's due to give a talk at the conference on Monday.
    她預定星期一在研討會上演講。
  3. Some of our team are due to attend a sales event next Friday.
    我們團隊有幾個人預定下星期五參加一場促銷活動。
  4. He's due to finish his dissertation by the end of June.
    他預定六月底前寫完論文。
  5. The President is due to visit Japan next month.
    總統預定下個月訪問日本。
  6. We're due to meet the lawyers at two.
    我們預定兩點和律師見面。
  7. My sister is due to have her baby in May.
    我姊姊預產期在五月。
  8. The new manager is due to arrive on Thursday.
    新經理預定星期四到任。
  9. I'm due to fly to London tomorrow morning.
    我預定明天早上飛倫敦。
  10. You're due to see the doctor at four.
    你預約了四點看醫生。
  11. The students are due to sit their exams in June.
    學生預定六月考試。
  12. Mr. Wang is due to retire at the end of the year.
    Wang 先生預定年底退休。

② 主詞是事情

  1. Our flight is due to board in twenty minutes.
    我們的班機預定二十分鐘後登機。
  2. The new shopping centre is due to open before Christmas.
    新的購物中心預定聖誕節前開幕。
  3. The meeting is due to start in ten minutes.
    會議預定十分鐘後開始。
  4. The film is due to come out in the summer.
    這部電影預定夏天上映。
  5. The next bus is due to leave at half past six.
    下一班公車預定六點半發車。
  6. The new library is due to open in the autumn.
    新圖書館預定秋天開放。
  7. His contract is due to end in March.
    他的合約預定三月到期。
  8. The concert is due to finish at eleven.
    音樂會預定十一點結束。
  9. The work on the road is due to take six weeks.
    道路工程預定進行六週。
  10. The ship is due to reach the port at dawn.
    船預定天亮時抵達港口。
  11. The repairs aren't due to finish before May.
    修繕工程預定五月以前不會完工。
  12. The museum isn't due to reopen until next year.
    博物館預定明年才會重新開放。

③ 主詞 + am / is / are + due to be + 過去分詞（被動）

  1. The exam results are due to be posted online this afternoon.
    考試成績預定今天下午公布在網路上。
  2. The old bridge is due to be repaired next year.
    舊橋預定明年修理。
  3. The old factory is due to be pulled down this summer.
    那座舊工廠預定今年夏天拆除。
  4. The winners are due to be named on Friday.
    得獎者預定星期五公布。
  5. The new rules are due to be introduced in January.
    新規定預定一月開始實施。
  6. The patient is due to be sent home on Monday.
    病人預定星期一出院。
  7. The painting is due to be sold at auction next month.
    這幅畫預定下個月拍賣。
