例句

① Have you + 過去分詞：問最近的事

  1. I left my mobile phone at your house yesterday. Have you seen it?
    我昨天把手機忘在你家了。你有看到嗎？
  2. I've just watched the football final with my family. Have you watched it?
    我剛和家人看完足球決賽。你看了嗎？
  3. You look different. Have you cut your hair?
    你看起來不一樣。你剪頭髮了嗎？
  4. Have you heard the news? Sarah is getting married!
    你聽說了嗎？Sarah 要結婚了！
  5. It's almost two o'clock. Have you had lunch? I'm going to the café.
    快兩點了。你吃午餐了嗎？我要去咖啡店。
  6. The teacher wants the essays today. Have you finished yours?
    老師今天要收作文。你寫完了嗎？

② Have you ever + 過去分詞：問經驗

  1. Have you ever been to Japan? It's beautiful in spring.
    你去過日本嗎？那裡春天很美。
  2. This restaurant is new in town. Have you ever eaten Ethiopian food?
    這家餐廳是鎮上新開的。你吃過衣索比亞菜嗎？
  3. You work in a big hotel. Have you ever served anyone famous?
    你在一家大飯店上班。你招待過名人嗎？
  4. The farm offers rides at the weekend. Have you ever been on a horse?
    那座農場週末有騎馬活動。你騎過馬嗎？
  5. I'm nervous about flying alone. Have you ever missed a connecting flight?
    我很緊張要一個人搭飛機。你錯過轉機航班過嗎？

③ Has + he / she / it + 過去分詞

  1. Your brother finished university in June. Has he started work yet?
    你哥哥六月大學畢業了。他開始工作了沒？
  2. Has the post arrived yet? I'm waiting for a letter.
    郵件到了沒？我在等一封信。
  3. Emma looks so happy these days. Has she told you about her new boyfriend?
    Emma 最近看起來好開心。她跟你說過她新男朋友的事了嗎？
  4. Has it stopped raining? I want to go out.
    雨停了嗎？我想出去。
  5. My little boy wants to play with him. Has your dog ever bitten anyone?
    我兒子想跟牠玩。你的狗咬過人嗎？

④ Have we / they + 過去分詞

  1. Have we met before? Your face looks familiar.
    我們以前見過嗎？你看起來很面熟。
  2. Their plane landed three hours ago. Have they arrived at the hotel yet?
    他們的飛機三個小時前就降落了。他們到飯店了沒？
  3. Twelve people are coming tonight. Have we bought enough food?
    今晚有十二個人要來。我們買的食物夠嗎？
  4. You moved in a month ago. Have your parents visited your new flat?
    你一個月前搬進去。你爸媽來過你的新公寓了嗎？

⑤ Have ... yet?：問已經做了沒

  1. The concert sells out fast. Have you booked the tickets yet?
    那場演唱會很快就賣完。你訂票了沒？
  2. We took the exam six weeks ago. Have the results come out yet?
    我們六個星期前考試。成績公布了沒？
  3. Has the film started yet? I've just arrived.
    電影開始了沒？我剛到。
  4. The landlord wrote to you on Monday. Have you answered him yet?
    房東星期一寫信給你。你回他了沒？

⑥ 簡答

  1. "Have you seen my glasses?" "Yes, I have. They're on the table."
    你有看到我的眼鏡嗎？有。在桌上。
  2. "You lent Mark fifty pounds last month. Has he paid you back?" "No, he hasn't."
    你上個月借 Mark 五十英鎊。他還你錢了嗎？還沒。
  3. "Have you ever been to India?" "No, I haven't, but I'd love to go."
    你去過印度嗎？沒有，可是我很想去。
  4. "The builders have gone. Have they finished the work?" "Yes, they have."
    工人走了。他們完成工作了嗎？完成了。

⑦ Have you got：有沒有

  1. Have you got a pen? I need to write this down.
    你有筆嗎？我得把這個記下來。
  2. Your new friend talks about her family a lot. Has she got any brothers or sisters?
    你的新朋友常講她的家人。她有兄弟姊妹嗎？
  3. My train doesn't leave until five. Have you got time for a coffee?
    我的火車五點才開。你有時間喝杯咖啡嗎？
