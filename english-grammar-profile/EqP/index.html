Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

我們可以星期五下午見面嗎？

問⁠句把 could 放⁠在句⁠首，主⁠詞後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

我們可以星期五下午見面嗎？

主⁠詞不⁠同，問⁠的事也不⁠同

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

Could I
請求許可

Could I sit here?

我可以坐這裡嗎？
Could you
請對方幫忙

Could you check my spelling?

你可以幫我檢查拼字嗎？
Could we
提議一起做

Could we start at nine?

我們可以九點開始嗎？

疑⁠問⁠詞放⁠在 could 前⁠面

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

你幾點可以來？

說過⁠去⁠的能⁠力用 could 回⁠答，答⁠應請⁠求用 can

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

過去的能力

Could you swim as a child? — Yes, I could.

你小時候會游泳嗎？會。
答應請求

Could I borrow this? — Yes, you can.

我可以借這個嗎？可以。

問⁠句把 could 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

We could meet tomorrow?

Could we meet tomorrow?

could 的問⁠句不⁠用 do 或 did

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

Did you could see the stage?

Could you see the stage?

主⁠詞是 she，動⁠詞也⁠不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

Could she comes with us?

Could she come with us?

Can 和 Could 都⁠能請⁠求或提⁠議，Could 比⁠較客⁠氣

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

我們星期一可以見面嗎？

Could 加 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，問過⁠去可⁠能發⁠生⁠的事

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

她會不會是沒趕上公車？

Would you like 問想不⁠想⁠要，Could you 請對⁠方做⁠事

Could we meet on Friday afternoon?

你要喝點茶嗎？

例句

① Could I + 原形動詞？

  1. Could I have a cup of tea?
    我可以喝杯茶嗎？
  2. Could I keep this drawing?
    這張畫我可以留著嗎？
  3. Could I talk to you for a minute?
    我可以跟你談一下嗎？
  4. Could I come to your house after school?
    我放學後可以去你家嗎？
  5. Could I sit by the window?
    我可以坐窗邊嗎？

② Could you + 原形動詞？

  1. Could you help me?
    你可以幫我嗎？
  2. Could you carry this bag for me?
    你可以幫我提這個袋子嗎？
  3. Could you write the word on the board?
    你可以把那個字寫在白板上嗎？
  4. Could you show me your new phone?
    你可以給我看看你的新手機嗎？
  5. Could you meet me at the station?
    你可以來車站接我嗎？

③ Could we + 原形動詞？

  1. Could we meet on Friday morning instead?
    我們可以改成星期五早上見面嗎？
  2. Could we go to the zoo on Sunday?
    我們星期天可以去動物園嗎？
  3. Could we have pizza tonight?
    我們今晚可以吃披薩嗎？
  4. Could we sit together?
    我們可以坐在一起嗎？
  5. Could we talk about this later?
    我們可以晚一點再談這件事嗎？

④ Could + he / she / they + 原形動詞？

  1. Could she come with us?
    她可以跟我們一起去嗎？
  2. Could he play the guitar when he was young?
    他年輕的時候會彈吉他嗎？
  3. Could they hear us from the garden?
    他們在花園裡聽得到我們嗎？
  4. Could your sister give me a lift?
    你姊姊可以載我一程嗎？
  5. Could the children stay up late tonight?
    孩子們今晚可以晚點睡嗎？

⑤ 疑問詞 + could + 主詞 + 原形動詞？

  1. Where could we park?
    我們可以把車停在哪裡？
  2. What could I bring to the party?
    我可以帶什麼去派對？
  3. When could you start?
    你什麼時候可以開始？
  4. How could I get to the airport?
    我要怎麼去機場？
  5. Who could help me with this?
    誰可以幫我處理這件事？
  6. Which bus could I take to the beach?
    我可以搭哪一班公車去海邊？

⑥ Could ...? 和簡答

  1. Could you swim when you were five? — No, I couldn't.
    你五歲的時候會游泳嗎？不會。
  2. Could I open the window? — Yes, of course.
    我可以開窗嗎？當然可以。
  3. Could you read the sign? — Yes, I could.
    你看得清那個標誌嗎？看得清。
  4. Could we leave early today? — Sorry, you can't.
    我們今天可以早點走嗎？抱歉，不行。
