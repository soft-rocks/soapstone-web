Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

明天早上我會在念書，所以你兩點來就可以了。

先說那⁠個時⁠間正⁠在做⁠的⁠事，再給對⁠方一⁠個時⁠間

Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

明天早上我會在念書，所以你兩點來就可以了。

時⁠間片⁠語可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在句⁠尾

Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

句首

At six I'll be cooking dinner.

六點的時候我會在煮晚餐。
句尾

I'll be ironing my shirts at eight.

八點的時候我會在燙襯衫。

when 子⁠句說未⁠來⁠的時⁠間，動⁠詞用現⁠在⁠式

醫生打來的時候，我會坐在電話旁邊。

用 so、but 或 because 把正⁠在做⁠的事和安⁠排連起⁠來

Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

so

I'll be shopping until four, so come over after that.

我四點以前會在買東西，所以你那之後再過來。
but

I'll be cleaning in the morning, but I'm free in the afternoon.

我早上會在打掃，不過下午有空。
because

Don't come at ten, because I'll be sleeping then.

別十點來，因為我那時候在睡覺。

will be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 的動⁠作在那⁠個時⁠間之⁠前就開⁠始了

Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

will + 原形動詞
七點開始

At seven I'll have breakfast.

我七點吃早餐。
will be + 動詞-ing
七點前已經開始

At seven I'll be having breakfast.

七點的時候我正在吃早餐。

when 子⁠句⁠裡不⁠用 will

When you will arrive, I'll be waiting at the gate.

When you arrive, I'll be waiting at the gate.

be 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

Tomorrow I'll be work all morning.

Tomorrow I'll be working all morning.

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠的 be

Tomorrow I'll be studying in the morning, so you can come at two.

Tomorrow I'll been studying all morning.

Tomorrow I'll be studying all morning.

will have been 是另一個句型。

例句

① 時間片語在句首

  1. Tomorrow morning I'll be revising, so come in the afternoon.
    明天早上我會在複習，所以你下午來。
  2. At nine we'll be boarding the plane, so I can't talk then.
    九點我們會在登機，所以那時候我沒辦法講電話。
  3. This time next week I'll be lying on a beach in Bali.
    下星期這個時候，我會躺在 Bali 的海灘上。
  4. All day on Friday the team will be moving into the new office.
    星期五一整天，團隊都會在搬進新辦公室。
  5. On Sunday afternoon Dad will be watching the match, so don't plan anything.
    星期天下午爸爸會在看球賽，所以別安排任何事。
  6. Between two and four I'll be seeing patients.
    兩點到四點我會在看病人。
  7. Tonight the kids will be doing their homework, so keep the TV off.
    今晚孩子們會在寫功課，所以別開電視。
  8. In the morning I'll be cleaning the house, but I'm free after lunch.
    早上我會在打掃房子，不過午餐後我就有空。
  9. Next month Ann will be preparing for her exams.
    下個月 Ann 會在準備考試。
  10. At noon the chef will be serving lunch to two hundred guests.
    中午主廚會在為兩百位客人供應午餐。

② 時間片語在句尾

  1. I'll be working late on Thursday, so let's meet on Friday.
    我星期四會加班，所以我們星期五見吧。
  2. Please don't come at four, because I'll be teaching then.
    請不要四點來，因為我那時候在上課。
  3. She'll be visiting her grandmother all weekend.
    她整個週末都會在探望她奶奶。
  4. We'll be painting the kitchen on Saturday morning.
    我們星期六早上會在油漆廚房。
  5. He'll be giving a talk on medieval treatises at three.
    他三點會在演講，講中世紀的論文。
  6. I'll be driving at five, so text me instead of calling.
    我五點會在開車，所以傳訊息給我，不要打電話。
  7. The nurses will be checking on you every hour tonight.
    今晚護理師每個小時都會來看你。
  8. They'll be repairing the road outside our house all next week.
    下星期一整個星期，他們都會在修我們家外面的路。
  9. You'll be sitting your final exam this time tomorrow.
    明天這個時候你正在考期末考。
  10. I'll be picking up the kids around three thirty, so call me before that.
    我三點半左右會在接小孩，所以那之前打給我。
  11. Mr. Wu will be interviewing candidates until five.
    Wu 先生到五點都會在面試應徵者。
  12. My brother will be using the car tomorrow, so I'll take the bus.
    我弟弟明天會在用車，所以我搭公車。

③ when 子句

  1. When you get to the station, I'll be standing by the ticket office.
    你到車站的時候，我會站在售票處旁邊。
  2. When the film starts, we'll be eating popcorn.
    電影開始的時候，我們會正在吃爆米花。
  3. Don't phone when the game is on. He'll be shouting at the TV.
    球賽播的時候別打電話，他會在對著電視大叫。
  4. When you wake up, I'll be flying to Tokyo.
    你醒來的時候，我會正在飛往東京的路上。
  5. When the bell rings, the students will still be writing.
    鐘響的時候，學生們都還在寫。
  6. When she gets home, her husband will be cooking dinner.
    她到家的時候，她先生會正在煮晚餐。
  7. When the guests come, we'll be setting the table.
    客人來的時候，我們會正在擺餐具。
  8. When the sun comes up, we'll be climbing the last part of the mountain.
    太陽出來的時候，我們會正在爬最後一段山路。
  9. When you call tomorrow, I'll probably be sleeping.
    你明天打來的時候，我大概正在睡覺。
