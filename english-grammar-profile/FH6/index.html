例句

① I'm asking

  1. My father is in hospital. I'm asking permission to be excused from work for at least three days.
    我父親住院了。我想請求准假，至少請三天。
  2. Considering the importance of the project, I am asking for your financial support.
    考慮到這個計畫的重要性，我想請求您在經費上的支持。
  3. I'm asking you as a friend. Please don't tell anyone.
    我是以朋友的身分拜託你。請不要告訴任何人。
  4. The school closes early tomorrow. We're asking all parents to collect their children by four.
    學校明天提早放學。我們想請所有家長在四點前接孩子。
  5. I know you're busy. I'm only asking for ten minutes of your time.
    我知道你很忙。我只想耽誤你十分鐘。
  6. The data arrived late, so I'm asking for an extra week on the deadline.
    資料太晚到了，所以我想請您把截止日延後一個星期。

② I'm hoping

  1. These forms are so confusing. I'm hoping you can help me with my visa application.
    這些表格好難懂。我希望你能幫我處理簽證申請。
  2. We're hoping to hear from you by the end of the month.
    我們希望能在月底前收到您的回音。
  3. I'm hoping to leave a bit early today, if that's OK.
    如果可以的話，我今天想早點走。
  4. We're hoping that you can join us for dinner on Friday.
    我們希望你星期五能來跟我們吃晚餐。
  5. I was going to call you. I'm hoping we can meet next week.
    我正想打給你。我希望我們下星期能見個面。
  6. The school is hoping to raise enough money for a new library.
    學校希望能募到足夠的錢蓋新圖書館。

③ I'm wondering

  1. You're so good at English. I'm wondering if you could check my essay before Monday.
    你英文好好。不知道你能不能在星期一前幫我看一下作文。
  2. Sorry to bother you. I'm wondering if there's a table for two.
    不好意思打擾了。請問還有兩個人的位子嗎？
  3. Our train gets in at nine. We're wondering whether the meeting could start a little later.
    我們的火車九點才到。我們在想，會議能不能晚一點開始。
  4. My car is at the garage. I'm wondering if I could borrow yours this weekend.
    我的車在修車廠。不知道這個週末能不能跟你借車。

④ I'm wanting

  1. If we're wanting to make progress in medicine, we have to be willing to spend money.
    如果我們想在醫學上進步，就得願意花錢。
  2. I'm wanting to talk to you about my hours. Do you have a minute?
    我想跟你談談我的工時。你有空嗎？
  3. Good evening. We're wanting to book a room for three nights, please.
    晚安。我們想訂一間房，住三個晚上。
  4. Are you wanting a drink while you wait?
    您在等的時候要喝點什麼嗎？
  5. Here's the form. Is your daughter wanting to join the swimming club?
    這是報名表。您女兒想加入游泳社嗎？

⑤ I'm looking for / I'm needing

  1. We're looking for volunteers to help at the school fair.
    我們在找志工幫忙學校的園遊會。
  2. I'm retiring next year. I'm looking for some advice about my pension.
    我明年要退休了。我想請教一些退休金的問題。
  3. I'm needing a bit more time to decide, if that's all right.
    如果可以的話，我需要多一點時間考慮。
  4. Our shop is getting busier. We're looking for someone to help on Saturdays.
    我們店越來越忙。我們想找人星期六來幫忙。

⑥ 服務和工作場合

  1. Are you looking for anything in particular today?
    您今天有特別想找什麼嗎？
  2. You know this project best. We're inviting you to share your ideas at the next meeting.
    你最了解這個計畫。我們想邀請你在下次會議分享你的想法。
  3. Good morning. I'm calling about the flat you advertised.
    早安。我打電話來是想問你們廣告上的那間公寓。
  4. Congratulations! We're offering you the job, starting on the first of June.
    恭喜！我們想給你這份工作，六月一號開始。
  5. Dear Sir or Madam, I'm writing to ask about the summer course.
    敬啟者：我寫信來是想詢問暑期課程的事。
