wondered + wh 詞 + 子⁠句，轉⁠述心⁠裡想⁠的問⁠題

我很好奇她寫了什麼。

問⁠句⁠的語⁠序改⁠成陳⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序，時⁠態後移

I wondered what she had written.

心裡的問題

"What has she written?"

「她寫了什麼？」
轉述

I wondered what she had written.

我很好奇她寫了什麼。

時⁠態後移

I wondered what she had written.

is → was

I wondered who he was.

我很好奇他是誰。
has gone → had gone

She wondered where he had gone.

她很好奇他去了哪裡。
did leave → had left

We wondered why she had left.

我們很納悶她為什麼走了。
will → would

I wondered what would happen.

我很好奇會發生什麼事。
can → could

He wondered how he could help.

他想著自己能怎麼幫忙。

yes/no 問⁠句用 if 或 whether

I wondered what she had written.

if

I wondered if she was angry with me.

我在想她是不是在生我的氣。

wh 詞 + to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，沒⁠有主⁠詞和時⁠態

I wondered what time it was.

整⁠句是陳⁠述⁠句，句⁠尾用句⁠號

He wondered why the shop was closed.

子⁠句⁠裡不⁠用 did

現⁠在⁠式 wonder，時⁠態不⁠後移

I wondered what she had written.

例句

① 現在簡單式 → 過去簡單式

  1. I wondered who the man in the photo was.
    我很好奇照片裡的男人是誰。
  2. She wondered what her new teacher's name was.
    她很好奇新老師叫什麼名字。
  3. We wondered how much the tickets cost.
    我們很好奇票要多少錢。
  4. He wondered why everyone was looking at him.
    他很納悶為什麼大家都在看他。
  5. I wondered where the nearest bus stop was.
    我在想最近的公車站在哪裡。

② 現在完成式 / 過去簡單式 → 過去完成式

  1. I wondered who had eaten the last piece of cake.
    我很好奇是誰吃了最後一塊蛋糕。
  2. She wondered how long he had lived in the city.
    她很好奇他在這個城市住了多久。
  3. We wondered why the plane had landed so early.
    我們很納悶飛機為什麼那麼早就降落了。
  4. They wondered what had happened to their old friend.
    他們很好奇他們的老朋友後來怎麼了。
  5. I wondered when she had learned to speak Japanese.
    我很好奇她是什麼時候學會說日語的。
  6. He wondered where he had seen her before.
    他在想他以前在哪裡見過她。

③ will → would / can → could

  1. I wondered what my parents would say.
    我在想我爸媽會說什麼。
  2. She wondered when the rain would stop.
    她在想雨什麼時候會停。
  3. We wondered how we could get home without a car.
    我們在想沒有車要怎麼回家。
  4. He wondered who would win the match.
    他很好奇誰會贏那場比賽。
  5. I wondered where I could buy a cheap phone.
    我在想去哪裡可以買到便宜的手機。

④ if / whether

  1. I wondered if I had made the right choice.
    我在想自己是不是做了對的選擇。
  2. She wondered whether her friends would remember her birthday.
    她在想朋友們會不會記得她的生日。
  3. We wondered if the museum was open on Sundays.
    我們在想博物館星期天有沒有開。
  4. He wondered whether he should apologize.
    他在想自己該不該道歉。
  5. They wondered if the hotel had a pool.
    他們在想飯店有沒有游泳池。

⑤ wh 詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. I wondered what to buy for my sister.
    我在想要買什麼給我妹妹。
  2. She wondered which dress to wear.
    她在想要穿哪一件洋裝。
  3. He wondered how to start the letter.
    他在想信要怎麼開頭。
  4. We wondered where to park the car.
    我們在想車要停在哪裡。

⑥ 故事裡的想法

  1. When I opened the box, I wondered who had sent it.
    打開盒子的時候，我很好奇是誰寄來的。
  2. I wondered who he was, but I didn't want to stare.
    我很好奇他是誰，可是我不想一直盯著他看。
  3. As we waited, we wondered what the doctor would tell us.
    我們等的時候，一直在想醫生會跟我們說什麼。
  4. She looked at the letter and wondered why he hadn't signed it.
    她看著那封信，納悶他為什麼沒簽名。
  5. The house was dark, and I wondered where everyone had gone.
    屋子裡一片漆黑，我在想大家都去哪了。
  6. He wondered how his life would be different in ten years.
    他在想十年後他的生活會有什麼不一樣。
