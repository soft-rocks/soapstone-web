Everyone has their own opinion.

每個人都有自己的看法。

單⁠數主⁠詞用 their

每個人都有自己的看法。

不⁠知⁠道是男是⁠女，就⁠用 their

Everyone has their own opinion.

his or her

Every student must bring his or her book.

每個學生都要帶自己的書。（正式，比較長）
their

Every student must bring their book.

每個學生都要帶自己的書。（常用）

公⁠司和團⁠體也用 their

Everyone has their own opinion.

公司

IBM ordered copies which show their new product.

IBM 訂了一批介紹他們新產品的影本。
球隊

The team played their best game of the season.

這支球隊打出了這一季最好的一場比賽。

everyone 動⁠詞用單⁠數

Everyone has their own opinion.

Everyone have their own opinion.

Everyone has their own opinion.

所⁠有⁠格是 their

Everyone has their own opinion.

Someone left there umbrella on the bus.

Someone left their umbrella on the bus.

名⁠詞前⁠面用 their，不⁠用 them

Everyone has their own opinion.

Each student must bring them own lunch.

Each student must bring their own lunch.

例句

① everyone / everybody + their

  1. People like different kinds of music, and everyone has their preference for one or another.
    大家喜歡的音樂不一樣，每個人都有自己偏好的類型。
  2. Everyone has their own opinion about the new law.
    對新法律，每個人都有自己的看法。
  3. Everybody brought their own food to the picnic.
    每個人都自己帶食物去野餐。
  4. Everyone in the office has their own desk and computer.
    辦公室裡每個人都有自己的桌子和電腦。

② someone / anyone / no one + their

  1. Someone left their phone in the classroom.
    有人把手機忘在教室了。
  2. If anyone loses their ticket, they have to buy a new one.
    如果有人把票弄丟了，就得再買一張。
  3. No one wants to lose their job.
    沒有人想丟掉工作。
  4. Somebody has parked their car in front of my garage.
    有人把車停在我車庫前面。

③ each / every / a + 單數名詞 + their

  1. Each child has to bring their own lunch on the trip.
    每個孩子校外教學都要自己帶午餐。
  2. Every student should check their answers before the end of the exam.
    每個學生考試結束前都應該檢查答案。
  3. A good teacher knows all their students' names.
    好老師知道每個學生的名字。
  4. When a customer complains, listen to their problem carefully.
    客人抱怨的時候，要仔細聽他們的問題。

④ 公司和團體 + their

  1. IBM is a very important customer for us, and they ordered 10,000 colour copies which show their new product.
    IBM 是我們很重要的客戶，他們訂了一萬份彩色影本，介紹他們的新產品。
  2. The band played all their old songs at the concert.
    那個樂團在演唱會上唱了他們所有的老歌。
  3. The company has moved their head office to Manchester.
    那家公司把總部搬到曼徹斯特了。
  4. The government has changed their plans again.
    政府又改了他們的計畫。

⑤ 規則和通知

  1. Each passenger may take one bag with them, and they must keep their ticket until the end of the journey.
    每位乘客可以帶一個包包，而且要保留車票到旅程結束。
  2. Anyone who wants to join the club should write their name on the list.
    想加入社團的人，請在名單上寫下名字。
  3. Every member must show their card at the door.
    每位會員在門口都要出示會員卡。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Whose bag is this?" "I don't know. Someone forgot their bag."
    這是誰的包包？不知道。有人忘了拿包包。
  2. "Is the test hard?" "It depends. Everyone has their strong subjects."
    考試難嗎？要看人。每個人都有自己擅長的科目。
  3. "Can we sit anywhere?" "No, each guest has their name on a card."
    我們可以隨便坐嗎？不行，每位客人都有寫名字的座位卡。
