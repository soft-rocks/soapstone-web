Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

燈突然熄了，我發現自己站在一片漆黑之中。

real、absolute、complete、total 加⁠強名⁠詞⁠的語⁠氣

Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

real

Parking is a real problem in this town.

停車在這個鎮上是個很大的問題。
absolute

Tom says cats can't swim. That's absolute nonsense.

湯姆說貓不會游泳。那完全是胡說八道。
total

Grandma is coming in an hour, and your bedroom is a total mess!

奶奶一個小時後就到了，你的臥室還亂七八糟！

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 a

Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

During the power cut, the street was in complete darkness.

停電的時候，整條街一片漆黑。

complete 放⁠在 be 後⁠面⁠時，意⁠思是完⁠成⁠的

Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

名詞前面

Her visit was a complete surprise to us.

她來看我們，完全出乎我們的意料。
be 後面

The work is complete.

工作完成了。

修⁠飾名⁠詞要⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

There was absolutely silence in the room.

There was absolute silence in the room.

darkness 前⁠面不⁠加 a

Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

We sat in a complete darkness.

We sat in complete darkness.

加⁠強語⁠氣⁠的 total 要放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

Suddenly the lights went out, and I found myself standing in complete darkness.

The party was total.

The party was a total success.

例句

① real + 名詞

  1. Noise from the bar next door is a real problem for us.
    隔壁酒吧的噪音對我們來說是個大問題。
  2. Making friends is easy, but finding a real friend is hard.
    交朋友很容易，但是要找到真正的朋友很難。
  3. There used to be an empty car park here. The new park is a real improvement.
    這裡以前是一個空的停車場。新的公園真的改善了很多。
  4. Thanks for cooking while I was sick. You've been a real help this week.
    謝謝你在我生病的時候煮飯。你這個禮拜真的幫了大忙。
  5. It's a real pity your brother missed the wedding.
    你哥哥錯過了婚禮，真的很可惜。
  6. The traffic in the morning is a real nightmare.
    早上的交通真是一場惡夢。

② absolute + 名詞

  1. When the results were read out, there was absolute silence.
    宣讀結果的時候，全場鴉雀無聲。
  2. It rained every day, so the camping trip was an absolute disaster.
    每天都下雨，所以那次露營完全是一場災難。
  3. Tom says cats can't swim. That's absolute nonsense.
    湯姆說貓不會游泳。那完全是胡說八道。
  4. The climb takes three hours, but the view from the top is an absolute joy.
    爬上去要三個小時，可是從山頂看出去的景色讓人無比開心。
  5. I've seen the same doctor for twenty years. I have absolute trust in her.
    我看同一位醫生看了二十年。我完全信任她。
  6. Our school play was an absolute hit with the parents.
    我們學校的話劇大受家長歡迎。

③ complete + 名詞

  1. During the power cut, the street was in complete darkness.
    停電的時候，整條街一片漆黑。
  2. The news was a complete surprise to everyone.
    這個消息讓大家完全意想不到。
  3. That man keeps waving at me, but he's a complete stranger to me.
    那個男人一直跟我揮手，可是他對我來說完全是個陌生人。
  4. We talked for two hours and decided nothing. The meeting was a complete waste of time.
    我們談了兩個小時，什麼都沒決定。那個會議完全是浪費時間。
  5. I've worked every weekend since March. I need a few days of complete rest.
    我從三月開始每個週末都在工作。我需要完全休息幾天。
  6. The kitchen was in complete chaos after the party.
    派對後廚房一片混亂。

④ total + 名詞

  1. Grandma is coming in an hour, and your bedroom is a total mess!
    奶奶一個小時後就到了，你的臥室還亂七八糟！
  2. The cake fell on the floor, and the oven broke. My first dinner party was a total disaster.
    蛋糕掉到地上，烤箱也壞了。我第一次辦的晚餐派對完全是一場災難。
  3. I waved at a woman. She wasn't my sister. I felt like a total fool.
    我跟一個女人揮手。她不是我姊姊。我覺得自己像個大笨蛋。
  4. We lost the game ten to nil. It was a total embarrassment.
    我們零比十輸了比賽。真是丟臉極了。
  5. He's a total stranger. I've never seen him before.
    他是個完全不認識的人，我以前從沒見過他。
  6. The head teacher said the school was going to close. There was total silence after the announcement.
    校長說學校要關閉了。宣布完之後，全場一片寂靜。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. You called the fire brigade about a cat in a tree? Is this a real emergency?
    你為了樹上的一隻貓打電話給消防隊？這是真正的緊急狀況嗎？
  2. It broke down three times in a month. Buying that car was a complete mistake.
    它一個月壞了三次。買那輛車完全是個錯誤。
  3. I didn't know you lived in Rome. What a total surprise to see you here!
    我不知道你住在羅馬。在這裡見到你，真是太意外了！
  4. The tickets are still on sale. It would be a real pity to miss the show.
    票還在賣。錯過這場表演真的很可惜。
  5. We didn't finish the app, but we learned a lot. The project was not a complete failure.
    我們沒有做完那個應用程式，可是我們學到很多。這個專案並沒有完全失敗。
  6. You fed my cat for a whole week? Thank you. You're an absolute star.
    你幫我餵了一整個星期的貓？謝謝你，你真是太棒了。
