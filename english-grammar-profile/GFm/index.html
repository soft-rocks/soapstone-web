I haven't seen you for ages.

好久沒看到你了。

否⁠定⁠句加 for，說一⁠段時⁠間到現⁠在都⁠沒⁠發⁠生

I haven't seen you for ages.

好久沒看到你了。

鄰居三年前搬走，兩個人就沒再見面。

for 後⁠面⁠接一⁠段時⁠間⁠的長⁠度

I haven't seen you for ages.

確切的長度for two weeks、for three years、for six months
不確切的長度for a long time、for a while、for ages、for years、for so long

否⁠定問⁠句把 haven't 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

I haven't seen you for ages.

你不知道山姆換新工作了？你很久沒看到他了嗎？

一⁠段時⁠間⁠的長⁠度用 for，不⁠用 since

I haven't seen you for ages.

My aunt hasn't visited us since three years.

My aunt hasn't visited us for three years.

到現⁠在都⁠沒⁠發⁠生，用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式

I haven't seen you for ages.

We don't go to the zoo for ages.

We haven't been to the zoo for ages.

一⁠段時⁠間⁠的長⁠度用 for，不⁠用 during

I haven't seen you for ages.

My dad hasn't eaten meat during two months.

My dad hasn't eaten meat for two months.

since 後⁠面⁠接上一⁠次發⁠生⁠的時⁠間點

I haven't seen you for ages.

嗨，露西！從婚禮以後我就沒看過你了。

過⁠去簡⁠單式加 for，時⁠間已⁠經結⁠束

I haven't seen you for ages.

我很熟英格蘭北部。我在里茲住過兩年。

肯⁠定⁠句加 for，狀⁠態持⁠續到現⁠在

I haven't seen you for ages.

我知道這條街上所有的店。我在這裡住了十年。

例句

① for + 不確切的長度

  1. Let's go to Mario's tonight. I haven't eaten Italian food for a long time.
    我們今晚去馬力歐的店吧。我很久沒吃義大利菜了。
  2. We both work too much. We haven't been on holiday for ages.
    我們兩個都工作太多。我們好久沒去度假了。
  3. I'm worried about my sister. She hasn't called me for a while.
    我很擔心我姊姊。她有一陣子沒打給我了。
  4. My fingers are slow now. I haven't played the piano for years.
    我的手指現在很不靈活。我好幾年沒彈鋼琴了。
  5. Dear Kate, sorry I haven't written for so long.
    親愛的凱特，抱歉我這麼久沒寫信給你。
  6. My cousins live far from their grandparents. They haven't visited them for a long time.
    我的表弟妹住得離爺爺奶奶很遠。他們很久沒去看爺爺奶奶了。
  7. My brother says he's too busy. He hasn't been to the gym for ages.
    我哥哥說他太忙了。他好久沒去健身房了。

② for + 確切的長度

  1. My dad stopped smoking in May. He hasn't had a cigarette for six months.
    我爸爸五月戒菸了。他六個月沒抽菸了。
  2. The farmers are worried. It hasn't rained for three weeks.
    農夫們很擔心。已經三個禮拜沒下雨了。
  3. We had a big argument about money. My sister and I haven't spoken for two years.
    我們為了錢大吵一架。我跟我姊姊兩年沒講話了。
  4. My brother is very busy in Dubai. He hasn't phoned for a month.
    我哥在杜拜很忙。他一個月沒打電話來了。
  5. I take the train everywhere. I haven't driven a car for ten years.
    我去哪裡都搭火車。我十年沒開車了。
  6. The owner is in hospital. The shop hasn't opened for two days.
    老闆住院了。那家店兩天沒開了。

③ 見面和聯絡

  1. Is Tom all right? We haven't heard from him for weeks.
    湯姆還好嗎？我們好幾個禮拜沒有他的消息了。
  2. I'm going to the school reunion. I haven't talked to my old classmates for years.
    我要去參加同學會。我好幾年沒跟以前的同學聊天了。
  3. I think my friend is angry with me. She hasn't answered my messages for days.
    我覺得我朋友在生我的氣。她好幾天沒回我的訊息了。
  4. Let's have lunch on Friday. We haven't met up for a long time.
    我們星期五一起吃午餐吧。我們很久沒見面了。

④ 習慣和活動

  1. Your old bike is in the garage. You haven't ridden it for months.
    你的舊腳踏車在車庫裡。你好幾個月沒騎了。
  2. We're trying to save money. We haven't eaten out for weeks.
    我們在努力存錢。我們好幾個禮拜沒在外面吃飯了。
  3. I'm always on my phone now. I haven't read a novel for ages.
    我現在一直在滑手機。我好久沒看小說了。
  4. Look at all the dirt on the glass. The office cleaners haven't cleaned the windows for a year.
    你看玻璃上都是灰塵。辦公室的清潔人員一年沒擦窗戶了。
  5. My grandmother loved to dance. She hasn't danced for years.
    我奶奶以前很愛跳舞。她好幾年沒跳舞了。

⑤ 否定問句：Haven't / Hasn't + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + for ...?

  1. You didn't know she was in Spain? Haven't you heard from her for a long time?
    你不知道她在西班牙？你很久沒有她的消息了嗎？
  2. Mia is looking for a job again. Hasn't she worked for months?
    米亞又在找工作了。她好幾個月沒工作了嗎？
  3. The grass next door is so long. Haven't the neighbours cut it for weeks?
    隔壁的草好長。鄰居好幾個禮拜沒割草了嗎？

⑥ 說原因

  1. I'm really hungry. I haven't eaten for hours.
    我好餓，我好幾個小時沒吃東西了。
  2. The plants look dry because we haven't watered them for a week.
    植物看起來很乾，因為我們一個禮拜沒澆水了。
  3. I'm a bit nervous. I haven't sung in public for years.
    我有點緊張，我好幾年沒在大家面前唱歌了。
  4. The car won't start. We haven't used it for months.
    車子發不動，我們好幾個月沒開了。
  5. I'm so tired. I haven't slept properly for days.
    我好累，我好幾天沒好好睡覺了。
