If someone is watching a film that they don't like, they can always choose another channel.

如果有人正在看一部自己不喜歡的電影，他隨時可以轉到別台。

If someone is watching a film that they don't like, they can always choose another channel.

如果有人正在看一部自己不喜歡的電影……

不⁠定代⁠名⁠詞用單⁠數動⁠詞，they 用複⁠數動⁠詞

If someone is watching a film that they don't like, they can always choose another channel.

單數動詞

Someone has left their coat here.

有人把外套忘在這裡了。
複數動詞

If anyone calls, they usually leave a message.

如果有人打電話來，對方通常會留言。

they、them、their、themselves

If someone is watching a film that they don't like, they can always choose another channel.

主詞

If anybody wants a drink, they can help themselves.

誰想喝飲料都可以自己拿。
受詞

If somebody asks for help, give it to them.

如果有人需要幫忙，就幫他。
所有格

Everyone put up their hand.

每個人都舉起了手。
反身代名詞

No one wants to hurt themselves.

誰都不想讓自己受傷。

名⁠詞前⁠面用 their

it 不⁠回指人

If somebody knocks on the door, don't let them in.

反⁠身代⁠名⁠詞是 themselves

Anyone can teach theirselves to cook.

Anyone can teach themselves to cook.

例句

① someone / somebody + they

  1. Someone called while you were out, but they didn't leave a message.
    你出去的時候有人打電話來，但對方沒有留言。
  2. Somebody has taken my pen, and they haven't given it back.
    有人拿走了我的筆，到現在還沒還。
  3. If someone feels sick on the bus, they should tell the driver.
    如果有人在公車上覺得不舒服，應該告訴司機。
  4. Someone in our office said they had seen the accident.
    我們辦公室有人說他看到了那場車禍。
  5. When somebody is sad, they often want to be alone.
    人難過的時候，常常想一個人待著。

② anyone / anybody + they

  1. If anyone needs a lift home, they can come with me.
    誰需要搭便車回家，都可以跟我走。
  2. Anybody can join the club if they are over sixteen.
    年滿十六歲的人都可以加入這個社團。
  3. Anyone who is late will have to wait until they are called.
    遲到的人要等到被叫到才能進去。
  4. If anyone wishes to speak, they should raise their hand.
    有人想發言的話，請先舉手。
  5. Has anybody finished their homework yet?
    有人寫完作業了嗎？

③ everyone / everybody + they

  1. Everyone wants their children to be happy.
    每個人都希望自己的孩子快樂。
  2. Everybody brought something to eat, and they shared it all.
    大家都帶了吃的來，然後全部拿出來分享。
  3. Everyone at the party said they had a great time.
    派對上每個人都說玩得很開心。
  4. Everybody has their own way of learning.
    每個人都有自己的學習方法。
  5. Everyone should check their bags before they leave the train.
    大家下車前都應該檢查一下自己的包包。

④ no one / nobody + they

  1. No one likes it when they lose a game.
    沒有人喜歡輸掉比賽。
  2. Nobody said they were unhappy with the plan.
    沒有人說他們不滿意這個計畫。
  3. No one in the class could believe their eyes.
    班上沒有人敢相信自己的眼睛。
  4. Nobody wants to spend their weekend at work.
    沒有人想把週末花在上班。
  5. No one could find their tickets at the gate.
    在入口的時候，沒有人找得到自己的票。

⑤ them 當受詞

  1. If someone asks for me, tell them I'll be back at three.
    如果有人找我，就說我三點回來。
  2. If anybody needs a pen, I can lend them one.
    如果有人需要筆，我可以借他。
  3. When someone helps you, you should thank them.
    別人幫了你，你應該謝謝人家。
  4. If anyone gets lost, we will go and find them.
    如果有人迷路，我們會去找。
  5. I met someone at the café, but I didn't ask them their name.
    我在咖啡店認識了一個人，但沒問對方叫什麼名字。

⑥ themselves 當反身代名詞

  1. Somebody has hurt themselves in the playground.
    有人在遊樂場受傷了。
  2. Everyone should look after themselves in this cold weather.
    這麼冷的天氣，大家都要照顧好自己。
  3. If anyone wants more food, they can serve themselves.
    誰還想吃，都可以自己去拿。
  4. Nobody enjoyed themselves at the boring meeting.
    那場無聊的會議，沒有人覺得開心。
  5. Someone locked themselves out of their car.
    有人把自己鎖在車外了。
  6. Anyone who cheats is only cheating themselves.
    作弊的人只是在騙自己。
