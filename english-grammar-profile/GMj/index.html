the other + 單⁠數名⁠詞

She saw his blue jacket in a shop on the other side of the street.

她在對街的一家店裡看到了他那件藍色夾克。

the other + 複⁠數名⁠詞

In the first lesson I met all the other people in the class.

第一堂課我認識了班上其他所有的人。

another、other、the other

She saw his blue jacket in a shop on the other side of the street.

another再一個或別的一個，不特定：another biscuit
other別的一些，不是全部：other people
the other雙方知道的，剩下的全部：the other sister

常⁠見⁠的說⁠法

She saw his blue jacket in a shop on the other side of the street.

the other side另一邊、對面
on the other hand另一方面
the other day前幾天
the other way round反過來

兩⁠個⁠裡⁠面剩⁠下⁠的用 the other

I lost one of my gloves, and I can't find another one.

I lost one of my gloves, and I can't find the other one.

名⁠詞前⁠面⁠的 other 不⁠加 s

The others students are in the hall.

The other students are in the hall.

剩⁠下⁠的全⁠部要加 the

Tom is here. Other children are still at school.

Tom is here. The other children are still at school.

改⁠天說 another day

I'm busy now. Let's meet the other day.

I'm busy now. Let's meet another day.

例句

① the other side

  1. The bank is on the other side of the road.
    銀行在馬路對面。
  2. My grandparents live on the other side of town.
    我爺爺奶奶住在鎮的另一頭。
  3. Please write your answer on the other side of the paper.
    請把答案寫在紙的背面。
  4. I could hear music from the other side of the wall.
    我聽得到牆另一邊傳來的音樂。

② 兩個裡面的另一個

  1. I have two cars. One is red, and the other one is black.
    我有兩台車。一台紅的，另一台黑的。
  2. My left ear is fine, but the other ear hurts.
    我的左耳沒事，可是另一隻耳朵很痛。
  3. One twin likes football. The other twin prefers music.
    雙胞胎一個喜歡足球，另一個比較喜歡音樂。
  4. The lift is broken, so use the other lift.
    這台電梯壞了，所以用另一台。

③ 之前的那一個

  1. I moved to this house because the other house was very small, and my brother and I had to share a bedroom.
    我搬到這間房子，因為之前那間太小，我和我哥哥得共用一間臥室。
  2. This job pays better than the other job did.
    這份工作的薪水比之前那份好。
  3. I like this teacher more than the other teacher.
    比起之前那位老師，我比較喜歡這位。

④ the other + 複數名詞

  1. In the first lesson we did some games in English, so I met all the other people in the class.
    第一堂課我們玩了一些英文遊戲，所以我認識了班上其他所有的人。
  2. We need a new delivery company, because the other companies are very expensive.
    我們需要一家新的送貨公司，因為其他公司都很貴。
  3. Anna finished first, and the other runners came in a few minutes later.
    Anna 第一個跑完，其他跑者幾分鐘後才到。
  4. Where are the other plates? I can only find three.
    其他盤子在哪裡？我只找到三個。
  5. Two of the windows are open. Please close the other windows too.
    有兩扇窗戶開著。請把其他窗戶也關上。

⑤ on the other hand

  1. The city is exciting. On the other hand, it's very expensive.
    城市很刺激。另一方面，生活費很貴。
  2. The flat is small. On the other hand, it's close to my office.
    公寓很小。不過另一方面，離我公司很近。
  3. I'd like to go out. On the other hand, I'm really tired.
    我想出去。可是另一方面，我真的很累。

⑥ the other day、the other way round

  1. I saw your brother in the supermarket the other day.
    我前幾天在超市看到你哥哥。
  2. The other day I found an old photo of my parents.
    前幾天我找到一張我爸媽的舊照片。
  3. We had a lovely dinner at that new restaurant the other day.
    前幾天我們在那家新餐廳吃了一頓很棒的晚餐。
  4. It's not my fault, it's the other way round.
    不是我的錯，正好相反。
  5. You put the battery in the other way round.
    你把電池裝反了。
  6. I thought he was older than her, but it's the other way round.
    我以為他比她大，可是正好相反。

⑦ 和 one 對照

  1. One of my shoes is under the bed, but where is the other shoe?
    我有一隻鞋在床底下，可是另一隻鞋在哪？
  2. Lucy is here, but where are the other girls?
    Lucy 在這裡，可是其他女生呢？
  3. Two students passed, and the other students failed.
    兩個學生通過，其他學生都沒過。
  4. One road goes to the beach, and the other road goes to the hills.
    一條路通往海邊，另一條路通往山丘。
  5. I'll carry this bag. You take the other bag.
    我提這個袋子。你拿另一個袋子。
