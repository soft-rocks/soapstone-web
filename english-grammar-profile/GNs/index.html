It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

希望這份報告能幫助社團改進，並滿足全校的需求。

it + 被⁠動⁠語⁠態 + that 子⁠句

It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

希望這份報告能幫助社團改進，並滿足全校的需求。

常⁠用⁠的動⁠詞

It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

看法

It is believed that the castle was built in the 12th century.

一般認為這座城堡建於十二世紀。
決定

It was decided that the meeting would be moved to Friday.

會議決定改到星期五開。
建議

It is recommended that visitors book early.

建議遊客提早訂票。

什⁠麼⁠時⁠候用

It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

不知道是誰

It is said that …

是誰不重要

It was found that …

口語

We hope that …

主⁠詞用 it

It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

Is believed that the painting is a copy.

It is believed that the painting is a copy.

被⁠動⁠語⁠態用過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

It is hope that the new rules will reduce waste.

It is hoped that the new rules will reduce waste.

不⁠用 there 當主⁠詞

It is hoped that this report will help the club to improve and meet the needs of the whole school.

There was agreed that the fees would stay the same.

It was agreed that the fees would stay the same.

主⁠詞 + 被⁠動⁠語⁠態 + to

這座城堡一般認為有八百年歷史。

主⁠動語態說⁠出是⁠誰

我們希望新圖書館能早點開放。

例句

① It is hoped that

  1. It is hoped that the new bridge will reduce traffic in the town centre.
    希望新的橋能減少市中心的車流。
  2. It is hoped that more students will join the drama club next year.
    希望明年有更多學生參加戲劇社。
  3. It was hoped that the talks would end the strike.
    當時希望這次談判能結束罷工。
  4. It is hoped that the money raised will pay for a new playground.
    希望募到的錢夠蓋一座新的遊樂場。
  5. It is hoped that the changes will make the website easier to use.
    希望這些改變能讓網站更好用。

② It is believed / thought that

  1. It is believed that the fire started in the kitchen.
    一般認為火是從廚房燒起來的。
  2. It is thought that around two thousand people attended the concert.
    據估計約有兩千人參加了演唱會。
  3. It is widely believed that exercise improves mood.
    一般普遍認為運動能改善心情。
  4. It was long thought that the illness could not be cured.
    長久以來人們都以為這種病治不好。
  5. It was considered to be the most important thing to supply every computer with Internet and E-mail access.
    當時認為最重要的是讓每台電腦都能上網和收發電子郵件。

③ It was decided / agreed that

  1. It was decided that the school trip would be cancelled.
    學校決定取消校外教學。
  2. It was agreed that each member would pay ten pounds.
    大家同意每位會員付十英鎊。
  3. It has been decided that the shop will close on Sundays.
    已經決定這家店星期天不營業。
  4. At the end of the meeting, it was agreed to review the budget in May.
    會議最後，大家同意五月再檢討預算。
  5. It was decided that a new manager should be appointed.
    會中決定要任命一位新經理。

④ It is said / reported that

  1. It is said that the house is haunted.
    據說那棟房子鬧鬼。
  2. It has been reported that the singer is planning a new tour.
    據報導那位歌手正在計畫新的巡迴演出。
  3. It is often said that travel broadens the mind.
    常有人說旅行能開闊眼界。
  4. It was announced that the factory would close in March.
    已經宣布工廠三月要關閉。
  5. It has been suggested that the park should stay open later in summer.
    有人建議公園夏天應該開放到更晚。

⑤ It is recommended / expected that

  1. It is recommended that students bring their own laptops.
    建議學生自備筆電。
  2. It is expected that prices will rise again next year.
    預計明年物價會再上漲。
  3. It is advised that passengers arrive two hours before their flight.
    建議旅客在班機起飛前兩小時抵達。
  4. It is required that all staff complete the safety course.
    規定所有員工都要完成安全課程。
  5. It is estimated that the work will take six months.
    估計這項工程要花六個月。

⑥ 報告和調查

  1. Firstly it was highlighted that so many people take advantage of the park, but our survey clearly contradicts this view.
    首先，有人強調很多人都會利用這座公園，可是我們的調查清楚地推翻了這個看法。
  2. It was found that most students use the library only before exams.
    調查發現大部分學生只在考試前去圖書館。
  3. It was noted that the changing rooms were often dirty.
    報告指出更衣室常常很髒。
  4. It is suggested that the club buys new balls and nets.
    建議社團購買新的球和球網。
  5. It was felt that the club meetings were too short.
    大家覺得社團聚會的時間太短。
  6. Finally, it is hoped that the school will take these suggestions seriously.
    最後，希望學校能認真看待這些建議。
