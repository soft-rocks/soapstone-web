My sister told me that this film is very good.

我姊姊跟我說這部電影很好看。

told + 聽⁠的人 + that + 子⁠句

我姊姊跟我說這部電影很好看。

代⁠名⁠詞以轉⁠述⁠的人⁠為準

My sister told me that this film is very good.

I / you → he / me

Leo told me that he can help me.

Leo 跟我說他可以幫我。
your / my → my / her

Mum told me my bag is in her car.

媽媽跟我說我的包包在她車上。
we / your → they / our

They told us they're our new neighbours.

他們跟我們說他們是我們的新鄰居。

還成⁠立⁠的事照原⁠話，已⁠經過⁠去⁠的事⁠後移

My sister told me that this film is very good.

還成立

The man told us the shop opens at nine.

那個男人跟我們說那家店九點開。
已經過去

Yesterday Amy told me she was at the station.

Amy 昨天跟我說她在車站。

told 後⁠面要接聽⁠的人

My sister told me that this film is very good.

My teacher told that the test is on Friday.

My teacher told us that the test is on Friday.

聽⁠的人直⁠接放⁠在 told 後⁠面

My sister told me that this film is very good.

The mechanic told to me that the brakes need changing.

The mechanic told me that the brakes need changing.

原話⁠的 I 是老⁠師，換⁠成 she

She told us that I'm our new teacher.

She told us that she's our new teacher.

聽⁠的人用受格

My sister told me that this film is very good.

Paul told I that he likes my drawing.

Paul told me that he likes my drawing.

that 子⁠句轉⁠述一⁠件⁠事，to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞轉⁠述命⁠令

My sister told me that this film is very good.

我姊姊叫我去看這部電影。

said 後⁠面直⁠接接 that 子⁠句

My sister told me that this film is very good.

我姊姊說結局很悲傷。

例句

① told me

  1. My best friend told me that she has a new boyfriend.
    我最好的朋友跟我說她有新男友了。
  2. The doctor told me that my leg isn't broken.
    醫生跟我說我的腿沒斷。
  3. Dad told me that dinner is ready.
    爸爸跟我說晚餐好了。
  4. My classmate told me that the library closes early today.
    我同學跟我說圖書館今天提早關門。
  5. Grandpa told me that he loves my cooking.
    爺爺跟我說他很喜歡我做的菜。

② told us / him / her / them

  1. The waiter told us that the kitchen is closed.
    服務生跟我們說廚房已經關了。
  2. I told him that I don't eat meat.
    我跟他說我不吃肉。
  3. She told them that the party is on Saturday.
    她跟他們說派對在星期六。
  4. We told her that we miss her.
    我們跟她說我們很想她。
  5. The coach told us that we can win this game.
    教練跟我們說我們可以贏這場比賽。

③ told + 名字或名詞

  1. I told my mum that I'm going to be late.
    我跟我媽說我會晚到。
  2. Sam told his boss that he needs a day off.
    Sam 跟老闆說他需要請一天假。
  3. The pilot told the passengers that the weather is good.
    機長跟乘客說天氣很好。
  4. She told the police that someone took her bike.
    她跟警察說有人把她的腳踏車騎走了。
  5. My brother told everyone that he passed his driving test.
    我哥跟大家說他駕照考過了。

④ 省略 that

  1. Ella told me she wants to be a nurse.
    Ella 跟我說她想當護士。
  2. He told me his phone is broken.
    他跟我說他的手機壞了。
  3. My neighbour told me the water is off this morning.
    我鄰居跟我說今天早上停水。
  4. They told us the bus is always late.
    他們跟我們說公車老是誤點。
  5. The teacher told me my English is getting better.
    老師跟我說我的英文越來越好了。

⑤ 代名詞換掉

  1. Jake told me that he'll pick me up at eight.
    Jake 跟我說他八點會來接我。
  2. Lucy told me that her brother knows my brother.
    Lucy 跟我說她哥哥認識我哥哥。
  3. The man told me that my car is blocking his gate.
    那個男人跟我說我的車擋到他家大門了。
  4. My parents told me that they're proud of me.
    我爸媽跟我說他們以我為榮。
  5. Our host told us that we can use her kitchen.
    我們的房東跟我們說我們可以用她的廚房。
  6. You told me that your sister lives in Canada.
    你跟我說過你姊姊住在加拿大。

⑥ 已經過去的事，時態後移

  1. My father told me that he would call after work.
    我爸跟我說他下班後會打給我。
  2. Ken told me that you had broken your arm skiing.
    Ken 跟我說你滑雪摔斷了手臂。
  3. The receptionist told us the room wasn't ready yet.
    櫃台人員跟我們說房間還沒準備好。
  4. She told me she was too tired to go out.
    她跟我說她太累了，不想出門。
  5. He told his teacher that he had forgotten his homework.
    他跟老師說他忘了帶作業。
