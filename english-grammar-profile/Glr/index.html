I don't think I'll live here forever.

我不覺得我會一輩子住在這裡。

否⁠定放⁠在 think 上，後⁠面⁠的子⁠句用肯⁠定⁠的形⁠式

我不覺得我會一輩子住在這裡。

believe、expect、suppose 的否⁠定也放⁠在想⁠法動⁠詞上

I don't think I'll live here forever.

believe

I don't believe she's telling the truth.

我不相信她說的是實話。
expect

I don't expect you remember me.

我想你應該不記得我了。
suppose

I don't suppose you've seen my glasses?

你應該沒看到我的眼鏡吧？

兩⁠種說⁠法都⁠對，說⁠話⁠時多把否⁠定放⁠在 think 上

I don't think I'll live here forever.

較常用

I don't think the plumber will come today.

我覺得水電工今天不會來。
較少用

I think the electrician won't be free until Monday.

我想電工星期一之前不會有空。

hope 的否⁠定放⁠在⁠後⁠面⁠的子⁠句

I don't think I'll live here forever.

希望寶寶在降落前不會醒來。

think 和子⁠句不⁠要都用否⁠定

I don't think I'll live here forever.

I don't think my sister won't like the gift.

I don't think my sister will like the gift.

兩個 not 會變成相反的意思：我覺得她會喜歡。

hope 不⁠用 don't hope

I don't think I'll live here forever.

I don't hope you get lost.

I hope you don't get lost.

主⁠詞是 he、she、it 時用 doesn't think

I don't think I'll live here forever.

She don't think he's coming.

She doesn't think he's coming.

例句

① I don't think + 子句

  1. I like the flat, but I don't think we'll stay more than a year.
    我喜歡這間公寓，可是我不覺得我們會住超過一年。
  2. Take this map if you want, but I don't think it'll help you.
    你想要就拿這張地圖，不過我覺得它幫不了你。
  3. I don't think we've met. I'm Laura.
    我們應該沒見過面。我是 Laura。
  4. I don't think this milk is fresh. It smells funny.
    我覺得這牛奶不新鮮。聞起來怪怪的。
  5. I don't think you need a coat. It's quite warm.
    我覺得你不用穿外套。天氣還滿暖的。
  6. Let's go on Saturday. I don't think the shop is open on Sundays.
    我們星期六去吧。我覺得那家店星期天沒開。
  7. I don't think it's a good idea to drive in this snow.
    我覺得在這種大雪裡開車不是好主意。
  8. I don't think Ben will come tonight. He's very busy.
    我覺得 Ben 今晚不會來。他很忙。
  9. I don't think I can finish the report by Friday.
    我覺得我星期五之前寫不完報告。

② I don't believe / I don't expect / I don't suppose + 子句

  1. I don't believe that the airline will return my suitcase.
    我不相信航空公司會把我的行李箱送回來。
  2. I don't believe she's telling the truth.
    我不相信她說的是實話。
  3. I don't expect the bus will come on time in this weather.
    這種天氣，我想公車不會準時來。
  4. I don't expect you remember me. We met ten years ago.
    我想你應該不記得我了。我們十年前見過面。
  5. I don't suppose you've seen my glasses?
    你應該沒看到我的眼鏡吧？
  6. I don't imagine the tickets will be cheap.
    我想票不會便宜。

③ 其他主詞 + doesn't / don't + think

  1. My mum doesn't think I should leave my job.
    我媽覺得我不應該辭職。
  2. Don't worry about my cough. The doctor doesn't think it's serious.
    別擔心我的咳嗽。醫生覺得不嚴重。
  3. Most people don't think the new road will help.
    大部分人覺得新的道路不會有幫助。
  4. Everyone says the old house has a ghost, but Tom doesn't believe it's true.
    大家都說那間老房子有鬼，可是 Tom 不相信那是真的。
  5. We don't think the plan will work.
    我們覺得這個計畫行不通。

④ didn't think + 子句：過去的想法

  1. I didn't think the exam would be so hard.
    我沒想到考試會這麼難。
  2. We didn't think anyone would come, but the room was full.
    我們以為不會有人來，結果房間坐滿了。
  3. She didn't believe she could win, but she did.
    她不相信自己會贏，可是她贏了。
  4. I didn't expect the hotel would be so nice.
    我沒料到飯店會這麼好。
  5. He didn't think the joke was funny.
    他覺得那個笑話不好笑。

⑤ I don't think so：簡答

  1. "Is it going to rain?" "I don't think so. The sky is clear."
    會下雨嗎？應該不會。天空很晴朗。
  2. "Is Anna coming to the party?" "I don't think so. She's on holiday."
    Anna 會來派對嗎？應該不會。她在度假。

⑥ I hope + 否定的子句

  1. I hope it doesn't rain on the day of the picnic.
    希望野餐那天不會下雨。
  2. I hope you don't mind, but I borrowed your umbrella.
    希望你不介意，我借了你的雨傘。
  3. We hope the noise didn't wake you up.
    希望那些聲音沒吵醒你。
  4. I hope I don't forget anyone's name at the party.
    希望我在派對上不會忘記任何人的名字。
