I know I shouldn't have made it during school time, but I had no other choice.

我知道我不該把約排在上課時間，但我也沒有別的辦法。

主⁠詞 + shouldn't have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

shouldn't have、過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

shouldn't have過去不應該。事情已經做了。
過去分詞過去不應該做的事。

承⁠認錯⁠誤

I know I shouldn't have made it during school time, but I had no other choice.

例句

I shouldn't have shouted at you. I'm sorry.

我不應該對你大吼。對不起。

I shouldn't have 常用來承認自己做錯了。

批⁠評別⁠人

例句

You shouldn't have driven so fast.

你不應該開那麼快的。

You shouldn't have 也可以批評別人做的事。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I know I shouldn't have made it during school time, but I had no other choice.

I shouldn't have ate so much.

I shouldn't have eaten so much.

should 的否⁠定是 shouldn't，不⁠用 didn't

I know I shouldn't have made it during school time, but I had no other choice.

I didn't should have said that.

I shouldn't have said that.

shouldn't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 have

I know I shouldn't have made it during school time, but I had no other choice.

We shouldn't had gone out.

We shouldn't have gone out.

例句

① I know I shouldn't have + 過去分詞, but …：解釋原因

  1. I have a dentist's appointment tomorrow. I know I shouldn't have made it during school time, but I had no other choice.
    我明天要看牙醫。我知道我不應該約在上課時間，可是我沒有別的選擇。
  2. I know I shouldn't have spent so much, but the shoes were perfect.
    我知道我不應該花那麼多錢，可是那雙鞋太完美了。
  3. I know I shouldn't have left early, but I felt ill.
    我知道我不應該提早走，可是我覺得不舒服。
  4. I know I shouldn't have opened your letter, but I thought it was for me.
    我知道我不應該拆你的信，可是我以為那是給我的。
  5. I know I shouldn't have taken the car, but the buses were on strike.
    我知道我不應該開車，可是公車在罷工。
  6. I know I shouldn't have stayed up so late, but the book was so good.
    我知道我不應該熬夜到那麼晚，可是那本書太好看了。

② I shouldn't have + 過去分詞：後悔和道歉

  1. I shouldn't have shouted at you this morning. I'm really sorry.
    我今天早上不應該對你大吼。真的很對不起。
  2. I feel sick. I shouldn't have eaten that third piece of cake.
    我覺得想吐。我不應該吃第三塊蛋糕的。
  3. I shouldn't have told her your secret. It just slipped out.
    我不應該把你的秘密告訴她的。我不小心說出來了。
  4. I shouldn't have believed everything the salesman said.
    我不應該相信那個推銷員說的每一句話。
  5. I shouldn't have lent him my camera. He broke it.
    我不應該把相機借給他的。他弄壞了。
  6. I shouldn't have drunk coffee so late. Now I can't sleep.
    我不應該那麼晚喝咖啡的。現在我睡不著。

③ You shouldn't have + 過去分詞：批評對方

  1. You shouldn't have driven so fast in the rain.
    你不應該在雨中開那麼快的。
  2. You shouldn't have left the door open. The cat got out.
    你不應該讓門開著的。貓跑出去了。
  3. You shouldn't have laughed at him. He was upset.
    你不應該笑他的。他很難過。
  4. You shouldn't have walked home alone so late.
    你不應該那麼晚一個人走路回家的。
  5. You shouldn't have touched the oven. It was still hot.
    你不應該碰烤箱的。它還很燙。
  6. You shouldn't have paid so much for that old bike.
    你不應該花那麼多錢買那台舊腳踏車的。

④ We / They + shouldn't have + 過去分詞

  1. We shouldn't have come on a Saturday. It's so crowded.
    我們不應該星期六來的。人好多。
  2. They shouldn't have built the houses so close to the river.
    他們不應該把房子蓋得離河那麼近的。
  3. We shouldn't have ordered so much food. We can't finish it.
    我們不應該點那麼多食物的。我們吃不完。
  4. They shouldn't have cut down the old oak tree.
    他們不應該砍掉那棵老橡樹的。
  5. We shouldn't have stayed in the sun all afternoon.
    我們不應該整個下午都待在太陽底下的。
  6. They shouldn't have started the match in such heavy rain.
    他們不應該在那麼大的雨中開始比賽的。

⑤ He / She + shouldn't have + 過去分詞

  1. He shouldn't have said those things to his mother.
    他不應該對他媽媽說那些話的。
  2. She shouldn't have gone back into the burning house.
    她不應該回到著火的房子裡的。
  3. He shouldn't have quit his job before finding a new one.
    他不應該在找到新工作之前就辭職的。
  4. She shouldn't have trusted that website with her card details.
    她不應該在那個網站上留她的信用卡資料的。
  5. He shouldn't have parked on the double yellow lines.
    他不應該把車停在雙黃線上的。
  6. She shouldn't have carried such a heavy bag with a bad back.
    她背不好，不應該提那麼重的袋子的。
  7. He shouldn't have skipped breakfast before the race.
    他比賽前不應該不吃早餐的。
