名⁠詞前⁠面用 those

It's black and pink, and I like those colors very much.

例句

① 前一句 + those + 名詞：東西

  1. My new bag is black and pink, and I like those colours very much.
    我的新書包是黑色和粉紅色的，我非常喜歡那兩種顏色。
  2. I went shopping yesterday and bought some jumpers and trousers. I bought those clothes because I'm going to Japan next Friday, and the weather is cold there.
    我昨天去買衣服，買了一些毛衣和長褲。我買那些衣服是因為下星期五要去日本，那裡天氣很冷。
  3. My grandmother gave me some old coins. I still keep those coins in a box.
    我奶奶給了我一些舊硬幣。我還把那些硬幣放在一個盒子裡。
  4. We ordered pizza and chips. Those dishes are always popular with the kids.
    我們點了披薩和薯條。孩子們總是很喜歡那些菜。

② 前一句 + those + 名詞：人

  1. I met two Italian students at the language school. Those girls are now my best friends.
    我在語言學校認識了兩個義大利學生。那兩個女孩現在是我最好的朋友。
  2. Some fans waited outside the hotel all night. Those people really love the band.
    有些歌迷在飯店外面等了一整晚。那些人真的很愛那個樂團。
  3. Two men asked me for money in the street. I didn't trust those men.
    街上有兩個男人跟我要錢。我不相信那兩個人。

③ 前一句 + those + 名詞：地方

  1. Last year I visited Paris and Rome. Those cities are full of history.
    去年我去了巴黎和羅馬。那兩座城市充滿了歷史。
  2. We stayed in two small villages in the mountains. Those places were very quiet.
    我們在山上兩個小村子住過。那些地方很安靜。
  3. My uncle lived in Brazil and Mexico. He loved those countries.
    我叔叔住過巴西和墨西哥。他很喜歡那兩個國家。

④ 前一句 + those + 名詞：時間和事情

  1. I had exams in May and June. I studied hard during those months.
    我五月和六月有考試。那兩個月我很用功。
  2. My parents told me about their first dates. I love those stories.
    我爸媽跟我講他們第一次約會的事。我很喜歡那些故事。
  3. We sang some songs from the 1980s. Those songs remind me of my childhood.
    我們唱了幾首八〇年代的歌。那些歌讓我想起我的童年。

⑤ 寫給朋友的信

  1. I've sent you some photos of the beach. I took those pictures on my new phone.
    我寄了幾張海邊的照片給你。那些照片是用我的新手機拍的。
  2. You asked about the books I read. I read those books for my English class.
    你問到我讀的那幾本書。那些書是我英文課要讀的。
  3. My aunt made two cakes for my birthday. Those cakes were delicious.
    我阿姨幫我做了兩個生日蛋糕。那兩個蛋糕很好吃。

⑥ 對話

  1. "I loved the red shoes and the blue boots." "Why didn't you buy those shoes?"
    我很喜歡那雙紅鞋和那雙藍靴子。你為什麼沒買那雙鞋？
  2. "Tom and Jack are coming tonight." "Oh no, those boys are so noisy!"
    Tom 和 Jack 今晚要來。不會吧，那兩個男生好吵！
  3. "We went to the museum and the old castle." "I've never been to those places."
    我們去了博物館和那座古堡。那些地方我都沒去過。
