It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

原來是我的老同學 Tom，我好久沒見到他了！

who 當受⁠詞，給前⁠面⁠的人補⁠充資⁠料

原來是我的老同學 Tom，我好久沒見到他了！

who 是 seen 的受詞。逗號表示後面是補充資料。

子⁠句在句⁠子中⁠間，前⁠後都加逗⁠號

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

句子中間

My cousin Mei, who you met at my wedding, is moving to Canada.

我表妹小美要搬去加拿大了，你在我的婚禮上見過她。

正⁠式⁠的書⁠面語用 whom，日常⁠用 who

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

whom

Professor Lee, whom we interviewed last week, has written a new book.

我們上禮拜訪問的李教授寫了一本新書。

子⁠句⁠的動⁠詞接介⁠系⁠詞，who 是介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

play with

I'm meeting Daniel, who I used to play football with.

我要跟 Daniel 見面，我以前常跟他一起踢足球。

who 已⁠經是受⁠詞，子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 him

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

I called my uncle, who I hadn't spoken to him for months.

I called my uncle, who I hadn't spoken to for months.

非限⁠定關⁠係子⁠句不⁠用 that

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

We visited Grandma, that we see every Sunday.

We visited Grandma, who we see every Sunday.

指人用 who，不⁠用 which

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

I invited Sam, which I met at university.

I invited Sam, who I met at university.

非限⁠定關⁠係子⁠句不⁠能省⁠略 who

It was my old classmate Tom, who I hadn't seen for ages!

This is Mr. Wang, I told you about.

This is Mr. Wang, who I told you about.

例句

① 子句在句尾

  1. At the station I saw Lucy, who I hadn't met since high school.
    我在車站看到 Lucy，我高中以後就沒見過她了。
  2. We stayed with my aunt, who we visit every summer.
    我們住在我阿姨家，我們每年夏天都會去看她。
  3. I've just had an email from Kevin, who I worked with in Tokyo.
    我剛收到 Kevin 的電子郵件，我在東京的時候跟他一起工作。
  4. Last night I phoned my grandfather, who I don't call often enough.
    我昨晚打電話給我爺爺，我太少打給他了。
  5. She's bringing her boyfriend, who none of us have met yet.
    她要帶她男朋友來，我們都還沒見過他。
  6. They hired Ms. Huang, who the manager had recommended.
    他們雇用了黃小姐，她是經理推薦的。

② 子句在句子中間

  1. Our neighbor, who we hardly ever see, left a cake at our door.
    我們很少見到的鄰居在我們門口放了一個蛋糕。
  2. My piano teacher, who I adored as a child, still lives in the same house.
    我小時候很喜歡的鋼琴老師還住在同一棟房子裡。
  3. The new coach, who the players really respect, has changed everything.
    新教練很受球員尊敬，他改變了一切。
  4. Ana, who I share a flat with, is learning Chinese.
    Ana 在學中文，我跟她合租一間公寓。
  5. Mr. Kuo, who we all called "the Professor," retired last month.
    郭先生上個月退休了，我們都叫他教授。
  6. My older brother, who I always argued with, is now my best friend.
    我以前老是跟我哥哥吵架，現在他是我最好的朋友。

③ 子句動詞後面接介系詞

  1. I ran into Peter, who I used to go to school with.
    我遇到 Peter，我以前跟他同校。
  2. We had dinner with the Changs, who we stayed with in Kaohsiung.
    我們跟張家吃了晚餐，我們在高雄的時候住在他們家。
  3. That's Rosa, who I sat next to at the conference.
    那就是 Rosa，我在研討會上坐在她旁邊。
  4. Our host, who we'd heard so much about, met us at the airport.
    我們的房東到機場接我們，我們聽說過他很多事。
  5. The nurse, who I spoke to on the phone, was really helpful.
    那個護理師真的幫了很大的忙，我在電話上跟她說過話。

④ whom（正式）

  1. The author, whom we invited to speak, sent a short reply.
    那位作者寄來了簡短的回覆，我們邀請過他來演講。
  2. Dr. Ho, whom the hospital appointed in May, starts on Monday.
    何醫師星期一開始上班，醫院是五月聘她的。
  3. The applicants, whom we interviewed yesterday, will hear from us by Friday.
    我們昨天面試的應徵者星期五之前會收到我們的通知。
  4. Mr. Lai, whom I contacted last week, has not replied.
    賴先生還沒回覆，我上禮拜聯絡過他。

⑤ 不同的時態和情境

  1. Have you met Joe, who my sister is going to marry?
    你見過 Joe 嗎？我姊姊要嫁給他。
  2. I'm going to visit Mrs. Tsai, who I'll miss a lot when she moves.
    我要去看蔡太太，她搬走以後我會很想她。
  3. We're inviting Ben, who everyone likes.
    我們要邀請 Ben，大家都喜歡他。
  4. My little sister, who I'm babysitting tonight, refuses to go to bed.
    我今晚幫忙照顧的妹妹不肯上床睡覺。
  5. My best friend, who I've known for twenty years, has always been there for me.
    我最好的朋友一直都在我身邊，我認識他二十年了。
  6. The zealous young reporter, who the mayor had refused to see, waited outside all day.
    那個熱血的年輕記者在外面等了一整天，市長拒絕見他。
  7. Our old landlord, who we never really liked, is selling the building.
    我們的舊房東要把大樓賣掉了，我們一直都不太喜歡他。
  8. I finally reconciled with my father, who I hadn't spoken to in two years.
    我終於跟我爸爸和好了，我兩年沒跟他說話了。
  9. Tell Ken, who you'll see before me, that the meeting is cancelled.
    你會比我先見到 Ken，跟他說會議取消了。
