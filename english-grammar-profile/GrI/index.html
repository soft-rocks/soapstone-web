It was sunny and really hot every day!

每天都出大太陽，真的好熱！

really、so、quite 的程⁠度不⁠一⁠樣

It was sunny and really hot every day!

really ≈ very

Don't jump in yet. The pool is really cold.

先別跳下去，游泳池的水真的很冰。
so，比 very 強

I'm so tired. I'm going to bed.

我好累，我要去睡了。
quite，比 very 弱

It's quite cold today. Take a jacket.

今天滿冷的，帶件外套吧。

名⁠詞前⁠面：a really，quite a

It was sunny and really hot every day!

a + really + 形容詞

We had a really nice dinner at the new restaurant.

我們在那家新餐廳吃了一頓很棒的晚餐。
quite + a + 形容詞

Take the bus. It's quite a long walk to the museum.

搭公車吧，走到博物館滿遠的。

quite 放⁠在 a 前⁠面

It was sunny and really hot every day!

They live in a quite big house.

They live in quite a big house.

形⁠容⁠詞後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞⁠時，用 such a

It was sunny and really hot every day!

It was a so nice day.

It was such a nice day.

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

It was sunny and really hot every day!

The shoes are expensive quite.

The shoes are quite expensive.

例句

① really + 形容詞

  1. Don't jump in yet. The pool is really cold.
    先別跳下去，游泳池的水真的很冰。
  2. Your new haircut looks really good.
    你的新髮型真的很好看。
  3. The math homework was really hard tonight.
    今天晚上的數學作業真的很難。
  4. Thanks for the help. You're really kind.
    謝謝你幫忙，你人真的很好。
  5. The bus was really crowded this morning.
    今天早上公車真的很擠。
  6. Is the hotel really expensive in July?
    那家旅館七月真的很貴嗎？
  7. We had a really nice dinner at the new restaurant.
    我們在那家新餐廳吃了一頓很棒的晚餐。

② so + 形容詞

  1. I'm so happy we can have a party for my birthday!
    我好開心我們可以一起幫我辦生日派對！
  2. Thank you for the flowers. They're so beautiful.
    謝謝你送的花，好漂亮。
  3. I'm so tired. I'm going to bed.
    我好累，我要去睡了。
  4. Look at that puppy in the window! It's so cute!
    你看櫥窗裡那隻小狗！好可愛！
  5. I almost fell asleep. The movie was so boring.
    我差點睡著，那部電影好無聊。
  6. I said sorry. Why are you still so upset?
    我已經道歉了，你為什麼還那麼不高興？
  7. Turn on the fan. The kitchen is so hot.
    開電扇吧，廚房好熱。

③ quite + 形容詞

  1. The jeans are quite expensive, but they're good quality.
    這條牛仔褲相當貴，不過品質很好。
  2. The town is quite small, so we walked everywhere.
    那個小鎮不大，所以我們到哪裡都用走的。
  3. The people in my new office are quite friendly.
    我新辦公室的同事還算友善。
  4. The exam was quite easy for most of us.
    這次考試對我們大部分的人來說還算簡單。
  5. It's quite cold today. Take a jacket.
    今天滿冷的，帶件外套吧。
  6. Her English is quite good for a beginner.
    以初學者來說，她的英文算很好了。

④ quite a + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. Take the bus. It's quite a long walk to the museum.
    搭公車吧，走到博物館滿遠的。
  2. They live in quite a big house near the park.
    他們住在公園附近一棟相當大的房子。
  3. Ken asked quite a strange question in class.
    Ken 在課堂上問了一個滿奇怪的問題。
  4. We had quite a good time at the beach.
    我們在海邊玩得滿開心的。
  5. She's quite a fast runner.
    她跑得滿快的。

⑤ 兩個形容詞

  1. The rooms were bright and really warm in winter.
    冬天房間很亮，而且真的很暖和。
  2. The room was clean and really quiet.
    房間很乾淨，而且真的很安靜。
  3. The teacher was so patient and so kind.
    老師好有耐心，人又好。
  4. The shop is small but quite busy.
    那家店很小，但是客人還算多。
  5. The cake looked so pretty and tasted really good.
    蛋糕看起來好漂亮，吃起來也真的很好吃。
