我到了那裡才發現，得獎的不只我一個。

that 子⁠句是 realized 的直⁠接受⁠詞

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

我到了那裡才發現，得獎的不只我一個。

心⁠理動⁠詞和轉⁠述動⁠詞都⁠可⁠以接 that 子⁠句

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

心理動詞

I understand that you're angry, but please let me explain.

我理解你很生氣，可是請讓我解釋。
轉述動詞

He finally admitted that he had eaten the last biscuit.

他終於承認最後一塊餅乾是他吃的。

動⁠詞用過⁠去⁠式⁠時，子⁠句常跟⁠著用過⁠去⁠式

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

過去式

She explained that the train had left.

她解釋說火車已經開走了。
現在還是真的

I totally understand that you don't want to go on holiday with your parents.

我完全理解你不想和爸媽去度假。

suggest 的子⁠句用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，或 should 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

原形動詞

The doctor suggested that he rest for a week.

醫生建議他休息一個星期。
should + 原形動詞

The doctor suggested that he should rest for a week.

醫生建議他休息一個星期。

動⁠詞後⁠面⁠接子⁠句⁠時用 that，不⁠用 to

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

I realized to I had lost my wallet.

I realized that I had lost my wallet.

explain 要說⁠出聽⁠話⁠的人⁠時，在人前⁠面加 to

He explained me that the shop was closed.

He explained to me that the shop was closed.

suggest 後⁠面不⁠接人加 to 不⁠定⁠詞

She suggested me to take a taxi.

She suggested that I take a taxi.

that 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句要⁠有主⁠詞

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

He admitted that made a mistake.

He admitted that he made a mistake.

主⁠詞相⁠同⁠時，decide 常接 to 不⁠定⁠詞

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

We decided to stay at home.

我們決定待在家。

名⁠詞後⁠面⁠的 that 是關⁠係⁠代⁠名⁠詞

When I got there, I realized that I wasn't the only winner.

I remember the song that you sang.

我記得你唱的那首歌。

例句

① realize / notice / find + that 子句

  1. On the way home, I realized that I had left my umbrella at the office.
    回家的路上，我才發現我把傘忘在辦公室了。
  2. "Why did you go back?" "I noticed that the back door was open."
    你為什麼回去？我注意到後門開著。
  3. After a week in the new job, she found that she really liked it.
    新工作做了一個星期，她發覺她真的很喜歡。
  4. We suddenly realized that the shop was closing.
    我們突然發現店要關門了。

② understand / believe + that 子句

  1. I understand that you're angry, but please let me explain.
    我理解你很生氣，可是請讓我解釋。
  2. Many people believe that the old castle is haunted.
    很多人相信那座老城堡鬧鬼。
  3. "Is the meeting still on?" "I believe that it's been moved to Thursday."
    會議還照常開嗎？我記得是改到星期四了。
  4. My parents finally understood that I wanted to study art.
    我爸媽終於理解我想念藝術。

③ remember / forget + that 子句

  1. I remember that we went to this beach when I was a child.
    我記得我小時候我們來過這個海灘。
  2. "Why are you here on a Sunday?" "I forgot that the office was closed today."
    你為什麼星期天在這裡？我忘了今天辦公室沒開。
  3. Don't forget that the shops close early on Christmas Eve.
    別忘了平安夜店家會提早關門。

④ expect + that 子句

  1. We expect that prices will go up next year.
    我們預料明年價格會上漲。
  2. "Was the test hard?" "Yes. I didn't expect that it would be so long."
    考試難嗎？難。我沒想到會那麼長。

⑤ explain / mention + that 子句

  1. The pilot explained that the flight was late because of the snow.
    機長解釋說班機因為下雪而誤點。
  2. "Did she say why she left?" "She mentioned that she wasn't feeling well."
    她有說她為什麼走嗎？她提到她不太舒服。
  3. The manager explained to us that the hotel was full.
    經理跟我們解釋說飯店客滿了。

⑥ admit / agree / complain + that 子句

  1. He finally admitted that he had eaten the last biscuit.
    他終於承認最後一塊餅乾是他吃的。
  2. "Did you two stop arguing?" "Yes, we agreed that we were both wrong."
    你們兩個不吵了嗎？對，我們都同意兩個人都有錯。
  3. The neighbours complained that our dog barked all night.
    鄰居抱怨我們的狗叫了一整晚。
  4. Everyone agreed that the film was too long.
    大家都同意那部電影太長了。

⑦ decide / promise + that 子句

  1. After a long talk, we decided that we would sell the house.
    談了很久以後，我們決定把房子賣掉。
  2. "Are you staying in London?" "No, I've decided that I'll move back home."
    你要留在倫敦嗎？不，我決定搬回老家。
  3. My son promised that he would tidy his room.
    我兒子答應會整理他的房間。
  4. The company has promised that nobody will lose their job.
    公司保證不會有人失業。

⑧ suggest + that 子句

  1. "I'm so tired." "I suggest that you go to bed early tonight."
    我好累。我建議你今晚早點睡。
  2. My teacher suggested that I should read more English books.
    我的老師建議我多讀英文書。
  3. The guide suggested that we visit the museum in the morning.
    導遊建議我們早上去參觀博物館。

⑨ 省略 that

  1. I realized I didn't have any cash, so I paid by card.
    我發現我沒帶現金，所以我刷卡。
  2. "Do you know Anna?" "I remember we met at a party."
    你認識 Anna 嗎？我記得我們在一個派對上見過。
  3. She admitted she was scared of the dark.
    她承認她怕黑。
  4. "Ben isn't answering his phone." "I suppose he's still at work."
    Ben 沒接電話。我猜他還在上班。
