I like her because she is like me.

我喜歡她，因為她跟我很像。

be like + 名⁠詞，意⁠思是像

I like her because she is like me.

我哥哥跟我爸爸很像。

動⁠詞 like 是喜⁠歡，be like 是像

I like her because she is like me.

我喜歡她，因為她跟我很像。

What is ... like? 問人或東⁠西怎⁠麼⁠樣

I like her because she is like me.

What's your new teacher like?

你的新老師人怎麼樣？

表⁠示⁠像，要⁠用 be like

I like her because she is like me.

She like her mother.

She is like her mother.

like 後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

I like her because she is like me.

My twin brother is like I in every way.

My twin brother is like me in every way.

說像什⁠麼用 like，不⁠用 as

I like her because she is like me.

It's as a little train.

It's like a little train.

例句

① be like + 代名詞

  1. I get on well with Ann because she's like me.
    我跟 Ann 很合得來，因為她跟我很像。
  2. He has your eyes and your laugh. Your son is just like you.
    他有你的眼睛和你的笑聲。你兒子跟你一模一樣。
  3. Ken is like his father. They're both very quiet.
    Ken 跟他爸爸很像，兩個人都很安靜。
  4. I love sport, and she loves books. My sister isn't like me at all.
    我愛運動，她愛看書。我姊姊跟我一點都不像。
  5. I've met both your parents now. Are you like your mother or your father?
    我現在見過你爸媽了。你比較像你媽媽還是你爸爸？
  6. Our neighbours have two kids and a dog too. We are like them in many ways.
    我們的鄰居也有兩個孩子和一隻狗。我們在很多方面跟他們很像。

② be like + 名詞

  1. Take the tram to the castle. It's like a little train that goes up the hill.
    搭電車去城堡。那像一台開上山的小火車。
  2. This café is like a library. Everyone is reading.
    這家咖啡店像圖書館，每個人都在看書。
  3. Nobody lived in the house for years. The garden was like a forest.
    那間房子好幾年沒人住。那座花園像一片森林。
  4. Mr Chen eats lunch with us and listens to our problems. Our teacher is like a friend to us.
    陳老師跟我們一起吃午餐，聽我們說煩惱。我們的老師對我們來說像朋友一樣。
  5. We watched the sun go down from the beach. The sky was like fire at sunset.
    我們在海灘上看太陽下山。夕陽西下時，天空像火一樣。
  6. She keeps old clocks and maps in every room. My grandmother's house is like a museum.
    她每個房間都放了舊時鐘和舊地圖。我奶奶家像一座博物館。

③ just like / a bit like / not like

  1. Look at their baby photos. The baby is just like her big sister.
    你看她們嬰兒時期的照片。那個小寶寶跟她姊姊一模一樣。
  2. There's a river and a small market here. This town is a bit like my hometown.
    這裡有一條河和一個小市場。這個小鎮有點像我的家鄉。
  3. Don't mix them up! Your new phone is just like mine.
    別拿錯了！你的新手機跟我的一模一樣。
  4. I've been in London for a month. The food here isn't like the food at home.
    我在倫敦一個月了。這裡的食物跟家裡的不一樣。
  5. Max reads poems at break time. He's not like the other boys in class.
    麥克斯下課時間讀詩。他跟班上其他男生不一樣。

④ What is ... like?

  1. What's the food like at the new Thai restaurant?
    新開的泰國餐廳的菜怎麼樣？
  2. Welcome back from your trip! What was the weather like in Tokyo?
    歡迎旅行回來！東京的天氣怎麼樣？
  3. So you moved last week. What's your new flat like?
    你上禮拜搬家了，新公寓怎麼樣？
  4. You've got a new boss. What's she like?
    你換了新主管，她人怎麼樣？
  5. You stayed by the lake last weekend. What was the hotel like?
    你上個週末住在湖邊。那家飯店怎麼樣？
  6. I've always lived in the city. What's it like to live by the sea?
    我一直住在城市裡。住在海邊是什麼感覺？

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. Now I'm quiet, but when I was young, I was like my brother. I never stopped talking.
    我現在很安靜，可是我小時候跟我哥哥很像。我一直講個不停。
  2. My cousin finished the maths test in ten minutes. The test was like a game for him.
    我表哥十分鐘就寫完數學考試。那次考試對他來說像遊戲一樣。
  3. We're moving next month. Will the new office be like the old one?
    我們下個月要搬。新辦公室會跟舊的一樣嗎？
  4. She signed my T-shirt after the concert. Meeting my favorite singer was like a dream.
    音樂會結束後她在我的 T 恤上簽名。見到我最喜歡的歌手就像一場夢。
  5. Try a cup. This tea is a bit like coffee.
    喝一杯看看。這個茶喝起來有點像咖啡。
  6. My twin cousins both love football and hate fish. Twins are often like each other in many ways.
    我的雙胞胎表弟都愛足球、討厭魚。雙胞胎常常在很多方面很像。
  7. Look up, Mum! That cloud is like a rabbit!
    媽，你看上面！那朵雲像一隻兔子！
