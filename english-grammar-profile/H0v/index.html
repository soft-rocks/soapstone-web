They were the happiest days of my life.

那些日子是我這輩子最快樂的時光。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語說⁠明在什⁠麼範⁠圍⁠裡最怎⁠麼⁠樣

They were the happiest days of my life.

那是亞洲最大的水族館。

地⁠方和團⁠體用 in，時⁠間和一⁠群用 of

They were the happiest days of my life.

in + 地方 / 團體

Grandpa is the oldest person in our village.

爺爺是我們村子裡年紀最大的人。
of + 時間 / 一群

Sports day was the best day of the school year.

運動會是整個學年最棒的一天。

最⁠高⁠級前⁠面要加 the

They were the happiest days of my life.

Lake Baikal is deepest lake on Earth.

Lake Baikal is the deepest lake on Earth.

範⁠圍是地⁠方或團⁠體⁠時用 in

They were the happiest days of my life.

Our hotel has the biggest swimming pool of the city.

Our hotel has the biggest swimming pool in the city.

在一⁠個範⁠圍⁠裡最怎⁠麼⁠樣，用最⁠高⁠級

They were the happiest days of my life.

That's the taller tree in the whole park.

That's the tallest tree in the whole park.

例句

① 最高級 + 名詞 + in the world / country / city

  1. We took the kids to see the pandas. Ueno has the oldest zoo in Japan.
    我們帶孩子們去看貓熊。上野有日本最古老的動物園。
  2. More than thirty million people live in and around Tokyo. It's the largest city in Japan.
    東京和周邊住了超過三千萬人。它是日本最大的城市。
  3. The guide stopped outside a small stone church. This is the oldest building in the city.
    導遊在一間石頭砌的小教堂外面停下來。這是這座城市裡最老的建築。
  4. Here's the last question in the quiz. What's the longest river in the world?
    這是問答比賽的最後一題。世界上最長的河是哪一條？
  5. Taipei 101 was once the tallest building in the world.
    台北 101 曾經是世界上最高的大樓。
  6. You can't visit Madrid without seeing the Prado. It's the most famous museum in the country.
    你去馬德里一定要去普拉多。那是這個國家最有名的博物館。

② 最高級 + 名詞 + in my class / family / team

  1. Mia is the shortest girl in our team.
    Mia 是我們隊上最矮的女生。
  2. We had a party for Grandma's ninety-fifth birthday. She's the oldest person in our family.
    我們為奶奶九十五歲生日辦了派對。她是我們家年紀最大的人。
  3. Put Lily in the last race. She's the fastest swimmer in our club.
    讓莉莉游最後一棒。她是我們俱樂部裡游得最快的人。
  4. Ken is only four. He's the youngest student in the school.
    Ken 才四歲。他是學校裡年紀最小的學生。
  5. You play basketball every weekend. Who is the best player in your team?
    你每個週末都打籃球。你們隊上最厲害的球員是誰？
  6. The waiter took us to a corner by the window. This is the quietest table in the restaurant.
    服務生帶我們到窗邊的角落。這是餐廳裡最安靜的桌子。

③ 最高級 + 名詞 + of my life / of the year

  1. Those summers at the beach were the best times of my childhood.
    在海邊度過的那些夏天是我童年最美好的時光。
  2. For us, Christmas Day is the happiest day of the year.
    對我們來說，聖誕節是一年當中最快樂的一天。
  3. We sell lots of toys before Christmas. December is the busiest month of the year.
    聖誕節前我們賣很多玩具。十二月是一年當中最忙的月份。
  4. Our car broke down in the snow, and we slept in it. That was the worst night of my life.
    我們的車在雪地裡拋錨，我們就睡在車上。那是我這輩子最糟的一晚。
  5. I have six classes and a piano lesson on Fridays. Friday is the longest day of the week for me.
    我星期五有六堂課和一堂鋼琴課。對我來說，星期五是一週裡最漫長的一天。
  6. Our goalkeeper saved a penalty in the last minute. It was the most exciting moment of the game.
    我們的守門員在最後一分鐘擋下了一記十二碼罰球。那是比賽中最刺激的一刻。

④ 最高級 + 名詞 + of all / of the three

  1. The shop assistant showed me five phones. This is the cheapest of all the phones.
    店員給我看了五支手機。這是所有手機裡最便宜的。
  2. Mrs Lee always helped me after class. She was the kindest of all my teachers.
    李老師總是在下課後幫我。她是我所有老師裡最和善的。
  3. Ann can reach the top shelf. She's the tallest of the three sisters.
    Ann 搆得到最上面那一層。她是三姊妹裡最高的。
  4. There are three ways to the station. This road is the shortest of the three.
    去車站有三條路。這條路是三條裡最短的。
  5. I failed the chemistry exam. It was the hardest of all the tests.
    我化學考試不及格。那是所有考試裡最難的。
  6. Invite Jack to the party. He's the funniest of my friends.
    邀傑克來派對吧。他是我朋友裡最好笑的。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. We have one day to see the temples. Which is the oldest temple in Tainan?
    我們只有一天可以看廟。台南最古老的廟是哪一間？
  2. We've walked for an hour and we're still in the park. Is this the biggest park in Taipei?
    我們走了一個小時，還在公園裡。這是台北最大的公園嗎？
  3. My cousin got married by a lake in June. It was the most beautiful wedding of the summer.
    我表姊六月在湖邊結婚。那是那個夏天最美的婚禮。
  4. I want to read a book from Korea. Who's the most popular writer in your country right now?
    我想讀一本韓國的書。你們國家現在最受歡迎的作家是誰？
  5. All the shops in our village close on Sundays. Sunday is the quietest day of the week here.
    我們村子裡的店星期天都關門。這裡一週最安靜的是星期天。
  6. Sit here, Grandpa. This is the most comfortable chair in the house.
    爺爺，坐這裡。這是家裡最舒服的椅子。
