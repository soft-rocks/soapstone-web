That period of my life was one of the worst.

那段時間是我人生中最糟的時期之一。

one of the + 最⁠高⁠級，後⁠面省⁠略複⁠數名⁠詞

That period of my life was one of the worst.

那年冬天是最冷的幾個冬天之一。

the 和 one of the 一⁠起用

That period of my life was one of the worst.

He's not the tallest player, but he's one of the tallest.

他不是最高的球員，不過是最高的之一。

在一⁠組東⁠西⁠裡比⁠較，最⁠高⁠級前⁠面加 the

That period of my life was one of the worst.

Of all the hotels, this one was cheapest.

Of all the hotels, this one was the cheapest.

one of 後⁠面要接 the

That period of my life was one of the worst.

That year was one of worst.

That year was one of the worst.

前⁠文沒⁠有可⁠以比⁠較⁠的東⁠西，名⁠詞不⁠能省⁠略

That period of my life was one of the worst.

I went to the party yesterday. It was the most boring.

I went to the party#2 yesterday. It was the most boring party I've been to.

例句

① the + 最高級

  1. I've had many embarrassing moments, and that was the most embarrassing.
    我有過很多尷尬的時刻，那次是最尷尬的。
  2. We tried all the sauces. The green one was the spiciest.
    我們每種醬都試了，綠色的最辣。
  3. Of all his films, the first is still the most famous.
    他所有的電影裡，第一部還是最有名的。
  4. There were many good answers, but Ann's was the clearest.
    好答案很多，不過 Ann 的最清楚。
  5. Of the three routes, the coast road is the most beautiful.
    三條路線裡，海岸公路最漂亮。
  6. All the rooms are small, but the one at the back is the quietest.
    房間都很小，不過後面那間最安靜。

② one of the + 最高級

  1. I've lived in many flats, and the one in Paris was one of the darkest.
    我住過很多間公寓，巴黎那間是最暗的之一。
  2. That winter was one of the coldest.
    那年冬天是最冷的幾個冬天之一。
  3. The storm last night was one of the worst in years.
    昨晚的暴風雨是這幾年最嚴重的之一。
  4. Her new song is one of the best on the album.
    她的新歌是專輯裡最好聽的之一。
  5. This bridge is one of the oldest in Europe.
    這座橋是歐洲最古老的橋之一。
  6. Our school is one of the biggest in the city.
    我們學校是這座城市最大的學校之一。

③ the + 最高級 和 one of the + 最高級 一起用

  1. Maybe it's not the greatest danger, but it's one of the greatest.
    也許不是最大的危險，不過是最大的之一。
  2. He's not the tallest player, but he's one of the tallest.
    他不是最高的球員，不過是最高的之一。
  3. It isn't the most expensive restaurant, but it's one of the most expensive.
    那家餐廳不是最貴的，不過是最貴的之一。
  4. This isn't the biggest problem, but it's one of the biggest.
    這不是最大的問題，不過是最大的問題之一。

④ the most + 形容詞，省略名詞

  1. I've read all her books, and this one is the most interesting.
    她的書我全都看過，這本最有趣。
  2. Of all the jobs I've had, this is the most stressful.
    我做過的工作裡，這份壓力最大。
  3. Choosing a school was difficult, but choosing a flat was the most difficult.
    選學校很難，不過選公寓最難。
  4. Of the three plans, the second is the most practical.
    三個計畫裡，第二個最實際。
  5. Of all the guests, your aunt was the most generous.
    所有客人裡，你阿姨最大方。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. You've tried on four coats. Which one felt the warmest?
    你試穿了四件外套，哪一件穿起來最保暖？
  2. Of all the cities you've visited, which was the friendliest?
    你去過的城市裡，哪一個最友善？
  3. Is this mountain one of the highest in Japan?
    這座山是日本最高的山之一嗎？
  4. Of all the subjects, math is the one I find the hardest.
    所有科目裡，我覺得數學最難。
  5. We had three dogs, and Max was the cleverest.
    我們養了三隻狗，Max 最聰明。
  6. We've made a lot of changes, and moving the office was one of the best.
    我們做了很多改變，搬辦公室是最好的改變之一。
  7. That day was one of the happiest for our family.
    那一天是我們家最快樂的日子之一。
  8. Of all our holidays, the one in Bali was one of the best.
    我們所有的假期裡，峇里島那次是最棒的之一。
  9. Of all the presents, the book from my uncle was the most useful.
    所有禮物裡，叔叔送的書最實用。
