Anything that puts you in contact with the English language is important.

任何能讓你接觸到英文的東西都很重要。

anything + 後位修⁠飾⁠語當主⁠詞

Anything that puts you in contact with the English language is important.

任何能讓你接觸到英文的東西都很重要。

後位修⁠飾⁠語：子⁠句、不⁠定⁠詞、else

Anything that puts you in contact with the English language is important.

關係子句

Anything that makes you laugh is good for your health.

任何能讓你笑的事都對健康有益。
不定詞

Anything to keep the children quiet would be welcome.

任何能讓孩子安靜下來的辦法都好。
else

Anything else can wait until tomorrow.

其他的事都可以等到明天。

後位修⁠飾⁠語：介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語、形⁠容⁠詞

介系詞片語

Anything with sugar in it is bad for your teeth.

任何含糖的東西都對牙齒不好。
形容詞

Anything new takes time to learn.

任何新東西都要花時間學。

anything 開⁠頭⁠的主⁠詞強⁠調範⁠圍很廣

Anything that puts you in contact with the English language is important.

你能幫上的任何忙，我們都很感激。

主⁠詞⁠的動⁠詞用單⁠數

Anything that puts you in contact with the English language is important.

Anything that helps you relax are worth trying.

Anything that helps you relax is worth trying.

關⁠係子⁠句⁠的動⁠詞也用單⁠數

Anything that puts you in contact with the English language is important.

Anything that make you happy is fine with me.

Anything that makes you happy is fine with me.

形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在 anything 後⁠面

Interesting anything is worth reading.

Anything interesting is worth reading.

動⁠詞前⁠面不⁠再⁠加 it

Anything that you find useful, it is worth keeping.

Anything that you find useful is worth keeping.

省⁠略子⁠句：逗⁠號後⁠面⁠接祈⁠使⁠句

作業裡有不懂的，就問老師。

everything 指全⁠部

這個行李箱裡的東西全都是我姊姊的。

例句

① anything that / which + 子句

  1. Anything that saves us time is a good idea.
    任何能幫我們省時間的方法都是好主意。
  2. Anything that is left over will be given to the staff.
    剩下的東西都會給員工。
  3. Anything which might cause a fire must be kept outside.
    任何可能引起火災的東西都必須放在外面。
  4. Anything that gets the students talking is useful in class.
    任何能讓學生開口說話的東西，在課堂上都很有用。
  5. Anything that comes from her kitchen tastes wonderful.
    她廚房做出來的東西都很好吃。
  6. Anything that happens at home stays at home.
    家裡發生的事就留在家裡。

② anything + 省略關係代名詞的子句

  1. Anything you say may be used against you.
    你說的任何話都可能被當作對你不利的證據。
  2. Anything you buy today comes with a free bag.
    今天買任何東西都送一個袋子。
  3. Anything she cooks is delicious.
    她煮的東西都很好吃。
  4. Anything we can do to help will make a difference.
    我們能幫上的任何忙都會有用。
  5. Anything you leave on the train goes to the lost property office.
    留在火車上的東西都會送到失物招領處。

③ anything + to 不定詞

  1. Anything to make the journey shorter would be good.
    任何能讓路程縮短的辦法都好。
  2. Anything to say thank you would be nice.
    任何表達謝意的方式都很好。
  3. Anything to read on the plane is better than nothing.
    在飛機上有東西看總比沒有好。
  4. Anything to keep warm was useful on that cold night.
    那個寒冷的晚上，任何能保暖的東西都派得上用場。
  5. Anything to avoid the traffic is worth a try.
    任何能避開塞車的辦法都值得一試。

④ anything else

  1. Anything else can be sent by post.
    其他的東西都可以用郵寄的。
  2. Anything else is extra and costs ten pounds.
    其他的都要另外付費，十英鎊。
  3. Anything else would be too expensive.
    其他的選擇都太貴了。
  4. Anything else that you need will be provided.
    你需要的其他東西都會提供。
  5. Anything else on the list can be bought later.
    清單上其他的東西都可以晚點再買。

⑤ anything + 介系詞片語

  1. Anything in this room is free to use.
    這個房間裡的東西都可以免費使用。
  2. Anything with a red label is half price.
    貼紅色標籤的東西都打五折。
  3. Anything about the new project must stay secret.
    跟新計畫有關的事都必須保密。
  4. Anything from that shop is good quality.
    那家店的東西品質都很好。
  5. Anything on the top shelf is too high for me.
    最上層架子的東西我都拿不到。

⑥ anything + 形容詞

  1. Anything new is exciting for young children.
    任何新東西對小孩子來說都很新奇。
  2. Anything sweet makes my tooth hurt.
    吃甜的東西我的牙就會痛。
  3. Anything cheaper would be better for us.
    便宜一點的對我們比較好。
  4. Anything important must be written down.
    任何重要的事都必須寫下來。
  5. Anything homemade tastes better than shop food.
    自己做的東西都比店裡買的好吃。
