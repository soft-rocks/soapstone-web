My pillows are getting old, and I need new ones.

說過的

舊地圖是錯的，所以我們買了新的。

（在鞋店）我可以試穿咖啡色那雙嗎？

例句

① 家裡的東西

  1. Our towels are getting thin. We should buy new ones.
    我們的毛巾越來越薄了，該買新的了。
  2. These batteries are dead. Are there any fresh ones in the drawer?
    這些電池沒電了，抽屜裡有新的嗎？
  3. The kids' beds are too short now, so we ordered longer ones.
    孩子的床現在太短了，所以我們訂了長一點的。
  4. I keep losing my sunglasses, so I always buy cheap ones.
    我老是弄丟太陽眼鏡，所以都買便宜的。
  5. The light bulbs in the hall keep going out. I'll get some brighter ones.
    走廊的燈泡一直壞，我去買亮一點的。

② 在店裡

  1. Could I see the silver ones, please?
    可以給我看看銀色的嗎？
  2. I'll take the small ones, please.
    我要小的，謝謝。
  3. Are the green ones ripe yet?
    綠色的熟了嗎？
  4. Which ones are on sale?
    哪些在特價？
  5. Sorry, the cheap ones are all sold out.
    不好意思，便宜的都賣完了。
  6. Do the blue ones come in a larger size?
    藍色的有大一點的尺寸嗎？

③ 學校

  1. The old textbooks were full of mistakes, so the school bought new ones.
    舊課本錯誤很多，所以學校買了新的。
  2. My pencils are blunt. Can I borrow your sharp ones?
    我的鉛筆鈍了，可以借你削好的那幾支嗎？
  3. We wrote short essays last term, and this term we're writing longer ones.
    上學期我們寫短文，這學期要寫長一點的。
  4. I can answer the easy questions, but not the tricky ones.
    簡單的題目我會，難的不會。
  5. Some of the exams were easy, but the last ones were really hard.
    有幾場考試很簡單，可是最後幾場真的很難。

④ 工作

  1. The company replaced the broken chairs with comfortable ones.
    公司把壞掉的椅子換成舒服的。
  2. Old customers are important, but we need new ones too.
    老客戶很重要，可是我們也需要新客戶。
  3. These figures are from March. Do you have the latest ones?
    這些數字是三月的，你有最新的嗎？
  4. Our printers are slow. The ones upstairs are much faster.
    我們的印表機很慢，樓上的快多了。
  5. The managers liked the first designs more than the later ones.
    主管比較喜歡最早的設計，不太喜歡後來的。

⑤ 旅行

  1. The rooms at the front are noisy, so ask for the quiet ones at the back.
    前面的房間很吵，你要後面安靜的。
  2. Direct flights are expensive, but cheaper ones usually have a stop.
    直飛的班機很貴，便宜的通常要轉機。
  3. We stayed in small villages rather than touristy ones.
    我們住在小村子，沒住觀光客多的地方。
  4. Lots of buses stop here. Which ones go to the airport?
    很多公車停這裡，哪幾路會到機場？
  5. The hotels near the beach were full, so we found cheaper ones in town.
    海邊的飯店都客滿了，所以我們在市區找了便宜一點的。

⑥ 朋友和日常

  1. I like dogs, especially big ones.
    我喜歡狗，尤其是大狗。
  2. I collect old coins, and the rare ones are worth a lot.
    我收集舊硬幣，稀有的很值錢。
  3. These photos are lovely. Did you take the ones on the fridge too?
    這些照片好漂亮。冰箱上那些也是你拍的嗎？
  4. Your earrings are pretty. Where did you find such nice ones?
    你的耳環好漂亮，在哪裡找到這麼好看的？
  5. Don't eat those biscuits. The fresh ones are in the tin.
    那些餅乾不要吃，新鮮的在罐子裡。
