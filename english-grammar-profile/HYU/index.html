例句

① Not only + was / is + 主詞

  1. My trip to work this morning was awful. Not only was the train late, but it was also packed.
    我今天早上上班的路程很糟。火車不但誤點，還擠滿了人。
  2. We won't rent the flat on King Street. Not only is it small, but it's also very dark.
    我們不會租國王街那間公寓。它不但小，還很暗。
  3. I wrote a review of the new restaurant. Not only was the food bad, but the service was slow.
    我寫了一篇新餐廳的評論。食物不但難吃，服務還很慢。
  4. Our new neighbours are great. Not only are they friendly, but they're also very helpful.
    我們的新鄰居很好。他們不但友善，還很幫忙。
  5. I loved my French class last year. Not only was the teacher kind, but she also made the lessons fun.
    我很喜歡去年的法文課。那位老師不但和善，還把課上得很有趣。

② Not only + did + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. Our new sofa arrived broken. Not only did the shop give us a refund, but it also sent a voucher.
    我們的新沙發送來的時候是壞的。那家店不但退錢給我們，還寄了一張禮券。
  2. Many fans travelled to the Olympics. Not only did they come for football, but they also watched the smaller events.
    很多球迷去看奧運。他們不但為了足球而來，還看了其他比較小的比賽。
  3. I left my bike at Uncle Joe's shop. Not only did he fix it, but he also cleaned it for free.
    我把腳踏車放在喬叔叔的店裡。他不但修好了它，還免費幫我擦乾淨。
  4. Our first day in Italy went badly. Not only did we miss the train, but we also lost our bags.
    我們在義大利的第一天很不順。我們不但錯過了火車，還弄丟了行李。
  5. The school trip was a disaster. Not only did the bus break down, but the driver also got lost.
    那次校外教學很糟。公車不但拋錨，司機還迷路了。

③ Not only + does / do + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. My grandma never buys clothes. Not only does she knit, but she also sews her own dresses.
    我奶奶從來不買衣服。她不但會織毛線，還自己縫洋裝。
  2. Let's meet at the bookshop by the station. Not only do they sell books, but they also serve lunch.
    我們在車站旁邊的書店碰面吧。他們不但賣書，還供應午餐。
  3. I cycle to work every day now. Not only does it save money, but it also gives me time to think.
    我現在每天騎腳踏車上班。這不但省錢，還讓我有時間思考。
  4. We bought a new printer for the office. Not only does it print in colour, but it also scans.
    我們買了一台新的辦公室印表機。它不但能彩色列印，還能掃描。

④ Not only + has / can / will + 主詞

  1. I've bought this coffee for years. Not only has the price gone down, but the quality has improved.
    我買這種咖啡好幾年了。價格不但降了，品質還提升了。
  2. Give Lily the main part in the school show. Not only can she sing, but she can also dance.
    讓莉莉演學校表演的主角。她不但會唱歌，還會跳舞。
  3. Switch off the lights when you leave a room. Not only will you save money, but you'll also help the planet.
    離開房間的時候把燈關掉。你不但能省錢，還能幫助地球。
  4. The builders worked fast this year. Not only have they finished the house, but they've also built a garden.
    工人今年做得很快。他們不但蓋好了房子，還做了一座花園。
  5. My cousin came to live with us in 2020. Not only had he lost his job, but he had also lost his flat.
    我表哥 2020 年來跟我們住。他不但丟了工作，還失去了公寓。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. We left at the break. Not only was the concert long, but it was also boring.
    我們中場休息就走了。演唱會不但很長，還很無聊。
  2. The school held a science fair last Friday. Not only did the kids enjoy it, but the parents loved it too.
    學校上星期五辦了科學展。孩子們不但玩得開心，家長也很喜歡。
  3. My teacher gave a talk to new students. Not only is English useful, but it's also fun to learn.
    我的老師對新生演講。英文不但有用，學起來也很有趣。
  4. I met my boss's wife at the party. Not only did I forget her name, but I called her by the wrong one.
    我在派對上遇到我老闆的太太。我不但忘了她的名字，還叫錯了。
  5. We won't stay at that hotel again. Not only were the rooms small, but they were also dirty.
    我們不會再住那家飯店了。房間不但小，還很髒。
  6. I went back to my home town after twenty years. Not only has the town grown, but it has also become cleaner.
    我隔了二十年回到家鄉。這個鎮不但變大了，還變乾淨了。
  7. The kids had a week off in March. Not only did the storm close the school, but it also damaged the roof.
    孩子們三月放了一個星期的假。暴風雨不但讓學校停課，還損壞了屋頂。
  8. Welcome to Lake Garda. Not only can you swim here, but you can also go sailing.
    歡迎來到加爾達湖。你在這裡不但能游泳，還能去划帆船。
  9. We had a great day in Washington. Not only was the museum free, but the guide was excellent.
    我們在華盛頓度過了很棒的一天。博物館不但免費，導覽員還很棒。
  10. The teacher wrote about Ken in his report. Not only does he work hard, but he also helps his classmates.
    老師在 Ken 的成績單上寫了評語。他不但很用功，還會幫同學。
  11. It was a great night for our local club. Not only did the team win, but they scored five goals.
    那天晚上對我們當地的球隊來說很棒。那支隊伍不但贏了，還進了五球。
