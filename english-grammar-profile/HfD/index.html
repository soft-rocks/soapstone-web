I have finally bought a mobile phone.

我終於買手機了。

副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠要動⁠詞前⁠面

我們每天也吃米飯。

副⁠詞放⁠在 be 後⁠面

它也很漂亮。

副⁠詞放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

I have finally bought a mobile phone.

我終於買手機了。

各⁠個副⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

I have finally bought a mobile phone.

also也，加上另一件事
only只，數量少或範圍小
finally終於，等了很久以後才發生
still還，事情到現在還在繼續
already已經，比預期的早

否⁠定⁠句⁠的也⁠不⁠用 either

我姊姊也不喜歡。

副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在主⁠要動⁠詞後⁠面

I like also pizza.

I also like pizza.

副⁠詞放⁠在 be 後⁠面

My aunt also is a nurse.

My aunt is also a nurse.

副⁠詞放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

I have finally bought a mobile phone.

We also can meet on Sunday.

We can also meet on Sunday.

否⁠定⁠句不⁠用 also

I don't also eat meat.

I don't eat meat either.

例句

① 主詞 + also + 主要動詞

  1. My brother plays the guitar. He also sings in a band.
    我哥會彈吉他，他也在樂團唱歌。
  2. The hotel has a pool. It also has a small gym.
    那間旅館有游泳池，也有一間小健身房。
  3. I also need some milk from the shop.
    我也需要從店裡買點牛奶。
  4. Our teacher also gave us a reading test.
    我們老師也給我們考了閱讀測驗。

② be + also

  1. Lisa is my classmate. She's also my neighbour.
    Lisa 是我的同學，也是我的鄰居。
  2. The museum is free, and it's also open on Mondays.
    那間博物館免費，而且星期一也有開。
  3. The weather was cold. It was also very windy.
    天氣很冷，風也很大。

③ 助動詞 + also

  1. You can also pay by card.
    你也可以刷卡。
  2. We should also buy some water for the trip.
    我們也該買些水帶去旅行。
  3. My parents will also be at the party.
    我爸媽也會去派對。

④ only

  1. I only have five euros in my wallet.
    我錢包裡只有五歐元。
  2. The bakery only opens in the morning.
    那家麵包店只有早上開。
  3. We can only stay for one night.
    我們只能住一個晚上。
  4. My son only eats pasta with cheese.
    我兒子只吃加起司的義大利麵。
  5. The swimming lesson was only half an hour long.
    游泳課只有半小時。

⑤ finally

  1. The bus finally arrived after forty minutes.
    公車等了四十分鐘終於來了。
  2. We have finally found a flat near the park.
    我們終於在公園附近找到一間公寓。
  3. My little brother can finally ride a bike.
    我弟弟終於會騎腳踏車了。
  4. She finally passed her driving test.
    她終於考過駕照了。
  5. After three hours, the rain finally stopped.
    三個小時後，雨終於停了。

⑥ still

  1. My grandparents still live in the same village.
    我爺爺奶奶還住在同一個村子。
  2. Are you still hungry?
    你還會餓嗎？
  3. It's ten o'clock and Tom is still in bed.
    已經十點了，Tom 還在床上。
  4. My old laptop still works, so I don't need a new one.
    我的舊筆電還能用，所以不需要買新的。

⑦ already

  1. I've already sent the invitations for the barbecue.
    烤肉的邀請我已經寄出去了。
  2. The children are already asleep.
    孩子們已經睡著了。
  3. My daughter already speaks a little Spanish.
    我女兒已經會說一點西班牙文了。
  4. We have already booked the hotel for July.
    我們已經訂好七月的旅館了。

⑧ 否定句的 either

  1. Ben can't swim, and his sister can't swim either.
    Ben 不會游泳，他妹妹也不會。
  2. I didn't go to the party. My friends didn't go either.
    我沒去派對，我朋友也沒去。
  3. The first hotel wasn't cheap, and the second one wasn't cheap either.
    第一間旅館不便宜，第二間也不便宜。
