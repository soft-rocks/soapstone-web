Our company needs to train some staff on how to deal with customer complaints.

我們公司需要訓練一些員工處理客訴。

介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞由動⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞組⁠成，後⁠面接⁠受⁠詞

Our company needs to train some staff on how to deal with customer complaints.

我們公司需要訓練一些員工處理客訴。

deal with 的意思是處理。

受⁠詞可⁠以是名⁠詞、代⁠名⁠詞、動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 或 wh 子⁠句

Our company needs to train some staff on how to deal with customer complaints.

名詞

She applied for the job.

她應徵了那份工作。
代名詞

You can rely on me.

你可以信賴我。
動詞⁠-⁠ing

He insisted on paying for dinner.

他堅持要請吃晚餐。
wh 子句

It depends on what time you arrive.

那要看你幾點到。

deal 後⁠面接⁠受⁠詞⁠時，要加介⁠系⁠詞 with

Our company needs to train some staff on how to deal with customer complaints.

We need to deal the problem today.

We need to deal with the problem today.

depend 的介⁠系⁠詞是 on

Our company needs to train some staff on how to deal with customer complaints.

It depends of the weather.

It depends on the weather.

介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

She succeeded in pass the exam.

She succeeded in passing the exam.

接 that 子⁠句⁠時，拿⁠掉介⁠系⁠詞

They complained about that the room was cold.

They complained that the room was cold.

agree with 接人或看⁠法，agree to 接提⁠議或計⁠畫

discuss 後⁠面直⁠接接⁠受⁠詞

We discussed about the problem.

We discussed the problem.

例句

① deal with

  1. "There's a customer at the desk, and she's angry." "Don't worry, I'll deal with it."
    櫃檯有個客人，她很生氣。別擔心，我來處理。
  2. New teachers often don't know how to deal with noisy classes.
    新老師常常不知道怎麼應付吵鬧的班級。
  3. The council dealt with the problem quickly.
    市議會很快處理了這個問題。

② depend on / rely on

  1. "Will the match go ahead?" "It depends on the rain. They'll decide at noon."
    比賽會照常舉行嗎？要看雨下得怎樣。他們中午會決定。
  2. The price depends on how many people come.
    價錢要看來多少人。
  3. My grandma lives alone, but she can rely on her neighbours.
    我奶奶一個人住，可是她可以依靠鄰居。
  4. "Will you help me move on Sunday?" "Of course. You can rely on me."
    星期天你會幫我搬家嗎？當然。你可以信賴我。

③ apply for

  1. I saw the job online, so I applied for it.
    我在網路上看到這份工作，所以就去應徵了。
  2. You need to apply for a visa before you travel.
    你要在出發前申請簽證。
  3. "Did you get the scholarship?" "I don't know yet. I applied for it last month."
    你拿到獎學金了嗎？還不知道。我上個月申請的。

④ complain about

  1. The guests complained about the noise from the bar.
    客人抱怨酒吧傳來的噪音。
  2. My dad always complains about the traffic.
    我爸老是在抱怨交通。
  3. My room was dirty, so I called reception and complained about it.
    我的房間很髒，所以我打電話給櫃檯抱怨。

⑤ consist of / refer to

  1. The team consists of four engineers and a designer.
    這個團隊由四位工程師和一位設計師組成。
  2. Breakfast consists of bread, eggs and coffee.
    早餐有麵包、蛋和咖啡。
  3. "Who is 'the boss' in your email?" "I'm referring to Mr Brown."
    你信裡說的『老闆』是誰？我指的是 Brown 先生。
  4. In his speech, the mayor referred to the new bridge.
    市長在演講中提到了那座新橋。

⑥ believe in / suffer from

  1. "Do you believe in ghosts?" "No, but I don't like dark houses."
    你相信有鬼嗎？不信，可是我不喜歡暗暗的房子。
  2. Our coach believes in hard work.
    我們教練很重視努力。
  3. My sister suffers from headaches when she doesn't sleep enough.
    我姊姊睡不夠的時候就會頭痛。
  4. Many people in the city suffer from stress.
    城市裡很多人都有壓力。

⑦ 受詞是動詞-ing

  1. "Let me pay." "No, I insist on paying. It's your birthday."
    我來付。不，一定要我付。今天是你生日。
  2. She finally succeeded in finding a flat near her office.
    她終於成功找到離公司很近的公寓。
  3. The students complained about having too much homework.
    學生們抱怨作業太多。
  4. He doesn't believe in spending money on expensive clothes.
    他不認為應該花錢買昂貴的衣服。

⑧ 受詞是代名詞

  1. "Are the boys in trouble again?" "Yes, but their teacher will deal with them."
    那些男生又惹麻煩了嗎？對，可是他們的老師會處理他們。
  2. "This is the report I told you about." "Thanks. I'll refer to it in my talk."
    這就是我跟你說的報告。謝謝。我演講時會提到它。
  3. Our dog is old, and he depends on us for everything.
    我們的狗老了，什麼都要靠我們。

⑨ 受詞是 wh 子句

  1. "Can we eat outside tonight?" "That depends on how cold it gets."
    我們今晚可以在外面吃嗎？要看會變多冷。
  2. Before the meeting, nobody agreed on what to do next.
    開會前，大家對下一步要做什麼沒有共識。
  3. The training deals with how to answer angry phone calls.
    這個訓練教的是怎麼接聽生氣的電話。
