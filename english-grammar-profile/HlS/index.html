Would you please do me a favour? Could you go and collect the clothes for me?

可以告訴我你的想法嗎？

Would you (please) + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

Would you你可以……嗎？有禮貌的請求。
Wouldn't that be那不是很……嗎？請對方同意。
wouldn't it?附加問句。

Would you let me know

例句

Would you let me know what you think?

可以讓我知道你的想法嗎？

Would you let me know 常用在信件結尾，請對方回覆。

附⁠加問⁠句

例句

It would be fantastic, wouldn't it?

那會很棒，對吧？

it would be …, wouldn't it? 請對方同意自己的看法。

would 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Would you please do me a favour? Could you go and collect the clothes for me?

Would you to help me?

Would you help me?

前⁠面是肯⁠定⁠句，附⁠加問⁠句用 wouldn't

It would be great, would it?

It would be great, wouldn't it?

問⁠句把 would 放⁠在句⁠首，不⁠用 do

Would you please do me a favour? Could you go and collect the clothes for me?

Do you would let me know?

Would you let me know?

例句

① Would you please + 原形動詞？：有禮貌的請求

  1. I'm stuck abroad for two more days. Would you please do me a favour and collect my dress from the cleaner's?
    我在國外還要多待兩天。可以請你幫我一個忙，去乾洗店拿我的洋裝嗎？
  2. Would you please close the window? There's a cold wind.
    可以請你關窗嗎？有冷風。
  3. Would you please speak a little more slowly?
    可以請你說慢一點嗎？
  4. Would you please water my plants while I'm on holiday?
    我度假的時候，可以請你幫我澆花嗎？
  5. Would you please turn your music down after ten?
    十點以後可以請你把音樂關小聲一點嗎？
  6. Would you please sign here and write the date?
    可以請你在這裡簽名，寫上日期嗎？

② Would you let me know + 名詞 / 子句？：請對方回覆

  1. I've attached my plan for the party. Would you let me know what you think?
    我附上了派對的計畫。可以讓我知道你的想法嗎？
  2. Would you let me know if you can come on Saturday?
    可以讓我知道你星期六能不能來嗎？
  3. Would you let me know when the parcel arrives?
    包裹到的時候可以讓我知道嗎？
  4. Would you let me know your new address?
    可以讓我知道你的新地址嗎？
  5. Would you let me know how much I owe you?
    可以讓我知道我欠你多少錢嗎？
  6. Would you let me know which day suits you best?
    可以讓我知道哪一天你最方便嗎？

③ Wouldn't that be + 形容詞？：請對方同意

  1. Think of the fun with your friends, playing sports and eating ice cream all day. Wouldn't that be wonderful?
    想想跟你的朋友一起玩，整天運動、吃冰淇淋。那不是很棒嗎？
  2. We could have a picnic by the lake. Wouldn't that be lovely?
    我們可以在湖邊野餐。那不是很好嗎？
  3. Imagine living in a house by the sea. Wouldn't that be amazing?
    想像住在海邊的房子。那不是很棒嗎？
  4. We could all go to the concert together. Wouldn't that be fun?
    我們可以一起去聽演唱會。那不是很好玩嗎？
  5. If the shop gave us a discount, wouldn't that be great?
    如果那家店給我們打折，那不是很好嗎？
  6. A whole week without homework. Wouldn't that be nice?
    整整一個星期沒有功課。那不是很好嗎？

④ It would be + 形容詞, wouldn't it?：附加問句

  1. I think a trip to the mountains would be fantastic, wouldn't it?
    我想去山上旅行會很棒，對吧？
  2. A party on the roof would be great fun, wouldn't it?
    在屋頂上辦派對會很好玩，對吧？
  3. It would be easier to take a taxi, wouldn't it?
    搭計程車會比較方便，對吧？
  4. It would be cheaper to cook at home, wouldn't it?
    在家煮飯會比較便宜，對吧？
  5. A dog would be good company for Grandma, wouldn't it?
    一隻狗會是奶奶很好的伴，對吧？
  6. It would be nice to see everyone again, wouldn't it?
    再見到大家會很好，對吧？

⑤ Would you + 原形動詞？：問對方的意願

  1. Would you look after my bag while I go to the toilet?
    我去廁所的時候，你可以幫我看一下包包嗎？
  2. Would you lend me your umbrella for an hour?
    你可以借我你的雨傘一個小時嗎？
  3. Would you show me how to use this machine?
    你可以教我怎麼用這台機器嗎？
  4. Would you take our photo in front of the fountain?
    你可以幫我們在噴泉前面拍張照嗎？
  5. Would you carry this box to the car for me?
    你可以幫我把這個箱子搬到車上嗎？
  6. Would you wait here while I get the tickets?
    我去拿票的時候，你可以在這裡等嗎？
  7. Would you read my essay before I hand it in?
    我交作文之前，你可以幫我讀一下嗎？
