We could take the bus, or we could ask my parents to take us there.

我們可以搭公車，或是請我爸媽載我們去。

用 or 連起兩⁠個 could，讓對⁠方選一⁠個做⁠法

We could take the bus, or we could ask my parents to take us there.

我們可以搭公車，或是請我爸媽載我們去。

說話者沒有決定要怎麼去。

We could 一⁠起⁠做，You could 建⁠議對⁠方自⁠己做

We could take the bus, or we could ask my parents to take us there.

We could

We could go by train, or we could drive.

我們可以搭火車，或是開車去。
You could

If you want to visit an interesting place near Paris, you could go to Versailles.

如果你想去巴黎附近有趣的地方，你可以去凡爾賽宮。

句⁠尾加 if you want，對⁠方不⁠想也沒⁠關⁠係

We could take the bus, or we could ask my parents to take us there.

你想的話，我們等一下可以去打保齡球。

例句

① We could + 原形動詞

  1. We could go to the park.
    我們可以去公園。
  2. We could have a picnic by the lake.
    我們可以在湖邊野餐。
  3. We could play tennis on Saturday.
    我們星期六可以打網球。
  4. We could make pancakes for breakfast.
    我們早餐可以做鬆餅。
  5. We could visit Grandma this weekend.
    我們這個週末可以去看奶奶。
  6. We could buy her a book for her birthday.
    我們可以買一本書送她當生日禮物。
  7. We could go swimming after school.
    我們放學後可以去游泳。
  8. We could listen to some music.
    我們可以聽點音樂。

② You could + 原形動詞

  1. If you want to visit an interesting place near Paris, you could go to Versailles.
    如果你想去巴黎附近有趣的地方，你可以去凡爾賽宮。
  2. You could wear your blue dress.
    你可以穿你那件藍色洋裝。
  3. You could ask your brother for help.
    你可以請你哥哥幫忙。
  4. You could take the subway. It's faster.
    你可以搭地鐵。比較快。
  5. You could write him a card.
    你可以寫張卡片給他。
  6. You could buy the tickets online.
    你可以在網路上買票。
  7. You could stay with us next time.
    下次你可以住我們家。
  8. You could visit the old town in the morning.
    你可以早上去逛舊城區。

③ We could ..., or we could ...

  1. We could watch a film, or we could play a board game.
    我們可以看電影，或是玩桌遊。
  2. We could eat at home, or we could go out.
    我們可以在家吃，或是出去吃。
  3. We could take a taxi, or we could walk to the hotel.
    我們可以搭計程車，或是走路去飯店。
  4. We could leave now, or we could wait for Tom.
    我們可以現在走，或是等 Tom。
  5. We could paint the room blue, or we could leave it white.
    我們可以把房間漆成藍色，或是維持白色。
  6. We could fly to Rome, or we could take the night train.
    我們可以搭飛機去羅馬，或是搭夜車。
  7. You could call her, or you could send her an email.
    你可以打電話給她，或是寄電子郵件給她。

④ could + 原形動詞, if you want

  1. We could have dinner after that, if you want.
    你想的話，我們之後可以去吃晚餐。
  2. You could borrow my notes, if you want.
    你想的話，可以借我的筆記去看。
  3. We could go shopping tomorrow, if you want.
    你想的話，我們明天可以去逛街。
  4. You could come with us, if you want.
    你想的話，可以跟我們一起來。
  5. We could meet at my house, if you want.
    你想的話，我們可以在我家碰面。
  6. We could share my umbrella, if you want.
    你想的話，我們可以一起撐我的傘。
  7. We could start a bit later, if you want.
    你想的話，我們可以晚一點開始。
