This may surprise you, but I've managed to lose some weight.

你可能覺得它很無聊，但其實不會。

This / It + may + surprise / sound + …, but + 句⁠子

may說對方可能有的想法或反應。
but帶出說話者真正的看法或意外的事實。

用在議⁠論文

例句

You may think that cleaning instruments is boring, but it isn't.

你可能覺得清潔樂器很無聊，可是並不會。

議論文常先說一般人的想法，再提出不同的看法。

may 前⁠後⁠的主⁠詞

This may surprise you, but I've managed to lose some weight.

例句

This may sound strange, but I love rainy days.

這可能聽起來很奇怪，可是我很喜歡下雨天。

may 那一句常用 you 或 this。but 後面的句子說說話者自己的看法。

may 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This may surprise you, but I've managed to lose some weight.

This may surprises you, but I passed.

This may surprise you, but I passed.

sound 後⁠面要接形⁠容⁠詞，像 strange、odd

It may sound, but I like the cold.

It may sound strange, but I like the cold.

may 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This may surprise you, but I've managed to lose some weight.

You may to think it's easy, but it isn't.

You may think it's easy, but it isn't.

例句

① This may surprise you, but + 句子

  1. This may surprise you, but I've started learning the violin.
    這可能會讓你很驚訝，可是我開始學小提琴了。
  2. This may surprise you, but I've never been on a plane.
    這可能會讓你很驚訝，可是我從來沒坐過飛機。
  3. This may surprise you, but my grandmother runs marathons.
    這可能會讓你很驚訝，可是我奶奶會跑馬拉松。
  4. This may surprise you, but I actually enjoyed the maths exam.
    這可能會讓你很驚訝，可是我其實很喜歡那次數學考試。
  5. This may surprise you, but Tom and I are getting married.
    這可能會讓你很驚訝，可是湯姆和我要結婚了。
  6. This may surprise you, but I've sold my car and bought a bike.
    這可能會讓你很驚訝，可是我把車賣了，買了一台腳踏車。

② It may sound + 形容詞, but + 句子

  1. It may sound strange, but I love walking in the rain.
    這可能聽起來很奇怪，可是我很喜歡在雨中散步。
  2. It may sound silly, but I'm afraid of small dogs.
    這可能聽起來很蠢，可是我怕小狗。
  3. It may sound easy, but making bread takes a lot of practice.
    這可能聽起來很簡單，可是做麵包要練習很多次。
  4. It may sound boring, but I really enjoy ironing.
    這可能聽起來很無聊，可是我真的很喜歡燙衣服。
  5. It may sound expensive, but the course is worth every penny.
    這可能聽起來很貴，可是這門課每一分錢都值得。
  6. It may sound unkind, but I don't want her at the party.
    這可能聽起來很不厚道，可是我不想要她來派對。

③ You may think (that) + 子句, but + 句子

  1. You may think that cleaning musical instruments is quite boring, but it is not.
    你可能覺得清潔樂器很無聊，可是並不會。
  2. You may think that living in the country is quiet, but our village is very busy.
    你可能覺得住在鄉下很安靜，可是我們村子很熱鬧。
  3. You may think I'm lazy, but I work twelve hours a day.
    你可能覺得我很懶，可是我一天工作十二個小時。
  4. You may think football is just a game, but for my dad it's everything.
    你可能覺得足球只是一場比賽，可是對我爸爸來說它就是一切。
  5. You may think that old people can't use computers, but my grandpa has his own website.
    你可能覺得老人不會用電腦，可是我爺爺有自己的網站。
  6. You may think this job is easy, but there's a lot to learn.
    你可能覺得這份工作很簡單，可是有很多東西要學。

④ You may believe / feel + 子句, but + 句子

  1. You may believe that money brings happiness, but many rich people are unhappy.
    你可能相信錢能帶來快樂，可是很多有錢人並不快樂。
  2. You may feel that one vote doesn't matter, but every vote counts.
    你可能覺得一票不重要，可是每一票都算數。
  3. You may believe that cats don't need much care, but they do.
    你可能相信貓不需要太多照顧，可是牠們其實需要。
  4. You may feel that homework is useless, but it helps you remember the lesson.
    你可能覺得功課沒有用，可是它幫你記住上課的內容。
  5. You may believe that the city is dangerous at night, but it's very safe.
    你可能相信這個城市晚上很危險，可是其實很安全。
  6. You may feel that you're too old to learn, but it's never too late.
    你可能覺得自己太老學不會，可是永遠不嫌晚。

⑤ 主詞 + may + 原形動詞, but + 句子：先讓步，再說重點

  1. The hotel may look old from outside, but the rooms are modern.
    那家飯店從外面看起來可能很舊，可是房間很新。
  2. My brother may seem shy, but he's very funny with his friends.
    我哥哥看起來可能很害羞，可是他跟朋友在一起很好笑。
  3. This dish may smell strong, but it tastes wonderful.
    這道菜聞起來可能味道很重，可是很好吃。
  4. Our team may be small, but we work very hard.
    我們的團隊可能很小，可是我們非常努力。
  5. The film may seem long, but you won't notice the time.
    那部電影可能看起來很長，可是你不會注意到時間。
  6. The walk may look difficult on the map, but it's quite easy.
    在地圖上那條步道可能看起來很難走，可是其實很輕鬆。
  7. She may be young, but she's the best player in the club.
    她可能很年輕，可是她是俱樂部裡最好的球員。
