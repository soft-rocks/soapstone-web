Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

可以麻煩你告訴 Thomas 先生和太太嗎？

mind 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

可以麻煩你告訴 Thomas 先生和太太嗎？

finish、keep、avoid、suggest 後⁠面也接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

finish

She finished writing the report.

她寫完了報告。
keep

My computer keeps freezing.

我的電腦一直當機。
avoid

The doctor told me to avoid eating late at night.

醫生叫我避免太晚吃東西。
suggest

I suggest trying that new pizza place.

我建議試試那家新開的披薩店。

Would you mind 是客⁠氣⁠的請⁠求

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

可以麻煩你關一下窗嗎？

答⁠應 Would you mind 的請⁠求，要說 No

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

No, not at all.

不會，一點也不介意。

not 放⁠在動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

我建議今晚不要出去。

mind 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，不⁠接不⁠定⁠詞

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

Would you mind to open the door?

Would you mind opening the door?

finish 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

I've finished clean my room.

I've finished cleaning my room.

suggest 後⁠面不⁠接人加不⁠定⁠詞

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

My friend suggested me to try yoga.

My friend suggested trying yoga.

stop to 的不⁠定⁠詞是停⁠下⁠來⁠的目⁠的

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

He stopped to buy a coffee.

他停下來買了一杯咖啡。

Would you like 是邀⁠請，後⁠面⁠接不⁠定⁠詞

Would you mind telling Mr. and Mrs. Thomas?

Would you like to join us for lunch on Sunday?

你星期天要跟我們一起吃午餐嗎？

例句

① Would you mind + 動詞-ing？（請求）

  1. I can't make the meeting tomorrow. Would you mind telling the others?
    我明天沒辦法去開會。可以麻煩你告訴其他人嗎？
  2. "It's cold in here. Would you mind closing the window?" "No, not at all."
    這裡好冷。可以麻煩你關一下窗嗎？好，沒問題。
  3. Excuse me, would you mind moving your bag? I'd like to sit down.
    不好意思，可以麻煩你把包包移開嗎？我想坐下。
  4. Would you mind speaking more slowly? My English isn't very good.
    可以麻煩你說慢一點嗎？我的英文不太好。

② Do you mind / I don't mind + 動詞-ing

  1. "Do you mind waiting a few minutes?" "That's fine. I'm not in a hurry."
    你介意等幾分鐘嗎？沒關係。我不趕時間。
  2. I don't mind working late if you need help.
    如果你需要幫忙，我不介意加班。
  3. My dad doesn't mind cooking, but he hates washing up.
    我爸不介意煮飯，可是他很討厭洗碗。

③ finish + 動詞-ing

  1. "Can we go out now?" "Not yet. I haven't finished doing my homework."
    我們現在可以出去了嗎？還不行。我功課還沒寫完。
  2. As soon as the workers finish painting the kitchen, we can move in.
    工人一把廚房油漆完，我們就可以搬進去了。
  3. She finished studying at ten and went straight to bed.
    她十點讀完書，就直接去睡了。

④ stop / keep + 動詞-ing

  1. It stopped raining, so we went for a walk.
    雨停了，所以我們出去散步。
  2. Please stop talking. The film is starting.
    請不要講話了。電影要開始了。
  3. The baby kept crying all night, and nobody slept.
    寶寶整晚一直哭，大家都沒睡。
  4. My computer keeps freezing. I think I need a new one.
    我的電腦一直當機。我想我需要一台新的。

⑤ avoid / suggest / consider + 動詞-ing

  1. The doctor told me to avoid eating late at night.
    醫生叫我避免太晚吃東西。
  2. "Where should we eat?" "I suggest trying that new pizza place."
    我們要去哪裡吃？我建議試試那家新開的披薩店。
  3. We're considering moving to a smaller flat.
    我們在考慮搬到小一點的公寓。
  4. We left at six to avoid getting stuck in traffic.
    我們六點出門，免得塞車。
  5. He suggested meeting at the café near the station.
    他建議在車站附近的咖啡店見面。

⑥ imagine / miss / practise + 動詞-ing

  1. "Do you want kids?" "Maybe. I can't imagine being a dad yet."
    你想要小孩嗎？也許吧。我還無法想像自己當爸爸。
  2. Since I moved to the city, I miss walking by the sea.
    我搬到城市以後，很懷念在海邊散步。
  3. Before the concert, she practised singing every day.
    演唱會之前，她每天練唱。
  4. Can you imagine winning all that money?
    你能想像贏到那麼多錢嗎？
  5. My grandparents miss seeing us every weekend.
    我爺爺奶奶很懷念每個週末見到我們的日子。

⑦ 動詞 + not + 動詞-ing

  1. "Let's drive." "I suggest not taking the car. Parking is a nightmare there."
    我們開車去吧。我建議不要開車。那裡很難停車。
  2. I don't mind not having a big wedding.
    不辦盛大的婚禮，我不介意。
  3. Imagine not having to get up early!
    想像一下不用早起的感覺！
  4. She practised not looking at her notes during the speech.
    她練習演講時不看筆記。

⑧ 不同的時態和人稱

  1. My brother has finished building his new desk.
    我哥已經把他的新書桌做好了。
  2. Ben kept asking me questions during the film.
    看電影的時候，Ben 一直問我問題。
  3. We will consider opening another shop next year.
    明年我們會考慮再開一家店。
  4. "Is your sister still at the gym?" "Yes, she's practising lifting heavier weights."
    你姊姊還在健身房嗎？對，她在練習舉更重的槓鈴。
