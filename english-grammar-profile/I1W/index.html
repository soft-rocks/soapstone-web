Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

Alice 很失望，她媽媽明明可以打電話給她，卻沒有打。

could have 說過⁠去有機⁠會⁠做、卻⁠沒⁠有做⁠的事

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

Alice 很失望，她媽媽明明可以打電話給她，卻沒有打。

說話者知道媽媽沒有打電話，所以這句不是推測。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，或 been 加其⁠他成⁠分

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

過去分詞

She could have taken the bus, but she walked.

她本來可以搭公車，可是她走路去。
been + 形容詞

The accident could have been much worse.

那場車禍本來可能嚴重得多。
been + 過去分詞
被動

All these problems could have been avoided.

這些問題本來都可以避免。

couldn't have 表⁠示這⁠件⁠事在過⁠去不⁠可⁠能發⁠生

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

票太貴了，我根本買不起。

說過⁠去⁠的⁠事，could 後⁠面⁠接 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

She could phoned me.

She could have phoned me.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

We could have went by train.

We could have gone by train.

go 的過去式是 went，過去分詞是 gone。

but 後⁠面用過⁠去⁠式 didn't

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

He could have helped, but he hasn't.

He could have helped, but he didn't.

推⁠測⁠的 could have 不⁠知⁠道結⁠果，過⁠去⁠的可⁠能知⁠道結⁠果

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

推測
不知道結果

He could have missed the bus. I don't know.

他可能沒趕上公車。我不知道。
過去的可能
知道沒有發生

My brother could have lent me the money, but he didn't.

我哥本來可以借我錢，可是他沒有。

could 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞說過⁠去⁠的能⁠力，這⁠件⁠事有發⁠生

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

我媽媽十歲就會做菜了。

would have 是一⁠定⁠會發⁠生⁠的結⁠果，could have 是⁠有機⁠會發⁠生

Alice was disappointed that her mother could have phoned her but didn't.

如果你那時打電話來，我就會去接你。

例句

① could have + 過去分詞, but ...

  1. She could have taken a taxi, but she wanted to save money.
    她本來可以搭計程車，可是她想省錢。
  2. He could have told the truth, but he lied.
    他本來可以說實話，可是他說謊了。
  3. We could have won the match, but we gave away two goals.
    我們本來可以贏這場比賽，可是我們讓對手進了兩球。
  4. Tom could have gone to Japan last summer, but he stayed home.
    Tom 去年夏天本來可以去日本，可是他待在家。
  5. My brother could have lent me the money, but he didn't.
    我哥本來可以借我錢，可是他沒有。
  6. They could have waited for us, but they left without us.
    他們本來可以等我們，可是他們沒等我們就走了。
  7. The teacher could have given us more time, but she didn't.
    老師本來可以多給我們一點時間，可是她沒有。
  8. Ann could have studied medicine, but she chose music.
    Ann 本來可以讀醫，可是她選了音樂。

② could have + 過去分詞，沒有說出 but

  1. The game could have gone either way.
    這場比賽本來誰贏都有可能。
  2. The firefighters said the fire could have spread to the whole street.
    消防員說，火本來可能燒到整條街。
  3. The driver could have hit the boy on the bike.
    那個司機差點撞到騎腳踏車的男孩。
  4. We could have walked home in twenty minutes.
    我們本來走二十分鐘就能到家。
  5. That mistake could have cost him his job.
    那個錯誤本來可能讓他丟了工作。
  6. Ken could have fallen off the ladder.
    Ken 差點從梯子上摔下來。
  7. With a better map, we could have found the hotel easily.
    要是有好一點的地圖，我們本來可以輕鬆找到飯店。

③ could have been + 形容詞或名詞

  1. Our holiday in Italy could have been a disaster.
    我們在義大利的假期本來可能搞砸。
  2. The damage from the flood could have been far more serious.
    洪水造成的損失本來可能嚴重得多。
  3. She could have been a great singer.
    她本來可以成為很棒的歌手。
  4. Without the storm, the harvest could have been twice as big.
    要不是那場暴風雨，收成本來可以多一倍。

④ could have been + 過去分詞（被動）

  1. The delay could have been avoided with better planning.
    計畫得好一點，本來可以避免延誤。
  2. The old bridge could have been repaired years ago.
    那座舊橋好幾年前就可以修了。
  3. Many lives could have been saved.
    本來可以救回更多人。
  4. The final could have been played indoors.
    決賽本來可以在室內比。

⑤ couldn't have + 過去分詞

  1. The flat was so small that we couldn't have lived there with a baby.
    那間公寓太小了，我們不可能帶著寶寶住在那裡。
  2. Without your help, I couldn't have finished the project.
    沒有你的幫忙，我不可能完成這個專案。
  3. We couldn't have arrived any earlier. The roads were closed.
    我們不可能更早到了。路都封了。
  4. I couldn't have paid for the course on my own.
    我一個人不可能付得起這門課的學費。
  5. I couldn't have done it without my parents.
    沒有我爸媽，我做不到。
  6. The children couldn't have carried that heavy box.
    那些孩子不可能搬得動那個重箱子。
  7. My grandmother couldn't have walked that far.
    我奶奶不可能走那麼遠。
