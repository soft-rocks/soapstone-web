I have a really good friend.

我有一個很要好的朋友。

very、really 放⁠在 be 後⁠面⁠的形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

我家很明亮。

名⁠詞前⁠面⁠的形⁠容⁠詞也⁠可⁠以加 very、really

I have a really good friend.

我有一個很要好的朋友。

複⁠數名⁠詞和不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 a

他們人真的很好。

not very 表⁠示不⁠太，疑⁠問⁠句也用 very

否定句

The hotel isn't very big.

那間飯店不太大。
疑問句

Is your school very far from here?

你的學校離這裡很遠嗎？

a 放⁠在程⁠度副⁠詞前⁠面

I have a really good friend.

She is very a nice teacher.

She is a very nice teacher.

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

The test was difficult very.

The test was very difficult.

修⁠飾形⁠容⁠詞用 really，不⁠用 real

I have a really good friend.

This soup is real hot.

This soup is really hot.

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面用 very，不⁠用 very much

The bag is very much expensive.

The bag is very expensive.

例句

① be + very + 形容詞

  1. The bedrooms in our new flat are very small.
    我們新公寓的臥室很小。
  2. The children were very tired after the long walk home.
    孩子們走了一大段路回家，累壞了。
  3. Thank you for the flowers, that's very kind of you.
    謝謝你送的花，你人真好。
  4. The roads are very icy this morning, so drive slowly.
    今天早上路面很滑，開慢一點。
  5. Our neighbours on the third floor are very quiet.
    我們三樓的鄰居很安靜。

② be + really + 形容詞

  1. The noodles at that little shop are really cheap.
    那家小店的麵很便宜。
  2. I skipped lunch, so I'm really hungry now.
    我沒吃午餐，現在好餓。
  3. My sister's new boyfriend is really funny.
    我姊姊的新男朋友很好笑。
  4. Your little boy is really tall for his age.
    以他的年紀來說，你兒子長得很高。
  5. The view from the top of the hill is really beautiful.
    從山頂看出去的風景真的很美。

③ a + very + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. My uncle drives a very old red truck.
    我叔叔開一台很舊的紅色卡車。
  2. We had a very long meeting on Monday afternoon.
    我們星期一下午開了一個很長的會。
  3. Dr Lee is a very patient dentist.
    李醫師是一位很有耐心的牙醫。
  4. They booked a very nice room near the beach.
    他們訂了一間在海灘附近、很不錯的房間。
  5. Taipei is a very crowded place at New Year.
    台北過年的時候人很多。

④ a + really + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. I had a really strange dream about my old school.
    我做了一個很奇怪的夢，夢到我以前的學校。
  2. Mum bought me a really warm coat for the winter.
    媽媽買了一件很保暖的外套給我過冬。
  3. My grandmother keeps a really big dog in her garden.
    我奶奶在院子裡養了一隻很大的狗。
  4. Thanks for a really lovely evening.
    謝謝你讓我們度過一個很愉快的晚上。
  5. Lisa is a really clever student.
    Lisa 是個很聰明的學生。

⑤ very / really + 形容詞 + 複數名詞或不可數名詞

  1. They sell very cheap shoes at the Sunday market.
    星期天的市集賣的鞋子很便宜。
  2. We had really sunny weather on our trip to Rome.
    我們去羅馬玩的時候天氣很晴朗。
  3. My parents grow very sweet tomatoes.
    我爸媽種的番茄很甜。
  4. Those shops only sell really expensive watches.
    那些店只賣很貴的手錶。
  5. My dad drinks very strong black coffee every morning.
    我爸每天早上都喝很濃的黑咖啡。

⑥ 否定句和疑問句

  1. The bus station isn't very far from my house.
    公車站離我家不太遠。
  2. Is the lake very deep in the middle?
    湖的中間很深嗎？
  3. I'm not really sure about the answer to question five.
    第五題的答案我不太確定。
  4. Were the questions in the test very hard?
    考試的題目很難嗎？
  5. The coffee in the office kitchen isn't very good.
    辦公室茶水間的咖啡不太好喝。
  6. Is your teacher really strict about homework?
    你的老師對作業真的很嚴格嗎？
