I shall be free on Sunday at six o'clock in the evening.

我星期天晚上 6 點有空。

I / We + shall + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

shall、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I shall be free on Sunday at six o'clock in the evening.

shall會、將。主要跟 I 和 we 一起用。
原形動詞以後會做的事。

shall 和 will

I shall be free on Sunday at six o'clock in the evening.

正式

Thank you for the invitation to the dinner. I shall be there.

謝謝您邀請我參加晚宴。我會到。
口語

Thanks for asking me to the party. I'll be there.

謝謝你邀我參加派對。我會去。

否⁠定

例句

The train leaves at nine, and we're already at the station. We shan't miss it.

火車九點開，我們已經在車站了。我們不會錯過。

否定是 shall not，縮寫是 shan't。

例句

① We shall + 原形動詞：安排和時間

  1. Everyone has agreed, so we shall start tomorrow at two o'clock in the library.
    大家都同意了，所以我們明天兩點在圖書館開始。
  2. Our flight is early, so we shall leave for the airport at five in the morning.
    我們的班機很早，所以我們早上五點出發去機場。
  3. We shall meet the new head teacher at the assembly on Monday.
    星期一朝會的時候，我們會見到新校長。
  4. On the first day of the tour, we shall have lunch in a small fishing village.
    旅行的第一天，我們會在一個小漁村吃午餐。
  5. If the traffic is light, we shall reach Oxford before the rain starts.
    如果路上車不多，我們會在下雨前到牛津。
  6. We shall finish the evening course just before the summer holidays.
    暑假前我們會上完夜間課程。

② I shall be + 形容詞或地方：說自己以後的狀態

  1. My Sunday afternoon is busy, but I shall be free on Sunday at six o'clock in the evening.
    我星期天下午很忙，可是我星期天傍晚六點有空。
  2. Ring me on Saturday. I shall be at home sorting out the garden all day.
    星期六打給我。我整天都會在家整理花園。
  3. I shall be eighteen next month, so I can finally learn to drive.
    我下個月就滿十八歲了，所以終於可以學開車了。
  4. I shall be in Paris for the whole of July, working at my uncle's café.
    我整個七月都會在巴黎，在我叔叔的咖啡館工作。
  5. If you need help with your maths, I shall be glad to sit with you after school.
    如果你數學需要幫忙，我放學後很樂意陪你一起做。
  6. I shall be away on business until the end of the month.
    我出差到月底才會回來。

③ I shall + 原形動詞：以後會做的事

  1. I shall write to you again as soon as I get my exam results.
    我一拿到考試成績就會再寫信給你。
  2. I shall phone you from the hotel when we arrive in Madrid.
    我們到馬德里的時候，我會從飯店打電話給你。
  3. I shall bring my old camera to your wedding and take lots of photos.
    我會帶我的舊相機去你的婚禮，拍很多照片。
  4. With my birthday money, I shall buy a new saddle for my bike.
    我會用我的生日禮金幫腳踏車買一個新坐墊。
  5. I shall think of you all when I'm far away in Canada.
    我在遙遠的加拿大時，會想念你們大家。
  6. I shall explain everything to my parents when they get home tonight.
    我爸媽今晚回家的時候，我會把一切都解釋給他們聽。

④ We shall + 原形動詞：大家一起的計畫

  1. If the weather is nice, we shall go swimming in the river after lunch.
    如果天氣好，我們午餐後會去河裡游泳。
  2. This summer we shall rent a little cottage on the Scottish coast.
    今年夏天我們會在蘇格蘭海邊租一間小屋。
  3. We shall need plenty of sandwiches for the long walk tomorrow.
    明天要走很長的路，我們會需要很多三明治。
  4. We shall take the slow train along the coast so we can see the sea.
    我們會搭沿著海岸的慢車，這樣就能看到海。
  5. On the second day, we shall explore the old streets around the cathedral.
    第二天，我們會去逛大教堂附近的老街。
  6. We shall cook sausages on the beach if the wind isn't too strong.
    如果風不太大，我們會在海灘上烤香腸。

⑤ I / We shan't + 原形動詞：不會

  1. Don't worry about the alarm. I shan't be late for the interview this time.
    別擔心鬧鐘的事。這次面試我不會遲到。
  2. Dad is taking the car to work, but we shan't need it tomorrow.
    爸爸要開車去上班，可是我們明天不會需要車子。
  3. You helped me so much last winter. I shan't ever forget it.
    你去年冬天幫了我很多。我永遠不會忘記。
  4. The concert finishes late, so we shan't get home until after midnight.
    音樂會很晚才結束，所以我們半夜以後才會到家。
  5. You can trust me with the news. I shan't say a word to your sister.
    這個消息你可以放心告訴我。我不會跟你妹妹說一個字。
  6. The trip is very short, so we shan't see much of the countryside.
    這趟旅行很短，所以我們看不到多少鄉村風景。
  7. I've had three slices already, so I shan't have any more cake tonight.
    我已經吃了三塊，所以今天晚上不會再吃蛋糕了。
