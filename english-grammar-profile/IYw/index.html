There are a lot of TV programs in my country, but only some of these are interesting.

我們國家的電視節目很多，可是只有其中一些好看。

數⁠量詞 + of + 指⁠示代⁠名⁠詞

There are a lot of TV programs in my country, but only some of these are interesting.

我們國家的電視節目很多，可是只有其中一些好看。

常⁠用⁠的數⁠量詞

There are a lot of TV programs in my country, but only some of these are interesting.

其中一個

We have three rooms free. You can choose one of these.

我們有三間空房，你可以選其中一間。
其中一部分

I've read all her books, and most of these are about love.

她的書我全讀過了，大部分是寫愛情的。
一個也沒有

He offered me biscuits, but I wanted none of those.

他請我吃餅乾，可是那些我都不想吃。

all of that：全⁠部

這座城市有海灘、博物館和公園，這些全部週末就看得完。

this / that 和 these / those

There are a lot of TV programs in my country, but only some of these are interesting.

複數名詞

We saw many towns. A few of these had castles.

我們看了很多小鎮，其中幾個有城堡。
一件事

He told me a long story, but I didn't believe much of that.

他跟我講了一個很長的故事，可是我大部分都不相信。

數⁠量詞後⁠面加 of

There are a lot of TV programs in my country, but only some of these are interesting.

I bought six eggs, but some these were broken.

I bought six eggs, but some of these were broken.

複⁠數名⁠詞用 these

There are a lot of TV programs in my country, but only some of these are interesting.

We tried three restaurants, and all of this were good.

We tried three restaurants, and all of these were good.

one of these：動⁠詞用單⁠數

I bought four books, and one of these was damaged.

數⁠量詞 + of + them

我有很多朋友，可是住在附近的沒幾個。

數⁠量詞 + 名⁠詞：不⁠加 of

有些節目很好看。

例句

① some of these / those

  1. I've got lots of old CDs. Some of these are quite rare.
    我有很多舊 CD，其中有些很少見。
  2. There were many questions in the test, and some of those were very hard.
    考卷上有很多題目，其中有些很難。
  3. The shop sells lots of cheap clothes, but only some of these are good.
    這家店賣很多便宜的衣服，可是只有一部分品質好。
  4. We picked a lot of apples. Some of these are for you.
    我們摘了很多蘋果，其中一些是給你的。
  5. He has many ideas, but some of those are crazy.
    他有很多點子，可是有些很瘋狂。

② one of these / those

  1. If you can't decide, you must choose one of these.
    如果你決定不了，就必須從這些裡面選一個。
  2. I have three old phones. You can have one of those.
    我有三支舊手機，你可以拿其中一支。
  3. Here are some keys. One of these opens the back door.
    這裡有幾把鑰匙，其中一把可以開後門。
  4. The museum has many old maps, and one of these is from 1500.
    博物館有很多古地圖，其中一張是一五〇〇年的。
  5. There are three paths through the forest. Take one of those on the left.
    穿過森林有三條小路，走左邊那幾條的其中一條。

③ all / most of these / those

  1. Australia has great beaches and wildlife. We have to go to Sydney to see all of that.
    澳洲有很棒的海灘和野生動物，我們得去雪梨才能看到這些。
  2. She took a hundred photos, and most of these are blurry.
    她拍了一百張照片，大部分都很模糊。
  3. Look at all those books! Have you read all of those?
    你看那些書！你全都讀過了嗎？
  4. We sent lots of letters, but most of those never arrived.
    我們寄了很多信，可是大部分都沒寄到。
  5. The teacher gave us ten exercises, and we finished all of these in class.
    老師給了我們十道練習題，我們在課堂上全部做完了。

④ none / any of these / those

  1. I tried on five dresses, but none of these fitted me.
    我試穿了五件洋裝，可是沒有一件合身。
  2. Here are some magazines. Do you want any of these?
    這裡有幾本雜誌，你要不要看哪一本？
  3. They gave us lots of advice, but none of that was useful.
    他們給了我們很多建議，可是一點用也沒有。
  4. I looked at the hotels online, and I didn't like any of those.
    我在網路上看了那些旅館，一家也不喜歡。
  5. He made several promises, but he kept none of those.
    他許了好幾個承諾，可是一個也沒做到。

⑤ much / a little of this / that

  1. She told me the whole plan, but I don't remember much of that.
    她把整個計畫都告訴我了，可是我記得的不多。
  2. This soup is very spicy. Just have a little of this.
    這湯很辣，吃一點就好。
  3. The news is bad, and much of this is hard to believe.
    消息很糟，很多都讓人很難相信。
  4. There is a lot of homework, but I've already done some of this.
    功課很多，可是我已經做了一部分。
  5. He spent a lot of money on the trip, but most of that was on food.
    他這趟旅行花了很多錢，大部分都花在吃的上面。

⑥ 介紹和建議

  1. There are many places to visit, and a few of these are free.
    可以參觀的地方很多，其中有幾個是免費的。
  2. The town has six cafés, and two of these have free wifi.
    鎮上有六家咖啡館，其中兩家有免費無線網路。
  3. We have lots of programmes for children, and many of these are educational.
    我們有很多兒童節目，其中很多有教育意義。
  4. Here are some hotels near the beach. Each of these has a pool.
    這裡有幾家海灘附近的旅館，每一家都有游泳池。
  5. I've listed ten good films. Watch at least three of these.
    我列了十部好電影，至少看其中三部。
  6. There are several courses this term, and both of those on Monday are full.
    這學期有好幾門課，星期一的那兩門都滿了。
