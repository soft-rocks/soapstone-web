When I said to my friends that I had met him, they didn't believe me.

我跟朋友說我見過他，他們都不相信。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語說⁠出聽⁠話⁠的⁠人，放⁠在轉⁠述動⁠詞和 that 子⁠句中⁠間

When I said to my friends that I had met him, they didn't believe me.

我跟朋友說我見過他，他們都不相信。

explain 後⁠面先接 to，tell 後⁠面直⁠接接人

When I said to my friends that I had met him, they didn't believe me.

explain
轉述動詞 + to + 人

She explained to me that the bus was late.

她跟我解釋說公車誤點了。
tell
tell + 人

She told me that the bus was late.

她跟我說公車誤點了。

不⁠必說⁠出聽⁠話⁠的人⁠時，介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語可⁠以省⁠略

When I said to my friends that I had met him, they didn't believe me.

有介系詞片語

He admitted to his boss that he was wrong.

他向老闆承認他錯了。
省略

He admitted that he was wrong.

他承認他錯了。

explain 後⁠面⁠接聽⁠話⁠的人⁠時，要加 to

When I said to my friends that I had met him, they didn't believe me.

He explained me that the room was full.

He explained to me that the room was full.

tell 後⁠面直⁠接接聽⁠話⁠的人

She told to us that the show was cancelled.

She told us that the show was cancelled.

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語放⁠在轉⁠述動⁠詞和 that 子⁠句中⁠間

When I said to my friends that I had met him, they didn't believe me.

I said that I was leaving to my boss.

I said to my boss that I was leaving.

to 後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

He mentioned to she that he was ill.

He mentioned to her that he was ill.

例句

① say to + 人 + that

  1. When I said to my mum that I wanted to leave school, she was very upset.
    我跟我媽說我想休學的時候，她很難過。
  2. "What did he say to you?" "He said to me that he was sorry."
    他跟你說了什麼？他跟我說他很抱歉。
  3. You should say to your parents that you want to go on holiday with your friends.
    你應該跟你爸媽說你想和朋友去度假。
  4. Nobody said to us that the meeting had moved to Room 5.
    沒有人跟我們說會議改到五號會議室了。

② explain to + 人 + that

  1. "Why can't we come in?" The guard explained to us that the museum was closed for repairs.
    我們為什麼不能進去？警衛跟我們解釋說博物館在整修，所以沒開。
  2. I explained to the teacher that I had been ill.
    我跟老師解釋說我之前生病了。
  3. The doctor explained to my dad that he needed an operation.
    醫生跟我爸解釋說他需要動手術。
  4. Can you explain to them that the flight is delayed?
    你可以跟他們解釋班機延誤了嗎？

③ mention to + 人 + that

  1. "Is Sam leaving?" "Yes, he mentioned to me that he'd found a new job."
    Sam 要走了嗎？對，他跟我提到他找到新工作了。
  2. Did Anna mention to you that she's having a party?
    Anna 有跟你提到她要辦派對嗎？
  3. I mentioned to the waiter that I don't eat meat.
    我跟服務生提到我不吃肉。

④ admit to + 人 + that

  1. He finally admitted to his wife that he had lost his job.
    他終於向太太承認他丟了工作。
  2. "Did you break the vase?" "Yes. I admitted to Mum that it was me."
    花瓶是你打破的嗎？對。我跟媽媽承認是我。
  3. She admitted to her friends that she was scared of flying.
    她向朋友承認她怕坐飛機。

⑤ complain to + 人 + that

  1. We complained to the hotel manager that the room was dirty.
    我們向飯店經理抱怨房間很髒。
  2. My neighbour complained to me that my music was too loud.
    我的鄰居向我抱怨我的音樂太大聲。
  3. The students complained to the head teacher that the classroom was too cold.
    學生們向校長抱怨教室太冷。

⑥ suggest / prove / point out to + 人 + that

  1. We were lost, so I suggested to the others that we ask at the café.
    我們迷路了，所以我向其他人建議去咖啡店問路。
  2. My teacher suggested to me that I should try for a scholarship.
    我的老師建議我試著申請獎學金。
  3. The young player wanted to prove to the coach that he was ready.
    那個年輕球員想向教練證明他準備好了。
  4. I pointed out to my boss that the report had a mistake.
    我向老闆指出報告裡有一個錯誤。

⑦ 聽話的人是代名詞

  1. "Why is she angry?" "I said to her that her dress was too short."
    她為什麼生氣？我跟她說她的洋裝太短了。
  2. He explained to us that he couldn't come because of work.
    他跟我們解釋說他因為工作不能來。
  3. They complained to him that the food was cold.
    他們向他抱怨食物是冷的。
  4. My son admitted to me that he hadn't done his homework.
    我兒子向我承認他沒寫作業。

⑧ 不說出聽話的人

  1. He admitted that he had made a mistake.
    他承認他犯了錯。
  2. The guide explained that the castle was over 800 years old.
    導遊解釋說那座城堡有八百多年的歷史了。
  3. "Did they like the hotel?" "No, they complained that it was noisy."
    他們喜歡那家飯店嗎？不喜歡，他們抱怨很吵。
  4. She mentioned that her brother lives in Paris.
    她提到她哥哥住在巴黎。
  5. My dad suggested that we leave early to avoid the traffic.
    我爸建議我們早點出發，避開車潮。
  6. The news says that more people are cycling to work this year.
    新聞說今年騎腳踏車上班的人變多了。
