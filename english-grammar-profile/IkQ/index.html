Not only must you study really hard during university, but you must also keep studying afterwards.

你不但讀大學的時候要非常用功，畢業以後也要繼續學習。

Not only + must + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞, but + 主⁠詞 + must also + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

not only … but also不只……也……。
never絕對不可以。
nor也不可以。接在前一個否定的句子後面。
on no account / under no circumstances在任何情況下都不可以。

一⁠般⁠的順⁠序和倒⁠裝

一般

You must never forget this.

你絕對不可以忘記這件事。
倒裝

Never must you forget this.

這件事你絕對不可以忘記。

倒裝的句子比一般的句子更強調，常用在演講和正式的寫作。

nor 接在否⁠定⁠句後⁠面

例句

We mustn't ignore the cost. Nor must we forget the risks.

我們不可以忽略成本。我們也不可以忘記風險。

Nor must we 接在另一個否定句後面，再加上一件不可以做的事。

not only 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，must 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Not only must you study really hard during university, but you must also keep studying afterwards.

Not only you must study hard, but you must also rest.

Not only must you study hard, but you must also rest.

nor 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，must 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Not only must you study really hard during university, but you must also keep studying afterwards.

Nor we must forget our history.

Nor must we forget our history.

倒⁠裝直⁠接把 must 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，不⁠加 do

Not only must you study really hard during university, but you must also keep studying afterwards.

Never do we must give up.

Never must we give up.

例句

① Not only must + 主詞 + 原形動詞, but …

  1. Graduates, not only must you study really hard at university, but you must also keep studying afterwards.
    各位畢業生，你們不只在大學時要非常用功，畢業以後也一定要繼續學習。
  2. The manager told the staff meeting that sales had fallen again. Not only must we cut costs, but we must also find new customers.
    經理在員工會議上說銷售又下滑了。我們不只必須減少開支，也必須找到新顧客。
  3. Rents in our city have doubled in ten years. Not only must the city build more houses, but it must also improve public transport.
    我們城市的房租十年漲了一倍。這個城市不只必須蓋更多房子，也必須改善大眾運輸。
  4. The new coach spoke to the team before the season. Not only must players be fit, but they must also work as a team.
    新教練在球季開始前對球隊講話。球員不只必須體能好，也必須團隊合作。
  5. The parenting book makes one point clearly. Not only must parents set rules, but they must also follow them.
    那本教養書把一個重點講得很清楚。父母不只必須訂規矩，自己也必須遵守。
  6. The editor gave us one final instruction. Not only must the report be accurate, but it must also be clear.
    編輯給了我們最後一個指示。這份報告不只必須準確，也必須清楚。

② Never must + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Education is necessary and useful to all of us, and never must it be called a waste of time.
    教育對我們所有人都必要而且有用，絕對不可以把它說成浪費時間。（被動）
  2. The mayor spoke at the memorial on Sunday. Never must we forget the people who died in the war.
    市長星期天在紀念碑前致詞。我們絕對不可以忘記在戰爭中死去的人。
  3. The swimming instructor repeated the first rule to the parents. Never must you leave a child alone near water.
    游泳教練對家長再說一次第一條規定。你絕對不可以把孩子單獨留在水邊。
  4. The hotel manager spoke to the new staff. Never must we treat our guests with anything less than respect.
    飯店經理對新員工講話。我們對客人絕對不可以有一點不尊重。
  5. Our professor of medicine ended her lecture with one rule. Never must a doctor lie to a patient.
    我們的醫學教授用一條規則結束她的課。醫生絕對不可以對病人說謊。
  6. The bank sent all its customers a warning. Never must you give your bank details to a stranger.
    銀行寄了一封警告給所有客戶。你絕對不可以把銀行資料給陌生人。

③ Nor must + 主詞 + 原形動詞：再加上一件不可以做的事

  1. We mustn't judge a leader only by results. Nor must we overlook their human qualities.
    我們不可以只用成績評斷一個領導者。我們也不可以忽略他們人性的特質。
  2. The council is discussing a new bridge over the river. We mustn't ignore the cost of building it. Nor must we forget the risks of doing nothing.
    議會在討論河上的新橋。我們不可以忽略建橋的成本。我們也不可以忘記什麼都不做的風險。
  3. The exam rules are strict. Students mustn't copy each other's work. Nor must they use their phones in the exam.
    考試規定很嚴格。學生不可以抄彼此的作業。考試時也不可以用手機。
  4. The workers wrote to the new owners. The company must not raise prices. Nor must it cut jobs.
    員工寫信給新老闆。公司不可以漲價。也不可以裁員。
  5. Our school's results were poor this year, the head said. We must not blame the children. Nor must we blame their teachers.
    校長說我們學校今年的成績很差。我們不可以責怪孩子。也不可以責怪他們的老師。
  6. The castle has new rules for tourists. Visitors must not feed the birds. Nor must they climb on the walls.
    城堡有給遊客的新規定。參觀者不可以餵鳥。也不可以爬牆。

④ On no account / Under no circumstances + must + 主詞 + 原形動詞：嚴格的禁止

  1. The sign on the washing machine in the factory says: "On no account must you open this door while the machine is running."
    工廠洗衣機上的牌子寫著：機器運轉的時候，你絕對不可以打開這扇門。
  2. The new IT rules came out today. Under no circumstances must staff share their passwords.
    新的資訊規定今天公布了。員工在任何情況下都不可以分享密碼。
  3. The camp leader told the helpers that the river was deep and fast. On no account must the children go near it alone.
    營隊領隊告訴幫手，那條河又深又急。孩子們絕對不可以自己靠近河邊。
  4. The driving teacher warned us on the first day. Under no circumstances must you drive without insurance.
    駕訓教練第一天就警告我們。你在任何情況下都不可以沒有保險就開車。
  5. The nurse wrote a note above Mr Brown's bed. On no account must the patient eat before the operation.
    護理師在 Brown 先生的床頭寫了一張字條。病人手術前絕對不可以吃東西。
  6. The sign at the entrance to the national park says: "Under no circumstances must visitors feed the bears."
    國家公園入口的牌子寫著：參觀者在任何情況下都不可以餵熊。

⑤ Not only must + 主詞 + be + 形容詞, but …：寫作的強調

  1. In my essay I describe the perfect teacher. Not only must a good teacher be patient, but he or she must also be fair.
    我在作文裡描寫理想的老師。好老師不只必須有耐心，也必須公平。
  2. The chef explained her rules to the new cooks. Not only must the food be fresh, but it must also look good.
    主廚對新廚師說明她的規則。食物不只必須新鮮，看起來也必須好看。
  3. In my review I explain what I look for in a hotel. Not only must it be clean, but it must also be quiet.
    我在評論裡說明我對飯店的要求。它不只必須乾淨，也必須安靜。
  4. The town is choosing a plan for the new playground. Not only must the plan be cheap, but it must also be safe.
    鎮上正在選新遊樂場的計畫。這個計畫不只必須便宜，也必須安全。
  5. Our designers know what customers want. Not only must a website be fast, but it must also be easy to use.
    我們的設計師知道顧客要什麼。網站不只必須快，也必須好用。
  6. The city wants a new bridge over the river. Not only must the bridge be strong, but it must also be beautiful.
    這個城市想在河上蓋一座新橋。這座橋不只必須堅固，也必須美觀。
  7. The article asks what makes a good leader. Not only must a leader be clever, but he or she must also be honest.
    那篇文章問什麼是好的領導者。領導者不只必須聰明，也必須誠實。
