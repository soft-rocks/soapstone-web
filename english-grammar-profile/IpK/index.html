I bought a jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

我買了一件夾克，因為我原本那件太小了。

too 表⁠示超⁠過想⁠要⁠的程⁠度，所⁠以有問⁠題

I bought a jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

這雙鞋太大了，有小一號的嗎？

very 只⁠說程⁠度⁠高，too 說程⁠度太⁠高

I bought a jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

very

My uncle's curry is very spicy.

我叔叔煮的咖哩很辣。
too

My uncle's curry is too spicy. I can't eat it.

我叔叔煮的咖哩太辣了，我吃不下。

not too 表⁠示不⁠會太怎⁠麼⁠樣

The shoes aren't too expensive. They're about 30 pounds.

那雙鞋不會太貴，大概三十英鎊。

稱⁠讚⁠時不⁠用 too

I bought a jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

Thank you for the meal. It was too delicious.

Thank you for the meal. It was really delicious.

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面只⁠用 too，不⁠加 much

I bought a jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

That car is too much expensive for us.

That car is too expensive for us.

太怎⁠麼⁠樣⁠的 too 有兩⁠個 o

I bought a jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

Grandma's lemonade is to sweet for me.

Grandma's lemonade is too sweet for me.

例句

① be + too + 形容詞

  1. I tried on the trainers in the shop. These shoes are too big. Do you have a smaller size?
    我在店裡試穿那雙運動鞋。這雙鞋太大了，有小一號的嗎？
  2. I can't drink this coffee. It's too hot.
    這杯咖啡我沒辦法喝，太燙了。
  3. We loved the green sofa, but we didn't buy it because it was too expensive.
    我們很喜歡那張綠色沙發，不過我們沒買，因為太貴了。
  4. Your music is too loud. I'm trying to sleep.
    你的音樂太大聲了，我想睡覺。
  5. We went to the lake in April. The water was too cold, so we didn't swim.
    我們四月去湖邊。水太冰了，所以我們沒游泳。
  6. You've been carrying that box for ten minutes. Is the box too heavy? I can help.
    你搬那個箱子搬了十分鐘。箱子會太重嗎？我可以幫忙。
  7. I grew a lot this year. This T-shirt is too small now. Do you want it?
    我今年長高很多。這件 T 恤我現在穿太小了，你要嗎？
  8. Thanks for asking me to the cinema, but I can't come. I'm too tired.
    謝謝你約我去看電影，不過我不能去，我太累了。

② too + 形容詞，說明原因

  1. I couldn't fit my laptop in my old bag, so I bought a new bag. My old one was too small.
    我的舊包包放不下筆電，所以我買了一個新包包。舊的太小了。
  2. We took a taxi home because it was too late. The buses had stopped.
    我們搭計程車回家，因為太晚了，公車已經停駛。
  3. My sister started the book in June, but she didn't finish it because it was too long.
    我姊姊六月開始看那本書，不過她沒看完，因為太長了。
  4. I couldn't hear my friends, so I left the party early. It was too noisy.
    我聽不到朋友說話，所以提早離開派對。那裡太吵了。
  5. My cousins moved in March because their flat was too dark.
    我表哥一家三月搬家了，因為他們的公寓太暗了。
  6. Leo only ate the rice. He didn't eat the curry because it was too spicy.
    Leo 只吃了飯。他沒吃咖哩，因為太辣了。

③ not too + 形容詞

  1. You should buy the brown shoes. They aren't too expensive. They're about 30 pounds.
    你應該買那雙咖啡色的鞋。那雙鞋不會太貴，大概三十英鎊。
  2. I was worried about the driving test, but it wasn't too hard.
    我本來很擔心駕照考試，不過不會太難。
  3. Don't worry about the hill. The walk to the castle isn't too long.
    別擔心那座山丘。走到城堡不會太遠。
  4. We're having a barbecue in the garden. I hope the weather isn't too cold tomorrow.
    我們要在花園烤肉。希望明天天氣不會太冷。
  5. My room at the hostel is small, but it's not too bad.
    我在青年旅館的房間很小，不過還不算太差。
  6. You're giving the baby a bath. Make sure the water isn't too hot.
    你要幫寶寶洗澡。要確定洗澡水不會太燙。

④ 問句

  1. You're studying in the next room. Is the TV too loud? I can turn it down.
    你在隔壁房間讀書。電視會太大聲嗎？我可以轉小聲。
  2. You didn't stay at the Ritz in the end. Was the hotel too expensive?
    你最後沒住麗池飯店。那家旅館太貴了嗎？
  3. I bought these trousers for the wedding. Are they too long?
    我買了這條褲子去參加婚禮。會太長嗎？
  4. I want to call Ann, but it's eleven o'clock. Is it too late now? She might be asleep.
    我想打給 Ann，不過已經十一點了。現在會太晚嗎？她可能睡了。
  5. You walked up to the top of the tower. Were the stairs too steep? You look tired.
    你走路爬到塔頂。樓梯太陡了嗎？你看起來很累。

⑤ look / feel / get 後面

  1. You've lost a lot of weight. You look too thin. Are you eating enough?
    你瘦了很多。你看起來太瘦了，你有吃飽嗎？
  2. This jumper is very thick. I feel too hot in it.
    這件毛衣很厚。我穿著它覺得太熱了。
  3. We can't see the ball any more. It's getting too dark. Let's go inside.
    我們看不到球了。天色太暗了，我們進去吧。
  4. I forgot about the loaf in the cupboard. The bread got too hard after two days.
    我忘了櫃子裡的麵包。麵包放了兩天就太硬了。
  5. I turned the heater off. The room felt too warm with it on.
    我把暖氣關了。開著暖氣，房間感覺太熱了。
