completely 放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面，表⁠示完⁠全

我完全確定。

幸好我表哥漸漸好起來了。

每⁠個態⁠度副⁠詞表⁠示不⁠同⁠的看⁠法

sadly很可惜，壞的結果

completely 不⁠放⁠在句⁠首

I am completely sure.

Completely, I understand your problem with the noise.

I completely understand your problem with the noise.

修⁠飾整⁠個句⁠子用副⁠詞 hopefully

Hopeful, the weather will be nice tomorrow.

Hopefully, the weather will be nice tomorrow.

修⁠飾整⁠個句⁠子用副⁠詞 sadly

Sad, the old cinema in our street closed last year.

Sadly, the old cinema in our street closed last year.

basic 加 ⁠-⁠ally

Basicly, the plan is simple.

Basically, the plan is simple.

例句

① luckily

  1. The car hit a tree, but luckily nobody inside was hurt.
    車子撞到樹，不過幸好車裡沒有人受傷。
  2. We missed the bus. Luckily, my dad was driving past.
    我們錯過了公車。幸好我爸爸剛好開車經過。
  3. Luckily, I had an umbrella with me.
    幸好我身上帶了傘。
  4. I left my phone on the train, but luckily a kind woman handed it in.
    我把手機忘在火車上，不過幸好一位好心的女士把它交了出來。

② sadly

  1. Sadly, the old cinema closed last year.
    很可惜，那家老電影院去年關了。
  2. I don't watch much TV. Sadly, I don't have time.
    我不常看電視。可惜我沒時間。
  3. Sadly, our dog died last month.
    很難過，我們的狗上個月死了。
  4. Our holiday in Greece was sadly too short.
    很可惜，我們在希臘的假期太短了。

③ hopefully

  1. Hopefully, I'll get the job.
    希望我能得到那份工作。
  2. The parcel will hopefully arrive tomorrow.
    包裹希望明天會到。
  3. Hopefully, we'll see you again soon.
    希望我們很快能再見到你。
  4. Hopefully, the rain will stop before the match.
    希望比賽前雨會停。

④ obviously、clearly

  1. Obviously, you need a passport to travel abroad.
    出國當然需要護照。
  2. He was obviously very tired.
    他顯然很累了。
  3. The baker clearly enjoys talking to his customers.
    那個麵包師傅顯然很喜歡跟顧客聊天。
  4. Clearly, the school needs a bigger car park.
    很明顯，學校需要一個更大的停車場。
  5. The driver clearly didn't see the red light.
    司機顯然沒看到紅燈。

⑤ honestly、basically

  1. Honestly, I didn't enjoy the second half of the film.
    老實說，我不喜歡那部電影的下半段。
  2. I honestly don't know where he is.
    我真的不知道他在哪裡。
  3. Basically, the job is answering the phone.
    基本上，這份工作就是接電話。
  4. Basically, the club is for anyone who likes walking.
    簡單地說，這個社團是給喜歡健行的人參加的。
  5. It's basically a small hotel with a restaurant.
    那基本上就是一家附設餐廳的小旅館。

⑥ surely

  1. Surely you remember her!
    你總不會不記得她吧！
  2. Surely the shops are open on Saturday.
    商店星期六應該有開吧。
  3. Surely they won't charge us for water.
    他們總不會連水都跟我們收錢吧。
  4. You're not leaving already, surely?
    你不會現在就要走了吧？

⑦ completely

  1. I completely forgot about the meeting.
    我完全忘了開會的事。
  2. The room was completely empty.
    房間完全是空的。
  3. I'm completely lost. Can you help me?
    我完全迷路了。你可以幫我嗎？
  4. My grandmother's cake recipe is completely different from mine.
    我奶奶的蛋糕食譜跟我的完全不一樣。
