例句

① a + very + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. I have a very important meeting at ten.
    我十點有一個很重要的會議。
  2. It was a very old house with a big garden.
    那是一棟很老的房子，有個大花園。
  3. My grandfather was a very kind man.
    我爺爺是個很和善的人。
  4. We had a very long day at the airport.
    我們在機場待了很漫長的一天。
  5. That's a very good question, Ken.
    Ken，這個問題問得很好。
  6. She lives in a very small village in the mountains.
    她住在山裡一個很小的村子。

② a + really + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. She's a really good friend. She always listens.
    她是個很好的朋友，總是會聽我說。
  2. We watched a really funny movie last night.
    我們昨天晚上看了一部很好笑的電影。
  3. My uncle still drives a really old car to work.
    我叔叔還是開一輛很舊的車去上班。
  4. It was a really hot afternoon, so we stayed inside.
    那天下午很熱，所以我們待在室內。
  5. Ann made a really nice cake for my birthday.
    Ann 為我的生日做了一個很棒的蛋糕。
  6. That was a really difficult test.
    那次考試很難。

③ an + extremely / a + pretty + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. We were tired after an extremely long flight from London.
    從倫敦飛來的航程非常長，我們都累了。
  2. She's an extremely talented singer.
    她是個非常有天分的歌手。
  3. That's an extremely expensive watch.
    那是一支非常貴的手錶。
  4. Thanks for inviting us. It was a pretty good party.
    謝謝你邀請我們，派對還不錯。
  5. He's a pretty fast runner.
    他跑得滿快的。
  6. We stayed in a pretty cheap hotel near the station.
    我們住在車站附近一家滿便宜的旅館。

④ the / my / this + 程度副詞 + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. My really old laptop finally stopped working.
    我那台很舊的筆電終於壞了。
  2. The very tall man at the back is my uncle.
    後面那個很高的男人是我叔叔。
  3. This really comfortable chair was only twenty dollars.
    這張很舒服的椅子只要二十塊。
  4. Their very noisy neighbors moved out last week.
    他們那些很吵的鄰居上禮拜搬走了。
  5. I lost my really nice sunglasses at the beach.
    我在海邊弄丟了我那副很好看的太陽眼鏡。
  6. The extremely heavy rain closed the roads.
    那場非常大的雨讓道路封閉了。

⑤ 程度副詞 + 形容詞 + 複數 / 不可數名詞

  1. They sell very fresh fish at that market.
    那個市場賣的魚很新鮮。
  2. We had really bad weather on our trip.
    我們旅行的時候天氣很差。
  3. These are very useful tips for new students.
    這些對新生來說是很有用的建議。
  4. My neighbors are really friendly people.
    我的鄰居都是很友善的人。
  5. The kids need very warm coats for the snow.
    孩子們需要很保暖的外套才能去雪地。
  6. This shop sells really cheap clothes.
    這家店賣很便宜的衣服。
