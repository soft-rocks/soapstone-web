商⁠業信⁠件用 concerning，日⁠常說⁠話用 about

Why don't you act upon my advice?

正式

We are writing concerning your recent complaint.

我們寫信是關於您最近的申訴。
日常

I'm calling about your complaint.

我打來是關於你的申訴。

regarding 前⁠面不⁠加 about

I'm writing about regarding the meeting.

I'm writing regarding the meeting.

兩⁠個之⁠間用 between，amongst 用在三⁠個以⁠上

I found the letter amongst the two books.

I found the letter between the two books.

upon 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞片⁠語或動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，不⁠接子⁠句

Why don't you act upon my advice?

Upon we arrived, we checked in.

Upon arriving, we checked in.

例句

① upon

  1. Upon hearing the news, she burst into tears.
    一聽到那個消息，她就哭了出來。
  2. Payment is due upon receipt of the invoice.
    收到發票後就要付款。
  3. Once upon a time, there was a poor fisherman.
    很久很久以前，有一個窮漁夫。
  4. The decision depends upon the results of the test.
    決定要看檢查的結果。
  5. Upon reflection, I think you were right.
    仔細想過之後，我覺得你是對的。

② amongst / amid

  1. The idea is popular amongst young voters.
    這個想法在年輕選民之間很受歡迎。
  2. She felt at home amongst her new colleagues.
    她跟新同事相處起來很自在。
  3. Amid all the noise, the baby slept peacefully.
    在一片吵雜聲中，寶寶睡得很安穩。
  4. There was a small cottage amongst the trees.
    樹林裡有一間小屋。
  5. The singer walked onto the stage amid loud applause.
    那位歌手在熱烈的掌聲中走上舞台。

③ toward / unlike

  1. The storm is moving toward the east coast.
    暴風雨正往東海岸移動。
  2. The children ran toward the ice cream van.
    孩子們朝冰淇淋車跑過去。
  3. Unlike its neighbors, the country has no army.
    跟鄰國不一樣，這個國家沒有軍隊。
  4. Unlike her sister, Mia hates spicy food.
    跟她姊姊不一樣，Mia 很討厭吃辣。
  5. The boat drifted slowly toward the shore.
    船慢慢地漂向岸邊。

④ regarding / concerning

  1. I have a question regarding the timetable.
    我有一個關於時刻表的問題。
  2. We received several complaints concerning the noise.
    我們收到好幾則關於噪音的投訴。
  3. Regarding your request, we will reply within a week.
    關於您的請求，我們會在一週內回覆。
  4. Please read the information concerning safety.
    請閱讀關於安全的資訊。
  5. Regarding the party, have you decided on a date?
    關於派對，你決定日期了嗎？

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. A strange silence fell upon the room.
    房間裡突然一片奇怪的寂靜。
  2. Upon entering the building, show your pass to the guard.
    進入大樓時，請向警衛出示通行證。
  3. The town lies amid green hills.
    那個小鎮坐落在青山之間。
  4. Unlike most cities, Venice has no cars.
    跟大部分的城市不一樣，威尼斯沒有車。
  5. The company has taken a new approach toward training.
    那家公司對員工訓練採取了新的做法。
  6. I am writing concerning your recent visit to our shop.
    我寫信是關於您最近光臨本店的事。
  7. There is some disagreement amongst the experts.
    專家之間有一些意見分歧。
  8. Upon request, we can send you a free sample.
    如有需要，我們可以寄給您免費的樣品。
  9. The story is based upon real events.
    這個故事是根據真實事件改編的。（is based 是被動）
  10. The team worked hard amid difficult conditions.
    團隊在困難的條件下努力工作。
