I really don't know what you should do, but I think that you might talk with your parents.

我覺得你或許可以跟你爸媽談談。

You + might + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

you might、you might want to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

you might你或許可以。
you might want to你也許會想。
原形動詞建議的事。

例句

① You might + 原形動詞：委婉的建議

  1. I'm not sure what's best, but you might talk to the school nurse.
    我不確定怎麼做最好，可是你或許可以跟學校護理師談談。
  2. If you can't sleep, you might try a warm drink before bed.
    如果你睡不著，你或許可以睡前喝點熱的。
  3. You might ask your teacher for extra help.
    你或許可以請你的老師多幫你一點。
  4. You might look for a job in a café during the summer.
    你暑假或許可以在咖啡館找份工作。
  5. If you're bored, you might join a sports club.
    如果你覺得無聊，你或許可以加入運動社團。
  6. You might write to the company and complain.
    你或許可以寫信給那家公司抱怨。

② You might need to + 原形動詞：提醒要準備的事

  1. The park has lots of trees and a beautiful view. You might need to bring some food and drink.
    公園裡有很多樹，風景很美。你也許需要帶一些吃的和喝的。
  2. You might need to book a table. The restaurant is popular.
    你也許需要訂位。那家餐廳很熱門。
  3. You might need to take a jacket. It gets cold at night.
    你也許需要帶件外套。晚上會變冷。
  4. You might need to show your student card.
    你也許需要出示學生證。
  5. You might need to leave early to avoid the traffic.
    你也許需要早點出發，避開塞車。
  6. You might need to change trains at the next station.
    你也許需要在下一站換車。

③ You might want to + 原形動詞：建議對方考慮

  1. You might want to check the price before you order.
    你點之前也許會想先確認價錢。
  2. You might want to bring a book. The journey is long.
    你也許會想帶本書。路程很長。
  3. You might want to read the contract carefully.
    你也許會想仔細讀一下合約。
  4. You might want to call before you go.
    你出發前也許會想先打個電話。
  5. You might want to wear comfortable shoes for the tour.
    參加導覽你也許會想穿舒服的鞋子。
  6. You might want to practise your speech in front of a mirror.
    你也許會想對著鏡子練習你的演講。

④ You might like to + 原形動詞：推薦

  1. You might like to visit the old harbour while you're here.
    你在這裡的時候，也許會想去看看舊港口。
  2. You might like to try the local cheese.
    你也許會想試試當地的起司。
  3. You might like to join us for dinner on Friday.
    你星期五也許會想跟我們一起吃晚餐。
  4. If you enjoy history, you might like to see the castle.
    如果你喜歡歷史，你也許會想去看看城堡。
  5. You might like to read her first novel.
    你也許會想讀她的第一本小說。
  6. You might like to take a walk along the river.
    你也許會想沿著河邊散個步。

⑤ It might be a good idea to + 原形動詞

  1. It might be a good idea to save some money every month.
    每個月存一點錢也許是個好主意。
  2. It might be a good idea to learn a few words of the language.
    學幾個當地語言的字也許是個好主意。
  3. It might be a good idea to ask for directions.
    問一下路也許是個好主意。
  4. It might be a good idea to take a break.
    休息一下也許是個好主意。
  5. It might be a good idea to tell your boss about the problem.
    把這個問題告訴你的老闆也許是個好主意。
  6. It might be a good idea to get there before the crowds.
    在人潮出現之前到那裡也許是個好主意。
  7. It might be a good idea to keep a copy of your passport.
    留一份護照影本也許是個好主意。
