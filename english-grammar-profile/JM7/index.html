If she wants to study away from home, please let her go.

如果她想離家去外地讀書，就請讓她去吧。

let + him / her / them + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，叫對⁠方允⁠許

If she wants to study away from home, please let her go.

他累了，讓他睡吧。

let 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接名⁠詞

If she wants to study away from home, please let her go.

It's Friday, and there's no school tomorrow. Let the kids watch one more episode.

今天是星期五，明天不用上學。讓孩子們再看一集。

let 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 to

If she wants to study away from home, please let her go.

Let them to borrow the car for the weekend.

Let them borrow the car for the weekend.

let 後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

If she wants to study away from home, please let her go.

Let she choose the film.

Let her choose the film.

對⁠方自⁠己想⁠做，用 let，不⁠用 make

If she wants to study away from home, please let her go.

He wants to drive. Make him drive.

He wants to drive. Let him drive.

例句

① Let him + 原形動詞

  1. When you play cards with Grandpa, let him win now and then.
    跟爺爺玩牌的時候，偶爾讓他贏。
  2. He's tired. Let him sleep.
    他累了，讓他睡吧。
  3. Don't stop Grandpa in the middle. Let him finish his story.
    別在爺爺說到一半的時候打斷他。讓他把故事說完。
  4. If he wants to sing, let him sing.
    如果他想唱，就讓他唱吧。
  5. Your son is twelve now. Let him choose his own clothes.
    你兒子現在十二歲了。讓他自己挑衣服。
  6. My nephew wants to ride the horse. Let him try it once.
    我姪子想騎馬。讓他試一次。

② Let her + 原形動詞

  1. If your daughter wants to join the band, let her join.
    如果你女兒想加入樂團，就讓她加入吧。
  2. Your daughter is twenty and knows what she wants. Let her decide for herself.
    你女兒二十歲了，知道自己要什麼。讓她自己決定。
  3. She's hungry. Let her have the last piece.
    她餓了，最後一塊給她吃吧。
  4. Everyone is talking at once in the meeting. Let her speak.
    會議上大家同時在講話。讓她說話。
  5. Your niece found a puppy in the park. She loves animals, so let her keep it.
    你姪女在公園撿到一隻小狗。她喜歡動物，就讓她養吧。

③ Let them + 原形動詞

  1. The students worked hard all week, so let them leave early today.
    學生們整個禮拜都很用功，所以今天讓他們早點走。
  2. The kids are too noisy in the house. Let them play in the garden.
    孩子們在屋子裡太吵了。讓他們在花園玩。
  3. Let them stay up late tonight. It's New Year's Eve.
    今晚讓他們晚點睡吧，今天是除夕。
  4. Your children are adults now. Let them make their own mistakes.
    你的孩子們現在都是大人了。讓他們自己去犯錯。
  5. The guests are early, and they're waiting at the door. Let them come in. It's raining.
    客人早到了，在門口等。讓他們進來吧，在下雨。

④ Let + 名詞 + 原形動詞

  1. It's Friday, and there's no school tomorrow. Let the kids watch one more episode.
    今天是星期五，明天不用上學。讓孩子們再看一集。
  2. There's a storm, and he's scared. Let the dog sleep on the sofa tonight.
    有暴風雨，牠很害怕。今晚讓狗睡在沙發上吧。
  3. The project will be more fun that way. Let the students choose a topic.
    這樣專題會比較有趣。讓學生自己選題目。
  4. It's just come off the stove. Let the soup cool down before you eat it.
    它剛從爐子上拿下來。湯先放涼再喝。
  5. You've played games for two hours. Let your sister use the computer.
    你已經打了兩個小時的電動。讓你妹妹用電腦。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. My friend's daughter loves the zoo. Please let her come with us.
    我朋友的女兒很愛動物園。請讓她跟我們一起去。
  2. Your son is good at maths. Why don't you let him help you with the bills?
    你兒子數學很好。你為什麼不讓他幫你算帳單？
  3. The cats are fighting again, but they'll stop soon. Just let them be.
    貓又在打架了，不過牠們很快就會停。就讓牠們去吧。
  4. Your brother's bike is broken. Let him borrow yours for the weekend.
    你哥哥的腳踏車壞了。週末讓他借你的。
  5. Your mother just came home from hospital. Let her rest for a few days.
    你媽媽剛出院回家。讓她休息幾天。
  6. The new staff are still learning the system. Let them take their time.
    新員工還在學這個系統。讓他們慢慢來。
  7. Wait, children. Let the guests sit down first.
    孩子們，等一下。讓客人先坐。
  8. You paid for dinner last week. Let him pay this time.
    你上星期付了晚餐錢。這次讓他付吧。
  9. The tourists want to see the old map. Let them have a look.
    那些觀光客想看那張舊地圖。讓他們看一下。
