I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

我不喜歡綠色，所以我決定用藍色。

so 前⁠面是原⁠因，後⁠面是結⁠果

我不喜歡綠色，所以我決定用藍色。

however 開⁠始一⁠個新⁠的句⁠子，後⁠面加逗⁠號

I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

那家旅館很舊。不過房間很乾淨。

however 比 but 正式。

also 放⁠在一⁠般動⁠詞前⁠面，或放⁠在 be 和助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

一般動詞前面

I bought a jacket. I also bought some socks.

我買了一件夾克。我也買了幾雙襪子。
be 後面

She is also a teacher.

她也是老師。
助動詞後面

I can also speak a little Spanish.

我也會說一點西班牙文。

however 不⁠和 and 一⁠起用

It was cold, and however we went swimming.

It was cold. However, we went swimming.

because 和 so 不⁠放⁠在同⁠一⁠句

I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

Because I was tired, so I went to bed.

I was tired, so I went to bed.

also 不⁠放⁠在句⁠尾，放⁠在句⁠尾用 too

I like tennis also.

I also like tennis.

also 不⁠放⁠在 be 前⁠面

She also is a doctor.

She is also a doctor.

so 後⁠面⁠接結⁠果，because 後⁠面⁠接原⁠因

I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

so
結果

It was raining, so we stayed home.

在下雨，所以我們待在家。
because
原因

We stayed home because it was raining.

我們待在家，因為在下雨。

but 用逗⁠號連⁠接同⁠一⁠句⁠裡⁠的子⁠句

but

The test was hard, but I passed.

考試很難，可是我過了。
however

The test was hard. However, I passed.

考試很難。不過我過了。

例句

① 子句 + , so + 子句

  1. I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.
    我餓了，所以做了一個三明治。
  2. It's late, so let's go home.
    很晚了，我們回家吧。
  3. My phone was dead, so I couldn't call you.
    我的手機沒電了，所以沒辦法打給你。
  4. The shop was closed, so we went to the market.
    那家店沒開，所以我們去了市場。
  5. She's ill, so she can't come to the party.
    她生病了，所以不能來派對。
  6. We missed the bus, so we took a taxi.
    我們錯過了公車，所以搭了計程車。
  7. The film was boring, so I fell asleep.
    電影很無聊，所以我睡著了。
  8. I don't have much money, so I usually cook at home.
    我錢不多，所以通常在家煮飯。

② 句子。However, + 句子。

  1. The clothes were cheap. However, they were good quality.
    那些衣服很便宜。不過品質很好。
  2. My flat is small. However, it's very close to work.
    我的公寓很小。不過離公司很近。
  3. We wanted to go to the beach. However, it rained all day.
    我們本來想去海邊。不過下了一整天的雨。
  4. The food was great. However, the service was slow.
    食物很好吃。不過服務很慢。
  5. He studied hard. However, he didn't pass the test.
    他很用功。不過考試沒過。
  6. I like my job. However, I want to try something new.
    我喜歡我的工作。不過我想嘗試新的東西。
  7. Tickets usually cost forty pounds. However, students pay twenty.
    票通常要四十英鎊。不過學生付二十英鎊就好。

③ also + 一般動詞

  1. I also bought a new pair of jeans.
    我也買了一條新的牛仔褲。
  2. She plays the piano. She also sings.
    她會彈鋼琴。她也會唱歌。
  3. We visited the castle, and we also went to the museum.
    我們參觀了城堡，也去了博物館。
  4. My dad also works on Saturdays.
    我爸星期六也要上班。
  5. They also want to come with us.
    他們也想跟我們一起去。
  6. I also need some milk.
    我也需要一些牛奶。

④ be + also、助動詞 + also

  1. My mother is a nurse. My aunt is also a nurse.
    我媽是護士。我阿姨也是護士。
  2. The hotel is cheap. It's also near the station.
    那家旅館很便宜。它也在車站附近。
  3. I can also speak a little Spanish.
    我也會說一點西班牙文。
  4. You should also bring a coat.
    你也應該帶件外套。
  5. We were also very tired.
    我們也很累。

⑤ so、however、also 在同一段話裡

  1. The train was late, so I missed the meeting. I also missed lunch.
    火車誤點了，所以我錯過了會議。我也錯過了午餐。
  2. The flat is small. However, it's cheap, so I'm going to take it.
    那間公寓很小。不過很便宜，所以我要租下來。
  3. I don't like coffee, so I usually drink tea. However, I sometimes have a latte.
    我不喜歡咖啡，所以我通常喝茶。不過我有時候會喝拿鐵。
  4. It was sunny. However, it was also windy, so we didn't go sailing.
    天氣晴朗。不過風也很大，所以我們沒去開帆船。
