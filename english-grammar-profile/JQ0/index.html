so 前⁠面是原⁠因，後⁠面是結⁠果

I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

我不喜歡綠色，所以我決定用藍色。

however 開⁠始一⁠個新⁠的句⁠子，後⁠面加逗⁠號

那家旅館很舊。不過房間很乾淨。

however 比 but 正式。

also 放⁠在一⁠般動⁠詞前⁠面，或放⁠在 be 和助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

一般動詞前面

I bought a jacket. I also bought some socks.

我買了一件夾克。我也買了幾雙襪子。
be 後面

She is also a teacher.

她也是老師。
助動詞後面

I can also speak a little Spanish.

我也會說一點西班牙文。

however 不⁠和 and 一⁠起用

It was cold, and however we went swimming.

It was cold. However, we went swimming.

because 和 so 不⁠放⁠在同⁠一⁠句

I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

Because my brother had a cold, so he stayed in bed.

My brother had a cold, so he stayed in bed.

also 不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I like also tennis.

I also like tennis.

also 不⁠放⁠在 be 前⁠面

She also is a doctor.

She is also a doctor.

so 後⁠面⁠接結⁠果，because 後⁠面⁠接原⁠因

I don't like green, so I decided to use blue.

so
結果

The bus was late, so I took a taxi.

公車誤點，所以我搭了計程車。
because
原因

I took a taxi because the bus was late.

我搭了計程車，因為公車誤點。

but 連⁠接同⁠一⁠句⁠裡⁠的子⁠句，however 開⁠始新⁠的句⁠子

but

The test was hard, but I passed.

考試很難，可是我過了。
however

The questions were difficult. However, everyone in my class passed.

題目很難。不過我們班每個人都過了。

例句

① 子句 + , so + 子句

  1. There was nothing in the fridge except bread and cheese. I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.
    冰箱裡只有麵包和起司。我餓了，所以做了一個三明治。
  2. The last train leaves at midnight. It's late, so let's go home.
    最後一班火車半夜開。很晚了，我們回家吧。
  3. Sorry about last night. My phone was dead, so I couldn't call you.
    昨晚的事很抱歉。我的手機沒電了，所以沒辦法打給你。
  4. We wanted to buy bread at the bakery. The shop was closed, so we went to the market.
    我們想在麵包店買麵包。那家店沒開，所以我們去了市場。
  5. Lucy has a fever. She's ill, so she can't come to the party.
    Lucy 發燒了。她生病了，所以不能來派對。
  6. The buses only run every hour. We missed the bus, so we took a taxi.
    公車一個小時才一班。我們錯過了公車，所以搭了計程車。
  7. Nothing happened for the first hour. The film was boring, so I fell asleep.
    第一個小時什麼事都沒發生。電影很無聊，所以我睡著了。
  8. I'm a student with a part-time job. I don't have much money, so I usually cook at home.
    我是有兼職工作的學生。我錢不多，所以通常在家煮飯。

② 句子。However, + 句子。

  1. I bought three shirts at the market. The clothes were cheap. However, they were good quality.
    我在市場買了三件襯衫。那些衣服很便宜。不過品質很好。
  2. I only have one room. My flat is small. However, it's very close to work.
    我只有一個房間。我的公寓很小。不過離公司很近。
  3. We packed our swimsuits on Saturday. We wanted to go to the beach. However, it rained all day.
    我們星期六帶了泳衣。我們本來想去海邊。不過下了一整天的雨。
  4. We waited forty minutes for dessert. The food was great. However, the service was slow.
    我們的甜點等了四十分鐘。食物很好吃。不過服務很慢。
  5. Tom studied every night for a month. He studied hard. However, he didn't pass the test.
    Tom 一個月每天晚上都在讀書。他很用功。不過考試沒過。
  6. I've worked at the bank for ten years. I like my job. However, I want to try something new.
    我在銀行工作了十年。我喜歡我的工作。不過我想嘗試新的東西。
  7. Show your student card at the door. Tickets usually cost forty pounds. However, students pay twenty.
    在門口出示學生證。票通常要四十英鎊。不過學生付二十英鎊就好。

③ also + 一般動詞

  1. I went shopping for a shirt for the wedding. I also bought a new pair of jeans.
    我去買婚禮要穿的襯衫。我也買了一條新的牛仔褲。
  2. Mia is in the school band. She plays the piano. She also sings.
    Mia 在學校樂團。她會彈鋼琴。她也會唱歌。
  3. We had a great day in Edinburgh. We visited the castle, and we also went to the museum.
    我們在愛丁堡玩得很開心。我們參觀了城堡，也去了博物館。
  4. My mum works from Monday to Friday. My dad also works on Saturdays.
    我媽星期一到星期五上班。我爸星期六也要上班。
  5. I told my cousins about the picnic. They also want to come with us.
    我跟我表弟妹說了野餐的事。他們也想跟我們一起去。
  6. Can you get some bread? I also need some milk.
    你可以買一些麵包嗎？我也需要一些牛奶。

④ be + also、助動詞 + also

  1. My dad is a teacher. My mum is also a teacher.
    我爸爸是老師。我媽媽也是老師。
  2. You should book the Park Hotel. It's cheap. It's also near the station.
    你應該訂 Park 飯店。它很便宜。它也在車站附近。
  3. I speak English and French. I can also speak a little Spanish.
    我會說英文和法文。我也會說一點西班牙文。
  4. Don't forget your hat and gloves. You should also bring a coat.
    別忘了帶帽子和手套。你也應該帶件外套。
  5. The kids were hungry after the long walk. We were also very tired.
    走了很遠的路，孩子們都餓了。我們也很累。

⑤ so、however、also 在同一段話裡

  1. The train was late, so I missed the meeting. I also missed lunch.
    火車誤點了，所以我錯過了會議。我也錯過了午餐。
  2. The flat is small. However, it's cheap, so I'm going to take it.
    那間公寓很小。不過很便宜，所以我要租下來。
  3. I don't like coffee, so I usually drink tea. However, I sometimes have a latte.
    我不喜歡咖啡，所以我通常喝茶。不過我有時候會喝拿鐵。
  4. It was sunny. However, it was also windy, so we didn't go sailing.
    天氣晴朗。不過風也很大，所以我們沒去開帆船。
