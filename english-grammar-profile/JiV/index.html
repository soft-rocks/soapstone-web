Might I suggest that we have more exciting publicity for the club?

我可以建議我們俱樂部做些更吸引人的宣傳嗎？

Might I suggest + (that) + 子⁠句？

Might I suggest我可以建議……嗎？
Might it not be wise / better……難道不是比較明智／比較好嗎？
子句或不定詞建議的內容。

正⁠式⁠的信⁠件

例句

Might I suggest that you arrange better seats?

我可以建議您安排更好的座位嗎？

這個說法很正式。常用在寫給主管、主辦單位或公司的信。

suggest 後⁠面⁠接 ⁠-⁠ing 或 that 子⁠句，不⁠接 to

Might I suggest that we have more exciting publicity for the club?

Might I suggest to change the date?

Might I suggest changing the date?

沒⁠有縮⁠寫⁠的時⁠候，not 放⁠在 it 後⁠面

Might not it be wise to wait?

Might it not be wise to wait?

suggest 後⁠面⁠接 that 子⁠句，不⁠接 you to

Might I suggest that we have more exciting publicity for the club?

Might I suggest you to book early?

Might I suggest that you book early?

例句

① Might I suggest that + 子句？：建議改進

  1. Might I suggest that we meet in a bigger room next term?
    我可以建議我們下學期在大一點的教室聚會嗎？
  2. For the musicals in London, might I suggest that you book seats where people can both see and hear?
    倫敦的音樂劇，我可以建議您訂看得到也聽得到的座位嗎？
  3. Might I suggest that the meetings start a little later?
    我可以建議聚會晚一點開始嗎？
  4. Might I suggest that you check the facts before you publish them?
    我可以建議您發表之前先查證事實嗎？
  5. Might I suggest that the menu includes some vegetarian dishes?
    我可以建議菜單加一些素食料理嗎？
  6. Might I suggest that staff get more training on the new system?
    我可以建議員工多接受一些新系統的訓練嗎？

② Might it not be + 形容詞 + if / to + …？：委婉的批評

  1. Last but not least, might it not be wise if you organised more trips?
    最後一點，你們多辦一些旅行難道不是比較明智嗎？
  2. Might it not be better to answer customers' emails more quickly?
    更快回覆顧客的電子郵件難道不是比較好嗎？
  3. Might it not be fairer to give everyone the same amount of time?
    給每個人一樣多的時間難道不是比較公平嗎？
  4. Might it not be wise to test the software before selling it?
    軟體賣出之前先測試，難道不是比較明智嗎？
  5. Might it not be kinder to tell her the truth?
    告訴她真相難道不是比較厚道嗎？
  6. Might it not be sensible to keep the shop open on Saturdays?
    星期六讓店開著難道不是比較合理嗎？

③ Might I suggest + -ing？

  1. Might I suggest adding a map to the website?
    我可以建議在網站上加一張地圖嗎？
  2. Might I suggest using larger print for the older readers?
    我可以建議為年長的讀者用大一點的字嗎？
  3. Might I suggest putting more bins in the park?
    我可以建議在公園多放一些垃圾桶嗎？
  4. Might I suggest asking the students what they want?
    我可以建議問問學生想要什麼嗎？
  5. Might I suggest offering a discount to regular customers?
    我可以建議給常客打個折嗎？
  6. Might I suggest moving the concert to a bigger hall?
    我可以建議把音樂會移到大一點的廳嗎？

④ Might I suggest + 名詞？

  1. Might I suggest a few changes to your report?
    我可以建議您的報告做幾個修改嗎？
  2. Might I suggest a better place for the notice board?
    我可以建議一個更好的位置放公告欄嗎？
  3. Might I suggest a shorter speech next time?
    我可以建議下次演講短一點嗎？
  4. Might I suggest a different colour for the posters?
    我可以建議海報換一個顏色嗎？
  5. Might I suggest a quieter room for the exam?
    我可以建議考試換一間安靜一點的教室嗎？
  6. Might I suggest a clearer title for the article?
    我可以建議文章換一個清楚一點的標題嗎？

⑤ Might you not + 原形動詞？：委婉地說對方可以做得更好

  1. Might you not ask the customers before changing the menu?
    您改菜單之前，難道不能先問問顧客嗎？
  2. Might you not give the new staff a little more time?
    您難道不能多給新員工一點時間嗎？
  3. Might you not explain the rules more clearly next time?
    您下次難道不能把規定解釋得清楚一點嗎？
  4. Might you not consider a cheaper venue for the party?
    您難道不考慮找一個便宜一點的派對場地嗎？
  5. Might you not reply to complaints a little faster?
    您回覆投訴難道不能快一點嗎？
  6. Might you not invite parents to the next meeting?
    您難道不能邀請家長參加下一次會議嗎？
  7. Might you not put the timetable on the website?
    您難道不能把時間表放在網站上嗎？
