See one of them and you'll want to see them all.

只要看過其中一個，你就會想把它們全部看完。

祈⁠使⁠句說條⁠件，and 接結⁠果

See one of them and you'll want to see them all.

好好讀書，你就會考過。

等於 If you study hard, you'll pass the exam.

用 or 時，不⁠這⁠樣做就⁠會有壞結⁠果

See one of them and you'll want to see them all.

Wear a hat, or you'll get sunburnt.

戴頂帽子，不然你會曬傷。

祈⁠使⁠句表⁠示條⁠件⁠時，用 and 接結⁠果

See one of them and you'll want to see them all.

Try this cake, then you'll love it.

Try this cake and you'll love it.

祈⁠使⁠句用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞開⁠頭

See one of them and you'll want to see them all.

Practicing every day and you'll get better.

Practice every day and you'll get better.

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

See one of them and you'll want to see them all.

Take the early bus and you'll arrived on time.

Take the early bus and you'll arrive on time.

例句

① 祈使句 + and + you'll

  1. Read one of her books and you'll want to read them all.
    只要讀過她的一本書，你就會想把全部都讀完。
  2. The doctor visited my sick grandfather. Eat well and you'll feel better soon.
    醫生來看我生病的爺爺。好好吃飯，你很快就會好起來。
  3. You don't need hours for English. Read for ten minutes a day and you'll learn a lot.
    學英文不需要花好幾個小時。每天讀十分鐘，你就會學到很多。
  4. My aunt makes the best pasta. Taste this sauce and you'll want the recipe.
    我阿姨做的義大利麵最好吃。嚐嚐這個醬汁，你一定會想要食譜。
  5. The main road is full of traffic. Take this road and you'll get there in ten minutes.
    大馬路塞滿了車。走這條路，你十分鐘就會到。
  6. Ken knows all the bus times. Ask him and he'll tell you.
    Ken 知道所有公車的時間。問他，他就會告訴你。

② 祈使句 + and + 其他結果

  1. You're nearly there. Turn left at the bank and you'll see the hotel.
    你快到了。在銀行左轉，你就會看到飯店。
  2. The new waiter is slow, but he learns fast. Give him a chance and he'll surprise you.
    新服務生動作很慢，可是他學得很快。給他一個機會，他會讓你驚訝的。
  3. My grandmother often said this to us. Smile and the world smiles with you.
    我奶奶常常跟我們說這句話。你笑，全世界都會跟著你笑。
  4. The door to our building is locked at night. Push the button and the door will open.
    我們大樓的門晚上會鎖。按這個按鈕，門就會開。
  5. The man at the flower shop gave me some advice. Water the plant once a week and it'll grow fast.
    花店的老闆給了我一些建議。這盆植物一週澆一次水，就會長得很快。

③ Spend / Give / Add ... and ...

  1. Spend a week in Kyoto and you'll never want to leave.
    在京都待一個禮拜，你就會再也不想走了。
  2. The soup is a bit boring. Add a little salt and it'll taste much better.
    這湯有點淡。加一點鹽，它就會好喝很多。
  3. You don't need expensive toys at the beach. Give the kids a ball and they'll play for hours.
    去海邊不需要貴的玩具。給孩子們一顆球，他們就能玩好幾個小時。
  4. Everyone loves this little island. Visit once and you'll come back every year.
    大家都愛這座小島。去過一次，你就會每年都想回去。
  5. It's too hot to sleep in here. Leave the window open and the room will cool down.
    這裡熱到睡不著。把窗戶開著，房間就會涼下來。

④ 祈使句 + or + 結果

  1. Your brother is about to swim. Run, or you'll miss the start of the race.
    你哥哥要游了。快跑，不然你會錯過比賽開始。
  2. Your flight is at seven in the morning. Set an alarm or you'll oversleep.
    你的班機早上七點起飛。設個鬧鐘，不然你會睡過頭。
  3. It's very windy today. Wear a coat or you'll catch a cold.
    今天風很大。穿件外套，不然你會感冒。
  4. Hold the rail on these stairs or you'll slip.
    走這些樓梯要扶著欄杆，不然你會滑倒。
  5. We're eating outside today. Cover the cake or the flies will get to it.
    我們今天在外面吃。把蛋糕蓋起來，不然蒼蠅會飛上去。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Don't call my sister fat. Say that again and I'll be very angry.
    別說我妹妹胖。你再說一次，我就要生氣了。
  2. The teacher saw the boy kick the door. Do that once more and you'll be in trouble.
    老師看到那個男孩踢門。你再那樣做一次，就麻煩了。
  3. The bakery has an offer on cakes this week. Buy two and you'll get one free.
    麵包店這星期蛋糕有優惠。買兩個，就送一個。
  4. The radio advert for the new gym says this. Call before ten and you'll get a discount.
    新健身房的電台廣告這樣說。十點以前打電話，就能打折。
  5. The guards at the stadium check everyone. Show your ticket and they'll let you in.
    體育場的警衛會檢查每個人。出示你的票，他們就會讓你進去。
  6. We're in the middle of the forest now. Listen carefully and you'll hear the birds.
    我們現在在森林中間。仔細聽，你就會聽到鳥叫聲。
  7. The project ends in March. Work hard now and you'll have more time later.
    這個專案三月結束。現在努力工作，以後你就會有更多時間。
  8. The hospital is a big building. Follow the signs and you won't get lost.
    醫院是一棟很大的建築。跟著指標走，你就不會迷路。
  9. Happy birthday, darling! Open the box and you'll find a surprise.
    親愛的，生日快樂！打開盒子，你會看到一個驚喜。
