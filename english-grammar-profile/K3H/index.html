例句

① was / were + 過去分詞 + by：過去的事

  1. My favourite book is Anna Karenina. It was written by an excellent author, Leo Tolstoy.
    我最喜歡的書是《安娜·卡列尼娜》。它是一位很優秀的作家 Leo Tolstoy 寫的。
  2. I love this bike. It was bought by my uncle for my birthday.
    我很愛這台腳踏車。是我叔叔買給我當生日禮物的。
  3. Look at this photo of the beach. It was taken by my sister.
    你看這張海灘的照片。是我姊姊拍的。
  4. We stayed in an old hotel in Rome. It was built by a rich family in 1890.
    我們住在羅馬一間老旅館。那是一個有錢人家在 1890 年蓋的。
  5. Have you seen the painting in our hall? It was painted by my grandmother.
    你看過我們走廊上那幅畫嗎？那是我外婆畫的。
  6. The cake at the party was great. It was made by Tom's mum.
    派對上的蛋糕很好吃。是 Tom 的媽媽做的。
  7. Our school has two new computer rooms. They were paid for by a local company.
    我們學校有兩間新的電腦教室。是本地一家公司出的錢。
  8. The song at the end of the film was beautiful. It was sung by a girl from my town.
    電影最後那首歌很好聽。是我們鎮上一個女生唱的。
  9. I found an old letter in the attic. It was written by my great-grandfather.
    我在閣樓找到一封舊信。是我曾祖父寫的。
  10. We can't open the shop today. The windows at the front were broken by the storm last night.
    我們今天不能開店。前面的窗戶昨晚被暴風雨打破了。

② is / are + 過去分詞 + by：現在的事

  1. I've got a new phone. It's made by Sony.
    我買了一支新手機。是 Sony 做的。
  2. My mum watches a cooking show every Sunday. It's presented by a famous chef.
    我媽媽每個星期天都看一個烹飪節目。節目是一位有名的廚師主持的。
  3. Our town has a small museum. It's visited by thousands of tourists every summer.
    我們鎮上有一座小博物館。每年夏天有好幾千名遊客來參觀。
  4. I joined a chess club last month. It's run by two retired teachers.
    我上個月參加了一個西洋棋社。社團是兩位退休老師在經營的。
  5. I bought these bags at the market. They're made by women in a small village in Peru.
    我在市集買了這些包包。它們是秘魯一個小村莊裡的婦女做的。
  6. Look at the name on our new shirts. Our football team is sponsored by the bakery on the corner.
    你看我們新球衣上的名字。我們的足球隊是轉角那家麵包店贊助的。
  7. We always get a discount at the shop downstairs. It's owned by my best friend's parents.
    我們在樓下那家店買東西都有折扣。那家店是我最好朋友的爸媽開的。
  8. The food in this restaurant is very fresh. Most of the vegetables are grown by local farmers.
    這家餐廳的食物很新鮮。大部分的蔬菜是本地農夫種的。

③ 主詞是前面提過的名詞

  1. We watched Romeo and Juliet at the theatre. The play was written by Shakespeare.
    我們在劇院看了《羅密歐與茱麗葉》。這齣戲是莎士比亞寫的。
  2. Next to the bridge there's a tall statue. The statue was designed by a student.
    橋旁邊有一座很高的雕像。這座雕像是一個學生設計的。
  3. My brother gave me a book about space. The book was signed by the writer.
    我哥哥送我一本講太空的書。這本書有作者的簽名。
  4. There's a new café on Main Street. The café is run by two sisters from Italy.
    大街上開了一家新的咖啡店。這家店是兩個從義大利來的姊妹在經營的。
  5. We have a small garden behind the house. The garden is looked after by my dad.
    我們家後面有一個小花園。花園是我爸爸在照顧的。

④ by 後面接東西

  1. There was a big storm last night. The old tree in the park was hit by lightning.
    昨晚有一場大暴風雨。公園裡那棵老樹被閃電打到了。
  2. My grandparents couldn't leave their house for days. The village was surrounded by water after the heavy rain.
    我爺爺奶奶好幾天不能出門。大雨過後，村子四周都是水。
  3. Most of the buildings here are new. Half the town was destroyed by a fire in 1850.
    這裡大部分的建築都是新的。這個鎮有一半在 1850 年的一場火災裡燒毀了。
  4. The road to the beach was blocked by snow, so we went home.
    往海灘的路被雪擋住了，所以我們就回家了。

⑤ 問答

  1. "Who made this lovely dress?" "It was made by my aunt."
    這件漂亮的洋裝是誰做的？是我阿姨做的。
  2. "That's a great picture. Who took it?" "It was taken by a friend of mine."
    這張照片拍得真好。誰拍的？我一個朋友拍的。
  3. "Do you know who designed this building?" "Yes, it was designed by a Japanese architect."
    你知道這棟樓是誰設計的嗎？知道，是一位日本建築師設計的。
  4. "Who's going to drive us?" "We're being picked up by Lucy's dad."
    誰要載我們？Lucy 的爸爸會來接我們。
