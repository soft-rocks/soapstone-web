He told me about himself and how he started playing the guitar.

他跟我聊他自己的事，還有他是怎麼開始彈吉他的。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞就⁠是主⁠詞，用反⁠身代⁠名⁠詞

他跟我聊他自己的事，還有他是怎麼開始彈吉他的。

myself、yourself、himself、herself 跟⁠著主⁠詞

He told me about himself and how he started playing the guitar.

myself

I'm really proud of myself for passing the test.

我很為自己通過考試感到驕傲。
yourself

Take care of yourself while you're travelling.

旅行的時候要照顧好自己。
himself

The old man was talking to himself on the bus.

公車上那個老人在自言自語。
herself

She looked at herself in the mirror and smiled.

她看著鏡子裡的自己笑了。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞是別⁠人，用受格

He told me about himself and how he started playing the guitar.

主詞自己

He bought a present for himself.

他買了一份禮物給自己。
別人

He bought a present for her.

他買了一份禮物給她。

說位⁠置⁠的介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面通⁠常用受格

He told me about himself and how he started playing the guitar.

next to

She put her bag next to her.

她把包包放在身邊。
behind

He closed the door behind him.

他隨手把門關上。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞就⁠是主⁠詞 I，用 myself

He told me about himself and how he started playing the guitar.

I was angry with me for losing my keys.

I was angry with myself for losing my keys.

說位⁠置⁠的 behind 後⁠面用受格

He shut the gate behind himself.

He shut the gate behind him.

祈⁠使⁠句⁠的主⁠詞是 you，用 yourself

He told me about himself and how he started playing the guitar.

Take care of you!

Take care of yourself!

例句

① myself

  1. I bought a coffee for myself and a tea for my mum.
    我幫自己買了咖啡，幫我媽買了茶。
  2. I was so angry with myself for forgetting her birthday.
    我好氣自己忘了她的生日。
  3. I don't like talking about myself in job interviews.
    我不喜歡在工作面試時談自己。
  4. After a long week, I need some time for myself.
    忙了一整週，我需要一些自己的時間。
  5. I couldn't stop laughing at myself when I watched the video.
    我看那段影片時一直忍不住笑自己。
  6. I kept the secret to myself for years.
    這個秘密我藏在心裡好幾年。
  7. I have to believe in myself a bit more.
    我得更相信自己一點。
  8. I made a promise to myself to read more this year.
    我答應過自己今年要多讀點書。

② yourself

  1. Take care of yourself while you're travelling.
    旅行的時候要照顧好自己。
  2. Tell me something about yourself.
    跟我說說你自己的事。
  3. You should feel proud of yourself after all that hard work.
    那麼努力之後，你應該為自己感到驕傲。
  4. Stop being so hard on yourself; everyone makes mistakes.
    別再對自己那麼嚴格了，每個人都會犯錯。
  5. Remember to save some of your salary for yourself, too.
    記得也要把一部分薪水留給你自己。
  6. Be honest with yourself: do you really want this job?
    對自己誠實一點：你真的想要這份工作嗎？
  7. You only ever think about yourself!
    你永遠只想到你自己！
  8. Keep this information to yourself, please.
    這個消息請你不要告訴別人。

③ himself

  1. He told me about himself and his family over lunch.
    吃午餐時，他跟我聊他自己和他家人的事。
  2. My son is very pleased with himself today.
    我兒子今天對自己很滿意。
  3. He made a cup of tea for himself and sat down.
    他幫自己泡了杯茶，然後坐下來。
  4. Tom felt sorry for himself after the breakup.
    分手後，Tom 覺得自己很可憐。
  5. He never spends money on himself.
    他從來不花錢在自己身上。
  6. My grandfather can still look after himself.
    我爺爺還能照顧自己。
  7. He wrote a note to himself so he wouldn't forget.
    他寫了張紙條給自己，免得忘記。
  8. The new manager talked about himself for the whole meeting.
    新經理整場會議都在談他自己。

④ herself

  1. She was very proud of herself after the race.
    比賽後，她很為自己感到驕傲。
  2. My sister bought a new laptop for herself.
    我姊姊幫自己買了一台新筆電。
  3. She looked at herself in the mirror and smiled.
    她看著鏡子裡的自己笑了。
  4. Lisa doesn't like talking about herself.
    Lisa 不喜歡談自己的事。
  5. My mum never has any time for herself.
    我媽從來沒有自己的時間。
  6. She was annoyed with herself for being late again.
    她氣自己又遲到了。
  7. Anna is learning to stand up for herself at work.
    Anna 正在學著在職場上為自己發聲。
  8. Grandma knitted a warm scarf for herself.
    奶奶幫自己織了一條溫暖的圍巾。
