I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

我以為我一定會被炒魷魚。可是經理原諒了我。

And yet 放⁠在第⁠二句開⁠頭

I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

我以為我一定會被炒魷魚。可是經理原諒了我。

And yet 後⁠面可⁠以加逗⁠號

I would probably make the same mistakes as my parents. And yet, I think I would be a good father.

我大概會犯和我爸媽一樣的錯。可是我想，我會是個好爸爸。

And yet 和 But

I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

But一般的對比
And yet強調出乎意料，語氣比 But 強
Yet單獨放在句首，比 And yet 正式

And yet 帶⁠出出⁠乎⁠意⁠料⁠的結⁠果或相⁠反⁠的看⁠法

I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

出乎意料的結果

I felt very embarrassed. And yet nobody laughed at me.

我覺得很丟臉。可是沒有人笑我。
相反的看法

The job is hard. And yet I wouldn't change it for anything.

這份工作很辛苦。可是我說什麼都不會換。

And 後⁠面⁠接 yet，不⁠接 but

I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

We trained hard. And but we lost the match.

We trained hard. And yet we lost the match.

And yet 後⁠面不⁠再⁠加 but

I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

The test was easy. And yet but many students failed.

The test was easy. And yet many students failed.

後⁠面⁠接出⁠乎⁠意⁠料⁠的事

It was raining. And yet we took our umbrellas.

It was raining. And yet we went for a walk without umbrellas.

yet 放⁠在 And 後⁠面

I was sure I would get the sack. And yet the manager forgave me.

I was tired. And I couldn't sleep yet.

I was tired. And yet I couldn't sleep.

例句

① 說出出乎意料的結果

  1. I felt very embarrassed and I was sure I would lose my job. And yet the manager forgave me and I stayed.
    我覺得很丟臉，也以為自己一定會丟掉工作。可是經理原諒了我，我就留了下來。
  2. The road was icy and the car was old. And yet we arrived without any problems.
    路上結冰，車子又舊。可是我們一路平安到了。
  3. Nobody in the class expected her to pass. And yet she got the top mark.
    班上沒有人以為她會及格。可是她拿了最高分。
  4. The restaurant had terrible reviews online. And yet the food was delicious.
    那家餐廳網路上的評價很差。可是東西很好吃。
  5. He had never played the piano in public before. And yet he gave a perfect performance.
    他以前從來沒在大家面前彈過鋼琴。可是他的演出很完美。

② 說出相反的看法

  1. The job is stressful and the hours are long. And yet I wouldn't change it for anything.
    這份工作壓力大，工時又長。可是我說什麼都不會換。
  2. Living in a big city is expensive. And yet I can't imagine living anywhere else.
    住在大城市很花錢。可是我想不出要住在別的地方。
  3. Learning Chinese takes years. And yet it is one of the most rewarding things I have done.
    學中文要花好幾年。可是這是我做過最有收穫的事之一。
  4. I know he has made many mistakes. And yet I still trust him.
    我知道他犯過很多錯。可是我還是相信他。
  5. The town is small and quiet. And yet there is always something to do.
    這個小鎮又小又安靜。可是總是有事情可以做。

③ And yet, + 句子

  1. I would probably make the same mistakes as my own parents did. And yet, I think I would be a loving father.
    我大概會犯和我爸媽一樣的錯。可是我想，我會是個有愛心的爸爸。
  2. The evidence against him seemed very strong. And yet, the jury found him not guilty.
    對他不利的證據看起來很有力。然而，陪審團判他無罪。
  3. We had planned everything carefully. And yet, something still went wrong on the day.
    我們什麼都仔細計畫好了。可是，當天還是出了差錯。
  4. The two sisters look almost the same. And yet, their personalities are completely different.
    這兩姊妹長得幾乎一樣。可是，她們的個性完全不同。

④ 故事和日記裡的句子

  1. I waited at the station for two hours. And yet she never came.
    我在車站等了兩個小時。可是她始終沒來。
  2. The house was empty for years. And yet the garden was full of flowers.
    那棟房子空了好幾年。可是花園裡開滿了花。
  3. We had argued all evening. And yet, when he left, I missed him immediately.
    我們吵了一整個晚上。可是他一走，我馬上就想他了。
  4. The letter was short and simple. And yet it made me cry.
    那封信又短又簡單。可是它讓我哭了。
  5. I had walked past that shop every day. And yet I had never noticed its name.
    我每天都走過那家店。可是我從來沒注意過它的店名。

⑤ 正式文章裡的句子

  1. The country has rich natural resources. And yet most of its people remain poor.
    這個國家有豐富的天然資源。然而大部分的人民仍然很窮。
  2. Doctors have warned about the dangers of smoking for decades. And yet millions of people still smoke.
    醫生警告吸菸的危險已經幾十年了。然而還是有幾百萬人吸菸。
  3. The project received large amounts of public money. And yet very little has been achieved.
    這個計畫拿了大筆的公帑。然而成果很少。
  4. Technology has made communication easier than ever. And yet many people feel lonely.
    科技讓溝通變得前所未有地容易。然而很多人覺得孤單。
  5. The government promised to build new homes. And yet house prices continue to rise.
    政府答應要蓋新房子。然而房價還在上漲。

⑥ 對話裡的句子

  1. "You said you hated camping." "I know. And yet here I am in a tent."
    你說過你很討厭露營。我知道。可是我現在就在帳篷裡。
  2. "He's so rude to everyone." "And yet everyone seems to like him."
    他對每個人都很沒禮貌。可是大家好像都很喜歡他。
  3. "The film got bad reviews." "And yet it made millions at the box office."
    那部電影評價很差。可是票房賺了幾百萬。
  4. "She's only sixteen." "And yet she speaks four languages."
    她才十六歲。可是她會說四種語言。

⑦ And yet 和 But 的比較

  1. The hotel was expensive. But the rooms were nice.
    那家飯店很貴。不過房間很好。
  2. The hotel was cheap and far from the centre. And yet it was the best part of our trip.
    那家飯店很便宜，又離市中心很遠。可是它是我們這趟旅行最棒的部分。
  3. The exam was hard. Yet everyone in the class passed.
    考試很難。然而全班都及格了。
