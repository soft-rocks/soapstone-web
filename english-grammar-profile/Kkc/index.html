She's the funniest person I've ever known!

她是我認識的人裡面最好笑的！

完⁠成⁠式子⁠句說⁠明比⁠較⁠的範⁠圍是說⁠話⁠的人⁠的經⁠驗

她是我認識的人裡面最好笑的！

子句前面省略了 that。

經⁠驗算到現⁠在用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式，算到過⁠去用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

現在完成式
算到現在

It's the best book I've ever read.

這是我讀過最好看的書。
過去完成式
算到過去

It was the best decision he had ever made.

那是他做過最好的決定。

that 可⁠以省⁠略，ever 可⁠以換⁠成時⁠間範⁠圍

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

這是我今年看過最爛的電影。

the first 和 the only 也接完⁠成⁠式子⁠句

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

這是我第一次來倫敦。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句用現⁠在⁠式，子⁠句用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

This is the best pizza I ever ate.

This is the best pizza I've ever eaten.

最⁠高⁠級加名⁠詞，前⁠面要⁠有 the

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

He's kindest man I've ever met.

He's the kindest man I've ever met.

名⁠詞已⁠經是子⁠句⁠的受⁠詞，子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

It's the best film I've ever seen it.

It's the best film I've ever seen.

ever 放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

She's the funniest person I've ever known!

She's the nicest teacher I've had ever.

She's the nicest teacher I've ever had.

例句

① 現在式 + 最高級名詞 + I've ever + 過去分詞

  1. You have to meet Kate. She's the funniest person I've ever known!
    你一定要認識 Kate。她是我認識的人裡面最好笑的！
  2. Thanks, Mum. This is the best cake I've ever tasted.
    謝謝媽，這是我吃過最好吃的蛋糕。
  3. Look at that water! It's the clearest sea I've ever swum in.
    你看那片水！這是我游過最清澈的海。
  4. That's the strangest question anyone has ever asked me.
    那是別人問過我最奇怪的問題。
  5. He's the most generous man I've ever met.
    他是我遇過最慷慨的人。
  6. This is the worst coffee I've ever had.
    這是我喝過最難喝的咖啡。

② 現在式 + 最高級名詞 + 其他主詞 + have ever + 過去分詞

  1. Brighton has the best promenade we have ever seen.
    布萊頓有我們看過最棒的海濱步道。
  2. That was the hardest exam our class has ever taken.
    那是我們班考過最難的考試。
  3. Mrs Hall is the most patient teacher my son has ever had.
    Hall 老師是我兒子遇過最有耐心的老師。
  4. This is the coldest winter they've ever had here.
    這是他們這裡遇過最冷的冬天。

③ 過去式 + 最高級名詞 + had ever + 過去分詞

  1. It was the best decision you had ever made.
    那是你做過最好的決定。
  2. It was the biggest fish he had ever caught.
    那是他釣過最大的魚。
  3. When my dad was in hospital, he said Ana was the kindest nurse he had ever known.
    我爸住院的時候，他說 Ana 是他遇過最親切的護士。
  4. That was the longest walk the children had ever done.
    那是孩子們走過最長的一段路。
  5. It was the most expensive meal they had ever eaten.
    那是他們吃過最貴的一餐。

④ 最高級名詞 + 子句 + 時間範圍

  1. Don't bother with that one. It's the dullest film I've watched this year.
    別看那部了，那是我今年看過最無聊的電影。
  2. That's the best holiday we've had in years.
    那是我們這幾年來最棒的一次假期。
  3. It was the hottest day I'd experienced in my life.
    那是我這輩子經歷過最熱的一天。
  4. This is the busiest week I've had since I started this job.
    這是我開始做這份工作以來最忙的一週。

⑤ 加了 that 的子句

  1. The best book that I've read is The Da Vinci Code.
    我讀過最好看的書是《達文西密碼》。
  2. It's the most interesting city that I've ever visited.
    那是我去過最有意思的城市。
  3. He's the tallest player that I've ever seen.
    他是我看過最高的球員。

⑥ 最高級名詞片語當主詞

  1. The best concert I've ever been to was in Berlin.
    我聽過最棒的演唱會是在柏林。
  2. The scariest film I've ever watched was about a hotel.
    我看過最恐怖的電影是講一家飯店的。
  3. The nicest people I've ever met live in this village.
    我遇過最好的人都住在這個村子。
  4. The most useful thing I've ever bought is this bag.
    我買過最實用的東西就是這個包包。

⑦ the first / the only + 名詞 + 完成式子句

  1. It's the first time I've ever been on a plane.
    這是我第一次搭飛機。
  2. You're the only person I've told about this.
    你是我唯一說過這件事的人。
  3. It was the first time she had seen snow.
    那是她第一次看到雪。
  4. Teaching is the only job my sister has ever wanted to do.
    教書是我姊姊唯一想做的工作。
  5. It's the first time we've stayed in such a big hotel.
    這是我們第一次住這麼大的飯店。
