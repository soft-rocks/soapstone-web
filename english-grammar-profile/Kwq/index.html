My town is growing very fast. In twenty years' time it will be bigger than it is now.

我覺得 20 年後，我住的小鎮會比現在大，因為它發展得很快。

主⁠詞 + will + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

will、I think、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

will會。預測未來。
I think說這是自己的看法。
原形動詞預測的事。

附⁠加問⁠句 won't + 代⁠名⁠詞？

例句

Without exercise you'll soon put on weight, won't you?

不運動的話，你很快就會變胖，對吧？

預測後面常加 won't + 代名詞？，請對方同意。

it'll 和 you'll

My town is growing very fast. In twenty years' time it will be bigger than it is now.

例句

It'll be harder to meet your old friends.

要見到老朋友會比較難。

口語常把 it will 縮寫成 it'll，you will 縮寫成 you'll。

前⁠面是肯⁠定⁠句，附⁠加問⁠句用 won't

You'll put on weight, will you?

You'll put on weight, won't you?

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

My town is growing very fast. In twenty years' time it will be bigger than it is now.

The town will growing bigger.

The town will grow bigger.

will 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

My town is growing very fast. In twenty years' time it will be bigger than it is now.

It will to be harder.

It will be harder.

例句

① I think + 主詞 + will + 原形動詞：說自己的預測

  1. Lots of families are moving here. I think that in twenty years' time my town will be much bigger than it is now.
    很多家庭搬來這裡。我想二十年後，我的家鄉會比現在大很多。
  2. I think people will work from home more and more.
    我想人們會越來越常在家工作。
  3. I think the price of petrol will go up again next year.
    我想明年油價會再上漲。
  4. I think our team will win the league this season.
    我想我們隊這一季會贏得聯賽冠軍。
  5. I think robots will do most of the housework in the future.
    我想以後機器人會做大部分的家事。
  6. I think it will snow tonight. The sky looks very grey.
    我想今天晚上會下雪。天空看起來很灰。

② 主詞 + will + be able to / be + 形容詞：預測以後的情況

  1. All your life you'll be able to see your parents, but after school it'll be harder to meet your old friends.
    你一輩子都能見到你的父母，可是畢業以後，要見到老朋友會比較難。
  2. With the new railway, people will be able to reach the city in half an hour.
    有了新鐵路，人們半小時就能到市區。
  3. If the factory closes, it will be hard for people here to find jobs.
    如果工廠關了，這裡的人會很難找到工作。
  4. In a few years, you'll be able to speak English fluently.
    再過幾年，你就能說流利的英文。
  5. The new bridge will be very useful for people in the village.
    新的橋對村子裡的人會很有用。
  6. Next year the exams will be harder, so start studying now.
    明年考試會比較難，所以現在就開始讀書。

③ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞, won't + 代名詞？：預測並請對方同意

  1. You're right. Without much sport you'll soon put on weight, won't you?
    你說得對。不太運動的話，你很快就會變胖，對吧？
  2. The shops will be very busy before Christmas, won't they?
    聖誕節前商店會很多人，對吧？
  3. The children will be tired after the long walk, won't they?
    走了那麼久的路，孩子們會很累，對吧？
  4. It'll be cold in the mountains at night, won't it?
    晚上山上會很冷，對吧？
  5. Your parents will be proud of you, won't they?
    你爸媽會以你為榮，對吧？
  6. The new teacher will be strict, won't she?
    新老師會很嚴格，對吧？

④ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + in + 時間：預測什麼時候

  1. In ten years, most cars on the road will be electric.
    十年後，路上大部分的車都會是電動車。
  2. In a few weeks, the trees will be full of blossom.
    再過幾個星期，樹上就會開滿花。
  3. In fifty years, the sea will cover part of this beach.
    五十年後，海水會淹沒這個海灘的一部分。
  4. In two years, my little sister will start school.
    兩年後，我妹妹會開始上學。
  5. In a month, the new shopping centre will be finished.
    一個月後，新的購物中心會完工。
  6. In a few hours, the snow will start to melt.
    再過幾個小時，雪就會開始融化。

⑤ 主詞 + won't + 原形動詞：預測不會發生

  1. Look at those dark clouds over the hills. The storm won't pass quickly.
    你看山丘上那些烏雲。暴風雨不會很快過去。
  2. My grandfather won't like his new mobile phone. He prefers the old one.
    我爺爺不會喜歡他的新手機。他比較喜歡舊的。
  3. With so many people wanting to live here, house prices won't fall any time soon.
    這麼多人想住在這裡，房價短時間內不會下跌。
  4. The match won't be easy. They're a very good team.
    這場比賽不會輕鬆。他們是一支很強的隊伍。
  5. People won't stop buying books, even with the internet.
    就算有了網路，人們也不會停止買書。
  6. The bus won't be here for another twenty minutes.
    公車還要二十分鐘才會到。
  7. You won't recognise the old school. They've changed everything.
    你會認不出那所老學校。他們把一切都改了。
