I'm not sure that the council has anticipated everything.

我不確定議會是不是每件事都預料到了。

I + am + not + sure / certain / convinced + that + 子⁠句

not sure / not certain我不確定。
not convinced我不太相信。
not likely不太可能。
not clear不清楚。

直⁠接和委⁠婉⁠的說⁠法

直接

You won't make progress.

你不會進步。
委婉

It's not likely that you'll make progress.

你不太可能會進步。

先贊⁠成，再提⁠出疑⁠問

例句

I like the plan, but I'm not sure that it will work.

我喜歡這個計畫，可是我不確定它會成功。

正式的信件常先說贊成的地方，再用 but I'm not sure that 提出疑問。

not 放⁠在 be 動⁠詞後⁠面，形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

I'm not sure that the council has anticipated everything.

I'm sure not that he is right.

I'm not sure that he is right.

sure 是形⁠容⁠詞，否⁠定用 be 動⁠詞加 not

I'm not sure that the council has anticipated everything.

I don't sure that they understand.

I'm not sure that they understand.

that 子⁠句要⁠有自⁠己⁠的主⁠詞

I'm not sure that the council has anticipated everything.

It's not likely that will rain.

It's not likely that it will rain.

例句

① I'm not sure / certain + that + 子句

  1. I'm not sure that the council has thought about parking.
    我不確定議會有沒有想過停車的問題。
  2. I'm not certain that the people near the dump have got used to the smell.
    我不確定住在垃圾場附近的人已經習慣那個味道了。
  3. I'm not sure that this is the best time to buy a house.
    我不確定現在是不是買房子的最好時機。
  4. I'm not certain that everyone received the email.
    我不確定每個人都收到了那封電子郵件。
  5. I'm not sure that the children will enjoy such a long film.
    我不確定孩子們會喜歡這麼長的電影。
  6. I'm not certain that the price includes breakfast.
    我不確定價格有沒有包含早餐。
  7. I'm not sure that we have enough chairs for everyone.
    我不確定我們的椅子夠不夠大家坐。
  8. I'm not certain that the new rules are fair.
    我不確定新規定是不是公平。

② I'm not convinced + that + 子句

  1. I'm not convinced that the new system is any better.
    我不太相信新系統有比較好。
  2. I'm not convinced that he's telling the truth.
    我不太相信他說的是實話。
  3. I'm not convinced that more roads will solve the traffic problem.
    我不太相信多蓋路就能解決交通問題。
  4. I'm not convinced that the diet really works.
    我不太相信這種飲食法真的有效。
  5. I'm not convinced that the shop will survive another year.
    我不太相信那家店能再撐一年。
  6. I'm not convinced that we need a second car.
    我不太相信我們需要第二台車。

③ It is not likely / certain + that + 子句

  1. If you study with the TV on, it's not likely that you'll make much progress.
    如果你開著電視讀書，你不太可能進步多少。
  2. It's not likely that the train will arrive on time.
    火車不太可能準時到。
  3. It is not certain that the ferry will sail in this wind.
    風這麼大，渡輪不一定會開。
  4. It's not likely that prices will fall soon.
    物價不太可能很快下跌。
  5. It is not certain that the vote will go our way.
    投票結果不一定對我們有利。
  6. It's not likely that he'll change his mind now.
    他現在不太可能改變主意。
  7. It is not certain that the bridge is safe.
    那座橋不一定安全。

④ It is not clear + that / whether / wh- 疑問詞 + 子句

  1. It is not clear that the plan will save any money.
    這個計畫會不會省錢並不清楚。
  2. It's not clear whether the meeting is still on.
    會議還開不開並不清楚。
  3. It is not clear that the new law has made any difference.
    新法律有沒有帶來任何改變並不清楚。
  4. It's not clear who will pay for the damage.
    誰會賠償損失並不清楚。
  5. It is not clear that students learn more with tablets.
    學生用平板學得比較多這一點並不清楚。

⑤ ..., but I'm not sure that + 子句：先贊成，再提出疑問

  1. The new menu is nice, but I'm not sure that the prices are right.
    新菜單不錯，可是我不確定價格是不是合理。
  2. The hotel looks lovely, but I'm not sure that it's near enough to the beach.
    那家飯店看起來很棒，可是我不確定它離海灘夠不夠近。
  3. Your idea is interesting, but I'm not sure that we have the money.
    你的想法很有趣，可是我不確定我們有沒有錢。
  4. The course sounds useful, but I'm not certain that I have the time.
    這門課聽起來很有用，可是我不確定我有沒有時間。
  5. The design is beautiful, but I'm not convinced that it's practical.
    設計很漂亮，可是我不太相信它實用。
