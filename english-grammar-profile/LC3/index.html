I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

我打開信封，把信拿了出來。

名⁠詞受⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

我打開信封，把信拿了出來。

take out 的意思是拿出來。

名⁠詞放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞前⁠後都⁠可⁠以，代⁠名⁠詞只⁠能放⁠在中⁠間

I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

名詞在後面

She turned off the phone.

她把手機關掉。
名詞在中間

She turned the phone off.

她把手機關掉。
代名詞

She turned it off.

她把它關掉。

受⁠詞很⁠長⁠時，放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

我得去領我在寫作比賽得的獎。

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞和小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間

I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

I found the letter and took out it.

I found the letter and took it out.

填⁠寫表⁠格是 fill in，in 不⁠能省⁠略

I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

Please fill this form.

Please fill in this form.

小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

My dad gave up smoke last year.

My dad gave up smoking last year.

很⁠長⁠的受⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間

I opened the envelope and took out the letter.

I wrote the name of the hotel where we were staying down.

I wrote down the name of the hotel where we were staying.

介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞只⁠能放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面，take off 說起⁠飛⁠時沒⁠有受⁠詞

介系詞動詞

I looked at the letter.

我看了那封信。
不及物片語動詞

The plane took off.

飛機起飛了。

例句

① take out / put away

  1. I opened my bag and took out my passport.
    我打開包包，拿出護照。
  2. "Can you help me put away the shopping?" "Sure. Where do the eggs go?"
    你可以幫我把買的東西收好嗎？好啊。蛋要放哪裡？
  3. The dentist took out two of my teeth.
    牙醫拔了我兩顆牙。
  4. Please put away your phones. The exam starts now.
    請把手機收起來。考試現在開始。

② pick up

  1. I won a writing contest, and I need to travel to Mexico City to pick up my award.
    我贏了寫作比賽，得去墨西哥市領獎。
  2. Dinner's ready! The children have to pick up their toys first.
    晚餐好了！孩子們得先把玩具撿起來。
  3. The dry cleaner called. You can pick up your suit after four.
    乾洗店打電話來了。你四點以後可以去拿西裝。

③ turn off / turn on

  1. She turned off the phone and walked to the bathroom.
    她把手機關掉，走去浴室。
  2. "It's hot in here." "I'll turn on the air conditioning."
    這裡好熱。我來開冷氣。
  3. Don't forget to turn off the oven when the cake is ready.
    蛋糕好了別忘了關烤箱。

④ put on / take off

  1. It was cold, so I put on a jumper.
    天氣很冷，所以我穿上一件毛衣。
  2. "Can I come in?" "Yes, but take off your shoes, please."
    我可以進來嗎？可以，可是請脫鞋。
  3. He put on his best suit for the interview.
    他穿上他最好的西裝去面試。

⑤ give up

  1. If I didn't need it for my degree, I would give up this class immediately.
    如果不是學位需要，我會馬上退掉這堂課。
  2. "Do you still play tennis?" "No, I gave up tennis when I hurt my knee."
    你還打網球嗎？沒有，我膝蓋受傷以後就不打了。
  3. My grandfather gave up smoking when he was fifty.
    我爺爺五十歲時戒了菸。
  4. She gave up her job to look after her baby.
    她辭掉工作照顧寶寶。

⑥ fill in / write down

  1. "What do I need to do?" "Fill in this form and sign at the bottom."
    我需要做什麼？填好這張表格，在最下面簽名。
  2. All visitors must fill in a card at the front desk.
    所有訪客都必須在櫃檯填一張卡片。
  3. Let me write down your phone number.
    我把你的電話號碼寫下來。
  4. The teacher told us to write down the new words.
    老師叫我們把新單字寫下來。

⑦ find out / look up

  1. I called the station to find out the time of the last train.
    我打電話到車站問末班車的時間。
  2. "What does this word mean?" "Look up the word in the dictionary."
    這個字是什麼意思？去字典查這個字。
  3. "Her parents were very angry." "Did they find out the truth about the party?"
    她爸媽很生氣。他們知道派對的真相了嗎？
  4. I looked up the address online before we left.
    我們出發前，我在網路上查了地址。

⑧ 受詞放在動詞和小品詞中間

  1. "Why is it so dark?" "Someone turned the lights off."
    怎麼這麼暗？有人把燈關了。
  2. I put my coat on and went outside.
    我穿上外套，走到外面。
  3. She took her keys out and opened the door.
    她拿出鑰匙，打開門。

⑨ 受詞是代名詞

  1. "Is this your umbrella?" "Yes, thanks. I'll put it away."
    這是你的傘嗎？對，謝謝。我把它收起來。
  2. "Here's the form." "OK, I'll fill it in now."
    這是表格。好，我現在填。
  3. I bought a new phone, but I can't turn it on.
    我買了一支新手機，可是開不了機。
