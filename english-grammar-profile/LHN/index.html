Compared to the campsite job, this one has its advantages.

跟營地的工作比起來，這份工作有它的好處。

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞子⁠句放⁠在句⁠首，先說⁠出比⁠較⁠的對⁠象

跟營地的工作比起來，這份工作有它的好處。

Based on、Given 和 Compared to 說依⁠據、考⁠慮和比⁠較

Compared to the campsite job, this one has its advantages.

Based on

Based on these figures, we expect sales to grow next year.

根據這些數字，我們預期明年的銷售會成長。
Given

Given the weather, the match was cancelled.

考慮到天氣，比賽取消了。

Located、Built、Written 說⁠明主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞，帶⁠有被⁠動⁠的意⁠思

Compared to the campsite job, this one has its advantages.

Located in

Located in the city center, the hotel is close to everything.

這家飯店位於市中心，到哪裡都很近。

和……比⁠起⁠來用 Compared to，不⁠用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Compared to the campsite job, this one has its advantages.

Comparing to last year, prices are much higher.

Compared to last year, prices are much higher.

過⁠去⁠分⁠詞說⁠明⁠的對⁠象要⁠是主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

Built in 1890, I visited the old church.

Built in 1890, the old church is the oldest building in town.

Built in 1890 說明的是教堂。

根⁠據⁠的意⁠思是 Based on

Based the survey, most customers are happy.

Based on the survey, most customers are happy.

例句

① Compared to / with

  1. Compared to my old flat, this one is huge.
    跟我以前的公寓比起來，這間好大。
  2. Compared with last summer, this year has been quite cool.
    跟去年夏天比起來，今年滿涼的。
  3. Compared to the city, life in the village is very slow.
    跟城市比起來，村子裡的生活步調很慢。
  4. Compared with other students, she learns very quickly.
    跟其他學生比起來，她學得很快。
  5. Compared to driving, the train is much more relaxing.
    跟開車比起來，搭火車輕鬆多了。
  6. Compared with the first film, the sequel was disappointing.
    跟第一集比起來，續集令人失望。

② Based on

  1. Based on the reviews, this restaurant is worth a visit.
    根據評價，這家餐廳值得一去。
  2. Based on my experience, I'd recommend booking early.
    根據我的經驗，我建議早點訂。
  3. Based on the test results, the doctor changed my medicine.
    根據檢查結果，醫生換了我的藥。
  4. Based on what you've told me, I think you should accept.
    根據你跟我說的，我覺得你應該接受。
  5. Based on the weather forecast, we'll go hiking on Sunday.
    根據天氣預報，我們星期天去健行。

③ Given

  1. Given the traffic, we should leave now.
    考慮到交通狀況，我們應該現在就出發。
  2. Given his age, he's in very good health.
    以他的年紀來說，他身體很好。
  3. Given the circumstances, I think we did quite well.
    在那種情況下，我覺得我們做得還不錯。
  4. Given the cost, most people take the bus.
    考慮到費用，大部分的人都搭公車。
  5. Given the bad weather, the match went ahead surprisingly smoothly.
    考慮到天氣很差，比賽進行得出奇順利。

④ Located / Based / Built + 地點或時間

  1. Located near the station, the café is popular with commuters.
    那家咖啡店位在車站附近，很受通勤族歡迎。
  2. Based in Taipei, the company has offices across Asia.
    那家公司總部在台北，在全亞洲都有辦公室。
  3. Built in the 1920s, the bridge still carries heavy traffic.
    那座橋建於一九二〇年代，到現在還承載很大的車流量。
  4. Designed for small kitchens, the oven fits on any counter.
    這台烤箱專為小廚房設計，任何流理台都放得下。
  5. Made from local wood, the furniture lasts for decades.
    這些家具用本地的木材做成，可以用好幾十年。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Compared to last month, our electricity bill is lower.
    跟上個月比起來，我們的電費比較低。
  2. Based on last year's sales, we're hiring two more staff.
    根據去年的銷售量，我們要再雇兩個員工。
  3. Given the delays, we expect some compensation.
    考慮到延誤，我們希望得到一些補償。
  4. Surrounded by mountains, the town gets very little wind.
    這個小鎮四面環山，很少颳風。
  5. Painted bright yellow, the house is easy to find.
    那棟房子漆成亮黃色，很好找。
  6. Compared with my brother, I'm quite thrifty.
    跟我哥哥比起來，我滿節儉的。
  7. Founded in 1950, the school has a long history.
    那所學校創立於一九五〇年，歷史很悠久。
  8. Given more time, I could have done a better job.
    要是有更多時間，我可以做得更好。
  9. Set in the 1960s, the film follows a young singer in London.
    那部電影的背景設在一九六〇年代，講一位年輕歌手在倫敦的故事。
