The weather was too hot.

天氣太熱了。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

天氣太熱了。

so 是非⁠常，quite 是相⁠當，too 是太⁠過

The weather was too hot.

so
非常

Your baby is so cute!

你的寶寶好可愛！
quite
相當、還算

The film was quite good.

那部電影還滿好看的。
too
超過想要的程度

These shoes are too small for me.

這雙鞋我穿太小了。

quite 可⁠以放⁠在 a 前⁠面修⁠飾名⁠詞

The weather was too hot.

到車站的路滿遠的。

稱⁠讚東⁠西用 so 或 very，不⁠用 too

The weather was too hot.

Your garden is too beautiful!

Your garden is so beautiful!

名⁠詞前⁠面不⁠用 so，用 such a

It was a so nice day.

It was such a nice day.

quite 不⁠放⁠在 a 和形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間

It's a quite big house.

It's quite a big house.

表⁠示太⁠過⁠的副⁠詞拼⁠成 too

The weather was too hot.

The box is to heavy.

The box is too heavy.

too 和 so 後⁠面可⁠以接結⁠果

The weather was too hot.

too + to
做不到的事

The water is too cold to swim in.

水太冷了，不能游泳。
so + that
造成的結果

I was so tired that I fell asleep on the bus.

我累到在公車上睡著了。

例句

① so + 形容詞

  1. I went to the night market. It was so busy.
    我去了夜市。人超多的。
  2. Your baby is so cute!
    你的寶寶好可愛！
  3. The view from the hotel was so beautiful.
    飯店看出去的風景好美。
  4. I'm so happy for you.
    我真替你高興。
  5. This sofa is so comfortable.
    這張沙發好舒服。
  6. Why are you so angry?
    你為什麼那麼生氣？
  7. The exam wasn't so difficult.
    考試沒有那麼難。

② quite + 形容詞

  1. It was quite expensive. It cost a hundred pounds.
    那個滿貴的。花了一百英鎊。
  2. The film was quite good.
    那部電影還滿好看的。
  3. My new school is quite big.
    我的新學校滿大的。
  4. The hotel was quite close to the beach.
    那家飯店離海灘滿近的。
  5. I'm quite tired, so I'll go to bed early.
    我滿累的，所以我會早點睡。
  6. Her English is quite good now.
    她的英文現在還滿好的。

③ quite a + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. It's quite a long way to the station.
    到車站的路滿遠的。
  2. He's quite a good singer.
    他唱歌滿好聽的。
  3. We had quite a nice time.
    我們玩得滿愉快的。

④ too + 形容詞

  1. This coffee is too hot. I can't drink it yet.
    這咖啡太燙了。我還不能喝。
  2. These shoes are too small for me.
    這雙鞋我穿太小了。
  3. The music is too loud!
    音樂太大聲了！
  4. It's too late to call him now.
    現在打給他太晚了。
  5. The test was too long, and I didn't finish it.
    考試太長了，我沒寫完。
  6. Is the bag too heavy for you?
    這個包包對你來說太重嗎？
  7. My room was too dark, so I bought a new lamp.
    我的房間太暗了，所以我買了一盞新燈。

⑤ look / feel / seem + so / quite / too + 形容詞

  1. You look so tired.
    你看起來好累。
  2. The new manager seems quite friendly.
    新經理看起來滿友善的。
  3. I feel too sick to go to school.
    我不舒服到沒辦法上學。
  4. The room looked quite small in the photo.
    照片裡的房間看起來滿小的。
  5. He seems so different now.
    他現在好像變了好多。

⑥ so ... that、too ... to

  1. The soup was so salty that I couldn't eat it.
    湯鹹到我喝不下去。
  2. She was too young to drive.
    她年紀太小，不能開車。
  3. The street was so noisy that we closed the window.
    街上吵到我們把窗戶關起來。
  4. The box was too heavy to lift.
    箱子太重了，搬不起來。
