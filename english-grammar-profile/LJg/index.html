The better the education you have, the better the opportunities you can get.

你受的教育越好，能得到的機會就越好。

The better the education you have, the better the opportunities you can get.

例句

① the more ..., the more ...

  1. The more I learn about space, the more I want to know.
    我對太空知道得越多，就越想知道更多。
  2. The more you smile, the more people smile back.
    你笑得越多，別人就越常對你笑。
  3. The more books you read, the more ideas you get.
    你讀的書越多，得到的想法就越多。
  4. The more he talked, the more bored she became.
    他講得越多，她就越覺得無聊。

② the more ..., the less ...

  1. The more I sleep, the less energy I seem to have.
    我睡得越多，好像越沒精神。
  2. The more you eat late at night, the less you sleep.
    你越常深夜吃東西，就睡得越少。
  3. The more you worry, the less you can enjoy life.
    你越擔心，就越不能享受生活。
  4. The more time I spend on my phone, the less I study.
    我花在手機上的時間越多，讀書的時間就越少。

③ the better ..., the better ...

  1. The better you know a city, the better you can enjoy it.
    你越了解一座城市，就越能好好享受它。
  2. The better the weather, the busier the beach.
    天氣越好，海灘就越擠。
  3. The better your English is, the better job you can find.
    你的英文越好，就越能找到好工作。

④ the + 形容詞 -er

  1. The older my grandfather gets, the happier he seems.
    我爺爺年紀越大，看起來越快樂。
  2. The bigger the house, the higher the rent.
    房子越大，房租就越高。
  3. The faster you drive, the more dangerous it is.
    你開得越快就越危險。
  4. The longer we waited, the angrier the customers got.
    我們等得越久，客人就越生氣。
  5. The cheaper the ticket, the earlier you have to book.
    票越便宜，就越要早點訂。

⑤ the more + 長的形容詞

  1. The more difficult the question, the more interesting the answer.
    問題越難，答案就越有意思。
  2. The more expensive the hotel, the more comfortable the beds.
    飯店越貴，床就越舒服。
  3. The more popular the restaurant, the longer you wait for a table.
    餐廳越受歡迎，等位子的時間就越長。

⑥ the worse ...

  1. You can watch one or two hours a day, but the more you watch, the worse you become.
    你一天可以看一兩個小時，可是看得越多，就變得越糟。
  2. The later you go to bed, the worse you feel in the morning.
    你越晚睡，早上就越不舒服。
  3. The worse the traffic, the later we arrive.
    交通越糟，我們就越晚到。
  4. The less you exercise, the worse your back gets.
    你越少運動，背就越不好。

⑦ 短句

  1. "When do you need the report?" "The sooner, the better."
    你什麼時候要這份報告？越快越好。
  2. "How many people can come to the party?" "The more, the merrier!"
    派對可以來多少人？人越多越熱鬧！
  3. "How big should the cake be?" "The bigger, the better."
    蛋糕要多大？越大越好。
  4. "Should I add more salt?" "The less, the better."
    要多加點鹽嗎？越少越好。
  5. "How spicy do you want it?" "The hotter, the better!"
    你要多辣？越辣越好！
  6. Book your flights early. The earlier, the better.
    早點訂機票。越早越好。
  7. Fresh bread tastes great. The fresher, the better.
    新鮮的麵包很好吃。越新鮮越好。
