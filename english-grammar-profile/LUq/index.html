If a driver has drunk too much or is tired, he shouldn't be driving.

駕駛如果喝了太多酒或很疲倦，就不應該開車。

主⁠詞 + should be + ⁠-⁠ing

should be + -⁠ing應該正在做，說一般的義務或理想。
shouldn't be + -⁠ing不應該正在做。
if 子句常說在什麼情況下。

說一⁠般⁠的情⁠況

例句

Wild animals should be running free.

野生動物應該自由地奔跑。

這個句型不只說現在這一刻，也說一般的情況。

先說理⁠想，再⁠說現⁠實

例句

Children should be playing outside, but many stay indoors.

孩子們應該在外面玩，可是很多人待在室內。

議論文常先用 should be + -⁠ing 說理想，再用 but 說現實。

shouldn't be 後⁠面⁠接 ⁠-⁠ing

If a driver has drunk too much or is tired, he shouldn't be driving.

He shouldn't be drive.

He shouldn't be driving.

should 和 ⁠-⁠ing 中⁠間要⁠有 be

If a driver has drunk too much or is tired, he shouldn't be driving.

Animals should running free.

Animals should be running free.

shouldn't 後⁠面直⁠接接 be

If a driver has drunk too much or is tired, he shouldn't be driving.

Children shouldn't to be working.

Children shouldn't be working.

例句

① 主詞 + shouldn't be + -ing + if + 子句：某種情況下不應該做

  1. If a car driver has drunk too much or is tired, he shouldn't be driving.
    如果一個駕駛喝太多酒或很累，他就不應該開車。
  2. If you have a high temperature, you shouldn't be going to work.
    如果你發高燒，你就不應該去上班。
  3. If the sea is rough, small boats shouldn't be going out.
    如果海浪很大，小船就不應該出海。
  4. If you're taking this medicine, you shouldn't be drinking alcohol.
    如果你在吃這種藥，你就不應該喝酒。
  5. If the ice is thin, nobody should be skating on the lake.
    如果冰很薄，就不應該有人在湖上溜冰。
  6. If a player is injured, he shouldn't be playing in the match.
    如果一個球員受傷了，他就不應該上場比賽。

② 主詞 + should be + -ing, but + 句子：理想和現實

  1. Wild animals should be running free, but I think zoos today have an important role in keeping them alive.
    野生動物應該自由地奔跑，可是我認為現在的動物園在保護牠們的生命上有重要的角色。
  2. Children should be playing outside, but many of them stay indoors all day.
    孩子們應該在外面玩，可是很多孩子整天待在室內。
  3. Teenagers should be sleeping nine hours a night, but most get only six.
    青少年每晚應該睡九個小時，可是大部分的人只睡六個小時。
  4. Doctors should be spending more time with patients, but they're too busy.
    醫生應該花更多時間陪病人，可是他們太忙了。
  5. Old people should be living near their families, but many live alone.
    老人應該住在家人附近，可是很多人一個人住。
  6. Students should be reading more books, but they spend hours online.
    學生應該多讀書，可是他們花好幾個小時上網。

③ 主詞 + shouldn't be + -ing：一般不應該發生的事

  1. Children under twelve shouldn't be working in factories.
    十二歲以下的孩子不應該在工廠工作。
  2. People shouldn't be sleeping on the streets in a rich country.
    在一個富裕的國家，不應該有人睡在街上。
  3. Families shouldn't be paying so much for basic food.
    家庭不應該為基本的食物付那麼多錢。
  4. Nurses shouldn't be working twelve-hour shifts every day.
    護理師不應該每天上十二個小時的班。
  5. Factories shouldn't be pouring waste into the river.
    工廠不應該把廢水倒進河裡。
  6. Pets shouldn't be living in tiny flats all day.
    寵物不應該整天住在很小的公寓裡。

④ 主詞 + should be + -ing：一般應該做的事

  1. Every school should be teaching children how to stay safe online.
    每所學校都應該教孩子怎麼安全上網。
  2. Parents should be reading to their children every evening.
    父母應該每天晚上唸書給孩子聽。
  3. Companies should be paying their workers a fair wage.
    公司應該付給員工公平的薪水。
  4. We should all be doing more to protect the environment.
    我們大家都應該為保護環境多做一點。
  5. Cities should be planting more trees along their streets.
    城市應該在街道兩旁種更多樹。
  6. Governments should be helping people find homes.
    政府應該幫助人們找到住處。

⑤ 主詞 + shouldn't be + -ing + while + 子句

  1. You shouldn't be texting while you're crossing the road.
    你過馬路的時候不應該傳訊息。
  2. Students shouldn't be eating while the teacher is talking.
    老師說話的時候，學生不應該吃東西。
  3. Drivers shouldn't be eating while the car is moving.
    車子在開的時候，駕駛不應該吃東西。
  4. You shouldn't be watching TV while you're doing your homework.
    你寫功課的時候不應該看電視。
  5. Workers shouldn't be using their phones while they're operating machines.
    操作機器的時候，工人不應該用手機。
  6. You shouldn't be swimming while there's lightning.
    打雷閃電的時候，你不應該游泳。
  7. People shouldn't be smoking while children are in the car.
    孩子在車裡的時候，大人不應該抽菸。
