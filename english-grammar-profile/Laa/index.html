This is what almost everyone thinks, but we really ought to think a little more about it.

我們真的應該再多想一想。

主⁠詞 + really + ought to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

really、ought to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This is what almost everyone thinks, but we really ought to think a little more about it.

really真的。放在 ought to 前面，加強語氣。
ought to應該。
原形動詞說話者強烈建議的事。

正⁠式⁠的 one

例句

One really ought to know the facts first.

人真的應該先了解事實。

正式的寫作常用 one 說一般人。

給具⁠體⁠的建⁠議

例句

You really ought to ask about the timetable.

你真的應該問一下時間表。

you really ought to 常用來給對方一個重要的提醒。

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This is what almost everyone thinks, but we really ought to think a little more about it.

You really ought to ask.

We really ought to think about it.

This is what almost everyone thinks, but we really ought to think a little more about it.

You really ought to check the details.

例句

① we really ought to + 原形動詞：議論文的意見

  1. Banning phones is what almost everyone thinks is right, but we really ought to think a little more about it.
    禁用手機幾乎是每個人都認為對的事，可是我們真的應該再多想一想。
  2. Young people will live with our decisions for the longest time. We really ought to listen to them more.
    年輕人要承受我們的決定最久。我們真的應該多聽聽他們的意見。
  3. A new survey says that half of all workers hate their jobs. We really ought to ask ourselves why.
    一份新的調查說，一半的上班族討厭自己的工作。我們真的應該問問自己為什麼。
  4. Many people say that crime is rising, but the numbers show the opposite. We really ought to look at the facts before we decide.
    很多人說犯罪在增加，可是數字顯示的是相反的情況。我們決定之前，真的應該看看事實。
  5. Families buy more and more things and still feel poor. We really ought to change the way we think about money.
    家庭買的東西越來越多，卻還是覺得窮。我們真的應該改變對錢的看法。
  6. The average teenager looks at a screen for seven hours a day. We really ought to spend less time on our phones.
    一般青少年一天看螢幕七個小時。我們真的應該少花一點時間在手機上。

② you really ought to + 原形動詞：重要的提醒

  1. Before you choose a school, you really ought to ask for details about the timetable, just to be sure it suits you.
    選學校之前，你真的應該問一下時間表的細節，確定它適合你。
  2. You've had toothache for a week. You really ought to see a dentist about it.
    你牙痛一個星期了。你真的應該去看牙醫。
  3. Your laptop is ten years old and very slow. You really ought to back up your files.
    你的筆電用了十年，又很慢。你真的應該備份你的檔案。
  4. That hotel looks cheap online. You really ought to read the reviews before you book.
    那家飯店在網路上看起來很便宜。你訂之前真的應該看看評論。
  5. You scratched your dad's car this morning. You really ought to tell your parents about the accident.
    你今天早上刮傷了你爸爸的車。你真的應該把那次意外告訴你爸媽。
  6. You really ought to get some rest. You look exhausted.
    你真的應該休息一下。你看起來累壞了。

③ one really ought to + 原形動詞：正式的一般建議

  1. When one chooses a present, one really ought to know the interests and wishes of the person who will receive it.
    選禮物的時候，人真的應該了解收禮的人有什麼興趣和願望。
  2. The guidebook says that Venice is like no other city. One really ought to visit it at least once.
    旅遊書說威尼斯跟其他城市都不一樣。人真的應該至少去一次。
  3. Our lawyer always gives the same advice. One really ought to read a contract before signing it.
    我們的律師總是給同樣的建議。人簽約之前真的應該先讀合約。
  4. Travellers abroad are often helped by strangers. One really ought to learn some of the local language.
    在國外旅行的人常常受到陌生人幫忙。人真的應該學一點當地的語言。
  5. My grandmother always said that one really ought to thank people who help.
    我奶奶總是說，人真的應該謝謝幫忙的人。
  6. Health is worth more than money, as the old saying goes. One really ought to take care of one's health.
    俗話說，健康比錢更有價值。人真的應該照顧自己的健康。

④ I really ought to + 原形動詞：自己應該做的事

  1. I really ought to go home. It's nearly midnight.
    我真的應該回家了。快半夜了。
  2. It's eight o'clock, and I haven't opened my school bag. I really ought to start my homework.
    已經八點了，我還沒打開書包。我真的應該開始寫功課了。
  3. Grandma lives alone and loves to chat. I really ought to call her more often.
    奶奶一個人住，很喜歡聊天。我真的應該更常打電話給她。
  4. I can't get the car into the garage any more. I really ought to clean it out this weekend.
    我的車已經開不進車庫了。這個週末我真的應該把車庫清乾淨。
  5. I've eaten takeaway five times this week. I really ought to learn to cook.
    我這個星期吃了五次外帶。我真的應該學煮飯。
  6. My wardrobe won't close. I really ought to stop buying so many clothes.
    我的衣櫃關不起來了。我真的應該停止買那麼多衣服。

⑤ 主詞 + really ought to + 原形動詞：說別人或機構

  1. It took me an hour to drive two miles this morning. The government really ought to do something about the traffic.
    我今天早上開兩英里開了一個小時。政府真的應該對交通做點什麼。
  2. The children wear their coats in class all winter. The school really ought to fix the heating.
    孩子們整個冬天都穿著外套上課。學校真的應該修暖氣。
  3. Your brother is thirty and still lives with your parents. He really ought to find a job.
    你哥哥三十歲了，還跟你爸媽住。他真的應該找份工作。
  4. I've written to the phone company three times about my bill. They really ought to answer my emails.
    我為了帳單寫信給電信公司三次了。他們真的應該回覆我的電子郵件。
  5. There's only one waiter in the café on Saturdays. They really ought to have more staff on at weekends.
    那家咖啡店星期六只有一個服務生。他們週末真的應該安排更多員工。
  6. The swings in our park have been broken for months. The council really ought to repair them.
    我們公園的鞦韆壞了好幾個月了。市議會真的應該修理。
  7. My mum hasn't had a day off all year. She really ought to take a holiday.
    我媽媽一整年都沒休過假。她真的應該去度個假。
