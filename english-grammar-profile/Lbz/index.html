I think the car industry will develop cars that run on solar energy on account of the levels of pollution in big cities.

我認為，由於大城市的污染程度，汽車業會開發用太陽能跑的車。

例句

① on account of / in view of

  1. She retired early on account of her health.
    她因為健康的關係提早退休了。
  2. The beach is closed on account of the strong currents.
    海灘因為水流很強而關閉。
  3. In view of the weather, we've moved the party indoors.
    考慮到天氣，我們把派對移到室內了。
  4. In view of his experience, we offered him the job.
    考慮到他的經驗，我們給了他這份工作。
  5. Many people moved away on account of the noise from the airport.
    很多人因為機場的噪音搬走了。

② with regard to / in accordance with

  1. With regard to your question, the answer is on page ten.
    關於您的問題，答案在第十頁。
  2. I have no comment with regard to the rumours.
    關於那些傳言，我不予置評。
  3. The building meets safety standards in accordance with the law.
    這棟建築依照法律符合安全標準。
  4. Please pay the fee in accordance with the contract.
    請依照合約支付費用。
  5. We acted in accordance with your instructions.
    我們依照您的指示行動。

③ by means of / in the event of

  1. You can attach the pages by means of a simple clip.
    你可以用一個簡單的夾子把這些頁面固定在一起。
  2. They crossed the river by means of a rope bridge.
    他們藉由一座吊橋過了河。
  3. In the event of an emergency, call this number.
    萬一有緊急情況，請打這個號碼。
  4. In the event of rain, the concert will take place in the hall.
    萬一下雨，音樂會將改在禮堂舉行。
  5. The company communicates with staff by means of a weekly newsletter.
    公司藉由每週的電子報跟員工溝通。

④ ahead of

  1. My grandmother's ideas were far ahead of her time.
    我奶奶的想法遠遠超前了她的時代。
  2. We finished the project two weeks ahead of schedule.
    我們比預定時間提早兩個禮拜完成了計畫。
  3. Please arrive ahead of the other guests.
    請比其他客人早一點到。
  4. The team is three points ahead of its rivals.
    這支隊伍領先對手三分。
  5. Ahead of the meeting, please read the attached report.
    開會之前，請先讀附件的報告。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. The museum will close early on account of the public holiday.
    博物館因為國定假日會提早關門。
  2. In view of recent events, we are reviewing our security.
    考慮到最近發生的事，我們正在檢討我們的保全措施。
  3. With regard to payment, we accept all major cards.
    關於付款，我們接受所有主要的信用卡。
  4. Staff must handle all waste in accordance with local rules.
    員工必須依照當地規定處理所有廢棄物。
  5. In the event of a delay, passengers will receive a free meal.
    萬一誤點，乘客可以得到一份免費的餐點。
  6. Prices have risen sharply on account of the poor harvest.
    因為收成不好，價格大幅上漲。
  7. She got the job ahead of more experienced candidates.
    她比更有經驗的應徵者先拿到那份工作。
  8. We collected the data by means of an online survey.
    我們藉由線上問卷收集資料。
  9. I am writing with regard to the invoice you sent last week.
    我寫信是關於您上週寄來的發票。
  10. In view of the cost, we have decided not to go ahead.
    考慮到成本，我們決定不進行了。
