First we had dinner in a very expensive restaurant, and then we went to a disco.

我們先在一家很貴的餐廳吃晚餐，然後去了一家迪斯可舞廳。

first 放⁠在第一⁠件⁠事前⁠面，and then 接第⁠二件事

我們先在一家很貴的餐廳吃晚餐，然後去了一家迪斯可舞廳。

after that 放⁠在新⁠的句⁠子⁠的句⁠首，後⁠面加逗⁠號

我們先玩了一些遊戲。之後一位魔術師表演了幾個魔術。

next 接下⁠一⁠步，finally 接最⁠後一⁠步

next

Next, put the eggs in the bowl.

接下來把蛋放進碗裡。
finally

Finally, add some salt.

最後加一點鹽。

then 可⁠以放⁠在 and 後⁠面，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

First we had dinner in a very expensive restaurant, and then we went to a disco.

and 後面

We went to the museum, and then we had lunch.

我們去了博物館，然後吃午餐。
主詞後面
比較正式

We went to the museum and we then had lunch.

我們去了博物館，接著吃了午餐。

新⁠的句⁠子⁠的句⁠首用 after that，不⁠用 after

We went shopping. After, we had a coffee.

We went shopping. After that, we had a coffee.

排步⁠驟用 first，不⁠用 at first

First we had dinner in a very expensive restaurant, and then we went to a disco.

At first, wash your hands. Then cut the vegetables.

First, wash your hands. Then cut the vegetables.

and 後⁠面⁠接 then，不⁠接 after

First we had dinner in a very expensive restaurant, and then we went to a disco.

I finished my work and after I went home.

I finished my work and then I went home.

排最⁠後一⁠步用 finally，不⁠用 at last

At last, put the cake in the oven.

Finally, put the cake in the oven.

at first 表⁠示一開⁠始，和後⁠來⁠的情⁠況對⁠比

一開始我不喜歡這個城市，可是現在我很愛。

at last 只表⁠示終⁠於

公車終於來了。

finally 可以排最後一步，也可以表示終於。

例句

① First ..., and then ...

  1. First I had a shower, and then I got dressed.
    我先洗了個澡，然後換好衣服。
  2. First we'll go to the bank, and then we'll go to the post office.
    我們先去銀行，再去郵局。
  3. First she called her mum, and then she called the police.
    她先打電話給她媽媽，然後才報警。
  4. First you turn on the computer, and then you type your password.
    你先開電腦，然後輸入密碼。

② then 接下一件事

  1. We had a picnic in the park, then we went home.
    我們在公園野餐，然後就回家了。
  2. He opened the letter and then smiled.
    他打開信，然後笑了。
  3. Let's eat first, then we can talk about the plan.
    我們先吃飯，然後再談計畫。
  4. Wait for the green light, then cross the road.
    等綠燈亮了再過馬路。
  5. Have a rest, and then we'll start again.
    休息一下，然後我們再開始。

③ After that, ...

  1. We visited the castle. After that, we went for a walk by the river.
    我們參觀了城堡。之後去河邊散步。
  2. I worked in a hotel for two years. After that, I moved to London.
    我在一家飯店工作了兩年。之後搬到倫敦。
  3. The teacher checked our homework. After that, we played a game.
    老師檢查了我們的作業。之後我們玩了一個遊戲。
  4. We'll have dinner at seven. After that, we can watch a film.
    我們七點吃晚餐。之後可以看部電影。
  5. My dad didn't say anything. After that, he never mentioned it again.
    我爸爸什麼都沒說。之後他再也沒提過這件事。

④ next 接下一步

  1. Next, put the eggs in the bowl.
    接下來把蛋放進碗裡。
  2. What should we do next?
    我們接下來要做什麼？
  3. First, fill in this form. Next, sign your name at the bottom.
    先填這張表。接下來在下面簽名。

⑤ finally 接最後一步

  1. Finally, close the lid and press the green button.
    最後蓋上蓋子，按下綠色的按鈕。
  2. We went to three shops, and finally we found the right shoes.
    我們去了三家店，最後找到了合適的鞋子。
  3. First, they had a meeting. Then they had lunch. Finally, they went back to the office.
    他們先開了個會。然後吃午餐。最後回到辦公室。
  4. Finally, I want to thank my parents.
    最後，我想謝謝我的父母。

⑥ 排步驟的祈使句

  1. First, boil some water.
    先燒一些水。
  2. Then put the tea bag in the cup.
    然後把茶包放進杯子裡。
  3. First, take the old battery out. Then put the new one in.
    先把舊電池拿出來。然後把新的放進去。
  4. Turn left at the corner, then go straight on.
    在轉角左轉，然後直走。
  5. Mix the flour and the butter first. After that, add the milk.
    先把麵粉和奶油拌在一起。之後加牛奶。

⑦ 說一天裡發生的事

  1. In the morning we went to the beach. Then we had lunch at a little café.
    早上我們去了海邊。然後在一家小咖啡館吃午餐。
  2. We played football for an hour. After that, we were very tired.
    我們踢了一個小時的足球。之後大家都很累。
  3. First the lights went out, and then the music stopped.
    先是燈熄了，然後音樂停了。
  4. She finished her exams, and then she went on holiday.
    她考完試，然後就去度假了。
  5. They got married in June, and after that they moved to Spain.
    他們在六月結婚，之後搬到西班牙。
  6. First we waited for an hour, then the doctor saw us.
    我們先等了一個小時，然後醫生才幫我們看診。
