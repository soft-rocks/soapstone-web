I must say + (that) + 子⁠句

I must say我必須說、說真的。強調自己的看法。
子句看法，常是稱讚。

正⁠式⁠的歡⁠迎和感⁠謝

I went to as many concerts as possible, and I must say that they were superb.

例句

I must say that we are delighted to have you here.

我必須說，我們非常高興您能來這裡。

I must say 常用在正式的演講，表達歡迎或感謝。

放⁠在句⁠尾

I went to as many concerts as possible, and I must say that they were superb.

例句

That's a lovely dress, I must say.

說真的，那件洋裝真漂亮。

口語裡 I must say 也可以放在句尾，前面加逗號。

must 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I went to as many concerts as possible, and I must say that they were superb.

I must to say the food was great.

I must say the food was great.

say 後⁠面不⁠直⁠接接人

I went to as many concerts as possible, and I must say that they were superb.

I must say you that it was superb.

I must say that it was superb.

must 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I went to as many concerts as possible, and I must say that they were superb.

I must saying, you look wonderful.

I must say, you look wonderful.

例句

① I must say (that) + 子句：強調好的評價

  1. I love music, so I went to as many concerts as possible, and I must say that they were superb.
    我很喜歡音樂，所以盡量多聽音樂會，而且我必須說，它們都太棒了。
  2. Thank you for inviting us to your wedding. I must say that the food was delicious.
    謝謝你邀請我們參加婚禮。我必須說，食物很好吃。
  3. We haven't spoken for a year. I must say your English has improved a lot.
    我們一年沒說話了。我必須說，你的英文進步很多。
  4. We had a lovely stay in Rome. I must say that the hotel staff were very helpful.
    我們在羅馬住得很愉快。我必須說，飯店的員工都很熱心幫忙。
  5. You've planted so many roses. I must say the garden looks beautiful this year.
    你種了好多玫瑰。我必須說，花園今年看起來很漂亮。
  6. Thanks for organising our trip to the lakes. I must say that I enjoyed every minute of it.
    謝謝你安排我們去湖區的旅行。我必須說，每一分鐘我都很享受。

② I must say (that) + 子句：正式的歡迎和感謝

  1. I must say that I'm delighted to have you here with us, Mr Saramago. My students will be very happy to hear you.
    我必須說，我非常高興您能來這裡，Saramago 先生。我的學生會很高興聽您演講。
  2. Thank you all for coming tonight. I must say that it's a great honour to receive this prize.
    謝謝各位今晚來到這裡。我必須說，得到這個獎是很大的榮幸。
  3. Dear Mrs Patel, thank you for the books you gave our library. I must say that we are very grateful.
    親愛的 Patel 女士，謝謝您捐給我們圖書館的書。我必須說，我們非常感謝。
  4. This is my last day before I retire. I must say it's been a pleasure to work with such a good team.
    這是我退休前的最後一天。我必須說，能跟這麼好的團隊工作很愉快。
  5. Thank you, Professor Lee. I must say that your talk was very interesting.
    謝謝您，Lee 教授。我必須說，您的演講很有趣。
  6. Welcome to our prize-giving evening. I must say we're very proud of all our students this year.
    歡迎來到我們的頒獎晚會。我必須說，我們很以今年所有的學生為榮。

③ I must say, + 句子：口語的稱讚

  1. Are you going to the interview now? I must say, you look wonderful in that suit.
    你現在要去面試嗎？說真的，你穿那套西裝很好看。
  2. Thanks for bringing me to your favourite place. I must say, this is the best pizza I've ever eaten.
    謝謝你帶我來你最喜歡的店。說真的，這是我吃過最好吃的披薩。
  3. Your kids said please and thank you all through dinner. I must say, they're very polite.
    你的孩子吃晚餐時一直說請和謝謝。說真的，他們很有禮貌。
  4. That was a great evening at the theatre. I must say, I didn't expect the show to be so funny.
    今晚在劇院很愉快。說真的，我沒想到那場表演這麼好笑。
  5. We moved to the new building on Monday. I must say, it's much nicer than the old one.
    我們星期一搬到新大樓。說真的，它比舊的好多了。
  6. You painted every room yourself? I must say, you've done a great job with the house.
    每個房間都是你自己漆的？說真的，你把房子整理得很好。

④ 句子, I must say.：放在句尾

  1. Where did you buy it? It really suits you, I must say.
    你在哪裡買的？說真的，它很適合你。
  2. I kept you waiting for an hour. You've been very patient, I must say.
    我讓你等了一個小時。說真的，你一直很有耐心。
  3. We won three–two in the last minute. It was a brilliant match, I must say.
    我們在最後一分鐘以三比二贏了。說真的，那是一場精彩的比賽。
  4. I could sit here all afternoon. This is a very comfortable chair, I must say.
    我可以在這裡坐一整個下午。說真的，這張椅子很舒服。
  5. I heard you in the choir on Sunday. You sing beautifully, I must say.
    我星期天聽到你在合唱團唱歌。說真的，你唱得很好聽。
  6. That was the best roast chicken I've had in years. Your mum is a great cook, I must say.
    那是我這幾年吃過最好吃的烤雞。說真的，你媽媽很會煮飯。

⑤ I must say + 子句：說意外或不滿

  1. I asked Tom why he quit his job. I must say I was surprised by his answer.
    我問 Tom 為什麼辭職。我必須說，他的回答讓我很驚訝。
  2. The team worked hard all year. However, I must say that I'm a little disappointed with the results.
    團隊一整年都很努力。不過，我必須說，我對結果有點失望。
  3. The first hour was great. But I must say I didn't enjoy the second half of the film.
    前一個小時很好看。可是我必須說，我不喜歡電影的後半段。
  4. We waited forty minutes for our main course. I must say the service was very slow.
    我們等主菜等了四十分鐘。我必須說，服務很慢。
  5. Your brother didn't say hello to anyone at the party. I must say that I find his behaviour rude.
    你哥哥在派對上沒跟任何人打招呼。我必須說，我覺得他的行為很沒禮貌。
  6. The repair to my car cost nine hundred pounds. I must say I was shocked by the price.
    我的車修了九百英鎊。我必須說，那個價錢讓我很震驚。
  7. Dear Sir, our room had no hot water for three days. I must say that I expected more from your hotel.
    先生您好，我們的房間三天沒有熱水。我必須說，我對貴飯店的期待比較高。
