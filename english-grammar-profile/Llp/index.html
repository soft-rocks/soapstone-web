Teaching is terribly challenging and demanding.

教書非常有挑戰性，也非常吃力。

例句

① 行業和工作

  1. I love my students, but teaching is terribly challenging and demanding.
    我很愛我的學生，可是教書非常有挑戰性，也非常吃力。
  2. Advertising seems to be very important to promote a new product.
    要推廣新產品，廣告似乎非常重要。
  3. Nursing is a hard job, but it's very rewarding.
    護理是很辛苦的工作，但是很有成就感。
  4. He left banking to open a small bakery.
    他離開銀行業，開了一家小麵包店。
  5. My daughter wants to study engineering at university.
    我女兒想在大學讀工程。

② 學習和成長

  1. Learning English has its rewards too.
    學英文也有它的收穫。
  2. Good training makes new staff more confident.
    好的訓練讓新員工更有自信。
  3. Parenting is the hardest job in the world.
    當父母是世界上最難的工作。
  4. Online learning became very popular in 2020.
    線上學習在 2020 年變得很普遍。

③ 生活和社會

  1. Housing in the city centre is very expensive.
    市中心的住房很貴。
  2. Public spending on schools has gone up this year.
    今年公共教育支出增加了。
  3. The museum depends on private funding.
    這座博物館靠私人資助。
  4. Online shopping has changed the way we live.
    網路購物改變了我們的生活方式。

④ 形容詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. Effective teaching needs good planning.
    有效的教學需要好的規劃。
  2. Careful listening is an important skill for doctors.
    仔細聆聽是醫生很重要的技能。
  3. Clear writing makes a report easy to read.
    清楚的寫作讓報告容易閱讀。
  4. Regular cleaning keeps the machine working well.
    定期清潔讓機器運作順暢。

⑤ the + 動詞-ing + of + 名詞

  1. The teaching of languages starts at primary school.
    語言教學從小學就開始。
  2. The building of the new bridge took three years.
    新橋的建造花了三年。
  3. The planning of the event was excellent.
    這次活動的規劃很出色。
  4. The opening of the new library is next week.
    新圖書館下禮拜開幕。

⑥ 介系詞 + 動詞-ing 抽象名詞

  1. She wants a career in nursing.
    她想從事護理工作。
  2. He has twenty years of experience in marketing.
    他有二十年的行銷經驗。
  3. I'm interested in a job in publishing.
    我對出版業的工作有興趣。
  4. There's a lot of money in advertising.
    廣告業很賺錢。

⑦ 動詞-ing 抽象名詞當受詞

  1. Many young people choose teaching because they love children.
    很多年輕人選擇教書，因為他們喜歡小孩。
  2. The government should improve housing for families.
    政府應該改善家庭的住房。
  3. We need better funding for research.
    我們需要更多的研究經費。
  4. My parents never understood my love of acting.
    我爸媽從來不懂我為什麼那麼愛演戲。
  5. The company is cutting spending this year.
    公司今年在削減支出。
  6. She gave up nursing after twenty years.
    她做了二十年護理後就不做了。
