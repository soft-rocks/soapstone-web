例句

① It + be + 比較級 + to 不定詞 + than + to 不定詞

  1. For some people, it's easier to work alone than to work in a team.
    對有些人來說，自己工作比團隊合作容易。
  2. The next bus is in forty minutes. It's quicker to walk than to wait for the bus.
    下一班公車要四十分鐘後才來。走路比等公車快。
  3. Don't sign the form if you don't understand it. It's better to ask than to guess.
    看不懂表格就別簽。問清楚比用猜的好。
  4. Come to Thailand with us next summer. It's more fun to travel with friends than to go alone.
    明年夏天跟我們一起去泰國吧。跟朋友旅行比自己去好玩。
  5. The streets near the station are dark. It's safer to take a taxi than to walk home at night.
    車站附近的街道很暗。晚上搭計程車比走路回家安全。
  6. You and your neighbour are angry about the fence. It's more appropriate to talk first than to go to court.
    你和你的鄰居為了圍籬在生氣。先談談比上法院恰當。

② 比較級 + than + to 不定詞（其他句型）

  1. All my friends from this job have gone. I'd find it harder to stay than to leave.
    我在這份工作的朋友都走了。我覺得留下來比離開難。
  2. My grandfather always told us this. Nothing is more important than to be honest.
    我爺爺總是跟我們說這句話。沒有什麼比誠實更重要。
  3. My sister wants a new laptop, but the sales start next month. She thinks it's wiser to wait than to buy now.
    我姊姊想買新筆電，可是特價下個月才開始。她覺得等一等比現在買明智。
  4. We need an answer from the hotel today. It's quicker to call than to write an email.
    我們今天就需要飯店的回覆。打電話比寫電子郵件快。
  5. My boss asked me to work on Saturday. It was easier to say yes than to explain.
    我老闆要我星期六上班。說好比解釋容易。

③ 比較級 + than expected / planned

  1. We need more staff at weekends. The new café is busier than expected.
    我們週末需要更多人手。新咖啡店比預期的熱鬧。
  2. We used the heater every day in January. Our electricity bill was higher than expected.
    我們一月每天都開暖氣。我們的電費比預期的高。
  3. The builders found more problems with the roof. The repairs were more expensive than planned.
    工人發現屋頂有更多問題。修理費比計畫的貴。
  4. Everyone agreed quickly. The meeting finished earlier than planned.
    大家很快就同意了。會議比計畫的早結束。
  5. There was a strong wind against us. The flight took longer than expected.
    我們遇到很強的逆風。飛行時間比預期的長。
  6. It rained every weekend of the festival. Ticket sales were lower than expected this year.
    音樂節每個週末都在下雨。今年的票房比預期的低。

④ 比較級 + than usual / ever / necessary

  1. There's a football match in town. The roads are busier than usual today.
    鎮上有一場足球賽。今天路上的車比平常多。
  2. Your new job really suits you. You look happier than ever.
    你的新工作真的很適合你。你看起來比以前都開心。
  3. The air in the basement is bad. Don't stay longer than necessary.
    地下室的空氣不好。不要待得比需要的久。
  4. I'm wearing my winter coat today. It's colder than usual for May.
    我今天穿冬天的大衣。以五月來說，今天比平常冷。
  5. We waited outside the bakery until ten. The shop opened later than usual.
    我們在麵包店外面等到十點。那家店比平常晚開門。

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. You look worried about the bill. Was the hotel more expensive than expected?
    你看起來在擔心帳單。那家飯店比預期的貴嗎？
  2. My aunt still won't speak to her brother. It's often harder to forgive than to forget.
    我阿姨還是不肯跟她哥哥說話。原諒常常比忘記難。
  3. Two of our engineers are ill. The project will take longer than planned.
    我們有兩個工程師生病了。這個專案會比計畫的久。
  4. We all slept until seven. The baby was quieter than usual last night.
    我們都睡到七點。寶寶昨晚比平常安靜。
  5. Leave home at seven for your interview. It's better to arrive early than to be late.
    面試那天七點就出門。早到總比遲到好。
  6. We didn't need our jackets on the walk. The weather was warmer than forecast.
    我們散步的時候不需要外套。天氣比預報的暖和。
  7. The shop is only ten minutes away. For short distances, it's healthier to walk than to drive.
    那家店只要十分鐘。短距離走路比開車健康。
  8. We're thinking of moving to Lisbon. Is it cheaper to rent than to buy there?
    我們在考慮搬到里斯本。在那裡租房子比買房子便宜嗎？
