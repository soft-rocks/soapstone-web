A bike, even a good one, is a lot cheaper than a car.

腳踏車就算是好的，也比汽車便宜很多。

a lot 放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面，表⁠示差很⁠多

A bike, even a good one, is a lot cheaper than a car.

尖峰時間搭火車比開車快很多。

長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞用 a lot more，good 用 a lot better

A bike, even a good one, is a lot cheaper than a car.

a lot more + 形容詞

Working from home is a lot more comfortable for me.

對我來說，在家工作舒服很多。
a lot better

Your cough sounds a lot better today.

你今天咳嗽的聲音好很多了。

A bike, even a good one, is a lot cheaper than a car.

It would be a lot cheaper to book the flights now.

現在訂機票會便宜很多。

A bike, even a good one, is a lot cheaper than a car.

This flat is a lot bigger than our old one.

A bike, even a good one, is a lot cheaper than a car.

例句

① a lot + 形容詞-er

  1. Taking the train is a lot faster than driving at rush hour.
    尖峰時間搭火車比開車快很多。
  2. Online tickets are a lot cheaper than tickets at the door.
    網路上買的票比現場買的便宜很多。
  3. The new library is a lot bigger than the old one on Main Street.
    新的圖書館比大街上那間舊的大很多。
  4. My grandmother is a lot calmer since she moved to the countryside.
    我奶奶搬到鄉下以後平靜很多。
  5. Our new puppy is a lot quieter than our last dog.
    我們的新小狗比之前那隻狗安靜很多。
  6. With the window open, the room is a lot cooler.
    窗戶打開以後，房間涼很多。
  7. Is the lake a lot deeper on the other side?
    湖的另一邊深很多嗎？

② a lot + more + 形容詞

  1. Hiring a guide was a lot more expensive than we planned.
    請導遊比我們預計的貴很多。
  2. His second speech was a lot more relaxed than the first.
    他第二次演講比第一次放鬆很多。
  3. The exam was a lot more difficult than the practice test.
    正式考試比模擬考難很多。
  4. Working from home is a lot more comfortable for me.
    對我來說，在家工作舒服很多。
  5. Our new manager is a lot more placid than the old one.
    我們的新經理比原本那個平和很多。
  6. Shopping online is a lot more convenient with two small kids.
    帶著兩個小孩，在網路上買東西方便很多。

③ a lot better / a lot worse

  1. Your cough sounds a lot better today.
    你今天咳嗽的聲音好很多了。
  2. The second half of the match was a lot better.
    比賽的下半場精彩很多。
  3. The traffic on the bridge was a lot worse than usual this morning.
    今天早上橋上的交通比平常糟很多。
  4. My Spanish is a lot better after six months in Madrid.
    在馬德里待了六個月以後，我的西班牙文好很多。
  5. The hotel food was a lot worse than the photos.
    飯店的菜比照片上差很多。

④ would be a lot + 比較級

  1. Don't you think it would be a lot easier to finish college first?
    你不覺得先把大學念完會容易很多嗎？
  2. It would be a lot cheaper to book the flights now.
    現在訂機票會便宜很多。
  3. The trip would be a lot shorter by plane.
    搭飛機的話，這趟路會短很多。
  4. A bigger table would be a lot more useful in this kitchen.
    這個廚房放一張大一點的桌子會好用很多。
  5. It would be a lot safer to wait until the rain stops.
    等雨停再走會安全很多。

⑤ look / feel / seem / get 後面

  1. You look a lot happier since you changed jobs.
    你換工作以後看起來開心很多。
  2. I feel a lot stronger after two weeks of rest.
    休息了兩個禮拜以後，我覺得身體好很多。
  3. The kids seem a lot calmer after their nap.
    孩子們睡完午覺以後，看起來平靜很多。
  4. It gets a lot colder here after sunset.
    這裡太陽下山以後會冷很多。
  5. The pain got a lot worse after I carried those boxes.
    我搬了那些箱子以後，痛得厲害很多。
  6. Ken's handwriting has got a lot neater this year.
    Ken 的字今年整齊很多。
  7. The streets get a lot busier at the weekend.
    週末街上熱鬧很多。
