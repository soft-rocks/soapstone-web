Have you ever checked your blood pressure? I think you should.

他想再跟她見面，但他知道自己不應該。

主⁠詞 + should.

should / shouldn't後面的動詞省略。
省略的部分前面已經說過，聽的人知道是什麼。

用在故⁠事⁠裡

例句

He wanted to call her, but he knew he shouldn't.

他想打給她，可是他知道他不應該打。

故事常用 he knew he shouldn't 寫人物心裡的掙扎。

回⁠答問⁠題

Have you ever checked your blood pressure? I think you should.

例句

"Should I tell him?" "Yes, you should."

「我該告訴他嗎？」「對，你應該說。」

回答 Should I …？ 時，常只說 should 或 shouldn't。

should 後⁠面不⁠接 to

Have you ever checked your blood pressure? I think you should.

I think you should to.

I think you should.

用 should 問，就⁠用 should 回⁠答

Have you ever checked your blood pressure? I think you should.

"Should I go?" "Yes, you do."

"Should I go?" "Yes, you should."

動⁠詞省⁠略以⁠後，句⁠子在 shouldn't 結⁠束

He wanted to reply, but he knew he shouldn't it.

He wanted to reply, but he knew he shouldn't.

例句

① 問句 + I think you should.：建議對方去做

  1. You get headaches every day. Have you ever checked your blood pressure? I think you should.
    你每天都頭痛。你有沒有量過血壓？我想你應該量一下。
  2. Have you told your parents about the new job? I think you should.
    你有沒有把新工作的事告訴你爸媽？我想你應該說。
  3. Have you ever tried yoga? I think you should. It helps with back pain.
    你試過瑜伽嗎？我想你應該試試。它對背痛有幫助。
  4. Have you read the contract carefully? I really think you should.
    你有沒有仔細讀合約？我真的覺得你應該讀。
  5. Have you booked your flight yet? I think you should. Prices are rising.
    你訂機票了嗎？我想你應該訂了。價格在上漲。
  6. Have you ever been to the dentist about that tooth? I think you should.
    你有沒有為那顆牙去看過牙醫？我想你應該去。

② 主詞 + wanted to …, but + 主詞 + knew + 主詞 + shouldn't.

  1. John spent the day thinking. He wanted to answer her letter and meet her again, but he knew that he shouldn't.
    約翰想了一整天。他想回她的信，再跟她見面，可是他知道他不應該。
  2. She wanted to eat the last biscuit, but she knew she shouldn't.
    她想吃最後一塊餅乾，可是她知道她不應該。
  3. He wanted to look at her diary, but he knew he shouldn't.
    他想看她的日記，可是他知道他不應該。
  4. I wanted to buy the expensive coat, but I knew I shouldn't.
    我想買那件很貴的外套，可是我知道我不應該。
  5. They wanted to swim in the lake, but they knew they shouldn't.
    他們想在湖裡游泳，可是他們知道他們不應該。
  6. We wanted to stay out all night, but we knew we shouldn't.
    我們想整晚待在外面，可是我們知道我們不應該。

③ "Should + 主詞 + …？" "Yes, 主詞 + should."

  1. "Should I call the doctor?" "Yes, you should. You've had a fever for three days."
    我該打電話給醫生嗎？對，你應該打。你已經發燒三天了。
  2. "Should we invite your cousins?" "Yes, we should. They invited us last year."
    我們該邀請你的表親嗎？對，我們應該邀。他們去年邀請過我們。
  3. "Should I apologise to her?" "I think you should."
    我該跟她道歉嗎？我想你應該。
  4. "Should we take the early train?" "Yes, we should, if we want seats."
    我們該搭早班火車嗎？對，如果我們想要有位子的話。
  5. "Should she take the job?" "I think she should."
    她該接受那份工作嗎？我想她應該接受。
  6. "Should I bring a present?" "Yes, you should. It's her birthday."
    我該帶禮物嗎？對，你應該帶。今天是她生日。

④ "Should + 主詞 + …？" "No, 主詞 + shouldn't."

  1. "Should I drive home?" "No, you shouldn't. You're too tired."
    我該開車回家嗎？不，你不應該。你太累了。
  2. "Should we tell Dad?" "No, we shouldn't. Not yet."
    我們該告訴爸爸嗎？不，我們不應該。還不要。
  3. "Should I lend him the money?" "I don't think you should."
    我該借他錢嗎？我覺得你不應該。
  4. "Should we go swimming?" "No, we shouldn't. There's a storm coming."
    我們該去游泳嗎？不，我們不應該。暴風雨要來了。
  5. "Should I eat this old yoghurt?" "No, you shouldn't!"
    我該吃這個過期的優格嗎？不，你不應該吃！
  6. "Should they sell the farm?" "I don't think they should."
    他們該賣掉農場嗎？我覺得他們不應該。

⑤ 主詞 + 動詞 + …, but + 主詞 + should / shouldn't.

  1. I don't exercise much, but I know I should.
    我不常運動，可是我知道我應該運動。
  2. He doesn't wear a helmet, but he should.
    他沒戴安全帽，可是他應該戴。
  3. She smokes, but she knows she shouldn't.
    她抽菸，可是她知道她不應該。
  4. We don't visit Grandma often, but we should.
    我們不常去看奶奶，可是我們應該去。
  5. He talks on the phone while driving, but he shouldn't.
    他開車的時候講電話，可是他不應該。
  6. I don't save much money, but I should.
    我存的錢不多，可是我應該多存。
  7. They leave the lights on all night, but they shouldn't.
    他們整晚都開著燈，可是他們不應該。
