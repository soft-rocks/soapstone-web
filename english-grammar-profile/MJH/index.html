If you'd rather go with your friend, you ought to talk to your parents first.

我覺得你週末應該去打網球或踢足球。

You + ought to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

you ought to、I think、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

you ought to你應該。
I think放在前面，讓建議更客氣。
原形動詞建議的事。

加⁠上 first

If you'd rather go with your friend, you ought to talk to your parents first.

例句

You want to change your essay topic? You ought to ask your teacher first.

你想換作文題目？你應該先問你的老師。

建議做事的順序時，常在句尾加 first。

ought 後⁠面要接 to

If you'd rather go with your friend, you ought to talk to your parents first.

Your neighbours' music keeps you awake. You ought talk to them.

Your neighbours' music keeps you awake. You ought to talk to them.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

If you'd rather go with your friend, you ought to talk to your parents first.

You've got a high temperature. I think you ought to went home.

You've got a high temperature. I think you ought to go home.

ought to 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠再⁠加 to

If you'd rather go with your friend, you ought to talk to your parents first.

You've run three races this week. You ought to to rest your legs.

You've run three races this week. You ought to rest your legs.

例句

① you ought to + 原形動詞 + first：先做的事

  1. If you'd rather go with your friend, you ought to talk to your parents first and tell them how you feel.
    如果你比較想跟朋友去，你應該先跟你爸媽談一談，告訴他們你的感受。
  2. Before you build the bookcase, you ought to read the instructions first.
    組書櫃之前，你應該先讀說明書。
  3. If you want Friday off, you ought to ask your boss first.
    如果你星期五想請假，你應該先問你的老闆。
  4. Those boots are two hundred pounds, so you ought to try them on first.
    那雙靴子要兩百英鎊，所以你應該先試穿。
  5. Before you climb Snowdon on Saturday, you ought to check the forecast first.
    你星期六爬 Snowdon 山之前，應該先看一下天氣預報。
  6. If you want to travel round Asia next year, you ought to save some money first.
    如果你明年想去亞洲旅行，你應該先存一些錢。

② I think you ought to + 原形動詞：客氣的建議

  1. You sit at your computer all week. I think you ought to play tennis or football at the weekend.
    你整個星期都坐在電腦前。我想你週末應該去打網球或踢足球。
  2. You shouted at your sister this morning. I think you ought to apologise to her.
    你今天早上對你妹妹大吼。我想你應該跟她道歉。
  3. You look exhausted after the trip. I think you ought to go to bed early tonight.
    旅行回來你看起來好累。我想你今天晚上應該早點睡。
  4. You've just moved here and you don't know anyone. I think you ought to join a club to meet new people.
    你剛搬來這裡，誰都不認識。我想你應該加入一個社團，認識新朋友。
  5. The traffic to Manchester is terrible on Fridays. I think you ought to take the train.
    星期五去曼徹斯特的路很塞。我想你應該搭火車。
  6. That's an expensive camera. I think you ought to keep the receipt.
    那是一台很貴的相機。我想你應該留著收據。

③ you ought to + 原形動詞：關於健康

  1. Headaches can come from not drinking enough. You ought to drink more water.
    頭痛可能是喝水不夠。你應該多喝水。
  2. Your dentist found three holes in your teeth. You ought to stop eating so much sugar.
    你的牙醫發現你有三顆蛀牙。你應該少吃一點糖。
  3. Your office is only a mile away. You ought to walk to work instead of driving.
    你的辦公室只有一英里遠。你應該走路上班，不要開車。
  4. The doctor says it's only a sprain. You ought to rest your leg for a few days.
    醫生說只是扭傷。你的腳應該休息幾天。
  5. The sun is very strong in Spain in August. You ought to wear sun cream at the beach.
    八月西班牙的太陽很大。你在海灘應該擦防曬乳。
  6. You've been studying until three every night. You ought to get more sleep before exams.
    你每天晚上都讀到三點。考試前你應該多睡一點。

④ you ought to + 原形動詞：關於學習和工作

  1. Your grammar is good, but you're shy when you talk. You ought to practise speaking English every day.
    你的文法很好，可是你說話很害羞。你應該每天練習說英文。
  2. The new topic is hard. You ought to ask questions if you don't understand.
    新的單元很難。如果你不懂，你應該發問。
  3. The museum needs a guide who speaks three languages. You ought to apply for that job.
    博物館需要會說三種語言的導覽員。你應該去應徵那份工作。
  4. You keep forgetting new words. You ought to write them down in a notebook.
    你一直忘記新單字。你應該把它們寫在筆記本裡。
  5. Your essay is due on Wednesday. You ought to start it this weekend.
    你的作文星期三要交。你這個週末應該開始寫。
  6. You don't know what to study at university? You ought to talk to a careers adviser.
    你不知道大學要念什麼？你應該找職涯顧問談談。

⑤ you ought to + 原形動詞 + because / so + 子句

  1. Want to see the band in May? You ought to book early because the tickets sell out fast.
    想看五月那個樂團的表演？你應該早點訂，因為票賣得很快。
  2. The desert is hot in the day, but you ought to take a jacket because it gets cold at night.
    沙漠白天很熱，可是你應該帶件外套，因為晚上會變冷。
  3. The train to Edinburgh takes five hours, so you ought to bring something to read.
    去愛丁堡的火車要五個小時，所以你應該帶點東西讀。
  4. Your mum phoned me twice today. You ought to call her because she's worried about you.
    你媽媽今天打給我兩次。你應該打給她，因為她很擔心你。
  5. It was minus five last night. The roads are icy, so you ought to drive slowly.
    昨晚零下五度。路上結冰，所以你應該開慢一點。
  6. The new space film is out this week. You ought to see it because you love science fiction.
    新的太空電影這個星期上映。你應該去看，因為你很喜歡科幻片。
  7. Welcome to the city centre. You ought to lock your bike because things get stolen here.
    歡迎來到市中心。你應該把腳踏車鎖好，因為這裡常有東西被偷。（被動）
