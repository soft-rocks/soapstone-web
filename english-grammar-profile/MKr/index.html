例句

① 家人 + 's

  1. Please help me find it. It's very important to me because it's my sister's jacket.
    請幫我找。它對我來說很重要，因為那是我姊姊的外套。
  2. I'm wearing my brother's football shirt.
    我穿著我哥哥的足球衣。
  3. My mum's birthday is in May.
    我媽媽的生日在五月。
  4. We stayed at my uncle's house for a week.
    我們在我叔叔家住了一個星期。
  5. My grandfather's garden has lots of apple trees.
    我爺爺的花園有很多蘋果樹。

② 人名 + 's

  1. I bought some new shoes. I'll wear them at Linda's party.
    我買了新鞋。我要穿去 Linda 的派對。
  2. Somebody took Tom's lunch from the fridge.
    有人從冰箱拿走了 Tom 的午餐。
  3. We're going to Anna's house after school.
    我們放學後要去 Anna 家。
  4. Mr Brown's class is in Room 12.
    Brown 老師的班在十二號教室。

③ 其他單數名詞 + 's

  1. Don't sit there. That's the teacher's chair.
    別坐那裡。那是老師的椅子。
  2. The dog's bed is in the kitchen.
    狗的床在廚房。
  3. My friend's flat is very small.
    我朋友的公寓很小。
  4. The shop's windows are full of Christmas lights.
    那家店的櫥窗掛滿了聖誕燈。

④ 寫給朋友的信

  1. I'm writing this on my cousin's laptop.
    我在用我表姊的筆電寫這封信。
  2. I went to my best friend's wedding last Saturday.
    我上星期六去了我最好朋友的婚禮。
  3. I'm staying at my grandma's house this weekend.
    我這個週末住在奶奶家。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. My aunt's dog is bigger than me!
    我阿姨的狗比我還大！
  2. The cat's food is in the cupboard.
    貓的食物在櫃子裡。
  3. I found my neighbour's keys in the street.
    我在街上撿到我鄰居的鑰匙。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Whose phone is this?" "It's Sam's phone."
    這是誰的手機？是 Sam 的手機。
  2. "Can I use your computer?" "It's not mine. It's my brother's computer."
    我可以用你的電腦嗎？那不是我的。是我哥哥的電腦。
  3. "Is that your bag?" "No, it's my mum's bag."
    那是你的包包嗎？不是，是我媽媽的包包。
