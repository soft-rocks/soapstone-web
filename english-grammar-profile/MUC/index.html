This college is famous for its art teachers and very skilled students, and yet it does not have its own gallery.

這所學院以美術老師和技術高超的學生出名，但它卻沒有自己的展覽館。

子⁠句 + , and yet + 子⁠句

This college is famous for its art teachers and very skilled students, and yet it does not have its own gallery.

這所學院以美術老師出名，但它卻沒有自己的展覽館。

連⁠接子⁠句，也連⁠接形⁠容⁠詞

This college is famous for its art teachers and very skilled students, and yet it does not have its own gallery.

子句

The host family was carefully selected, yet it was four miles away from school.

寄宿家庭是仔細挑過的，卻離學校四英里遠。
形容詞

The design is simple yet elegant.

這個設計簡單卻高雅。

yet 和 but

連⁠接⁠詞 yet 和副⁠詞 yet

連接詞

It was late, yet nobody wanted to leave.

已經很晚了，卻沒有人想走。
副詞

I haven't finished my homework yet.

我還沒寫完功課。

and 後⁠面⁠接 yet，不⁠接 but

This college is famous for its art teachers and very skilled students, and yet it does not have its own gallery.

The room was small, and but it was very comfortable.

The room was small, and yet it was very comfortable.

yet 後⁠面不⁠再⁠加 but

This college is famous for its art teachers and very skilled students, and yet it does not have its own gallery.

He was tired, yet but he kept working.

He was tired, yet he kept working.

表⁠示更⁠正用 not A but B

The meeting is not on Monday yet on Tuesday.

The meeting is not on Monday but on Tuesday.

形⁠容⁠詞之⁠間不⁠加逗⁠號

The hotel was cheap, yet clean.

The hotel was cheap yet clean.

例句

① 子句 + , yet + 子句

  1. The host family was carefully selected, yet it was four miles away from school.
    寄宿家庭是仔細挑過的，卻離學校四英里遠。
  2. She had studied for weeks, yet she still felt nervous before the interview.
    她準備了好幾個星期，面試前還是很緊張。
  3. The restaurant looks expensive from outside, yet the prices are quite reasonable.
    這家餐廳從外面看很貴，價錢卻很合理。
  4. We left home two hours early, yet we nearly missed the flight.
    我們提早兩個小時出門，卻差點趕不上飛機。
  5. The report was carefully checked, yet it still contained several errors.
    報告仔細檢查過了，卻還是有好幾個錯誤。
  6. He grew up by the sea, yet he never learned to swim.
    他在海邊長大，卻一直沒學會游泳。

② 子句 + , and yet + 子句

  1. Our team trained every day, and yet we lost the final match.
    我們隊每天訓練，最後的決賽卻輸了。
  2. The town has two big hotels, and yet tourists often cannot find a room in summer.
    鎮上有兩家大飯店，夏天觀光客卻常常訂不到房間。
  3. I explained the rules twice, and yet some players still got them wrong.
    規則我講了兩次，還是有些選手搞錯。
  4. The company made a large profit last year, and yet it cut staff salaries.
    公司去年賺了很多錢，卻還是減了員工的薪水。
  5. She lives only a few minutes from the office, and yet she is late almost every day.
    她住的地方離辦公室只要幾分鐘，卻幾乎每天遲到。
  6. The film had a huge budget, and yet the special effects looked cheap.
    那部電影預算很高，特效看起來卻很廉價。

③ 形容詞 + yet + 形容詞

  1. The lecture was long yet never boring.
    那堂課很長，卻一點也不無聊。
  2. It was a small yet very friendly hotel near the station.
    那是車站附近一家小而很親切的旅館。
  3. Her speech was funny yet moving.
    她的演講很好笑，卻也讓人感動。
  4. The new phone is light yet strong.
    新手機很輕，卻很堅固。
  5. The old castle looked dark yet beautiful in the evening light.
    傍晚的光線裡，古堡看起來陰暗卻美麗。
  6. He gave a polite yet firm answer.
    他給了一個客氣卻堅定的回答。

④ 副詞或片語 + yet + 副詞或片語

  1. The doctor spoke slowly yet confidently.
    醫生說話很慢，卻很有把握。
  2. The plan worked well in theory yet badly in practice.
    這個計畫在理論上可行，實際做起來卻很糟。
  3. He drove carefully yet quickly through the busy streets.
    他在熱鬧的街上開得小心卻很快。
  4. The changes were introduced gradually yet steadily.
    這些改變是慢慢地、卻穩定地推行的。

⑤ yet 帶出出乎意料的結果

  1. The exam looked easy, yet only half the class passed.
    考試看起來很簡單，卻只有半班的人及格。
  2. Nobody expected him to win, yet he came first.
    沒有人覺得他會贏，他卻得了第一名。
  3. The shop was in a quiet street, yet it was always full of customers.
    那家店在一條安靜的街上，卻總是擠滿了客人。
  4. The house was very old, and yet everything in it worked perfectly.
    那棟房子很舊，裡面的東西卻都很好用。

⑥ yet 放在句首

  1. The hotel was beautiful. Yet the service was disappointing.
    飯店很漂亮，服務卻令人失望。
  2. Most people said the project would fail. Yet it was a great success.
    大多數人說這個計畫會失敗，結果卻很成功。
  3. The museum is free. Yet very few local people visit it.
    博物館不收門票，卻很少有當地人去參觀。

⑦ yet 和 but 的比較

  1. Our garden is narrow but full of flowers.
    我們的花園很窄，但種滿了花。
  2. The flat is small yet surprisingly comfortable.
    這間公寓很小，卻出乎意料地舒服。
  3. The bag isn't black but dark blue.
    這個包包不是黑色的，是深藍色的。
  4. He is rich, yet he lives in a tiny flat.
    他很有錢，卻住在一間很小的公寓。
