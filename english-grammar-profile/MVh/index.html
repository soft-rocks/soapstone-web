I shall always remember it as the city of lights.

我會永遠記得它是一座光之城。

I / we + shall + always / never + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

shall、always / never、shall not

I shall always remember it as the city of lights.

shall表示說話者的決心。這是英式英語比較正式的說法。
always / never放在 shall 後面，表示永遠會、永遠不會。
shall not說長期不會做的事。

always 和 never

例句

I shall always love this place.

我會永遠喜歡這個地方。
例句

I shall never lose hope.

我永遠不會失去希望。

長⁠期⁠的決⁠定

例句

We shall not send any more staff on that course.

我們不會再派員工去上那個課程。

主詞是 we 時，常用在公司或團體的長期決定。

always 放⁠在 shall 後⁠面、動⁠詞前⁠面

I shall always remember it as the city of lights.

I always shall remember you.

I shall always remember you.

never 已⁠經是否⁠定，不⁠再⁠加 not

I shall not never forget this.

I shall never forget this.

shall 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I shall always remember it as the city of lights.

We shall always keeps our promise.

We shall always keep our promise.

例句

① I / we + shall always + 原形動詞

  1. I shall always remember our last evening by the river.
    我會永遠記得我們在河邊的最後一個晚上。
  2. I shall always keep your letter.
    我會永遠留著你的信。
  3. We shall always be grateful for your help.
    我們會永遠感謝你的幫忙。
  4. I shall always think of you as my best friend.
    我會永遠把你當成我最好的朋友。
  5. We shall always welcome you in our home.
    我們家永遠歡迎你。
  6. I shall always try to do my best.
    我會一直盡我最大的努力。
  7. We shall always come back here for our holidays.
    我們會一直回來這裡度假。
  8. We shall always stand by you.
    我們會永遠支持你。
  9. I shall always be proud of my students.
    我會永遠以我的學生為榮。
  10. I shall always keep this lesson in mind.
    我會永遠記住這個教訓。
  11. We shall always remember the people who helped us.
    我們會永遠記得幫助過我們的人。

② I / we + shall never + 原形動詞

  1. If I believe in myself, I shall never feel lost.
    只要相信自己，我就永遠不會迷失。
  2. I shall never forget the day we met.
    我永遠不會忘記我們見面的那一天。
  3. We shall never give up on our dream.
    我們永遠不會放棄我們的夢想。
  4. I shall never trust him again.
    我永遠不會再相信他。
  5. We shall never sell this house.
    我們永遠不會賣掉這間房子。
  6. After that night, I shall never touch a cigarette again.
    那晚之後，我再也不會碰香菸了。
  7. I shall never understand why she left.
    我永遠不會明白她為什麼離開。
  8. We shall never forgive them for what they did.
    我們永遠不會原諒他們做的事。
  9. I shall never be able to thank you enough.
    我永遠感謝不完你。
  10. We shall never go back to that hotel.
    我們再也不會去那間飯店了。
  11. I shall never stop loving music.
    我永遠不會停止熱愛音樂。

③ I / we + shall not + 原形動詞

  1. We shall not send any further candidates there.
    我們不會再送任何應徵者去那裡。
  2. I shall not work for a company I don't respect.
    我不會替我不尊重的公司工作。
  3. We shall not work with that company again.
    我們不會再和那家公司合作。
  4. I shall not live in a big city.
    我不會住在大城市。
  5. We shall not repeat the mistakes of the past.
    我們不會重蹈過去的覆轍。
  6. I shall not marry for money.
    我不會為了錢結婚。
  7. We shall not buy from that shop again.
    我們不會再跟那家店買東西。
  8. I shall not let anything stop me.
    我不會讓任何事情阻止我。
  9. We shall not move from this village.
    我們不會離開這個村子。
