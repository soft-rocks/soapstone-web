例句

① might / may / could have been：不確定

  1. My wallet's gone. I don't remember how I lost it; it might have been stolen.
    我的錢包不見了。我不記得是怎麼弄丟的，可能是被偷了。
  2. "Why hasn't the parcel arrived?" "It may have been sent to our old address."
    包裹怎麼還沒到？可能寄到我們的舊地址了。
  3. Don't blame Sam for the broken cup. It could have been knocked over by the cat.
    別怪 Sam 打破杯子。可能是貓碰倒的。
  4. Nobody knows who built this wall. It might have been built by the Romans.
    沒人知道這道牆是誰蓋的。可能是羅馬人蓋的。
  5. I didn't get an answer from the bank. My email may have been deleted by mistake.
    我沒收到銀行的回覆。我的電子郵件可能被誤刪了。

② must have been：很確定

  1. The house is freezing. A window must have been left open all night.
    屋子裡好冷。一定有一扇窗戶整晚沒關。
  2. Look at the floor! The dog must have been let in with muddy feet.
    你看地板！一定有人讓狗帶著一腳泥進來了。
  3. All the cakes are gone already. They must have been eaten during the break.
    蛋糕已經全部沒了。一定是休息時間被吃掉了。
  4. He knew all the answers. He must have been told about the test.
    他每一題都會。一定有人跟他講過考試的內容。

③ can't / couldn't have been：很確定沒有

  1. The letter can't have been posted. It's still on the kitchen table.
    信不可能寄出去了。它還在餐桌上。
  2. "Was the car stolen?" "It can't have been. I've got the only key."
    車被偷了嗎？不可能。唯一的鑰匙在我這裡。
  3. Those photos couldn't have been taken in Spain. We never went there.
    那些照片不可能是在西班牙拍的。我們從來沒去過。
  4. This painting can't have been done by a child. It's far too good.
    這幅畫不可能是小孩畫的。畫得太好了。

④ should have been：應該做卻沒做

  1. Some groups were better than others, but they all played well. They should have been given a chance!
    有些團體比別人好，可是每一團都表現得不錯。應該給他們一個機會的！
  2. The bins are still full. They should have been emptied on Monday.
    垃圾桶還是滿的。星期一就應該清了。
  3. I'm sorry I missed the meeting. I should have been told about it earlier.
    很抱歉我錯過了會議。應該早點通知我的。
  4. The report should have been finished by now. What's taking so long?
    報告現在應該已經寫完了。怎麼拖這麼久？
  5. That accident was terrible. The road should have been closed after the snow.
    那場車禍很嚴重。下雪之後就該封路了。

⑤ shouldn't have been：不應該做卻做了

  1. I read the article about our school. That old photo shouldn't have been used in this kind of article.
    我看了那篇講我們學校的文章。那張舊照片不應該用在這種文章裡。
  2. The children shouldn't have been left alone in the house. They're only six.
    那些孩子不該被單獨留在家裡。他們才六歲。
  3. Our team lost because of the referee. That goal shouldn't have been allowed.
    我們隊因為裁判輸了。那一球不該算進。
  4. My shirt is pink now. It shouldn't have been washed with the red towels.
    我的襯衫變粉紅色了。它不該跟紅毛巾一起洗。

⑥ could have been：本來可能發生，可是沒有發生

  1. The concert was a disaster. I think all these problems could have been avoided.
    那場音樂會糟透了。我覺得這些問題本來都可以避免。
  2. Nobody was hurt in the fire, but people could have been killed.
    火災裡沒有人受傷，可是本來可能會有人喪命。
  3. The project was finished in two weeks. It could have been done in one if we'd started earlier.
    這個計畫兩個星期做完。如果早點開始，一個星期就可以做完。
  4. Thanks for calling the doctor. My dad could have been seriously hurt.
    謝謝你叫了醫生。我爸爸本來可能傷得很重。

⑦ 問句和回答

  1. "Could the money have been taken by someone in the office?" "I hope not."
    錢可能是辦公室裡的人拿走的嗎？希望不是。
  2. "Should the bill have been paid last week?" "Yes, I forgot. I'll pay it today."
    帳單是不是上星期就該付了？對，我忘了。我今天付。
  3. "Where's my blue jacket?" "It might have been given away with the old clothes."
    我的藍外套在哪裡？可能跟舊衣服一起送人了。
  4. "Was the meeting cancelled?" "It must have been. Nobody's here."
    會議取消了嗎？一定是。都沒有人在。
  5. "Do you think the fire was started on purpose?" "It may have been. The police are looking into it."
    你覺得火是有人故意放的嗎？可能是。警察在調查。
