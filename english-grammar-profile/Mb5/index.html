My husband is a teacher.

我先生是老師。

限⁠定詞放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

我先生是老師。

限定詞說明指的是哪一個人或東西。

a 和 the 說⁠明聽⁠話⁠的人知不⁠知⁠道是哪⁠一⁠個

My husband is a teacher.

a / an
第一次提到

There is a park near my house.

我家附近有一座公園。
the
聽話的人知道是哪一個

Can you close the window? It's cold in here.

你可以關一下窗戶嗎？這裡好冷。

所⁠有⁠格限⁠定詞說⁠明人或東⁠西屬⁠於誰

My husband is a teacher.

my

My mum is a nurse at the hospital.

我媽媽是那家醫院的護士。
their

Their children go to the same school as my son.

他們的孩子跟我兒子上同一所學校。

this 和 that 接單⁠數名⁠詞，these 和 those 接複⁠數名⁠詞

this + 單數名詞

This bag is very heavy. Can you help me?

這個袋子好重。你可以幫我一下嗎？
those + 複數名詞

Those shoes are too small for me.

那雙鞋我穿太小了。

單⁠數可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有限⁠定詞

My husband is a teacher.

My sister is doctor.

My sister is a doctor.

名⁠詞⁠的發⁠音以母⁠音開⁠頭⁠時用 an

My husband is a teacher.

I eat a apple every morning.

I eat an apple every morning.

複⁠數名⁠詞前⁠面用 these 或 those

I like this shoes.

I like these shoes.

一⁠個名⁠詞前⁠面只⁠放一⁠個限⁠定詞

My husband is a teacher.

This is the my bag.

This is my bag.

例句

① a / an + 單數名詞

  1. Look, there's a cat on our car again.
    你看，又有一隻貓在我們的車上。
  2. My brother works in a bank in the city centre.
    我哥哥在市中心的一家銀行上班。
  3. Can I have an orange juice, please?
    請給我一杯柳橙汁。
  4. We need an umbrella today. It's going to rain.
    我們今天要帶傘，會下雨。
  5. My daughter wants a bike for her birthday.
    我女兒生日想要一台腳踏車。

② the + 名詞

  1. The bathroom is on the left, next to the kitchen.
    浴室在左邊，廚房旁邊。
  2. Can you close the window? It's cold in here.
    你可以關一下窗戶嗎？這裡好冷。
  3. The children are in the garden with their dad.
    孩子們跟他們的爸爸在院子裡。
  4. Excuse me, is the post office open on Saturday afternoons?
    不好意思，郵局星期六下午有開嗎？

③ 所有格限定詞 + 名詞

  1. My mum is a nurse at the hospital.
    我媽媽是那家醫院的護士。
  2. What's your phone number? I'll call you tonight.
    你的電話號碼是多少？我今晚打給你。
  3. Her parents live in a small village by the sea.
    她爸媽住在海邊的一個小村子。
  4. Our teacher gives us homework every day.
    我們老師每天都出功課給我們。
  5. The neighbours are on holiday, and their dog is staying with us.
    鄰居去度假了，他們的狗住在我們家。
  6. His sister plays the guitar in a band.
    他姊姊在一個樂團彈吉他。

④ this / that + 單數名詞

  1. This bag is very heavy. Can you help me?
    這個袋子好重。你可以幫我一下嗎？
  2. Mum, who's that woman in the blue coat?
    媽，那個穿藍色大衣的女人是誰？
  3. I want to buy this book. How much is it?
    我想買這本書。多少錢？
  4. That restaurant across the road is very good.
    對面那家餐廳很好吃。

⑤ these / those + 複數名詞

  1. These apples are from my grandma's garden.
    這些蘋果是我奶奶院子裡種的。
  2. Are those keys yours? They were on the table.
    那些鑰匙是你的嗎？剛剛放在桌上。
  3. I love these photos of your trip.
    我很喜歡你旅行的這些照片。
  4. Those boys are in my class.
    那幾個男生跟我同班。

⑥ 名詞片語當主詞和補語

  1. My husband is a teacher at a primary school.
    我先生是小學老師。
  2. Our flat is near the station.
    我們的公寓在車站附近。
  3. Your brother is a really good cook.
    你哥哥很會做菜。
  4. That's why I like this place.
    所以我才喜歡這個地方。

⑦ 複數名詞前面的 the 和所有格限定詞

  1. The shops close at six on Sundays here.
    這裡的商店星期天六點就關門了。
  2. My friends are coming for dinner tonight.
    我朋友今晚要來吃晚餐。
  3. Please put your bags under the seats.
    請把你們的包包放在座位下面。
  4. Their children go to the same school as my son.
    他們的孩子跟我兒子上同一所學校。
