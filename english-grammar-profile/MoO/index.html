When you come back, we should rent some films, shouldn't we?

等你回來，我們應該租幾部電影來看，對吧？

主⁠詞 + should + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …, shouldn't + 代⁠名⁠詞？

When you come back, we should rent some films, shouldn't we?

前面的句子肯定句，用 should。
附加問句用否定的 shouldn't 加代名詞。
代名詞跟前面的主詞一樣，像 we、I、you。

問意⁠見

例句

I should call her, shouldn't I?

我應該打給她，對吧？

I should …, shouldn't I? 用來問對方，自己的想法對不對。

附⁠加問⁠句⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用主⁠格 we

When you come back, we should rent some films, shouldn't we?

We should go, shouldn't us?

We should go, shouldn't we?#2

附⁠加問⁠句用代⁠名⁠詞

Tom should come, shouldn't Tom?

Tom should come, shouldn't he?

附⁠加問⁠句重⁠複 should，不⁠用 do

When you come back, we should rent some films, shouldn't we?

I should write about our history, don't I?

I should write about our history, shouldn't I?

例句

① we should …, shouldn't we?：提議一起做

  1. I'll miss you while you're away. When you come back, we should rent some films, shouldn't we?
    你不在的時候我會想你。等你回來，我們應該租幾部電影來看，對吧？
  2. It's such a sunny day. We should go to the beach, shouldn't we?
    今天好晴朗。我們應該去海灘，對吧？
  3. Mum is very tired. We should cook dinner tonight, shouldn't we?
    媽媽很累。我們今天晚上應該煮晚餐，對吧？
  4. The tickets are selling fast. We should book now, shouldn't we?
    票賣得很快。我們應該現在訂，對吧？
  5. We haven't seen Grandma for ages. We should visit her this weekend, shouldn't we?
    我們好久沒見到奶奶了。我們這個週末應該去看她，對吧？
  6. The kitchen is a mess. We should clean it before Dad gets home, shouldn't we?
    廚房一團亂。我們應該在爸爸回家前打掃，對吧？

② I should …, shouldn't I?：問自己的想法對不對

  1. The report is for our new customers. I think I should write about the history of our company, shouldn't I?
    這份報告是給新客戶看的。我想我應該寫公司的歷史，對吧？
  2. She's been ill all week. I should phone her, shouldn't I?
    她病了一整個星期。我應該打電話給她，對吧？
  3. It's a formal dinner. I should wear a suit, shouldn't I?
    那是正式晚宴。我應該穿西裝，對吧？
  4. The essay is due on Monday. I should start it today, shouldn't I?
    作文星期一要交。我應該今天開始寫，對吧？
  5. He helped me a lot. I should buy him a present, shouldn't I?
    他幫了我很多。我應該買個禮物給他，對吧？
  6. My tooth still hurts. I should see a dentist, shouldn't I?
    我的牙還在痛。我應該去看牙醫，對吧？

③ you should …, shouldn't you?：提醒對方

  1. Your train leaves at six. You should go now, shouldn't you?
    你的火車六點開。你應該現在走了，對吧？
  2. You've got an exam tomorrow. You should be in bed, shouldn't you?
    你明天要考試。你應該在床上了，對吧？
  3. It's very cold outside. You should take a scarf, shouldn't you?
    外面很冷。你應該帶條圍巾，對吧？
  4. You borrowed her bike. You should give it back, shouldn't you?
    你借了她的腳踏車。你應該還給她，對吧？
  5. You broke the vase. You should tell Mum, shouldn't you?
    你打破了花瓶。你應該告訴媽媽，對吧？
  6. You're the captain. You should speak first, shouldn't you?
    你是隊長。你應該先發言，對吧？

④ he / she / they should …, shouldn't + 代名詞？

  1. Tom knows the area well. He should be our guide, shouldn't he?
    湯姆很熟悉這一帶。他應該當我們的嚮導，對吧？
  2. Anna is the best singer. She should sing the first song, shouldn't she?
    安娜唱得最好。她應該唱第一首歌，對吧？
  3. The children are tired. They should have a rest, shouldn't they?
    孩子們累了。他們應該休息一下，對吧？
  4. The new boss doesn't know anyone. She should meet the whole team, shouldn't she?
    新老闆誰都不認識。她應該跟整個團隊見個面，對吧？
  5. The builders are late again. They should call us, shouldn't they?
    工人又遲到了。他們應該打電話給我們，對吧？
  6. My brother is good with computers. He should fix it, shouldn't he?
    我哥哥很會用電腦。應該由他來修，對吧？

⑤ There should be …, shouldn't there?

  1. It's a big station. There should be a café, shouldn't there?
    那是一個大車站。應該有咖啡館，對吧？
  2. We ordered enough. There should be food for everyone, shouldn't there?
    我們訂了很多。應該夠每個人吃，對吧？
  3. It's a long walk. There should be a bench somewhere on the way, shouldn't there?
    要走很久。路上應該有長椅，對吧？
  4. The museum is popular. There should be a guide, shouldn't there?
    那間博物館很受歡迎。應該有導覽員，對吧？
  5. It's a new hotel. There should be a pool, shouldn't there?
    那是一家新飯店。應該有游泳池，對吧？
  6. The match starts soon. There should be more people here, shouldn't there?
    比賽快開始了。這裡應該有更多人，對吧？
  7. The bus is late. There should be another one soon, shouldn't there?
    公車誤點了。應該很快會有下一班，對吧？
