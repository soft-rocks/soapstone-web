Actually, I'd arranged an appointment with my doctor before calling you.

打電話給你之前，我已經跟醫生約好看診時間了。

主⁠詞 + had + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 + before + ⁠-⁠ing / 子⁠句

had + 過去分詞比另一件過去的事更早發生。
before在……之前。

回⁠頭想過⁠去⁠的事

例句

He then thought how stupid he had been.

他接著想到自己剛才多麼愚蠢。

thought how … had been 說人物回頭想之前的事。

不⁠需⁠要過⁠去完⁠成⁠式⁠的時⁠候

Actually, I'd arranged an appointment with my doctor before calling you.

例句

I called you after I arrived.

我到了以後打給你。

兩件事用 before 或 after 清楚說出順序時，也可以用過去式。

說過⁠去之⁠前⁠的事用 had

I has arranged an appointment before I called you.

I had arranged an appointment before I called you.

before 後⁠面⁠接 ⁠-⁠ing 或子⁠句

Actually, I'd arranged an appointment with my doctor before calling you.

I'd booked a table before call you.

I'd booked a table before calling you.

had 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

He realised he had took the wrong bag.

He realised he had taken the wrong bag.

例句

① 主詞 + 'd + 過去分詞 + before + -ing

  1. I'm so sorry about Friday. Actually, I'd arranged an appointment with my doctor before calling you.
    星期五的事真的很抱歉。其實我在打電話給你之前，就已經跟醫生約好了。
  2. She'd checked the weather before leaving the house.
    她出門之前已經看過天氣了。
  3. We'd packed everything before going to bed.
    我們睡覺之前已經把東西都收好了。
  4. He'd read the whole book before seeing the film.
    他看電影之前已經讀完整本書了。
  5. I'd saved enough money before booking the flight.
    我訂機票之前已經存夠了錢。
  6. They'd cleaned the house before inviting the guests.
    他們邀請客人之前已經打掃好房子了。

② 主詞 + thought / realised + how / what + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞

  1. He shouted at his best friend and walked away. He then thought how stupid he had been.
    他對他最好的朋友大吼，然後走開了。他接著想到自己剛才多麼愚蠢。
  2. She realised what a mistake she had made.
    她發現自己犯了多大的錯。
  3. I remembered how happy we had been in that little flat.
    我想起我們在那間小公寓裡曾經多麼快樂。
  4. He suddenly understood what his father had meant.
    他突然明白他爸爸當時的意思。
  5. They saw how much the town had changed.
    他們看到這個鎮改變了多少。
  6. I wondered why she had left so early.
    我想知道她為什麼那麼早走。

③ 主詞 + 過去式 + that + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞：發現之前的事

  1. When I got to the airport, I realised that I had left my passport at home.
    我到機場的時候，發現我把護照留在家裡了。
  2. She found out that her brother had borrowed her car.
    她發現她哥哥借走了她的車。
  3. We heard that the old cinema had closed.
    我們聽說那家老電影院關了。
  4. He noticed that someone had opened his letters.
    他注意到有人拆了他的信。
  5. I discovered that I had paid twice for the same thing.
    我發現同一樣東西我付了兩次錢。
  6. They learned that the train had been cancelled.
    他們得知火車取消了。（被動）

④ Before + 主詞 + 過去式, + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞

  1. Before I moved to London, I had never lived in a big city.
    我搬到倫敦之前，從來沒住過大城市。
  2. Before the guests arrived, we had set the table.
    客人到之前，我們已經擺好了餐具。
  3. Before she became a doctor, she had studied for seven years.
    她當醫生之前讀了七年書。
  4. Before the storm came, the farmers had brought in the sheep.
    暴風雨來之前，農夫們已經把羊趕回來了。
  5. Before he left, he had said goodbye to everyone.
    他離開之前已經跟每個人道別了。
  6. Before the exam started, I had read my notes twice.
    考試開始之前，我已經把筆記讀了兩遍。

⑤ 主詞 + 過去式 + 名詞 + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞：說之前的東西

  1. I wore the dress my grandmother had made for me.
    我穿了我奶奶為我做的那件洋裝。
  2. She showed me the photos she had taken in Italy.
    她給我看她在義大利拍的照片。
  3. We ate the cake my sister had baked that morning.
    我們吃了我姊姊那天早上烤的蛋糕。
  4. He finally found the keys he had lost the week before.
    他終於找到了他前一個星期弄丟的鑰匙。
  5. I read the letter my friend had sent from Canada.
    我讀了我朋友從加拿大寄來的信。
  6. They returned the books they had borrowed from the library.
    他們把從圖書館借的書還回去了。
  7. She sold the bike she had bought only a year before.
    她賣掉了她一年前才買的腳踏車。
