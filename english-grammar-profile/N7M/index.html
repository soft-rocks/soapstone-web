例句

① This is + 名詞

  1. This is the city where my parents first met.
    這是我爸媽第一次見面的城市。
  2. This is the famous bridge from the film.
    這就是電影裡那座有名的橋。
  3. This is our classroom. It's on the second floor.
    這是我們的教室，在二樓。
  4. This is the best ice cream I've ever had.
    這是我吃過最好吃的冰淇淋。
  5. This is my grandmother's old house.
    這是我奶奶的老房子。

② This + 動詞 + 形容詞

  1. This looks delicious. Did you make it?
    這看起來好好吃，是你做的嗎？
  2. This seems a bit too small for me.
    這個對我來說好像有點太小。
  3. This smells wonderful. What is it?
    這聞起來好香，是什麼？
  4. This was very expensive, so be careful with it.
    這個很貴，所以小心點。
  5. This sounds great. Who's singing?
    這首歌聽起來很棒，是誰唱的？

③ this 當受詞

  1. Hold this while I tie my shoe.
    我綁鞋帶的時候，幫我拿著這個。
  2. Please give this to your teacher.
    請把這個交給你的老師。
  3. Do you want this? I don't need it.
    你要這個嗎？我用不到。
  4. I'll take this. How much is it?
    我要買這個，多少錢？
  5. Smell this! It's fresh bread.
    聞聞看！是剛出爐的麵包。

④ 問句

  1. What's this? It looks like a key.
    這是什麼？看起來像一把鑰匙。
  2. Is this the way to the beach?
    這是往海灘的路嗎？
  3. Whose is this? It was on my desk.
    這是誰的？它在我的書桌上。
  4. Is this what you ordered?
    這是你點的東西嗎？
  5. Who gave you this? It's beautiful.
    這是誰送你的？好漂亮。

⑤ 介系詞後面

  1. I'm very happy about this.
    我對這件事很開心。
  2. My dad made a table out of this.
    我爸爸用這個做了一張桌子。
  3. I paid only five pounds for this.
    這個我只花了五英鎊。
  4. Can you help me with this? It's very heavy.
    你可以幫我搬這個嗎？很重。
  5. I've never seen anything like this before.
    我以前從來沒看過這樣的東西。

⑥ 明信片

  1. This is a very interesting place. There are lots of old buildings.
    這是一個很有意思的地方，有很多老建築。
  2. This is the view from our hotel window.
    這是從我們旅館窗戶看出去的風景。
  3. This is the market where we buy our fruit every morning.
    這是我們每天早上買水果的市場。
  4. This is a photo of the castle. It's eight hundred years old!
    這是城堡的照片，它有八百年歷史了！
  5. This is a lovely little town, and the people are very friendly.
    這是一個可愛的小鎮，這裡的人很友善。
  6. This is our last day here. I don't want to go home!
    今天是我們在這裡的最後一天，我不想回家！
