In my opinion, a car can't be harmful in itself.

我認為汽車本身不可能有害。

in itself 說這⁠件⁠事本⁠身

我認為汽車本身不可能有害。

說的是汽車這個東西，不是開車的人。

in itself 放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞或名⁠詞片⁠語後⁠面，也⁠可⁠以緊⁠接在主⁠詞後⁠面

In my opinion, a car can't be harmful in itself.

形容詞

The plan is not wrong in itself; we just started it too late.

這個計畫本身沒錯，我們只是太晚開始。
名詞片語

Finishing the marathon was an achievement in itself.

跑完馬拉松本身就是一項成就。
主詞後面

Money in itself doesn't make people happy.

錢本身不會讓人快樂。

句⁠子常接⁠著說問⁠題出在別⁠的地⁠方

In my opinion, a car can't be harmful in itself.

對比

Social media isn't dangerous in itself. It depends on how you use it.

社群媒體本身並不危險，要看你怎麼用。

複⁠數名⁠詞用 in themselves

In my opinion, a car can't be harmful in itself.

These problems are not serious in itself.

These problems are not serious in themselves.

固⁠定說⁠法是 in itself，不⁠是 in it

In my opinion, a car can't be harmful in itself.

The plan isn't bad in it.

The plan isn't bad in itself.

表⁠示本⁠身⁠的固⁠定說⁠法用介⁠系⁠詞 in

In my opinion, a car can't be harmful in itself.

Money on itself won't solve the problem.

Money in itself won't solve the problem.

例句

① 形容詞 + in itself

  1. Eating chocolate isn't bad in itself, but you shouldn't eat a whole bar every day.
    吃巧克力本身不是壞事，可是你不該每天吃一整條。
  2. The job was boring in itself, but I liked my colleagues.
    那份工作本身很無聊，可是我喜歡我的同事。
  3. A little stress is useful in itself; it helps you focus.
    一點壓力本身是有用的，能幫你專心。
  4. The mistake wasn't serious in itself, but it caused a lot of confusion.
    這個錯誤本身不嚴重，可是造成了很多混亂。
  5. Is technology harmful in itself, or only when people misuse it?
    科技本身有害嗎？還是只有在人們誤用的時候才有害？
  6. Spending time with your family is important in itself.
    和家人相處本身就很重要。
  7. Criticism isn't unkind in itself, as long as it's fair.
    批評本身並不刻薄，只要它是公正的。
  8. Waiting for the results was stressful in itself.
    等結果這件事本身就很有壓力。
  9. The novel is interesting in itself, but the film adds a lot.
    這本小說本身很有趣，可是電影又加了很多東西。
  10. Borrowing money isn't wrong in itself. Just make sure you can pay it back.
    借錢本身沒有錯，只要確定你還得起就好。
  11. A quiet evening at home can be relaxing in itself.
    在家度過一個安靜的晚上本身就很放鬆。

② a / an + 名詞 + in itself

  1. Finding a parking space in this city is a challenge in itself.
    在這個城市找到停車位本身就是一項挑戰。
  2. Getting to the island is an adventure in itself. You need two boats and a bus.
    去那座島本身就是一趟冒險，你要搭兩班船和一班公車。
  3. Being invited to an interview is a good sign in itself.
    被邀請去面試本身就是個好兆頭。
  4. For my grandmother, using a smartphone is a big step in itself.
    對我奶奶來說，用智慧型手機本身就是一大步。
  5. Learning a new language is a reward in itself.
    學一門新語言本身就是一種收穫。
  6. The walk up to the castle is a workout in itself.
    走上城堡本身就是一種運動。
  7. Writing the grant application was a full-time job in itself.
    寫那份補助申請書本身就像一份全職工作。
  8. Meeting the president was an honour in itself.
    能見到總統本身就是一種榮幸。
  9. Keeping a garden alive in this heat is an art in itself.
    在這麼熱的天氣讓花園保持生機本身就是一門學問。
  10. Getting five kids out the door by eight is a small miracle in itself.
    八點前讓五個孩子都出門本身就是個小奇蹟。
  11. Just being nominated for the award was a victory in itself.
    光是獲得提名本身就是一種勝利。

③ 主詞 + in itself

  1. The idea in itself isn't bad, but it would cost too much.
    這個想法本身不壞，可是會花太多錢。
  2. A degree in itself won't get you a job; you need experience too.
    學位本身不會讓你找到工作，你還需要經驗。
  3. The law in itself is fair. The problem is how the police enforce it.
    這條法律本身是公平的，問題在於警察怎麼執行。
  4. This medicine in itself is safe, but don't take it with alcohol.
    這種藥本身很安全，可是不要和酒一起吃。
  5. Exercise in itself won't help you lose weight unless you also eat well.
    運動本身不會讓你變瘦，除非你也吃得健康。
  6. The house in itself wasn't special, but the view was amazing.
    房子本身沒什麼特別，可是景色很棒。
  7. Failure in itself is nothing to be ashamed of.
    失敗本身沒什麼好丟臉的。
  8. The app in itself is free; you only pay for the extra features.
    這個應用程式本身是免費的，你只需要付額外功能的錢。
  9. A long flight in itself doesn't bother me. It's the jet lag I hate.
    長途飛行本身不會讓我困擾，我討厭的是時差。
  10. Does the treatment in itself carry any risk, doctor?
    醫生，這個療法本身有任何風險嗎？
