I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

希望您能仔細看看隨信附上的文件。

副⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在動⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞中⁠間

I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

希望您能仔細看看隨信附上的文件。

副詞說明動作怎麼做。

副⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面或動⁠詞前⁠面

I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

動詞和介系詞中間

Please listen carefully to the instructions.

請仔細聽說明。
受詞後面

Please listen to the instructions carefully.

請把說明仔細聽清楚。
動詞前面

She quickly looked at her phone.

她很快看了一下手機。

受⁠詞很⁠長⁠時，副⁠詞常放⁠在動⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞中⁠間

I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

我們得好好處理上個月客人的投訴。

副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

Please look at carefully the map.

Please look carefully at the map.

說⁠明動⁠作怎⁠麼⁠做，用副⁠詞，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

You should think serious about the offer.

You should think seriously about the offer.

介⁠系⁠詞放⁠在受⁠詞前⁠面

I hope you will look carefully at the enclosed papers.

He listened closely the teacher to.

He listened closely to the teacher.

片⁠語動⁠詞⁠的副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間，程⁠度副⁠詞常放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

片語動詞

She turned the music down quickly.

她很快把音樂調小聲。
程度副詞

I completely agree with you.

我完全同意你的看法。

例句

① look + 副詞 + at

  1. Before you sign the contract, look carefully at every page.
    簽合約之前，每一頁都要仔細看。
  2. The doctor looked closely at the cut on my arm.
    醫生仔細看了我手臂上的傷口。
  3. "Do you like my drawing?" The teacher looked quickly at it and said, "It's lovely."
    你喜歡我的畫嗎？老師很快看了一眼，說：很好看。
  4. If you look carefully at the photo, you can see a cat in the window.
    如果你仔細看這張照片，可以看到窗戶裡有一隻貓。

② deal + 副詞 + with

  1. I want to know more about horses and learn how to deal properly with them.
    我想多了解馬，學會怎麼好好照顧牠們。
  2. The new manager dealt quickly with the problem.
    新經理很快處理了這個問題。
  3. "Is the complaint finished?" "Yes, we dealt fairly with it."
    那個投訴處理完了嗎？處理完了，我們處理得很公平。
  4. Teachers have to deal patiently with noisy children.
    老師得耐心對待吵鬧的孩子。

③ listen + 副詞 + to

  1. Listen carefully to the question before you answer.
    回答之前，先仔細聽問題。
  2. The students listened closely to the guide in the museum.
    在博物館裡，學生們仔細聽導覽員說話。
  3. "Did he hear the news?" "Yes, he listened quietly to it and said nothing."
    他聽到消息了嗎？聽到了，他靜靜聽完，什麼都沒說。
  4. A good friend listens patiently to your problems.
    好朋友會耐心聽你說煩惱。

④ think + 副詞 + about

  1. "They offered me a job in Tokyo." "Think seriously about it before you say no."
    他們給了我東京的工作。拒絕之前好好想一想。
  2. We need to think carefully about the cost.
    我們得仔細考慮費用。
  3. She thought hard about the question, but she couldn't answer it.
    她努力想了這個問題，可是答不出來。

⑤ talk / speak + 副詞 + about / to

  1. In the meeting, the boss talked openly about the company's problems.
    開會時，老闆坦白談了公司的問題。
  2. My grandfather never talked much about the war.
    我爺爺從來不太談戰爭的事。
  3. The nurse spoke kindly to the frightened child.
    護士溫柔地對那個嚇壞的孩子說話。
  4. "Was the shop assistant rude?" "No, she spoke politely to everyone."
    店員很沒禮貌嗎？沒有，她對每個人都很客氣。

⑥ wait / rely / depend + 副詞 + 介系詞

  1. The dog waited patiently for its owner outside the shop.
    狗在店外耐心等主人。
  2. "Is your brother still at the hospital?" "Yes, we're waiting nervously for news."
    你哥哥還在醫院嗎？對，我們很緊張地在等消息。
  3. Many small shops rely heavily on tourists.
    很多小店很依賴觀光客。
  4. The price depends mainly on the size of the room.
    價錢主要看房間的大小。

⑦ 副詞放在受詞後面

  1. Please read the instructions and look at the diagram carefully.
    請讀說明，仔細看圖。
  2. "What did the coach say?" "He told us to listen to the referee carefully."
    教練說了什麼？他叫我們仔細聽裁判的話。
  3. They dealt with the angry customer very politely.
    他們很有禮貌地應付那位生氣的客人。

⑧ 受詞很長，副詞放在動詞和介系詞中間

  1. I hope you will look carefully at the papers that I have sent with this letter.
    希望您能仔細看看我隨信寄上的文件。
  2. We must deal seriously with the complaints from people who live near the airport.
    我們必須認真處理住在機場附近的居民的投訴。
  3. The committee will think carefully about all the ideas that students have sent in.
    委員會會仔細考慮學生們寄來的所有想法。
  4. Please listen closely to everything the flight attendant says during the safety talk.
    安全說明的時候，請仔細聽空服員說的每一句話。
  5. Visitors should look carefully at the signs that show the way to the exits.
    遊客應該仔細看指示出口方向的標誌。
