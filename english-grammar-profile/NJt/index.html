Tomorrow evening there is a Laura Pausini concert in Turin. Shall we go?

看完電影後，我們去韓國餐廳好嗎？

Shall we + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

Shall we我們要……嗎？我們……好嗎？
原形動詞建議一起做的事。

先說情⁠況，再建⁠議

Tomorrow evening there is a Laura Pausini concert in Turin. Shall we go?

例句

It's a lovely day. Shall we eat outside?

天氣很好。我們在外面吃好嗎？

常先說一個情況或消息，再用 Shall we …？ 建議。

shall 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Tomorrow evening there is a Laura Pausini concert in Turin. Shall we go?

The band is playing in Leeds on Friday. Shall we to go to the concert?

The band is playing in Leeds on Friday. Shall we go to the concert?

建⁠議⁠的問⁠句把 shall 放⁠在 we 前⁠面

Tomorrow evening there is a Laura Pausini concert in Turin. Shall we go?

There's nothing on TV tonight. We shall go to the cinema?

There's nothing on TV tonight. Shall we go to the cinema?

主⁠詞用 we，不⁠用 us

Tomorrow evening there is a Laura Pausini concert in Turin. Shall we go?

It's already eight o'clock. Shall us have dinner?

It's already eight o'clock. Shall we have dinner?

例句

① 消息 + Shall we go?：建議去活動

  1. Tomorrow evening there's a Laura Pausini concert in Turin. Shall we go?
    明天晚上在杜林有 Laura Pausini 的演唱會。我們要去嗎？
  2. There's a free jazz festival in the park this weekend. Shall we go together?
    這個週末公園有免費的爵士音樂節。我們一起去好嗎？
  3. The circus is in town until Sunday. Shall we take the kids?
    馬戲團在城裡待到星期天。我們帶孩子們去好嗎？
  4. The new art gallery opens on Friday night. Shall we go and have a look?
    新的美術館星期五晚上開幕。我們去看看好嗎？
  5. My cousin is playing in a football final on Saturday. Shall we go and cheer for him?
    我表哥星期六要踢足球決賽。我們去幫他加油好嗎？
  6. There's a market by the harbour every Sunday. Shall we go after breakfast?
    每個星期天港口邊都有市集。我們吃完早餐去好嗎？

② Shall we + 原形動詞 + after + 名詞？：建議接下來做的事

  1. The film finishes at nine, and we won't have eaten. Shall we go to a Korean restaurant after it?
    電影九點結束，我們那時還沒吃飯。看完電影我們去吃韓國料理好嗎？
  2. It's so hot today. Shall we get an ice cream after our swimming lesson?
    今天好熱。上完游泳課我們去吃冰淇淋好嗎？
  3. The sunset will be beautiful tonight. Shall we walk home along the beach after dinner?
    今晚的夕陽會很美。吃完晚餐我們沿著海灘走回家好嗎？
  4. Dad gave us a new chess set. Shall we play a game after we finish our homework?
    爸爸送我們一副新的西洋棋。寫完功課我們下一盤棋好嗎？
  5. The museum closes at four, and the shops are open late. Shall we look round them after the museum?
    博物館四點關門，商店開得很晚。參觀完博物館我們去逛逛商店好嗎？
  6. Grandma sent us both a present. Shall we call her after we open them?
    奶奶寄了禮物給我們兩個。拆完禮物我們打電話給她好嗎？

③ 情況 + Shall we + 原形動詞？：根據天氣和心情

  1. It's such a warm evening. Shall we eat outside in the garden?
    今天傍晚好暖和。我們在花園裡吃飯好嗎？
  2. It's pouring with rain. Shall we stay in and play cards?
    雨下得好大。我們待在家裡玩牌好嗎？
  3. I'm bored of studying. Shall we take a short break?
    我讀書讀膩了。我們休息一下好嗎？
  4. The snow is perfect for sledging. Shall we go up the hill?
    這場雪很適合滑雪橇。我們上山坡去好嗎？
  5. You look tired after work. Shall we just order a takeaway tonight?
    你下班看起來很累。我們今天晚上就叫外賣好嗎？
  6. The beach is too crowded today. Shall we drive to the next bay?
    今天海灘人太多了。我們開車去下一個海灣好嗎？

④ Shall we + 原形動詞？：建議做計畫

  1. Your parents' anniversary is next month. Shall we organise a surprise party?
    你爸媽的結婚紀念日在下個月。我們辦一個驚喜派對好嗎？
  2. We both want to get fit. Shall we join the gym together?
    我們都想變健康。我們一起加入健身房好嗎？
  3. Summer is coming. Shall we plan a camping trip with the others?
    夏天快到了。我們跟其他人計畫一次露營好嗎？
  4. The flat looks so plain. Shall we paint the living room green?
    公寓看起來好單調。我們把客廳漆成綠色好嗎？
  5. We never see Uncle Tom. Shall we visit him next weekend?
    我們從來沒見到 Tom 叔叔。我們下個週末去看他好嗎？
  6. Our Spanish isn't improving. Shall we find a conversation class?
    我們的西班牙文都沒進步。我們找一個會話課好嗎？

⑤ Shall we + 原形動詞？：在店裡和餐廳

  1. The waiter is busy. Shall we pay at the counter?
    服務生很忙。我們去櫃台付錢好嗎？
  2. These two sofas are both nice. Shall we sit on them before we decide?
    這兩張沙發都不錯。我們決定之前先坐坐看好嗎？
  3. The dessert menu looks amazing. Shall we share a chocolate cake?
    甜點菜單看起來好棒。我們分一個巧克力蛋糕好嗎？
  4. It's very noisy at this table. Shall we ask to move upstairs?
    這桌好吵。我們要求換到樓上好嗎？
  5. The queue is very long. Shall we come back later?
    隊伍好長。我們晚點再來好嗎？
  6. There's a sale on winter coats. Shall we have a quick look?
    冬天外套在特價。我們很快看一下好嗎？
  7. We've got lots of bags. Shall we take a taxi home from the shopping centre?
    我們有好多袋子。我們從購物中心搭計程車回家好嗎？
