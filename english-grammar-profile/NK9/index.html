例句

① have to：現在

  1. "Are you coming to the party?" "I can't. I have to work on Saturday."
    你要來派對嗎？沒辦法。我星期六得上班。
  2. All students have to wear a uniform at my school.
    我們學校所有學生都得穿制服。
  3. My dad has to take the train at six every morning.
    我爸每天早上六點就得搭火車。
  4. "Why are you studying so late?" "I have to pass this exam."
    你怎麼念書念到這麼晚？我得通過這個考試。
  5. The baby has to sleep at seven, so we eat early.
    寶寶七點就得睡覺，所以我們很早吃晚餐。

② have got to：現在

  1. Dear class, for Saturday's lesson you've got to bring paintbrushes, paper and paint.
    各位同學，星期六的課你們得帶畫筆、紙和顏料。
  2. Sorry, I've got to go now. My bus is coming.
    抱歉，我現在得走了。我的公車來了。
  3. "Can you play tonight?" "No, I've got to finish my report."
    你今晚可以一起玩嗎？不行，我得把報告寫完。
  4. She's got to be at the airport by five.
    她五點以前得到機場。
  5. We've got to buy a present for Grandma before Sunday.
    星期天以前我們得幫奶奶買個禮物。
  6. You've really got to try this cake. It's amazing!
    你真的一定要吃吃看這個蛋糕。太好吃了！

③ had to：過去

  1. The doctor was very busy, so I had to wait an hour.
    醫生很忙，所以我等了一個小時。
  2. Our flight was cancelled, and we had to stay one more night in Rome.
    我們的班機取消了，所以我們得在羅馬多待一晚。
  3. When I was young, I had to walk to school every day.
    我小時候每天都得走路上學。
  4. "Why were you late?" "I had to take my son to the dentist."
    你怎麼遲到了？我得帶我兒子去看牙醫。

④ will have to：未來

  1. The shop is closed today. We'll have to come back tomorrow.
    這家店今天沒開。我們得明天再來。
  2. If you miss the last bus, you'll have to take a taxi.
    你要是錯過最後一班公車，就得搭計程車了。
  3. My car is broken, so I'll have to cycle to work this week.
    我的車壞了，所以這星期我得騎腳踏車上班。

⑤ don't have to：不必

  1. Dinner is on me. You don't have to pay.
    晚餐我請。你不用付錢。
  2. You don't have to bring anything except your clothes. We have towels.
    除了衣服，你什麼都不用帶。我們有毛巾。
  3. It's Sunday tomorrow, so the kids don't have to get up early.
    明天是星期天，所以孩子們不用早起。
  4. My sister doesn't have to work on Fridays.
    我妹妹星期五不用上班。
  5. We didn't have to wait. The table was ready.
    我們不用等。位子已經準備好了。

⑥ 問句

  1. "Do I have to pay now?" "No, you can pay when you leave."
    我現在就得付錢嗎？不用，你走的時候再付就好。
  2. "Does your brother have to wear a suit to work?" "Only on Mondays."
    你哥哥上班得穿西裝嗎？只有星期一要。
  3. "Have we got to finish this today?" "Yes, the boss wants it tonight."
    我們今天就得把這個做完嗎？對，老闆今晚就要。
  4. "Did you have to show your passport?" "Yes, twice."
    你得出示護照嗎？要，兩次。
  5. "What time have you got to be at work?" "At half past eight."
    你幾點得到公司？八點半。

⑦ 第三人稱單數：has to / has got to

  1. "Where's Anna?" "She's got to stay late at the office tonight."
    Anna 在哪裡？她今晚得在辦公室待到很晚。
  2. Tom has got to see the doctor about his knee.
    Tom 得去看醫生，檢查他的膝蓋。
  3. "Can your son come out to play?" "Not now. He has to tidy his room first."
    你兒子可以出來玩嗎？現在不行。他得先整理房間。
