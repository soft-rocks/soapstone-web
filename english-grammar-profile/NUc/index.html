The products we received were not exactly the ones we ordered.

我們收到的產品跟我們訂的不完全一樣。

the ones + 後置修⁠飾⁠語

The products we received were not exactly the ones we ordered.

我們收到的產品跟我們訂的不完全一樣。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語和子⁠句

The products we received were not exactly the ones we ordered.

介系詞片語

Our buses are slower than the ones in the capital.

我們的公車比首都的慢。
關係子句

Throw away the glasses that are chipped and keep the ones that aren't.

有缺口的杯子丟掉，沒缺口的留著。
省略關係代名詞

These pens are better than the ones we had before.

這些筆比我們以前用的好。

分⁠詞片⁠語

The products we received were not exactly the ones we ordered.

過去分詞

The parcels sent on Monday arrived, but the ones posted on Friday didn't.

星期一寄的包裹到了，星期五寄的還沒到。
現在分詞

I like all your paintings, especially the ones hanging in the kitchen.

你的畫我都喜歡，尤其是掛在廚房的那幾幅。

who、that、which

The products we received were not exactly the ones we ordered.

當受詞，可省略

the ones (that) we ordered

指人用 who 或 that，指東西用 that 或 which
當主詞，不省略

the ones that broke

不能寫成 the ones broke

複⁠數名⁠詞用 the ones

The products we received were not exactly the ones we ordered.

The tickets we got were not the one we booked.

The tickets we got were not the ones we booked.

說地⁠點用 in，不⁠用 of

The prices here are lower than the ones of my country.

The prices here are lower than the ones in my country.

指人用 who

The ones which helped us most were our neighbours.

The ones who helped us most were our neighbours.

those + 後置修⁠飾⁠語：正⁠式

結果比去年的好。

單⁠數用 the one

我的車是車燈壞掉的那一輛。

例句

① 買東西

  1. These books aren't the ones I ordered.
    這些書不是我訂的那幾本。
  2. Can I swap these for the ones in blue?
    這些可以換成藍色的嗎？
  3. Most of these jackets are full price. The ones on sale are at the back.
    這些外套大多是原價，特價的在後面。
  4. We returned two of the chairs and kept the ones that matched the table.
    我們退了兩張椅子，留下跟桌子搭的那幾張。
  5. The ones made in Italy cost twice as much.
    義大利製的貴一倍。
  6. Do you still have those sandals? I'd like the ones I saw on your website.
    你們還有那款涼鞋嗎？我想要我在網站上看到的那雙。

② 比較地方

  1. The beaches here are cleaner than the ones near my hometown.
    這裡的海灘比我老家附近的乾淨。
  2. Their trains are much faster than the ones back home.
    他們的火車比我們家鄉的快很多。
  3. Rents in the city centre are higher than the ones in the suburbs.
    市中心的房租比郊區的高。
  4. The schools here start later than the ones I went to.
    這裡的學校比我以前念的晚上課。
  5. The winters here aren't as dreary as the ones up north.
    這裡的冬天沒有北部那麼陰沉。

③ 人

  1. Students, listen. The ones who finish early can go home.
    同學注意，先寫完的人可以回家。
  2. The teachers I remember best are the ones who made us laugh.
    我記得最清楚的老師，是會逗我們笑的那幾位。
  3. Lots of people came to the talk, but the ones sitting at the back couldn't hear.
    很多人來聽演講，可是坐在後面的人聽不到。
  4. My cousins? The ones from the south are coming next week.
    我表親？南部那幾個下週要來。
  5. Our neighbours are friendly, especially the ones across the road.
    我們的鄰居很友善，尤其是對面那幾戶。
  6. Not all the staff need to come. The ones invited to the meeting got an email yesterday.
    不是全部員工都要來。受邀開會的人昨天收到了電子郵件。

④ 工作和學校

  1. Here are the forms. Please mark the ones you've already checked.
    表格在這裡。請把你檢查過的標出來。
  2. The reports she writes are always more concise than the ones I write.
    她寫的報告總是比我寫的簡潔。
  3. The firms young people prefer are the ones with ethical policies.
    年輕人偏好的公司，是有道德規範的那些。
  4. The questions on page two are harder than the ones on page one.
    第二頁的題目比第一頁的難。
  5. Print all the letters, starting with the ones marked "urgent".
    把所有的信印出來，先印標著急件的那些。

⑤ 在家裡

  1. Use the towels in the cupboard, not the ones hanging in the bathroom.
    用櫃子裡的毛巾，不要用浴室裡掛著的那些。
  2. The plants by the window grow faster than the ones in the hall.
    窗邊的植物比走廊上的長得快。
  3. I can't find the keys. I mean the ones for the car.
    我找不到鑰匙。我是說車子的那幾把。
  4. Can you send me the photos? Just the ones we took last summer.
    可以把照片傳給我嗎？只要我們去年夏天拍的那些。
  5. The cups we got as a wedding present are the ones on the top shelf.
    我們結婚收到的杯子，是最上層那些。

⑥ 看法和抱怨

  1. The films I enjoy most are the ones based on true stories.
    我最喜歡的電影，是改編自真實故事的那些。
  2. The rules that really matter are the ones nobody writes down.
    真正重要的規則，是沒人寫下來的那些。
  3. The hotels we stayed in were nothing like the ones in the brochure.
    我們住的飯店跟手冊上的完全不一樣。
  4. Of all my coworkers, the ones complaining loudest do the least work.
    我的同事裡，抱怨最大聲的那些做的事最少。
