The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.

這個故事發生在第二次世界大戰後的維也納。

說故⁠事情⁠節用現⁠在簡⁠單式

這個故事發生在第二次世界大戰後的維也納。

情⁠節、歷⁠史、比⁠賽和笑⁠話都⁠可⁠以這⁠樣說

The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.

情節

At the end, she finds out the truth.

最後她發現了真相。
歷史

In 1492, Columbus sails west.

一四九二年，哥倫布向西航行。
比賽

Smith passes to Jones, and Jones scores!

Smith 傳給 Jones，Jones 射門得分！
笑話

So I walk into the shop, and this man looks at me.

然後我走進店裡，這個男人看著我。

背⁠景⁠裡正⁠在進⁠行⁠的事用進⁠行⁠式

The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.

背景

On the train, a man is sleeping.

火車上，一個男人在睡覺。
事件

He dreams of his old friend.

他夢到他的老朋友。

整⁠段都用現⁠在簡⁠單式

The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.

She meets a soldier, and they fell in love.

She meets a soldier, and they fall in love.

單⁠數主⁠詞⁠的動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠s

The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.

At the end, the hero find the treasure.

At the end, the hero finds the treasure.

take place 不⁠用被⁠動

The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.

The story is taken place in Paris.

The story takes place in Paris.

例句

① 小說的情節

  1. My book report is about a novel. The story takes place in Vienna after the Second World War.
    我的讀書報告寫一本小說。這個故事發生在第二次世界大戰後的維也納。
  2. The book begins with a girl who finds a letter in her grandmother's house.
    這本書一開始，一個女孩在她奶奶家發現一封信。
  3. Life on the farm is hard. In the second chapter, the family moves to the city.
    農場的生活很辛苦。第二章裡，這一家人搬到城市。
  4. Harry meets a strange old man, and his life changes.
    Harry 遇到一個奇怪的老人，他的人生從此改變。
  5. At the end, she finds out that her best friend is the thief.
    最後她發現她最好的朋友就是小偷。
  6. The two boys become friends, but then a war starts.
    兩個男孩變成朋友，可是後來戰爭爆發了。

② 電影的劇情

  1. Here's the plot. The film opens in a fishing village in Sicily.
    劇情是這樣的。電影從西西里的一個漁村開始。
  2. A young doctor arrives in the village and falls in love with a teacher.
    一個年輕醫生來到村子，愛上了一位老師。
  3. In the next scene, the police find the car in the river.
    下一幕，警察在河裡找到那輛車。
  4. The scene takes place on a train. On this train, a man is sleeping.
    這一幕發生在火車上。火車上，一個男人在睡覺。
  5. He dreams of a colleague who lost his job last year.
    他夢到一個去年丟了工作的同事。
  6. The hero decides to go back home, but his car breaks down.
    主角決定回家，可是他的車拋錨了。

③ 歷史事件

  1. In 1492, Columbus sails west and reaches the Americas.
    一四九二年，哥倫布向西航行，抵達美洲。
  2. In 1969, two men walk on the moon for the first time.
    一九六九年，兩個人第一次在月球上漫步。
  3. The war ends in 1945, and the city begins to rebuild.
    戰爭在一九四五年結束，城市開始重建。
  4. This part of history is sad. The king dies suddenly, and his young son becomes king.
    這段歷史很令人難過。國王突然過世，他年幼的兒子成為國王。
  5. The museum guide says, "Here the soldiers wait for three days."
    博物館導覽員說：士兵們在這裡等了三天。

④ 運動比賽的實況

  1. Silva runs down the left. He passes to Jones, and Jones scores!
    Silva 從左路帶球。他傳給 Jones，Jones 射門得分！
  2. It's match point. She serves, and he hits the ball into the net.
    賽末點。她發球，他把球打進網裡。
  3. What a race! The runner from Kenya takes the lead in the last lap.
    好精采的比賽！肯亞的選手在最後一圈領先。
  4. Here comes the shot. The goalkeeper jumps, but he can't reach the ball.
    射門了。守門員跳起來，可是他碰不到球。
  5. Only one minute to go. The home team gets a free kick.
    只剩一分鐘。主隊得到一個自由球。

⑤ 說笑話和親身經歷

  1. Listen to this. So I walk into the bank, and the man at the desk is my old teacher.
    你聽我說。然後我走進銀行，櫃檯那個人竟然是我以前的老師。
  2. A man goes into a café and asks for a cup of tea.
    有個男人走進一家咖啡店，點了一杯茶。
  3. Then my phone rings, and it's my boss.
    然後我的手機響了，是我老闆打來的。
  4. So I open the door, and there's a dog in my kitchen!
    然後我打開門，我家廚房裡有一隻狗！

⑥ 介紹作品

  1. The play tells the story of two sisters in a small town.
    這齣戲講的是一個小鎮上兩姊妹的故事。
  2. The book ends happily. The family is together again.
    這本書的結局很圓滿。一家人又團聚了。
  3. The film shows life in Hong Kong in the 1960s.
    這部電影呈現一九六○年代香港的生活。
  4. In this chapter, the writer describes her childhood in the mountains.
    這一章裡，作者描述她在山上的童年。
