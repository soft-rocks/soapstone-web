The program was due to start at midday.

節目原定中午開始。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are / was / were + due to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

be due to預定、照計畫會。
be to照安排要做。比較正式，常用在新聞、公告和商業信件。
過去式說過去的安排。這個安排有時候沒有實現。

現⁠在⁠式：說未⁠來⁠的安⁠排

例句

The new law is due to come into force in 2028.

新法律預定在 2028 年生效。
例句

The minister is to visit the school next week.

部長下星期要訪問這間學校。

過⁠去⁠式：說原⁠本⁠的安⁠排

例句

The plane was due to land at six, but it was late.

飛機原定六點降落，可是誤點了。
例句

I was to take exams in two weeks.

我兩個星期後要考試。

due 後⁠面要⁠有 to，再⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

The program was due to start at midday.

The train is due arrive at ten.

The train is due to arrive at ten.

to 前⁠面要⁠有 be 動⁠詞

The program was due to start at midday.

I to go on a business trip next week.

I am to go on a business trip next week.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

The program was due to start at midday.

The show was due to starting at noon.

The show was due to start at noon.

例句

① am / is / are + due to + 原形動詞：未來的安排

  1. The new directive is due to come into force by 2028.
    新的指令預定在 2028 年前生效。
  2. The next bus is due to arrive in five minutes.
    下一班公車預定五分鐘後到。
  3. My sister's first baby is due to arrive just after Christmas.
    我姊的第一個寶寶預產期在聖誕節剛過。
  4. The new stadium is due to open next spring.
    新體育場預定明年春天啟用。
  5. The band is due to release a new album in the autumn.
    樂團預定秋天推出新專輯。
  6. The report is due to come out next Tuesday.
    報告預定下星期二公布。
  7. My cousin is due to finish university in July.
    我表哥預定七月大學畢業。

② was / were + due to + 原形動詞：原本的安排

  1. The wedding was due to start at two, but the bride was an hour late.
    婚禮原定兩點開始，可是新娘晚了一個小時。
  2. The flight was due to land at six, but it was two hours late.
    班機原定六點降落，可是晚了兩個小時。
  3. The parade was due to pass our house at ten, but it took a different route.
    遊行原定十點經過我們家，可是走了另一條路線。
  4. The new bridge was due to open last year.
    新橋原定去年通車。
  5. We were due to fly home on Sunday, but the airport closed.
    我們原定星期天飛回家，可是機場關閉了。
  6. The concert was due to begin at eight, so we arrived at half past seven.
    演唱會原定八點開始，所以我們七點半就到了。
  7. The builders were due to finish the work in March.
    工人原定三月完工。

③ am / is / are + to + 原形動詞：正式的安排

  1. I cannot attend the meeting, as I am to go on a business trip to New York then.
    我沒辦法參加會議，因為那時候我要去紐約出差。
  2. The royal couple are to open the new children's hospital in June.
    王室夫婦六月要為新的兒童醫院揭幕。
  3. All students are to be in the hall by nine.
    所有學生九點前要到禮堂。
  4. The museum is to close for six months for repairs.
    博物館要關閉六個月整修。
  5. The two leaders are to meet in Geneva on Friday.
    兩位領袖星期五要在日內瓦會面。
  6. The company is to open three new stores this year.
    那家公司今年要開三家新店。
  7. You are to report to the manager at ten.
    你十點要向經理報到。

④ was / were + to + 原形動詞：過去的安排

  1. I was to take exams in two weeks, so I had to study hard.
    兩個星期後我就要考試，所以我得用功讀書。
  2. The two teams were to play the final in Madrid.
    兩隊原定在馬德里打決賽。
  3. She was to start her new job the following Monday.
    她下一個星期一就要開始新工作。
  4. The wedding was to take place in June.
    婚禮原定在六月舉行。
  5. They were to leave for Paris the next morning.
    他們隔天早上要出發去巴黎。
  6. The new hospital was to have three hundred beds.
    新醫院原本要設三百張病床。
  7. The money was to pay for a new school roof.
    這筆錢是要用來付學校新屋頂的費用。

⑤ 否定和疑問

  1. The train isn't due to leave for another hour.
    火車還要一個小時才開。
  2. When is the new manager due to arrive?
    新經理預定什麼時候到？
  3. You are not to touch anything in this room.
    這個房間裡的東西你都不可以碰。
