Dear Neighbours, I would like to invite you to my home for dinner.

我想邀請你來我家吃晚餐。

I / We + would like to invite + 人 + to + 活⁠動 / 地⁠方

would like to invite、人、to + 活⁠動或地⁠方

Dear Neighbours, I would like to invite you to my home for dinner.

would like to invite想邀請。很有禮貌。
人被邀請的人，像 you、all of you。
to + 活動或地方邀請去哪裡或參加什麼。

加⁠上時⁠間和地⁠點

例句

I would like to invite you to dinner at my house on Saturday at half past seven.

我想邀請你星期六七點半來我家吃晚餐。

邀請常接著說時間和地點。

邀⁠請去某⁠個活⁠動，要加 to

Dear Neighbours, I would like to invite you to my home for dinner.

I'd like to invite you my party.

I'd like to invite you to my party.

would like 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時要加 to

Dear Neighbours, I would like to invite you to my home for dinner.

I would like invite you to dinner.

I would like to invite you to dinner.

邀⁠請去某⁠個地⁠方用 to

Dear Neighbours, I would like to invite you to my home for dinner.

We'd like to invite you for our house.

We'd like to invite you to our house.

例句

① I would like to invite you to + 活動 + 時間：邀請參加活動

  1. Dear Neighbours, I've just moved into flat six. I would like to invite you to my home for dinner on Friday.
    親愛的鄰居們，我剛搬進六號公寓。我想邀請你們星期五來我家吃晚餐。
  2. I would like to invite you to dinner at my house on Saturday at half past seven.
    我想邀請你星期六七點半來我家吃晚餐。
  3. I would like to invite you to my birthday party next Sunday.
    我想邀請你參加我下星期天的生日派對。
  4. I would like to invite you to our school concert on the twelfth of May.
    我想邀請你參加我們五月十二日的學校音樂會。
  5. I would like to invite you to a barbecue in our garden this weekend.
    我想邀請你這個週末來我們花園烤肉。
  6. I would like to invite you to the opening of my art exhibition.
    我想邀請你參加我的畫展開幕。

② We would like to invite you to + 名詞：正式的邀請

  1. We would like to invite you to our wedding on the third of June.
    我們想邀請您參加我們六月三日的婚禮。
  2. We would like to invite all parents to the school open day.
    我們想邀請所有家長參加學校的開放日。
  3. We would like to invite you to a short meeting on Monday afternoon.
    我們想邀請您參加星期一下午的簡短會議。
  4. We would like to invite you to visit our new office in London.
    我們想邀請您參觀我們在倫敦的新辦公室。
  5. We would like to invite you to give a talk at our conference.
    我們想邀請您在我們的研討會上演講。
  6. We would like to invite you to join our tennis club.
    我們想邀請您加入我們的網球俱樂部。

③ I'd like to invite you to + 地方：邀請來家裡或去旅行

  1. I'd like to invite you to stay with us in Scotland this summer.
    我想邀請你今年夏天來蘇格蘭跟我們住。
  2. I'd like to invite you to my grandparents' farm for the weekend.
    我想邀請你週末去我祖父母的農場。
  3. I'd like to invite you to our holiday cottage by the lake.
    我想邀請你去我們湖邊的度假小屋。
  4. I'd like to invite you to lunch at my favourite restaurant.
    我想邀請你去我最喜歡的餐廳吃午餐。
  5. I'd like to invite you to come skiing with my family in February.
    我想邀請你二月跟我們家去滑雪。
  6. I'd like to invite you to watch the match at our house.
    我想邀請你來我們家看比賽。

④ I would like to invite + 人 + to + 原形動詞：邀請一起做

  1. I would like to invite you to come to the theatre with me.
    我想邀請你跟我一起去看戲。
  2. I would like to invite my classmates to celebrate the end of the exams.
    我想邀請我的同學一起慶祝考試結束。
  3. I would like to invite you to have dinner with my parents.
    我想邀請你跟我爸媽一起吃晚餐。
  4. We would like to invite you to spend Christmas with us.
    我們想邀請你跟我們一起過聖誕節。
  5. I would like to invite you to try my homemade cake.
    我想邀請你嚐嚐我自己做的蛋糕。
  6. We would like to invite you to join us for a walk on Sunday.
    我們想邀請你星期天跟我們一起去散步。

⑤ I would like to invite you …, + 細節：邀請卡的內容

  1. I would like to invite you to my party. It starts at eight, and there will be music and dancing.
    我想邀請你參加我的派對。八點開始，會有音樂和跳舞。
  2. I would like to invite you to dinner. Please bring your partner too.
    我想邀請你來吃晚餐。請也帶你的另一半來。
  3. I would like to invite you to my graduation. It's at the town hall at two.
    我想邀請你參加我的畢業典禮。兩點在市政廳。
  4. We would like to invite you to our new house. We'll send you the address.
    我們想邀請你來我們的新家。我們會把地址寄給你。
  5. I would like to invite you to a picnic. Please bring something to drink.
    我想邀請你去野餐。請帶點喝的來。
  6. We would like to invite you to our anniversary dinner. Please reply by Friday.
    我們想邀請您參加我們的週年晚宴。請在星期五前回覆。
  7. I would like to invite you to my leaving party. I'll miss you all.
    我想邀請你們參加我的歡送會。我會想念你們大家。
