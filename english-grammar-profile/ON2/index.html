My bedroom is not as big as yours, but it is lovely.

我的臥室沒有你的大，但是很可愛。

第⁠二⁠個 as 後⁠面用 yours，不⁠用再⁠說一⁠次名⁠詞

我的臥室沒有你的大，但是很可愛。

as ... as 是一⁠樣，not as ... as 是沒⁠有那⁠麼

My bedroom is not as big as yours, but it is lovely.

as ... as

My phone is as old as yours.

我的手機跟你的一樣舊。
not as ... as

Your garden isn't as big as mine.

你的花園沒有我的大。

as ... as 中⁠間也⁠可⁠以放副⁠詞

My bedroom is not as big as yours, but it is lovely.

副詞

Does your watch keep time as well as mine?

你的錶跟我的一樣準嗎？

比⁠較⁠的是東⁠西，用 yours，不⁠用 you

My bedroom is not as big as yours, but it is lovely.

My car is as fast as you.

My car is as fast as yours.

as ... as 前⁠後都用 as

My bedroom is not as big as yours, but it is lovely.

Her cake isn't as sweet than mine.

Her cake isn't as sweet as mine.

as ... as 中⁠間用形⁠容⁠詞⁠的原⁠形

My bedroom is not as big as yours, but it is lovely.

My room is as bigger as yours.

My room is as big as yours.

例句

① as ... as yours

  1. Is my soup as spicy as yours, or should I add more chili?
    我的湯跟你的一樣辣嗎？還是我該多加點辣椒？
  2. My suitcase is as heavy as yours.
    我的行李箱跟你的一樣重。
  3. I hope my presentation is as good as yours.
    我希望我的報告跟你的一樣好。
  4. Is my handwriting as messy as yours?
    我的字跡跟你的一樣潦草嗎？
  5. My hair is almost as long as yours now.
    我的頭髮現在差不多跟你的一樣長了。

② not as ... as yours

  1. My flat isn't as close to the station as yours.
    我的公寓沒有你的那麼靠近車站。
  2. My laptop isn't as fast as yours.
    我的筆電沒有你的快。
  3. My score wasn't as high as yours.
    我的分數沒有你的高。
  4. My town is not as busy as yours.
    我住的小鎮沒有你的那麼熱鬧。
  5. His voice isn't as deep as yours.
    他的聲音沒有你的低沉。

③ as ... as mine

  1. I hope you have a teacher as kind as mine.
    希望你有一位跟我的老師一樣好心的老師。
  2. Her dog is as lazy as mine.
    她的狗跟我的一樣懶。
  3. Your bike is as old as mine.
    你的腳踏車跟我的一樣舊。
  4. Is your commute as long as mine?
    你的通勤時間跟我的一樣長嗎？
  5. Their kitchen is about as small as mine.
    他們的廚房大概跟我的一樣小。

④ not as ... as mine

  1. Your cat isn't as friendly as mine.
    你的貓沒有我的親人。
  2. Her accent isn't as strong as mine.
    她的口音沒有我的重。
  3. His office isn't as noisy as mine.
    他的辦公室沒有我的吵。
  4. The traffic on your street is nothing like as bad as mine.
    你那條街的交通遠遠沒有我這條糟。
  5. The view from your window isn't as good as mine.
    你窗外的景色沒有我的好。

⑤ 副詞和不同的情境

  1. My computer starts up as quickly as yours.
    我的電腦開機跟你的一樣快。
  2. Her car runs as smoothly as mine.
    她的車跟我的一樣好開。
  3. My son doesn't eat as slowly as yours.
    我兒子吃飯沒有你兒子那麼慢。
  4. I wish my memory worked as well as yours.
    真希望我的記性跟你的一樣好。
  5. My plants don't grow as fast as yours.
    我的植物長得沒有你的快。
  6. My salary isn't as high as yours, but I enjoy my job.
    我的薪水沒有你的高，可是我很喜歡我的工作。
  7. Your coffee is twice as strong as mine.
    你的咖啡比我的濃兩倍。
  8. My essay isn't nearly as long as yours.
    我的作文遠遠沒有你的長。
  9. I want a garden as beautiful as yours.
    我想要一座跟你的一樣漂亮的花園。
  10. My grandmother's cooking is just as good as yours.
    我奶奶煮的菜跟你煮的一樣好吃。
