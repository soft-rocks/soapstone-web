I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

我寧可睡帳篷，也不要住房間。

A 是 to 不⁠定⁠詞，rather than 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

我寧可走路，也不要搭公車。

A 是動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，B 也用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

我們喜歡健行，不喜歡逛街。

Rather than 放⁠在句⁠首

I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

Rather than wait for the bus, we walked home.

我們沒有等公車，直接走回家。

to read 後⁠面⁠的 B 不⁠用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

I'd prefer to read rather than watching TV.

I'd prefer to read rather than watch TV.

swimming 後⁠面⁠的 B 不⁠用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

She enjoys swimming rather than run.

She enjoys swimming rather than running.

rather 後⁠面要接 than

I would prefer to sleep in tents rather than stay in rooms.

We could cycle rather take the train.

We could cycle rather than take the train.

例句

① to 不定詞 + rather than + 原形動詞

  1. I've had a long week at work. I'd prefer to stay in rather than go to the party.
    我這個星期工作很累。我寧可待在家，也不要去派對。
  2. The babysitter cancelled at the last minute. We decided to cook dinner at home rather than go out.
    保母在最後一刻取消了。我們決定在家煮晚餐，不出門了。
  3. My cousin's parents are both lawyers. She chose to study medicine rather than become a lawyer.
    我表姊的爸媽都是律師。她選擇讀醫，不當律師。
  4. This is about your job, Tom. I'd like to talk to you in person rather than send an email.
    湯姆，這是關於你工作的事。我想當面跟你談，不想寄電子郵件。
  5. My brother and his wife may move abroad next year. They want to rent a flat rather than buy one.
    我哥哥和他太太明年可能會搬到國外。他們想租公寓，不想買。
  6. My classmate never brings a laptop to lectures. She likes to take notes by hand rather than type them.
    我同學上課從來不帶筆電。她喜歡手寫筆記，不用打字。

② 原形動詞 + rather than + 原形動詞

  1. It only needs new brakes. Why not fix your old bike rather than buy a new one?
    它只需要換新的煞車。為什麼不修你的舊腳踏車，而要買新的？
  2. We've spent too much this month. Let's eat in rather than go to a restaurant.
    我們這個月花太多錢了。我們在家吃吧，不要上館子。
  3. The code for the door is long. Write it down rather than try to remember it.
    門的密碼很長。把它寫下來，不要試著記在腦子裡。
  4. You still have a high temperature. You should rest rather than go to work today.
    你還在發高燒。你今天應該休息，不要去上班。
  5. Parking in the city costs a lot. We could take the train rather than drive.
    在市區停車很貴。我們可以搭火車，不要開車。

③ 動詞-ing + rather than + 動詞-ing

  1. He makes dinner for us every Friday. My son is keen on cooking rather than eating out.
    他每個星期五都煮晚餐給我們吃。我兒子喜歡下廚，不喜歡外食。
  2. My sister has a long trip to work. She prefers working from home rather than going to the office.
    我姊姊上班要通勤很久。她比較喜歡在家工作，不喜歡進辦公室。
  3. He's outside with his roses all day. My grandfather enjoys gardening rather than watching TV.
    他整天都在外面照顧他的玫瑰。我爺爺喜歡園藝，不喜歡看電視。
  4. My boss didn't send any emails. I spent the weekend relaxing rather than working.
    我老闆沒寄任何電子郵件。我週末都在放鬆，沒有工作。
  5. My friend just finished his PhD. He's interested in teaching rather than doing research.
    我朋友剛讀完博士。他對教書有興趣，對做研究沒興趣。

④ Rather than 放在句首

  1. It was a lovely evening after work. Rather than go straight home, we stopped at the beach.
    那天下班後的傍晚很美。我們沒有直接回家，而是在海邊停了一下。
  2. The manager spoke to the angry staff. Rather than complain, try to fix the problem.
    經理跟生氣的員工說話。與其抱怨，不如試著解決問題。
  3. My mum wanted to save money. Rather than buying new clothes, she fixed her old ones.
    我媽媽想省錢。她沒有買新衣服，而是把舊衣服修好。
  4. My son was too shy to speak to the girl. Rather than calling, he sent a text.
    我兒子太害羞，不敢跟那個女孩說話。他沒有打電話，而是傳了簡訊。
  5. The hotels in the park were full. Rather than staying in a hotel, we camped by the lake.
    公園裡的飯店都滿了。我們沒住飯店，而是在湖邊露營。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Look at this rain. Should we watch a film at home rather than walk to the cinema?
    你看這場雨。我們要不要在家看電影，不要走去電影院？
  2. The bank hasn't answered my question for a week. I'd prefer to call rather than wait for an email.
    銀行一個星期都沒回答我的問題。我寧可打電話，也不要等電子郵件。
  3. The students couldn't finish the project alone. They asked for help rather than give up.
    學生們沒辦法自己完成專題。他們尋求幫助，而不是放棄。
  4. My son broke the neighbour's window with his ball. He decided to tell the truth rather than lie.
    我兒子的球打破了鄰居的窗戶。他決定說實話，不說謊。
  5. Our city has new bike lanes everywhere. Many people choose to cycle rather than drive to work.
    我們城市到處都有新的自行車道。很多人選擇騎腳踏車上班，不開車。
  6. The doctor talked to parents about health. Kids should play outside rather than sit in front of screens.
    醫生跟家長談健康。孩子應該去外面玩，不要坐在螢幕前面。
  7. The new manager changed my sister's hours. She decided to quit rather than work every weekend.
    新經理改了我姊姊的工時。她決定辭職，不要每個週末都上班。
  8. The film cut half the story. I prefer reading the book rather than watching the film.
    電影刪掉了一半的故事。我比較喜歡看書，而不是看電影。
  9. My uncle got lost because he didn't use the map. He laughed rather than admit he was wrong.
    我叔叔沒看地圖，結果迷路了。他笑了笑，不肯承認自己錯了。
