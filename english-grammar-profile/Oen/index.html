The thing is, if I could move, I'd go to the countryside.

其實，如果我能搬家，我會搬到鄉下去。

言談標⁠記引⁠出聽⁠的人還不⁠知⁠道⁠的事

其實，如果我能搬家，我會搬到鄉下去。

The thing is 引出說話的人真正想做的事：搬到鄉下。言談標記放在句首時，後面加逗號。

you see 引⁠出聽⁠的人不⁠知⁠道⁠的原⁠因或背⁠景

句首

You see, I'm allergic to cats. That's why I can't stay at your place.

是這樣的，我對貓過敏，所以不能住你家。
句尾

I can't come tonight. I'm working late, you see.

我今晚不能來。我得加班，你知道的。

句尾的 you see 前面加逗號。

the thing is 引⁠出問⁠題或真⁠正⁠的原⁠因

The thing is, if I could move, I'd go to the countryside.

the thing is,

I'd love to come. The thing is, I've got an exam on Monday.

我很想去。問題是，我星期一有考試。
the thing is that

The thing is that nobody told us about the meeting.

問題是沒人通知我們開會。
the thing was
過去的事

We wanted to go camping. The thing was, it rained all weekend.

我們本來想去露營。問題是，整個週末都在下雨。

that 前面不加逗號。

the thing is 的 thing 用單⁠數，動⁠詞用 is

The thing is, if I could move, I'd go to the countryside.

The things are, my car won't start.

The thing is, my car won't start.

說過⁠去⁠的事也用 you see

You saw, I was ill that week.

You see, I was ill that week.

you see 引⁠出陳⁠述⁠句，句⁠尾用句⁠號

You see, I've lost my ticket?

You see, I've lost my ticket.

動⁠詞 see 後⁠面有受⁠詞

you see
言談標記

You see, the shop was already closed.

是這樣的，那家店已經關了。
see
動詞

Do you see that bird on the roof?

你有看到屋頂上那隻鳥嗎？

言談標記 you see 拿掉以後，句子還是完整的。

as you can see 引⁠出聽⁠的人眼⁠前看得⁠到⁠的事

as you can see
看得到的事

As you can see, the kitchen is quite small.

你也看得到，廚房很小。
you see
不知道的事

You see, I'm allergic to cats. That's why I can't stay at your place.

是這樣的，我對貓過敏，所以不能住你家。

例句

① you see 放在句首

  1. I'm nervous about renting a car in London. You see, I've never driven on the left before.
    在倫敦租車讓我很緊張。是這樣的，我以前從來沒在左邊開過車。
  2. You see, my boss is a bit of a martinet.
    你要知道，我老闆管得很嚴。
  3. You see, the floor was slippery, so I fell.
    是這樣的，地板很滑，所以我跌倒了。
  4. You see, he isn't lazy. He just finds reading laborious.
    你要知道，他不是懶。他只是覺得讀書很吃力。
  5. You see, tickets are cheaper online, so I booked them last night.
    是這樣的，網路上的票比較便宜，所以我昨晚就訂了。

② you see 放在句尾

  1. We had to leave early. The baby was tired, you see.
    我們得提早走。寶寶累了嘛。
  2. I don't drink booze any more. Doctor's orders, you see.
    我現在不喝酒了。醫生交代的嘛。
  3. Don't let her carry the glasses. She's a bit clumsy, you see.
    別讓她拿杯子。她有點笨手笨腳的，你知道的。
  4. Sorry, I don't know the area. We've only just moved here, you see.
    抱歉，我對這一帶不熟。我們才剛搬來，你知道的。

③ the thing is

  1. The thing is, I don't have the nerve to ask her out.
    問題是，我沒膽約她出去。
  2. The thing is, we can't afford a bigger flat.
    問題是，我們租不起大一點的公寓。
  3. I don't want to sell you my car. The thing is, it makes a strange noise every morning.
    我不想把車賣給你。問題是，它每天早上都會發出怪聲。
  4. I know it's a good deal. The thing is, I don't really need a new phone.
    我知道這很划算。問題是，我其實不需要新手機。
  5. Talking to Ben won't help. The thing is, he never listens to anyone.
    跟 Ben 談沒有用。問題是，他從來不聽任何人的話。

④ the thing is that

  1. The thing is that I'm in a bit of a quandary about the job offer.
    問題是，那份工作的邀約讓我很為難。
  2. I can't pick up the cake after work. The thing is that the shop closes at six.
    我下班後沒辦法去拿蛋糕。問題是那家店六點就關了。
  3. Please don't tell anyone about my new job. The thing is that my parents don't know about it yet.
    請別跟任何人說我換新工作的事。問題是我爸媽還不知道。

⑤ the thing was

  1. We drove around for an hour. The thing was, nobody knew the way to the hotel.
    我們開車繞了一個小時。問題是，沒人知道去飯店的路。
  2. I liked the flat. The thing was, it was too far from work.
    我喜歡那間公寓。問題是，它離公司太遠了。
  3. I went back for my bag, but the classroom was locked. The thing was that the teacher had already left.
    我回去拿包包，可是教室鎖住了。問題是老師已經走了。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Why are you so late?" "You see, the bus broke down halfway."
    你怎麼這麼晚？是這樣的，公車半路拋錨了。
  2. "Why don't you want the job?" "The thing is, I'd have to move to another city."
    你為什麼不想要那份工作？問題是，我得搬到別的城市。
  3. "Can you lend me some money?" "The thing is, I'm a bit short myself this month."
    你可以借我一點錢嗎？問題是，我這個月自己也有點缺錢。
  4. "Why didn't you say yes?" "You see, I'd already promised to help Mum."
    你為什麼沒答應？是這樣的，我已經答應要幫我媽了。
  5. "Are you coming to the party?" "I'd like to. The thing is, I don't know anyone there."
    你要來派對嗎？我很想去。問題是，我在那裡誰都不認識。
  6. "Why is the garden such a mess?" "You see, we've been away for a month."
    花園怎麼這麼亂？是這樣的，我們出門一個月了。
  7. "Shall we eat outside?" "The thing is, it's going to rain any minute."
    我們要不要在外面吃？問題是，隨時都會下雨。
  8. "Why didn't you call me back?" "My phone died, you see."
    你怎麼沒回我電話？我手機沒電了嘛。
  9. "Why's your sister so quiet?" "She's a bit shy with new people, you see."
    你妹妹怎麼這麼安靜？她跟不熟的人在一起會有點害羞，你知道的。

⑦ 寫給朋友的信

  1. Sorry I didn't reply sooner. You see, I've been away all week.
    抱歉沒早點回信。是這樣的，我整個星期都不在家。
  2. I'd love to visit you in July. The thing is, I don't get much holiday this year.
    我很想七月去找你。問題是，我今年假不多。
  3. We didn't go to the beach in the end. The thing was, the weather was awful.
    我們最後沒去海邊。問題是，天氣太糟了。
