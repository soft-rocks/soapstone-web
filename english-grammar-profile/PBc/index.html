I tried to phone her house, but her phone wasn't working.

我試著打電話到她家，可是她的電話壞了。

I / he / she / it + wasn't + ⁠-⁠ing

wasn't、weren't、⁠-⁠ing

wasn'twas not 的縮寫。
weren'twere not 的縮寫。
-⁠ing當時沒有在進行的動作。

說東⁠西壞⁠了

I tried to phone her house, but her phone wasn't working.

例句

The lift wasn't working, so we took the stairs.

電梯壞了，所以我們走樓梯。

wasn't working 常用來說機器或東西當時壞了。

說身⁠體不⁠舒⁠服

例句

She wasn't feeling very well, so she stayed at home.

她覺得不太舒服，所以待在家裡。

wasn't feeling well 說當時身體不舒服。

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式⁠的否⁠定用 wasn't 或 weren't

I tried to phone her house, but her phone wasn't working.

The phone didn't working.

The phone wasn't working.

主⁠詞是 you，用 weren't

You wasn't listening to me.

You weren't listening to me.

wasn't 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞要加 ⁠-⁠ing

I tried to phone her house, but her phone wasn't working.

He wasn't plan to stay.

He wasn't planning to stay.

例句

① 名詞 + wasn't / weren't working：東西壞了

  1. I called her house five times, but nobody answered. Her phone wasn't working.
    我打到她家五次，可是沒人接。她的電話壞了。
  2. The lift wasn't working, so we carried the sofa up the stairs.
    電梯壞了，所以我們把沙發搬上樓。
  3. The heating wasn't working, so we wore our coats inside.
    暖氣壞了，所以我們在室內穿外套。
  4. The traffic lights weren't working, and there was a long queue.
    紅綠燈壞了，排了很長的車。
  5. My computer wasn't working, so I wrote the essay by hand.
    我的電腦壞了，所以我手寫作文。
  6. The cash machines weren't working at the station.
    車站的提款機都壞了。

② 主詞 + wasn't + feeling + 形容詞：當時身體不舒服

  1. She wasn't feeling very well, so she went home early.
    她覺得不太舒服，所以提早回家了。
  2. I wasn't feeling hungry, so I skipped dinner.
    我不覺得餓，所以沒吃晚餐。
  3. He wasn't feeling confident before the interview.
    面試前他覺得沒信心。
  4. My grandmother wasn't feeling strong enough to walk far.
    我奶奶覺得體力不夠，走不了很遠。
  5. We weren't feeling tired, so we stayed up late.
    我們不覺得累，所以熬夜到很晚。
  6. I wasn't feeling brave enough to jump into the lake.
    我覺得自己沒有勇氣跳進湖裡。

③ 主詞 + wasn't / weren't + planning / going to + 原形動詞

  1. When he started playing football, he wasn't planning to become a professional player.
    他開始踢足球的時候，並沒有打算成為職業球員。
  2. We weren't planning to move house until the children finished school.
    我們本來沒打算在孩子們畢業前搬家。
  3. I wasn't going to tell anyone, but she guessed.
    我本來不打算告訴任何人，可是她猜到了。
  4. They weren't planning to stay so long.
    他們本來沒打算待那麼久。
  5. She wasn't going to come, but her friends persuaded her.
    她本來不打算來，可是她的朋友說服了她。
  6. I wasn't planning to buy anything, but the shoes were on sale.
    我本來沒打算買任何東西，可是那雙鞋在特價。

④ We were sad / surprised + when + 主詞 + heard + 主詞 + weren't + -ing

  1. We felt so bad when we heard you weren't working at the school any more.
    我們聽說你不在學校工作了，覺得很難過。
  2. I was surprised when I heard they weren't living in London any more.
    我聽說他們不住在倫敦了，很驚訝。
  3. We were sorry to hear that your mother wasn't feeling well.
    聽說你媽媽不太舒服，我們很難過。
  4. I was worried when I heard you weren't eating properly.
    我聽說你沒有好好吃飯，很擔心。
  5. We were glad to hear the baby wasn't crying so much.
    聽說寶寶沒有那麼常哭了，我們很高興。
  6. I was disappointed when I heard the band wasn't playing at the festival.
    我聽說那個樂團不在節慶表演，很失望。

⑤ 主詞 + wasn't / weren't + -ing, so + 句子：沒在做，所以

  1. I wasn't paying attention, so I missed my stop.
    我沒注意，所以錯過了我要下的那一站。
  2. They weren't listening, so they didn't hear the instructions.
    他們沒在聽，所以沒聽到說明。
  3. He wasn't wearing gloves, so his fingers went white in the snow.
    他沒戴手套，所以手指在雪地裡凍白了。
  4. We weren't looking at the map, so we got lost.
    我們沒看地圖，所以迷路了。
  5. She wasn't watching the road, so she nearly hit a cyclist.
    她沒看路，所以差點撞到一個騎腳踏車的人。
  6. I wasn't expecting a parcel, so I didn't answer the door.
    我沒在等包裹，所以沒去開門。
  7. The children weren't sleeping, so I read them another story.
    孩子們沒在睡，所以我又唸了一個故事給他們聽。
