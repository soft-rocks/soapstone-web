I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

我問她要不要一起去吃晚餐。

asked + if / whether + 子⁠句，轉⁠述是⁠非問⁠句

我問她要不要一起去吃晚餐。

do 拿⁠掉，you 換⁠成 she，want 後移成 wanted

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

原本的問題

"Do you want to go and have dinner together?"

「你要不要一起去吃晚餐？」
轉述

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

我問她要不要一起去吃晚餐。

主⁠詞放⁠回動⁠詞前⁠面，時⁠態後移

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

do

He asked me if I liked jazz.

他問我喜不喜歡爵士樂。
be 動詞

She asked if I was tired.

她問我累不累。
助動詞

They asked me whether I could swim.

他們問我會不會游泳。
現在完成式

She asked if I had seen the film.

她問我有沒有看過那部電影。

or not 放⁠在句⁠尾，或緊⁠接在 whether 後⁠面

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

句尾

She asked if I was ready or not.

她問我準備好了沒。
whether 後面

She asked whether or not I was ready.

她問我準備好了沒有。

時⁠態不⁠後移：引⁠述動⁠詞是現⁠在⁠式，或問⁠的事還成⁠立

現在式

I'm writing to ask whether you can come to my party.

我寫信來問你能不能來我的派對。
還成立

She asked me if I like spicy food.

她問過我喜不喜歡吃辣。

是⁠非問⁠句用 if，不⁠用 that

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

He asked me that I wanted some coffee.

He asked me if I wanted some coffee.

if 後⁠面用陳⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

I asked him if was he OK.

I asked him if he was OK.

間⁠接引⁠述⁠的句⁠尾用句⁠號

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

She asked me whether I was coming to the party?

She asked me whether I was coming to the party.

or not 只⁠能緊⁠接在 whether 後⁠面

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

My dad asked if or not I had finished.

My dad asked whether or not I had finished.

問⁠的內⁠容和條⁠件

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

問的內容

Ask Tom if he's free.

問問 Tom 他有沒有空。
條件

If Tom's free, ask him.

如果 Tom 有空，就問他。

是⁠非問⁠句用 if，wh 問⁠句用 wh 詞

I asked her if she wanted to go and have dinner together.

是非問句

I asked her if she lived nearby.

我問她是不是住在附近。
wh 問句

I asked her which part of town she lived in.

我問她住在城裡哪一區。

例句

① asked + 人 + if

  1. The waiter asked us if we were ready to order.
    服務生問我們準備好點餐了沒。
  2. My friend asked me if I wanted to join her book club.
    我朋友問我要不要加入她的讀書會。
  3. The coach asked me if I wanted to be on the team.
    教練問我想不想加入球隊。
  4. I asked my teacher if I could leave early.
    我問老師我可不可以早點走。
  5. She asked me if I had ever been to Italy.
    她問我有沒有去過義大利。

② asked if（沒有被問的人）

  1. Someone at the door asked if Mr Wong was home.
    門口有人問黃先生在不在家。
  2. The driver asked if everyone had a seat.
    司機問大家是不是都有位子。
  3. Jack asked if the meeting had been cancelled.
    Jack 問會議是不是取消了。
  4. My little sister asked if dogs could see colours.
    我妹妹問狗看不看得到顏色。

③ whether

  1. The nurse asked whether I was allergic to anything.
    護士問我有沒有對什麼東西過敏。
  2. They asked me whether I could start on Monday.
    他們問我星期一能不能開始上班。
  3. The manager asked whether we had any questions.
    經理問我們有沒有問題。
  4. He asked his wife whether she had locked the car.
    他問太太有沒有鎖車。

④ or not

  1. Mum asked if I had brushed my teeth or not.
    媽媽問我刷牙了沒。
  2. The officer asked whether or not we had anything to declare.
    海關人員問我們有沒有東西要申報。
  3. She asked if the price included breakfast or not.
    她問價錢有沒有含早餐。
  4. They asked us whether or not we wanted a receipt.
    他們問我們要不要收據。

⑤ 時態後移

  1. He asked me if I was OK after the fall.
    他在我跌倒後問我還好嗎。
  2. She asked if the train would be late.
    她問火車會不會誤點。
  3. My uncle asked if I was still living in London.
    我叔叔問我是不是還住在倫敦。
  4. The interviewer asked if I had any experience with children.
    面試官問我有沒有帶小孩的經驗。
  5. We asked if we could bring our dog.
    我們問可不可以帶狗去。

⑥ 時態不後移

  1. I'm calling to ask if you have any rooms available.
    我打電話來問你們還有沒有空房。
  2. Lisa asked me last week if I still play tennis.
    Lisa 上週問我現在還打不打網球。
  3. Can you ask the waiter whether they take cards?
    你可以問一下服務生他們收不收卡嗎？
  4. Everyone keeps asking me if I'm OK.
    每個人一直問我還好嗎。

⑦ wanted to know if / whether

  1. My boss wanted to know if the report was finished.
    我老闆想知道報告寫完了沒。
  2. The kids wanted to know whether they could stay up late.
    孩子們想知道他們可不可以晚點睡。
  3. Her parents wanted to know if she had passed the exam.
    她爸媽想知道她考試有沒有過。
  4. The hotel wanted to know whether we needed a taxi.
    飯店想知道我們需不需要叫計程車。
  5. I wanted to know if anyone had found my wallet.
    我想知道有沒有人撿到我的錢包。
