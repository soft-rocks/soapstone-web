Those are the things I like the most about her.

那些就是我最喜歡她的地方。

those 指前⁠面說⁠過⁠的事⁠物

Those are the things I like the most about her.

那些就是我最喜歡她的地方。

those 指什⁠麼

Those are the things I like the most about her.

列出的幾件事

She is funny and kind. Those are her best points.

她很幽默又很體貼。那些是她最好的地方。
複數名詞

I love Paris and Rome. I'd live in cities like those.

我很喜歡巴黎和羅馬，我願意住在那樣的城市。
對方說過的

"I loved the songs." "Yes, those were great."

「我很喜歡那些歌。」「對，那些歌很棒。」

those 和 these

這是我的計畫，這些是我想做的事。

好幾⁠件⁠事用 those

Those are the things I like the most about her.

She is patient and honest. That are the things I admire.

She is patient and honest. Those are the things I admire.

those 的動⁠詞用複⁠數

Those are the things I like the most about her.

I bought new boots last week, but those is too small.

I bought new boots last week, but those are too small.

those 後⁠面不⁠加 they

Those are the things I like the most about her.

You asked about the old photos. Those they are in the box.

You asked about the old photos. Those are in the box.

those + 名⁠詞

你還記得在西班牙的那些假期嗎？

those who：指人

認識她的人都說她很害羞。

例句

① those 當主詞做總結

  1. He never lies and he always helps people. Those are the reasons I trust him.
    他從不說謊，又總是幫助別人。那些就是我信任他的原因。
  2. We need bread, milk and eggs. Those are the only things on the list.
    我們需要麵包、牛奶和蛋。清單上就只有那些。
  3. Sun, sea and good food. Those were the best parts of the holiday.
    陽光、海和美食。那些是假期裡最棒的部分。
  4. Swimming and cycling are good for you. Those are my favourite sports.
    游泳和騎腳踏車對身體很好。那些是我最喜歡的運動。
  5. She speaks French and German. Those are useful languages for her job.
    她會說法語和德語。那些語言對她的工作很有用。

② those 和 these 對比

  1. These cups are clean, but those are still dirty.
    這些杯子是乾淨的，可是那些還是髒的。
  2. I like these photos, but those are a bit dark.
    我喜歡這些照片，可是那些有點暗。
  3. These shoes are mine. Those are my brother's.
    這雙鞋是我的，那雙是我哥的。
  4. These boxes go in the kitchen, and those go upstairs.
    這些箱子放廚房，那些放樓上。
  5. These seats are free, but those are for teachers.
    這些位子沒人坐，可是那些是給老師坐的。

③ like those

  1. I would love to live in a city like Paris or Rome. I'm very keen on cities like those.
    我很想住在巴黎或羅馬那樣的城市。我很喜歡那樣的城市。
  2. She makes cakes with fruit and cream. I've never had cakes like those.
    她做的蛋糕有水果和奶油。我從來沒吃過那樣的蛋糕。
  3. He tells stories about ghosts. I don't like stories like those.
    他會講鬼故事。我不喜歡那樣的故事。
  4. They sell old toys from the 1960s. You can't find toys like those anymore.
    他們賣一九六〇年代的舊玩具。現在已經找不到那樣的玩具了。
  5. My grandmother grew roses and lilies. I want a garden with flowers like those.
    我奶奶種玫瑰和百合。我想要一個有那樣的花的花園。

④ 回答對方說過的事物

  1. "Did you see the photos from the wedding?" "Yes, those were lovely."
    你看了婚禮的照片嗎？看了，那些照片很漂亮。
  2. "What about the old chairs?" "Oh, I threw those away."
    那些舊椅子呢？喔，我把那些丟掉了。
  3. "I made some biscuits for you." "Thanks, those look delicious."
    我做了一些餅乾給你。謝謝，那些看起來很好吃。
  4. "Where are my blue socks?" "I put those in the washing machine."
    我的藍襪子在哪裡？我把那些丟進洗衣機了。
  5. "Have you read the emails from Tom?" "No, I haven't opened those yet."
    你讀了湯姆寄來的電子郵件嗎？沒有，那些我還沒打開。

⑤ those 當受詞

  1. I don't need these papers, but please keep those.
    這些文件我不需要，可是那些請留著。
  2. Put these plates in the cupboard, and wash those later.
    這些盤子放進櫃子，那些等一下再洗。
  3. I've read these books, but I haven't read those yet.
    這些書我讀過了，可是那些還沒讀。
  4. You can eat these cookies, but don't touch those.
    這些餅乾你可以吃，可是那些別碰。
  5. I've washed these shirts. Can you iron those?
    這些襯衫我洗好了，你可以燙那些嗎？

⑥ 寫信和短文

  1. My town has a park, a museum and a beach. Those are the places I'd show you.
    我住的鎮上有公園、博物館和海灘。那些是我會帶你去的地方。
  2. I miss my friends and my dog. Those are the things I think about every day.
    我想念我的朋友和我的狗。那些是我每天都會想到的。
  3. She was kind and patient with everyone. Those are rare qualities today.
    她對每個人都很和善、很有耐心。那些特質現在很少見。
  4. The rooms were clean and the staff were helpful. Those were the good points of the hotel.
    房間很乾淨，員工也很幫忙。那些是這家旅館的優點。
  5. Music, books and long walks. Those are the things that make me happy.
    音樂、書和散步。那些就是讓我快樂的事。
  6. I made some mistakes last year, but those are in the past now.
    我去年犯了一些錯，不過那些都過去了。
