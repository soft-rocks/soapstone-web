現⁠在完⁠成⁠式報⁠導新消⁠息，不⁠說時⁠間

A new shopping center has opened in our town.

我們鎮上新開了一家購物中心。

說話者寫信給筆友，開頭報告家鄉的新消息。

第一⁠句用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式，細⁠節改⁠用過⁠去簡⁠單式

市中心一家老飯店被大火燒毀了。

The fire started at 2 a.m.

火是凌晨兩點燒起來的。

例句

① 開幕或關閉

  1. A new Italian restaurant has opened near the station.
    車站附近新開了一家義大利餐廳。
  2. The old cinema on Bridge Street has closed.
    橋街上那家老電影院關門了。
  3. They've opened a second swimming pool at the sports center.
    運動中心開了第二座游泳池。
  4. Our local post office has shut down.
    我們這裡的郵局關掉了。
  5. A bookshop has opened where the bank used to be.
    以前銀行的位置開了一家書店。

② 宣布或決定

  1. The government has announced a new tax on sugar.
    政府宣布要對糖課新稅。
  2. The city council has decided to build a new bus station.
    市議會決定要蓋一座新的公車總站。
  3. The two countries have agreed to end the war.
    兩國同意結束戰爭。
  4. The school has changed its uniform.
    學校換了制服。
  5. The airline has cancelled all flights to Paris.
    航空公司取消了所有飛往巴黎的班機。

③ 贏得或失去

  1. Our national team has won the World Cup.
    我們國家隊贏得了世界盃。
  2. The president has lost the election.
    總統輸掉了選舉。
  3. A local girl has won a prize for her poems.
    一個本地的女孩憑她的詩得了獎。
  4. The company has lost its biggest customer.
    這家公司失去了最大的客戶。
  5. Our town has lost its only hospital.
    我們鎮上唯一的醫院沒了。

④ 出現或消失

  1. A famous painting has disappeared from the museum.
    一幅名畫從博物館裡不見了。
  2. The first snow has arrived in the mountains.
    山上下了第一場雪。
  3. The coach has left the club after ten years.
    教練在待了十年後離開了球隊。
  4. Thousands of birds have returned to the lake.
    上千隻鳥飛回湖邊了。
  5. A strange light has appeared in the night sky.
    夜空中出現了一道奇怪的光。

⑤ just：剛發生的事

  1. The prime minister has just arrived in London.
    首相剛抵達倫敦。
  2. We've just heard some great news.
    我們剛聽到一個好消息。
  3. My cousin has just had twins.
    我表姊剛生了雙胞胎。
  4. The results have just come out.
    結果剛出來。
  5. The storm has just hit the coast.
    暴風雨剛襲擊海岸。

⑥ 現在完成式開頭，過去簡單式說細節

  1. There has been an accident on the motorway. It happened at six this morning.
    高速公路上出了車禍。事情發生在今天早上六點。
  2. Bread prices have gone up again. They rose by ten cents on Monday.
    麵包又漲價了。星期一漲了十分錢。
  3. Ben has broken his arm. He fell off his bike on Monday.
    Ben 的手臂斷了。他星期一騎腳踏車摔下來。
  4. The police have caught the thief. They found him at the airport.
    警察抓到小偷了。他們在機場找到他。
  5. Our neighbors have sold their house. They moved to Spain in June.
    我們的鄰居把房子賣了。他們六月搬去西班牙了。
