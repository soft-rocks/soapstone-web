mouse 的複⁠數不⁠加 ⁠-⁠⁠s，改⁠成 mice

不⁠規⁠則複⁠數改母⁠音、加 ⁠-⁠⁠en、不⁠變或改⁠成 ⁠-⁠⁠ves

The floor in the kitchen has many holes, and I think there are mice.

例句

① man / woman / child

  1. It was a sale, and I bought some clothes for my children.
    那時候在特價，我幫我的孩子買了一些衣服。
  2. Three men came to fix the roof.
    三個男人來修屋頂。
  3. The women in my family are all very tall.
    我家的女生都很高。
  4. My neighbour has four children, and they're all under ten.
    我鄰居有四個小孩，全都不到十歲。

② foot / tooth / mouse / goose

  1. She has black eyes, a beautiful round face and nice teeth.
    她有黑色的眼睛、漂亮的圓臉和好看的牙齒。
  2. My feet hurt after the long walk.
    走了那麼長的路，我的腳好痛。
  3. I think there are mice in the kitchen.
    我覺得廚房裡有老鼠。
  4. Brush your teeth before bed.
    睡前要刷牙。
  5. There are some geese on the lake.
    湖上有幾隻鵝。

③ person / people

  1. Twenty people came to my party.
    二十個人來參加我的派對。
  2. Most people in my town work in the factory.
    我們鎮上大部分的人都在工廠上班。
  3. How many people live in your house?
    你家住了幾個人？

④ 單複數同形

  1. The fishermen caught a lot of fish this morning.
    漁夫們今天早上抓到很多魚。
  2. There are hundreds of sheep on the hills.
    山丘上有好幾百隻羊。
  3. We saw two deer in the forest.
    我們在森林裡看到兩隻鹿。
  4. My son has three fish in a tank.
    我兒子在魚缸裡養了三條魚。

⑤ -f / -fe → -ves

  1. Can you put the knives and forks on the table?
    你可以把刀叉放到桌上嗎？
  2. The leaves turn red in autumn.
    秋天葉子會變紅。
  3. Cut the oranges into halves.
    把柳橙切成兩半。
  4. The farmers and their wives sat at one table.
    農夫們和他們的太太坐在同一桌。

⑥ 不規則複數名詞當主詞

  1. Quiet, please! The children are asleep upstairs.
    請安靜！孩子們在樓上睡覺。
  2. People in this village always say hello in the street.
    這個村子的人在街上都會打招呼。
  3. My teeth are very sensitive to cold drinks.
    我的牙齒對冷飲很敏感。
  4. The men in the office all wear ties.
    辦公室的男生都打領帶。

⑦ 不規則複數名詞當受詞

  1. I need new shoes because my feet have grown.
    我需要新鞋，因為我的腳變大了。
  2. The cat caught two mice last night.
    貓昨晚抓到兩隻老鼠。
  3. At the wedding I didn't know many people, so I danced with my aunt.
    婚禮上我認識的人不多，所以我跟我阿姨跳舞。
  4. My grandmother looks after the children on Fridays.
    我奶奶星期五照顧孩子們。

⑧ 數字 + 不規則複數名詞

  1. We need three sharp knives for the cooking class.
    烹飪課需要三把鋒利的刀。
  2. Two women and three children were waiting at the bus stop.
    兩個女人和三個小孩在公車站等車。
  3. This table is for six people.
    這張桌子是六人座。
