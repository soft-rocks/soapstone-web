I spent a week in Dubai, and I am going to spend another week in Al Ain.

我在杜拜待了一個星期，接下來還要在艾因再待一個星期。

a week，再 another week

I spent a week in Dubai, and I am going to spend another week in Al Ain.

我在杜拜待了一個星期，接下來還要在艾因再待一個星期。

another + 數⁠字 + 複⁠數名⁠詞

another two

We need another two chairs.

我們還需要兩張椅子。
another three

The trip takes another three hours by bus.

搭公車還要再三個小時。

another reason、another thing

another reason

Another reason is that the rent is cheap.

另一個原因是房租便宜。
another thing

Another thing I like about my job is the people.

我喜歡這份工作的另一點是同事。

再一⁠個用 another

I spent a week in Dubai, and I am going to spend another week in Al Ain.

I'm going to stay for other week.

I'm going to stay for another week.

接複⁠數名⁠詞要⁠有數⁠字

We need another chairs.

We need another two chairs.

another 不⁠再⁠加 more

Can I have another more cookie?

Can I have another cookie?

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞加 slice of

Can I have another bread?

Can I have another slice of bread?

例句

① 再一段時間

  1. The doctor told me to stay in bed for another day.
    醫生要我在床上多躺一天。
  2. We loved Lisbon so much that we stayed another night.
    我們太喜歡里斯本了，所以多住了一晚。
  3. I'll give you another week to finish the report.
    我再給你一星期把報告寫完。
  4. Can we wait another five minutes for Sam?
    我們可以再等 Sam 五分鐘嗎？

② another + 數字 + 複數名詞

  1. The road is closed for another three months.
    那條路還要再封三個月。
  2. We need another two players for the team.
    我們隊上還需要兩名球員。
  3. It's another twenty kilometres to the next town.
    到下一個鎮還有二十公里。
  4. The ticket costs another fifteen euros if you buy it on the train.
    如果在車上買票，要再多付十五歐元。

③ 再一份吃的喝的

  1. Could I have another cup of coffee, please?
    可以再給我一杯咖啡嗎？
  2. The waiter brought us another plate of chips.
    服務生又給我們送來一盤薯條。
  3. My son asked for another bowl of soup.
    我兒子又要了一碗湯。
  4. Have another piece of chocolate. There's plenty.
    再吃一塊巧克力吧。還有很多。

④ another reason、another thing

  1. Another reason I moved here was the good schools.
    我搬來這裡的另一個原因是學校好。
  2. Here's another example of the problem.
    這是這個問題的另一個例子。
  3. Another thing I don't understand is the price.
    我不懂的另一件事是價錢。
  4. Good food is another reason why people visit Italy.
    美食是人們去義大利的另一個原因。

⑤ 寫信和電子郵件

  1. Dear Mary, I spent a week in Dubai, and now I'm spending another week in Oman.
    親愛的 Mary：我在杜拜待了一個星期，現在在阿曼再待一個星期。
  2. I'm attaching another photo of the flat.
    我再附上一張公寓的照片。
  3. Please let me know if you need another copy of the form.
    如果你還需要一份表格，請告訴我。
  4. I have another question about the course.
    關於這門課，我還有一個問題。

⑥ 在工作和學校

  1. The manager hired another assistant last month.
    經理上個月又請了一位助理。
  2. You can take the test another time if you fail.
    如果沒考過，你可以再考一次。
  3. We need another meeting to make a decision.
    我們還要再開一次會才能做決定。
  4. The teacher gave us another chance to hand in our homework.
    老師又給我們一次交作業的機會。

⑦ 用 one more 說同樣的意思

  1. One more question, and then we'll finish.
    再一個問題，然後我們就結束。
  2. Just one more game, Mum, please!
    媽，再玩一局就好，拜託！
  3. We need one more person for the trip.
    這趟旅行我們還需要一個人。
  4. I'll wait for one more day and then call him.
    我再等一天，然後打給他。
  5. Can I stay up for one more hour tonight?
    我今晚可以再晚一個小時睡嗎？
  6. Add one more spoonful of sugar to the sauce.
    醬汁裡再加一匙糖。
