I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

我買了你們的產品，結果發現品質跟廣告說的不一樣。

only to 接出⁠沒⁠料到⁠的結⁠果

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

我買了你們的產品，結果發現品質跟廣告說的不一樣。

After having 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，說先完⁠成⁠的事

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

我讀了你的文章以後，決定寫信給你。

分⁠詞片⁠語和 Following 加名⁠詞

動詞-ing

Following

Following advice from a friend, I bought the English version of your product.

我聽了朋友的建議，買了你們產品的英文版。

which 代⁠替前⁠面整⁠件⁠事

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

My flight was cancelled, which ruined the first day of my holiday.

我的班機取消了，假期的第一天就這樣毀了。

only to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

We hurried to the cinema, only to find that the film had started.

分⁠詞片⁠語⁠的主⁠詞和主⁠句相⁠同

主⁠句用過⁠去簡⁠單式

After having looked at three flats, we chose the one near the park.

正⁠面⁠的結⁠果用 and

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

I studied hard and passed the exam easily.

表⁠示目⁠的⁠的 to 和 only to

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

目的

We needed something for breakfast. I went to the shop to buy some bread.

我們需要早餐吃的東西。我去店裡買麵包。
結果

It was a holiday. I went to the shop, only to find that it was closed.

那天是假日。我去了店裡，結果發現店沒開。

after 已⁠經說⁠出先⁠後

I bought your product, only to find out that its quality did not match your advertisement.

過去完成式

The waiter added a dessert to our bill. After I had paid, I noticed the mistake.

服務生在我們的帳單上多加了一份甜點。我付完錢以後才發現錯了。
動詞-ing

The shop charged me twice for the milk. After paying, I noticed the mistake.

店家的牛奶多算了我一次錢。付完錢以後，我才發現錯了。

例句

① only to + 原形動詞

  1. I ran all the way to the station, only to see my train pull away.
    我一路跑到車站，結果看到我的火車開走了。
  2. We drove all the way to the beach, only to discover that it was closed.
    我們一路開到海邊，結果發現海灘不開放。
  3. She ran to the gate, only to be told that boarding had finished.
    她跑到登機門，結果被告知登機已經結束了。
  4. He finally found the restaurant, only to realise he had left his wallet at home.
    他終於找到餐廳，結果發現錢包忘在家裡。
  5. I spent hours on the report, only to lose it when my computer crashed.
    我花了好幾個小時寫報告，結果電腦當機，報告不見了。
  6. They queued for two hours, only to hear that the tickets had sold out.
    他們排了兩個小時，結果聽說票賣完了。

② After having + 過去分詞

  1. Dear Members, after having discussed the club fees at the meeting, we came up with the following decisions.
    各位會員好，我們在會議上討論過會費的問題後，做了以下的決定。
  2. After having lived in Spain for five years, she moved back to Taiwan.
    她在西班牙住了五年以後，搬回台灣。
  3. After having read your article, I decided to write to you.
    我讀了你的文章以後，決定寫信給你。
  4. After having checked the figures twice, he found the error.
    他把數字檢查了兩次，才找到那個錯誤。
  5. Our friends never came to the café. After having waited for an hour, we went home.
    我們的朋友一直沒來咖啡店。我們等了一個小時以後就回家了。

③ After / Before + 動詞-ing

  1. After finishing my degree, I worked abroad for a year.
    我拿到學位以後，在國外工作了一年。
  2. Before leaving the office, she turned off all the lights.
    她離開辦公室前，把燈都關了。
  3. After visiting the museum, we had lunch by the river.
    我們參觀完博物館，在河邊吃午餐。
  4. Before signing the contract, I read it very carefully.
    我簽合約之前仔細讀過了。
  5. After saying goodbye, he walked slowly to his car.
    他道別以後，慢慢走向他的車。

④ 分詞片語和 Following + 名詞

  1. Following advice from a friend, I bought the English version of your product.
    我聽了朋友的建議，買了你們產品的英文版。
  2. Hearing a noise downstairs, she went to see what it was.
    她聽到樓下有聲音，就下去看看是什麼。
  3. Following the meeting, the manager sent everyone a summary.
    開完會以後，經理寄了一份摘要給大家。
  4. Opening the box, I saw that the screen was broken.
    我打開箱子，看到螢幕破了。
  5. Realising he was late, he took a taxi.
    他發現自己遲到了，就搭計程車。

⑤ which 接出結果

  1. The hotel lost our booking, which meant we had to find another room at midnight.
    飯店把我們的訂房弄丟了，害我們半夜還得另外找房間。
  2. My flight was cancelled, which ruined the first day of my holiday.
    我的班機取消了，假期的第一天就這樣毀了。
  3. She forgot to set the alarm, which made her late for the interview.
    她忘了設鬧鐘，結果面試遲到了。
  4. The guide spoke only French, which meant nobody in our group understood the tour.
    導遊只說法文，結果我們團裡沒有人聽得懂導覽。
  5. The concert started an hour late, which annoyed a lot of people.
    音樂會晚了一個小時開始，很多人都很不高興。

⑥ 一句話裡排出三件事

  1. Following the instructions, I installed the software, only to find that it did not work.
    我照著說明安裝了軟體，結果發現不能用。
  2. After having paid for the course, I received an email saying it was cancelled.
    我付了課程的錢以後，收到一封電子郵件說課程取消了。
  3. Arriving at the hotel late, we went straight to bed, which was a mistake because we missed dinner.
    我們很晚才到飯店，就直接去睡了。這是個錯誤，因為我們錯過了晚餐。
  4. Having booked the tickets online, we went to the theatre, only to learn that the show had been moved.
    我們在網路上訂了票去劇院，結果才知道表演改期了。
  5. After lending him the money, I heard nothing from him for months, which made me very angry.
    我借他錢以後，好幾個月都沒有他的消息，讓我很生氣。
