Your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.

我是從 Allan Westwood 那裡得知您的名字的，我上週跟他見過面。

東⁠西當主⁠詞，收⁠到⁠的人放⁠在 to 後⁠面

你的名字是 Allan Westwood 給我的。

收⁠到⁠的人或東⁠西當主⁠詞

Your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.

收到的人當主詞

I was given your name.

我得到了你的名字。
東西當主詞

Your name was given to me.

你的名字是別人給我的。

大⁠部⁠分動⁠詞用 to，buy、make 用 for

Your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.

to

The house was sold to a young couple.

房子賣給一對年輕夫妻。
for

This cake was made for you by Grandma.

這個蛋糕是奶奶為你做的。

收⁠到⁠的人前⁠面用 to，不⁠用 from

Your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.

Your name was given from me by Allan.

Your name was given to me by Allan.

buy、make、cook 用 for

Your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.

This cake was made to you by Grandma.

This cake was made for you by Grandma.

to 後⁠面接⁠受格

Your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.

The keys were sent to I last week.

The keys were sent to me last week.

例句

① was / were + 過去分詞 + to

  1. Dear Ms Hall, your name was given to me by Allan Westwood, whom I met last week.
    親愛的 Hall 女士：我是從 Allan Westwood 那裡得知您的名字的，我上週跟他見過面。
  2. Our neighbours moved out in May. Their house was sold to a young couple from London.
    我們的鄰居五月搬走了。他們的房子賣給一對倫敦來的年輕夫妻。
  3. The concert made a lot of money. All of it was given to the hospital.
    那場音樂會賺了很多錢。全部都捐給了醫院。
  4. The letters were sent to the wrong address, so we never got them.
    那些信寄到錯的地址，所以我們一直沒收到。
  5. My old bike was lent to my cousin for the summer.
    我的舊腳踏車暑假借給我表弟了。
  6. The prize was given to the youngest player in the team.
    獎頒給了隊上年紀最小的球員。

② is / are + 過去分詞 + to

  1. If more training is given to staff, they will be more interested in their work.
    如果給員工更多訓練，他們會對工作更有興趣。
  2. In our school, free lunches are given to children from poor families.
    在我們學校，窮人家的孩子有免費午餐。
  3. English is taught to children from the age of six.
    孩子六歲開始學英文。
  4. Show your student card at the door. These tickets are sold only to students.
    在門口出示你的學生證。這些票只賣給學生。

③ has / have been + 過去分詞 + to

  1. Unfortunately, an interview for a job has been offered to me at the same time as your class.
    很不巧，有一份工作的面試時間跟你們的課撞期了。
  2. Your report has been sent to the manager. She'll call you tomorrow.
    你的報告已經寄給經理了。她明天會打給你。
  3. The flat has been rented to a student, so it isn't free any more.
    那間公寓已經租給一個學生了，所以沒空房了。
  4. More help has been promised to the families who lost their homes.
    有人答應要給失去家園的家庭更多幫助。

④ will be + 過去分詞 + to

  1. The certificates will be given to all the students at the end of the course.
    課程結束時，所有學生都會拿到證書。
  2. Your new card will be sent to you by post.
    你的新卡會用郵寄給你。
  3. The chef has finished the new menu. It will be shown to the staff on Monday.
    主廚把新菜單做好了。星期一會拿給員工看。

⑤ 用 for 的動詞

  1. Happy birthday! This cake was made for you by Grandma.
    生日快樂！這個蛋糕是奶奶為你做的。
  2. A room has been booked for you at the Grand Hotel.
    已經幫你在 Grand Hotel 訂了一間房間。
  3. Dinner was cooked for us by the kids last night.
    昨晚孩子們煮晚餐給我們吃。
  4. A new playground was built for the children in our street.
    我們這條街幫孩子們蓋了一座新的遊樂場。
  5. These shoes were bought for me by my aunt.
    這雙鞋是我阿姨買給我的。

⑥ 和收到的人當主詞比較

  1. I was given the job, but the car was given to someone else.
    那份工作給了我，可是車子給了別人。
  2. We were sent a free book, and a free pen was sent to our teacher.
    我們收到一本免費的書，老師收到一支免費的筆。
  3. The children were taught French, and Spanish was taught to the older students.
    孩子們學法文，年紀大一點的學生學西班牙文。

⑦ 寫信和通知

  1. A copy of the contract has been sent to you by email.
    合約的副本已經用電子郵件寄給您了。
  2. The details of the trip will be given to parents next week.
    校外教學的細節下星期會發給家長。
  3. Your request was passed to our manager, who will reply soon.
    您的要求已經轉給我們的經理，他很快會回覆。

⑧ 問句

  1. "Who was the money given to?" "To the animal hospital."
    錢給了誰？給動物醫院。
  2. "Was the letter sent to you or to your mother?" "To me."
    信是寄給你還是寄給你媽媽？寄給我。
  3. "Has the key been given to the new tenant?" "Yes, this morning."
    鑰匙交給新房客了嗎？交了，今天早上。
