I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

我要送你一些花，因為你身體不舒服。

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式是 be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

我要送你一些花，因為你身體不舒服。

be 依⁠照主⁠詞⁠選 am、is 或 are

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

I + am

I am cooking dinner.

我在煮晚餐。
單數 + is

My brother is sleeping.

我哥哥在睡覺。
複數、you + are

The kids are playing outside.

孩子們在外面玩。

口⁠語常把主⁠詞和 be 縮⁠寫在⁠一⁠起

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

I'm

I'm waiting for the bus.

我在等公車。
it's

It's raining again.

又在下雨了。
we're

We're watching a film.

我們在看電影。

有⁠些動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠ing 前要改拼⁠法

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

直接加read → reading，play → playing
去掉字尾的 ewrite → writing，make → making
重複字尾的子音sit → sitting，begin → beginning
ie 改成 ylie → lying，die → dying

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式要⁠有 am、is 或 are

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

I sending you some flowers.

I am sending you some flowers.

複⁠數⁠的主⁠詞用 are

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

My parents is visiting us this week.

My parents are visiting us this week.

be 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠ing

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

She is cook dinner.

She is cooking dinner.

字尾是⁠不⁠發⁠音⁠的 e，先去⁠掉 e 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ing

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

I'm writeing an email to my teacher.

I'm writing an email to my teacher.

run 的字尾要重⁠複 n 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ing

I am sending you some flowers because you are not well.

The children are runing in the park.

The children are running in the park.

例句

① I am + 動詞-ing

  1. Hi Grandma, I am writing to say thank you for the birthday present.
    奶奶好，我寫信是想謝謝您送的生日禮物。
  2. Don't call me now. I am driving to work.
    現在先別打給我。我在開車上班。
  3. We moved in last week. I'm sending you a photo of our new house.
    我們上星期搬進去了。我寄一張我們新家的照片給你。
  4. "Where are you?" "I'm standing outside the cinema."
    你在哪裡？我站在電影院外面。
  5. Sorry, I can't talk. I'm cooking dinner for my parents.
    抱歉，我現在不能講話。我在幫我爸媽煮晚餐。
  6. I'm sitting on the train, so the line might go dead.
    我坐在火車上，所以電話可能會斷。

② he / she / it + is + 動詞-ing

  1. Can you turn the TV down? The baby is sleeping upstairs.
    電視可以小聲一點嗎？寶寶在樓上睡覺。
  2. Mum is making pancakes. Come down for breakfast.
    媽媽在做鬆餅。下來吃早餐吧。
  3. I'll drive you to the station. It's raining quite hard.
    我開車送你去車站。雨下得滿大的。
  4. My brother can't come to the phone. He's having a shower.
    我哥哥沒辦法接電話。他在洗澡。
  5. Look at the dog. It's lying in the sun.
    你看那隻狗。牠躺在太陽底下。
  6. Watch out! The clumsy waiter is carrying six plates at once.
    小心！那個笨手笨腳的服務生一次端著六個盤子。
  7. Copy these down. Our teacher is writing the answers on the board.
    抄下來。我們老師正在白板上寫答案。
  8. She's working at home today because the office is closed.
    今天辦公室關門，所以她在家工作。

③ you / we / they + are + 動詞-ing

  1. We are having a great time here in Rome.
    我們在羅馬這裡玩得很開心。
  2. Don't worry about the noise. The children are playing in the garden.
    別在意那些聲音。孩子們在花園裡玩。
  3. You're doing really well. Keep going.
    你做得很好。繼續加油。
  4. Dear Ben, we're thinking of you and hoping you feel better soon.
    親愛的 Ben，我們都很掛念你，希望你早日康復。
  5. The spare room is full. My grandparents are staying with us this week.
    客房住滿了。我爺爺奶奶這個星期住在我們家。
  6. Hurry up. They're waiting for us in the car.
    快點。他們在車上等我們。
  7. Please wait outside. The students are taking their exam right now.
    請在外面等。學生們現在正在考試。
  8. You're standing on my foot!
    你踩到我的腳了！

④ 去掉字尾的 e 再加 -ing

  1. I'm making a cake for Sarah's party.
    我在幫 Sarah 的派對做蛋糕。
  2. His car is at the garage, so he's riding his bike to school today.
    他的車在修車廠，所以他今天騎腳踏車上學。
  3. The weather is lovely, so we're having lunch in the park.
    天氣很好，所以我們在公園吃午餐。
  4. Grandma is taking photos of the roses in her garden.
    奶奶在拍她花園裡的玫瑰。

⑤ 重複字尾的子音再加 -ing

  1. It's so hot today. The kids are swimming in the lake.
    今天好熱。孩子們在湖裡游泳。
  2. Dad is sitting in the kitchen with a cup of tea.
    爸爸坐在廚房裡喝茶。
  3. Look, the bus is stopping. Let's get on.
    你看，公車停下來了。我們上車吧。
  4. The film is beginning. Turn off your phone.
    電影開始了。把手機關掉。
  5. Run! The shop is shutting in five minutes.
    快跑！店再五分鐘就要關門了。
