例句

① always / usually / often / sometimes + 過去簡單式

  1. My grandfather always seemed so calm.
    我爺爺看起來總是很冷靜。
  2. We usually walked to school together.
    我們通常一起走路上學。
  3. She often read in bed until midnight.
    她常常在床上看書看到半夜。
  4. My mother always made pancakes on Sundays.
    我媽每個星期天都會做鬆餅。
  5. I sometimes helped my uncle in his shop.
    我有時候會去叔叔的店裡幫忙。

② rarely / never + 過去簡單式

  1. My parents never argued in front of us.
    我爸媽從來不在我們面前吵架。
  2. We rarely ate out when I was young.
    我小時候我們很少在外面吃飯。
  3. He never did his homework on time.
    他從來沒有準時寫完功課。

③ 過去簡單式 + every + 時間

  1. We spent August at my aunt's farm every year.
    我們以前每年八月都在阿姨的農場過。
  2. I phoned my grandmother every Sunday evening.
    我以前每個星期天晚上都打給奶奶。
  3. The ice-cream van came round every Friday afternoon.
    冰淇淋車以前每個星期五下午都會來。
  4. Every morning, she swam in the lake before breakfast.
    她以前每天早上吃早餐前都會去湖裡游泳。

④ 過去的狀態

  1. My grandparents lived in a small village near the coast.
    我爺爺奶奶以前住在海邊附近的一個小村子。
  2. When I was a child, I was very shy.
    我小時候很害羞。
  3. We had a dog called Rufus.
    我們以前養過一隻叫 Rufus 的狗。
  4. Everybody knew everybody in our street.
    我們那條街上大家都互相認識。
  5. The house was always full of people.
    那間房子裡總是擠滿了人。

⑤ 說出那段時期

  1. When I was at school, I played football every afternoon.
    我讀書的時候每天下午都踢足球。
  2. While we lived in Rome, we ate pizza every Friday.
    我們住在羅馬的時候，每個星期五都吃披薩。
  3. In those days, people wrote letters instead of emails.
    那時候，大家寫信，不寫電子郵件。
  4. As a student, I worked in a café at weekends.
    我當學生的時候，週末在咖啡店打工。

⑥ 否定

  1. We didn't have a television until I was ten.
    我十歲以前家裡沒有電視。
  2. My dad didn't drive, so we went everywhere by bus.
    我爸不開車，所以我們去哪裡都搭公車。
  3. The teachers didn't allow phones in class.
    以前老師不准上課用手機。

⑦ 問句

  1. Did you walk to school when you were little?
    你小時候走路上學嗎？
  2. Where did you usually go on holiday?
    你們以前通常去哪裡度假？
  3. What did you do at weekends when you were a student?
    你當學生的時候週末都做什麼？

⑧ 回憶以前的生活

  1. Our teacher was very strict, and she rarely smiled.
    我們的老師很嚴格，很少笑。
  2. My best friend lived next door, and we spent every afternoon together.
    我最好的朋友住在隔壁，我們每天下午都在一起。
  3. In winter, it always got dark at four o'clock.
    冬天總是四點天就黑了。
