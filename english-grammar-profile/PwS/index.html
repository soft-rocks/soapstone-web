For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

他們付了那麼多錢，你們本來可以給他們更好的服務。

could have 指⁠出主⁠詞有機⁠會⁠做、卻⁠沒⁠有做⁠的事

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

他們付了那麼多錢，你們本來可以給他們更好的服務。

旅行社有能力提供更好的服務，卻沒有提供。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，或 been 加其⁠他成⁠分

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

過去分詞

You could have locked the back door.

你本來可以把後門鎖上的。
been + 形容詞

You could have been more honest with me.

你本來可以對我誠實一點。
been + 過去分詞
被動

The mistake could have been spotted earlier.

這個錯誤本來可以早一點發現。

at least 放⁠在 could 後⁠面，或放⁠在過⁠去⁠分⁠詞前⁠面

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

could at least have

She could at least have knocked.

她至少可以先敲個門吧。
could have at least

She could have at least knocked.

她至少可以敲一下門吧。

at least 表示主詞連最基本的事都沒做。

Couldn't you have 的否⁠定疑⁠問⁠句也⁠是批⁠評

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

你就不能把車停近一點嗎？

說話者不是在問問題。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

You could have gave us a discount.

You could have given us a discount.

give 的過去式是 gave，過去分詞是 given。

批⁠評過⁠去沒⁠做⁠的⁠事，could 後⁠面⁠接 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

You could tell me yesterday.

You could have told me yesterday.

could 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用原⁠形，寫 have

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

Couldn't you had waited for me?

Couldn't you have waited for me?

同⁠樣⁠的 could have，也⁠可⁠以是推⁠測

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

批評
確定沒做

You could have told me you were going to be late.

你本來可以跟我說你會晚到。
推測
不知道做了什麼

He's late. He could have missed the bus.

他遲到了，他可能是沒趕上公車。

should have 說主⁠詞做⁠錯⁠了，could have 說主⁠詞有更⁠好⁠的做⁠法

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

主⁠詞是 I 時，could have 表⁠示後⁠悔

For the money they had paid, you could have given them a better service.

我本來可以更用功一點的。

這句不是批評別人。

例句

① could have + 過去分詞

  1. You could have told me you were going to be late.
    你本來可以跟我說你會晚到。
  2. He could have asked before borrowing my car.
    他借我的車之前本來可以先問一聲。
  3. They could have warned us about the roadworks.
    他們本來可以先提醒我們有道路施工。
  4. The hotel could have offered us a different room.
    飯店本來可以幫我們換一間房。
  5. You could have cleaned up after yourself.
    你本來可以自己收拾乾淨。
  6. The council could have repaired the road before winter.
    市政府本來可以在冬天之前把路修好。
  7. You could have replied to my email.
    你本來可以回我的電子郵件。
  8. The airline could have given us a meal voucher.
    航空公司本來可以給我們餐券。
  9. Our landlord could have fixed the heating months ago.
    我們房東幾個月前就可以把暖氣修好了。
  10. The organisers could have chosen a bigger venue.
    主辦單位本來可以選一個大一點的場地。
  11. You could have mentioned that the party was formal.
    你本來可以先說派對要穿正式服裝。
  12. My brother could have helped me move, but he went fishing instead.
    我哥本來可以幫我搬家，結果他跑去釣魚。
  13. The waiter could have brought us some water while we waited.
    我們等的時候，服務生本來可以先給我們一點水。
  14. Somebody could have turned off the lights.
    總可以有人把燈關掉吧。
  15. The company could have given its staff more notice.
    公司本來可以早一點通知員工。
  16. The manager could have dealt with the complaint himself.
    經理本來可以自己處理這個客訴。
  17. The speaker could have made his talk a little less banal.
    講者本來可以讓演講別那麼老套。

② could have been + 形容詞

  1. You could have been more careful with my camera.
    你本來可以對我的相機小心一點。
  2. He could have been a little more patient with the kids.
    他本來可以對孩子們多一點耐心。
  3. The instructions could have been clearer.
    說明本來可以寫得清楚一點。
  4. You could have been a bit more polite to her.
    你本來可以對她客氣一點。

③ could at least have、could have at least

  1. You could at least have said thank you.
    你至少可以說聲謝謝吧。
  2. They could have at least apologised.
    他們至少可以道個歉吧。
  3. He could at least have called to say he wasn't coming.
    他至少可以打個電話說他不來。
  4. You could have at least cleared the table.
    你至少可以把桌子收一收吧。

④ Couldn't + 主詞 + have + 過去分詞

  1. Couldn't you have waited five more minutes?
    你就不能多等五分鐘嗎？
  2. Couldn't they have told us sooner?
    他們就不能早點告訴我們嗎？
  3. Couldn't he have picked a quieter restaurant?
    他就不能挑一間安靜一點的餐廳嗎？

⑤ could have been + 過去分詞（被動）

  1. The problem could have been avoided with a bit of planning.
    只要稍微規劃一下，這個問題本來是可以避免的。
  2. The tickets could have been sent earlier.
    票本來可以早點寄出來。
  3. This could have been handled much better.
    這件事本來可以處理得好很多。
