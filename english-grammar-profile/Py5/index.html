Is he going to give it back to me or show it to everybody?

他是要把它還給我，還是要拿給大家看？

第⁠二⁠個選⁠項省⁠掉開⁠頭，or 後⁠面直⁠接接動⁠詞

他是要把它還給我，還是要拿給大家看？

選擇疑問句列出兩個以上的選項，問對方選哪一個。

兩⁠個選⁠項共⁠用一⁠個開⁠頭

Is he going to give it back to me or show it to everybody?

開頭是助動詞和主詞，也可以包括後面的字，像 Is he going to。

動⁠詞 B 的形⁠式要和開⁠頭相⁠合

Does she still teach the evening class or runs the library now?

Does she still teach the evening class or run the library now?

開頭是 Does。
動詞 A 和動詞 B 都用原形。

兩⁠個選⁠項⁠的主⁠詞不⁠同⁠時，不⁠省⁠略

Is he going to give it back to me or show it to everybody?

Is he going to give it back or she keep it?

Is he going to give it back, or is she going to keep it?

第二個選項的主詞是 she，和開頭的 he 不同。
第二個選項寫成完整的問句。

主⁠詞和助⁠動⁠詞一⁠起省⁠掉

Is he going to give it back to me or show it to everybody?

Did you call the landlord or you email him?

Did you call the landlord or email him?

or 後面直接接動詞。

or 後⁠面要接動⁠詞

Is he going to give it back to me or show it to everybody?

Do you agree with the plan or against it?

Do you agree with the plan or oppose it?

against it 是介系詞片語，不是動詞。

or 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞或 not 的問⁠句，不⁠屬⁠於省⁠略⁠的選⁠擇疑⁠問⁠句

or + 名詞

Would you like tea or coffee?

你要喝茶還是咖啡？
or not

Are you coming or not?

你到底來不來？

or 後面接名詞時，不接動詞。
or not 代替否定的選項。回答用 yes 或 no。

例句

① Do、Does、Did + 主詞 + 原形 or 原形

  1. Do you walk to school or take the bus?
    你走路上學，還是搭公車？
  2. Does your brother live with you or have his own place?
    你哥哥跟你一起住，還是自己在外面住？
  3. Did you call her or send her a message?
    你是打電話給她，還是傳訊息給她？
  4. Did the plane land on time or arrive late?
    飛機是準時降落，還是誤點了？
  5. Does the pain stay the same or come and go?
    這個痛是一直都一樣，還是一陣一陣的？
  6. Do they sell tickets online or make you buy them at the door?
    他們是在網路上賣票，還是要你到現場買？
  7. Did he say sorry or just walk away?
    他有道歉，還是直接走掉了？
  8. Does she teach at the school or just help out there?
    她是在學校教書，還是只是去幫忙？

② be + 主詞 + 動詞-ing or 動詞-ing

  1. Are you working tonight or staying home?
    你今晚要上班，還是待在家？
  2. Was she crying or laughing?
    她那時是在哭，還是在笑？
  3. Is he sleeping or just resting his eyes?
    他是睡著了，還是只是閉目養神？
  4. Are they building a new house or fixing the old one?
    他們是在蓋新房子，還是在修舊的？
  5. Was the dog barking at us or chasing a cat?
    那隻狗是在對我們叫，還是在追貓？

③ have、has + 主詞 + 過去分詞 or 過去分詞

  1. Have you finished the book or given up on it?
    你是看完那本書了，還是放棄了？
  2. Has the shop closed for good or just moved?
    那家店是收掉了，還是只是搬家了？
  3. Has the manager quit or gone on holiday?
    經理是辭職了，還是去度假了？

④ 情態動詞 + 主詞 + 原形 or 原形

  1. Will you drive or take the train?
    你要開車，還是搭火車？
  2. Should we wait for him or start without him?
    我們該等他，還是先開始？
  3. Could you email the file or bring it on a USB stick?
    你可以把檔案用 email 寄過來，還是用隨身碟帶來？
  4. Would you like to eat out or cook at home?
    你想出去吃，還是在家煮？
  5. Should I text you or call you when I get there?
    我到了以後，要傳訊息給你，還是打給你？

⑤ be + 主詞 + going to + 原形 or 原形

  1. Are you going to fix it yourself or call someone?
    你要自己修，還是找人來修？
  2. Is she going to stay in Tokyo or move back home?
    她要留在東京，還是搬回老家？
  3. Are we going to walk or get a taxi?
    我們要走路，還是叫計程車？

⑥ it + be + 形容詞 + to + 原形 or (to) 原形

  1. Is it cheaper to fly or take the train?
    搭飛機比較便宜，還是搭火車？
  2. Is it better to call her now or wait until morning?
    現在打給她比較好，還是等到早上？
  3. Is it easier to rent a car or to take the bus?
    租車比較方便，還是搭公車？
  4. Would it be kinder to tell him now or to let him find out for himself?
    現在就告訴他比較體貼，還是讓他自己發現？

⑦ 選項不只兩個

  1. Do you want to watch a film, play cards or go for a walk?
    你想看電影、打牌，還是去散步？
  2. Did you lose your phone, break it or leave it at home?
    你的手機是弄丟了、摔壞了，還是忘在家裡？
  3. Should we paint the room, change the curtains or leave it as it is?
    我們該粉刷房間、換窗簾，還是維持原樣？
  4. Are you going to study, work or take a year off after high school?
    高中畢業後，你要念書、工作，還是休息一年？
