She likes to listen to music and watch TV.

她喜歡聽音樂和看電視。

主⁠詞一⁠樣⁠時，第⁠二⁠個動⁠作省⁠略主⁠詞

She likes to listen to music and watch TV.

我們去公園踢足球。

助⁠動⁠詞和 to 也一⁠起省⁠略

She likes to listen to music and watch TV.

我可以洗碗或擦碗。

省⁠略 to 時，第⁠二⁠個動⁠詞也用原⁠形

She likes to listen to music and watch TV.

My dad likes to fish and sleeping in the sun.

My dad likes to fish and sleep in the sun.

兩⁠個動⁠作都⁠是過⁠去⁠的⁠事，都用過⁠去⁠式

She likes to listen to music and watch TV.

Last week we visited my aunt and stay for dinner.

Last week we visited my aunt and stayed for dinner.

主⁠詞不⁠一⁠樣⁠時，不⁠能省⁠略

She likes to listen to music and watch TV.

I cook dinner and washes the dishes.

I cook dinner and my brother washes the dishes.

例句

① 現在式 + and + 動詞

  1. Every morning I walk the dog and buy a newspaper.
    我每天早上遛狗，然後買份報紙。
  2. We have a short break at ten. After the break, we go back to class and study.
    我們十點有一段短短的下課時間。下課後，我們回教室讀書。
  3. My mornings are busy. I get up at seven and take a shower.
    我的早上很忙。我七點起床，然後洗澡。
  4. My mum works late on Mondays. My dad comes home and cooks dinner.
    我媽媽星期一工作到很晚。我爸爸回家煮晚餐。
  5. My grandmother doesn't like supermarkets. She goes to the market and buys fresh fruit.
    我奶奶不喜歡超市。她去市場買新鮮水果。
  6. I'm free on Saturday afternoons. Sometimes I meet my friends and play tennis.
    我星期六下午有空。有時候我跟朋友見面打網球。

② to + 動詞 + and + 動詞

  1. My daughter is very quiet. She likes to read and draw.
    我女兒很安靜。她喜歡看書和畫畫。
  2. We've walked for three hours. I want to rest and eat something.
    我們走了三個小時。我想休息一下，吃點東西。
  3. My cousins always start the party. They like to sing and dance.
    我的表親們總是帶動派對。他們喜歡唱歌跳舞。
  4. Grandma is coming tomorrow. We need to clean the kitchen and wash the car.
    奶奶明天要來。我們要打掃廚房，還要洗車。
  5. My brother finished school last month. He wants to travel and see the world.
    我哥哥上個月畢業了。他想去旅行，看看這個世界。

③ can / will + 動詞 + or / and + 動詞

  1. I cooked yesterday, so it's your turn. You can cook or wash up tonight.
    我昨天煮飯了，所以換你。今晚你可以煮飯或洗碗。
  2. The meeting is over. You can stay here or go home.
    會議結束了。你可以待在這裡，或是回家。
  3. Here's the plan for Saturday. We'll have lunch and go to the museum.
    這是星期六的計畫。我們會吃午餐，然後去博物館。
  4. She's only four, but my little sister can read and write her name.
    她才四歲，可是我妹妹會讀，也會寫自己的名字。
  5. My plane lands at nine. I'll call you or send a text.
    我的飛機九點降落。我會打給你，或是傳簡訊。

④ 過去式 + and + 過去式

  1. Last Sunday was sunny. We drove to the coast and walked along the cliffs.
    上星期天天氣很好。我們開車去海邊，沿著懸崖散步。
  2. We sat down at the restaurant. The waiter brought our drinks and took our order.
    我們在餐廳坐下來。服務生送來飲料，幫我們點菜。
  3. I was very tired last night. I finished my homework and went to bed.
    我昨天晚上很累。我寫完作業就去睡了。
  4. My parents went south for the weekend. They visited Tainan and ate a lot of food.
    我爸媽週末去南部。他們去台南吃了很多東西。
  5. My sister got the job. She called her mother and told her the news.
    我姊姊得到那份工作了。她打給她媽媽，告訴她這個消息。

⑤ but / 否定和不同的句型

  1. Coffee keeps me awake. I like coffee but don't drink it at night.
    咖啡會讓我睡不著。我喜歡咖啡，但是晚上不喝。
  2. My son ran in the school race. He tried hard but didn't win.
    我兒子參加了學校的賽跑。他很努力，但是沒贏。
  3. The party is getting boring. Do you want to stay or go?
    派對越來越無聊了。你想留下還是走？
  4. Please turn the music down. I'm tired and want to sleep.
    請把音樂關小聲。我很累，想睡覺。
  5. My aunt takes the train every day. She lives in Taipei but works in Taoyuan.
    我阿姨每天搭火車。她住在台北，但在桃園上班。
  6. We stayed home on Saturday night. We cooked dinner, watched a film and went to bed.
    我們星期六晚上待在家。我們煮了晚餐，看了電影，然後去睡覺。
  7. I can't sleep well these days. My cat sleeps all day and plays all night.
    我最近睡不好。我的貓整天睡覺，整晚玩。
  8. My grandfather is ninety and very healthy. He doesn't smoke or drink.
    我爺爺九十歲了，身體很好。他不抽菸，也不喝酒。
  9. Ann is always in a hurry in the morning. She reads the news and has breakfast at the same time.
    Ann 早上總是很趕。她一邊看新聞一邊吃早餐。
