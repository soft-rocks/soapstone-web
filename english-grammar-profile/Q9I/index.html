It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

白天確實下過雨，但並不是突然下的大雨。

It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

I did lock the door.

It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

Our school did raised more money than last year.

Our school did raise more money than last year.

be 動⁠詞不⁠用 did 加⁠強

It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

The hotel did be very noisy at night.

The hotel really was very noisy at night.

did 後⁠面不⁠接 could

It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

did 回⁠應對⁠方⁠的懷⁠疑

It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

一般

Our school fair was a big success. We had 34 games at the fair.

我們學校的園遊會很成功。園遊會有 34 個遊戲。
加強

You say there was nothing for children to do. We did have 34 games at the fair.

您說沒有給小孩玩的東西。園遊會確實有 34 個遊戲。

代⁠替動⁠詞⁠的 did 後⁠面不⁠接動⁠詞

It is true that it did rain during the day, but it was not a sudden downpour.

加強

Don't blame me. I did tell him about the meeting.

別怪我。我真的有跟他說開會的事。
代替動詞

Tom missed the meeting. He said I hadn't told him, but I did.

湯姆沒來開會。他說我沒跟他說，可是我有說。

例句

① 回應懷疑或否認

  1. I did lock the door. I remember turning the key.
    我真的有鎖門，我記得有轉鑰匙。
  2. Dad, it's Sunday. You did promise to take me to the zoo!
    爸，今天是星期天。你明明答應過要帶我去動物園！
  3. I did send the email. Check your spam folder.
    我真的有寄那封電子郵件，你看一下垃圾郵件匣。
  4. The waiter says we didn't pay. She did pay the bill. I saw her do it.
    服務生說我們沒付錢。她真的有付帳，我看到她付了。
  5. Your car is in the ditch. I did warn you about the ice on the road.
    你的車掉進溝裡了。我有提醒過你路上結冰了。
  6. We did book a table for four. It's under the name Wilson.
    我們真的有訂四人桌，用威爾森的名字訂的。

② 承認一部分事實，再用 but 說出不同的地方

  1. The match wasn't cancelled. The wind did blow hard, but the players could still play.
    比賽沒有取消。風確實吹得很大，不過球員還是可以比賽。
  2. The train did arrive late, but only by five minutes.
    火車確實晚到了，不過只晚了五分鐘。
  3. Mum, I did eat some of the cake, but I didn't finish it.
    媽，那個蛋糕我確實吃了一些，可是我沒有吃完。
  4. The report is too negative. Prices did rise last year, but not as much as people expected.
    這份報告太悲觀了。去年物價確實漲了，但沒有大家預期的那麼多。
  5. Don't be too hard on Ben. He did make a few mistakes, but overall it was a good speech.
    別對班太嚴格。他確實犯了幾個錯，不過整體來說是一場好的演講。

③ 正式書信：說明事實

  1. Dear Editor, we did reach the target figure, and we have sent most of the money to the hospital.
    編輯您好，我們確實達到了目標金額，大部分的錢已經捐給醫院了。
  2. Dear Sir, there were plenty of things to do. We did have a stall with drinks and snacks.
    敬啟者，活動有很多東西可以玩。我們確實有一個賣飲料點心的攤位。
  3. Dear Mrs Hill, our staff did contact you on the day of the delivery.
    希爾太太您好，我們的員工在送貨當天確實有聯絡您。
  4. Dear Mr Ross, we did inform all guests about the change of time.
    羅斯先生您好，我們確實通知了所有賓客時間改了。
  5. Dear Sir, the company did offer a full refund, which you refused.
    敬啟者，公司確實提出全額退款，是您拒絕的。

④ 跟預期對比：最後真的發生了

  1. Everyone said the new plan would fail, but it did work in the end.
    大家都說新計畫會失敗，結果最後真的成功了。
  2. I didn't think Uncle Joe would come to the party, but he did turn up at the end.
    我以為喬叔叔不會來派對，結果他最後真的出現了。
  3. We nearly missed the flight, but we did make it.
    我們差點趕不上飛機，不過還是趕上了。
  4. My sister was nervous about the exam, but she did pass.
    我妹妹很擔心那場考試，不過她真的考過了。
  5. It took ages, but the parcel did arrive eventually.
    等了好久，包裹最後還是到了。

⑤ 加強感謝或感受

  1. I did enjoy the party last night. Thank you for inviting me.
    我昨晚在派對上玩得很開心，謝謝你邀請我。
  2. Thank you for everything, Sam. We did appreciate your help with the move.
    山姆，謝謝你做的一切。你幫我們搬家，我們真的很感激。
  3. I did like your presentation. It was very clear.
    我真的很喜歡你的報告，講得很清楚。
  4. Thank you for coming to the school. The children did love the puppet show.
    謝謝你們來學校。孩子們真的很喜歡那場木偶戲。
  5. Welcome home! I did miss you while you were away.
    歡迎回家！你不在的時候我真的很想你。

⑥ 加上 really 或 actually

  1. Please don't be angry about my grade. I really did try my best.
    請別因為我的成績生氣。我真的盡力了。
  2. My little brother entered the chess contest. He actually did win the competition!
    我弟弟參加了西洋棋比賽。他真的贏了比賽！
  3. My grandparents had very little money. They really did build the whole house themselves.
    我爺爺奶奶以前沒什麼錢。那整棟房子真的是他們自己蓋的。
  4. I actually did see a fox in the garden this morning.
    我今天早上真的在花園裡看到一隻狐狸。
  5. She really did say that. I was there.
    她真的那樣說了，我當時在場。
