Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

Nisha 跟我說你上禮拜出了意外。

間接⁠受⁠詞是聽⁠話⁠的⁠人，子⁠句是告⁠訴對⁠方⁠的內⁠容

Nisha 跟我說你上禮拜出了意外。

子句前面省略了 that。

有 that 和省⁠略 that，意⁠思一⁠樣

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

有 that

My brother told me that he was getting married.

我哥哥跟我說他要結婚了。
省略 that

My brother told me he was getting married.

我哥哥跟我說他要結婚了。

tell 後⁠面有聽⁠話⁠的⁠人，say 後⁠面沒⁠有

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

tell 後⁠面要接聽⁠話⁠的人

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

He told that he was sorry.

He told me that he was sorry.

要說聽⁠話⁠的⁠人，用 tell，不⁠用 say

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

She said me the shop was closed.

She told me the shop was closed.

代⁠名⁠詞⁠的間接⁠受⁠詞用受格

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

You promised I that you would help.

You promised me that you would help.

tell 後⁠面直⁠接接間接⁠受⁠詞，不⁠加 to

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

My dad told to me that he was busy.

My dad told me that he was busy.

tell + 間接⁠受⁠詞 + to 說⁠的⁠是要對⁠方做⁠的事

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

My mum told me to clean my room.

我媽叫我打掃房間。

say 要說⁠出聽⁠話⁠的人⁠時，在人前⁠面加 to

Nisha told me you had an accident last week.

She said to me that she was happy.

她對我說她很開心。

例句

① tell + 間接受詞 + 子句

  1. "How is Tom?" "Not great. His wife told me he has a bad cold."
    Tom 怎麼樣？不太好。他太太跟我說他得了重感冒。
  2. I told my boss I was ill, so I stayed at home.
    我跟老闆說我生病了，所以我待在家。
  3. Did I tell you that my sister has a new car?
    我有跟你說過我姊姊買了新車嗎？
  4. We were hungry, but the waiter told us the chef had gone home.
    我們很餓，可是服務生跟我們說廚師已經回家了。
  5. "Why are you so happy?" "My teacher told me I passed the exam!"
    你為什麼這麼開心？老師跟我說我考試及格了！
  6. Please tell your parents that the school trip is on Friday.
    請跟你們的家長說校外教學是星期五。

② promise + 間接受詞 + 子句

  1. You promised me that you would help me paint my bedroom.
    你答應過我會幫我油漆臥室。
  2. "Can I have a dog?" "I promise you we'll talk about it at the weekend."
    我可以養狗嗎？我答應你，我們週末會談這件事。
  3. He promised his mum he would be home by ten.
    他答應他媽媽十點前會回家。
  4. The shop promised us the sofa would arrive on Monday.
    店家答應我們沙發星期一會送到。

③ show + 間接受詞 + 子句

  1. The test results showed the doctor that my leg was broken.
    檢查結果讓醫生看出我的腿斷了。
  2. Her smile showed me she liked the present.
    她的笑容讓我看出她喜歡這個禮物。
  3. This photo shows us that the old town was very small.
    這張照片讓我們看到舊城區以前很小。

④ remind + 間接受詞 + 子句

  1. "Remind me that I have a meeting at three." "OK, I'll call you at half past two."
    提醒我三點有個會。好，我兩點半打給你。
  2. My phone reminded me it was Anna's birthday.
    我的手機提醒我今天是 Anna 的生日。
  3. The teacher reminded the class that the library closes early on Fridays.
    老師提醒全班圖書館星期五提早關門。

⑤ teach + 間接受詞 + 子句

  1. My grandfather taught me that hard work is important.
    我爺爺教會我努力很重要。
  2. That trip taught us we needed a bigger tent.
    那次旅行讓我們學到我們需要一個大一點的帳篷。
  3. Living alone taught her that she can look after herself.
    一個人住讓她學到她能照顧好自己。

⑥ 間接受詞是名詞

  1. Mia told the police she saw a man in a red coat.
    Mia 跟警察說她看到一個穿紅外套的男人。
  2. Please tell the driver you want to get off at the bank.
    請跟司機說你要在銀行那站下車。
  3. I promised my best friend I would keep her secret.
    我答應我最好的朋友會幫她保守秘密。
  4. The guide told the tourists the museum was free on Sundays.
    導遊跟遊客說博物館星期天免費。

⑦ 問句

  1. "Did she tell you she was leaving?" "No, I had no idea."
    她有跟你說她要走了嗎？沒有，我完全不知道。
  2. Who told you that the party was cancelled?
    誰跟你說派對取消了？
  3. "Didn't I tell you the shop was closed?" "Oh, sorry. I forgot."
    我不是跟你說店關了嗎？喔，抱歉。我忘了。

⑧ 否定

  1. You didn't tell me the meeting was at nine!
    你沒跟我說會議是九點！
  2. Nobody told us the train was late, so we waited for an hour.
    沒有人跟我們說火車誤點，所以我們等了一個小時。
  3. My son didn't tell me he had homework.
    我兒子沒跟我說他有作業。
  4. "Does your boss know about the new job?" "No, I haven't told anyone that I'm leaving."
    你老闆知道新工作的事嗎？不知道，我還沒跟任何人說我要離職。
  5. He never told his parents that he failed the test.
    他從來沒跟爸媽說他考試沒過。
