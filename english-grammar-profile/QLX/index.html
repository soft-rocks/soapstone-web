長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面加 the most

It is the most famous place in Edinburgh.

足球是巴西最受歡迎的運動。

三⁠個音⁠節以⁠上和 ⁠-⁠ful 結⁠尾⁠的形⁠容⁠詞用 the most

It is the most famous place in Edinburgh.

三個音節以上

Health is the most important thing in life.

健康是人生中最重要的事。
-ful / -ous

She's the most careful driver I know.

她是我認識開車最小心的人。

長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠est

It is the most famous place in Edinburgh.

most 前⁠面要加 the

This is the most beautiful beach in Taiwan.

用了 most，形⁠容⁠詞就⁠不⁠加 ⁠-⁠est

It is the most famous place in Edinburgh.

She's the most famousest singer in Korea.

She's the most famous singer in Korea.

例句

① the most + 三個音節以上的形容詞

  1. I didn't buy the phone. It was the most expensive one in the shop.
    我沒買那支手機，那是店裡最貴的一支。
  2. Football is the most popular sport in Brazil.
    足球是巴西最受歡迎的運動。
  3. Health is the most important thing in life.
    健康是人生中最重要的事。
  4. This is the most beautiful beach on the island.
    這是島上最美的海灘。
  5. History is the most interesting subject for me.
    對我來說，歷史是最有趣的科目。
  6. The most dangerous part of the road is near the bridge.
    這條路最危險的路段在橋附近。
  7. Which was the most difficult question?
    哪一題最難？

② the most + -ful / -ous 形容詞

  1. The night market is the most famous place to eat in Taichung.
    夜市是台中最有名的美食去處。
  2. She's the most careful driver I know.
    她是我認識開車最小心的人。
  3. Who is the most famous person from your town?
    你們鎮上最有名的人是誰？
  4. That was the most useful lesson of the course.
    那是這門課最有用的一堂。
  5. He's the most nervous player on the team.
    他是隊上最緊張的球員。

③ the most + -ing / -ed 形容詞

  1. That was the most boring meeting ever.
    那是有史以來最無聊的會議。
  2. Everyone agreed the final was the most exciting match of the season.
    大家都同意決賽是這一季最刺激的比賽。
  3. She was the most surprised of all of us.
    她是我們所有人裡最驚訝的。
  4. For me, the garden is the most relaxing spot at home.
    對我來說，花園是家裡最讓人放鬆的地方。
  5. I was the most tired person on the bus.
    我是公車上最累的人。

④ the most + 形容詞，省略名詞

  1. All the bags are nice, but the red one is the most expensive.
    所有的包包都很好看，不過紅色的最貴。
  2. Of all the teachers, Mrs. Chen is the most patient.
    所有老師裡，陳老師最有耐心。
  3. We looked at four laptops. The silver one was the most powerful.
    我們看了四台筆電，銀色那台效能最好。
  4. The last test was the most difficult.
    最後一次考試最難。
  5. Of the three books, this one is the most interesting.
    三本書裡，這本最有趣。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. What's the most delicious thing you've ever eaten?
    你吃過最好吃的東西是什麼？
  2. Who's the most generous person in your family?
    你們家誰最大方？
  3. Is this the most comfortable chair you have?
    這是你們最舒服的椅子嗎？
  4. Which city is the most crowded in summer?
    哪個城市夏天最擁擠？
  5. From the top of the tower, we had the most amazing view of the harbor.
    從塔頂上，我們看到了港口最壯觀的景色。
  6. It was the most stressful week of the year.
    那是今年壓力最大的一週。
  7. Visiting the castle was the most memorable part of our trip.
    參觀城堡是我們旅程中最難忘的部分。
  8. What's the most important thing to pack?
    打包最重要的東西是什麼？
