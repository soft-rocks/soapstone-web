Will he have changed?

他會不會已經變了？

是⁠非疑⁠問⁠句

Will he have changed?

例句

Won't the film have started by eight?

八點以前電影不是已經開始了嗎？
例句

Will you have finished by six? — Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

你六點前會做完嗎？——會。／不會。

否定疑問句用 Won't 開頭，說話者以為事情會發生。

wh⁠-⁠ 疑⁠問⁠句

例句

How much will we have spent by the end of the trip?

到旅行結束，我們會花掉多少錢？
例句

Who will have heard the news by then?

到那時候，誰會已經聽到這個消息？

疑問詞是主詞時，句子不換位置。

例句

① Will + 主詞 + have + 過去分詞 ...?

  1. Will the kids have grown much by the time we see them again?
    等我們再見到孩子們的時候，他們會不會已經長大很多？
  2. Will you have finished the report by Friday?
    你星期五前會寫完報告嗎？
  3. Will the guests have arrived by the time we get back?
    等我們回來的時候，客人會已經到了嗎？
  4. Will they have repaired the road before winter?
    冬天以前他們會把路修好嗎？
  5. Will she have heard the news by then?
    到那時候，她會已經聽到消息了嗎？
  6. Will the shops have closed when the film ends?
    電影結束的時候，商店會不會已經關門了？
  7. Will the bank have approved our loan by Monday?
    到星期一，銀行會核准我們的貸款嗎？
  8. Will the paint have dried by tomorrow?
    到明天油漆會乾了嗎？
  9. Will you have read the book before the next class?
    下一堂課以前你會讀完那本書嗎？
  10. Will the children have eaten when we pick them up?
    我們去接孩子的時候，他們會已經吃過飯了嗎？
  11. Will the professor have marked the dissertations by June?
    到六月，教授會改完論文嗎？
  12. Will the snow have melted by the weekend?
    到週末，雪會融化了嗎？
  13. Won't they have left by the time we arrive?
    等我們到的時候，他們不是已經走了嗎？
  14. Won't the meeting have finished by four?
    四點以前會議不是已經結束了嗎？
  15. Will the town have grown much in ten years?
    十年後這個城鎮會不會已經變大很多？
  16. Will my parcel have arrived before I go on holiday?
    我去度假之前，包裹會送到嗎？

② wh- 疑問詞 + will + 主詞 + have + 過去分詞 ...?

  1. How much will you have paid back by next year?
    到明年，你會還掉多少錢？
  2. How many books will you have read by the end of the year?
    到年底，你會讀完幾本書？
  3. Where will they have moved to by next year?
    到明年，他們會搬到哪裡去？
  4. How long will you have lived here by Christmas?
    到聖誕節，你住在這裡就會滿多久了？
  5. What will you have done by the time you're thirty?
    到三十歲的時候，你會做完哪些事？
  6. How far will we have walked by lunchtime?
    到午餐時間，我們會走了多遠？
  7. When will the builders have finished the house?
    工人什麼時候會蓋好房子？
  8. How many countries will she have visited by the end of the tour?
    到巡迴結束，她會去過幾個國家？
  9. What will the city have done about the traffic by then?
    到那時候，市政府會對交通做了什麼？

③ Who / What + will have + 過去分詞 ...?

  1. Who will have scored the most goals by the end of the season?
    到球季結束，誰會進最多球？
  2. Who will have won the league by May?
    到五月，誰會已經贏得聯賽冠軍？
  3. What will have happened to the old house by then?
    到那時候，那間老房子會變成什麼樣子？
  4. Who will have taken over the company by next year?
    到明年，誰會已經接手這家公司？
  5. What will have changed in the town when we come back?
    等我們回來的時候，鎮上會有什麼改變？
  6. Who will have finished first, do you think?
    你覺得誰會先完成？
