因為她坐在角落，所以整間咖啡廳她都看得到。

主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠式 + because + 主⁠詞 + was / were + ⁠-⁠ing

because因為。
was / were + -⁠ing當時正在進行的事，當作原因。

because 子⁠句放⁠在⁠前⁠面

Yesterday a TV company came to my school because they were doing a science quiz.

例句

Because she was sitting in the corner, she could see the whole café.

因為她坐在角落，她可以看到整個咖啡館。

because 子句放在句首時，後面加逗號。

解⁠釋自⁠己為⁠什⁠麼沒⁠做

例句

I didn't answer because I was driving.

我沒接，因為我在開車。

過去進行式常用來解釋為什麼沒接電話或沒來。

⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面要⁠有 was 或 were

Yesterday a TV company came to my school because they were doing a science quiz.

I didn't hear you because I listening to music.

I didn't hear you because I was listening to music.

主⁠詞是 they，用 were

Yesterday a TV company came to my school because they were doing a science quiz.

They came because they was making a film.

They came because they were making a film.

because 和 so 不⁠一⁠起用

Yesterday a TV company came to my school because they were doing a science quiz.

Because it was raining, so we stayed inside.

Because it was raining, we stayed inside.

例句

① 主詞 + 過去式 + because + 主詞 + was / were + -ing

  1. Yesterday a TV company came to my school because they were making a science quiz show.
    昨天一家電視公司來我們學校，因為他們在做一個科學問答節目。
  2. I didn't answer your call because I was driving.
    我沒接你的電話，因為我在開車。
  3. She missed the bus because she was talking to a friend.
    她錯過了公車，因為她在跟朋友說話。
  4. We stayed inside because it was raining heavily.
    我們待在室內，因為雨下得很大。
  5. He didn't hear the doorbell because he was having a shower.
    他沒聽到門鈴，因為他在洗澡。
  6. I burnt the rice because I was watching TV.
    我把飯煮焦了，因為我在看電視。

② Because + 主詞 + was / were + -ing, + 主詞 + 過去式

  1. Because she was sitting in the corner, she could see the whole café in front of her.
    因為她坐在角落，她可以看到前面整個咖啡館。
  2. Because I was walking near the river, I saw the boat race.
    因為我在河邊散步，我看到了划船比賽。
  3. Because they were playing so loudly, the neighbours complained.
    因為他們玩得太大聲，鄰居抱怨了。
  4. Because we were standing at the front, we could see the band clearly.
    因為我們站在前面，我們可以清楚看到樂團。
  5. Because the trains were running late, many people missed their flights.
    因為火車誤點，很多人錯過了班機。
  6. Because it was getting dark, we turned back.
    因為天快黑了，我們就往回走。

③ 主詞 + couldn't + 原形動詞 + because + 主詞 + was / were + -ing

  1. I couldn't sleep because the dog was barking all night.
    我睡不著，因為狗叫了一整晚。
  2. We couldn't hear the teacher because the workers were drilling outside.
    我們聽不到老師，因為工人在外面鑽地。
  3. She couldn't concentrate because her brother was playing the drums.
    她沒辦法專心，因為她弟弟在打鼓。
  4. I couldn't take any photos because my brother was using the camera.
    我沒辦法拍照，因為我哥哥在用相機。
  5. They couldn't land because a storm was coming.
    他們沒辦法降落，因為暴風雨要來了。
  6. He couldn't come to the phone because he was cooking.
    他沒辦法來接電話，因為他在煮飯。

④ 主詞 + was late / tired + because + 主詞 + was / were + -ing

  1. I was late for work because the trains were running slowly.
    我上班遲到了，因為火車開得很慢。
  2. She was tired because she was working night shifts.
    她很累，因為她在上夜班。
  3. He was happy because he was going on holiday the next day.
    他很高興，因為他隔天要去度假。
  4. We were nervous because the plane was shaking a lot.
    我們很緊張，因為飛機晃得很厲害。
  5. I was worried because my son was driving in the snow.
    我很擔心，因為我兒子在雪中開車。
  6. They were excited because they were moving to a new house.
    他們很興奮，因為他們要搬到新家。

⑤ The reason was that + 主詞 + was / were + -ing

  1. I missed the meeting. The reason was that I was waiting for a delivery.
    我錯過了會議。原因是我在等一個快遞。
  2. The road was closed. The reason was that they were repairing the bridge.
    那條路封了。原因是他們在修橋。
  3. She left early. The reason was that her son was feeling ill.
    她提早走了。原因是她兒子覺得不舒服。
  4. The shop was busy. The reason was that it was having a big sale.
    那家店很多人。原因是它在辦大特價。
  5. I didn't reply. The reason was that I was travelling in the mountains.
    我沒回覆。原因是我在山裡旅行。
  6. The town was full of people. The reason was that a festival was taking place.
    鎮上都是人。原因是正在舉辦一個節慶。
  7. He was quiet at dinner. The reason was that he was thinking about his exam.
    他吃晚餐的時候很安靜。原因是他在想他的考試。
