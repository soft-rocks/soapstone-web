Mr Miller, the Sales Director, will visit us next Thursday.

業務總監 Miller 先生下週四會來拜訪我們。

主⁠詞 + will + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + 時⁠間

will、時⁠間、won't be able to

will會。說確定的計畫。
時間常是日期、星期幾或幾點。
won't be able to說到時候沒辦法做的事。

正⁠式⁠的通⁠知

Mr Miller, the Sales Director, will visit us next Thursday.

例句

The meeting will start at nine.

會議九點開始。

公司、學校和機構的通知常用 will 說安排。

說不⁠能出⁠席

例句

I'm sorry, but I won't be able to attend your class tomorrow.

很抱歉，我明天沒辦法來上您的課。

I won't be able to attend 常用在請假和道歉。

will 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Mr Miller, the Sales Director, will visit us next Thursday.

The director will to visit us.

The director will visit us.

be able 後⁠面要接 to

I won't be able attend the meeting.

I won't be able to attend the meeting.

will 後⁠面不⁠能接 can，要⁠用 be able to

Mr Miller, the Sales Director, will visit us next Thursday.

I won't can come tomorrow.

I won't be able to come tomorrow.

例句

① 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + 時間：公司的安排

  1. Please tidy your desks. Mr Miller, the Sales Director, will visit us next Thursday.
    請整理好你們的桌子。業務總監米勒先生下星期四會來拜訪我們。
  2. The new manager will start work on the first of March.
    新經理三月一日開始上班。
  3. The office will close at three on Christmas Eve.
    聖誕夜辦公室三點關門。
  4. Our team will move to the fourth floor in June.
    我們的團隊六月會搬到四樓。
  5. The training course will take place on the tenth and eleventh of May.
    訓練課程會在五月十日和十一日舉行。
  6. The company will announce the results on Monday morning.
    公司星期一早上會公布結果。

② I won't be able to + 原形動詞 + 時間：說不能出席

  1. I'm sorry, but I won't be able to attend your class tomorrow. I have a hospital appointment.
    很抱歉，我明天沒辦法來上您的課。我有醫院的預約。
  2. I won't be able to come to the meeting on Friday.
    我星期五沒辦法來開會。
  3. I'm afraid I won't be able to finish the report until Monday.
    恐怕我要到星期一才能完成報告。
  4. We won't be able to join you for dinner on Saturday.
    我們星期六沒辦法跟你們一起吃晚餐。
  5. She won't be able to work next week because of her operation.
    她下星期因為手術沒辦法上班。
  6. I won't be able to answer emails while I'm on holiday.
    我度假的時候沒辦法回電子郵件。

③ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + 時刻：時刻表和行程

  1. The train to Edinburgh will leave from platform four at ten past eight.
    往愛丁堡的火車八點十分從第四月台出發。
  2. The tour bus will pick you up from the hotel at nine.
    遊覽車九點會到飯店接你。
  3. The flight will land in Madrid at half past two.
    班機兩點半會降落在馬德里。
  4. The museum tour will begin in the main hall at eleven.
    博物館導覽十一點在大廳開始。
  5. The ferry will arrive on the island at noon.
    渡輪中午會抵達島上。
  6. Lunch will be served at one o'clock in the dining room.
    午餐一點在餐廳供應。（被動）

④ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + 日期：學校和活動的安排

  1. The school will hold its sports day on the fifteenth of June.
    學校會在六月十五日舉行運動會。
  2. The exams will start on the second of May.
    考試五月二日開始。
  3. The new library will open to the public in September.
    新圖書館九月會對大眾開放。
  4. The book fair will stay open late on the last Thursday of the month.
    書展在這個月最後一個星期四會開到很晚。
  5. The concert will take place in the park on Saturday evening.
    音樂會會在星期六傍晚於公園舉行。
  6. Term will finish on the twentieth of December.
    學期在十二月二十日結束。

⑤ 主詞 + will + be + 地方 + 時間：說某人會在哪裡

  1. I will be in Paris from Monday to Wednesday next week.
    我下星期一到星期三會在巴黎。
  2. The doctor will be at the clinic every Tuesday afternoon.
    醫生每個星期二下午會在診所。
  3. Our sales team will be at the trade fair in Berlin next month.
    我們的業務團隊下個月會在柏林的商展。
  4. The head teacher will be away at a conference on Friday.
    校長星期五會去參加研討會，不在學校。
  5. I will be at my desk until six if you need me.
    如果你需要我，我六點前都會在座位上。
  6. The band will be in London for two nights only.
    那個樂團只會在倫敦待兩個晚上。
  7. My parents will be on holiday in Greece for the first two weeks of July.
    我爸媽七月的前兩個星期會在希臘度假。
