neither ... nor 強⁠調兩⁠件⁠事都⁠沒⁠發⁠生

They neither got married nor had children.

他既沒打電話，也沒寫信。

句子裡不再加 not。

Neither 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，兩⁠個子⁠句都倒⁠裝

They neither got married nor had children.

Neither did she reply, nor did she come to the party.

她既沒回覆，也沒來派對。

neither ... nor 已⁠經是否⁠定，不⁠再⁠加 not

They neither got married nor had children.

We don't neither walk nor exercise.

We neither walk nor exercise.

neither 配 nor

They neither got married nor had children.

She neither smiled or spoke.

She neither smiled nor spoke.

兩⁠個動⁠詞用同⁠一⁠個時⁠態

They neither got married nor had children.

He neither ate nor drinks anything.

He neither ate nor drank anything.

例句

① 過去式

  1. My uncle left home at fifteen. He neither finished school nor learned a trade.
    我叔叔十五歲就離家了。他既沒讀完書，也沒學一技之長。
  2. My birthday was last week. My cousin neither phoned nor sent a card.
    我的生日在上星期。我表哥既沒打電話，也沒寄卡片。
  3. The nurse gave my son two injections. He neither cried nor complained.
    護理師幫我兒子打了兩針。他既沒哭，也沒抱怨。
  4. The police asked about the robbery next door. We neither saw nor heard anything.
    警察問隔壁搶案的事。我們什麼都沒看到，也沒聽到。
  5. I returned the broken lamp a month ago. The shop neither replied to my email nor sent the money back.
    我一個月前退回那盞壞掉的燈。那家店既沒回我的電子郵件，也沒退錢。
  6. The road to the mountains was very bumpy. We neither slept nor rested on the night bus.
    去山上的路很顛簸。在夜車上我們既沒睡，也沒休息。

② 現在式

  1. We take the bus everywhere. We neither own a car nor ride bikes.
    我們去哪裡都搭公車。我們既沒有車，也不騎腳踏車。
  2. He's ninety and still walks every day. My grandfather neither smokes nor drinks.
    他九十歲了，還每天走路。我爺爺既不抽菸，也不喝酒。
  3. My flatmate leaves everything to me. She neither cooks nor cleans at home.
    我室友什麼都丟給我。她在家既不煮飯，也不打掃。
  4. I'm taking it back to the shop. The new phone neither looks good nor works well.
    我要把它拿回店裡。那支新手機既不好看，也不好用。
  5. My brother doesn't know about the election. He neither reads the news nor watches TV.
    我哥哥不知道選舉的事。他既不看新聞，也不看電視。

③ 助動詞 + neither + 動詞 + nor + 動詞

  1. The school nurse gave the parents some advice. Children should neither skip breakfast nor eat too many sweets.
    學校護理師給家長一些建議。小孩既不該不吃早餐，也不該吃太多甜食。
  2. A police officer knocked on my car window. You can neither park nor stop here.
    一個警察敲了我的車窗。這裡既不能停車，也不能暫停。
  3. I saw a bear on the path in front of me. I could neither move nor speak.
    我看到前面的小路上有一隻熊。我既動不了，也說不出話。
  4. The builders damaged our wall. They will neither pay nor apologize.
    工人弄壞了我們的牆。他們既不會付錢，也不會道歉。
  5. Our son is only nine months old. He can neither walk nor talk yet.
    我們的兒子才九個月大。他還不會走路，也不會說話。

④ neither + 動詞 + 受詞 + nor + 動詞 + 受詞

  1. My old boss offered me a part-time job. I neither wanted the job nor needed the money.
    我以前的老闆給我一份兼職工作。我既不想要那份工作，也不需要那筆錢。
  2. We stayed there for five nights. The hotel neither cleaned our room nor changed the towels.
    我們在那裡住了五晚。飯店既沒打掃我們的房間，也沒換毛巾。
  3. I went to my sister's flat after work. She neither answered the door nor picked up the phone.
    我下班後去我姊姊的公寓。她既沒應門，也沒接電話。
  4. I had a bad cough for two weeks. The doctor neither gave me medicine nor told me to rest.
    我咳得很嚴重，咳了兩個星期。醫生既沒開藥給我，也沒叫我休息。

⑤ Neither + 倒裝 和不同的句型

  1. Our guests never came to dinner. Neither did they call, nor did they send a message.
    我們的客人一直沒來吃晚餐。他們既沒打電話，也沒傳訊息。
  2. Our neighbours' tree fell on our car. Neither did they help, nor did they say sorry.
    我們鄰居的樹倒在我們的車上。他們既沒幫忙，也沒道歉。
  3. I asked my boss for a pay rise. She neither agreed nor said no.
    我跟我老闆要求加薪。她既沒同意，也沒拒絕。
  4. The new teacher had a hard first day. The children neither listened nor sat still.
    新老師的第一天很辛苦。孩子們既沒聽話，也沒坐好。
  5. My ex-boyfriend left town last month. I neither know nor care where he went.
    我前男友上個月離開了這個鎮。我不知道，也不在乎他去了哪裡。
  6. The manager wants to change the delivery routes. But the plan neither saves time nor cuts costs.
    經理想改送貨路線。可是這個計畫既省不了時間，也降不了成本。
  7. A reporter asked the actor about his secret wedding. He neither confirmed nor denied the story.
    一個記者問那個演員他秘密結婚的事。他對那件事既沒承認，也沒否認。
  8. The score was two all at the end. We neither won nor lost; it was a draw.
    最後比數是二比二。我們既沒贏也沒輸，是平手。
  9. I posted a letter to my aunt in Peru in May. The letter neither arrived nor came back.
    我五月寄了一封信給我在秘魯的阿姨。那封信既沒寄到，也沒退回來。
  10. My old friends moved to Spain ten years ago. They neither visited us nor invited us to their wedding.
    我的老朋友十年前搬去西班牙。他們既沒來看我們，也沒邀請我們參加婚禮。
