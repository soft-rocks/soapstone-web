There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

有一個難題是我們應該處理的。

受⁠詞移⁠到前⁠面，介⁠系⁠詞留⁠在句⁠尾

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

有一個難題是我們應該處理的。

一般的語序是 deal with one difficulty。

關⁠係子⁠句、wh 問⁠句和 wh 子⁠句⁠的介⁠系⁠詞都⁠可⁠以留⁠在句⁠尾

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

關係子句

This is the job which I applied for.

這就是我應徵的工作。
wh 問句

Who are you waiting for?

你在等誰？
wh 子句

I don't know who this bag belongs to.

我不知道這個包包是誰的。

to 不⁠定⁠詞和被⁠動句⁠的介⁠系⁠詞也留⁠在⁠後⁠面

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

名詞 + to 不定詞

I need someone to talk to.

我需要一個可以說話的人。
形容詞 + to 不定詞

This music is easy to listen to.

這種音樂聽起來很輕鬆。
被動

The problem was dealt with quickly.

這個問題很快就處理好了。

正⁠式⁠的語⁠序把介⁠系⁠詞放⁠在 which 或 whom 前⁠面

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

有一個難題是我們應該處理的。

這個語序比較正式，常用在書面。

受⁠詞移⁠到前⁠面以⁠後，介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面不⁠再放受⁠詞

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

This is the flat which I told you about it.

This is the flat which I told you about.

留⁠在句⁠尾⁠的介⁠系⁠詞不⁠能省⁠略

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

Who are you waiting?

Who are you waiting for?

介⁠系⁠詞移⁠到前⁠面⁠時用 which，不⁠用 that

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

the job for that I applied

the job for which I applied

介⁠系⁠詞移⁠到前⁠面⁠時用 whom，不⁠用 who

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

the woman with who I work

the woman with whom I work

片⁠語介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞⁠的小⁠品⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞都留⁠在句⁠尾

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

She's someone I look up to.

她是我尊敬的人。

說地⁠方⁠時，關⁠係子⁠句可⁠以用 where

There is one difficulty which we should deal with.

This is the town where I live.

這是我住的城鎮。

例句

① 關係子句

  1. "Is this the café you were talking about?" "Yes, the cakes are amazing."
    這就是你說的那家咖啡店嗎？對，蛋糕超好吃。
  2. Here's the report which I referred to in the meeting.
    這就是我開會時提到的報告。
  3. The hotel we stayed at was right next to the beach.
    我們住的旅館就在海邊。
  4. My grandmother is the person I worry about most.
    我奶奶是我最擔心的人。
  5. "Who's that?" "That's the boy my sister used to play with."
    那是誰？那是我姊姊小時候常一起玩的男生。
  6. There are some rules that all the students must agree to.
    有一些規定是所有學生都必須同意的。

② wh 問句

  1. "Who are you waving at?" "My cousin. She's across the street."
    你在對誰揮手？我表姊。她在對街。
  2. "What are you laughing about?" "Nothing. Just a funny message."
    你在笑什麼？沒什麼。只是一則好笑的訊息。
  3. "Which bus are you waiting for?" "The 23. It's late again."
    你在等哪一班公車？23 號。它又遲到了。
  4. "Who did you go to the cinema with?" "Some friends from work."
    你跟誰去看電影？幾個同事。
  5. "What are you thinking about?" "My exam tomorrow."
    你在想什麼？明天的考試。

③ wh 子句

  1. I'd like to give you some ideas of what people in my town want to listen to.
    我想給你一些想法，說說我們鎮上的人想聽什麼。
  2. Nobody knew what the teacher was talking about.
    沒有人知道老師在說什麼。
  3. Tell me who you're angry with.
    跟我說你在氣誰。
  4. I don't understand what she's so worried about.
    我不懂她到底在擔心什麼。

④ 名詞 + to 不定詞

  1. "Is my test result bad?" "No, there's nothing to worry about."
    我的檢查結果不好嗎？不會，沒什麼好擔心的。
  2. It's a long drive, so I need something to listen to.
    這段車程很長，所以我需要一些可以聽的東西。
  3. When I moved here, I had no one to talk to.
    我剛搬來的時候，沒有人可以說話。
  4. My son is bored after school. He needs a hobby to focus on.
    我兒子放學後很無聊。他需要一個可以專心投入的嗜好。

⑤ 形容詞 + to 不定詞

  1. Rules about school clothes aren't strict here, but our clothes have to be clean and nice to look at.
    這裡對學校服裝的規定不嚴格，可是我們的衣服要乾淨，看起來好看。
  2. "Is your new boss nice?" "Yes, she's easy to talk to."
    你的新老闆人好嗎？好啊，她很好溝通。
  3. My flatmate never cleans the kitchen. He's hard to live with.
    我室友從來不清廚房。跟他住很辛苦。

⑥ 被動

  1. "Did someone feed the cat?" "Yes, she's been looked after."
    有人餵貓了嗎？有，有人照顧牠了。
  2. Your complaint will be dealt with by the manager.
    您的投訴會由經理處理。
  3. This old song is still listened to by millions of people.
    這首老歌至今仍有幾百萬人在聽。
  4. The new park was talked about for months before it opened.
    新公園開放之前，大家已經談論了好幾個月。

⑦ 介系詞放在前面的正式語序

  1. The company has one problem with which it must deal immediately.
    公司有一個必須立刻處理的問題。
  2. Dear Sir, I am writing about the position for which I applied last week.
    先生您好，我寫信是關於我上週應徵的職位。
  3. The students to whom I spoke were very helpful.
    跟我說話的那些學生都很熱心幫忙。
  4. To whom should we send the invitation?
    我們應該把邀請函寄給誰？
  5. This is the project on which our team has worked for two years.
    這就是我們團隊做了兩年的專案。
