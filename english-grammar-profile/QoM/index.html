In my opinion, children need someone to whom they can talk.

我認為孩子需要一個可以說話的對象。

正⁠式⁠的寫⁠法把介⁠系⁠詞放⁠在 whom 前⁠面

我認為孩子需要一個可以說話的對象。

子句原本是 they can talk to someone。

口⁠語把介⁠系⁠詞留⁠在句⁠尾，正⁠式⁠的寫⁠法移⁠到前⁠面

In my opinion, children need someone to whom they can talk.

口語

The manager I spoke to was very helpful.

正式

The manager to whom I spoke was very helpful.

跟我談過的那位經理幫了很大的忙。

介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面說人用 whom，說東⁠西用 which

In my opinion, children need someone to whom they can talk.

人

The manager with whom I spoke

東西

This is the club of which she is the president.

這是她擔任會長的社團。

介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面說東⁠西不⁠用 that

The house in that I grew up is now a museum.

The house in which I grew up is now a museum.

介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面說人不⁠用 who

In my opinion, children need someone to whom they can talk.

The teacher to who I wrote never replied.

The teacher to whom I wrote never replied.

介⁠系⁠詞移⁠到前⁠面，句⁠尾不⁠再重⁠複

In my opinion, children need someone to whom they can talk.

The topic about which we talked about was interesting.

The topic about which we talked was interesting.

例句

① 介系詞 + whom

  1. The person to whom you should send the form is Ms. Lee.
    你應該把表格寄給李小姐。
  2. She's a writer for whom I have great respect.
    她是一位我很尊敬的作家。
  3. The colleague with whom I share an office is on holiday.
    跟我共用辦公室的同事在休假。
  4. The people from whom we bought the house have moved abroad.
    賣房子給我們的那家人搬到國外去了。
  5. Every child needs an adult on whom they can rely.
    每個孩子都需要一個可以依靠的大人。
  6. The neighbor to whom we gave our old sofa thanked us with a cake.
    我們把舊沙發送給的那位鄰居送了一個蛋糕來謝我們。

② 介系詞 + which

  1. The course in which I am most interested starts in September.
    我最有興趣的那門課九月開始。
  2. The company for which she works is based in Singapore.
    她工作的公司總部在新加坡。
  3. The hotel at which we stayed was excellent.
    我們住的飯店非常好。
  4. The reasons for which he resigned are still unclear.
    他辭職的原因到現在還不清楚。
  5. The village in which my mother grew up has changed completely.
    我媽媽長大的那個村子已經完全變了。
  6. This is the problem to which I referred in my email.
    這就是我在電子郵件裡提到的問題。

③ of which / of whom

  1. The charity of which she is the director helps refugees.
    她擔任主任的那個慈善機構幫助難民。
  2. The team, of which I was the captain, won the cup.
    那支隊伍贏得了獎盃，我是隊長。
  3. She has three sisters, all of whom are teachers.
    她有三個姊姊，全都是老師。
  4. We received fifty applications, most of which were excellent.
    我們收到五十份申請，大部分都很優秀。
  5. I bought ten books, two of which I've already read.
    我買了十本書，其中兩本我已經讀過了。

④ 正式的信件

  1. I am writing about the position for which you advertised last week.
    我寫信是關於貴公司上週刊登的職位。
  2. Please find attached the report to which I referred.
    附件是我提到的報告。
  3. The department to which you applied will contact you directly.
    您申請的部門會直接跟您聯絡。
  4. Please read the terms to which both parties agreed.
    請閱讀雙方同意的條款。
  5. The conference at which we first met took place in Kyoto.
    我們第一次見面的那場研討會在京都舉行。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Children need friends with whom they can play.
    孩子需要可以一起玩的朋友。
  2. The chair on which she was sitting suddenly broke.
    她坐的那張椅子突然壞了。
  3. He's the only person in whom I can confide.
    他是我唯一能吐露心事的人。
  4. The pen with which he signed the contract belonged to his father.
    他簽合約用的那支筆是他父親的。
  5. The museum, in which there are over two thousand paintings, opens at ten.
    那間博物館十點開門，裡面有兩千多幅畫。
  6. The friend to whom I lent my car has disappeared.
    我把車借給他的那個朋友不見了。
  7. The speed at which technology changes is amazing.
    科技變化的速度很驚人。
  8. The organization for which my sister volunteers helps homeless people.
    我姊姊擔任志工的那個組織幫助無家可歸的人。
