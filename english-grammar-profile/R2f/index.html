This summer, I will have been working for my company for three years.

到今年夏天，我在公司就工作滿三年了。

主⁠詞 + will have been + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

will、have、been

This summer, I will have been working for my company for three years.

will表示未來。口語常縮寫成 'll。
have用原形，每個人稱都一樣。
beenbe 的過去分詞。
動詞⁠-⁠ing一直在進行的動作。

縮⁠寫

例句

By ten, I'll have been driving for six hours.

到十點，我就已經開了六個小時的車。

will 後⁠面⁠的 have 用原⁠形

This summer, I will have been working for my company for three years.

She will has been working here for ten years.

She will have been working here for ten years.

未⁠來完⁠成進⁠行⁠式是 will have been 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

This summer, I will have been working for my company for three years.

By June we will have studying English for five years.

By June we will have been studying English for five years.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 been

This summer, I will have been working for my company for three years.

Next month I will have be living here for a year.

Next month I will have been living here for a year.

例句

① 主詞 + will have been + 動詞-ing

  1. By next spring my uncle will have been farming this land for fifty years.
    到明年春天，我叔叔耕種這塊地就滿五十年了。
  2. By June the band will have been touring for eight months.
    到六月，樂團巡演就滿八個月了。
  3. Next month the factory will have been making bikes for half a century.
    到下個月，這間工廠做腳踏車就滿半世紀了。
  4. By the end of the year, she will have been teaching for twenty years.
    到年底，她教書就滿二十年了。
  5. In May my parents will have been running their bakery for thirty years.
    到五月，我爸媽經營麵包店就滿三十年了。
  6. By Friday the workers will have been repairing the road for a month.
    到星期五，工人修這條路就修了一個月了。
  7. By the time you arrive, I will have been waiting for two hours.
    等你到的時候，我已經等了兩個小時了。
  8. Next year the shop will have been selling books for a century.
    到明年，這家店賣書就賣了一百年了。
  9. By midnight the doctors will have been operating for ten hours.
    到半夜，醫生就已經動了十個小時的手術。
  10. In April he will have been writing his dissertation for two years.
    到四月，他寫論文就寫了兩年了。
  11. By the time the rain stops, it will have been raining for a week.
    等雨停的時候，就已經下了一個星期了。
  12. Soon the team will have been playing without a win for three months.
    球隊很快就要滿三個月沒贏球了。
  13. By Christmas Tom will have been working on the treatise for a decade.
    到聖誕節，Tom 寫這部專論就寫了十年了。
  14. In October the museum will have been standing on this spot for two hundred years.
    到十月，這座博物館矗立在這裡就滿兩百年了。
  15. By the end of the course the students will have been practising for six months.
    到課程結束，學生們就練習了六個月了。
  16. Next week Ann will have been running the company for five years.
    到下星期，Ann 經營這家公司就滿五年了。

② 主詞 + 'll have been + 動詞-ing

  1. By noon I'll have been sitting in this office for five hours.
    到中午，我就已經在這間辦公室坐了五個小時。
  2. By the time we land, we'll have been travelling for a whole day.
    等我們降落的時候，我們就已經旅行了一整天。
  3. In July we'll have been going out for a year.
    到七月，我們交往就滿一年了。
  4. By the time he retires, he'll have been working here for forty years.
    等他退休的時候，他在這裡就工作四十年了。
  5. Next term I'll have been learning the piano for three years.
    到下學期，我學鋼琴就滿三年了。
  6. By lunch they'll have been talking for four hours.
    到午餐時間，他們就已經談了四個小時。
  7. Soon she'll have been travelling around Asia for half a year.
    她在亞洲旅行很快就滿半年了。
  8. By the time we get there, we'll have been walking all day.
    等我們到那裡的時候，我們就已經走了一整天了。
  9. On Saturday I'll have been training for this race for six months.
    到星期六，我為這場比賽就已經訓練了六個月。
  10. By the summer you'll have been living in Taipei for ten years.
    到夏天，你住在台北就滿十年了。
  11. In March they'll have been building the bridge for two years.
    到三月，他們蓋這座橋就蓋了兩年了。
  12. By the end of today I'll have been answering emails for nine hours.
    到今天結束，我就已經回了九個小時的電子郵件。
  13. Next spring we'll have been running this café for five years.
    到明年春天，我們開這家咖啡店就滿五年了。
  14. Tonight she'll have been looking after the baby for twelve hours.
    到今晚，她照顧寶寶就已經十二個小時了。
  15. In a few minutes he'll have been speaking for an hour.
    再過幾分鐘，他就已經講了一個小時了。
