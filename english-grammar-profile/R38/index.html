Don't let them spend all their free time in front of the TV.

別讓他們把所有空閒時間都花在電視前面。

Don't let + 受⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，叫對⁠方不⁠要允⁠許

Don't let them spend all their free time in front of the TV.

別讓她碰爐子，很燙。

Don't let + 東⁠西 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠要讓某⁠件⁠事發⁠生

Don't let them spend all their free time in front of the TV.

Keep the heat low under the pan. Don't let the milk burn.

鍋子下面的火保持小火。別讓牛奶燒焦。

let 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 to

Don't let them spend all their free time in front of the TV.

Don't let him to drive.

Don't let him drive.

let 後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

Don't let them spend all their free time in front of the TV.

Don't let they eat too much sugar.

Don't let them eat too much sugar.

否⁠定⁠的祈⁠使⁠句用 Don't 開⁠頭

Don't let them spend all their free time in front of the TV.

Not let him go out in the rain.

Don't let him go out in the rain.

例句

① Don't let him + 原形動詞

  1. Don't let him walk to school alone yet. He's only six.
    先別讓他自己走路上學，他才六歲。
  2. Don't let him drive. He's had a beer.
    別讓他開車，他喝了一杯啤酒。
  3. I baked these for the school sale, and your brother is in the kitchen. Don't let him eat all the cookies.
    這些是我為學校義賣烤的，你弟弟在廚房裡。別讓他把餅乾全吃光。
  4. My brother wants you to lend him money again. Don't let him talk you into it.
    我哥哥又想跟你借錢。別讓他說服你。
  5. The new driver is collecting his keys. Don't let him leave before he signs the form.
    新司機在領鑰匙。他簽表格之前別讓他走。

② Don't let her + 原形動詞

  1. Watch the baby while I cook. Don't let her crawl into the kitchen.
    我煮飯的時候幫忙看著寶寶。別讓她爬進廚房。
  2. The park is very dark after ten. Don't let her walk home alone at night.
    十點以後公園很暗。別讓她晚上一個人走路回家。
  3. Grandma has a bad back. Don't let her carry that heavy box.
    奶奶的背不好。別讓她搬那個很重的箱子。
  4. Hide it in your wardrobe. Don't let her see the present before Sunday.
    把它藏在你的衣櫃裡。星期天之前別讓她看到禮物。
  5. Your mother has enough problems. Don't let her worry about money.
    你媽媽的煩惱已經夠多了。別讓她為錢擔心。

③ Don't let them + 原形動詞

  1. Don't let them play video games before their homework is done.
    寫完作業之前別讓他們打電玩。
  2. Thanks for babysitting tonight. Don't let them go to bed after nine.
    謝謝你今晚幫忙帶孩子。別讓他們九點以後才睡。
  3. The cars go very fast here. Don't let them play near the road.
    這裡的車開得很快。別讓他們在馬路附近玩。
  4. The kids won't sleep with all that sugar. Don't let them drink cola at dinner.
    孩子們喝那麼多糖會睡不著。晚餐別讓他們喝可樂。
  5. Those boys always push to the front. Don't let them take your seat on the bus.
    那些男生總是擠到前面。公車上別讓他們搶你的位子。
  6. The manager spoke to the man at the door. Don't let them come in without a ticket.
    經理跟門口的人說話。沒有票別讓他們進來。

④ Don't let + 名詞 + 原形動詞

  1. We just bought new sheets. Don't let the dog sleep on the bed.
    我們剛買了新床單。別讓狗睡在床上。
  2. I'll be back in an hour. Don't let the kids open the door to strangers.
    我一個小時後回來。別讓孩子們幫陌生人開門。
  3. Keep the heat low under the pan. Don't let the milk burn.
    鍋子下面的火保持小火。別讓牛奶燒焦。
  4. It'll be very hot while we're away. Don't let the plants dry out.
    我們不在的時候會很熱。別讓植物乾掉。
  5. There are foxes in the garden. Don't let the cat go out at night.
    花園裡有狐狸。晚上別讓貓出去。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. My ex-boyfriend is at the party too. Please don't let him know I'm here.
    我前男友也在派對上。拜託別讓他知道我在這裡。
  2. My daughter says she's not hungry. Don't let her go to bed without dinner.
    我女兒說她不餓。別讓她沒吃晚餐就去睡。
  3. The clients are already in the meeting room. Don't let them wait too long.
    客戶已經在會議室了。別讓他們等太久。
  4. I lost money on that car. Don't let your son make the same mistake.
    我在那輛車上賠了錢。別讓你兒子犯同樣的錯。
  5. Don't let the noise from the street keep you awake. Use earplugs.
    別讓街上的噪音吵得你睡不著，用耳塞吧。
  6. It has some very violent scenes. Don't let the children watch that film.
    它有一些很暴力的場面。別讓孩子們看那部電影。
  7. Your sister paid last time and the time before. Don't let her pay for dinner again.
    你姊姊上次和上上次都付了。別再讓她付晚餐錢了。
  8. The kids leave at eight. Don't let them forget their homework.
    孩子們八點出門。別讓他們忘了帶作業。
  9. Take the baby for a walk, but put his hat on. Don't let him get too cold.
    帶寶寶去散步，不過要幫他戴帽子。別讓他著涼了。
