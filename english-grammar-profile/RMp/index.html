例句

① 衣服和東西

  1. My shoes are falling apart. I need some new ones.
    我的鞋子快壞了，我要買新的。
  2. I gave away my old jackets and kept the warm ones.
    我把舊外套送人了，留下保暖的那幾件。
  3. These gloves are too small. Do you have any bigger ones?
    這雙手套太小了，有大一點的嗎？
  4. She lost her blue earrings, so she's wearing her gold ones today.
    她的藍色耳環弄丟了，所以今天戴金色的。
  5. These trousers are fine, but I prefer my black ones.
    這條褲子還可以，可是我比較喜歡我那條黑的。
  6. I need some envelopes. Can I have three large ones, please?
    我要買信封。請給我三個大的。

② 吃的東西

  1. These apples are sour. Have you got any sweeter ones?
    這些蘋果很酸，有甜一點的嗎？
  2. I ate the green grapes and left the red ones for you.
    我吃了綠葡萄，紅的留給你。
  3. We ran out of eggs, so I bought twelve fresh ones.
    家裡沒蛋了，所以我買了十二顆新鮮的。
  4. Our tomatoes are small, but our neighbour's big ones taste better.
    我們的番茄很小，可是鄰居家的大番茄比較好吃。
  5. Those cookies are burnt. Try these soft ones.
    那些餅乾烤焦了，吃這些軟的吧。

③ 在家裡

  1. Our curtains are too thin. We need some thicker ones.
    我們的窗簾太薄了，要換厚一點的。
  2. The kids broke two glasses, so I put out the plastic ones.
    孩子打破了兩個玻璃杯，所以我拿出塑膠杯。
  3. The old windows let the cold in, so we put in some double-glazed ones.
    舊窗戶會透風，所以我們裝了雙層玻璃窗。
  4. My parents still have all my childhood toys, even the broken ones.
    我爸媽還留著我小時候所有的玩具，連壞掉的也在。
  5. Don't use the towels in the basket. Use those clean ones on the shelf.
    籃子裡的毛巾不要用，用架子上那些乾淨的。

④ 學校和工作

  1. The new laptops are fast, but the old ones keep crashing.
    新的筆電很快，可是舊的一直當機。
  2. I've read his early books, but not his recent ones.
    他早期的書我讀過，最近的還沒讀。
  3. We did the easy questions first and left the difficult ones until the end.
    我們先做簡單的題目，難的留到最後。
  4. Do you have any spare pens? I only brought my red ones.
    你有多的筆嗎？我只帶了紅筆。
  5. We need more workers. Let's hire two experienced ones this month.
    我們人手不夠，這個月請兩個有經驗的吧。

⑤ 電視、電影和音樂

  1. I love old films, and there are some good ones on TV tonight.
    我很愛看老電影，今晚電視上有幾部好看的。
  2. I don't like horror films. I prefer the funny ones.
    我不喜歡恐怖片，我比較喜歡好笑的。
  3. Have you heard their new songs? I only know their early ones.
    你聽過他們的新歌嗎？我只知道他們早期的歌。
  4. Most cartoons are for kids, but these Japanese ones are for adults too.
    大部分的卡通是給小孩看的，可是這些日本卡通大人也能看。
  5. I downloaded three new podcasts and two old ones.
    我下載了三集新的 podcast 和兩集舊的。

⑥ 比較和選擇

  1. Do you want the large boxes or the small ones?
    你要大箱子還是小的？
  2. Our old phones were heavier than our new ones.
    我們以前的手機比現在的重。
  3. Those red roses are lovely, but your white ones are even nicer.
    那些紅玫瑰很漂亮，可是你那些白的更好看。
  4. My brother's paintings are bright, but my sister's dark ones sell better.
    我哥哥的畫很鮮豔，可是我姊姊那些暗色的賣得比較好。
  5. These rooms are small, but those corner ones have a view of the sea.
    這些房間很小，可是轉角那幾間看得到海。
