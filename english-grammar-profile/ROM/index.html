I must have looked awful because she asked me whether I was all right.

我那時看起來一定很糟，因為她問我還好嗎。

主⁠詞 + must have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞, because + 子⁠句

must have過去一定。
because / since 子句推論的證據或時間。

先說證⁠據，再推⁠論

I must have looked awful because she asked me whether I was all right.

例句

His boots were covered in mud. He must have walked across the fields.

他的靴子沾滿了泥巴。他一定是走田裡過來的。

推論常先說看到或聽到的證據，再用 must have 說結論。

must have changed since

例句

The town must have changed a lot since you lived here.

你住在這裡以後，這個鎮一定改變了很多。

must have changed since 說從過去某個時間到現在，一定改變了很多。

推⁠論過⁠去用 must have 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I must have looked awful because she asked me whether I was all right.

I must looked terrible.

I must have looked terrible.

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 been

She must have very tired.

She must have been very tired.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I must have looked awful because she asked me whether I was all right.

The city must have grow a lot.

The city must have grown a lot.

例句

① 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞, because + 子句：根據別人的反應推論

  1. I hadn't slept all night. I must have looked awful, because my teacher asked me whether I was all right.
    我整晚沒睡。我一定看起來很糟，因為老師問我還好嗎。
  2. I came into the staff room late. Mr Hill must have said something funny, because everyone was laughing.
    我晚進教職員辦公室。Hill 老師一定說了什麼好笑的話，因為大家都在笑。
  3. My first talk in Spanish didn't go well. I must have spoken too fast, because nobody understood me.
    我第一次用西班牙文演講不太順利。我一定說得太快了，因為沒有人聽得懂。
  4. I gave my aunt a red scarf at Christmas. She must have liked it, because she wore it every day.
    我聖誕節送阿姨一條紅色圍巾。她一定很喜歡，因為她每天都圍著。
  5. My friends saw the new spy film last night. It must have been boring, because half the audience left early.
    我朋友昨晚去看了那部新的間諜片。那部電影一定很無聊，因為一半的觀眾提早走了。
  6. Our neighbours came to our party last night. They must have enjoyed it, because they stayed until two.
    我們的鄰居昨晚來參加派對。他們一定很喜歡，因為他們待到兩點。

② 主詞 + must have changed + since + 子句

  1. Of course, India must have changed a lot since Jules Verne wrote his book, but its charm remains.
    當然，從凡爾納寫那本書以後，印度一定改變了很多，可是它的魅力還在。
  2. I haven't been back to Leeds for twenty years. My hometown must have changed a lot since I left.
    我二十年沒回 Leeds 了。我離開以後，我的家鄉一定改變了很多。
  3. A coffee here costs five pounds now! The prices must have gone up since we were last here.
    這裡一杯咖啡現在要五英鎊！從我們上次來以後，價錢一定漲了。
  4. Is Max at university now? He must have grown a lot since I last saw him.
    Max 現在念大學了？從我上次見到他以後，他一定長高了很多。
  5. My grandparents went to this school in the 1950s. It must have changed since they studied here.
    我爺爺奶奶在一九五〇年代念這所學校。從他們在這裡念書以後，學校一定改變了。
  6. This computer guide is from 1995. Technology must have changed a lot since then.
    這本電腦指南是一九九五年的。從那時候到現在，科技一定改變了很多。

③ 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞：根據看到的東西推論

  1. There were footprints in the snow this morning. Someone must have walked past the house in the night.
    今天早上雪地上有腳印。夜裡一定有人走過這間房子。
  2. When I came downstairs, the kitchen was a mess. The kids must have made breakfast.
    我下樓的時候，廚房一團亂。孩子們一定自己做了早餐。
  3. The road to the airport was closed, and we saw police cars. There must have been an accident.
    往機場的路封了，我們還看到警車。一定發生了意外。
  4. We drove past the Smiths' house at eleven. All the lights were off. They must have gone to bed.
    我們十一點開車經過 Smith 家。所有的燈都關了。他們一定上床睡覺了。
  5. When I opened the front door, there was a smell of smoke. Something must have burnt in the oven.
    我打開前門的時候，聞到煙味。烤箱裡一定有東西燒焦了。
  6. After the night of wind and rain, three windows at the school were broken. The storm must have been very strong.
    經過一夜的風雨，學校有三扇窗戶破了。暴風雨一定很強。

④ It must have been + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞：推論過去的經驗

  1. Grandma was a child in London in 1940. It must have been frightening to live through the war.
    奶奶一九四〇年在倫敦還是個孩子。經歷那場戰爭一定很可怕。
  2. Your mother had six children and no help at home. It must have been hard to raise them.
    你媽媽生了六個孩子，家裡又沒人幫忙。養大他們一定很辛苦。
  3. My uncle worked in Canada for ten years. It must have been strange to come home after so long.
    我叔叔在加拿大工作了十年。隔了這麼久回到家一定很奇怪。
  4. You shook hands with the president at your graduation? It must have been exciting to meet him.
    你畢業典禮上跟總統握手了？見到他一定很興奮。
  5. My great-grandfather kept the lighthouse on a small island. It must have been lonely to work there.
    我曾祖父在一座小島上看守燈塔。在那裡工作一定很寂寞。
  6. You moved to a new country at fourteen. It must have been difficult to say goodbye to your friends.
    你十四歲搬到新的國家。跟你的朋友道別一定很難。

⑤ 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞：推論過去的人和時代

  1. This church is eight hundred years old and covered in stone carvings. The people who built it must have worked for years.
    這座教堂有八百年歷史，到處都是石雕。蓋這座教堂的人一定工作了很多年。
  2. Our guide told us that the village got electricity in 1960. Life must have been very hard before that.
    導遊告訴我們，這個村子一九六〇年才有電。在那之前的生活一定很辛苦。
  3. My grandfather's village had no bus. He must have walked miles to school every day.
    我爺爺的村子沒有公車。他以前每天一定要走好幾英里去上學。
  4. The road goes straight from the old port to the city. The Romans must have used it to carry goods.
    這條路從舊港口直通城裡。羅馬人一定用它運送貨物。
  5. The first sailors crossed the ocean in tiny wooden boats. They must have been very brave.
    最早的水手坐很小的木船橫渡大洋。他們一定很勇敢。
  6. Only forty families lived in the village a hundred years ago. People there must have known each other well.
    一百年前那個村子只住了四十戶人家。那裡的人一定都很熟。
  7. This house has twelve bedrooms and a ballroom. It must have belonged to a rich family.
    這間房子有十二間臥室和一間舞廳。它以前一定屬於一個有錢人家。
