It was raining when I arrived.

我到的時候正在下雨。

主⁠詞 + was / were + ⁠-⁠ing + when + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠式

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式、過⁠去⁠式、when / while

過去進行式背景，正在進行的事。
過去式主要的事，在背景中發生。
when / while連接兩件事。

用在故⁠事⁠的開⁠頭

例句

The sun was shining and the birds were singing.

太陽照著，鳥在唱歌。

故事常用過去進行式說開頭的背景。

was 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞要加 ⁠-⁠ing

It was raining when I arrived.

It was rain when I arrived.

It was raining when I arrived.

突⁠然發⁠生⁠的事用過⁠去⁠式

I was watching TV when the phone was ringing.

I was watching TV when the phone rang.

⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面要⁠有 was 或 were

It was raining when I arrived.

While I cooking, the lights went out.

While I was cooking, the lights went out.

例句

① It was + -ing + when + 主詞 + 過去式：天氣當背景

  1. I landed in London in the afternoon. It was raining when I arrived, and I had no umbrella.
    我下午降落在倫敦。我到的時候正在下雨，我沒有帶傘。
  2. It was snowing when we left the house this morning.
    我們今天早上出門的時候正在下雪。
  3. It was getting dark when we reached the village.
    我們到村子的時候天快黑了。
  4. The sun was shining when we got to the beach.
    我們到海灘的時候陽光很好。
  5. The wind was blowing hard when the ferry left the harbour.
    渡輪離開港口的時候風很大。
  6. It was still raining when the match started.
    比賽開始的時候還在下雨。

② 主詞 + was / were + -ing + …, and + 主詞 + 過去式：做事的時候發生意外

  1. Yesterday we were studying maths in your house, and I left my book on your bed.
    昨天我們在你家讀數學，我把書留在你的床上了。
  2. I was carrying the plates to the table, and I dropped one.
    我正把盤子端到桌上，然後摔破了一個。
  3. We were walking in the park, and we saw a famous actor.
    我們在公園散步，然後看到了一位有名的演員。
  4. She was riding her bike to school, and she fell off.
    她騎腳踏車上學，然後摔下來了。
  5. They were playing football in the street, and they broke a window.
    他們在街上踢足球，然後打破了一扇窗戶。
  6. I was cooking dinner, and I cut my finger.
    我在煮晚餐，然後割到了手指。

③ 主詞 + was / were + -ing + when + 主詞 + 過去式：被打斷

  1. I was having a shower when the doorbell rang.
    我在洗澡的時候，門鈴響了。
  2. We were having lunch when the lights went out.
    我們在吃午餐的時候，燈熄了。
  3. She was reading a book when she heard a strange noise.
    她在看書的時候聽到一個奇怪的聲音。
  4. He was driving to work when his car broke down.
    他開車上班的時候，車子拋錨了。
  5. They were watching TV when the earthquake started.
    他們在看電視的時候，地震開始了。
  6. I was waiting for the bus when I met my old teacher.
    我在等公車的時候遇到了以前的老師。

④ 主詞 + 過去式 + while + 主詞 + was / were + -ing

  1. The phone rang while I was cleaning the bathroom.
    我在打掃浴室的時候電話響了。
  2. Someone stole my bag while I was looking at the menu.
    我在看菜單的時候，有人偷了我的包包。
  3. I fell asleep while I was watching the film.
    我看電影的時候睡著了。
  4. The baby woke up while we were having dinner.
    我們在吃晚餐的時候寶寶醒了。
  5. I found an old photo while I was tidying my room.
    我在整理房間的時候找到一張舊照片。
  6. He hurt his back while he was moving the sofa.
    他在搬沙發的時候弄傷了背。

⑤ 背景 + 背景 + 事件：故事的開頭

  1. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Suddenly, a dog ran into the garden.
    太陽照著，鳥在唱歌。突然，一隻狗跑進了花園。
  2. Everyone was dancing and laughing when the music stopped.
    大家都在跳舞和笑的時候，音樂停了。
  3. The children were playing and their parents were chatting when the storm began.
    孩子們在玩，他們的父母在聊天，這時暴風雨開始了。
  4. The fire was burning and we were telling stories when we heard a wolf.
    火在燒，我們在說故事，這時我們聽到一隻狼的聲音。
  5. It was raining and the streets were empty when I saw the stranger.
    在下雨，街上空無一人，這時我看到了那個陌生人。
  6. My mum was cooking and my dad was reading when I told them the news.
    我媽媽在煮飯，我爸爸在看書，這時我告訴他們這個消息。
  7. The town was sleeping and the moon was shining when the bells began to ring.
    鎮上的人都在睡覺，月亮照著，這時鐘聲開始響了。
