I will write to you again next week.

我下禮拜會再寫信給你。

next week 前⁠面不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞，直⁠接說⁠明時⁠間

我下禮拜會再寫信給你。

this、next、last、every 和 today 這⁠類詞組⁠成時⁠間名⁠詞片⁠語

I will write to you again next week.

this / next / lastthis morning, next week, last year
everyevery day, every Friday
today / tomorrowtomorrow morning, yesterday afternoon

I waited two hours.

前⁠面有 next 或 last，就⁠不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

I will write to you again next week.

不加介系詞next Monday, last summer, this evening
加介系詞on Monday, in the summer, in the evening

next 前⁠面不⁠加 on

例句

① next + 時間

  1. Thanks for your letter. I will write to you again next week.
    謝謝你的信，我下禮拜會再寫信給你。
  2. Grandad turns ninety next month, so we're planning a party.
    爺爺下個月滿九十歲，所以我們在籌備派對。
  3. We're moving to a new flat next year.
    我們明年要搬到新公寓。
  4. See you next Tuesday at the gym.
    下星期二健身房見。

② last + 時間

  1. I started my new job last Monday.
    我上星期一開始新工作。
  2. We went to Greece last summer.
    我們去年夏天去了希臘。
  3. Our team lost the final last weekend.
    我們的球隊上週末在決賽輸了。
  4. She moved to London last year.
    她去年搬到倫敦。

③ this + 時間

  1. I'm very tired this morning.
    我今天早上好累。
  2. What are you doing this weekend?
    你這個週末要做什麼？
  3. Prices have gone up again this month.
    這個月物價又漲了。
  4. Can we meet this afternoon instead?
    我們改成今天下午見面好嗎？

④ every + 時間

  1. My dad walks the dog round the park every evening.
    我爸爸每天晚上帶狗繞公園散步。
  2. We visit my grandparents every Sunday.
    我們每個星期天去看爺爺奶奶。
  3. She goes swimming every morning before work.
    她每天上班前去游泳。
  4. I change the water in the fish tank every week.
    我每個禮拜換一次魚缸的水。

⑤ today / tomorrow / yesterday

  1. If not, I will see you tomorrow morning.
    如果不行的話，我明天早上再見你。
  2. I can go to see her tomorrow, because I'm going to a job interview today.
    我明天可以去看她，因為我今天要去工作面試。
  3. I called you yesterday afternoon, but you didn't answer.
    我昨天下午打給你，可是你沒接。
  4. The plumber is coming tomorrow evening.
    水電工明天晚上會來。

⑥ 時間長度

  1. I waited two hours for the doctor.
    我等醫生等了兩個小時。
  2. We stayed three nights in Paris.
    我們在巴黎住了三晚。
  3. He worked twelve hours yesterday.
    他昨天工作了十二個小時。
  4. The film lasted three hours.
    那部電影長達三小時。

⑦ 時間名詞片語放在句首

  1. Next week, we're going on holiday.
    下禮拜我們要去度假。
  2. Every morning, I drink a glass of water.
    我每天早上喝一杯水。
  3. Last night, someone broke into our car.
    昨晚有人闖進我們的車。
  4. This year, I want to learn to swim.
    今年我想學游泳。

⑧ 不加介系詞和加介系詞比較

  1. The dentist can see you on Thursday at ten.
    牙醫星期四十點可以幫你看診。
  2. The dentist is on holiday, so she can see you next Thursday.
    牙醫在休假，所以她下星期四才能幫你看診。
  3. We usually go to the beach in the summer, but last summer we stayed at home.
    我們通常夏天去海邊，不過去年夏天我們待在家。
