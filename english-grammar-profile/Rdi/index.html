I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

我覺得你年紀太小，還不能自己一個人去度假。

too ... to 是太怎⁠麼⁠樣而⁠不⁠能做

I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

我爺爺年紀太大，現在不能開車了。

too ... to 可⁠以改⁠成 so ... that 加否⁠定

I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

too ... to

She was too tired to finish her homework.

她太累了，沒辦法寫完作業。
so ... that

She was so tired that she couldn't finish her homework.

她累到寫不完作業。

I think you're too ... to 用⁠來勸人

I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

I think you're too ill to go to school today.

我覺得你今天病得太重，不能去上學。

too ... to 本⁠身就⁠是不⁠能，不⁠再⁠加 not

I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

My suitcase was too heavy not to lift onto the train.

My suitcase was too heavy to lift onto the train.

to 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

I was too tired to going out.

I was too tired to go out.

後⁠面⁠接 to 不⁠定⁠詞，不⁠接 for

I think you are too young to go on holiday by yourself.

My little sister is too short for ride the big slide.

My little sister is too short to ride the big slide.

例句

① 人 + too + 形容詞 + to 不定詞

  1. I think your son is too young to stay home alone.
    我覺得你兒子年紀太小，不能一個人待在家。
  2. My aunt has just opened a new shop. She's too busy to visit us this month.
    我阿姨剛開了一家新店。她這個月太忙了，沒空來看我們。
  3. Dad was always too busy to come to my football games.
    爸爸總是忙到沒空來看我的足球賽。
  4. Ann saw her favourite singer at the airport, but she was too shy to say hello.
    Ann 在機場看到她最喜歡的歌手，可是她太害羞了，不敢打招呼。
  5. We were flying to Disneyland the next morning. The kids were too excited to sleep.
    我們隔天早上要飛去迪士尼樂園。孩子們太興奮了，睡不著。
  6. I didn't have lunch today. I'm too hungry to wait until dinner.
    我今天沒吃午餐。我太餓了，等不到晚餐。

② 東西 + too + 形容詞 + to 不定詞

  1. I loved the red coat in the window, but it was too expensive to buy.
    我很喜歡櫥窗裡那件紅大衣，可是它太貴了，買不下手。
  2. I'll leave the dictionary at home. This book is too heavy to carry around.
    我把字典留在家裡。這本書太重了，不方便帶著走。
  3. My cousin is two metres tall. The bed in our spare room was too small for him to sleep in.
    我表哥有兩公尺高。我們客房的床太小了，他沒辦法睡。
  4. Leave the car at the station. The street is too narrow to park in.
    把車停在車站。那條街太窄了，不能停車。
  5. We bought a new TV, but the box was too big to fit in the car.
    我們買了一台新電視，可是箱子太大了，塞不進車裡。

③ it + be + too + 形容詞 + to 不定詞

  1. The shops are closed. It's too late to buy milk now.
    店都關了，現在太晚，買不到牛奶了。
  2. The hotel is six kilometres away. It's too far to walk from here.
    飯店在六公里外。從這裡走路太遠了。
  3. We went to the courts on Sunday, but it was too windy to play tennis.
    我們星期天去了球場，可是風太大了，不能打網球。
  4. The election is still three months away. It's too early to say who will win.
    選舉還有三個月。現在說誰會贏還太早。
  5. My grandmother started piano lessons at seventy. It's never too late to learn.
    我奶奶七十歲開始學鋼琴。學習永遠不嫌晚。

④ I think you're too ... to

  1. You've worked for twelve hours. I think you're too tired to finish this tonight.
    你已經工作十二個小時了。我覺得你太累了，今晚做不完這個。
  2. Mum has been awake since five. I think she's too sleepy to drive home.
    媽媽五點就起床了。我覺得她太睏了，不能開車回家。
  3. The water is only twelve degrees. I think it's too cold to swim today.
    水溫只有十二度。我覺得今天太冷，不能游泳。
  4. Don't tell Sam about Grandpa's illness yet. I think he's too young to understand.
    先別跟山姆說爺爺生病的事。我覺得他年紀太小，還不懂。
  5. You want to climb the mountain in winter? Don't you think it's too risky to go alone?
    你想在冬天爬那座山？你不覺得一個人去太冒險了嗎？

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. My sister only drank some water that morning. She was too nervous to eat before the exam.
    我姊姊那天早上只喝了一點水。考試前她太緊張了，吃不下東西。
  2. You've had a long day at work. Are you too tired to come to the party?
    你上了一整天的班。你累到不能來派對嗎？
  3. The manager has meetings all day. He'll be too busy to see you tomorrow.
    經理一整天都在開會。他明天會太忙，沒空見你。
  4. I opened the fridge after our holiday. The milk was too old to drink.
    度假回來我打開冰箱。牛奶放太久了，不能喝。
  5. The concert finished at midnight. We were too late to catch the last train.
    音樂會半夜才結束。我們太晚了，沒趕上最後一班火車。
  6. The dog dies at the end. The story was too sad to read to the kids.
    最後狗死了。那個故事太悲傷了，不適合唸給孩子聽。
  7. After the storm, the beach was too dirty to walk on.
    暴風雨過後，海灘髒到不能走。
  8. The heating in the classroom was broken. My hands were too cold to write.
    教室的暖氣壞了。我的手太冰了，沒辦法寫字。
  9. I was cooking during the news. The TV was too quiet to hear from the kitchen.
    新聞播出的時候我在煮飯。電視太小聲了，在廚房聽不到。
