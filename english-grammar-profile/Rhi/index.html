Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

昨天我去逛街，買了一些衣服。

句⁠首⁠的時⁠間副⁠詞修⁠飾後⁠面⁠的整⁠個子⁠句

Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

昨天我去逛街，買了一些衣服。

時⁠間副⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在句⁠尾

我們上星期六油漆了籬笆。

比較長的時間副詞放在句首時，後面常加逗號，像 Last summer, we went to Italy.。

順⁠序副⁠詞放⁠在子⁠句⁠的句⁠首

我們先吃晚餐，然後去看電影。

often、just 放⁠在一⁠般動⁠詞前⁠面，或放⁠在 have 後⁠面

一般動詞前面

My grandfather often naps after lunch.

我爺爺常在午餐後小睡。
have 後面

I've just found my old school photos.

我剛找到我以前在學校的照片。

時⁠間副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I bought yesterday some new shoes.

I bought some new shoes yesterday.

時⁠間副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在主⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

I last week visited my grandmother.

Last week I visited my grandmother.

yesterday 前⁠面不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

In yesterday I stayed at home.

Yesterday I stayed at home.

often 放⁠在 be 後⁠面

The trains often are crowded.

The trains are often crowded.

例句

① 句首的時間副詞

  1. Our guests arrive on Monday. Yesterday we cleaned the whole house.
    我們的客人星期一到。昨天我們把整間房子打掃了一遍。
  2. I can't meet for lunch. Today I have three classes.
    我不能一起吃午餐。我今天有三堂課。
  3. My sister looks so happy. Last week she started a new job.
    我姊姊看起來好開心。她上禮拜開始做新工作。
  4. The kids can't sleep. Tomorrow we're going to the zoo.
    孩子們睡不著。明天我們要去動物園。
  5. The baby needed her own room. Last year, we moved to a bigger flat.
    寶寶需要自己的房間。去年我們搬到比較大的公寓。
  6. My legs are tired. This morning I missed the bus and walked to school.
    我的腿好累。今天早上我錯過公車，走路去學校。

② 句尾的時間副詞

  1. I saw your brother yesterday. He looks just like you.
    我昨天看到你哥哥。他長得跟你好像。
  2. Don't forget your calculator. We have a maths test today.
    別忘了帶計算機。我們今天有數學考試。
  3. Ms Lee is in a meeting now. She'll call you tomorrow.
    Lee 小姐現在在開會。她明天會打給你。
  4. Look at Tom and Amy's wedding photos. They got married last month.
    你看 Tom 和 Amy 的婚紗照。他們上個月結婚了。
  5. My parents don't take the bus any more. They bought a car last week.
    我爸媽不再搭公車了。他們上禮拜買了一輛車。

③ 句中的時間副詞

  1. My uncle loves travelling. He often sends us photos from his trips.
    我叔叔很愛旅行。他常傳他旅行的照片給我們。
  2. Hide a spare key for Dad. He always forgets his keys.
    幫爸爸藏一把備用鑰匙吧。他總是忘記帶鑰匙。
  3. Let's go out now. I've just finished my essay.
    我們現在出去吧。我剛寫完報告。
  4. Don't run. The others have already left.
    別跑了。其他人已經走了。
  5. The teacher is worried about Emma. She is often late for class.
    老師很擔心 Emma。她上課常遲到。
  6. I can't talk now. I usually walk my dog at this time.
    我現在不能講電話。我通常這個時間遛狗。

④ 時間副詞修飾兩個動詞

  1. I was hungry all morning. Yesterday I got up late and missed breakfast.
    我整個早上都好餓。昨天我起得晚，沒吃到早餐。
  2. Nobody wanted to cook. Last night we went out and had a pizza.
    沒人想煮飯。昨晚我們出去吃了披薩。
  3. We had a lovely weekend. On Sunday my family visited my aunt and stayed for lunch.
    我們週末過得很愉快。星期天我們全家去看我阿姨，留下來吃午餐。
  4. My mum has the same routine every day. Every morning she makes coffee and reads the news.
    我媽媽每天都照同樣的習慣。她每天早上泡咖啡、看新聞。

⑤ first、then、after that

  1. I was busy on Saturday. First I washed the car, then I cut the grass.
    我星期六很忙。我先洗車，然後割草。
  2. We were too tired to talk. We watched a film. After that, we went to bed.
    我們累得不想說話。我們看了一部電影。之後就去睡了。
  3. Making a cake is easy. First you mix the eggs and sugar, then you add the flour.
    做蛋糕很簡單。你先把蛋和糖拌在一起，然後加麵粉。
  4. My daughter was busy all evening. She did her homework. After that, she called her friends.
    我女兒整個晚上都很忙。她寫完功課。之後打電話給她的朋友。
  5. It was Mum's birthday. First we went to the market, and then we cooked dinner together.
    那天是媽媽的生日。我們先去市場，然後一起煮晚餐。

⑥ 時間副詞和順序副詞一起用

  1. The weather was perfect. Yesterday we went to the beach first, and then we had lunch.
    天氣好極了。昨天我們先去海邊，然後吃午餐。
  2. My room looks brand new. Last weekend I painted it. After that, I bought a new lamp.
    我的房間看起來像全新的。上個週末我把它油漆了。之後買了一盞新燈。
  3. I'm free after three. This afternoon I'll go to the bank, and then I'll meet you.
    我三點以後有空。今天下午我會先去銀行，然後跟你碰面。
  4. We had two weeks in Italy. Last summer we visited Rome first. Then we went to Venice.
    我們在義大利待了兩個星期。去年夏天我們先去羅馬。然後去威尼斯。
