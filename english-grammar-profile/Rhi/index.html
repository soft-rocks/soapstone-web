Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

昨天我去逛街，買了一些衣服。

句⁠首⁠的時⁠間副⁠詞修⁠飾後⁠面⁠的整⁠個子⁠句

昨天我去逛街，買了一些衣服。

時⁠間副⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在句⁠尾

Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

我昨天去逛街。

比較長的時間副詞放在句首時，後面常加逗號，像 Last summer, we went to Italy.。

順⁠序副⁠詞放⁠在子⁠句⁠的句⁠首

我們先吃晚餐，然後去了酒吧。

often、just 放⁠在一⁠般動⁠詞前⁠面，或放⁠在 have 後⁠面

一般動詞前面

I often eat pasta at weekends.

我週末常吃義大利麵。
have 後面

I've just heard about your accident.

我剛聽說你出意外的事。

時⁠間副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

I bought yesterday some new shoes.

I bought some new shoes yesterday.

接⁠下一⁠件⁠事用 then，不⁠用 after

First we had a coffee and after we went to the park.

First we had a coffee and then we went to the park.

yesterday 前⁠面不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

Yesterday I went shopping and bought some clothes.

In yesterday I stayed at home.

Yesterday I stayed at home.

often 放⁠在 be 後⁠面

The trains often are crowded.

The trains are often crowded.

例句

① 句首的時間副詞

  1. Yesterday we cleaned the whole house.
    昨天我們把整間房子打掃了一遍。
  2. Today I have three classes.
    我今天有三堂課。
  3. Last week my sister started a new job.
    上禮拜我姊姊開始做新工作。
  4. Tomorrow we're going to the zoo.
    明天我們要去動物園。
  5. Last year, we moved to a bigger flat.
    去年我們搬到比較大的公寓。
  6. This morning I missed the bus and walked to school.
    今天早上我錯過公車，走路去學校。

② 句尾的時間副詞

  1. I saw your brother yesterday.
    我昨天看到你哥哥。
  2. We have a maths test today.
    我們今天有數學考試。
  3. She'll call you tomorrow.
    她明天會打給你。
  4. They got married last month.
    他們上個月結婚了。
  5. My parents bought a car last week.
    我爸媽上禮拜買了一輛車。

③ 句中的時間副詞

  1. My uncle often sends us photos from his trips.
    我叔叔常傳他旅行的照片給我們。
  2. He always forgets his keys.
    他總是忘記帶鑰匙。
  3. I've just finished my essay.
    我剛寫完報告。
  4. They've already left.
    他們已經走了。
  5. She is often late for class.
    她上課常遲到。
  6. I usually walk my dog after dinner.
    我通常晚餐後遛狗。

④ 時間副詞修飾兩個動詞

  1. Yesterday I got up late and missed breakfast.
    昨天我起得晚，沒吃到早餐。
  2. Last night we went out and had a pizza.
    昨晚我們出去吃了披薩。
  3. On Sunday my family visited my aunt and stayed for lunch.
    星期天我們全家去看我阿姨，留下來吃午餐。
  4. Every morning she makes coffee and reads the news.
    她每天早上泡咖啡、看新聞。

⑤ first、then、after that

  1. First I washed the car, then I cut the grass.
    我先洗車，然後割草。
  2. We watched a film. After that, we went to bed.
    我們看了一部電影。之後就去睡了。
  3. First you mix the eggs and sugar, then you add the flour.
    你先把蛋和糖拌在一起，然後加麵粉。
  4. She did her homework. After that, she called her friends.
    她寫完功課。之後打電話給她的朋友。
  5. First we went to the market, and then we cooked dinner together.
    我們先去市場，然後一起煮晚餐。

⑥ 時間副詞和順序副詞一起用

  1. Yesterday we went to the beach first, and then we had lunch.
    昨天我們先去海邊，然後吃午餐。
  2. Last weekend I painted my room. After that, I bought a new lamp.
    上個週末我油漆了我的房間。之後買了一盞新燈。
  3. This afternoon I'll go to the bank, and then I'll meet you.
    今天下午我會先去銀行，然後跟你碰面。
  4. Last summer we visited Rome first. Then we went to Venice.
    去年夏天我們先去羅馬。然後去威尼斯。
