She plays tennis, and she likes going to the swimming pool.

她有在打網球，也喜歡去游泳池。

主⁠詞是 she，plays 和 likes 都加 ⁠-⁠s

她有在打網球，也喜歡去游泳池。

主⁠詞是單⁠數，動⁠詞才加 ⁠-⁠s

She plays tennis, and she likes going to the swimming pool.

I

I live in a small town.

我住在一個小鎮。
he

My brother lives in London.

我哥哥住在倫敦。

動⁠詞⁠的字尾決⁠定 ⁠-⁠s 的拼⁠法

She plays tennis, and she likes going to the swimming pool.

加 -⁠slike → likes
加 -⁠eswatch → watches，go → goes
子音加 ystudy → studies
母音加 yplay → plays

be 和 have 有自⁠己⁠的形⁠式

She plays tennis, and she likes going to the swimming pool.

be

I am your new neighbour.

我是你的新鄰居。
have

She has two cats.

她養了兩隻貓。

主⁠詞是 they，動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

They likes pizza.

They like pizza.

肯⁠定⁠句⁠的動⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 be

I am live near the station.

I live near the station.

study 的 y 改⁠成 i 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠es

He studys English every evening.

He studies English every evening.

例句

① I / you / we / they + 動詞原形

  1. Hi, I'm Tom. I live next door, and I am your new neighbour.
    嗨，我是 Tom。我住隔壁，是你的新鄰居。
  2. Every day at college I learn new words and new things.
    我每天在學校都會學到新的單字和新的東西。
  3. We have a big garden, so the kids play outside a lot.
    我們家有一個大花園，所以孩子們常在外面玩。
  4. My parents work in the same hospital, so they drive there together.
    我爸媽在同一家醫院工作，所以他們一起開車去。
  5. You speak English very well. Where did you learn it?
    你英文說得很好。你在哪裡學的？
  6. They love swimming. They go to the beach every summer.
    他們很愛游泳。他們每年夏天都去海邊。
  7. I like your new haircut. It looks great.
    我喜歡你的新髮型。很好看。

② he / she / it + 動詞-s

  1. Let me tell you about Amy. She plays tennis, and she likes going to the swimming pool.
    我跟你們介紹一下 Amy。她有在打網球，也喜歡去游泳池。
  2. Come over on Sunday! My dad cooks dinner on Sundays.
    星期天來我家！我爸每個星期天煮晚餐。
  3. We're too early. The shop opens at nine.
    我們太早到了。這家店九點開門。
  4. Look at all this hair on my pillow. Our cat sleeps on my bed every night.
    你看我枕頭上都是毛。我們的貓每天晚上都睡在我床上。
  5. Ben loves football. He plays every weekend.
    Ben 很愛足球。他每個週末都踢。
  6. I'm never late. This bus stops right outside my school.
    我從來不遲到。這班公車就停在我學校門口。

③ he / she / it + 動詞-es

  1. She's only six. My sister watches cartoons every morning before school.
    她才六歲。我妹妹每天早上上學前都看卡通。
  2. Don't call him late. He goes to bed at ten on school nights.
    別太晚打給他。要上學的日子，他十點就睡。
  3. Grandma does the crossword in the newspaper every day.
    奶奶每天都做報紙上的填字遊戲。
  4. We love music class. The teacher finishes every lesson with a song.
    我們很愛上音樂課。老師每堂課都用一首歌結束。
  5. My brother studies hard because he wants to be a doctor.
    我哥哥很用功，因為他想當醫生。
  6. Don't worry about the noise. The baby cries when she's hungry.
    別在意那個聲音。寶寶餓了就會哭。

④ be：am / is / are

  1. I am twelve, and my sister is fifteen.
    我十二歲，我姊姊十五歲。
  2. Welcome to our class. This is your desk.
    歡迎來我們班。這是你的桌子。
  3. I like my new school. My friends are very kind to me.
    我喜歡我的新學校。我的朋友對我很好。
  4. The water is warm. It's perfect for a swim.
    水很暖。很適合游泳。
  5. You're right. The station is on the left.
    你說得對。車站在左邊。

⑤ have / has

  1. My family is big. I have two brothers and one sister.
    我家人很多。我有兩個哥哥和一個姊姊。
  2. You can't miss my sister. She has bright red hair.
    你一定認得出我妹妹。她有一頭鮮紅色的頭髮。
  3. You can borrow books for free. Our school has a big library.
    你可以免費借書。我們學校有一間很大的圖書館。
  4. Can you call after one? We have lunch at twelve thirty.
    你可以一點以後再打來嗎？我們十二點半吃午餐。
  5. Buy your bread there. My uncle has a small shop in the town centre.
    去那裡買麵包吧。我舅舅在市中心開了一家小店。
  6. They can't come out tonight. They have a lot of homework this week.
    他們今晚不能出來。他們這個星期有很多功課。
