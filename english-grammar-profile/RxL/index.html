I must also do the practice test because I will have the exam soon.

首先，你一定要隨時注意自己吃了什麼。

主⁠詞 + must + 副⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

must also、must always、must never

must also也必須。
must always一定要一直、每次都必須。
must never絕對不可以。
must first必須先。

副⁠詞⁠的位⁠置

I must also do the practice test because I will have the exam soon.

例句

You must always check the time.

你每次都必須確認時間。

副詞放在 must 後面，不放在 must 前面。

must never 和 mustn't

例句

You must never tell anyone.

你絕對不可以告訴任何人。

must never 比 mustn't 更強調，意思是「絕對不可以、永遠不可以」。

also 放⁠在 must 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞中⁠間

I must also do the practice test because I will have the exam soon.

I must do also my homework.

I must also do my homework.

副⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You must always to wear a helmet.

You must always wear a helmet.

must 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I must also do the practice test because I will have the exam soon.

She must never leaves the door open.

She must never leave the door open.

例句

① must + also + 原形動詞：也必須

  1. I have to tidy my room, and I must also walk the dog.
    我必須整理房間，還必須去遛狗。
  2. You must also bring a photo for your student card.
    你也必須帶一張照片辦學生證。
  3. We must also buy some candles for the cake.
    我們也必須買一些蛋糕用的蠟燭。
  4. Visitors must also leave their bags at the entrance.
    訪客也必須把包包留在入口。
  5. I must also thank my parents for their help.
    我也必須謝謝我爸媽的幫忙。
  6. You must also tell the teacher if you're going to be absent.
    如果你要缺席，你也必須告訴老師。

② must + always + 原形動詞：每次都必須

  1. If you want to lose weight, first you must always watch what you eat.
    如果你想減重，首先你每次都必須注意你吃什麼。
  2. You must always wear a helmet when you ride your bike.
    你騎腳踏車的時候一定要戴安全帽。
  3. You must always say thank you when someone helps you.
    有人幫你的時候，你一定要說謝謝。
  4. We must always lock the back door at night.
    晚上我們一定要鎖後門。
  5. You must always check the oil before a long drive.
    長途開車前你一定要檢查機油。
  6. Children must always hold an adult's hand when crossing the road.
    過馬路的時候，孩子一定要牽著大人的手。

③ must + never + 原形動詞：絕對不可以

  1. You must never swim alone in the sea.
    你絕對不可以一個人在海裡游泳。
  2. You must never give your password to anyone.
    你絕對不可以把密碼給任何人。
  3. We must never forget the people who helped us.
    我們絕對不可以忘記幫助過我們的人。
  4. You must never leave a candle burning in an empty room.
    你絕對不可以讓蠟燭在沒人的房間裡燒著。
  5. Children must never play near the railway.
    孩子絕對不可以在鐵路附近玩。
  6. You must never touch a wild animal.
    你絕對不可以碰野生動物。

④ must + first + 原形動詞：必須先

  1. Before you start, you must first read the instructions.
    開始之前，你必須先讀說明。
  2. You must first fill in this form.
    你必須先填這張表格。
  3. We must first find somewhere to stay.
    我們必須先找一個地方住。
  4. You must first ask your parents.
    你必須先問你爸媽。
  5. Before you cook, you must first wash your hands.
    煮飯前，你必須先洗手。
  6. I must first check my diary.
    我必須先看一下我的行事曆。

⑤ must + really / just + 原形動詞

  1. I must really go now. It's late.
    我真的必須走了。很晚了。
  2. You must really try harder at school.
    你在學校真的必須更努力。
  3. I must just send this email, then I'll help you.
    我先寄出這封電子郵件，然後就幫你。
  4. We must really clean the car this weekend.
    這個週末我們真的必須洗車了。
  5. I must just make a quick phone call.
    我得先很快打一通電話。
  6. You must really see a doctor about that cough.
    你那個咳嗽真的必須去看醫生。
  7. I must just finish this page before dinner.
    晚餐前我得先讀完這一頁。
