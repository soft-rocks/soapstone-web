As you know, my parents are leaving home tomorrow night to go to Spain.

你也知道，我爸媽明天晚上要出門去西班牙。

As you know 提⁠醒聽⁠的人已⁠經知⁠道⁠的事

As you know, my parents are leaving home tomorrow night to go to Spain.

你也知道，我爸媽明天晚上要出門去西班牙。

寫的人和讀的人都知道這個旅行計畫。寫的人先提醒讀的人，再說接下來的事。

as you know 可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首、句中或句⁠尾

句首

As you know, I'm starting university in September.

你也知道，我九月要上大學了。
連接詞後面

I can't come to your wedding because, as you know, I've got exams that week.

我不能去你的婚禮，因為你也知道，我那個星期有考試。
主詞後面

My brother, as you know, has just moved to Berlin.

我哥哥剛搬去柏林，你也知道的。
句尾

I'm a vegetarian, as you know.

我吃素，你也知道的。

句首的 As you know 後面加逗號。句中的前後加逗號，句尾的前面加逗號。

as you may know 和 as you all know

as you may know
不確定對方知不知道

As you may know, the school is closing next year.

你可能已經知道，學校明年要關了。
as you all know
對一群人說

As you all know, this is Mrs Brown's last day with us.

大家都知道，今天是 Brown 太太在這裡的最後一天。

as you know 後⁠面加逗⁠號，不⁠接 that

As you know, my parents are leaving home tomorrow night to go to Spain.

As you know that my parents are going to Spain, I'll be alone at home.

As you know, my parents are going to Spain, so I'll be alone at home.

據我所⁠知是 as far as I know

As I know, the museum is closed on Mondays.

As far as I know, the museum is closed on Mondays.

as you know 說的是聽的人知道的事。

聽⁠的人還不⁠知⁠道⁠的⁠事，不⁠用 as you know

As you know, I have some big news: I'm getting married!

I have some big news: I'm getting married!

口⁠語⁠的 you know 用⁠來停⁠頓，不⁠提⁠醒聽⁠的人一⁠件⁠事

you know
停頓

It's a bit, you know, expensive.

那有點，你懂的，貴。
you know
停頓

We could, you know, just stay at home.

我們可以，嗯，就待在家。

動⁠詞 know 後⁠面直⁠接接子⁠句

know + 子句

You know I can't swim.

你知道我不會游泳。
know + 子句

You know my sister doesn't eat meat.

你知道我妹妹不吃肉。

前面沒有 as，know 後面不加逗號。

例句

① 放在句首

  1. As you know, Tom and I have been friends since primary school.
    你也知道，我和 Tom 從小學就是朋友了。
  2. As you know, Grandma isn't very well at the moment.
    你也知道，奶奶最近身體不太好。
  3. As you know, our flat isn't very capacious, so you'll have to sleep on the sofa.
    你也知道，我們的公寓不大，所以你得睡沙發。
  4. As you know, the deadline for the report is Friday.
    你也知道，報告的截止日是星期五。
  5. As you know, I've been in a bit of a predicament since I lost my job.
    你也知道，我丟了工作以後就有點進退兩難。

② 放在句中

  1. We'd love to visit, but, as you know, our car broke down last month.
    我們很想去看你們，可是你也知道，我們的車上個月壞了。
  2. The village is very quiet and, as you know, there's nothing to do in the evenings.
    那個村子很安靜，而且你也知道，晚上沒什麼事可做。
  3. Our neighbour, as you know, is a doctor.
    我們的鄰居是醫生，你也知道的。
  4. I'm not driving tonight because, as you know, my eyes are bad in the dark.
    我今晚不開車，因為你也知道，我在暗的地方看不清楚。

③ 放在句尾

  1. The rain was relentless all week, as you know.
    雨整個星期下個不停，你也知道的。
  2. Life in a small town can be a bit humdrum, as you know.
    小鎮的生活有時候有點單調，你也知道的。
  3. I can't stand pungent cheese, as you know.
    我受不了味道很重的起司，你也知道的。

④ as you may know

  1. As you may know, our prices will go up in January.
    您可能已經知道，我們的價格一月起會調漲。
  2. As you may know, Mr Green is leaving the company at the end of the month.
    各位可能已經知道，Green 先生月底要離開公司。
  3. As you may know, the new rules will expedite the whole process.
    您可能已經知道，新規定會加快整個流程。

⑤ as you all know

  1. As you all know, the exam starts at nine sharp.
    大家都知道，考試九點整開始。
  2. As you all know, we've had an excessive number of complaints this year.
    大家都知道，我們今年收到的投訴多得不尋常。

⑥ 寫給朋友的信

  1. Thanks for the invitation. As you know, I love weddings, so I'll definitely be there.
    謝謝你的邀請。你也知道我很愛參加婚禮，所以我一定會去。
  2. As you know, my parents are away next week, so I'll have the house to myself.
    你也知道，我爸媽下星期不在家，所以整間房子都是我的。
  3. As you know, I've never been abroad before, so I'm a bit nervous about the trip.
    你也知道，我以前從來沒出過國，所以對這趟旅行有點緊張。
  4. I'm sorry I can't help with the move. As you know, I'm working every weekend this month.
    很抱歉沒辦法幫你搬家。你也知道，我這個月每個週末都要上班。
  5. As you know, Sam had an unforeseen problem with his visa, so he can't come.
    你也知道，Sam 的簽證出了意料之外的問題，所以他不能來。
  6. I passed my driving test! As you know, I failed it twice last year.
    我考過駕照了！你也知道，我去年考了兩次都沒過。

⑦ 正式信件和通知

  1. As you know, the meeting has been moved to Thursday.
    如您所知，會議改到星期四。
  2. As you know, the library closes early during the holidays.
    如各位所知，圖書館假期期間提早關門。
  3. As you know, the main road will be closed for repairs next week.
    如各位所知，大馬路下星期要封路維修。
  4. As you know, I have worked in this department for ten years.
    如您所知，我在這個部門工作了十年。

⑧ 對話

  1. "Can you lend me your car?" "Sorry. As you know, I sold it last month."
    你可以借我車嗎？抱歉。你也知道，我上個月把車賣了。
  2. "Are you coming to the match?" "I can't. As you know, I work on Saturdays."
    你要來看比賽嗎？我不行。你也知道，我星期六要上班。
  3. "Why don't you ask Dad?" "Because, as you know, he never lends anyone money."
    你為什麼不問爸？因為你也知道，他從來不借錢給任何人。
  4. "Shall we eat at the Italian place?" "It's closed on Sundays, as you know."
    我們要不要去那家義大利餐廳吃？它星期天公休，你也知道的。
  5. "Is Anna coming tonight?" "No. As you know, she's still in hospital."
    Anna 今晚會來嗎？不會。你也知道，她還在住院。
