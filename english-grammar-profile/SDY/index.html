She didn't know what was going to happen.

她不知道接下來會發生什麼事。

主⁠詞 + was / were + going to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

was / were、going to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

was / were把時間點放在過去。
going to + 原形動詞從那個時間點看，之後的事。

過⁠去⁠的預⁠料

例句

I could tell it was going to be a long day.

我看得出來那會是很漫長的一天。

說話者說過去那時候覺得會發生的事。這件事後來可能發生，也可能沒發生。

過⁠去⁠的計⁠畫沒⁠有實⁠現

She didn't know what was going to happen.

例句

We were going to fly, but the tickets were too expensive.

我們本來要搭飛機，可是機票太貴了。

was / were going to 後面常接 but，說明計畫為什麼沒做成。

放⁠在 know、think、wonder 後⁠面

She didn't know what was going to happen.

例句

I wondered who was going to win.

我在想誰會贏。

時⁠間點在過⁠去，be 動⁠詞也用過⁠去⁠式

She didn't know what is going to happen.

She didn't know what was going to happen.

know 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句不⁠是疑⁠問⁠句，主⁠詞放⁠在 was 前⁠面

She didn't know what was going to happen.

I didn't know what was he going to do.

I didn't know what he was going to do.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She didn't know what was going to happen.

We were going to went to Rome.

We were going to go to Rome.

例句

① 過去的預料

  1. When I saw the dark clouds, I knew it was going to rain.
    我看到烏雲，就知道會下雨。
  2. Everyone could see that the old tree was going to fall.
    大家都看得出來那棵老樹會倒。
  3. From the first page, I knew the hero was going to die at the end.
    從第一頁開始，我就知道主角最後會死。
  4. The sky over the mountains looked like it was going to clear.
    山上的天空看起來會放晴。
  5. By midnight it was clear that our team was going to lose.
    到了半夜，很明顯我們隊會輸。
  6. The pilot told us the flight was going to be bumpy.
    機長告訴我們飛行途中會有點顛簸。
  7. We realised the journey was going to cost more than we thought.
    我們發現這趟旅程會比我們想的還要花錢。
  8. My grandmother always said I was going to be a singer.
    我奶奶總是說我會當歌手。
  9. Nobody guessed that the shy boy was going to become famous.
    沒有人猜到那個害羞的男孩會出名。
  10. The forecast said the storm was going to reach the coast by Friday.
    預報說暴風雨星期五會到海岸。

② 過去的計畫沒有實現：was / were going to ..., but ...

  1. I was going to cook dinner, but we ordered a pizza instead.
    我本來要煮晚餐，可是我們改叫了披薩。
  2. They were going to get married in May, but they waited until autumn.
    他們本來五月要結婚，可是等到了秋天。
  3. Tom was going to repair the bike himself, but he couldn't find the tools.
    Tom 本來要自己修腳踏車，可是他找不到工具。
  4. We were going to walk to the lake, but it started to snow.
    我們本來要走路去湖邊，可是開始下雪了。
  5. I was going to send her a card, but I forgot her address.
    我本來要寄卡片給她，可是我忘了她的地址。
  6. The school was going to close for a week, but the heating came back on.
    學校本來要停課一週，可是暖氣恢復了。
  7. My dad was going to retire last year, but he loves his job too much.
    我爸去年本來要退休，可是他太愛他的工作了。
  8. We were going to camp by the river, but the ground was too wet.
    我們本來要在河邊露營，可是地上太濕了。
  9. She was going to take the early train, but she overslept.
    她本來要搭早班火車，可是她睡過頭了。
  10. The band was going to play outside, but they moved the concert indoors.
    樂團本來要在戶外表演，可是他們把音樂會移到室內。

③ know / think / wonder + what / who / if + was / were going to

  1. I didn't know who was going to meet me at the airport.
    我不知道誰會來機場接我。
  2. We had no idea how long the repairs were going to take.
    我們完全不知道修理會花多久。
  3. I thought you were going to bring the drinks.
    我以為你會帶飲料來。
  4. She wondered if her parents were going to be angry.
    她在想她爸媽會不會生氣。
  5. The players didn't know if the match was going to go ahead.
    球員們不知道比賽會不會照常進行。
  6. I wasn't sure what I was going to say to him.
    我不確定我要跟他說什麼。
  7. He asked me where I was going to spend the summer.
    他問我暑假要去哪裡過。
  8. Nobody told us when the train was going to leave.
    沒有人告訴我們火車什麼時候開。
  9. I thought the film was going to be funny, but it was really sad.
    我以為那部電影會很好笑，結果很悲傷。
  10. We wondered how we were going to pay for the repairs.
    我們在想要怎麼付修理費。
  11. Did you know that Lucy was going to leave the company?
    你知道 Lucy 要離開公司嗎？
