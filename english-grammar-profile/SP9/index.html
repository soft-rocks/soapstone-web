Can you bring orange juice, please? I don't have any.

你可以帶柳橙汁來嗎？我這裡沒有。

否⁠定⁠句 + any

Can you bring orange juice, please? I don't have any.

柳橙汁……我這裡沒有。

複⁠數名⁠詞和不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞

Can you bring orange juice, please? I don't have any.

複數名詞

Please bring some good CDs. I don't have any.

請帶幾張好聽的 CD 來，我一張也沒有。
不可數名詞

I wanted to make tea, but we didn't have any.

我想泡茶，可是家裡沒有茶葉。

問⁠句⁠裡⁠的 any

Can you bring orange juice, please? I don't have any.

我需要幾張郵票，你有嗎？

肯⁠定⁠句用 some

Can you bring orange juice, please? I don't have any.

We need bread. I'll buy any.

We need bread. I'll buy some.

not 後⁠面用 any

Can you bring orange juice, please? I don't have any.

I wanted milk, but there wasn't no.

I wanted milk, but there wasn't any.

單⁠數名⁠詞用 one

I need a pen. I don't have any.

I need a pen. I don't have one.

any + 名⁠詞

我一毛錢也沒有。

none：動⁠詞不⁠加 not

我找了蛋，可是一顆也沒有。

例句

① don't have any

  1. Can you bring some board games? We don't have any.
    你可以帶幾個桌遊來嗎？我們家沒有。
  2. Bring some snacks for the trip. I don't have any.
    旅行帶一點零食，我這裡沒有。
  3. My friends all have cats, but I don't have any.
    我的朋友都有養貓，可是我沒有。
  4. She asked for a tissue, but I didn't have any.
    她跟我要面紙，可是我沒有。
  5. We need more chairs, but we don't have any.
    我們需要更多椅子，可是一張也沒有。

② 複數名詞

  1. I love his books, but I don't own any.
    我很喜歡他的書，可是一本也沒有。
  2. He collects stamps, but I don't collect any.
    他收集郵票，可是我沒有收集。
  3. There were lots of apples on the tree, but we didn't pick any.
    樹上有很多蘋果，可是我們一顆也沒摘。
  4. Everyone took photos at the wedding, but I didn't take any.
    婚禮上大家都在拍照，可是我一張也沒拍。
  5. I looked for cheap flights, but I couldn't find any.
    我找便宜的機票，可是一張也找不到。

③ 不可數名詞

  1. I'd like some coffee, but there isn't any left.
    我想喝咖啡，可是一點也不剩了。
  2. We need sugar for the cake. We don't have any.
    做蛋糕要用糖，我們家沒有糖。
  3. He asked for advice, but I couldn't give him any.
    他要我給他建議，可是我給不出任何建議。
  4. I wanted to buy cheese, but the shop didn't have any.
    我想買起司，可是店裡沒有。
  5. "Is there any milk?" "No, there isn't any."
    有牛奶嗎？沒有，一點也沒有。

④ 其他動詞

  1. There were lots of cakes, but I didn't eat any.
    有很多蛋糕，可是我一塊也沒吃。
  2. Mum made biscuits, but my brother didn't want any.
    媽媽做了餅乾，可是我弟弟一塊也不想吃。
  3. I bought some flowers, but she didn't like any.
    我買了一些花，可是她一朵也不喜歡。
  4. The teacher gave us homework, but I haven't done any.
    老師出了作業，可是我一題也還沒寫。
  5. We saw lots of fish in the river, but we didn't catch any.
    我們在河裡看到很多魚，可是一條也沒釣到。

⑤ 問句

  1. We need eggs. Have you got any?
    我們需要蛋，你有嗎？
  2. I forgot to bring presents. Did you bring any?
    我忘了帶禮物，你有帶嗎？
  3. I'm making soup. Would you like any?
    我在煮湯，你要喝一點嗎？
  4. There are biscuits in the tin. Did you have any?
    罐子裡有餅乾，你吃了嗎？
  5. These grapes are sweet. Do you want any?
    這些葡萄很甜，你要吃嗎？

⑥ 在信和留言裡

  1. Please bring some music to the party. I don't have any.
    請帶一些音樂來派對，我這裡沒有。
  2. Can you buy some bread on your way home? There isn't any.
    你回家路上可以買麵包嗎？家裡沒有了。
  3. I wanted to send you some photos, but I didn't take any.
    我想寄一些照片給你，可是我一張也沒拍。
  4. Don't bring food. We don't need any.
    不用帶吃的，我們不需要。
  5. We have lots of plates, so you don't need to bring any.
    我們家盤子很多，你不用帶。
  6. I asked about tickets, but they didn't have any left.
    我問了票的事，可是他們一張也不剩了。
