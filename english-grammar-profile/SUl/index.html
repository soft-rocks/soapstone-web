The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

我最喜歡的是一款新的電腦遊戲。

The thing 和子⁠句先說範⁠圍，be 後⁠面放重⁠點

我最喜歡的是一款新的電腦遊戲。

子句前面省略了 that。

The thing 的句⁠子把重⁠點移⁠到句⁠尾

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

一般的句子

I need a holiday.

我需要休個假。
The thing 的句子

The thing I need is a holiday.

我需要的是休個假。

be 後⁠面可⁠以接名⁠詞片⁠語、that 子⁠句或不⁠定⁠詞

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

名詞片語

The thing I prefer is the swimming pool.

我比較喜歡的是游泳池。
that 子句

The thing is that I don't have any money.

問題是我沒有錢。
to + 原形動詞

The thing to do is ask the teacher.

該做的是去問老師。

thing 前⁠面可⁠以加 only、best、worst、first

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

我唯一不喜歡的是食物。

The thing 已⁠經是受⁠詞，子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

The thing I like it most is the music.

The thing I like most is the music.

子⁠句後⁠面要⁠有 be，再⁠接重⁠點

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

The thing I need a holiday.

The thing I need is a holiday.

The thing 後⁠面不⁠加 what

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

The thing what I hate is the traffic.

The thing I hate is the traffic.

主⁠詞是 The things，be 用 are

The thing I enjoyed most was a new computer game.

The things I miss most is my friends and my dog.

The things I miss most are my friends and my dog.

例句

① The thing + 子句 + was + 名詞片語

  1. The party was great. The thing I enjoyed most was the music.
    派對很棒，我最喜歡的是音樂。
  2. The thing I enjoyed most about the trip was a new computer game my cousin showed me.
    這趟旅行我最喜歡的是我表哥給我看的一款新電腦遊戲。
  3. The thing that surprised me was the price.
    讓我驚訝的是價錢。
  4. The thing I remember best is my grandmother's kitchen.
    我記得最清楚的是我奶奶的廚房。
  5. The thing we forgot was the tent!
    我們忘了帶的是帳篷！

② The thing + 子句 + is + 名詞片語

  1. The thing I prefer at the hotel is the swimming pool.
    那家飯店我比較喜歡的是游泳池。
  2. The thing I need right now is a cup of tea.
    我現在需要的是一杯茶。
  3. The thing I hate about winter is the dark mornings.
    冬天我討厭的是早上天都黑黑的。
  4. The thing I like about my job is the people.
    我喜歡我工作的地方是那裡的人。
  5. The thing that worries me is the cost.
    讓我擔心的是費用。

③ The things + 子句 + are + 複數名詞

  1. The things I miss most are my friends and my dog.
    我最想念的是我的朋友和我的狗。
  2. The things I packed first were my passport and my phone.
    我最先打包的是護照和手機。
  3. The things I like best about London are the parks.
    我最喜歡倫敦的地方是那些公園。

④ The thing is + 子句

  1. I'd love to come. The thing is, I have to work on Saturday.
    我很想去，問題是我星期六要上班。
  2. The thing is that I don't have enough money.
    問題是我的錢不夠。
  3. The thing is, she never listens.
    問題是她從來不聽。
  4. I like the flat. The thing is, it's too far from the station.
    我喜歡那間公寓，問題是它離車站太遠了。

⑤ The thing + 子句 + is + to + 原形動詞

  1. The thing to do is ask the teacher.
    該做的是去問老師。
  2. The thing I want to do now is sleep.
    我現在想做的就是睡覺。
  3. The thing you need to do is call the bank.
    你要做的是打電話給銀行。
  4. The thing I'd like to do this summer is learn to swim.
    今年夏天我想做的是學游泳。

⑥ The only / best / worst / first thing + 子句 + be

  1. The best thing I did on holiday was the boat trip.
    我度假時做過最棒的事是搭船出遊。
  2. The only thing I can't eat is fish.
    我唯一不能吃的是魚。
  3. The worst thing that happened on the flight was the lost bag.
    那趟飛行最糟的是行李不見了。
  4. The first thing I do in the morning is check my phone.
    我早上做的第一件事是看手機。

⑦ The thing + 子句 + be + 子句

  1. The thing I still don't understand is why she sold the house.
    我到現在還不懂的是她為什麼把房子賣了。
  2. The thing I love about her is how kind she is.
    我喜歡她的地方是她很善良。
  3. The thing we need to decide is where to go.
    我們要決定的是去哪裡。
  4. The thing nobody knows is who took the money.
    沒有人知道的是誰拿了那筆錢。
  5. The thing that matters is that you're safe.
    重要的是你平安。
  6. The thing I'm worried about is what my parents will say.
    我擔心的是我爸媽會怎麼說。
