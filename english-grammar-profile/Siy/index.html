我在那裡待了兩個星期，一次雨都沒下。

我在那裡待了兩個星期，一次雨都沒下。

never 的動⁠詞不⁠再⁠加 not

My uncle doesn't never wear a tie.

My uncle never wears a tie.

否⁠定⁠句⁠的 still 放⁠在否⁠定⁠的助⁠動⁠詞前⁠面

The school hasn't still sent the report.

The school still hasn't sent the report.

直⁠述⁠句⁠的 ever 要跟⁠著否⁠定⁠的字

I have never ridden a camel.

例句

① never + 動詞

  1. We went camping last weekend, and the sun never came out.
    我們上週末去露營，太陽一次都沒出來。
  2. My grandfather never drives at night. He can't see well in the dark.
    我爺爺從來不在晚上開車。他在暗處看不清楚。
  3. She never forgets a face. She remembers everyone she meets.
    她見過的臉從來不會忘。每個見過的人她都記得。
  4. We've never been to Australia, but we'd love to go.
    我們從來沒去過澳洲，可是很想去。
  5. Our neighbours never say hello. They're not very friendly.
    我們的鄰居從來不打招呼。他們不太友善。
  6. I never saw him again after we left school.
    畢業以後我就再也沒見過他。

② be + never

  1. Tom is never late for work. He always takes the early train.
    Tom 上班從來不遲到。他都搭早班的火車。
  2. The shop was never busy on Mondays, so it closed.
    那家店星期一從來沒什麼客人，所以後來關了。

③ ever 在否定的字後面

  1. The ship sank in deep water. Nobody will ever find it.
    那艘船沉在深海裡。永遠不會有人找到它。
  2. Don't ever do that again! You scared me.
    不准再那樣做！你嚇到我了。
  3. Nothing ever happens in this village.
    這個村子從來沒發生過什麼事。
  4. The food made me ill. I won't ever eat at that restaurant again.
    那裡的菜讓我生病了。我再也不會去那家餐廳吃飯了。
  5. No one ever tells me anything in this office.
    在這間辦公室，從來沒有人告訴我任何事。

④ 否定的動詞 + yet

  1. "Where are you going on holiday?" "I haven't decided yet."
    你要去哪裡度假？我還沒決定。
  2. Don't eat the cake. It isn't ready yet.
    別吃蛋糕。還沒好。
  3. I've sent the invitations, but nobody has replied yet.
    我寄了邀請函，可是還沒有人回覆。
  4. The new library hasn't opened yet. It opens in May.
    新的圖書館還沒開放。五月才開。
  5. We can't go in yet. The doors open at seven.
    我們還不能進去。七點才開門。
  6. My sister hasn't found a job yet, but she's looking.
    我姊姊還沒找到工作，可是她在找。

⑤ still + 否定的助動詞

  1. She said sorry, but I still don't believe her.
    她說了對不起，可是我還是不相信她。
  2. It's ten o'clock, and the plumber still hasn't come.
    都十點了，水電工還是沒來。
  3. I've read the instructions three times, and I still don't understand them.
    說明我讀了三遍，還是看不懂。
  4. He's twenty-five, and he still can't cook an egg.
    他二十五歲了，還是不會煎蛋。
  5. We applied in March, and we still haven't heard from the bank about the loan.
    我們三月就申請了，到現在還是沒收到銀行貸款的消息。
  6. My little brother still doesn't like vegetables.
    我弟弟到現在還是不喜歡吃青菜。

⑥ yet 和 still 說同一件事

  1. The bus hasn't come yet. Let's wait five more minutes.
    公車還沒來。我們再等五分鐘吧。
  2. The bus still hasn't come. We've been here for an hour!
    公車到現在都還沒來。我們已經在這裡等了一個小時了！
  3. I haven't finished my essay yet. It's due on Friday.
    我的作文還沒寫完。星期五要交。
  4. I still haven't finished my essay, and it's due tomorrow!
    我的作文到現在還沒寫完，明天就要交了！

⑦ 否定疑問句 + yet

  1. Haven't they answered your email yet? It's been three days.
    他們還沒回你的信嗎？已經三天了。
  2. Hasn't the parcel arrived yet? I sent it a week ago.
    包裹還沒到嗎？我一個星期前就寄了。
