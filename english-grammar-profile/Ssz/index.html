I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

我覺得新班的老師比上一個好很多。

much 放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面，表⁠示差很⁠多

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

這間公寓比我們以前那間大很多。

短⁠的形⁠容⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠er，長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞用 more

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

much + 形容詞-er

The express train is much faster than the local one.

快車比區間車快很多。
much + more + 形容詞

Flying is much more expensive than taking the train.

搭飛機比搭火車貴很多。

so much 的語⁠氣比 much 強

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

這間房子很漂亮，而且比之前那間大太多了。

much 加比⁠較⁠級也⁠可⁠以放⁠在 look、feel 後⁠面

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

孩子們在新學校看起來開心多了。

修⁠飾比⁠較⁠級用 much，不⁠用 very

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

My new phone is very faster than my old one.

My new phone is much faster than my old one.

much 後⁠面要⁠用比⁠較⁠級

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

This test was much easy than the last one.

This test was much easier than the last one.

much 放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

The city is bigger much than the village.

The city is much bigger than the village.

修⁠飾比⁠較⁠級用 so much，不⁠用 so many

I think the teacher in the new class is much better than the last one.

The new bed is so many softer.

The new bed is so much softer.

例句

① much + 形容詞-er

  1. This flat is much bigger than our old one.
    這間公寓比我們以前那間大很多。
  2. The express train is much faster than the local one.
    快車比區間車快很多。
  3. My sister is much older than her husband.
    我姊姊比她先生大很多。
  4. The lake was much colder than we expected.
    湖水比我們想的冷很多。
  5. Our new neighbors are much quieter.
    我們的新鄰居安靜多了。
  6. Her second book was much shorter than her first.
    她的第二本書比第一本短很多。
  7. Is the hotel much cheaper on weekdays?
    那家旅館平日便宜很多嗎？
  8. The shy girl in my class is much braver now.
    我們班上那個害羞的女生現在勇敢多了。

② much + more + 形容詞

  1. The museum was much more interesting than I thought.
    博物館比我想的有趣很多。
  2. Flying is much more expensive than taking the train.
    搭飛機比搭火車貴很多。
  3. Our new manager is much more reticent than the old one.
    我們的新經理比原本那個話少很多。
  4. The second test was much more difficult.
    第二次考試難很多。
  5. This chair is much more comfortable than the sofa.
    這張椅子比沙發舒服很多。
  6. Life in the city is much more stressful.
    在城市生活壓力大很多。

③ much better / much worse

  1. The weather today is much better than yesterday.
    今天的天氣比昨天好很多。
  2. Your English is much better than mine.
    你的英文比我的好很多。
  3. The traffic was much worse this morning.
    今天早上的交通糟很多。
  4. Is your grandmother much better now?
    你奶奶現在好多了嗎？
  5. The food here is much better than at the old place.
    這裡的食物比以前那家好吃很多。

④ so much + 比較級

  1. The house is beautiful, and so much bigger than the previous one.
    這間房子很漂亮，而且比之前那間大太多了。
  2. This new laptop is so much lighter.
    這台新的筆電輕太多了。
  3. Ann's cooking is so much better than mine.
    Ann 的廚藝比我的好太多了。
  4. The beach was so much cleaner this year.
    今年的海灘乾淨太多了。
  5. Walking to work is so much cheaper than driving.
    走路上班比開車省太多了。

⑤ look / feel / seem / get 後面

  1. The children look much happier in the new school.
    孩子們在新學校看起來開心多了。
  2. It's getting much colder at night.
    晚上變得冷多了。
  3. I feel much calmer after a long walk.
    走了一大段路以後，我覺得平靜多了。
  4. The new boss seems much friendlier.
    新老闆看起來友善多了。
  5. The streets got much busier after the new mall opened.
    新的購物中心開幕以後，街上熱鬧多了。
  6. My grandfather feels so much healthier these days.
    我爺爺最近覺得身體好太多了。
