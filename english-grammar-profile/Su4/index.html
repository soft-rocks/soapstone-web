主⁠詞 + had been + ⁠-⁠ing + for + 一⁠段時⁠間

for、since

I know her very well because we had been exchanging letters for a long time.

for加一段時間，像 for a long time、for five years。
since加開始的時間，像 since 2010、since she was ten。

背⁠景資⁠訊

例句

She had been going there since she was ten years old.

她從十歲起就一直去那裡。

這個句型常放在故事裡，說明人物過去的習慣或經歷。

一⁠段時⁠間用 for，開⁠始⁠的時⁠間點用 since

I know her very well because we had been exchanging letters for a long time.

She had been going there since five years.

She had been going there for five years.

had 和 ⁠-⁠ing 中⁠間要⁠有 been

I know her very well because we had been exchanging letters for a long time.

We had exchanging letters for years.

We had been exchanging letters for years.

had been 後⁠面⁠接 ⁠-⁠ing

I know her very well because we had been exchanging letters for a long time.

He had been smoked since he was twelve.

He had been smoking since he was twelve.

例句

① 主詞 + had been + -ing + for + 一段時間

  1. When we finally met, I knew her very well, because we had been exchanging letters for a long time.
    我們終於見面的時候，我已經很了解她，因為我們已經通信很久了。
  2. They had been living next door to us for twenty years when they moved to Spain.
    他們搬去西班牙的時候，已經在我們隔壁住了二十年。
  3. My grandfather had been working at the factory for forty years when it closed.
    工廠關門的時候，我爺爺已經在那裡工作了四十年。
  4. We had been travelling around Asia for six months when we ran out of money.
    我們在亞洲旅行了六個月，然後錢就用完了。
  5. She had been teaching at the school for ten years before she became head.
    她當上校長之前，已經在那所學校教了十年書。
  6. He had been learning the violin for three years when he joined the orchestra.
    他加入管弦樂團的時候，已經學了三年小提琴。

② 主詞 + had been + -ing + since + 時間點

  1. She knew everyone at the riding school, because she had been going there since she was ten years old.
    她認識馬術學校的每一個人，因為她從十歲起就一直去那裡。
  2. The old man had been smoking since he was a child, since he was twelve.
    那個老人從小就開始抽菸，從他十二歲開始。
  3. My parents had been running the bakery since 1985.
    我爸媽從 1985 年起就一直經營那家麵包店。
  4. He had been feeling tired since the beginning of the month.
    他從月初就一直覺得很累。
  5. They had been building the new road since last spring.
    他們從去年春天就一直在蓋那條新路。
  6. I had been waiting for her call since breakfast.
    我從早餐後就一直在等她的電話。

③ 主詞 + 過去式, because + 主詞 + had been + -ing + for / since：說明原因

  1. He spoke perfect Italian, because he had been living in Rome since he was a boy.
    他的義大利文說得很好，因為他從小就住在羅馬。
  2. She was a very good cook, because she had been helping in her mother's restaurant for years.
    她很會煮飯，因為她多年來一直在她媽媽的餐廳幫忙。
  3. The dog knew the way home, because it had been walking that path every day for years.
    那隻狗認得回家的路，因為牠多年來每天都走那條路。
  4. I recognised him at once, because I had been watching his films since I was a teenager.
    我一眼就認出他了，因為我從十幾歲就一直在看他的電影。
  5. They were good friends, because they had been playing in the same team since primary school.
    他們是好朋友，因為他們從小學就一直在同一支隊伍。
  6. She understood the problem, because she had been working in that department for five years.
    她了解這個問題，因為她已經在那個部門工作了五年。

④ By + 時間, + 主詞 + had been + -ing + for + 一段時間

  1. By 2010, my family had been farming the same land for a hundred years.
    到 2010 年，我們家已經在同一塊地上耕作了一百年。
  2. By the end of the summer, I had been working at the café for three months.
    到夏天結束的時候，我已經在咖啡館工作了三個月。
  3. By midnight, the band had been playing for four hours.
    到半夜，樂團已經演奏了四個小時。
  4. By the time of the wedding, they had been living together for six years.
    到婚禮的時候，他們已經一起住了六年。
  5. By March, it had been snowing on and off for weeks.
    到三月，雪已經斷斷續續下了好幾個星期。
  6. By his tenth birthday, he had been swimming in competitions for two years.
    到他十歲生日的時候，他已經參加游泳比賽兩年了。

⑤ 故事的背景：主詞 + had been + -ing + …, when + 主詞 + 過去式

  1. The old man was sitting by the fire, smoking his pipe. He had been smoking since he was a child. Suddenly, the curtain moved.
    那個老人坐在火爐旁，抽著煙斗。他從小就開始抽菸。突然，窗簾動了一下。
  2. The family had been living in the castle for centuries when the war began.
    戰爭開始的時候，那一家人已經在城堡裡住了好幾百年。
  3. She had been writing in her diary every night for years when she lost it.
    她多年來每晚都寫日記，然後她把日記弄丟了。
  4. The two friends had been meeting at the café every Friday since school when one of them moved away.
    那兩個朋友從學生時代起每個星期五都在咖啡館見面，後來其中一個搬走了。
  5. The fisherman had been going out to sea every morning for fifty years when he saw the strange light.
    那個漁夫五十年來每天早上都出海，然後他看到了那道奇怪的光。
  6. The girl had been dreaming of the city since she was small when the letter arrived.
    那個女孩從小就夢想著那座城市，這時那封信來了。
  7. The village had been waiting for rain since spring when the storm finally came.
    那個村子從春天就一直在等雨，這時暴風雨終於來了。
