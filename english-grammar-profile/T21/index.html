Luckily, I arrived in time.

幸好我及時趕到了。

態⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首，修⁠飾整⁠個句⁠子

幸好我及時趕到了。

態⁠度副⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞或連⁠接⁠詞後⁠面，前⁠後加逗⁠號

Luckily, I arrived in time.

主詞後面

My sister, unfortunately, missed the last bus.

我妹妹不巧錯過了最後一班公車。
連接詞後面

But, obviously, you prefer going with your friends.

不過，你顯然比較想跟朋友一起去。

obviously、clearly 可⁠以放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面或 be 後⁠面，不⁠加逗⁠號

動詞前面

She obviously didn't like the food.

她顯然不喜歡那些菜。
be 後面

The answer is clearly wrong.

這個答案顯然是錯的。

各⁠個態⁠度副⁠詞表⁠示不⁠同⁠的看⁠法

luckily、fortunately幸好
unfortunately、sadly可惜，不幸
obviously、clearly顯然
surprisingly出乎意料
honestly、frankly、seriously老實說，說真的
hopefully希望

修⁠飾整⁠個句⁠子用副⁠詞，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

Luckily, I arrived in time.

Lucky, I found my keys.

Luckily, I found my keys.

unfortunate 保⁠留 e 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ly

Unfortunatly, the shop was closed.

Unfortunately, the shop was closed.

態⁠度副⁠詞和主⁠詞用逗⁠號隔⁠開⁠時，後⁠面也加逗⁠號

My boss unfortunately, was ill.

My boss, unfortunately, was ill.

態⁠度副⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接句⁠子，不⁠加 that

Hopefully that the rain will stop soon.

Hopefully, the rain will stop soon.

例句

① 態度副詞放在句首

  1. Fortunately, nobody was hurt in the accident.
    幸好那場車禍沒有人受傷。
  2. Sadly, our neighbours are moving to Leeds.
    可惜我們的鄰居要搬去里茲了。
  3. Surprisingly, the exam was quite easy.
    沒想到考試還滿簡單的。
  4. Honestly, I don't remember her name.
    老實說，我不記得她的名字。
  5. Hopefully, the swelling will go down by Monday.
    希望星期一前能消腫。
  6. Unfortunately, the museum is closed on Mondays.
    可惜博物館星期一休館。
  7. Frankly, the film was too long.
    坦白說，那部電影太長了。
  8. Luckily, my phone was still in my bag.
    幸好我的手機還在包包裡。

② 態度副詞放在主詞後面

  1. The lift, unfortunately, was out of order.
    不巧電梯故障了。
  2. My grandmother, surprisingly, loves video games.
    沒想到我奶奶很愛打電玩。
  3. The hotel, luckily, had one room left.
    幸好那家飯店還剩一間房。
  4. Our teacher, sadly, is leaving at the end of term.
    可惜我們老師這學期結束就要走了。

③ 態度副詞放在連接詞後面

  1. I wanted to go, but, unfortunately, I had to work.
    我很想去，可是不巧我得上班。
  2. We got lost, and, surprisingly, we found a great café.
    我們迷路了，結果沒想到找到一間很棒的咖啡館。
  3. It rained all day, so, obviously, we stayed in.
    下了一整天雨，所以我們當然待在家裡。
  4. The tickets were expensive, but, luckily, my uncle paid.
    票很貴，不過幸好我叔叔出錢。

④ 態度副詞放在動詞前面或 be 後面

  1. He clearly forgot about our meeting.
    他顯然忘了我們有約。
  2. You're obviously tired. Go to bed.
    你顯然累了，去睡吧。
  3. The kids were clearly bored.
    孩子們顯然很無聊。
  4. He unfortunately missed the start of the film.
    他不巧錯過了電影的開頭。

⑤ 不同的看法

  1. Obviously, you can't park here.
    這裡當然不能停車。
  2. Clearly, someone has been in my room.
    顯然有人進過我的房間。
  3. Sadly, the dog didn't survive.
    可惜那隻狗沒活下來。
  4. Frankly, your essay needs a lot more work.
    坦白說，你的報告還要大改。
  5. Fortunately, the bank was still open.
    幸好銀行還開著。
  6. Surprisingly, my cousin passed his driving test first time.
    沒想到我表哥第一次考駕照就過了。
  7. Hopefully, the heating engineer can come on Friday.
    希望修暖氣的師傅星期五能來。
  8. Honestly, the soup needs more salt.
    老實說，這湯要再加點鹽。

⑥ 疑問句和否定句

  1. Unfortunately, the printer doesn't work.
    不巧印表機壞了。
  2. Luckily, the train wasn't full.
    幸好火車上人不多。
  3. Honestly, do you like my new haircut?
    說真的，你喜歡我的新髮型嗎？
  4. Seriously, are you moving to Canada?
    說真的，你要搬去加拿大？
