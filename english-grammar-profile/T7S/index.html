The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

那位女士說，如果我願意，她可以讓我借住她家。

引⁠述動⁠詞是過⁠去⁠式，原話⁠的 can 改⁠成 could

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

原話

I can put you up if you want.

你願意的話，我可以讓你住我家。
轉述

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

那位女士說，如果我願意，她可以讓我借住她家。

女士說的 I 和 you，轉述時改成 she 和 me。

told 後⁠面⁠接聽⁠話⁠的⁠人，said 後⁠面不⁠接

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

原話是 can't，轉⁠述寫 couldn't

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

她說她找不到她的鑰匙。

轉⁠述問⁠句用 asked if，could 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

他問能不能開窗。

原話是 Can I open the window?。句尾不用問號。

explained、admitted、promised 後⁠面也接 could

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

她解釋說她只能待一個小時。

could 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

He said he could came with us.

He said he could come with us.

could 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

She told me she could to help.

She told me she could help.

asked if 後⁠面⁠的主⁠詞放⁠在 could 前⁠面

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

He asked if could he use the phone.

He asked if he could use the phone.

told 後⁠面要接聽⁠話⁠的人

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

She told she could drive.

She told me she could drive.

不寫聽話的人，就用 said。

原話⁠說⁠的事到現⁠在還成⁠立，can 可⁠以不⁠改

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

他說他星期五可以跟我們碰面。

不⁠是轉⁠述⁠的 could 說⁠的⁠是過⁠去⁠的能⁠力

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

她五歲就會游泳了。

原話是 will 轉⁠述成 would，原話是 can 才轉⁠述成 could

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

will → would

He said he would call me later.

他說他晚點會打給我。
can → could

The woman said she could put me up if I wanted.

那位女士說，如果我願意，她可以讓我借住她家。

例句

① 主詞 + said (that) + 主詞 + could

  1. She said she could pick us up at the station.
    她說她可以到車站接我們。
  2. My boss said I could leave early today.
    我老闆說我今天可以早點走。
  3. The doctor said I could go home the next day.
    醫生說我隔天就可以回家。
  4. He said that he could fix the bike in an hour.
    他說他一個小時就能修好腳踏車。
  5. They said we could stay as long as we liked.
    他們說我們想待多久都可以。
  6. The receptionist said that we could check in after two.
    櫃檯人員說我們兩點以後可以入住。
  7. Ann said she could lend me her dictionary.
    Ann 說她可以借我她的字典。
  8. The man at the shop said he could order it for me.
    店裡的人說他可以幫我訂。
  9. My neighbour said she could water the plants while we were away.
    我鄰居說我們不在的時候，她可以幫忙澆花。
  10. The teacher said we could use our notes in the test.
    老師說考試的時候我們可以看筆記。
  11. You said you could speak a little French.
    你說過你會說一點法文。
  12. The guide said that we could take photos inside.
    導遊說我們可以在裡面拍照。

② 主詞 + told + 受詞 + (that) + 主詞 + could

  1. She told me she could help with the move.
    她跟我說她可以幫忙搬家。
  2. The mechanic told us that he could have the car ready by Friday.
    技師跟我們說，他星期五之前可以把車修好。
  3. I told my mother I could cook dinner for once.
    我跟我媽說，這次晚餐可以由我來煮。
  4. He told the manager he could work on Sunday.
    他跟經理說他星期天可以上班。
  5. They told us we could park behind the building.
    他們跟我們說可以把車停在大樓後面。

③ couldn't

  1. I told them I couldn't come because I had to work.
    我跟他們說我不能來，因為我得上班。
  2. She said she couldn't remember his name.
    她說她想不起他的名字。
  3. The bank said they couldn't change the date of the payment.
    銀行說他們不能更改付款日期。
  4. He told me he couldn't see anything without his glasses.
    他跟我說，他沒戴眼鏡什麼都看不到。
  5. My sister said she couldn't stand the noise any longer.
    我姊說她再也受不了那個噪音了。
  6. The hotel said that they couldn't give us a refund.
    飯店說他們不能退錢給我們。

④ asked if / whether + 主詞 + could

  1. He asked if he could borrow my pen.
    他問能不能借我的筆。
  2. She asked whether she could bring a friend.
    她問能不能帶一個朋友來。
  3. A tourist asked me if I could take a photo of them.
    一個觀光客問我能不能幫他們拍張照。
  4. The kids asked if they could stay up late.
    孩子們問能不能晚點睡。
  5. I asked the waiter whether we could sit outside.
    我問服務生我們能不能坐在外面。

⑤ explained、admitted、promised + that + 主詞 + could

  1. She explained that she couldn't drive at night.
    她解釋說她晚上不能開車。
  2. He admitted that he couldn't swim.
    他承認他不會游泳。
  3. The landlord promised that we could move in on the first.
    房東答應我們一號就可以搬進去。
