Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

一切都那麼熟悉，好像他從來沒離開過這個地方。

as if 子⁠句用想⁠像⁠的情⁠況說⁠明前⁠面⁠的動⁠作

Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

他衝出房間，好像有人在追他一樣。

比主⁠要⁠子⁠句更早⁠的⁠事，用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式

Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

He looked pale, as though he had seen a ghost.

他臉色蒼白，好像見到鬼一樣。

正⁠式⁠的寫⁠法用 were

Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

She talks to her cat as if it were a person.

她跟她的貓說話，好像牠是人一樣。

as if 後⁠面⁠接子⁠句，要⁠有主⁠詞和動⁠詞

Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

He ran out, as if a ghost.

He ran out, as if he had seen a ghost.

表⁠示好⁠像，用 as if 或 as though

Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

She smiled, as nothing had happened.

She smiled, as if nothing had happened.

用 as if，不⁠用 like if

Everything was so familiar, as though he had never left the place.

He ran away like if he was scared.

He ran away as if he was scared.

例句

① 動作 + as if + 過去進行式

  1. I found my brother under the bed, as if he was hiding from someone.
    我發現我弟弟躲在床底下，好像在躲什麼人。
  2. I watched a man walk through the dark car park. He kept turning around, as if someone was following him.
    我看著一個男人走過黑暗的停車場。他一直回頭，好像有人在跟蹤他。
  3. My sister carried Grandma's old plates to the car. She held the box carefully, as if it was full of eggs.
    我姊姊把奶奶的舊盤子搬到車上。她小心地拿著那個箱子，好像裡面裝滿了蛋。
  4. We were watching TV at midnight. The dog barked at the door, as if someone was outside.
    我們半夜在看電視。狗對著門叫，好像外面有人。
  5. My date didn't listen to me at dinner. He kept looking at his watch, as if he was late for something.
    我的約會對象晚餐時都沒在聽我說話。他一直看手錶，好像有什麼事要遲到了。

② 狀態 + as though + 過去完成式

  1. The house felt warm and familiar, as though we had lived there for years.
    那間房子感覺溫暖又熟悉，好像我們在那裡住了好多年。
  2. We had a big fight on Friday. On Monday she smiled at me, as though nothing had happened.
    我們星期五大吵一架。星期一她對我笑，好像什麼事都沒發生過。
  3. My brother came home from his exams. He was very tired, as if he hadn't slept for days.
    我哥哥考完試回家。他非常累，好像好幾天沒睡了。
  4. The kids made pancakes while I was out. The kitchen was a mess, as though a storm had passed through it.
    我出門的時候孩子們做了鬆餅。廚房一團亂，好像被暴風吹過一樣。
  5. I met my boyfriend's parents for the first time. They greeted me warmly, as if they had known me for years.
    我第一次見我男朋友的爸媽。他們熱情地跟我打招呼，好像認識我好幾年了。

③ 心跳和身體的感覺

  1. I waited outside the exam room. My heart was beating so hard, as if it was going to jump out.
    我在考場外面等。我的心跳得好用力，好像要跳出來了。
  2. I only climbed the stairs to the fifth floor. My legs were shaking, as if I had run a marathon.
    我只是爬樓梯到五樓。我的腿在發抖，好像跑了一場馬拉松。
  3. The coach shouted at the boy in front of the team. His face went red, as though he was about to cry.
    教練在全隊面前對那個男孩大吼。他的臉紅了，好像快哭了。
  4. I stood up too fast after the blood test. My head was spinning, as if I was on a boat.
    抽完血我站起來太快。我的頭在轉，好像在船上一樣。

④ as if + were

  1. Our neighbour tells everyone where to park. He talks about the town as if he were the mayor.
    我們的鄰居告訴每個人要停在哪裡。他說起這個鎮，好像他是鎮長一樣。
  2. My cousin earns very little, but he spends money as if he were a millionaire.
    我表哥賺得很少，可是他花錢好像他是百萬富翁一樣。
  3. I'm twenty-five, but my parents still check my homework. They treat me as though I were a child.
    我二十五歲了，可是我爸媽還在檢查我的作業。他們把我當小孩看。
  4. My grandmother loves the village parties. She dances as if nobody were watching.
    我奶奶很愛村子裡的派對。她跳舞好像沒人在看一樣。

⑤ 現在式和不同的句型

  1. The new manager has only been here a week. He talks as if he knows everything.
    新經理才來一個星期。他說話好像什麼都懂。
  2. My sister is wearing new shoes today. She walks as though her feet hurt.
    我姊姊今天穿新鞋。她走路好像腳在痛。
  3. Look at him in his cot. The baby is laughing as if someone is tickling him.
    你看嬰兒床裡的他。寶寶在笑，好像有人在搔他癢。
  4. I just want to sell the car and buy a boat. Don't look at me as if I'm crazy.
    我只是想賣掉車子買一艘船。別用好像我瘋了的眼神看我。
  5. The new boy puts his feet on the desks. He behaves as though he owns the place.
    那個新來的男生把腳放在桌上。他的舉止好像這裡是他的。
  6. We've been inside for three days. It's raining as if it will never stop.
    我們已經在室內待了三天。雨下得好像永遠不會停。
  7. I'm sitting right next to you. Why are you shouting as if I can't hear you?
    我就坐在你旁邊。你為什麼在吼，好像我聽不到一樣？
  8. Our neighbours packed the car at midnight. They left in a hurry, as if they were afraid of something.
    我們的鄰居半夜把東西裝上車。他們匆匆離開，好像在怕什麼。
  9. Every evening I saw him on the harbour wall. The old man stared at the sea, as though he was waiting for someone.
    每天傍晚我都看到他在港口的堤上。那個老人望著海，好像在等人。
  10. My daughter left for university last week. She packed all her bags, as if she would never come back.
    我女兒上星期去上大學。她打包了所有的行李，好像再也不會回來了。
  11. We arrived at three in the afternoon on a Sunday. The town was silent, as though everyone had gone away.
    我們星期天下午三點到。小鎮一片寂靜，好像大家都離開了。
  12. The teacher asked the hardest question in the test. He answered calmly, as if the question was easy.
    老師問了考試裡最難的問題。他冷靜地回答，好像這個問題很簡單。
