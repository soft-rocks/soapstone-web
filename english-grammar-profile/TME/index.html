In an accident, one is more likely to be badly injured when riding a bicycle.

發生意外時，騎腳踏車的人比較容易受重傷。

主⁠詞 + be + (much) more likely + to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 (+ than ...)

more likely比較可能。
less likely比較不可能。
much加強比較，「可能得多」。
than ...比較的對象。上下文清楚時可以省略。

用 much 加⁠強

例句

Young drivers are much more likely to have accidents.

年輕駕駛發生車禍的可能性高得多。

同⁠一⁠句⁠裡同⁠時用 more 和 less

例句

They are more likely to have better jobs and much less likely to cause problems.

他們比較可能找到好工作，也比較不會造成問題。

likely 的比⁠較⁠級用 more likely，不⁠加 ⁠-⁠er

In an accident, one is more likely to be badly injured when riding a bicycle.

They are more likelier to win.

They are more likely to win.

比⁠較⁠的對⁠象用 than

Girls are more likely to read that boys.

Girls are more likely to read than boys.

加⁠強比⁠較⁠級用 much，不⁠用 very

Old cars are very more likely to break down.

Old cars are much more likely to break down.

例句

① 主詞 + be + more likely + to + 原形動詞

  1. People who live alone are more likely to feel lonely at Christmas.
    獨居的人在聖誕節比較容易覺得寂寞。
  2. Students who sleep well are more likely to remember what they learn.
    睡得好的學生比較能記住學到的東西。
  3. People who exercise are more likely to live longer.
    有運動的人比較可能活得久。
  4. You're more likely to find a job in the city.
    你在城市比較可能找到工作。
  5. Children are more likely to catch colds in winter.
    小孩在冬天比較容易感冒。
  6. Shoppers are more likely to buy things near the checkout.
    購物的人比較可能買結帳區附近的東西。
  7. Fresh fruit is more likely to go bad in hot weather.
    新鮮水果在熱天比較容易壞。
  8. Tired drivers are more likely to make mistakes.
    疲勞的駕駛比較容易犯錯。

② 主詞 + be + less likely + to + 原形動詞

  1. Older people are less likely to use social media.
    老人家比較少用社群媒體。
  2. Well-trained dogs are less likely to bite.
    受過良好訓練的狗比較不會咬人。
  3. If you book early, you're less likely to pay a high price.
    如果你早點訂，就比較不會付高價。
  4. Small classes are less likely to be noisy.
    小班比較不會吵。
  5. People who eat breakfast are less likely to feel hungry before lunch.
    有吃早餐的人午餐前比較不會覺得餓。
  6. A good lock makes your bike less likely to be stolen.
    好的鎖讓你的腳踏車比較不容易被偷。（被動）
  7. Cars with winter tyres are less likely to slide on ice.
    裝冬季輪胎的車在冰上比較不會打滑。

③ 主詞 + be + much more / much less + likely + to + 原形動詞

  1. Young drivers are much more likely to have accidents at night.
    年輕駕駛晚上發生車禍的可能性高得多。
  2. Smokers are much more likely to get heart disease.
    抽菸的人得心臟病的可能性高得多。
  3. You're much less likely to get lost if you take a map.
    如果你帶地圖，迷路的可能性會低得多。
  4. Children who read at home are much more likely to enjoy school.
    在家讀書的孩子喜歡上學的可能性高得多。
  5. Houses near the river are much more likely to flood.
    河邊的房子淹水的可能性高得多。
  6. Buses are much less likely to be late on Sunday mornings.
    星期天早上公車誤點的可能性低得多。

④ more likely ... and less likely ...：同時比較

  1. They are more likely to have better jobs and much less likely to cause problems for other people.
    他們比較可能找到好工作，也比較不會給別人造成問題。
  2. Happy workers are more likely to stay and less likely to look for new jobs.
    快樂的員工比較會留下來，也比較不會找新工作。
  3. Well-rested students are more likely to concentrate and less likely to make mistakes.
    睡飽的學生比較能專心，也比較不會犯錯。
  4. Customers who feel welcome are more likely to return and less likely to complain.
    覺得受歡迎的顧客比較會再來，也比較不會抱怨。
  5. Dogs that walk every day are more likely to sleep well and less likely to bark.
    每天散步的狗比較睡得好，也比較不會叫。
  6. People who cook at home are more likely to eat well and less likely to spend too much.
    在家煮飯的人比較吃得健康，也比較不會花太多錢。

⑤ It is more / less likely that + 子句

  1. It is more likely that the meeting will be on Thursday.
    會議比較可能在星期四。
  2. It's less likely that we'll see snow in the city than in the mountains.
    在城市看到雪的可能性比在山上低。
  3. It is much more likely that he forgot than that he lied.
    他忘了的可能性比他說謊大得多。
  4. It's more likely that the keys are in your coat.
    鑰匙比較可能在你的外套裡。
