Come and stay with us this weekend!

這個週末來我們家住吧！

come and + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，邀⁠請對⁠方來做某⁠件⁠事

Come and stay with us this weekend!

春天來看我們。

邀⁠請加⁠入和提⁠供東⁠西也用祈⁠使⁠句

Come and stay with us this weekend!

邀請加入

例句

① Come and + 原形動詞

  1. Come and visit us in the spring. The garden will be beautiful.
    春天來看我們，花園會很漂亮。
  2. Our dog had five babies last night. Come and look at the puppies!
    我們的狗昨天晚上生了五隻。來看看小狗！
  3. You need a holiday after all that work. Come and stay at our cabin in the mountains.
    你工作那麼累，需要休假。來我們山上的小屋住吧。
  4. We're at the bar across the street. Come and have a drink with us.
    我們在對街的酒吧。來跟我們喝一杯。
  5. You're wet from the rain. Come and sit by the fire.
    你被雨淋濕了。來火爐邊坐。
  6. We have a big new TV. Come and watch the game at our place.
    我們有一台新的大電視。來我們家看比賽。

② Come over / Come with + 人

  1. I'm cooking my grandmother's lamb stew. Come over for dinner on Friday.
    我要煮我奶奶的燉羊肉。星期五來我家吃晚餐。
  2. There's a spare seat in the car. Come with us to the beach!
    車上還有一個空位。跟我們一起去海邊吧！
  3. We're home all weekend. Come over anytime.
    我們整個週末都在家，隨時過來玩。
  4. I'm turning thirty! Come to my birthday party next Saturday.
    我要三十歲了！下個星期六來參加我的生日派對。
  5. Come along! It'll be fun.
    一起來吧！會很好玩的。

③ Join / Stay

  1. The whole team is going to the Thai place. Join us for lunch tomorrow.
    整個團隊都要去那家泰國餐廳。明天跟我們一起吃午餐。
  2. We need one more player. Join our team on Thursday!
    我們還缺一個人，星期四加入我們這隊吧！
  3. Don't go yet, the kettle is on. Stay for a coffee.
    先別走，水在燒了。留下來喝杯咖啡吧。
  4. Stay the night. It's too late to drive.
    留下來過夜吧，太晚了不好開車。
  5. Stay with us until your flat is ready.
    你的公寓弄好之前就住我們家吧。

④ Have / Take / Help yourself（提供東西）

  1. Your cup is empty. Have some more tea.
    你的杯子空了。再喝點茶吧。
  2. Have a piece of cake. I made it this morning.
    吃塊蛋糕吧，我今天早上做的。
  3. Take a seat. I'll get you a drink.
    請坐，我去幫你拿喝的。
  4. I'll be in the garden with the kids. Help yourself to the snacks in the kitchen.
    我會跟孩子們在花園裡。廚房裡的零食自己拿吧。
  5. The baker gave out new cookies at the door. Take one. They're free.
    麵包師傅在門口發新口味的餅乾。拿一個吧，免費的。

⑤ please 和不同的句型

  1. We finally moved into the new flat. Please come to our housewarming party next Sunday.
    我們終於搬進新公寓了。下個星期天請來參加我們的喬遷派對。
  2. Do come and see us when you're in Taipei.
    你來台北的時候一定要來看我們。
  3. We're having a barbecue on Sunday. Bring your family too!
    我們星期天要烤肉。也帶你的家人來吧！
  4. Put your bag down and take off your coat. Make yourself at home.
    把包包放下，把外套脫了。就當自己家。
  5. Come in, come in! It's cold outside.
    進來，進來！外面很冷。
  6. There's lots left, and you look hungry. Have another slice of pizza.
    還剩很多，你看起來很餓。再吃一片披薩吧。
  7. We're having a dinner on 5 May. Please join us for our tenth anniversary.
    我們五月五日要辦晚宴。請來參加我們的十週年紀念。
  8. She makes the best dumplings in town. Come and try my mother's cooking.
    她做的餃子是鎮上最好吃的。來嚐嚐我媽媽的手藝。
  9. We're out all day tomorrow. Feel free to use the pool.
    我們明天整天都不在。游泳池請隨意使用。
