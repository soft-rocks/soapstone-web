I promise that I shall cover all the work and give it to you tomorrow.

我後天會去上課。

I / We + shall + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

shall、shall be + ⁠-⁠ing

shall會、將。說計畫和打算，比 will 正式。
shall be + -⁠ing說已經安排好、到時候正在做的事。

正⁠式⁠的信⁠件

I promise that I shall cover all the work and give it to you tomorrow.

例句

Dear Mr Hall, thank you for your patience. I shall send you the report on Monday.

Hall 先生您好，謝謝您的耐心。我星期一會把報告寄給您。

正式的信常用 I shall 說自己會做的事。

提⁠醒已⁠經安⁠排好⁠的事

例句

It's going to rain on Saturday. Remember that we shall meet inside the café.

星期六會下雨。記得我們會在咖啡館裡面碰面。

We shall 也用來提醒大家已經決定的安排。

shall 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I promise that I shall cover all the work and give it to you tomorrow.

Thank you for your order. I shall to send it tomorrow.

Thank you for your order. I shall send it tomorrow.

shall be 後⁠面⁠接 ⁠-⁠ing

I'm sorry I missed today's lecture. I shall be attend class again on Thursday.

I'm sorry I missed today's lecture. I shall be attending class again on Thursday.

shall 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I promise that I shall cover all the work and give it to you tomorrow.

The coach leaves Oxford at nine. We shall met outside the main entrance.

The coach leaves Oxford at nine. We shall meet outside the main entrance.

例句

① I shall + 原形動詞：寫給老師或上司的承諾

  1. I'm sorry I missed three classes. I promise that I shall cover all the work and give it to you tomorrow.
    很抱歉我缺了三堂課。我保證我會把所有的進度補上，明天交給您。
  2. Thank you for your comments on my draft, Professor Lee. I shall send you the final version by Friday.
    Lee 教授，謝謝您對我草稿的意見。我星期五前會把定稿寄給您。
  3. I know the deadline is very close. I shall do my best to finish the project on time.
    我知道截止日期很近了。我會盡力準時完成這個專案。
  4. Thank you for your advice. I shall read the chapters you suggested.
    謝謝您的建議。我會讀您建議的那幾章。
  5. Mr Green wasn't happy with the price, I know. I shall call him first thing tomorrow morning.
    我知道 Green 先生對價格不滿意。我明天一早就會打電話給他。
  6. If there are any problems, I shall let you know immediately.
    如果有任何問題，我會馬上通知您。

② I shall be + -ing：已經安排好的事

  1. My cousin's wedding is tomorrow, so I'm sorry I'll miss the English class. I shall be attending class the day after tomorrow.
    我表姊明天結婚，所以很抱歉我會缺英文課。我後天會來上課。
  2. Please send any questions to my assistant. I shall be travelling to Berlin on business next week.
    有任何問題請寄給我的助理。我下星期會去柏林出差。
  3. The office is being painted this week. I shall be working from home on Thursday and Friday.
    辦公室這個星期在油漆。星期四和星期五我會在家工作。
  4. Thank you for the invitation to speak. I shall be staying at the Grand Hotel during the conference.
    謝謝您邀請我演講。開會期間我會住在 Grand 飯店。
  5. Dear Ms Ford, I am writing to tell you that I shall be leaving the company at the end of March.
    Ford 女士您好，我寫信是要告訴您，我三月底會離開公司。
  6. Thank you for the agenda. I shall be giving a short talk at the start of the meeting.
    謝謝您寄來議程。會議一開始我會做一個簡短的報告。

③ We shall + 原形動詞：提醒大家的安排

  1. Remember that we shall start at nine sharp on Monday, not half past.
    記得我們星期一九點整準時開始，不是九點半。
  2. There's no time today, so we shall look at your proposals on Tuesday.
    今天沒有時間了，所以我們星期二會看你們的提案。
  3. Thank you for entering the photo competition. We shall announce the winners on Saturday evening.
    謝謝您參加攝影比賽。我們星期六晚上會宣布得獎者。
  4. Thank you for booking with our theatre. We shall send the tickets to you by post.
    謝謝您在我們劇院訂票。我們會把票郵寄給您。
  5. A message to all staff: we shall close the office early on Christmas Eve.
    給所有員工的通知：聖誕夜辦公室會提早關門。
  6. Thank you for applying to Bridge College. We shall review your application within two weeks.
    謝謝您申請 Bridge 學院。我們會在兩個星期內審核您的申請。

④ I shall + 原形動詞：說個人的決定

  1. I've thought about it carefully, and I shall accept the job in Madrid.
    我仔細想過了，我會接受馬德里的那份工作。
  2. I shall never forget the kindness of the people in that village.
    我永遠不會忘記那個村子裡的人的好意。
  3. When I retire, I shall spend more time in my garden.
    我退休以後，會花更多時間在我的花園裡。
  4. I didn't see my grandparents much last year. I shall try to visit them every month this year.
    我去年不常去看爺爺奶奶。今年我會盡量每個月去看他們。
  5. I spent all my money in Greece. After this holiday, I shall start saving for a car.
    我在希臘把錢都花光了。這次假期以後，我會開始存錢買車。
  6. I've always wanted to be a doctor. I shall study medicine if I get good enough grades.
    我一直想當醫生。如果我的成績夠好，我會念醫學。

⑤ We shall be + -ing：團體和公司的計畫

  1. Our first shop has been a great success. We shall be opening a second one in the city centre next spring.
    我們的第一家店很成功。明年春天我們會在市中心開第二家。
  2. Dear members, the roof needs work. We shall be closing the pool for repairs during August.
    親愛的會員，屋頂需要整修。八月我們會關閉游泳池進行維修。
  3. The choir has a busy summer ahead. We shall be visiting three countries on our tour.
    合唱團今年夏天會很忙。我們的巡迴會去三個國家。
  4. Our team has grown to forty people. We shall be moving to a bigger office in the autumn.
    我們的團隊已經增加到四十人。秋天我們會搬到比較大的辦公室。
  5. Because of the rain, we shall be holding this year's sports day indoors.
    因為下雨，今年的運動會我們會在室內舉行。
  6. The school trip to Paris has been cancelled. We shall be contacting all the parents by email this week.
    去巴黎的校外教學取消了。這個星期我們會用電子郵件聯絡所有的家長。
  7. Welcome to the Lake Hotel. We shall be serving dinner from seven o'clock.
    歡迎來到 Lake 飯店。我們七點開始供應晚餐。
