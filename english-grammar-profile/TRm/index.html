例句

① of hers

  1. I've been an admirer of hers since her first film.
    從她的第一部電影起，我就很欣賞她。
  2. Some colleagues of hers organized a surprise party.
    她的幾個同事辦了一場驚喜派對。
  3. A neighbor of hers found the lost dog.
    她的一個鄰居找到了那隻走失的狗。
  4. That's a favorite song of hers.
    那是她最愛的歌之一。
  5. A student of hers won the national competition.
    她的一個學生贏得了全國比賽。
  6. He's a distant relative of hers.
    他是她的遠親。

② of his

  1. He lent me a jacket of his for the interview.
    他借我他的一件夾克去面試。
  2. A colleague of his drove him to the airport.
    他的一個同事開車送他去機場。
  3. Some classmates of his are helping him paint the flat.
    他的幾個同學在幫他粉刷公寓。
  4. That's a painting of his from when he was young.
    那是他年輕時畫的一幅畫。
  5. A former student of his is now a famous scientist.
    他以前的一個學生現在是有名的科學家。
  6. I'm reading a novel of his that I found in the library.
    我在讀我在圖書館找到的他的一本小說。

③ of theirs

  1. A friend of theirs lent them a cabin in the mountains.
    他們的一個朋友借他們一間山上的小木屋。
  2. Some neighbors of theirs helped them move.
    他們的幾個鄰居幫他們搬家。
  3. A cousin of theirs is getting married in June.
    他們的一個表親六月要結婚。
  4. The bikes in the hall are old bikes of theirs.
    走廊上的腳踏車是他們以前的舊腳踏車。
  5. A son of theirs works in Japan.
    他們的一個兒子在日本工作。
  6. A favorite restaurant of theirs is closing next month.
    他們最愛的一家餐廳下個月要關門了。

④ fan / admirer / friend

  1. My daughter has been a fan of theirs since the band started.
    樂團一成立，我女兒就是他們的粉絲了。
  2. He was a close friend of hers at university.
    他大學時是她的好朋友。
  3. She was never a great admirer of his.
    她從來就不怎麼欣賞他。
  4. Is she a colleague of theirs?
    她是他們的同事嗎？
  5. My uncle was a teammate of his in the 1990s.
    我叔叔是他九〇年代的隊友。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. I met the sister of a colleague of his at the party.
    我在派對上遇到他一個同事的姊姊。
  2. A habit of his is reading the newspaper in the bath.
    他有一個習慣，就是在浴缸裡看報紙。
  3. That dog of theirs barks all night.
    他們那隻狗整晚都在叫。
  4. A cousin of hers lives just around the corner.
    她的一個表親就住在轉角那邊。
  5. A business partner of his lent him the money.
    他的一個生意夥伴借錢給他。
  6. Some old photos of theirs were in the attic.
    他們的一些舊照片在閣樓裡。
  7. I met a former boss of hers at the conference.
    我在研討會上遇到了她以前的一個老闆。
