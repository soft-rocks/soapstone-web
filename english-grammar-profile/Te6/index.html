Our debates do not draw as large a number of students as the guest appearances.

我們的辯論吸引的學生沒有來賓演講那麼多。

as + 形⁠容⁠詞 + a + 名⁠詞 + as，形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在 a 前⁠面

Our debates do not draw as large a number of students as the guest appearances.

這場比賽沒有上禮拜那場好看。

so + 形⁠容⁠詞 + a + 名⁠詞 + that 說⁠明結⁠果

Our debates do not draw as large a number of students as the guest appearances.

那天熱到路面都融化了。

too 也一⁠樣，形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在 a 前⁠面

Our debates do not draw as large a number of students as the guest appearances.

He is as fast a runner as his father.

Our debates do not draw as large a number of students as the guest appearances.

例句

① as + 形容詞 + a + 名詞 + as（肯定句）

  1. She is as good a player as her brother.
    她跟她哥哥一樣是個好球員。
  2. This was as cold a winter as last year's.
    這個冬天跟去年冬天一樣冷。
  3. He is as kind a man as you will ever meet.
    他是你這輩子會遇到最好心的人。
  4. This is as good a time as any to start.
    現在就是開始的好時機。
  5. Paris is as expensive a city as London.
    巴黎跟倫敦一樣是物價很高的城市。
  6. It was as long a meeting as last month's.
    這次會議跟上個月的一樣長。

② not as + 形容詞 + a + 名詞 + as（否定句）

  1. The evening class doesn't attract as large a group as the morning one.
    晚上的課吸引的人沒有早上的多。
  2. It wasn't as good a game as last week's.
    這場比賽沒有上禮拜那場好看。
  3. He isn't as fast a runner as he used to be.
    他跑得沒有以前那麼快了。
  4. The new hotel isn't as big a building as the old one.
    新飯店的建築沒有舊的那麼大。
  5. It wasn't as difficult a test as I expected.
    那次考試沒有我想的那麼難。
  6. This isn't as serious a problem as it looks.
    這個問題沒有看起來那麼嚴重。

③ so + 形容詞 + a + 名詞 + that

  1. It was so hot a day that the roads melted.
    那天熱到路面都融化了。
  2. He had so strong a shock that he couldn't speak.
    他受到的驚嚇大到說不出話來。
  3. It was so good a film that I watched it twice.
    那部電影好看到我看了兩次。
  4. She gave so clear an answer that nobody asked again.
    她回答得清楚到沒有人再問。
  5. It was so long a journey that we slept on the train.
    那趟旅程長到我們在火車上睡著了。
  6. He is so honest a man that everyone trusts him.
    他為人誠實到每個人都信任他。

④ so + 形容詞 + a + 名詞（沒有 that 子句）

  1. I've never seen so beautiful a garden.
    我從來沒看過這麼漂亮的花園。
  2. How could so small a mistake cause so much trouble?
    這麼小的錯誤怎麼會造成這麼多麻煩？
  3. Few people have so happy a childhood.
    很少人有這麼快樂的童年。
  4. It's rare to find so quiet a beach in summer.
    夏天很難找到這麼安靜的海灘。

⑤ too + 形容詞 + a + 名詞

  1. It's too big a risk to take.
    這個風險太大了，不能冒。
  2. That's too long a walk for the kids.
    那段路對孩子們來說太遠了。
  3. It was too good a chance to miss.
    那個機會太好了，不能錯過。
  4. This is too important a decision to make alone.
    這個決定太重要了，不能一個人做。
  5. Ten pounds is too high a price for a sandwich.
    一個三明治十英鎊太貴了。
  6. It's too nice a day to stay inside.
    天氣這麼好，不該待在室內。
  7. That was too difficult a question for a beginner.
    那個問題對初學者來說太難了。
  8. He's too good a friend to lose over money.
    他是很好的朋友，不值得為了錢失去他。
