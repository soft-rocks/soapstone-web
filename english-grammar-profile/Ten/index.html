例句

① 書面資料 + says (that)

  1. The menu says that all the meat is local.
    菜單上說所有的肉都是本地的。
  2. The email says the train leaves at ten past nine.
    信上說火車九點十分開。
  3. The forecast says it's going to snow tonight.
    天氣預報說今晚會下雪。
  4. My ticket says I'm in seat 14B.
    我的票上寫說我是 14B 的座位。
  5. The report says that most students walk to school.
    報告上說大部分學生走路上學。

② It says

  1. It says here that breakfast is included.
    這裡寫說含早餐。
  2. It says on the box that it's suitable for children over three.
    盒子上寫說適合三歲以上的孩子。
  3. I read the article, and it says the factory is closing.
    我看了那篇報導，上面說那家工廠要關了。
  4. It says on the website that the pool is open until nine.
    網站上寫說游泳池開到九點。
  5. Look at the sign. It says you can't take photos inside.
    你看那個牌子，上面寫著裡面不能拍照。

③ 書面資料 + tells + 人

  1. Your advertisement tells us that the trip includes two nights in a hotel.
    你們的廣告上寫說，這趟旅行包含兩晚的飯店住宿。
  2. The map tells you that the toilets are next to the café.
    地圖上標說廁所在咖啡館旁邊。
  3. This leaflet tells you that the museum is free for students.
    這份傳單上寫說博物館對學生免費。
  4. The note on the door tells visitors that the office is upstairs.
    門上的紙條告訴訪客辦公室在樓上。
  5. The letter tells me that my passport is ready.
    信上通知我護照辦好了。

④ 複數的書面資料 + say

  1. The rules say that you can't bring food into the library.
    規定上說不能帶食物進圖書館。
  2. The instructions say you should wait ten minutes.
    說明書上說要等十分鐘。
  3. The signs say the beach is closed for cleaning.
    告示上說海灘因為清潔暫時關閉。
  4. The reviews say the hotel is clean but noisy.
    評論上說那家飯店很乾淨，但是很吵。
  5. Her notes say that the meeting starts at two.
    她的筆記上寫說會議兩點開始。

⑤ explains / warns / claims

  1. The guide explains how to get to the castle on foot.
    導覽手冊上說明怎麼走路去城堡。
  2. The article explains why prices are going up.
    那篇文章解釋了物價為什麼一直漲。
  3. The sign warns that the water is deep.
    告示上警告說水很深。
  4. The label warns that the medicine may make you sleepy.
    標籤上警告說這個藥可能會讓人想睡。
  5. The company claims that its phones last for ten years.
    那家公司宣稱他們的手機可以用十年。
  6. The advert claims you can learn French in a month.
    那則廣告宣稱一個月就能學會法文。

⑥ 省略 that

  1. The timetable says the last bus is at midnight.
    時刻表上說末班公車是午夜。
  2. Your letter says you want to change your booking.
    你的信上說你想更改訂位。
  3. The app says we're five minutes away.
    App 上說我們還有五分鐘就到了。
  4. The recipe says you need three eggs.
    食譜上說需要三顆蛋。
  5. This book says the castle was built in 1200.
    這本書上說那座城堡是在 1200 年蓋的。
