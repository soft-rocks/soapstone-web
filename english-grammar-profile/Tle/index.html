Sometimes I go to the beach.

有時候我會去海邊。

副⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面照⁠樣接主⁠詞和動⁠詞

Sometimes I go to the beach.

有時候我會去海邊。

常放⁠在句⁠首⁠的副⁠詞

Sometimes I go to the beach.

sometimes有時候
maybe也許，說不定
today、yesterday、tomorrow說明哪一天
now現在
then然後，接著說下一件事

直⁠述⁠句⁠的 always 和 never 不⁠放⁠在句⁠首

我都比我爸媽早起。

表⁠示也⁠許⁠的 maybe 寫⁠成一⁠個字

May be we can go swimming on Sunday.

Maybe we can go swimming on Sunday.

always 放⁠在主⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

Always I take the same bus.

I always take the same bus.

句⁠首⁠的副⁠詞後⁠面，主⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

Sometimes I go to the beach.

Sometimes visit we our cousins in Tainan.

Sometimes we visit our cousins in Tainan.

句⁠首⁠的副⁠詞後⁠面要⁠有主⁠詞

Tomorrow will be at home all day.

Tomorrow I will be at home all day.

例句

① sometimes 放在句首

  1. My brother is very good at numbers. Sometimes he helps me with my maths homework.
    我哥哥數學很好。有時候他會教我數學作業。
  2. Our English lessons are fun. Sometimes our teacher plays songs in class.
    我們的英文課很有趣。有時候我們老師會在課堂上放歌。
  3. I always leave home early. Sometimes the bus doesn't come at all.
    我都很早出門。有時候公車根本不來。
  4. I like singing, but sometimes I'm too shy.
    我喜歡唱歌，可是有時候我太害羞了。
  5. Take the stairs if you're in a hurry. Sometimes, the lift in our building stops working.
    趕時間的話走樓梯吧。有時候我們大樓的電梯會故障。
  6. Bring a good raincoat. Sometimes it rains for a whole week here.
    帶一件好的雨衣。這裡有時候會連下一整個禮拜的雨。

② maybe 放在句首

  1. The taxi is too expensive. Maybe Dad can drive us to the station.
    計程車太貴了。也許爸爸可以載我們去車站。
  2. Mum's phone is off. Maybe she's still at the office.
    媽媽的手機關機了。她說不定還在辦公室。
  3. Your phone isn't on the table. Maybe it's in your other bag.
    你的手機不在桌上。說不定在你另一個包包裡。
  4. I can't meet you for dinner. Maybe I'll see you at the party.
    我不能跟你吃晚餐。也許我會在派對上見到你。
  5. Nobody is inside yet. Maybe the shop opens at ten.
    裡面還沒有人。那家店說不定十點才開。
  6. Our guests are very late. Maybe they don't know the way to our new house.
    我們的客人遲到好久了。也許他們不知道怎麼去我們的新家。

③ today、yesterday、tomorrow 放在句首

  1. Today I'm wearing my new shoes. They're a bit tight.
    今天我穿了新鞋子。有點緊。
  2. I'm still full. Yesterday we had a big lunch at my grandma's house.
    我現在還很飽。昨天我們在我奶奶家吃了一頓豐盛的午餐。
  3. Can you help me clean the spare room? Tomorrow my cousin is coming to stay with us.
    你可以幫我打掃客房嗎？明天我表姊要來我們家住。
  4. Bring the kids. Today, the museum is free for children.
    帶孩子們去吧。今天博物館兒童免費。
  5. I'm so sad. Yesterday I lost the pen my dad gave me.
    我好難過。昨天我把我爸給我的筆弄丟了。
  6. I can't go out tonight. Tomorrow there's a test in English class.
    我今晚不能出去。明天英文課有考試。

④ now 放在句首

  1. I lived in a big house as a child. Now I live in a small flat near the river.
    我小時候住在大房子。現在我住在河邊的一間小公寓。
  2. It's been forty minutes. Now you can take the cake out of the oven.
    已經四十分鐘了。現在你可以把蛋糕從烤箱拿出來了。
  3. I chose the last one. Now it's your turn to choose a game.
    上一個是我選的。現在輪到你選遊戲了。
  4. The bell has gone. Now, please sit down and listen.
    鈴響了。現在請坐下來聽好。
  5. My sister studied maths at university. Now she works in a bank.
    我姊姊大學念數學。現在她在銀行上班。

⑤ then 放在句首

  1. My mornings are simple. I have a shower. Then I make breakfast.
    我的早上很簡單。我先洗澡，然後做早餐。
  2. We visited the castle. Then we had lunch in a café.
    我們參觀了城堡，然後去咖啡店吃午餐。
  3. Your new printer is easy to set up. Open the box. Then you'll find the instructions on top.
    你的新印表機很容易裝。把盒子打開，然後你會看到說明書在最上面。
  4. Amy finished her homework. Then she called her friend.
    Amy 寫完作業，接著打電話給朋友。
  5. The post office is close. Turn left at the bank. Then you'll see it on your right.
    郵局很近。在銀行那裡左轉，然後你就會看到它在你右邊。

⑥ 句首的副詞後面接否定句

  1. Bring your own coffee. Today the café isn't open because of the holiday.
    自己帶咖啡吧。今天放假，咖啡店沒開。
  2. I had an eye operation last month. Now I don't need my glasses for reading.
    我上個月動了眼睛手術。現在我看書不用戴眼鏡了。
  3. The roads were too dangerous. Yesterday the children didn't go to school because of the snow.
    路上太危險了。昨天孩子們因為下雪沒去上學。
