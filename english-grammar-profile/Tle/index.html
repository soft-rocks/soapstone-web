Sometimes I go to the beach.

有時候我會去海邊。

副⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面照⁠樣接主⁠詞和動⁠詞

有時候我會去海邊。

常放⁠在句⁠首⁠的副⁠詞

Sometimes I go to the beach.

sometimes有時候
maybe也許，說不定
today、yesterday、tomorrow說明哪一天
now現在
then然後，接著說下一件事

always 和 never 不⁠放⁠在句⁠首

我都七點起床。

表⁠示也⁠許⁠的 maybe 寫⁠成一⁠個字

Sometimes I go to the beach.

May be we can go swimming on Sunday.

Maybe we can go swimming on Sunday.

always 放⁠在主⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

Always I get up at seven.

I always get up at seven.

句⁠首⁠的副⁠詞後⁠面，主⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

Sometimes I go to the beach.

Sometimes go I to the park.

Sometimes I go to the park.

句⁠首⁠的副⁠詞後⁠面要⁠有主⁠詞

Tomorrow will be at home all day.

Tomorrow I will be at home all day.

例句

① sometimes 放在句首

  1. Sometimes my brother helps me with my maths homework.
    有時候我哥哥會教我數學作業。
  2. Sometimes our teacher plays songs in class.
    有時候我們老師會在課堂上放歌。
  3. Sometimes the bus doesn't come at all.
    有時候公車根本不來。
  4. I like singing, but sometimes I'm too shy.
    我喜歡唱歌，可是有時候我太害羞了。
  5. Sometimes, the lift in our building stops working.
    有時候我們大樓的電梯會故障。
  6. Sometimes it rains for a whole week here.
    這裡有時候會連下一整個禮拜的雨。

② maybe 放在句首

  1. Maybe Dad can drive us to the station.
    也許爸爸可以載我們去車站。
  2. Maybe she's still at the office.
    她說不定還在辦公室。
  3. Maybe your phone is in your other bag.
    你的手機說不定在你另一個包包裡。
  4. Maybe, I'll see you at the party.
    也許我會在派對上見到你。
  5. Maybe the shop opens at ten.
    那家店說不定十點才開。
  6. Maybe they don't know the way to our new house.
    也許他們不知道怎麼去我們的新家。

③ today、yesterday、tomorrow 放在句首

  1. Today I'm wearing my new shoes.
    今天我穿了新鞋子。
  2. Yesterday we went to my grandma's house.
    昨天我們去了我奶奶家。
  3. Tomorrow my cousin is coming to stay with us.
    明天我表姊要來我們家住。
  4. Today, the museum is free for children.
    今天博物館兒童免費。
  5. Yesterday I lost my favourite pen.
    昨天我把我最喜歡的筆弄丟了。
  6. Tomorrow there's a test in English class.
    明天英文課有考試。

④ now 放在句首

  1. Now I live in a small flat near the river.
    現在我住在河邊的一間小公寓。
  2. Now you can take the cake out of the oven.
    現在你可以把蛋糕從烤箱拿出來了。
  3. Now it's your turn to choose a game.
    現在輪到你選遊戲了。
  4. Now, please sit down and listen.
    現在請坐下來聽好。
  5. Now my sister works in a bank.
    我姊姊現在在銀行上班。

⑤ then 放在句首

  1. I have a shower. Then I make breakfast.
    我先洗澡，然後做早餐。
  2. We visited the castle. Then we had lunch in a café.
    我們參觀了城堡，然後去咖啡店吃午餐。
  3. Open the box. Then take out the instructions.
    把盒子打開，然後把說明書拿出來。
  4. She finished her homework. Then she called her friend.
    她寫完作業，接著打電話給朋友。
  5. Turn left at the bank. Then walk straight on for two minutes.
    在銀行那裡左轉，然後直走兩分鐘。

⑥ 句首的副詞後面接否定句

  1. Today the café isn't open because of the holiday.
    今天放假，咖啡店沒開。
  2. Now I don't need my glasses for reading.
    現在我看書不用戴眼鏡了。
  3. Yesterday the children didn't go to school because of the snow.
    昨天孩子們因為下雪沒去上學。
