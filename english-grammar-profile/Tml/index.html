You've just had a big lunch. You ought not to be hungry.

你應該不會餓才對。

主⁠詞 + ought to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

ought to應該會。根據情況推論。
ought not to應該不會。
原形動詞推論的事。

義⁠務和可⁠能⁠性

義務

It's your grandmother's birthday. You ought to call her.

今天是你奶奶的生日。你應該打給她。
可能性

The train left the last station five minutes ago. It ought to be here soon.

火車五分鐘前從上一站開出。它應該很快就會到。

ought to 可以說義務，也可以說可能性。

說產⁠品或計⁠畫應⁠有⁠的效⁠果

例句

The new software does the monthly figures by itself. It ought to save us time.

新軟體會自己算每個月的數字。它應該能幫我們節省時間。

報告常用 ought to 說一個東西應該能帶來的效果。

not 放⁠在 ought 後⁠面

You've just had a big lunch. You ought not to be hungry.

You've just had two sandwiches. You not ought to be hungry.

You've just had two sandwiches. You ought not to be hungry.

ought 後⁠面要接 to

You've just had a big lunch. You ought not to be hungry.

It's ten past nine. The bus ought arrive soon.

It's ten past nine. The bus ought to arrive soon.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You've just had a big lunch. You ought not to be hungry.

We've done this test before. It ought to being easy.

We've done this test before. It ought to be easy.

例句

① 主詞 + ought not to + 原形動詞：應該不會

  1. You had a huge plate of pasta an hour ago. You ought not to be hungry.
    你一個小時前吃了一大盤義大利麵。你應該不會餓才對。
  2. The test ought not to be difficult. We've practised a lot.
    考試應該不會很難。我們練習很多次了。
  3. Let's drive to the coast early. The roads ought not to be busy on a Sunday morning.
    我們早點開車去海邊吧。星期天早上路上應該不會很塞。
  4. It ought not to rain today. The sky is clear.
    今天應該不會下雨。天空很晴朗。
  5. It's only a small crack in the screen. The repair ought not to cost much.
    螢幕只有一道小裂痕。修理應該不會花很多錢。
  6. She had a big feed at ten. The baby ought not to wake up before six.
    她十點喝了很多奶。寶寶六點前應該不會醒。

② 主詞 + ought to + 原形動詞：產品和計畫應有的效果

  1. Accounting software records daily business. Besides, it ought to provide useful information for making decisions.
    會計軟體記錄日常的業務。此外，它應該能提供做決定時有用的資訊。
  2. We've bought a new packing machine for the factory. It ought to make the work much faster.
    我們幫工廠買了一台新的包裝機。它應該能讓工作快很多。
  3. The manager wants to close two small offices. These changes ought to save the company money.
    經理想關掉兩間小辦公室。這些改變應該能幫公司省錢。
  4. The buses will now come every eight minutes. The new timetable ought to reduce waiting times.
    公車以後每八分鐘一班。新的時刻表應該能減少等待的時間。
  5. Take two of these tablets with water. This medicine ought to help with the pain.
    這種藥一次吃兩顆，配水吃。它應該能減輕疼痛。
  6. New staff will spend a week with an experienced colleague. The training ought to give them more confidence.
    新員工會跟資深同事一起工作一個星期。這次訓練應該能讓他們更有信心。

③ 主詞 + ought to be + 形容詞或地方：根據情況推論

  1. He left an hour ago, so he ought to be home by now.
    他一個小時前走的，所以他現在應該到家了。
  2. The film starts at eight, so it ought to be finished by ten.
    電影八點開始，所以十點前應該演完了。
  3. She's trained hard, so the race ought to be easy for her.
    她訓練得很努力，所以這場比賽對她來說應該很輕鬆。
  4. The forecast says sunshine all weekend. The weather ought to be good for the picnic.
    天氣預報說整個週末都是晴天。野餐那天天氣應該會很好。
  5. With all this sun, the tomatoes ought to be ready soon.
    陽光這麼充足，番茄應該很快就可以採了。
  6. It's a new car, so it ought to be reliable.
    這是一台新車，所以應該很可靠。

④ There ought to be + 名詞：推論應該有

  1. It's a big hotel, so there ought to be a restaurant.
    那是一家大飯店，所以應該有餐廳。
  2. I ordered twelve pizzas, so there ought to be plenty left over for tomorrow.
    我訂了十二個披薩，所以明天應該還剩很多。
  3. Don't worry about missing this one. There ought to be a bus every ten minutes in the city centre.
    別擔心錯過這班。市中心應該每十分鐘就有一班公車。
  4. The concert has only just started. There ought to be some seats left at the back.
    音樂會才剛開始。後面應該還有一些位子。
  5. You need cash for the taxi? There ought to be a cash machine at the station.
    你需要現金搭計程車？車站應該有提款機。
  6. You've sent all the documents, and your passport is new. There ought to be no problem with your visa.
    你所有的文件都寄了，護照也是新的。你的簽證應該不會有問題。

⑤ 主詞 + ought to + 原形動詞：預測結果

  1. If we leave now, we ought to arrive by lunchtime.
    如果我們現在出發，應該午餐時間前就會到。
  2. With her experience, she ought to get the job.
    以她的經驗，她應該能拿到那份工作。
  3. I paid for express delivery, so the new printer ought to come before Friday.
    我付了快遞費，所以新的印表機星期五前應該會到。
  4. Their best player is injured. Our team ought to win easily this time.
    他們最好的球員受傷了。我們隊這次應該能輕鬆贏。
  5. If you follow the recipe, the cake ought to taste great.
    如果你照著食譜做，蛋糕應該會很好吃。
  6. Bruges is a small city. Two days ought to be enough to see it.
    布魯日是個小城市。兩天應該夠看完。
  7. It's filled with goose feathers. This coat ought to keep you warm all winter.
    它裡面是鵝毛。這件外套應該能讓你整個冬天都很暖和。
