例句

① I thought you might + 原形動詞

  1. Here's the book about Spain. I thought you might enjoy it before your trip.
    這是那本關於西班牙的書。我想你去旅行前可能會喜歡看。
  2. We're going to the beach on Saturday. I thought you might want to come with us.
    我們星期六要去海邊。我想你可能會想跟我們一起去。
  3. You've been working all afternoon. I thought you might be hungry, so I made some sandwiches.
    你整個下午都在工作。我想你可能餓了，所以做了一些三明治。
  4. You're studying for your driving test. I saw this article and thought you might find it useful.
    你在準備駕照考試。我看到這篇文章，想說你可能用得到。
  5. You're moving house today. I thought you might need some help with the boxes.
    你今天搬家。我想你搬那些箱子可能需要幫忙。

② I thought it would be + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. There are a lot of stories in the office. I thought it would be a good idea to let you know what is really going on.
    辦公室裡有很多傳言。我想讓你知道真正的情況會比較好。
  2. We've only spoken by email. I thought it would be useful to meet before the conference.
    我們只用電子郵件聯絡過。我想開會前先見個面會有幫助。
  3. The family next door moved in last week. I thought it would be nice to invite them for dinner.
    隔壁那家人上禮拜搬來。我想請他們來吃晚餐應該不錯。
  4. This is a difficult problem. I thought it would be better to discuss it in person.
    這是個棘手的問題。我想這件事當面談比較好。
  5. I'm leaving the company in June. I thought it would be fair to tell you before anyone else.
    我六月要離開公司。我想先告訴你才公平。

③ I thought we could + 原形動詞

  1. It's been ages since we talked. I thought we could have lunch together on Friday.
    我們好久沒聊了。我想我們星期五可以一起吃午餐。
  2. Parking in the city is terrible. I thought we could take the train instead of driving.
    城裡很難停車。我想我們可以搭火車，不要開車。
  3. We have a lot to talk about today. I thought we could start with the budget and then move on to the schedule.
    我們今天有很多事要談。我想我們可以先談預算，再談時程。
  4. We're both going to the airport. I thought we could share the cost of the taxi.
    我們兩個都要去機場。我想我們可以一起分攤計程車錢。
  5. It's a lovely evening. I thought we could go for a walk after dinner.
    今天晚上天氣很好。我想我們晚餐後可以去散個步。

④ I thought I'd + 原形動詞

  1. Hi, Grandma, it's me. I thought I'd call to see how you are.
    嗨，奶奶，是我。我想打個電話看看你好不好。
  2. Hi, Ben. I thought I'd let you know that the meeting has been moved to Thursday.
    嗨，班。我想跟你說一聲，會議改到星期四了。
  3. I was in the area, so I thought I'd drop in.
    我剛好在附近，就想說過來看看。
  4. I need to go to the shops. I thought I'd ask before I borrow your bike.
    我得去商店一趟。我想先問一下再借你的腳踏車。
  5. Our meeting is on Monday. I thought I'd send you the report early in case you had questions.
    我們星期一要開會。我想早點把報告寄給你，萬一你有問題可以先問。

⑤ 書信和電子郵件

  1. Dear Tom, the reason I'm writing is that I thought you might be interested in joining our club.
    親愛的湯姆，我寫信的原因是，我想你可能會有興趣加入我們的社團。
  2. Dear Members, we thought you would like to know about the new opening hours.
    各位會員好，我們想各位會想知道新的開放時間。
  3. Dear Students, I thought I should mention that the deadline is next Monday.
    各位同學好，我想我應該提一下，截止日是下星期一。
  4. Dear Mrs Green, as you are new to the area, we thought you might appreciate a map of the town.
    格林太太您好，您剛搬來這一帶，我們想您可能會需要一張鎮上的地圖。
  5. Dear Sir, your office has not answered my calls. I thought it best to write to you directly.
    敬啟者，貴辦公室沒有回我的電話。我想直接寫信給您最好。

⑥ 回應對方，委婉地提出不同的意見

  1. You want to ask John to the party? I thought perhaps we could ask Mary first.
    你想邀約翰來派對？我想我們或許可以先問問瑪莉。
  2. The red dress is nice. I thought the blue one might look better on you.
    紅色洋裝很好看。我想藍色那件你穿起來可能比較好看。
  3. You said we should leave at five. I thought we might leave a bit earlier to avoid the traffic.
    你說我們五點出發。我想我們可以早一點出發，避開塞車。
  4. You want to buy the tickets at the station? I thought it might be cheaper to book online.
    你想在車站買票？我想上網訂可能比較便宜。
  5. You booked the big meeting room? I thought maybe a smaller room would be enough.
    你訂了大會議室？我想也許小一點的房間就夠了。
  6. You said you had a headache. I thought you'd prefer a quiet table, so I booked one in the corner.
    你說你頭痛。我想你會比較喜歡安靜的位子，所以訂了角落那一桌。
