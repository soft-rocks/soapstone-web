At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

說到底，一切都由你決定。

固⁠定說⁠法放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面加逗⁠號

說到底，一切都由你決定。

固⁠定說⁠法先說⁠出後⁠面那⁠句⁠話⁠的作⁠用

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

總結

All things considered, I'd recommend the third proposal.

全面考量之後，我會推薦第三個提案。
真正的重點

When it comes down to it, everyone likes a surprise.

說穿了，每個人都喜歡驚喜。
讓步

Having said that, the hotel was very clean.

話雖如此，那家飯店很乾淨。
請對方想一想

When you think about it, the rent here is very high.

你想想看，這裡的房租很高。

固⁠定說⁠法後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接問⁠句

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

說穿了，誰不喜歡放假？

固⁠定說⁠法⁠的字不⁠能換

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

At the finish of the day, it's your decision.

At the end of the day, it's your decision.

固⁠定說⁠法⁠的字不⁠能少

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

At end of the day, money isn't everything.

At the end of the day, money isn't everything.

主⁠要⁠子⁠句是直⁠述⁠句，主⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

All in all, was the week a success.

All in all, the week was a success.

All in all 用⁠來總⁠結，說第一⁠點用 To begin with

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

All in all, the first thing we need is a map.

To begin with, the first thing we need is a map.

放⁠在句⁠尾⁠的 at the end of the day 是時⁠間

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

句首
說到底

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

說到底，一切都由你決定。
句尾
一天結束時

We got home at the end of the day.

我們在一天結束時回到家。

In the end 表⁠示一⁠段時⁠間之⁠後⁠的結⁠果

At the end of the day, everything is up to you.

最後我們搭了計程車。

At the end of the day 表示說話的人的結論。

例句

① 總結

  1. All in all, the activity week was a great experience.
    整體來說，這次的活動週是很棒的經驗。
  2. All things considered, the trip went quite well.
    全面考量之後，這趟旅行還算順利。
  3. On the whole, the students were happy with the course.
    大致上，學生對這門課很滿意。
  4. In short, we need more money and more time.
    總之，我們需要更多錢和更多時間。
  5. All in all, it was a day to remember.
    整體來說，那是值得紀念的一天。
  6. All things considered, I think we did the right thing.
    全面考量之後，我覺得我們做得對。

② 說出真正的重點

  1. At the end of the day, we all want the same thing.
    說到底，我們要的都一樣。
  2. At the end of the day, the customer is always right.
    說到底，顧客永遠是對的。
  3. When it comes down to it, family is what matters most.
    說穿了，家人才是最重要的。
  4. When all is said and done, he's still my brother.
    不管怎麼說，他還是我哥哥。
  5. At the end of the day, you have to make your own choices.
    說到底，你得自己做決定。
  6. When it comes down to it, nobody wants to pay more tax.
    說穿了，沒有人想多繳稅。

③ 讓步

  1. Having said that, the food was excellent.
    話雖如此，食物非常好吃。
  2. Even so, I'm glad we went.
    即使如此，我還是很高興我們去了。
  3. That said, the price is a bit high for students.
    話是這麼說，可是這個價錢對學生來說有點高。
  4. Having said that, I wouldn't go back there.
    話雖如此，我不會再去那裡了。
  5. Even so, we should thank them for their help.
    即使如此，我們還是應該謝謝他們的幫忙。

④ 請對方想一想

  1. When you think about it, a car costs a lot to run.
    你想想看，養一輛車要花很多錢。
  2. Come to think of it, I haven't seen Mark all week.
    這麼說來，我整個星期都沒看到 Mark。
  3. When you think about it, the old system was simpler.
    你想想看，舊的制度比較簡單。
  4. Come to think of it, she did look a bit tired.
    這麼說來，她看起來確實有點累。

⑤ 後面接問句

  1. At the end of the day, what else could we do?
    說到底，我們還能怎麼辦？
  2. When it comes down to it, who really needs a second car?
    說穿了，誰真的需要第二輛車？
  3. When you think about it, can we really afford a flat in London?
    你想想看，我們真的買得起倫敦的公寓嗎？
  4. All things considered, isn't this the best option?
    全面考量之後，這不就是最好的選擇嗎？

⑥ 其他固定說法

  1. To be honest, I didn't enjoy the film.
    老實說，我不喜歡那部電影。
  2. As a matter of fact, I've already booked the tickets.
    事實上，我已經訂好票了。
  3. By the way, your package arrived this morning.
    對了，你的包裹今天早上到了。
  4. In other words, the plan has failed.
    換句話說，這個計畫失敗了。
  5. Believe it or not, the shop has been open for a hundred years.
    信不信由你，這家店已經開了一百年。
  6. On second thoughts, I'll have the fish.
    我再想想，還是點魚好了。
