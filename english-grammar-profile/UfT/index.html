現⁠在完⁠成⁠式說這輩⁠子到現⁠在⁠的經⁠驗

I've seen the British Museum.

我去看過大英博物館。

說話者沒有說是哪一年去的。

說經⁠驗常加 ever、never、次⁠數

I've seen the British Museum.

ever
問句

There's a new restaurant on our street. Have you ever eaten Thai food?

我們這條街開了一家新餐廳。你吃過泰國菜嗎？
never
從來沒有

I'd love to see Paris. I've never been to France.

我好想去看看巴黎。我從來沒去過法國。
次數
句尾

I love the shows on Broadway. I've been to New York twice.

我很愛百老匯的表演。我去過紐約兩次。

been to 去⁠過，gone to 去⁠了還⁠沒⁠回⁠來

I've seen the British Museum.

been to
已經回來

Ask my mum about Tokyo. She's been to Japan.

去問我媽媽東京的事。她去過日本。
gone to
還在那裡

My mum isn't home this week. She's gone to Japan.

我媽媽這個禮拜不在家。她去日本了。

說⁠出過⁠去⁠的時⁠間，用過⁠去簡⁠單式

I've seen the British Museum.

My parents have visited the Louvre in 2019.

My parents visited the Louvre in 2019.

去⁠過、已⁠經回⁠來，用 been to

I've seen the British Museum.

My boss has gone to Paris three times.

My boss has been to Paris three times.

never 已⁠經是否⁠定，不⁠加 not

I've seen the British Museum.

My dad hasn't never tried Korean food.

My dad has never tried Korean food.

先說有經⁠驗，再用過⁠去簡⁠單式說細⁠節

I've seen the British Museum.

經驗

I've been to Rome.

我去過羅馬。
細節

I went there with my school in 2018.

我二〇一八年跟學校一起去的。

加 just 說剛發⁠生⁠的一⁠件⁠事

I've seen the British Museum.

我剛在公車站看到你哥。他在等你。

已⁠經去⁠世⁠的人⁠的經⁠驗，用過⁠去簡⁠單式

I've seen the British Museum.

我已故的爺爺很愛旅行。他去過中國兩次。

例句

① 肯定句：做過的事

  1. Rome is a great city for art. We've visited several museums there.
    羅馬是看藝術的好地方。我們參觀過那裡的幾間博物館。
  2. You can ask me about hiking in Japan. I've climbed Mount Fuji.
    日本健行的事你可以問我。我爬過富士山。
  3. My sister knows a lot about first aid. She has worked in a hospital.
    我姊姊很懂急救。她在醫院工作過。
  4. My grandparents love Norway. They've seen the Northern Lights.
    我爺爺奶奶很愛挪威。他們看過北極光。
  5. I'm not scared of heights. I've done a bungee jump.
    我不怕高。我玩過高空彈跳。
  6. My uncle was a famous chef. He's met the president.
    我叔叔以前是有名的廚師。他見過總統。

② never：從來沒有

  1. I'm so excited about tonight. I've never been to a concert.
    我好期待今晚。我從來沒去聽過演唱會。
  2. My grandma is nervous about the trip. She's never flown in a plane.
    我奶奶對這趟旅行很緊張。她從來沒坐過飛機。
  3. We're taking them to the beach. My parents have never seen the sea.
    我們要帶他們去海邊。我爸媽從來沒看過海。
  4. We take the bus everywhere. We've never owned a car.
    我們去哪裡都搭公車。我們從來沒有過車。
  5. Don't ask my husband to make dinner. He's never cooked a meal in his life.
    別叫我先生煮晚餐。他這輩子從來沒煮過一頓飯。

③ 問句：Have you ever ...?

  1. I'm planning a trip to Delhi. Have you ever been to India?
    我在計畫去德里玩。你去過印度嗎？
  2. My sister wants to study in Canada. Has your brother ever lived abroad?
    我姊姊想去加拿大念書。你哥在國外住過嗎？
  3. You're a really good singer. Have you ever won a competition?
    你唱歌真好聽。你在比賽得過獎嗎？
  4. My parents are thinking about a cruise. Have your parents ever travelled by ship?
    我爸媽在考慮坐郵輪。你爸媽搭過船旅行嗎？
  5. We need a fourth player for Saturday. Has your wife ever played golf?
    我們星期六還缺一個人。你太太打過高爾夫球嗎？

④ 次數：once / twice / three times

  1. I love the shows on Broadway. I've been to New York twice.
    我很愛百老匯的表演。我去過紐約兩次。
  2. My daughter loves Matilda. She's read that book three times.
    我女兒很愛《瑪蒂達》。那本書她看過三次。
  3. My dad was in the army. We've moved house four times.
    我爸爸以前是軍人。我們搬過四次家。
  4. I don't know your new boyfriend well. I've only met him once.
    我跟你的新男朋友不熟。我只見過他一次。
  5. My brother is very nervous about tomorrow. He's failed his driving test twice.
    我哥哥很擔心明天。他考駕照沒過兩次。

⑤ been to 和 gone to

  1. My aunt can tell you about the pyramids. She has been to Egypt.
    我阿姨可以跟你說金字塔的事。她去過埃及。
  2. My uncle won't be at the party. He has gone to Brazil for work.
    我叔叔不會來派對。他去巴西出差了。
  3. Everyone is talking about the new shopping center. Have you been there?
    大家都在聊那家新的購物中心。你去過了嗎？
  4. Where's Sam? — He's gone to the gym. He'll be back at six.
    山姆呢？他去健身房了。他六點會回來。

⑥ 先說經驗，再用過去簡單式說細節

  1. I've eaten frogs' legs. I tried them in France.
    我吃過田雞腿，在法國吃的。
  2. My sister is careful on her bike. She's broken her arm before. It happened when she was ten.
    我妹妹騎腳踏車很小心。她以前手臂骨折過，是十歲的時候。
  3. Let's book the Sea View Hotel. We've stayed there before, and we loved it.
    我們訂海景飯店吧。我們住過那裡，很喜歡。
  4. Have you ever ridden a camel? — Yes, I rode one in Morocco.
    你騎過駱駝嗎？騎過，在摩洛哥騎的。
  5. I've met a famous actor. I met him at the airport last year.
    我見過一位有名的演員。我去年在機場遇到他。
