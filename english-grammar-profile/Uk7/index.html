例句

① 規則動詞：didn't + 原形動詞

  1. I didn't realise how late it was.
    我沒發現已經這麼晚了。
  2. The plan didn't work, so we tried something else.
    計畫沒成功，所以我們試了別的方法。
  3. She didn't answer my last three messages.
    她沒回我最後三則訊息。
  4. They didn't notice the mistake until the next day.
    他們直到隔天才發現錯誤。
  5. We didn't expect such a large crowd.
    我們沒想到會有這麼多人。

② 不規則動詞：didn't + 原形動詞

  1. I didn't sleep at all on the plane.
    我在飛機上完全沒睡。
  2. He didn't tell anyone about the accident.
    他沒跟任何人說那場意外的事。
  3. We didn't win a single game last season.
    我們上一季一場比賽都沒贏。
  4. My brother didn't take the job in the end.
    我哥最後沒接那份工作。
  5. You didn't eat much at dinner.
    你晚餐沒吃什麼。
  6. The children didn't understand the joke.
    孩子們沒聽懂那個笑話。

③ 片語動詞：didn't + 原形動詞 + 小品詞

  1. Things didn't turn out the way we planned.
    事情的結果跟我們計畫的不一樣。
  2. The meeting didn't go on for long.
    會議沒開很久。
  3. She didn't give up after the first rejection.
    她第一次被拒絕之後沒有放棄。
  4. The car didn't slow down at the crossing.
    那輛車在路口沒有減速。

④ wasn't / weren't

  1. I'm sure it wasn't my fault.
    我確定那不是我的錯。
  2. The hotel wasn't as spacious as the photos suggested.
    飯店沒有照片看起來那麼寬敞。
  3. We weren't ready when the taxi arrived.
    計程車到的時候我們還沒準備好。
  4. My parents weren't at home that weekend.
    那個週末我爸媽不在家。

⑤ 完整形式 did not

  1. The company did not respond to our complaint.
    公司沒有回應我們的投訴。
  2. The witness did not see the driver's face.
    證人沒有看到司機的臉。
  3. I did not receive the refund you promised.
    我沒有收到你們答應的退款。
  4. The council did not approve the plans for the new car park.
    市議會沒有核准新停車場的計畫。

⑥ didn't + 原形動詞 + and + 過去式

  1. She didn't wait for an answer and left the room.
    她沒等回答就離開了房間。
  2. We didn't take a taxi and walked home instead.
    我們沒搭計程車，改走路回家。
  3. He didn't argue and signed the contract.
    他沒有爭辯，就簽了合約。

⑦ didn't + 原形動詞 + or + 原形動詞

  1. He didn't call or write for months.
    他好幾個月沒打電話，也沒寫信。
  2. I didn't eat or drink anything all day.
    我一整天沒吃也沒喝。

⑧ didn't + any / anybody / anything

  1. They didn't give us any information.
    他們沒給我們任何資訊。
  2. She didn't say anything about the new job.
    她完全沒提新工作的事。
  3. The doctor didn't find anything wrong with my heart.
    醫生沒發現我的心臟有什麼問題。
