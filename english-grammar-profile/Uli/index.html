If I had been in her shoes, I would not have put up with that sort of behavior.

例句

① that sort of / that kind of + 名詞

  1. I told her that if I had been in her shoes, I would not have put up with that sort of behavior.
    我跟她說，如果我是她，我才不會忍受那種行為。
  2. I'm not going to listen to that kind of nonsense.
    我才不要聽那種廢話。
  3. We don't allow that sort of language at the dinner table.
    我們家吃飯的時候不准講那種話。
  4. My parents never let me watch that kind of program when I was young.
    我小時候，爸媽從來不讓我看那種節目。
  5. You can't speak to customers with that kind of attitude.
    你不能用那種態度跟客人說話。

② that + 名詞

  1. I'm surprised she married that man. He's so rude.
    我很驚訝她嫁給了那個男人。他好沒禮貌。
  2. Turn off that awful music! I'm trying to sleep.
    把那難聽的音樂關掉！我要睡覺。
  3. I never want to see that dog in my garden again.
    我再也不想看到那隻狗跑進我的花園。
  4. Why do you still wear that old jumper? It's full of holes.
    你為什麼還穿那件舊毛衣？上面都是洞。

③ those + 複數名詞

  1. Celebrities are often worshipped by crowds of fans who can't see through the mask that those so-called heroes wear.
    名人常被一群群的粉絲崇拜，這些粉絲看不穿那些所謂英雄戴的面具。
  2. I'm sick of those people who park in front of my garage.
    那些把車停在我車庫前面的人讓我很受不了。
  3. Don't believe those adverts. The product doesn't work.
    別相信那些廣告。那個產品沒效。
  4. Those politicians only care about winning the next election.
    那些政客只在乎贏下一次選舉。

④ those so-called + 複數名詞

  1. Those so-called experts on TV know nothing about real life.
    電視上那些所謂的專家對真實生活一無所知。
  2. I've read those so-called health websites, and most of them are wrong.
    我看過那些所謂的健康網站，大部分都是錯的。
  3. Those so-called friends of hers never help her.
    她那些所謂的朋友從來不幫她。

⑤ 論說文和評論

  1. Young people copy the stars they see on TV, and that kind of influence worries many parents.
    年輕人模仿電視上的明星，那種影響讓很多家長擔心。
  2. Those reality shows teach children that fame is more important than hard work.
    那些實境節目教小孩成名比努力更重要。
  3. Schools should not accept that sort of bullying.
    學校不應該容忍那種霸凌。

⑥ 對話

  1. "He shouted at the waiter." "I can't stand that kind of person."
    他對服務生大吼。我受不了那種人。
  2. "My boss wants me to work on Sunday again." "Don't accept that sort of treatment."
    我老闆又要我星期天上班。別接受那種待遇。
  3. "Have you heard what those boys said about her?" "Yes. It was horrible."
    你聽到那些男生說她什麼了嗎？聽到了。好過分。
