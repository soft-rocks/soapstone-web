How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

他們怎麼影響了我的人生，很難說清楚。

整⁠個 How 子⁠句當主⁠詞，動⁠詞用單⁠數

他們怎麼影響了我的人生，很難說清楚。

子句裡照直述句的語序，主詞在動詞前面。

How 說方⁠式，Why 說原⁠因，Where 說地⁠點

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

How
方式

How you say it matters more than what you say.

你怎麼說比你說什麼更重要。
Why
原因

Why she left is still a mystery.

她為什麼離開，到現在還是個謎。
Where
地點

Where they hid the money was never found out.

他們把錢藏在哪裡，一直沒有人查出來。

主⁠詞子⁠句後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接 be 以⁠外⁠的動⁠詞

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

shapes

How we are brought up shapes our character.

我們怎麼被養大，塑造了我們的性格。
doesn't concern

Why he quit doesn't concern you.

他為什麼辭職，跟你沒關係。

主⁠詞子⁠句不⁠用疑⁠問⁠句⁠的語⁠序

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

Where did he go was a mystery.

Where he went was a mystery.

整⁠個子⁠句是一⁠個主⁠詞，動⁠詞用單⁠數

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

How people get their things are also important.

How people get their things is also important.

people 是子句裡的主詞，不是整句的主詞。

子⁠句有動⁠詞，整⁠句也⁠要有動⁠詞

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

How they met a funny story.

How they met is a funny story.

Why 子⁠句要⁠有自⁠己⁠的主⁠詞

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

Why left the company is unclear.

Why she left the company is unclear.

子⁠句放⁠在句⁠首，強⁠調子⁠句⁠的內⁠容

What 子⁠句表⁠示東⁠西，Why 子⁠句表⁠示原⁠因

Why 子句

問⁠句用疑⁠問⁠句⁠的語⁠序，主⁠詞子⁠句用直⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序

How they influenced my life is hard to tell.

問句

Where did they go?

他們去哪裡了？
主詞子句

Where he had gone was a mystery.

他去了哪裡，沒有人知道。

例句

① How 子句

  1. How we are brought up shapes the way we think.
    我們怎麼被養大，影響了我們的思考方式。
  2. How you treat people says a lot about you.
    你怎麼對待別人，很能看出你是什麼樣的人。
  3. How the fire started is still unclear.
    火是怎麼燒起來的，到現在還不清楚。
  4. How she managed to finish the marathon amazes me.
    她到底是怎麼跑完馬拉松的，讓我很佩服。
  5. How much you spend on rent depends on the area.
    你在房租上花多少錢，要看是哪個地區。
  6. How the company treated its workers was a scandal.
    那家公司當時怎麼對待員工，是一樁醜聞。
  7. How people got their furniture didn't matter to the judges.
    大家的家具是怎麼來的，評審不在乎。
  8. How long the repairs will take is anyone's guess.
    修理要花多久，誰也說不準。

② Why 子句

  1. Why she left the band was never explained.
    她為什麼退團，一直沒有人解釋。
  2. Why he lied to us is a mystery to me.
    他為什麼對我們說謊，我想不通。
  3. Why the train stopped was not announced.
    火車為什麼停下來，廣播沒有說。
  4. Why they chose this town doesn't matter now.
    他們為什麼選這個小鎮，現在已經不重要了。
  5. Why the prices keep rising worries a lot of families.
    物價為什麼一直漲，讓很多家庭很擔心。
  6. Why you want this job is the first thing they'll ask.
    你為什麼想要這份工作，是他們第一個會問的問題。
  7. Why the project failed was obvious to everyone.
    這個計畫為什麼失敗，大家都看得很清楚。

③ Where 子句

  1. Where he had gone was a mystery.
    他去了哪裡，沒有人知道。
  2. Where we spend the holidays is up to you.
    我們去哪裡過節，由你決定。
  3. Where the keys ended up is anybody's guess.
    鑰匙最後跑到哪裡去了，誰也不知道。
  4. Where you live affects how much you pay for insurance.
    你住在哪裡，會影響你的保費。
  5. Where the new school will be built hasn't been decided yet.
    新學校要蓋在哪裡，還沒有決定。
  6. Where the river meets the sea is a good spot for birdwatching.
    河流出海的地方很適合賞鳥。
  7. Where she got the money from remains a secret.
    她的錢從哪裡來，到現在還是個祕密。

④ be 動詞加補語

  1. How they influenced my life is hard to put into words.
    他們怎麼影響了我的人生，很難用言語形容。
  2. Why the meeting was cancelled is not clear.
    會議為什麼取消，還不清楚。
  3. Where the party is going to be is still a secret.
    派對要在哪裡辦，還是個祕密。
  4. How the two brothers fell out was a long story.
    那兩兄弟是怎麼鬧翻的，說來話長。

⑤ 其他動詞

  1. How you answer the phone makes a big difference to customers.
    你怎麼接電話，對客人的感受影響很大。
  2. Why the old man refused the money puzzled the whole village.
    那個老人為什麼不收那筆錢，讓全村的人都想不透。
  3. Where the children play worries the parents.
    孩子們在什麼地方玩，讓家長很擔心。
  4. How the film ended surprised all of us.
    那部電影最後怎麼收尾，讓我們都很意外。
  5. Why the shop closed so suddenly confused its regular customers.
    那家店為什麼突然關門，讓常客都摸不著頭緒。
