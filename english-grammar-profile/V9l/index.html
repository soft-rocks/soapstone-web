例句

① aren't / are not being + 過去分詞

  1. Some schools have everything and some have nothing. Students are not being educated equally.
    有些學校什麼都有，有些學校什麼都沒有。學生並沒有受到平等的教育。
  2. Some people hate zoos. But having animals in a zoo doesn't mean that they aren't being treated right.
    有些人很討厭動物園。可是動物養在動物園裡，不代表牠們沒有受到好的對待。
  3. The new rules aren't being followed by most drivers.
    大部分的駕駛都沒有遵守新規定。
  4. I'm worried about the old trees in the park. They aren't being looked after.
    我很擔心公園裡的老樹。沒人在照顧它們。
  5. Our emails aren't being answered. I think the office is closed.
    我們的電子郵件都沒人回。我想辦公室沒開。
  6. The cleaners are on strike, so the streets aren't being cleaned this week.
    清潔工在罷工，所以這星期街上沒人打掃。

② isn't / is not being + 過去分詞

  1. We should keep as many languages alive as possible, so that part of human history is not being lost.
    我們應該讓愈多語言活下來愈好，才不會失去一部分人類的歷史。
  2. Don't sit there. That chair is broken, and it isn't being used.
    別坐那裡。那張椅子壞了，沒人在用。
  3. The project isn't being managed well. We're already two weeks late.
    這個計畫沒有管理好。我們已經晚了兩個星期。
  4. My brother is upset because he isn't being paid enough at work.
    我哥哥很不高興，因為他工作的薪水太少。
  5. The heating isn't being turned on until next month, so wear a jumper.
    暖氣下個月才會開，所以穿件毛衣。
  6. Lucy is angry with her friends. She feels she isn't being listened to.
    Lucy 在生朋友的氣。她覺得沒人在聽她說話。

③ I'm not being + 過去分詞

  1. I'm not being paid for this extra work, so I'm going home at five.
    這些加班我沒拿到錢，所以我五點就回家。
  2. Can somebody tell me what's going on? I'm not being told everything, am I?
    誰可以告訴我發生什麼事？你們沒跟我說全部，對吧？
  3. I don't like this job. I'm not being given anything interesting to do.
    我不喜歡這份工作。我沒有分到任何有趣的事做。
  4. I'm not being sent the club's emails any more. Can you check my address?
    我已經收不到社團的電子郵件了。你可以查一下我的地址嗎？

④ 現在這段時間

  1. The old factory isn't being used at the moment. It's empty.
    那間舊工廠現在沒在用。裡面是空的。
  2. Sorry, that room isn't being cleaned today. The cleaner is ill.
    抱歉，那個房間今天沒人打掃。清潔工生病了。
  3. These books aren't being read any more. Let's give them away.
    這些書已經沒人在看了。我們送人吧。
  4. The match isn't being shown on TV this year.
    今年那場比賽沒有電視轉播。

⑤ 安慰和保證

  1. Don't worry. You aren't being blamed for the accident.
    別擔心。沒有人怪你造成這場意外。
  2. The trees in the square aren't being cut down after all. The council changed its mind.
    廣場上的樹最後不會被砍了。市議會改變主意了。
  3. Relax. You aren't being tested on this chapter.
    放輕鬆。這一章不會考。
  4. Your car isn't being taken away. You can leave it here until six.
    你的車不會被拖走。你可以停到六點。

⑥ 抱怨

  1. Why aren't these boxes being moved? They've been here all week.
    這些箱子怎麼沒人搬？它們在這裡放一整個星期了。
  2. The children aren't being taught enough maths at that school.
    那間學校教孩子的數學不夠。
  3. I think our money isn't being spent wisely.
    我覺得我們的錢沒有花在對的地方。
  4. Someone should talk to the nurses. The patients on this floor aren't being given enough water.
    應該有人去跟護士說。這層樓的病人沒拿到足夠的水。

⑦ 問句

  1. "Isn't the kitchen being painted this week?" "No, it's next week now."
    廚房這星期不是要油漆嗎？不是，改成下星期了。
  2. "Why isn't anything being done about the traffic?" "Nobody wants to pay for it."
    交通的問題為什麼沒人處理？沒人想出錢。
  3. "Aren't you being met at the station?" "No, I'm taking a taxi."
    沒有人去車站接你嗎？沒有，我搭計程車。
