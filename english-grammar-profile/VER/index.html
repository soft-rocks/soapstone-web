I want to invite you for dinner.

我想邀請你來吃晚餐。

want 後⁠面⁠接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

我想邀請你來吃晚餐。

前⁠面⁠的動⁠詞照時⁠態和主⁠詞變⁠化，不⁠定⁠詞不⁠變

I want to invite you for dinner.

第三人稱單數

My brother wants to be a doctor.

我哥想當醫生。
過去

We wanted to stay longer.

我們本來想多待一會兒。

否⁠定和問⁠句⁠的 do 放⁠在第⁠一⁠個動⁠詞前⁠面

I want to invite you for dinner.

否定

Sometimes I don't like to go out because it's very cold.

有時候天氣很冷，我就不喜歡出門。
問句

Do you want to come with us?

你想跟我們一起去嗎？

want 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時，動⁠詞前⁠面加 to

I want to invite you for dinner.

I want go home.

I want to go home.

to 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用原⁠形

I want to invite you for dinner.

She wants to buys a new bike.

She wants to buy a new bike.

不⁠定⁠詞⁠的 to 後⁠面不⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I want to invite you for dinner.

I need to finishing my homework.

I need to finish my homework.

否⁠定用 don't，放⁠在 want 前⁠面

I want to invite you for dinner.

I not want to go.

I don't want to go.

情⁠態動⁠詞和接名⁠詞⁠的 want 後⁠面不⁠加 to

I want to invite you for dinner.

情態動詞

I can swim.

我會游泳。
want + 名詞

I want a new phone.

我想要一支新手機。

例句

① want to

  1. Dear Mrs Chen, I want to invite you for dinner on Friday.
    陳太太您好，我想邀請您星期五來吃晚餐。
  2. "What do you want to eat?" "Pizza, please!"
    你想吃什麼？披薩，拜託！
  3. My son wants to be a firefighter when he grows up.
    我兒子長大想當消防員。
  4. We wanted to see the film, but the tickets were sold out.
    我們本來想看那部電影，可是票賣完了。
  5. "Do you want to come to the beach with us?" "Yes, I'd love that."
    你想跟我們去海邊嗎？好啊，我很想去。

② like to / love to

  1. On Sunday mornings I like to go to the zoo with my kids.
    星期天早上我喜歡帶孩子去動物園。
  2. Sometimes I don't like to go out because it's very cold.
    有時候天氣太冷，我就不喜歡出門。
  3. My grandma loves to tell stories about her childhood.
    我奶奶很喜歡講她小時候的故事。
  4. "What do you like to do at the weekend?" "I like to cook for my friends."
    你週末喜歡做什麼？我喜歡煮飯給朋友吃。

③ need to

  1. I need to buy some milk on the way home.
    我回家路上得買點牛奶。
  2. "Are you OK?" "I'm tired. I need to sleep."
    你還好嗎？我很累。我需要睡覺。
  3. "Can I go to the party tonight?" "Yes, but you need to be home by eleven."
    我今晚可以去派對嗎？可以，可是你得十一點以前回家。
  4. "Does your sister need to take the bus to school?" "No, my dad drives her."
    你妹妹得搭公車上學嗎？不用，我爸開車載她。

④ try to

  1. I'm trying to learn Japanese, but it's hard.
    我正在試著學日文，可是很難。
  2. Please try to arrive before nine.
    請盡量九點以前到。
  3. He tried to open the box, but it was locked.
    他試著打開那個箱子，可是箱子鎖住了。

⑤ hope to

  1. Thank you for the lovely weekend. I hope to see you again soon.
    謝謝你讓我度過一個很棒的週末。希望很快能再見到你。
  2. My parents hope to visit Japan next spring.
    我爸媽希望明年春天去日本。
  3. We hope to finish the new kitchen by Christmas.
    我們希望聖誕節前把新廚房弄好。

⑥ learn to / start to

  1. My daughter learned to ride a bike when she was five.
    我女兒五歲就學會騎腳踏車了。
  2. "Can you drive?" "Not yet. I'm learning to drive this year."
    你會開車嗎？還不會。我今年在學開車。
  3. We were at the beach when it started to rain.
    我們在海邊的時候開始下雨了。
  4. The baby started to cry when the dog barked.
    狗一叫，寶寶就開始哭了。

⑦ 否定

  1. I don't want to go to school today. I feel sick.
    我今天不想上學。我不舒服。
  2. My brother doesn't like to share his toys.
    我弟弟不喜歡分享他的玩具。
  3. We didn't want to wake the baby, so we talked quietly.
    我們不想吵醒寶寶，所以小聲說話。
  4. You don't need to bring anything. We have everything.
    你什麼都不用帶。我們都有了。

⑧ 問句

  1. "Do you want to dance?" "Sure, why not?"
    你想跳舞嗎？好啊，有何不可？
  2. "Does Tom like to play football?" "Yes, he plays every day."
    Tom 喜歡踢足球嗎？喜歡，他每天都踢。
  3. "Did you want to talk to me?" "Yes, do you have a minute?"
    你想跟我說話嗎？對，你有空嗎？
  4. "Where do you want to go for your birthday?" "To the new Italian restaurant."
    你生日想去哪裡？去那家新開的義大利餐廳。
