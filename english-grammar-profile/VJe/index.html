Haven't you seen it yet?

你還沒看過嗎？

haven't 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面，表⁠示說⁠話者覺⁠得意⁠外

Haven't you seen it yet?

你還沒看過嗎？

說話者以為朋友早就看過那部電影了。

疑⁠問⁠詞放⁠在 haven't 前⁠面

Haven't you seen it yet?

你媽媽在電話旁邊等。你為什麼還沒打給她？

否⁠定疑⁠問⁠句⁠的用⁠法

Haven't you seen it yet?

驚訝

Haven't you finished yet? It's been three hours!

你還沒做完嗎？已經三個小時了！
確認想法

Your face looks familiar. Haven't we met before?

你看起來很面熟。我們以前是不是見過？
提醒對方

Haven't you forgotten something? Your keys are still on the table.

你是不是忘了什麼？你的鑰匙還在桌上。

回⁠答照事⁠實，沒⁠做過用 No

Haven't you seen it yet?

Everyone is talking about the new Spider-Man film. Haven't you seen it yet? — No, I haven't.

大家都在聊新的蜘蛛人電影。你還沒看過嗎？對，還沒看。

主⁠詞後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Haven't you seen it yet?

Haven't you see the new teacher yet?

Haven't you seen the new teacher yet?

不⁠縮⁠寫⁠時，not 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

Haven't you seen it yet?

Have not you checked the train times for Sunday?

Have you not checked the train times for Sunday?

沒⁠吃就回⁠答 No, I haven't

Haven't you seen it yet?

Haven't you eaten lunch? — Yes, I haven't.

Haven't you eaten lunch? — No, I haven't.

一⁠般問⁠句不⁠帶預⁠期

Haven't you seen it yet?

電影院有一部新電影。你看過了嗎？

有過⁠去⁠的時⁠間，用 didn't

Haven't you seen it yet?

比賽有電視轉播。你昨晚沒看嗎？

附⁠加問⁠句先說想⁠法，再請對⁠方確⁠認

Haven't you seen it yet?

你知道那部電影的結局。你看過了，對吧？

例句

① Haven't + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. You're still at your desk at ten o'clock. Haven't you finished your homework?
    都十點了，你還在書桌前。你功課還沒寫完嗎？
  2. Everyone is bringing food on Saturday. Haven't they told you about the party?
    星期六大家都要帶吃的來。他們沒跟你說派對的事嗎？
  3. Wait, this bill is for the hotel. Haven't we paid for this already?
    等一下，這是飯店的帳單。這個我們不是已經付過錢了嗎？
  4. The school is closed tomorrow. Haven't you heard the news?
    學校明天停課。你還沒聽說那個消息嗎？
  5. The film starts in five minutes. Haven't your friends arrived?
    電影再五分鐘就開始了。你朋友還沒到嗎？
  6. Take your shoes off at the door. Haven't I told you a hundred times?
    在門口把鞋子脫掉。我不是跟你說過一百遍了嗎？

② Hasn't + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. You called Mia this morning. Hasn't she called you back?
    你早上打給米亞。她還沒回你電話嗎？
  2. You're still coughing a lot. Hasn't the doctor given you any medicine?
    你還咳得很厲害。醫生沒開藥給你嗎？
  3. I saw a wedding photo on your wall. Hasn't your brother got married?
    我看到你牆上有一張婚紗照。你哥不是已經結婚了嗎？
  4. It's been sunny all week. Hasn't the snow on the roof melted by now?
    這個禮拜都是晴天。屋頂上的雪到現在還沒融化嗎？
  5. You ordered the shoes two weeks ago. Hasn't the package arrived?
    你兩個禮拜前就訂了鞋子。包裹還沒到嗎？

③ 加 yet：以為早該發生了

  1. Your teacher wrote to you on Monday. Haven't you replied to his email yet?
    你的老師星期一寫信給你。你還沒回他的信嗎？
  2. The kitchen is still full of water. Hasn't the plumber come yet?
    廚房還是滿地的水。水電工還沒來嗎？
  3. The shop closes in ten minutes. Haven't the kids decided which ice cream they want yet?
    店再十分鐘就關了。孩子們還沒決定要哪一種冰淇淋嗎？
  4. You lent Jack fifty pounds in May. Hasn't he paid you back yet?
    你五月借傑克五十英鎊。他還沒還你錢嗎？
  5. Haven't you packed yet? The taxi's here!
    你還沒收好行李嗎？計程車到了！

④ 疑問詞 + haven't / hasn't

  1. I sent you five texts today. Why haven't you answered my messages?
    我今天傳了五則簡訊給你。你為什麼都不回我訊息？
  2. We've waited for half an hour. Why hasn't the bus come?
    我們等了半小時了。公車為什麼還沒來？
  3. The meeting started at nine. Why hasn't anyone told me about it?
    會議九點就開始了。為什麼都沒有人告訴我？
  4. The office is freezing again. Why haven't they fixed the heating?
    辦公室又冷得要命。他們為什麼還沒修好暖氣？

⑤ 想確認自己的想法

  1. Excuse me, you look familiar. Haven't I seen you somewhere before?
    不好意思，你看起來很面熟。我是不是在哪裡見過你？
  2. My grandma bought bread here when I was little. Hasn't this shop been here for years?
    我小時候奶奶在這裡買麵包。這家店不是開好幾年了嗎？
  3. You speak French very well. Haven't you lived in Canada?
    你的法文說得很好。你不是在加拿大住過嗎？
  4. I remember that painting on the wall. Haven't we been to this restaurant before?
    我記得牆上那幅畫。我們以前是不是來過這家餐廳？

⑥ 完整形式：Have / Has + 主詞 + not

  1. Everyone at work loves the coffee there. Have you not tried the new café?
    公司的人都很愛那裡的咖啡。你還沒去過那家新咖啡店嗎？
  2. The report was due on Friday. Has Emma not finished it?
    報告星期五就該交了。艾瑪還沒寫完嗎？
  3. We wrote to the council a month ago. Have they not received our letter?
    我們一個月前寫信給市議會。他們沒收到我們的信嗎？

⑦ 回答

  1. This is my friend Sam. Haven't you met him? — No, I haven't.
    這是我朋友山姆。你沒見過他嗎？對，沒見過。
  2. Lucy's bag isn't here. Hasn't she left? — Yes, she has. She left an hour ago.
    露西的包包不在這裡。她不是走了嗎？對，她走了，一個小時前走的。
  3. I can't find any clean plates. Haven't you done the dishes? — Yes, I have. They're in the cupboard.
    我找不到乾淨的盤子。你還沒洗碗嗎？洗了，都在櫥櫃裡。
