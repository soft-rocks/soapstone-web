You lost your phone? Did you find it?

你昨天有買東西嗎？

Did + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

You lost your phone? Did you find it?

Did放在主詞前面，表示過去。
原形動詞did 後面的動詞用原形。
疑問詞what、when、where、why 放在句首。

疑⁠問⁠詞⁠的問⁠句

例句

Your fridge is empty. When did you last go to the supermarket?

你的冰箱空空的。你上次去超市是什麼時候？

did 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You lost your phone? Did you find it?

Did you posted my letter to the bank this morning?

Did you post my letter to the bank this morning?

一⁠般動⁠詞⁠的問⁠句要⁠用 did

You lost your phone? Did you find it?

Where you parked the car this morning?

Where did you park the car this morning?

簡答用 did

You lost your phone? Did you find it?

"Did you find your keys?" "Yes, I found."

"Did you find your keys?" "Yes, I did."

例句

① Did you + 原形動詞 + 名詞？：問對方的事

  1. You lost your phone at school last week? Did you find it?
    你上星期在學校弄丟了手機？你找到了嗎？
  2. Your bag looks full. Did you buy something at the market yesterday?
    你的袋子看起來好滿。你昨天在市場有買東西嗎？
  3. Thanks for coming to my birthday. Did you enjoy the party on Saturday?
    謝謝你來我的生日。你喜歡星期六的派對嗎？
  4. You went out with Ben last night. Did you see the new film at the cinema?
    你昨晚跟班出去。你看了電影院的那部新電影嗎？
  5. You're playing games now. Did you finish your homework before dinner?
    你現在在玩遊戲。你晚餐前寫完功課了嗎？
  6. She was wearing a green dress. Did you meet my cousin at the wedding?
    她穿著一件綠色洋裝。你在婚禮上遇到我表姊了嗎？

② When / Where + did you + 原形動詞？

  1. You look great in that coat. When did you go shopping last?
    你穿那件外套很好看。你上次去買東西是什麼時候？
  2. I need shoes for the party. Where did you buy those lovely shoes?
    我需要一雙去派對穿的鞋。你在哪裡買那雙漂亮的鞋？
  3. Your garden is beautiful. When did you plant all those roses?
    你的花園好美。你什麼時候種了那些玫瑰？
  4. You have a great suntan. Where did you go on holiday last summer?
    你曬得好漂亮。你去年夏天去哪裡度假？
  5. You know all the shops here. When did you move to this town?
    這裡的店你都知道。你什麼時候搬到這個鎮的？
  6. That cake is amazing. Where did you find the recipe?
    那個蛋糕太棒了。你在哪裡找到食譜的？

③ What / Why + did you + 原形動詞？

  1. You came home with a big box. What did you buy at the shopping centre?
    你帶著一個大箱子回家。你在購物中心買了什麼？
  2. We looked for you at eleven. Why did you leave the party so early?
    我們十一點的時候找你。你為什麼那麼早離開派對？
  3. Good morning, everyone. What did you do at the weekend?
    大家早。你週末做了什麼？
  4. You live very far away. Why did you choose this school?
    你住得很遠。你為什麼選這所學校？
  5. I saw you at the concert hall. What did you think of the concert?
    我在音樂廳看到你。你覺得那場音樂會怎麼樣？
  6. I saw a missed call on my phone. Why did you call me at midnight?
    我看到手機有一通未接來電。你為什麼半夜打給我？

④ Did you + 原形動詞？："Yes, I did." / "No, I didn't."

  1. You still have a cough. "Did you take your medicine this morning?" "Yes, I did."
    你還在咳嗽。你今天早上有吃藥嗎？有，我吃了。
  2. We're on the motorway now. "Did you lock the front door?" "No, I didn't. Sorry."
    我們已經上高速公路了。你有鎖前門嗎？沒有。抱歉。
  3. It was Grandma's birthday yesterday. "Did you call her?" "Yes, I did. She's fine."
    昨天是奶奶的生日。你有打給她嗎？有，她很好。
  4. Good morning! "Did you sleep well?" "No, I didn't. The bed was hard."
    早安！你睡得好嗎？不好。床很硬。
  5. The cat is crying in the kitchen. "Did you feed her?" "Yes, I did, twice."
    貓在廚房叫。你有餵她嗎？有，餵了兩次。
  6. You're smiling. "Did you pass the driving test?" "Yes, I did!"
    你在笑。你駕照考過了嗎？過了！

⑤ How + did you + 原形動詞？：問方法和感覺

  1. The traffic was terrible today. How did you get to the airport so quickly?
    今天交通很糟。你怎麼那麼快就到機場了？
  2. You ran ten kilometres. How did you feel after the race?
    你跑了十公里。跑完比賽你覺得怎麼樣？
  3. Thank you for the cake! How did you know it was my birthday?
    謝謝你的蛋糕！你怎麼知道今天是我生日？
  4. Can I have some more? How did you make this delicious soup?
    我可以再來一點嗎？你怎麼做出這麼好喝的湯？
  5. You two are always together. How did you first become friends at school?
    你們兩個總是在一起。你們在學校是怎麼變成朋友的？
  6. My laptop works again. How did you fix the computer?
    我的筆電又能用了。你是怎麼修好電腦的？
  7. You're back from Rome. How did you find the hotel? Was it nice?
    你從羅馬回來了。你覺得那家飯店怎麼樣？好嗎？
