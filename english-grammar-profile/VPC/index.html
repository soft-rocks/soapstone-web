We didn't get a tour guide. We ought to have booked one!

我們早該請個導遊的！

主⁠詞 + ought to have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

ought to have、過⁠去⁠分⁠詞、ought to have been + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

ought to have過去應該、可是沒有。
過去分詞過去應該做的事。
ought to have been + 過去分詞被動，說事情應該被怎麼處理。

後⁠悔和批⁠評

後悔自己

You told me the shop would be closed. I ought to have listened to you.

你跟我說過那家店會關門。我當時應該聽你的。
批評別人

The river had flooded twice before. The government ought to have known.

那條河之前已經淹過兩次水。政府當時應該知道。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

We didn't get a tour guide. We ought to have booked one!

The museum was so crowded. We ought to have book a guide.

The museum was so crowded. We ought to have booked a guide.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 have

We didn't get a tour guide. We ought to have booked one!

You knew about the party. You ought to had told me.

You knew about the party. You ought to have told me.

說過⁠去⁠的⁠事，to 後⁠面要⁠有 have

We didn't get a tour guide. We ought to have booked one!

The road was closed for a week. They ought to known about it.

The road was closed for a week. They ought to have known about it.

例句

① We / I + ought to have + 過去分詞：後悔自己沒做

  1. There were no guides at the museum, so we saw very little. We ought to have booked one!
    博物館沒有導遊，所以我們看到的很少。我們應該先預約一個的！
  2. I ought to have brought an umbrella. I'm soaking wet.
    我應該帶傘的。我全身濕透了。
  3. We ought to have left earlier. Now we've missed the start.
    我們應該早點出發的。現在錯過開頭了。
  4. Mum said that boy wasn't a real friend, and she was right. I ought to have listened to her.
    媽媽說那個男生不是真正的朋友，她說對了。我當時應該聽她的話。
  5. We drove to the castle, and it was closed on Mondays. We ought to have checked the opening times.
    我們開車到城堡，結果星期一不開放。我們應該先查一下開放時間的。
  6. Now I'm sixty and I have no savings. I ought to have saved more money when I was young.
    我現在六十歲了，沒有存款。我年輕的時候應該多存點錢的。

② 主詞 + ought to have + 過去分詞：批評別人

  1. The water is polluted because of the petrol station. The government ought to have known that.
    水因為加油站被污染了。政府當時應該知道這一點的。
  2. There was drilling outside our room every morning. The hotel ought to have told us about the building work.
    每天早上我們房間外面都在鑽洞。飯店當時應該告訴我們有施工的事。
  3. The new heaters caught fire in some homes. The company ought to have warned its customers.
    新的暖氣機在一些家裡起火了。公司當時應該警告它的顧客。
  4. You ought to have asked me before you borrowed my car.
    你借我的車之前應該先問我的。
  5. My son broke his arm at school, and nobody phoned me until four. The school ought to have contacted the parents immediately.
    我兒子在學校摔斷了手臂，到四點才有人打電話給我。學校當時應該馬上聯絡家長。
  6. Our kitchen still has no walls. The builders ought to have finished the job last month.
    我們的廚房還沒有牆。工人上個月就應該完工了。

③ 主詞 + ought to have been + 過去分詞：事情應該被處理得更好

  1. One thing which I think ought to have been better managed was the boat trip on the Thames.
    有一件事我覺得應該安排得更好，就是泰晤士河的乘船之旅。（被動）
  2. The concert is tomorrow, and we still haven't got our tickets. They ought to have been sent a week ago.
    音樂會明天就開始了，我們還沒收到票。票一個星期前就應該寄出了。（被動）
  3. People have complained about the noise from the airport for ten years. The problem ought to have been solved years ago.
    大家抱怨機場的噪音抱怨了十年。這個問題好幾年前就應該解決了。（被動）
  4. The holes in the road are full of ice now. It ought to have been repaired before winter.
    路上的坑洞現在都結冰了。這條路應該在冬天之前修好。（被動）
  5. Half of us had to stand at the back. The meeting ought to have been held in a bigger room.
    我們有一半的人只能站在後面。這次會議應該在大一點的房間舉行。（被動）
  6. The family moved away and the children only found out the day before. They ought to have been told the truth earlier.
    那家人搬走了，孩子們前一天才知道。他們應該早點被告知真相。（被動）

④ 主詞 + ought to have + 過去分詞, but + 句子

  1. Sorry, I forgot your birthday. I ought to have called you, but I was too busy.
    抱歉，我忘了你的生日。我應該打給你的，可是我太忙了。
  2. We were starving when we arrived at the beach. We ought to have stopped for lunch, but we wanted to get there quickly.
    我們到海灘的時候餓壞了。我們應該停下來吃午餐的，可是我們想快點到。
  3. Mia failed her chemistry exam. She ought to have studied harder, but she didn't have time.
    Mia 化學考試沒及格。她應該更用功的，可是她沒有時間。
  4. Jack broke his friend's guitar. He ought to have said sorry, but he was too proud.
    Jack 弄壞了朋友的吉他。他應該道歉的，可是他太驕傲了。
  5. My parents sat in traffic for four hours. They ought to have taken the train, but they drove instead.
    我爸媽塞車塞了四個小時。他們應該搭火車的，可是他們開車去了。
  6. I'm so tired this morning. I ought to have gone to bed early, but I watched a film.
    我今天早上好累。我應該早點睡的，可是我看了一部電影。

⑤ 主詞 + ought to have + 過去分詞 + by now：現在應該已經

  1. I posted the parcel on Monday, so it ought to have arrived by now.
    我星期一寄出包裹，所以它現在應該已經到了。
  2. Their flight left at six, so they ought to have landed in Rome by now.
    他們的班機六點起飛，所以他們現在應該已經降落在羅馬了。
  3. The meeting started at nine. Surely it ought to have finished by now.
    會議九點開始。現在應該早就結束了吧。
  4. We painted the fence yesterday. It ought to have dried by now.
    我們昨天油漆了圍籬。它現在應該已經乾了。
  5. I sent the letter two weeks ago. You ought to have received it by now.
    我兩個星期前寄出那封信。你現在應該已經收到了。
  6. The dough has been in a warm place for an hour, so it ought to have risen by now.
    麵團放在溫暖的地方一個小時了，所以它應該已經發起來了。
  7. She left the office at five. She ought to have got home by now.
    她五點離開辦公室。她現在應該已經到家了。
