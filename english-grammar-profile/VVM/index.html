It was more expensive than the other clothes.

它比其他衣服貴。

長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面加 more

It was more expensive than the other clothes.

這張沙發比我的床舒服。

哪⁠些形⁠容⁠詞用 more

It was more expensive than the other clothes.

三個音節以上

Your health is more important than money.

你的健康比錢重要。
-ful、-ous、-ing、-ed

She is more famous than her sister.

她比她姊姊有名。

more + 形⁠容⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

It was more expensive than the other clothes.

我想要一份比較有趣的工作。

長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠er

It was more expensive than the other clothes.

This book is interestinger than that one.

This book is more interesting than that one.

用了 more，形⁠容⁠詞就⁠不⁠加 ⁠-⁠er

It was more expensive than the other clothes.

The test was more difficulter than I thought.

The test was more difficult than I thought.

要說跟誰⁠比，就加 than

It was more expensive than the other clothes.

Gold is more expensive silver.

Gold is more expensive than silver.

例句

① 三個音節以上的形容詞

  1. This sofa is more comfortable than my bed.
    這張沙發比我的床舒服。
  2. Gold is more expensive than silver.
    黃金比銀貴。
  3. Math is more difficult than art for me.
    對我來說，數學比美術難。
  4. History became more interesting with Mr. Lin.
    林老師教的歷史變得比較有趣。
  5. Your health is more important than money.
    你的健康比錢重要。
  6. The city is more beautiful at night.
    這座城市晚上比較漂亮。
  7. Motorbikes are more dangerous than bikes.
    機車比腳踏車危險。
  8. The new phone is more expensive than I expected.
    新手機比我預期的貴。
  9. Is English more difficult than Japanese?
    英文比日文難嗎？
  10. This exercise is more useful for beginners.
    這個練習對初學者比較有用。
  11. Leather seats feel more comfortable.
    皮椅坐起來比較舒服。
  12. Today's lesson was more enjoyable.
    今天的課比較好玩。

② -ful、-ous、-ing、-ed 的形容詞

  1. You must be more careful.
    你一定要更小心。
  2. She is more famous than her sister.
    她比她姊姊有名。
  3. The meeting was more boring than usual.
    會議比平常無聊。
  4. I felt more tired after the trip.
    旅行回來以後，我覺得更累了。
  5. My new job is more stressful.
    我的新工作壓力比較大。
  6. Be more patient with your little brother.
    對你弟弟要更有耐心。
  7. Ski trips are more exciting than beach trips.
    去滑雪比去海邊刺激。
  8. Ken looks more relaxed now.
    Ken 現在看起來比較放鬆。

③ more + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. I want a more interesting job.
    我想要一份比較有趣的工作。
  2. The company hired a more careful driver.
    公司請了一個更小心的司機。
  3. She bought a more expensive bag.
    她買了一個比較貴的包包。
  4. Can you give me a more useful example?
    你可以給我一個比較有用的例子嗎？
  5. They moved to a more beautiful area.
    他們搬到一個比較漂亮的地區。

④ 不同的時態和句型

  1. Tomorrow's game will be more important.
    明天的比賽會更重要。
  2. Was the hotel more comfortable than the last one?
    這家旅館比上一家舒服嗎？
  3. Ann became more confident after the course.
    上完課之後，Ann 變得比較有自信。
  4. Nothing is more important than your family.
    沒有什麼比你的家人更重要。
  5. The roads are more crowded on weekends.
    週末路上比較擁擠。
