It doesn't cost very much.

那不會很貴。

主⁠詞是 it，用 doesn't，cost 用原⁠形

那不會很貴。

這⁠些動⁠詞常⁠用在否⁠定⁠句

It doesn't cost very much.

need

You don't need a ticket for children.

小孩不用買票。
matter

It doesn't matter.

沒關係。
mind

I don't mind the rain.

我不在意下雨。
mean

That doesn't mean you're wrong.

那不代表你錯了。

doesn't 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

It doesn't cost very much.

It doesn't costs very much.

It doesn't cost very much.

複⁠數名⁠詞⁠的主⁠詞用 don't

It doesn't cost very much.

These shoes doesn't fit me.

These shoes don't fit me.

need 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時要加 to

It doesn't cost very much.

You don't need bring a coat today.

You don't need to bring a coat today.

動⁠詞⁠的否⁠定用 doesn't，不⁠用 no

It doesn't cost very much.

It no matters.

It doesn't matter.

例句

① doesn't cost / doesn't take

  1. Come and join the class. It doesn't cost very much.
    來上課吧。不會很貴。
  2. Try the market. Fruit doesn't cost much there.
    去市場看看。那裡的水果不貴。
  3. Leave the car here. It doesn't take long to walk to the beach.
    車停這裡吧。走到海邊不用很久。
  4. The bus doesn't take you to the door, but it's close.
    公車不會載你到門口，可是很近。
  5. Let's cook at home. Making soup doesn't take much time.
    我們在家煮吧。煮湯不用花很多時間。

② don't / doesn't need

  1. Thanks, but I don't need the video because I've got the DVD.
    謝謝，可是我不需要錄影帶，因為我有 DVD 了。
  2. Here's the plan for Monday. You don't bring anything because it's the first day.
    這是星期一的安排。第一天你不用帶任何東西。
  3. My grandma is eighty, but she doesn't need glasses for reading.
    我奶奶八十歲了，可是她看書不用戴眼鏡。
  4. Water them once a week. The plants don't need much water in winter.
    一個星期澆一次就好。冬天植物不用澆很多水。
  5. We don't need a car. We live near the station.
    我們不需要車。我們住在車站附近。

③ doesn't matter / don't mind

  1. "Sorry I'm late." "It doesn't matter. We haven't started yet."
    抱歉我遲到了。沒關係。我們還沒開始。
  2. I don't mind the rain. I like walking in it.
    我不在意下雨。我喜歡在雨中散步。
  3. My parents don't mind if I stay out until ten.
    我爸媽不介意我在外面待到十點。
  4. It doesn't matter what you wear. It's a casual party.
    你穿什麼都沒關係。這是個輕鬆的派對。
  5. Ask Sam to drive. He doesn't mind long journeys.
    請 Sam 開車吧。他不介意長途開車。

④ don't / doesn't mean / belong / fit

  1. This book doesn't belong to me. It's Ben's.
    這本書不是我的。是 Ben 的。
  2. One bad test doesn't mean you're a bad student.
    考壞一次不代表你是壞學生。
  3. I need new clothes. These jeans don't fit me any more.
    我需要買新衣服了。這條牛仔褲我穿不下了。
  4. Be careful with that word. It doesn't mean the same thing in English.
    那個字要小心用。它在英文裡不是同一個意思。
  5. The red car doesn't belong to us. Ours is the blue one.
    那台紅色的車不是我們的。我們的是藍色那台。

⑤ 其他動詞

  1. Don't try to call me this weekend. My phone doesn't work in the mountains.
    這個週末別打給我。我的手機在山裡沒訊號。
  2. I don't understand this letter. Can you help me?
    我看不懂這封信。你可以幫我嗎？
  3. There's no rush. The shop doesn't open until ten on Sundays.
    不用急。這家店星期天十點才開。
  4. Leave your dog at home. They don't allow dogs in the restaurant.
    把狗留在家吧。這家餐廳不准帶狗。
  5. He was only four then. My brother doesn't remember anything about his first school.
    他那時候才四歲。我哥哥對他的第一所學校一點印象都沒有。
  6. I need a new laptop. This old one doesn't save my files any more.
    我需要一台新筆電。這台舊的已經存不了我的檔案了。
  7. You'll be tired for a while. Babies don't sleep through the night at first.
    你會累一陣子。嬰兒一開始不會整夜睡。
  8. She laughs at scary films. My grandma doesn't believe in ghosts.
    她看恐怖片都在笑。我奶奶不相信有鬼。
  9. We love our teacher. She doesn't give homework at weekends.
    我們很喜歡我們老師。她週末不出功課。
  10. You go without me. I don't feel like going out tonight.
    你們去吧，不用等我。我今晚不想出去。
