例句

① due to / thanks to

  1. The ferry isn't running today due to strong winds.
    渡輪今天因為強風停駛。
  2. Due to a staff shortage, the café is closing early today.
    因為人手不足，咖啡店今天提早關門。
  3. Thanks to the new bridge, the journey takes only ten minutes.
    多虧了新橋，這段路只要十分鐘。
  4. We won the game thanks to our goalkeeper.
    多虧我們的守門員，我們贏了比賽。
  5. The road is closed due to an accident.
    那條路因為車禍封閉了。
  6. Thanks to my sister, I found a great flat.
    多虧我姊姊，我找到了一間很棒的公寓。

② instead of

  1. We took the night train instead of flying.
    我們搭了夜車，沒有搭飛機。
  2. I had soup instead of a sandwich for lunch.
    我午餐喝了湯，沒吃三明治。
  3. Why don't you call her instead of sending a text?
    你何不打給她，不要傳簡訊？
  4. She stayed home instead of going to the party.
    她待在家，沒去派對。
  5. Use oil instead of butter in this recipe.
    這個食譜用油，不要用奶油。

③ up to / out of / outside of

  1. The battery lasts up to twelve hours.
    電池最多可以撐十二個小時。
  2. The bus holds up to sixty people.
    這輛公車最多可以坐六十個人。
  3. A little boy came running out of the classroom.
    一個小男孩從教室裡跑出來。
  4. My parents live just outside of Hsinchu.
    我爸媽住在新竹市區外面一點。
  5. Get out of the water, it's getting dark!
    從水裡出來，天快黑了！

④ according to / apart from

  1. According to my map, the museum is on the left.
    根據我的地圖，博物館在左邊。
  2. According to the teacher, the test will be easy.
    根據老師的說法，考試會很簡單。
  3. Apart from Mia, everyone came to the meeting.
    除了 Mia，大家都來開會了。
  4. I like all vegetables apart from peppers.
    除了青椒，我所有的蔬菜都喜歡。
  5. According to the news, prices will rise again.
    根據新聞報導，物價還會再漲。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Due to the holiday, the bank will be closed on Monday.
    因為放假，銀行星期一不營業。
  2. Thanks to the rain, the garden looks beautiful.
    多虧下了雨，花園看起來很漂亮。
  3. I'll take the stairs instead of the lift.
    我要走樓梯，不搭電梯。
  4. Kids up to the age of six travel free.
    六歲以下的小孩免費搭乘。
  5. Apart from the weather, it was a perfect holiday.
    除了天氣以外，那是一次完美的假期。
  6. Thanks to the free buses, more people visit the old town.
    多虧有免費公車，更多人來逛老城區。
  7. According to the sign, we can't park here.
    根據告示，我們不能在這裡停車。
  8. She ran out of the room in tears.
    她哭著跑出房間。
  9. Due to a family emergency, I need to leave early today.
    因為家裡有急事，我今天得提早走。
