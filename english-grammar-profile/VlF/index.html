This place is lovely.

我的廚房很小。

This place is lovely.

否定

Don't worry. The dog isn't dangerous.

別擔心，那隻狗不兇。
問句

Is your new room big?

你的新房間大嗎？

過⁠去用 was 和 were

This place is lovely.

The hotel rooms were small but clean.

飯店的房間很小，但是很乾淨。

主⁠詞和形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間要⁠有 be

This place is lovely.

My bedroom big.

My bedroom is big.

主⁠詞是複⁠數，用 are

This place is lovely.

My parents is happy.

My parents are happy.

主⁠詞是複⁠數⁠時，形⁠容⁠詞也⁠不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

This place is lovely.

The rooms are bigs.

The rooms are big.

例句

① 主詞 + is + 形容詞

  1. Come in! My flat is small, but it's warm.
    進來吧！我的公寓很小，不過很暖和。
  2. I like my home because my bedroom is big.
    我喜歡我家，因為我的臥室很大。
  3. Look at the sea from this window. This place is beautiful.
    從這扇窗看海，這個地方好美。
  4. Your new kitchen is lovely. Did you paint it yourself?
    你的新廚房好漂亮，是你自己漆的嗎？
  5. Ann's bike is new. She got it for her birthday.
    Ann 的腳踏車是新的，是她的生日禮物。
  6. My grandfather is old, but he walks every day.
    我爺爺年紀大了，但是他每天都去散步。
  7. Wait a minute. The pizza is very hot.
    等一下，披薩很燙。

② 主詞 + am / are + 形容詞

  1. I'm hungry. What's for dinner, Mom?
    媽，我餓了，晚餐吃什麼？
  2. My parents are happy with my new school.
    我爸媽很滿意我的新學校。
  3. The kids are quiet today. Are they sick?
    孩子們今天很安靜，他們生病了嗎？
  4. We're ready. Let's go to the station.
    我們準備好了，去車站吧。
  5. Your hands are cold. Put on your gloves.
    你的手好冰，戴上手套吧。
  6. These bananas are still green, so don't eat them yet.
    這些香蕉還是綠的，先不要吃。

③ 主詞 + was / were + 形容詞

  1. The concert was loud, and my ears hurt.
    演唱會很吵，我耳朵好痛。
  2. Our holiday was great. The weather was sunny every day.
    我們的假期很棒，每天都出太陽。
  3. The hotel rooms were small but clean.
    飯店的房間很小，但是很乾淨。
  4. I was tired after the long walk.
    走了一大段路以後，我很累。
  5. The questions on the test were easy for me.
    考試的題目對我來說很簡單。

④ 否定

  1. Don't worry. The dog isn't dangerous.
    別擔心，那隻狗不兇。
  2. My room isn't big, but it has a nice view.
    我的房間不大，不過景色很好。
  3. These shoes aren't new. They're my sister's old ones.
    這雙鞋不是新的，是我姊姊的舊鞋。
  4. I'm not sleepy yet. Can I watch TV?
    我還不睏，可以看電視嗎？
  5. The bus wasn't late today, for once.
    公車今天難得沒有誤點。
  6. The museum isn't open on Mondays.
    博物館星期一不開放。

⑤ 問句

  1. Is your new room big?
    你的新房間大嗎？
  2. You didn't eat lunch. Are you hungry now?
    你沒吃午餐，現在餓了嗎？
  3. Is the coffee still hot?
    咖啡還是熱的嗎？
  4. Was the party fun last night?
    昨晚的派對好玩嗎？
  5. Are the people at your new job friendly?
    你新工作的同事友善嗎？
  6. Is the station far from your hotel?
    車站離你的飯店遠嗎？
