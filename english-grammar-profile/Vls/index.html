Don't you like pizza?

你不喜歡披薩嗎？

否⁠定疑⁠問⁠句表⁠示驚⁠訝、希⁠望對⁠方同⁠意或提⁠醒

Don't you like pizza?

驚訝

Don't you recognize me? I used to live next door.

你認不出我嗎？我以前住你隔壁。
希望對方同意

Look at that blue sky. Isn't it a beautiful day?

你看那片藍天。今天天氣不是很好嗎？
提醒

I passed my driving test. Didn't I tell you?

我考過駕照了，我沒跟你說嗎？

回⁠答看事⁠實，不⁠看問⁠題是否⁠定

Don't you like pizza?

A: Don't you drink coffee? B: Yes, I do. Just not at night.

A：你不喝咖啡嗎？B：喝啊，只是晚上不喝。

n't 跟⁠著助⁠動⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首

Don't you like pizza?

Not you like pizza?

Don't you like pizza?

doesn't 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Don't you like pizza?

Doesn't she speaks French?

Doesn't she speak French?

餓就說 Yes，回⁠答看事⁠實

Don't you like pizza?

A: Aren't you hungry? B: No, I am.

A: Aren't you hungry? B: Yes, I am.

例句

① Don't / Doesn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Don't you like spicy food? I made a mild curry too.
    你不喜歡吃辣嗎？我也煮了不辣的咖哩。
  2. Don't you recognize me? I used to live next door.
    你認不出我嗎？我以前住你隔壁。
  3. Doesn't she speak French? She lived in Paris.
    她不會說法文嗎？她在巴黎住過。
  4. Everyone else is eating. Don't you want a slice of birthday cake?
    其他人都在吃。你不想吃一片生日蛋糕嗎？
  5. All the stalls are closed tonight. Doesn't the night market open on Mondays?
    今晚所有的攤子都沒開。夜市星期一不開嗎？

② Isn't / Aren't + 主詞 ...

  1. Look at that blue sky. Isn't it a beautiful day?
    你看那片藍天。今天天氣不是很好嗎？
  2. Aren't you hungry? You haven't eaten all day.
    你不餓嗎？你一整天都沒吃東西。
  3. Look at the man in the red cap. Isn't that your brother over there?
    你看那個戴紅帽子的男人。那邊那個不是你哥哥嗎？
  4. You're travelling alone across Africa. Aren't your parents worried about you?
    你一個人在非洲旅行。你爸媽不擔心你嗎？
  5. We finally reached the top of the hill. Isn't the view amazing from here?
    我們終於到了山頂。從這裡看出去的景色不是很棒嗎？
  6. It's nearly midnight, and the kids are asleep on the sofa. Isn't it time to go home?
    快半夜了，孩子們都在沙發上睡著了。不是該回家了嗎？

③ Didn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. I passed my driving test. Didn't I tell you?
    我考過駕照了，我沒跟你說嗎？
  2. The guard came to my car window. Didn't you see the "No Parking" sign by the gate?
    警衛走到我的車窗旁邊。你沒看到大門旁邊禁止停車的牌子嗎？
  3. The receptionist can't find your booking. Didn't the hotel send you a confirmation email?
    櫃台人員找不到你的訂房。飯店沒寄確認信給你嗎？
  4. Your face looks familiar. Didn't we sit next to each other on the flight to Tokyo?
    你看起來很面熟。我們不是在飛東京的班機上坐隔壁嗎？
  5. Why are you studying on Friday night? Didn't the teacher say the test was on Monday?
    你為什麼星期五晚上在讀書？老師不是說考試在星期一嗎？

④ Can't / Haven't / Won't + 主詞 ...

  1. It's two in the morning. Can't you get to sleep?
    已經凌晨兩點了，你睡不著嗎？
  2. Haven't you packed your suitcase yet? We leave at six.
    你還沒打包行李嗎？我們六點就要出發了。
  3. I've made a big pot of soup. Won't you stay for dinner?
    我煮了一大鍋湯。你不留下來吃晚餐嗎？
  4. I'm trying to study. Can't you turn the music down a bit?
    我在讀書。你不能把音樂關小聲一點嗎？
  5. I remember that fountain. Haven't we been here before?
    我記得那座噴泉。我們以前不是來過這裡嗎？

⑤ 回答和不同的句型

  1. A: Didn't you get my message? B: No, I didn't. My phone was off.
    A：你沒收到我的訊息嗎？B：沒有，我的手機關機了。
  2. A: Aren't you cold? B: No, I'm fine.
    A：你不冷嗎？B：不冷，我還好。
  3. We walked out of the cinema smiling. Wasn't the film great?
    我們笑著走出電影院。那部電影不是很好看嗎？
  4. I think I saw you in the crowd on Saturday. Weren't you at the concert too?
    我好像星期六在人群裡看到你。你不是也有去演唱會嗎？
  5. It's ten o'clock on a Monday. Shouldn't you be at work?
    今天星期一，現在十點。你不是應該在上班嗎？
  6. You're an hour late. Couldn't you find a parking space?
    你晚了一個小時。你找不到停車位嗎？
  7. Taste it, Mum. Doesn't this soup need more salt?
    媽，你嚐嚐。這湯不用再加點鹽嗎？
  8. I saw a cake in the shop and thought of you. Isn't your birthday next week?
    我在店裡看到一個蛋糕就想到你。你的生日不是下禮拜嗎？
  9. He came on Monday and Tuesday. Hasn't the plumber fixed the shower yet?
    他星期一和星期二都來了。水電工還沒修好淋浴間嗎？
