Don't forget your electric guitar.

別忘了你的電吉他。

否⁠定祈⁠使⁠句用 Don't 開⁠頭，沒⁠有主⁠詞

別忘了你的電吉他。

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面加 be

Don't forget your electric guitar.

別遲到。

告⁠示和說⁠明書用 Do not

Don't forget your electric guitar.

請勿將行李留置無人看管。

please 可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首或句⁠尾

Don't forget your electric guitar.

句首

Please don't touch the paintings.

請不要摸畫。
句尾

Don't park here, please.

請不要在這裡停車。

否⁠定祈⁠使⁠句不⁠用 not 開⁠頭

Don't forget your electric guitar.

Not forget your keys.

Don't forget your keys.

Don't 後⁠面要接動⁠詞，形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面加 be

Don't forget your electric guitar.

Don't nervous.

Don't be nervous.

祈⁠使⁠句不⁠分人⁠稱，不⁠用 doesn't

Don't forget your electric guitar.

Doesn't touch that, Tom.

Don't touch that, Tom.

Don't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 ⁠-⁠ing

Don't forget your electric guitar.

Don't worrying about it.

Don't worry about it.

例句

① Don't + 原形動詞

  1. See you at band practice on Saturday. Don't forget your electric guitar.
    星期六練團見。別忘了你的電吉他。
  2. Don't touch the plate. It's very hot.
    別碰盤子。很燙。
  3. Don't worry about the dishes. I'll do them later.
    碗盤不用管。我等一下洗。
  4. Come at five, and don't bring anything. We have lots of food.
    五點來，什麼都不用帶。我們有很多吃的。
  5. Don't tell Mum about the broken window. I'll fix it.
    破窗戶的事別跟媽媽說。我會修好。
  6. Don't open the door to strangers when I'm out.
    我不在的時候，別幫陌生人開門。
  7. Don't eat the cake yet. It's for the party.
    先別吃蛋糕。那是派對要用的。
  8. Don't run by the pool, kids! You'll fall.
    孩子們，別在泳池旁邊跑！會跌倒。
  9. Don't drink the water from the tap here. Buy bottled water.
    這裡的自來水別喝。買瓶裝水。
  10. Don't wait for me. I'll meet you at the cinema.
    別等我。我在電影院跟你們會合。
  11. Don't forget to send me a postcard from Rome.
    別忘了從羅馬寄明信片給我。
  12. Don't laugh! I'm trying my best.
    別笑！我已經盡力了。
  13. Don't call me after ten. I go to bed early.
    十點以後別打給我。我很早睡。
  14. Don't miss the last train. It leaves at 11.30.
    別錯過最後一班火車。十一點半開。

② Don't + be

  1. Don't be late. The film starts at eight.
    別遲到。電影八點開始。
  2. Don't be nervous. You know all the answers.
    別緊張。答案你都知道。
  3. Don't be silly. Of course you can come with us.
    別傻了。你當然可以跟我們一起去。
  4. Don't be sad. We'll visit you in the summer.
    別難過。我們夏天會去看你。
  5. Don't be a baby. It's only a small cut.
    別像個小孩一樣。只是一個小傷口。
  6. Don't be afraid of the dog. He only wants to play.
    別怕那隻狗。牠只是想玩。

③ Please don't / Don't ..., please

  1. Please don't touch the paintings.
    請不要摸畫。
  2. Please don't talk during the film.
    看電影的時候請不要講話。
  3. Don't park in front of the garage, please.
    請不要在車庫前面停車。
  4. Please don't be angry with me. It was an accident.
    請不要生我的氣。那是意外。

④ Do not：告示和說明書

  1. Do not leave bags unattended.
    請勿將行李留置無人看管。
  2. Do not use the lift in case of fire.
    火災時請勿搭乘電梯。
  3. Do not take this medicine on an empty stomach.
    本藥請勿空腹服用。
  4. Do not put metal objects in the microwave.
    請勿將金屬物品放入微波爐。

⑤ 叫別人做另一件事

  1. Don't take a taxi. Take the underground. It's faster.
    別搭計程車。搭地鐵。比較快。
  2. Don't buy a new bike. You can have my old one.
    別買新的腳踏車。你可以拿我的舊車去騎。
  3. Don't shout. Just tell me what happened.
    別大吼。好好跟我說發生了什麼事。
