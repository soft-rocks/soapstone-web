You look very pale. You can't be feeling well.

你臉色很蒼白，一定是身體不舒服。

主⁠詞 + can't / cannot + be + 形⁠容⁠詞 / 名⁠詞

can't / cannot、be + 形⁠容⁠詞 / 名⁠詞、be + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

can't / cannot說話者很確定某件事不是真的。
be + 形容詞 / 名詞推測現在的狀態。
be + 動詞⁠-⁠ing推測現在正在發生的事。

回⁠應別⁠人⁠的話

例句

"Tom won the lottery." "That can't be true!"

「Tom 中樂透了。」「不可能吧！」

It can't be true. 或 That can't be right. 常用來回應很難相信的消息。

can't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 be

You look very pale. You can't be feeling well.

He can't is at home. His car isn't there.

He can't be at home. His car isn't there.

can't 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞⁠時，中⁠間要⁠有 be

You look very pale. You can't be feeling well.

You can't hungry. You just ate.

You can't be hungry. You just ate.

推⁠測正⁠在發⁠生⁠的⁠事，用 can't be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

You look very pale. You can't be feeling well.

She can't working now. It's Sunday.

She can't be working now. It's Sunday.

例句

① can't + be + 形容詞

  1. The shop can't be closed. The lights are on.
    那家店不可能關門了，燈還亮著。
  2. You can't be hungry. We had lunch an hour ago.
    你不可能餓，我們一個小時前才吃過午餐。
  3. That restaurant can't be expensive. It's always full of students.
    那家餐廳不可能很貴，裡面總是坐滿學生。
  4. Your coffee can't be hot any more. You made it an hour ago.
    你的咖啡不可能還是熱的，你一個小時前就泡了。
  5. The answer can't be right. Check your maths again.
    這個答案不可能對，再檢查一次你的計算。
  6. The film can't be very good. Nobody is talking about it.
    那部電影不可能很好看，都沒有人在談論。
  7. She can't be serious about leaving her job.
    她不可能是認真要辭職的。
  8. The water can't be cold. The sun has been on it all day.
    水不可能冷，太陽照了一整天。

② can't + be + 名詞 / 地點

  1. He can't be a criminal. He's too young.
    他不可能是罪犯，他太年輕了。
  2. That can't be Anna. She's in Australia this month.
    那不可能是 Anna，她這個月在澳洲。
  3. This can't be the right road. We've passed that church twice.
    這不可能是對的路，我們經過那間教堂兩次了。
  4. They can't be at home. All the lights are off.
    他們不可能在家，燈全都關著。
  5. It can't be his car. He sold it last year.
    那不可能是他的車，他去年就賣掉了。
  6. You can't be his sister! You look so different.
    你不可能是他姊姊！你們長得一點都不像。
  7. That can't be the post office. It closed years ago.
    那不可能是郵局，它好幾年前就關了。

③ can't + be + 動詞-ing

  1. She can't be working today. It's a holiday.
    她今天不可能在上班，今天放假。
  2. You can't be leaving already! The party has just started.
    你不可能已經要走了吧！派對才剛開始。
  3. They can't be telling the truth. Their stories are different.
    他們不可能說的是實話，他們的說法不一樣。
  4. The baby can't be sleeping with all this noise.
    這麼吵，寶寶不可能在睡覺。
  5. He can't be driving. His car is outside.
    他不可能在開車，他的車停在外面。
  6. You can't be thinking of buying that old house!
    你不可能在考慮買那間舊房子吧！
  7. It can't be raining again!
    不可能又在下雨吧！

④ It / That + can't + be + true / right

  1. That can't be true! I saw him yesterday.
    不可能是真的！我昨天還看到他。
  2. "The bill is two hundred pounds." "That can't be right!"
    帳單是兩百英鎊。不可能吧！
  3. Ten o'clock already? That can't be right.
    已經十點了？不可能吧。
  4. "Our teacher is leaving." "It can't be true. She loves this school."
    我們老師要走了。不可能是真的，她很愛這間學校。
  5. That can't be the end of the story.
    故事不可能就這樣結束。
  6. That can't be all the money. Where's the rest?
    不可能只有這些錢，其他的呢？

⑤ It cannot be + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. It cannot be good for these animals to live away from their natural home.
    讓這些動物離開牠們的自然環境，不可能對牠們有好處。
  2. It can't be easy to look after five children.
    照顧五個小孩不可能很輕鬆。
  3. It can't be healthy to eat fast food every day.
    每天吃速食不可能健康。
