have 和 do 後⁠面沒⁠有別⁠的動⁠詞⁠時，是一⁠般動⁠詞

do
做

I do my homework after dinner.

我吃完晚餐寫功課。

例句

① 助動詞 have：肯定

  1. Sorry, I think I have left my suitcase at your house.
    抱歉，我好像把行李箱留在你家了。
  2. Dear Maria, it has been a long time since I last saw you.
    親愛的 Maria，好久沒見到你了。
  3. "Is Tom still at work?" "No, he's gone home."
    Tom 還在上班嗎？沒有，他回家了。
  4. We've booked a table for eight o'clock.
    我們訂了八點的位子。

② 助動詞 have：否定

  1. I'm sorry I haven't emailed you in a long time.
    抱歉我很久沒寫電子郵件給你了。
  2. "Can we eat?" "Not yet. Dad hasn't come home."
    可以吃了嗎？還不行。爸爸還沒回家。
  3. I haven't read the book, so don't tell me the ending.
    我還沒看那本書，所以別告訴我結局。

③ 助動詞 have：問句

  1. "Have you got a mobile phone?" "Yes, but it's very old."
    你有手機嗎？有，可是很舊了。
  2. "Have you ever been to Japan?" "Yes, twice."
    你去過日本嗎？去過，兩次。
  3. "Has the bus left?" "Yes, you just missed it."
    公車走了嗎？走了，你剛好錯過。

④ 助動詞 do：現在簡單式的否定

  1. My daughter doesn't eat vegetables, so I hide them in her soup.
    我女兒不吃蔬菜，所以我把蔬菜藏在她的湯裡。
  2. We don't have class on Wednesday afternoons.
    我們星期三下午沒課。
  3. "Why are you walking?" "My car doesn't start in cold weather."
    你怎麼用走的？我的車天冷就發不動。

⑤ 助動詞 do：現在簡單式的問句

  1. "Do you like shopping?" "Not really. I prefer buying things online."
    你喜歡逛街嗎？不太喜歡。我比較喜歡在網路上買東西。
  2. "Does your sister work here?" "Yes, she works in the shop downstairs."
    你姊姊在這裡上班嗎？對，她在樓下的店上班。
  3. "What time do you usually get up?" "At half past six."
    你平常幾點起床？六點半。

⑥ 助動詞 do：過去簡單式的否定和問句

  1. Why didn't you come to my birthday party? We missed you.
    你為什麼沒來我的生日派對？我們都很想你。
  2. "Did you buy a new mobile phone?" "Yes, my old one broke."
    你買了新手機嗎？對，我的舊手機壞了。
  3. I didn't sleep well last night because of the noise next door.
    隔壁太吵，我昨晚沒睡好。
  4. "Where did you go on holiday?" "We went to the beach in Kenting."
    你去哪裡度假？我們去了墾丁的海邊。
  5. The children didn't want to leave the zoo.
    孩子們不想離開動物園。

⑦ 簡答

  1. "Do you want to come with us?" "Yes, I do. I'm really excited about the trip."
    你想跟我們一起去嗎？想。我很期待這趟旅行。
  2. "Does your flat have a balcony?" "No, it doesn't."
    你的公寓有陽台嗎？沒有。
  3. "Did you lock the door?" "Yes, I did. Don't worry."
    你鎖門了嗎？鎖了。別擔心。
  4. "Have you met my brother?" "No, I haven't."
    你見過我哥哥嗎？沒見過。
  5. "Has your son started school?" "Yes, he has. He loves it."
    你兒子開始上學了嗎？開始了。他很喜歡。

⑧ 代替前面的動詞

  1. I thought I wouldn't find nice clothes in that shop, but surprisingly, I did.
    我以為在那家店找不到好看的衣服，沒想到找到了。
  2. My sister likes horror films, but I don't.
    我姊姊喜歡恐怖片，可是我不喜歡。
  3. "I need a coffee before the meeting." "So do I. Let's go to the café."
    開會前我需要來杯咖啡。我也是。我們去咖啡廳吧。
  4. Everyone said he would fail the test, but he didn't.
    大家都說他考試會不及格，結果他沒有。
  5. "Who broke the window?" "Tom did."
    誰打破窗戶的？Tom。
