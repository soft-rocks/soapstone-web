Aren't you excited about it?

這件事你不覺得興奮嗎？

否⁠定疑⁠問⁠句把助⁠動⁠詞和 n't 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

這件事你不覺得興奮嗎？

do、can 這⁠類助⁠動⁠詞也一⁠樣放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Aren't you excited about it?

Don't

Doesn't she like fish?

她不喜歡魚嗎？
Can't

Can't you hear the music from next door?

你聽不到隔壁的音樂嗎？

不⁠縮⁠寫⁠時，not 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

Aren't you excited about it?

你沒聽到警報嗎？

這個形式很正式，說話時很少用。

肯⁠定⁠的直⁠述⁠句接否⁠定⁠的附⁠加問⁠句，否⁠定⁠的接肯⁠定⁠的

Aren't you excited about it?

肯定 + 否定

Your cousin works in Paris, doesn't he?

你表哥在巴黎工作，對吧？
否定 + 肯定

You haven't seen my keys, have you?

你沒看到我的鑰匙吧？

直述句沒有助動詞時，附加問句用 do、does 或 did。

I am 的否⁠定附⁠加問⁠句用 aren't I

Aren't you excited about it?

下一個是我，對吧？

n't 和助⁠動⁠詞連在⁠一⁠起，放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Aren't you excited about it?

Is it n't time for lunch?

Isn't it time for lunch?

附⁠加問⁠句不⁠重⁠複動⁠詞

You've finished, haven't you finished?

You've finished, haven't you?

直⁠述⁠句沒⁠有助⁠動⁠詞⁠時，附⁠加問⁠句用 do

She lives near you, isn't she?

She lives near you, doesn't she?

附⁠加問⁠句用代⁠名⁠詞，不⁠用名⁠詞

Aren't you excited about it?

Your brother is a doctor, isn't your brother?

Your brother is a doctor, isn't he?

I am 的附⁠加問⁠句不⁠是 amn't I

I'm in the team, amn't I?

I'm in the team, aren't I?

例句

① 否定疑問句：Aren't / Isn't + 主詞

  1. You start your new job tomorrow. Aren't you nervous?
    你明天開始新工作。你不緊張嗎？
  2. You've been walking all day. Aren't you tired?
    你走了一整天了。你不累嗎？
  3. Isn't that your sister over there by the door?
    門邊那個人不是你姊姊嗎？
  4. Let's go to the zoo instead. Isn't the gallery shut today?
    我們改去動物園吧。美術館今天不是沒開嗎？

② 否定疑問句：Don't / Doesn't / Didn't + 主詞

  1. Everybody loves this game. Don't you think it's fun?
    大家都很愛這個遊戲。你不覺得很好玩嗎？
  2. Don't you remember me? We were in the same class.
    你不記得我了嗎？我們以前同班。
  3. Ask him about the flights. Doesn't your brother work at the airport?
    問他航班的事吧。你哥哥不是在機場工作嗎？
  4. Didn't you get my message? I sent it this morning.
    你沒收到我的訊息嗎？我今天早上傳的。
  5. Didn't they tell you about the change of plan?
    他們沒跟你說計畫改了嗎？

③ 否定疑問句：Can't / Won't / Haven't + 主詞

  1. Your dad really wants you at the concert. Can't you meet your friends another day?
    你爸很希望你去聽音樂會。你不能改天再跟朋友見面嗎？
  2. Won't you stay for dinner? There's plenty of food.
    你不留下來吃晚餐嗎？吃的東西很多。
  3. Haven't you finished yet? We're going to be late.
    你還沒好嗎？我們要遲到了。
  4. Can't you sleep? Do you want some warm milk?
    你睡不著嗎？要不要喝點熱牛奶？

④ 肯定的直述句 + 否定的附加問句

  1. Let's drive to the coast. You're free on Saturday, aren't you?
    我們開車去海邊吧。你星期六有空，對吧？
  2. A picnic by the river would be lovely, wouldn't it?
    在河邊野餐會很棒，對吧？
  3. Close the window, please. It's cold today, isn't it?
    請把窗戶關上。今天很冷，對吧？
  4. Come and say hello. You've met my husband, haven't you?
    過來打個招呼。你見過我先生，對吧？
  5. Can you give Anna a lift? She lives near the station, doesn't she?
    你可以載 Anna 一程嗎？她住在車站附近，對吧？
  6. We should leave now. The film starts at eight, doesn't it?
    我們該出發了。電影八點開始，對吧？
  7. Ask the Browns about Rome. They went to Italy last summer, didn't they?
    去問 Brown 一家羅馬的事。他們去年夏天去了義大利，對吧？
  8. We're going sailing tomorrow. You can swim, can't you?
    我們明天要去駕帆船。你會游泳，對吧？
  9. Don't cook dinner for us. We'll be back by six, won't we?
    別幫我們煮晚餐。我們六點以前會回來，對吧？
  10. I signed up last week. I'm on the list, aren't I?
    我上星期報名了。名單上有我，對吧？

⑤ 否定的直述句 + 肯定的附加問句

  1. I'm making fish tonight. You don't eat meat, do you?
    我今晚要煮魚。你不吃肉，對吧？
  2. Let's walk to the restaurant. It isn't far, is it?
    我們走路去餐廳吧。不遠，對吧？
  3. Sam promised to ring this morning. He hasn't called yet, has he?
    Sam 答應今天早上會打來。他還沒打來，對吧？
  4. We're nearly at the theatre. You didn't leave the tickets at home, did you?
    我們快到劇院了。你沒把票留在家裡吧？
  5. Speak quietly. The neighbours can't hear us, can they?
    小聲一點。鄰居聽不到我們說話，對吧？

⑥ 助動詞 + 主詞 + not：不縮寫的否定疑問句

  1. Do you not agree with the committee's decision?
    你不同意委員會的決定嗎？
  2. Is it not true that the council knew about the problem?
    市議會早就知道這個問題，不是嗎？
  3. Has the manager not explained the new rules to you?
    經理還沒跟你說明新的規定嗎？
