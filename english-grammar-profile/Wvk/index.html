We can meet tomorrow.

我們可以明天見面。

時⁠間副⁠詞放⁠在句⁠尾

我們可以明天見面。

受⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞後⁠面、時⁠間副⁠詞前⁠面

We can meet tomorrow.

我今晚打電話給你。

地⁠點在⁠前⁠面，時⁠間在⁠後⁠面

We can meet tomorrow.

我們昨天去了動物園。

句⁠尾⁠的時⁠間副⁠詞

We can meet tomorrow.

today、tomorrow、yesterday、tonight哪一天或哪一個晚上
now現在
soon不久以後
later等一下，晚一點
early、late比平常或約好的時間早、晚
again再一次

late 是很⁠晚，lately 是最⁠近

late

I got up late.

我起得很晚。
lately

Have you seen Tom lately?

你最近有看到 Tom 嗎？

時⁠間副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I will call tonight my grandma.

I will call my grandma tonight.

tomorrow 前⁠面不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

We can meet tomorrow.

See you at tomorrow.

See you tomorrow.

地⁠點放⁠在時⁠間前⁠面

We went yesterday to the beach.

We went to the beach yesterday.

表⁠示很晚用 late

On Saturdays I wake up lately.

On Saturdays I wake up late.

例句

① today、tomorrow、yesterday、tonight

  1. My cousin is flying back to Canada tomorrow.
    我表哥明天要飛回加拿大。
  2. There's a football match on TV tonight.
    今晚電視上有足球比賽。
  3. I finished the whole book yesterday.
    我昨天把整本書看完了。
  4. Can you pick up the kids from school today?
    你今天可以去學校接孩子嗎？
  5. The new supermarket opens tomorrow.
    新的超市明天開幕。
  6. Mum is making lasagne tonight.
    媽媽今晚要做千層麵。

② now、soon、later

  1. I'm busy at the moment, so I'll text you later.
    我現在在忙，晚點再傳訊息給你。
  2. The plumber said he would be here soon.
    水電工說他很快就會到。
  3. Please turn off your phones now.
    現在請把手機關機。
  4. Dinner will be ready soon.
    晚餐快好了。
  5. We can talk about the holiday later.
    我們晚點再討論假期的事。
  6. My sister lives in Manchester now.
    我姊姊現在住在曼徹斯特。

③ early、late

  1. The train arrived ten minutes late.
    火車晚了十分鐘到。
  2. I want to go to bed early tonight.
    我今晚想早點睡。
  3. Sorry, I'm going to be a bit late.
    抱歉，我會晚一點到。
  4. Our neighbours always get up early.
    我們的鄰居都很早起床。
  5. He worked late every night last week.
    他上禮拜每天晚上都工作到很晚。

④ again

  1. I failed my driving test, but I'm taking it again in May.
    我駕照考試沒過，不過五月要再考一次。
  2. I lost my umbrella again.
    我又把雨傘弄丟了。
  3. We'd love to visit your town again.
    我們很想再去你們那裡玩。
  4. Let's try the new Thai restaurant again next month.
    我們下個月再去那家新的泰式餐廳吧。
  5. The printer isn't working again.
    印表機又壞了。

⑤ 受詞 + 時間副詞

  1. I'll send you the photos tomorrow.
    我明天把照片傳給你。
  2. She cleaned her bedroom yesterday.
    她昨天打掃了她的房間。
  3. Don't forget to water the plants tonight.
    今晚別忘了給植物澆水。
  4. We watched a scary film yesterday.
    我們昨天看了一部恐怖片。

⑥ 地點 + 時間副詞

  1. My parents are coming to my school tomorrow.
    我爸媽明天要來我學校。
  2. I was at the dentist's yesterday.
    我昨天去看牙醫了。
  3. Let's meet at the café later.
    我們晚點在咖啡店碰面吧。
  4. My brother got home from the party really late.
    我哥哥參加完派對，很晚才回到家。
  5. We're going to the cinema tonight.
    我們今晚要去看電影。
