This year I've met lots of new people that I didn't use to talk to.

我們以前都不跟彼此講話。

主⁠詞 + didn't use to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

didn't use to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

didn't use to以前不常、以前不會。
原形動詞過去不做的事。

現⁠在不⁠一⁠樣了

This year I've met lots of new people that I didn't use to talk to.

例句

We didn't use to talk to each other. Now we're good friends.

我們以前不跟對方說話。現在我們是好朋友。

didn't use to 常接著說現在的情況。

否⁠定要⁠用 didn't

This year I've met lots of new people that I didn't use to talk to.

I not used to like fish.

I didn't use to like fish.

use to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This year I've met lots of new people that I didn't use to talk to.

She didn't use to going out.

She didn't use to go out.

use 後⁠面要接 to

This year I've met lots of new people that I didn't use to talk to.

We didn't use talk much.

We didn't use to talk much.

例句

① 主詞 + didn't use to + 原形動詞, but now + 句子

  1. My sister and I fought all the time. We didn't use to talk to each other, but now we're very close.
    我妹妹和我以前一直吵架。我們以前不跟對方說話，可是現在我們很親近。
  2. I didn't use to like coffee, but now I drink three cups a day.
    我以前不喜歡咖啡，可是現在一天喝三杯。
  3. He didn't use to exercise, but now he runs every morning.
    他以前不運動，可是現在每天早上都跑步。
  4. We didn't use to have a car, but now we drive everywhere.
    我們以前沒有車，可是現在去哪裡都開車。
  5. She didn't use to wear glasses, but now she needs them to read.
    她以前不戴眼鏡，可是現在讀書需要戴。
  6. I didn't use to enjoy cooking, but now it's my favourite hobby.
    我以前不喜歡煮飯，可是現在那是我最喜歡的嗜好。

② 主詞 + didn't used to + 原形動詞：常見的寫法

  1. This year I've met lots of new people that I didn't used to talk to, like Monica and Nina.
    今年我認識了很多以前不會說話的新朋友，像莫妮卡和妮娜。
  2. I didn't used to watch the news, but now I watch it every night.
    我以前不看新聞，可是現在每天晚上都看。
  3. My brother didn't used to help at home, but he does now.
    我哥哥以前不在家幫忙，可是他現在會了。
  4. We didn't used to go abroad for our holidays.
    我們以前放假不出國。
  5. She didn't used to like her neighbours, but now they're friends.
    她以前不喜歡她的鄰居，可是現在他們是朋友了。
  6. The town didn't used to have a cinema.
    這個鎮以前沒有電影院。

③ There didn't use to be + 名詞：以前沒有

  1. There didn't use to be so many cars on this road.
    這條路以前沒有這麼多車。
  2. There didn't use to be a supermarket in our village.
    我們村子以前沒有超市。
  3. There didn't use to be any traffic lights here.
    這裡以前沒有紅綠燈。
  4. There didn't use to be so much homework at our school.
    我們學校以前沒有這麼多功課。
  5. There didn't use to be a bridge across the river.
    以前沒有橋可以過河。
  6. There didn't use to be any tourists in this part of town.
    城裡的這一帶以前沒有觀光客。

④ 主詞 + didn't use to + be + 形容詞：以前不是這樣

  1. I didn't use to be so busy before I started this job.
    我開始這份工作以前沒有這麼忙。
  2. The beach didn't use to be so crowded in summer.
    夏天海灘以前沒有這麼擠。
  3. My dad didn't use to be very patient with us.
    我爸爸以前對我們沒有很有耐心。
  4. Food didn't use to be so expensive.
    食物以前沒有這麼貴。
  5. He didn't use to be interested in politics.
    他以前對政治沒興趣。
  6. The streets didn't use to be so dirty.
    街道以前沒有這麼髒。

⑤ I didn't use to + 原形動詞, + until / when + 子句

  1. I didn't use to read much until I discovered detective stories.
    我以前不太讀書，直到我發現偵探小說。
  2. We didn't use to eat fish until we moved to the coast.
    我們以前不吃魚，直到我們搬到海邊。
  3. She didn't use to travel alone until she went to university.
    她以前不會一個人旅行，直到她上大學。
  4. I didn't use to sleep well until I stopped drinking tea at night.
    我以前睡不好，直到我晚上不再喝茶。
  5. He didn't use to speak in class until he joined the debate club.
    他以前上課不發言，直到他加入辯論社。
  6. We didn't use to recycle until the town gave us special bins.
    我們以前不做回收，直到鎮上給了我們專用的垃圾桶。
  7. I didn't use to like dogs until my neighbour's puppy followed me home.
    我以前不喜歡狗，直到鄰居的小狗跟著我回家。
