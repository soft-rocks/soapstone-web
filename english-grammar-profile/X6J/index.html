她哭了很久，但是沒有放棄。

不⁠及物片⁠語動⁠詞後⁠面不⁠接⁠受⁠詞

She cried a lot, but she didn't give up.

她哭了很久，但是沒有放棄。

give up 的意思是放棄。

意⁠思和動⁠詞本⁠身⁠的意⁠思不⁠同

She cried a lot, but she didn't give up.

break down
故障

Our car broke down on the motorway.

我們的車在高速公路上拋錨了。
take off
起飛

The plane took off at ten.

飛機十點起飛。
work out
健身

I work out three times a week.

我一個星期健身三次。

沒有受詞

冷⁠靜⁠下⁠來是 calm down

例句

① give up / carry on

  1. "This puzzle is too hard." "Don't give up! You're nearly there."
    這個拼圖太難了。別放棄！你快完成了。
  2. I tried to learn the guitar, but I gave up after a month.
    我試過學吉他，可是一個月後就放棄了。
  3. The teacher stopped for a moment and then carried on.
    老師停了一下，然後繼續講。
  4. "Should I stop?" "No, carry on. It's a good story."
    我要停下來嗎？不用，繼續說。這個故事很好聽。

② hang out / stay up

  1. My life was too busy, so I just needed to hang out with friends for a while.
    我的生活太忙了，所以我需要跟朋友出去玩一陣子。
  2. "What did you do at the weekend?" "Nothing much. We just hung out at the park."
    你週末做了什麼？沒什麼。我們就在公園閒晃。
  3. I stayed up until two to finish my essay.
    我熬夜到兩點寫完報告。
  4. The kids can stay up late on Saturdays.
    孩子們星期六可以晚睡。

③ run out / break down

  1. My phone died because the battery had run out.
    我的手機關機了，因為電池沒電了。
  2. "Can I have some more coffee?" "Sorry, it's run out."
    可以再給我一點咖啡嗎？抱歉，沒了。
  3. The bus broke down, so we had to walk to school.
    公車拋錨了，所以我們得走路去學校。
  4. Our washing machine broke down again last night.
    我們的洗衣機昨晚又壞了。

④ set off / take off

  1. "What time are we leaving?" "We'll set off at seven to avoid the traffic."
    我們幾點走？我們七點出發，避開車潮。
  2. They set off early and reached the top of the mountain by noon.
    他們很早出發，中午就到了山頂。
  3. Please turn off your phones. The plane is about to take off.
    請關手機。飛機要起飛了。
  4. Our flight took off two hours late.
    我們的班機晚了兩個小時起飛。

⑤ calm down / slow down

  1. "Calm down. Tell me what happened." "Someone took my bag!"
    冷靜一點。跟我說發生了什麼事。有人拿走了我的包包！
  2. After the match, it took the fans an hour to calm down.
    比賽結束後，球迷過了一個小時才冷靜下來。
  3. "You're driving too fast. Slow down!" "OK, OK."
    你開太快了。慢一點！好啦好啦。
  4. The doctor told my dad to slow down and work less.
    醫生叫我爸放慢腳步，少工作一點。

⑥ grow up / move in / move out

  1. "Where did you grow up?" "In a small town near the sea."
    你在哪裡長大的？在海邊的一個小鎮。
  2. When I grow up, I want to be a vet.
    我長大以後想當獸醫。
  3. Our new neighbours moved in last week.
    我們的新鄰居上星期搬進來了。
  4. My brother is moving out next month. He's found a flat near his office.
    我哥哥下個月要搬出去。他找到一間離公司很近的公寓。

⑦ work out / look out

  1. "You look fit." "Thanks. I work out at the gym every morning."
    你看起來很健康。謝謝。我每天早上都去健身房運動。
  2. My sister works out at home because the gym is too expensive.
    我姊姊在家運動，因為健身房太貴了。
  3. Look out! There's a car coming!
    小心！有車來了！
  4. "Look out! The floor is wet." "Thanks, I didn't see it."
    小心！地板是濕的。謝謝，我沒看到。

⑧ 否定和問句

  1. Lisa was very sad, but she didn't give up.
    Lisa 很難過，可是她沒有放棄。
  2. "Why are you late? Did the car break down again?" "Yes, near the bridge."
    你怎麼遲到了？車子又拋錨了嗎？對，在橋附近。
  3. Why didn't the plane take off on time?
    飛機為什麼沒有準時起飛？
