例句

① 過去簡單式加 by

  1. Guess what! My school was chosen by the TV company because it is one of the newest in town.
    你猜怎樣！那家電視公司選了我們學校，因為它是鎮上最新的學校之一。
  2. Look at this old photo of our street. It was taken by my grandfather in 1965.
    你看這張我們這條街的舊照片。是我爺爺 1965 年拍的。
  3. "Who broke the window?" "It was broken by the boy next door. He was playing football."
    誰打破窗戶的？是隔壁的男孩打破的。他在踢足球。
  4. We visited the castle on Saturday. It was built by a rich family in the 15th century.
    我們星期六去參觀那座城堡。它是十五世紀一個有錢人家蓋的。
  5. I love the cake at your party. Was it made by your mum?
    我很喜歡你派對上的蛋糕。是你媽媽做的嗎？
  6. My little brother cried all night. He was bitten by a dog in the park.
    我弟弟哭了一整晚。他在公園被狗咬了。
  7. The winner of the competition was chosen by the students, not the teachers.
    比賽的冠軍是學生選的，不是老師選的。

② 有名的人和作品

  1. We read Romeo and Juliet in class. It was written by Shakespeare over four hundred years ago.
    我們在課堂上讀《羅密歐與茱麗葉》。這是莎士比亞四百多年前寫的。
  2. Everyone in the museum stops in front of this painting. It was painted by Van Gogh.
    博物館裡每個人都會停在這幅畫前面。這是梵谷畫的。
  3. We sing "Yesterday" in our music class. It was written by Paul McCartney.
    我們音樂課唱〈Yesterday〉。這首歌是保羅麥卡尼寫的。
  4. Our science teacher showed us an old phone. She told us the telephone was invented by Alexander Graham Bell.
    自然老師給我們看一支舊電話。她說電話是貝爾發明的。
  5. We're watching a film tonight. It was directed by Steven Spielberg.
    我們今晚要看一部電影。是史蒂芬史匹柏導的。

③ 機構和公司

  1. Our football club had no money for new kit this year. In the end, the shirts were paid for by the local bank.
    我們足球社今年沒錢買新球衣。最後球衣是當地的銀行出錢買的。
  2. There were lots of people in town last week. The new hospital was opened by the mayor.
    上星期鎮上人很多。新醫院由市長主持開幕。
  3. My cousin is very happy. She was offered a job by a big company in London.
    我表姊很開心。倫敦一家大公司要給她一份工作。
  4. The concert is free. It's paid for by the city council.
    這場音樂會免費。錢是市議會出的。
  5. Sorry I'm late. My train was delayed by the snow.
    抱歉我遲到了。我的火車因為下雪誤點了。

④ 現在簡單式和現在完成式加 by

  1. We're too busy to do the gardening ourselves. Our garden is looked after by an old man from the village.
    我們太忙，沒空自己整理花園。我們的花園是村子裡一個老先生在照顧。
  2. My daughter wants to write for this magazine. It's read by millions of teenagers every week.
    我女兒想替這本雜誌寫稿。每個星期有幾百萬個青少年看這本雜誌。
  3. Don't worry about the dog. He's fed by my sister when I'm away.
    別擔心狗。我不在的時候我妹妹會餵牠。
  4. You can't borrow books there any more. The old library has been bought by a hotel company.
    那裡已經不能借書了。舊圖書館被一家旅館公司買下來了。
  5. Have you heard? Our project has been chosen by the judges for the final.
    你聽說了嗎？我們的作品被評審選進決賽了。

⑤ 和別人對比

  1. "Did you paint this?" "No, it was painted by my sister. I only did the small one."
    這是你畫的嗎？不是，是我妹妹畫的。我只畫了小的那張。
  2. The first part of the report was written by Anna, and the second part was written by me.
    報告的第一部分是 Anna 寫的，第二部分是我寫的。
  3. The tickets weren't bought by Dad. They were bought by Grandma as a birthday present.
    票不是爸爸買的。是奶奶買的，當生日禮物。
  4. The dinner was cooked by the children tonight, so be nice about it!
    今晚的晚餐是孩子們煮的，所以說話客氣一點！

⑥ 問誰做的

  1. "This bread is lovely. Who was it made by?" "By the baker on the corner."
    這麵包很好吃。是誰做的？轉角那家麵包店做的。
  2. "Was the fire started by a cigarette?" "The police think so."
    火是菸蒂引起的嗎？警察是這樣認為。
  3. "Who were you taught by at university?" "By Professor Hall. She was great."
    你大學時是誰教你的？Hall 教授。她教得很好。
  4. "Were these flowers sent by your boyfriend?" "No, by my mum!"
    這些花是你男朋友送的嗎？不是，是我媽媽送的！
  5. "Who was the email signed by?" "By the head teacher."
    這封電子郵件是誰署名的？校長。
