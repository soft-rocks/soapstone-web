例句

① 名詞 's + 家人 's + 地方

  1. We had the party at my best friend's brother's flat. He was away for the weekend.
    我們在我最好朋友的哥哥家辦派對。他週末不在家。
  2. The wedding was in Tom's uncle's garden. It was full of roses.
    婚禮在 Tom 的叔叔家的花園舉行。那裡開滿了玫瑰。
  3. I spent the summer at my cousin's aunt's farm in Wales. I fed the sheep every morning.
    我那年夏天在威爾斯，住在我表哥的阿姨的農場。我每天早上餵羊。
  4. We're staying at my wife's sister's cottage this week. It's right by the sea.
    我們這星期住在我太太的姊姊的小屋。它就在海邊。

② 名詞 's + 家人 's + 東西

  1. Nobody knew what he looked like. There were no photographs of him in Ann's mother's albums.
    沒人知道他長什麼樣子。Ann 她媽媽的相簿裡沒有一張他的照片。
  2. I found my grandfather's father's watch in an old box. It's over a hundred years old.
    我在一個舊箱子裡找到我曾祖父的手錶。它有一百多年了。
  3. My sister got married last June. She wore our mother's mother's wedding ring.
    我姊姊去年六月結婚。她戴著我們外婆的結婚戒指。
  4. The story is based on the writer's father's diaries. He found them after the funeral.
    這個故事是根據作者父親的日記寫的。他在葬禮後找到那些日記。

③ 名詞 's + 朋友或同事 's + 東西

  1. My car was at the garage. I borrowed my sister's friend's car for the weekend.
    我的車在修車廠。我週末借了我姊姊的朋友的車。
  2. The police found the thief in his neighbour's son's garage. He was hiding behind some boxes.
    警察在小偷的鄰居兒子的車庫裡找到他。他躲在一些箱子後面。
  3. Have you read my brother's girlfriend's novel? It's really good.
    你讀過我哥哥女朋友寫的小說嗎？寫得很好。
  4. Don't blame my manager. The plan was my manager's boss's idea, not hers.
    別怪我主管。這個計畫是我主管的上司想的，不是她想的。

④ 小說和報導

  1. The detective recognised the victim's brother's handwriting on the letter.
    偵探認出信上是被害人的哥哥的筆跡。
  2. Social workers visited the family. The child had been living at his mother's partner's house for a year.
    社工去了那一家。那個孩子在他媽媽的伴侶家住了一年。
  3. An auction house in London sold the singer's late husband's paintings. They made millions.
    倫敦一家拍賣行賣出了那位歌手已故丈夫的畫。那些畫賣了好幾百萬。
  4. Thieves broke into the museum. They took the king's son's sword.
    小偷闖進博物館。他們偷走了國王兒子的劍。

⑤ 家人和朋友

  1. We're invited to my colleague's daughter's christening. It's on Sunday at the church.
    我們受邀去參加我同事女兒的受洗典禮。典禮在星期天，在教堂舉行。
  2. The dog in the photo is my friend's grandmother's. It's very friendly.
    照片裡的狗是我朋友的奶奶養的。牠很友善。
  3. Jack's wife's family live in Canada. He's moving there with her next year.
    Jack 太太的家人住在加拿大。他明年要跟她搬去那裡。
  4. My daughter needed a dress for the school dance. She borrowed my sister's friend's old dress.
    我女兒需要一件參加學校舞會的洋裝。她借了我姊姊的朋友以前穿的洋裝。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Whose bike is this?" "It's my flatmate's brother's. He left it here."
    這是誰的腳踏車？是我室友的哥哥的。他放在這裡。
  2. "Where did you meet him?" "At Sarah's sister's birthday party."
    你在哪裡認識他的？在 Sarah 她姊姊的生日派對上。
  3. "Is that your coat?" "No, it's my husband's father's."
    那是你的外套嗎？不是，是我公公的。
  4. "Who's that baby?" "That's my cousin's friend's son."
    那個寶寶是誰？是我表姊的朋友的兒子。

⑦ 學校和工作

  1. We went on a school trip. We visited our teacher's father's farm.
    我們去校外教學。我們參觀了我們老師的爸爸的農場。
  2. The office was empty. I left the files on my boss's assistant's desk.
    辦公室裡沒人。我把文件放在我老闆的助理的桌上。
  3. Lucy got the job easily. Her uncle's friend's company needed a designer.
    Lucy 很輕鬆就拿到那份工作。她叔叔的朋友的公司需要一個設計師。
  4. Mum is driving the team to the match today. My brother's coach's car broke down.
    媽媽今天開車載球隊去比賽。我弟弟的教練的車壞了。
  5. It was the best night of the year. We danced at our neighbour's son's wedding.
    那是一年中最棒的晚上。我們在鄰居兒子的婚禮上跳舞。
  6. My friend's dad's jokes are terrible, but I always laugh at them.
    我朋友的爸爸講的笑話很冷，可是我每次都會笑。
  7. You need a new phone? My brother's friend's shop sells cheap phones.
    你需要新手機嗎？我哥哥的朋友的店在賣便宜的手機。
