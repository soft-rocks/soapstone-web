例句

① 名詞 's + 家人 's + 地方

  1. After fourteen months of dating, they spent two weeks together at Emily's grandmother's house.
    交往了十四個月以後，他們在 Emily 的奶奶家一起待了兩個星期。
  2. We had the party at my best friend's parents' flat.
    我們在我最好朋友的爸媽家辦派對。
  3. The wedding was in Tom's uncle's garden.
    婚禮在 Tom 的叔叔家的花園舉行。
  4. I spent the summer at my cousin's aunt's farm in Wales.
    我那年夏天在威爾斯，住在我表哥的阿姨的農場。

② 名詞 's + 家人 's + 東西

  1. Nobody knew what he looked like. There were no photographs of him in Ann's mother's albums.
    沒人知道他長什麼樣子。Ann 她媽媽的相簿裡沒有一張他的照片。
  2. I found my grandfather's father's watch in an old box.
    我在一個舊箱子裡找到我曾祖父的手錶。
  3. She wore her mother's mother's wedding ring.
    她戴著她外婆的結婚戒指。
  4. The story is based on the writer's father's diaries.
    這個故事是根據作者父親的日記寫的。

③ 名詞 's + 朋友或同事 's + 東西

  1. I borrowed my sister's friend's car for the weekend.
    我週末借了我姊姊的朋友的車。
  2. The police found the thief in his neighbour's son's garage.
    警察在他鄰居兒子的車庫裡找到那個小偷。
  3. Have you read my brother's girlfriend's novel? It's really good.
    你讀過我哥哥女朋友寫的小說嗎？寫得很好。
  4. The plan was my manager's boss's idea, not hers.
    這個計畫是我主管的上司想的，不是她想的。

④ 小說和報導

  1. The detective noticed that the letter was written in the victim's brother's handwriting.
    偵探發現那封信是被害人的哥哥的筆跡。
  2. The child had been living at his mother's partner's house for a year.
    那個孩子在他媽媽的伴侶家住了一年。
  3. The singer's late husband's paintings were sold for millions.
    那位歌手已故丈夫的畫賣了好幾百萬。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. We're invited to my colleague's daughter's christening.
    我們受邀去參加我同事女兒的受洗典禮。
  2. The dog in the photo is my friend's grandmother's.
    照片裡的狗是我朋友的奶奶養的。
  3. Jack's wife's family live in Canada.
    Jack 太太的家人住在加拿大。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Whose bike is this?" "It's my flatmate's brother's. He left it here."
    這是誰的腳踏車？是我室友的哥哥的。他放在這裡。
  2. "Where did you meet him?" "At Sarah's sister's birthday party."
    你在哪裡認識他的？在 Sarah 她姊姊的生日派對上。
  3. "Is that your coat?" "No, it's my husband's father's."
    那是你的外套嗎？不是，是我公公的。
