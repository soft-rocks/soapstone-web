For a long time, many politicians have been able to work almost exclusively for their own interests.

很長一段時間以來，很多政客幾乎都只為自己的利益做事。

For a long time, many politicians have been able to work almost exclusively for their own interests.

很多政客幾乎都只為自己的利益做事。

例句

① almost、nearly + exclusively + 介系詞片語

  1. The company makes furniture almost exclusively for hotels.
    這家公司做的家具幾乎都是賣給旅館的。
  2. The charity relies almost exclusively on donations from the public.
    這個慈善團體幾乎完全仰賴民眾的捐款。
  3. My grandfather listens nearly exclusively to jazz from the 1950s.
    我爺爺幾乎只聽 1950 年代的爵士樂。
  4. The research focused almost exclusively on young adults.
    這項研究幾乎只針對年輕人。
  5. The architect designed the building almost exclusively for elderly residents.
    建築師幾乎是專為年長住戶設計這棟大樓的。

② completely、entirely + independently of

  1. Two research teams carried out the studies completely independently of each other.
    兩個研究團隊完全各自獨立進行這兩項研究。
  2. The judges reach their decisions entirely independently of the government.
    法官完全不受政府影響，自行做出判決。
  3. By the age of ten, most children can study quite independently of their parents.
    到了十歲，大部分孩子都能相當程度地不靠父母自己讀書。
  4. The app works completely independently of the internet.
    這個應用程式完全不需要網路就能運作。
  5. The two inventors developed the idea entirely independently of one another.
    兩位發明家完全各自想出了這個點子。

③ quite、completely、entirely + separately from

  1. The hospital runs the new wing quite separately from the main building.
    醫院把新的側樓和主建築分開管理。
  2. Our town collects glass completely separately from other rubbish.
    我們鎮上把玻璃和其他垃圾完全分開回收。
  3. The police interviewed the children entirely separately from their parents.
    警方在父母完全不在場的情況下訪談孩子們。
  4. We need to deal with these two issues quite separately from each other.
    我們得把這兩個問題分開處理。

④ very、rather、quite + differently from、to

  1. Boys and girls often react very differently to the same situation.
    男孩和女孩面對同樣的情況，反應常常很不一樣。
  2. The second film ends rather differently from the book.
    第二部電影的結局和原著相當不同。
  3. My brother thinks quite differently from the rest of the family.
    我哥的想法和家裡其他人很不一樣。
  4. Young people today spend their money very differently from their parents.
    現在的年輕人花錢的方式跟他們的父母很不一樣。
  5. Prices in the capital have developed rather differently to those in the north.
    首都的房價走勢跟北部相當不同。

⑤ remarkably、very、quite、surprisingly + similarly to

  1. The new model behaves remarkably similarly to the old one.
    新型號的表現和舊型號非常相似。
  2. The twins dress very similarly to each other.
    那對雙胞胎穿得很像。
  3. The disease spreads quite similarly to the common cold.
    這種病傳播的方式和普通感冒很像。
  4. In the test, the cheap battery performed surprisingly similarly to the expensive one.
    測試中，便宜的電池表現出乎意料地和貴的很接近。

⑥ entirely、completely、quite + consistently with、regardless of

  1. The staff acted entirely consistently with the company's rules.
    員工的行為完全符合公司的規定。
  2. The government spent the money completely consistently with its promises.
    政府花這筆錢的方式和它的承諾完全一致。
  3. The law applies to everyone, completely regardless of age.
    這條法律適用於每個人，完全不分年齡。
  4. The prize is given to the best student, quite regardless of background.
    這個獎頒給最優秀的學生，完全不看家庭背景。

⑦ so、far too + 副詞 + 介系詞片語

  1. It was so completely in harmony with the sun and the sea that I decided to buy it.
    它和陽光、大海是那麼地和諧，我當場決定買下來。
  2. The committee made the decision far too quickly for anyone to object.
    委員會做決定做得太快，誰都來不及反對。
  3. She sings so differently from her mother that nobody believes they're related.
    她唱歌的方式和她媽媽差太多了，沒人相信她們是母女。
