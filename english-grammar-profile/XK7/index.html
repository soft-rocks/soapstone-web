If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

如果你來巴西，可以住我家。

if 子⁠句用現⁠在簡⁠單⁠式，主⁠要⁠子⁠句用情⁠態動⁠詞

If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

如果明天下雪，我們可能會在家工作。

can 說可⁠以做⁠的事

If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

can

If you finish early, you can go home.

如果你提早做完，可以回家。

may、might、could 說可⁠能發⁠生⁠的事

may

If the traffic is bad, we may miss the start of the film.

如果塞車，我們可能會錯過電影開頭。
could

If we leave now, we could catch the early train.

如果我們現在出發，或許能趕上早班火車。

should、must 說應⁠該做⁠的事

should

If you see the manager, you should ask about the job.

如果你見到經理，應該問問那份工作。
must

If you go to Kyoto, you must see the old temples.

如果你去京都，一定要去看那些古寺。

should 說建議。must 說非做不可的事，或很強的建議。

if 子⁠句放⁠在⁠後⁠面

前面不加逗號

We can go to the museum if it rains.

如果下雨，我們可以去博物館。

原句：邀⁠請對⁠方來住

If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

如果你來巴西，可以住我家。

if 子⁠句不⁠用 will

If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

If you will have time tomorrow, you can help me paint the fence.

If you have time tomorrow, you can help me paint the fence.

情⁠態動⁠詞後⁠面不⁠加 to

If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

If the shop is closed, you can to buy milk at the station.

If the shop is closed, you can buy milk at the station.

情⁠態動⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

If you come to Brazil, you can stay at my home.

If she gets the visa, she can moves to Canada next year.

If she gets the visa, she can move to Canada next year.

will 說確⁠定⁠的結⁠果

will
確定的結果

If you come early, you will see the parade.

如果你早點來，就會看到遊行。
can
可以做的事

If you come early, you can help us set up the chairs.

如果你早點來，可以幫我們擺椅子。

過⁠去簡⁠單式說不⁠太可⁠能⁠的事

現在簡單式
有可能

If you visit Lisbon, you can stay with my aunt.

如果你去里斯本，可以住我阿姨家。
過去簡單式
不太可能

If you visited Lisbon in winter, you could stay with my aunt.

要是你冬天去里斯本，可以住我阿姨家。

例句

① 主要子句用 can

  1. If you're free on Thursday, you can join us for dinner.
    如果你星期四有空，可以跟我們一起吃晚餐。
  2. If it stops raining, the kids can play in the garden.
    如果雨停了，孩子們可以在花園玩。
  3. If you pass your driving test, you can borrow my car.
    如果你考過駕照，可以借我的車。
  4. If we get there early, we can find good seats.
    如果我們早點到，就能找到好位子。
  5. If he needs a lift, he can come with us.
    如果他需要搭便車，可以跟我們一起走。
  6. If you don't like fish, you can have the chicken instead.
    如果你不喜歡魚，可以改點雞肉。
  7. If the hotel is full, we can try the campsite.
    如果飯店客滿，我們可以試試露營區。

② 主要子句用 may 或 might

  1. If the traffic is heavy, we might be a bit late.
    如果車很多，我們可能會晚一點到。
  2. If you ask nicely, she may lend you her camera.
    如果你好好拜託，她可能會借你相機。
  3. If it's sunny tomorrow, we might go to the beach.
    如果明天出太陽，我們可能會去海邊。
  4. If they don't hurry, they may miss the last bus.
    如果他們不快一點，可能會錯過末班公車。
  5. If I finish my homework, I might watch a film later.
    如果我寫完作業，晚點可能會看部電影。
  6. If the price goes down, more people may buy it.
    如果降價，可能會有更多人買。
  7. If you leave the window open, the cat might get out.
    如果你讓窗戶開著，貓可能會跑出去。

③ 主要子句用 could

  1. If we leave at six, we could be there by lunch.
    如果我們六點出發，中午前可能就到了。
  2. If you don't wear a coat, you could catch a cold.
    如果你不穿外套，可能會感冒。
  3. If the weather stays fine, we could have a barbecue.
    如果天氣一直很好，我們可以烤肉。
  4. If you're bored, you could help me in the kitchen.
    如果你很無聊，可以來廚房幫我。
  5. If she gets the scholarship, she could study in Japan.
    如果她拿到獎學金，就可以去日本讀書。
  6. If the meeting ends early, we could go for a coffee.
    如果會議提早結束，我們可以去喝杯咖啡。

④ 主要子句用 should 或 must

  1. If you go to Paris, you should visit the Louvre.
    如果你去巴黎，應該去羅浮宮看看。
  2. If your tooth still hurts tomorrow, you should see a dentist.
    如果你的牙明天還痛，應該去看牙醫。
  3. If you borrow my bike, you must bring it back by Friday.
    如果你借我的腳踏車，星期五前一定要還。
  4. If the alarm goes off, everyone must leave the building.
    如果警報響了，每個人都必須離開大樓。
  5. If you want a good seat, you should book early.
    如果你想要好位子，應該早點訂。
  6. If he wants to join the team, he must come to training.
    如果他想加入球隊，就必須來練習。
  7. If you see Anna, you should tell her the news.
    如果你見到 Anna，應該把這個消息告訴她。

⑤ 主要子句在前面

  1. You can borrow my laptop if yours breaks.
    如果你的筆電壞了，可以借我的。
  2. We might go swimming if the pool is open.
    如果游泳池有開，我們可能會去游泳。
  3. You should take an umbrella if the sky looks grey.
    如果天色看起來很灰，你應該帶把傘。
  4. I could pick you up if your train arrives late.
    如果你的火車晚到，我可以去接你。
  5. The children may get bored if the film is too long.
    如果電影太長，孩子們可能會覺得無聊。
