I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.

我知道，我買了太多東西，可是每一樣我都很喜歡。

too many 加複⁠數名⁠詞

我買了太多東西，

so many 很⁠多，too many 太⁠多

I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.

so many

We visited so many places.

我們去了好多地方。
too many

I bought too many things.

我買了太多東西。

so many 常接 that 子⁠句

I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.

有 that

There were so many people that we couldn't get in.

人多到我們進不去。
沒有 that

I had so many messages I couldn't read them all.

我的訊息多到看不完。

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞用 too much

I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.

I spent too many money in London.

I spent too much money in London.

many 後⁠面⁠接複⁠數名⁠詞

I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.

There are too many car in the city centre.

There are too many cars in the city centre.

放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.

I bought things too many.

I bought too many things.

例句

① so many + 複數名詞

  1. The party was great fun. We played so many games, like dog and bone and four corners.
    派對好好玩。我們玩了好多遊戲，像搶手帕和四個角落。
  2. We visited so many places in Rome, mostly monuments. The weather was perfect.
    我們在羅馬去了好多地方，大部分是古蹟。天氣好極了。
  3. Thank you for all your help. You've done so many nice things for me.
    謝謝你的幫忙。你為我做了好多好事。
  4. There are so many good films on at the cinema this month.
    這個月電影院上映好多好電影。
  5. I've got so many photos from the trip. I'll show you tomorrow.
    我這趟旅行拍了好多照片。明天給你看。

② too many + 複數名詞

  1. I know, I bought too many things, but I liked all of them.
    我知道，我買了太多東西，可是每一樣我都很喜歡。
  2. I can't walk here. There are too many cars in the city centre.
    這裡沒辦法走路。市中心的車太多了。
  3. You've put too many eggs in the cake. It's very heavy.
    你蛋糕裡放了太多蛋。蛋糕好紮實。
  4. Our teacher gives us too many tests.
    我們老師考太多試了。
  5. There were too many people on the bus, so I waited for the next one.
    公車上人太多，所以我等下一班。

③ so many ... that

  1. There were so many people at the concert that we couldn't see the stage.
    演唱會的人多到我們看不到舞台。
  2. He made so many mistakes that he had to write the letter again.
    他錯太多了，只好重寫那封信。
  3. She has so many books that she needs another shelf.
    她的書多到需要再買一個書架。

④ too many：抱怨

  1. My little brother eats too many sweets. His teeth are bad.
    我弟弟吃太多糖果了。他的牙齒不好。
  2. The office has too many meetings. We never finish our work.
    公司的會開太多了。我們的工作從來做不完。
  3. There are too many tourists in the old town in August.
    八月的時候老城區的觀光客太多了。

⑤ 不同的時態

  1. I've had so many emails this week.
    我這星期收到好多電子郵件。
  2. We'll have too many guests if we invite your cousins too.
    如果你的表兄弟姊妹也請，客人就太多了。
  3. They were selling so many things at the market.
    市集上在賣好多東西。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Are you coming to the party?" "No, I've got too many things to do."
    你要來派對嗎？不行，我要做的事太多了。
  2. "How was New York?" "Amazing! There are so many tall buildings."
    紐約怎麼樣？很棒！高樓好多。
  3. "Why are you so tired?" "Too many late nights, I think."
    你怎麼這麼累？我想是熬太多夜了。
  4. "Do you like the new shopping centre?" "Yes, it has so many shops."
    你喜歡那個新的購物中心嗎？喜歡，那裡店好多。
