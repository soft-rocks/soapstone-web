Luckily for you, I kept notes on everything we did.

算你運氣好，我把我們做過的每件事都記下來了。

看⁠法副⁠詞 + for + 人

Luckily for you, I kept notes on everything we did.

算你運氣好，我做了筆記。

for 片⁠語說⁠明看⁠法對誰成⁠立

Luckily for you, I kept notes on everything we did.

說話者的看法

Luckily, the rain stopped.

幸好雨停了。
限於某個人

Luckily for the farmers, the rain stopped.

對農夫來說很幸運，雨停了。

常⁠見⁠的組⁠合

Luckily for you, I kept notes on everything we did.

luckily for、fortunately for對某人來說很幸運
unfortunately for、sadly for對某人來說很不幸
conveniently for對某人來說很方便

especially、probably + 介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

especially + 時間

Especially in winter, the roads here can be dangerous.

特別是冬天，這裡的路可能很危險。
probably + 時間

Probably in the next ten years, engineers will be respected again.

大概在未來十年內，工程師會再受到尊重。

說⁠明對誰好⁠用 for

Luckily for you, I kept notes on everything we did.

Luckily to us, the shop was still open.

Luckily for us, the shop was still open.

for 後⁠面接⁠受格代⁠名⁠詞

Unfortunately for he, the last bus had gone.

Unfortunately for him, the last bus had gone.

特⁠別是⁠用 especially

It's very busy here, specially in August.

It's very busy here, especially in August.

片⁠語後⁠面不⁠加 but

Luckily for you, I kept notes on everything we did.

Unfortunately for me, but I had left my wallet at home.

Unfortunately for me, I had left my wallet at home.

例句

① luckily for、fortunately for

  1. Luckily for me, my sister lent me her car.
    算我運氣好，我姊姊把她的車借給我。
  2. Fortunately for the passengers, the pilot landed the plane safely.
    對乘客來說很幸運，機長讓飛機平安降落。
  3. Luckily for the team, their best player recovered in time.
    對球隊來說很幸運，他們最好的球員及時康復了。
  4. Fortunately for us, the hotel had one room left.
    我們很幸運，那間旅館還剩一間房。
  5. Luckily for the children, school was closed because of the snow.
    孩子們很幸運，學校因為下雪停課了。

② unfortunately for、sadly for

  1. Unfortunately for Tom, the exam was on the one topic he hadn't studied.
    對 Tom 來說很不幸，考試考的正是他唯一沒讀的那個主題。
  2. Sadly for the fans, the concert was cancelled at the last minute.
    對歌迷來說很遺憾，演唱會在最後一刻取消了。
  3. Unfortunately for the company, the new product failed.
    對那家公司來說很不幸，新產品失敗了。
  4. Sadly for my grandmother, she couldn't travel to the wedding.
    對我奶奶來說很遺憾，她沒辦法去參加婚禮。
  5. Unfortunately for the tourists, the museum was closed for repairs.
    對遊客來說很不巧，博物館正在整修，沒有開放。

③ conveniently for

  1. Conveniently for us, the station is right next to the hotel.
    對我們來說很方便，車站就在旅館旁邊。
  2. Conveniently for parents, the school has a free car park.
    對家長來說很方便，學校有免費的停車場。
  3. Conveniently for the staff, the canteen stays open until eight.
    對員工來說很方便，員工餐廳開到八點。
  4. Conveniently for me, the meeting was moved to Friday.
    對我來說剛好方便，會議改到星期五了。

④ especially、particularly + 時間片語

  1. Especially in winter, the roads here can be dangerous.
    特別是冬天，這裡的路可能很危險。
  2. Prices went up sharply, particularly during the last three months.
    價格大幅上漲，特別是最近三個月。
  3. The city is very crowded, especially at weekends.
    這座城市很擠，特別是週末。
  4. Particularly in the early mornings, the park is very peaceful.
    特別是清晨，公園非常寧靜。
  5. Sales fell, especially between January and March.
    銷售量下滑，特別是一月到三月之間。

⑤ especially、particularly + 地點片語

  1. Especially in big cities, rents have become very high.
    特別是在大城市，房租變得非常高。
  2. The disease is common, particularly in tropical countries.
    這種病很常見，特別是在熱帶國家。
  3. Many shops have closed, especially in the town centre.
    很多商店都關了，特別是在市中心。
  4. Particularly in rural areas, buses are not very frequent.
    特別是在鄉下地區，公車班次不多。
  5. English is widely spoken, especially in tourist areas.
    英語在很多地方都通，特別是在觀光區。

⑥ probably + 介系詞片語

  1. Probably in the next few years, most cars will be electric.
    大概在未來幾年內，大部分的車都會是電動車。
  2. Probably by the end of the month, the bridge will be finished.
    大概到月底，這座橋就會完工。
  3. Probably in about two weeks, you'll get the results.
    大概再過兩個禮拜左右，你就會拿到結果。
  4. Probably after the summer, we'll move to a bigger flat.
    大概過了夏天，我們就會搬到大一點的公寓。

⑦ 片語放在句中

  1. The rain, luckily for us, stopped before the wedding.
    對我們來說很幸運，雨在婚禮前停了。
  2. The ferry, unfortunately for the passengers, left six hours late.
    對乘客來說很不幸，渡輪晚了六個小時才開。
