That night Kevin did not sleep; nor did Kristin.

那天晚上 Kevin 沒睡，Kristin 也沒睡。

否⁠定子⁠句後⁠面⁠接 nor，nor 後⁠面⁠倒裝

That night Kevin did not sleep; nor did Kristin.

我不吃肉，也不吃魚。

主⁠詞不⁠同、動⁠作一⁠樣⁠時，nor 後⁠面只⁠寫助⁠動⁠詞和主⁠詞

That night Kevin did not sleep; nor did Kristin.

She doesn't like coffee, nor does her husband.

她不喜歡咖啡，她先生也不喜歡。

nor 後⁠面要倒⁠裝

That night Kevin did not sleep; nor did Kristin.

We can't stay long, nor we can come back tomorrow.

We can't stay long, nor can we come back tomorrow.

nor 已⁠經是否⁠定，不⁠再⁠加 not

That night Kevin did not sleep; nor did Kristin.

He didn't call, nor didn't he write.

He didn't call, nor did he write.

nor 接在否⁠定子⁠句後⁠面

That night Kevin did not sleep; nor did Kristin.

She likes coffee, nor does her husband.

She doesn't like coffee, nor does her husband.

例句

① nor + 助動詞 + 不同的主詞

  1. On the last night of the trip, I didn't sleep well; nor did my roommate.
    旅行最後一晚我沒睡好，我室友也沒有。
  2. The police asked us about the crash at the corner. I didn't see the accident, nor did my friend.
    警察問我們轉角那場車禍的事。我沒看到那場車禍，我朋友也沒看到。
  3. Don't make an omelette for breakfast. My brother doesn't eat eggs, nor does my dad.
    早餐別做歐姆蛋。我哥哥不吃蛋，我爸爸也不吃。
  4. A visitor asked a hard question about the old castle. The teacher didn't know the answer, nor did the students.
    一個訪客問了一個關於古堡的難題。老師不知道答案，學生也不知道。
  5. I'm the first in my family with a degree. My parents never went to university, nor did my grandparents.
    我是家裡第一個有學位的人。我爸媽沒讀過大學，我爺爺奶奶也沒有。

② nor + be + 主詞

  1. I complained to the waiter. The coffee wasn't hot, nor was the soup.
    我跟服務生抱怨。咖啡不熱，湯也不熱。
  2. It was a holiday, but we didn't know. The shop wasn't open, nor was the bank.
    那天是假日，可是我們不知道。那家店沒開，銀行也沒開。
  3. We had a big lunch at Grandma's. I'm not hungry, nor is Ann.
    我們在奶奶家吃了很豐盛的午餐。我不餓，Ann 也不餓。
  4. My driving lessons were hard. The first test wasn't easy, nor was the second.
    我的開車課很難。第一次考試不簡單，第二次也不簡單。
  5. Take a taxi home after the concert. The road isn't safe at night, nor is the park.
    音樂會結束後搭計程車回家。那條路晚上不安全，公園也不安全。

③ nor + 助動詞 + 同一個主詞 + 不同的動作

  1. We live next to the station. We don't have a car, nor do we need one.
    我們住在車站旁邊。我們沒有車，也不需要。
  2. Can I have some juice, please? I don't drink tea, nor do I like coffee.
    可以給我一點果汁嗎？我不喝茶，也不喜歡咖啡。
  3. My daughter was out until two. She didn't text, nor did she email.
    我女兒在外面待到兩點。她沒傳簡訊，也沒寄電子郵件。
  4. Someone called me at midnight. I didn't know the caller, nor did I recognize the number.
    半夜有人打給我。我不認識打來的人，也不認得那個號碼。
  5. My grandmother lives alone at ninety. She never complains, nor does she ask for help.
    我奶奶九十歲了還一個人住。她從不抱怨，也從不求助。

④ nor + can / will / have + 主詞

  1. He only started school last month. My son can't read yet, nor can he write.
    他上個月才開始上學。我兒子還不會讀，也不會寫。
  2. The company closed our factory without warning. We won't forget, nor will we forgive.
    公司沒有預告就關了我們的工廠。我們不會忘記，也不會原諒。
  3. My neighbour broke my window a month ago. He hasn't paid, nor has he said sorry.
    我的鄰居一個月前打破了我的窗戶。他沒付錢，也沒道歉。
  4. The hotel has strict rules. Guests can't bring pets, nor can they smoke in the rooms.
    那家飯店的規定很嚴格。客人不能帶寵物，也不能在房間抽菸。
  5. Our guests were due at seven. They haven't arrived yet, nor have they called.
    我們的客人七點應該要到。他們還沒到，也沒打電話來。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. The council wants a new bridge over the river. The plan isn't cheap, nor is it easy.
    市議會想在河上蓋一座新橋。這個計畫不便宜，也不容易。
  2. We'd love to see Kyoto one day. I have never been to Japan, nor has my brother.
    我們希望有一天能去京都看看。我沒去過日本，我哥哥也沒去過。
  3. The landlord didn't fix the heater, nor did he answer our emails.
    房東沒修暖氣，也沒回我們的電子郵件。
  4. I didn't know the meeting was moved. Nobody told me, nor did I ask.
    我不知道會議改時間了。沒人告訴我，我也沒問。
  5. We left the cinema early. The film wasn't funny, nor was it exciting.
    我們提早離開電影院。那部電影不好笑，也不刺激。
  6. Our cousins had a big wedding. We didn't get an invitation, nor did we want one.
    我們的表親辦了盛大的婚禮。我們沒收到邀請，也不想要。
  7. We stayed in the tent all weekend. The rain didn't stop, nor did the wind.
    我們整個週末都待在帳篷裡。雨沒停，風也沒停。
  8. The nurse worked a double shift. She hadn't eaten all day, nor had she slept.
    那位護理師連上了兩班。她一整天沒吃東西，也沒睡覺。
  9. You want to close the library on Saturdays? I don't agree with you, nor does anyone else here.
    你想讓圖書館星期六關門？我不同意你，這裡也沒有別人同意。
  10. My aunt sent me a birthday letter and a parcel from Canada. The letter never arrived, nor did the parcel.
    我阿姨從加拿大寄了生日信和包裹給我。那封信一直沒到，包裹也沒到。
