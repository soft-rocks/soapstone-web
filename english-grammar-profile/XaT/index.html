Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

他一走到家門口，就聽到電話在響。

hardly 放⁠在句⁠首，had 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

他一走到家門口，就聽到電話在響。

第一⁠件⁠事用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式，第⁠二件事用過⁠去簡⁠單式

Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

第一件事had + 主詞 + 過去分詞
第二件事when + 主詞 + 過去式動詞

一⁠般⁠的語⁠序和倒⁠裝⁠句

Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

一般的語序

He had hardly sat down when the bell rang.

他才剛坐下，鈴就響了。
倒裝句，語氣比較強

Hardly had he sat down when the bell rang.

他才一坐下，鈴就響了。

scarcely 接 when，no sooner 接 than

scarcely ... when

Scarcely had we left the house when it started to rain.

我們才剛出門就開始下雨了。
no sooner ... than

No sooner had we left the house than it started to rain.

我們一出門就開始下雨了。

hardly 接 when，不⁠接 than

Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

Hardly had I sat down than the phone rang.

Hardly had I sat down when the phone rang.

had 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

Hardly he had left when his boss called.

Hardly had he left when his boss called.

hardly 和 not 不⁠一⁠起用

Hardly hadn't we arrived when the show started.

Hardly had we arrived when the show started.

when 子⁠句用過⁠去簡⁠單式

Hardly had he reached his front door when he heard the phone ringing.

Hardly had I opened the door when the cat had run out.

Hardly had I opened the door when the cat ran out.

例句

① Hardly had I + 過去分詞 + when

  1. Hardly had I closed my eyes when the baby started crying.
    我才剛閉上眼睛，小寶寶就開始哭了。
  2. Hardly had I paid for the shoes when I saw them cheaper online.
    我才剛付完鞋子的錢，就在網路上看到更便宜的價格。
  3. Hardly had I started my new job when the company moved to Leeds.
    我才剛開始新工作，公司就搬到里茲去了。
  4. Hardly had I got into the bath when someone rang the doorbell.
    我才剛進浴缸，就有人按門鈴。
  5. Hardly had I sent the email when I noticed the mistake.
    我才剛把電子郵件寄出去，就發現了錯誤。

② Hardly had he、she + 過去分詞 + when

  1. Hardly had she finished her complaint when the manager walked away.
    她才剛抱怨完，經理就走開了。
  2. Hardly had he taken his seat when the lights went down.
    他才剛坐好，燈就暗了下來。
  3. Hardly had she put the phone down when it rang again.
    她才剛掛上電話，電話又響了。
  4. Hardly had he begun his speech when the microphone stopped working.
    他才剛開始演講，麥克風就壞了。
  5. Hardly had she arrived in Madrid when she lost her passport.
    她才剛到馬德里就把護照弄丟了。

③ Hardly had we、they + 過去分詞 + when

  1. Hardly had they arrived when they saw a black car leaving the yard.
    他們才剛到，就看到一輛黑色的車開出院子。
  2. Hardly had we started the meal when the power went off.
    我們才剛開始吃飯就停電了。
  3. Hardly had we set up the tent when the storm began.
    我們才剛搭好帳篷，暴風雨就來了。
  4. Hardly had they moved into the flat when the neighbours began complaining.
    他們才剛搬進那間公寓，鄰居就開始抱怨了。
  5. Hardly had we left the harbour when the sea became rough.
    我們才剛離開港口，海面就變得波濤洶湧。

④ Hardly had + 名詞主詞 + 過去分詞 + when

  1. Hardly had the film started when the fire alarm went off.
    電影才剛開始，火災警報就響了。
  2. Hardly had the plane taken off when the pilot turned back.
    飛機才剛起飛，機長就折返了。
  3. Hardly had the teacher left the room when the students started talking.
    老師才剛離開教室，學生就開始聊天了。
  4. Hardly had the match begun when it started to snow.
    比賽才剛開始就下起雪來。
  5. Hardly had the guests sat down when the waiter dropped a tray.
    客人才剛坐下，服務生就把托盤摔了。

⑤ 一般的語序：had hardly + 過去分詞 + when

  1. I had hardly fallen asleep when the alarm went off.
    我才剛睡著，鬧鐘就響了。
  2. We had hardly left the car park when the tyre went flat.
    我們才剛離開停車場，輪胎就沒氣了。
  3. She had hardly started cooking when her guests arrived.
    她才剛開始煮飯，客人就到了。
  4. The shop had hardly opened when the queue reached the corner.
    那家店才剛開門，排隊的人就排到了轉角。

⑥ Scarcely had + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + when

  1. Scarcely had I unpacked my suitcase when my mother called.
    我才剛把行李箱打開整理，我媽就打電話來了。
  2. Scarcely had the concert begun when the singer lost her voice.
    音樂會才剛開始，歌手就失聲了。
  3. Scarcely had we reached the top when the clouds came down.
    我們才剛到山頂，雲就壓了下來。

⑦ No sooner had + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + than

  1. No sooner had I hung out the washing than it started to rain.
    我才剛把衣服晾出去就下雨了。
  2. No sooner had he signed the contract than he regretted it.
    他才剛簽完合約就後悔了。
  3. No sooner had the bus stopped than everyone rushed for the door.
    公車一停，大家就衝向車門。
