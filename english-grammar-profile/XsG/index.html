Why are you moving to a different area?

你為什麼要搬到別的地區？

Am / Is / Are + 主⁠詞 + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing ...?

am / is / are + 主詞be 動詞放在主詞前面。
動詞⁠-⁠ing對方安排好的事。

問原⁠因：Why

例句

Why is she leaving her job?

她為什麼要辭職？

問方⁠式和時⁠間長⁠短：How / How long

例句

How are you getting to the wedding?

你要怎麼去婚禮？

疑⁠問⁠詞後⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

Why are you moving to a different area?

Why you moving to a different area?

Why are you moving to a different area?

主⁠詞是單⁠數，用 is

Why are your sister leaving?

Why is your sister leaving?

plan 的字尾是一⁠個母⁠音加一⁠個子⁠音，要重⁠複 n 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ing

Are you visiting London and planing a tour?

Are you visiting London and planning a tour?

例句

① Am / Is / Are + 主詞 + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. Are you visiting my country on your next holiday?
    你下次放假要來我的國家玩嗎？
  2. Are you taking the children to the dentist on Monday?
    你星期一要帶孩子們去看牙醫嗎？
  3. Is your brother moving to Bristol next month?
    你哥下個月要搬去布里斯托嗎？
  4. Are they spending the summer at their villa in Greece?
    他們今年夏天要在希臘的別墅度過嗎？
  5. Is the company opening a new office in Madrid?
    那家公司要在馬德里開新辦公室嗎？
  6. Are you celebrating your birthday at home this year?
    你今年要在家過生日嗎？
  7. Is your mum driving you to the airport?
    你媽要開車載你去機場嗎？
  8. Are you doing anything special at the weekend?
    你週末有什麼特別的安排嗎？
  9. Is the band touring Europe again next year?
    樂團明年還要在歐洲巡迴嗎？
  10. Are your neighbours selling their house?
    你的鄰居要賣房子嗎？
  11. Is your sister bringing her new boyfriend to dinner?
    你姊要帶她新男朋友來吃晚餐嗎？
  12. Are you renting a car in Spain?
    你在西班牙要租車嗎？

② Why + am / is / are + 主詞 + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. Why are you giving up your piano lessons?
    你為什麼不上鋼琴課了？
  2. Why is the library closing early on Friday?
    圖書館星期五為什麼提早關門？
  3. Why are your parents selling the car?
    你爸媽為什麼要賣車？
  4. Why is Tom leaving the football team?
    Tom 為什麼要退出足球隊？
  5. Why are you changing schools in the middle of the year?
    你為什麼要在學期中轉學？
  6. Why are they cancelling the concert?
    他們為什麼要取消演唱會？
  7. Why is she taking a week off work?
    她為什麼要請一個星期的假？
  8. Why are we meeting so early tomorrow?
    我們明天為什麼這麼早見面？

③ Where / What / Who + am / is / are + 主詞 + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. Where are you spending your honeymoon?
    你們要去哪裡度蜜月？
  2. What are you cooking for the dinner party?
    晚宴你要煮什麼？
  3. Who is looking after your dog while you're away?
    你不在的時候誰要照顧你的狗？
  4. Where is the school taking the students on the trip?
    學校這次要帶學生去哪裡？
  5. What film are you showing at the club on Friday?
    你們星期五要在社團放哪部電影？
  6. Who are you inviting to the wedding?
    你要邀請誰來參加婚禮？

④ How / How long + am / is / are + 主詞 + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. How long are you staying in Japan?
    你要在日本待多久？
  2. How are you celebrating the end of the exams?
    考完試你要怎麼慶祝？
  3. How long is your grandmother staying with you?
    你奶奶要在你家住多久？
  4. How are you travelling around the island?
    你要怎麼在島上移動？
  5. How long are they keeping the museum closed?
    他們要讓博物館關閉多久？
