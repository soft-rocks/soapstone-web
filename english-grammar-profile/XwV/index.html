She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

她前一天就跟我說過，她有重要的事要告訴我。

轉⁠述過⁠去說⁠的⁠話，時⁠態和時⁠間詞都後移

她前一天就跟我說過，她有重要的事要告訴我。

原話是 "I have something important to tell you."

簡⁠單式和進⁠行⁠式往過⁠去移一格

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

have → had

She said she had something to tell me.

她說她有事要告訴我。
am working → was working

He said he was working late.

他說他要加班。
lost → had lost

He told me he had lost his keys.

他跟我說他把鑰匙弄丟了。

完⁠成⁠式和未⁠來⁠的說⁠法也往過⁠去移一格

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

have finished → had finished

She said she had finished.

她說她做完了。
will → would

Tom said he would call me.

Tom 說他會打給我。
are going to → were going to

They said they were going to move.

他們說他們要搬家。

日⁠子⁠的時⁠間詞換⁠成以原話那一⁠天為準⁠的說⁠法

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

yesterdaythe day before、the previous day
todaythat day
tonightthat night
tomorrowthe next day、the following day

週、ago、now、here、this 也換⁠成以原話為準⁠的說⁠法

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

last weekthe week before、the previous week
next weekthe following week
two days agotwo days before、two days earlier
nowthen、at that time
herethere
thisthat

tomorrow 和 yesterday 轉⁠述⁠時換⁠掉

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

tomorrow

He said he would see me the next day.

他說他隔天會跟我見面。
yesterday

She told me she had seen her the day before.

她跟我說，她前一天見過她。

說⁠話⁠的時⁠間已⁠經過⁠去，tomorrow 換⁠成 the next day

Last month, Tom told me he would call me tomorrow.

Last month, Tom told me he would call me the next day.

the following day 已⁠經過⁠去，will 後移成 would

She said that she will come the following day.

She said that she would come the following day.

比說⁠話更早⁠的⁠事，用過⁠去完⁠成⁠式

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

He told me the day before that he has lost his keys.

He told me the day before that he had lost his keys.

tell 後⁠面要接聽⁠的人

She had told me the previous day that she had something important to tell me.

She told that she was tired.

She told me that she was tired.

到現⁠在還成⁠立⁠的⁠事，時⁠態可⁠以不⁠後移

她說她住在巴黎。

同⁠一天轉⁠述，today 不⁠用換

Mark 今天早上說他今天要走。

例句

① yesterday → the day before / the previous day

  1. She told me she had finished the book the day before.
    她跟我說，她前一天把那本書看完了。
  2. He said he had sent the email the previous day.
    他說他前一天就寄了那封信。
  3. They told us they had arrived the day before.
    他們跟我們說，他們前一天就到了。
  4. My boss said she had spoken to the client the previous day.
    我老闆說她前一天跟客戶談過了。
  5. Anna said she had been ill the day before.
    Anna 說她前一天生病了。

② tomorrow → the next day / the following day

  1. He said he would pay me back the next day.
    他說他隔天會把錢還我。
  2. She told us the shop would open the following day.
    她跟我們說，那家店隔天會開。
  3. The teacher said we were going to have a test the next day.
    老師說我們隔天要考試。
  4. My friend told me he was flying to Rome the following day.
    我朋友跟我說他隔天要飛去羅馬。
  5. They said the results would come out the next day.
    他們說結果隔天會出來。

③ today / tonight → that day / that night

  1. She said she was too busy to see me that day.
    她說她那天太忙，沒空見我。
  2. He told me he was working late that night.
    他跟我說他那天晚上要加班。
  3. The nurse said the doctor wasn't coming in that day.
    護士說醫生那天不會來。
  4. My sister said she would cook dinner that night.
    我妹妹說那天晚上她會煮晚餐。
  5. They told me the bridge was closed that day.
    他們跟我說那座橋那天封閉。

④ last week / ago → the week before / before

  1. He said he had moved house the week before.
    他說他前一週剛搬家。
  2. She told me she had started the course two months before.
    她跟我說，她兩個月前就開始上那門課了。
  3. The manager said they had fixed the machine the previous week.
    經理說他們前一週修好了那台機器。
  4. My uncle said he had sold his car a year earlier.
    我叔叔說他一年前就把車賣了。
  5. They told us the hotel had opened three weeks before.
    他們跟我們說，那家飯店三週前才開幕。

⑤ next week → the following week

  1. She said she was going on holiday the following week.
    她說她下一週要去度假。
  2. He told me the new rules would start the following week.
    他跟我說新規定下一週開始實施。
  3. The school said the exams would be the following week.
    學校說考試在下一週。
  4. My landlord said he was going to paint the flat the following week.
    我房東說他下一週要粉刷公寓。

⑥ now / here / this → then / there / that

  1. He said he was too tired to talk then.
    他說他當時太累了，不想講話。
  2. She told me she was living in a small flat at that time.
    她跟我說她那時住在一間小公寓。
  3. The waiter told us the fish there was very fresh.
    服務生跟我們說那裡的魚很新鮮。
  4. She said she would finish the report that week.
    她說她那週會把報告寫完。

⑦ 引述動詞用過去完成式

  1. I had told my parents the week before that I wanted to study abroad.
    我在前一週就跟爸媽說過我想出國念書。
  2. He had promised me the day before that he would help.
    他前一天就答應過我會幫忙。
  3. We had warned them the previous day that the road was dangerous.
    我們前一天就警告過他們那條路很危險。
