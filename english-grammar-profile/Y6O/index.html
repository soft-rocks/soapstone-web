Why didn't you come with me?

你為什麼沒跟我一起去？

疑⁠問⁠詞 + 助⁠動⁠詞加 n't + 主⁠詞

Why didn't you come with me?

你昨晚為什麼沒來應門？

Why don't we / you ...? 是提⁠出建⁠議

Why didn't you come with me?

Why don't we

Why don't we eat outside? It's a lovely day.

我們何不在外面吃？天氣很好。
Why don't you

You look exhausted. Why don't you take a day off?

你看起來累壞了。你何不請一天假？

疑⁠問⁠詞後⁠面先接助⁠動⁠詞，再⁠接主⁠詞

Why didn't you come with me?

Why your parents didn't come to the play?

Why didn't your parents come to the play?

didn't 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Why didn't you come with me?

Why didn't you brought your swimsuit?

Why didn't you bring your swimsuit?

not 跟⁠著助⁠動⁠詞，縮⁠寫成 didn't

Why didn't you come with me?

Why not did the bus stop for us?

Why didn't the bus stop for us?

例句

① Why didn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞（問原因）

  1. We waited for you at the station on Sunday. Why didn't you come to the beach with us?
    我們星期天在車站等你。你為什麼沒跟我們一起去海邊？
  2. The report is due tomorrow, and now you tell me? Why didn't you mention the problem earlier?
    報告明天就要交，你現在才跟我說？你為什麼沒早點提這個問題？
  3. I sent her a long message on Monday. Why didn't your sister reply to it?
    我星期一傳了一則很長的訊息給她。你姊姊為什麼沒回？
  4. Everyone from our class was there except Ken. Why didn't they invite him to the party?
    我們班除了 Ken 每個人都在。他們為什麼沒邀請他來派對？
  5. You're soaking wet! Why didn't you wait for the rain to stop?
    你全身都濕透了！你為什麼沒等雨停？
  6. We pressed the button, but the bus went past. Why didn't the driver let us off?
    我們按了鈴，可是公車開過去了。司機為什麼沒讓我們下車？

② Why don't / doesn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞（問原因）

  1. You're always hungry by ten. Why don't you eat breakfast in the morning?
    你總是十點就餓了。你早上為什麼不吃早餐？
  2. He takes two buses to work every day. Why doesn't your brother drive?
    他每天搭兩班公車上班。你哥哥為什麼不開車？
  3. The zoo trip is tomorrow. Why don't the kids want to go?
    去動物園是明天。孩子們為什麼不想去？
  4. I changed the bulb this morning. Why doesn't this light work?
    我今天早上換了燈泡。這盞燈為什麼不亮？
  5. I've written to you five times this month. Why don't you ever answer my emails?
    我這個月寫了五次信給你。你為什麼從來都不回我的電子郵件？

③ Why don't we / you ...?（建議）

  1. It's a sunny afternoon. Why don't we rent bikes and ride around the lake?
    今天下午天氣很好。我們何不租腳踏車環湖騎一圈？
  2. He has a big van. Why don't you ask your cousin to help you move?
    他有一台大廂型車。你何不找你表哥幫你搬家？
  3. Why don't we eat outside? It's a lovely day.
    我們何不在外面吃？天氣很好。
  4. You look exhausted. Why don't you take a day off?
    你看起來累壞了。你何不請一天假？
  5. The office is too noisy to talk. Why don't we meet at the café instead?
    辦公室太吵了，不好談。我們何不改在咖啡店見面？

④ Why can't / couldn't / won't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Why couldn't you come on Saturday? We missed you.
    你星期六為什麼不能來？我們很想你。
  2. All my friends are going, Mum. Why can't I go to the party?
    媽，我所有的朋友都要去。我為什麼不能去派對？
  3. I filled it with petrol yesterday. Why won't the car start?
    我昨天才加滿油。車子為什麼發不動？
  4. I rang three times. Why can't Grandpa hear the doorbell?
    我按了三次。爺爺為什麼聽不到門鈴？
  5. We told Tom the ice was thin. Why wouldn't he listen to us?
    我們跟湯姆說冰很薄。他為什麼就是不聽我們的？

⑤ Who / What / Which + 否定 和不同的句型

  1. I count only twenty-eight books on my desk. Who didn't hand in their homework?
    我桌上只有二十八本。誰沒交作業？
  2. You gave it only two stars. What didn't you like about the hotel?
    你只給了兩顆星。那家飯店你哪裡不喜歡？
  3. You ran out of time in the test. Which questions didn't you answer?
    你考試時間不夠。你哪幾題沒回答？
  4. The teacher called about Lucy. Why isn't she at school today?
    老師打電話來問露西的事。她今天為什麼沒去上學？
  5. It's freezing outside. Why aren't you wearing a coat?
    外面冷死了。你為什麼沒穿外套？
  6. They sent it two weeks ago. Why hasn't the parcel arrived yet?
    他們兩個星期前就寄了。包裹為什麼還沒到？
  7. We got a red letter today. Why haven't you paid the electricity bill yet?
    我們今天收到一封催繳信。你為什麼還沒繳電費？
  8. We drove there at ten on a Tuesday. Why wasn't the shop open?
    我們星期二十點開車去。那家店為什麼沒開？
  9. The boss asked about you this morning. Why weren't you at the meeting?
    老闆今天早上問起你。你為什麼沒來開會？
