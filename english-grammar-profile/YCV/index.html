You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

你沒有謹慎地完成你的任務。

正⁠式⁠的文⁠字寫完⁠整形⁠式 have not

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

你沒有謹慎地完成你的任務。

經理寫正式的警告信給員工。

完⁠整形⁠式和縮⁠寫意⁠思一⁠樣，正⁠式程⁠度不⁠同

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

完整形式

The company has not paid the invoice.

公司還沒有支付這張發票。
縮寫

The company hasn't paid the invoice.

公司還沒付這張發票。

not yet 說應⁠該⁠做、還⁠沒⁠做⁠的事

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

委員會還沒有做出決定。

not 放⁠在 have 後⁠面

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

You not have fulfilled your task.

You have not fulfilled your task.

has not 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

The supplier has not deliver the goods.

The supplier has not delivered the goods.

否⁠定⁠句⁠的受⁠詞前⁠面用 any，不⁠用 no

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

We have not received no reply.

We have not received any reply.

did not 說過⁠去一⁠個時⁠間點沒⁠做⁠的事

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

房客三月沒有付房租。

have no 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞，have 是「有」

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

我們沒有你付款的紀錄。

have never 說⁠到現⁠在一⁠次也⁠沒⁠有

You have not fulfilled your task with due care.

銀行從來沒有向我們收過手續費。

例句

① 正式信件：主詞 + have not + 過去分詞

  1. We have not received your application form.
    我們沒有收到你的申請表。
  2. You have not followed the safety rules.
    你沒有遵守安全規定。
  3. I have not seen any changes to the contract.
    我沒有看到合約有任何修改。
  4. They have not replied to our letter of 4 May.
    他們沒有回覆我們五月四日的信。
  5. You have not completed the required training.
    你沒有完成規定的訓練。
  6. We have not been able to contact you by phone.
    我們沒辦法用電話聯絡上你。
  7. I have not changed my opinion on this matter.
    我對這件事的看法沒有改變。

② 第三人稱單數：主詞 + has not + 過去分詞

  1. The company has not paid its workers for two months.
    這家公司兩個月沒有付員工薪水了。
  2. The report has not given any reasons for the delay.
    報告沒有說明延誤的原因。
  3. The building has not passed the fire inspection.
    這棟大樓沒有通過消防檢查。
  4. The library has not bought any new books since 2019.
    圖書館從二〇一九年起就沒有買新書。
  5. The minister has not commented on the news.
    部長沒有對這則新聞發表評論。
  6. The hotel has not refunded my deposit.
    飯店沒有退還我的訂金。
  7. My landlord has not repaired the heating.
    我的房東沒有修暖氣。

③ not yet：應該做、還沒做的事

  1. The council has not yet approved the plan.
    議會還沒有批准這個計畫。
  2. We have not yet received your payment.
    我們還沒有收到你的付款。
  3. The judges have not yet announced the winner.
    評審還沒有公布得獎者。
  4. The police have not yet found the driver.
    警方還沒有找到那名駕駛。
  5. The parcel has not arrived yet.
    包裹還沒有送到。
  6. I have not decided yet.
    我還沒有決定。

④ 報告和公告

  1. The new law has not reduced crime.
    新的法律沒有讓犯罪減少。
  2. Sales have not grown as much as we expected.
    銷售量沒有我們預期的那麼多。
  3. The trains have not run on time this month.
    這個月的火車都沒有準時。
  4. The two sides have not agreed on a price.
    雙方還沒有談定價格。
  5. The museum has not allowed photos since the fire.
    博物館從那場火災後就不准拍照。

⑤ 完整形式和縮寫

  1. The bank has not approved your loan.
    銀行沒有核准你的貸款。
  2. The bank hasn't approved my loan.
    銀行沒核准我的貸款。
  3. The managers have not explained their decision.
    經理們沒有解釋他們的決定。
  4. My boss hasn't explained why.
    我老闆沒解釋為什麼。
  5. Our records show that you have not attended the last three meetings.
    我們的紀錄顯示你沒有出席最近三次會議。
