The construction of such a centre would undoubtedly improve our quality of life.

舉辦以家庭為主的活動會讓會員人數大幅增加。

主⁠詞 + would + 副⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

would undoubtedly / obviously、would significantly、would possibly

would undoubtedly / obviously無疑會、顯然會。
would significantly會大大地。
would possibly可能會。
would eventually / inevitably最後會、必然會。

說影⁠響⁠的大⁠小

例句

This would significantly increase membership.

這會大大增加會員人數。

報告常用 significantly 說影響很大。

說個⁠人⁠的看⁠法

例句

I would personally choose the smaller school.

我個人會選比較小的學校。

I would personally 強調這是自己的看法。

副⁠詞放⁠在 would 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞中⁠間

The construction of such a centre would undoubtedly improve our quality of life.

This would increase significantly membership.

This would significantly increase membership.

放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面要⁠用副⁠詞

It would obvious help the town.

It would obviously help the town.

would 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

The construction of such a centre would undoubtedly improve our quality of life.

The plan would eventually costs more.

The plan would eventually cost more.

例句

① 主詞 + would undoubtedly / obviously + 原形動詞：很確定的影響

  1. I believe that a new leisure centre would undoubtedly improve our quality of life.
    我相信一座新的休閒中心無疑會改善我們的生活品質。
  2. A new hospital would undoubtedly save many lives.
    一間新醫院無疑會拯救很多生命。
  3. Cheaper buses would obviously reduce the number of cars.
    比較便宜的公車顯然會減少車子的數量。
  4. More trees would undoubtedly make the streets cooler in summer.
    更多的樹無疑會讓夏天的街道涼快一點。
  5. A longer holiday would obviously please the staff.
    更長的假期顯然會讓員工高興。
  6. Better lighting would undoubtedly make the park safer at night.
    更好的照明無疑會讓公園晚上更安全。

② 主詞 + would significantly + 原形動詞：影響很大

  1. Organising family activities would significantly increase membership and make the society more popular.
    辦家庭活動會大大增加會員人數，讓這個協會更受歡迎。
  2. A new railway line would significantly reduce travel times.
    一條新的鐵路線會大大縮短交通時間。
  3. Higher taxes would significantly affect poorer families.
    提高稅會大大影響比較窮的家庭。
  4. Online booking would significantly cut the queues.
    網路訂票會大大減少排隊的人。
  5. A four-day week would significantly improve the health of office workers.
    一星期工作四天會大大改善上班族的健康。
  6. A bigger car park would significantly help the local shops.
    大一點的停車場會大大幫助當地的商店。

③ 主詞 + would eventually / inevitably + 原形動詞：最後的結果

  1. Without repairs, the old bridge would eventually fall down.
    不修理的話，那座老橋最後會倒塌。
  2. More cars would inevitably lead to more pollution.
    更多車子必然會導致更多污染。
  3. If prices keep rising, people would eventually stop buying.
    如果價格一直上漲，人們最後會停止購買。
  4. Cutting the budget would inevitably mean fewer teachers.
    刪減預算必然意味著老師會變少。
  5. With practice, you would eventually speak like a native.
    經過練習，你最後會說得像母語人士一樣。
  6. Building on the field would inevitably cause flooding.
    在那塊田上蓋房子必然會造成水災。

④ I would personally + 原形動詞：說個人的看法

  1. I would personally choose the smaller school in the countryside.
    我個人會選鄉下那所比較小的學校。
  2. I would personally prefer a quiet holiday.
    我個人比較喜歡安靜的假期。
  3. I would personally never buy a car without a test drive.
    我個人絕對不會沒試開就買車。
  4. I would personally like to see more trees in the town centre.
    我個人希望市中心有更多的樹。
  5. I would personally recommend the train over the bus.
    我個人會推薦搭火車，不推薦公車。
  6. I would personally avoid that area at night.
    我個人晚上會避開那一區。

⑤ 主詞 + would possibly / normally + 原形動詞

  1. The new plan would possibly cost less than the old one.
    新計畫可能會比舊的花費少。
  2. I would normally take the bus, but today I'm walking.
    我通常會搭公車，可是今天我走路。
  3. The trip would possibly take two days.
    這趟旅行可能要兩天。
  4. She would normally be at work by now.
    她現在通常已經在上班了。
  5. A smaller school would possibly suit him better.
    小一點的學校可能比較適合他。
  6. We would normally eat dinner at seven.
    我們通常七點吃晚餐。
  7. The changes would possibly take effect next year.
    這些改變可能明年生效。
