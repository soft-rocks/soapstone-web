例句

① I haven't + 過去分詞

  1. It has a thousand pieces. I haven't finished the jigsaw puzzle you gave me.
    它有一千片。你送我的拼圖我還沒拼完。
  2. Please don't tell me the ending. I haven't seen that film.
    請不要告訴我結局。我沒看過那部電影。
  3. My brother moved to Australia in March. I haven't heard anything from him.
    我哥哥三月搬去澳洲。我沒有聽到他的任何消息。
  4. The party starts in an hour. I haven't decided what to wear.
    派對再一個小時就開始了。我還沒決定要穿什麼。
  5. Bring him to lunch on Sunday. I haven't met your new boyfriend.
    星期天帶他來吃午餐吧。我還沒見過你的新男朋友。
  6. I got home late from work. I haven't cooked anything for dinner.
    我下班很晚才到家。晚餐我什麼都還沒煮。
  7. Don't worry, your secret is safe. I haven't told anyone about it.
    別擔心，你的祕密很安全。這件事我沒跟任何人說。
  8. Can I borrow it after you? I haven't read today's newspaper.
    你看完可以借我嗎？今天的報紙我還沒看。

② I haven't + 過去分詞 + for + 一段時間

  1. Let's go there on Friday. I haven't eaten at that Greek restaurant for a long time.
    我們星期五去那裡吧。我很久沒去那家希臘餐廳吃飯了。
  2. I'm going to give this dress away. I haven't worn it for years.
    我要把這件洋裝送人。我好幾年沒穿了。
  3. We had a big argument at my birthday party. I haven't spoken to my cousin for months.
    我們在我的生日派對上大吵一架。我好幾個月沒跟我表哥講話了。
  4. I really need a break. I haven't had a holiday for two years.
    我真的需要休息。我兩年沒放假了。
  5. Let's go and see a film tonight. I haven't been to the cinema for ages.
    我們今晚去看電影吧。我好久沒去看電影了。
  6. My new neighbours are very noisy. I haven't slept well for a week.
    我的新鄰居很吵。我一個禮拜都沒睡好。

③ I haven't + 過去分詞 + yet

  1. Please don't say anything at dinner. I haven't told my parents about the new job yet.
    晚餐的時候請什麼都別說。新工作的事我還沒跟爸媽說。
  2. The heating is still broken. I haven't heard back from the landlord yet.
    暖氣還是壞的。我還沒收到房東的回音。
  3. I'm saving it for my birthday. I haven't opened your present yet.
    我要留到生日那天。你送的禮物我還沒拆。
  4. My boss wrote to me this morning. I haven't answered her email yet.
    我老闆今天早上寫信給我。她的電子郵件我還沒回。
  5. I'm going away next week. I haven't found anyone to look after the cat yet.
    我下禮拜要出門。我還沒找到人幫我照顧貓。

④ I haven't + 過去分詞 + since + 起點

  1. My uncle lives in Canada now. I haven't seen him since Christmas.
    我叔叔現在住在加拿大。我從聖誕節以後就沒見過他。
  2. I'm a bit nervous about the bike tour. I haven't ridden a bike since I was a child.
    我對單車之旅有點緊張。我從小時候以後就沒騎過腳踏車。
  3. Can we stop for lunch? I haven't eaten anything since breakfast.
    我們可以停下來吃午餐嗎？我從早餐以後就沒吃東西了。
  4. Do you want to borrow my camera? I haven't used it since our trip to Spain.
    你要借我的相機嗎？我從我們去西班牙玩以後就沒用過它。

⑤ 正式的完整形式：I have not

  1. Dear Sir, you sent the papers last month. I have not received the documents.
    敬啟者，貴公司上個月寄出文件。我還沒收到這些文件。
  2. Dear Ms Lee, please send the bill to my home. I have not changed my address.
    李女士您好，請把帳單寄到我家。我沒有改地址。
  3. Dear Mr Jones, I have some questions about the flat. I have not signed the contract yet.
    瓊斯先生您好，我對那間公寓有些問題。合約我還沒簽。
  4. Dear Madam, I wrote to you three weeks ago. I have not had any reply from your office.
    敬啟者，我三週前寫信給您。我還沒收到你們辦公室的任何回覆。

⑥ 道歉和解釋

  1. Hi Sarah, sorry I haven't been in touch for so long.
    嗨，莎拉，抱歉我這麼久沒跟你聯絡。
  2. I'm sorry, Mum. I haven't done the washing-up.
    媽，抱歉，碗我還沒洗。
  3. Work has been crazy this month. I haven't had time to call you.
    這個月工作忙翻了。我一直沒時間打電話給你。
  4. Sorry I missed the meeting. I haven't been well this week.
    抱歉我沒去開會。我這個禮拜身體不太好。
