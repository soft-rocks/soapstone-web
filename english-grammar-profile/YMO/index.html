Either I talk to her now or I won't ever be able to talk to her.

我要嘛現在就跟她談，要嘛就永遠沒辦法跟她談了。

either 放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面兩⁠個子⁠句選一⁠個

Either I talk to her now or I won't ever be able to talk to her.

你要嘛把作業寫完，要嘛今晚待在家。

主⁠詞一⁠樣⁠時，either 放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

Either I talk to her now or I won't ever be able to talk to her.

我們可以搭公車，或是走路。

either 配 or，neither 才配 nor

Either I talk to her now or I won't ever be able to talk to her.

You can either call me nor send an email.

You can either call me or send an email.

第⁠二⁠個選⁠擇前⁠面用 or

Either I talk to her now or I won't ever be able to talk to her.

Either you call her, either you send her a text.

Either you call her or you send her a text.

or 後⁠面⁠的形⁠式跟 either 後⁠面一⁠樣

Either I talk to her now or I won't ever be able to talk to her.

You should either rest or going to the doctor.

You should either rest or go to the doctor.

例句

① Either + 主要子句 + or + 主要子句

  1. The concert tickets are twenty pounds. Either you pay now or you pay at the door.
    音樂會的票二十英鎊。你要嘛現在付，要嘛在門口付。
  2. It's ten past eight, and nobody is at the stop. Either the bus is late or I've missed it.
    已經八點十分了，公車站一個人都沒有。不是公車誤點，就是我錯過了。
  3. My brother isn't answering his phone. Either he's at work or he's asleep.
    我哥哥不接電話。他不是在上班，就是在睡覺。
  4. Mum needs to know about the broken window. Either you tell her the truth or I will.
    媽媽需要知道窗戶破掉的事。你要嘛告訴她實話，要嘛就由我來說。
  5. The bakery's windows are empty. Either the shop is closed or it's moved.
    麵包店的櫥窗是空的。那家店不是關了，就是搬走了。
  6. Our friends said they'd meet us at the gate. Either they're late or we're at the wrong station.
    我們的朋友說會在閘門跟我們碰面。不是他們遲到了，就是我們走錯車站了。

② can / could + either + 動詞 + or + 動詞

  1. Let's talk about the plan on Saturday. We can either meet at the café or come to your house.
    我們星期六談計畫吧。我們可以在咖啡店碰面，或是去你家。
  2. The doctor says you need more exercise. You can either take a yoga class or swim twice a week.
    醫生說你需要多運動。你可以上瑜伽課，或是一週游泳兩次。
  3. Nobody wants to cook tonight. We could either eat out or order a pizza.
    今晚沒有人想煮飯。我們可以出去吃，或是叫披薩。
  4. The hotel receptionist gave me two choices. You can either pay now or pay later.
    飯店的櫃台人員給我兩個選擇。你可以現在付，或是晚點付。
  5. The doctor is very busy today. The patients can either wait here or come back tomorrow.
    醫生今天很忙。病人可以在這裡等，或是明天再來。

③ should + either + 動詞 + or + 動詞

  1. She sent you a birthday present. You should either call your aunt or write her a card.
    她送了你生日禮物。你應該打給你阿姨，或是寫張卡片給她。
  2. You made your sister cry. You should either apologize to her or keep quiet.
    你把你妹妹弄哭了。你應該跟她道歉，不然就別說話。
  3. It's been in the garage for a year. We should either fix the car or sell it.
    它已經在車庫裡放了一年。我們應該把車修好，或是賣掉。
  4. The new manager cancelled the trip without a reason. She should either change her mind or explain.
    新經理沒有理由就取消了旅行。她應該改變主意，或是解釋清楚。

④ 主詞 + either + 動詞 + or + 動詞

  1. My weekends are quiet. On Sundays I either read or go for a walk.
    我的週末很安靜。星期天我不是看書，就是去散步。
  2. My husband is never home before nine. He either works late or plays football after work.
    我先生從來不會九點前回家。他下班後不是加班，就是去踢足球。
  3. My daughter never walks. She either takes the bus or rides her bike to school.
    我女兒從來不走路。她不是搭公車，就是騎腳踏車上學。
  4. My cat is very lazy. She either sleeps or eats all day.
    我的貓很懶。她整天不是睡覺就是吃東西。
  5. We don't travel much. We either stay at home or visit my grandparents at the weekend.
    我們不常旅行。週末我們不是待在家，就是去看我爺爺奶奶。

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. Your phone was on this table an hour ago. Either you lost it or someone took it.
    你的手機一個小時前還在這張桌上。不是你弄丟了，就是有人拿走了。
  2. Take your boots on the trip. Either it'll rain or it'll snow tomorrow.
    旅行帶你的靴子。明天不是下雨就是下雪。
  3. Try this blue cheese. You'll either love it or hate it.
    吃吃看這個藍紋起司。你要嘛超愛，要嘛超討厭。
  4. I won't go to the party without you. Either we go together or we don't go at all.
    沒有你我不去派對。我們要嘛一起去，要嘛都不去。
  5. Don't worry about your results. The doctor will either call you tonight or send a text.
    別擔心你的檢查結果。醫生今晚會打給你，或是傳簡訊。
  6. Anna isn't here yet. Either she missed the bus or she forgot about the meeting.
    安娜還沒到。她不是錯過了公車，就是忘了開會的事。
  7. We haven't booked our trip yet. We'll either fly or take the train to Kaohsiung.
    我們還沒訂行程。我們會搭飛機或是搭火車去高雄。
  8. The map shows a bridge here, but I can only see water. Either I'm wrong or this map is wrong.
    地圖上這裡有一座橋，可是我只看到水。不是我錯了，就是這張地圖錯了。
  9. Your homework is full of mistakes. You either do it properly or you don't do it.
    你的作業錯一堆。你要嘛就好好做，要嘛就別做。
  10. The screen won't turn on. Either the battery is dead or the phone is broken.
    螢幕打不開。不是電池沒電了，就是手機壞了。
