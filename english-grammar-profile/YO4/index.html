We are going to take a taxi to get to the sports center.

我們要搭計程車去運動中心。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are + going to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We are going to take a taxi to get to the sports center.

am / is / are跟著主詞變化。I 用 am，he、she、it 和單數名詞用 is，you、we、they 和複數名詞用 are。
going to表示未來。
原形動詞未來要發生的事。

縮⁠寫

We are going to take a taxi to get to the sports center.

I'm going to

I'm going to wear them on holiday.

我要在度假時穿它們。
it's going to

It's going to be hot.

天氣會很熱。
we're going to

We're going to have pizza.

我們要吃披薩。

口語常把 am、is、are 縮寫。

going to 前⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

We are going to take a taxi to get to the sports center.

We going to take a taxi.

We are going to take a taxi.

going to 的 to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We are going to take a taxi to get to the sports center.

I'm going to buying a new phone.

I'm going to buy a new phone.

主⁠詞是 she，用 is

She are going to visit her aunt.

She is going to visit her aunt.

going 後⁠面要⁠有 to

We are going to take a taxi to get to the sports center.

It is going start at half past four.

It is going to start at half past four.

例句

① 主詞 + am / is / are + going to + 原形動詞

  1. We are going to stay with friends in Scotland.
    我們要去蘇格蘭，住在朋友家。
  2. The match is going to be on TV tonight.
    比賽今晚會在電視上轉播。
  3. I am going to visit my grandparents on Sunday.
    我星期天要去看我的祖父母。
  4. My sister is going to study in Canada.
    我姊姊要去加拿大念書。
  5. They are going to paint the kitchen yellow.
    他們要把廚房漆成黃色。
  6. The weather is going to be cold tomorrow.
    明天天氣會很冷。
  7. Our class is going to have a party on Friday.
    我們班星期五要辦派對。
  8. My parents are going to buy a new car.
    我爸媽要買新車。
  9. He is going to cook dinner tonight.
    他今晚要煮晚餐。
  10. The kids are going to play in the garden.
    孩子們要在花園裡玩。
  11. I am going to learn Japanese next year.
    我明年要學日文。
  12. The bus is going to stop at the museum.
    公車會停在博物館。
  13. You are going to love this cake.
    你一定會喜歡這個蛋糕。
  14. Ann is going to sing at the school concert.
    Ann 要在學校音樂會上唱歌。
  15. The shop is going to close early today.
    那家店今天會提早關門。

② 縮寫：I'm / he's / she's / it's / we're / you're / they're going to

  1. I'm going to wear my new jeans on holiday.
    我要在度假時穿我的新牛仔褲。
  2. It's going to snow in the mountains tonight.
    今晚山上會下雪。
  3. We're going to spend the weekend at the beach.
    我們週末要去海邊過。
  4. She's going to call you later.
    她晚一點會打給你。
  5. They're going to move to a new house.
    他們要搬新家。
  6. He's going to be a doctor.
    他將來要當醫生。
  7. I'm going to clean my room after lunch.
    我午餐後要打掃房間。
  8. You're going to need an umbrella.
    你會需要一把傘。
  9. Look at those dark clouds. It's going to rain soon.
    你看那些烏雲，很快就要下雨了。
  10. We're going to watch the football on TV.
    我們要看電視轉播的足球賽。
  11. She's going to have a baby in May.
    她五月要生寶寶了。
  12. I'm going to tell her the truth about the money tonight.
    我今晚要跟她說那筆錢的實話。
  13. They're going to get married next year.
    他們明年要結婚。
  14. He's going to sell his bike.
    他要賣掉他的腳踏車。
  15. We're going to walk to school today.
    我們今天要走路去學校。
  16. I'm going to have a shower.
    我要去洗澡。
