You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

你一定要穿運動鞋，也一定要帶球拍！

主⁠詞 + must + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

must、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

must必須、一定要。所有主詞都用 must，不加 -⁠s。
原形動詞必須做的事。

must 後⁠面不⁠加 to

You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

例句

I must feed the neighbours' fish while they're away.

鄰居不在的時候，我必須幫他們餵魚。

must 是情態動詞。後面直接接原形動詞。

must 只⁠有現⁠在⁠式

You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

例句

Last winter we had to sell our old piano.

去年冬天我們必須賣掉那台舊鋼琴。

must 沒有過去式。說過去必須做的事，用 had to。

must 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

You must to switch off your phone in here.

You must switch off your phone in here.

must 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

She musts finish her homework.

She must finish her homework.

must 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You must wear your sports shoes and you must also bring your racket!

He must goes home now.

He must go home now.

例句

① You must + 原形動詞：提醒朋友

  1. We're going hiking on Sunday. You must bring a waterproof jacket!
    我們星期天要去健行。你一定要帶防水外套！
  2. The pool has new rules this year. You must bring your swimming hat.
    游泳池今年有新規定。你一定要帶泳帽。
  3. You're coming to my house on Friday? You must try my mum's chocolate cake.
    你星期五要來我家？你一定要吃吃看我媽媽做的巧克力蛋糕。
  4. The concert hall fills up fast. You must come early to get a good seat.
    音樂廳很快就會坐滿。你一定要早點來才有好位子。
  5. It's late and the roads are dark. You must call me when you get home.
    很晚了，路上又暗。你到家的時候一定要打電話給我。
  6. You're visiting my town next month? You must see the new park. It's beautiful.
    你下個月要來我住的城市？你一定要去看看新的公園。很漂亮。

② I must + 原形動詞：自己必須做的事

  1. My mum is waiting for me. I must be back at a quarter past ten.
    我媽媽在等我。我必須十點十五分回到家。
  2. Sorry, I can't come to the cinema. I must study for my English test tonight.
    抱歉，我不能去看電影。我今天晚上必須準備英文考試。
  3. My sister turns twelve on Saturday. I must buy a present for her.
    我妹妹星期六滿十二歲。我必須買禮物給她。
  4. My friends are coming at five. I must clean my room before they arrive.
    我朋友五點會來。他們到之前，我必須打掃房間。
  5. My parents are away this weekend. I must remember to feed the cat.
    我爸媽這個週末不在家。我必須記得餵貓。
  6. I can't stay at the party. I must go to bed early. I have a match tomorrow.
    我不能留在派對。我必須早點睡覺。我明天有比賽。

③ We must + 原形動詞：大家必須做的事

  1. It's ten to eight. We must leave now or we'll miss the bus.
    七點五十了。我們必須現在走，不然會錯過公車。
  2. Shh, Mum just put the baby down. We must be quiet.
    噓，媽媽剛哄寶寶睡著。我們必須安靜。
  3. The teacher is coming back after the break. We must tidy the classroom before the bell.
    老師下課後會回來。鐘響前我們必須整理教室。
  4. There's no bread left. We must buy some for breakfast tomorrow.
    麵包吃完了。我們必須買一些當明天的早餐。
  5. It was so good to see you after all these years. We must meet again soon.
    這麼多年後見到你真好。我們一定要很快再見面。
  6. Look at the sky over the sea! We must take some photos of the sunset.
    看海上的天空！我們一定要拍幾張夕陽的照片。

④ He / She / They + must + 原形動詞

  1. My grandma has a bad chest infection. She must take her medicine three times a day.
    我奶奶胸腔感染很嚴重。她一天必須吃三次藥。
  2. My brother can't see small letters well. He must wear glasses for reading.
    我哥哥看不清楚小字。他看書必須戴眼鏡。
  3. The football fans are queuing outside the stadium. They must show their tickets at the gate.
    球迷在體育場外面排隊。他們必須在門口出示門票。
  4. My tennis club is very traditional. Players must wear white shirts there.
    我的網球俱樂部很傳統。球員在那裡必須穿白色上衣。
  5. Our street has no lights. The children must be home before dark.
    我們這條街沒有路燈。孩子們必須在天黑前回家。
  6. The school trip is to a farm with no café. Everyone must bring a packed lunch.
    校外教學去的農場沒有餐廳。每個人都必須帶便當。

⑤ must + 原形動詞 + because + 子句

  1. I still need milk for the cake. I must hurry because the shop closes at six.
    我做蛋糕還需要牛奶。我必須快一點，因為那家店六點關門。
  2. Are you going out to play? You must wear a coat because it's very cold outside.
    你要出去玩嗎？你必須穿外套，因為外面很冷。
  3. Look at the garden, it's so dry. We must water the plants because it hasn't rained for weeks.
    你看花園好乾。我們必須幫植物澆水，因為好幾個星期沒下雨了。
  4. I'll do my homework later. I must phone my grandma first because it's her birthday.
    我等一下再寫功課。我必須先打電話給我奶奶，因為今天是她的生日。
  5. The match is at midday in July. You must drink lots of water because it's so hot.
    比賽在七月的中午。你必須多喝水，因為天氣很熱。
  6. My aunt is going to London tomorrow. She must get up early because her train leaves at seven.
    我阿姨明天要去倫敦。她必須早起，因為她的火車七點開。
  7. Let's have dinner at Rosa's on Friday. We must book a table because it's always full.
    我們星期五去 Rosa's 吃晚餐吧。我們必須訂位，因為那裡總是客滿。
