Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

你可以幫我粉刷房間嗎？

Could you 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，客⁠氣⁠地請別⁠人做⁠事

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

你可以幫我粉刷房間嗎？

對方可以答應，也可以拒絕。

help 後⁠面⁠接⁠人，再⁠接動⁠詞，to 可⁠加可不⁠加

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

我手上都是東西。你可以幫我提這些袋子嗎？

先說⁠出原⁠因，再提⁠出請⁠求

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

我昨天把傘忘在你家了。你明天可以帶來學校嗎？

答應常說 Sure.、Of course.、No problem.。拒絕常說 Sorry, I can't.，再說原因。

could 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

Could you to shut the gate behind you?

Could you shut the gate behind you?

could 後⁠面用 give，不⁠用 gave

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

Could you gave me a pen?

Could you give me a pen?

help me 後⁠面用 move，不⁠用 moving

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

Could you help me moving the piano upstairs?

Could you help me move the piano upstairs?

Could you 比 Can you 客⁠氣

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

寶寶睡著了。你可以把音樂關小聲一點嗎？

Could I 問自⁠己可⁠不⁠可⁠以⁠做，Could you 請對⁠方做

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

Could I

I forgot mine. Could I use your ruler?

我忘了帶我的。我可以用你的尺嗎？
Could you

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

你可以幫我粉刷房間嗎？

Would you mind 更客⁠氣，後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠⁠ing

Could you help me to paint my bedroom?

醫生正在看病人。你介意等幾分鐘嗎？

例句

① Could you + 原形動詞？

  1. I'm carrying two boxes of books. Could you hold the lift for me?
    我抱著兩箱書。你可以幫我按住電梯嗎？
  2. Excuse me, I'm lost. Could you tell me the way to the station?
    不好意思，我迷路了。你可以告訴我去車站怎麼走嗎？
  3. I left my wallet at home. Could you lend me some money for lunch?
    我把錢包忘在家裡了。你可以借我一點錢買午餐嗎？
  4. I can't see the words on this page. Could you turn on the light?
    我看不清楚這頁的字。你可以把燈打開嗎？
  5. You took some great pictures at the wedding. Could you send me the photos?
    你在婚禮上拍了很多好照片。你可以把照片傳給我嗎？
  6. I need to pay for the parking. Could you wait here for a minute?
    我要去付停車費。你可以在這裡等一下嗎？
  7. I'm writing down your booking. Could you spell your name?
    我在記你的訂位。你可以拼一下你的名字嗎？
  8. I'm in a meeting right now. Could you call me back later?
    我現在在開會。你可以晚一點回電給我嗎？
  9. I'm going to visit my parents for a week. Could you feed my cat while I'm away?
    我要回爸媽家一個星期。我不在的時候，你可以幫我餵貓嗎？
  10. We've run out of milk. Could you pick up some on your way home?
    家裡的牛奶喝完了。你回家的路上可以買一點嗎？

② Could you + 原形動詞 + please？

  1. My English isn't very good. Could you speak more slowly, please?
    我的英文不太好。可以請你說慢一點嗎？
  2. Sorry, the train was too loud. Could you please repeat that?
    抱歉，火車太吵了。可以請你再說一次嗎？
  3. Welcome to the clinic. Could you fill in this form, please?
    歡迎來診所。可以請你填一下這張表格嗎？
  4. We're ready to order. Could you bring us the menu, please?
    我們準備好要點餐了。可以請你拿菜單給我們嗎？
  5. It's getting cold in here. Could you close the window, please?
    這裡越來越冷了。可以請你把窗戶關起來嗎？
  6. The film has started. Could you please be quiet?
    電影已經開始了。可以請你安靜一點嗎？

③ Could you help me (to) + 原形動詞？

  1. This sofa is too heavy for me. Could you help me move it?
    這張沙發對我來說太重了。你可以幫我搬嗎？
  2. I dropped my keys somewhere in the garden. Could you help me to find them?
    我把鑰匙掉在花園的某個地方了。你可以幫我找嗎？
  3. The bank sent me a strange bill. Could you help me write a letter to them?
    銀行寄給我一張奇怪的帳單。你可以幫我寫一封信給他們嗎？
  4. My bike has a flat tyre. Could you help me fix it?
    我的腳踏車爆胎了。你可以幫我修嗎？
  5. Ten people are coming tonight. Could you help me to cook dinner?
    今晚有十個人要來。你可以幫我煮晚餐嗎？
  6. It's my mum's birthday on Sunday. Could you help me choose a present for her?
    星期天是我媽媽的生日。你可以幫我挑一份禮物送她嗎？

④ 先說原因，再用 Could you

  1. I can't reach the top shelf. Could you get that box for me?
    我搆不到最上面那層。你可以幫我拿那個箱子嗎？
  2. My phone is broken. Could you call my dad for me?
    我的手機壞了。你可以幫我打電話給我爸爸嗎？
  3. I'm late for work. Could you take the kids to school?
    我上班要遲到了。你可以送孩子去上學嗎？
  4. I don't understand this word. Could you explain it to me?
    我不懂這個字。你可以解釋給我聽嗎？
  5. It's very cold in here. Could you turn the heating on?
    這裡很冷。你可以把暖氣打開嗎？
  6. I forgot my pencil case. Could you lend me a pen?
    我忘了帶鉛筆盒。你可以借我一支筆嗎？
  7. The baby is sleeping. Could you talk quietly?
    寶寶在睡覺。你可以小聲一點說話嗎？
  8. I'm going on holiday next week. Could you water my plants?
    我下週要去度假。你可以幫我澆花嗎？
