Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

如果要我從中選一個，我會選最後那個。

Were 加代⁠名⁠詞加 to 不⁠定⁠詞，說假⁠設⁠的事

如果要我從中選一個，我會選最後那個。

Were I to choose 等於 If I were to choose。

所⁠有⁠的人⁠稱都用 were，主⁠要⁠子⁠句常⁠用 would

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

Were we

Were we to lose this customer, the business would be in trouble.

要是我們失去這個客戶，公司就會有麻煩。

正⁠式⁠的信⁠件用 Were you to 客⁠氣⁠地說⁠明

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

請求

Were you to require any further details, please contact our office.

如果您需要任何進一步的細節，請聯絡我們的辦公室。

否⁠定⁠的時⁠候，not 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面、to 前⁠面

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

否定

Were they not to agree, we would need a new plan.

要是他們不同意，我們就需要新的計畫。

倒⁠裝⁠的條⁠件子⁠句一⁠律用 Were

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

Was I to win the lottery, I would buy a house.

Were I to win the lottery, I would buy a house.

倒⁠裝已⁠經代⁠替 if，不⁠再⁠加 if

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

If were we to move abroad, we would sell the car.

Were we to move abroad, we would sell the car.

代⁠名⁠詞後⁠面⁠接 to 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

Were you lose your job, what would you do?

Were you to lose your job, what would you do?

說假⁠設⁠的結⁠果，主⁠要⁠子⁠句用 would

Were I to choose one of them, I would select the last one.

Were she to leave, the team will struggle.

Were she to leave, the team would struggle.

例句

① Were I to

  1. Were I to retire early, I would open a small bookshop.
    要是我提早退休，我會開一家小書店。
  2. Were I to live anywhere in the world, I would choose New Zealand.
    要是我可以住在世界上任何地方，我會選紐西蘭。
  3. Were I to tell you the truth, you wouldn't believe me.
    要是我跟你說實話，你也不會相信我。
  4. Were I to win the prize, I would give the money to charity.
    要是我得獎，我會把錢捐給慈善機構。
  5. Were I to meet the president, I would ask about the new law.
    要是我見到總統，我會問新法律的事。

② Were you to

  1. Were you to have any problems with the order, please contact our support team.
    如果您的訂單有任何問題，請聯絡我們的客服團隊。
  2. Were you to change your mind, please let us know by May.
    如果您改變主意，請在五月前告訴我們。
  3. Were you to walk to the station, it would take about forty minutes.
    要是你走路去車站，大概要四十分鐘。
  4. Were you to ask my mother, she would say the same thing.
    要是你去問我媽媽，她也會說一樣的話。
  5. Were you to accept our offer, we could start next week.
    如果您接受我們的提議，我們下週就可以開始。
  6. Were you to cancel now, you would lose your deposit.
    要是您現在取消，就會失去訂金。

③ Were he / she / it to

  1. Were she to apply, she would certainly get the job.
    要是她去應徵，一定會得到那份工作。
  2. Were he to resign, who would replace him?
    要是他辭職，誰會接替他？
  3. Were it to rain on the day, the wedding would move indoors.
    要是那天下雨，婚禮會改在室內舉行。
  4. Were she to find out, she would be furious.
    要是她發現了，她會氣炸。
  5. Were it to snow tonight, the roads would be dangerous.
    要是今晚下雪，路會很危險。

④ Were we / they to

  1. Were we to hire more nurses, patients would wait less.
    要是我們多雇一些護理師，病人就不用等那麼久。
  2. Were they to raise the price again, we would find another supplier.
    要是他們再漲價，我們就會找別的供應商。
  3. Were we to leave at six, we would avoid the traffic.
    要是我們六點出發，就會避開塞車。
  4. Were they to offer me more money, I might stay.
    要是他們給我更多錢，我可能會留下來。
  5. Were we to sell the house, where would we live?
    要是我們把房子賣了，我們要住哪裡？
  6. Were they to close the factory, the town would suffer.
    要是他們關掉工廠，這個小鎮會受到很大的影響。

⑤ 否定和不同的情境

  1. Were we not to act now, the problem would only get worse.
    要是我們現在不採取行動，問題只會更嚴重。
  2. Were you not to return the form, your application could not proceed.
    如果您不交回表格，您的申請就無法進行。
  3. Were I to need a lawyer, I would call my cousin first.
    要是我需要律師，我會先打給我表哥。
  4. Were she not to come, the meeting would be pointless.
    要是她不來，這場會議就沒有意義了。
  5. Were it to happen again, we would call the police immediately.
    要是這種事再發生，我們會立刻報警。
  6. Were they to specify a budget, we could prepare a proposal.
    要是他們說明預算，我們就能準備提案。
  7. Were you to reconcile with your brother, your parents would be delighted.
    要是你跟你哥哥和好，你爸媽會很高興。
  8. Were I to lose my phone, I wouldn't remember a single number.
    要是我把手機弄丟了，我一個電話號碼都記不得。
