Are you going to the new art class?

你要去上新的美術課嗎？

助⁠動⁠詞 be 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面，後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Are you going to the new art class?

你星期天要來 Ken 的烤肉會嗎？

have 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面，後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞或 got

Are you going to the new art class?

have + 過去分詞

Have you finished the book I lent you?

我借你的那本書你看完了嗎？
have + got

I need to sign this form. Have you got a pen I can borrow?

我要簽這張表格。你有筆可以借我嗎？

已⁠經有助⁠動⁠詞 be，不⁠再⁠加 do

Are you going to the new art class?

Do you are moving to a new flat?

Are you moving to a new flat?

主⁠詞是 she，用 has

Are you going to the new art class?

Have she finished her lunch?

Has she finished her lunch?

have 後⁠面用過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Are you going to the new art class?

Have you ate yet?

Have you eaten yet?

例句

① Are you + 動詞-ing?

  1. The school has a list for after-school clubs. Are you going to the swimming lessons this term?
    學校有一張課後社團的名單。你這學期要去上游泳課嗎？
  2. Your knee looks better now. Are you playing in the match on Saturday?
    你的膝蓋看起來好多了。你星期六要上場比賽嗎？
  3. I haven't seen you for years. Are you still working at the bank?
    我好幾年沒見到你了。你還在銀行上班嗎？
  4. Are you listening to the radio, or can I turn it off?
    你在聽收音機嗎？還是我可以關掉？
  5. Your parents are going abroad in July. Are you staying with your aunt this summer?
    你爸媽七月要出國。你今年夏天要住在你阿姨家嗎？

② Is / Are + 主詞 + 動詞-ing / going to

  1. We want to go skiing next week. Is it snowing in the mountains yet?
    我們下星期想去滑雪。山上開始下雪了嗎？
  2. I see him training every morning. Is your brother running in the city marathon this year?
    我每天早上都看到他在練跑。你哥哥今年要跑城市馬拉松嗎？
  3. There are cans of green paint by the gate. Are your neighbors going to paint their fence green?
    大門旁邊有幾罐綠色油漆。你鄰居打算把籬笆漆成綠色嗎？
  4. Your sister got a letter from a British university. Is she going to study in London?
    你姊姊收到一所英國大學的信。她打算去倫敦讀書嗎？
  5. I can hear cartoons from the living room. Are the kids watching TV again?
    我聽到客廳傳來卡通的聲音。孩子們又在看電視了嗎？

③ Have / Has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. She needs an answer about the trip today. Have you replied to Ann's email yet?
    她今天就需要旅行的答覆。你回 Ann 的電子郵件了嗎？
  2. We're planning our honeymoon in the Maldives. Have you ever stayed in a hotel on the water?
    我們在計畫去馬爾地夫度蜜月。你住過蓋在水上的飯店嗎？
  3. Has the plumber called you back about the leak?
    水電工有回你電話說漏水的事嗎？
  4. They moved to the city in August. Have your cousins found a school for their son?
    他們八月搬到城裡。你表哥他們幫兒子找到學校了嗎？
  5. She sent it last Monday. Has the parcel from Grandma arrived?
    她上星期一寄的。奶奶寄的包裹到了嗎？
  6. The football starts in five minutes. Have you seen the remote control anywhere?
    足球賽五分鐘後開始。你有看到遙控器嗎？

④ Have / Has + 主詞 + got

  1. The show is on Friday night. Have you got a costume for the school play?
    表演在星期五晚上。你有學校話劇要穿的戲服嗎？
  2. I need to sign this form. Have you got a pen I can borrow?
    我要簽這張表格。你有筆可以借我嗎？
  3. I want to grow tomatoes. Has your flat got a balcony?
    我想種番茄。你的公寓有陽台嗎？
  4. Your dog keeps running next door. Have your neighbors got a garden?
    你的狗一直跑去隔壁。你的鄰居有花園嗎？
  5. Lisa is good with computers. Has she got time to help us?
    麗莎很會用電腦。她有時間幫我們嗎？

⑤ 簡短回答和不同的句型

  1. A: Is your sister coming too? B: No, she isn't.
    A：你姊姊也要來嗎？B：不來。
  2. A: Has your hotel got a pool? B: Yes, it has.
    A：你們飯店有游泳池嗎？B：有。
  3. Sorry, you sounded busy last night. Were you having dinner when I phoned?
    抱歉，你昨天晚上聽起來很忙。我打電話的時候你在吃晚餐嗎？
  4. Your sister's husband seems very nice. Had you met him before the wedding?
    你姊姊的先生看起來人很好。婚禮以前你見過他嗎？
  5. I need to print my report. Is anybody using this printer at the moment?
    我要印我的報告。現在有人在用這台印表機嗎？
  6. You've hardly eaten your dinner. Are you feeling nervous about tomorrow's interview?
    你晚餐幾乎都沒吃。你對明天的面試感到緊張嗎？
  7. You love fresh bread. Have you heard about the new bakery on Elm Street?
    你很愛新鮮麵包。你聽說榆樹街上那家新開的麵包店了嗎？
  8. The police asked me about the visitor. Was the man at the door wearing a uniform?
    警察問我那個訪客的事。門口那個男人當時穿著制服嗎？
  9. Has the baby stopped crying, or should I go up?
    寶寶不哭了嗎？還是我該上樓去看看？
