I hadn't expected to receive a letter from her.

我沒想到會收到她的信。

過⁠去完⁠成⁠式⁠的否⁠定在 had 後⁠面加 not

我沒想到會收到她的信。

說的是收到信之前的想法。

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式⁠的否⁠定在 was 或 were 後⁠面加 not

I hadn't expected to receive a letter from her.

你打來的時候我沒在睡覺。

說的是過去某個時間點正在發生的事。

hadn't 說⁠的⁠是過⁠去某⁠件⁠事發⁠生之⁠前⁠的情⁠況

I hadn't expected to receive a letter from her.

我們到電影院的時候，電影還沒開始。

過⁠去完⁠成⁠式⁠的否⁠定不⁠用 did

I hadn't expected to receive a letter from her.

The builders didn't had finished the roof.

The builders hadn't finished the roof.

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式⁠的否⁠定⁠句要⁠有 was 或 were

We not watching the game when the lights went out.

We weren't watching the game when the lights went out.

複⁠數主⁠詞用 weren't

The children wasn't sleeping.

The children weren't sleeping.

hadn't 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

I hadn't expected to receive a letter from her.

She hadn't went to a concert before.

She hadn't been to a concert before.

說「去過」用 been。

例句

① wasn't / weren't + 動詞-ing

  1. I wasn't driving fast when the police stopped me. My rear light was broken.
    警察攔下我的時候，我沒開快車。是我的車尾燈壞了。
  2. Sorry, what did you say? I wasn't listening.
    抱歉，你剛剛說什麼？我沒在聽。
  3. When you called, I wasn't sleeping. I was in the shower.
    你打來的時候我沒在睡覺。我在洗澡。
  4. The kids weren't doing their homework when I got home. They were playing games.
    我回家的時候，孩子們沒在寫功課。他們在玩遊戲。
  5. He wasn't wearing a coat, so he got very wet.
    他沒穿外套，所以淋得全身濕透。
  6. We weren't expecting visitors, so the house was a mess.
    我們沒料到會有客人，所以家裡亂七八糟。
  7. The lift wasn't working, so the delivery man left the parcel downstairs.
    電梯壞了，所以送貨員把包裹留在樓下。
  8. I wasn't feeling well at the party, so I left early.
    我在派對上不太舒服，所以提早走了。
  9. They weren't living in London when they got married. They were still in Leeds.
    他們結婚的時候不住在倫敦。他們還住在 Leeds。

② was not / were not + 動詞-ing（不縮寫）

  1. The driver was not paying attention when the accident happened.
    意外發生的時候，司機沒有專心開車。
  2. The machines were not running during the strike.
    罷工期間，機器都停著。

③ hadn't + 過去分詞

  1. I hadn't expected so many people at the funeral.
    我沒想到葬禮會來這麼多人。
  2. When we got to the cinema, the film hadn't started.
    我們到電影院的時候，電影還沒開始。
  3. She hadn't seen the sea before her trip to Spain.
    她去西班牙之前沒看過海。
  4. I was hungry because I hadn't eaten all day.
    我很餓，因為我一整天都沒吃東西。
  5. We hadn't booked a table, so we waited for an hour.
    我們沒有訂位，所以等了一個小時。
  6. He failed the test because he hadn't studied.
    他考試沒過，因為他沒念書。
  7. The guests arrived, and I hadn't finished cooking.
    客人到了，我還沒煮完飯。
  8. They hadn't met before the wedding, but they got on well.
    他們在婚禮之前沒見過面，可是相處得很好。
  9. I hadn't heard the news until my sister told me.
    我姊姊跟我說之前，我還沒聽說這個消息。
  10. The shop had closed, and we hadn't bought any milk.
    店已經關了，我們還沒買牛奶。
  11. My parents hadn't been to Japan before last year.
    我爸媽去年之前沒去過日本。

④ had not + 過去分詞（不縮寫）

  1. The company had not paid the workers for three months.
    那家公司已經三個月沒付薪水給員工了。
  2. By ten o'clock the plane had still not arrived.
    到了十點，飛機還沒到。
  3. The police said the thief had not taken anything.
    警方說小偷什麼都沒拿。

⑤ wasn't / weren't + going to + 原形動詞

  1. I wasn't going to tell you, but you'll find out anyway.
    我本來不打算跟你說，可是你反正會知道。
  2. We weren't going to buy a car, but the old one broke down.
    我們本來沒打算買車，可是舊車壞了。

⑥ hadn't 和 wasn't 在同一句

  1. I wasn't surprised that he left. He hadn't enjoyed the job for months.
    他辭職我並不意外。他好幾個月來都不喜歡那份工作。
  2. The baby wasn't crying, so I knew she hadn't woken up.
    寶寶沒在哭，所以我知道她還沒醒。
  3. When I got to the station, the train wasn't there. It hadn't arrived yet.
    我到車站的時候，火車不在那裡。它還沒到。
  4. She wasn't speaking to me because I hadn't called her on her birthday.
    她不跟我說話，因為我在她生日那天沒打電話給她。
