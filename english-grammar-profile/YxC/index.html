I was wondering if I could stay with you again on my next visit.

不知道我下次來訪時，能不能再麻煩您一次。

I was wondering if + 主⁠詞 + could + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I was wondering if、I was thinking that、could

I was wondering if我在想能不能……。客氣的請求。
I was thinking that我在想……。客氣的建議。
could讓請求或建議更委婉。

現⁠在⁠的請⁠求

例句

I was wondering if you could help me.

不知道你能不能幫我。（現在請你幫忙）

這裡的過去進行式說的是現在的請求，不是過去的事。

提⁠出建⁠議

例句

I was thinking that you could come to my house.

我在想你可以來我家。

I was thinking that you could 用來提出建議，不會太強硬。

if 後⁠面用直⁠述⁠句⁠的順⁠序

I was wondering if I could stay with you again on my next visit.

I was wondering if could you help me.

I was wondering if you could help me.

⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面要⁠有 was

I was wondering if I could stay with you again on my next visit.

I wondering if I could borrow your car.

I was wondering if I could borrow your car.

wondering 後⁠面要⁠用 if 帶⁠出子⁠句

I was wondering if I could stay with you again on my next visit.

I was wondering you could call me.

I was wondering if you could call me.

例句

① I was wondering if I could + 原形動詞：客氣地請求許可

  1. Thank you for having me last month. I was wondering if I could stay with you again on my next visit.
    謝謝您上個月讓我住。我在想，下次去的時候能不能再打擾您。
  2. I was wondering if I could use your sewing machine to fix my curtains.
    我在想能不能借用你的縫紉機修我的窗簾。
  3. I was wondering if I could leave work an hour early on Friday.
    我在想星期五能不能提早一個小時下班。
  4. I was wondering if I could ask you a few questions about the job.
    我在想能不能問您幾個關於這份工作的問題。
  5. I was wondering if I could bring a friend to the party.
    我在想能不能帶一個朋友去派對。
  6. I was wondering if I could pay the rest of the money next month.
    我在想剩下的錢能不能下個月再付。

② I was wondering if you could + 原形動詞：客氣地請對方做

  1. I was wondering if you could help me with my English homework.
    不知道你能不能幫我寫英文功課。
  2. I was wondering if you could give me a lift to the station.
    不知道你能不能載我去車站。
  3. I was wondering if you could send me the photos from the wedding.
    不知道你能不能把婚禮的照片寄給我。
  4. I was wondering if you could look after my dog next week.
    不知道你下星期能不能幫我照顧狗。
  5. I was wondering if you could recommend a good dentist.
    不知道您能不能推薦一位好牙醫。
  6. I was wondering if you could check my CV before I send it.
    不知道你能不能在我寄出之前幫我看一下履歷。

③ I was thinking (that) you could + 原形動詞：客氣的建議

  1. For the fancy-dress party, I was thinking that you could wear your Marilyn Monroe dress.
    化裝舞會的時候，我在想你可以穿你那件瑪麗蓮夢露的洋裝。
  2. I was thinking that you could come to my house and watch the film.
    我在想你可以來我家看電影。
  3. I was thinking you could bring your guitar to the barbecue.
    我在想你可以帶你的吉他來烤肉。
  4. I was thinking that you could ask your teacher for more time.
    我在想你可以請你的老師多給一點時間。
  5. I was thinking you could stay for dinner if you're free.
    我在想如果你有空，可以留下來吃晚餐。
  6. I was thinking that you could paint the bedroom green.
    我在想你可以把臥室漆成綠色。

④ I was thinking (that) we could + 原形動詞：建議一起做

  1. I was thinking we could go to the beach on Saturday.
    我在想我們星期六可以去海灘。
  2. I was thinking that we could have a picnic in the park.
    我在想我們可以去公園野餐。
  3. I was thinking we could try that new Thai restaurant.
    我在想我們可以去試試那家新的泰國餐廳。
  4. I was thinking that we could share a taxi to the airport.
    我在想我們可以一起搭計程車去機場。
  5. I was thinking we could visit Grandma on Sunday.
    我在想我們星期天可以去看奶奶。
  6. I was thinking that we could study together for the exam.
    我在想我們可以一起準備考試。

⑤ I was hoping (that) + 主詞 + could / would + 原形動詞

  1. I was hoping you could help me move house on Saturday.
    我希望你星期六能幫我搬家。
  2. I was hoping that you would come to my birthday party.
    我希望你會來我的生日派對。
  3. I was hoping I could talk to you about my grades.
    我希望能跟您談談我的成績。
  4. I was hoping you could lend me some money until Friday.
    我希望你能借我一點錢到星期五。
  5. I was hoping we could meet for coffee next week.
    我希望我們下星期能見面喝杯咖啡。
  6. I was hoping that you would say yes.
    我希望你會答應。
  7. I was hoping you could explain this letter to me.
    我希望你能幫我解釋這封信。
