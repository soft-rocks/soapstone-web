The number of Dutch people eating a big, hot breakfast may not have been as high as in England, but it has certainly fallen.

工作能幫助他們認識別人，學到待在家裡可能學不到的技能。

主⁠詞 + may not have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

may not have、過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

may not have可能沒有。
過去分詞過去可能沒發生的事。

用在報⁠告和議⁠論文

例句

Earlier figures may not have been accurate.

早期的數字可能不準確。

報告和議論文常用這個句型，小心地說過去的情況，不說得太肯定。

用在關⁠係子⁠句

The number of Dutch people eating a big, hot breakfast may not have been as high as in England, but it has certainly fallen.

例句

She learned skills that she may not have learned at home.

她學到了在家裡可能學不到的技能。

may not have 也常放在 that 或 which 帶出的子句裡。

not 放⁠在 may 和 have 中⁠間

The number of Dutch people eating a big, hot breakfast may not have been as high as in England, but it has certainly fallen.

He may have not seen it.

He may not have seen it.

may 後⁠面⁠接 not have

The number of Dutch people eating a big, hot breakfast may not have been as high as in England, but it has certainly fallen.

She may hasn't heard the news.

She may not have heard the news.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The number of Dutch people eating a big, hot breakfast may not have been as high as in England, but it has certainly fallen.

They may not have know about it.

They may not have known about it.

例句

① may not have been + …：比較過去的情況

  1. The town may not have been rich, but it had a fine theatre.
    那個鎮以前可能不富有，可是有一座很好的劇院。
  2. Life in the village may not have been easy, but people were happy.
    以前村子裡的生活可能不輕鬆，可是大家很快樂。
  3. The first computers may not have been fast, but they changed the world.
    第一批電腦可能不快，可是它們改變了世界。
  4. Earlier figures may not have been accurate.
    早期的數字可能不準確。
  5. The old school may not have been modern, but the teachers were excellent.
    那所老學校可能不現代，可是老師都很優秀。
  6. The weather may not have been perfect, but we had a great holiday.
    天氣可能不完美，可是我們度了一個很棒的假。

② that / which + 主詞 + may not have + 過去分詞

  1. Working outside the home helps couples meet other people and gain skills that they may not have got by staying at home.
    在外面工作讓夫妻認識其他人，學到待在家裡可能學不到的技能。
  2. Travelling gave me ideas which I may not have had at home.
    旅行給了我在家裡可能不會有的想法。
  3. The course taught me things that I may not have learned at university.
    這門課教了我在大學可能學不到的東西。
  4. Volunteering gave her friends that she may not have met otherwise.
    當志工讓她交到了原本可能不會認識的朋友。
  5. The trip showed the children places that they may not have seen on television.
    這趟旅行帶孩子們看了電視上可能看不到的地方。
  6. I made some mistakes that you may not have noticed.
    我犯了一些你可能沒注意到的錯。

③ may not have + 過去分詞：猜別人沒做的事

  1. Call her again. She may not have seen your message.
    再打給她一次。她可能沒看到你的訊息。
  2. The new waiter may not have written down your order correctly.
    新來的服務生可能沒把你點的菜記對。
  3. They may not have known that the shop was closed.
    他們可能不知道那家店關了。
  4. The students may not have read the whole chapter.
    學生可能沒讀完整章。
  5. You may not have heard, but the meeting has moved to Friday.
    你可能沒聽說，可是會議改到星期五了。
  6. The driver may not have seen the cyclist in the dark.
    天黑了，司機可能沒看到騎腳踏車的人。

④ may not have + 過去分詞：歷史和研究

  1. The king may not have written the letter himself.
    那封信可能不是國王親手寫的。
  2. People in the past may not have eaten as much sugar as we do.
    以前的人吃的糖可能沒有我們現在多。
  3. The early settlers may not have known how cold the winters were.
    早期的移民可能不知道冬天有多冷。
  4. The scientists may not have tested the drug on enough people.
    科學家可能沒有在夠多的人身上試驗這種藥。
  5. The writer may not have planned the ending from the start.
    作者可能一開始沒有計畫好結局。
  6. The ancient Romans may not have used this road very often.
    古羅馬人可能不常走這條路。

⑤ may not have + 過去分詞, but + 句子：承認，再說重點

  1. I may not have won the race, but I beat my best time.
    我可能沒有贏得比賽，可是我打破了自己最好的紀錄。
  2. The plan may not have worked perfectly, but we learned a lot.
    這個計畫可能沒有完全成功，可是我們學到很多。
  3. She may not have said much, but she listened to everyone.
    她可能沒說很多話，可是她聽了每個人的意見。
  4. We may not have made much money, but the customers were happy.
    我們可能沒賺很多錢，可是顧客很滿意。
  5. He may not have studied art, but his paintings are wonderful.
    他可能沒學過藝術，可是他的畫很棒。
  6. The hotel may not have had a pool, but the beach was close.
    那家飯店可能沒有游泳池，可是海灘很近。
  7. The team may not have played well, but they never gave up.
    球隊可能踢得不好，可是他們從來沒有放棄。
