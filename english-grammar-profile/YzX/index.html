Hardly had he taken them from the counter when the monster vanished again.

她才剛說完這些話，那位音樂家就明白了，生活不能再這樣下去。

Hardly + had + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 + when + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠式

hardly、had + 主⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞、when

hardly才剛。放在句首。
had + 主詞 + 過去分詞先發生的事，倒裝。
when後面接馬上接著發生的事，用過去式。

一⁠般⁠的順⁠序

Hardly had he taken them from the counter when the monster vanished again.

例句

We wanted to play tennis. I had hardly arrived when it rained.

我們想打網球。我才剛到就下雨了。

不倒裝的時候，hardly 放在 had 後面。

hardly 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，had 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Hardly had he taken them from the counter when the monster vanished again.

Hardly the guests had sat down when the door opened.

Hardly had the guests sat down when the door opened.

hardly 和 when 一⁠起用

Hardly had he taken them from the counter when the monster vanished again.

Hardly had we arrived than it began to snow.

Hardly had we arrived when it began to snow.

had 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Hardly had he taken them from the counter when the monster vanished again.

Hardly had I took my seat when the film started.

Hardly had I taken my seat when the film started.

例句

① Hardly had + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + when + 句子：故事的轉折

  1. A small monster sat on the counter next to a pair of gold glasses. Hardly had he taken them from the counter when the monster vanished again.
    一隻小怪物坐在櫃台上，旁邊有一副金色的眼鏡。他才剛從櫃台上拿起眼鏡，那隻怪物就又消失了。
  2. His wife told him that she was leaving. Hardly had she spoken these words when the musician realised that life could not continue like that.
    他的太太說她要離開了。她才剛說出這些話，那位音樂家就明白生活不能再這樣下去了。
  3. Hardly had the captain left the ship when it began to sink.
    船長才剛離開船，船就開始沉了。
  4. Hardly had the princess opened the box when a white bird flew out.
    公主才剛打開盒子，一隻白鳥就飛了出來。
  5. Hardly had the clock struck twelve when the lights went out.
    時鐘才剛敲十二下，燈就熄了。
  6. Hardly had the old man closed his eyes when he heard footsteps.
    那個老人才剛閉上眼睛，就聽到了腳步聲。

② Hardly had I + 過去分詞 + when + 句子：自己的經歷

  1. Hardly had I arrived at the beach when it started to pour.
    我才剛到海灘，就開始下大雨了。
  2. Hardly had I sat down to dinner when the doorbell rang.
    我才剛坐下來吃晚餐，門鈴就響了。
  3. Hardly had I got into bed when the baby began to cry.
    我才剛上床，寶寶就開始哭了。
  4. Hardly had I started my speech when the microphone stopped working.
    我才剛開始演講，麥克風就壞了。
  5. Hardly had I turned on the computer when the power went off.
    我才剛打開電腦，就停電了。
  6. Hardly had I stepped off the train when I saw my old teacher.
    我才剛下火車，就看到我以前的老師。

③ Hardly had + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + when + 天氣或意外

  1. Hardly had we set off when the car broke down.
    我們才剛出發，車子就拋錨了。
  2. Hardly had the game started when it began to snow.
    比賽才剛開始，就開始下雪了。
  3. Hardly had they put up the tent when the wind blew it away.
    他們才剛搭好帳篷，風就把它吹走了。
  4. Hardly had she finished painting the wall when the children drew on it.
    她才剛油漆完牆壁，孩子們就在上面畫畫。
  5. Hardly had the plane landed when the storm hit the airport.
    飛機才剛降落，暴風雨就襲擊了機場。
  6. Hardly had the fishermen pulled in their nets when huge waves began to crash over the boat.
    漁夫們才剛收網，巨浪就開始打上船。

④ 主詞 + had hardly + 過去分詞 + when + 句子：一般的順序

  1. I had hardly opened the door when the dog ran out.
    我才剛打開門，狗就跑出去了。
  2. She had hardly finished her coffee when the bus arrived.
    她才剛喝完咖啡，公車就來了。
  3. We had hardly started eating when the phone rang.
    我們才剛開始吃，電話就響了。
  4. He had hardly left the house when he realised he had forgotten his keys.
    他才剛出門，就發現他忘了帶鑰匙。
  5. My two new neighbours had hardly met when they started to argue about the parking.
    我的兩個新鄰居才剛見面，就開始為了停車的事吵架。
  6. The teacher had hardly begun the lesson when the fire alarm went off.
    老師才剛開始上課，火災警報就響了。

⑤ Hardly had + 主詞 + 過去分詞 + when + 主詞 + realised / saw

  1. Hardly had she opened the letter when she realised it was not for her.
    她才剛打開信，就發現那不是給她的。
  2. Hardly had he entered the room when he saw the broken window.
    他才剛走進房間，就看到了破掉的窗戶。
  3. Hardly had I left the shop when I noticed the wrong change.
    我才剛離開那家店，就注意到找錯錢了。
  4. Hardly had they driven away when they saw smoke behind them.
    他們才剛開車離開，就看到後面冒煙。
  5. Hardly had we sat down when we realised we were in the wrong cinema.
    我們才剛坐下，就發現我們走錯了電影院。
  6. Hardly had she put down the phone when she remembered the question she had forgotten to ask.
    她才剛放下電話，就想起她忘了問的問題。
  7. Hardly had the boy woken up when he smelt the pancakes.
    那個男孩才剛醒來，就聞到了鬆餅的味道。
