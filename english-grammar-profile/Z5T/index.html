She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

她漂亮、聰明、風趣，而且非常可靠。

前⁠面⁠的形⁠容⁠詞用逗⁠號隔⁠開，最⁠後一⁠個前⁠面放 and

She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

她漂亮、聰明、風趣，而且非常可靠。

very 放⁠在它修⁠飾⁠的形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

not very 也放在形容詞前面，像 not very handsome。

and 前⁠面可⁠以加逗⁠號，也⁠可⁠以不⁠加

She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

不加逗號

Our classroom is large, bright and noisy.

我們的教室很大、很明亮，也很吵。
加逗號

The lesson was short, clear, and useful.

那堂課很短、很清楚，也很有用。

兩種寫法都對，同一篇文章裡用同一種。

最⁠後一⁠個形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面要加 and

She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

My teacher is kind, funny, smart.

My teacher is kind, funny and smart.

be 和第⁠一⁠個形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間不⁠加逗⁠號

She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

He is, tall, thin and friendly.

He is tall, thin and friendly.

be 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞，不⁠接副⁠詞

She is beautiful, intelligent, funny and very reliable.

The work was quick, easy and carefully.

The work was quick, easy and careful.

例句

① 三個形容詞

  1. Our classroom is large, bright and noisy.
    我們的教室很大、很明亮，也很吵。
  2. The wind was cold, strong and wet.
    風又冷又強，還很潮濕。
  3. Our new teacher is young, friendly and patient.
    我們的新老師年輕、友善又有耐心。
  4. The hotel was clean, cheap and comfortable.
    那家旅館乾淨、便宜又舒服。
  5. The streets are narrow, old and busy.
    那些街道又窄又舊，還很熱鬧。
  6. His story was long, sad and true.
    他的故事很長、很悲傷，而且是真的。
  7. The fish was fresh, salty and cheap.
    那條魚很新鮮、很鹹，也很便宜。
  8. My dog is small, white and very lazy.
    我的狗小小的、白白的，而且非常懶。
  9. The test was short, easy and fair.
    考試很短、很簡單，也很公平。
  10. Ken is tall, thin and not very handsome.
    Ken 又高又瘦，長得不太帥。
  11. The lake was calm, blue and very deep.
    湖面很平靜、很藍，而且非常深。
  12. Was the movie long, slow and boring?
    那部電影是不是又長又慢又無聊？

② 四個以上的形容詞

  1. The town is small, quiet, safe and friendly.
    那個小鎮又小又安靜，很安全，也很友善。
  2. The apartment is big, bright, quiet and cheap.
    那間公寓又大又亮，很安靜，也很便宜。
  3. My grandmother is kind, wise, strong and always cheerful.
    我奶奶善良、有智慧、堅強，而且總是很開朗。
  4. The trip was long, hot, tiring and wonderful.
    這趟旅行很長、很熱、很累，可是很棒。
  5. Our team was fast, strong, smart and lucky.
    我們隊速度快、體力好、腦筋靈活，運氣也好。
  6. The kitchen is small, old, dark and dirty.
    廚房又小又舊，又暗又髒。
  7. Ann is honest, hardworking, polite and very generous.
    Ann 誠實、勤奮、有禮貌，而且非常大方。
  8. The café is warm, cozy, friendly and not expensive.
    那家咖啡館溫暖、舒適、親切，而且不貴。

③ and 前面加逗號

  1. The museum was big, modern, and very interesting.
    那間博物館很大、很現代，而且非常有趣。
  2. My brother is loud, messy, and always hungry.
    我弟弟很吵、很亂，而且老是肚子餓。
  3. The roads were wet, icy, and dangerous.
    路面濕滑、結冰，而且很危險。
  4. The new phone is light, fast, and easy to use.
    新手機很輕、很快，而且很好用。
  5. The lesson was short, clear, and useful.
    那堂課很短、很清楚，也很有用。

④ 不同的時態

  1. The children will be tired, hungry and dirty after the game.
    比賽完以後，孩子們會又累又餓又髒。
  2. The house was old, cold and empty.
    那棟房子又舊又冷，空蕩蕩的。
  3. My neighbors are quiet, tidy and polite.
    我的鄰居很安靜、很愛乾淨，也很有禮貌。
  4. My first car was small, slow and very cheap.
    我的第一輛車又小又慢，而且非常便宜。
  5. The questions were strange, hard and a little unfair.
    那些題目很奇怪、很難，而且有點不公平。
