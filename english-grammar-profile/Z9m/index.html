They were obliged to give up their house.

他們不得不放棄自己的房子。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are / was / were + obliged to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

be obliged to不得不、有義務。
原形動詞被要求做的事。

正⁠式⁠的通⁠知

例句

All staff are obliged to attend the training.

所有員工都必須參加訓練。

公司和機構的通知常用這個說法。

否⁠定：not obliged to

例句

You are not obliged to answer.

你不必回答。

not obliged to 表示「不必」，沒有義務，不是「不可以」。

這⁠個片⁠語是 be obliged to，用過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 obliged

They were obliged to give up their house.

We are oblige to pay the fee.

We are obliged to pay the fee.

obliged 前⁠面要⁠有 be 動⁠詞

They were obliged to give up their house.

Everyone obliged to have a permit.

Everyone is obliged to have a permit.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

They were obliged to give up their house.

They were obliged to leaving the country.

They were obliged to leave the country.

例句

① am / is / are + obliged to：規定和義務

  1. All sales representatives are obliged to take part in the conference.
    所有業務代表都必須參加研討會。
  2. Employers are obliged to provide a safe place to work.
    雇主有義務提供安全的工作環境。
  3. Parents are obliged to send their children to school.
    父母有義務送孩子上學。
  4. Tenants are obliged to keep the flat clean.
    房客有義務保持公寓清潔。
  5. Doctors are obliged to keep patients' information private.
    醫生有義務為病人的資料保密。
  6. Shops are obliged to give a refund for faulty goods.
    商店有義務為瑕疵商品退款。
  7. Drivers are obliged to carry their licence at all times.
    駕駛必須隨時攜帶駕照。
  8. Members are obliged to pay the fee by the end of January.
    會員必須在一月底前繳費。

② was / were + obliged to：過去不得不

  1. The family were obliged to borrow money from the bank.
    那家人不得不向銀行借錢。
  2. The company was obliged to close two of its factories.
    那家公司不得不關掉兩間工廠。
  3. We were obliged to wait three hours at the border.
    我們在邊境不得不等了三個小時。
  4. The pilot was obliged to land at a different airport.
    飛行員不得不降落在另一個機場。
  5. I was obliged to stay late to finish the report.
    我不得不留到很晚把報告寫完。
  6. The school was obliged to cancel the trip because of the strike.
    因為罷工，學校不得不取消旅行。
  7. The team were obliged to play without their captain.
    球隊不得不在沒有隊長的情況下比賽。
  8. She was obliged to sell her car to pay her debts.
    她不得不賣車來還債。

③ will be + obliged to：未來的規定

  1. From January, everybody will be obliged to have a parking permit for the company car park.
    從一月起，大家使用公司停車場都必須有停車證。
  2. Visitors will be obliged to show identity cards at the entrance.
    訪客在入口必須出示身分證。
  3. All new staff will be obliged to complete a safety course.
    所有新員工都必須完成安全課程。
  4. Restaurants will be obliged to show the calories on their menus.
    餐廳必須在菜單上標示熱量。
  5. Students will be obliged to wear uniforms from next year.
    從明年起學生必須穿制服。
  6. Passengers will be obliged to wear seat belts on all buses.
    所有公車上的乘客都必須繫安全帶。
  7. Companies will be obliged to report their profits every quarter.
    公司每一季都必須申報利潤。

④ not obliged to：不必

  1. You are not obliged to answer any of these questions.
    這些問題你都不必回答。
  2. Customers are not obliged to buy anything.
    顧客不必買任何東西。
  3. You're not obliged to come to the party if you're tired.
    如果你累了，不必來派對。
  4. We weren't obliged to stay, so we left early.
    我們不必留下來，所以我們提早走了。
  5. Students are not obliged to take part in the competition.
    學生不必參加比賽。
  6. You aren't obliged to give a reason for leaving.
    你不必說明離開的理由。
  7. Guests are not obliged to leave a tip.
    客人不必給小費。
  8. You are under no obligation, and you are not obliged to sign today.
    你沒有任何義務，今天不必簽約。
