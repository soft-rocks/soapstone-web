I had the opportunity to teach Vietnamese children whose parents had immigrated to Australia.

我有機會教一些越南孩子，他們的父母之前移民到了澳洲。

whose 加名⁠詞說⁠明是哪⁠些⁠人，不⁠加逗⁠號

我有機會教一些越南孩子，他們的父母之前移民到了澳洲。

whose 常接家⁠人或工⁠作關⁠係⁠的名⁠詞

I had the opportunity to teach Vietnamese children whose parents had immigrated to Australia.

家人

I know a woman whose son plays for the national team.

我認識一位女士，她兒子是國家隊的球員。
家人

Students whose parents work late can stay in the library until seven.

父母工作到很晚的學生可以在圖書館待到七點。

whose 加名⁠詞也⁠可⁠以當子⁠句⁠的受⁠詞

I had the opportunity to teach Vietnamese children whose parents had immigrated to Australia.

受詞

She's the author whose books my daughter loves.

她就是寫了我女兒很喜歡的那些書的作者。

說誰⁠的家⁠人用 whose，不⁠用 who's

I had the opportunity to teach Vietnamese children whose parents had immigrated to Australia.

I have a friend who's father is a pilot.

I have a friend whose father is a pilot.

whose 已⁠經表⁠示他⁠的，不⁠用 that his

I had the opportunity to teach Vietnamese children whose parents had immigrated to Australia.

I met a man that his wife is from Brazil.

I met a man whose wife is from Brazil.

動⁠詞跟⁠著 whose 後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞變⁠化

I had the opportunity to teach Vietnamese children whose parents had immigrated to Australia.

I know a girl whose parents is teachers.

I know a girl whose parents are teachers.

例句

① 家人的關係

  1. Children whose parents read to them often learn to read early.
    父母常唸書給他們聽的孩子通常很早就學會閱讀。
  2. I sat next to a man whose daughter had just won a gold medal.
    我坐在一位男士旁邊，他女兒剛拿下金牌。
  3. We need a babysitter whose kids are already grown up.
    我們需要一位孩子已經長大的保母。
  4. There's a girl in my class whose twin brother goes to another school.
    我們班有個女生，她的雙胞胎哥哥讀別的學校。
  5. Families whose homes the flood damaged can apply for help.
    房子在水災中受損的家庭可以申請補助。
  6. I know a couple whose wedding lasted three days.
    我認識一對夫妻，他們的婚禮辦了三天。

② 工作和朋友的關係

  1. I spoke to a nurse whose patients all love her.
    我跟一位護理師聊過，她的病人都很喜歡她。
  2. He's a manager whose team always finishes on time.
    他是一位經理，他帶的團隊總是準時完成工作。
  3. We're looking for a designer whose ideas are fresh.
    我們在找想法新穎的設計師。
  4. She works for a man whose company makes bicycles.
    她在一個開腳踏車公司的人手下工作。
  5. Employees whose contracts end in June must speak to HR.
    合約六月到期的員工要去找人資談。
  6. I have a friend whose boss lets her work from home.
    我有個朋友，她老闆讓她在家工作。

③ 東西和地方

  1. I want a car whose seats fold down flat.
    我想要一輛座椅可以完全放平的車。
  2. Choose a password whose letters are hard to guess.
    選一組字母很難被猜到的密碼。
  3. They live in a building whose lift is always broken.
    他們住在一棟電梯老是壞掉的大樓。
  4. We stayed at a hotel whose owner cooked breakfast for us every morning.
    我們住在一家飯店，老闆每天早上幫我們做早餐。
  5. It's a country whose history goes back thousands of years.
    那是一個歷史有好幾千年的國家。

④ whose + 名詞當受詞

  1. He's the singer whose songs my mother sings in the shower.
    他就是唱那些我媽媽洗澡時會哼的歌的歌手。
  2. Is she the student whose essay you read out in class?
    她就是你在課堂上唸出作文的那個學生嗎？
  3. That's the neighbor whose dog we walk on weekends.
    那就是我們週末幫忙遛狗的鄰居。
  4. I finally met the man whose house we're renting.
    我終於見到我們租的那間房子的屋主了。
  5. He's an artist whose paintings I've seen in Paris.
    他是一位藝術家，我在巴黎看過他的畫。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Anyone whose ticket has a red star wins a prize.
    票上有紅色星星的人可以得獎。
  2. People whose jobs involve a lot of travel often feel tired.
    工作要常出差的人常覺得累。
  3. Is there anyone here whose phone isn't switched off?
    這裡有誰的手機還沒關嗎？
  4. The boy whose bike you borrowed is waiting outside.
    你跟他借腳踏車的那個男孩在外面等。
  5. Mary was an orphan whose only relative was an old aunt.
    Mary 是個孤兒，她唯一的親人是一位年老的阿姨。
  6. I met a writer whose wry sense of humor made everyone laugh.
    我認識了一位作家，他冷面的幽默感讓大家都笑了。
  7. Patients whose blood pressure is high should avoid salt.
    血壓高的病人應該少吃鹽。
  8. She's one of those people whose smile makes you feel welcome.
    她是那種笑容讓人覺得很受歡迎的人。
