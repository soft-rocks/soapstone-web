She makes me smile when I'm sad.

我難過的時候，她總能讓我笑出來。

make 的受⁠詞後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

我難過的時候，她總能讓我笑出來。

make 是讓⁠人⁠做，let 是允⁠許人做

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

make
不想做

My boss made me work late.

我老闆叫我加班。
let
允許

My parents let me go to the concert.

我爸媽讓我去聽演唱會。
let
讓人能夠

This app lets you pay with your phone.

這個應用程式讓你可以用手機付錢。

Let me know 請對⁠方告⁠訴自⁠己，Let's 提⁠議一⁠起做

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

Let me know

Let me know if Friday is OK for you.

星期五你方便的話跟我說一聲。
Let's

Let's go for a walk.

我們去散步吧。

make 的受⁠詞後⁠面不⁠加 to

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

That joke made everyone to laugh.

That joke made everyone laugh.

let 的受⁠詞後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

My parents let me going out on Saturdays.

My parents let me go out on Saturdays.

時⁠態只標在 make 上

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

The teacher made us stayed after class.

The teacher made us stay after class.

let 的過⁠去⁠式和原⁠形一⁠樣

They letted us use the kitchen.

They let us use the kitchen.

被⁠動⁠的 make 和 allow 後⁠面要加 to

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

被動的 make

I was made to wait for two hours.

我被迫等了兩個小時。
allow

The school allows students to use the library at lunch.

學校准許學生午餐時間使用圖書館。

make 的受⁠詞後⁠面也⁠可⁠以接形⁠容⁠詞

She makes me smile when I'm sad.

The sunny weather made the beach busy.

晴朗的天氣讓海灘很熱鬧。

例句

① make + 受詞 + 原形動詞（讓人有反應）

  1. My best friend is so funny. She always makes me laugh.
    我最好的朋友好好笑。她總是能讓我笑。
  2. "Why are you crying?" "This song always makes me cry."
    你為什麼在哭？這首歌每次都讓我哭。
  3. The smell of the bakery made us feel hungry.
    麵包店的香味讓我們覺得餓了。
  4. Don't make me laugh. I'm trying to be serious.
    別逗我笑。我想正經一點。
  5. The long journey made the children feel tired and bored.
    長途旅行讓孩子們覺得又累又無聊。

② make + 受詞 + 原形動詞（叫人做不想做的事）

  1. My mum made me tidy my room before I went out.
    我媽要我把房間整理好才能出門。
  2. "Why are you so tired?" "My boss made me work until ten."
    你怎麼這麼累？我老闆叫我工作到十點。
  3. The teacher made the whole class stay after school.
    老師叫全班放學後留下來。
  4. You can't make him eat vegetables if he doesn't want to.
    他不想吃的話，你沒辦法逼他吃蔬菜。
  5. Try to make your parents understand how you feel.
    試著讓你爸媽了解你的感受。

③ let + 受詞 + 原形動詞（允許）

  1. You're eighteen now. Your parents should let you decide.
    你已經十八歲了。你爸媽應該讓你自己決定。
  2. "Can I go to the party?" "OK, I'll let you go, but be home by midnight."
    我可以去派對嗎？好，我讓你去，可是要在午夜前回家。
  3. My landlord doesn't let us keep pets.
    我的房東不讓我們養寵物。
  4. The guard let us take photos inside the museum.
    警衛讓我們在博物館裡面拍照。
  5. My sister never lets me borrow her clothes.
    我姊姊從來不讓我借她的衣服。

④ Let me + 原形動詞（說要幫忙或請對方告訴自己）

  1. Please read the plan and let me know if it is suitable for you.
    請看一下這個計畫，跟我說它適不適合你。
  2. "This box is heavy." "Let me help you."
    這個箱子好重。我來幫你。
  3. Let me think about it and I'll call you tomorrow.
    讓我想一想，我明天打電話給你。
  4. "Who's at the door?" "Let me see. It's the postman."
    誰在門口？我看看。是郵差。

⑤ Let's + 原形動詞（提議）

  1. "I'm bored." "Let's go to the cinema."
    我好無聊。我們去看電影吧。
  2. It's late. Let's finish the rest tomorrow.
    很晚了。剩下的我們明天再做吧。
  3. We've walked all morning. Let's stop and have lunch here.
    我們走了一整個早上了。我們在這裡停下來吃午餐吧。

⑥ 否定和問句

  1. My dad didn't let me drive his new car.
    我爸不讓我開他的新車。
  2. "Did the teacher make you rewrite the essay?" "Yes, all of it."
    老師要你重寫作文嗎？對，整篇。
  3. Will your parents let you go away by yourself?
    你爸媽會讓你自己一個人出遠門嗎？
  4. "Can I stay up to watch the match?" "Ask your mum. She won't let you stay up after ten."
    我可以晚點睡看比賽嗎？問你媽。她不會讓你十點以後還不睡的。

⑦ 主詞是東西或事情

  1. The cold weather made my hands hurt.
    冷天讓我的手很痛。
  2. The new bridge lets people cross the river in five minutes.
    新的橋讓大家五分鐘就能過河。
  3. The bad news made everyone go quiet.
    那個壞消息讓大家都安靜了下來。
  4. "Can you read the board now?" "Yes, my new glasses let me see everything clearly."
    你現在看得到黑板嗎？看得到，我的新眼鏡讓我什麼都看得很清楚。
  5. Her kind words made me feel much better.
    她親切的話讓我覺得好多了。
