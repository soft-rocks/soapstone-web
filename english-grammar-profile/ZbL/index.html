My relatives gave me presents, but the computer game was the best.

我的親戚送了我一些禮物，不過最棒的是那個電腦遊戲。

前⁠面說⁠過是什⁠麼，the + 最⁠高⁠級後⁠面省⁠略名⁠詞

My relatives gave me presents, but the computer game was the best.

所有房間都不錯，不過這間最大。

長⁠的形⁠容⁠詞用 the most

My relatives gave me presents, but the computer game was the best.

All the questions were hard, but the last one was the most difficult.

所有題目都很難，不過最後一題最難。

在一⁠組東⁠西⁠裡比⁠較，最⁠高⁠級前⁠面加 the

My relatives gave me presents, but the computer game was the best.

Of all the presents, Mark's was most beautiful.

Of all the presents, Mark's was the most beautiful.

三⁠個以⁠上比⁠較，用最⁠高⁠級

My relatives gave me presents, but the computer game was the best.

I have three brothers, and Tom is the older.

I have three brothers, and Tom is the oldest.

加⁠了 ⁠-⁠est 就⁠不⁠加 most

My relatives gave me presents, but the computer game was the best.

Of the three rooms, this one is the most biggest.

Of the three rooms, this one is the biggest.

例句

① the best / the worst

  1. I tried three cakes, and the chocolate one was the best.
    我試了三種蛋糕，巧克力的最好吃。
  2. We saw a lot of films, but the first one was the best.
    我們看了很多部電影，不過第一部最好看。
  3. All the singers were good, but Ann was the best.
    所有歌手都很好，不過 Ann 最好。
  4. The traffic is bad every day, but Monday is the worst.
    交通每天都很糟，不過星期一最糟。
  5. I've had many jobs, and this one is the best.
    我做過很多工作，這份最好。
  6. The hotel food was bad, but the coffee was the worst.
    飯店的菜很差，不過咖啡最難喝。

② the + 形容詞-est

  1. All the rooms are nice, but this one is the biggest.
    所有房間都不錯，不過這間最大。
  2. I have three brothers, and Tom is the oldest.
    我有三個兄弟，Tom 最大。
  3. Of all the roads, this one is the shortest.
    所有路裡面，這條最短。
  4. We looked at four flats. The one on Park Street was the cheapest.
    我們看了四間公寓，Park Street 那間最便宜。
  5. The kids all ran, but Ken was the fastest.
    孩子們都在跑，不過 Ken 最快。
  6. Which of these bags is the lightest?
    這些包包哪一個最輕？

③ the most + 形容詞

  1. Everyone brought a present, but Mark's was the most beautiful.
    每個人都帶了禮物，不過 Mark 的最漂亮。
  2. We visited three museums. The science museum was the most interesting.
    我們參觀了三間博物館，科學博物館最有趣。
  3. All the questions were hard, but the last one was the most difficult.
    所有題目都很難，不過最後一題最難。
  4. Which of these phones is the most expensive?
    這些手機哪一支最貴？
  5. Of all my teachers, Mr. Wu was the most patient.
    我所有的老師裡，吳老師最有耐心。

④ the one + 最高級 / 一組人

  1. All the dresses are nice, but the red one is the prettiest.
    所有洋裝都很好看，不過紅色那件最漂亮。
  2. There are three paths. The middle one is the safest.
    有三條路，中間那條最安全。
  3. My sisters are all tall, but Lucy is the tallest.
    我姊妹都很高，不過 Lucy 最高。
  4. Of all the players, he's the youngest.
    所有球員裡，他最年輕。
  5. All the boxes are heavy, but this one is the heaviest.
    箱子都很重，不過這個最重。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. Which season is the coldest here?
    這裡哪個季節最冷？
  2. Who is the youngest in your family?
    你們家誰最小？
  3. Of the three hotels, which one was the nicest?
    三家飯店裡，哪一家最好？
  4. We tried every pizza, and the cheese one was the most popular.
    我們每種披薩都試了，起司的最受歡迎。
  5. These apples are all sweet, but the green ones are the sweetest.
    這些蘋果都很甜，不過綠色的最甜。
  6. Which way to the station is the quickest?
    去車站哪條路最快？
  7. Of all the beaches here, this one is the cleanest.
    這裡所有的海灘裡，這片最乾淨。
  8. I liked all the songs, but the last one was the loveliest.
    我每首歌都喜歡，不過最後一首最好聽。
