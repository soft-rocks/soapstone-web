I like English lessons, but I don't like physics.

我喜歡英文課，但我不喜歡物理。

and、but、or 連⁠接兩⁠個主⁠要⁠子⁠句

I like English lessons, but I don't like physics.

and：加上另一件事

My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher.

我爸爸是醫生，我媽媽是老師。
but：不一樣的事

I want to go out, but it's raining.

我想出門，但是在下雨。
or：選一件

The museum is two kilometres away. We can take the bus or we can walk.

博物館在兩公里外。我們可以搭公車，或是走路。

三⁠個以⁠上⁠的子⁠句用逗⁠號隔⁠開，最⁠後加 and 或 or

I like English lessons, but I don't like physics.

Everyone helps at home. My mother cooks, my father cleans and I wash the dishes.

家裡每個人都會幫忙。我媽媽煮飯，我爸爸打掃，我洗碗。

兩⁠個主⁠要⁠子⁠句要⁠用連⁠接⁠詞連起⁠來

I like English lessons, but I don't like physics.

I like tea, I don't like coffee.

I like tea, but I don't like coffee.

but 前⁠面不⁠再⁠加 although

I like English lessons, but I don't like physics.

Although the shop was closed, but the owner let us in.

The shop was closed, but the owner let us in.

每⁠個主⁠要⁠子⁠句都⁠要有主⁠詞

I like English lessons, but I don't like physics.

I want to go out, but is raining.

I want to go out, but it's raining.

例句

① and

  1. I love my new school and my teachers are kind.
    我喜歡我的新學校，老師也很和善。
  2. My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher.
    我爸爸是醫生，我媽媽是老師。
  3. Here is my school day. I get up at seven and I have breakfast at half past seven.
    這是我上學的一天。我七點起床，七點半吃早餐。
  4. Our house is never quiet. We have a dog and we have two cats.
    我們家從來都不安靜。我們養了一隻狗，還養了兩隻貓。
  5. Ken plays the guitar and his sister sings.
    Ken 彈吉他，他妹妹唱歌。
  6. We can't buy milk today. It's Sunday and the shops are closed.
    我們今天買不到牛奶。今天是星期天，店都沒開。

② but

  1. I like cooking, but I don't like washing up.
    我喜歡煮飯，但我不喜歡洗碗。
  2. On Saturdays I play tennis, but I often stay home on Sundays.
    星期六我會打網球，但星期天我常常待在家。
  3. I don't want to move. My flat is old, but the rent is cheap.
    我不想搬家。我的公寓很舊，但是房租很便宜。
  4. Look at the red coat in the window. I want to buy that coat, but it costs too much.
    你看櫥窗裡那件紅外套。我想買那件外套，但是太貴了。
  5. My little sister is six. She can swim, but she can't ride a bike.
    我妹妹六歲。她會游泳，但是不會騎腳踏車。
  6. We ate at a small café near the station. The food was cheap, but it was very good.
    我們在車站附近一家小咖啡館吃飯。食物很便宜，但是很好吃。

③ or

  1. The museum is two kilometres away. We can take the bus or we can walk.
    博物館在兩公里外。我們可以搭公車，或是走路。
  2. Tell me when your train arrives. You can call me or you can send a text.
    你的火車到的時候告訴我。你可以打給我，或是傳簡訊。
  3. I don't know which jacket to wear. Is it cold outside or is it warm?
    我不知道要穿哪件外套。外面冷還是暖和？
  4. I can't come to your house on Thursday. I'll come on Friday or I'll come on Saturday.
    我星期四沒辦法去你家。我會星期五來，或是星期六來。
  5. Come in and sit down. Do you want tea or do you want coffee?
    進來坐。你要喝茶還是喝咖啡？

④ 三個以上的子句

  1. On Sundays, we sleep late, we have a big breakfast and we go for a walk.
    星期天我們會睡晚一點、吃頓豐盛的早餐，然後去散步。
  2. My weekdays are always the same. I go to school, I do my homework and I play football.
    我平日每天都一樣。我上學、寫作業，然後踢足球。
  3. Everyone helps at home. My mother cooks, my father cleans and I wash the dishes.
    家裡每個人都會幫忙。我媽媽煮飯，我爸爸打掃，我洗碗。
  4. We had a great day at the beach. We swam, we played games and we ate ice cream.
    我們在海邊玩了很開心的一天。我們游泳、玩遊戲，還吃了冰淇淋。
  5. It's raining, so we can't go to the park. You can read, you can draw or you can watch a film.
    下雨了，所以我們不能去公園。你可以看書、畫畫，或是看電影。

⑤ 不同的時態

  1. Let's go to the beach now. It rained yesterday, but today it's sunny.
    我們現在去海邊吧。昨天下雨，但是今天出太陽。
  2. I had football practice after school. I was tired, but I finished my homework.
    我放學後有足球練習。我很累，但我寫完作業了。
  3. Last Sunday was very hot. We went to the lake and we had a great time.
    上星期天很熱。我們去了湖邊，玩得很開心。
  4. The doctor's secretary has your results. She will call you or she will send an email.
    醫生的秘書有你的檢查結果。她會打給你，或是寄電子郵件。
  5. We didn't see each other much in those years. I lived in Tainan and my brother lived in Taipei.
    那幾年我們不常見面。我住在台南，我哥哥住在台北。
  6. My brother played games all week. He didn't study, but he passed the test.
    我哥哥整個星期都在打電動。他沒讀書，但考試及格了。
  7. I worked for ten hours today. I'm hungry and I'm tired.
    我今天工作了十個小時。我又餓又累。
  8. We watched a film about the moon last night. It was long, but it was very interesting.
    我們昨天晚上看了一部關於月球的電影。它很長，但是很有趣。
