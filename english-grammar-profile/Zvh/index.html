I was constantly trying to balance my school life and was always running out of time.

他們聽說我老是在惹麻煩。

主⁠詞 + was / were + always / constantly + ⁠-⁠ing

always / constantly、⁠-⁠ing

always / constantly總是、一直。
-⁠ing一再發生、不想要的事。

說自⁠己⁠的壞習⁠慣

例句

I was always getting into trouble at school.

我以前在學校總是闖禍。

這個句型常用來說自己以前的壞習慣或麻煩。

說控⁠制不⁠了⁠的事

例句

I was constantly losing my keys.

我一直在弄丟鑰匙。

也可以說控制不了的情況。

副⁠詞放⁠在 was 和 ⁠-⁠ing 中⁠間

I was constantly trying to balance my school life and was always running out of time.

I was running constantly out of time.

I was constantly running out of time.

過⁠去進⁠行⁠式用 was 或 were 加 ⁠-⁠ing

I was constantly trying to balance my school life and was always running out of time.

I did always getting into trouble.

I was always getting into trouble.

was 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞要加 ⁠-⁠ing

I was constantly trying to balance my school life and was always running out of time.

She was always lose her phone.

She was always losing her phone.

例句

① I was constantly / always + -ing：自己控制不了的事

  1. In my last year at school, I was constantly trying to balance my studies and my job, and I was always running out of time.
    高中最後一年，我一直在努力平衡學業和打工，而且總是時間不夠。
  2. I was constantly losing my keys when I lived in that flat.
    我住在那間公寓的時候，一直在弄丟鑰匙。
  3. I was always feeling tired during the winter months.
    冬天的那幾個月我總是覺得很累。
  4. I was constantly worrying about money when I was a student.
    我當學生的時候一直在擔心錢的事。
  5. I was always missing the bus in the mornings.
    我早上總是錯過公車。
  6. I was constantly forgetting people's names at my new job.
    我在新工作一直忘記別人的名字。

② 主詞 + was always getting into + 名詞：一再惹麻煩

  1. My parents were told that I was always getting into mischief, so they bought me a diary to calm me down.
    我爸媽聽說我總是在惹麻煩，所以他們買了一本日記給我，讓我冷靜一點。
  2. My little brother was always getting into fights at school.
    我弟弟在學校總是在打架。
  3. As a child, I was always getting into trouble with my teachers.
    我小時候總是惹老師生氣。
  4. Our dog was always getting into the neighbours' garden.
    我們的狗總是跑進鄰居的花園。
  5. He was always getting into arguments with his boss.
    他總是跟他的老闆吵架。
  6. The kids were always getting into the biscuit tin.
    孩子們總是偷吃餅乾罐裡的餅乾。

③ 名詞 + was constantly + -ing：東西一再出問題

  1. Our old car was constantly breaking down on the way to work.
    我們的舊車在上班的路上一再拋錨。
  2. The computer at the office was constantly crashing.
    辦公室的電腦一再當機。
  3. The roof was constantly leaking when it rained.
    下雨的時候屋頂一再漏水。
  4. The washing machine was always flooding the kitchen.
    洗衣機總是把廚房弄得都是水。
  5. The heating was constantly switching off in the night.
    暖氣半夜一再自己關掉。
  6. The trains were always running late that winter.
    那年冬天火車總是誤點。

④ 主詞 + was / were + always + -ing：別人讓人煩的習慣

  1. My roommate was always leaving dirty dishes in the sink.
    我的室友總是把髒碗盤留在水槽裡。
  2. The man next door was constantly playing the drums.
    隔壁的男人一直在打鼓。
  3. My sister was always reading my diary.
    我姊姊總是偷看我的日記。
  4. The students were constantly talking in the back row.
    學生們一直在最後一排說話。
  5. He was always taking my pens without asking.
    他總是沒問就拿我的筆。
  6. Our boss was constantly changing his mind.
    我們老闆一直在改變主意。

⑤ 主詞 + was always + -ing, so + 句子：結果

  1. I was always arriving late, so my teacher moved me to the front.
    我總是遲到，所以老師把我換到前面。
  2. He was constantly spending money, so he never had any savings.
    他一直在花錢，所以他從來沒有存款。
  3. She was always forgetting her lunch, so her mum brought it to school.
    她總是忘了帶午餐，所以她媽媽把午餐送到學校。
  4. They were constantly arguing, so they decided to live apart.
    他們一直在吵架，所以決定分開住。
  5. I was always eating junk food, so I put on weight.
    我總是吃垃圾食物，所以變胖了。
  6. The baby was constantly waking up, so we never slept well.
    寶寶一直醒來，所以我們從來沒睡好。
  7. He was always making excuses, so nobody believed him any more.
    他總是在找藉口，所以再也沒有人相信他。
