The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

問題是，我只要得出門，就一定會遲到。

The problem is 先點出後⁠面那⁠句⁠話是一⁠個問⁠題

問題是，我只要得出門，就一定會遲到。

每⁠個名⁠詞點出不⁠同⁠的東⁠西

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

thing
麻煩或藉口

The thing is, I've already promised to help Jane.

問題是，我已經答應要幫 Jane 了。
fact
事實

The fact is that we can't afford a new car.

事實是，我們買不起新車。
point
重點

The point is that the car itself doesn't cause accidents.

重點是，車子本身不會造成事故。
reason
原因

The reason is that I enjoy working with children.

原因是我喜歡和小孩一起工作。

that 可⁠以省⁠略，省⁠略⁠時 is 後⁠面常加逗⁠號

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

有 that

The fact is that nobody read the report.

事實是，根本沒有人看那份報告。
沒有 that

The fact is, nobody read the email.

事實是，根本沒有人看那封電子郵件。

名⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 The

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

Problem is I don't have a car.

The problem is I don't have a car.

is 後⁠面直⁠接接 that 子⁠句

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

The point is it that the car doesn't cause the accident.

The point is that the car doesn't cause the accident.

名⁠詞用單⁠數，和 is 一⁠致

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

The problems is that the rooms are small.

The problem is that the rooms are small.

完⁠整⁠的子⁠句用 that 帶⁠出⁠來，不⁠用 what

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

The fact is what she never called back.

The fact is that she never called back.

名⁠詞前⁠面有修⁠飾⁠語，就說⁠出了問⁠題⁠的性⁠質

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

沒有修飾語

The problem is the flat is too far from the station.

問題是那間公寓離車站太遠了。
有修飾語

The only problem is that it's expensive.

唯一的問題是很貴。

the fact that 是名⁠詞片⁠語，後⁠面另⁠有動⁠詞

The problem is I am always late when I have to leave my house.

他說了謊，這件事讓我很難過。

例句

① The thing is

  1. The thing is, I don't really like parties.
    問題是，我不太喜歡派對。
  2. The thing is that we've already paid for the hotel.
    問題是我們已經付了飯店的錢。
  3. The thing is, Alison has lived with her father since she was three.
    問題是，Alison 從三歲起就跟她爸爸住。
  4. The thing is, I can't drive.
    問題是我不會開車。
  5. The thing was, nobody knew his phone number.
    問題是，沒有人知道他的電話號碼。

② The fact is

  1. The fact is that not all animals can live in a zoo.
    事實是，不是所有動物都能在動物園生活。
  2. The fact is, science needs computers.
    事實是，科學需要電腦。
  3. The fact is we spent too much on the wedding.
    事實是我們在婚禮上花太多錢了。
  4. The fact is that most people never read the instructions.
    事實是，大部分的人從來不看說明書。
  5. The fact was that he had never been to France.
    事實是他從來沒去過法國。

③ The point is

  1. The point is that we need to decide today.
    重點是我們今天就得決定。
  2. The point is, she didn't tell anyone.
    重點是，她沒有告訴任何人。
  3. The point is you can't keep borrowing money.
    重點是你不能一直借錢。
  4. The point is that the rules apply to everyone.
    重點是規則對每個人都適用。
  5. The point was that nobody asked the students.
    重點是根本沒有人問學生的意見。

④ The problem is

  1. The problem is I know myself too well.
    問題是我太了解自己了。
  2. The problem is that the bus doesn't run on Sundays.
    問題是公車星期天不開。
  3. The problem is, the shop closes at five.
    問題是，那家店五點就關門了。
  4. The problem was that we didn't have a map.
    問題是我們沒有地圖。
  5. The problem is the walls are too thin.
    問題是牆太薄了。
  6. The problem is that nobody wants to clean the kitchen.
    問題是沒有人想打掃廚房。

⑤ The reason is

  1. The reason is that the road was closed after the storm.
    原因是暴風雨過後那條路封了。
  2. The reason is, I've got an exam tomorrow.
    原因是我明天要考試。
  3. The reason is that the train was cancelled.
    原因是火車停駛了。
  4. The reason was that she didn't feel well.
    原因是她不太舒服。
  5. The reason is I want to improve my English.
    原因是我想加強我的英文。

⑥ 省略 that，is 後面加逗號

  1. The thing is, my boss won't let me leave early.
    問題是，我老闆不讓我早走。
  2. The fact is, we're running out of time.
    事實是，我們快沒時間了。
  3. The point is, you should have asked first.
    重點是，你應該先問一聲。
  4. The problem is, nobody has a key.
    問題是，沒有人有鑰匙。
  5. The reason is, the café was full.
    原因是那家咖啡館客滿了。
