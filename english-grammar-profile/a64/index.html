You needn't come if you're busy.

你如果忙的話，就不用來了。

needn't 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

你如果忙的話，就不用來了。

半情⁠態動⁠詞多⁠半用在否⁠定⁠句和問⁠句

兩種說法的意思一樣。

needn't 後⁠面不⁠加 to

You needn't come if you're busy.

You needn't to worry about the money.

You needn't worry about the money.

半情⁠態動⁠詞不⁠照主⁠詞變⁠化

You needn't come if you're busy.

He needn't come tomorrow.

needn't 是不⁠必，mustn't 是禁⁠止

You needn't come if you're busy.

例句

① needn't：不必

  1. Thanks, but you needn't drive me. I can take the bus.
    謝謝，可是你不必開車送我。我可以搭公車。
  2. You needn't worry about where we can stay. My aunt has a big house.
    你不必擔心我們要住哪裡。我阿姨家很大。
  3. "Shall I bring some food?" "No, you needn't. We've got plenty."
    要我帶點吃的嗎？不用，你不必帶。我們有很多。
  4. You needn't shout. I can hear you.
    你不必用吼的。我聽得到。
  5. The test is easy. You needn't study all night.
    考試很簡單。你不必念一整晚。
  6. We needn't leave yet. The film starts at nine.
    我們還不用走。電影九點才開始。

② Need I ...?：問句

  1. "Need I bring my passport?" "No, your ID card is enough."
    我需要帶護照嗎？不用，你的身分證就夠了。
  2. Need I say more? You know what I mean.
    還需要我多說嗎？你知道我的意思。
  3. "Need we book a table?" "No, it's never busy on Mondays."
    我們需要訂位嗎？不用，星期一從來不會客滿。

③ daren't：不敢

  1. My parents hate driving, but they daren't sell their car. They live far from the bus stop.
    我爸媽很討厭開車，可是他們不敢把車賣掉。他們住得離公車站很遠。
  2. I daren't tell my mum about the broken vase.
    我不敢跟我媽說花瓶破了的事。
  3. She daren't go into the sea because of the jellyfish.
    她因為有水母，不敢下海。
  4. "Why don't you ask your boss for a day off?" "I daren't. We're too busy this month."
    你為什麼不跟老闆請一天假？我不敢。我們這個月太忙了。
  5. My little brother daren't sleep with the light off.
    我弟弟不敢關燈睡覺。

④ How dare you ...!：生氣

  1. "I gave your old jacket to a friend." "How dare you give away my things!"
    我把你的舊外套送給朋友了。你怎麼敢把我的東西送人！
  2. "I took your car this morning." "How dare you take it without asking!"
    我今天早上開了你的車。你怎麼敢不問一聲就開走！
  3. How dare he talk to my mother like that!
    他怎麼敢那樣跟我媽說話！
  4. How dare they park in front of our garage again!
    他們怎麼敢又停在我們車庫前面！

⑤ Dare you ...? 和 I dare say

  1. "There's a big spider on the wall. Dare you pick it up?" "No way!"
    牆上有一隻大蜘蛛。你敢把牠抓起來嗎？才不要！
  2. "Is the new restaurant good?" "I dare say it is. It's always full."
    那家新餐廳好吃嗎？我想應該不錯。它總是客滿。
  3. "Will the shops be open on Sunday?" "I dare say some of them will."
    星期天商店會開嗎？我想有些會開吧。

⑥ 一般動詞：don't need to

  1. You don't need to take your shoes off. Come in.
    你不需要脫鞋。進來吧。
  2. "Do I need to print the ticket?" "No, you can show it on your phone."
    我需要把票印出來嗎？不用，你可以用手機出示。
  3. My son doesn't need to wear glasses any more.
    我兒子不需要再戴眼鏡了。
  4. "Did you have to change trains?" "No, we didn't need to. The train went straight there."
    你們得換車嗎？不用，我們不需要換。那班車直達。

⑦ 一般動詞：don't dare (to)

  1. Nobody dared to say anything when the boss was angry.
    老闆生氣的時候，沒有人敢說話。
  2. The dog was so big that I didn't dare go past it.
    那隻狗好大，我不敢從牠旁邊走過去。
  3. She doesn't dare to drive in the city at night.
    她不敢晚上在市區開車。

⑧ needn't 和 mustn't

  1. You needn't wear a suit, but you mustn't wear shorts.
    你不必穿西裝，可是不可以穿短褲。
  2. You needn't finish the book today, but you mustn't lose it. It's from the library.
    你不必今天看完這本書，可是不可以弄丟。這是圖書館的書。
  3. "Need I call first?" "No, you needn't. Just come any time."
    我需要先打電話嗎？不用，你不必打。隨時來就好。
