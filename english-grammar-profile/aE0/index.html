The computer systems should be working again once you restart them.

你們只要重新啟動電腦系統，它應該就能恢復運作了。

主⁠詞 + should be + ⁠-⁠ing

should be + ⁠-⁠ing、⁠-⁠ing

The computer systems should be working again once you restart them.

should be + -⁠ing預期某件事會正在發生。
-⁠ing預期的動作或變化，像 working、improving、waiting、arriving。

說情⁠況會改⁠善

例句

Sales should be improving by the summer.

到夏天銷售額應該會好轉。

報告常用 should be improving 說情況預期會變好。

說安⁠排好⁠的事

例句

The driver should be waiting outside the airport.

司機應該在機場外面等。

例句

① 主詞 + should be working + again：預期恢復正常

  1. We've fixed the problem. The computer systems should be working again once you restart them.
    我們修好問題了。你們重新開機以後，電腦系統應該就會恢復正常運作。
  2. The engineer has repaired the lift, so it should be working again by lunchtime.
    工程師修好了電梯，所以午餐前應該就能用了。
  3. The trains should be running normally again by Monday.
    到星期一火車應該就會恢復正常行駛。
  4. Your phone should be working again after the update.
    更新以後你的手機應該就能用了。
  5. The heating should be working again by the time you get home.
    你到家的時候暖氣應該就會恢復了。
  6. The website should be running again in about an hour.
    網站大約一個小時後應該就會恢復運作。

② 主詞 + should be improving：預期情況變好

  1. I think the situation should be improving in the next six months, especially in the third quarter.
    我想未來六個月情況應該會好轉，特別是在第三季。
  2. The weather should be getting better by the weekend.
    到週末天氣應該會變好。
  3. With the new medicine, she should be feeling better in a few days.
    吃了新藥，她幾天後應該就會覺得好一點。
  4. House prices should be rising again next year.
    明年房價應該會再上漲。
  5. Your English should be improving quickly now that you live in London.
    你現在住在倫敦，英文應該會進步得很快。
  6. The traffic should be getting lighter after seven.
    七點以後交通應該會變得順暢一點。

③ 主詞 + should be waiting：按照安排在等

  1. At a traditional wedding, the groom should be waiting at the front of the church for the bride.
    在傳統的婚禮上，新郎應該在教堂前面等新娘。
  2. A driver should be waiting for you outside the arrivals hall.
    應該會有一位司機在入境大廳外面等你。
  3. Your teacher should be waiting for you in room twelve.
    你的老師應該在十二號教室等你。
  4. The guide should be waiting at the hotel entrance at nine.
    導遊九點應該會在飯店門口等。
  5. My parents should be waiting for us at the station.
    我爸媽應該在車站等我們。
  6. The doctor should be waiting for your results this afternoon.
    醫生今天下午應該在等你的檢查結果。

④ 主詞 + should be arriving / leaving：預期的時間

  1. Our guests should be arriving at any minute.
    我們的客人隨時應該會到。
  2. The parcel should be arriving between ten and twelve.
    包裹應該在十點到十二點之間到。
  3. The plane should be landing in about twenty minutes.
    飛機大約二十分鐘後應該會降落。
  4. The last bus should be leaving soon.
    最後一班公車應該快開了。
  5. The new students should be starting next Monday.
    新生應該下星期一開始上課。
  6. The builders should be finishing the roof this week.
    工人這個星期應該會把屋頂完成。

⑤ 主詞 + should be + -ing + by + 時間：到某個時間預期的狀態

  1. By this time tomorrow, we should be lying on a beach in Spain.
    明天這個時候，我們應該已經躺在西班牙的海灘上了。
  2. By next year, the new factory should be producing cars.
    到明年，新工廠應該已經在生產汽車了。
  3. By five o'clock, the party should be getting started.
    到五點，派對應該已經開始熱鬧起來了。
  4. By June, the baby should be walking.
    到六月，寶寶應該已經會走路了。
  5. By the end of the month, we should be living in our new flat.
    到月底，我們應該已經住在新公寓了。
  6. By ten, the band should be playing their first song.
    到十點，樂團應該已經在唱第一首歌了。
  7. By the summer, the new shop should be making a profit.
    到夏天，新店應該已經在賺錢了。
