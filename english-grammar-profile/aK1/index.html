He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

他說那是他的手機，他把它忘在一家咖啡廳了。

said + that + 子⁠句，代⁠名⁠詞換⁠掉，時⁠態後移

他說那是他的手機，他把它忘在一家咖啡廳了。

my 換⁠成 his，is 和 left 往過⁠去移

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

原話

"It's my phone. I left it in a cafe."

「這是我的手機。我把它忘在一家咖啡廳了。」
轉述

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

他說那是他的手機，他把它忘在一家咖啡廳了。

時⁠態後移：現⁠在⁠式

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

work → worked

She said she worked at a bank.

她說她在銀行上班。
am seeing → was seeing

Kate told me she was seeing someone new.

Kate 跟我說她在跟一個新對象交往。
have lost → had lost

He said he had lost his wallet.

他說他的皮夾丟了。

時⁠態後移：過⁠去⁠式和助⁠動⁠詞

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

met → had met

She said she had met him in Paris.

她說她是在巴黎認識他的。
will → would

He told me he would help me.

他跟我說他會幫我。
can → could

She said she could drive.

她說她會開車。
have to → had to

He said he had to go.

他說他得走了。

代⁠名⁠詞以轉⁠述⁠的人⁠為準

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

we → they

They said they were moving to a new flat.

他們說他們要搬到新公寓。
your → my

The man told me my bag was ready.

那個男人跟我說我的包包好了。

and 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句也⁠要換

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

他說他是 Lucy 的哥哥，還說我們以前見過面。

原話⁠的 I 和 my 是 Anna，換⁠成 she 和 her

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

Anna said that I had lost my keys.

Anna said that she had lost her keys.

say 後⁠面不⁠接聽⁠的人

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

She said me that the last train had already gone.

She told me that the last train had already gone.

已⁠經不⁠成⁠立⁠的事要後移

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

When I met him in 2015, he said he is a student.

When I met him in 2015, he said he was a student.

第⁠二⁠個子⁠句也⁠要後移

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

Last night Mia said she was tired and she wants to go home.

Last night Mia said she was tired and she wanted to go home.

that 後⁠面不⁠接原話

He said that it was his phone and he had left it in a cafe.

He said that, "I'm busy tonight."

He said that he was busy that night.

到現⁠在還成⁠立⁠的⁠事，可⁠以不⁠後移

我表姊說她在醫院上班。

直⁠接引⁠述保⁠留原話

他說：「這是我的手機。」

例句

① 現在簡單式 → 過去簡單式

  1. She said that she had a meeting at three.
    她說她三點要開會。
  2. My brother said he didn't like the film.
    我哥哥說他不喜歡那部電影。
  3. They said that our school was the best place for the film.
    他們說我們學校是拍那部電影最好的地方。
  4. The doctor said I needed more rest.
    醫生說我需要多休息。

② 現在進行式 → 過去進行式

  1. He said that he was looking for a new job.
    他說他在找新工作。
  2. My neighbour told me they were building a garage.
    我鄰居跟我說他們在蓋車庫。
  3. She said she was feeling much better.
    她說她覺得好多了。
  4. Tom said his parents were staying with him that week.
    Tom 說他爸媽那個星期住在他那裡。

③ 現在完成式 / 過去簡單式 → 過去完成式

  1. She said she had won the lottery.
    她說她中了樂透。
  2. He told me he had never been on a plane.
    他跟我說他從沒坐過飛機。
  3. My friend said she had seen my sister at the station.
    我朋友說她在車站看到我妹妹。
  4. The boy said he had broken the window by accident.
    男孩說他不小心打破了窗戶。
  5. They said they had already eaten.
    他們說他們已經吃過了。

④ will → would / can → could

  1. My aunt said she would send me a postcard.
    我阿姨說她會寄明信片給我。
  2. He told us he couldn't come to the wedding.
    他跟我們說他不能來參加婚禮。
  3. The mechanic said the car would be ready at five.
    技師說車子五點會好。
  4. She said she could speak three languages.
    她說她會說三種語言。

⑤ must / have to → had to

  1. He said he had to work on Saturday.
    他說他星期六得上班。
  2. My mum said we had to be home by ten.
    我媽說我們得在十點前回家。
  3. The teacher said that everyone had to wear a helmet.
    老師說每個人都得戴安全帽。

⑥ told + 人 + that

  1. Mary told me that she was getting married.
    Mary 跟我說她要結婚了。
  2. He told his boss he was sick.
    他跟老闆說他生病了。
  3. The guide told us the museum was closed.
    導遊跟我們說博物館沒開。
  4. I told her that I had lost her book.
    我跟她說我把她的書弄丟了。
  5. Our teacher told us she was leaving the school.
    我們老師跟我們說她要離開學校了。

⑦ 用 and 連起來的子句

  1. She said that she had won some money and she wanted to share it with me.
    她說她贏了一些錢，想分一些給我。
  2. He told me he was coming to Madrid and he would call me.
    他跟我說他要來馬德里，還會打給我。
  3. They said their car had broken down and they were waiting for help.
    他們說他們的車拋錨了，正在等人來幫忙。
  4. My dad said he was tired and he was going to bed.
    我爸說他累了，要去睡了。
  5. The woman said she had lost her dog and she was looking everywhere for it.
    那個女人說她的狗走丟了，她到處在找。
  6. Ben told us he had failed the test and he had to take it again.
    Ben 跟我們說他考試沒過，得重考。
