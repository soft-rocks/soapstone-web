Going away with friends will be more fun than sitting at home.

跟朋友出去玩會比坐在家裡有趣。

than 前⁠後比⁠的⁠是兩⁠件⁠事，兩⁠邊都用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Going away with friends will be more fun than sitting at home.

走路上班比搭公車便宜。

less + 形⁠容⁠詞 + than 表⁠示沒⁠有那⁠麼怎⁠麼⁠樣

Going away with friends will be more fun than sitting at home.

I can read or sleep on the way to work. Taking the bus is less tiring than driving.

我上班路上可以看書或睡覺。搭公車比開車不累。

than 後⁠面用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，不⁠用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Going away with friends will be more fun than sitting at home.

Taking the train is faster than drive.

Taking the train is faster than driving.

前⁠面用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，than 後⁠面也用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Going away with friends will be more fun than sitting at home.

Reading is more relaxing than to watch TV.

Reading is more relaxing than watching TV.

動⁠詞當主⁠詞⁠時用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Going away with friends will be more fun than sitting at home.

Swim is better than running for your knees.

Swimming is better than running for your knees.

例句

① 動詞-ing + 比較級 + than + 動詞-ing

  1. Parking at the airport costs twenty pounds a day. Sharing a taxi is cheaper than paying for parking.
    機場停車一天要二十英鎊。共乘計程車比付停車費便宜。
  2. You've had three cups already. Taking a nap is better than drinking more coffee.
    你已經喝了三杯了。睡個午覺比再喝咖啡好。
  3. The trainer said this to the new gym members. Stretching is more important than lifting heavy weights.
    教練跟健身房的新會員這樣說。伸展比舉重更重要。
  4. Put the tablet away and go to the park. Playing outside is healthier than sitting at a screen.
    把平板收起來，去公園吧。在外面玩比坐在螢幕前健康。
  5. The roads are always full at rush hour. Taking the train is faster than driving in the city.
    尖峰時間路上總是很塞。在市區搭火車比開車快。
  6. I get nervous on the phone. Writing an email is easier than calling.
    我講電話會緊張。寫電子郵件比打電話容易。

② more fun / more interesting + than + 動詞-ing

  1. Camping with friends will be more fun than staying in a hotel.
    跟朋友去露營會比住飯店好玩。
  2. My son is seventeen now. He says traveling with friends is more interesting than spending the holiday with parents.
    我兒子現在十七歲。他說跟朋友旅行比跟爸媽一起過假期有趣。
  3. Turn off the TV and come to the park. Playing football is more fun than watching it.
    關掉電視，來公園吧。踢足球比看足球好玩。
  4. Our science teacher lets us do experiments. Learning by doing is more interesting than reading about it.
    我們的自然老師讓我們做實驗。從做中學比讀書本有趣。
  5. You find small cafés and markets that way. Visiting a city on foot is more fun than taking a tour bus.
    那樣你會找到小咖啡館和市場。走路逛城市比搭觀光巴士好玩。

③ less + 形容詞 + than + 動詞-ing

  1. Every teacher knows your name there. Going to a small school is less complicated than going to a large one.
    那裡每個老師都知道你的名字。讀規模小的學校比讀大的學校單純。
  2. I can read or sleep on the way to work. Taking the bus is less tiring than driving.
    我上班路上可以看書或睡覺。搭公車比開車不累。
  3. My company lets me stay home on Fridays. Working from home is less stressful than commuting every day.
    我的公司讓我星期五待在家。在家工作比每天通勤壓力小。
  4. My sister likes to try clothes on. For her, buying online is less fun than going to the shops.
    我姊姊喜歡試穿衣服。對她來說，網購沒有去逛街好玩。
  5. My mum is in the operating room. For me, waiting quietly is less painful than worrying about everything.
    我媽媽在手術室。對我來說，安靜地等沒有什麼都擔心那麼痛苦。

④ better / harder / more expensive + than + 動詞-ing

  1. Our flat is on the tenth floor. Walking up the stairs is harder than taking the lift.
    我們的公寓在十樓。走樓梯比搭電梯辛苦。
  2. Your sister is upset with you. Talking to her is better than sending a text.
    你姊姊在生你的氣。跟她談比傳簡訊好。
  3. Don't carry the sofa by yourself. Asking for help is smarter than trying to do it alone.
    別自己搬沙發。尋求幫助比試著自己做聰明。
  4. The mechanic gave us the price. Fixing the old car is more expensive than buying a new one.
    修車師傅給了我們價錢。修舊車比買新車貴。
  5. I always miss my morning run. Getting up early is harder than going to bed early.
    我總是錯過早上的跑步。早起比早睡難。

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. You went to Rome by plane. Was flying cheaper than taking the train?
    你搭飛機去羅馬。搭飛機比搭火車便宜嗎？
  2. It took us four weekends. Painting the room ourselves was harder than paying someone.
    我們花了四個週末。自己漆房間比請人漆難。
  3. I don't want to visit museums on holiday. Nothing is better than lying on the beach.
    我度假不想逛博物館。沒有什麼比躺在海灘上更好的了。
  4. For me, studying in the morning works better than studying at night.
    對我來說，早上讀書比晚上讀書有效。
  5. The trucks drive very close to the edge. Riding a bike is more dangerous than walking in this traffic.
    卡車開得很靠路邊。在這種交通狀況下，騎腳踏車比走路危險。
  6. You forgot the milk again. Making a list is quicker than trying to remember everything.
    你又忘了買牛奶。列清單比試著記住每件事快。
  7. It's Saturday night, and you're in your pyjamas. Is staying home really better than going out?
    今天是星期六晚上，你穿著睡衣。待在家真的比出門好嗎？
  8. Our band only plays other people's music. Learning a song is easier than writing one.
    我們樂團只演奏別人的歌。學一首歌比寫一首歌容易。
  9. Rent in the city is very high. Sharing a flat will be cheaper than living alone.
    城市的房租很高。跟人合租公寓會比一個人住便宜。
