What did we use to eat?

我們以前都吃些什麼？

Did + 主⁠詞 + use to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞？

Did、use to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

What did we use to eat?

Did放在主詞前面。
use todid 後面用 use，不加 -⁠d。
原形動詞問的那件事。

問⁠句要⁠用 did

What did we use to eat?

What we used to eat?

What did we use to eat?

問⁠句把 did 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

What did we use to eat?

Where you did use to live?

use to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

例句

① What did + 主詞 + use to + 原形動詞？：報告和討論

  1. Today many families eat frozen food. But what did we use to eat fifty years ago?
    現在很多家庭吃冷凍食品。可是五十年前我們都吃些什麼？
  2. What did people use to do in the evenings before television?
    有電視以前，人們晚上都做些什麼？
  3. What did children use to play with before computers?
    有電腦以前，孩子們都玩什麼？
  4. What did your grandparents use to do for a living?
    你的祖父母以前做什麼工作？
  5. What did this building use to be?
    這棟建築以前是做什麼的？
  6. What did you use to want to be when you were small?
    你小時候想當什麼？

② Did you use to + 原形動詞？：問對方的過去

  1. Did you use to live near the sea when you were a child?
    你小時候住在海邊嗎？
  2. Did you use to play any sports at school?
    你以前在學校有玩什麼運動嗎？
  3. Did you use to have a pet when you were young?
    你小時候有養寵物嗎？
  4. Did you use to walk to school or take the bus?
    你以前走路上學還是搭公車？
  5. Did you use to get on well with your brother?
    你以前跟你哥哥處得好嗎？
  6. Did you use to wear a uniform at your school?
    你們學校以前要穿制服嗎？

③ Where / When / How + did + 主詞 + use to + 原形動詞？

  1. Where did you use to go on holiday when you were young?
    你小時候都去哪裡度假？
  2. How did people use to travel before there were cars?
    有汽車以前，人們怎麼旅行？
  3. When did you use to do your homework, before or after dinner?
    你以前什麼時候寫功課，晚餐前還是晚餐後？
  4. Where did your family use to live before you moved here?
    你們家搬來這裡以前住在哪裡？
  5. How did you use to get to work before the new road opened?
    新路開通以前，你怎麼去上班？
  6. Where did you use to buy your clothes?
    你以前都在哪裡買衣服？

④ Did + 名詞 + use to + 原形動詞？：問別人或地方的過去

  1. Did this street use to have a market on Saturdays?
    這條街以前星期六有市集嗎？
  2. Did your father use to work in the factory?
    你爸爸以前在那間工廠工作嗎？
  3. Did the town use to be bigger than it is now?
    這個鎮以前比現在大嗎？
  4. Did your sister use to sing in the school choir?
    你姊姊以前在學校合唱團唱歌嗎？
  5. Did people use to swim in this river?
    人們以前會在這條河裡游泳嗎？
  6. Did your teachers use to give you a lot of homework?
    你的老師以前給你很多功課嗎？

⑤ Didn't + 主詞 + use to + 原形動詞？：確認過去的事

  1. Didn't you use to have long hair?
    你以前不是留長頭髮嗎？
  2. Didn't this café use to be a shoe shop?
    這家咖啡館以前不是一家鞋店嗎？
  3. Didn't your family use to live next to the church?
    你們家以前不是住在教堂旁邊嗎？
  4. Didn't he use to play for our town's football team?
    他以前不是在我們鎮上的足球隊踢球嗎？
  5. Didn't we use to come here every summer?
    我們以前不是每年夏天都來這裡嗎？
  6. Didn't you use to work at the bank on the high street?
    你以前不是在主要街道上那家銀行工作嗎？
  7. Didn't your grandmother use to make her own bread?
    你奶奶以前不是自己做麵包嗎？
