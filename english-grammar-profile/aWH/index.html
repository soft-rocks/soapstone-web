If + 主⁠詞 + 現⁠在⁠式, + I / we + shall + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

If we do not receive a reply this time, we shall take the matter to court.

if 子句條件，用現在式。
shall會。主要跟 I 和 we 一起用，比 will 正式。
原形動詞條件成立時會做的事。

警⁠告

例句

If the bill is not paid, we shall cut off the electricity.

如果帳單沒付，我們會停止供電。

正式的警告信常用 if … we shall 說會採取的行動。

例句

① If …, we shall + 原形動詞：正式的警告

  1. This is our third letter. If we do not receive a reply this time, we shall take the matter to court.
    這是我們第三封信。如果這次我們再收不到回覆，我們會把這件事告上法院。
  2. Dear Mr Kent, you are two months behind with the rent. If it is not paid by Friday, we shall ask you to leave the flat.
    Kent 先生您好，您的房租已經遲交兩個月了。如果星期五前沒付，我們會請您搬出公寓。
  3. A letter to the people in flat six: if the noise continues after midnight, we shall call the police.
    給六號公寓住戶的信：如果半夜以後噪音還繼續，我們會報警。
  4. Your library books are three months late. If you do not return them, we shall charge you for them.
    您的圖書館書已經逾期三個月了。如果您不還書，我們會向您收書錢。
  5. We ordered the desks in March. If the goods do not arrive by Monday, we shall cancel the order.
    我們三月就訂了書桌。如果貨物星期一前沒到，我們會取消訂單。
  6. The machines have broken down three times this month. If the problem is not fixed, we shall look for another supplier.
    機器這個月壞了三次。如果問題沒有解決，我們會找另一家供應商。

② If …, we shall + be + 形容詞或過去分詞：預測嚴重的結果

  1. People live longer now, which is good, but if things get out of hand, we shall be doomed to live in chaos.
    現在的人活得比較久，這很好，可是如果事情失控，我們注定會活在一片混亂中。
  2. Half the rainforest has already gone. If we keep cutting down the forests, we shall be left with nothing.
    一半的雨林已經消失了。如果我們一直砍伐森林，我們最後會什麼都沒有。（被動）
  3. Our village is right next to the river. If it floods again, we shall be in serious trouble.
    我們的村子就在河邊。如果河水又氾濫，我們會有大麻煩。
  4. Energy bills doubled last winter. If we don't save energy now, we shall be sorry later.
    去年冬天能源費漲了一倍。如果我們現在不節約能源，以後會後悔。
  5. House prices went up fifteen per cent this year. If they keep rising, we shall be unable to buy a house.
    房價今年漲了百分之十五。如果一直上漲，我們會買不起房子。
  6. Our village has no bus service. If the trains stop running, we shall be cut off from the city.
    我們村子沒有公車。如果火車停駛，我們會跟城市斷絕聯繫。（被動）

③ If …, I shall + 原形動詞：說自己的決定

  1. My car is at the garage this week. If the weather is good, I shall walk to work tomorrow.
    我的車這個星期在修車廠。如果天氣好，我明天會走路上班。
  2. My exams finish in June. If I pass them, I shall go to university in Edinburgh.
    我的考試六月結束。如果我考過了，我會去愛丁堡念大學。
  3. I'm working late tonight. If you need anything, I shall be in my office until six.
    我今天要加班。如果你需要什麼，我六點前都在辦公室。
  4. I had a great interview at the bank. If they offer me the job, I shall accept it immediately.
    我在銀行的面試很順利。如果他們給我那份工作，我會馬上接受。
  5. I want to return these shoes today. If the shop is closed, I shall try again on Monday.
    我今天想退這雙鞋。如果那家店沒開，我星期一會再去。
  6. My project is due on Friday. If I have time after that, I shall visit you at the weekend.
    我的專題星期五要交。如果之後有時間，我週末會去看你。

④ If …, we shall + 原形動詞：計畫和安排

  1. We've sent out the invitations for the talk. If enough people come, we shall hire a bigger room.
    我們寄出了演講的邀請函。如果夠多人來，我們會租一間比較大的房間。
  2. The forecast says sun on Saturday. If it's right, we shall have the party in the garden.
    天氣預報說星期六是晴天。如果預報是對的，我們會在花園辦派對。
  3. We've seen the cottage twice and we love it. If the price is reasonable, we shall buy it.
    我們看過那間小屋兩次，很喜歡。如果價格合理，我們會買下來。
  4. It's been a long week at school. If the children are tired, we shall stay at home on Sunday.
    學校這個星期很累。如果孩子們累了，我們星期天會待在家裡。
  5. We are testing a new self-service till in one shop. If the plan works, we shall use it in all our shops.
    我們在一家店測試新的自助收銀機。如果這個計畫成功，我們會在所有的店使用它。
  6. The drive to the coast takes four hours. If we leave at eight, we shall arrive before lunch.
    開車到海邊要四個小時。如果我們八點出發，我們會在午餐前到。

⑤ Unless / As long as …, we shall + 原形動詞

  1. Sales have fallen for six months. Unless we act now, we shall lose the support of our customers.
    銷量下滑了六個月。除非我們現在行動，否則會失去顧客的支持。
  2. The new project is huge, but as long as we work together, we shall succeed.
    新專案很龐大，可是只要我們合作，我們就會成功。
  3. The pitch is under water. Unless the weather improves, we shall cancel the match.
    球場都淹水了。除非天氣好轉，否則我們會取消比賽。
  4. We've just left Leeds. As long as the roads are clear, we shall be there by six.
    我們剛離開 Leeds。只要路上不塞，我們六點前會到。
  5. This is your final notice. Unless you pay the fine, we shall take further action.
    這是給您的最後通知。除非您繳罰款，否則我們會採取進一步的行動。
  6. There are forty boxes to move. As long as everyone helps, we shall finish the job today.
    有四十個箱子要搬。只要每個人都幫忙，我們今天會完成工作。
  7. We lost money every month this year. Unless something changes, we shall have to close the shop.
    我們今年每個月都虧錢。除非有所改變，否則我們必須關店。
