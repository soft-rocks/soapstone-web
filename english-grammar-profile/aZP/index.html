This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.

他的這個特點表現在他生活中的每一個場合。

this 加名⁠詞，再⁠加 of 和所⁠有⁠格代⁠名⁠詞

他的這個特點在每個場合都看得出來。

強⁠調⁠的說⁠法

This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.

一般的說法

His patience is shown in every situation.

他的耐心表現在每一個場合。
強調的說法

This patience of his is shown in every situation.

他的這份耐心表現在每一個場合。

讚⁠美和不⁠耐⁠煩

This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.

讚美

This rare talent of theirs makes us all proud.

他們這種難得的天分讓我們都感到驕傲。
不耐煩

This bad habit of yours has to stop.

你這個壞習慣一定要改。

of 後⁠面用 his

This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.

This talent of him is amazing.

This talent of his is amazing.

複⁠數名⁠詞用 these

This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.

This ideas of yours are great.

These ideas of yours are great.

his 移⁠到後⁠面

This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.

This his talent is rare.

This talent of his is rare.

例句

① this + 名詞 + of his / hers

  1. My grandfather never gets angry. This characteristic of his is shown in every situation in his life.
    我爺爺從來不生氣。他的這個特點表現在他生活中的每一個場合。
  2. She always remembers people's names. This gift of hers makes everyone feel welcome.
    她總是記得別人的名字。她的這個本事讓每個人都覺得受歡迎。
  3. He gives away half of his salary. This generosity of his is well known in the town.
    他把一半的薪水捐出去。他的這份慷慨在鎮上很有名。
  4. This sense of humour of hers helped her through difficult times.
    她的這種幽默感幫她度過了困難的時候。

② this + 名詞 + of theirs

  1. Our athletes are amazing. Not only is this rare talent of theirs used for public pleasure, but also for international sports success, which makes us all proud.
    我們的運動員很了不起。他們這種難得的天分不只帶給大眾樂趣，也在國際體壇上爭光，讓我們都感到驕傲。
  2. The team never gives up. This fighting spirit of theirs wins them a lot of games.
    那支球隊從不放棄。他們的這種鬥志讓他們贏了很多比賽。
  3. My parents believe in hard work. This attitude of theirs has influenced me a lot.
    我爸媽相信努力。他們的這種態度對我影響很大。

③ these + 複數名詞 + of + 所有格代名詞

  1. These ideas of yours are very interesting. Can you write them down?
    你的這些想法很有意思。你可以寫下來嗎？
  2. These paintings of hers are the best in the exhibition.
    她的這些畫是展覽裡最好的。
  3. These stories of his always make the children laugh.
    他的這些故事總是逗得孩子們哈哈大笑。

④ this + 名詞 + of mine / yours / ours

  1. This dream of mine finally came true when I visited Japan.
    我去了日本，我的這個夢想終於實現了。
  2. This plan of ours will save the company a lot of money.
    我們的這個計畫會幫公司省下很多錢。
  3. This bad habit of yours has to stop. You're always late.
    你這個壞習慣一定要改。你老是遲到。

⑤ 論說文和人物介紹

  1. Mandela forgave the people who put him in prison. This quality of his inspired the whole world.
    曼德拉原諒了把他關進監獄的人。他的這種品格鼓舞了全世界。
  2. The writer describes ordinary life in great detail. This skill of hers makes her books feel real.
    那位作家把平凡的生活寫得很細膩。她的這個技巧讓她的書讀起來很真實。
  3. My best friend always tells the truth. This honesty of hers is why I trust her.
    我最好的朋友總是說實話。她的這份誠實就是我信任她的原因。

⑥ 對話

  1. "He's been helping his neighbours every weekend." "That's typical. This kindness of his is rare these days."
    他每個週末都去幫鄰居。他就是這樣。他的這種善良現在很少見了。
  2. "I've got another idea for the party." "Not another one! These ideas of yours always cost too much."
    派對我又想到一個點子。又來了！你的這些點子總是太花錢。
