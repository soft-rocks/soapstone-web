A lot of people think that television can both entertain and educate.

很多人認為電視既能娛樂，也能教育。

both + 動⁠詞 + and + 動⁠詞

A lot of people think that television can both entertain and educate.

電視既能娛樂，也能教育。

連⁠接動⁠詞片⁠語和介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

A lot of people think that television can both entertain and educate.

動詞片語

I could both get some enjoyment and make some money.

我既能玩得開心，又能賺點錢。
介系詞片語

We are famous both at home and abroad.

我們在國內外都很有名。

both 的位⁠置

A lot of people think that television can both entertain and educate.

情態動詞後面can both entertain and educate
介系詞片語前面both at home and abroad
主詞相同主詞放前面，連接後面的部分

兩⁠邊⁠的結⁠構相⁠同

A lot of people think that television can both entertain and educate.

The course helps you both to speak better and writing faster.

The course helps you both speak better and write faster.

and 後⁠面不⁠加 also

A lot of people think that television can both entertain and educate.

This job is both well paid and also interesting.

This job is both well paid and interesting.

both 放⁠在第⁠一⁠個片⁠語前⁠面

We both went to Paris and to Rome.

We went both to Paris and to Rome.

不⁠連⁠接兩⁠個完⁠整⁠的子⁠句

Both I was tired and I was hungry.

I was both tired and hungry.

例句

① 情態動詞 + both + 動詞 + and + 動詞

  1. Music can both relax and inspire people.
    音樂既能讓人放鬆，也能啟發人。
  2. A good teacher can both explain and listen.
    好老師既會講解，也會傾聽。
  3. This machine can both wash and dry your clothes.
    這台機器既能洗衣服，也能烘衣服。
  4. Exercise can both prevent and cure many problems.
    運動既能預防，也能治好很多毛病。
  5. Children should both play and learn at school.
    孩子在學校應該又玩又學。

② both + 動詞片語 + and + 動詞片語

  1. I could both get some enjoyment and make some money.
    我既能玩得開心，又能賺點錢。
  2. She both works full-time and studies at night.
    她白天全職上班，晚上還念書。
  3. The app lets you both book a table and pay the bill.
    這個應用程式既能訂位，也能付帳。
  4. My uncle both grows vegetables and sells them at the market.
    我叔叔既種菜，又在市場賣菜。
  5. We want to both see the city and relax by the sea.
    我們既想逛逛城市，也想在海邊放鬆。

③ both + 介系詞片語 + and + 介系詞片語

  1. We have won a high reputation both at home and abroad.
    我們在國內外都贏得了很高的聲譽。
  2. The singer is popular both in Asia and in Europe.
    這位歌手在亞洲和歐洲都很受歡迎。
  3. I use my laptop both at work and at home.
    我上班和在家都用筆電。
  4. You can see the castle both from the bridge and from the hill.
    從橋上和山丘上都看得到城堡。
  5. This road is busy both in the morning and in the evening.
    這條路早上和晚上都很塞。

④ be + both + 形容詞 + and + 形容詞

  1. This job is both well paid and interesting.
    這份工作薪水好，也很有趣。
  2. The old market is both noisy and colourful.
    舊市場很吵，也很繽紛。
  3. My new neighbour is both kind and funny.
    我的新鄰居人很好，也很風趣。
  4. The soup was both hot and delicious.
    湯很熱，也很好喝。
  5. Learning a language is both hard and rewarding.
    學一種語言很難，可是收穫也很多。

⑤ both + 過去分詞 + and + 過去分詞

  1. The house was both painted and cleaned before we moved in.
    我們搬進去之前，房子已經粉刷過，也打掃過了。
  2. The report has been both checked and signed.
    報告已經檢查過，也簽好名了。
  3. The building was both designed and built by local workers.
    這棟大樓是本地工人設計的，也是本地工人蓋的。
  4. Her new book is both praised and criticised.
    她的新書有人稱讚，也有人批評。

⑥ 主詞相同時用 both … and 連接後半部

  1. I was both tired and hungry after the trip.
    旅行回來我又累又餓。
  2. He both cooked dinner and washed the dishes.
    他煮了晚餐，也洗了碗。
  3. My grandmother both knits and sews her own clothes.
    我奶奶自己織毛衣，也自己縫衣服。
  4. Anna both sings and plays the guitar in the band.
    Anna 在樂團裡既唱歌，也彈吉他。

⑦ both + to 不定詞 + and + to 不定詞

  1. I came to London both to work and to improve my English.
    我來倫敦，既是為了工作，也是為了把英文練好。
  2. We need the money both to pay the rent and to buy food.
    我們需要這筆錢付房租，也要買食物。
