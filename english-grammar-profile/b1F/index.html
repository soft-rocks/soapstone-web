句中副⁠詞放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

I have just seen your notice.

我剛看到你的公告。

說話者剛看到公告，馬上寫信給管理員。

常⁠用⁠的句中副⁠詞

I have just seen your notice.

just
剛發生

Your taxi has just arrived.

你叫的計程車剛到。
already
已經做了

Put your money away. I've already paid for the tickets.

把錢收起來。票我已經付過錢了。
never
從來沒有

She's never met my parents.

她從來沒見過我爸媽。
always
一直都是

I've always liked this song.

我一直都很喜歡這首歌。

問⁠句⁠的 ever 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

I have just seen your notice.

我們五月要去愛丁堡。你去過蘇格蘭嗎？

副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在 have 前⁠面

I have just seen your notice.

I just have seen your message about the party.

I have just seen your message about the party.

never 已⁠經是否⁠定，不⁠加 not

I have just seen your notice.

Our cat hasn't never caught a mouse.

Our cat has never caught a mouse.

already 不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I have just seen your notice.

The builders have finished already the new kitchen.

The builders have already finished the new kitchen.

美⁠式英⁠語常⁠用過⁠去簡⁠單式加 just

I have just seen your notice.

我剛看到你貼在電梯旁的公告。我可以幫忙整理花園。

ever 用在問⁠句，否⁠定⁠的回⁠答用 never

I have just seen your notice.

問句

This French restaurant is famous for snails. Have you ever eaten snails?

這家法國餐廳的蝸牛很有名。你吃過蝸牛嗎？
否定的回答

No, I've never eaten them.

沒有，我從來沒吃過。

例句

① just

  1. Come and have some while it's hot. I've just taken the bread out of the oven.
    趁熱來吃一點。我剛把麵包從烤箱拿出來。
  2. Hurry up and sit down. The match has just started on TV.
    快點坐下。電視上的比賽剛開始。
  3. Our daughter wants to learn music. We've just bought a second-hand piano.
    我們女兒想學音樂。我們剛買了一台二手鋼琴。
  4. Don't wait for Kate. She's just phoned to say she's late.
    不用等凱特。她剛打電話來說她會晚到。
  5. Get your coat. Your taxi has just arrived.
    去拿外套。你叫的計程車剛到。
  6. Thanks for writing. I have just received your email about the meeting.
    謝謝你來信。我剛收到你寄來關於開會的電子郵件。

② already

  1. You can sit down and relax. I've already done the washing-up.
    你可以坐下來休息。碗我已經洗好了。
  2. You missed the party. Our guests have already gone home.
    你錯過派對了。我們的客人已經回家了。
  3. My son got his pocket money on Monday. He's already spent all of it.
    我兒子星期一拿到零用錢。他已經全部花光了。
  4. Let's choose something else. We've already seen this film.
    我們挑別的吧。這部電影我們已經看過了。
  5. You'll have to send the parcel tomorrow. The post office has already closed for the day.
    你得明天再寄包裹了。郵局今天已經關門了。

③ never

  1. She's a bit scared of the flight. My grandmother has never been on a plane.
    她有點怕坐飛機。我奶奶從來沒坐過飛機。
  2. My mum still cooks for him. My brother has never cooked a meal for himself.
    我媽媽還在幫他煮飯。我哥從來沒自己煮過一頓飯。
  3. We've always lived in flats. We've never had a garden of our own.
    我們一直都住公寓。我們從來沒有過自己的花園。
  4. I'm taking my girlfriend home this weekend. She's never met my parents.
    我這個週末要帶女朋友回家。她從來沒見過我爸媽。
  5. Look at the white sand. I have never seen such a beautiful beach.
    你看那白沙。我從來沒看過這麼漂亮的海灘。
  6. Our team is very strong at home. They've never lost a game here.
    我們的球隊在主場很強。他們在這裡從來沒輸過。

④ always

  1. Turn up the radio. I've always liked this song.
    把收音機開大聲一點。我一直都很喜歡這首歌。
  2. She knows everyone here. My aunt has always lived in this village.
    她認識這裡的每個人。我阿姨一直都住在這個村子。
  3. My family is very close. We've always spent Christmas together.
    我們家很親。我們聖誕節一直都一起過。
  4. My brother is learning Japanese. He's always wanted to visit Japan.
    我哥哥在學日文。他一直都想去日本。
  5. Let's come back on a weekday. This café has always been busy on Saturdays.
    我們平日再來吧。這家咖啡店星期六一直都很多人。

⑤ 問句：ever / already / just

  1. There's a new restaurant in town. Have you ever tried Korean food?
    城裡開了一家新餐廳。你吃過韓國菜嗎？
  2. My sister is going to London next week. Has your sister ever been there?
    我姊姊下禮拜要去倫敦。你姊姊去過那裡嗎？
  3. Your parents are coming for Christmas. Have they already booked their flights home?
    你爸媽要來過聖誕節。他們已經訂好回家的機票了嗎？
  4. The platform is empty. Has the last train to Leeds just left?
    月台上沒有人。往里茲的末班火車剛開走嗎？
  5. You travel a lot for work. Have you ever lost your passport?
    你常常出差。你弄丟過護照嗎？

⑥ 寫信和留言的開頭

  1. Dear Sir, I have just read your advertisement in the newspaper. I would like to apply for the job.
    敬啟者，我剛在報紙上看到你們的廣告。我想應徵這份工作。
  2. Dear Mr Smith, we have already sent you the money for the hotel room.
    史密斯先生您好，飯店房間的錢我們已經寄給您了。
  3. Congratulations on the new baby! I've just heard the good news.
    恭喜你們生寶寶了！我剛聽到這個好消息。
  4. Dear Ms Brown, I would like to apply for the job at your school. I have always wanted to work with children.
    布朗女士您好，我想應徵貴校的工作。我一直都想從事跟小孩有關的工作。
