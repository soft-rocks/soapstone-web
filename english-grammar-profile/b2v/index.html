My mother will get us there, and she will bring us back to my house after.

我媽媽會送我們過去，之後她會再載我們回我家。

主⁠詞 + will + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

will、I'll、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

will願意、會。說願意做的事。
I'll我來。當下決定主動幫忙。
原形動詞願意做的事。

I'll 主⁠動幫⁠忙

例句

That bag looks heavy. I'll carry it.

那個袋子看起來很重。我來提。

說話的當下決定幫忙，常用 I'll。

won't 說不⁠願⁠意

例句

The door won't open.

門打不開。
例句

He won't listen to me.

他不肯聽我說。

won't 可以說人或東西不願意、不肯。

當⁠下決⁠定幫⁠忙要⁠用 I'll

The phone is ringing. I answer it.

The phone is ringing. I'll answer it.

will 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

My mother will get us there, and she will bring us back to my house after.

My dad will to drive us.

My dad will drive us.

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

My mother will get us there, and she will bring us back to my house after.

She will brings us home.

She will bring us home.

例句

① 主詞 + will + 原形動詞：家人願意幫忙

  1. Don't worry about getting to the pool. My mother will take us there, and she will bring us back to my house afterwards.
    別擔心怎麼去游泳池。我媽媽會載我們去，之後也會載我們回我家。
  2. My dad will pick us up after the party, so you don't need a taxi.
    派對結束後我爸爸會來接我們，所以你不需要叫計程車。
  3. My grandmother will look after the dog while we're away.
    我們不在的時候，我奶奶會照顧狗。
  4. My brother will help you move the furniture on Saturday.
    我哥哥星期六會幫你搬家具。
  5. You haven't got a sleeping bag? My aunt will lend you hers.
    你沒有睡袋？我阿姨會借你她的。
  6. My parents will pay for your ticket, so don't worry about the money.
    我爸媽會付你的票錢，所以不用擔心錢的事。

② I'll + 原形動詞：當下決定主動幫忙

  1. The phone is ringing. I'll answer it.
    電話在響。我來接。
  2. You look tired. I'll make dinner tonight.
    你看起來很累。今天晚上我來煮晚餐。
  3. That box is too heavy for you. I'll carry it upstairs.
    那個箱子對你來說太重了。我幫你搬上樓。
  4. You don't have a pen? I'll lend you mine.
    你沒有筆？我借你我的。
  5. The baby's crying. I'll go and see what's wrong.
    寶寶在哭。我去看看怎麼了。
  6. It's raining hard. I'll drive you to the station.
    雨下得好大。我開車送你去車站。

③ We'll + 原形動詞：大家一起幫忙

  1. Don't worry about the party food. We'll bring some sandwiches and a cake.
    別擔心派對的食物。我們會帶一些三明治和一個蛋糕。
  2. We'll wash up after dinner. You sit down and relax.
    晚餐後我們來洗碗。你坐下來休息。
  3. We'll paint your fence on Sunday if you like.
    如果你想要，我們星期天幫你油漆圍籬。
  4. We'll wait for you outside the cinema.
    我們會在電影院外面等你。
  5. We'll help you look for your keys.
    我們幫你找鑰匙。
  6. We'll call the doctor for you.
    我們幫你打電話給醫生。

④ 主詞 + won't + 原形動詞：不願意、不肯

  1. My little brother won't eat his vegetables.
    我弟弟不肯吃他的蔬菜。
  2. I've tried three times, but the old lawnmower won't start this morning.
    我試了三次，可是那台舊割草機今天早上就是發不動。
  3. My sister has been crying all evening, but she won't say a word about it.
    我妹妹哭了一整晚，可是她一個字都不肯說。
  4. The window won't open. It's stuck.
    窗戶打不開。卡住了。
  5. My dad won't let me go to the concert.
    我爸爸不讓我去聽演唱會。
  6. The dog won't come inside. It loves the snow.
    狗不肯進來。牠很喜歡雪。

⑤ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + if + 子句：願意的條件

  1. My uncle will drive us to the airport if we leave before six.
    如果我們六點前出發，我叔叔會開車載我們去機場。
  2. I'll help you with your homework if you help me with mine.
    如果你幫我寫功課，我就幫你寫你的。
  3. The neighbours will water our plants if we ask them.
    如果我們拜託，鄰居會幫我們澆花。
  4. My sister will cut your hair if you buy her a coffee.
    如果你請我姊姊喝咖啡，她會幫你剪頭髮。
  5. Mr Lee will go over the grammar again after class if anyone is still confused.
    如果還有人不懂，李老師下課後會再講一次文法。
  6. I'll come with you to the dentist if you're nervous.
    如果你很緊張，我會陪你去看牙醫。
  7. The shop will deliver the sofa if you pay a small fee.
    如果你付一點費用，那家店會幫你送沙發。
