例句

① said

  1. "I'll be back in an hour," she said.
    我一個小時後回來。她說。
  2. "This soup is delicious," my dad said.
    這湯真好喝。我爸說。
  3. "I've never seen snow before," Maria said.
    我以前從沒看過雪。Maria 說。
  4. "We missed the last train," they said.
    我們錯過最後一班火車了。他們說。
  5. "Excuse me, you forgot your umbrella," the waitress said.
    不好意思，您忘了帶傘。女服務生說。
  6. "It's my turn to pay," I said.
    這次換我付。我說。

② asked

  1. "Where do you live?" she asked.
    你住在哪裡？她問。
  2. "Can I borrow your pen?" the boy asked.
    我可以借你的筆嗎？男孩問。
  3. "Why is everyone so quiet?" Dad asked.
    大家怎麼都這麼安靜？爸爸問。
  4. "Have you finished your homework?" my mother asked.
    你功課寫完了嗎？我媽問。
  5. "Where has everybody gone?" I asked.
    大家都跑去哪了？我問。

③ answered / replied

  1. "Would you like to come to my party?" "Yes, I'd love to," she answered.
    你要來我的派對嗎？好，我很樂意。她回答。
  2. "How long will it take to the airport?" "About twenty minutes," the driver replied.
    到機場要多久？大概二十分鐘。司機回答。
  3. "I'm not sure yet which university I'll choose," he answered.
    我還不確定要選哪一所大學。他回答。
  4. "When do you work at the café?" "Only on weekends," Sara replied.
    你什麼時候在咖啡廳上班？只有週末。Sara 回答。
  5. "No, thanks, I'm full," I replied.
    不用了，謝謝，我吃飽了。我回答。

④ shouted / whispered / cried

  1. "Run! The dog is coming!" he shouted.
    快跑！那隻狗過來了！他大喊。
  2. "Get off my bike!" the girl shouted.
    從我的腳踏車上下來！女孩大喊。
  3. "Don't make a sound," she whispered.
    別出聲。她小聲說。
  4. "I want my mummy!" the child cried.
    我要媽媽！那個孩子哭著說。
  5. "Happy birthday!" everyone shouted.
    生日快樂！大家一起喊。

⑤ thought / asked myself

  1. "Have I made a mistake?" I thought.
    我是不是做錯了？我心想。
  2. "He looks familiar," she thought.
    他看起來好眼熟。她心想。
  3. "This is going to be a long day," I thought.
    今天會很漫長。我心想。
  4. "Did I lock the door?" he asked himself.
    我有鎖門嗎？他問自己。
  5. "Why did I say that?" she asked herself.
    我為什麼要說那種話？她問自己。

⑥ 故事裡的對話

  1. "Is anybody home?" I asked. "In the kitchen!" my mum answered.
    有人在家嗎？我問。在廚房！我媽回答。
  2. "Can you help me?" the old man asked. "Of course," I said.
    你可以幫我嗎？老先生問。當然。我說。
  3. "Where are my keys?" she asked. "On the table," he replied.
    我的鑰匙在哪裡？她問。在桌上。他回答。
  4. "Is this seat free?" he asked. "Sorry, it's taken," she said.
    這個位子有人坐嗎？他問。抱歉，有人了。她說。
  5. "Let's take a break," the coach said. "Great idea!" we shouted.
    我們休息一下吧。教練說。好主意！我們大喊。
