very 修⁠飾有程⁠度高⁠低⁠的形⁠容⁠詞

我的老師很好。

very 加形⁠容⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

My teacher is very good.

a + very + 形容詞 + 名詞

It's a very hot day.

今天很熱。
very + 形容詞 + 名詞

She has very long hair.

她的頭髮很長。

表⁠示是或不⁠是⁠的形⁠容⁠詞，前⁠面不⁠加 very

The plant is dead.

alive、married 前面也不加 very。

表⁠示程⁠度最⁠高⁠的形⁠容⁠詞，用 absolutely 或 really 加⁠強

absolutely

really

已⁠經表⁠示程⁠度最⁠高⁠的形⁠容⁠詞，前⁠面不⁠加 very

My teacher is very good.

The concert was excellent.

perfect、amazing、huge 前面也不加 very。

very 修⁠飾形⁠容⁠詞，不⁠修⁠飾名⁠詞

My teacher is very good.

修⁠飾形⁠容⁠詞用 very，不⁠用 very much

The bed is very comfortable.

very 放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

例句

① 主詞 + be + very + 形容詞

  1. My home is very warm in winter.
    我家冬天很暖和。
  2. Our neighbours are very friendly.
    我們的鄰居很友善。
  3. The bread is very fresh.
    這個麵包很新鮮。
  4. This question is very difficult.
    這個問題很難。
  5. Her parents were very angry.
    她的爸媽很生氣。
  6. The weather was very bad yesterday.
    昨天天氣很差。
  7. Are you very busy this week?
    你這禮拜很忙嗎？
  8. The kitchen isn't very big.
    廚房不太大。

② very + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. Rome is a very beautiful city.
    羅馬是個很美的城市。
  2. I have a very important question.
    我有一個很重要的問題。
  3. That was a very interesting lesson.
    那是一堂很有意思的課。
  4. She wears very bright colours.
    她穿很鮮豔的顏色。
  5. We had a very quick lunch.
    我們很快地吃了午餐。
  6. He lives in a very quiet street.
    他住在一條很安靜的街上。
  7. They sell very cheap clothes.
    他們賣很便宜的衣服。

③ 連綴動詞 + very + 形容詞

  1. The cake smells very good.
    蛋糕聞起來很香。
  2. Your sister looks very pretty in that dress.
    你姊姊穿那件洋裝很漂亮。
  3. I felt very sad when he left.
    他離開的時候我很難過。
  4. The music sounds very loud from here.
    從這裡聽，音樂很大聲。
  5. It got very dark outside.
    外面天色變得很暗。

④ absolutely + 表示程度最高的形容詞

  1. The view from the top was absolutely amazing.
    從頂端看出去的景色美極了。
  2. Their new house is absolutely huge.
    他們的新房子大得不得了。
  3. After the long walk, we were absolutely starving.
    走了那麼久，我們都餓死了。
  4. The hotel staff were absolutely brilliant.
    飯店的員工棒極了。

⑤ really + 形容詞

  1. His jokes are really funny.
    他的笑話真的很好笑。
  2. I'm really sorry about your cat.
    你的貓的事，我真的很遺憾。
  3. This soup is really delicious.
    這碗湯真的很好喝。
  4. The shoes were really cheap.
    那雙鞋真的很便宜。

⑥ 疑問句和回答

  1. Is your town very old?
    你住的城鎮很古老嗎？
  2. "Was the exam difficult?" "Yes, very difficult."
    考試難嗎？難，很難。
  3. "Are you tired?" "Not very."
    你累嗎？不太累。
  4. Do you live in a very big house?
    你住的房子很大嗎？
