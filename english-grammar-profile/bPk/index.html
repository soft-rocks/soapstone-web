Rome is really fantastic and you must go there!

羅馬真的很棒，你一定要去！

You + must + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

you must、we must、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

you must你一定要。推薦對方。
we must我們一定要。建議大家一起做。
原形動詞推薦做的事。

we must … again

例句

We must do that again, don't you think?

我們一定要再來一次，你不覺得嗎？

玩得很開心以後，常說 We must do that again，意思是「我們一定要再來一次」。

加⁠上理⁠由

例句

You must see this film. It's brilliant.

你一定要看這部電影。太精彩了。

熱情的建議常接著說理由。

must 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Rome is really fantastic and you must go there!

You must to visit the old town.

You must visit the old town.

must 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Rome is really fantastic and you must go there!

Everyone musts try this cake.

Everyone must try this cake.

again 放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面

We must do again that.

We must do that again.

例句

① You must + go / visit + 地方：推薦旅遊

  1. Venice is beautiful, and you must see it at least once.
    威尼斯很美，你至少要去看一次。
  2. You must visit the castle when you're in Edinburgh.
    你在愛丁堡的時候一定要去參觀城堡。
  3. If you go to Japan, you must go in the spring.
    如果你去日本，一定要在春天去。
  4. You must go up the tower. The view is amazing.
    你一定要上那座塔。風景好極了。
  5. When you're in Lisbon, you must ride the yellow tram up the hill.
    你在里斯本的時候，一定要搭黃色電車上山。
  6. You must take a boat trip on the river.
    你一定要搭船遊河。

② We must + 原形動詞：建議大家一起去

  1. This club opened just two weeks ago. I mean, we must see it.
    這家俱樂部兩個星期前才開。我是說，我們一定要去看看。
  2. There's a new Thai restaurant in town. We must try it.
    城裡新開了一家泰國餐廳。我們一定要去吃吃看。
  3. The circus is coming next month. We must get tickets.
    馬戲團下個月要來。我們一定要買票。
  4. We must go camping before the summer ends.
    夏天結束前我們一定要去露營。
  5. We must visit Grandma at the weekend.
    週末我們一定要去看奶奶。
  6. The new exhibition sounds great. We must go together.
    新的展覽聽起來很棒。我們一定要一起去。

③ We must + 原形動詞 + again：說一定要再來

  1. What a great day out! We must do that again, don't you think?
    今天玩得真開心！我們一定要再來一次，你不覺得嗎？
  2. That was a lovely meal. We must come here again.
    那一餐很棒。我們一定要再來這裡。
  3. It was so good to see you. We must meet again soon.
    見到你真好。我們一定要很快再見面。
  4. We must go to that beach again next summer.
    明年夏天我們一定要再去那個海灘。
  5. The concert was brilliant. We must see them again.
    音樂會太精彩了。我們一定要再去看他們。
  6. We must play tennis again next week.
    下星期我們一定要再打網球。

④ You must + 原形動詞：推薦電影、書和食物

  1. You must read this book. I couldn't stop reading.
    你一定要讀這本書。我一讀就停不下來。
  2. You must see the new Spider-Man film. It's brilliant.
    你一定要看新的蜘蛛人電影。太精彩了。
  3. You must taste the cheese from this farm.
    你一定要嚐嚐這個農場的起司。
  4. You must listen to this podcast. It's so funny.
    你一定要聽這個 podcast。超好笑的。
  5. You must try the hot chocolate at that café.
    你一定要試試那家咖啡館的熱可可。
  6. You must watch the sunset from the hill.
    你一定要從山丘上看夕陽。

⑤ You must + 原形動詞：鼓勵對方

  1. You must enter the singing competition. Your voice is beautiful.
    你一定要參加歌唱比賽。你的聲音很好聽。
  2. You must send your story to a magazine.
    你一定要把你的故事投稿到雜誌。
  3. You must apply for that job. You'd be perfect.
    你一定要去應徵那份工作。你很適合。
  4. You must show your paintings to the art teacher.
    你一定要把你的畫給美術老師看。
  5. You must join the choir. They need more singers.
    你一定要加入合唱團。他們需要更多人唱歌。
  6. You must start your own cooking blog.
    你一定要開一個自己的料理部落格。
  7. You must take up running again. You loved it.
    你一定要再開始跑步。你以前很喜歡。
