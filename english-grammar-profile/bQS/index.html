Waiting tables would be boring, and we have no experience whatsoever.

當服務生會很無聊，而且我們完全沒有經驗。

whatsoever 放⁠在否⁠定⁠的名⁠詞片⁠語後⁠面，加⁠強否⁠定

當服務生會很無聊，而且我們完全沒有經驗。

否⁠定⁠的名⁠詞片⁠語可⁠以是 no、not any 或 nothing

Waiting tables would be boring, and we have no experience whatsoever.

no + 名詞

There was no traffic whatsoever on the way home.

回家的路上一點車也沒有。
not any + 名詞

There isn't any parking whatsoever near the station.

車站附近一個停車位都沒有。
nothing

She knows nothing whatsoever about football.

她對足球一竅不通。

whatsoever 比 at all 強，也比⁠較正⁠式

Waiting tables would be boring, and we have no experience whatsoever.

at all

I have no idea at all.

我完全不知道。
whatsoever

I have no idea whatsoever where she lives.

我完全不知道她住哪裡。

whatsoever 只⁠用在否⁠定⁠句

Waiting tables would be boring, and we have no experience whatsoever.

We have experience whatsoever.

We have no experience whatsoever.

whatsoever 放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面

Waiting tables would be boring, and we have no experience whatsoever.

There is no whatsoever problem.

There is no problem whatsoever.

whatsoever 不⁠跟⁠著動⁠詞，否⁠定動⁠詞用 at all

Waiting tables would be boring, and we have no experience whatsoever.

I don't like whatsoever the new menu.

I don't like the new menu at all.

例句

① no + 名詞 + whatsoever

  1. I can't help with the website. I have no computer skills whatsoever.
    我沒辦法幫忙做網站。我一點電腦技能也沒有。
  2. The new rules will have no effect whatsoever on your pay.
    新的規定對你的薪水完全沒有影響。
  3. The hotel was so quiet that there was no noise whatsoever at night.
    那家飯店很安靜，晚上一點聲音也沒有。
  4. He left the company with no explanation whatsoever.
    他什麼解釋都沒有就離開了公司。
  5. I had no idea whatsoever that she was moving abroad.
    我完全不知道她要搬到國外。
  6. The children showed no interest whatsoever in the museum.
    孩子們對博物館一點興趣也沒有。
  7. There is no doubt whatsoever that he is the best player on the team.
    毫無疑問，他是隊上最好的球員。
  8. The storm was strong, but it caused no damage whatsoever to our house.
    暴風雨很強，可是我們家完全沒有受損。

② not + any + 名詞 + whatsoever

  1. The police searched the house, but they didn't find any evidence whatsoever.
    警方搜了那間房子，可是完全沒找到任何證據。
  2. We didn't have any problems whatsoever with the car.
    我們的車一點問題也沒有。
  3. She didn't give me any help whatsoever with the project.
    這個案子她一點忙都沒幫我。
  4. I don't have any money whatsoever until next Friday.
    下星期五以前我身上一毛錢也沒有。
  5. There wasn't any food whatsoever in the fridge.
    冰箱裡一點吃的也沒有。

③ nothing + whatsoever

  1. I know nothing whatsoever about cars, so I take mine to a mechanic.
    我對車子一竅不通，所以我的車都交給修車師傅。
  2. We talked for three hours, and the meeting achieved nothing whatsoever.
    我們談了三個小時，結果那次會議完全沒有任何成果。
  3. He said nothing whatsoever about his new job.
    他對新工作的事一個字也沒提。
  4. There's nothing whatsoever wrong with your phone. Just restart it.
    你的手機完全沒問題。重新開機就好。

④ none + whatsoever

  1. "Did you have any trouble at the border?" "None whatsoever."
    你過邊境的時候有遇到麻煩嗎？完全沒有。
  2. I asked for feedback on my essay, but I got none whatsoever.
    我請別人給我作文的意見，可是一個意見都沒收到。
  3. "Any questions?" "None whatsoever. It was very clear."
    有問題嗎？完全沒有。講得很清楚。

⑤ nobody / no one + whatsoever

  1. It was early in the morning, and there was nobody whatsoever on the beach.
    那時是清晨，海灘上一個人都沒有。
  2. I've told no one whatsoever about our plan.
    我們的計畫我一個人都沒告訴。

⑥ 正式的文章

  1. The employees will notice no change whatsoever in their working conditions.
    員工的工作條件完全不會有任何改變。
  2. The council has made no attempt whatsoever to repair the road.
    市議會完全沒有試著修那條路。
  3. The report contains no information whatsoever about the costs.
    報告裡完全沒有任何費用的資料。
  4. There is no evidence whatsoever that the drug is dangerous.
    完全沒有證據顯示這種藥很危險。
  5. The company accepts no responsibility whatsoever for lost items.
    本公司對遺失物品不負任何責任。

⑦ 說話時加強語氣

  1. "Do you mind if I open the window?" "No problem whatsoever."
    你介意我開窗嗎？完全沒問題。
  2. I have no plans whatsoever for the weekend. I'm going to sleep.
    我週末完全沒有計畫。我要睡覺。
  3. Sorry, I have no memory whatsoever of meeting you.
    抱歉，我完全不記得見過你。
  4. That film made no sense whatsoever. Can you explain the ending?
    那部電影完全看不懂。你可以解釋一下結局嗎？
