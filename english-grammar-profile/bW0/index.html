It smelled like roses.

那聞起來像玫瑰花。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞 + like + 名⁠詞，表⁠示像什⁠麼

It smelled like roses.

look like

It's round and silver, with lights all around it. The new library looks like a spaceship.

它是圓的、銀色的，四周都是燈。新的圖書館看起來像太空船。
taste like

I didn't put any sugar in it, but this tea tastes like honey.

我沒加糖，可是這茶喝起來像蜂蜜。
feel like

I slept for ten hours on our first night. The hotel bed felt like a cloud.

我們第一晚我睡了十個小時。飯店的床躺起來像雲一樣。

be similar to 是差⁠不⁠多，不⁠是一⁠模⁠一⁠樣

It smelled like roses.

你的故事跟我的夢很像。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞⁠時要加 like

It smelled like roses.

This tastes chicken.

This tastes like chicken.

similar 後⁠面⁠接 to

It smelled like roses.

Her bag is similar with mine.

Her bag is similar to mine.

similar 是形⁠容⁠詞，前⁠面要⁠有 be

It smelled like roses.

The weather similar to Spain.

The weather is similar to Spain's.

例句

① look like + 名詞

  1. People say I look like a pop star in this jacket.
    大家說我穿這件夾克看起來像流行歌手。
  2. We're on the fiftieth floor. From up here, the cars look like ants.
    我們在五十樓。從這上面看，車子看起來像螞蟻。
  3. You look like a real chef in that apron.
    你穿那件圍裙看起來像個真正的廚師。
  4. My son had a window seat on his first flight. From the plane, the houses looked like toys.
    我兒子第一次搭飛機坐靠窗的位子。從飛機上看，房子看起來像玩具。
  5. It's round and silver, with lights all around it. The new library looks like a spaceship.
    它是圓的、銀色的，四周都是燈。新的圖書館看起來像太空船。

② sound / smell / taste like + 名詞

  1. A car tyre exploded outside our house. The noise sounded like a gunshot.
    我們家外面有一個車胎爆了。那個聲音聽起來像槍聲。
  2. I bought this at the market in Sicily. This soap smells like lemons.
    我在西西里的市場買了這個。這塊肥皂聞起來像檸檬。
  3. I didn't put any sugar in it, but this tea tastes like honey.
    我沒加糖，可是這茶喝起來像蜂蜜。
  4. So we leave early and miss the traffic? Your plan sounds like a good idea.
    所以我們早點出發，避開車潮？你的計畫聽起來是個好主意。
  5. Grandma has been baking all morning. The kitchen smells like fresh bread.
    奶奶整個早上都在烤東西。廚房聞起來像剛烤好的麵包。
  6. The vegetarian burger tasted like real meat.
    那個素漢堡吃起來像真的肉。

③ feel / seem like + 名詞

  1. Up there, I felt like a bird.
    在上面，我覺得自己像一隻鳥。
  2. I slept for ten hours on our first night. The hotel bed felt like a cloud.
    我們第一晚我睡了十個小時。飯店的床躺起來像雲一樣。
  3. Is it really only two years? It seems like a long time since we met.
    真的才兩年嗎？我們好像很久沒見了。
  4. The boss was away, and we left work early every day. That week felt like a holiday.
    老闆不在，我們每天都提早下班。那個禮拜感覺像放假一樣。
  5. We matched pictures and words on a tablet. The test seemed like a game.
    我們在平板上把圖片和單字配對。那次考試感覺像遊戲一樣。

④ be similar to + 名詞

  1. Your idea is similar to one I had last week.
    你的點子跟我上禮拜想到的很像。
  2. It's warm and wet in Hong Kong in April. The weather here is similar to Taipei's in spring.
    香港四月又暖又濕。這裡的天氣跟台北春天的差不多。
  3. Both use a lot of rice and soup. Japanese food is similar to Korean food in some ways.
    兩種都常用米飯和湯。日本菜在某些方面跟韓國菜很像。
  4. I still answer customers' emails all day. My new job is similar to my old one.
    我還是整天回客人的電子郵件。我的新工作跟舊的差不多。
  5. It's green and has big soft cushions. Our new sofa is similar to the one at your parents' house.
    它是綠色的，有大大軟軟的靠墊。我們的新沙發跟你爸媽家那張很像。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. The teacher thinks I wrote this note. Does this look like my handwriting?
    老師以為這張紙條是我寫的。這看起來像我的字嗎？
  2. Take the train and rent bikes there? It doesn't sound like a bad plan.
    搭火車去，在那裡租腳踏車？聽起來不像個壞主意。
  3. I saw a girl with your smile at the station. Your sister looks similar to you.
    我在車站看到一個笑起來跟你一樣的女孩。你妹妹長得跟你很像。
  4. I've never tried durian. What does it taste like?
    我從來沒吃過榴槤。吃起來像什麼？
  5. My first flat had no air conditioning. The room felt like a sauna in summer.
    我的第一間公寓沒有冷氣。夏天這個房間熱得像三溫暖。
  6. My dad hums along to the new hit. That song sounds similar to an old one from the eighties.
    我爸爸跟著那首新歌哼。那首歌聽起來跟一首八〇年代的老歌很像。
  7. I can read some Italian already. Is Spanish similar to Italian?
    我已經看得懂一些義大利文。西班牙文跟義大利文很像嗎？
  8. Bring the washing in. It smells like rain.
    把晾的衣服收進來。聞起來像要下雨了。
  9. I thought Mr Lee answered the phone, but it was his son. His voice sounds like his father's.
    我以為是李先生接的電話，可是那是他兒子。他的聲音聽起來像他爸爸的。
