The popularity of this TV game in Russia clearly demonstrates the nature of human fears and dreams.

這個電視遊戲節目在俄羅斯這麼受歡迎，清楚顯示了人類恐懼和夢想的本質。

例句

① demonstrate / show

  1. In my conclusion, I write that the popularity of this TV game in Russia clearly demonstrates the nature of human fears and dreams.
    我在結論裡寫，這個電視遊戲節目在俄羅斯這麼受歡迎，清楚顯示了人類恐懼和夢想的本質。
  2. We tested the drug on two hundred patients. The trial demonstrates that it is safe.
    我們在兩百位病人身上試了這種藥。試驗證明它是安全的。
  3. Sales doubled after the advert. This clearly demonstrates the power of television.
    廣告播出後銷量增加了一倍。這清楚證明了電視的影響力。
  4. Look at the second table. It shows that girls read more than boys.
    看第二個表格。它顯示女生比男生讀更多書。
  5. The history of the city demonstrates how quickly a small port can grow.
    這座城市的歷史證明一個小港口可以多快發展起來。

② illustrate

  1. Here is my answer to Task 1. The two charts illustrate the number of employees in three factories in 2003.
    這是我寫作第一題的答案。這兩張圖表呈現二〇〇三年三家工廠的員工人數。
  2. Describe the figure in your answer. The pie chart illustrates how families spend their money.
    在答案裡描述這張圖。這張圓餅圖呈現家庭怎麼花錢。
  3. Her story illustrates the problems that young farmers face today.
    她的故事說明了年輕農夫現在面對的問題。
  4. Turn to page 12 of your science book. The diagram illustrates the stages in the life of a frog.
    翻到自然課本第十二頁。這張圖解呈現青蛙一生的各個階段。
  5. Two teams both thought the room was theirs. This example illustrates why we need clear rules.
    兩組人都以為那間會議室是他們的。這個例子說明了我們為什麼需要清楚的規定。

③ indicate / suggest

  1. Prices went up again in May. The figures indicate that the market is still strong.
    五月物價又漲了。這些數字顯示市場仍然強勁。
  2. Researchers followed five hundred families for a year. The study suggests that children who play outside sleep better.
    研究人員追蹤了五百個家庭一年。這項研究顯示在戶外玩的孩子睡得比較好。
  3. Early results indicate a fall in crime in the city centre.
    初步結果顯示市中心的犯罪減少了。
  4. Our survey of local shops suggests that most customers prefer to pay by card.
    我們對本地商店的調查顯示大部分顧客比較喜歡刷卡。
  5. More rivers are polluted than ten years ago. These findings suggest that the problem is getting worse.
    受污染的河比十年前更多。這些發現顯示問題越來越嚴重。

④ reveal / highlight

  1. We asked a thousand office workers. The survey reveals that many of them feel lonely at work.
    我們問了一千位上班族。調查揭露他們很多人上班時覺得孤單。
  2. The museum has just put her letters online. The letters reveal a different side of the famous writer.
    博物館剛把她的信放上網。這些信件揭露了這位名作家的另一面。
  3. Hospitals are short of staff. The report highlights the need for more nurses.
    醫院人手不足。這份報告特別指出需要更多護理師。
  4. In the final chapter, the author highlights the role of women in the war.
    在最後一章，作者特別指出女性在戰爭中扮演的角色。
  5. Twenty cars crashed on the motorway this morning. The accident highlights the dangers of driving in fog.
    今天早上高速公路上有二十輛車相撞。這起事故凸顯了在霧中開車的危險。

⑤ argue / claim

  1. In her essay on education, the writer argues that schools should start later.
    在她討論教育的文章裡，作者主張學校應該晚一點上課。
  2. The ice is melting every summer. Some scientists argue that the climate is changing faster than we think.
    冰每年夏天都在融化。有些科學家主張氣候變化得比我們想的還快。
  3. Don't believe the advert. The company claims that its new phone is the fastest in the world.
    別相信廣告。這家公司宣稱它的新手機是世界上最快的。
  4. Many people claim that money doesn't bring happiness, but the data say otherwise.
    很多人宣稱錢不能帶來快樂，可是資料顯示的不是這樣。
  5. In this paper, I argue that social media changes how we make friends.
    在這篇論文裡，我主張社群媒體改變了我們交朋友的方式。

⑥ confirm

  1. Earlier studies found the same link. The new study confirms these findings.
    之前的研究發現了同樣的關聯。新的研究證實了這些發現。
  2. Good news for the campers. Tests confirm that the water in the lake is safe to drink.
    露營的人有好消息了。檢驗證實湖裡的水可以安全飲用。
  3. A tourist took pictures of the robbery. The photos confirm what the witnesses told the police.
    一位觀光客拍下了搶劫的過程。這些照片證實了目擊者告訴警方的話。

⑦ 否定句

  1. The rise in sales is small. The data don't demonstrate that the new design works.
    銷量只增加了一點點。資料不能證明新設計有效。
  2. Only ten people took part. The results don't indicate a clear trend.
    只有十個人參加。結果並沒有顯示出明確的趨勢。
