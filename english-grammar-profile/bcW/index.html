例句

① when

  1. When I opened the door, the dog ran out into the street.
    我一開門，狗就跑到街上去了。
  2. We were drenched when we finally got home.
    我們終於到家的時候，全身都濕透了。
  3. My mother cried when she heard the news.
    我媽聽到消息的時候哭了。
  4. The children were gleeful when they saw the snow.
    孩子們看到雪的時候開心極了。

② while

  1. While I lived in Osaka, I learned to cook.
    我住在大阪的時候學會了做菜。
  2. Someone stole my bike while I was in the library.
    我在圖書館的時候，有人偷了我的腳踏車。
  3. While she waited for the bus, she read the whole newspaper.
    她等公車的時候，把整份報紙看完了。

③ before

  1. I checked all the windows before I left the house.
    我出門前把所有窗戶都檢查了一遍。
  2. Before we moved here, we lived in a tiny flat above a shop.
    搬來這裡以前，我們住在一家店樓上的小公寓。
  3. She phoned her parents before she got on the plane.
    她上飛機前打了電話給爸媽。

④ as soon as

  1. As soon as we went down to fifteen metres, we saw lots of coloured fish.
    我們一潛到十五公尺深，就看到很多彩色的魚。
  2. I called you as soon as I heard.
    我一聽到消息就打給你了。
  3. As soon as the teacher left the room, the students started talking.
    老師一離開教室，學生就開始講話。

⑤ once

  1. Once I found the right key, the door opened easily.
    找到對的鑰匙以後，門一下就開了。
  2. Once the guests left, we cleaned up the kitchen.
    客人一走，我們就把廚房收拾乾淨。
  3. Once she made up her mind, nothing could change it.
    她一下了決定，什麼都改變不了。

⑥ until

  1. We waited until the rain stopped.
    我們等到雨停。
  2. I didn't know about the party until Tom told me.
    直到 Tom 跟我說，我才知道有派對。
  3. He stayed at the office until everyone else went home.
    他在辦公室待到其他人都回家了。

⑦ if

  1. If it rained, we played cards in the kitchen.
    以前下雨的時候，我們就在廚房玩牌。
  2. If my grandfather didn't like a meal, he never said so.
    我爺爺不喜歡一道菜的話，從來不說出口。
  3. If you missed the last bus, you had to walk home.
    那時候要是錯過末班車，就只能走路回家。

⑧ since

  1. I've lived in this flat since I left university.
    我大學畢業以後就一直住在這間公寓。
  2. She has looked haggard since she started the new job.
    她開始新工作以後，就一直很憔悴。
  3. Since it was late, we took a taxi home.
    因為很晚了，我們搭計程車回家。
  4. Since the hotel was full, we stayed with a friend.
    因為飯店客滿，我們就住在朋友家。

⑨ so

  1. The house was dilapidated, so we decided not to buy it.
    那棟房子很破舊，所以我們決定不買。
  2. I was tired, so I went to bed early.
    我累了，所以早早就睡了。
  3. The shop was closed, so we bought bread at the station.
    那家店沒開，所以我們在車站買了麵包。

⑩ 一句裡用兩個從屬連接詞

  1. When we arrived at the hotel, the room wasn't ready, so we went for a walk.
    我們到飯店的時候房間還沒準備好，所以我們就去散步了。
  2. As soon as the rain stopped, we went out and stayed in the park until it got dark.
    雨一停我們就出門，在公園待到天黑。
  3. While we were in Rome, it rained every day, so we spent a lot of time in museums.
    我們在羅馬的時候天天下雨，所以花了很多時間逛博物館。
  4. Once the baby fell asleep, we watched a film until midnight.
    寶寶一睡著，我們就看電影看到半夜。
