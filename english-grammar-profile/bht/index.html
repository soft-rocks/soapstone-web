I was thinking of painting my bedroom blue. It would look awesome.

我在想要把我的房間漆成藍色。那樣會超好看的。

It + would + be + 形⁠容⁠詞

would會。說想像的情況。
原形動詞想像中會發生的事。

先說想⁠法，再想⁠像結⁠果

I was thinking of painting my bedroom blue. It would look awesome.

例句

Let's go to the lake. It would be fun there.

我們去湖邊吧。在那裡會很好玩。

常先說自己的想法或計畫，再用 would 想像結果。

would 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I was thinking of painting my bedroom blue. It would look awesome.

It would looks great.

It would look great.

would 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I was thinking of painting my bedroom blue. It would look awesome.

It would to be fun.

It would be fun.

would 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 be

I was thinking of painting my bedroom blue. It would look awesome.

It would is nice to live there.

It would be nice to live there.

例句

① 想法 + It would + look / be + 形容詞：想像結果

  1. My bedroom walls are so boring. I was thinking of painting them blue. It would look awesome.
    我房間的牆好無聊。我在想要不要把它們漆成藍色。那一定會很好看。
  2. Let's have the party by the lake. It would be fun there.
    我們在湖邊辦派對吧。在那裡會很好玩。
  3. Why don't you cut your hair short? It would look great on you.
    你何不把頭髮剪短？你剪短一定很好看。
  4. We could put a big plant in the corner. It would make the room brighter.
    我們可以在角落放一盆大植物。那會讓房間亮一點。
  5. Let's take the train along the coast. It would be a lovely trip.
    我們搭沿著海岸的火車吧。那會是一趟很棒的旅行。
  6. I'd like to grow tomatoes in the garden. They would taste much better.
    我想在花園裡種番茄。它們會好吃很多。

② It would be + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞：想像做某件事

  1. It would be wonderful to live in a house by the sea.
    住在海邊的房子會很棒。
  2. It would be nice to see all my old school friends again.
    再見到我所有的老同學會很好。
  3. It would be easier to take a taxi than the bus.
    搭計程車會比搭公車方便。
  4. It would be great to have a swimming pool in our garden.
    我們的花園有一座游泳池會很棒。
  5. It would be cheaper to cook at home than eat out.
    在家煮飯會比外食便宜。
  6. It would be exciting to sail across the Atlantic in a small boat.
    坐小船橫越大西洋會很刺激。

③ 主詞 + would + 原形動詞：想像別人的反應

  1. My mum would love this scarf. It's her favourite colour.
    我媽媽會很喜歡這條圍巾。那是她最喜歡的顏色。
  2. Dad would be so angry if he saw this mess.
    如果爸爸看到這一團亂，他會很生氣。
  3. My grandmother would cry if she heard this song.
    如果我奶奶聽到這首歌，她會哭。
  4. The children would enjoy a day at the zoo.
    孩子們會喜歡在動物園玩一天。
  5. My teacher would be surprised to see me here.
    我的老師看到我在這裡會很驚訝。
  6. Our dog would go crazy in all this snow.
    我們的狗在這麼多雪裡會瘋掉。

④ 主詞 + would + 原形動詞：想像以後的生活

  1. With a bigger flat, we would have room for guests.
    有大一點的公寓，我們就有地方給客人住。
  2. With a car, I would visit my parents more often.
    有車的話，我會更常去看我爸媽。
  3. In a quieter town, I would sleep much better.
    住在安靜一點的小鎮，我會睡得好很多。
  4. A dog would be good company for my grandfather.
    一隻狗會是我爺爺很好的伴。
  5. A new kitchen would make cooking much more fun.
    新廚房會讓煮飯好玩很多。
  6. A bike would save me a lot of time.
    一台腳踏車會幫我省很多時間。

⑤ That would be + 形容詞：回應別人的提議

  1. "Let's have a picnic on Sunday." "That would be lovely."
    我們星期天去野餐吧。那會很棒。
  2. "I can drive you to the airport." "That would be really helpful."
    我可以開車載你去機場。那會很有幫助。
  3. "Shall we go to the beach?" "That would be perfect."
    我們去海灘好嗎？那太完美了。
  4. "We could eat at the Italian place." "That would be nice."
    我們可以去那家義大利餐廳吃。那會很不錯。
  5. "I'll bring a cake to the party." "That would be great, thanks."
    我會帶一個蛋糕去派對。那太好了，謝謝。
  6. "Let's watch the sunset from the hill." "That would be beautiful."
    我們從山丘上看夕陽吧。那會很美。
  7. "My brother can help you move." "That would be wonderful."
    我哥哥可以幫你搬家。那太好了。
