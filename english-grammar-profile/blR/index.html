Without your help, I wouldn't have got this result.

要不是那一天，她就不會遇到 George 了。

主⁠詞 + wouldn't have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

wouldn't have、without

wouldn't have過去不會。說和事實相反的結果。
without沒有……的話。

感⁠謝別⁠人

Without your help, I wouldn't have got this result.

例句

Without you, we wouldn't have finished on time.

沒有你，我們不會準時完成。

Without your help, I wouldn't have 常用來感謝別人。

倒⁠裝：Had it not been for

例句

Had it not been for that day, she would not have met George.

要不是那一天，她不會遇到喬治。

很正式的寫作會用 Had it not been for 代替 If it hadn't been for。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Without your help, I wouldn't have got this result.

I wouldn't have went without you.

I wouldn't have gone without you.

would 的否⁠定是 wouldn't

Without your help, I wouldn't have got this result.

I didn't would have known.

I wouldn't have known.

if 子⁠句用 hadn't 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

If I wouldn't have missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late.

If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late.

例句

① Without + 名詞, + 主詞 + wouldn't have + 過去分詞：感謝

  1. You helped me every week for three months. Without your help, I wouldn't have got this result.
    您三個月來每個星期都幫我。沒有您的幫忙，我不會得到這個成績。
  2. Without my parents' support, I wouldn't have gone to university.
    沒有我爸媽的支持，我不會去念大學。
  3. Without the map, we wouldn't have found the hotel.
    沒有那張地圖，我們不會找到飯店。
  4. Without your advice, I wouldn't have taken this job.
    沒有你的建議，我不會接受這份工作。
  5. Without the volunteers, the festival wouldn't have happened.
    沒有那些志工，這個節慶不會辦成。
  6. Without my glasses, I wouldn't have seen the sign.
    沒有眼鏡，我不會看到那個標誌。

② Had it not been for + 名詞, + 主詞 + would not have + 過去分詞

  1. She missed her train that day. Had it not been for that day, she would not have met George.
    那天她錯過了火車。要不是那一天，她不會遇到喬治。
  2. Had it not been for the rain, we would not have stayed indoors.
    要不是下雨，我們不會待在室內。
  3. Had it not been for her teacher, she would not have become a writer.
    要不是她的老師，她不會成為作家。
  4. Had it not been for the doctor, he would not have survived.
    要不是那位醫生，他不會活下來。
  5. Had it not been for the strike, the trip would not have been cancelled.
    要不是罷工，旅行不會取消。（被動）
  6. Had it not been for my dog, I would not have woken up during the fire.
    要不是我的狗，火災時我不會醒過來。

③ If + 主詞 + hadn't + 過去分詞, + 主詞 + wouldn't have + 過去分詞

  1. If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late for the exam.
    如果我沒錯過公車，考試就不會遲到了。
  2. If we hadn't taken the wrong road, we wouldn't have got lost.
    如果我們沒走錯路，就不會迷路了。
  3. If she hadn't fallen, she wouldn't have broken her arm.
    如果她沒跌倒，就不會摔斷手臂了。
  4. If you hadn't reminded me, I wouldn't have remembered his birthday.
    如果你沒提醒我，我就不會記得他的生日了。
  5. If they hadn't left the door open, the cat wouldn't have escaped.
    如果他們沒讓門開著，貓就不會跑掉了。
  6. If I hadn't eaten so much, I wouldn't have felt sick.
    如果我沒吃那麼多，就不會覺得不舒服了。

④ 主詞 + wouldn't have + 過去分詞 + if + 子句：if 子句放在後面

  1. I wouldn't have bought the car if I'd known about the problems.
    如果我知道那些問題，我就不會買那台車了。
  2. We wouldn't have gone camping if we'd seen the forecast.
    如果我們看了天氣預報，就不會去露營了。
  3. She wouldn't have said that if she'd been less tired.
    如果她沒那麼累，她就不會那樣說了。
  4. They wouldn't have won if they hadn't bought a new striker.
    如果他們沒有買一個新前鋒，他們不會贏。
  5. I wouldn't have met my best friend if I hadn't moved here.
    如果我沒搬來這裡，就不會認識我最好的朋友。
  6. He wouldn't have passed if he hadn't studied so hard.
    如果他沒那麼用功，他就不會考過了。

⑤ 主詞 + wouldn't have + 過去分詞：說過去不會做的事

  1. I wouldn't have chosen that colour for the kitchen.
    我不會為廚房選那個顏色。
  2. My grandmother wouldn't have liked this modern music.
    我奶奶不會喜歡這種現代音樂。
  3. I wouldn't have known what to do without the instructions.
    沒有說明書，我不會知道要怎麼做。
  4. Most drivers wouldn't have seen that small sign in the dark.
    天黑的時候，大部分的駕駛不會看到那個小標誌。
  5. Most people wouldn't have waited so long.
    大部分的人不會等那麼久。
  6. I wouldn't have believed it if I hadn't seen it myself.
    如果不是我親眼看到，我不會相信。
  7. A careful driver wouldn't have parked there.
    一個小心的駕駛不會把車停在那裡。
