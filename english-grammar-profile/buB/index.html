例句

① Why can't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Your job is in the morning and the club meets in the evening. Why can't you do both things?
    你的工作在早上，社團在晚上。你為什麼不能兩件事都做？
  2. Everyone else is going. Why can't I go to the party?
    其他人都要去。我為什麼不能去派對？
  3. Why can't we take the car? It's raining.
    我們為什麼不能開車？外面在下雨。
  4. I know you weren't at the library. Why can't you just tell me the truth?
    我知道你不在圖書館。你為什麼就是不能跟我說實話？
  5. It's sunny today. Why can't the children play in the garden?
    今天出太陽。孩子們為什麼不能在院子裡玩？
  6. The shop is open. Why can't I pay by card?
    店有開。我為什麼不能刷卡？

② Why couldn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. I shouted at my brother again. Why couldn't I control myself?
    我又對我弟弟大吼了。我當時為什麼控制不了自己？
  2. We waited for you for half an hour. Why couldn't you come to the meeting yesterday?
    我們等了你半小時。你昨天為什麼不能來開會？
  3. They slept in their car last night. Why couldn't they find a hotel in the city?
    他們昨晚睡在車上。他們為什麼在城裡找不到飯店？
  4. Anna looks very tired this morning. Why couldn't she sleep last night?
    Anna 今天早上看起來很累。她昨晚為什麼睡不著？

③ Why won't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. I've called five times. Why won't he answer the phone?
    我打了五次。他為什麼不接電話？
  2. Why won't the door open? Is it locked?
    門為什麼打不開？鎖住了嗎？
  3. You look exhausted. Why won't you take a break?
    你看起來累壞了。你為什麼不肯休息一下？
  4. Why won't she talk to me? What did I do?
    她為什麼不跟我說話？我做了什麼？
  5. I charged it all night. Why won't my computer turn on?
    我充了一整晚的電。我的電腦為什麼開不了機？

④ Why wouldn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. You have a good job. Why wouldn't the bank give you a loan?
    你有份好工作。銀行為什麼不肯貸款給你？
  2. He left the house in a hurry. Why wouldn't he tell us where he was going?
    他匆匆忙忙出門。他為什麼不肯跟我們說他要去哪裡？
  3. It's a great offer. Why wouldn't you accept it?
    這是很好的條件。你為什麼不接受？

⑤ Why shouldn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. She's my best friend. Why shouldn't I tell her?
    她是我最好的朋友。我為什麼不應該告訴她？
  2. Why shouldn't we take a holiday? We've worked hard all year.
    我們為什麼不應該去度假？我們辛苦了一整年。
  3. Some parents want to call their kids during the day. Why shouldn't children use phones at school?
    有些家長白天想打電話給孩子。孩子為什麼不應該在學校用手機？

⑥ 其他疑問詞 + 情態動詞 + n't

  1. I'm planning the menu for your visit. What foods can't you have?
    我在安排你來訪時的菜單。你有哪些食物不能吃？
  2. There are yellow lines everywhere. Where can't we park in this street?
    到處都是黃線。這條街哪裡不能停車？
  3. Who can't come on Friday? Put your hand up.
    星期五誰不能來？請舉手。
  4. You said the test was hard. Which parts couldn't you finish?
    你說考試很難。你有哪些部分寫不完？
  5. The sign says "No entry". Where can't we go?
    告示上寫禁止進入。我們哪裡不能去？

⑦ 表示不滿或不解

  1. This is the third time this week. Why can't you remember your keys?
    這星期第三次了。你為什麼就是記不住帶鑰匙？
  2. There are bottles all over the beach again. Why can't people put their rubbish in the bin?
    海灘上又到處是瓶子。大家為什麼不能把垃圾丟進垃圾桶？
  3. I said the bridge was closed, but they drove that way anyway. Why won't anyone listen to me?
    我說橋封了，他們還是往那邊開。為什麼沒有人要聽我說？
  4. Why couldn't you call me? I was so worried.
    你為什麼不能打個電話給我？我好擔心。
  5. First my glasses, now my phone. Why can't I find anything in this house?
    先是眼鏡，現在是手機。為什麼我在這個家裡什麼都找不到？
