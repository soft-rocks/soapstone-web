例句

① has / have to have + 過去分詞：現在找工作的條件

  1. Companies want more and more these days. Nowadays, one has to have finished at least senior high school for any employer to consider hiring you.
    現在公司的要求越來越多。現在一個人至少要念完高中，雇主才會考慮僱用你。
  2. My cousin graduated last summer. These days you have to have done an internship to get a good job.
    我表哥去年夏天畢業了。現在你必須實習過，才找得到好工作。
  3. My sister wants to work at the city hospital. To work as a nurse here, you have to have trained for three years.
    我姊姊想在市立醫院工作。要在這裡當護理師，你必須受過三年訓練。
  4. My friend wants to teach in a state school. Teachers have to have passed a special exam before they can work in state schools.
    我朋友想在公立學校教書。老師必須通過一項特別的考試，才能在公立學校工作。
  5. I asked a pilot about her job. Pilots have to have flown a thousand hours before they can fly passengers.
    我問了一位飛行員她的工作。飛行員必須飛滿一千個小時，才能載乘客。
  6. The council has cheap flats for local people. Applicants have to have lived in the city for at least a year.
    市議會有給本地人的便宜公寓。申請人必須已經在這個城市住滿至少一年。
  7. The factory has new rules this year. Staff have to have completed the safety training before they use the machines.
    工廠今年有新規定。員工必須完成安全訓練才能使用機器。

② has / have to have + 過去分詞：現在其他的規定

  1. The tennis club is choosing a new chair. To vote in the club elections, members have to have paid their fees.
    網球俱樂部在選新主席。要在社團選舉投票，會員必須已經繳了會費。
  2. My neighbour applied for a passport here. To become a citizen, you have to have lived in the country for ten years.
    我的鄰居在這裡申請護照。要成為公民，你必須已經在這個國家住滿十年。
  3. The school sent a letter to new parents. Children have to have had all their injections before they start school.
    學校寄了一封信給新生家長。孩子入學前必須打完所有的預防針。
  4. Our coach is very careful. After a head injury, players have to have rested for two days before they play again.
    我們的教練很謹慎。頭部受傷後，球員必須休息滿兩天才能再上場。
  5. Check your documents before you book. You have to have renewed your passport in the last ten years.
    訂票之前先檢查你的證件。你必須在最近十年內換過護照。
  6. I want to sign up for the physics course. To take the course, students have to have studied maths at school.
    我想報名物理課。要修這門課，學生必須在中學學過數學。

③ had to have + 過去分詞：過去的規定

  1. My grandfather was a soldier for two years. In his day, you had to have served in the army to get a government job.
    我爺爺當了兩年兵。在他那個年代，你必須服過兵役才能找到政府的工作。
  2. My uncle flew planes for forty years. Before 1990, pilots at this airline had to have served in the air force.
    我叔叔開了四十年的飛機。1990 年以前，這家航空公司的飛行員必須在空軍服役過。
  3. My grandfather studied at Oxford. To get in back then, students had to have learned Latin.
    我爺爺在牛津念書。那時候要進牛津，學生必須學過拉丁文。
  4. My grandmother worked at the mill all her life. Back then, you had to have worked for the company for twenty years to get a pension.
    我奶奶一輩子都在磨坊工作。那時候，你必須在公司工作滿二十年才能領退休金。
  5. My uncle became a doctor in 1970. In the past, doctors had to have studied for seven years.
    我叔叔一九七〇年當上醫生。以前醫生必須讀七年書。
  6. The old club had strict rules. To join the club in those days, you had to have grown up in the town.
    那個老俱樂部規定很嚴格。那個年代要加入這個俱樂部，你必須是在這個鎮上長大的。

④ had to have been + -ing：過去要求持續的事

  1. My father wanted to study medicine. To get into medical school, you had to have been studying science for two years.
    我爸爸想讀醫學系。要進醫學院，你必須已經念了兩年的理科。
  2. We moved to the area in 2015. To get the grant, you had to have been living in the area for three years.
    我們二〇一五年搬到這一區。要拿到補助，你必須已經在這個地區住了三年。
  3. The best players grew up at the club. To join the team, players had to have been training with the club since childhood.
    最好的球員都是在俱樂部長大的。要加入這支球隊，球員必須從小就在俱樂部訓練。
  4. The college wanted experienced staff. To apply for the job, you had to have been teaching for at least five years.
    那所學院想要有經驗的員工。要應徵這份工作，你必須已經教了至少五年書。
  5. The free concert was very popular. To get a ticket, you had to have been queuing since the morning.
    那場免費演唱會很熱門。要拿到票，你必須從早上就在排隊。
  6. My grandmother finally got a room. To get a place in the home, you had to have been paying into the fund for years.
    我奶奶終於得到一個床位。要在那家養老院得到床位，你必須已經繳了好幾年的基金。

⑤ will have to have + 過去分詞：未來的規定

  1. The government has new road rules. From next year, all drivers will have to have taken a first aid course.
    政府有新的交通規定。從明年起，所有駕駛都必須上過急救課程。
  2. Teacher training is changing. New teachers will have to have spent a year in a school before they qualify.
    師資培訓在改變。新老師取得資格之前，必須已經在學校待過一年。
  3. The city has a plan for clean energy. From 2030, every new house will have to have passed an energy test before people move in.
    市政府有一個乾淨能源的計畫。從 2030 年起，每間新房子都必須先通過能源檢測，才能讓人入住。
  4. The reading list for the course is out. Students will have to have read the book before the first class.
    這門課的書單出來了。學生必須在第一堂課之前讀完這本書。
  5. The company is buying new software. All workers will have to have learned basic computer skills by the end of the year.
    公司要買新的軟體。所有工人到年底都必須學會基本的電腦技能。
  6. The museum is very busy in summer. Visitors will have to have booked their tickets online before they arrive.
    博物館夏天很多人。參觀者到達之前必須已經在網路上訂好門票。
