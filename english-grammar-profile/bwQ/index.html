Nowadays, one has to have finished at least senior high school for any employer to consider hiring you.

現在，你至少要高中畢業，雇主才會考慮用你。

主⁠詞 + have to / has to + have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

have to have + 過去分詞現在的規定，要求某件事已經完成。
had to have + 過去分詞過去的規定，要求某件事已經完成。
had to have been + -⁠ing過去的規定，要求某件事已經持續一段時間。

現⁠在和過⁠去⁠的規⁠定

現在

You have to have lived here for five years.

你必須已經在這裡住滿五年。
過去

In those days, you had to have served in the army.

那個年代你必須已經服過兵役。

用 one 和 you 說一⁠般⁠人

Nowadays, one has to have finished at least senior high school for any employer to consider hiring you.

例句

One has to have saved some money before buying a house.

買房子之前，人必須先存一些錢。

正式的寫作常用 one 說一般人。口語用 you。

第⁠二⁠個 have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Nowadays, one has to have finished at least senior high school for any employer to consider hiring you.

You have to have finish school.

You have to have finished school.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 have

Nowadays, one has to have finished at least senior high school for any employer to consider hiring you.

She had to had studied there.

She had to have studied there.

have been 後⁠面⁠接 ⁠-⁠ing

You had to have been study in a public school.

You had to have been studying in a public school.

例句

① has / have to have + 過去分詞：現在找工作的條件

  1. To work at the bank, you have to have finished a business degree.
    要在那家銀行工作，你必須念完商學學位。
  2. These days you have to have done an internship to get a good job.
    現在你必須實習過，才找得到好工作。
  3. To work as a nurse here, you have to have trained for three years.
    要在這裡當護理師，你必須受過三年訓練。
  4. Teachers have to have passed a special exam before they can work in state schools.
    老師必須通過一項特別的考試，才能在公立學校工作。
  5. Pilots have to have flown a thousand hours before they can fly passengers.
    飛行員必須飛滿一千個小時，才能載乘客。
  6. Applicants have to have lived in the city for at least a year.
    申請人必須已經在這個城市住滿至少一年。
  7. Staff have to have completed the safety training before they use the machines.
    員工必須完成安全訓練才能使用機器。

② has / have to have + 過去分詞：現在其他的規定

  1. To vote in the club elections, members have to have paid their fees.
    要在社團選舉投票，會員必須已經繳了會費。
  2. To become a citizen, you have to have lived in the country for ten years.
    要成為公民，你必須已經在這個國家住滿十年。
  3. Children have to have had all their injections before they start school.
    孩子入學前必須打完所有的預防針。
  4. Players have to have rested for two days after a head injury.
    頭部受傷後，球員必須休息兩天。
  5. Your passport has to have been issued in the last ten years.
    你的護照必須是最近十年內核發的。（被動）
  6. To take the course, students have to have studied maths at school.
    要修這門課，學生必須在中學學過數學。

③ had to have + 過去分詞：過去的規定

  1. In my grandfather's time, you had to have served in the army to get a government job.
    在我爺爺的年代，你必須服過兵役才能找到政府的工作。
  2. Before 1990, women had to have married to get a loan from this bank.
    1990 年以前，女性必須已婚才能向這家銀行貸款。
  3. To get into the old university, students had to have learned Latin.
    要進那所古老的大學，學生必須學過拉丁文。
  4. Back then, you had to have worked for the company for twenty years to get a pension.
    那時候，你必須在公司工作滿二十年才能領退休金。
  5. In the past, doctors had to have studied for seven years.
    以前醫生必須讀七年書。
  6. To join the club in those days, you had to have been born in the town.
    那個年代要加入這個俱樂部，你必須是在這個鎮上出生的。（被動）

④ had to have been + -ing：過去要求持續的事

  1. To get into a state university, you usually had to have been studying in a public school.
    要進州立大學，你通常必須一直在公立學校就讀。
  2. To get the grant, you had to have been living in the area for three years.
    要拿到補助，你必須已經在這個地區住了三年。
  3. To join the team, players had to have been training with the club since childhood.
    要加入這支球隊，球員必須從小就在俱樂部訓練。
  4. To apply for the job, you had to have been teaching for at least five years.
    要應徵這份工作，你必須已經教了至少五年書。
  5. To get a free ticket, you had to have been waiting in the queue since the morning.
    要拿到免費的票，你必須從早上就在排隊。
  6. To get a place in the home, you had to have been paying into the fund for years.
    要在那家養老院得到床位，你必須已經繳了好幾年的基金。

⑤ will have to have + 過去分詞：未來的規定

  1. From next year, all drivers will have to have taken a first aid course.
    從明年起，所有駕駛都必須上過急救課程。
  2. New teachers will have to have spent a year in a school before they qualify.
    新老師取得資格之前，必須已經在學校待過一年。
  3. By 2030, builders will have to have put solar panels on every new house.
    到了 2030 年，建商必須已經在每間新房子裝好太陽能板。
  4. Students will have to have read the book before the first class.
    學生必須在第一堂課之前讀完這本書。
  5. All workers will have to have learned basic computer skills by the end of the year.
    所有工人到年底都必須學會基本的電腦技能。
  6. Visitors will have to have booked their tickets online.
    參觀者必須已經在網路上訂好門票。
