The building itself looks splendid.

這棟建築本身看起來很壯觀。

itself 緊⁠接在單⁠數名⁠詞後⁠面，強⁠調是這⁠個東⁠西本⁠身

這棟建築本身看起來很壯觀。

名⁠詞加 itself 可⁠以當主⁠詞、受⁠詞，或放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

The building itself looks splendid.

主詞

The hotel was hard to find, but the room itself was lovely.

那家飯店很難找，可是房間本身很漂亮。
受詞

She liked the garden more than the house itself.

比起房子本身，她更喜歡花園。
介系詞後面

I paid more for the frame than for the painting itself.

我買畫框花的錢比買畫本身還多。

強⁠調⁠的 itself 拿⁠掉⁠後，句⁠子還⁠是完⁠整⁠的

The building itself looks splendid.

強調

The building itself looks splendid.

拿掉 itself，句子還是完整的。
受詞

The cat washed itself.

itself 是 washed 的受詞，不能拿掉。

複⁠數名⁠詞用 themselves

The building itself looks splendid.

The rooms itself were clean.

The rooms themselves were clean.

itself 緊⁠接在名⁠詞後⁠面

The building itself looks splendid.

Itself the town is small.

The town itself is small.

itself 是一⁠個⁠字，中⁠間不⁠空⁠格

The building itself looks splendid.

The museum it self is free.

The museum itself is free.

例句

① 名詞是主詞

  1. The film itself is only ninety minutes long, but there are twenty minutes of adverts before it.
    電影本身只有九十分鐘，可是前面有二十分鐘的廣告。
  2. The vase itself is translucent, so it glows when the sun hits it.
    那個花瓶本身是半透明的，所以陽光照到時會發亮。
  3. The form was approved in a week, but the reimbursement itself took three months.
    表格一週就核准了，可是退款本身花了三個月才入帳。
  4. The job itself is fine, but the commute is exhausting.
    工作本身還好，可是通勤很累人。
  5. The idea itself is good. We just need more money to make it work.
    這個點子本身很好，我們只是需要更多錢來實現它。
  6. The village itself has only one shop, so we drive to town for groceries.
    村子本身只有一家店，所以我們開車去鎮上買菜。
  7. Don't worry. The surgery itself will take about an hour.
    別擔心，手術本身大概只要一個小時。
  8. The phone itself was cheap, but the case cost almost as much.
    手機本身很便宜，可是手機殼幾乎一樣貴。
  9. Does the course itself cost anything, or do we only pay for the books?
    課程本身要收費嗎？還是我們只付書錢？
  10. The illness itself is mild; the stigma is what hurts patients most.
    這種病本身不嚴重，最傷害病人的是汙名。
  11. The castle itself was closed, so we walked around the gardens.
    城堡本身沒開放，所以我們在花園裡逛了逛。
  12. The testimony itself lasted ten minutes, but the questions went on all afternoon.
    證詞本身十分鐘就講完了，可是提問持續了一整個下午。
  13. The software itself works well; the problem is your old computer.
    軟體本身運作得很好，問題出在你那台舊電腦。
  14. The beach itself stays quiet, even in August.
    海灘本身一直很安靜，就算是八月也一樣。
  15. The wedding itself was short, but the party lasted until two in the morning.
    婚禮本身很短，可是派對一直到凌晨兩點才結束。
  16. The card itself was simple, but your message made me cry.
    卡片本身很簡單，可是你寫的話讓我哭了。

② 名詞是受詞或在介系詞後面

  1. I didn't like our hotel much, but I loved the city itself.
    我不太喜歡我們住的飯店，可是我很愛那座城市本身。
  2. We never actually visited the museum itself; we only saw the gift shop.
    我們其實沒去看博物館本身，只逛了禮品店。
  3. Don't blame the app itself. Check your internet connection first.
    不要怪應用程式本身。先檢查你的網路連線。
  4. My daughter cares more about the box than the present itself.
    比起禮物本身，我女兒更在意盒子。
  5. The reviews were terrible, but I enjoyed the book itself.
    評論很差，可是我很喜歡那本書本身。
  6. Nobody complained about the food itself, only about the slow service.
    沒有人抱怨食物本身，只抱怨上菜太慢。
  7. The insurance cost more than the car itself.
    保險比車子本身還貴。
  8. I'm not worried about the exam itself, just about the results.
    我擔心的不是考試本身，而是成績。
  9. He spent more time on the cover than on the report itself.
    他花在封面上的時間比花在報告本身還多。
  10. The guide talked a lot about the history, but very little about the temple itself.
    導遊講了很多歷史，可是很少講寺廟本身。
  11. Let's fix the leak itself instead of mopping the floor every morning.
    我們來處理漏水本身，不要每天早上都在拖地。
  12. The protest wasn't about the new tax itself but about the way it was introduced.
    這次抗議的重點不是新稅本身，而是它推出的方式。
  13. Most visitors take photos in the square, and few go inside the cathedral itself.
    大部分遊客只在廣場拍照，很少人走進大教堂本身。
  14. The flights were expensive, so we spent less on the holiday itself.
    機票很貴，所以我們在旅遊本身上花得比較少。
  15. The council will repair the road to the castle before it starts on the castle itself.
    市議會會先修通往城堡的路，再開始修城堡本身。
  16. Don't judge the restaurant by its sign. Try the food itself.
    不要用招牌來評斷這家餐廳，嚐嚐食物本身。
