I bought myself a yellow T-shirt.

我給自己買了一件黃色 T 恤。

主⁠詞和受⁠詞是同⁠一個⁠人，受⁠詞用 myself

我給自己買了一件黃色 T 恤。

enjoy、hurt 常和 myself、yourself 一⁠起用

I bought myself a yellow T-shirt.

enjoy

I enjoyed myself at the party.

我在派對上玩得很開心。
hurt

Be careful with that knife. Don't hurt yourself.

小心那把刀，別傷到自己。

主⁠詞和受⁠詞是同⁠一個⁠人用 myself，不⁠是用 me

I bought myself a yellow T-shirt.

myself

I bought myself a cake.

我買了一個蛋糕給自己。
me

My mom bought me a cake.

我媽媽買了一個蛋糕給我。

主⁠詞和受⁠詞都⁠是⁠我，用 myself

I bought myself a yellow T-shirt.

I bought me a new phone.

I bought myself a new phone.

enjoy 後⁠面要⁠有受⁠詞

I bought myself a yellow T-shirt.

I really enjoyed at the beach.

I really enjoyed myself at the beach.

單⁠數是 yourself

Did you hurt yourselve?

Did you hurt yourself?

例句

① enjoy myself / yourself

  1. I really enjoyed myself at my cousin's wedding.
    我在我表姊的婚禮上玩得很開心。
  2. Go to the festival and enjoy yourself!
    去音樂節好好玩吧！
  3. Did you enjoy yourself at the concert?
    你在演唱會上玩得開心嗎？
  4. I didn't enjoy myself at all at the party.
    我在派對上一點都不開心。
  5. Enjoy yourself, and send me some photos!
    好好玩，記得傳照片給我！
  6. I always enjoy myself when I go camping.
    我每次去露營都玩得很開心。

② buy myself / yourself

  1. I bought myself a new pair of trainers.
    我買了一雙新球鞋給自己。
  2. Buy yourself something nice for your birthday.
    生日買點好東西給自己吧。
  3. I'm going to buy myself a coffee before the train.
    搭火車前我要先買杯咖啡給自己。
  4. Did you buy yourself a new jacket?
    你給自己買了新夾克嗎？
  5. I bought myself a little present after the exam.
    考完試後我買了一個小禮物給自己。

③ hurt myself / yourself

  1. I fell off my bike and hurt myself.
    我從腳踏車上摔下來，受傷了。
  2. Be careful on the stairs. Don't hurt yourself.
    樓梯小心，別受傷了。
  3. Did you hurt yourself when you fell?
    你跌倒的時候有受傷嗎？
  4. I hurt myself playing football yesterday.
    我昨天踢足球的時候受傷了。
  5. You'll hurt yourself if you lift that box alone.
    你一個人搬那個箱子會受傷的。

④ make myself / yourself

  1. I made myself a sandwich for lunch.
    我午餐給自己做了一個三明治。
  2. Make yourself a cup of tea.
    你自己泡杯茶吧。
  3. I'm going to make myself some pasta.
    我要給自己煮點義大利麵。
  4. Did you make yourself breakfast this morning?
    你今天早上有給自己做早餐嗎？
  5. I made myself a new dress for the party.
    我給自己做了一件新洋裝去派對。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. I taught myself to play the guitar.
    我自學彈吉他。
  2. Help yourself to anything in the fridge.
    冰箱裡的東西你自己拿。
  3. I looked at myself in the mirror.
    我看著鏡子裡的自己。
  4. You need to look after yourself while I'm away.
    我不在的時候你要好好照顧自己。
  5. I cut myself while I was cooking.
    我煮飯的時候切到自己了。
  6. Look at yourself! You're covered in mud.
    看看你自己！你全身都是泥巴。
  7. I made myself a promise to exercise every day.
    我給自己一個承諾，要每天運動。
  8. Are you talking to yourself?
    你在自言自語嗎？
  9. I gave myself a day off after the project.
    計畫結束後我給自己放了一天假。
