There is nothing interesting on TV.

電視上沒什麼好看的。

形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在 something 這⁠類代⁠名⁠詞後⁠面

There is nothing interesting on TV.

我想為她的生日煮點特別的東西。

someone、somewhere 後⁠面也接形⁠容⁠詞

There is nothing interesting on TV.

someone + 形容詞

We need someone strong to move the piano.

我們需要一個力氣大的人來搬鋼琴。
somewhere + 形容詞

Let's go somewhere quiet.

我們去個安靜的地方吧。

形⁠容⁠詞不⁠放⁠在代⁠名⁠詞前⁠面

There is nothing interesting on TV.

Can you tell me interesting something about your town?

Can you tell me something interesting about your town?

形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在 to 不⁠定⁠詞前⁠面

There is nothing interesting on TV.

I have something to tell you important.

I have something important to tell you.

nothing 前⁠面不⁠加 not

There is nothing interesting on TV.

There isn't nothing cheap in this shop.

There's nothing cheap in this shop.

例句

① something + 形容詞

  1. My sister got her first salary. She bought something expensive for our mother.
    我姊姊領了第一份薪水。她買了很貴的東西給我們媽媽。
  2. Sorry I'm late. Something terrible happened on the way home. A car hit a cyclist.
    抱歉我晚到了。回家的路上發生了一件很可怕的事。一輛車撞到一個騎腳踏車的人。
  3. It's snowing, and I only have a thin jacket. I need something warm to wear.
    下雪了，我只有一件薄外套。我需要一件保暖的衣服穿。
  4. We always go to the cinema. Let's do something different this weekend.
    我們每次都去看電影。這個週末我們做點不一樣的事吧。
  5. I called you three times, but you didn't answer. Is there something wrong with your phone?
    我打給你三次，你都沒接。你的手機有什麼問題嗎？
  6. She said something funny, and we all laughed.
    她說了一句好笑的話，我們全都笑了。

② anything + 形容詞

  1. Did the doctor say anything worrying about your knee?
    醫生有說你的膝蓋有什麼令人擔心的地方嗎？
  2. The neighbours say someone broke into the garage. I didn't hear anything strange last night.
    鄰居說有人闖進了車庫。我昨晚沒聽到什麼奇怪的聲音。
  3. I just got home from football, Mum. Is there anything good to eat?
    媽，我剛踢完足球回家。有什麼好吃的嗎？
  4. Your aunt's friends are coming for dinner. Don't say anything rude to the guests.
    你阿姨的朋友要來吃晚餐。別對客人說沒禮貌的話。
  5. We didn't do anything special for New Year.
    我們過年沒做什麼特別的事。

③ nothing / everything + 形容詞

  1. Dad put the paper down after two minutes. There's nothing interesting in the newspaper today.
    爸爸看了兩分鐘就把報紙放下。今天報紙上沒什麼有趣的新聞。
  2. The mechanic checked everything. There was nothing wrong with the car.
    修車師傅全部檢查過了。車子沒什麼問題。
  3. The kids were fine with the babysitter. Nothing bad happened, so don't worry.
    孩子們跟保母在一起很好。沒發生什麼壞事，別擔心。
  4. Take this to the bank with you. Everything important is in this folder.
    帶這個去銀行。所有重要的東西都在這個資料夾裡。
  5. My mum moved to a smaller flat. She gave away everything old in her closet.
    我媽搬到一間比較小的公寓。她把衣櫃裡所有舊的東西都送人了。

④ someone / anyone / no one + 形容詞

  1. We need someone strong to move the piano.
    我們需要一個力氣大的人來搬鋼琴。
  2. I've just moved to this city. I want to meet someone new.
    我剛搬到這個城市。我想認識新朋友。
  3. Look at all the photographers outside. Is there anyone famous at the party?
    你看外面那些攝影師。派對上有什麼名人嗎？
  4. I left Tom's party at nine. No one interesting was there.
    我九點就離開湯姆的派對了。那裡沒有什麼有趣的人。
  5. You can't reach the top shelf. Ask someone older for help.
    你搆不到最上面那一層。找一個年紀比較大的人幫忙。

⑤ somewhere / anywhere / nowhere + 形容詞

  1. Our cat always finds somewhere dark to hide.
    我們的貓總是找個暗暗的地方躲起來。
  2. I'm tired of the long, cold winters here. I want to live somewhere warm.
    我受夠了這裡又長又冷的冬天。我想住在溫暖的地方。
  3. Is there anywhere cheap to eat near here?
    這附近有便宜的地方吃飯嗎？
  4. The park benches are all wet. There's nowhere dry to sit after the rain.
    公園的長椅都是濕的。雨後沒有乾的地方可以坐。
  5. We haven't been anywhere exciting this year.
    我們今年沒去過什麼刺激的地方。
  6. The children can open that drawer. Put the medicine somewhere safe.
    孩子們打得開那個抽屜。把藥放在安全的地方。
  7. It's our anniversary tonight. Let's find somewhere nice for dinner.
    今天晚上是我們的結婚紀念日。我們找個好地方吃晚餐吧。
  8. My aunt goes to Spain every year. This time she wanted to go somewhere new for her holiday.
    我阿姨每年都去西班牙。這次她放假想去一個沒去過的地方。
  9. There's nowhere quiet to study in this house.
    這間房子裡沒有安靜的地方可以讀書。
