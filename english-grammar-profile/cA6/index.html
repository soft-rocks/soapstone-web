例句

① Let's not + 原形動詞（日常建議）

  1. It's a holiday. Let's not check our work emails.
    今天放假，我們別看工作的電子郵件了。
  2. It's half past eight, and the food is getting cold. Let's not wait for the others. They're not coming.
    已經八點半了，菜都快涼了。我們別等其他人了，他們不會來了。
  3. Look at the queue outside that café. Let's not go there. It's too crowded.
    你看那家咖啡館外面的隊伍。我們別去那裡了，人太多。
  4. I worked ten hours today. Let's not eat out tonight. I'm tired.
    我今天工作了十個小時。今晚我們別出去吃了，我很累。
  5. The radio says the traffic is stopped. Let's not take the highway. There's an accident.
    廣播說車流停住了。我們別走高速公路，那裡有車禍。
  6. We might move to Spain next year. Let's not tell our parents until it's certain.
    我們明年可能會搬到西班牙。在確定之前，我們先別告訴我們的爸媽。

② Let's not ... again / next time

  1. It was great to see you. Let's not wait ten years next time!
    很高興見到你，下次我們別再等十年了！
  2. Last year we booked too late. Let's not do that again.
    去年我們太晚訂了，別再那樣了。
  3. We only got the last two tickets for the show. Let's not leave it so late next time.
    我們只買到那場表演的最後兩張票。下次我們別拖到那麼晚。
  4. We fought about money all last week. Let's not have another argument.
    我們上星期整個星期都在為錢吵。我們別再吵架了。

③ Let's not forget that ...

  1. We need to clean the spare room. Let's not forget that Grandma is coming on Friday.
    我們要打掃客房。我們別忘了，奶奶星期五要來。
  2. Our daughter wants to go to the festival alone. Let's not forget that she's only sixteen.
    我們的女兒想自己去音樂節。我們別忘了，她才十六歲。
  3. Don't blame Tom for missing the start. Let's not forget that the bus was late too.
    別怪湯姆錯過開頭。我們別忘了，公車也誤點了。
  4. Our shop is doing well now. Let's not forget who helped us when we started.
    我們的店現在生意很好。我們別忘了，我們剛起步時是誰幫了我們。
  5. The charity run was a big success. Let's not forget to thank the volunteers.
    慈善路跑很成功。我們別忘了感謝志工們。

④ Let's not make ... / Let's not get ...

  1. The plan is already hard to follow. Let's not make things more complicated.
    這個計畫已經很難懂了。我們別把事情弄得更複雜。
  2. The bank hasn't said yes to the loan. Let's not get too excited yet.
    銀行還沒同意貸款。我們先別太興奮。
  3. Dad only broke one plate. Let's not make a big deal of it.
    爸爸只打破了一個盤子。我們別把這件事搞得太嚴重。
  4. We're sharing a flat for a whole year. Let's not get angry about small things.
    我們要合租一整年。我們別為了小事生氣。
  5. We're both too tired to think clearly. Let's not make any decisions tonight.
    我們兩個都累到沒辦法好好想。今晚我們先別做任何決定。

⑤ 回應和不同的句型

  1. A: Tom is late again. Shall we call him? B: No, let's not.
    A：湯姆又遲到了。我們要打給他嗎？B：不，別打了。
  2. It's your birthday. Let's not open any bills today.
    今天是你生日。我們今天別拆任何帳單。
  3. The exam results come out in August. Let's not worry about that now.
    考試成績八月才公布。我們現在先別擔心那件事。
  4. We're saving for a new car. Let's not spend too much on the party.
    我們在存錢買新車。派對我們別花太多錢。
  5. The kids have school tomorrow, so let's not watch the late film.
    孩子們明天要上學，所以我們別看晚場電影。
  6. We've talked about the colour for an hour. Let's not waste any more time.
    我們已經討論顏色討論了一個小時。我們別再浪費時間了。
  7. The train leaves at noon. Let's not rush. We have plenty of time.
    火車中午才開。我們別急，時間還很多。
  8. People say the new boy started the fight. Let's not judge him before we hear his side.
    大家說是那個新來的男生先打架的。在聽他的說法之前，我們別急著評斷他。
  9. It's getting late, and there are no lights up there. Let's not take the mountain road in the dark.
    時間晚了，山上又沒有路燈。天黑了，我們別走山路。
  10. We both know the business is in trouble. Let's not pretend everything is fine.
    我們兩個都知道生意出問題了。我們別假裝一切都沒事。
