It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

在阿根廷，常常會聽到大家在談經濟蕭條。

感⁠官⁠動⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

在阿根廷，常常會聽到大家在談經濟蕭條。

原⁠形⁠動⁠詞說整⁠個動⁠作，從開⁠始到結⁠束

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

我看到她上了計程車。

她走過去，打開門，坐進去。

被⁠動句⁠的原⁠形⁠動⁠詞前⁠面要加 to

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

主動

We saw him leave the building.

我們看到他離開了大樓。
被動

He was seen to leave the building.

有人看到他離開了大樓。

主⁠動句⁠的受⁠詞直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

I heard her to sing in the next room.

I heard her sing in the next room.

時⁠態只標在感⁠官⁠動⁠詞上

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

We watched the plane landed.

We watched the plane land.

感⁠官⁠動⁠詞後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

Did you see he take the money?

Did you see him take the money?

被⁠動句⁠的 to 不⁠能省⁠略

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

She was heard leave the house at midnight.

She was heard to leave the house at midnight.

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 說正⁠在進⁠行⁠的事

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

From the window I saw my neighbour painting his fence.

我從窗戶看到鄰居正在油漆他家的圍籬。

make 和 let 後⁠面也接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，但不⁠是感⁠官⁠動⁠詞

It is very common in Argentina to hear people talk about the economic depression.

My boss let me leave early on Friday.

我老闆讓我星期五提早走。

例句

① hear + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. You often hear people complain about the price of houses in this city.
    在這座城市常常聽到大家抱怨房價。
  2. "Is your sister home?" "Yes, I heard her come in about ten minutes ago."
    你姊姊在家嗎？在，我大概十分鐘前聽到她進門。
  3. I heard the front door shut, and then the house was quiet.
    我聽到前門關上，接著屋子就安靜了。
  4. Have you ever heard him sing? He has a beautiful voice.
    你聽過他唱歌嗎？他的聲音很好聽。
  5. We heard someone call our names from the back of the bus.
    我們聽到有人從公車後面叫我們的名字。

② see + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. "Did Anna leave?" "Yes, I saw her get into a taxi."
    Anna 走了嗎？走了，我看到她上了計程車。
  2. I'd like to see my city develop without destroying the environment.
    我希望看到我的城市發展，又不破壞環境。
  3. The police officer saw the man drop something into the river.
    警察看到那個男人把東西丟進河裡。
  4. Did you see the ball hit the window?
    你有看到球打到窗戶嗎？
  5. I've never seen my dad cry before.
    我以前從來沒看過我爸哭。

③ watch + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. We watched the plane take off and then drove home.
    我們看著飛機起飛，然後開車回家。
  2. The whole class watched the teacher light the candle.
    全班看著老師點蠟燭。
  3. "Did you see the goal?" "Yes, I watched him kick the ball into the net."
    你有看到那一球嗎？有，我看著他把球踢進網。
  4. Thousands of people watched the president give his speech.
    數千人看著總統發表演講。

④ notice + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Did you notice anyone go into the office after six?
    你有注意到六點以後有誰進了辦公室嗎？
  2. I didn't notice her leave the party.
    我沒注意到她什麼時候離開派對。
  3. The shop assistant noticed the boy put a chocolate bar in his pocket.
    店員注意到那個男孩把一條巧克力放進口袋。

⑤ listen to + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. We sat in silence and listened to the old man tell his story.
    我們安靜地坐著，聽那位老先生講他的故事。
  2. Every night she listened to her daughter read a chapter of her book.
    每天晚上，她都聽女兒讀一章書。
  3. I love listening to my grandmother play the piano.
    我很喜歡聽我奶奶彈鋼琴。

⑥ feel + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. I felt something touch my foot under the table.
    我感覺到桌子底下有東西碰了我的腳。
  2. "What's wrong?" "I just felt the building move. Was that an earthquake?"
    怎麼了？我剛剛感覺到大樓動了一下。是地震嗎？
  3. She felt her face go red when everyone looked at her.
    大家都看著她的時候，她感覺到自己的臉紅了。

⑦ 否定和問句

  1. "Where are my keys?" "I don't know. I didn't see you put them anywhere."
    我的鑰匙在哪裡？不知道。我沒看到你把它們放在哪裡。
  2. "Did you hear the phone ring?" "No, I was in the garden."
    你有聽到電話響嗎？沒有，我在花園裡。
  3. I've never heard her say a bad word about anyone.
    我從來沒聽過她說任何人的壞話。
  4. Did anyone see the accident happen?
    有人看到車禍發生的經過嗎？

⑧ 被動：be + 過去分詞 + to + 原形動詞

  1. The suspect was seen to leave the building at ten o'clock.
    有人看到嫌犯十點離開了大樓。
  2. The minister was heard to say that taxes would go up.
    有人聽到部長說稅會增加。
  3. Two men were seen to enter the bank just before it closed.
    有人看到兩個男人在銀行關門前走了進去。
  4. The singer was never heard to complain about her fans.
    從來沒有人聽過那位歌手抱怨她的歌迷。
