例句

① said, "…"

  1. My grandmother said, "Eat your vegetables first."
    我奶奶說：先把青菜吃掉。
  2. The pilot said, "We will land in ten minutes."
    機長說：我們十分鐘後降落。
  3. Then she said, "I think we're lost."
    接著她說：我覺得我們迷路了。
  4. Mr Hill said, "Put your phones away, please."
    Hill 老師說：請把手機收起來。
  5. Everybody said, "Congratulations!"
    大家都說：恭喜！

② asked, "…?"

  1. The woman at the desk asked, "Do you have a reservation?"
    櫃台的女士問：您有訂位嗎？
  2. My little brother asked, "Why is the sky blue?"
    我弟弟問：天空為什麼是藍色的？
  3. She asked, "Who left the door open?"
    她問：誰沒關門？
  4. The customer asked, "Do you take credit cards?"
    那位客人問：你們收信用卡嗎？
  5. At the garage, I asked, "How long will it take?"
    在修車廠，我問：要多久？

③ answered / replied

  1. When I asked if he had visited Japan, he answered, "No, I've never been there."
    我問他有沒有去過日本，他回答：沒有，我從沒去過那裡。
  2. The doctor replied, "It's nothing serious."
    醫生回答：沒什麼大礙。
  3. When the teacher asked why she was late, Lily answered, "Because I missed the bus."
    老師問她為什麼遲到，Lily 回答：因為我沒趕上公車。
  4. When I suggested pizza, my friend replied, "Sounds good to me."
    我提議吃披薩，我朋友回答：好啊。
  5. The shop assistant answered, "It's on the second floor."
    店員回答：在二樓。

④ 冒號

  1. The sign said: "No parking."
    告示上寫著：禁止停車。
  2. Her text said: "Running late, sorry!"
    她的簡訊寫著：我會晚到，抱歉！
  3. The teacher asked: "Who knows the answer?"
    老師問：誰知道答案？
  4. The note on the fridge said: "Back at six."
    冰箱上的紙條寫著：六點回來。

⑤ said to + 聽的人 / asked + 聽的人

  1. She said to her son, "Hold my hand."
    她對兒子說：牽著我的手。
  2. The coach said to us, "You played really well."
    教練對我們說：你們打得很好。
  3. I said to the driver, "Stop here, please."
    我對司機說：請在這裡停。
  4. He asked me, "Do you want some tea?"
    他問我：你要喝點茶嗎？
  5. The officer asked us, "Where are you going?"
    警察問我們：你們要去哪裡？

⑥ thought

  1. I thought, "I've seen this man before."
    我心想：我見過這個人。
  2. She thought, "Should I call him or not?"
    她心想：我該不該打給他？
  3. After a bad day at work, he thought, "Maybe tomorrow will be better."
    上班上得很不順，他心想：也許明天會好一點。
  4. When I saw the price, I thought, "That's far too expensive."
    看到價錢的時候，我心想：這也太貴了。

⑦ 引號後面接另一個子句

  1. She asked, "Are you hungry?" and I said no.
    她問：你餓了嗎？我說不餓。
  2. He said, "See you tomorrow," and walked out.
    他說：明天見。然後走了出去。
  3. Mum asked, "Who broke the vase?" and nobody answered.
    媽媽問：誰打破了花瓶？沒有人回答。
