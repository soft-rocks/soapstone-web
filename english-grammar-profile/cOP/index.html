She closed her eyes as if to push those thoughts away.

她閉上眼睛，好像想把那些念頭推開。

as if + to 不⁠定⁠詞，猜動⁠作⁠的目⁠的

She closed her eyes as if to push those thoughts away.

The cat looked up as if listening to something.

例句

① as if + to 不定詞

  1. The baby next to us on the plane was screaming. My mother shut her eyes as if to block out the noise.
    飛機上坐我們旁邊的寶寶在尖叫。我媽媽閉上眼睛，好像想隔絕噪音。
  2. I stood at the top of the climbing frame. Dad held out his arms as if to catch me.
    我站在攀爬架的最上面。爸爸張開雙臂，好像要接住我。
  3. I asked the shy new girl to sit with us. She nodded as if to say yes.
    我請那個害羞的新來女孩跟我們坐。她點點頭，好像在說好。
  4. My brother looked at me across the dinner table. He opened his mouth as if to say something, then stopped.
    我哥哥在餐桌另一頭看著我。他張開嘴，好像要說什麼，接著又停了。
  5. I bent down to say hello to my friend's dog. It lifted its paw as if to shake hands.
    我彎下腰跟我朋友的狗打招呼。牠抬起腳掌，好像要握手。
  6. My friend read the letter from her father. She turned away as if to hide her tears.
    我朋友讀了她爸爸的信。她轉過身，好像要藏住眼淚。
  7. The striker stepped up to take the penalty. The goalkeeper spread his arms as if to fill the whole goal.
    前鋒走上前踢十二碼罰球。守門員張開雙臂，好像要把整個球門擋住。

② as if + to 不定詞，表示猜測的目的

  1. We were still chatting at the end of the test. The teacher looked at the clock as if to remind us of the time.
    考試快結束了我們還在聊天。老師看了看時鐘，好像在提醒我們時間。
  2. I came home after two years abroad. Grandpa hugged me tightly, as if to make sure I was real.
    我在國外兩年後回家。爺爺緊緊抱住我，好像要確定我是真的。
  3. The chairman stood at the front of the noisy room. He coughed loudly as if to get our attention.
    主席站在吵鬧的房間前面。他大聲咳嗽，好像想引起我們注意。
  4. I was very nervous before my speech. My teacher smiled at me as if to say, "Don't worry."
    我演講前很緊張。老師對我笑了笑，好像在說別擔心。
  5. It was our son's first winter. He reached out as if to touch the snow.
    那是我們兒子的第一個冬天。他伸出手，好像想摸雪。

③ as if + 動詞-ing

  1. I walked into the dark house alone. My stomach was turning over, as if trying to warn me.
    我一個人走進黑漆漆的房子。我的胃在翻攪，好像想警告我什麼。
  2. We watched a deer in the forest. It froze as if hearing a noise.
    我們在森林裡看一隻鹿。牠突然不動，好像聽到了什麼聲音。
  3. My father came home after a long day at the factory. He walked slowly, as if carrying something heavy.
    我爸爸在工廠忙了一整天才回家。他走得很慢，好像扛著很重的東西。
  4. I saw a woman at the station entrance. She stood very still, as if waiting for someone.
    我在車站入口看到一個女人。她站著一動也不動，好像在等人。
  5. I told the farmer that the summer was too dry. The old man nodded as if agreeing with me.
    我跟農夫說夏天太乾了。那個老人點點頭，好像同意我的話。
  6. We couldn't sleep during the storm. The wind was howling, as if trying to break the windows.
    暴風雨的時候我們睡不著。風在呼嘯，好像想把窗戶吹破。

④ as if + 動詞-ing，表示猜測的原因

  1. Dad shouted at us in the morning. In the evening he cooked our favorite meal, as if trying to say sorry.
    爸爸早上對我們大吼。晚上他煮了我們最愛吃的菜，好像想道歉。
  2. I told the salesman I didn't want the car. He kept talking, as if hoping to change my mind.
    我跟業務員說我不要那輛車。他一直說，好像希望我改變主意。
  3. My flatmate stopped at the door before work. She looked around the room as if searching for her keys.
    我室友上班前在門口停下來。她環顧房間，好像在找鑰匙。
  4. The head teacher walked towards them. The boy hid behind his mother, as if expecting trouble.
    校長朝他們走過去。那個男孩躲在他媽媽身後，好像預料會有麻煩。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. I waved at Lisa in the canteen. Why did she turn her face away, as if to avoid me?
    我在餐廳跟麗莎揮手。她為什麼把臉轉開，好像在躲我？
  2. The baby had just fallen asleep. My wife put her finger to her lips as if to say, "Be quiet."
    寶寶剛睡著。我太太把手指放在嘴唇上，好像在說安靜。
  3. The pianist finished the last piece. He bowed deeply, as if to thank the whole audience.
    鋼琴家彈完最後一首曲子。他深深一鞠躬，好像在感謝全場觀眾。
  4. We sat down to eat our sandwiches. The puppy wagged its tail as if begging for food.
    我們坐下來吃三明治。小狗搖著尾巴，好像在討東西吃。
  5. We lay on the grass on a windy afternoon. The clouds moved fast, as if racing each other.
    我們在一個風很大的下午躺在草地上。雲移動得很快，好像在互相比賽。
  6. My sister looked down from the top of the tower. She laughed as if to hide her fear.
    我姊姊從塔頂往下看。她笑了，好像想掩飾她的恐懼。
  7. My friend didn't eat his dinner. He looked at his phone as if expecting a call.
    我朋友沒吃他的晚餐。他看著手機，好像在等電話。
  8. The lights in the cave went out. The children went quiet, as if sensing danger.
    洞穴裡的燈熄了。孩子們安靜下來，好像察覺到危險。
