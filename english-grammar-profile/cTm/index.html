My smile slowly disappeared as I finished reading.

讀完的時候，我臉上的笑容慢慢消失了。

情⁠狀副⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

My smile slowly disappeared as I finished reading.

讀完的時候，我臉上的笑容慢慢消失了。

情⁠狀副⁠詞最⁠常放⁠在動⁠詞後⁠面，有受⁠詞就放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面

動詞後面

You have to choose your words carefully.

你得小心挑選用詞。
受詞後面

The doctor explained the results clearly.

醫生把檢查結果解釋得很清楚。

有助⁠動⁠詞⁠時，情⁠狀副⁠詞放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

天氣會漸漸好轉。

情⁠狀副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

He fed quickly the chickens.

He fed the chickens quickly.

fast、hard、well、late 只放⁠在動⁠詞後⁠面

My brother well cooks.

My brother cooks well.

修⁠飾動⁠詞用副⁠詞，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

The judge looked at the boy serious.

The judge looked at the boy seriously.

表⁠示努⁠力用 hard，hardly 是幾⁠乎不

The students tried hardly in the exam.

The students tried hard in the exam.

例句

① 情狀副詞 + 動詞

  1. The old man got a letter from his son in Canada. He slowly opened it.
    老先生收到他在加拿大的兒子寄來的信。他慢慢把信打開。
  2. The workers started drilling next door. The noise gradually got louder.
    工人開始在隔壁鑽牆。噪音漸漸變大了。
  3. The baby was finally asleep. Mum quietly closed the bedroom door.
    寶寶終於睡著了。媽媽輕輕地關上臥室的門。
  4. We were in the middle of dinner. The lights suddenly went out.
    我們正在吃晚餐。燈突然熄了。
  5. We were moving house the next day. My husband carefully wrapped the glasses in newspaper.
    我們隔天要搬家。我先生用報紙小心地把玻璃杯包好。
  6. There was only one swing. The children patiently waited for their turn.
    只有一個鞦韆。孩子們很有耐心地等著輪到自己。
  7. My grandfather was in the army for twenty years. He proudly showed us his medal.
    我爺爺在軍隊待了二十年。他很驕傲地把他的獎章拿給我們看。
  8. Nobody was looking. The cat silently jumped onto the table.
    沒有人在看。貓悄悄跳上了桌子。

② 助動詞 + 情狀副詞 + 動詞

  1. Take an umbrella today. The weather will gradually improve this week.
    今天帶把傘。這個星期天氣會漸漸好轉。
  2. The pay is much better. You should seriously think about the job offer.
    薪水好很多。你應該認真考慮那份工作邀約。
  3. The passengers clapped. The pilot had safely landed the plane.
    乘客們鼓掌了。機長已經把飛機安全降落了。
  4. Anna is busy that weekend. She has politely refused the invitation.
    Anna 那個週末很忙。她已經客氣地婉拒了邀請。

③ 動詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. Please drive carefully. The road is icy.
    請小心開車，路上結冰了。
  2. Don't worry about Grandma. She's resting comfortably in her room.
    別擔心奶奶。她在房間裡舒服地休息。
  3. Two hundred people were in the hall. The new head teacher smiled nervously before her speech.
    大廳裡有兩百個人。新校長演講前緊張地笑了笑。
  4. The play was excellent. The audience clapped loudly at the end.
    那齣戲很精彩。最後觀眾大聲鼓掌。
  5. Someone was knocking. The old dog walked slowly to the door.
    有人在敲門。老狗慢慢走到門邊。
  6. The clown fell over his own shoes. The kids laughed happily at him.
    小丑被自己的鞋子絆倒了。孩子們看著他開心地笑。

④ 動詞 + 受詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. I wasn't worried after the visit. The doctor explained the results clearly.
    看完醫生以後我就不擔心了。醫生把檢查結果解釋得很清楚。
  2. The machine is new. Read the instructions carefully before you start.
    這台機器是新的。開始之前，先仔細讀說明。
  3. Everyone likes working for Mr Brown. He treats his staff fairly.
    大家都喜歡替 Mr Brown 工作。他對待員工很公平。
  4. My mum never buys anything she doesn't need. She spends money frugally.
    我媽從來不買不需要的東西。她花錢很節省。
  5. The puppy was only a few weeks old. My sister held it gently in her arms.
    那隻小狗才幾個星期大。我姊姊把牠輕輕抱在懷裡。
  6. We told the waiter we had a train to catch. He served the soup quickly.
    我們跟服務生說要趕火車。他很快就把湯端上來了。

⑤ fast、hard、well、late

  1. Our new puppy already knows how to sit. He learns fast.
    我們新養的小狗已經會坐下了。牠學得很快。
  2. My dad had two jobs for thirty years. He worked hard all his life.
    我爸三十年都做兩份工作。他辛苦工作了一輩子。
  3. The patient was very angry. The new nurse handled the situation well.
    那個病人很生氣。新來的護士把狀況處理得很好。
  4. The party ended at three in the morning. I got up late on Sunday.
    派對到凌晨三點才結束。我星期天很晚才起床。

⑥ 否定句和疑問句

  1. I asked Tom about the missing money. He didn't answer the question honestly.
    我問 Tom 那筆不見的錢的事。他沒有老實回答那個問題。
  2. The back door keeps blowing open. Did you lock it properly?
    後門一直被風吹開。你有鎖好嗎？
  3. I told Lucy to take a coat, but she went out without one. She didn't take my advice seriously.
    我叫 Lucy 帶件外套，可是她沒穿外套就出門了。她沒有把我的勸告當一回事。
  4. The patient's heart was weak. Did he breathe normally after the operation?
    那個病人的心臟很弱。他手術後呼吸正常嗎？
