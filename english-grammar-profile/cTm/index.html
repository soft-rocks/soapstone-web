My smile slowly disappeared as I finished reading.

讀完的時候，我臉上的笑容慢慢消失了。

情⁠狀副⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

讀完的時候，我臉上的笑容慢慢消失了。

情⁠狀副⁠詞最⁠常放⁠在動⁠詞後⁠面，有受⁠詞就放⁠在受⁠詞後⁠面

My smile slowly disappeared as I finished reading.

動詞後面

You have to choose carefully.

你得小心選。
受詞後面

The doctor explained the results clearly.

醫生把檢查結果解釋得很清楚。

有助⁠動⁠詞⁠時，情⁠狀副⁠詞放⁠在助⁠動⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

My smile slowly disappeared as I finished reading.

天氣會漸漸好轉。

fast、hard、well、late 只放⁠在動⁠詞後⁠面

My brother well cooks.

My brother cooks well.

情⁠狀副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

My smile slowly disappeared as I finished reading.

He answered politely the question.

He answered the question politely.

修⁠飾動⁠詞用副⁠詞，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

She looked at me serious.

She looked at me seriously.

表⁠示努⁠力用 hard，hardly 是幾⁠乎不

He works hardly every day.

He works hard every day.

例句

① 情狀副詞 + 動詞

  1. The old man slowly opened the letter.
    老先生慢慢打開那封信。
  2. The noise gradually got louder.
    噪音漸漸變大了。
  3. She quietly closed the bedroom door.
    她輕輕地關上臥室的門。
  4. The lights suddenly went out.
    燈突然熄了。
  5. He carefully wrapped the glasses in newspaper.
    他用報紙小心地把玻璃杯包好。
  6. The children patiently waited for their turn.
    孩子們很有耐心地等著輪到自己。
  7. My grandfather proudly showed us his medal.
    我爺爺很驕傲地把他的獎章拿給我們看。
  8. The cat silently jumped onto the table.
    貓悄悄跳上了桌子。

② 助動詞 + 情狀副詞 + 動詞

  1. The weather will gradually improve this week.
    這個星期天氣會漸漸好轉。
  2. You should seriously think about the job offer.
    你應該認真考慮那份工作邀約。
  3. The pilot had safely landed the plane.
    機長已經把飛機安全降落了。
  4. She has politely refused the invitation.
    她已經客氣地婉拒了邀請。

③ 動詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. Please drive carefully. The road is icy.
    請小心開車，路上結冰了。
  2. Grandma is resting comfortably in her room.
    奶奶在房間裡舒服地休息。
  3. She smiled nervously before her speech.
    她演講前緊張地笑了笑。
  4. The audience clapped loudly at the end.
    最後觀眾大聲鼓掌。
  5. The old dog walked slowly to the door.
    老狗慢慢走到門邊。
  6. The kids laughed happily at the clown.
    孩子們看著小丑開心地笑。

④ 動詞 + 受詞 + 情狀副詞

  1. The doctor explained the results clearly.
    醫生把檢查結果解釋得很清楚。
  2. Read the instructions carefully before you start.
    開始之前，先仔細讀說明。
  3. He treats his staff fairly.
    他對待員工很公平。
  4. My mum spends money frugally.
    我媽花錢很節省。
  5. She held the puppy gently in her arms.
    她把小狗輕輕抱在懷裡。
  6. The waiter served the soup quickly.
    服務生很快就把湯端上來了。

⑤ fast、hard、well、late

  1. Our new puppy learns fast.
    我們新養的小狗學得很快。
  2. My dad worked hard all his life.
    我爸辛苦工作了一輩子。
  3. The new nurse handled the situation well.
    新來的護士把狀況處理得很好。
  4. I got up late on Sunday.
    我星期天很晚才起床。

⑥ 否定句和疑問句

  1. He didn't answer the question honestly.
    他沒有老實回答那個問題。
  2. Did you lock the back door properly?
    你後門有鎖好嗎？
  3. She didn't take my advice seriously.
    她沒有把我的勸告當一回事。
  4. Did the patient breathe normally after the operation?
    病人手術後呼吸正常嗎？
