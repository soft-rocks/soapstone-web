be to 說主⁠詞必⁠須做⁠的事

They are to have their passports with them.

他們必須隨身攜帶護照。

隨身帶護照是學校的規定。

轉⁠述規⁠定⁠時，主⁠詞常是第⁠三⁠人⁠稱

They are to have their passports with them.

所有訪客都必須在櫃檯登記。

主⁠詞用 you 時，be to 是直⁠接⁠的指⁠示

They are to have their passports with them.

你在這裡等我回來。

否⁠定⁠的 be not to 表⁠示不⁠准

They are to have their passports with them.

孩子們不准在河邊玩。

被⁠動 to be 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞說事⁠情必⁠須被怎⁠麼處⁠理

They are to have their passports with them.

這些表格必須在星期五前交回。

must 是說⁠話者定⁠的規⁠定，be to 是轉⁠述或正⁠式⁠的指⁠示

They are to have their passports with them.

must
說話者定的

You must be home by ten.

你十點前一定要到家。
be to
轉述、正式指示

You are to be home by ten.

你十點前要到家。

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

They are to have their passports with them.

You are to leaving your bags at the door.

You are to leave your bags at the door.

被⁠動是 to be 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

They are to have their passports with them.

All phones are to switched off.

All phones are to be switched off.

not 放⁠在 be 後⁠面、to 前⁠面

They are to have their passports with them.

Students are to not use phones in class.

Students are not to use phones in class.

例句

① 說規定和義務

  1. They are to have their passports with them at all times.
    他們必須隨時帶著護照。
  2. All new members are to pay the fee in advance.
    所有新會員都必須預先繳費。
  3. Each student is to bring a packed lunch.
    每個學生都要自己帶午餐。
  4. The players are to be at the stadium by six.
    球員們六點前必須到球場。
  5. Staff are to wear their name badges in the office.
    員工在辦公室必須戴名牌。
  6. Drivers are to stop at the red line.
    駕駛人必須在紅線停車。
  7. Every guest is to leave the room by eleven.
    每位房客都必須在十一點前退房。
  8. Candidates are to write their names on every page.
    考生必須在每一頁寫上名字。
  9. The new nurses are to report to the head nurse.
    新來的護理師要向護理長報到。

② 用 you 給指示

  1. You're to sit in front of the TV and watch the film.
    你坐在電視前面看這部電影。
  2. You're to go straight home after school.
    你放學後要直接回家。
  3. You are to finish your homework before dinner.
    你晚餐前要把功課寫完。
  4. You're to call me as soon as you land.
    你一落地就要打給我。
  5. You are to give this letter to the manager.
    你要把這封信交給經理。
  6. You're to stay in bed until the doctor comes.
    醫生來之前你都要躺在床上。
  7. You are to take one tablet three times a day.
    你一天要吃三次藥，一次一顆。
  8. You're to tell no one about this.
    這件事你不准告訴任何人。

③ 主詞 + am / is / are + not + to + 原形動詞

  1. Guests are not to smoke in the rooms.
    房客不准在房間裡抽菸。
  2. You are not to touch anything on my desk.
    我桌上的東西你一樣都不准碰。
  3. Students are not to leave the room during the test.
    考試時學生不准離開教室。
  4. Patients are not to eat after midnight.
    病人半夜之後不准進食。
  5. You're not to open the door to strangers.
    你不准幫陌生人開門。
  6. The staff are not to accept gifts from customers.
    員工不准收顧客的禮物。

④ 主詞 + am / is / are + to be + 過去分詞（被動）

  1. The library books are to be returned within two weeks.
    圖書館的書必須在兩週內歸還。
  2. Gorillas are to be respected like any other living thing.
    大猩猩應該和其他生物一樣受到尊重。
  3. The medicine is to be kept in the fridge.
    這個藥必須放在冰箱裡。
  4. All phones are to be switched off during the show.
    演出期間所有手機都必須關機。
  5. The doors are to be locked at ten every night.
    每天晚上十點都要鎖門。
  6. This dissertation is to be submitted by the end of May.
    這篇論文必須在五月底前繳交。
  7. Dogs are to be kept on a lead in the park.
    狗在公園裡必須繫上狗鍊。
  8. The test papers are to be handed in face down.
    考卷要蓋著交上來。
