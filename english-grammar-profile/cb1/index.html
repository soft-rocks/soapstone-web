already 強⁠調已⁠經做⁠完，常比預⁠期早

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

她已經在國際賽事拿過三面金牌了。

這位選手今年才十九歲。

already 放⁠在句⁠尾，常表⁠示驚⁠訝

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

我十分鐘前才把考卷給你。你已經寫完了？

好⁠幾件做⁠完⁠的⁠事，可⁠以只⁠在第⁠一件加 already

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

The party is ready to go. I've already booked the hall and sent the invitations.

派對都準備好了。我已經訂好場地，也寄出邀請函了。

already 不⁠放⁠在 won 和受⁠詞中⁠間

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

My cousin has won already three races this season.

My cousin has already won three races this season.

否⁠定⁠句用 yet，不⁠用 already

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

Dad hasn't already painted the garden fence.

Dad hasn't painted the garden fence yet.

有 last week，用過⁠去簡⁠單式

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

We have already bought the concert tickets last week.

We bought the concert tickets last week.

yet 用在問⁠句和否⁠定⁠句，放⁠在句⁠尾

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

比賽在星期六。你買票了沒？

問⁠句加 already，表⁠示覺⁠得對⁠方做⁠得比預⁠期早

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

旅行要到七月才去。你已經買好票了？

still 加否⁠定⁠句，說比預⁠期晚

She has already won three gold medals at international tournaments.

演唱會就在明天，我到現在還沒買票。

例句

① I / we / you / they + have already + 過去分詞

  1. Don't worry about Mum's birthday. I've already planned the party.
    媽媽的生日你別擔心。派對我已經計畫好了。
  2. You can throw out the old chair. We've already bought a new armchair.
    你可以把舊椅子丟掉。我們已經買了一張新的扶手椅。
  3. My neighbours got the keys on Friday. They've already moved into their new house.
    我的鄰居星期五拿到鑰匙。他們已經搬進新房子了。
  4. Not the fishing trip again, Grandpa. You've already told me that story.
    爺爺，又是釣魚那次啊。那個故事你已經跟我說過了。
  5. Put your coat on. I've already called a taxi.
    把外套穿上。我已經叫計程車了。
  6. The wedding is still four months away, and we've already spent half our budget.
    婚禮還有四個月，我們已經花掉一半的預算了。

② he / she / it + has already + 過去分詞

  1. Sorry, Dad isn't here. He's already left for work.
    抱歉，爸爸不在。他已經去上班了。
  2. My daughter loves the Harry Potter books. She has already read the whole series.
    我女兒很愛哈利波特。那整套書她已經看完了。
  3. We should go home now. It's already started to get dark.
    我們該回家了。天已經開始黑了。
  4. We don't need to sign him up for lessons. My son has already learned to swim.
    我們不用幫他報名游泳課。我兒子已經學會游泳了。
  5. He only started in May. The new manager has already changed a lot of things.
    他五月才來。新經理已經改了很多事情。

③ 比預期早

  1. It's only March, and we've already used up our holiday days.
    現在才三月，我們的假已經休完了。
  2. She's only twenty, but she has already written two novels.
    她才二十歲，就已經寫了兩本小說。
  3. It's only October, and the shop has already put up its Christmas decorations.
    現在才十月，這家店已經掛上聖誕節的裝飾了。
  4. She's only six months old, and our baby has already got four teeth.
    我們的寶寶才六個月大，就已經長了四顆牙。
  5. I've only been here a week, and I've already made lots of friends.
    我才來一個禮拜，就已經交到很多朋友了。

④ 不用再做了

  1. Put your wallet away. Don't worry, I've already paid.
    把錢包收起來。別擔心，我已經付過了。
  2. You don't need to cook. I've already ordered pizza.
    你不用煮了，我已經叫了披薩。
  3. We've already seen this film. Let's watch something else.
    這部電影我們已經看過了，看別的吧。
  4. Stop shouting at Ben. He's already apologized twice.
    別再對班大吼了。他已經道過兩次歉了。
  5. Check your phone for the party details. I've already sent you the address.
    看一下手機上派對的資料。地址我已經傳給你了。

⑤ 好幾件做完的事

  1. Sit down and relax. I've already cleaned the kitchen and done the shopping.
    坐下來休息吧。我已經打掃好廚房，也買完東西了。
  2. You've just missed your friend. She has already packed her bags and checked out.
    你剛好錯過你朋友。她已經收好行李，也退房了。
  3. You can stay in bed. We've already fed the dog and taken him for a walk.
    你可以繼續睡。我們已經餵過狗，也帶牠去散步了。

⑥ already 放在句尾：驚訝

  1. I made that cake an hour ago. Have you eaten it all already?
    那個蛋糕我一個小時前才做好。你已經全部吃完了？
  2. I can hear the car outside. Have the guests arrived already?
    我聽到外面有車子的聲音。客人已經到了？
  3. Where's Tom? It's only eight. He's gone home already?
    湯姆呢？才八點。他已經回家了？
  4. I gave it to you yesterday. You've finished the book already?
    我昨天才給你的。你已經把書看完了？

⑦ 問句：Have you already ...?

  1. The flight isn't due until six. Has the plane already landed?
    班機要六點才到。飛機已經降落了？
  2. The deadline is next Friday. Has your sister already handed in her essay?
    截止日是下個星期五。你姊姊已經交報告了？
