例句

① 天氣

  1. We're having a great time at the beach. It's warm and windy.
    我們在海邊玩得很開心。天氣很暖和，風也很大。
  2. Take your coat. It's very cold outside.
    帶外套。外面很冷。
  3. It was sunny all week, so we went swimming every day.
    整個星期都是晴天，所以我們每天都去游泳。
  4. It's snowing! Let's build a snowman.
    下雪了！我們來堆雪人吧。
  5. It will be hot tomorrow, so drink lots of water.
    明天會很熱，所以要多喝水。

② 時間

  1. I'm waiting for you at the station. Now it's 3 pm.
    我在車站等你。現在三點了。
  2. It's late. I'm going to bed.
    很晚了。我要去睡了。
  3. What time is it? Is it six o'clock yet?
    現在幾點？六點了嗎？
  4. The party was great, but it was two in the morning before I got to bed.
    派對很棒，可是我上床的時候已經凌晨兩點了。

③ 日期和日子

  1. It's our wedding anniversary next week. Let's book a table somewhere nice.
    下星期是我們的結婚紀念日。我們找個好地方訂位吧。
  2. It's Friday today. I love Fridays!
    今天星期五。我好愛星期五！
  3. It was Christmas, so all the shops were closed.
    那天是聖誕節，所以店都關了。

④ 距離

  1. It's about ten minutes to the station from here.
    從這裡到車站大概十分鐘。
  2. It's a long way to my grandmother's house.
    到我奶奶家要走很遠。
  3. Is it far to the city centre?
    到市中心遠嗎？

⑤ 明信片和信

  1. Hi Anna! It's lovely here. It's sunny every day.
    嗨，Anna！這裡好美。每天都是晴天。
  2. It rained a lot last week, but it's dry now.
    上星期下了很多雨，可是現在乾了。
  3. It's eight o'clock in the evening, and I'm writing this on the balcony.
    現在晚上八點，我在陽台寫這封信。

⑥ 對話

  1. "What's the weather like?" "It's cloudy, but it isn't cold."
    天氣怎麼樣？陰天，可是不冷。
  2. "What day is it today?" "It's Tuesday."
    今天星期幾？星期二。
  3. "Is it raining?" "No, it's stopped."
    在下雨嗎？沒有，停了。
