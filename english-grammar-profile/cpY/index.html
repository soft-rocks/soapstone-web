We want to present ourselves in the best way possible.

我們想用最好的方式呈現自己。

possible 和 ever 放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面，加⁠強最⁠高⁠級

We want to present ourselves in the best way possible.

名詞 + possible

We'll finish in the shortest time possible.

我們會用最短的時間完成。
名詞 + ever

It was the hottest summer ever.

那是有史以來最熱的夏天。
名詞 + by far

In our chess club, Lucy is the strongest player by far.

在我們的西洋棋社，Lucy 是最強的棋手，其他人差遠了。

possible 可⁠以放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面或後⁠面

We want to present ourselves in the best way possible.

ever 放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面

We want to present ourselves in the best way possible.

We had the ever biggest crowd at the stadium.

We had the biggest crowd ever at the stadium.

到目⁠前⁠為⁠止用 ever，不⁠用 never

We want to present ourselves in the best way possible.

My grandma bakes the best cookies never.

My grandma bakes the best cookies ever.

possible 前⁠面要⁠用最⁠高⁠級

We want to present ourselves in the best way possible.

Call me at the early time possible.

Call me at the earliest time possible.

例句

① the + 最高級 + 名詞 + possible

  1. The art students are planning their end-of-year show. They want to show their work in the best way possible.
    美術系的學生在準備期末展。他們想用最好的方式展示他們的作品。
  2. This cream heals small cuts in the shortest time possible.
    這款藥膏能在最短時間內讓小傷口癒合。
  3. The concert usually sells out in a week. Book your tickets at the earliest date possible.
    這場音樂會通常一個星期就賣完。請盡早訂票。
  4. The builder looked at our kitchen. He said, "We'll do it at the lowest cost possible."
    建築工人看了我們的廚房。他說：我們會用最低的成本完成。
  5. The band put posters all over town. They want the biggest audience possible.
    樂團在全鎮貼滿了海報。他們想要越多觀眾越好。
  6. The children are only six. Please answer in the simplest words possible.
    孩子們才六歲。請用最簡單的字回答。

② the + 最高級 + 名詞 + ever

  1. The summer of 2023 was the wettest summer ever here.
    2023 年的夏天是這裡有史以來最多雨的夏天。
  2. Dad says it's the tastiest pizza ever!
    爸爸說那是有史以來最好吃的披薩！
  3. We've moved two kilometres in an hour. This is the worst traffic ever.
    我們一個小時才前進兩公里。這是有史以來最糟的交通。
  4. We talked for five hours. It was the longest phone call ever.
    我們講了五個小時，那是有史以來最長的一通電話。
  5. My daughter gave her teacher a card on the last day. It said, "You're the best teacher ever!"
    我女兒最後一天給老師一張卡片。上面寫：你是全世界最好的老師！
  6. Everything is seventy per cent off. It's the biggest sale ever at this shop.
    所有東西都打三折。這是這家店有史以來最大的特賣。

③ 所有格 + 最高級 + 名詞 + ever

  1. Many fans say it was her greatest show ever.
    很多粉絲說那是她有史以來最精彩的演出。
  2. A hotel wants ten thousand towels from our factory. This is our biggest order ever.
    一家飯店向我們工廠訂了一萬條毛巾。這是我們有史以來最大的一筆訂單。
  3. I lost my phone and missed my train. Yesterday was my worst day ever.
    我弄丟了手機，又錯過了火車。昨天是我最倒楣的一天。
  4. Our town's football club won twenty games last year. It was the team's best season ever.
    我們鎮上的足球隊去年贏了二十場。那是這支球隊有史以來最好的一季。
  5. My brother swam across the lake last Sunday. That was his longest swim ever.
    我哥哥上星期天游過了那座湖。那是他游過最長的一次。

④ the most + 形容詞 + 名詞 + possible / ever

  1. The ambulance drivers had no time to lose. They took the most direct road possible to the hospital.
    救護車司機沒有時間可以浪費。他們走了最直接的路去醫院。
  2. The final ended five to four after extra time. It was the most exciting game ever.
    決賽延長賽後以五比四結束。那是有史以來最刺激的比賽。
  3. We drive across the desert every summer. We need the most reliable car possible.
    我們每年夏天都開車穿越沙漠。我們需要最可靠的車。
  4. The kids said the museum tour was the most boring trip ever.
    孩子們說那次博物館導覽是有史以來最無聊的出遊。
  5. This letter has my bank details in it. Please send it in the most secure way possible.
    這封信裡有我的銀行資料。請用最安全的方式寄出。

⑤ the + 最高級 + 名詞 + by far 和不同的句型

  1. In our chess club, Lucy is the strongest player by far.
    在我們的西洋棋社，Lucy 是最強的棋手，其他人差遠了。
  2. I looked at ten hotels near the station. The Green Inn is the cheapest hotel by far.
    我看了車站附近十家飯店。綠旅館絕對是最便宜的。
  3. It can go at four hundred kilometres an hour. Is this the fastest car ever made?
    它時速可以到四百公里。這是有史以來最快的車嗎？
  4. The cat opened the fridge by itself? That's the strangest thing ever.
    貓自己打開冰箱？那是最奇怪的事。
  5. My son hates waiting at airports. We'll get there at the latest time possible.
    我兒子討厭在機場等。我們會盡量晚一點到。
  6. Hundreds of trees fell down last night. Was that the biggest storm ever in this area?
    昨天晚上倒了好幾百棵樹。那是這一帶有史以來最大的暴風雨嗎？
  7. Her skin gets red very easily. Give the baby the softest blanket possible.
    她的皮膚很容易發紅。給寶寶最柔軟的毯子。
  8. With the kids away, we had the quietest Christmas ever.
    孩子們不在，我們過了有史以來最安靜的聖誕節。
