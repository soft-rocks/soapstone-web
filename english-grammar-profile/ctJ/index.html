Larry decided to tell his mother the truth, but then she showed him his suitcase.

Larry 決定把實話告訴他媽媽，可是接著她把他的行李箱拿給他看。

連⁠接⁠詞說⁠明子⁠句之⁠間⁠的關⁠係

Larry decided to tell his mother the truth, but then she showed him his suitcase.

公車沒來，所以我們用走的，接著就開始下雨了。

but 後⁠面是出⁠乎⁠意⁠料⁠的事

Larry decided to tell his mother the truth, but then she showed him his suitcase.

I lost my keys, so I called my sister, but she was at work.

我弄丟了鑰匙，所以打給我姊姊，可是她在上班。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句之⁠間要⁠用連⁠接⁠詞

Larry decided to tell his mother the truth, but then she showed him his suitcase.

We missed the train, we took a taxi, it was expensive.

We missed the train, so we took a taxi, but it was expensive.

so 前⁠面不⁠再⁠加 because

Larry decided to tell his mother the truth, but then she showed him his suitcase.

Because the café was full, so we sat outside.

The café was full, so we sat outside.

then 連⁠接子⁠句⁠時，前⁠面加 and 或 but

Larry decided to tell his mother the truth, but then she showed him his suitcase.

She opened the box then she screamed.

She opened the box, and then she screamed.

例句

① and / and then

  1. We checked into the hotel, and then we had a swim.
    我們辦好飯店入住，接著去游了泳。
  2. I was alone in the house that night. The phone rang, and then the lights went out.
    那天晚上我一個人在家。電話響了，接著燈就熄了。
  3. He finished his work, turned off the lights, and then he went home.
    他做完工作，關了燈，接著回家了。
  4. My uncle stood up to give a speech at the wedding. He knocked over his glass, and everyone started to laugh.
    我叔叔在婚禮上站起來致詞。他打翻了他的杯子，大家都笑了起來。
  5. My friends came over on Friday. We had dinner, and then we watched a film.
    我的朋友們星期五來我家。我們吃了晚餐，接著看了一部電影。

② so

  1. The lift was broken, so we took the stairs, and then we were out of breath.
    電梯壞了，所以我們走樓梯，結果喘不過氣來。
  2. Monday was a bad day. My alarm didn't go off, so I missed my first class.
    星期一很倒楣。我的鬧鐘沒響，所以我錯過了第一堂課。
  3. The keys were still missing, so Dad decided to call a locksmith.
    鑰匙還是找不到，所以爸爸決定叫鎖匠。
  4. I was hungry, so I made a sandwich, and then I felt better.
    我餓了，所以做了一個三明治，吃完就覺得好多了。
  5. It was the hottest day of the summer. The pool was full, so the kids played in the park instead.
    那是夏天最熱的一天。游泳池人滿了，所以孩子們改去公園玩。

③ but / but then

  1. The printer broke, so I wrote it by hand, but nobody could read it.
    印表機壞了，所以我用手寫，可是沒人看得懂。
  2. It was snowing on the night of my birthday. I made dinner for six, but only two friends came.
    我生日那天晚上在下雪。我做了六人份的晚餐，可是只來了兩個朋友。
  3. My brother woke up and ran to the bathroom. He thought he was late, but then he saw the clock. It was only five.
    我哥哥醒來就衝進浴室。他以為自己遲到了，可是接著他看到了時鐘。才五點而已。
  4. I tried on a blue dress in the shop. I wanted to buy the dress, but then I saw the price.
    我在店裡試穿了一件藍色洋裝。我想買那件洋裝，可是接著我看到了價錢。
  5. We scored in the first five minutes. The game started well, but then it began to snow.
    我們在前五分鐘就進球了。比賽開始時很順利，可是接著開始下雪了。

④ or

  1. You look tired, but it's your choice. Go to the concert, or stay home and rest.
    你看起來很累，不過你自己決定。去聽演唱會，或是待在家休息。
  2. The next bus is in forty minutes. We can wait here, or we can take a taxi.
    下一班公車還要四十分鐘。我們可以在這裡等，或是搭計程車。
  3. I need your answer about the trip. Call me tonight, or send me a message tomorrow.
    我需要你回覆旅行的事。今晚打給我，或是明天傳訊息給我。
  4. I don't know that word either. You could ask your teacher, or you could look it up online.
    我也不知道那個字。你可以問老師，或是上網查。

⑤ 長一點的敘述

  1. My phone died, so I couldn't call anyone, and then I got lost.
    我的手機沒電了，所以沒辦法打給任何人，接著我就迷路了。
  2. We got to the airport early, but the flight was late, so we had lunch.
    我們很早到機場，可是班機誤點，所以我們吃了午餐。
  3. It started to rain, so we ran into a café, and there we met an old friend.
    開始下雨了，所以我們跑進一家咖啡店，在那裡遇到一個老朋友。
  4. Ann made a cake, but she forgot the sugar, so nobody ate it.
    Ann 做了蛋糕，可是忘了加糖，所以沒人吃。
  5. I studied all night, but I was so tired that I fell asleep in the exam.
    我熬夜讀書，可是太累了，考試時睡著了。
  6. The car broke down, so we called for help, and then we waited for two hours.
    車子拋錨了，所以我們打電話求救，接著等了兩個小時。
  7. The postman had a parcel for us. He knocked on the door, but nobody answered, so he left a note.
    郵差有一個我們的包裹。他敲了門，可是沒人應，所以他留了一張紙條。
  8. We got lost, so we asked a farmer, but he didn't speak English.
    我們迷路了，所以問了一個農夫，可是他不會說英文。
  9. Someone left the gate open. The dog ran into the garden, and then it dug a big hole.
    有人沒關大門。那隻狗跑進花園，接著挖了一個大洞。
  10. My sister wanted to cancel lunch. She called her friend, but the line was busy, so she sent a text.
    我姊姊想取消午餐。她打給朋友，可是電話忙線，所以她傳了簡訊。
  11. We found the hotel, but our room wasn't ready, so we went for a walk.
    我們找到了飯店，可是房間還沒準備好，所以我們去散步。
