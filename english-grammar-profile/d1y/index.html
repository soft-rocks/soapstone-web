You might not have been able to change the world, but maybe you have made a difference to one or two people.

總的來說，這次也許不算大成功，但大家都玩得很開心。

主⁠詞 + might + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞, but + 句⁠子

might承認一個缺點或別人的看法。
but帶出安慰或理由。

安⁠慰

You might not have been able to change the world, but maybe you have made a difference to one or two people.

例句

It might not have been a huge success, but people enjoyed themselves.

這可能不算非常成功，可是大家都玩得很開心。

說過去的事，常用 might not have 加過去分詞，再用 but 說好的一面。

為自⁠己⁠的看⁠法辯⁠護

例句

This might sound strange, but it's true.

這可能聽起來很奇怪，可是這是真的。

議論文常先用 might sound 或 might seem 承認看法聽起來有問題，再說明理由。

You might not have won, but you did well.

例句

① 主詞 + might not have + 過去分詞, but + 句子：安慰別人

  1. You might not have saved the old cinema, but you made people care about it.
    你可能沒救回那家老電影院，可是你讓大家開始關心它。
  2. You might not have got the job, but the interview was good practice.
    你可能沒拿到那份工作，可是那次面試是很好的練習。
  3. Your team might not have won the cup, but they played brilliantly.
    你們隊可能沒贏得獎盃，可是他們踢得很精彩。
  4. The cake might not have looked perfect, but it tasted wonderful.
    蛋糕可能看起來不完美，可是很好吃。
  5. You might not have passed this time, but you're much closer.
    你這次可能沒考過，可是你已經很接近了。
  6. Your painting might not have won a prize, but everyone stopped to look at it.
    你的畫可能沒得獎，可是每個人都停下來看。

② It might not have been + 名詞或形容詞, but + 句子：總結活動

  1. All in all, it might not have been a huge success, but people enjoyed themselves.
    總之，這可能不算非常成功，可是大家都玩得很開心。
  2. It might not have been the best holiday, but we laughed a lot.
    那可能不是最好的假期，可是我們笑得很開心。
  3. It might not have been a big crowd, but they were very enthusiastic.
    觀眾可能不多，可是他們非常熱情。
  4. It might not have been an easy year, but we learned a lot.
    這可能不是輕鬆的一年，可是我們學到很多。
  5. It might not have been the result we wanted, but we did our best.
    這可能不是我們想要的結果，可是我們盡力了。
  6. It might not have been a five-star hotel, but it was clean and friendly.
    那可能不是五星級飯店，可是很乾淨，人也很友善。

③ 主詞 + might sound / seem + 形容詞, but + 句子：為看法辯護

  1. The idea that fashion shows people's attitudes might sound superficial, but on closer examination it turns out to be true.
    時尚反映人們態度的說法可能聽起來很膚淺，可是仔細看就會發現是真的。
  2. Learning Latin might seem useless, but it helps you understand many English words.
    學拉丁文可能看起來沒用，可是它幫助你了解很多英文字。
  3. A four-day week might sound strange, but many companies find it works.
    一星期工作四天可能聽起來很奇怪，可是很多公司發現它行得通。
  4. My advice might seem hard, but it's for your own good.
    我的建議可能看起來很嚴厲，可是是為了你好。
  5. Getting up at five might sound crazy, but it gives me time to think.
    五點起床可能聽起來很瘋狂，可是這讓我有時間思考。
  6. The rule might seem unfair, but it keeps everyone safe.
    這條規定可能看起來不公平，可是它保護每個人的安全。

④ 主詞 + might be + 形容詞, but + 句子：承認缺點

  1. Going to the gym to keep fit might be boring sometimes, but most young people enjoy some kind of sport.
    去健身房保持身材有時候可能很無聊，可是大部分年輕人都喜歡某種運動。
  2. The work might be hard, but the pay is good.
    這份工作可能很辛苦，可是薪水很好。
  3. The flat might be small, but it's close to everything.
    這間公寓可能很小，可是離什麼都很近。
  4. The journey might be long, but the views are amazing.
    這趟路程可能很長，可是風景很美。
  5. My grandfather might be slow, but he never forgets anything.
    我爺爺可能動作很慢，可是他什麼都不會忘。
  6. The course might be expensive, but it will help your career.
    這門課可能很貴，可是它對你的事業有幫助。

⑤ I might have + 過去分詞, but + 句子：為自己做的事辯護

  1. I might have been late, but I did finish the work.
    我可能遲到了，可是我確實把工作做完了。
  2. I might have spent too much, but it was a special day.
    我可能花太多錢了，可是那天是特別的日子。
  3. I might have said the wrong thing, but I was trying to help.
    我可能說錯話了，可是我是想幫忙。
  4. We might have left early, but we had a long drive home.
    我們可能太早走了，可是我們要開很久的車回家。
  5. I might have made mistakes, but I learned from them.
    我可能犯過錯，可是我從中學到了東西。
  6. She might have shouted, but she was very worried about you.
    她可能大吼了，可是她非常擔心你。
  7. He might have forgotten your birthday, but he's planning a big surprise.
    他可能忘了你的生日，可是他正在計畫一個大驚喜。
