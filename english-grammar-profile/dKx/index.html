This is the reason why we are changing our working hours.

這就是我們要調整工作時間的原因。

the reason why 加子⁠句，說⁠明是什⁠麼事⁠的原⁠因

這就是我們要調整工作時間的原因。

The reason why 放⁠在句⁠首，is 後⁠面說⁠出原⁠因

This is the reason why we are changing our working hours.

that 子句

The reason why I'm late is that my train broke down.

我遲到是因為我的火車故障了。
名詞片語

The reason why I moved was the noise.

先說一⁠件⁠事，再用 That's the reason why 說結⁠果

This is the reason why we are changing our working hours.

結果

The roads are icy. That's the reason why the school is closed.

路上結冰了，所以學校停課。

why 可⁠以省⁠略，也⁠可⁠以換⁠成 that

This is the reason why we are changing our working hours.

省略

The reason I called is to invite you to dinner.

我打電話來是要邀你吃晚餐。

The reason why 子⁠句已⁠經是主⁠詞，不⁠再⁠加 it

The reason why I was late it was the traffic.

The reason why I was late was the traffic.

why 後⁠面用一⁠般⁠的順⁠序，不⁠加 did

This is the reason why we are changing our working hours.

That's the reason why did he quit.

That's the reason why he quit.

reason 後⁠面直⁠接接 why，不⁠加 for

This is the reason why we are changing our working hours.

This is the reason for why I came.

This is the reason why I came.

例句

① the reason why + 子句

  1. Nobody told us the reason why the meeting was cancelled.
    沒人告訴我們會議為什麼取消。
  2. Do you know the reason why the shop closed early today?
    你知道那家店今天為什麼提早關門嗎？
  3. I finally understood the reason why he was so quiet.
    我終於懂他為什麼那麼安靜了。
  4. The doctor explained the reason why I was feeling so tired.
    醫生解釋了我為什麼一直覺得很累。
  5. Can you specify the reason why you want to change your flight?
    你可以說明你為什麼要改班機嗎？
  6. There's no reason why we can't finish today.
    我們沒有理由今天做不完。

② The reason why ... is that ...（強調原因）

  1. The reason why I love this café is that it's never crowded.
    我喜歡這家咖啡店是因為這裡從來都不擠。
  2. The reason why she moved to Hualien is that she wanted a quieter life.
    她搬到花蓮是因為她想過比較安靜的生活。
  3. The reason why we're selling the car is that we hardly ever drive.
    我們要賣車是因為我們幾乎不開車。
  4. The reason why I didn't call you was that my phone was dead.
    我沒打給你是因為我手機沒電了。
  5. The reason why he gets up so early is his long commute.
    他那麼早起是因為他通勤時間很長。
  6. The reason why the film is so popular is the music.
    那部電影那麼受歡迎是因為它的音樂。

③ the main / only / one reason why

  1. The main reason why I chose this job was the people.
    我選這份工作的主要原因是這裡的人。
  2. One reason why I like living here is the food.
    我喜歡住這裡的其中一個原因是這裡的食物。
  3. The only reason why I went to the party was to see you.
    我去那個派對唯一的原因是要見你。
  4. Money isn't the only reason why people change jobs.
    錢不是人們換工作唯一的原因。
  5. That's one of the reasons why I never take taxis.
    這是我從來不搭計程車的原因之一。

④ This is / That's the reason why

  1. It rained all week. That's the reason why the grass is so green.
    下了一整個禮拜的雨，所以草才這麼綠。
  2. He's very thrifty. That's the reason why he never eats out.
    他很節儉，所以他從來不在外面吃。
  3. This is the reason why we need more volunteers.
    這就是我們需要更多志工的原因。
  4. Traffic was terrible. That's the reason why we missed the start.
    交通很糟，所以我們錯過了開頭。
  5. The heater broke last night. That's the reason why it's so cold in here.
    暖氣昨晚壞了，所以這裡才這麼冷。

⑤ 省略 why 或換成 that

  1. The reason I'm texting is to remind you about the dentist.
    我傳訊息是要提醒你看牙醫的事。
  2. Nobody knows the reason she left the band.
    沒人知道她為什麼離開樂團。
  3. Is that the reason you didn't come yesterday?
    那就是你昨天沒來的原因嗎？
  4. The reason that I'm learning Korean is my love of K-dramas.
    我學韓文是因為我很愛韓劇。
  5. The reason we chose this hotel is that it has a pool.
    我們選這家飯店是因為這裡有游泳池。
  6. Tell me the real reason you're leaving.
    告訴我你要走的真正原因。
  7. The reason that the bus is late is the snow on the mountain road.
    公車誤點是因為山路上有積雪。
  8. That isn't the reason I'm angry.
    我生氣不是因為這個。
