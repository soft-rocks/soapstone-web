We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

我們學生委員會很不希望看到那麼大的空間白白浪費掉。

would hate 後⁠面⁠接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

我們學生委員會很不希望看到那麼大的空間白白浪費掉。

主詞用 would hate to 強調自己很不希望某件事發生。

口⁠語常把 would 縮⁠寫成 'd

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

我很不想讓你等。

would 表示假設的情況。

to 後⁠面可⁠以是原⁠形⁠動⁠詞、被⁠動或 see + 受⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

原形動詞

They would hate to give up the flat.

他們很不想放棄那間公寓。
被動

She would hate to be told what to do.

她很不希望被人指揮。
see + 受詞 + 原形動詞

She would hate to see the old bakery close.

她很不希望看到那家老麵包店關門。

not to 否⁠定⁠的⁠是後⁠面⁠的動⁠作，不⁠是 hate

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

要是沒能當面向他們道謝，我會很過意不去。

主詞想當面道謝。

would hate 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時，動⁠詞前⁠面加 to

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

We would hate see the space go to waste.

We would hate to see the space go to waste.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

I would hate to missing the last bus.

I would hate to miss the last bus.

這個 to 是不定詞的 to，不是介系詞。

would 後⁠面⁠的 hate 用原⁠形

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

She would hates to let them down.

She would hate to let them down.

被⁠動是 to be 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

He would hate to be mistake for a liar.

He would hate to be mistaken for a liar.

沒⁠有 would，說⁠的就⁠不⁠是假⁠設⁠的情⁠況

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

would hate to
假設的情況

I'd hate to keep you waiting.

我很不想讓你等。
hate to
真的要做

I hate to leave so early, but I have to catch a train.

我也不想這麼早走，可是我得趕火車。
hate + 動詞-ing
平常的感受

I hate working late on Fridays.

我很討厭星期五加班。

I hate to say this, but 是固⁠定說⁠法

我也不想這麼說，可是你錯了。

would love to 的句⁠型相⁠同，意⁠思相⁠反

We in the student committee would hate to see all that space go to waste.

例句

① would hate to + 原形動詞

  1. I'd hate to wake the baby.
    我很不想吵醒寶寶。
  2. She would hate to miss your wedding.
    她很不想錯過你的婚禮。
  3. We would hate to lose you.
    我們很不想失去你。
  4. I'd hate to be late on my first day.
    我很不想第一天就遲到。
  5. They would hate to move again so soon.
    他們很不想這麼快又搬家。
  6. He would hate to let the team down.
    他很不想讓球隊失望。
  7. You'd hate to get lost in this city at night.
    你一定不想晚上在這座城市迷路。
  8. I would hate to think what this will cost.
    這要花多少錢，我真不敢想。
  9. Wouldn't you hate to hear about it from someone else?
    要是從別人那裡聽到這件事，你不會很不舒服嗎？
  10. My dad would hate to sell the old car.
    我爸很不想賣掉那台舊車。
  11. I'd hate to give you the wrong idea.
    我很不想讓你誤會。
  12. She told me she would hate to work on weekends.
    她跟我說她很不想週末上班。
  13. We'd hate to waste all this food.
    我們很不想浪費這些食物。
  14. The teacher would hate to punish the whole class.
    老師很不想處罰全班。
  15. I'd hate to say no to my best friend.
    我很不想拒絕我最好的朋友。
  16. He'd hate to leave without saying goodbye.
    他很不想沒道別就離開。
  17. Ann would hate to cook for twenty people.
    Ann 很不想幫二十個人做飯。
  18. I'd hate to forget her birthday again.
    我很不想又忘了她的生日。
  19. They'd hate to stand in the rain for two hours.
    他們很不想在雨中站兩個小時。

② would hate to be + 過去分詞（被動）

  1. He would hate to be seen in that hat.
    他很不想被人看到他戴那頂帽子。
  2. She'd hate to be left out.
    她很不想被冷落。
  3. She would hate to be called a quitter.
    她很不想被人說是半途而廢的人。
  4. They would hate to be treated like children.
    他們很不想被當成小孩子看待。

③ would hate to see + 受詞 + 原形動詞

  1. We would hate to see the park close.
    我們很不希望看到公園關閉。
  2. I'd hate to see you get hurt.
    我很不希望看到你受傷。
  3. The fans would hate to see their team lose at home.
    球迷很不希望看到自己的球隊在主場輸球。
  4. My grandma would hate to see the old house fall down.
    我奶奶很不希望看到那間老房子倒掉。

④ would hate not to + 原形動詞

  1. I'd hate not to say goodbye to her.
    要是沒跟她道別，我會很難過。
  2. I'd hate not to have a chance to thank him.
    要是沒機會謝謝他，我會很遺憾。
  3. We'd hate not to see you before you leave.
    你走之前要是沒見到你，我們會很遺憾。
  4. He would hate not to finish what he started.
    他要是沒把開始做的事做完，會很不甘心。
