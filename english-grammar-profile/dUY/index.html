The main character in the program is a journalist who is visiting the town.

這個節目的主角是一個到鎮上來的記者。

兩⁠個以⁠上⁠的形⁠容⁠詞照順⁠序排

The main character in the program is a journalist who is visiting the town.

我們買了一張漂亮的舊木桌。

順序：評價、大小、年紀、顏色、來源、材料。

長⁠短放⁠在顏⁠色前⁠面

The main character in the program is a journalist who is visiting the town.

我姊姊有一頭漂亮的深色長髮。

兩⁠個形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間可⁠以加 and

The main character in the program is a journalist who is visiting the town.

Let's watch this one tonight. It's an amusing and exciting action film.

我們今天晚上看這部吧。那是一部好笑又刺激的動作片。

單⁠獨⁠的形⁠容⁠詞放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

The main character in the program is a journalist who is visiting the town.

We ate food delicious at the market.

We ate delicious food at the market.

顏⁠色放⁠在材⁠料前⁠面

The main character in the program is a journalist who is visiting the town.

My aunt wears a cotton white shirt to work.

My aunt wears a white cotton shirt to work.

a 或 an 看緊⁠接在⁠後⁠面⁠的字

It was a amazing trip.

It was an amazing trip.

例句

① 一個形容詞 + 名詞

  1. We had dinner at an expensive hotel near the airport.
    我們在機場附近一家很貴的飯店吃晚餐。
  2. I watched a new TV series last night. The main character is a young journalist.
    我昨天晚上看了一部新的電視影集。主角是一個年輕的記者。
  3. Your boss and your best friend both need you on Saturday. You're in a complicated situation.
    你老闆和你最好的朋友星期六都需要你。你的處境很複雜。
  4. We tried the local food at the night market.
    我們在夜市吃了當地的食物。
  5. He told us an amusing story about his first job.
    他跟我們講了一個他第一份工作的趣事。
  6. My grandmother wears traditional clothes at New Year.
    我奶奶過年會穿傳統服裝。
  7. My uncle runs a small bakery in Tainan.
    我叔叔在台南開一家小麵包店。

② 形容詞 + 複數 / 不可數名詞

  1. This market sells delicious fresh fruit.
    這個市場賣好吃的新鮮水果。
  2. Busy people often skip breakfast.
    忙碌的人常常不吃早餐。
  3. It's going to snow in the mountains. We need warm clothes for the trip.
    山上會下雪。這趟旅行我們需要保暖的衣服。
  4. The town has narrow streets and old houses.
    這個小鎮有窄窄的街道和老房子。
  5. My kids won't eat green vegetables. They always leave the peas on the plate.
    我的孩子不吃綠色蔬菜。他們總是把豌豆留在盤子裡。
  6. The hotel has friendly staff and clean rooms.
    那家飯店的員工很友善，房間也很乾淨。

③ 兩個以上的形容詞

  1. My new teacher has curly red hair.
    我的新老師有一頭紅色的捲髮。
  2. The police asked me about the two robbers. The second man had short curly fair hair.
    警察問我那兩個搶匪的樣子。第二個男人有一頭金色的短捲髮。
  3. We found a lovely old brick cottage near the lake.
    我們在湖邊找到一間可愛的舊磚造小屋。
  4. My aunt gave me a pretty blue silk scarf.
    我阿姨送我一條漂亮的藍色絲巾。
  5. I'd like a nice hot bath after this long walk.
    走了這麼長的路，我想泡個舒服的熱水澡。
  6. They live in a lovely little house by the sea.
    他們住在海邊一間可愛的小房子裡。

④ 形容詞 + and + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. Let's watch this one tonight. It's an amusing and exciting action film.
    我們今天晚上看這部吧。那是一部好笑又刺激的動作片。
  2. My son loves his piano lessons with Ms Lin. She's a kind and patient teacher.
    我兒子很喜歡跟林老師上鋼琴課。她是個和善又有耐心的老師。
  3. We drove for ten hours yesterday. We had a long and tiring day.
    我們昨天開了十個小時的車。我們度過了漫長又累人的一天。
  4. Book a room at the Lake View. It's a quiet and comfortable hotel.
    去湖景飯店訂一間房。那是一家安靜又舒服的飯店。
  5. I asked my manager about the meeting time. He wrote back a short and simple email.
    我問經理開會的時間。他回了一封簡短又簡單的電子郵件。

⑤ the / my / this + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. We all like the big flat by the park. The main problem is the cost.
    我們都喜歡公園旁邊那間大公寓。最主要的問題是費用。
  2. My favorite singer is coming to Taipei.
    我最喜歡的歌手要來台北了。
  3. This old photo shows my parents at school.
    這張舊照片是我爸媽在學校的樣子。
  4. Start studying this weekend. The final exam is next Friday.
    這個週末就開始讀書。期末考在下個禮拜五。
  5. Have you seen the new Italian restaurant on Park Street? There's a queue outside every night.
    你看過公園街那家新開的義大利餐廳嗎？每天晚上外面都在排隊。
  6. Our next-door neighbor is a famous writer.
    我們隔壁的鄰居是一位有名的作家。
