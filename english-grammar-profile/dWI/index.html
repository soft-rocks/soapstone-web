He was my confidant and I was his.

他是我的知己，我也是他的。

his 後⁠面不⁠接名⁠詞，就⁠是所⁠有⁠格代⁠名⁠詞

他是我的知己，我也是他的。

his 可⁠以當主⁠詞、受⁠詞，也⁠可⁠以放⁠在 be 和介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

He was my confidant and I was his.

主詞

Mine was a quiet childhood, but his was very difficult.

我的童年很平靜，可是他的很辛苦。
受詞

My bike is broken, so I'm using his.

我的腳踏車壞了，所以我在用他的。
be 後面The black jacket is his.
介系詞後面My office is next to his.

his 常和 mine、hers 放⁠在同⁠一⁠句對⁠照

He was my confidant and I was his.

對照

She kept her promise, and he kept his.

她遵守了她的承諾，他也遵守了他的。

his 當所⁠有⁠格代⁠名⁠詞⁠時不⁠再⁠加 's

He was my confidant and I was his.

Those shoes are his's.

Those shoes are his.

he's 是 he is，所⁠有⁠格代⁠名⁠詞是 his

He was my confidant and I was his.

The red pen is he's.

The red pen is his.

比⁠較⁠的是手⁠機，用 his，不⁠用 him

He was my confidant and I was his.

My phone is newer than him.

My phone is newer than his.

例句

① his 當主詞

  1. My idea was too expensive, but his cost almost nothing.
    我的點子太花錢了，可是他的幾乎不用錢。
  2. Our cars are both old, but his breaks down more often.
    我們的車都很舊，可是他的比較常拋錨。
  3. Her room is upstairs. His is next to the kitchen.
    她的房間在樓上。他的在廚房旁邊。
  4. His was the loudest voice in the choir.
    合唱團裡他的聲音最大。
  5. My parents are strict, but his let him do anything.
    我爸媽很嚴格，可是他爸媽什麼都讓他做。
  6. Everyone's essays were good, but his was the best.
    每個人的作文都很好，可是他的最好。

② his 當受詞

  1. I lost my map, so we used his.
    我把地圖弄丟了，所以我們用他的。
  2. She borrowed my notes because she couldn't read his.
    她借了我的筆記，因為她看不懂他的。
  3. I've heard her story. Now I want to hear his.
    我聽過她的故事了。現在我想聽他的。
  4. We finished our drinks, but he left his on the table.
    我們喝完了我們的飲料，可是他把他的留在桌上。
  5. The kids ate their cake, and Grandpa saved his for later.
    孩子們吃了他們的蛋糕，爺爺把他的留著晚點吃。

③ be + his

  1. The black jacket on the chair is his.
    椅子上那件黑色夾克是他的。
  2. Those muddy boots are his.
    那雙沾滿泥巴的靴子是他的。
  3. The decision was his, not ours.
    那個決定是他做的，不是我們。
  4. I didn't recognize the voice at first, but it was his.
    我一開始沒認出那個聲音，可是那是他的。
  5. The bed by the window used to be his.
    窗邊那張床以前是他的。

④ 介系詞 + his

  1. My desk is right next to his.
    我的書桌就在他的旁邊。
  2. I'd love a garden like his.
    我很想要一座像他那樣的花園。
  3. Her handwriting is very different from his.
    她的字跡跟他的很不一樣。
  4. My grades are better than his.
    我的成績比他的好。
  5. Her flat is two floors below his.
    她的公寓在他的樓下兩層。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. She trusted his judgment, and he trusted hers.
    她信任他的判斷，他也信任她的。
  2. The handwriting on the envelope was definitely his.
    信封上的字跡絕對是他的。
  3. Your shift starts at six, and his starts at ten.
    你的班六點開始，他的十點開始。
  4. Mine is the small room. His is the one with the balcony.
    我的是小房間。他的是有陽台的那間。
  5. Of all the paintings, his sold first.
    所有的畫裡，他的最先賣出去。
  6. Our children go to school together, and his are very polite.
    我們的孩子一起上學，他的孩子很有禮貌。
  7. I thought the wallet was mine, but it was his.
    我以為那個皮夾是我的，結果是他的。
  8. My plan is simple. His is far too complicated.
    我的計畫很簡單。他的太複雜了。
  9. Her family lives in Spain, and his lives in Taiwan.
    她的家人住在西班牙，他的住在台灣。
