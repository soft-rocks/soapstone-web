時⁠間副⁠詞語說⁠出什⁠麼⁠時⁠候

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

去年夏天，我在加拿大的一個夏令營當幫手。

說話者在求職信裡介紹經驗。

說⁠出時⁠間點

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

last

They painted the kitchen last month.

ago

The bus left five minutes ago.

in / on / at

My grandfather was born in 1948.

yesterday

She called you earlier today.

說⁠出持⁠續多⁠久

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

for

We waited at the airport for four hours.

我們在機場等了四個小時。
during

Nobody spoke during the meeting.

開會的時候沒有人說話。

last 前⁠面不⁠加介⁠系⁠詞

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

I visited my aunt in last summer.

I visited my aunt last summer.

ago 搭⁠配過⁠去簡⁠單式

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

I have moved to Taipei two years ago.

I moved to Taipei two years ago.

ago 放⁠在一⁠段時⁠間後⁠面

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

I started this job ago three months.

I started this job three months ago.

往回算⁠用 ago，不⁠用 before

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

We met before five years.

We met five years ago.

持⁠續多⁠久用 for

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

She stayed in London during three weeks.

She stayed in London for three weeks.

ago 和 before

I worked as a helper at a summer camp in Canada last summer.

從現在算

I lost my keys two days ago.

我兩天前弄丟了鑰匙。
從過去算

I realised I had lost them two days before.

我才發現是兩天前就弄丟了。

例句

① last + 時間

  1. I went to bed very late last night.
    我昨天晚上很晚才睡。
  2. We had a vigorous discussion about the budget last week.
    我們上週針對預算熱烈地討論了一番。
  3. Did you see the match last Saturday?
    你上週六有看那場比賽嗎？
  4. The shop did very well last year.
    那家店去年生意很好。
  5. They painted the kitchen last month.
    他們上個月把廚房漆好了。

② 一段時間 + ago

  1. My sister got married three years ago.
    我姊姊三年前結婚了。
  2. The bus left five minutes ago.
    公車五分鐘前開走了。
  3. I bought this coat a long time ago.
    這件外套我很久以前就買了。
  4. A few weeks ago, I received a strange letter from an old friend.
    幾週前，我收到一位老朋友寄來的一封奇怪的信。

③ in / on / at + 時間

  1. My grandfather was born in 1948.
    我爺爺生於 1948 年。
  2. The shop opened on Monday morning.
    那家店星期一早上開張了。
  3. The train arrived at exactly six o'clock.
    火車六點整到站。
  4. In the spring, we planted tomatoes in the garden.
    春天的時候，我們在花園裡種了番茄。
  5. The parcel arrived on the morning of my birthday.
    包裹在我生日那天早上送到了。

④ for + 一段時間、during + 名詞

  1. He worked hard for quite a long time to earn the money he needed.
    他努力工作了好長一段時間，才賺到需要的錢。
  2. We waited at the airport for four hours.
    我們在機場等了四個小時。
  3. I lived with my grandparents for a year.
    我跟爺爺奶奶住了一年。
  4. Something funny happened to me last year, during the Christmas holiday.
    去年聖誕假期，我遇到了一件好笑的事。
  5. The baby woke up three times during the night.
    寶寶夜裡醒了三次。
  6. Nobody spoke during the meeting.
    開會的時候沒有人說話。

⑤ yesterday、the other day、earlier today

  1. I saw your brother at the supermarket yesterday.
    我昨天在超市看到你哥哥。
  2. The other day, a very affable man helped me carry my bags.
    前幾天，有一位很親切的先生幫我提袋子。
  3. I ran into our old teacher the other day.
    我前幾天碰到我們以前的老師。
  4. She called you earlier today.
    她今天稍早打過電話給你。
  5. Earlier today, the manager announced a new holiday policy.
    今天稍早，經理宣布了新的休假規定。

⑥ 故事裡的 then、at that time、in the end

  1. We waited for the bus, but it didn't come. Then we took a taxi.
    我們等公車，可是車一直沒來。後來我們就搭了計程車。
  2. She read the letter twice and then tore it up.
    她把信讀了兩遍，然後就撕掉了。
  3. At that time, I didn't speak any English.
    那時候我一句英文都不會說。
  4. My parents ran a small café at that time.
    我爸媽那時候開了一家小咖啡店。
  5. We argued for hours, but in the end we agreed.
    我們吵了好幾個小時，不過最後還是達成了共識。
  6. In the end, we decided to stay at home.
    最後我們決定待在家裡。

⑦ 兩個時間副詞語一起用

  1. Last summer, I worked in a hotel for two months.
    去年夏天，我在一家飯店工作了兩個月。
  2. We stayed in Kyoto for a week in April.
    我們四月在京都待了一個禮拜。
  3. Two years ago, during the rainy season, the river flooded the whole town.
    兩年前的雨季，河水淹沒了整個鎮。
