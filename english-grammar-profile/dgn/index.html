I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

我想我會付現金，除非你們收信用卡。

unless 說例⁠外

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

我每天騎腳踏車上學，除非路上有積雪。

說現⁠在或未⁠來都用現⁠在簡⁠單式

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

現在的習慣

My grandmother goes to the market every morning unless she feels unwell.

我奶奶每天早上都去市場，除非她身體不舒服。
未來的計畫

We're going to drive to the coast unless the weather turns bad.

我們打算開車去海邊，除非天氣變壞。

加逗⁠號補⁠充例⁠外

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

後來才想到
前面加逗號

You can stay at our flat, unless you prefer a hotel.

你可以住我們的公寓，除非你比較想住飯店。

unless 子⁠句放⁠在⁠前⁠面

放在前面
後面加逗號

Unless the bus is late, I'll be home by six.

除非公車誤點，不然我六點前會到家。

原句：付⁠錢前才想⁠到例⁠外

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

我想我會付現金，除非你們收信用卡。

說話的人說完才想到例外，所以 unless 前面加逗號。

unless 子⁠句不⁠用 will

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

I'll take the bus unless my dad will drive me.

I'll take the bus unless my dad drives me.

unless 後⁠面不⁠再⁠加 not

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

We'll go swimming unless the pool isn't open.

We'll go swimming unless the pool is closed.

要說如⁠果就⁠用 if

I think I am going to pay in cash, unless you accept credit cards.

I'll be happy unless I pass the exam.

I'll be happy if I pass the exam.

unless 等⁠於 if ... not

unless

I'll leave the window open unless you feel cold.

我會開著窗，除非你覺得冷。
if ... not

I'll turn the heating on if you don't feel warm enough.

如果你覺得不夠暖，我就開暖氣。

except 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞

except
名詞

The shop opens every day except Sunday.

那家店除了星期天，每天都開。
unless
子句

The shop opens every day unless there's a holiday.

那家店每天都開，除非遇到假日。

例句

① 現在的習慣

  1. I always take the stairs unless I'm carrying something heavy.
    我都走樓梯，除非我拿著很重的東西。
  2. My father reads the paper at breakfast unless he's in a hurry.
    我爸早餐時都會看報紙，除非他趕時間。
  3. We eat dinner at seven unless someone has a late class.
    我們七點吃晚餐，除非有人晚上有課。
  4. The office closes at five unless there's a meeting.
    辦公室五點關門，除非有會議。
  5. She doesn't wear make-up unless she's going somewhere special.
    她不化妝，除非她要去特別的場合。
  6. Our cat sleeps on the sofa unless the dog gets there first.
    我們的貓睡在沙發上，除非狗先佔了位子。
  7. I don't usually drink coffee after lunch unless I have a long drive.
    我午餐後通常不喝咖啡，除非要開很久的車。

② 未來的計畫

  1. We're going to have the party in the garden unless it's too windy.
    我們打算在花園辦派對，除非風太大。
  2. I'll finish the painting tomorrow unless my brother needs my help.
    我明天會把油漆刷完，除非我弟弟需要我幫忙。
  3. The match will start at three unless the pitch is too wet.
    比賽三點開始，除非場地太濕。
  4. I'm going to buy the blue one unless they have it in green.
    我打算買藍色的，除非他們有綠色的。
  5. We'll stay at home this weekend unless our friends invite us out.
    這個週末我們會待在家，除非朋友約我們出去。
  6. I'm going to apply for the job unless the salary is too low.
    我打算應徵這份工作，除非薪水太低。
  7. The museum will reopen in May unless the repairs take longer.
    博物館五月會重新開放，除非修繕拖得更久。

③ 逗號 + unless

  1. Let's meet at the cinema, unless you want to eat first.
    我們在電影院見，除非你想先吃東西。
  2. I'll make pasta tonight, unless you're tired of it.
    我今晚煮義大利麵，除非你吃膩了。
  3. Take my umbrella, unless you have your own.
    拿我的傘去，除非你自己有帶。
  4. I think I'll walk, unless you're driving past the station.
    我想我用走的，除非你會開車經過車站。
  5. The answer is no, unless your parents agree.
    答案是不行，除非你爸媽同意。
  6. We'll order a pizza, unless anyone has a better idea.
    我們叫披薩吧，除非有人有更好的主意。
  7. I'll send the letter today, unless there's anything you want to add.
    我今天會寄出這封信，除非你有想補充的東西。

④ Unless 在句首

  1. Unless the shop has my size, I'll order the shoes online.
    除非店裡有我的尺寸，不然我就在網路上訂那雙鞋。
  2. Unless you show your student card, you pay the full price.
    除非你出示學生證，不然要付全額。
  3. Unless the doctor says otherwise, I'll go back to work on Monday.
    除非醫生另有指示，不然我星期一會回去上班。
  4. Unless it's very cold, the children play outside after lunch.
    除非天氣很冷，不然孩子們午餐後都在外面玩。
  5. Unless you water these herbs every day, they'll dry out.
    除非你每天幫這些香草澆水，不然它們會乾掉。
  6. Unless the technician fixes the printer soon, we won't have the leaflets ready.
    除非技術人員很快把印表機修好，不然我們沒辦法把傳單準備好。

⑤ 建議和規定

  1. If I were you, I'd take the train, unless the tickets are too expensive.
    如果我是你，我會搭火車，除非票太貴。
  2. Passengers must stay in their seats unless the crew tells them otherwise.
    乘客必須留在座位上，除非機組人員另有指示。
  3. Students may not use phones in class unless the teacher allows it.
    學生上課不能用手機，除非老師允許。
  4. You should rest for a week unless the pain goes away sooner.
    你應該休息一週，除非疼痛提早消失。
  5. Unless you exercise regularly, you'll find the hike very hard.
    除非你規律運動，不然你會覺得這趟健行很辛苦。
