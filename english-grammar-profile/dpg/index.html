I can't say I was very impressed with the level of the classes.

我不能說我對課程的程度很滿意。

I can't say、I can tell you / I can say、I can assure you

I can't say「我不能說……」，委婉地表示不滿意。
I can tell you / I can say「我可以告訴你」，強調後面的話。
I can assure you「我向你保證」，正式地強調。
as you can see / imagine「你可以看到 / 想像」，引出讀者能理解的事。

I can't say 和 not very

I can't say I was very impressed with the level of the classes.

例句

I can't say I enjoyed the film.

我不能說我喜歡那部電影。

I can't say 常接 very 加形容詞，或接 I like、I enjoyed。

I can assure you 用在正⁠式⁠的信⁠件

例句

I can assure you that this will not happen again.

我向您保證，這種事不會再發生。

assure 後⁠面要接⁠人，再⁠接 that 子⁠句

I can assure that the order will arrive.

I can assure you that the order will arrive.

as you can see 要⁠有主⁠詞 you

As can see, the numbers are falling.

As you can see, the numbers are falling.

assure 後⁠面直⁠接接⁠人，不⁠加 to

I can assure to you that the room is ready.

I can assure you that the room is ready.

例句

① I can't say + 子句：委婉地批評

  1. I can't say I was very impressed with the service at the restaurant.
    我不能說我對那家餐廳的服務很滿意。
  2. I can't say I enjoyed the meal. The fish was cold.
    我不能說我喜歡那一餐，魚是冷的。
  3. I can't say I'm a big fan of modern art.
    我不能說我很喜歡現代藝術。
  4. I can't say the hotel was very clean.
    我不能說那家飯店很乾淨。
  5. I can't say I agree with everything in the report.
    我不能說我同意報告裡的每一點。
  6. I can't say I'm surprised. He's always late.
    我不能說我很意外，他總是遲到。
  7. I can't say the new manager has made a good start.
    我不能說新經理有個好的開始。

② I can tell you / I can say + (that) + 子句：強調

  1. About my country, I can tell you that we went through many wars in the last century.
    關於我的國家，我可以告訴你，我們在上個世紀經歷了很多戰爭。
  2. I can tell you that it was the hardest exam I've ever taken.
    我可以告訴你，那是我考過最難的考試。
  3. I can tell you, the view from the top is amazing.
    我跟你說，從山頂看下去的景色好美。
  4. I can say that this year has been a success for the team.
    我可以說，今年對這支隊伍來說很成功。
  5. I can tell you that nobody worked harder than her.
    我可以告訴你，沒有人比她更努力。
  6. I can say with confidence that our product is the best on the market.
    我可以很有信心地說，我們的產品是市場上最好的。

③ I can assure you + that + 子句：正式地強調

  1. I can assure you that this will not happen again.
    我向您保證，這種事不會再發生。
  2. I can assure you that the room will be ready by three.
    我向您保證，房間三點前會準備好。
  3. I can assure you that your money is safe with us.
    我向您保證，您的錢放在我們這裡很安全。
  4. I can assure you that we take every complaint seriously.
    我向您保證，我們認真處理每一件投訴。
  5. I can assure you that the problem has been fixed.
    我向您保證，問題已經修好了。（被動）
  6. I can assure you that the information will remain private.
    我向您保證，這些資料會保密。

④ As you can see / imagine, + 句子：引出重點

  1. So, as you can see, there are no serious consequences for us so far.
    所以，你可以看到，目前為止對我們沒有嚴重的影響。
  2. As you can see from the chart, sales have doubled.
    你從圖表上可以看到，銷售量增加了一倍。
  3. As you can imagine, we were very worried when the plane was late.
    你可以想像，飛機誤點的時候我們有多擔心。
  4. As you can see, the kitchen needs a lot of work.
    你可以看到，廚房需要大整修。
  5. As you can imagine, the children were very excited about the trip.
    你可以想像，孩子們對這趟旅行有多興奮。
  6. As you can see in the photo, the house is right next to the sea.
    你從照片可以看到，房子就在海邊。
  7. As you can imagine, it was a difficult decision for us.
    你可以想像，這對我們來說是很難的決定。
  8. As you can see, I've changed my hair.
    你看得到吧，我換了髮型。

⑤ I can't tell you / I can't imagine / I can't thank you enough：加強感受

  1. I can't tell you how happy I am to see you again.
    我說不出我再見到你有多開心。
  2. I can't imagine life without my dog.
    我無法想像沒有我的狗的生活。
  3. I can't tell you how sorry I am about your loss.
    你失去了重要的人，我說不出我有多難過。
  4. I can't thank you enough for your help.
    你的幫忙我怎麼感謝都不夠。
  5. I can't imagine how hard it must be for you.
    我無法想像那對你來說一定有多辛苦。
  6. We can't thank you enough for everything you've done.
    你為我們做的一切，我們怎麼感謝都不夠。
