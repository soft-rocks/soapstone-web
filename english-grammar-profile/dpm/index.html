I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

我買了一件襯衫，因為我一件也沒有。

否⁠定⁠句⁠的 any 可⁠以代⁠替前⁠面說⁠過⁠的名⁠詞

我買了一件襯衫，因為我一件也沒有。

any 指一⁠個也⁠沒⁠有，many 和 much 指不⁠多

I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

any

I wanted some milk, but there wasn't any.

我想喝點牛奶，可是一點也沒有。
many
可數名詞

I like her friends, but she doesn't have many.

我喜歡她的朋友，可是她的朋友不多。
much
不可數名詞

You can have some cake, but there isn't much.

你可以吃點蛋糕，可是剩下的不多。

anything、anyone、anywhere 前⁠面不⁠用先說⁠過名⁠詞

I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

anything

Don't bring anything.

什麼都不用帶。
anyone

There wasn't anyone at home.

家裡沒有人。
anywhere

We didn't go anywhere last weekend.

我們上個週末哪裡都沒去。

否⁠定⁠句用 any，不⁠用 some

I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

I wanted coffee, but there wasn't some.

I wanted coffee, but there wasn't any.

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞用 much

I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

I need money, but I haven't got many.

I need money, but I haven't got much.

可⁠數⁠名⁠詞⁠的複⁠數用 many

I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

I like books, but I don't have much.

I like books, but I don't have many.

句⁠子已⁠經有 not，後⁠面用 anything

I bought a shirt because I haven't got any.

I didn't buy nothing.

I didn't buy anything.

例句

① 否定句 + any，代替可數名詞

  1. I need a shirt for the interview, and I haven't got any.
    我面試需要一件襯衫，可是我一件也沒有。
  2. We wanted to buy tickets, but there weren't any left.
    我們想買票，可是一張也沒剩。
  3. Do you have any stamps? I didn't buy any at the post office.
    你有郵票嗎？我在郵局一張也沒買。
  4. She asked for photos of the wedding, but I didn't take any.
    她跟我要婚禮的照片，可是我一張也沒拍。
  5. I looked for mistakes in your essay, but I couldn't find any.
    我在你的作文裡找錯誤，可是一個也找不到。

② 否定句 + any，代替不可數名詞

  1. I wanted some milk in my tea, but there wasn't any.
    我想在茶裡加點牛奶，可是一點也沒有。
  2. Can you lend me some money? I haven't got any.
    你可以借我一點錢嗎？我身上一毛錢也沒有。
  3. He offered me some cake, but I didn't want any.
    他請我吃蛋糕，可是我一點也不想吃。
  4. We needed bread for breakfast, but the shop didn't have any.
    我們早餐要吃麵包，可是那家店一點麵包也沒有了。

③ 否定句 + many

  1. As for close friends, I don't have many.
    說到知心朋友，我沒有幾個。
  2. I usually read a lot of books, but I haven't read many this year.
    我平常讀很多書，可是今年沒讀幾本。
  3. There are lots of good restaurants in Rome, but there aren't many in my town.
    羅馬有很多好餐廳，可是我住的鎮上沒有幾家。
  4. We expected lots of guests, but not many came.
    我們以為會來很多客人，可是沒來幾個。

④ 否定句 + much

  1. You can borrow some rice, but I haven't got much.
    你可以借一點米，可是我的米不多。
  2. There's some soup left, but not much.
    還剩一點湯，可是不多。
  3. I ate some of the pasta, but I didn't eat much.
    我吃了一點義大利麵，可是沒吃多少。
  4. He knows a little about cars, but not much.
    他對車子懂一點，可是懂得不多。

⑤ 否定句 + anything

  1. It's only a short trip, so don't pack anything heavy.
    只是短途旅行，所以別帶重的東西。
  2. I went to the market, but I didn't buy anything.
    我去了市集，可是什麼都沒買。
  3. Please speak up. I can't hear anything.
    請大聲一點。我什麼都聽不到。
  4. At her first interview, she was so nervous that she couldn't say anything.
    第一次面試的時候，她緊張得一句話都說不出來。
  5. Don't touch anything in the lab without asking.
    沒問過之前，實驗室裡的東西都不要碰。

⑥ 否定句 + anyone / anybody

  1. I knocked on the door, but there wasn't anyone at home.
    我敲了門，可是家裡沒有人。
  2. Please don't tell anyone about the surprise party.
    請不要把驚喜派對的事告訴任何人。
  3. I'm new in town, and I don't know anybody here yet.
    我剛搬來這裡，還一個人都不認識。
  4. I worked alone in the office during the holiday. I didn't see anyone all day.
    放假期間我一個人在辦公室上班。我一整天都沒看到任何人。

⑦ 否定句 + anywhere

  1. I've looked in every room, and the cat isn't anywhere.
    我每個房間都找過了，貓哪裡都不在。
  2. We didn't go anywhere last weekend. We stayed at home.
    我們上個週末哪裡都沒去。我們待在家裡。
  3. You can't park anywhere near the stadium on match days.
    比賽日的時候，體育場附近哪裡都不能停車。

⑧ 簡答裡的 not many / not much

  1. "How many people came to the meeting?" "Not many. Only six."
    有多少人來開會？沒幾個。只有六個。
  2. "Is there any coffee left?" "Not much. Just half a cup."
    還有咖啡嗎？不多了。只剩半杯。
