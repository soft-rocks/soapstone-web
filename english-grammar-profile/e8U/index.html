The movie shows how a woman can make people feel better.

這部電影講的是一個女人怎麼讓別人心情變好。

電⁠影⁠的內⁠容不⁠會⁠變，所⁠以用 shows

這部電影講的是一個女人怎麼讓別人心情變好。

轉⁠述動⁠詞後⁠面⁠接 that 子⁠句或疑⁠問⁠詞子⁠句

The movie shows how a woman can make people feel better.

The movie shows how a woman can make people feel better.

例句

① 書、電影、文章 + says / shows

  1. I loved this film. The movie shows how a woman can make people feel better.
    我好愛這部電影。這部電影講的是一個女人怎麼讓別人心情變好。
  2. Read this article. It says that walking every day is good for your heart.
    看看這篇文章。上面說每天走路對心臟好。
  3. You'll like this one. The book shows what life was like a hundred years ago.
    你會喜歡這本的。這本書呈現了一百年前的生活是什麼樣子。
  4. The sign says "No swimming", so let's stay on the beach.
    告示上寫著禁止游泳，所以我們待在沙灘上吧。
  5. Take a map at the gate. The map shows where all the toilets are.
    在門口拿一張地圖。地圖上標出了所有廁所的位置。
  6. Look at this old picture. It shows my grandparents on their wedding day.
    你看這張老照片。這是我爺爺奶奶結婚那天拍的。
  7. Bring the plants inside. The weather report says it will snow tonight.
    把植物搬進來。氣象報告說今晚會下雪。

② 人 + says

  1. I know a man who moved here two years ago. He says that it improved his social life.
    我認識一個兩年前搬來這裡的人。他說這讓他的社交生活變好了。
  2. I'm going to bed early this week. My doctor says I need more sleep.
    我這個星期要早點睡。我的醫生說我需要多睡一點。
  3. Come downstairs, everyone. Mum says dinner is ready.
    大家下樓吧。媽媽說晚餐好了。
  4. Let's try it this weekend. Everyone says the new café is great.
    我們這個週末去試試吧。大家都說新開的咖啡店很棒。
  5. I practise every day now. My teacher says my English is getting better.
    我現在每天都練習。我老師說我的英文越來越好了。
  6. Look how big it is. They say it's the oldest tree in the country.
    你看它好大。大家都說這是全國最老的樹。

③ 主詞 + tells + 聽的人

  1. I'll miss her so much. My sister tells me she's moving to Canada.
    我會好想她。我姊姊跟我說她要搬去加拿大。
  2. I'm worried about him. He always tells his friends that he's fine.
    我很擔心他。他總是跟朋友說他很好。
  3. Your letter tells me you're enjoying your new school.
    從你的信看得出來你很喜歡新學校。
  4. Download this app. It tells you when the bus is coming.
    下載這個 app。它會告訴你公車什麼時候來。

④ writes / explains

  1. In her email, Kate writes that she misses us all.
    Kate 在 email 裡寫說她很想念我們大家。
  2. Join the tour at two. The guide explains how people built the old castle.
    兩點參加導覽。導遊會解釋當時的人怎麼蓋這座古堡。
  3. My friend writes that the weather in Spain is lovely.
    我朋友寫信說西班牙的天氣很好。
  4. Don't call a mechanic. This video explains how to change a tyre.
    不用叫技師。這支影片說明怎麼換輪胎。
  5. Nobody gets confused. The teacher explains the rules before every game.
    沒有人會搞不清楚。每次比賽前老師都會說明規則。

⑤ 圖表和研究 + shows

  1. Look at the line going up. The chart shows that more people cycle to work now.
    你看那條往上的線。圖表顯示現在有更多人騎腳踏車上班。
  2. Start English early. This study shows that children learn languages quickly.
    早點開始學英文吧。這項研究顯示小孩學語言很快。
  3. We tested the river water again. The results show that our town is getting cleaner.
    我們又檢驗了一次河水。結果顯示我們的鎮越來越乾淨。
  4. We asked 500 students. The survey shows what they think about school lunches.
    我們問了五百個學生。這份調查呈現了他們對學校午餐的看法。

⑥ 疑問句和否定句

  1. You look worried. What does the letter say?
    你看起來很擔心。信上寫了什麼？
  2. We're a bit lost. Does the map show the way to the beach?
    我們有點迷路了。地圖上有標出去海邊的路嗎？
  3. The book doesn't say who the killer is until the last page.
    這本書到最後一頁才說出兇手是誰。
  4. The report came out today. What does it say about the new road?
    報告今天出來了。裡面對新的道路有什麼說法？
