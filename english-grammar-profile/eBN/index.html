Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

也許這能幫我聽懂我那兩隻狗彼此在說什麼。

one another 當介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞

Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

我那兩隻狗彼此在說什麼。

動⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞

Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

動詞的受詞

New members can get to know one another at the welcome dinner.

新成員可以在迎新晚宴上互相認識。
介系詞的受詞

The members of the committee rarely agree with one another.

委員會的成員很少意見一致。

所⁠有⁠格 one another's 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞

那兩位科學家都尊重對方的研究。

one another 比 each other 正⁠式

Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

one another

We have known one another for many years.

我們認識彼此很多年了。
each other

We've known each other for years.

我們認識好多年了。

互⁠相做⁠的⁠事，不⁠用反⁠身代⁠名⁠詞

Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

At the end of the meeting, the two leaders thanked themselves.

At the end of the meeting, the two leaders thanked one another.

listen 後⁠面先接 to

Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

The students listened one another carefully.

The students listened to one another carefully.

discuss 後⁠面直⁠接接⁠受⁠詞

The members discussed about one another's ideas.

The members discussed one another's ideas.

所⁠有⁠格寫⁠成 one another's

The neighbours look after one anothers' gardens.

The neighbours look after one another's gardens.

相⁠互代⁠名⁠詞是 one another，不⁠是 one other

Perhaps it will help me understand what my two dogs say to one another.

Team members should support one other.

Team members should support one another.

themselves 指回主⁠詞自⁠己

themselves

The children enjoyed themselves at the party.

孩子們在派對上玩得很開心。
one another

The children helped one another at the party.

孩子們在派對上互相幫忙。

one another 不⁠當主⁠詞，主⁠詞用 each

我們都知道對方想要什麼。

例句

① 動詞 + one another

  1. The guests were asked to introduce one another.
    賓客們被要求互相介紹。
  2. The former classmates had not seen one another for twenty years.
    那些老同學已經二十年沒見面了。
  3. The players hugged one another after the final whistle.
    終場哨聲響起後，球員們互相擁抱。
  4. Neighbours in this village trust one another.
    這個村子裡的鄰居都互相信任。
  5. Members of the club are expected to respect one another.
    俱樂部的成員應當互相尊重。
  6. The three sisters phone one another every Sunday.
    那三姊妹每個星期天都會互相打電話。

② 介系詞 + one another

  1. The two countries have been at war with one another for years.
    那兩個國家已經交戰多年。
  2. Delegates were able to talk to one another freely.
    代表們可以自由地彼此交談。
  3. The children in the class are very kind to one another.
    班上的孩子們對彼此都很好。
  4. Scientists from different countries learn from one another.
    各國的科學家互相學習。
  5. The houses in the old town are built very close to one another.
    老城區的房子蓋得很密。
  6. The pupils sat in a circle, facing towards one another.
    學生們坐成一圈，面向彼此。

③ one another's + 名詞

  1. The two families often stay in one another's homes.
    那兩家人常常到對方家裡住。
  2. The writers commented on one another's work.
    作家們互相評論對方的作品。
  3. Staff are asked to respect one another's privacy.
    請員工尊重彼此的隱私。
  4. The twins finish one another's sentences.
    那對雙胞胎常常接對方的話。
  5. The leaders shook one another's hands.
    領袖們互相握手。

④ 兩個人或兩隻動物

  1. My two cats groom one another every evening.
    我那兩隻貓每天晚上都會互相舔毛。
  2. The couple wrote to one another every day during the war.
    戰爭期間，那對夫妻每天都寫信給對方。
  3. The two old friends greeted one another warmly.
    兩個老朋友熱情地互相問候。
  4. Both drivers blamed one another for the accident.
    兩個駕駛都把事故怪到對方身上。
  5. The two brothers have not spoken to one another since the funeral.
    那兩兄弟從葬禮之後就沒跟對方說過話。

⑤ 正式的場合

  1. We must work with one another to protect the environment.
    我們必須攜手合作保護環境。
  2. Guests are kindly requested to be patient with one another.
    敬請各位賓客互相體諒。
  3. The two companies have agreed to share information with one another.
    兩家公司已同意互相分享資訊。
  4. The aim of the course is for participants to learn from one another's experience.
    這門課的目的是讓學員從彼此的經驗中學習。
  5. The parties promised to treat one another fairly.
    各方承諾公平對待彼此。

⑥ 不同的時態

  1. We had never met one another before the conference.
    我們在研討會之前從沒見過面。
  2. They will be competing against one another in the final.
    他們將在決賽中互相較量。
  3. The members have always supported one another.
    成員們一直都互相支持。
  4. In the past, the villages used to trade with one another.
    過去那些村子會互相做買賣。
