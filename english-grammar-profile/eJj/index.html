I really like it, not only because it is nice and colorful but also because I spent most of my childhood summers there.

我真的很喜歡那裡，不只是因為那裡漂亮又多彩多姿，也因為我童年大部分的暑假都是在那裡過的。

not only A but also B，強⁠調 B

I really like it, not only because it is nice and colorful but also because I spent most of my childhood summers there.

她不只會唱歌，還自己寫歌。

A 和 B 也⁠可⁠以是兩⁠個 because 子⁠句

I really like it, not only because it is nice and colorful but also because I spent most of my childhood summers there.

We found a café near the museum. It was nice, not only because it was quiet but also because the staff were friendly.

我們在博物館附近找到一家咖啡店。那家咖啡店很好，不只是因為安靜，也因為員工很友善。

A 是動⁠詞，B 也用動⁠詞

I really like it, not only because it is nice and colorful but also because I spent most of my childhood summers there.

He not only plays the piano but also singing.

He not only plays the piano but also sings.

not only 配 but also

I really like it, not only because it is nice and colorful but also because I spent most of my childhood summers there.

She is not only smart, also kind.

She is not only smart but also kind.

not only 放⁠在 A 前⁠面

I really like it, not only because it is nice and colorful but also because I spent most of my childhood summers there.

Not only she plays tennis but also golf.

She plays not only tennis but also golf.

例句

① not only + 動詞 + but also + 動詞

  1. Look at the garden behind the restaurant. The chef not only cooks but also grows his own herbs.
    你看餐廳後面的花園。那個廚師不只會做菜，還自己種香草。
  2. My brother has a small workshop in the garage. He not only repairs bikes but also builds them.
    我哥哥在車庫有一間小工作室。他不只會修腳踏車，還會自己組裝。
  3. I order my groceries with this app now. It not only saves time but also saves money.
    我現在用這個 app 訂日用品。它不只省時間，還省錢。
  4. My grandmother is very busy for her age. She not only volunteers at the library but also teaches swimming.
    以她的年紀來說，我奶奶很忙。她不只在圖書館當志工，還教游泳。
  5. The builders did more than we asked. They not only fixed the roof but also painted the house.
    工人做的比我們要求的還多。他們不只修好了屋頂，還粉刷了房子。

② not only + 形容詞 + but also + 形容詞

  1. She gave us all a day off after the busy month. My new boss is not only fair but also generous.
    忙了一個月以後她讓我們每個人都放一天假。我的新老闆不只公正，還很大方。
  2. We were lucky in Rome. The hotel was not only cheap but also clean.
    我們在羅馬運氣很好。那家飯店不只便宜，還很乾淨。
  3. Nobody finished the chemistry paper. The test was not only long but also difficult.
    沒有人寫完化學考卷。考試不只很長，還很難。
  4. Have you been inside the new library? It's not only big but also beautiful.
    你進過新圖書館嗎？它不只大，還很漂亮。
  5. My brother spoke at his best friend's wedding. His speech was not only funny but very moving.
    我哥哥在他最好朋友的婚禮上致詞。他的演講不只好笑，還很感人。

③ not only + 名詞 + but also + 名詞

  1. My aunt worked in Asia for twenty years. She speaks not only Thai but also Vietnamese.
    我阿姨在亞洲工作了二十年。她不只會說泰文，還會說越南文。
  2. I spend hours at the bookshop on the corner. The shop sells not only books but also coffee.
    我在轉角那家書店一待就是好幾個小時。那家店不只賣書，還賣咖啡。
  3. Our garden party was very big. We invited not only our friends but also our neighbors.
    我們的花園派對很盛大。我們不只邀請了朋友，還邀請了鄰居。
  4. A pan caught fire while we were out. The fire damaged not only the kitchen but also the living room.
    我們出門的時候一個鍋子起火了。那場火不只燒壞了廚房，還燒壞了客廳。
  5. My sister had a terrible day at the airport. She lost not only her bag but also her passport.
    我姊姊在機場過了很糟的一天。她不只弄丟了包包，連護照也弄丟了。

④ not only because ... but also because ...

  1. Tom's birthday was on Saturday. The party was fun, not only because the music was good but also because everyone danced.
    湯姆的生日在星期六。派對很好玩，不只是因為音樂好，也因為大家都在跳舞。
  2. We found a café near the museum. It was nice, not only because it was quiet but also because the staff were friendly.
    我們在博物館附近找到一家咖啡店。那家咖啡店很好，不只是因為安靜，也因為員工很友善。
  3. My friend asked why I became a nurse. I chose this job not only because the pay is good but also because I love the work.
    我朋友問我為什麼當護理師。我選這份工作，不只是因為薪水好，也因為我喜歡這個工作。
  4. We left the city last year. We moved here not only because it's cheap but because it's near my parents.
    我們去年離開了城市。我們搬來這裡，不只是因為便宜，也因為離我爸媽很近。
  5. Tom missed the start of his exam. He was late not only because the traffic was bad but also because he overslept.
    湯姆錯過了考試的開頭。他遲到不只是因為塞車，也因為他睡過頭了。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. The city is giving free bus passes to students. The plan will help not only students but also their families.
    市政府要發免費公車卡給學生。這個計畫不只會幫助學生，還會幫助他們的家人。
  2. We ate noodles at a street stall in Bangkok. It was not only the best meal of the trip but also the cheapest.
    我們在曼谷的路邊攤吃麵。那不只是這趟旅行最好吃的一餐，也是最便宜的一餐。
  3. Go for a run after work. Exercise not only keeps you fit but also helps you sleep.
    下班後去跑步吧。運動不只讓你保持健康，還能幫助睡眠。
  4. You're going to India next month? You need not only a passport but also a visa.
    你下個月要去印度？你不只需要護照，還需要簽證。
  5. The cinemas were full every weekend. The film was popular not only with kids but also with adults.
    電影院每個週末都客滿。那部電影不只受小孩歡迎，也受大人歡迎。
  6. We stayed home for two days last week. The storm not only closed the roads but also cut the power.
    我們上星期在家待了兩天。暴風雨不只讓道路封閉，還造成停電。
  7. My mum started English classes at fifty. Learning English is useful not only for work but also for travel.
    我媽媽五十歲開始上英文課。學英文不只對工作有用，對旅行也有用。
  8. My sister was worried about her maths exam. She not only passed the exam but got the highest score.
    我姊姊很擔心她的數學考試。她不只考試及格，還拿到最高分。
  9. Take the kids to the science museum. It's not only interesting but also free.
    帶孩子們去科學博物館吧。那間博物館不只有趣，還免費。
  10. I'm very angry with my boyfriend. He not only forgot my birthday but also forgot our dinner.
    我很生我男朋友的氣。他不只忘了我的生日，還忘了我們的晚餐約會。
