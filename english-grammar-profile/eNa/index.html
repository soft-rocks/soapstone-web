Thank you for this.

謝謝你給我這個。

this 指前⁠面提⁠過⁠的東⁠西

Thank you for this.

謝謝你給我這個。

一⁠個東⁠西和一⁠組東⁠西

Thank you for this.

this

I found an old photo of you. Look at this!

我找到一張你的舊照片，你看這張！
all this

I bought shirts, shoes and a hat. Why all this?

我買了襯衫、鞋子和帽子。為什麼買這麼多？
all this + 單數

Bread, cheese, fruit … All this costs only five pounds.

麵包、起司、水果……這些全部只要五英鎊。

先⁠用 this，再用 it

Thank you for this.

謝謝你送的圍巾，我好喜歡！我每天都戴。

一⁠組東⁠西用 all this

Thank you for this.

I bought pens, paper and glue. Why did I buy all these?

I bought pens, paper and glue. Why did I buy all this?

all this 接單⁠數動⁠詞

Thank you for this.

Plates, cups, glasses … all this are for the party.

Plates, cups, glasses … all this is for the party.

this 後⁠面不⁠加 it

Thank you for this.

Thanks for the chocolates. I really love this it.

Thanks for the chocolates. I really love this.

同⁠一⁠種⁠的好⁠幾⁠個：these

我有兩件新襯衫，我很喜歡。

this 指一整⁠件⁠事

我們錯過了公車，所以遲到了。

例句

① 謝謝送的東西

  1. Thank you for the lovely book. I can't wait to read this!
    謝謝你送的好書，我等不及要讀了！
  2. Thanks for the flowers. This is so kind of you.
    謝謝你的花，你真好。
  3. My aunt sent me a cake. I'll share this with my friends.
    我阿姨寄了一個蛋糕給我，我會跟朋友分享。
  4. Thank you for the photo. I'll keep this forever.
    謝謝你的照片，我會永遠留著。
  5. Thanks for the birthday card. This made me smile.
    謝謝你的生日卡，這張卡讓我笑了。

② all this 指一組東西

  1. I went shopping yesterday. I bought three green T-shirts, a pair of sneakers and some socks. You might ask, why did I buy all this?
    我昨天去買東西，買了三件綠色 T 恤、一雙運動鞋和一些襪子。你可能會問，我為什麼要買這麼多？
  2. Look at all these clothes on the floor! Who's going to tidy all this?
    地上這麼多衣服！誰要來收拾這些？
  3. We've got sandwiches, crisps and juice. Can you carry all this?
    我們有三明治、洋芋片和果汁，你拿得動這些嗎？
  4. There are books, papers and cups on my desk. I need to move all this.
    我的書桌上有書、紙和杯子，我得把這些都移走。
  5. Mum made soup, pasta and a salad. We can't eat all this!
    媽媽煮了湯、義大利麵和沙拉，我們吃不完這麼多！

③ this 當受詞

  1. I found a pen on the floor. Is this yours?
    我在地上找到一支筆，這是你的嗎？
  2. I bought a new lamp for my room. I really like this.
    我買了一盞新檯燈放在房間，我真的很喜歡。
  3. Here's a map of the town. You'll need this.
    這是鎮上的地圖，你會用到。
  4. My grandfather gave me a watch. I'll never sell this.
    我爺爺給了我一支手錶，我永遠不會賣掉。
  5. I've made you a sandwich. Eat this before you go.
    我幫你做了三明治，出門前吃掉。

④ all this 當主詞

  1. Tents, sleeping bags, food … all this has to go in the car.
    帳篷、睡袋、食物……這些全部都要放進車裡。
  2. Computers, phones, tablets … all this is new technology for my grandparents.
    電腦、手機、平板……這些對我祖父母來說都是新科技。
  3. Coffee, tea and cakes. All this is free for visitors.
    咖啡、茶和蛋糕，這些訪客都可以免費享用。
  4. Hats, scarves, gloves … all this is in the box by the door.
    帽子、圍巾、手套……這些都在門邊的箱子裡。
  5. Shoes, bags, jackets … all this was in the sale.
    鞋子、包包、外套……這些都在特價。

⑤ 介系詞後面

  1. I got a new camera. I took all my holiday photos with this.
    我買了一台新相機，假期的照片都是用它拍的。
  2. The box is very heavy. Can you help me with this?
    這個箱子很重，你可以幫我搬嗎？
  3. My phone is broken. How much will it cost to fix this?
    我的手機壞了，修這個要花多少錢？
  4. She gave me a scarf. I'll think of her every time I look at this.
    她送我一條圍巾，我每次看到它都會想起她。
  5. I've got a new bag for school. My books all fit in this.
    我買了新的上學用包包，我的書全放得進去。

⑥ 寫給朋友的謝卡

  1. Thank you for the CD. I've listened to this three times already!
    謝謝你送的 CD，我已經聽了三遍了！
  2. Thanks for the T-shirt. I'm wearing this right now.
    謝謝你送的 T 恤，我現在就穿著。
  3. Thank you for the game. My brother already wants to borrow this!
    謝謝你送的遊戲，我弟弟已經想跟我借了！
  4. You sent me chocolates, a book and a card. Thank you for all this!
    你寄給我巧克力、一本書和一張卡片，謝謝你送我這麼多！
  5. Thank you for the recipe. I'm going to try this at the weekend.
    謝謝你給我的食譜，我週末要來試試看。
  6. Thanks for the poster. This is going on my bedroom wall!
    謝謝你送的海報，我要把它貼在臥室牆上！
