I would be more than pleased to have you at my home, plus you won't have to pay a thing!

我非常樂意讓你來我家，而且你一毛錢都不用花！

子⁠句 + , plus + 子⁠句

I would be more than pleased to have you at my home, plus you won't have to pay a thing!

我非常樂意讓你來我家，而且你一毛錢都不用花！

句⁠子。 + Plus, + 句⁠子。

I would be more than pleased to have you at my home, plus you won't have to pay a thing!

It's pretty cheap. Plus, it's really fun!

它滿便宜的。而且真的很好玩！

plus 的用⁠法

I would be more than pleased to have you at my home, plus you won't have to pay a thing!

意思方向和前面相同，常是另一個好處
句首Plus 後面常加逗號
正式的文章用 in addition、moreover、what's more

相⁠反⁠的意⁠思用 But

The hotel is very cheap. Plus, the rooms are dirty.

The hotel is very cheap. But the rooms are dirty.

Plus 後⁠面⁠接完⁠整⁠的句⁠子

The app is free. Plus, very easy to use.

The app is free. Plus, it's very easy to use.

plus 後⁠面不⁠加 also

The flat is near my office. Plus, it's also very quiet.

The flat is near my office. Plus, it's very quiet.

正⁠式⁠的文⁠章用 In addition

The new system reduces costs. Plus, it improves safety.

The new system reduces costs. In addition, it improves safety.

例句

① 邀請 + plus + 好處

  1. You should come and stay with us, plus you'll save money on hotels.
    你應該來我們家住，而且可以省下住飯店的錢。
  2. You can stay in our spare room, plus my mum will cook for you.
    你可以住我們的客房，而且我媽會煮飯給你吃。
  3. Join our team, plus you'll get a free T-shirt!
    加入我們的隊伍，而且還會送你一件 T 恤！
  4. You can borrow my car, plus I'll fill it up for you.
    你可以借我的車，而且我會幫你把油加滿。

② 推薦 + Plus, + 另一個好處

  1. It's around 200 pesos, so it's pretty cheap for a good game. Plus, it's really addictive!
    它大概 200 披索，以一款好遊戲來說滿便宜的。而且真的很容易上癮！
  2. The café has great coffee. Plus, the cakes are homemade.
    那家咖啡館的咖啡很好喝。而且蛋糕都是自己做的。
  3. This phone has a brilliant camera. Plus, the battery lasts two days.
    這支手機的相機很棒。而且電池可以撐兩天。
  4. You should try the yoga class. Plus, the first lesson is free.
    你應該去上那堂瑜伽課。而且第一堂課免費。

③ 理由 + Plus, + 另一個理由

  1. It will be fun because everyone is going to be there. Plus, the park is the best place for a picnic.
    會很好玩，因為大家都會去。而且公園是野餐最好的地方。
  2. I don't want to go out tonight. I'm really tired. Plus, it's raining.
    我今晚不想出去。我很累。而且外面在下雨。
  3. Let's take the train. It's faster than the bus. Plus, we can read on the way.
    我們搭火車吧。火車比公車快。而且路上可以看書。
  4. I love living here. My job is close. Plus, I've made lots of friends.
    我很喜歡住在這裡。我上班的地方很近。而且我交了很多朋友。

④ 子句 + , plus + 子句

  1. The hotel is right on the beach, plus breakfast is included.
    飯店就在海灘上，而且有附早餐。
  2. My new job pays well, plus I can work from home on Fridays.
    我的新工作薪水不錯，而且星期五可以在家上班。
  3. The course is short, plus you get a certificate at the end.
    這門課很短，而且結束時會拿到證書。
  4. The bike is light, plus it folds up small.
    這台腳踏車很輕，而且可以折得很小。

⑤ 抱怨 + Plus, + 另一個缺點

  1. I can't stand my flat. It's too small. Plus, the neighbours are noisy.
    我受不了我的公寓。太小了。而且鄰居很吵。
  2. I didn't enjoy the trip. The weather was awful. Plus, I lost my camera.
    我這趟旅行玩得不開心。天氣很糟。而且我把相機弄丟了。
  3. We left the restaurant early. The food was cold. Plus, the music was too loud.
    我們提早離開餐廳。菜是冷的。而且音樂太大聲。
  4. I don't want that job. The hours are long. Plus, it's a long way from home.
    我不想要那份工作。工時很長。而且離家很遠。

⑥ 勸說 + Plus, + 補充

  1. You should go to bed early. You have an exam tomorrow. Plus, you look tired.
    你應該早點睡。你明天要考試。而且你看起來很累。
  2. Don't buy a new laptop yet. Yours still works. Plus, prices will drop next month.
    先別買新筆電。你的還能用。而且下個月會降價。
  3. Take an umbrella. It's cloudy. Plus, the forecast says rain later.
    帶把傘吧。天氣陰陰的。而且氣象預報說等一下會下雨。
  4. Walk to work. It's good exercise. Plus, you won't have to find a parking space.
    走路去上班吧。這是很好的運動。而且你不用找停車位。

⑦ 訊息和電子郵件裡的 plus

  1. Thanks for the lovely present! Plus, the card made me laugh.
    謝謝你送的禮物，我好喜歡！而且那張卡片讓我笑了。
  2. Can't wait to see you! Plus, I've got some big news to tell you.
    好期待見到你！而且我有個大消息要告訴你。
  3. The weather here is amazing. Plus, the food is incredible.
    這裡天氣超好。而且東西超好吃。
  4. Sorry I missed your call. I was driving. Plus, my phone was on silent.
    抱歉沒接到你的電話。我在開車。而且我的手機調成靜音了。
  5. The kids loved the zoo, plus they slept all the way home.
    孩子們很喜歡動物園，而且回家的路上全程都在睡。
  6. We found a great flat, plus it's only five minutes from the station.
    我們找到一間很棒的公寓，而且離車站只要五分鐘。
