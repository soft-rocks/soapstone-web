You can eat very well and pay almost nothing.

你可以吃得很好，又幾乎不用花錢。

例句

① almost + 不定代名詞

  1. Almost everyone in my class has a phone.
    我們班上幾乎每個人都有手機。
  2. There was almost nothing in the fridge.
    冰箱裡幾乎什麼都沒有。
  3. Almost nobody uses that old printer.
    幾乎沒有人用那台舊印表機。
  4. She remembers almost everything about her childhood.
    她小時候的事她幾乎都記得。

② nearly + 不定代名詞

  1. Nearly everybody voted for the new plan.
    幾乎每個人都投票支持新方案。
  2. The storm destroyed nearly everything in the garden.
    暴風雨把花園裡的東西幾乎都毀了。
  3. Nearly everyone at the wedding was in tears.
    婚禮上幾乎每個人都哭了。
  4. He knows nearly everybody in the village.
    村子裡的人他幾乎都認識。

③ practically + 不定代名詞

  1. That old sofa cost practically nothing.
    那張舊沙發幾乎沒花錢。
  2. Practically everyone was late because of the snow.
    因為下雪，幾乎每個人都遲到了。
  3. My little cousin believes practically anything you tell him.
    你跟我小表弟說什麼，他幾乎都相信。
  4. Practically nobody came to the meeting.
    幾乎沒有人來開會。

④ absolutely + 不定代名詞

  1. You can ask me absolutely anything.
    你什麼都可以問我。
  2. The thieves took absolutely everything.
    小偷把東西全都拿走了。
  3. Absolutely nobody believed his story.
    完全沒有人相信他的說法。
  4. There's absolutely nothing to do in this town.
    這個小鎮完全沒什麼事可做。
  5. She'd do absolutely anything for her kids.
    為了孩子，她什麼事都願意做。
  6. Absolutely everyone needs a good night's sleep.
    每個人都需要好好睡一覺。

⑤ hardly + 不定代名詞

  1. There's hardly anybody on the beach today.
    今天海灘上幾乎沒人。
  2. We had hardly anything to eat on the trip.
    我們旅途中幾乎沒東西吃。
  3. Hardly anyone reads the local paper now.
    現在幾乎沒有人看地方報紙了。
  4. He said hardly anything at dinner.
    他吃晚飯的時候幾乎沒說話。

⑥ 不同的不定代名詞

  1. Almost everybody in the office takes the train.
    辦公室裡幾乎每個人都搭火車上班。
  2. My little brother is scared of almost everything.
    我弟弟幾乎什麼都怕。
  3. The new manager changed nearly everything.
    新經理幾乎什麼都改了。
  4. I found absolutely nothing wrong with the car.
    我完全沒發現車子有什麼問題。
  5. Almost anything can happen in a final.
    決賽什麼事都可能發生。
  6. We charge almost nothing for delivery.
    我們的運費幾乎不收錢。

⑦ 疑問句和否定句

  1. Did almost everyone pass the test?
    幾乎每個人都考過了嗎？
  2. Is there absolutely nothing we can do?
    我們真的完全沒辦法了嗎？
  3. Was nearly everything sold by noon?
    中午以前東西幾乎都賣完了嗎？
  4. Can you really eat practically anything?
    你真的幾乎什麼都能吃嗎？
