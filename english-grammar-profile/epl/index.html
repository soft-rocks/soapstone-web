例句

① 過去簡單式

  1. After the TV show, she was offered a contract to record a single.
    上完那個電視節目之後，有一家公司給她一份合約，請她錄一首單曲。
  2. My grandfather was awarded a medal for saving a child from the river.
    我爺爺因為從河裡救起一個孩子，得到一枚獎章。
  3. At the border, we were refused entry because our visas were wrong.
    在邊境，我們被拒絕入境，因為簽證有問題。
  4. The workers were promised a pay rise, but it never came.
    工人們被承諾會加薪，可是一直沒有加。
  5. I was charged twenty pounds for a taxi from the station!
    我從車站搭計程車被收了二十英鎊！
  6. When I arrived at the gate, I was handed a map and a ticket.
    我到大門的時候，有人遞給我一張地圖和一張票。

② 現在完成式

  1. I was very happy to hear that you have been offered two jobs, one in a restaurant and one in a museum.
    聽說你得到兩份工作，一份在餐廳，一份在博物館，我很高興。
  2. I felt pleased that your firm has been given some extra money to spend on the cinema.
    我很高興你們公司拿到一筆額外的錢，可以花在電影院上。
  3. Our school has been awarded a prize for its new garden.
    我們學校的新花園得了獎。
  4. My sister has been granted a visa, so she can work in Canada now.
    我妹妹拿到簽證了，所以她現在可以在加拿大工作。
  5. We've been promised a new teacher next term.
    學校答應我們下學期會有新老師。

③ 現在簡單式

  1. At this hotel, guests are given a free breakfast every morning.
    這間旅館的客人每天早上都有免費早餐。
  2. In some countries, students are paid a small amount to study.
    在一些國家，學生讀書可以拿到一點錢。
  3. Children under five are allowed free entry to the zoo.
    五歲以下的孩子可以免費進動物園。
  4. New staff are shown the building on their first day.
    新員工第一天會有人帶他們看這棟大樓。

④ will / won't be

  1. After you register, you will be sent a password by email.
    你註冊之後，會用電子郵件收到一組密碼。
  2. The best student will be awarded a free trip to Paris.
    最好的學生會得到一趟免費的巴黎之旅。
  3. Don't worry. You won't be charged for the delivery.
    別擔心。運費不會跟你收。
  4. Everyone will be given a number at the start of the race.
    比賽開始時每個人都會拿到一個號碼。

⑤ 情態動詞

  1. In my opinion, children should be taught first aid at school.
    我認為學校應該教孩子急救。
  2. Visitors can be lent an umbrella if it rains.
    下雨的話可以借遊客雨傘。
  3. The players must be paid their money by the end of the month.
    球員的錢一定要在月底前付清。
  4. If you ask nicely, you might be given a discount.
    你客氣地問的話，也許會打折。

⑥ 否定句

  1. I was angry because I wasn't given a choice.
    我很生氣，因為沒有人給我選擇。
  2. We haven't been told the date of the exam yet.
    還沒人告訴我們考試的日期。
  3. The people in the old flats weren't offered new homes.
    舊公寓的住戶沒有拿到新房子。
  4. He was denied the chance to speak at the meeting.
    他在會議上沒有得到發言的機會。

⑦ 問句

  1. "Were you given a receipt?" "Yes, it's in my bag."
    有給你收據嗎？有，在我包包裡。
  2. "Have you been offered a seat on the committee?" "Not yet."
    委員會有給你一個位子嗎？還沒有。
  3. "How much were you charged for the repair?" "Fifty pounds."
    修理費收了你多少？五十英鎊。
  4. "Will we be given lunch on the trip?" "Yes, but bring some water."
    旅行有供午餐嗎？有，可是要帶水。
