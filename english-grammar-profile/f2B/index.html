這是讓他們專門來這家店購物的好方法。

例句

① 生意和行銷

  1. A loyalty card is a good way to get customers to shop at this particular store.
    會員卡是讓客人專門來這家店購物的好方法。
  2. We need some new ideas to get young people to visit the museum.
    我們需要一些新點子，讓年輕人來參觀博物館。
  3. Free coffee is a nice way to get people to stay longer in the shop.
    免費咖啡是讓客人在店裡待久一點的好方法。
  4. The festival is great, but it must be difficult to get people to come in the rain.
    音樂節很棒，可是下雨天要讓人來一定很難。
  5. We want to explain the town to our friends, to get them to visit us more often.
    我們想向朋友介紹我們的小鎮，讓他們更常來看我們。

② 家人和朋友

  1. I finally got my brother to help me move the sofa.
    我終於說服我哥哥幫我搬沙發。
  2. I can't get the baby to sleep. She's been crying for an hour.
    我沒辦法讓寶寶睡覺。她已經哭了一個小時了。
  3. My mum got my dad to stop smoking last year.
    我媽媽去年讓我爸爸戒菸了。
  4. How did you get the kids to eat their vegetables?
    你是怎麼讓孩子們吃青菜的？
  5. Try to get Sam to come to the party. He never goes out.
    想辦法讓 Sam 來派對。他從來不出門。
  6. I'll get my sister to pick you up from the station.
    我會叫我姊姊去車站接你。

③ 工作

  1. Can you get someone to fix the printer? It's broken again.
    你可以找人來修印表機嗎？它又壞了。
  2. The manager got everyone to work late on Friday.
    經理讓大家星期五加班。
  3. I'll get my assistant to send you the details.
    我會叫我的助理把細節寄給你。
  4. It took weeks to get the bank to answer my letter.
    花了好幾個星期才讓銀行回覆我的信。

④ 學校

  1. Our teacher got us to write a story about our holidays.
    老師叫我們寫一篇關於假期的故事。
  2. It's hard to get teenagers to read books these days.
    現在很難讓青少年看書。
  3. The coach got the players to run ten laps.
    教練讓球員跑十圈。
  4. She got the whole class to sing a song for the head teacher.
    她讓全班唱一首歌給校長聽。

⑤ 東西也可以當受詞

  1. I can't get the car to start. I think the battery is dead.
    我發動不了車子。我想是電池沒電了。
  2. I finally got the computer to work again.
    我終於讓電腦又能用了。
  3. Can you get this window to open? It's stuck.
    你能把這扇窗戶打開嗎？它卡住了。

⑥ 不同的時態

  1. I've been trying to get him to call his mother for weeks.
    我已經想辦法讓他打電話給他媽媽好幾個星期了。
  2. We're going to get the neighbours to turn their music down.
    我們要讓鄰居把音樂關小聲。
  3. You'll never get her to change her mind.
    你永遠沒辦法讓她改變主意。
  4. If you can get them to agree, we can start next week.
    如果你能讓他們同意，我們下星期就可以開始。

⑦ 否定句

  1. We couldn't get anyone to take the old piano.
    我們找不到人把舊鋼琴搬走。
  2. I didn't get her to sign the form, so we can't send it yet.
    我沒讓她在表格上簽名，所以我們還不能寄出。

⑧ 問句

  1. "How did you get him to say sorry?" "I just asked him nicely."
    你是怎麼讓他道歉的？我只是好好地問他。
  2. "Can you get Lucy to call me back?" "Sure, I'll tell her."
    你可以叫 Lucy 回我電話嗎？好，我會跟她說。
  3. "Did you get the shop to give you your money back?" "Yes, in the end."
    你有讓店家退錢給你嗎？有，最後退了。
