例句

① 店

  1. I left my clothes at the dry cleaner's last week, and I can't collect them.
    我上禮拜把衣服留在乾洗店，現在拿不回來。
  2. Can you get some sausages from the butcher's on your way home?
    你回家路上可以去肉店買點香腸嗎？
  3. I bought this cake at the baker's on the corner.
    這個蛋糕是我在轉角的麵包店買的。
  4. She's at the hairdresser's until four.
    她在美髮店待到四點。
  5. Go to the chemist's and ask for something for your cough.
    去藥局問問有沒有治咳嗽的藥。
  6. The newsagent's sells stamps too.
    那家書報攤也有賣郵票。

② 診所

  1. I have to go to the dentist's. I've got a terrible toothache.
    我得去一趟牙醫診所。我牙齒痛死了。
  2. My son has an appointment at the doctor's at ten.
    我兒子十點在診所有約。
  3. I waited at the doctor's for an hour this morning.
    我今天早上在診所等了一個小時。
  4. She works as a receptionist at the vet's.
    她在獸醫院當櫃台人員。

③ 某個人的家

  1. We're having dinner at Tom's tonight.
    我們今晚在 Tom 家吃晚餐。
  2. I stayed at my aunt's for the summer.
    我暑假住在我阿姨家。
  3. Shall we go back to my place or to Jenny's?
    我們要回我家還是去 Jenny 家？
  4. The kids are sleeping at their grandma's tonight.
    孩子們今晚睡在他們奶奶家。
  5. There's a party at Mark's on Saturday.
    星期六 Mark 家有派對。

④ 複數名詞 + 撇號

  1. We're spending Christmas at my parents' this year.
    我們今年在我爸媽家過聖誕節。
  2. I left my jacket at my grandparents'.
    我把夾克忘在我爺爺奶奶家了。
  3. She's staying at her cousins' in Leeds.
    她住在她表兄弟姊妹在里茲的家。

⑤ 店名

  1. I'll pick you up at eight near McDonald's, OK?
    我八點在麥當勞附近接你，好嗎？
  2. There's a Sainsbury's next to the station.
    車站旁邊有一家 Sainsbury's 超市。
  3. Let's get a burger at McDonald's after the film.
    看完電影我們去麥當勞吃漢堡吧。

⑥ 在 at / to / from 後面

  1. I'm just going to the post office and the baker's.
    我只是要去一趟郵局和麵包店。
  2. I'm calling from the doctor's. Can you pick me up?
    我在診所打給你，你可以來接我嗎？
  3. Dad's gone to the butcher's for the meat.
    爸爸去肉店買肉了。
  4. We ran into our teacher at the chemist's.
    我們在藥局碰到我們老師。

⑦ 問句裡的地方

  1. Where's Mum? Is she still at the baker's?
    媽呢？她還在麵包店嗎？
  2. Have you been to the new hairdresser's on Bridge Street?
    你去過 Bridge 街那家新開的美髮店嗎？
  3. My throat hurts. Is there a chemist's near the hotel?
    我喉嚨痛。飯店附近有藥局嗎？
  4. Can we stop at the butcher's? We need meat for tomorrow.
    我們可以去一下肉店嗎？明天要用的肉還沒買。
  5. Why were you at the doctor's yesterday? Are you OK?
    你昨天為什麼去診所？你還好嗎？
  6. Do you want to come round to Lucy's after school?
    放學後你要不要一起去 Lucy 家？
