I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

我想邀請你星期天來吃晚餐。

would like 後⁠面⁠接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

我想邀請你星期天來吃晚餐。

would like to 比 want to 客氣。

Would you like to 用⁠來邀⁠請別⁠人

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

Would you like to join our quiz team?

你想加入我們的問答隊嗎？

拒⁠絕邀⁠請常說 I'd like to, but ...

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

I'd like to, but I've got a cold.

我很想，可是我感冒了。

否⁠定用 wouldn't like to

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

I wouldn't like to live in a big city.

我不會想住在大城市。

would like 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時，動⁠詞前⁠面加 to

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

I would like visit the old castle tomorrow.

I would like to visit the old castle tomorrow.

would 後⁠面⁠的 like 用原⁠形

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

Grandpa would likes to see the sea.

Grandpa would like to see the sea.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

We would like to booking a table for four.

We would like to book a table for four.

would like 的問⁠句把 would 放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

Do you would like to try my cake?

Would you like to try my cake?

like to 說平⁠常喜⁠歡，would like to 說現⁠在想⁠做

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

like to
平常喜歡

I like to swim before breakfast.

我喜歡早餐前游泳。
would like to
現在想做

I'd like to swim in the lake this afternoon.

我今天下午想去湖裡游泳。

would like 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以直⁠接接名⁠詞

I'd like to invite you to dinner on Sunday.

麻煩給我們一張窗邊的兩人桌。

例句

① I'd like to：邀請

  1. Hi Ben, I'd like to show you our new garden. Are you free on Sunday?
    嗨，Ben，我想帶你看看我們的新花園。你星期天有空嗎？
  2. We'd like to invite you to our wedding in June.
    我們想邀請你參加我們六月的婚禮。
  3. I'd like to take you out for lunch to say thank you.
    我想請你吃午餐，謝謝你。

② I'd like to：說自己想做的事

  1. Good morning. I'd like to open a bank account, please.
    早安。我想開一個銀行帳戶。
  2. I'd like to meet you to give you some information about the shops near your hotel.
    我想跟你見個面，給你一些飯店附近商店的資訊。
  3. "Can I help you?" "Yes, I'd like to try on these jeans."
    需要幫忙嗎？好，我想試穿這件牛仔褲。
  4. I'd like to visit my grandparents this weekend.
    我這個週末想去看我爺爺奶奶。
  5. After university, I'd like to work abroad for a year.
    大學畢業以後，我想去國外工作一年。

③ 主詞是第三人稱單數或複數

  1. My mother would like to speak to the manager.
    我媽想跟經理說話。
  2. The students would like to ask you some questions.
    學生們想問你幾個問題。
  3. Our son would like to learn the guitar.
    我們兒子想學吉他。
  4. Tom says he'd like to come with us to the lake.
    Tom 說他想跟我們一起去湖邊。

④ 正式的信和電話

  1. "Good evening, Sunny Hostel." "Hello. I would like to cancel a booking for tomorrow."
    晚安，Sunny Hostel。你好。我想取消明天的訂房。
  2. Dear Ms Wang, I would like to apply for the job of shop assistant.
    王小姐您好，我想應徵店員的工作。
  3. "Good morning, City Hotel. How can I help you?" "Hello, I'd like to change my booking."
    早安，City Hotel，需要什麼服務？你好，我想更改我的訂房。
  4. We would like to tell you about our new opening hours.
    我們想告訴您我們新的營業時間。

⑤ Would you like to ...?：邀請

  1. "Would you like to come to my party on Saturday?" "Yes, I'd love to!"
    你星期六想來我的派對嗎？好啊，我很想去！
  2. Would you like to see my photos from Japan?
    你想看我在日本拍的照片嗎？
  3. "Would you like to sit down?" "Thanks, I'm fine."
    你想坐下嗎？謝謝，我沒關係。
  4. Would your children like to play in the garden?
    你的孩子們想去院子裡玩嗎？
  5. "Would you like to have a coffee after work?" "Sure, where?"
    下班後想喝杯咖啡嗎？好啊，去哪裡？

⑥ 問對方想做什麼

  1. "What would you like to do tonight?" "Let's stay in and watch a film."
    你今晚想做什麼？我們待在家看電影吧。
  2. "Where would you like to go for your holiday?" "Somewhere warm."
    你想去哪裡度假？暖和的地方。
  3. "What would you like to eat?" "Something light, please."
    你想吃什麼？清淡一點的。

⑦ 回答邀請

  1. "Would you like to join our team?" "I'd like to, but I don't have time."
    你想加入我們的隊嗎？我很想，可是我沒時間。
  2. "Do you want to come to the cinema?" "Yes, I'd like to."
    你想去看電影嗎？好啊，我想去。
  3. "Would you like to stay for dinner?" "I'd like to, but my mum is waiting for me."
    你要留下來吃晚餐嗎？我很想，可是我媽在等我。

⑧ 否定：wouldn't like to

  1. I wouldn't like to live in a big city. It's too noisy.
    我不會想住在大城市。太吵了。
  2. My sister goes to bed at nine, so she wouldn't like to work at night.
    我姊姊九點就睡了，所以她不會想上夜班。
  3. It's a very cold country. I wouldn't like to go there in winter.
    那是個很冷的國家。我不會想冬天去那裡。
  4. "Would you like to be famous?" "No, I wouldn't like to."
    你想出名嗎？不，我不想。
