We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.

我們不斷地問自己：我們從哪裡來，為什麼會在這裡。

persistently 放⁠在 be 和動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 中⁠間

我們不斷地問自己：我們從哪裡來，為什麼會在這裡。

正⁠式⁠的文⁠字用更⁠多種頻⁠率副⁠詞

We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.

persistently

She is persistently ignoring my emails.

她一直不理我的 email。
perpetually

He's perpetually running late.

他永遠都在遲到。
endlessly

The kids are endlessly arguing over the remote.

孩子們為了遙控器吵個沒完。
repeatedly

The company is repeatedly breaking its promises.

那家公司一次又一次違背承諾。

incessantly 常放⁠在動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 後⁠面

We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.

incessantly

The dog next door is barking incessantly.

隔壁的狗叫個不停。

其他副詞多半放在動詞⁠-⁠ing 前面。

副⁠詞放⁠在 be 後⁠面

We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.

She persistently is ignoring my advice.

She is persistently ignoring my advice.

修⁠飾動⁠詞用副⁠詞 perpetually，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞

We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.

He is perpetual complaining about his salary.

He is perpetually complaining about his salary.

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式要⁠有 am、is 或 are

We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.

The media endlessly repeating the same story.

The media are endlessly repeating the same story.

例句

① be persistently + 動詞-ing

  1. It's human nature. We are persistently asking ourselves questions about where we are from and why we are here.
    這是人的天性。我們不斷地問自己，我們從哪裡來，為什麼會在這裡。
  2. I've written to him three times. He's persistently ignoring my emails.
    我寫信給他三次了。他一直不理我的 email。
  3. We've been cold for weeks. The landlord is persistently refusing to fix the heating.
    我們冷了好幾個星期。房東一直拒絕修暖氣。
  4. Some students are persistently arriving late, so we're locking the door at nine.
    有些學生一直遲到，所以我們九點就鎖門。
  5. Nothing ever gets decided. The committee is persistently avoiding the real issue.
    什麼事都決定不了。委員會一直在迴避真正的問題。

② be perpetually + 動詞-ing

  1. Don't wait for Tim. He's perpetually running late.
    別等 Tim 了。他永遠都在遲到。
  2. My uncle is perpetually complaining about his salary, but he never looks for another job.
    我舅舅永遠在抱怨薪水，可是從來不找別的工作。
  3. In this job you're perpetually fighting against deadlines.
    做這份工作，你永遠都在跟截止日期拚。
  4. Politicians are perpetually promising change before an election.
    政治人物每到選舉前就說要改變，永遠都一樣。
  5. The poor man is perpetually apologising for things that aren't his fault.
    那個可憐的人永遠在為不是他的錯道歉。

③ be endlessly + 動詞-ing

  1. Turn the TV off. The kids are endlessly arguing over the remote.
    把電視關掉。孩子們為了遙控器吵個沒完。
  2. I've stopped watching. The news channels are endlessly repeating the same story.
    我不看了。新聞台沒完沒了地重播同一則新聞。
  3. He's endlessly talking about his trip to Japan. We've heard it all.
    他沒完沒了地講他去日本的事。我們全都聽過了。
  4. Scientists are endlessly searching for a cure for the common cold.
    科學家一直在尋找治感冒的方法。

④ be repeatedly + 動詞-ing

  1. No wonder people are leaving. The company is repeatedly breaking its promises to its staff.
    難怪大家都在離職。那家公司一次又一次違背對員工的承諾。
  2. We're repeatedly reminding residents not to park on the grass.
    我們一再提醒住戶不要把車停在草地上。
  3. Look at this again. The same mistake is repeatedly appearing in your essays.
    你再看一次這個。同一個錯誤一再出現在你的作文裡。
  4. We need to fix this fast. Customers are repeatedly reporting problems with the new update.
    我們得趕快修好。客戶一再回報新版更新的問題。
  5. My son is repeatedly losing his school bag. This is the third one this year.
    我兒子一再弄丟書包。這已經是今年第三個了。

⑤ be permanently + 動詞-ing

  1. During life we are permanently learning and increasing our knowledge.
    人一生都在學習，不斷增加知識。
  2. The hospital needs more staff. Doctors in this ward are permanently working under pressure.
    醫院需要更多人手。這個病房的醫生一直在壓力下工作。
  3. Our planet is permanently changing, even if we can't see it.
    我們的地球一直在變，就算我們看不出來。

⑥ be + 動詞-ing + incessantly

  1. I can't sleep. The dog next door is barking incessantly.
    我睡不著。隔壁的狗叫個不停。
  2. My phone is buzzing incessantly with messages from the group chat.
    群組的訊息讓我的手機震個不停。
  3. The baby has a cold and is crying incessantly.
    寶寶感冒了，一直哭個不停。
  4. She's talking incessantly about the wedding. It's all she thinks about.
    她不停地講婚禮的事。她滿腦子都是這件事。

⑦ 疑問句

  1. The park is still full of rubbish. Why is the council repeatedly ignoring our complaints?
    公園還是滿地垃圾。市議會為什麼一再無視我們的投訴？
  2. Why are you perpetually apologising? It's fine.
    你為什麼老是在道歉？沒關係的。
  3. We shout at our kids, just like our parents did. Are we endlessly repeating their mistakes?
    我們跟我們的父母一樣對孩子大吼。我們是不是一直在重複他們犯過的錯？
  4. My boss is getting annoyed. Why is he persistently calling me at work?
    我老闆開始不高興了。他為什麼一直打電話到我公司？
