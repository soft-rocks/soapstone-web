The mobile market is growing incredibly fast in Britain.

英國的手機市場成長得快得驚人。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在被修⁠飾⁠的副⁠詞前⁠面

英國的手機市場成長得快得驚人。

enough 放⁠在被修⁠飾⁠的副⁠詞後⁠面

你說話不夠大聲。

各⁠個程⁠度副⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

The mobile market is growing incredibly fast in Britain.

incredibly、remarkably、extraordinarily非常，程度高得讓人意外
surprisingly、unusually出乎意料地，比平常更
quite相當
reasonably、fairly還算，程度中等
relatively和別的比起來還算
almost、nearly、virtually幾乎，差一點就到全部

被修⁠飾⁠的副⁠詞可⁠以是情⁠狀、頻⁠率、時⁠間副⁠詞

The mobile market is growing incredibly fast in Britain.

情狀副詞

Our fund-raising day went reasonably well.

我們的募款日辦得還算順利。
頻率副詞

We eat out fairly often these days.

我們最近滿常外食的。
時間副詞

She arrived unusually early this morning.

她今天早上比平常早到。

表⁠示全⁠部⁠的副⁠詞前⁠面用 almost、nearly、virtually

年輕人的態度幾乎完全變了。

修⁠飾副⁠詞⁠的字也⁠要用副⁠詞

The mobile market is growing incredibly fast in Britain.

The market is growing incredible fast.

The market is growing incredibly fast.

修⁠飾動⁠詞用 well，不⁠用 good

The day went reasonably good.

The day went reasonably well.

completely 前⁠面不⁠加 very

The town has very completely changed.

The town has almost completely changed.

enough 放⁠在副⁠詞後⁠面

She didn't drive enough carefully.

She didn't drive carefully enough.

例句

① incredibly、remarkably、extraordinarily、surprisingly + 情狀副詞

  1. The new staff learned the system remarkably quickly.
    新來的員工學這套系統學得出奇地快。
  2. Your daughter plays the violin incredibly well.
    你女兒小提琴拉得好得不得了。
  3. That shrewd old farmer bargains extraordinarily hard.
    那個精明的老農夫殺價殺得非常兇。
  4. The meeting went surprisingly smoothly.
    會議進行得出乎意料地順利。
  5. Rents have risen incredibly sharply this year.
    今年房租漲得非常兇。

② reasonably、fairly、relatively + 情狀副詞

  1. Our fund-raising day went reasonably well.
    我們的募款日辦得還算順利。
  2. He speaks Japanese fairly fluently.
    他日文說得相當流利。
  3. The software runs relatively smoothly on older laptops.
    這個軟體在舊筆電上跑得還算順。
  4. I thought you handled the complaint reasonably calmly.
    我覺得你處理那件客訴還算冷靜。
  5. We played fairly badly in the first half.
    我們上半場打得滿差的。

③ 程度副詞 + 頻率副詞

  1. Moreover, I use those negotiation techniques quite frequently.
    而且，那些談判技巧我還滿常用的。
  2. We eat out fairly often these days.
    我們最近滿常外食的。
  3. My uncle visits us relatively rarely.
    我叔叔比較少來看我們。
  4. Our neighbours complain about the dog remarkably seldom.
    鄰居抱怨我們家狗的次數少得出奇。
  5. The trains here break down surprisingly often.
    這裡的火車故障得出奇頻繁。

④ 程度副詞 + 時間副詞

  1. She arrived unusually early this morning.
    她今天早上比平常早到。
  2. We'll know the results fairly soon.
    我們很快就會知道結果了。
  3. That bakery opened relatively recently.
    那家麵包店是最近才開的。
  4. The builders finished the job remarkably early.
    工人們提早很多就把工程做完了。
  5. I only found out about it quite recently.
    我也是最近才知道這件事的。

⑤ almost、nearly、virtually + 表示全部的副詞

  1. The snow had nearly completely melted by noon.
    到了中午，雪幾乎全融了。
  2. My grandfather is virtually always in the garden.
    我爺爺幾乎都待在花園裡。
  3. She almost never offends anyone.
    她幾乎從不得罪人。
  4. I agree with you almost entirely.
    我幾乎完全同意你的看法。
  5. We've almost always paid the rent on time.
    我們幾乎每次都準時付房租。
  6. He has virtually never missed a match.
    他幾乎一場比賽都沒錯過。

⑥ 副詞 + enough

  1. She didn't explain the rules clearly enough.
    她規則解釋得不夠清楚。
  2. Are we leaving early enough to catch the train?
    我們這時候出門，趕得上火車嗎？
  3. He ran fast enough to win a medal.
    他跑得夠快，拿到了獎牌。
  4. Our stingy landlord never fixes anything quickly enough.
    我們那個小氣的房東修東西從來都不夠快。
  5. Could you speak slowly enough for the children to follow?
    你可以說得慢一點，讓孩子們跟得上嗎？
