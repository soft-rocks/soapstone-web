It's important that you eat healthy food.

你要吃健康的食物，這很重要。

It + is / was + important + (that) + 主⁠詞 + 動⁠詞

it形式主詞，指後面的子句。
important重要。
that可以省略。
子句重要的那件事，常用現在簡單式。

給建⁠議

It's important that you eat healthy food.

例句

It's important that you take breaks.

你要記得休息，這很重要。

It's important that you ... 是很常見的建議方式。比 You must ... 委婉。

前⁠面加 I think

例句

I think it's important that we listen to each other.

我覺得我們互相傾聽很重要。

I think 讓建議更委婉。

句⁠子要⁠有主⁠詞

Is important that you sleep well.

It's important that you sleep well.

that 子⁠句要⁠有自⁠己⁠的主⁠詞

It's important that you eat healthy food.

It's important that eat healthy food.

It's important that you eat healthy food.

that 子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

It's important that you eat healthy food.

It's important that you to drink water.

It's important that you drink water.

例句

① It's important that + you + 動詞

  1. It's important that you take breaks and don't work all the time.
    你要休息，不要一直工作，這很重要。
  2. It's important that you drink enough water in hot weather.
    天氣熱的時候要喝足夠的水，這很重要。
  3. It's important that you arrive on time for the interview.
    面試要準時到，這很重要。
  4. It's important that you tell your teacher about the problem.
    你要把這個問題告訴老師，這很重要。
  5. It's important that you wear a helmet on your bike.
    騎腳踏車要戴安全帽，這很重要。
  6. It's important that you read the instructions first.
    先讀說明很重要。
  7. It's important that you get enough sleep before the exam.
    考試前睡飽很重要。
  8. It's important that you keep your passport in a safe place.
    護照要放在安全的地方，這很重要。
  9. It's important that you don't forget your medicine.
    別忘了吃藥，這很重要。

② It's important that + 其他主詞 + 動詞

  1. It's important that children learn to swim.
    小孩學游泳很重要。
  2. It's important that everyone knows the fire exits.
    每個人都知道逃生出口在哪裡很重要。
  3. It's important that we leave before the traffic gets bad.
    在交通變糟之前出發很重要。
  4. It's important that the food is fresh.
    食物新鮮很重要。
  5. It's important that students practise speaking every day.
    學生每天練習說話很重要。
  6. It's important that the team trains together.
    團隊一起練習很重要。
  7. It's important that parents talk to their children.
    父母跟孩子聊天很重要。
  8. It's important that the room is quiet during the test.
    考試時教室保持安靜很重要。
  9. It's important that we recycle our rubbish.
    垃圾回收很重要。
  10. It's important that your boss knows about your plans.
    讓你老闆知道你的計畫很重要。

③ I think it's important that + 子句

  1. I think it's important that you look at each point before you decide.
    我覺得你做決定前把每一點都看過很重要。
  2. I think it's important that we help our neighbours.
    我覺得幫助鄰居很重要。
  3. I think it's important that young people vote.
    我覺得年輕人去投票很重要。
  4. I think it's important that families eat together.
    我覺得家人一起吃飯很重要。
  5. I think it's important that you enjoy your job.
    我覺得喜歡自己的工作很重要。
  6. My dad thinks it's important that I learn another language.
    我爸覺得我學另一種語言很重要。

④ It was / It is very important that + 子句

  1. It was important that we found the dog before dark.
    天黑前找到那隻狗很重要。
  2. It is very important that you keep this secret.
    你要保守這個祕密，這非常重要。
  3. It was important that everyone arrived early.
    每個人都早點到很重要。
  4. It is very important that the doors stay closed.
    門一直關著非常重要。
  5. It was really important that she passed that exam.
    她通過那次考試真的很重要。
  6. It is very important that nobody touches these wires.
    非常重要的是，誰都不能碰這些電線。
