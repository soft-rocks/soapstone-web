She didn't dare think about him.

她不敢去想他。

主⁠詞 + didn't dare + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

didn't dare、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She didn't dare think about him.

didn't dare過去不敢。
原形動詞不敢做的事。to 可以省略，省略的寫法在小說和正式的文章裡常見。

寫恐⁠懼和緊⁠張

例句

We didn't dare move our feet.

我們連腳都不敢動。

這個句型常寫人在危險或緊張的情況下的反應。

後⁠面⁠接 because 或 in case

例句

I didn't dare speak in case he got angry.

我不敢說話，怕他生氣。

不敢的原因常放在後面。

didn't 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞都用原⁠形

She didn't dare think about him.

She didn't dare thought about him.

She didn't dare think about him.

didn't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 dare

She didn't dare think about him.

We didn't dared move.

We didn't dare move.

dare 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，或接 to 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She didn't dare think about him.

He didn't dare looking down.

He didn't dare look down.

例句

① 主詞 + didn't dare + 原形動詞：害怕

  1. She didn't dare think about what the test might show.
    她不敢去想檢查結果可能會是什麼。
  2. We didn't dare move until the guard walked away.
    警衛走開之前，我們都不敢動。
  3. The boy didn't dare open his eyes during the storm.
    暴風雨的時候，那個男孩不敢睜開眼睛。
  4. I didn't dare breathe when the bear walked past our tent.
    熊走過我們帳篷的時候，我連呼吸都不敢。
  5. He didn't dare look down from the edge of the cliff.
    他站在懸崖邊不敢往下看。
  6. They didn't dare make a sound while the soldiers searched the house.
    士兵搜房子的時候，他們一點聲音都不敢出。
  7. She didn't dare turn around, because she heard footsteps behind her.
    她不敢回頭，因為她聽到身後有腳步聲。
  8. The children didn't dare go near the old well.
    孩子們不敢靠近那口老井。
  9. I didn't dare check my phone until the plane landed.
    飛機降落之前，我不敢看手機。
  10. We didn't dare walk on the ice in case it broke.
    我們不敢走在冰上，怕冰會裂開。

② 主詞 + didn't dare + 原形動詞：不敢面對

  1. She didn't dare tell her husband about the letter.
    她不敢跟丈夫說那封信的事。
  2. He didn't dare ask what had happened to his brother.
    他不敢問他哥哥發生了什麼事。
  3. I didn't dare imagine what the doctor would say.
    我不敢想像醫生會說什麼。
  4. They didn't dare hope for good news.
    他們不敢期待好消息。
  5. My mother didn't dare read the rest of the letter.
    我媽媽不敢讀信的其他部分。
  6. He didn't dare admit that he had lost the money.
    他不敢承認他把錢弄丟了。
  7. We didn't dare speak about the accident at dinner.
    我們不敢在晚餐時談那場意外。
  8. She didn't dare open the envelope with her exam results.
    她不敢打開裝著考試成績的信封。
  9. He didn't dare call her after their argument.
    吵架之後，他不敢打電話給她。
  10. I didn't dare say what I really thought.
    我不敢說出我真正的想法。

③ didn't dare + 原形動詞 + in case / because ...

  1. I didn't dare speak in case he got angry again.
    我不敢說話，怕他又生氣。
  2. We didn't dare leave the house because of the wolves.
    因為有狼，我們不敢離開房子。
  3. She didn't dare sleep in case the baby woke up.
    她不敢睡，怕寶寶醒來。
  4. He didn't dare stop running in case they caught him.
    他不敢停下來，怕被他們抓到。
  5. I didn't dare touch anything because it all looked so expensive.
    我什麼都不敢碰，因為看起來都很貴。
  6. They didn't dare light a fire in case someone saw the smoke.
    他們不敢生火，怕有人看到煙。

④ didn't dare to + 原形動詞：加 to 的寫法

  1. She didn't dare to look at the photographs.
    她不敢看那些照片。
  2. We didn't dare to go back into the burning building.
    我們不敢回到失火的大樓裡。
  3. He didn't dare to complain about the food.
    他不敢抱怨那些食物。
  4. I didn't dare to ask for more money.
    我不敢要求更多錢。
  5. They didn't dare to cross the bridge in the wind.
    起風的時候，他們不敢過那座橋。
