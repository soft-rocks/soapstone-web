This weekend I have to go to a party.

這個週末我得去參加一個派對。

I / you / we / they + have to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

have to / has to、have got to / has got to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

have to / has to必須。
have got to / has got to必須。比較口語，常用在英式英文。
原形動詞必須做的事。

縮⁠寫

例句

We've got to bring two pencils.

我們必須帶兩支鉛筆。
例句

She's got to leave at six.

她必須六點離開。

have got to 常縮寫成 've got to，has got to 常縮寫成 's got to。

過⁠去和未⁠來

例句

I had to wait an hour.

我必須等一個小時。
例句

You'll have to ask your mum.

你必須問你媽媽。

過去用 had to。未來用 will have to。

主⁠詞是 he、she、it 或單⁠數名⁠詞，用 has to

My brother have to work on Saturdays.

My brother has to work on Saturdays.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This weekend I have to go to a party.

I have to cleaning my room.

I have to clean my room.

have got 後⁠面要接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

This weekend I have to go to a party.

You've got bring a towel.

You've got to bring a towel.

例句

① I / you / we / they + have to + 原形動詞

  1. I have to be at the dentist's at nine tomorrow.
    我明天九點必須到牙醫診所。
  2. You have to bring your swimming things on Friday.
    星期五你必須帶游泳用品。
  3. We have to take our shoes off when we go into the temple.
    我們進寺廟的時候必須脫鞋。
  4. They have to wear a uniform at their new school.
    他們在新學校必須穿制服。
  5. I have to finish my homework before I can play football.
    我必須先寫完功課才能去踢足球。
  6. On Sundays we have to visit my grandparents.
    星期天我們必須去看我的祖父母。
  7. You have to turn left at the bank to find the cafe.
    要找那家咖啡館，你必須在銀行左轉。

② he / she / it + has to + 原形動詞

  1. We'll meet at four because my mum has to go to the shopping centre then.
    我們四點見面，因為我媽媽那時候必須去購物中心。
  2. My dad has to get up at five for work.
    我爸爸為了上班必須五點起床。
  3. My little sister has to go to bed at eight.
    我妹妹必須八點上床睡覺。
  4. Our teacher has to mark sixty tests this weekend.
    我們老師這個週末必須改六十份考卷。
  5. The dog has to go out twice a day.
    那隻狗一天必須出去兩次。
  6. Tom has to take the bus because his bike is broken.
    湯姆必須搭公車，因為他的腳踏車壞了。

③ 主詞 + have got to / has got to + 原形動詞

  1. For the art lessons we've got to bring a rubber, two pencils and some coloured pencils.
    上美術課我們必須帶一塊橡皮擦、兩支鉛筆和一些色鉛筆。
  2. It's going to be very sunny, so you've got to bring a cap.
    天氣會很晴朗，所以你必須帶一頂帽子。
  3. I've got to phone my mum. She's waiting for me.
    我必須打電話給我媽媽。她在等我。
  4. She's got to practise the piano every evening.
    她每天晚上必須練鋼琴。
  5. We've got to buy a present for Jack's birthday.
    我們必須幫傑克買生日禮物。
  6. He's got to leave early to catch his train.
    他必須早點走才趕得上火車。
  7. They've got to tidy the classroom before they go home.
    他們回家前必須整理教室。

④ had to + 原形動詞：過去必須

  1. Yesterday I had to go back home for my keys.
    昨天我必須回家拿鑰匙。
  2. We had to walk home because it was too late for a taxi.
    太晚叫不到計程車，我們只好走路回家。
  3. She had to stay in bed for a week when she broke her leg.
    她摔斷腿的時候必須在床上躺一個星期。
  4. Last year my brother had to share a room with me.
    去年我哥哥必須跟我住同一個房間。
  5. We had to leave the beach early because of the rain.
    因為下雨，我們必須提早離開海灘。

⑤ will have to + 原形動詞：未來必須

  1. If you want to come camping, you'll have to ask your mum.
    如果你想來露營，你必須問你媽媽。
  2. We'll have to get up early to see the sunrise.
    我們必須早起才看得到日出。
  3. The shop is closed, so I'll have to buy milk tomorrow.
    商店關了，所以我必須明天再買牛奶。
  4. You'll have to wear warm clothes. It's cold up the mountain.
    你必須穿保暖的衣服。山上很冷。
  5. Next year she'll have to take the train to school.
    明年她必須搭火車上學。
  6. If it rains, we'll have to stay inside.
    如果下雨，我們必須待在室內。
