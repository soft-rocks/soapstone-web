I have to travel to Argentina for health reasons. What shall I do with next week's work?

她的婚禮我該買什麼送她？

What / Which / Where + shall + I + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞？

原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

原形動詞不知道怎麼做的事。

問選⁠擇

例句

I need to get to Bristol by ten tomorrow. Shall I take the bus or the train?

我明天十點前要到 Bristol。我該搭公車還是火車？

Shall I … or …？ 請對方從兩個選擇中挑一個。

疑⁠問⁠詞後⁠面先放 shall，再放主⁠詞

I have to travel to Argentina for health reasons. What shall I do with next week's work?

It's Mum's birthday on Sunday. What I shall buy for her?

It's Mum's birthday on Sunday. What shall I buy for her?

shall 的問⁠句不⁠用 do

I have to travel to Argentina for health reasons. What shall I do with next week's work?

I've locked my keys in the car. What do I shall do?

I've locked my keys in the car. What shall I do?

shall 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I have to travel to Argentina for health reasons. What shall I do with next week's work?

The wedding is at four. Which dress shall I to wear?

The wedding is at four. Which dress shall I wear?

例句

① What shall I do + with / about + 名詞？：請老師和上司給建議

  1. I have to go to Argentina for health reasons. What shall I do with next week's work?
    我因為健康的原因必須去阿根廷。下星期的作業我該怎麼辦？
  2. I've lost my library card. What shall I do about the books I borrowed?
    我的借書證掉了。我借的書該怎麼辦？
  3. The client hasn't paid yet. What shall I do about the invoice?
    客戶還沒付錢。這張發票我該怎麼處理？
  4. My flight has been cancelled. What shall I do about the hotel booking?
    我的班機取消了。飯店的預約我該怎麼辦？（被動）
  5. I missed the exam because I was ill. What shall I do now?
    我生病錯過了考試。我現在該怎麼辦？
  6. The printer has broken again. What shall I do with these documents?
    印表機又壞了。這些文件我該怎麼處理？

② What shall I + buy / give / get + 人？：問送什麼禮物

  1. My best friend is getting married in June. What shall I buy for her wedding?
    我最好的朋友六月要結婚。我該買什麼給她當結婚禮物？
  2. It's my dad's fiftieth birthday. What shall I give him?
    我爸爸五十歲生日。我該送他什麼？
  3. My teacher is leaving the school. What shall I get her as a goodbye present?
    我的老師要離開學校了。我該送她什麼道別禮物？
  4. My neighbour helped me move house. What shall I buy him to say thank you?
    我的鄰居幫我搬家。我該買什麼給他表示感謝？
  5. My sister has just had a baby. What shall I take when I visit?
    我姊姊剛生了寶寶。我去看她的時候該帶什麼？
  6. I'm invited to dinner at my boss's house. What shall I bring?
    我被邀請去老闆家吃晚餐。我該帶什麼？

③ Which / Where + shall I + 原形動詞？：問哪一個

  1. Both universities have offered me a place. Which one shall I choose?
    兩所大學都錄取我了。我該選哪一所？
  2. I've got two job interviews on the same day. Which shall I go to?
    我同一天有兩個面試。我該去哪一個？
  3. I want to learn a new language. Which shall I start with, French or Spanish?
    我想學一種新語言。我該從哪一個開始，法文還是西班牙文？
  4. I'm visiting Japan for a week. Where shall I stay in Tokyo?
    我要去日本一個星期。我在東京該住哪裡？
  5. I've saved some money. Where shall I put it?
    我存了一些錢。我該把它存在哪裡？
  6. There are three dresses I like. Which shall I wear to the party?
    我喜歡三件洋裝。我該穿哪一件去派對？

④ Shall I + 原形動詞 + or + 原形動詞？：從兩個選擇中挑

  1. It's a long way to the airport. Shall I take a taxi or go by train?
    去機場很遠。我該搭計程車還是坐火車？
  2. My old phone still works. Shall I keep it or buy a new one?
    我的舊手機還能用。我該留著還是買一支新的？
  3. He hasn't answered my message. Shall I call him or wait?
    他沒回我的訊息。我該打給他還是等？
  4. I'm not sure about this job offer. Shall I accept it or say no?
    我對這個工作機會不太確定。我該接受還是拒絕？
  5. The course starts in September. Shall I apply now or next year?
    課程九月開始。我該現在申請還是明年？
  6. My hair is getting long. Shall I cut it short or let it grow?
    我的頭髮越來越長。我該剪短還是留長？

⑤ Shall I + 原形動詞？：問對方覺得該不該做

  1. She's upset with me. Shall I phone her and say sorry?
    她在生我的氣。我該打電話給她道歉嗎？
  2. My exam results were bad. Shall I tell my parents tonight?
    我的考試成績很差。我該今天晚上告訴我爸媽嗎？
  3. The restaurant overcharged us. Shall I complain to the manager?
    那家餐廳多收了我們錢。我該向經理投訴嗎？
  4. I've found a wallet in the street. Shall I take it to the police?
    我在路上撿到一個皮夾。我該拿去警察局嗎？
  5. My friend wants to borrow money again. Shall I lend it to her?
    我朋友又想跟我借錢。我該借她嗎？
  6. My cat hasn't eaten for two days. Shall I take her to the vet?
    我的貓兩天沒吃東西了。我該帶她去看獸醫嗎？
  7. Nobody has replied to my application. Shall I write to them again?
    沒有人回覆我的申請。我該再寫信給他們嗎？
