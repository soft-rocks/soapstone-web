原話⁠的 tomorrow morning 換⁠成 the next morning

At the airport they told him to come the next morning.

原話

「明天早上再來。」
轉述

They told him to come the next morning.

他們叫他隔天早上再來。

轉⁠述說⁠出⁠的⁠事，接 that 子⁠句，will 換⁠成 would

例句

① tomorrow → the next day / the following day

  1. The mechanic said my car would be ready the next day.
    技師說我的車隔天就會修好。
  2. My mum told me she would bake a cake the following day.
    我媽跟我說她隔天要烤蛋糕。
  3. The airline said our bags would arrive the next day.
    航空公司說我們的行李隔天會到。
  4. Leo promised he would return the book the following day.
    Leo 答應隔天會把書還來。
  5. The hotel told us the pool would be closed the next day.
    飯店跟我們說泳池隔天不開放。

② tomorrow morning / tomorrow night → the next morning / the following night

  1. The doctor said the operation would be the next morning.
    醫生說手術在隔天早上。
  2. They told us the ferry would leave early the next morning.
    他們跟我們說渡輪隔天一早就開。
  3. My dad said he would drive me to the station the following morning.
    我爸說他隔天早上會開車送我去車站。
  4. Sam said the band was playing in town the following night.
    Sam 說那個樂團隔天晚上在城裡表演。
  5. The guide said the sky would be clear the next night.
    導遊說隔天晚上會是晴天。

③ next week / month / year → the following week / month / year

  1. The company said I would get my reimbursement the following month.
    公司說我墊的錢隔月會退給我。
  2. She told me she was starting a new job the following week.
    她跟我說她隔週要開始新工作。
  3. His lawyer said he would receive his inheritance the following year.
    他的律師說他隔年會拿到遺產。
  4. The landlord told us the rent would go up the following month.
    房東跟我們說房租隔月要漲。
  5. They said the new bridge would be finished the following year.
    他們說新橋隔年會完工。
  6. My teacher said we would start a new book the following week.
    老師說我們隔週要換新課本。

④ told + 聽的人 + to + 動詞 + 時間詞

  1. The teacher told us to bring our swimsuits the next day.
    老師叫我們隔天帶泳衣來。
  2. The bank told me to call back the following morning.
    銀行叫我隔天早上再打來。
  3. My boss told me to take the next day off.
    我老闆叫我隔天休假。
  4. The doctor told him not to go to work the following week.
    醫生叫他隔週別去上班。
  5. They told the students to meet at the gate the next morning.
    他們叫學生隔天早上在校門口集合。

⑤ 時間還沒到，時間詞和時態照原話

  1. My sister said she's flying to Tokyo next week.
    我姊姊說她下週要飛東京。
  2. The weather forecast said it will snow tomorrow.
    氣象預報說明天會下雪。
  3. Ben told me he'll be at the party tonight.
    Ben 跟我說他今晚會來派對。
  4. The school said the new term starts next Monday.
    學校說新學期下週一開始。
  5. Grandma said she's going to visit us next month.
    奶奶說她下個月要來看我們。

⑥ 時間還沒到，will 換成 would

  1. Mark said he would send me the photos tomorrow.
    Mark 說他明天會把照片傳給我。
  2. Anna told me she would finish the report next week.
    Anna 跟我說她下週會把報告寫完。
  3. The coach said I would be in the team next season.
    教練說我下一季會進球隊。

⑦ 時間還沒到，if 子句照原話

  1. The coach said that if I train hard, I'll play in the next match.
    教練說如果我努力練習，下一場比賽就能上場。
  2. My mum said that if it's sunny tomorrow, we'll go to the beach.
    我媽說如果明天天氣好，我們就去海邊。
