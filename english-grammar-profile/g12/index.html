very 放⁠在程⁠度副⁠詞前⁠面

I like my job very much.

我很喜歡我的工作。

very 放⁠在時⁠間副⁠詞前⁠面

I like my job very much.

希望你很快就會好起來。

常⁠見⁠的組⁠合

I like my job very much.

very much很，非常，修飾 like、love、enjoy 這類動詞
very soon很快，馬上
very often很常
very early很早
very late很晚

否⁠定⁠句⁠的 not very often 是不⁠常

I like my job very much.

我不常看電視。

very 修⁠飾動⁠詞要加 much

I like my job very much.

I enjoyed the concert very.

I enjoyed the concert very much.

very 不⁠修⁠飾 a lot

I like my job very much.

Thank you very a lot.

Thank you very much.

soon 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠ly

The bus will come very soonly.

The bus will come very soon.

表⁠示馬⁠上用 right now

Please call me very now.

Please call me right now.

例句

① very much

  1. We enjoyed our holiday in Spain very much.
    我們在西班牙的假期玩得很開心。
  2. My little sister wants a pet rabbit very much.
    我妹妹非常想要養一隻兔子。
  3. Thank you very much for your help.
    非常謝謝你的幫忙。
  4. I appreciate your kind letter very much.
    我非常感謝你那封貼心的信。
  5. Our whole family enjoyed the boat trip around the island very much.
    我們全家都很喜歡那趟環島的坐船之旅。
  6. Do you miss your family very much?
    你很想念你的家人嗎？

② very soon

  1. The film starts very soon. Let's go in.
    電影馬上就要開始了，我們進去吧。
  2. I'll write to you again very soon.
    我很快會再寫信給你。
  3. My cousin is coming to visit very soon.
    我表姊很快就要來拜訪了。
  4. The shop is closing very soon.
    那家店馬上就要關門了。
  5. We have to leave very soon.
    我們很快就得走了。

③ very often

  1. My parents don't go out for dinner very often.
    我爸媽不常出去吃晚餐。
  2. Do you go to the cinema very often?
    你常去看電影嗎？
  3. Our neighbours have barbecues in the garden very often.
    我們的鄰居很常在院子裡烤肉。
  4. It doesn't snow here very often.
    這裡不常下雪。
  5. We play tennis very often in summer.
    我們夏天很常打網球。

④ very early

  1. I get up very early on Mondays.
    我星期一起得很早。
  2. The farmers start work very early.
    農夫很早就開始工作。
  3. We arrived at the airport very early.
    我們很早就到機場了。
  4. My baby sister wakes up very early.
    我妹妹很早就醒了。

⑤ very late

  1. My dad works very late on Fridays.
    我爸星期五工作到很晚。
  2. The train arrived very late last night.
    火車昨晚很晚才到。
  3. Don't stay up very late. You have school tomorrow.
    別熬到很晚，你明天要上學。
  4. We had dinner very late because of the traffic.
    因為塞車，我們很晚才吃晚餐。
  5. She often goes to bed very late.
    她常常很晚才睡。

⑥ 否定句和疑問句

  1. My son doesn't enjoy swimming lessons very much.
    我兒子不太喜歡上游泳課。
  2. Do you see your grandparents very often?
    你常去看你爺爺奶奶嗎？
  3. Did you get home very late last night?
    你昨晚很晚才到家嗎？
  4. They don't visit us very often.
    他們不常來看我們。
  5. We didn't stay very late at the party.
    我們沒有在派對待到很晚。
