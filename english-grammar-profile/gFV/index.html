None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

我的朋友都沒興趣，所以我找不到人可以聊。

none of 本⁠身是否⁠定，動⁠詞用肯⁠定⁠的形⁠式

我的朋友都沒興趣，所以我找不到人可以聊。

neither of 說兩⁠個，none of 說三⁠個或更⁠多

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

neither of
兩個

Neither of us is free on Friday.

我們兩個星期五都沒空。
none of
三個或更多

I tried on three dresses, but I bought none of them.

我試穿了三件洋裝，可是一件都沒買。

of 後⁠面用受格代⁠名⁠詞或帶限⁠定詞⁠的名⁠詞

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

受格代名詞us、you、them
限定詞 + 名詞the students、my friends、these keys

neither of 的動⁠詞在正⁠式⁠的文⁠章用單⁠數

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

正式的文章

Neither of the twins is ready for school.

那對雙胞胎都還沒準備好上學。
說話

Neither of them are coming.

他們兩個都不來。

none of 接不可數名詞時用單數動詞。

none of 後⁠面⁠接可⁠數⁠名⁠詞⁠的複⁠數

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

None of my neighbour have a garden.

None of my neighbours have a garden.

of 後⁠面用受格

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

Neither of we can drive.

Neither of us can drive.

of 後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞要⁠有限⁠定詞

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

None of photos on the website were clear.

None of the photos on the website were clear.

說兩⁠個人用 neither of

None of my friends are interested, so there is no one I can talk to.

I have two brothers, but none of them lives nearby.

I have two brothers, but neither of them lives nearby.

例句

① neither of + 代名詞：兩個裡面沒有一個

  1. My wife and I are both busy, so neither of us can pick up the children.
    我太太和我都很忙，所以我們兩個都沒辦法去接孩子。
  2. I tried on two jackets, but neither of them fitted me.
    我試穿了兩件外套，可是兩件都不合身。
  3. The twins are eight, and neither of them can ride a bike yet.
    那對雙胞胎八歲了，兩個都還不會騎腳踏車。
  4. We had a small accident, but neither of us is to blame.
    我們出了一點小車禍，可是我們兩個都沒有錯。
  5. I called both hotels, and neither of them had a free room.
    我打給兩家飯店，兩家都沒有空房。

② neither of + 名詞片語

  1. Neither of my parents speaks English.
    我爸媽都不會說英文。
  2. Neither of the two films was very good.
    那兩部電影都不太好看。
  3. Neither of these roads goes to the beach.
    這兩條路都不通往海灘。
  4. Neither of my grandparents went to university.
    我爺爺奶奶都沒上過大學。

③ none of + 代名詞：三個或更多裡面沒有一個

  1. We met a group of French students, and none of them spoke English.
    我們遇到一群法國學生，他們都不會說英文。
  2. I asked all my friends for help, but none of them had time.
    我找了所有朋友幫忙，可是他們都沒空。
  3. I've read three of her books. None of them were very exciting.
    她的書我讀了三本。沒有一本很精彩。
  4. None of us knew the way, so we used the map on my phone.
    我們都不知道路，所以用我手機上的地圖。
  5. None of you have to stay late tonight. Go home and rest.
    你們今晚都不用留下來加班。回家休息吧。

④ none of + 名詞片語

  1. I love jazz, but none of my friends enjoy it.
    我很喜歡爵士樂，可是我的朋友都不喜歡。
  2. None of the students passed the first test, so the teacher explained it again.
    學生第一次考試都沒及格，所以老師又講解了一次。
  3. None of the shops in the village sell newspapers any more.
    村子裡的店都不賣報紙了。
  4. None of these keys open the back door.
    這些鑰匙都打不開後門。
  5. The town raised a lot of money, but none of the money went to the school.
    鎮上募了很多錢，可是那筆錢一毛都沒給學校。
  6. None of the food at the party was vegetarian.
    派對上的食物都不是素食。

⑤ 動詞或介系詞 + neither of / none of

  1. I saw two flats yesterday, but I liked neither of them.
    我昨天看了兩間公寓，可是兩間都不喜歡。
  2. She wrote to five companies, but she heard from none of them.
    她寫信給五家公司，可是一家都沒回音。
  3. He has two sisters, and he talks to neither of them.
    他有兩個姊姊，可是他跟兩個都不說話。
  4. I bought a lot of postcards in Rome, and I sent none of them.
    我在羅馬買了很多明信片，可是一張都沒寄。

⑥ 簡答：Neither / None

  1. "Which colour do you want, red or blue?" "Neither. I'd like green."
    你要紅色還是藍色？都不要。我想要綠色。
  2. "How many of the questions did you answer?" "None. They were too hard."
    那些題目你答了幾題？一題都沒答。太難了。
  3. "Do you want tea or coffee?" "Neither, thanks. Just water."
    你要茶還是咖啡？都不用，謝謝。給我水就好。

⑦ neither of / none of 當主詞，後面說結果

  1. Neither of us is liable in this case, so the insurance will pay.
    這件事我們兩個都沒有責任，所以保險會賠。
  2. None of the guests stayed after midnight.
    客人都沒待到半夜以後。
  3. None of my friends live near me, so I'm often alone at weekends.
    我的朋友都沒住在我附近，所以我週末常一個人。
  4. Neither of the shops was open, so we went home without bread.
    兩家店都沒開，所以我們沒買到麵包就回家了。
