I was allowed to go out with my friends on condition that my parents knew where I was.

只要我爸媽知道我在哪裡，我就可以跟朋友出去。

連⁠接⁠詞引⁠導條⁠件子⁠句

I was allowed to go out with my friends on condition that my parents knew where I was.

只要你不安裝任何東西，就可以用我的筆電。

連⁠接⁠詞⁠的意⁠思

so long as
只要

We'll start the meeting at nine so long as everyone arrives on time.

只要大家準時到，我們九點開始開會。
on condition that
條件是

My aunt lent us her flat on condition that we watered her plants.

我阿姨把公寓借給我們，條件是我們要幫她澆花。
in the event that
萬一

In the event that the power goes off, the computer saves your work.

萬一停電，電腦會儲存你的檔案。

on condition that 和 in the event that 多用在正式的文字。

條⁠件子⁠句⁠的時⁠態

現在簡單式
未來的事

We'll start the meeting at nine so long as everyone arrives on time.

只要大家準時到，我們九點開始開會。
現在簡單式
一般的事實

My cat is friendly so long as you don't touch her tail.

只要你不碰牠的尾巴，我的貓都很友善。
過去式
過去的事

My aunt lent us her flat on condition that we watered her plants.

我阿姨把公寓借給我們，條件是我們要幫她澆花。

條⁠件子⁠句放⁠在⁠前⁠面，後⁠面加逗⁠號

主要子句前面

In the event that the power goes off, the computer saves your work.

萬一停電，電腦會儲存你的檔案。
主要子句前面

So long as the weather holds, we'll finish the roof tomorrow.

只要天氣一直這麼好，我們明天就能把屋頂做完。

原句：說小⁠時⁠候⁠的⁠事，兩⁠個子⁠句都用過⁠去⁠式

I was allowed to go out with my friends on condition that my parents knew where I was.

只要我爸媽知道我在哪裡，我就可以跟朋友出去。

允許的人是爸媽。爸媽提出的條件是要知道說話的人在哪裡。

說未⁠來⁠的⁠事，條⁠件子⁠句不⁠用 will

We'll lend you the van on condition that you will return it tomorrow.

We'll lend you the van on condition that you return it tomorrow.

on condition that 的介⁠系⁠詞是 on

I was allowed to go out with my friends on condition that my parents knew where I was.

You can stay in condition that you keep quiet.

You can stay on condition that you keep quiet.

in the event of 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞，不⁠接子⁠句

In the event of the train is late, take a taxi.

In the event that the train is late, take a taxi.

接名詞時用 in the event of，像 in the event of a delay。

as long as 說條⁠件，也說時⁠間⁠的長⁠度

條件
只要

As long as you're home by ten, you can go to the party.

只要十點前回家，你就可以去派對。
時間的長度

You can stay as long as you like.

你想待多久就待多久。

in case 說預⁠先做⁠的事

in case
預先做的事

Take an umbrella in case it rains.

帶把傘，以防下雨。
in the event that
條件

In the event that it rains, we'll eat inside.

萬一下雨，我們就在裡面吃。

現在就帶傘，不管後來有沒有下雨。下雨時才在裡面吃。

例句

① so long as 說未來的事

  1. You can borrow my car so long as you bring it back by Sunday.
    只要你星期天前還回來，就可以借我的車。
  2. So long as the weather holds, we'll finish the roof tomorrow.
    只要天氣一直這麼好，我們明天就能把屋頂做完。
  3. I don't mind the music so long as it stops by midnight.
    只要半夜前停下來，我不介意那些音樂。
  4. So long as you don't contradict him in public, he'll listen to your ideas.
    只要你不在大家面前反駁他，他就會聽你的想法。
  5. The old sofa will do so long as nobody sits on the left arm.
    只要沒有人坐左邊的扶手，這張舊沙發還能用。
  6. "Can I come too?" "So long as you're ready by eight."
    我也可以去嗎？只要你八點前準備好。

② so long as 說一般的事實

  1. Children are happy so long as they have space to play.
    只要有空間玩，孩子就很開心。
  2. My grandfather eats anything so long as it isn't spicy.
    只要不辣，我爺爺什麼都吃。
  3. So long as a coat is impermeable, I don't care how it looks.
    只要外套不透水，我不在乎它好不好看。
  4. The landlord doesn't care who lives here so long as the rent arrives on time.
    只要房租準時到，房東不在乎誰住在這裡。

③ on condition that 說未來的事

  1. I'll lend you the money on condition that you pay it back next month.
    我會借你錢，條件是你下個月要還我。
  2. The bank will approve the loan on condition that you provide a guarantor.
    銀行會核准貸款，條件是你要提供保證人。
  3. They'll let us use the hall on condition that we leave it clean.
    他們會讓我們用禮堂，條件是我們要把它打掃乾淨。
  4. You can join the club on condition that you attend at least two meetings a month.
    你可以加入社團，條件是每個月至少要參加兩次聚會。
  5. The company will drop the case on condition that we apologize publicly.
    公司會撤告，條件是我們要公開道歉。

④ in the event that 說可能發生的事

  1. In the event that the lift breaks down, please use the stairs.
    萬一電梯故障，請走樓梯。
  2. In the event that you lose your card, call the bank immediately.
    萬一卡片遺失，請馬上打電話給銀行。
  3. In the event that the river becomes turbid, the town stops taking water from it.
    萬一河水變混濁，鎮上就不從河裡取水。
  4. We'll refund the full price in the event that the band cancels the concert.
    萬一樂團取消音樂會，我們會全額退款。
  5. In the event that it snows, the wedding will take place in the hotel.
    萬一下雪，婚禮會在飯店裡舉行。
  6. In the event that the parcel arrives damaged, keep the box and contact us.
    萬一包裹送到時有損壞，請留著箱子並跟我們聯絡。

⑤ 說過去的事

  1. My father lent me the car on condition that I filled up the tank.
    我爸把車借給我，條件是我要把油加滿。
  2. She agreed to the interview on condition that the paper didn't print her name.
    她答應接受採訪，條件是報紙不能登出她的名字。
  3. My parents let me keep the puppy so long as I walked it every day.
    我爸媽讓我養那隻小狗，條件是我每天要遛牠。
  4. The judge released him on condition that he reported to the police every week.
    法官放了他，條件是他每週要向警方報到。
  5. We could stay out late so long as we came home together.
    只要我們一起回家，就可以在外面待到很晚。
  6. The museum lent us the painting on condition that we kept it in a locked room.
    博物館把那幅畫借給我們，條件是我們要把它放在上鎖的房間。

⑥ 合約、規定和公告

  1. Tenants may keep pets on condition that they obtain written permission from the landlord.
    房客可以養寵物，條件是要取得房東的書面同意。
  2. In the event that the buyer fails to pay, the seller may cancel the contract.
    萬一買方沒有付款，賣方可以取消合約。
  3. Staff may work from home so long as their manager approves.
    只要主管同意，員工可以在家工作。
  4. In the event that a fire alarm sounds, leave the building by the nearest exit.
    萬一火警警報響起，請從最近的出口離開大樓。
  5. Students keep the scholarship on condition that they maintain an average of B or above.
    學生可以保有獎學金，條件是平均成績要維持在 B 以上。
