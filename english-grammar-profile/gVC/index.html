例句

① a little + 不可數名詞

  1. I went to the cinema with my friends so we could spend a little time together.
    我跟朋友去看電影，這樣我們可以一起待一下。
  2. Add a little salt to the water before you cook the pasta.
    煮義大利麵之前，在水裡加一點鹽。
  3. The soup is too thick. Put a little water in it.
    湯太濃了。加一點水進去。
  4. I only speak a little Spanish, but I can order food.
    我只會說一點西班牙文，可是我會點菜。
  5. You'll pass the test. You just need a little practice.
    你會通過考試的。你只需要多練習一點。

② a bit of + 不可數名詞

  1. On Saturday I had a bit of free time, so I took the bus to my friend's house.
    星期六我有一點空閒時間，所以搭公車去朋友家。
  2. Could you give me a bit of help with these boxes?
    你可以幫我搬一下這些箱子嗎？
  3. I've got a bit of money left. Let's get an ice cream.
    我還剩一點錢。我們去買冰淇淋吧。
  4. Good luck in the race tomorrow! You'll need a bit of luck with the weather.
    祝你明天比賽順利！天氣方面你需要一點運氣。
  5. There was a bit of rain in the morning, but the afternoon was sunny.
    早上下了一點雨，可是下午出太陽。

③ a little bit of + 不可數名詞

  1. I just watch a little bit of TV when I come home from school.
    我放學回家只看一點點電視。
  2. Can I have a little bit of cake? I'm not very hungry.
    我可以吃一點點蛋糕嗎？我不太餓。
  3. There's a little bit of coffee on your shirt.
    你的襯衫上沾到一點咖啡。
  4. I'm doing a little bit of exercise every morning now.
    我現在每天早上做一點點運動。

④ 問句和請求

  1. Would you like a little sugar in your coffee?
    你的咖啡要加一點糖嗎？
  2. Can you lend me a bit of money until Friday?
    你可以借我一點錢到星期五嗎？
  3. Could I have a little bit of quiet, please? I'm trying to study.
    可以安靜一點嗎？我在讀書。
  4. Do you want a bit of advice? Take an umbrella.
    要不要聽我一句建議？帶把傘。

⑤ 寫給朋友的信

  1. I'm having a great time here. I've even learned a bit of French.
    我在這裡玩得很開心。我甚至學了一點法文。
  2. After the exams, I want a little bit of peace before the summer job starts.
    考完試以後，暑期打工開始之前，我想清靜一下。
  3. Bring a little food for the journey. The train has no café.
    帶一點吃的在路上吃。火車上沒有餐車。

⑥ 不同的情境

  1. The plant looks dry. Give it a little water every day.
    這株植物看起來很乾。每天給它澆一點水。
  2. My phone has only a bit of battery left.
    我的手機只剩一點電了。
  3. There's a little bit of snow on the mountains this morning.
    今天早上山上有一點雪。
  4. With a bit of hard work, you can finish it by Monday.
    多努力一點，你星期一之前就能做完。

⑦ 對話

  1. "Do you want milk?" "Just a little, please."
    你要牛奶嗎？一點就好，謝謝。
  2. "Are you busy?" "A bit. I've got a little bit of work to finish."
    你在忙嗎？有一點。我還有一點工作要做完。
  3. "How was the beach?" "Lovely. We had a bit of sun in the afternoon."
    海邊怎麼樣？很好。下午出了一點太陽。
  4. "Can you play the piano?" "A little. I had a bit of practice as a child."
    你會彈鋼琴嗎？會一點。我小時候練過一點。
