Will you be able to come this time?

你這次能來嗎？

主⁠詞 + will be able to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

will be able to未來能夠。
won't be able to未來沒辦法。
原形動詞能不能做的事。

為⁠什⁠麼不⁠用 will can

Will you be able to come this time?

例句

Next year I will be able to drive.

明年我就會開車了。

can 是情態動詞，前面不能再加 will。說未來的能力或可能，用 will be able to。

縮⁠寫

I'll be able to

I'll be able to help you tomorrow.

我明天可以幫你。
won't be able to

I won't be able to come.

我沒辦法來。

will 和 can 不⁠能連⁠用

Will you be able to come this time?

I will can help you tomorrow.

I will be able to help you tomorrow.

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 be，再⁠接 able to

Will you be able to come this time?

Will you able to come?

Will you be able to come?

able 後⁠面要⁠有 to

Will you be able to come this time?

I won't be able come on Friday.

I won't be able to come on Friday.

例句

① 主詞 + will / 'll + be able to + 原形動詞

  1. After the course, you will be able to speak basic Spanish.
    上完這門課，你就能說基本的西班牙文。
  2. I'll be able to help you with your homework after dinner.
    晚餐後我可以幫你寫功課。
  3. With the new bridge, we'll be able to drive to the island.
    有了新橋，我們就能開車到島上。
  4. Next year my sister will be able to vote.
    明年我姊就可以投票了。
  5. When the snow melts, we'll be able to walk to the lake.
    雪融化以後，我們就能走路到湖邊。
  6. If you save enough money, you'll be able to buy a car.
    如果你存夠錢，你就能買車。
  7. The doctor says I'll be able to play tennis again in a month.
    醫生說我一個月後就能再打網球。
  8. Once the baby is older, we'll be able to travel more.
    等寶寶大一點，我們就能多去旅行。
  9. With this ticket, you'll be able to visit all the museums in the city.
    有了這張票，你就能參觀城裡所有的博物館。

② 主詞 + won't / will not + be able to + 原形動詞

  1. Without my glasses, I will not be able to read the menu.
    沒有眼鏡，我就沒辦法看菜單。
  2. I won't be able to play the guitar at the party, because I've never learned.
    我沒辦法在派對上彈吉他，因為我從來沒學過。
  3. We won't be able to come to the wedding, because we'll be in Canada.
    我們沒辦法去參加婚禮，因為我們會在加拿大。
  4. Sorry, I won't be able to meet you at the station.
    抱歉，我沒辦法去車站接你。
  5. If you don't hurry, you won't be able to catch the bus.
    如果你不快一點，就趕不上公車了。
  6. The shop is closed tomorrow, so we won't be able to buy bread.
    那家店明天沒開，所以我們買不到麵包。
  7. She won't be able to drive for six weeks after the operation.
    手術後六個星期她都不能開車。
  8. Without the internet, I won't be able to send you the photos.
    沒有網路，我就沒辦法把照片寄給你。
  9. They won't be able to finish the house before winter.
    他們沒辦法在冬天前蓋好房子。
  10. Without the key, the neighbours won't be able to get in.
    沒有鑰匙，鄰居就進不去。

③ Will + 主詞 + be able to + 原形動詞 ...?

  1. Will you be able to lend me your camera next week?
    你下星期能借我相機嗎？
  2. Will you be able to pick me up from the airport?
    你能來機場接我嗎？
  3. Will we be able to see the sea from the hotel?
    我們從飯店看得到海嗎？
  4. Will your brother be able to fix my bike?
    你哥能修好我的腳踏車嗎？
  5. Will I be able to pay by card in the market?
    我在市場可以刷卡嗎？
  6. Will your parents be able to join us for the school play?
    你爸媽能來看學校的話劇嗎？
  7. Will you be able to finish the report by Friday?
    你星期五前能完成報告嗎？
  8. When will we be able to move into the new flat?
    我們什麼時候能搬進新公寓？

④ 回答：Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

  1. "Will you be able to help me?" "Yes, I will."
    你能幫我嗎？可以。
  2. "Will you be able to come on Sunday?" "No, I won't, sorry."
    你星期天能來嗎？抱歉，沒辦法。
  3. "Will she be able to walk again?" "Yes, she will, but it will take time."
    她還能再走路嗎？可以，可是需要時間。
  4. "Will we be able to park near the theatre?" "No, we won't. It's always full."
    我們能把車停在劇院附近嗎？沒辦法，那裡總是停滿了。
