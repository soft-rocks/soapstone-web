Will you come by train?

你會搭火車來嗎？

Will + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 ...?

Will、主⁠詞、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Will you come by train?

Will放在主詞前面。
主詞常是 you，問對方的計畫。也可以是別人。
原形動詞對方打算做的事。

是⁠非疑⁠問⁠句

例句

Will you stay for the whole weekend?

你整個週末都會待著嗎？

問對方會不會做某件事。

wh⁠-⁠ 疑⁠問⁠句

例句

What time will you leave?

你幾點要出發？

問計畫的細節，像時間、地點、方法。

回⁠答

例句

Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

會。／不會。

will 的疑⁠問⁠句不⁠加 do

例句

① Will + 主詞 + 原形動詞 ...?

  1. Will you come by car or by bus?
    你會開車來還是搭公車來？
  2. Will you have a mobile phone with you?
    你身上會帶手機嗎？
  3. Will you stay at a hotel or with friends?
    你會住飯店還是住朋友家？
  4. Will you bring your sister to the party?
    你會帶你妹妹來派對嗎？
  5. Will you study abroad next year?
    你明年會出國念書嗎？
  6. Will your parents come to the concert?
    你爸媽會來音樂會嗎？
  7. Will you sell your old car?
    你會賣掉你的舊車嗎？
  8. Will Ann join the swimming team?
    Ann 會加入游泳隊嗎？
  9. Will you take the job in London?
    你會接受倫敦那份工作嗎？
  10. Will you visit your grandparents this summer?
    你今年夏天會去看你的祖父母嗎？
  11. Will they get married next year?
    他們明年會結婚嗎？
  12. Will you wear a suit to the interview?
    你面試會穿西裝嗎？
  13. Will your brother live with you in the new flat?
    你哥會跟你一起住在新公寓嗎？
  14. Will you learn to drive after you finish school?
    你畢業後會去學開車嗎？
  15. Will you take your laptop on the trip?
    你旅行會帶筆電嗎？

② wh- 疑問詞 + will + 主詞 + 原形動詞 ...?

  1. What time will you leave tomorrow?
    你明天幾點出發？
  2. Which hotel will you book for your trip to Tokyo?
    你去東京會訂哪一間飯店？
  3. How will you get to the concert?
    你要怎麼去音樂會？
  4. When will you start your new job?
    你什麼時候開始新工作？
  5. What will you buy with the money?
    你要拿這筆錢買什麼？
  6. Who will you invite to your birthday party?
    你的生日派對會邀請誰？
  7. How long will you stay in Australia?
    你會在澳洲待多久？
  8. Which university will you choose?
    你會選哪一所大學？
  9. What will you wear to the wedding?
    你會穿什麼去參加婚禮？
  10. Where will your family go on holiday?
    你們家會去哪裡度假？
  11. When will they move into their new house?
    他們什麼時候搬進新家？
  12. What will you study at university?
    你上大學會念什麼？
  13. How many guests will you have at the wedding?
    你的婚禮會有多少客人？
  14. What will you name the puppy?
    你會幫小狗取什麼名字？
  15. Where will you put the new sofa?
    新沙發你要放在哪裡？
  16. How will you spend your birthday this year?
    你今年要怎麼過生日？
