I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

我記得我一整天都覺得很難過、很困惑。

remember 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是記⁠得以⁠前做過⁠的事

我記得我一整天都覺得很難過、很困惑。

感受在前，記得在後。

remember：不⁠定⁠詞是之⁠後要⁠做，動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是做過⁠了

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

remember to
記得要去做

Remember to lock the door.

記得要鎖門。
remember + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
記得做過

I remember locking the door.

我記得我鎖過門了。

forget：不⁠定⁠詞是沒⁠有⁠做，動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是忘⁠記做過

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

forget to
忘了去做

I forgot to call my mum.

我忘了打電話給我媽。
forget + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
忘記做過

I'll never forget meeting the Queen.

我永遠不會忘記見到女王的那一次。

stop：不⁠定⁠詞是停⁠下⁠來去⁠做，動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是⁠不⁠再⁠做

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

try：不⁠定⁠詞是努⁠力去⁠做，動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是試⁠試⁠看

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

try to
努力去做

I tried to open the window, but it was stuck.

我試著開窗，可是窗戶卡住了。
try + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
試試看

Try opening the window. It might get cooler.

開窗試試看。可能會涼一點。

regret：不⁠定⁠詞是遺⁠憾⁠地通⁠知，動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是後⁠悔做過

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

regret to
遺憾地通知

We regret to inform you that the flight is cancelled.

我們很遺憾地通知您，班機取消了。
regret + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
後悔做過

I regret saying that to her.

我後悔對她說了那些話。

go on：不⁠定⁠詞是接⁠著做另一⁠件⁠事，動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 是繼⁠續做

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

go on to
接著做別的事

After the history lesson, she went on to teach us maths.

上完歷史課，她接著教我們數學。
go on + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
繼續做

She went on talking for an hour.

她繼續講了一個小時。

need 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 和被⁠動⁠的意⁠思相⁠同

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

need to
主詞去做

I need to wash the car.

我得洗車了。
need + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
主詞被處理

The car needs washing.

這輛車該洗了。

還⁠沒⁠做⁠的事用 to 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

Remember posting this letter on your way to work.

Remember to post this letter on your way to work.

想說記得要買。

不⁠再做用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

My dad stopped to smoke ten years ago.

My dad stopped smoking ten years ago.

stop to smoke 的意思是停下來去抽菸。

後⁠悔做過⁠的事用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

I regret to buy this car.

I regret buying this car.

regret to 用在正式的通知。

need 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 時不⁠加 being

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

The plants need being watered.

The plants need watering.

start、begin、like 接哪⁠一⁠種，意⁠思都差⁠不⁠多

I remember feeling very unhappy and confused all day long.

不定詞

It started to rain.

開始下雨了。
動詞⁠-⁠ing

It started raining.

開始下雨了。

例句

① remember + to + 原形動詞 / remember + 動詞-ing

  1. If you need to change the time, remember to contact him first.
    如果你要改時間，記得先跟他聯絡。
  2. "Did you lock the car?" "Yes, I remember locking it. I heard the beep."
    你鎖車了嗎？鎖了，我記得我鎖了。我有聽到嗶的一聲。
  3. Your exam is at nine tomorrow. Remember to set your alarm.
    你明天九點考試。記得設鬧鐘。
  4. I remember crying on my first day at nursery.
    我記得我上幼兒園第一天哭了。
  5. Do you remember visiting this beach when we were kids?
    你記得我們小時候來過這個海灘嗎？

② forget + to + 原形動詞 / forget + 動詞-ing

  1. "Why is the plant dead?" "Sorry, I forgot to water it while you were away."
    這盆植物怎麼死了？抱歉，你不在的時候我忘了澆水。
  2. Don't forget to turn off the oven before you leave.
    出門前別忘了關烤箱。
  3. I'll never forget seeing the mountains for the first time.
    我永遠不會忘記第一次看到那些山的樣子。
  4. "What was the best day of your life?" "I'll never forget holding my daughter for the first time."
    你人生中最棒的一天是哪一天？我永遠不會忘記第一次抱我女兒的那一刻。
  5. She forgot to send the email, so the client never got the price list.
    她忘了寄那封信，所以客戶一直沒收到價目表。

③ stop + to + 原形動詞 / stop + 動詞-ing

  1. We had been driving for five hours, so we stopped to have a coffee.
    我們開了五個小時的車，所以停下來喝杯咖啡。
  2. My dad stopped eating meat last year.
    我爸去年開始不吃肉了。
  3. You're always so busy. Have you ever stopped to think about your future?
    你總是那麼忙。你有沒有停下來想過自己的未來？
  4. "Is your knee better?" "Yes, it stopped hurting yesterday."
    你的膝蓋好點了嗎？好了，昨天就不痛了。
  5. After I had a baby, I needed to stop working for a while.
    我生了孩子以後，得暫時停止工作。

④ try + to + 原形動詞 / try + 動詞-ing

  1. "Why are you so tired?" "I tried to finish the report last night, but I couldn't."
    你怎麼這麼累？我昨晚想把報告寫完，可是沒寫完。
  2. "I can't sleep." "Try drinking some warm milk before bed."
    我睡不著。睡前喝點溫牛奶試試看。
  3. He tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy.
    他想把箱子抬起來，可是太重了。
  4. If the computer freezes, try turning it off and on again.
    電腦當機的話，試試看把它關掉再開。

⑤ regret + to + 原形動詞 / regret + 動詞-ing

  1. We regret to tell you that your order will arrive late.
    我們很遺憾地通知您，您的訂單會晚到。
  2. "Do you regret leaving your job?" "No. I'm much happier now."
    你後悔辭職嗎？不後悔。我現在快樂多了。
  3. The school regrets to announce that the trip has been cancelled.
    學校很遺憾地宣布，這次旅行取消了。
  4. I regret not learning to swim when I was young.
    我後悔小時候沒學游泳。

⑥ go on + to + 原形動詞 / go on + 動詞-ing

  1. He started as a waiter and went on to open his own restaurant.
    他從服務生做起，後來開了自己的餐廳。
  2. The teacher asked them to be quiet, but they went on talking.
    老師叫他們安靜，可是他們繼續講話。
  3. After winning the local race, she went on to win a national medal.
    贏了地方的比賽以後，她接著拿到了全國賽的獎牌。
  4. The band went on playing until two in the morning.
    樂團一直演奏到凌晨兩點。

⑦ need + to + 原形動詞 / need + 動詞-ing

  1. "The grass is so long." "I know. It needs cutting."
    草好長。我知道。該剪了。
  2. I need to cut my hair. It's getting in my eyes.
    我得剪頭髮了。頭髮都蓋到眼睛了。
  3. Your shirt needs ironing before the interview.
    面試前你的襯衫要燙一下。
  4. "Can I help with the party?" "Yes, we need to buy more chairs."
    派對有什麼我能幫忙的嗎？有，我們需要再買幾張椅子。
