例句

① Have you got / Do you have many ...?

  1. I hope you're happy at your new school. Have you got many friends in England?
    希望你在新學校過得開心。你在英格蘭有很多朋友嗎？
  2. You read so well, Lucy. Do you have many books in English at home?
    Lucy，你讀得很好。你家裡有很多英文書嗎？
  3. Have you got many plans for the summer?
    你暑假有很多計畫嗎？
  4. Let's meet after school. Do you have many lessons on Friday?
    我們放學後見吧。你星期五課很多嗎？
  5. Do your grandparents have many visitors?
    你爺爺奶奶常有很多人去看他們嗎？

② Did / Were there many ...?

  1. How was the concert? Were there many people there?
    演唱會怎麼樣？人很多嗎？
  2. Did you take many photos on holiday?
    你度假時拍了很多照片嗎？
  3. Sorry I missed the talk. Did many parents turn up?
    抱歉我錯過了演講。很多家長去了嗎？
  4. Were there many mistakes in my essay?
    我的作文錯很多嗎？

③ How many + 複數名詞

  1. I'm thinking of moving to the UK. How many channels are there on British TV?
    我在考慮搬去英國。英國電視有幾個頻道？
  2. I need to buy drinks. How many people are coming to your party?
    我要去買飲料。你的派對有幾個人要來？
  3. Your house is so big! How many bedrooms has it got?
    你家好大！有幾間臥室？
  4. How many hours do you sleep every night?
    你每天晚上睡幾個小時？
  5. How many languages can your teacher speak?
    你的老師會說幾種語言？

④ How many ... + 過去式

  1. You travel so much! How many countries did you visit last year?
    你好常旅行！你去年去了幾個國家？
  2. How many goals did your team score on Saturday?
    你們隊星期六進了幾球？
  3. How many times did you call me? I didn't hear the phone.
    你打了幾次電話給我？我沒聽到電話響。

⑤ 間接問句和否定句

  1. Can you tell me how many tickets are left?
    你可以告訴我還剩幾張票嗎？
  2. I don't know how many people live in this town.
    我不知道這個鎮住了多少人。
  3. We usually shop in town. There aren't many shops in my village.
    我們通常去鎮上買東西。我們村子沒有很多商店。
  4. It was too hot. We didn't see many animals at the zoo.
    天氣太熱了。我們在動物園沒看到很多動物。

⑥ Are there many ...?

  1. Are there many good restaurants near your hotel?
    你的旅館附近有很多好餐廳嗎？
  2. I heard your school is very international. Are there many students from other countries?
    聽說你們學校很國際化。有很多外國學生嗎？
  3. My flight lands at midnight. Are there many buses to the city at that time?
    我的班機半夜到。那個時間有很多班公車去市區嗎？

⑦ 不同的情境

  1. You look tired. Have you had many late nights this week?
    你看起來很累。你這星期熬了很多次夜嗎？
  2. I'm going to the market. Do we need many eggs for the cake?
    我要去市場。做蛋糕需要很多蛋嗎？
  3. You can't sleep? How many cups of coffee have you had today?
    你睡不著？你今天喝了幾杯咖啡？

⑧ 對話

  1. "Have you got many friends at work?" "A few. Most people are friendly."
    你在公司有很多朋友嗎？有幾個。大部分人都很友善。
  2. "How many sweets did you eat?" "Only three, Mum!"
    你吃了幾顆糖？只有三顆，媽！
  3. "Do you get many emails?" "Yes, about a hundred a day."
    你收到很多電子郵件嗎？對，一天大概一百封。
  4. "How many people work in your office?" "About twenty."
    你們辦公室有多少人？大約二十個。
