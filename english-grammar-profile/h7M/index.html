例句

① mine / yours

  1. Can I borrow your ruler? I can't find mine.
    我可以借你的尺嗎？我找不到我的。
  2. This seat isn't yours. It's mine.
    這個位子不是你的，是我的。
  3. Did you get my message? I didn't get yours.
    你收到我的訊息了嗎？我沒收到你的。
  4. The charger on the sofa is yours, not mine.
    沙發上的充電器是你的，不是我的。
  5. Your bike is outside, and mine is in the garage.
    你的腳踏車在外面，我的在車庫裡。

② his / hers

  1. I forgot my book, so Tom lent me his.
    我忘了帶書，所以 Tom 借我他的。
  2. That's not my scarf. It's hers.
    那不是我的圍巾，是她的。
  3. My grades are good, but his are even better.
    我的成績很好，可是他的更好。
  4. My room is downstairs, and hers is upstairs.
    我的房間在樓下，她的在樓上。
  5. Whose coat is this? Is it his?
    這是誰的外套？是他的嗎？

③ ours / theirs

  1. Their garden is beautiful, but ours is full of weeds.
    他們的花園很漂亮，可是我們的長滿了雜草。
  2. Is this table ours or theirs?
    這張桌子是我們的還是他們的？
  3. Our flight leaves at six, and theirs leaves at nine.
    我們的班機六點起飛，他們的九點起飛。
  4. The house on the corner is theirs.
    轉角那間房子是他們的。
  5. Our kids go to the same school as theirs.
    我們的孩子跟他們的孩子讀同一間學校。

④ 雙重所有格

  1. A cousin of hers works at the hospital.
    她的一個表姊在醫院上班。
  2. Some neighbors of theirs complained about the noise.
    他們的幾個鄰居抱怨噪音。
  3. A colleague of mine recommended this restaurant.
    我的一個同事推薦了這家餐廳。
  4. Was it a patient of yours on the phone?
    剛剛電話裡是你的病人嗎？
  5. An uncle of his owns a restaurant in Tainan.
    他的一個叔叔在台南開餐廳。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. A: Whose keys are these? B: I think they're hers.
    A：這是誰的鑰匙？B：我想是她的。
  2. Their car broke down, so they borrowed ours.
    他們的車拋錨了，所以借了我們的。
  3. My idea was good, but yours was better.
    我的點子不錯，可是你的更好。
  4. I recognized the voice. It was definitely his.
    我認得那個聲音，絕對是他的。
  5. Our team scored first, but theirs won the game.
    我們隊先得分，可是他們隊贏了比賽。
  6. Every family has its own rules, and ours are quite strict.
    每個家庭都有自己的規矩，我們家的相當嚴格。
  7. The decision isn't mine to make. It's theirs.
    這不是我能決定的，要由他們決定。
  8. She showed me her photos, and I showed her mine.
    她給我看她的照片，我也給她看我的。
  9. Your parents are very kind. So are mine.
    你爸媽人很好。我爸媽也是。
  10. Our car is the red one. Theirs is the one behind it.
    我們的車是紅色那輛。他們的是後面那輛。
