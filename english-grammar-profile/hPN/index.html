Whether or not it is better to educate boys and girls in separate schools has been a widely discussed issue in recent years.

男女分校是不是比較好，是近年來大家廣泛討論的問題。

whether or not 引⁠出條⁠件子⁠句，主⁠要⁠子⁠句在兩⁠種情⁠況下都成⁠立

不管你同不同意，會議都會照常舉行。

條件子句說未來的事時用現在式，不用 will。

條⁠件子⁠句⁠的位⁠置和 or not 的位⁠置

主要子句前面

Whether or not the weather improves, the ceremony will take place outdoors.

不管天氣會不會好轉，典禮都會在戶外舉行。
主要子句後面

We will play the match whether or not it rains.

不管下不下雨，我們都會比賽。
or not 在子句後面

Whether you like it or not, the rules apply to everyone.

不管你喜不喜歡，規定對每個人都適用。

條件子句放在前面時，後面加逗號。

whether or not 引⁠出名⁠詞子⁠句，意⁠思是是⁠不⁠是

Whether or not it is better to educate boys and girls in separate schools has been a widely discussed issue in recent years.

當主詞

Whether or not the changes are permanent is still unclear.

這些改變是不是永久的，目前還不清楚。
當受詞

The committee will decide whether or not the school should close.

委員會會決定這所學校是否應該關閉。
當受詞
用 will

Please let us know whether or not you will attend the conference.

請告訴我們您是否會出席研討會。

原句：whether or not 子⁠句是句⁠子⁠的主⁠詞

Whether or not it is better to educate boys and girls in separate schools has been a widely discussed issue in recent years.

男女分校是不是比較好，是近年來大家廣泛討論的問題。

整個子句是主詞，動詞 has been 用單數。

or not 不⁠接在 if 後⁠面

Whether or not it is better to educate boys and girls in separate schools has been a widely discussed issue in recent years.

If or not it's sunny, we'll have the picnic.

Whether or not it's sunny, we'll have the picnic.

條⁠件子⁠句說未⁠來⁠的事用現⁠在⁠式

Whether or not it will rain tomorrow, we will play the match.

Whether or not it rains tomorrow, we will play the match.

主⁠要⁠子⁠句前⁠面不⁠再⁠加 but 或 so

Whether or not you like it, but you have to go.

Whether or not you like it, you have to go.

whether 後⁠面⁠接兩⁠個選⁠項⁠時，or 後⁠面是另⁠一⁠個選⁠項

by bus or by train

Whether you go by bus or by train, the journey takes two hours.

不管你搭公車還是搭火車，都要兩個小時。
win or lose

Whether we win or lose, we'll celebrate after the match.

不管我們贏還是輸，比賽完都要慶祝。

even if 只⁠說一⁠種情⁠況：就⁠算這⁠件⁠事發⁠生

even if

Even if it rains, we will play the match.

就算下雨，我們也會比賽。
even if

Even if you apologise, she won't forgive you.

就算你道歉，她也不會原諒你。

whether or not 包括發生和不發生兩種情況。

例句

① 條件子句放在主要子句前面

  1. Whether or not you agree with the decision, you must respect it.
    不管你同不同意這個決定，你都必須尊重它。
  2. Whether or not the board concurs, we will publish the results next month.
    不管董事會同不同意，我們下個月都會公布結果。
  3. Whether or not it is profitable, the project will continue until 2030.
    不管有沒有獲利，這個計畫都會持續到 2030 年。
  4. Whether or not they receive approbation from their peers, scientists must report their findings honestly.
    不管有沒有得到同行的認可，科學家都必須誠實報告研究結果。
  5. Whether or not the evidence is equivocal, the court must reach a decision.
    不管證據是不是模稜兩可，法院都必須做出判決。
  6. Whether or not the trend continues, the industry will need to adapt.
    不管這個趨勢會不會持續，這個產業都得調整。
  7. Whether or not one agrees with the author, the book raises important questions.
    不管是否同意作者的看法，這本書都提出了重要的問題。

② 條件子句放在主要子句後面

  1. Employees must attend the training whether or not they have worked here before.
    不管以前有沒有在這裡工作過，員工都必須參加訓練。
  2. The hotel charges the full fee whether or not guests use the facilities.
    不管房客有沒有使用設施，飯店都收全額費用。
  3. The government must act whether or not the public supports the plan.
    不管民眾支不支持這個計畫，政府都必須採取行動。
  4. Every citizen has the right to vote whether or not they own property.
    不管有沒有財產，每個公民都有投票權。
  5. Many people disparage modern art whether or not they understand it.
    很多人不管看不看得懂現代藝術，都會貶低它。
  6. Guests may use the pool whether or not they are staying at the hotel.
    不管有沒有住在飯店，客人都可以使用游泳池。
  7. Parents should praise their children's efforts whether or not the children win.
    不管孩子有沒有贏，父母都應該稱讚他們的努力。

③ or not 放在條件子句後面

  1. Whether the plan succeeds or not, we will have learned a great deal.
    不管計畫成不成功，我們都會學到很多。
  2. The flight will leave on time whether the passengers are ready or not.
    不管乘客準備好了沒有，班機都會準時起飛。
  3. Whether she was right or not, her remarks caused grave offence.
    不管她說得對不對，她的話都嚴重冒犯了別人。
  4. Whether the delay was unforeseen or not, the company should have informed its customers.
    不管這次延誤是不是無法預見，公司都應該通知顧客。

④ 名詞子句當主詞

  1. Whether or not children should have homework is a frequently debated topic.
    孩子該不該有回家功課，是常被討論的話題。
  2. Whether or not the company knew about the risks is still a mystery.
    這家公司是否知道這些風險，到現在還是個謎。
  3. Whether or not tourists should follow local customs is a debatable issue.
    觀光客是否應該遵守當地習俗，是一個有爭議的問題。

⑤ 名詞子句當受詞

  1. Nobody knows yet whether or not the new policy will work.
    還沒有人知道新政策是否有效。
  2. The minister refused to say whether or not she knew about the problem.
    部長拒絕說明她是否知道這個問題。
  3. Researchers are investigating whether or not the drug has any side effects.
    研究人員正在調查這種藥是否有副作用。
  4. The report does not say whether or not the costs were excessive.
    報告沒有說明這些費用是否過高。
  5. Scientists still disagree about whether or not screen time harms young children.
    科學家對於使用螢幕的時間是否傷害幼童，看法仍然不一致。
  6. Students can decide for themselves whether or not they go to university.
    要不要上大學，學生可以自己決定。

⑥ 正式信件和通知

  1. Whether or not you have booked in advance, please report to reception on arrival.
    不管您有沒有事先預約，抵達時請先到櫃台報到。
  2. Please inform us whether or not you wish to renew your membership.
    請告知我們您是否要續約會員資格。
  3. We will consider all applications whether or not they include a photograph.
    不管有沒有附照片，我們都會審核所有的申請。
  4. Whether or not the strike goes ahead, the office will remain open.
    不管罷工會不會照常進行，辦公室都會照常開放。
  5. We would be grateful if you could confirm whether or not the room is still available.
    如果您能確認那個房間是否還有空，我們會很感謝。
