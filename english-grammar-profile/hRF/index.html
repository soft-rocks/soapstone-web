例句

① latest

  1. My sister knows all the latest songs.
    我姊姊會唱所有最新的歌。
  2. Turn on the radio for the latest news about the storm.
    打開收音機聽暴風雨的最新消息。
  3. He always wants the latest phone.
    他總是想要最新的手機。
  4. What's the latest time I can check in?
    我最晚幾點可以入住？
  5. Her latest book came out last week.
    她的最新作品上禮拜出版了。

② largest / widest

  1. On Sundays, we go to the largest park in the city.
    星期天我們都去這座城市最大的公園。
  2. I like this flat because the living room is the largest.
    我喜歡這間公寓，因為它的客廳最大。
  3. Can I have the largest size, please?
    請給我最大的尺寸。
  4. This is the widest part of the river.
    這是河最寬的地方。
  5. The widest road in town goes to the station.
    鎮上最寬的那條路通往車站。

③ nicest / cutest

  1. The nicest thing at my party was the cake.
    我的派對上最棒的是蛋糕。
  2. She's the nicest person in our office.
    她是我們辦公室人最好的。
  3. That's the nicest thing anyone has said to me.
    那是別人跟我說過最好聽的話。
  4. That's the cutest puppy I've ever seen!
    那是我看過最可愛的小狗！
  5. The cutest photo was of the baby asleep.
    最可愛的那張照片是寶寶睡著的樣子。

④ safest / closest

  1. The train is the safest way to travel.
    火車是最安全的交通方式。
  2. Keep your passport in the safest place you can find.
    把護照放在你能找到最安全的地方。
  3. Where's the closest bus stop?
    最近的公車站在哪裡？
  4. I told my closest friend about the new job first.
    我先把新工作的事告訴我最要好的朋友。
  5. Which is the closest beach to the hotel?
    離飯店最近的海灘是哪一個？

⑤ 其他 -st 形容詞和不同的句型

  1. Grandpa is the wisest man I know.
    爺爺是我認識最有智慧的人。
  2. That was the rudest thing he could say.
    那是他能說出最沒禮貌的話。
  3. The ripest bananas are on top.
    最熟的香蕉在上面。
  4. Is this the latest model?
    這是最新的款式嗎？
  5. Which hotel has the largest pool?
    哪家飯店的游泳池最大？
  6. You have the nicest garden on the street.
    你們家的花園是這條街上最漂亮的。
  7. The bravest kid went into the water first.
    最勇敢的那個孩子先下水。
  8. This is the purest water in the area.
    這是這一帶最純淨的水。
  9. The tamest animals are in the petting zoo.
    最溫馴的動物在親子動物區。
  10. What's the latest you've ever stayed up?
    你最晚熬夜熬到幾點？
