I can't wait to see you.

我等不及要見你了。

not 放⁠在情⁠態動⁠詞後⁠面

I can't wait to see you.

爺爺在那裡聽不到你說話。

couldn't、won't、shouldn't 的意⁠思

I can't wait to see you.

couldn't（過去不能）

I couldn't open the jar, so I asked my dad.

我打不開那個罐子，所以請我爸爸幫忙。
won't（不會）

The car won't start, so we'll take the bus.

車子發不動，所以我們搭公車吧。
shouldn't（不應該）

The new director is coming today. We shouldn't be late for the meeting.

新主任今天要來。我們開會不應該遲到。

I can't wait 是等⁠不⁠及

I can't wait to see you.

We can't wait to try your new restaurant.

我們等不及要去你的新餐廳吃了。

not 放⁠在情⁠態動⁠詞後⁠面，不⁠放⁠在⁠前⁠面

I can't wait to see you.

This old car not can go up steep hills.

This old car can't go up steep hills.

情⁠態動⁠詞⁠的否⁠定不⁠用 don't

I can't wait to see you.

Our dog doesn't can climb the stairs.

Our dog can't climb the stairs.

will not 縮⁠寫成 won't

I can't wait to see you.

I willn't forget your birthday again.

I won't forget your birthday again.

例句

① can't + 原形動詞

  1. Sorry, I've got work, so I can't join you for lunch on Saturday.
    抱歉，我要上班，所以星期六不能跟你們吃午餐。
  2. Speak up a little. Grandpa can't hear you from there.
    大聲一點，爺爺在那裡聽不到你說話。
  3. The sign says we can't leave bikes in front of the gate.
    牌子上說不能把腳踏車停在大門前面。
  4. The car park is huge, and I can't remember where we parked.
    停車場好大，我記不得我們把車停在哪裡了。
  5. My brother can't ride a bike, so he walks to school.
    我弟弟不會騎腳踏車，所以都走路上學。
  6. We can't wait to try your new restaurant.
    我們等不及要去你的新餐廳吃了。

② couldn't + 原形動詞

  1. I'm sorry you couldn't come to the wedding.
    很遺憾你沒辦法來婚禮。
  2. When I lived abroad, I couldn't call my family every day.
    我住在國外的時候，沒辦法每天打電話給家人。
  3. A tall man sat in front of us, so we couldn't see the screen.
    一個高個子坐在我們前面，所以我們看不到螢幕。
  4. The dog next door barked all night, and my sister couldn't fall asleep.
    隔壁的狗叫了一整晚，我姊姊睡不著。
  5. I couldn't open the jar, so I asked my dad.
    我打不開那個罐子，所以請我爸爸幫忙。

③ won't / wouldn't + 原形動詞

  1. It's warm in July, so you won't need your winter boots.
    七月很暖和，所以你不需要冬天的靴子。
  2. Once you try this cake, you won't want any other.
    你一旦吃過這個蛋糕，就不會想吃別的了。
  3. Don't worry. I won't say a word to your parents.
    別擔心，我不會跟你爸媽說一個字。
  4. The car won't start, so we'll take the bus.
    車子發不動，所以我們搭公車吧。
  5. Dinner took an hour last night. My son wouldn't eat his vegetables.
    昨天晚餐吃了一個小時。我兒子就是不肯吃蔬菜。

④ shouldn't / mustn't + 原形動詞

  1. The doctor says Dad shouldn't eat so much salt.
    醫生說爸爸不應該吃那麼多鹽。
  2. The new director is coming today. We shouldn't be late for the meeting.
    新主任今天要來。我們開會不應該遲到。
  3. The electrician is still working on the light. You mustn't touch the wires.
    電工還在修那盞燈。你絕對不可以碰那些電線。
  4. Passengers mustn't stand near the doors while the train is moving.
    火車行駛時，乘客絕對不可以站在車門附近。
  5. If you've had a glass of wine, you shouldn't drive home.
    如果你喝了一杯酒，就不應該開車回家。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Thank you, but I cannot accept such an expensive gift.
    謝謝你，可是我不能收這麼貴的禮物。
  2. We still have to drive the long way round. The new bridge won't open until next spring.
    我們還是得開車繞遠路。新橋要到明年春天才會通車。
  3. Ten dollars for a coffee? You can't be serious!
    一杯咖啡十塊錢？你不是說真的吧！
  4. My dad saw his old school friend on TV. He couldn't believe his eyes.
    我爸爸在電視上看到他的老同學。他簡直不敢相信自己的眼睛。
  5. You want to quit your job by email? I wouldn't do that if I were you.
    你想用電子郵件辭職？如果我是你，我不會那樣做。
  6. Bring some cash to the market. The shop can't take credit cards.
    去市場帶點現金。那家店不能刷信用卡。
  7. Don't wait up for us. We won't be home until late.
    別等我們。我們會很晚才到家。
  8. The exam is easy. You shouldn't lose sleep over it.
    考試很簡單，你不必為它睡不著。
  9. Can I use your computer tonight? My old laptop won't connect to the Wi-Fi.
    我今天晚上可以用你的電腦嗎？我的舊筆電連不上 Wi-Fi。
