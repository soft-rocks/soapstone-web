Children living in cities may never get the chance to come near any animals at all.

住在城市的孩子可能根本沒有機會接近任何動物。

主⁠詞 + may + 副⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

may even、may only、may already

may even甚至可能。
may only可能只。
may already可能已經。常接 have 加過去分詞。
may never可能永遠不。

副⁠詞⁠的位⁠置

Children living in cities may never get the chance to come near any animals at all.

例句

She may already be at the station.

她可能已經在車站了。

副詞放在 may 後面。動詞是 be 的時候，副詞也放在 may 和 be 中間。

may just

例句

If we run, we may just catch the bus.

如果我們用跑的，說不定還趕得上公車。

may just 說一個不太大、可是有希望的可能。

even 放⁠在 may 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞中⁠間

Children living in cities may never get the chance to come near any animals at all.

You may find even new words.

You may even find new words.

副⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

They may never to see the sea.

They may never see the sea.

說已⁠經發⁠生，要⁠用 have 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

He may already left.

He may already have left.

例句

① may never + 原形動詞：可能永遠不

  1. Without a passport, she may never be able to visit her family abroad.
    沒有護照，她可能永遠沒辦法去國外看她的家人。
  2. If the factory closes, many people here may never work again.
    如果工廠關了，這裡很多人可能再也找不到工作。
  3. Some old songs may never be heard again.
    有些老歌可能再也不會有人聽到了。（被動）
  4. If you don't ask her now, you may never know the answer.
    如果你現在不問她，你可能永遠不會知道答案。
  5. Some of these birds may never return to the lake.
    這些鳥有一些可能再也不會回到湖邊。
  6. Without help, poor families may never own a home.
    沒有幫助，窮困的家庭可能永遠買不起房子。

② may even + 原形動詞：甚至可能

  1. In an old library, you may even find books from the 1600s.
    在老圖書館裡，你甚至可能找到十七世紀的書。
  2. If you keep reading English novels, you may even start to dream in English.
    如果你一直讀英文小說，你甚至可能開始用英文做夢。
  3. A short walk every day may even help you live longer.
    每天散一小段步甚至可能讓你活得更久。
  4. The rain may even stop before lunch.
    雨甚至可能在午餐前就停了。
  5. Learning to cook may even save you money.
    學煮飯甚至可能幫你省錢。

③ may only + 原形動詞：可能只

  1. The ticket may only cost a few pounds if you book early.
    如果你早點訂，票可能只要幾英鎊。
  2. The repair may only take an hour.
    修理可能只要一個小時。
  3. We may only stay in Paris for one night.
    我們可能只在巴黎住一晚。
  4. The festival may only happen once every four years.
    這個節慶可能每四年才辦一次。
  5. Some students may only need a little extra help.
    有些學生可能只需要一點額外的幫助。

④ may already + 原形動詞：可能已經

  1. Don't wait for her. She may already be at the restaurant.
    別等她了。她可能已經在餐廳了。
  2. Hurry up! The film may already have started.
    快一點！電影可能已經開始了。
  3. You may already know some of the people in the group.
    這個團體裡的一些人你可能已經認識了。
  4. He may already have sold the car.
    他可能已經把車賣掉了。
  5. The tickets may already be sold out.
    票可能已經賣完了。（被動）

⑤ may just + 原形動詞：說不定

  1. If we leave now, we may just catch the last train.
    如果我們現在走，說不定還趕得上最後一班火車。
  2. Keep trying. You may just win next time.
    繼續努力。說不定你下次會贏。
  3. Ask the manager. He may just give you a discount.
    問問經理。說不定他會給你折扣。
  4. Send your CV. They may just call you for an interview.
    寄你的履歷去。說不定他們會打電話找你面試。
  5. The weather may just stay dry for the wedding.
    說不定婚禮那天不會下雨。

⑥ may sometimes + 原形動詞：有時候可能

  1. Travelling alone may sometimes feel lonely.
    一個人旅行有時候可能會覺得寂寞。
  2. Parents may sometimes forget what it's like to be young.
    父母有時候可能會忘了年輕是什麼感覺。
  3. The lift may sometimes stop between floors.
    電梯有時候可能會停在兩層樓中間。
  4. Online reviews may sometimes give the wrong idea.
    網路評論有時候可能會讓人誤會。
  5. Even good friends may sometimes disagree.
    就算是好朋友，有時候也可能意見不合。
