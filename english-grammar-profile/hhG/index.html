Let me tell you my story.

我來跟你說說我的故事。

Let me 引⁠出接⁠下⁠來要說⁠的事

Let me tell you my story.

我來解釋這是怎麼運作的。

常⁠見⁠的說⁠法：舉⁠例、示⁠範、想一⁠下

Let me tell you my story.

舉例

The speaker talked about saving money. Let me give you an example from my own life.

講者談到存錢。我舉一個我自己生活中的例子。
示範

The new coffee machine is a bit tricky. Let me show you how to use the machine.

新的咖啡機有點難用。我示範給你看這台機器怎麼用。
想一下

You want me to move to Paris with you? Let me think about it.

你要我跟你搬去巴黎？讓我想一想。

let me 後⁠面不⁠加 to

Let me tell you my story.

Let me to explain.

Let me explain.

let 後⁠面用受格 me

Let me tell you my story.

Let I tell you something.

Let me tell you something.

let me 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Let me tell you my story.

Let me showing you the way.

Let me show you the way.

例句

① Let me tell / explain

  1. Sit down, you won't believe this. Let me tell you what happened last night.
    坐下，你一定不會相信。我來告訴你昨晚發生了什麼事。
  2. The coach gathered the team after training. Let me explain the plan for tomorrow.
    教練在練習後把隊員集合起來。我來說明明天的計畫。
  3. Not everyone knows this card game. Let me explain the rules before we play.
    不是每個人都會玩這個紙牌遊戲。玩之前我先解釋規則。
  4. You're nervous about Monday? Let me tell you a funny thing about my first day at work.
    你為了星期一緊張？我跟你說一件我上班第一天的趣事。
  5. I'm sorry, everyone. Let me explain why I was late.
    各位，對不起。我來解釋我為什麼遲到。
  6. I got back last night. Let me tell you about my trip to Japan.
    我昨天晚上回來的。我來跟你說說我的日本之旅。

② Let me answer / give

  1. I'm on my phone on the train now. Let me answer your email properly tomorrow.
    我現在在火車上用手機。我明天再好好回覆你的電子郵件。
  2. The speaker talked about saving money. Let me give you an example from my own life.
    講者談到存錢。我舉一個我自己生活中的例子。
  3. I've worked at that company for five years. Let me give you a piece of advice about the interview.
    我在那家公司工作了五年。關於面試，我給你一個建議。
  4. You asked about the price and the date. Let me answer that one first.
    你問了價錢和日期。我先回答那個問題。
  5. Call me if you need help with the move. Let me give you my phone number.
    搬家需要幫忙就打給我。我給你我的電話號碼。

③ Let me show / start

  1. Welcome to the office on your first day. Let me show you where the kitchen and the meeting rooms are.
    歡迎你第一天來上班。我帶你看廚房和會議室在哪裡。
  2. The new coffee machine is a bit tricky. Let me show you how to use the machine.
    新的咖啡機有點難用。我示範給你看這台機器怎麼用。
  3. The manager opened the meeting. Let me start with the good news.
    經理開始開會。我先說好消息。
  4. The bride stood up to speak at the wedding. Let me start by saying thank you.
    新娘在婚禮上站起來致詞。首先我要說聲謝謝。
  5. You couldn't come in June, so let me show you the photos from our wedding.
    你六月沒辦法來，所以我給你看看我們婚禮的照片。

④ Let me think / see / check

  1. You want me to move to Paris with you? Let me think about it.
    你要我跟你搬去巴黎？讓我想一想。
  2. The receptionist looked at her computer. Let me see. Your room is on the third floor.
    櫃台人員看了看電腦。我看看，你的房間在三樓。
  3. Lunch next week? Let me check my calendar.
    下星期一起吃午餐？我查一下我的行事曆。
  4. When did you last see your keys? Let me think. Was it Monday or Tuesday?
    你最後一次看到你的鑰匙是什麼時候？我想想，是星期一還是星期二？
  5. I'd love a day off on Friday. Let me check with my boss first.
    我很想星期五請一天假。我先問一下我的老闆。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. This is my first time bringing family to the office party. Let me introduce my sister, Ann.
    這是我第一次帶家人來公司派對。我來介紹我妹妹 Ann。
  2. The meeting is nearly over. Let me say a few words about the project.
    會議快結束了。我來說幾句關於這個專案的話。
  3. You keep stopping me in the middle. Let me finish, please.
    你一直在我說到一半的時候打斷我。請讓我把話說完。
  4. The teacher looked at the class. Let me be clear about one thing. No phones in the exam.
    老師看著全班。有一件事我要說清楚。考試不能用手機。
  5. You look confused. Let me put it another way.
    你看起來很困惑。我換個方式說。
  6. I'm thinking of taking the flat. Let me ask you a quick question about the rent.
    我在考慮租這間公寓。我問你一個關於房租的問題。
  7. You've got three bags and an umbrella. Let me help you with that bag.
    你拿了三個袋子和一把傘。我幫你提那個袋子。
  8. You haven't seen any photos yet. Let me describe the house to you first.
    你還沒看過照片。我先跟你描述一下那間房子。
  9. You want to buy a present after work? Let me remind you that the shop closes at six.
    你想下班後去買禮物？提醒你，那家店六點關門。
