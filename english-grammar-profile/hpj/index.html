It looks good on you.

這個你穿起來很好看。

look 連⁠接主⁠詞和補⁠語，意⁠思是看⁠起⁠來

這個你穿起來很好看。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞說看⁠起⁠來、聽⁠起⁠來、感⁠覺起⁠來或變⁠得

It looks good on you.

sound

The new song sounds great.

新歌聽起來很棒。
feel

This blanket feels warm.

這條毯子摸起來很暖。
get

The roads will get icy tonight.

今晚路面會結冰。
become

My neighbour became a good friend.

我的鄰居成了好朋友。

補⁠語說⁠明主⁠詞，用形⁠容⁠詞，不⁠用副⁠詞

It looks good on you.

咖哩聞起來很香。

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞後⁠面⁠的補⁠語用形⁠容⁠詞

It looks good on you.

That dress looks beautifully on you.

That dress looks beautiful on you.

連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接形⁠容⁠詞，不⁠加 like

Your lemonade tastes refreshing.

主⁠詞是 it，現⁠在⁠式動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠⁠s

It looks good on you.

The water feels cold this morning.

例句

① look + 形容詞

  1. Try this hat. It looks great with your coat.
    戴戴看這頂帽子。它跟你的外套很搭。
  2. You look pale this morning. Are you feeling OK?
    你今天早上臉色好蒼白，你還好嗎？
  3. The cake looks delicious. Can I have some?
    那個蛋糕看起來好好吃，我可以吃一點嗎？
  4. Your garden looks lovely in spring.
    你的花園春天看起來好漂亮。

② sound + 形容詞

  1. I'd like to join the art class because it sounds interesting.
    我想參加美術課，因為聽起來很有意思。
  2. A picnic on Sunday? That sounds great.
    星期天去野餐？聽起來很棒。
  3. You sound worried. What's wrong?
    你聽起來很擔心，怎麼了？
  4. His plan sounds a bit dangerous to me.
    他的計畫我聽起來有點危險。

③ feel + 形容詞

  1. I feel sorry for you. I hope you get better soon.
    我替你難過，希望你快點好起來。
  2. Can we stop the car? I feel dizzy on these mountain roads.
    可以停車嗎？在這些山路上我覺得頭好暈。
  3. The new carpet feels soft under my feet.
    新地毯踩起來好軟。
  4. We felt very happy after the match.
    比賽後我們覺得很開心。

④ get + 形容詞

  1. Wear something over your clothes, so they won't get dirty.
    在衣服外面套件東西，這樣衣服才不會弄髒。
  2. It gets dark early in winter.
    冬天天黑得很早。
  3. Hurry up! Your dinner is getting cold.
    快點！你的晚餐要涼了。
  4. My grandmother gets tired quickly these days.
    我奶奶最近很容易累。

⑤ become + 形容詞 / 名詞片語

  1. After the trip, the two boys became close friends.
    那趟旅行之後，兩個男孩成了好朋友。
  2. She became a doctor after ten years of study.
    她讀了十年書後成了醫生。
  3. The weather became very hot in July.
    七月天氣變得很熱。
  4. The town became famous after the film.
    那部電影之後，這個小鎮出名了。

⑥ smell / taste + 形容詞

  1. Something smells good. Are you cooking?
    有東西好香，你在煮飯嗎？
  2. This milk tastes strange. Is it old?
    這牛奶喝起來怪怪的，是不是放太久了？
  3. The fresh sheets smell lovely after a day in the sun.
    新床單曬了一天太陽，聞起來好香。
  4. Grandma's apple pie always tastes amazing.
    奶奶做的蘋果派總是好吃得不得了。

⑦ 過去式

  1. The film looked boring, so we didn't go.
    那部電影看起來很無聊，所以我們沒去。
  2. Her voice sounded strange on the phone.
    她在電話裡的聲音聽起來怪怪的。
  3. My son felt proud when he scored his first goal.
    我兒子進了他的第一球時覺得很驕傲。
  4. It got very cold in the night.
    夜裡變得很冷。

⑧ 疑問句

  1. Do I look OK in this dress?
    我穿這件洋裝好看嗎？
  2. How does my hair look? I cut it myself.
    我的頭髮看起來怎麼樣？我自己剪的。
  3. Does your ankle still feel sore after the run?
    跑完步後你的腳踝還會痠痛嗎？
