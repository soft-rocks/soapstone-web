My neighbors are making too much noise.

我的鄰居實在太吵了。

too much 加不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞

我的鄰居實在太吵了。

so much 很⁠多，too much 太⁠多

My neighbors are making too much noise.

so much

I have so much work at the moment that I can't help you.

我現在工作多到沒辦法幫你。
too much

My neighbors are making too much noise.

我的鄰居實在太吵了。

so much 常接 that 子⁠句

My neighbors are making too much noise.

有 that

There was so much snow that the school closed.

雪多到學校停課了。
沒有 that

I ate so much food I couldn't move.

我吃了好多東西，撐到動不了。

可⁠數⁠名⁠詞用 too many

My neighbors are making too much noise.

There were too much people at the party.

There were too many people at the party.

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面只⁠用 too

My neighbors are making too much noise.

This bag is too much heavy.

This bag is too heavy.

名⁠詞前⁠面不⁠用 very much

My neighbors are making too much noise.

I have very much work today.

I have so much work today.

例句

① too much + 不可數名詞

  1. I can't sleep. My neighbors are making too much noise.
    我睡不著。我的鄰居實在太吵了。
  2. Grandpa has to be careful with his heart. Don't put too much salt in the soup.
    爺爺的心臟要小心。湯裡不要放太多鹽。
  3. Put it down and go outside! You spend too much time on your phone.
    放下來去外面走走！你花太多時間在手機上了。
  4. I can't eat this. There's too much sugar in it.
    我不能吃這個。裡面糖太多了。
  5. We left early because there was too much traffic.
    我們提早出門，因為車太多了。
  6. The teacher gave us too much homework this weekend.
    老師這個週末出了太多功課。

② so much + 不可數名詞

  1. Hello Marina. Unfortunately, I have so much work at the moment that I can't help you.
    嗨，Marina。很抱歉，我現在工作多到沒辦法幫你。
  2. Thank you so much for the present. I had so much fun at your party!
    謝謝你的禮物。你的派對我玩得好開心！
  3. There's so much food left. Take some home with you.
    剩下好多食物。帶一些回家吧。
  4. She has so much energy. She never sits down.
    她精力好旺盛。她從來不坐下來。
  5. I've learned so much English this year.
    我今年學了好多英文。

③ so much ... that

  1. There was so much snow that the school closed for two days.
    雪多到學校停課兩天。
  2. He drank so much coffee that he couldn't sleep.
    他喝了太多咖啡，結果睡不著。
  3. We had so much rain last month that the river flooded.
    上個月雨下得太多，河水氾濫了。
  4. There was so much smoke that we couldn't see the road.
    煙多到我們看不到路。

④ too much：抱怨

  1. The new phone costs too much money for a student.
    這支新手機對學生來說太貴了。
  2. There's too much rubbish on the beach. Someone should clean it up.
    海灘上垃圾太多了。應該有人來清。
  3. No wonder he has bad teeth. My brother eats too much chocolate.
    難怪他牙齒不好。我弟弟吃太多巧克力了。
  4. This juice tastes of nothing. You've added too much water to it.
    這果汁一點味道都沒有。你在裡面加太多水了。

⑤ 不同的時態

  1. I had too much homework yesterday, so I didn't call you.
    我昨天功課太多，所以沒打給你。
  2. We'll have so much time when the exams are over.
    考完試以後我們會有好多時間。
  3. There has been too much talk and not enough action.
    說得太多，做得太少。
  4. They were making so much noise that the police came.
    他們吵到警察都來了。

⑥ 工作和學校

  1. I have so much work this week that I can't come to the party.
    我這星期工作多到不能去派對。
  2. They have exams and extra classes every week. Students today are under too much pressure.
    他們每個星期都有考試和補習。現在的學生壓力太大了。
  3. There's so much information online that it's hard to know what's true.
    網路上的資訊多到很難知道什麼是真的。

⑦ 對話

  1. "Do you want more cake?" "No, thanks. I've had too much cake already."
    還要蛋糕嗎？不用了，謝謝。我已經吃太多蛋糕了。
  2. "Why are you late?" "There was so much traffic on the bridge."
    你怎麼遲到了？橋上車好多。
  3. "Is the music OK?" "It's a bit loud. It's making too much noise for the baby."
    音樂可以嗎？有點大聲。對寶寶來說太吵了。
  4. "How was your holiday?" "Great! We had so much fun."
    你的假期怎麼樣？很棒！我們玩得好開心。
  5. "Can you help me move on Saturday?" "Sorry, I've got so much homework."
    你星期六可以幫我搬家嗎？抱歉，我有好多功課。
