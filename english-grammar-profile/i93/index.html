The restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.

那家餐廳沒開，因為正在重新裝潢。

was 加 being，再⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

因為正在重新裝潢。

when 和 while 接正⁠在進⁠行⁠的事

The restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.

when

We arrived when the room was being cleaned.

我們到的時候，房間正在打掃。
while

We stayed with friends while our car was being repaired.

我們的車在修的時候，我們住在朋友家。

was being 正⁠在進⁠行，was 已⁠經做⁠完

The restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.

正在進行

The bridge was being built in 2010.

2010 年那座橋正在蓋。
做完了

The bridge was built in 2010.

那座橋是 2010 年蓋的。

用 was being，不⁠用 was been

The restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.

We couldn't use the kitchen because it was been painted.

We couldn't use the kitchen because it was being painted.

複⁠數主⁠詞用 were

The restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.

The rooms was being cleaned when we arrived.

The rooms were being cleaned when we arrived.

being 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.

The car was being repairing.

The car was being repaired.

例句

① was being + 過去分詞

  1. We wanted to have dinner at Mario's, but the restaurant was closed because it was being redecorated.
    我們想去 Mario's 吃晚餐，可是那家餐廳沒開，因為正在重新裝潢。
  2. I couldn't use the computer this morning. It was being repaired.
    我今天早上不能用電腦。電腦在修。
  3. Sorry I didn't answer. My phone was being charged in the other room.
    抱歉我沒接。我的手機在另一個房間充電。
  4. Our hotel was very noisy because a new pool was being built next to it.
    我們的旅館很吵，因為旁邊在蓋新的游泳池。
  5. When I got home, dinner was being cooked and the table was already set.
    我回到家的時候，晚餐正在煮，桌子已經擺好了。
  6. The old man looked behind him. He felt he was being followed.
    那個老人回頭看。他覺得有人在跟蹤他。

② were being + 過去分詞

  1. We had to wait outside because the rooms were being cleaned.
    我們只能在外面等，因為房間正在打掃。
  2. When I visited the farm, the cows were being milked.
    我去農場的時候，他們正在擠牛奶。
  3. The roads were being cleared of snow all night, so we could drive in the morning.
    路上的雪整晚都在清，所以我們早上可以開車。
  4. I saw lots of police at the station. Some young men were being questioned.
    我在車站看到很多警察。有幾個年輕人正在接受盤問。
  5. We didn't sleep much on the ferry. The cars were being loaded all night.
    我們在渡輪上沒睡多少。車子整晚都在裝船。

③ while + was / were being

  1. We stayed at your house while our car was being repaired.
    我們的車在修的時候，我們住在你家。
  2. While the house was being painted, we lived with my parents.
    房子在油漆的時候，我們住在我爸媽家。
  3. The children played in the garden while the party food was being prepared.
    派對的食物在準備的時候，孩子們在花園玩。
  4. While the plane was being cleaned, the passengers waited at the gate.
    飛機在清潔的時候，乘客在登機門等。

④ when 和過去進行式被動

  1. When we arrived at the airport, our bags were being checked.
    我們到機場的時候，行李正在檢查。
  2. The fire started when the old factory was being pulled down.
    舊工廠在拆的時候起火了。
  3. I took a photo of the statue when it was being moved to the new square.
    雕像搬到新廣場的時候，我拍了一張照片。
  4. When I opened the door, the new carpet was being put down.
    我打開門的時候，新地毯正在鋪。

⑤ 人當主詞

  1. After they explained everything to him, he knew he was being tricked by them.
    他們把一切解釋給他聽之後，他才知道自己被他們耍了。
  2. I couldn't talk to the nurse. I was being examined by the doctor.
    我沒辦法跟護士說話。醫生正在幫我檢查。
  3. Tom wasn't at school yesterday. He was being interviewed for a TV programme.
    Tom 昨天沒來學校。他在接受一個電視節目的訪問。
  4. We felt we were being watched the whole time we were in the shop.
    我們在店裡的時候，一直覺得有人在看我們。
  5. The new students were being shown around the school when the fire alarm went off.
    新生正在參觀學校的時候，火災警報響了。

⑥ 解釋為什麼不能做

  1. We couldn't get to the beach because the road was being repaired.
    我們沒辦法去海邊，因為路在修。
  2. The museum was closed on Monday. A new exhibition was being set up.
    博物館星期一沒開。他們在布置新的展覽。
  3. Sorry you couldn't reach me last night. Our phone line was being fixed.
    抱歉你昨晚聯絡不到我。我們的電話線在修。
  4. The match was stopped because a player was being treated on the pitch.
    比賽暫停了，因為有一個球員在場上接受治療。

⑦ 問句

  1. "Why were you so late?" "My train was being checked for a problem with the doors."
    你怎麼這麼晚？我的火車在檢查車門的問題。
  2. "What was happening when you got there?" "The prizes were being given out."
    你到的時候在做什麼？正在頒獎。
  3. "Was the house being sold when you moved in next door?" "Yes, it was for sale for months."
    你搬到隔壁的時候，那間房子在賣嗎？對，掛了好幾個月。
