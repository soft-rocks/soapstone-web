Why didn't you say something about it?

這件事你為什麼都不說點什麼？

Why didn't 問過⁠去為⁠什⁠麼沒⁠做

這件事你為什麼都不說點什麼？

現⁠在用 don't，到現⁠在還⁠沒⁠做用 haven't

Why didn't you say something about it?

don't
現在

Why don't you go to your doctor?

Why haven't you told me before?

Why didn't you say something about it?

例句

① Why didn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞：問過去為什麼沒做

  1. Your room has been wet for weeks. Why didn't you say something about it?
    你的房間濕了好幾個星期。這件事你為什麼都不說點什麼？
  2. You wrote that the concert was cancelled. Why didn't you check with the ticket office first?
    你寫說演唱會取消了。你為什麼沒先問售票處？
  3. I waited for an hour. Why didn't you call me?
    我等了一個小時。你為什麼沒打給我？
  4. Why didn't you come to my party? Everyone asked about you.
    你為什麼沒來我的派對？大家都在問你。
  5. You knew the answer. Why didn't you put your hand up?
    你知道答案。你為什麼沒舉手？
  6. We were all late for school. Why didn't your clock ring this morning?
    我們上學都遲到了。你的鬧鐘今天早上為什麼沒響？
  7. We've known them for years. Why didn't they ask us to be at the wedding?
    我們認識他們好多年了。他們為什麼沒請我們去參加婚禮？
  8. Why didn't she take the job? It paid very well.
    她為什麼沒接那份工作？薪水很高耶。

② Why don't / doesn't + 主詞 + 原形動詞：問現在的情況

  1. You still have this terrible headache. Why don't you go to your doctor?
    你還在嚴重頭痛。你為什麼不去看醫生？
  2. Grandma always asks about him. Why doesn't your brother ever come to family dinners?
    奶奶每次都問起他。你哥哥為什麼從來都不來家族聚餐？
  3. Why don't they fix the road? It's been broken for months.
    他們為什麼不修那條路？已經壞好幾個月了。
  4. I've turned it on twice. Why doesn't any water come out of this tap?
    我開了兩次。這個水龍頭為什麼都不出水？
  5. Why don't you like Italian food? Everyone likes pizza!
    你為什麼不喜歡義大利菜？每個人都喜歡披薩啊！
  6. I've texted Mia three times today. Why doesn't she ever reply?
    我今天傳了三次訊息給 Mia。她為什麼都不回？

③ Why haven't / hasn't + 主詞 + 過去分詞：問到現在為什麼還沒做

  1. We've been friends for years. Why haven't you told me before?
    我們做朋友好多年了。你為什麼之前都沒告訴我？
  2. Why haven't you finished your homework? You've had all weekend.
    你為什麼還沒寫完功課？你有一整個週末的時間。
  3. The kitchen is still flooded. Why hasn't the plumber called back?
    廚房還在淹水。水電工為什麼還沒回電？
  4. I sent it a month ago. Why haven't they replied to my application?
    我一個月前就寄出了。他們為什麼還沒回覆我的申請？
  5. There are dirty plates everywhere. Why hasn't anyone cleaned the kitchen?
    到處都是髒盤子。為什麼沒有人打掃廚房？

④ 其他疑問詞 + don't / didn't

  1. What don't you understand? I'll explain it again.
    你哪裡不懂？我再解釋一次。
  2. Your test has some empty spaces. Which parts didn't you do?
    你的考卷有些空白。你哪些部分沒寫？
  3. I only have twenty notebooks here, and there are twenty-five of you. Who didn't bring their homework today?
    我這裡只有二十本作業簿，你們有二十五個人。今天誰沒帶功課？
  4. You gave the film two stars. What didn't you enjoy about it?
    你給那部電影兩顆星。你不喜歡它哪裡？
  5. Let's cross some places off the list first. Where don't you want to go on holiday?
    我們先從清單上刪掉一些地方。你度假不想去哪裡？

⑤ 表示責備或失望

  1. I found out from your sister. Why didn't you mention you were moving abroad?
    我是從你姊姊那裡知道的。你為什麼沒提你要搬去國外？
  2. Why didn't you listen to me? I told you it was dangerous.
    你為什麼不聽我的？我跟你說過很危險。
  3. I cooked dinner and I washed the dishes again. Why don't you ever help with the washing-up?
    我煮了晚餐，又洗了碗。你為什麼從來都不幫忙洗碗？
  4. Why haven't you called your grandmother? It's her birthday.
    你為什麼還沒打給奶奶？今天是她生日。

⑥ 表示驚訝

  1. I drove an hour to get here, and the room is empty. Why didn't anyone tell me the meeting was cancelled?
    我開了一小時的車過來，會議室卻是空的。為什麼都沒有人告訴我會議取消了？
  2. You love animals. Why don't you get a dog?
    你那麼愛動物。你為什麼不養狗？
  3. Why didn't we think of that before? It's so simple!
    我們之前為什麼沒想到？這麼簡單！
