The food was delicious, especially the cake, which Sandra had made herself.

食物很好吃，尤其是蛋糕，那是 Sandra 自己做的。

反⁠身代⁠名⁠詞放⁠在句⁠尾，強⁠調是這⁠個人自⁠己做⁠的

食物很好吃，尤其是蛋糕，那是 Sandra 自己做的。

放⁠在句⁠尾，強⁠調沒⁠有請別⁠人做

The food was delicious, especially the cake, which Sandra had made herself.

myself

I made this dress myself.

這件洋裝是我自己做的。
yourself

You should decide yourself what to do.

你應該自己決定要怎麼做。
himself

He built the bookshelf himself.

書架是他自己做的。

緊⁠接在名⁠詞或代⁠名⁠詞後⁠面，強⁠調是這⁠個人本⁠人

The food was delicious, especially the cake, which Sandra had made herself.

名詞後面

It was the director himself who showed us around the garden.

帶我們參觀花園的是導演本人。
代名詞後面

I myself have never been to London.

我本人從來沒去過倫敦。

強⁠調⁠的反⁠身代⁠名⁠詞拿⁠掉⁠後，句⁠子還⁠是完⁠整⁠的

The food was delicious, especially the cake, which Sandra had made herself.

強調

She designed the logo herself.

拿掉 herself，句子還是完整的。
受詞

She hurt herself.

herself 是 hurt 的受詞，不能拿掉。

my brother 是男⁠性，用 himself

The food was delicious, especially the cake, which Sandra had made herself.

My brother fixed the car herself.

My brother fixed the car himself.

正⁠確⁠的形⁠式是 himself

He painted the house hisself.

He painted the house himself.

主⁠詞用 I，myself 放⁠在句⁠尾

Myself will call the hotel.

I'll call the hotel myself.

例句

① 放在句尾

  1. Did you paint this yourself? It's beautiful.
    這是你自己畫的嗎？好漂亮。
  2. She planned the whole wedding herself.
    整場婚禮都是她自己策劃的。
  3. I'll call the manager myself if nobody else does.
    如果沒人打，我就自己打給經理。
  4. If you don't believe me, go and look yourself.
    你不相信的話，就自己去看。
  5. My dad cut my hair himself to save money.
    為了省錢，我爸自己幫我剪頭髮。
  6. Grandma grows all her vegetables herself.
    奶奶的蔬菜都是自己種的。
  7. I haven't read the report myself, but my boss says it's good.
    我自己還沒看那份報告，可是我老闆說寫得不錯。
  8. Why don't you ask her yourself?
    你為什麼不自己去問她？
  9. He told me the news himself.
    這個消息是他親口告訴我的。
  10. I fixed the washing machine myself, and it works perfectly.
    洗衣機是我自己修的，現在運作得很好。
  11. You can't do everything yourself, you know.
    你知道嗎，你沒辦法每件事都自己來。
  12. Our music teacher wrote all the songs herself.
    我們的音樂老師自己寫了所有的歌。
  13. Did he really cook all this food himself?
    這些菜真的都是他自己煮的嗎？

② 緊接在名詞或代名詞後面

  1. The president herself came to open the new hospital.
    總統本人親自來為新醫院揭幕。
  2. You yourself said the plan wouldn't work.
    你自己說過這個計畫行不通。
  3. The author himself signed my copy of the book.
    作者本人在我那本書上簽了名。
  4. The chef herself came out to talk to us.
    主廚本人出來跟我們聊天。
  5. The manager himself apologized for the mistake.
    經理本人親自為這個錯誤道歉。
  6. I myself think the price is too high.
    我本人覺得價錢太高了。
  7. Did the singer herself reply to your message?
    歌手本人有回你的訊息嗎？
  8. You yourself are the best person to answer that.
    你自己就是最適合回答這個問題的人。
  9. The king himself attended the ceremony.
    國王本人出席了典禮。
  10. Even the teacher herself didn't know the answer.
    連老師本人都不知道答案。
  11. I myself would never lend anyone that much money.
    換成我自己，絕對不會借任何人那麼多錢。
  12. The owner himself greeted us at the door.
    老闆本人在門口迎接我們。
  13. You yourself must sign the form; nobody can sign it for you.
    你本人必須在表格上簽名，沒有人可以替你簽。
  14. I myself grew up in a small fishing village.
    我自己就是在一個小漁村長大的。
  15. The doctor herself admitted that the treatment was risky.
    醫生本人也承認這個療法有風險。
  16. Even the coach himself was surprised by the result.
    連教練本人都對結果感到意外。
  17. My sister herself admits that she spends too much on clothes.
    我姊姊自己也承認她花太多錢買衣服。
  18. I myself prefer tea, but my husband drinks coffee.
    我自己比較喜歡茶，可是我先生喝咖啡。
