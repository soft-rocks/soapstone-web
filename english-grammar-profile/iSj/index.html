The colour is blue, and I like that colour because it's my favourite.

顏色是藍色的。我喜歡那個顏色，因為那是我最喜歡的顏色。

that 指前⁠面說⁠過⁠的東⁠西

The colour is blue, and I like that colour because it's my favourite.

顏色是藍色的。我喜歡那個顏色，因為那是我最喜歡的顏色。

前⁠面說⁠過，後⁠面用 that

The colour is blue, and I like that colour because it's my favourite.

過去的事

I bought a skirt at a small shop in town. You should go to that shop.

我在鎮上一家小店買了一條裙子。你應該去那家店看看。
對方剛說的

What's that book called?

那本書叫什麼名字？

this 和現⁠在有⁠關，that 是過⁠去⁠的

The colour is blue, and I like that colour because it's my favourite.

this

I have a new job. This job is very interesting.

我有一份新工作。這份工作很有趣。
that

I had a job at a bank. That job was very boring.

我以前在銀行上班。那份工作很無聊。

go 後⁠面⁠接 to

You should go that shop.

You should go to that shop.

再提⁠到同⁠一⁠個東⁠西用 that

The colour is blue, and I like that colour because it's my favourite.

We stayed at a hotel in Rome. A hotel was very old.

We stayed at a hotel in Rome. That hotel was very old.

前⁠面是複⁠數，後⁠面用 those

My friend recommended two films. I watched that films last night.

My friend recommended two films. I watched those films last night.

that 後⁠面不⁠加 the

The colour is blue, and I like that colour because it's my favourite.

He told me about a café. I went to that the café yesterday.

He told me about a café. I went to that café yesterday.

例句

① 說過去買的東西

  1. I bought a jacket in the sales. That jacket was only £20.
    我在特價時買了一件夾克。那件夾克才二十英鎊。
  2. My dad had an old car when I was a child. We drove all over Europe in that car.
    我小時候我爸爸有一台舊車。我們開那台車跑遍了歐洲。
  3. I got a guitar for my tenth birthday. I still play that guitar.
    我十歲生日收到一把吉他。我現在還在彈那把吉他。
  4. She found a lovely lamp at the market. That lamp is now in her bedroom.
    她在市場找到一盞很漂亮的燈。那盞燈現在在她的臥室裡。

② 推薦地方

  1. There's a great Thai restaurant near the station. You should try that restaurant.
    車站附近有一家很棒的泰國餐廳。你應該去吃吃看那家餐廳。
  2. We went to a beautiful beach last summer. That beach is in the south of Spain.
    我們去年夏天去了一個很漂亮的海灘。那個海灘在西班牙南部。
  3. I stayed at a small hotel in Kyoto. I'd love to go back to that hotel.
    我在京都住過一間小旅館。我很想再去住那間旅館。
  4. My aunt took me to a museum in Paris. That museum has lots of paintings by Monet.
    我阿姨帶我去巴黎的一間博物館。那間博物館有很多莫內的畫。

③ 說喜歡和不喜歡

  1. My new bedroom is green. I don't really like that colour.
    我的新房間是綠色的。我不太喜歡那個顏色。
  2. Have you heard the new song by Adele? I love that song.
    你聽過 Adele 的新歌嗎？我好愛那首歌。
  3. We had fish soup for lunch. My brother didn't like that soup at all.
    我們午餐喝魚湯。我弟弟一點也不喜歡那個湯。
  4. My mum often wears a red scarf. That scarf looks great on her.
    我媽媽常圍一條紅色圍巾。那條圍巾她圍起來很好看。

④ 說學校和課程

  1. I want to learn Japanese. That language is useful for my job.
    我想學日文。那個語言對我的工作很有用。
  2. There's a cooking class on Tuesday evenings. I want to join that class.
    星期二晚上有一堂烹飪課。我想參加那堂課。
  3. My sister studied at Oxford. She loved her time at that university.
    我姊姊在牛津讀書。她很喜歡在那所大學的日子。
  4. I took a history course last year. That course was really hard.
    我去年修了一門歷史課。那門課真的很難。

⑤ 回應對方說的東西

  1. "I met a woman called Sarah at the party." "I think I know that woman."
    我在派對上認識了一個叫 Sarah 的女人。我好像認識那個女人。
  2. "We watched a film about penguins." "Was that film good?"
    我們看了一部講企鵝的電影。那部電影好看嗎？
  3. "I'm going to a new dentist." "Is that dentist expensive?"
    我要去看一個新的牙醫。那個牙醫很貴嗎？
  4. "My cousin works for a big bank." "How long has he worked at that bank?"
    我表哥在一家大銀行上班。他在那家銀行上班多久了？

⑥ 說過去的人

  1. When I was ten, I had a teacher called Mr Lee. That teacher changed my life.
    我十歲的時候，有一位老師叫李老師。那位老師改變了我的人生。
  2. A kind man helped me with my bags at the station. I never saw that man again.
    在車站有一個好心的人幫我提行李。我再也沒見過那個人。
  3. I shared a flat with a girl from Brazil. That girl is still my best friend.
    我以前跟一個巴西女生合租公寓。那個女生現在還是我最好的朋友。
  4. My grandmother had a neighbour called Rosa. That neighbour made the best cakes in the village.
    我奶奶以前有一個鄰居叫 Rosa。那個鄰居做的蛋糕是村子裡最好吃的。
  5. I had a piano teacher who was very strict. I was afraid of that teacher.
    我以前有一個很嚴格的鋼琴老師。我很怕那位老師。

⑦ 說過去的一件事

  1. We had a big storm last week. That storm broke three windows in our street.
    我們上星期有一場很大的暴風雨。那場暴風雨打破了我們這條街上三扇窗戶。
  2. I had a bad cold in March. I missed two weeks of school because of that cold.
    我三月得了重感冒。因為那場感冒，我有兩個星期沒去上學。
  3. There was a party at Tom's house on Friday. Everyone danced at that party.
    星期五 Tom 家有一個派對。那個派對上大家都在跳舞。
  4. We had a big argument about money. After that argument, we didn't speak for a week.
    我們為了錢大吵了一架。那次吵完以後，我們一個星期都沒講話。
  5. My brother entered a singing competition. He won that competition!
    我哥哥參加了一個歌唱比賽。他贏了那個比賽！
