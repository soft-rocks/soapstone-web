I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

我最好就此打住，繼續唸我的書。

片⁠語介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞由動⁠詞、小⁠品⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞組⁠成

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

我最好就此打住，繼續唸我的書。

get on with 的意思是繼續做。

受⁠詞放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面，代⁠名⁠詞也一⁠樣

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

名詞

We ran out of milk.

我們的牛奶喝完了。
代名詞

We ran out of it.

我們把它用完了。

同⁠一⁠個動⁠詞，小⁠品⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞不⁠同，意⁠思不⁠同

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

get on with
相處

I get on with my sister.

我和我姊姊處得很好。
get away from
離開

I need to get away from the office.

我需要離開辦公室一下。
get out of
下車

He got out of the car.

他下了車。

catch up 後⁠面⁠接人⁠時，要加介⁠系⁠詞 with

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

I need to catch up the others.

I need to catch up with the others.

小⁠品⁠詞不⁠能省⁠略

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

We ran of sugar.

We ran out of sugar.

run out 的介⁠系⁠詞是 of

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

We ran out from milk.

We ran out of milk.

介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I'd better stop now and get on with my studying.

Let's get on with clean the kitchen.

Let's get on with cleaning the kitchen.

介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞沒⁠有小⁠品⁠詞，片⁠語動⁠詞沒⁠有介⁠系⁠詞

介系詞動詞

We talked about the trip.

我們聊了這趟旅行。
片語動詞

She gave up.

她放棄了。

例句

① get on with + 受詞（繼續做）

  1. "Sorry to interrupt." "That's OK. I'll get on with my work."
    抱歉打擾你。沒關係。我繼續工作。
  2. The teacher left the room and told us to get on with our reading.
    老師離開教室，叫我們繼續閱讀。
  3. Stop chatting and get on with your homework.
    別聊天了，繼續寫你的作業。
  4. The rain stopped, so the players got on with the game.
    雨停了，所以球員們繼續比賽。

② get on with + 受詞（相處）

  1. "How's your new flatmate?" "Great. I get on with her really well."
    你的新室友怎麼樣？很好。我跟她處得很好。
  2. He doesn't get on with his boss, so he wants a new job.
    他和老闆處不好，所以想換工作。
  3. Do your children get on with each other?
    你的孩子們彼此處得好嗎？

③ catch up with + 受詞

  1. I missed two weeks of school, so I have to catch up with the work.
    我缺了兩個星期的課，所以得把功課補上。
  2. You go ahead. I'll catch up with you at the station.
    你先走。我會在車站追上你。
  3. She walked so fast that I couldn't catch up with her.
    她走得好快，我追不上她。
  4. After my holiday, it took me two days to catch up with my emails.
    度假回來以後，我花了兩天才把電子郵件處理完。

④ get away from / keep away from + 受詞

  1. If you want a peaceful holiday, you should get away from the city.
    想要一個安靜的假期，就該離開城市。
  2. Keep away from the edge of the cliff!
    離懸崖邊遠一點！
  3. My doctor told me to keep away from sugar.
    醫生叫我少碰糖。
  4. On Fridays I like to get away from my desk at lunchtime.
    星期五午餐時間，我喜歡離開辦公桌一下。
  5. My brother has a cold, so I'm keeping away from him this week.
    我哥哥感冒了，所以這星期我離他遠一點。

⑤ run out of + 受詞

  1. "Can you buy some bread?" "Sure. Have we run out of milk too?"
    你可以買點麵包嗎？好啊。我們的牛奶也喝完了嗎？
  2. The car stopped because we ran out of petrol.
    車子停了，因為我們沒油了。
  3. I'm running out of ideas for dinner.
    我快想不出晚餐要煮什麼了。
  4. The printer has run out of paper again.
    印表機又沒紙了。

⑥ look forward to / go out with + 受詞

  1. My parents are looking forward to their holiday in Spain.
    我爸媽很期待去西班牙度假。
  2. "Who is Mark going out with?" "A girl from his office."
    Mark 在跟誰交往？他辦公室的一個女生。
  3. She went out with him for two years.
    她和他交往了兩年。

⑦ 受詞是代名詞

  1. "Where's the sugar?" "Sorry, we've run out of it."
    糖在哪裡？抱歉，我們用完了。
  2. Your sister is lovely. I get on with her very well.
    你姊姊人很好。我跟她處得很好。
  3. That dog bites. Keep away from it.
    那隻狗會咬人。離牠遠一點。
  4. The bus left, but I ran and caught up with it at the next stop.
    公車開走了，可是我跑到下一站趕上了它。

⑧ 受詞是動詞-ing

  1. It's late. I'd better get on with cooking dinner.
    很晚了。我最好繼續煮晚餐。
  2. "Are you ready for the trip?" "Not yet. I need to get on with packing."
    你準備好去旅行了嗎？還沒。我得繼續打包。
  3. We're looking forward to visiting you next month.
    我們很期待下個月去拜訪你。
  4. After lunch, the workers got on with painting the house.
    午餐後，工人們繼續油漆房子。
