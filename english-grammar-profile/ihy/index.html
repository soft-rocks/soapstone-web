Should you need any further information, don't hesitate to contact us.

如需更多資訊，歡迎隨時與我們聯絡。

Should + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞, + 句⁠子

Should you need any further information, don't hesitate to contact us.

Should放在句首，代替 if。
主詞放在 should 後面。
原形動詞可能發生的情況。

商⁠業信⁠件⁠的結⁠尾

例句

Should you have any questions, please contact me.

如果您有任何問題，請與我聯絡。

商業信件常用這句話結尾。

倒⁠裝⁠的時⁠候不⁠用 if

If should you need help, call us.

Should you need help, call us.

倒⁠裝把 should 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Should you need any further information, don't hesitate to contact us.

You should have any questions, please write.

Should you have any questions, please write.

should 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Should you need any further information, don't hesitate to contact us.

Should the parcel arrives late, tell us.

Should the parcel arrive late, tell us.

例句

① Should you + need / have + 名詞, + 祈使句：商業信件的結尾

  1. Thank you for choosing our company. Should you need any further information, don't hesitate to contact us.
    謝謝您選擇我們公司。如果您需要任何進一步的資訊，請隨時與我們聯絡。
  2. Should you have any questions about your order, please call our office.
    如果您對訂單有任何問題，請打電話到我們的辦公室。
  3. Should you require a receipt, please ask at the till.
    如果您需要收據，請在收銀台詢問。
  4. Should you need help with your luggage, our staff will be happy to assist.
    如果您的行李需要幫忙，我們的員工很樂意協助。
  5. Should you have any problems with the room, please call reception.
    如果房間有任何問題，請打電話給櫃台。
  6. Should you wish to cancel your booking, please let us know by Friday.
    如果您想取消預約，請在星期五前通知我們。

② Should you + 原形動詞, + 句子：正式的警告

  1. Should you withhold any important information or documents, this could stop any payment to you.
    如果您隱瞞任何重要的資訊或文件，可能會導致無法付款給您。
  2. Should you fail to pay by the end of the month, we will charge a fee.
    如果您月底前未付款，我們會收取費用。
  3. Should you break the rules again, you will be asked to leave the club.
    如果您再違反規定，您會被要求退出俱樂部。（被動）
  4. Should you miss more than three classes, you may not pass the course.
    如果您缺課超過三堂，您可能無法通過這門課。
  5. Should you lose your card, you must report it at once.
    如果您的卡遺失，您必須馬上報失。
  6. Should you damage the car, you will have to pay for the repairs.
    如果您損壞車子，您必須支付修理費。

③ Should + 名詞 + 原形動詞, + 句子：可能的情況

  1. Should the weather be bad, the concert will move to the town hall.
    如果天氣不好，音樂會會移到市政廳舉行。
  2. Should the flight be delayed, a driver will wait for you.
    如果班機延誤，司機會等您。（被動）
  3. Should the goods arrive damaged, we will replace them immediately.
    如果貨物到的時候有損壞，我們會馬上更換。
  4. Should the price change, we will tell you before you pay.
    如果價格有變動，我們會在您付款前通知您。
  5. Should the speaker be late, we will start with the questions.
    如果講者遲到，我們會先開始問答。
  6. Should the course be full, your name will go on a waiting list.
    如果課程額滿，您的名字會列入候補名單。

④ Should + 主詞 + 原形動詞, + 主詞 + can：提供選擇

  1. Should you prefer a quieter room, we can move you to the top floor.
    如果您比較喜歡安靜的房間，我們可以幫您換到頂樓。
  2. Should you wish to bring a guest, you can buy an extra ticket.
    如果您想帶客人，您可以多買一張票。
  3. Should you want to stay longer, you can extend your booking online.
    如果您想多住幾天，您可以在網路上延長預約。
  4. Should you be interested in the job, you can apply on our website.
    如果您對這份工作有興趣，您可以在我們的網站上申請。
  5. Should you need a parking space, we can reserve one for you.
    如果您需要停車位，我們可以幫您保留一個。
  6. Should you not be able to come, you can send a colleague.
    如果您不能來，您可以派一位同事來。

⑤ Should + 主詞 + 原形動詞, + 主詞 + will：正式的承諾

  1. Should you decide to join us, we will send you a welcome pack.
    如果您決定加入我們，我們會寄給您一份歡迎資料。
  2. Should the machine stop working, an engineer will visit within a day.
    如果機器停止運作，工程師會在一天內來。
  3. Should you not be satisfied, we will refund your money.
    如果您不滿意，我們會退錢給您。
  4. Should there be any changes to the timetable, we will email you.
    如果時間表有任何變動，我們會寄電子郵件給您。
  5. Should you arrive early, our staff will show you around.
    如果您提早到，我們的員工會帶您參觀。
  6. Should the item be out of stock, we will tell you at once.
    如果商品缺貨，我們會馬上通知您。
  7. Should you need to change your plans, we will do our best to help.
    如果您需要改變計畫，我們會盡力幫忙。
