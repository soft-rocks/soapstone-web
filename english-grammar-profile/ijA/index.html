Do you remember Julie?

你還記得 Julie 嗎？

Do、Does、Did 放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Do you remember Julie?

Do

Do you like spicy food?

你喜歡吃辣嗎？
Does

Excuse me, driver. Does this bus go to the airport?

不好意思，司機。這班公車有到機場嗎？
Did

You arrived in Vienna this morning. Did you sleep well on the night train?

你今天早上到維也納。你在夜車上睡得好嗎？

簡⁠短回⁠答用 do、does、did

Do you remember Julie?

A: Does your brother live near here? B: Yes, he does.

A：你哥哥住在這附近嗎？B：對，他住這附近。

does 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Do you remember Julie?

Does your cat sleeps on your bed?

Does your cat sleep on your bed?

did 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Do you remember Julie?

Did the plumber fixed the shower?

Did the plumber fix the shower?

一⁠般動⁠詞不⁠能放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，要加 do

Do you remember Julie?

Plays your son the violin?

Does your son play the violin?

例句

① Do you + 原形動詞?

  1. I found an old class photo. Do you remember our first teacher?
    我找到一張舊的班級照片。你記得我們的第一個老師嗎？
  2. Your fridge is always full. Do you cook at home most nights?
    你的冰箱總是滿的。你大多數晚上都在家煮飯嗎？
  3. Excuse me, we're new in town. Do you know the way to the stadium?
    不好意思，我們剛來這個鎮。你知道去體育場的路嗎？
  4. My tooth has hurt for a week. Do you know a good dentist?
    我的牙痛了一個星期。你知道哪裡有好的牙醫嗎？
  5. You look very fit. Do you play any sports?
    你看起來身材很好。你有做什麼運動嗎？
  6. You're carrying a lot. Do you need help with that box?
    你拿了好多東西。那個箱子你需要幫忙嗎？

② Does he / she / it + 原形動詞?

  1. I need some advice about my mum's medicine. Does your sister still work at the hospital?
    我需要問一下我媽媽吃藥的事。你姊姊還在醫院上班嗎？
  2. Your new flatmate lived in Osaka for a year. Does she speak Japanese?
    你的新室友在大阪住過一年。她會說日文嗎？
  3. Excuse me, driver. Does this bus go to the airport?
    不好意思，司機。這班公車有到機場嗎？
  4. My son wants to pat your dog. Does it bite?
    我兒子想摸你的狗。牠會咬人嗎？
  5. I need some paint for the weekend. Does the shop open on Sundays?
    我週末需要一些油漆。那家店星期天有開嗎？

③ Do they / we + 原形動詞?

  1. You're having fifty people at the house. Do your parents know about the party?
    你要請五十個人來家裡。你爸媽知道派對的事嗎？
  2. We can go after lunch. Do we need tickets for the museum?
    我們午餐後可以去。博物館需要門票嗎？
  3. I want to post this card today. Do they sell stamps at the supermarket?
    我今天想寄這張卡片。超市有賣郵票嗎？
  4. The twins go to different schools now. Do they take the same bus?
    那對雙胞胎現在讀不同的學校。她們搭同一班公車嗎？
  5. They're a bit tight on my toes. Do these shoes come in a bigger size?
    它們在我的腳趾有點緊。這雙鞋有大一號的嗎？

④ Did + 主詞 + 原形動詞?

  1. Your old one was very slow. Did you buy a new laptop for college?
    你的舊筆電很慢。你為了上大學買了新筆電嗎？
  2. You arrived in Vienna this morning. Did you sleep well on the night train?
    你今天早上到維也納。你在夜車上睡得好嗎？
  3. Lisa said she'd tell you the news. Did she call you last night?
    麗莎說她會告訴你那個消息。她昨晚有打給你嗎？
  4. Did your parents enjoy the boat trip around the island?
    你爸媽喜歡環島的乘船之旅嗎？
  5. There was a storm in Kaohsiung. Did it rain in Tainan yesterday?
    高雄有暴風雨。台南昨天有下雨嗎？
  6. We're halfway to the airport. Did you turn off the gas before we left?
    我們已經在去機場的半路上了。我們出門前你有關瓦斯嗎？

⑤ 簡短回答和不同的句型

  1. A: There's a concert in the park. Do you like jazz? B: Yes, I do.
    A：公園有一場音樂會。你喜歡爵士樂嗎？B：喜歡。
  2. A: Ken took his driving test today. Did he pass? B: No, he didn't.
    A：Ken 今天考駕照。他過了嗎？B：沒有。
  3. Your cup is empty. Do you want some more tea?
    你的杯子空了。你要再喝點茶嗎？
  4. I'm driving to your work tomorrow. Does your office have a car park?
    我明天要開車去你上班的地方。你們辦公室有停車場嗎？
  5. Our town was on TV. Did you see the news this morning?
    我們鎮上了電視。你今天早上有看新聞嗎？
  6. I'm making coffee for everyone. Do you take milk in your coffee?
    我在幫大家泡咖啡。你的咖啡要加牛奶嗎？
  7. We're booking a room for our trip to Bath. Does the hotel have a lift?
    我們在訂去巴斯旅行的房間。那家飯店有電梯嗎？
  8. You didn't come at ten. Did you get my text about the meeting?
    你十點沒來。你有收到我傳的開會簡訊嗎？
