I'm not a good swimmer.

我游泳游得不好。

be 是主⁠要動⁠詞⁠時，not 放⁠在 be 後⁠面

我游泳游得不好。

be 依⁠照主⁠詞和時⁠態用 am、is、are、was 或 were

I'm not a good swimmer.

is not

My college is not very big.

我的學校不太大。
are not

We are not ready yet.

我們還沒準備好。
was not

The film was not very good.

電影不太好看。

縮⁠寫可⁠以縮主⁠詞和 be，也⁠可⁠以縮 be 和 not

I'm not a good swimmer.

主詞和 be 縮寫

He's not dangerous.

牠不危險。
be 和 not 縮寫

It isn't far from here.

從這裡過去不遠。

I am not 只能縮成 I'm not。過去式只有 wasn't 和 weren't。

不⁠縮⁠寫⁠的 not 用在正⁠式⁠的文⁠章，也用⁠來加⁠強語⁠氣

I'm not a good swimmer.

我真的不累。

說話者回應別人說他累了。

be 的否⁠定不⁠用 don't

I'm not a good swimmer.

I don't happy today.

I'm not happy today.

否⁠定⁠句⁠裡也⁠要有 be

I'm not a good swimmer.

My sister not at home.

My sister isn't at home.

not 放⁠在 be 後⁠面

I'm not a good swimmer.

She not is my teacher.

She is not my teacher.

am 和 not 沒⁠有縮⁠寫⁠的形⁠式

I'm not a good swimmer.

I amn't ready.

I'm not ready.

例句

① 主詞 + am not

  1. Please don't ask me to sing at the party. I'm not a good singer.
    請不要叫我在派對上唱歌。我唱歌不好聽。
  2. You can stop worrying about me. I'm not angry with you.
    你不用擔心我。我沒有生你的氣。
  3. Can you call back in an hour? I am not at my desk right now.
    你可以一個小時後再打來嗎？我現在不在位子上。
  4. I can walk to the station on my own. I am not a child.
    我可以自己走去車站。我不是小孩子了。

② 主詞 + is not / isn't

  1. Let's eat somewhere else. This place isn't very clean.
    我們去別的地方吃吧。這家店不太乾淨。
  2. My college is not very big, so I know most of the students.
    我的學校不太大，所以大部分的學生我都認識。
  3. Don't take the bus to the museum. It isn't far from here.
    別搭公車去博物館。從這裡過去不遠。
  4. Tom isn't at home. He's still at the office.
    Tom 不在家。他還在公司。
  5. Your answer is not wrong, but you need to explain it.
    你的答案沒有錯，不過你要說明理由。
  6. This soup isn't hot enough. Can you warm it up?
    這碗湯不夠熱。你可以熱一下嗎？

③ 主詞 + is 的縮寫 + not

  1. Don't worry about the dog. He's not dangerous.
    不用怕那隻狗。牠不危險。
  2. Take an umbrella with you. It's not sunny any more.
    帶把傘吧。天已經不晴了。
  3. Ask Anna to help you. She's not busy this afternoon.
    找 Anna 幫你吧。她今天下午不忙。

④ 主詞 + are not / aren't

  1. You aren't late. The meeting starts at ten.
    你沒遲到。會議十點才開始。
  2. These shoes are not comfortable. I want to change them.
    這雙鞋不好穿。我想換掉。
  3. My parents aren't at home this week. They're on holiday in Spain.
    我爸媽這個星期不在家。他們在西班牙度假。
  4. Let's go shopping on Saturday. The shops in my town aren't open on Sundays.
    我們星期六去買東西吧。我們鎮上的商店星期天不開。
  5. We are not ready yet. Can you give us five more minutes?
    我們還沒準備好。可以再給我們五分鐘嗎？

⑤ 主詞 + are 的縮寫 + not

  1. Sit down and rest. You're not well.
    坐下來休息。你身體不舒服。
  2. Thanks, but we're not hungry. We ate on the train.
    謝謝，不過我們不餓。我們在火車上吃過了。
  3. Don't buy those apples. They're not fresh.
    別買那些蘋果。它們不新鮮。

⑥ 主詞 + was not / wasn't

  1. I went to bed early because I wasn't well.
    我身體不舒服，所以很早就去睡了。
  2. The film was not very good, so we left before the end.
    電影不太好看，所以我們沒看完就走了。
  3. Sorry I missed your call. My phone wasn't in my bag.
    抱歉沒接到你的電話。我的手機不在包包裡。
  4. The test wasn't difficult. Everyone finished early.
    考試不難。每個人都提早寫完了。

⑦ 主詞 + were not / weren't

  1. We called the hotel, but the rooms weren't free.
    我們打電話給飯店，可是房間都沒空。
  2. The beds in that hostel were not clean, so we slept in our sleeping bags.
    那間青年旅館的床不乾淨，所以我們睡在睡袋裡。
  3. You weren't at the party last night. Where were you?
    你昨晚沒來派對。你去哪裡了？
  4. The tickets weren't expensive, so we bought four.
    票不貴，所以我們買了四張。

⑧ 問答裡的簡答

  1. "Are you cold?" "No, I'm not. I'm fine."
    你冷嗎？不冷。我很好。
  2. "Is the bank open today?" "No, it isn't. It's a holiday."
    銀行今天有開嗎？沒有。今天放假。
  3. "Were they at school yesterday?" "No, they weren't. They were ill."
    他們昨天有去學校嗎？沒有。他們生病了。
