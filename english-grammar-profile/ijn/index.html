that 常⁠用⁠的位⁠置

The meeting ends at three. Call me after that.

The flat is near the station, and I like that.

例句

① that 當受詞

  1. My new bike is really light, and I like that.
    我的新腳踏車很輕，我很喜歡這一點。
  2. The teacher always gives us lots of homework. I hate that!
    老師總是給我們很多功課，我很討厭這樣！
  3. You know that blue is my favourite colour, so we are going to use that.
    你知道藍色是我最喜歡的顏色，所以我們就用藍色。
  4. The café has free Wi-Fi, and students love that.
    這家咖啡店有免費 Wi-Fi，學生很喜歡這一點。
  5. He forgot my birthday. I can't believe that!
    他忘了我的生日，我真不敢相信！

② that's why

  1. I didn't sleep well last night. That's why I'm so tired.
    我昨晚沒睡好，所以我才這麼累。
  2. She loves animals. That's why she wants to be a vet.
    她很喜歡動物，所以她想當獸醫。
  3. The tickets were cheap. That's why we went by bus.
    車票很便宜，所以我們才搭巴士去。
  4. My phone was off. That's why I didn't answer.
    我的手機關機了，所以我才沒接。
  5. It was raining. That's why we stayed at home.
    那時在下雨，所以我們才待在家裡。

③ before that 和 after that

  1. Please try to come after 4.30 because I won't be there before that.
    請盡量四點半以後來，因為在那之前我不在。
  2. We had lunch in the park. After that, we went to the museum.
    我們在公園吃午餐，之後去了博物館。
  3. I'm moving to London in May. Before that, I need to find a flat.
    我五月要搬到倫敦，在那之前我得找到公寓。
  4. The shop closes at six, so try to get there before that.
    那家店六點關門，所以盡量在那之前到。
  5. I'll be at work until five. After that, I'm free.
    我會工作到五點，之後就有空了。

④ that 當主詞

  1. "I've passed all my exams." "That was quick!"
    我所有考試都過了。這麼快！
  2. We spent the whole day at the beach. That was so much fun.
    我們在海邊玩了一整天，真的很好玩。
  3. I left my keys in the car. That was a stupid mistake.
    我把鑰匙留在車上，真是個愚蠢的錯誤。
  4. She gave me her old laptop. That was really kind of her.
    她把她的舊筆電給了我，她真好。
  5. "The bus leaves at 7.15." "Is that right? I thought it was 7.30."
    公車七點十五分開。是嗎？我以為是七點半。

⑤ 指別人說的話

  1. "The museum is closed on Mondays." "Oh, I didn't know that."
    博物館星期一休館。喔，我不知道。
  2. "You should take a coat." "Yes, I'll do that."
    你應該帶件外套。好，我會帶。
  3. "Can we meet at the station?" "Yes, let's do that."
    我們可以在車站碰面嗎？好，就這樣吧。
  4. "Tom is leaving the company." "Who told you that?"
    湯姆要離開公司了。誰跟你說的？
  5. "I think Anna is angry with me." "Why do you think that?"
    我覺得安娜在生我的氣。你為什麼這樣想？

⑥ 寫信給朋友

  1. My new flat has a garden, and I really love that.
    我的新公寓有花園，我真的很喜歡這一點。
  2. These boots look really nice on me. That's why I bought them.
    這雙靴子我穿起來很好看，所以我才買。
  3. Our English teacher is very funny. We all like that about her.
    我們的英文老師很有趣，我們都喜歡她這一點。
  4. I'm free on Saturday afternoon. Is that OK for you?
    我星期六下午有空，你可以嗎？
  5. My parents are coming at the weekend. I'm really looking forward to that.
    我爸媽週末要來，我很期待。
  6. You said you wanted to visit Scotland. Let's plan that for next summer!
    你說你想去蘇格蘭，我們來計畫明年夏天去吧！
