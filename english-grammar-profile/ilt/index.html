Of course, if you are not at all interested in sports, neither of these games will be of any interest to you.

當然，如果你對運動完全沒興趣，這兩場比賽你都不會感興趣。

例句

① neither of + 限定詞 + 複數名詞

  1. There's a football match and a tennis match on Saturday. Of course, if you are not at all interested in sports, neither of these games will be of any interest to you.
    星期六有一場足球賽和一場網球賽。當然，如果你對運動完全沒興趣，這兩場比賽你都不會感興趣。
  2. We visited two schools for our son, but neither of the schools had a swimming pool.
    我們幫兒子看了兩間學校，可是兩間都沒有游泳池。
  3. I'm the first in my family to study at university. Neither of my parents went.
    我是家裡第一個上大學的。我爸媽兩個都沒上過。
  4. We looked at two flats, but neither of the flats had a garden.
    我們看了兩間公寓，可是兩間都沒有花園。
  5. Neither of the answers you gave is correct. Try again.
    你給的兩個答案都不對。再試一次。

② either of + 限定詞 + 複數名詞

  1. You can take either of the two roads. They both go to the village.
    兩條路你走哪一條都可以。它們都通到村子。
  2. Either of the measures is recommended, and both will help the company.
    兩個措施採用哪一個都好，兩個都會對公司有幫助。
  3. I don't mind which film we see. Either of those films is fine with me.
    我不在乎看哪一部電影。那兩部我都可以。
  4. Have either of your brothers been to Japan?
    你兩個哥哥有誰去過日本嗎？
  5. If either of the children feels ill, call me straight away.
    兩個孩子如果有誰不舒服，馬上打給我。

③ neither of us / you / them

  1. You forgot to say that neither of us could predict the rise in the price of wheat.
    你忘了說，我們雙方都沒辦法預料小麥價格會上漲。
  2. Neither of us wanted to cook, so we ordered a pizza.
    我們兩個都不想煮飯，所以叫了披薩。
  3. I've met your two cousins, but I don't remember either of them.
    我見過你兩個表哥，可是兩個我都不記得了。
  4. "Which of you broke the window?" "Neither of us!"
    你們兩個誰打破窗戶的？我們兩個都沒有！
  5. Neither of them said a word during the whole trip.
    整趟旅行他們兩個都沒說一句話。

④ either of us / you / them

  1. Don't book a taxi. Either of us can drive you to the airport tomorrow.
    別叫計程車。我們兩個誰都可以明天載你去機場。
  2. Is either of you free on Friday evening?
    你們兩個有誰星期五晚上有空嗎？
  3. I've got two spare tickets. Would either of you like them?
    我多了兩張票。你們兩個有誰想要嗎？
  4. If either of them calls, tell them I'm in a meeting.
    他們兩個如果有誰打來，跟他們說我在開會。

⑤ not ... either of

  1. Sorry, Tom. I don't agree with either of your ideas.
    Tom，抱歉。你的兩個點子我都不同意。
  2. She didn't answer either of my emails.
    我寄的兩封電子郵件她都沒回。
  3. We couldn't find either of the keys, so we called a locksmith.
    兩把鑰匙我們都找不到，所以叫了鎖匠。
  4. Everyone's talking about that writer, but I haven't read either of his books.
    大家都在談那個作家，可是他的兩本書我都還沒讀過。

⑥ 比較和選擇

  1. Both hotels are expensive, and neither of them is near the beach.
    兩間旅館都很貴，而且兩間都不在海灘附近。
  2. The two phones look the same. Neither of the shops could tell me the difference.
    兩支手機看起來一樣。兩家店都說不出差別在哪。
  3. We need a bigger car. Neither of our cars has room for five people.
    我們需要一台大一點的車。我們的兩台車都坐不下五個人。
  4. Look at the board, everyone. Choose either of the two topics for your essay.
    大家看黑板。你們的作文從兩個題目裡任選一個。

⑦ 正式的信和報告

  1. The meeting ended without a decision. Neither of the proposals was accepted by the committee.
    會議沒有做出決定就結束了。委員會兩個提案都沒有接受。
  2. Please let us know if either of these dates is suitable.
    如果這兩個日期有一個合適，請告訴我們。
  3. Neither of the candidates has enough experience for the job.
    兩位應徵者的經驗都不足以勝任這份工作。
  4. We regret that neither of the rooms you asked for is available.
    很抱歉，您要的兩間房間都沒有空。
