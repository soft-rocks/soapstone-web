You don't know the place.

你不熟那個地方。

don't 放⁠在 know 前⁠面，know 用原⁠形

主⁠詞是單⁠數用 doesn't

我不吃肉。

My brother doesn't like coffee.

我哥哥不喜歡咖啡。

例句

① I / you / we / they + don't

  1. Let me pick you up. You don't know the place.
    我去接你吧。你不熟那個地方。
  2. No sausages for me, thanks. I don't like meat and fish.
    香腸我不用了，謝謝。我不喜歡肉和魚。
  3. We don't have a car, so we take the bus everywhere.
    我們沒有車，所以去哪都搭公車。
  4. Sorry, I don't know the answer. Ask the teacher.
    抱歉，我不知道答案。問老師吧。
  5. They don't live here any more. They moved to Leeds.
    他們不住這裡了。他們搬去里茲了。
  6. You go to the party. I don't want to dance with strangers.
    你去派對吧。我不想跟陌生人跳舞。
  7. Leave your passport at home. You don't need it for this trip.
    護照放家裡吧。這趟旅行用不到。
  8. Send Grandma a letter. My grandparents don't use the internet.
    寄封信給奶奶吧。我爺爺奶奶不用網路。
  9. Cats don't like water, so bathing ours is hard.
    貓不喜歡水，所以幫我們家的貓洗澡很難。

② he / she / it + doesn't

  1. My brother doesn't eat breakfast. He's never hungry in the morning.
    我哥哥不吃早餐。他早上從來不餓。
  2. She doesn't speak French, so I translate for her.
    她不會說法文，所以我幫她翻譯。
  3. Use the stairs. The lift doesn't work.
    走樓梯吧。電梯壞了。
  4. Tom doesn't like football, but he loves tennis.
    Tom 不喜歡足球，可是他很愛網球。
  5. This shop doesn't sell stamps. Try the post office.
    這家店不賣郵票。去郵局看看。
  6. My mum doesn't drive, so my dad takes her to work.
    我媽不開車，所以我爸載她去上班。
  7. Bring sun cream, not an umbrella. It doesn't rain much here in summer.
    帶防曬乳，不用帶傘。這裡夏天不太下雨。
  8. He always plays alone. He doesn't have any brothers or sisters.
    他總是一個人玩。他沒有兄弟姊妹。

③ I'm not / isn't / aren't

  1. No more cake for me, thanks. I'm not hungry.
    我不用再吃蛋糕了，謝謝。我不餓。
  2. Come back tomorrow. The doctor isn't here today.
    明天再來吧。醫生今天不在。
  3. Can we sit down for a bit? These shoes aren't very comfortable.
    我們可以坐一下嗎？這雙鞋不太好穿。
  4. Everyone thinks so, but I'm not Scottish. My parents are from Wales.
    大家都這麼以為，可是我不是蘇格蘭人。我爸媽是威爾斯人。
  5. The museum isn't far. You can walk there.
    博物館不遠。你可以走過去。
  6. My parents aren't at home. Can I take a message?
    我爸媽不在家。要我幫你留話嗎？
  7. It isn't my bag. Mine is blue.
    這不是我的包包。我的是藍色的。

④ do not / does not（不縮寫）

  1. Keep your money. Children under five do not pay on the bus.
    錢收起來吧。五歲以下的小孩搭公車不用付錢。
  2. Bring some sandwiches. The ticket does not include lunch.
    帶點三明治吧。票價不含午餐。
  3. It's very relaxed here. Students do not wear uniforms at this school.
    這裡很自由。這所學校的學生不穿制服。
  4. We can't take the dog. The hotel does not allow pets.
    我們不能帶狗去。這家飯店不准帶寵物。
  5. Can you help me, Miss? I do not understand this question at all.
    老師，你可以幫我嗎？這一題我完全看不懂。
  6. Our flat does not have a garden, but it has a big balcony.
    我們的公寓沒有花園，可是有一個大陽台。
