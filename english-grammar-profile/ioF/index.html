All of us were dancing while my father sang my favorite song.

我爸爸在唱我最喜歡的歌，我們大家都在跳舞。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句 + 從⁠屬連⁠接⁠詞 + 子⁠句

All of us were dancing while my father sang my favorite song.

我爸爸在唱我最喜歡的歌，我們大家都在跳舞。

子⁠句放⁠在句⁠首加逗⁠號

If I need a camera, I will use my mobile phone.

如果我需要相機，我會用我的手機。

if、when、while、so 的意⁠思

All of us were dancing while my father sang my favorite song.

if如果，說出條件
when當……的時候，說出時間
while在……的同時，兩件事同時進行
so所以，說出結果

if、when 子⁠句說未⁠來用現⁠在⁠式

if

If you come to my house, I will show you my new bike.

如果你來我家，我會給你看我的新腳踏車。
when

When I finish my homework, I will call you.

我寫完功課就打給你。

if 子⁠句不⁠用 will

If it will rain tomorrow, we will stay at home.

If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.

從⁠屬子⁠句不⁠單⁠獨成⁠句

I was very happy. When I saw my grandmother.

I was very happy when I saw my grandmother.

so 放⁠在結⁠果前⁠面

So I was hungry, I made a sandwich.

I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.

while 子⁠句要⁠有主⁠詞

All of us were dancing while my father sang my favorite song.

My mum cooked dinner while was watching TV.

My mum cooked dinner while my dad was watching TV.

例句

① if：說出條件

  1. If you don't take your car, you can rent a bike in the park.
    如果你不開車去，可以在公園裡租腳踏車。
  2. If I have time this weekend, I'll visit my grandparents.
    如果這個週末有空，我會去看我爺爺奶奶。
  3. If you are cold, you can wear my jacket.
    如果你覺得冷，可以穿我的外套。
  4. If the shop is closed, we can go to the supermarket.
    如果那家店沒開，我們可以去超市。
  5. We'll go to the beach if the weather is nice.
    如果天氣好，我們就去海邊。

② when：說出時間

  1. When I was a child, I lived in a small village.
    我小時候住在一個小村子裡。
  2. My little brother laughed when he saw the clown.
    我弟弟看到小丑就笑了。
  3. When you arrive at the station, call me.
    你到車站的時候打電話給我。
  4. When my brother gets home, he always plays computer games.
    我弟弟一回到家就打電動。
  5. We had lunch in the garden when the sun came out.
    太陽出來的時候，我們在花園裡吃午餐。

③ while：說出同時進行的事

  1. I read a book while I was waiting for the bus.
    我等公車的時候在看書。
  2. My dad washed the car while my mum was shopping.
    我媽媽去買東西的時候，我爸爸在洗車。
  3. Someone stole my bag while I was sleeping on the train.
    我在火車上睡覺的時候，有人偷了我的包包。
  4. The children played in the garden while we cooked dinner.
    我們煮晚餐的時候，孩子們在花園裡玩。
  5. I always sing while I take a shower.
    我洗澡的時候總是會唱歌。

④ so：說出結果

  1. I didn't like green, so I chose blue.
    我不喜歡綠色，所以我選了藍色。
  2. It was raining, so we took a taxi.
    那時在下雨，所以我們搭了計程車。
  3. The tickets were expensive, so we watched the match at home.
    票很貴，所以我們在家看比賽。
  4. The bakery was closed, so I bought bread at the supermarket.
    麵包店關了，所以我在超市買了麵包。
  5. The museum was free, so we went in.
    博物館不用錢，所以我們就進去了。

⑤ 子句放在句首

  1. If you need help, ask your teacher.
    如果你需要幫忙，就問老師。
  2. When the teacher came in, everyone stopped talking.
    老師走進來的時候，大家都不說話了。
  3. While I was cooking, the phone rang.
    我在煮飯的時候，電話響了。
  4. If you buy two coffees, you get a free cake.
    如果你買兩杯咖啡，就送一塊蛋糕。

⑥ 子句放在句尾

  1. I'll lend you my umbrella if you need it.
    如果你需要，我可以借你雨傘。
  2. My grandmother smiled when she opened the present.
    我奶奶打開禮物的時候笑了。
  3. We sang songs while we were walking home.
    我們走路回家的時候一路唱歌。
  4. I left early, so I didn't see the end of the party.
    我很早就走了，所以沒看到派對的結尾。

⑦ 信件和日記裡的句子

  1. I was so happy when I saw my exam results.
    我看到考試成績的時候好開心。
  2. If you come to my city, I will show you the old town.
    如果你來我住的城市，我會帶你去看舊城區。
  3. My friends danced while the band played our favorite song.
    樂團演奏我們最喜歡的歌時，我的朋友們都在跳舞。
  4. The hotel was full, so we stayed with my cousin.
    飯店客滿了，所以我們住在我表哥家。
