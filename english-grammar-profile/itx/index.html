I couldn't close the window.

我沒辦法把窗戶關上。

否⁠定⁠句在 could 後⁠面加 not，再⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I couldn't close the window.

我沒辦法把窗戶關上。

說話者昨晚試過，可是做不到。

正⁠式文⁠章寫 could not，說⁠話寫 couldn't

I couldn't close the window.

couldn't

The car park was full. We couldn't find a parking space.

停車場滿了。我們找不到停車位。
could not

Mark was ill, so he could not go to the game.

馬克生病了，所以沒辦法去看比賽。

所⁠有主⁠詞都用同⁠一⁠個形⁠式

I couldn't close the window.

I / you / we / they

The car park was full. We couldn't find a parking space.

停車場滿了。我們找不到停車位。
he / she / it

My grandma is quite short. She couldn't reach the top shelf.

我奶奶個子很矮。她搆不到最上層的架子。

not 放⁠在 could 後⁠面

I couldn't close the window.

I not could find my passport at the airport.

I couldn't find my passport at the airport.

could 的否⁠定不⁠用 didn't

I couldn't close the window.

She didn't could come to the meeting on Friday.

She couldn't come to the meeting on Friday.

couldn't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I couldn't close the window.

They couldn't saw the screen from the back row.

They couldn't see the screen from the back row.

撇號放⁠在 n 和 t 中⁠間

I couldn't close the window.

I could'nt hear you over the traffic.

I couldn't hear you over the traffic.

撇號代替 not 的 o。

can't 說現⁠在，couldn't 說過⁠去

I couldn't close the window.

鎖壞了。我關不上後門。

Too bad you couldn't，說對⁠方沒⁠能⁠做，覺⁠得可⁠惜

I couldn't close the window.

我們在海邊玩得很開心。可惜你沒能來。

couldn't 也⁠能說現⁠在不⁠可⁠能

I couldn't close the window.

帳單上寫我們點了十個披薩。那不可能是對的。

例句

① I / you / we / they + couldn't + 原形動詞

  1. It rained all the way home. I couldn't find my umbrella this morning.
    回家一路都在下雨。我今天早上找不到我的傘。
  2. We sat at the back of the hall, so we couldn't hear the music.
    我們坐在大廳的最後面，所以聽不到音樂。
  3. The workers lost the key, so they couldn't get into the building.
    工人弄丟了鑰匙，所以進不了那棟大樓。
  4. There were thick clouds last night. You couldn't see the moon at all.
    昨晚雲很厚。你根本看不到月亮。
  5. I bought too much at the market. I couldn't carry all the shopping home.
    我在市場買太多東西了。我沒辦法把買的東西全部提回家。
  6. We drove around for twenty minutes. We couldn't find a parking space near the cinema.
    我們開車繞了二十分鐘。我們在電影院附近找不到停車位。
  7. The exam was very hard for my classmates. They couldn't answer the last question.
    這次考試對我的同學來說很難。他們答不出最後一題。
  8. Sorry I'm late, boss. I couldn't wake up this morning.
    老闆，抱歉我遲到了。我今天早上起不來。

② he / she / it + couldn't + 原形動詞

  1. My brother was scared of bikes. He couldn't ride one until he was eight.
    我弟弟以前很怕腳踏車。他到八歲才會騎。
  2. The train was very crowded. She couldn't find a seat.
    火車非常擠。她找不到座位。
  3. We found a bird in the garden. It couldn't fly because its wing was hurt.
    我們在花園裡發現一隻鳥。牠飛不起來，因為翅膀受傷了。
  4. I told my mum I got full marks. She couldn't believe it.
    我跟媽媽說我考了滿分。她不敢相信。
  5. I called the clinic on Wednesday. The doctor couldn't see me until Monday.
    我星期三打電話去診所。醫生要到星期一才能幫我看診。
  6. There was ice on the road. Our old car couldn't go up the hill.
    路上結冰了。我們那輛舊車開不上山坡。
  7. My dad told a really funny story at dinner. My little brother couldn't stop laughing.
    我爸爸晚餐時講了一個很好笑的故事。我弟弟笑個不停。

③ 主詞 + could not + 原形動詞

  1. Mark was ill, so he could not go to the game.
    馬克生病了，所以沒辦法去看比賽。
  2. The weather turned bad, and the climbers could not reach the top of the mountain.
    天氣變壞了，登山客沒辦法爬到山頂。
  3. The school had strict rules. The students could not use their phones during the test.
    學校的規定很嚴。學生考試時不能用手機。
  4. When I opened the letter from the university, I could not believe my eyes.
    我打開大學寄來的信時，我不敢相信自己的眼睛。
  5. The final was cancelled. The team could not play because of the storm.
    決賽取消了。因為暴風雨，那支隊伍沒辦法比賽。

④ Too bad / Sorry + couldn't

  1. The party was great last night. Too bad you couldn't come.
    昨晚的派對很棒。可惜你沒能來。
  2. My phone died yesterday. Sorry I couldn't call you.
    我的手機昨天沒電了。抱歉我沒辦法打給你。
  3. The hotel was full for the weekend. Too bad we couldn't stay longer.
    那間飯店週末都客滿了。可惜我們沒辦法待久一點。
  4. We don't sell that part in this shop. Sorry we couldn't help you.
    我們店裡沒有賣那個零件。抱歉我們沒能幫上你的忙。
  5. Anna had to work late. It's a pity she couldn't join us for dinner.
    安娜得加班。可惜她沒能跟我們一起吃晚餐。

⑤ couldn't + 原形動詞，加上原因

  1. I couldn't close the door because the wind was too strong.
    風太大了，我關不上門。
  2. We went to the beach, but we couldn't swim because the sea was too rough.
    我們去了海邊，可是海浪太大，我們沒辦法游泳。
  3. The concert was sold out. She couldn't buy a ticket, so she stayed at home.
    演唱會的票賣光了。她買不到票，所以待在家裡。
  4. I asked my son a question, but he couldn't talk because his mouth was full.
    我問我兒子一個問題，可是他嘴裡塞滿了東西，沒辦法說話。
  5. Sorry, I couldn't hear you just now. The line was bad.
    抱歉，我剛剛聽不到你說話。收訊不好。
