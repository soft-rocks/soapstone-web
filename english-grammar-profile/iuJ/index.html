Were this suggestion to be put into practice, our town would change completely.

如果這個建議真的付諸實行，我們的城鎮會徹底改變。

were 放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，取⁠代 if

Were this suggestion to be put into practice, our town would change completely.

如果我真的拿到這份獎學金，我會去日本讀書。

這個句型說未來一件假設的事，用在正式的書面文字和演講。

各⁠種主⁠詞都用 were

Were this suggestion to be put into practice, our town would change completely.

she

Were she to resign, the board would need a new chair.

如果她辭職，董事會就得找新的主席。
I

Were I to lose my job, I would move back to my parents' house.

如果我丟了工作，我會搬回爸媽家。

被⁠動和否⁠定

to be + 過去分詞
被動

Were the law to be changed, thousands of families would benefit.

如果法律修改，成千上萬的家庭會受惠。
not to + 原形動詞
否定

Were you not to sign the contract, the deal would collapse.

如果你不簽合約，這筆交易會告吹。

not 放在主詞後面，to 前面。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以用 could 或 might

could
可以做到的事

Were we to leave now, we could still catch the last train.

如果我們現在走，還趕得上最後一班火車。
might
可能發生的事

Were you to speak to him directly, he might listen.

如果你直接跟他談，他可能會聽。

原句：to be put into practice 是被⁠動

Were this suggestion to be put into practice, our town would change completely.

如果這個建議真的付諸實行，我們的城鎮會徹底改變。

建議是被執行的東西。would change 說實行後的結果。

這⁠個倒⁠裝只⁠用 were

Were this suggestion to be put into practice, our town would change completely.

Was the manager to hear about this, he would cancel the trip.

Were the manager to hear about this, he would cancel the trip.

倒⁠裝已⁠經取⁠代 if

Were this suggestion to be put into practice, our town would change completely.

If were the shop to close, the street would be very quiet.

Were the shop to close, the street would be very quiet.

保留 if 時，用 If the factory were to close。

倒⁠裝⁠的句⁠型不⁠縮⁠寫

Weren't the bank to lend us the money, we would have to sell the house.

Were the bank not to lend us the money, we would have to sell the house.

If + 主⁠詞 + were to 意⁠思一⁠樣，也用在口⁠語

If you were to

If you were to ask her, she would say yes.

如果你去問她，她會答應。
If the price were to

If the price were to rise, we would buy less.

如果價格上漲，我們會少買一點。

Should 的倒⁠裝說可⁠能發⁠生⁠的事

祈使句

Should you need any help, please call me.

如果你需要幫忙，請打電話給我。
will

Should the shipment arrive late, we will contact you.

如果貨晚到，我們會跟您聯絡。

Were 的倒裝說假設的事，主要子句用 would、could 或 might。

例句

① 主詞是 I 或 we

  1. Were I to lose my job, I would move back to my parents' house.
    如果我丟了工作，我會搬回爸媽家。
  2. Were we to cancel the trip now, we would lose our deposit.
    如果我們現在取消旅行，訂金就拿不回來了。
  3. Were I to choose again, I would study medicine.
    如果讓我再選一次，我會讀醫學。
  4. Were we to accept their offer, we would have to move to Berlin.
    如果我們接受他們的條件，就得搬到柏林。
  5. Were I to extol every feature of this hotel, I would need several pages.
    如果我要把這家飯店的每個優點都誇一遍，會需要好幾頁。

② 主詞是 you

  1. Were you to visit Kyoto in spring, you would see the cherry blossoms.
    如果你春天去京都，就會看到櫻花。
  2. Were you to ask my opinion, I would advise against it.
    如果你問我的意見，我會勸你不要。
  3. Were you to read the report carefully, you would find several mistakes.
    如果你仔細讀這份報告，會發現好幾個錯誤。
  4. Were you to accept this position, you would report directly to the director.
    如果您接受這個職位，您會直接向主任報告。
  5. Were you to compare the two plans, you would see that ours is cheaper.
    如果您比較這兩個方案，會發現我們的比較便宜。

③ 主詞是第三人稱

  1. Were the government to raise taxes, many small businesses would close.
    如果政府加稅，很多小公司會倒閉。
  2. Were the president to resign, the vice president would take over.
    如果總統辭職，副總統會接任。
  3. Were someone to find the wallet, they would probably take it to the police.
    如果有人撿到錢包，那個人大概會交給警察。
  4. Were the river to flood, the whole village would be under water.
    如果河水氾濫，整個村子都會泡在水裡。
  5. Were a cataclysm of that size to happen today, few cities would survive.
    如果今天發生那麼大的災難，很少城市能撐過去。
  6. Were the company to precipitate a price war, its rivals would suffer most.
    如果這家公司掀起價格戰，受害最深的會是它的對手。

④ 被動

  1. Were the old factory to be demolished, the town would gain a large park.
    如果舊工廠拆掉，鎮上就會多一座大公園。（被動）
  2. Were the results to be published, the public would demand answers.
    如果結果公布出來，大眾會要求解釋。（被動）
  3. Were his book to be abridged, more students would read it.
    如果他的書出刪節版，會有更多學生讀。（被動）
  4. Were the museum to be closed, the city would lose its main attraction.
    如果博物館關閉，這座城市就少了最主要的景點。（被動）
  5. Were this rule to be applied strictly, half the staff would be breaking it.
    如果嚴格執行這條規定，一半的員工都會違規。（被動）

⑤ 否定

  1. Were they not to agree, we would look for another partner.
    如果他們不同意，我們會另外找合作對象。
  2. Were the rain not to stop, the organizers would postpone the match.
    如果雨不停，主辦單位會把比賽延期。
  3. Were I not to tell you the truth, you would find out anyway.
    如果我不跟你說實話，你也會自己發現。
  4. Were the doctors not to operate, the patient would die within weeks.
    如果醫生不開刀，病人幾週內就會死。

⑥ could 和 might 說可能的結果

  1. Were we to hire two more nurses, the clinic could open on Sundays.
    如果我們再請兩個護理師，診所星期天就能開。
  2. Were the price of electric cars to fall, more families could afford one.
    如果電動車的價格下跌，更多家庭買得起。
  3. Were you to speak to him directly, he might listen.
    如果你直接跟他談，他可能會聽。
  4. Were the volcano to erupt, the ash might reach the capital.
    如果火山爆發，火山灰可能會飄到首都。

⑦ 論說文

  1. Were schools to ban mobile phones, students might concentrate better.
    如果學校禁止手機，學生可能會比較專心。
  2. Were politicians to show more humility, voters would trust them more.
    如果政治人物謙虛一點，選民會更信任他們。
  3. Were cities to build more cycle lanes, fewer people would drive to work.
    如果城市多蓋一些自行車道，開車上班的人會變少。
