Could I play with it now, please?

請問我現在可以玩這個嗎？

Could I 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，請對⁠方准⁠許自⁠己做一⁠件⁠事

Could I play with it now, please?

請問我現在可以玩這個嗎？

it 指媽媽買的那款遊戲。

please 放⁠在句⁠尾，或放⁠在 I 後⁠面

Could I play with it now, please?

句尾

Could I open the window, please?

請問我可以開窗嗎？
I 後面

Could I please leave a bit early today?

請問我今天可以早一點走嗎？

Could we 請對⁠方准⁠許說⁠話者和其⁠他人

Could I play with it now, please?

請問我們可以坐這裡嗎？

回⁠答用 can，不⁠用 could

Could I play with it now, please?

答應

Yes, of course you can.

當然可以。
拒絕

Sorry, you can't.

抱歉，不行。

Could I 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠加 to

Could I play with it now, please?

Could I to use your pen?

Could I use your pen?

問⁠句把 could 放⁠在 I 前⁠面

Could I play with it now, please?

I could borrow your bike?

Could I borrow your bike?

答⁠應別⁠人⁠的請⁠求，用 can

Could I play with it now, please?

Yes, you could.

Yes, you can.

Yes, you could 聽起來像是在說一個可能。

Can I 和 Could I 都⁠能請⁠求許⁠可，Could I 比⁠較客⁠氣

Could I play with it now, please?

我可以喝杯水嗎？

對長輩、老師或不熟的人，常用 Could I。

Could you 請對⁠方做⁠事，Could I 請對⁠方准⁠許自⁠己做⁠事

Could I play with it now, please?

可以請你把鹽遞給我嗎？

May I 比 Could I 更正⁠式

Could I play with it now, please?

我可以進來嗎？

例句

① Could I + 原形動詞？

  1. Could I use your phone?
    我可以用一下你的手機嗎？
  2. Could I sit next to you?
    我可以坐你旁邊嗎？
  3. Could I ask you a question?
    我可以問你一個問題嗎？
  4. Could I take a photo of your garden?
    我可以拍一張你花園的照片嗎？
  5. Could I try these shoes on?
    我可以試穿這雙鞋嗎？
  6. Could I bring a friend to the party?
    我可以帶一個朋友去派對嗎？
  7. Could I pay by card?
    我可以刷卡嗎？
  8. Could I see the menu?
    我可以看一下菜單嗎？
  9. Could I stay at your place this weekend?
    這個週末我可以住你家嗎？
  10. Could I speak to the manager?
    我可以跟經理說話嗎？

② Could I + 原形動詞 + please？

  1. Could I open my present now, please?
    請問我現在可以拆禮物嗎？
  2. Could I have the bill, please?
    麻煩給我帳單好嗎？
  3. Could I have a bit more time, please?
    請問我可以多一點時間嗎？
  4. Could I borrow your notes, please?
    請問我可以借你的筆記嗎？
  5. Could I leave my bag here, please?
    請問我可以把包包放在這裡嗎？
  6. Could I change my seat, please?
    請問我可以換座位嗎？

③ Could I please + 原形動詞？

  1. Could I please go to the toilet?
    請問我可以去廁所嗎？
  2. Could I please watch TV after dinner?
    請問我吃完晚餐可以看電視嗎？
  3. Could I please have my ball back?
    請問可以把我的球還給我嗎？
  4. Could I please call my mum?
    請問我可以打電話給我媽媽嗎？

④ Could I + 原形動詞，加上理由

  1. There is an important game tomorrow. Could I take the class that I will miss on Saturday?
    明天有一場重要的比賽。我可以星期六補上我會缺的那堂課嗎？
  2. I feel a bit cold. Could I close the door?
    我覺得有點冷。我可以把門關上嗎？
  3. My phone is dead. Could I charge it here?
    我的手機沒電了。我可以在這裡充電嗎？
  4. I missed the first part. Could I watch it again?
    我錯過了前面那段。我可以再看一次嗎？
  5. My hands are dirty. Could I wash them first?
    我的手很髒。我可以先洗手嗎？

⑤ Could we + 原形動詞？

  1. Could we sit at the table by the window?
    我們可以坐靠窗那桌嗎？
  2. Could we park our car here?
    我們可以把車停在這裡嗎？
  3. Could we finish the game first?
    我們可以先把遊戲玩完嗎？
  4. Could we take a short break?
    我們可以休息一下嗎？
  5. Could we come back tomorrow?
    我們可以明天再來嗎？
