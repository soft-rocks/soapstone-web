It is highly unlikely that the goods could vanish from your warehouse without anyone noticing.

貨物極不可能在沒人發現的情況下從你們的倉庫消失。

It + is + 副⁠詞 + 形⁠容⁠詞 + that + 子⁠句

副⁠詞、形⁠容⁠詞

副詞放在形容詞前面，加強語氣。
形容詞說話者的判斷。

absolutely 和極⁠端⁠的形⁠容⁠詞

例句

It is absolutely essential that you arrive on time.

你絕對要準時到。

副⁠詞放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

It is unlikely highly that he will come.

It is highly unlikely that he will come.

修⁠飾形⁠容⁠詞要⁠用副⁠詞 painfully

It is painful obvious that the plan failed.

It is painfully obvious that the plan failed.

加⁠強形⁠容⁠詞用 highly

It is highly unlikely that the goods could vanish from your warehouse without anyone noticing.

It is high unlikely that we will win.

It is highly unlikely that we will win.

例句

① It is + highly + 形容詞

  1. It is highly unlikely that the bank made a mistake.
    銀行極不可能出錯。
  2. It is highly likely that interest rates will rise this year.
    今年利率極有可能上升。
  3. It's highly unusual for him to miss a meeting.
    他缺席會議是非常少見的事。
  4. It is highly unlikely that the shop will give you a refund.
    那家店極不可能退錢給你。
  5. It is highly probable that the minister knew about the problem.
    部長極有可能知道這個問題。
  6. It's highly unlikely that we'll see each other again.
    我們極不可能再見面了。

② It is + painfully / perfectly + 形容詞

  1. It is painfully obvious that the team needs a new coach.
    再明顯不過，這支隊伍需要新教練。
  2. It was painfully clear that she didn't want to be there.
    再清楚不過，她不想待在那裡。
  3. It is perfectly clear that the rules have changed.
    規定已經改了，這點非常清楚。
  4. It's perfectly possible that the letter got lost in the post.
    那封信非常有可能在郵寄途中弄丟了。
  5. It is perfectly normal to feel nervous before an exam.
    考試前覺得緊張是完全正常的。
  6. It was painfully obvious that the actor had forgotten his lines.
    再明顯不過，那個演員忘詞了。
  7. It's perfectly reasonable that you want more time.
    你想要更多時間是完全合理的。

③ It is + absolutely / extremely + 形容詞

  1. It is absolutely essential that you bring your passport.
    你一定要帶護照。
  2. It's absolutely certain that the factory will close.
    工廠一定會關閉，這點絕對確定。
  3. It is extremely important that you keep this information private.
    你要把這個資訊保密，這極為重要。
  4. It is absolutely impossible to finish the job by Friday.
    星期五前完成這項工作是絕對不可能的。
  5. It's extremely difficult to get a visa for that country.
    要拿到那個國家的簽證極為困難。
  6. It is absolutely vital that we protect the forest.
    我們一定要保護森林，這極為重要。
  7. It is extremely unlikely that he will apologise.
    他極不可能道歉。

④ It is + deeply / bitterly / incredibly + 形容詞

  1. It is deeply worrying that so many young people can't find work.
    這麼多年輕人找不到工作，令人非常擔心。
  2. It was bitterly disappointing that we lost in the last minute.
    我們在最後一分鐘輸了，令人非常失望。
  3. It's incredibly lucky that nobody was hurt.
    沒有人受傷，真是非常幸運。（被動）
  4. It is deeply unfair that the workers lost their pensions.
    工人失去了退休金，這非常不公平。
  5. It's incredibly kind of you to drive us home.
    你開車送我們回家，真是太好了。

⑤ 主詞 + is + 副詞 + 形容詞

  1. The traffic in the city centre is painfully slow at rush hour.
    尖峰時間市中心的交通慢得要命。
  2. Her reasons for leaving the club are perfectly understandable.
    她退出社團的理由完全可以理解。
  3. This medicine is highly effective against headaches.
    這種藥對頭痛非常有效。
  4. The new manager is extremely popular with the staff.
    新經理非常受員工歡迎。
  5. Her decision was absolutely right.
    她的決定完全正確。
  6. The results were deeply disappointing for the whole team.
    這個結果讓整個團隊非常失望。
