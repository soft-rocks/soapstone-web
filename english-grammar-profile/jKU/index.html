What did you do yesterday?

疑⁠問⁠詞後⁠面加 did，再⁠接主⁠詞和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

例句

① What + do / does / did

  1. You didn't answer my messages all weekend. What did you do yesterday?
    你整個週末都沒回我訊息。你昨天做了什麼？
  2. I want to paint my room blue. What do you think?
    我想把房間漆成藍色。你覺得呢？
  3. What does your sister do? Is she a nurse?
    你姊姊是做什麼的？她是護理師嗎？
  4. You were at the dentist for two hours. What did she do to your tooth?
    你在牙醫那裡待了兩個小時。她對你的牙齒做了什麼？
  5. What do you usually have for breakfast?
    你早餐通常吃什麼？
  6. We need to leave soon. What time does the match kick off?
    我們得快點出發了。比賽幾點開打？

② Where + do / does / did

  1. You look very brown. Where did you go on holiday?
    你曬得好黑。你去哪裡度假？
  2. Where do you live? Is it near the school?
    你住哪裡？離學校近嗎？
  3. Where does this bus go?
    這班公車開到哪裡？
  4. I love your scarf. Where did you get it?
    我好喜歡你的圍巾。你在哪裡買的？
  5. There's no space outside the office. Where do your colleagues leave their cars?
    辦公室外面沒有空位。你的同事都把車停在哪裡？

③ When + do / does / did

  1. Welcome to England! When did you arrive in London?
    歡迎來英國！你什麼時候到倫敦的？
  2. I need to buy a present tomorrow. When does the bookshop close on Saturdays?
    我明天得買禮物。那家書店星期六幾點關門？
  3. You play the violin so well. When did you first pick it up?
    你小提琴拉得真好。你什麼時候開始拉的？
  4. I can pick you up later. When do you finish work today?
    我晚點可以去接你。你今天幾點下班？

④ How + do / does / did

  1. You never told me about him. How do you know?
    你從來沒跟我說過他的事。你怎麼知道的？
  2. The roads are full of snow. How did you drive here in this weather?
    路上都是雪。這種天氣你是怎麼開車過來的？
  3. I've never used a coffee machine like this. How does it work?
    我從來沒用過這種咖啡機。它怎麼用？
  4. How do you say "thank you" in Japanese?
    謝謝的日文怎麼說？
  5. You two look so happy. How did you and your wife first meet?
    你們兩個看起來好幸福。你跟你太太是怎麼認識的？

⑤ Why + do / does / did

  1. You were gone by nine. Why did you leave the party so early?
    你九點就不見了。你為什麼那麼早離開派對？
  2. Why does the baby cry every night?
    寶寶為什麼每天晚上都哭？
  3. Why do you want to study medicine?
    你為什麼想念醫學？
  4. We're in the middle of nowhere. Why did the train stop?
    我們在荒郊野外。火車為什麼停了？

⑥ Why don't you / Why don't we：建議

  1. You love that jacket. Why don't you buy it?
    你很愛那件外套。你何不買下來？
  2. Why don't you ask your teacher for help?
    你何不請老師幫忙？
  3. Everyone is bored. Why don't we play a board game?
    大家都很無聊。我們何不玩個桌遊？

⑦ Who + do / did（受詞）

  1. You were at the school reunion last night. Who did you chat with?
    你昨晚去了同學會。你跟誰聊了天？
  2. Who do you want to invite to your birthday?
    你生日想邀請誰？
  3. I saw you outside the cinema. Who did you go with?
    我在電影院外面看到你。你跟誰去的？
  4. Who does your little brother look like, your mum or your dad?
    你弟弟長得像誰，你媽媽還是你爸爸？
