It's better to be with people who are the same age as you.

跟和你同年紀的人在一起比較好。

the same + 名⁠詞 + as，名⁠詞說哪⁠一方⁠面一⁠樣

It's better to be with people who are the same age as you.

我姊姊跟我一樣高。

名⁠詞可⁠以不⁠寫，as 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以是所⁠有⁠格代⁠名⁠詞

It's better to be with people who are the same age as you.

Your bag is the same as mine.

你的包包跟我的一樣。

same 前⁠面要加 the

It's better to be with people who are the same age as you.

We're in same class as Ken.

We're in the same class as Ken.

the same 後⁠面⁠接 as，不⁠接 than

It's better to be with people who are the same age as you.

Her dress is the same color than mine.

Her dress is the same color as mine.

the same 後⁠面⁠接 as，不⁠接 with

It's better to be with people who are the same age as you.

This cake tastes the same as the one at the bakery.

例句

① the same age / height / size + as

  1. My son is the same height as his teacher now.
    我兒子現在跟他的老師一樣高。
  2. Ken and my brother were born in the same month. Ken is the same age as my brother.
    Ken 和我哥哥在同一個月出生。Ken 跟我哥哥同年。
  3. I don't need to try them on. These shoes are the same size as my old ones.
    我不用試穿。這雙鞋跟我的舊鞋一樣大。
  4. Our neighbours have three bedrooms too. Their house is the same size as ours.
    我們的鄰居也有三個臥室。他們的房子跟我們的一樣大。
  5. My son is starting school in September. Is your son the same age as mine?
    我兒子九月要上學了。你兒子跟我兒子同年嗎？
  6. I've grown a lot this year. I'm almost the same weight as my father.
    我今年長大很多。我的體重跟我爸爸差不多。

② the same color / class / level + as

  1. Look at the man in the green tie. His tie is the same color as the walls.
    你看那個打綠領帶的男人。他的領帶跟牆壁同一個顏色。
  2. I know Mia from netball. She's in the same team as my daughter.
    我是在籃網球認識 Mia 的。她跟我女兒在同一隊。
  3. We both passed Grade 5 this year. My cousin is at the same level as me in piano.
    我們今年都考過了五級。我表弟的鋼琴程度跟我一樣。
  4. Look, we both drive a blue Honda. My car is the same model as yours.
    你看，我們都開藍色的本田。我的車跟你的同款。
  5. They take the bus together every morning. Ann goes to the same school as my daughter.
    她們每天早上一起搭公車。Ann 跟我女兒讀同一所學校。
  6. My son is called Henry. He has the same name as his grandfather.
    我兒子叫亨利。他跟他爺爺同名。

③ the same things / problem + as

  1. He always wants the latest football game. My little brother likes the same games as his friends.
    他總是要最新的足球遊戲。我弟弟喜歡的遊戲跟他朋友一樣。
  2. Your laptop won't start? I had the same problem as you last year.
    你的筆電開不了機？我去年也遇到跟你一樣的問題。
  3. My sister couldn't decide what to eat. She ordered the same dish as me.
    我姊姊不知道要吃什麼。她點了跟我一樣的菜。
  4. We did the science experiment twice. We got the same results as the other team.
    我們做了兩次科學實驗。我們得到的結果跟另一組一樣。
  5. I see the Browns every time I visit home. They live on the same street as my parents.
    我每次回家都會看到布朗一家。他們跟我爸媽住在同一條街。

④ the same as + 代名詞 / 名詞

  1. Look at Lily and her mother. Her new haircut is the same as her mum's.
    你看莉莉和她媽媽。她的新髮型跟她媽媽的一樣。
  2. I got eighty-one, and you got eighty-two. My exam score was almost the same as yours.
    我考八十一分，你考八十二分。我的考試分數跟你的幾乎一樣。
  3. Why did you pay so much for the new model? This phone looks the same as the old one.
    你為什麼花那麼多錢買新款？這支手機看起來跟舊的一樣。
  4. Lucy says the answer to question five is twelve. Is your answer the same as hers?
    露西說第五題的答案是十二。你的答案跟她的一樣嗎？
  5. It's raining again. The weather today is the same as yesterday.
    又在下雨了。今天的天氣跟昨天一樣。

⑤ not the same / 不同的句型

  1. The new table is round. It isn't the same shape as the old one.
    新桌子是圓的。它的形狀跟舊的不一樣。
  2. The shops in our village close at five. Life here isn't the same as in the city.
    我們村子的店五點就關門了。這裡的生活跟城市不一樣。
  3. The sofa arrived today, but it's not the same color as the photo.
    沙發今天送到了，可是它的顏色跟照片上的不一樣。
  4. You're in Year 4 now. Do you have the same teacher as last year?
    你現在四年級了。你的老師跟去年一樣嗎？
  5. Kids learn faster with classmates at the same level as them.
    孩子跟程度一樣的同學在一起學得比較快。
  6. My son showed the barber a photo. "I want the same haircut as my friend."
    我兒子給理髮師看了一張照片。我想剪跟我朋友一樣的髮型。
  7. They changed the ending. The film wasn't the same as the book.
    他們改了結局。電影跟書不一樣。
  8. We ran to the stop and didn't have to wait. We arrived at the same time as the bus.
    我們跑到公車站，不用等。我們跟公車同時到。
