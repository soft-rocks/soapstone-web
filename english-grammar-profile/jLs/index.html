She is joining the course on Friday.

她週五會加入這個課程。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing + 未⁠來⁠的時⁠間

She is joining the course on Friday.

am / is / are + 動詞⁠-⁠ing已經安排好的事。人、時間、地點常常都定好了。
未來的時間像 on Friday、on 5th January、next term。

常⁠用⁠的動⁠詞

例句

We're throwing a party on 5th January.

我們一月五日要辦派對。

說安排好的活動，常用這些動詞。

join 後⁠面直⁠接接名⁠詞，不⁠加 to

She is joining to the course on Friday.

She is joining the course on Friday.

be 動⁠詞後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

They are hold a meeting next week.

They are holding a meeting next week.

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

She is joining the course on Friday.

The band releasing a new album in May.

The band is releasing a new album in May.

例句

① join / throw / hold / host

  1. We're throwing a surprise party for Dad on his birthday.
    爸爸生日那天，我們要幫他辦驚喜派對。
  2. A new teacher is joining the department next term.
    下學期有一位新老師要加入這個系。
  3. The club is holding its annual meeting on Thursday evening.
    俱樂部星期四晚上要開年度大會。
  4. Our town is hosting the regional games this summer.
    我們鎮今年夏天要主辦地區運動會。
  5. I'm joining a gym near the office in January.
    我一月要加入辦公室附近的健身房。
  6. They're throwing a leaving party for their boss on Friday.
    他們星期五要幫老闆辦歡送派對。
  7. The school is holding a concert to raise money for the hospital.
    學校要辦一場音樂會，為醫院募款。
  8. My parents are hosting a dinner for the whole family on Sunday.
    我爸媽星期天要請全家吃晚餐。

② launch / release / publish

  1. The company is launching its new phone in September.
    那家公司九月要推出新手機。
  2. My favourite singer is releasing a new album next month.
    我最喜歡的歌手下個月要出新專輯。
  3. The magazine is publishing my article in the spring issue.
    那本雜誌春季號要刊登我的文章。
  4. They're launching a campaign to clean up the beach.
    他們要發起一個淨灘活動。
  5. The studio is releasing the film in cinemas on 12th March.
    片商三月十二日要在電影院上映這部電影。

③ sign / interview / hire

  1. The club is signing a new striker next week.
    球隊下星期要簽一位新前鋒。
  2. We're interviewing three candidates for the job tomorrow.
    我們明天要面試三位應徵者。
  3. The restaurant is hiring two new chefs for the summer.
    那家餐廳夏天要僱兩位新廚師。
  4. I'm signing the contract for the flat on Monday.
    我星期一要簽公寓的租約。
  5. The director is interviewing actors for the new play this week.
    導演這週要為新戲面試演員。

④ perform / present / give

  1. Our class is performing a play for the parents next Friday.
    我們班下星期五要演戲給家長看。
  2. I'm presenting my project to the board on Tuesday.
    我星期二要向董事會報告我的專案。
  3. The orchestra is performing in the town hall on Saturday.
    管弦樂團星期六要在市政廳演出。
  4. She's giving a speech at her sister's wedding.
    她要在她姊的婚禮上致詞。
  5. Two students are presenting their research at the conference.
    有兩位學生要在研討會上發表研究。

⑤ 其他動詞

  1. We're redecorating the living room over the holidays.
    我們放假時要重新裝潢客廳。
  2. The council is repairing the bridge in October.
    市議會十月要修那座橋。
  3. I'm volunteering at the animal shelter on Saturday mornings from next week.
    從下星期開始，我星期六早上要去動物收容所當志工。
  4. My brother is competing in a chess tournament next weekend.
    我哥下個週末要參加西洋棋比賽。
  5. The hotel is installing a new swimming pool before the summer.
    飯店夏天前要裝一座新游泳池。
  6. We're collecting the keys to our new house on Wednesday.
    我們星期三要去拿新家的鑰匙。
  7. The museum is lending some paintings to a gallery in Paris.
    博物館要把幾幅畫借給巴黎的一家美術館。
  8. I'm babysitting for my neighbours on Saturday night.
    我星期六晚上要幫鄰居顧小孩。
