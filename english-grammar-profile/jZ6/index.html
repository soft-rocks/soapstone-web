On Sundays my dad will wash the car and then read the paper.

例句

① 主詞 + will often + 原形動詞：人的習慣

  1. Our new neighbour is very kind. She will often knock on the door to see how you are.
    我們的新鄰居很好心。她常常會來敲門，看看你好不好。
  2. My grandfather will often sit in the garden for hours, just watching the birds.
    我爺爺常常會在花園裡坐好幾個小時，只是看鳥。
  3. My little brother is very shy. He will often hide under the table when visitors come.
    我弟弟很害羞。有客人來的時候，他常常會躲到桌子底下。
  4. Everyone loves our English lessons. Our teacher will often tell us a joke at the start of the lesson.
    大家都很喜歡我們的英文課。我們老師常常會在上課一開始講一個笑話。
  5. The old man next door will often talk to his plants. I can hear him through the fence.
    隔壁的老先生常常會跟他的植物說話。我隔著圍籬聽得到他。
  6. Our kitchen is never quiet. My mum will often sing while she's cooking.
    我們的廚房從來都不安靜。我媽媽煮飯的時候常常會唱歌。

② 名詞 + will usually + 原形動詞：東西的典型特徵

  1. A Mexican dish will usually contain a sauce made of hot pepper, tomatoes, garlic and onion.
    墨西哥菜通常會有一種用辣椒、番茄、大蒜和洋蔥做的醬汁。
  2. I'm looking for a cookbook for my mum. A good cookbook will usually have a photo of every dish.
    我在找一本食譜書送我媽媽。好的食譜書通常每道菜都會有一張照片。
  3. Don't worry if your new kitten sleeps all day. A cat will usually sleep for more than twelve hours a day.
    如果你的新小貓整天睡覺，不用擔心。貓通常一天會睡超過十二個小時。
  4. Don't worry if you arrive late at night. A good hotel will usually have someone at the desk all night.
    如果你很晚才到，不用擔心。好的飯店通常整晚都會有人在櫃台。
  5. Keep the bread in the freezer. Fresh bread will usually go hard after two days.
    把麵包放在冷凍庫。新鮮的麵包通常兩天後就會變硬。
  6. The café on the corner serves a full breakfast. A typical English breakfast will include eggs, bacon and beans.
    轉角那家咖啡館有供應全套早餐。典型的英式早餐會有蛋、培根和豆子。

③ 主詞 + won't usually + 原形動詞：通常不會

  1. On a bike you can go to beautiful places that you won't usually be able to reach by car, such as riversides.
    騎腳踏車可以去很多開車通常到不了的美麗地方，像河邊。
  2. I feed my dog meat and rice. My dog won't usually eat vegetables, but he loves carrots.
    我餵我的狗吃肉和飯。我的狗通常不吃蔬菜，可是牠很喜歡紅蘿蔔。
  3. We take the baby for a walk after lunch. The baby won't usually sleep in the afternoon.
    我們午餐後帶寶寶去散步。寶寶下午通常不睡覺。
  4. We love our holiday cottage. Tourists won't usually find this little beach.
    我們很喜歡我們的度假小屋。觀光客通常找不到這個小海灘。
  5. My dad won't usually drink coffee after lunch. He says it keeps him awake.
    我爸爸午餐後通常不喝咖啡。他說咖啡會讓他睡不著。
  6. Don't go to the bakery too early. The shop won't usually open before ten.
    不要太早去麵包店。那家店通常十點前不會開。

④ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + when / if + 子句：典型的反應

  1. We can never sleep late on Saturdays. The dog will always bark when the postman comes.
    我們星期六從來都沒辦法睡晚一點。郵差來的時候，狗總是會叫。
  2. Let's not watch that film with my sister. She will always cry if a film is sad.
    我們別跟我妹妹看那部電影。如果電影很悲傷，她總是會哭。
  3. Our baby cries a lot at night. Babies will usually stop crying if you sing to them.
    我們的寶寶晚上常哭。如果你唱歌給寶寶聽，他們通常會停止哭泣。
  4. Here are some tips for your new garden. Plants will die if you forget to water them.
    以下是給你的新花園的建議。如果你忘了澆水，植物會死。
  5. Our class trip to the zoo was very noisy. Children will always ask questions when they're interested.
    我們班去動物園的校外教學很吵。孩子們有興趣的時候總是會發問。
  6. Put the shopping in the fridge. Milk will go sour if you leave it in the sun.
    把買的東西放進冰箱。如果你把牛奶放在太陽下，它會變酸。

⑤ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + for hours：長時間的習慣

  1. My brother will play computer games for hours if nobody stops him.
    如果沒人阻止，我哥哥會打好幾個小時的電動。
  2. Don't ask Grandma about her school days. My grandmother will talk about her childhood for hours.
    不要問奶奶她上學時候的事。我奶奶會講她小時候的事講好幾個小時。
  3. Our cat loves the window seat. The cat will lie in the sun for hours in the afternoon.
    我們的貓很喜歡窗邊的座位。下午貓會在太陽下躺好幾個小時。
  4. My friend Sam is a quiet student. He will read in the library for hours without looking up.
    我朋友山姆是個安靜的學生。他會在圖書館讀書好幾個小時，頭都不抬。
  5. Don't call my aunt on a Sunday. My aunt will chat on the phone for hours with her friends.
    不要在星期天打給我阿姨。我阿姨會跟她的朋友講電話講好幾個小時。
  6. We always bring buckets and spades. The children will play in the sand for hours at the beach.
    我們總是帶水桶和鏟子。孩子們在海灘上會玩沙玩好幾個小時。
  7. Fishing makes my uncle happy. My uncle will fish by the lake for hours without catching anything.
    釣魚讓我叔叔很開心。我叔叔會在湖邊釣好幾個小時的魚，什麼都沒釣到。
