She will often knock on the door to see how you are.

她常常會來敲門找你。

主⁠詞 + will + (often / usually) + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She will often knock on the door to see how you are.

will常常會、通常會。說習慣或典型的情況。
often / usually放在 will 後面，說多常發生。
原形動詞習慣的行為。

不⁠是說未⁠來

She will often knock on the door to see how you are.

例句

My grandfather will sit in the garden for hours.

我爺爺常常會在花園裡坐好幾個小時。

這個 will 說的是一般的習慣，不是以後的事。

說東⁠西⁠的典⁠型特⁠徵

She will often knock on the door to see how you are.

例句

A good curry will usually contain garlic and onion.

好的咖哩通常會有大蒜和洋蔥。

will 也可以說東西通常的樣子，常用在介紹和說明。

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She will often knock on the door to see how you are.

She will often knocks on the door.

She will often knock on the door.

will 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

She will often knock on the door to see how you are.

He wills often go for walks.

He will often go for walks.

副⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

The dish will usually to contain rice.

The dish will usually contain rice.

例句

① 主詞 + will often + 原形動詞：人的習慣

  1. Our new neighbour is very kind. She will often knock on the door to see how you are.
    我們的新鄰居很好心。她常常會來敲門，看看你好不好。
  2. My grandfather will often sit in the garden for hours, just watching the birds.
    我爺爺常常會在花園裡坐好幾個小時，只是看鳥。
  3. My little brother will often hide under the table when visitors come.
    有客人來的時候，我弟弟常常會躲到桌子底下。
  4. Our teacher will often tell us a joke at the start of the lesson.
    我們老師常常會在上課一開始講一個笑話。
  5. The old man next door will often talk to his plants.
    隔壁的老先生常常會跟他的植物說話。
  6. My mum will often sing while she's cooking.
    我媽媽煮飯的時候常常會唱歌。

② 名詞 + will usually + 原形動詞：東西的典型特徵

  1. A Mexican dish will usually contain a sauce made of hot pepper, tomatoes, garlic and onion.
    墨西哥菜通常會有一種用辣椒、番茄、大蒜和洋蔥做的醬汁。
  2. A good recipe will normally result in a spicy but healthy dish.
    一個好的食譜通常會做出又辣又健康的菜。
  3. A cat will usually sleep for more than twelve hours a day.
    貓通常一天會睡超過十二個小時。
  4. A good hotel will usually have someone at the desk all night.
    好的飯店通常整晚都會有人在櫃台。
  5. Fresh bread will usually go hard after two days.
    新鮮的麵包通常兩天後就會變硬。
  6. A typical English breakfast will include eggs, bacon and beans.
    典型的英式早餐會有蛋、培根和豆子。

③ 主詞 + won't usually + 原形動詞：通常不會

  1. On a bike you can go to beautiful places that you won't usually be able to reach by car, such as riversides.
    騎腳踏車可以去很多開車通常到不了的美麗地方，像河邊。
  2. My dog won't usually eat vegetables, but he loves carrots.
    我的狗通常不吃蔬菜，可是他很喜歡紅蘿蔔。
  3. The baby won't usually sleep in the afternoon.
    寶寶下午通常不睡覺。
  4. Tourists won't usually find this little beach.
    觀光客通常找不到這個小海灘。
  5. My dad won't usually drink coffee after lunch.
    我爸爸午餐後通常不喝咖啡。
  6. The shop won't usually open before ten.
    那家店通常十點前不會開。

④ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + when / if + 子句：典型的反應

  1. The dog will always bark when the postman comes.
    郵差來的時候，狗總是會叫。
  2. My sister will cry if she watches a sad film.
    我妹妹看悲傷的電影會哭。
  3. Babies will usually stop crying if you sing to them.
    如果你唱歌給寶寶聽，他們通常會停止哭泣。
  4. Plants will die if you forget to water them.
    如果你忘了澆水，植物會死。
  5. Children will always ask questions when they're interested.
    孩子們有興趣的時候總是會發問。
  6. Milk will go sour if you leave it in the sun.
    如果你把牛奶放在太陽下，它會變酸。

⑤ 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + for hours：長時間的習慣

  1. My brother will play computer games for hours if nobody stops him.
    如果沒人阻止，我哥哥會打好幾個小時的電動。
  2. My grandmother will talk about her childhood for hours.
    我奶奶會講她小時候的事講好幾個小時。
  3. The cat will lie in the sun for hours in the afternoon.
    下午貓會在太陽下躺好幾個小時。
  4. He will read in the library for hours without looking up.
    他會在圖書館讀書好幾個小時，頭都不抬。
  5. My aunt will chat on the phone for hours with her friends.
    我阿姨會跟她的朋友講電話講好幾個小時。
  6. The children will play in the sand for hours at the beach.
    孩子們在海灘上會玩沙玩好幾個小時。
  7. My uncle will fish by the lake for hours without catching anything.
    我叔叔會在湖邊釣好幾個小時的魚，什麼都沒釣到。
