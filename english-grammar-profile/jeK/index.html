My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

我的房間好像很冷，一點都不溫馨。

seem 後⁠面⁠接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

我的房間好像很冷，一點都不溫馨。

時態標在 seems 上，to 後面用原形動詞。

不⁠定⁠詞可⁠以是否⁠定、進⁠行或完⁠成⁠的形⁠式

My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

to + 原形動詞

He managed to finish the report.

他設法把報告寫完了。
not to + 原形動詞

She decided not to go.

她決定不去。
to be + 動詞⁠-⁠ing

You seem to be working too hard.

你好像工作得太辛苦了。
to have + 過去分詞

He seems to have lost his keys.

他好像把鑰匙弄丟了。

not 否⁠定⁠的⁠是不⁠定⁠詞，不⁠是前⁠面⁠的動⁠詞

My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

她決定不去。

她做了決定，決定的內容是不去。

to have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞說⁠的事比動⁠詞早發⁠生

My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

他好像把鑰匙弄丟了。

afford 後⁠面⁠接不⁠定⁠詞，不⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

We can't afford buying a new car.

We can't afford to buy a new car.

refuse 後⁠面要加 to，再⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

She refused answer my question.

She refused to answer my question.

時⁠態只標在⁠前⁠面⁠的動⁠詞上

My room seems to be very cold and unfriendly.

I managed to passed the exam.

I managed to pass the exam.

enjoy 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，不⁠接不⁠定⁠詞

We finished painting the fence before lunch.

我們午餐前把圍籬漆完了。

表⁠示目⁠的⁠的不⁠定⁠詞不⁠是受⁠詞

I'm going to buy pictures to hang on the wall.

我要買畫掛在牆上。

例句

① seem / appear + 不定詞

  1. "Is Tom OK?" "I don't know. He seems to be upset about something."
    Tom 還好嗎？不知道。他好像在為什麼事難過。
  2. The new manager appears to know a lot about the business.
    新經理看起來很懂這一行。
  3. This printer doesn't seem to work. Can you have a look?
    這台印表機好像壞了。你可以看一下嗎？
  4. Your plan seems to be practicable, but it will cost a lot.
    你的計畫看起來可行，可是要花很多錢。

② decide / choose / agree + 不定詞

  1. It was too cold at the beach, so we decided to go home early.
    海邊太冷了，所以我們決定早點回家。
  2. "Why did you change class?" "I decided to study with a different teacher."
    你為什麼換班？我決定跟另一位老師學。
  3. "Why do you cycle to work?" "I choose to cycle because it keeps me fit."
    你為什麼騎腳踏車上班？我選擇騎車，因為這樣能保持身材。
  4. My boss agreed to give me Friday off.
    我老闆同意讓我星期五休假。

③ manage / fail + 不定詞

  1. The traffic was terrible, but we managed to catch the train.
    交通很糟，可是我們還是趕上了火車。
  2. "Did you find the hotel?" "Yes, we managed to find it in the end."
    你們找到飯店了嗎？找到了，最後找到了。
  3. The team failed to win a single game this season.
    這支球隊這一季一場都沒贏。
  4. The letter failed to arrive in time.
    那封信沒有及時寄到。

④ afford / refuse / promise / offer + 不定詞

  1. We can't afford to go on holiday this year.
    我們今年沒錢去度假。
  2. The little boy refused to eat his vegetables.
    小男孩不肯吃他的蔬菜。
  3. "You're late again!" "Sorry. I promise to be on time tomorrow."
    你又遲到了！對不起。我保證明天準時。
  4. My neighbour offered to water my plants while I was away.
    我不在的時候，鄰居主動說要幫我澆花。
  5. The taxi driver refused to take us to the airport in the snow.
    計程車司機不肯在下雪天載我們去機場。

⑤ pretend / tend / deserve + 不定詞

  1. When his mum came in, he pretended to be asleep.
    他媽媽進來的時候，他假裝睡著了。
  2. In winter, people tend to stay at home more.
    冬天的時候，大家往往比較常待在家裡。
  3. You worked really hard. You deserve to have a rest.
    你真的很努力。你應該好好休息一下。
  4. She pretended not to see me in the street.
    她在街上假裝沒看到我。

⑥ 動詞 + not to + 原形動詞

  1. I've decided not to buy that phone. It's too expensive.
    我決定不買那支手機了。太貴了。
  2. "Don't tell Mum." "OK, I promise not to say anything."
    別告訴媽媽。好，我保證什麼都不說。
  3. Most people choose not to work on Sundays.
    大部分的人選擇星期天不工作。

⑦ 動詞 + to be + 動詞-ing

  1. The baby seems to be sleeping. Let's talk quietly.
    寶寶好像在睡覺。我們小聲講話吧。
  2. The prices appear to be going up every month.
    物價好像每個月都在漲。
  3. "What's Anna doing?" "She seems to be studying, but I think she's on her phone."
    Anna 在做什麼？她好像在讀書，可是我覺得她在玩手機。

⑧ 動詞 + to have + 過去分詞

  1. It's freezing in here. Someone seems to have left the window open.
    這裡好冷。好像有人沒關窗。
  2. "Where's Dad?" "He appears to have gone out. His coat isn't here."
    爸爸呢？他好像出去了。他的外套不在。
  3. The shop seems to have closed down. The windows are empty.
    那家店好像收掉了。櫥窗都空了。
  4. He pretended to have read the book, but he didn't know the ending.
    他假裝讀過那本書，可是他不知道結局。
