Some of the motorists changed to bicycles or to public transport, but far too few did.

有些駕駛人改騎腳踏車或搭大眾運輸，但這樣做的人實在太少了。

very / too / so + few / many

Some of the motorists changed to bicycles or to public transport, but far too few did.

……但這樣做的人實在太少了。

very、too 和 so

Some of the motorists changed to bicycles or to public transport, but far too few did.

very

We invited a lot of people, but very few came.

我們邀了很多人，可是來的人非常少。
too

I tried to count the birds, but there were too many.

我想數那些鳥，可是太多了。
so

So many applied that the office closed the list early.

申請的人這麼多，辦公室提早截止了。

the very few

Some of the motorists changed to bicycles or to public transport, but far too few did.

……只有極少數用得到的人能用。

too 前⁠面用 far

Some of the motorists changed to bicycles or to public transport, but far too few did.

We needed helpers, but very too few came.

We needed helpers, but far too few came.

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞用 little

Some of the motorists changed to bicycles or to public transport, but far too few did.

I wanted some water, but there was too few.

I wanted some water, but there was too little.

動⁠詞用複⁠數

Too many was waiting outside the door.

Too many were waiting outside the door.

too many + 名⁠詞

市中心的車太多了。

a few 和 very few

a few

A few of my friends came to the party.

我有幾個朋友來參加派對。
very few

We invited a lot of people, but very few came.

我們邀了很多人，可是來的人非常少。

例句

① very few

  1. Many people start the course, but very few finish it.
    很多人開始上這門課，可是很少人上完。
  2. We sent out two hundred letters and very few replied.
    我們寄出兩百封信，回信的非常少。
  3. Lots of students applied, but very few got a place.
    很多學生申請，可是錄取的非常少。
  4. He has written many songs, but very few are well known.
    他寫了很多歌，可是有名的非常少。
  5. I asked everyone in the office, and very few knew the answer.
    我問了辦公室裡每個人，知道答案的非常少。

② very many

  1. Not very many came to the meeting last night.
    昨晚來開會的人不太多。
  2. Were there many mistakes? Not very many.
    錯很多嗎？不太多。
  3. She has friends all over the world, though not very many in her own town.
    她在世界各地都有朋友，可是在自己住的鎮上不多。
  4. There used to be lots of bookshops here, but not very many are left.
    這裡以前有很多書店，可是現在剩下的不多。
  5. I've seen some of his films, but not very many.
    我看過他幾部電影，但看得不多。

③ too few / far too few

  1. We need volunteers, but too few have signed up.
    我們需要志工，可是報名的人太少了。
  2. The bus company says too few use the night service.
    公車公司說搭夜間班次的人太少。
  3. Many young people want to become teachers, but far too few stay in the job.
    很多年輕人想當老師，可是留下來的人實在太少了。
  4. The town has some cycle lanes, but far too few.
    這個鎮有一些自行車道，可是實在太少了。
  5. Doctors are working hard, but there are too few to meet the demand.
    醫生都很努力，可是人數太少，應付不了需求。

④ too many / far too many

  1. I bought some apples, but I bought too many.
    我買了一些蘋果，可是買太多了。
  2. Tourists are welcome, but far too many arrive in the summer.
    觀光客很受歡迎，可是夏天來的實在太多了。
  3. We tried to answer all the emails, but there were too many.
    我們想回覆所有的電子郵件，可是太多了。
  4. Too many have lost their jobs this year.
    今年失業的人太多了。
  5. He took a few photos at first, then far too many.
    他一開始只拍幾張照片，後來拍得實在太多了。

⑤ so few / so many

  1. So few came that we cancelled the concert.
    來的人這麼少，我們就取消了音樂會。
  2. There were birds everywhere. I had never seen so many in one place.
    到處都是鳥，我從來沒在一個地方看過這麼多。
  3. Lots of people move here, but why do so few stay longer than a year?
    很多人搬來這裡，可是為什麼待超過一年的人這麼少？
  4. So many wanted tickets that the website crashed.
    要買票的人這麼多，網站當掉了。
  5. There are so many to choose from that I can't decide.
    可以選的這麼多，我決定不了。

⑥ the very few

  1. Only the very few who trained every day finished the race.
    只有極少數每天練習的人跑完了比賽。
  2. Private schools were once open to the very few who could pay.
    私立學校以前只開放給極少數付得起學費的人。
  3. The very few who knew the secret kept it to themselves.
    知道秘密的極少數人都守口如瓶。
  4. Cars were once a luxury for the very few.
    汽車以前是極少數人才有的奢侈品。
  5. The prize goes to the very few who reach the final round.
    獎項只頒給打進決賽的極少數人。
  6. Travel abroad used to be possible only for the very few with money.
    以前只有極少數有錢人能出國旅行。
