When I have free time, I go to the park with my dog.

我有空的時候，會帶我的狗去公園。

介⁠系⁠詞加名⁠詞片⁠語，說⁠明去哪⁠裡和跟誰一⁠起

我有空的時候，會帶我的狗去公園。

名⁠詞片⁠語可⁠以只⁠有名⁠詞，也⁠可⁠以加限⁠定詞和形⁠容⁠詞

When I have free time, I go to the park with my dog.

只有名詞at home, by bus, in July
限定詞 + 名詞to the park, with my dog
+ 形容詞in a small town, with my best friend

好⁠幾⁠個介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語，常⁠見⁠的順⁠序是先地⁠點後時⁠間

When I have free time, I go to the park with my dog.

我星期六會跟我哥哥去健身房。

可⁠數⁠名⁠詞單⁠數前⁠面要⁠有限⁠定詞

When I have free time, I go to the park with my dog.

There's a big dog in garden.

There's a big dog in the garden.

說交⁠通方⁠式，by 後⁠面不⁠加 the

My grandparents came here by the plane.

My grandparents came here by plane.

去某⁠個地⁠方，名⁠詞片⁠語前⁠面要加 to

When I have free time, I go to the park with my dog.

Are you coming the beach with us?

Are you coming to the beach with us?

例句

① 地點

  1. You don't have to go far for coffee. There's a nice café on the corner of my street.
    你不用跑很遠去喝咖啡。我家那條街的轉角有一家很不錯的咖啡店。
  2. Come and look! The ducks are swimming in the pond behind the school.
    快來看！鴨子在學校後面的池塘裡游泳。
  3. I got wet on the way to work. I left my umbrella on the train this morning.
    我上班途中淋濕了。我今天早上把傘忘在火車上了。
  4. It was very hot at the lake, so we sat under a big tree and had a picnic.
    湖邊很熱，所以我們坐在一棵大樹下野餐。
  5. Are you looking for your glasses? They're next to the computer.
    你在找你的眼鏡嗎？在電腦旁邊。
  6. My aunt and uncle run a noodle shop opposite the temple. Their beef noodles are great.
    我阿姨和姨丈在廟的對面開了一家麵店。他們的牛肉麵很好吃。

② 時間

  1. Come and visit my village. We have lots of fresh fruit in the summer.
    來我住的村子玩吧。我們夏天有很多新鮮水果。
  2. Sorry, I'm not free tonight. I go swimming on Tuesday evenings.
    抱歉，我今晚沒空。我星期二晚上會去游泳。
  3. We can get fresh bread before work. The bakery opens at seven in the morning.
    我們上班前可以去買新鮮的麵包。那家麵包店早上七點開。
  4. Our house gets very busy in February. My cousins visit us at Chinese New Year.
    我們家二月會很熱鬧。我的表兄弟姊妹過年會來我們家。
  5. We can't travel in early July. The school holidays start in the middle of July.
    我們七月初不能去旅行。學校的暑假七月中才開始。

③ 一起的人和東西

  1. I'll be late today. I'm going to the dentist with my little brother. He has a toothache.
    我今天會晚到。我要陪我弟弟去看牙醫。他牙痛。
  2. Don't put milk in his cup. He always drinks tea with lemon.
    別在他的杯子裡加牛奶。他喝茶都會加檸檬。
  3. Her home is never quiet. She lives with her parents and her two brothers.
    她家從來不安靜。她跟她爸媽和她兩個哥哥一起住。
  4. Do you want rice with your chicken?
    你的雞肉要配飯嗎？
  5. I had a lovely afternoon. I went for a walk with my old school friend.
    我下午過得很愉快。我跟我的老同學去散步。

④ 方法和交通方式

  1. I live close to the school, so I go to school by bike.
    我住得離學校很近，所以我騎腳踏車上學。
  2. She didn't have any cash, so she paid for the tickets with her credit card.
    她身上沒有現金，所以她用信用卡付了票錢。
  3. My sister called me last night. We talked on the phone for an hour.
    我姊姊昨晚打給我。我們講了一個小時的電話。
  4. His pen friend lives in Australia, so he wrote the letter in English.
    他的筆友住在澳洲，所以他用英文寫了那封信。
  5. They didn't want to drive, so they travelled around Taiwan by train.
    他們不想開車，所以他們搭火車環島。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. We sometimes meet friends at the sports center on Saturdays. We play basketball together.
    我們星期六有時候會在運動中心跟朋友碰面。我們一起打籃球。
  2. My cat doesn't use her basket. She sleeps on my bed every night.
    我的貓不睡她的籃子。她每天晚上都睡在我床上。
  3. Thanks, they were cheap! I bought these shoes in a shop near the station.
    謝謝，這雙很便宜！我在車站附近的一家店買的。
  4. My desk is too small for both of us. Let's do our homework at the kitchen table.
    我的書桌太小，坐不下我們兩個。我們在廚房的餐桌寫功課吧。
  5. Welcome to my room! There's a photo of my family on the wall.
    歡迎來我房間！牆上有一張我家人的照片。
  6. My dad is never home on Sundays. He goes fishing with his friends at the weekend.
    我爸星期天都不在家。他週末會跟他朋友去釣魚。
  7. If you need the car, the keys are in the drawer under the TV.
    你如果要用車，鑰匙在電視下面的抽屜裡。
  8. Look at this cover. I'm reading a book about a girl from Mars.
    你看這個封面。我在讀一本講一個火星女孩的書。
  9. The hotel was by the sea, so we walked along the beach after dinner.
    飯店在海邊，所以我們晚餐後沿著海灘散步。
