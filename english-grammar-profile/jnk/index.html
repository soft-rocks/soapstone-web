On Thursday, I did the best I could.

星期四那天，我已經盡力了。

can 和 could 後⁠面省⁠略了動⁠詞

On Thursday, I did the best I could.

我會盡力。

現⁠在和未⁠來用 can，過⁠去用 could

On Thursday, I did the best I could.

例句

① do the best + 主詞 + can

  1. The report is due at five, and it's already two. I'll do the best I can with the time I have.
    報告五點要交，現在已經兩點了。我會在有限的時間內盡力。
  2. Your driving test is tomorrow. Don't worry. Just do the best you can.
    你明天考駕照。別擔心，盡力就好。
  3. Your flight was cancelled, madam? We'll do the best we can to help you.
    女士，你的班機取消了嗎？我們會盡力幫你。
  4. Your father is still in the operating room. The doctors are doing the best they can.
    你爸爸還在手術室。醫生們正在盡力。
  5. My husband never complains about long hours. He always does the best he can at work.
    我先生從來不抱怨工時長。他工作總是盡全力。
  6. The coach spoke to the runners before the race. Do the best you can and don't give up.
    教練在比賽前跟跑者說話。盡你所能，不要放棄。

② did the best + 主詞 + could

  1. Last night I did the best I could, but the cake still burned.
    我昨晚盡力了，但蛋糕還是烤焦了。
  2. My mother raised four children alone. She did the best she could with very little money.
    我媽媽獨自養大四個孩子。她用很少的錢盡力做到最好。
  3. The other team had much taller players. We did the best we could, but we lost the game.
    另一隊的球員高很多。我們盡力了，但還是輸了比賽。
  4. The storm broke the biggest branch. The gardeners did the best they could to save the old tree.
    暴風雨把最大的樹枝打斷了。園丁們盡力想救那棵老樹。
  5. Don't be angry with Tom about the late delivery. He did the best he could, and that's all we can ask.
    別為了晚送貨的事生湯姆的氣。他盡力了，我們也只能要求這麼多。

③ try the best + 主詞 + can

  1. My dad isn't good at drawing, but he tries the best he can.
    我爸爸不太會畫畫，但他很盡力。
  2. My little brother annoys me every day. I try the best I can to stay calm.
    我弟弟每天都惹我生氣。我盡量保持冷靜。
  3. Grandpa was asleep on the sofa. The kids tried the best they could to keep quiet.
    爺爺在沙發上睡著了。孩子們盡量保持安靜。
  4. My cousin works in his family's shop after school. He tries the best he can to help his parents.
    我表弟放學後在家裡的店工作。他盡力幫他爸媽的忙。

④ offer / give the best + 主詞 + can

  1. Our small team offers the best it can on a tight budget.
    我們這個小團隊在有限的預算下盡力提供最好的。
  2. Our café is small, but we bake everything fresh. We give our customers the best we can.
    我們的咖啡館很小，可是每樣東西都是現烤的。我們盡力給顧客最好的。
  3. All the big rooms were taken. The hotel gave us the best it could on such a busy night.
    大房間都被訂走了。在那麼忙的晚上，飯店盡力給了我們最好的安排。
  4. Our school has very old books and computers. The teachers give their students the best they can.
    我們學校的書和電腦都很舊。老師們盡力給學生最好的。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. You failed the test, but that's OK. Did you do the best you could?
    你考試沒過，不過沒關係。你盡力了嗎？
  2. You can't control the judges' scores. All you can do is the best you can.
    你沒辦法控制評審的分數。你能做的就是盡力而已。
  3. Anna came second in the swimming race. I'm sure she did the best she could.
    安娜游泳比賽拿第二名。我相信她已經盡力了。
  4. Can you promise me you'll do the best you can on Monday?
    你能答應我星期一會盡力嗎？
  5. My son had only ten minutes for the essay. He wrote the best he could in such a short time.
    我兒子只有十分鐘寫作文。在那麼短的時間內，他盡力寫了。
  6. We painted the fence the best we could before the rain.
    下雨前，我們盡力把籬笆漆好了。
  7. Don't use a dictionary in the test. Answer the questions the best you can.
    考試不能用字典。盡你所能回答這些問題。
  8. The pitch was wet and muddy. The team played the best they could in the rain.
    球場又濕又泥濘。隊員們在雨中盡力比賽。
  9. I fixed the bike the best I could, but it's still noisy.
    我盡力修了腳踏車，不過它還是很吵。
  10. My little sister asked me about the stars. I explained it the best I could.
    我妹妹問我星星的事。我盡力解釋了。
  11. My grandparents live far from the shops. They managed the best they could without a car.
    我爺爺奶奶住得離商店很遠。他們在沒有車的情況下盡力應付。
