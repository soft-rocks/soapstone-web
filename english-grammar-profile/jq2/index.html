例句

① 否定的直述句 + is / are / was / were

  1. Your dog keeps shaking. That isn't normal, is it?
    你的狗一直發抖。這不正常，對吧？
  2. Don't worry about the boss. He isn't really angry, is he?
    別擔心老闆。他不是真的生氣，對吧？
  3. Let's buy the food today. The shops aren't open on public holidays, are they?
    我們今天就把吃的買好吧。國定假日商店不開，對吧？
  4. I didn't see you there. You weren't at the meeting yesterday, were you?
    我沒看到你。你昨天沒來開會，對吧？
  5. I won't stay there again. The hotel wasn't very clean, was it?
    我不會再住那裡了。那家飯店不太乾淨，對吧？
  6. I'm sure we'll find a seat. The concert isn't sold out, is it?
    我們一定找得到位子。演唱會沒賣完，對吧？

② 否定的直述句 + do / does

  1. Let's not go to the disco. Your parents don't like discos, do they?
    我們別去舞廳了。你爸媽不喜歡舞廳，對吧？
  2. You don't need a lift, do you? I'm leaving now.
    你不用人載吧？我現在要走了。
  3. Your sister doesn't drink coffee, does she? I'll make tea.
    你姊姊不喝咖啡，對吧？我來泡茶。
  4. Excuse me, I need to get off at Oxford. This train doesn't stop there, does it?
    不好意思，我要在牛津下車。這班火車不停那裡，對吧？
  5. I need to post this card. They don't sell stamps here, do they?
    我要寄這張卡片。這裡不賣郵票，對吧？
  6. I'm thinking of buying one too. The new phone doesn't cost much, does it?
    我也在考慮買一支。新手機不太貴，對吧？

③ 否定的直述句 + did

  1. You didn't tell Mum about the broken glass, did you?
    你沒跟媽媽說玻璃杯破了的事，對吧？
  2. Nobody saw us leave early. The teacher didn't notice, did she?
    沒人看到我們提早走。老師沒發現，對吧？
  3. Wait, we need to go back. We didn't lock the back door, did we?
    等一下，我們得回去。我們沒鎖後門，對吧？
  4. The tin is empty. You didn't eat all the biscuits, did you?
    盒子空了。你沒把餅乾全部吃掉吧？
  5. We left at nine and arrived at half past. It didn't take long to get here, did it?
    我們九點出發，九點半就到了。來這裡沒花多久時間，對吧？

④ 否定的直述句 + have / has

  1. I'll show you the way. You haven't been to my house before, have you?
    我帶你走。你以前沒來過我家，對吧？
  2. We haven't seen each other for about a year, have we?
    我們差不多一年沒見面了，對吧？
  3. Don't tell Mia the ending. She hasn't finished the book yet, has she?
    別跟 Mia 說結局。她還沒讀完那本書，對吧？
  4. I can't open the car. You haven't seen my keys, have you?
    我打不開車門。你沒看到我的鑰匙吧？
  5. I'm waiting for a letter from the bank. The post hasn't come yet, has it?
    我在等銀行的信。郵件還沒來，對吧？
  6. The sign is still in the garden. They haven't sold the house yet, have they?
    牌子還在院子裡。他們還沒把房子賣掉，對吧？

⑤ 省略主詞的說法

  1. Doesn't sound very interesting, does it?
    聽起來沒什麼意思，對吧？
  2. Not very warm in here, is it? Shall I close the window?
    這裡不太暖和，對吧？要我把窗戶關上嗎？
  3. Didn't go well, did it? You look upset.
    不太順利，對吧？你看起來很難過。

⑥ 用來請對方幫忙

  1. You don't have a phone charger, do you? My battery is nearly dead.
    你沒有手機充電器吧？我的手機快沒電了。
  2. You haven't got a pen, have you? Mine has run out.
    你沒有筆，對吧？我的沒水了。
  3. You don't know the way to the station, do you?
    你不知道去車站的路，對吧？

⑦ 用來表示驚訝或擔心

  1. He isn't important, is he? Why is everyone so nervous?
    他不是什麼重要人物吧？為什麼大家都這麼緊張？
  2. You aren't leaving already, are you? It's only nine o'clock.
    你不會已經要走了吧？才九點而已。
