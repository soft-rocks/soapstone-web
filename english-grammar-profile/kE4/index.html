As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

只要工作跟小孩有關，我很樂意夏天替你工作。

只⁠要條⁠件成⁠立，主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的事就發⁠生

As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

只要你每週澆一次水，這盆植物會長得很好。

條件子句說未來的事，但不用 will。

意⁠思都⁠是只⁠要

As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

as long as
口語

We'll go camping this weekend as long as it doesn't rain.

只要不下雨，我們這個週末就去露營。
provided (that)
正式

Provided that the train is on time, we'll arrive before lunch.

只要火車準時，我們午餐前會到。
providing (that)
正式

You can take the day off providing you finish the report first.

只要你先把報告寫完，就可以請一天假。

that 可以省略。

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式說安⁠排好⁠的事

provided

Provided you're staying for the whole week, the hotel will give you a discount.

只要你住滿一整週，飯店會給你折扣。
as long as

As long as you're driving tomorrow, I'll leave my car at home.

只要你明天會開車，我就把我的車留在家。

條⁠件子⁠句⁠的位⁠置和逗⁠號

As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

放在前面
後面加逗號

Providing the doctor agrees, Grandpa can come home on Monday.

只要醫生同意，爺爺星期一就可以回家。
放在後面
前面通常不加逗號

Visitors may take photos provided that they don't use a flash.

只要不用閃光燈，參觀者可以拍照。

原句：客⁠氣⁠地說樂⁠意做

As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

只要工作跟小孩有關，我很樂意夏天替你工作。

it 指那份工作。I'd 是 I would。

條⁠件子⁠句不⁠用 will

As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

You can borrow the camera as long as you will be careful with it.

You can borrow the camera as long as you're careful with it.

連⁠接⁠詞是 provided，不⁠是 provide

Provide that you book early, you'll get a good seat.

Provided that you book early, you'll get a good seat.

long 前⁠後各⁠有一⁠個 as

As long as it involves children, I'd be delighted to work for you during the summer.

As long you tell the truth, I won't be angry.

As long as you tell the truth, I won't be angry.

unless 說條⁠件成⁠立就⁠不⁠發⁠生

as long as

We'll eat outside as long as it stays dry.

只要天氣保持乾燥，我們就在外面吃。
unless

We'll eat outside unless it rains.

除非下雨，不然我們就在外面吃。

動⁠詞 provided 後⁠面接⁠受⁠詞

動詞

The school provided free lunches.

學校提供了免費的午餐。
連接詞

Provided you bring your own lunch, you can join the trip.

只要你自己帶午餐，就可以參加這趟旅行。

例句

① as long as

  1. As long as the weather stays fine, we'll have the party in the garden.
    只要天氣一直很好，我們就在花園辦派對。
  2. You can go out to play as long as you're back before dark.
    只要天黑前回來，你就可以出去玩。
  3. As long as we leave before seven, we'll miss the traffic.
    只要我們七點前出發，就能避開車潮。
  4. I'll hold the ladder as long as you don't climb too high.
    只要你不爬太高，我就幫你扶梯子。
  5. The meeting will finish early as long as nobody raises a new topic.
    只要沒有人提出新的話題，會議會提早結束。
  6. As long as your answers aren't ambiguous, the examiner will give you full marks.
    只要你的答案不模稜兩可，考官就會給你滿分。
  7. Mum won't scold you as long as you tell her the truth.
    只要你跟媽媽說實話，她不會罵你。
  8. "Can I borrow your bike?" "Sure, as long as you lock it up."
    可以借你的腳踏車嗎？可以啊，只要你有上鎖。

② provided (that)

  1. Provided that the flight is domestic, you won't need your passport.
    只要是國內航班，你就不需要護照。
  2. You can keep the kitten provided you look after it yourself.
    只要你自己照顧，就可以養這隻小貓。
  3. Provided the sellers accept our offer, we'll move in May.
    只要賣方接受我們的出價，我們五月就會搬家。
  4. Provided that the board concurs, the new policy will start in January.
    只要董事會同意，新政策會在一月開始實施。
  5. I'll lend you my notes provided you give them back tomorrow.
    只要你明天還我，我就借你我的筆記。
  6. Provided that nobody objects, we'll close the office early on Friday.
    只要沒有人反對，我們星期五會提早關辦公室。
  7. Visitors may take photos provided that they don't use a flash.
    只要不用閃光燈，參觀者可以拍照。

③ providing (that)

  1. Providing that the train isn't delayed, I'll be home by six.
    只要火車沒誤點，我六點前會到家。
  2. You can come with us providing you pay for your own ticket.
    只要你自己出票錢，就可以跟我們去。
  3. Providing we make these changes, sales will rise next year.
    只要我們做出這些改變，明年的銷售額會成長。
  4. Providing it doesn't rain, the farmers will finish the harvest this week.
    只要不下雨，農夫們這週會完成收割。
  5. Your plan should work providing that everyone does their part.
    只要每個人都做好自己的部分，你的計畫應該行得通。
  6. Providing the doctor agrees, Grandpa can come home on Monday.
    只要醫生同意，爺爺星期一就可以回家。

④ 現在進行式

  1. As long as you're living under my roof, you'll follow my rules.
    只要你還住在我家，就要守我的規矩。
  2. Provided that you're travelling with a child, you can board first.
    只要你帶著小孩同行，就可以先登機。
  3. As long as you're driving tomorrow, I'll leave my car at home.
    只要你明天會開車，我就把我的車留在家。
  4. Providing we're meeting at the station, I'll bring the tickets.
    只要我們是在車站碰面，我就帶車票去。
  5. You can use my desk tomorrow as long as I'm not working from home.
    只要我明天不在家工作，你就可以用我的書桌。

⑤ 主要子句用 would 或祈使句

  1. As long as it's not too far, I'd be happy to drive you to the airport.
    只要不會太遠，我很樂意開車送你去機場。
  2. Provided the price is right, we'd be interested in ordering two hundred units.
    只要價格合理，我們有興趣訂兩百件。
  3. I'd gladly join the project providing it doesn't take up my weekends.
    只要不佔用我的週末，我很樂意加入這個專案。
  4. I'd be glad to help as long as you don't mind waiting until Friday.
    只要你不介意等到星期五，我很樂意幫忙。
  5. As long as you're careful, borrow whatever you need.
    只要你小心一點，需要什麼就拿去用。
  6. Provided it isn't after midnight, call me when you land.
    只要還沒過半夜，你落地後就打給我。
