I couldn't stand being there.

我受不了待在那裡。

can't stand 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

我受不了待在那裡。

片⁠語動⁠詞⁠的動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I couldn't stand being there.

像 put off、give up、carry on。

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 可⁠以是否⁠定、被⁠動或完⁠成⁠的形⁠式

I couldn't stand being there.

not + 動詞⁠-⁠ing

He admitted not locking the door.

他承認沒有鎖門。
being + 過去分詞

I can't stand being told what to do.

我受不了被人指使。
having + 過去分詞

He denied having met her before.

他否認以前見過她。

having + 過去分詞 說的事比動詞早發生。

deny 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，不⁠接不⁠定⁠詞

I couldn't stand being there.

He denied to break the window.

He denied breaking the window.

give up 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，不⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I couldn't stand being there.

My aunt gave up play the violin at sixty.

My aunt gave up playing the violin at sixty.

小⁠品⁠詞緊⁠跟在動⁠詞後⁠面

I couldn't stand being there.

The band carried playing on.

The band carried on playing.

appreciate 後⁠面不⁠接人加不⁠定⁠詞

I couldn't stand being there.

I'd appreciate you to call me back.

I'd appreciate it if you called me back.

afford 後⁠面⁠接不⁠定⁠詞

I couldn't stand being there.

She refused to answer the reporter's questions.

她拒絕回答記者的問題。

be used to 的 to 是介⁠系⁠詞，後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I couldn't stand being there.

I'm used to working at night.

我習慣晚上工作。

例句

① can't stand / can't bear + 動詞-ing

  1. The hotel was so noisy. I couldn't stand staying there another night.
    那家飯店太吵了。我受不了再住一晚。
  2. "Do you want to come shopping?" "No, thanks. I can't stand waiting in queues."
    你要一起去購物嗎？不了，謝謝。我受不了排隊。
  3. My grandmother can't bear living alone. She misses my grandfather every day.
    我奶奶受不了一個人住。她每天都很想念我爺爺。
  4. My brother hates driving to work. He can't stand sitting in traffic every morning.
    我哥很討厭開車上班。他受不了每天早上塞在車陣裡。

② deny / admit + 動詞-ing

  1. The student denied cheating in the exam, but the teacher had seen his notes.
    那個學生否認考試作弊，可是老師看到了他的小抄。
  2. "Did you eat the last piece of cake?" "OK, I admit eating it. Sorry!"
    最後一塊蛋糕是你吃的嗎？好啦，我承認是我吃的。對不起！
  3. The driver admitted going too fast.
    司機承認他開太快了。
  4. The minister denied having taken the money.
    部長否認拿了那筆錢。

③ risk / involve + 動詞-ing

  1. If you don't book now, you risk losing your seat.
    你現在不訂的話，可能會沒位子。
  2. "What does the job involve?" "It involves travelling a lot and meeting clients."
    這份工作要做什麼？要常出差，還要見客戶。
  3. He didn't want to risk missing the last train, so he left early.
    他不想冒險錯過末班車，所以提早走了。
  4. Being a nurse involves working at night and at weekends.
    當護士要上夜班，週末也要上班。

④ delay / put off + 動詞-ing

  1. We delayed telling the children about the move until the end of term.
    我們拖到學期末才告訴孩子們要搬家。
  2. "Have you called the bank yet?" "No, I keep putting off calling them."
    你打電話給銀行了嗎？還沒，我一直拖著沒打。
  3. Don't put off seeing a doctor if the pain gets worse.
    如果痛得更厲害，不要拖著不去看醫生。

⑤ give up / carry on + 動詞-ing

  1. "You look healthy!" "Thanks. I gave up eating sugar three months ago."
    你看起來很健康！謝謝。我三個月前戒糖了。
  2. Even after the rain started, the band carried on playing.
    就算開始下雨，樂團還是繼續演奏。
  3. "Are you still learning Japanese?" "No, I gave up going to classes last year."
    你還在學日文嗎？沒有，我去年就不去上課了。
  4. The teacher told us to carry on working while she answered the phone.
    老師接電話的時候，叫我們繼續做功課。

⑥ appreciate / mention / recommend / fancy + 動詞-ing

  1. Thanks for the desk in your office. I really appreciate having a quiet place to work.
    謝謝你讓我用你辦公室的桌子。有個安靜的地方工作，我真的很感謝。
  2. Did Sarah mention having a party on Saturday?
    Sarah 有提到星期六要辦派對嗎？
  3. "Where should we eat in Rome?" "I'd recommend trying the little place near our hotel."
    我們在羅馬要去哪裡吃？我推薦試試我們飯店附近那家小店。
  4. "Do you fancy going out for a curry tonight?" "Sounds great!"
    今晚想出去吃咖哩嗎？好啊！
  5. Most people dislike being woken up early on Sundays.
    大部分的人都不喜歡星期天一大早被吵醒。

⑦ 動詞 + not + 動詞-ing

  1. "Any tips for the long flight?" "If you want to sleep, I'd recommend not drinking coffee."
    長途飛行有什麼建議嗎？想睡覺的話，我建議不要喝咖啡。
  2. The boy admitted not doing his homework.
    那個男孩承認沒有寫功課。
  3. I can't stand not knowing the end of a story.
    不知道故事的結局，我會受不了。

⑧ being + 過去分詞、having + 過去分詞

  1. "Why did you leave the team?" "I couldn't stand being shouted at by the coach."
    你為什麼離開球隊？我受不了被教練吼。
  2. Nobody likes being kept waiting.
    沒有人喜歡被晾在一邊等。
  3. The man admitted having stolen the bike.
    那個男人承認偷了腳踏車。
  4. He denied having been told about the meeting.
    他否認有人告訴過他開會的事。
