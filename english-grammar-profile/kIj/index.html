Sales will possibly have fallen to 5,000 units by the end of the year.

到年底，銷售量可能已經掉到 5,000 件了。

主⁠詞 + will + 副⁠詞 + have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

will、副⁠詞、have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Sales will possibly have fallen to 5,000 units by the end of the year.

will表示未來。
副詞放在 will 後面、have 前面，這是句中副詞的位置。
have + 過去分詞到那個時間點已經完成的事。
表示可能性probably、possibly、certainly、surely、definitely

否⁠定⁠句⁠裡⁠的位⁠置

例句

He probably won't have finished by five.

到五點，他大概還沒做完。

否定句裡，表示可能性的副詞放在 won't 前面。

縮⁠寫

例句

By then you'll certainly have forgotten all about it.

到那時候，你一定早就把這件事忘光了。

'll 後面也可以接副詞。

句中副⁠詞放⁠在 will 後⁠面、have 前⁠面

Sales will possibly have fallen to 5,000 units by the end of the year.

By June we already will have moved.

By June we will already have moved.

未⁠來完⁠成⁠式要⁠有 have

Sales will possibly have fallen to 5,000 units by the end of the year.

She will probably finished by then.

She will probably have finished by then.

否⁠定⁠句⁠裡，probably 放⁠在 won't 前⁠面

He won't probably have arrived by noon.

He probably won't have arrived by noon.

例句

① 表示可能性的副詞

  1. By the end of the trip, we will possibly have visited twelve cities.
    到旅行結束，我們可能已經去過十二個城市了。
  2. If you have read his stories, you will surely have discovered why he is famous.
    如果你讀過他的故事，你一定已經發現他為什麼有名。
  3. By the time you get this letter, I'll probably have left Japan.
    等你收到這封信的時候，我大概已經離開日本了。
  4. The storm will certainly have passed by the morning.
    到早上，暴風雨一定已經過去了。
  5. By next year, prices will definitely have gone up.
    到明年，物價一定已經漲了。
  6. The guests will probably have eaten before they come.
    客人來之前大概已經吃過了。
  7. By the time we arrive, the film will possibly have started.
    等我們到的時候，電影可能已經開始了。
  8. By the weekend, the doctor will surely have seen the results.
    到週末，醫生一定已經看到結果了。
  9. By the end of the week, the river will probably have risen even more.
    到週末，河水大概又漲得更高了。
  10. By the next election, the voters will probably have changed their minds.
    到下次選舉，選民大概已經改變主意了。

② 表示時間的副詞

  1. By June we will already have moved to the new flat.
    到六月，我們就已經搬進新公寓了。
  2. By the time the firefighters arrive, the fire will already have spread.
    等消防員到的時候，火早就蔓延開了。
  3. When you wake up, I'll already have gone to work.
    等你醒來的時候，我已經去上班了。
  4. By ten, the shop will already have sold out of bread.
    到十點，這家店的麵包早就賣光了。
  5. The bus will just have left when we get to the stop.
    我們到站牌的時候，公車應該剛好開走。
  6. By the summer, she will already have finished her dissertation.
    到夏天，她就已經寫完論文了。
  7. By the time the guests sit down, I will already have opened the wine.
    等客人坐下的時候，我就已經把酒開好了。
  8. The meeting will already have begun when the boss arrives.
    老闆到的時候，會議已經開始了。
  9. By the end of the year, I will already have spent my whole holiday allowance.
    到年底，我就已經把年假全部用完了。
  10. The train will just have arrived when you get to the platform.
    你到月台的時候，火車應該剛好進站。
  11. By the time the show ends, the last bus will already have gone.
    等表演結束的時候，最後一班公車早就開走了。

③ 否定句：副詞 + won't have + 過去分詞

  1. He probably won't have finished the painting by the exhibition.
    到展覽的時候，他大概還沒畫完那幅畫。
  2. The parcel probably won't have arrived by Friday.
    到星期五，包裹大概還不會送到。
  3. They certainly won't have sold the house by June.
    到六月，他們一定還沒把房子賣掉。
  4. The workers probably won't have cleared the roads by the morning.
    到早上，工人大概還沒把道路清理好。
  5. She definitely won't have heard the news by then.
    到那時候，她一定還沒聽到這個消息。
  6. The kids probably won't have gone to bed when we get home.
    等我們到家的時候，孩子們大概還沒上床睡覺。
  7. The builders certainly won't have finished the roof before the rain.
    下雨前，工人一定還沒把屋頂蓋好。
  8. I probably won't have read the report before the meeting.
    開會前我大概還沒讀完報告。
  9. The new library possibly won't have opened by the autumn.
    到秋天，新圖書館可能還沒開放。
  10. We definitely won't have saved enough by the summer.
    到夏天，我們一定還存不夠錢。
