例句

① Why don't we + 原形動詞：提議一起做

  1. It's raining too hard to play outside. Why don't we watch a film?
    雨太大了，不能在外面玩。我們何不看部電影？
  2. We're both hungry. Why don't we stop for lunch?
    我們都餓了。我們何不停下來吃午餐？
  3. The weather is perfect. Why don't we have a picnic in the park?
    天氣好極了。我們何不在公園野餐？
  4. Why don't we share a taxi? We're going the same way.
    我們何不一起搭計程車？我們順路。
  5. The library is closed on Monday. Why don't we study at my place?
    圖書館星期一休館。我們何不在我家念書？
  6. We've got a long weekend. Why don't we visit your parents?
    我們有連假。我們何不去看你爸媽？
  7. Why don't we ask Sam? He knows a lot about cars.
    我們何不問 Sam？他對車子很懂。
  8. I'm tired of pizza. Why don't we cook something different tonight?
    我吃膩披薩了。我們今晚何不煮點不一樣的？
  9. This plan isn't working. Why don't we start again from the beginning?
    這個計畫行不通。我們何不從頭再來一次？

② Why don't you + 原形動詞：建議對方

  1. You look tired. Why don't you go to bed early tonight?
    你看起來很累。你今晚何不早點睡？
  2. Your headache hasn't gone. Why don't you see a doctor?
    你的頭痛還沒好。你何不去看醫生？
  3. Why don't you ask your teacher? She'll know the answer.
    你何不問你的老師？她會知道答案。
  4. That dress looks great on you. Why don't you buy it?
    那件洋裝你穿起來很好看。你何不買下來？
  5. That's a long phone number. Why don't you write it down so you don't forget?
    那個電話號碼很長。你何不把它寫下來，免得忘記？
  6. If you're bored, why don't you learn a new language?
    如果你很無聊，何不學一種新的語言？
  7. Why don't you take the train? It's faster than driving.
    你何不搭火車？比開車快。

③ Why don't you + 原形動詞：邀請對方

  1. I'm going to the market. Why don't you come with me?
    我要去市集。你何不跟我一起去？
  2. We're having a barbecue on Sunday. Why don't you join us?
    我們星期天要烤肉。你何不一起來？
  3. Why don't you stay for dinner? I've made plenty.
    你何不留下來吃晚餐？我煮了很多。
  4. Why don't you come and visit us in the summer?
    你夏天何不來看我們？
  5. There's plenty of space in the car. Why don't you bring your cousin along?
    車上空間很多。你何不帶你表妹一起來？

④ Why don't I + 原形動詞：主動幫忙

  1. You're busy. Why don't I make the dinner tonight?
    你很忙。今晚何不讓我來煮晚餐？
  2. Your bags look heavy. Why don't I carry one of them?
    你的袋子看起來好重。何不讓我幫你拿一個？
  3. It's Mum's birthday on Friday. Why don't I call the restaurant and book a table?
    星期五是媽媽的生日。何不讓我打電話給餐廳訂位？

⑤ Why not + 原形動詞：更簡短的說法

  1. Why not take a day off? You deserve it.
    何不請一天假？你應該休息一下。
  2. Why not ask for help? Everyone needs it sometimes.
    何不請人幫忙？每個人有時候都需要。

⑥ 回答建議

  1. "Why don't we go for a swim?" "Good idea! I'll get my towel."
    我們何不去游泳？好主意！我去拿毛巾。
  2. "Why don't you join us for coffee?" "I'd love to, thanks."
    你何不跟我們一起喝咖啡？我很樂意，謝謝。
  3. "Why don't we go out tonight?" "Sorry, I'm too tired."
    我們今晚何不出去？抱歉，我太累了。
  4. "Why don't you try the chocolate cake?" "OK, I will."
    你何不試試巧克力蛋糕？好，我試試。
  5. "Why don't we paint the kitchen yellow?" "Hmm, maybe. Let's look at some colours first."
    我們何不把廚房漆成黃色？嗯，也許吧。我們先看看顏色。
