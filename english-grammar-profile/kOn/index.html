My house is opposite the post office.

我家在郵局對面。

opposite 說在……對⁠面

我家在郵局對面。

更⁠多介⁠系⁠詞說⁠明精⁠確⁠的位⁠置

My house is opposite the post office.

between
在兩個之間between the bank and the school
behind
在……後面behind the door
above / below
上方、下方above the sofa
along / across
沿著、橫越along the river, across the road

during、until 和 by 說⁠明時⁠間

My house is opposite the post office.

during
在某段時間裡during the film
until
直到until Friday
by
最晚在……以前

Please send me the photos by Friday.

請在星期五以前把照片寄給我。

like、without 和 about 說比⁠較、沒⁠有和主⁠題

My house is opposite the post office.

like
像blue like the sky
without
沒有coffee without sugar
about
關於a film about dogs

opposite 後⁠面直⁠接接名⁠詞，不⁠加 of

My house is opposite the post office.

The school is opposite of the park.

The school is opposite the park.

最⁠晚在某⁠個時⁠間以⁠前完⁠成用 by，持⁠續到某⁠個時⁠間用 until

You must finish the essay until Monday.

You must finish the essay by Monday.

說時⁠間⁠的長⁠度用 for，不⁠用 during

I lived in Paris during three years.

I lived in Paris for three years.

例句

① opposite / between / behind

  1. You can't miss it. The bus stop is opposite the hospital.
    你一定找得到。公車站在醫院對面。
  2. I sat between my mother and my aunt at dinner.
    晚餐時我坐在我媽媽和我阿姨中間。
  3. The cat is scared of the vacuum cleaner. She's hiding behind the sofa.
    貓怕吸塵器。她躲在沙發後面。
  4. The kids can play outside. There's a small garden behind our building.
    孩子們可以到外面玩。我們大樓後面有一個小花園。
  5. Are you watching the baseball tonight? The match is between Taiwan and Japan.
    你今晚要看棒球嗎？這場比賽是台灣對日本。
  6. We loved the view from our room. Our hotel was opposite a beautiful old church.
    我們很喜歡房間看出去的景色。我們的飯店在一座漂亮的老教堂對面。

② near / above / along / across

  1. We go swimming every day in summer. My grandparents' house is near the beach.
    我們夏天每天都去游泳。我爺爺奶奶家在海灘附近。
  2. You don't need your phone to check the time. There's a clock above the door.
    你不用看手機看時間。門上方有一個時鐘。
  3. The weather was lovely, so we walked along the river for an hour.
    天氣很好，所以我們沿著河邊走了一個小時。
  4. When the bell rang, the children ran across the playground.
    鐘聲一響，孩子們就跑過操場。
  5. Take the lift to the fourth floor. My office is on the floor above the gym.
    搭電梯到四樓。我的辦公室在健身房上面那一層。

③ during / before / after

  1. Please turn off your phone during the concert.
    演唱會期間請把手機關掉。
  2. I don't know how it ended. I fell asleep during the second half of the film.
    我不知道結局。我在電影的下半場睡著了。
  3. Mum said we have to get home before midnight.
    媽媽說我們得在半夜以前到家。
  4. Let's go shopping now. The shops get very busy after five o'clock.
    我們現在去買東西吧。五點以後店裡會變得很擠。
  5. The garden is all wet. It rained heavily during the night.
    院子全濕了。夜裡下了很大的雨。

④ until / by

  1. We still have time to go in. The museum is open until six.
    我們還有時間進去。博物館開到六點。
  2. Please return the book by next Monday.
    請在下星期一以前還書。
  3. Take the other road to work. The roadworks will continue until the end of March.
    走另一條路上班吧。道路施工會持續到三月底。
  4. The manager needs it for the meeting. The report must be ready by Thursday.
    經理開會要用。報告星期四以前要好。
  5. It was a perfect day. We stayed at the beach until sunset.
    那天很完美。我們在海邊待到日落。

⑤ like / without / about

  1. Have you met the new girl? Her eyes are green like the sea.
    你見過那個新來的女生嗎？她的眼睛像海一樣綠。
  2. I always pack it when I travel. I can't sleep without my pillow.
    我旅行都會帶著它。沒有我的枕頭我睡不著。
  3. The kids loved TV last night. We watched a programme about elephants.
    孩子們昨晚很愛看電視。我們看了一個關於大象的節目。
  4. Did you make this? This cake tastes like my mother's.
    這是你做的嗎？這個蛋糕吃起來像我媽媽做的。
  5. No milk for me, thanks. I drink my tea without milk.
    我不用牛奶，謝謝。我喝茶不加牛奶。
  6. It's good to see you! Tell me about your new job.
    真高興見到你！跟我說說你的新工作。

⑥ 不同的情境

  1. Is there a hotel near the station? I arrive late at night.
    車站附近有飯店嗎？我很晚才會到。
  2. Our neighbours live below us, so please don't jump on the floor.
    我們的鄰居住在我們樓下，所以請不要在地板上跳。
  3. Look, you can see fish swimming below the surface of the water.
    你看，可以看到魚在水面下游。
  4. In summer, there are flowers along the road to the village.
    夏天的時候，往村子的路上沿路都是花。
  5. I always check my email before breakfast.
    我都在早餐前看電子郵件。
  6. We can go for a walk after lunch. The rain will stop by then.
    我們午餐後可以去散步。那時候雨就會停了。
