I'll be waiting for you.

我會等著你。

未⁠來進⁠行⁠式說未⁠來某⁠時正⁠在進⁠行⁠的動⁠作

I'll be waiting for you.

我會等著你。

朋友到站的時候，Ann 已經開始等，還在等。

每⁠個⁠人稱都用 will be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I'll be waiting for you.

I

I'll be studying in my room.

我會在房間念書。
He

He'll be playing tennis at four.

他四點會在打網球。
The guests

The guests will be arriving soon.

客人很快就會到了。

口⁠語常把 will 縮⁠寫成 'll

I'll be waiting for you.

will be
書面、正式場合

We will be starting at twelve o'clock.

我們十二點開始。
'll be
口語

I'll be sitting near the door.

我會坐在門邊。

will 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞說決⁠定，will be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 說進⁠行中⁠的動⁠作

I'll be waiting for you.

will be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 的動⁠作在那⁠個時⁠間之⁠前就開⁠始了

I'll be waiting for you.

will + 原形動詞
八點開始

At eight I'll have dinner.

我八點吃晚餐。
will be + 動詞-ing
八點前已經開始

At eight I'll be having dinner.

八點的時候我正在吃晚餐。

will 後⁠面先接 be，再⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I'll be waiting for you.

I will waiting for you.

I will be waiting for you.

be 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用 ⁠-⁠ing 形⁠式

I'll be waiting for you.

We will be start at noon.

We will be starting at noon.

will 後⁠面⁠的 be 用原⁠形，不⁠隨主⁠詞變⁠化

I'll be waiting for you.

They will are staying with us.

They will be staying with us.

will 是情⁠態動⁠詞，第⁠三⁠人⁠稱單⁠數也⁠不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

I'll be waiting for you.

She wills be working late.

She will be working late.

例句

① 主詞 + will be + 動詞-ing

  1. We will be starting at twelve o'clock this afternoon.
    我們今天中午十二點開始。
  2. The manager will be waiting for you in the lobby.
    經理會在大廳等你。
  3. My parents will be staying at a hotel near the airport.
    我爸媽會住在機場附近的飯店。
  4. The library will be closing in ten minutes.
    圖書館再十分鐘就要關門了。
  5. Professor Lin will be reading our dissertations over the summer.
    Lin 教授暑假會看我們的論文。
  6. The workers will be painting the hall all day tomorrow.
    工人明天一整天都會在油漆大廳。
  7. Our team will be playing in the final on Sunday.
    我們隊星期天會打決賽。
  8. Tom will be driving through the mountains at that time.
    那個時候 Tom 會正在開車穿過山區。
  9. The students will be taking their exam at nine.
    學生九點會在考試。
  10. The band will be playing until midnight.
    樂團會一直演奏到半夜。
  11. Grandpa will be sleeping when you get home.
    你到家的時候爺爺會已經在睡覺了。
  12. The shop will be selling the new phone from Friday.
    這家店星期五開始賣那支新手機。
  13. Ms. Chen will be teaching your class next term.
    Chen 老師下學期會教你們班。
  14. Most people will be watching the game tonight.
    今晚大部分的人都會在看比賽。
  15. The company will be furnishing the new office next month.
    公司下個月會替新辦公室添置家具。
  16. The kids will be swimming in the lake all afternoon.
    孩子們整個下午都會在湖裡游泳。

② 主詞 + 'll be + 動詞-ing

  1. I'll be sitting near the door, so look for me there.
    我會坐在門邊，你到那裡找我。
  2. She'll be working late tonight.
    她今晚會加班到很晚。
  3. They'll be staying with us next week.
    他們下星期會住在我們家。
  4. We'll be having dinner when you arrive.
    你到的時候我們會正在吃晚餐。
  5. He'll be wearing a blue jacket.
    他會穿一件藍色外套。
  6. I'll be thinking of you on your big day.
    你的大日子那天我會想著你。
  7. You'll be living in a new city this time next year.
    明年這個時候你就住在新的城市了。
  8. She'll be writing her treatise on the history of tea all winter.
    她整個冬天都會在寫那篇關於茶的歷史的專論。
  9. We'll be flying over the ocean at midnight.
    半夜的時候我們會正在飛越海洋。
  10. I'll be cooking, so just come into the kitchen.
    我會在煮飯，你直接進廚房來。
  11. They'll be celebrating all weekend.
    他們整個週末都會在慶祝。
  12. You'll be feeling better in a few days.
    過幾天你就會覺得好一點了。
  13. He'll be travelling around Japan in April.
    他四月會在日本各地旅行。
  14. I'll be working from home on Monday.
    我星期一會在家工作。
  15. Don't call at six. We'll be eating then.
    六點別打來，我們那時候在吃飯。
