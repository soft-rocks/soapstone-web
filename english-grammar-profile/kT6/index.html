I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

我可以上網查一些有用的資料。

受⁠詞接在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

我可以上網查一些有用的資料。

look up 的意思是查。

受⁠詞可⁠以是名⁠詞、動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing、that 子⁠句或 wh 子⁠句

I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

名詞

The council has called off the parade.

市議會取消了遊行。
動詞⁠-⁠ing

I keep putting off calling the dentist.

我一直拖著不打電話給牙醫。
that 子句

She pointed out that the shop was closed on Mondays.

她指出那家店星期一沒開。
wh 子句

We need to work out how much the trip will cost.

我們得算出這趟旅行要花多少錢。

that 子⁠句只⁠能放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

He pointed that the date was wrong out.

He pointed out that the date was wrong.

put off 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Don't put off to see a doctor.

Don't put off seeing a doctor.

執⁠行是 carry out，carry on 是繼⁠續

I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

The scientists carried on the test last week.

The scientists carried out the test last week.

取⁠消是 call off，被⁠動句⁠的 off 接在過⁠去⁠分⁠詞後⁠面

I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

The match was called because of the rain.

The match was called off because of the rain.

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞放⁠在中⁠間，look at 的受⁠詞只⁠能放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

I can surf the internet and look up some useful information.

代名詞

I'll look it up.

我查一下。
介系詞動詞

I looked at the information.

我看了那些資料。

例句

① look up / work out

  1. "When does the museum open?" "I don't know. I'll look up the opening times."
    博物館幾點開？不知道。我查一下開放時間。
  2. Students use the internet to look up information for their projects.
    學生用網路查專題要用的資料。
  3. We spent an hour trying to work out the answer.
    我們花了一個小時想答案。
  4. Can you work out how much we each have to pay?
    你可以算出我們每個人要付多少嗎？

② carry out / set up

  1. The hospital is carrying out tests on the new medicine.
    醫院正在對新藥做試驗。
  2. The police carried out a search of the house.
    警方搜查了那間房子。
  3. My uncle set up his own company when he was twenty-five.
    我叔叔二十五歲時開了自己的公司。
  4. "Is the projector ready?" "Not yet. I'm still setting up the computer."
    投影機好了嗎？還沒。我還在架電腦。

③ point out / bring up

  1. My teacher pointed out a few mistakes in my essay.
    老師指出了我作文裡的幾個錯誤。
  2. "The plan looks good." "Yes, but I should point out that it's very expensive."
    這個計畫看起來不錯。對，可是我得指出它很貴。
  3. At dinner, my dad brought up the subject of my exam results.
    吃晚餐時，我爸提起了我的考試成績。
  4. Don't bring up politics at the party. It always ends in an argument.
    派對上別提政治。每次都會吵起來。

④ put off / call off

  1. I had already booked my seat on the ferry, so I couldn't put off the trip.
    我已經訂了渡輪的位子，所以不能延後這趟旅行。
  2. She keeps putting off going to the doctor.
    她一直拖著不去看醫生。
  3. "Is the wedding still on?" "No, they called off the wedding last week."
    婚禮還辦嗎？沒有，他們上星期取消婚禮了。
  4. The school called off the trip because of the storm.
    學校因為暴風雨取消了校外教學。

⑤ take over / hand out

  1. A big company took over the small bakery on our street.
    一家大公司接手了我們街上那家小麵包店。
  2. When the manager left, his assistant took over the project.
    經理離開以後，他的助理接手了這個專案。
  3. The teacher handed out the exam papers.
    老師發下考卷。
  4. Volunteers were handing out free water at the marathon.
    志工在馬拉松比賽中發免費的水。

⑥ clean up / build up

  1. The concert finished very late, and after it I had to clean up the hall.
    演唱會很晚才結束，結束後我還得打掃會場。
  2. After the party, my flatmates helped me clean up the kitchen.
    派對結束後，我的室友幫我打掃廚房。
  3. "How's your leg after the accident?" "Better. I'm swimming to build up my strength."
    車禍後你的腿怎麼樣？好多了。我在游泳，把體力練回來。
  4. It takes years to build up a good business.
    經營出一個好事業要好幾年。

⑦ 受詞是 that 子句或 wh 子句

  1. During the meeting, Anna pointed out that we had no money left for advertising.
    開會時，Anna 指出我們已經沒有錢做廣告了。
  2. "Did you find the address?" "Yes, I looked up where the hotel was."
    你找到地址了嗎？找到了，我查了旅館在哪裡。
  3. Nobody could work out why the machine had stopped.
    沒有人想得出機器為什麼停了。
  4. We need to find out what time the last bus leaves.
    我們得查出末班公車幾點開。

⑧ 受詞很長

  1. The town council has set up a centre for people who have lost their jobs.
    鎮議會設立了一個中心，幫助失業的人。
  2. The company is carrying out a survey of customers who shop online.
    公司正在對網購的顧客做調查。
  3. The organisers handed out maps of the park and lists of all the events.
    主辦單位發了公園地圖和所有活動的清單。
