It must not have been easy for him to be a post-war son of a widow.

這對他來說一定不容易。

It + must not have been + 形⁠容⁠詞 + (for 人) + (to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞)

It must not have been easy for him to be a post-war son of a widow.

must not have been過去一定不是。
easy最常用的形容詞。must not have been easy 表示當時一定很辛苦。
mustn'tmust not 的縮寫。

表⁠示理⁠解和同⁠情

例句

Your dad was away at sea for months. It must not have been easy for your mother.

你爸爸出海好幾個月。這對你媽媽來說一定很不容易。

這個說法常用來理解別人過去的辛苦。

推⁠論數⁠量或時⁠間

例句

It mustn't have been more than an hour.

一定不超過一個小時。

mustn't have been more than 推論數量或時間一定沒有超過某個數字。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 been

It must not have been easy for him to be a post-war son of a widow.

My grandparents moved to Canada at sixty. That must not have be easy.

My grandparents moved to Canada at sixty. That must not have been easy.

形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 been

It must not have been easy for him to be a post-war son of a widow.

You spent your first winter in Moscow. It must not have easy.

You spent your first winter in Moscow. It must not have been easy.

must not 和 been 中⁠間要⁠有 have

It must not have been easy for him to be a post-war son of a widow.

The team lost the final in the last minute. It must not been easy for them.

The team lost the final in the last minute. It must not have been easy for them.

例句

① It must not have been easy for + 人 + to + 原形動詞

  1. My father grew up after the war with only his mother. It must not have been easy for him to be the son of a widow.
    我父親戰後跟他媽媽兩個人長大。當一個寡婦的兒子，對他來說一定很不容易。
  2. You moved to Australia at eighteen. It must not have been easy for you to leave your family.
    你十八歲搬去澳洲。離開你的家人對你來說一定很不容易。
  3. My aunt lost her job at the bank and became a nurse. It must not have been easy for her to start a new job at fifty.
    我阿姨在銀行失業後當了護理師。五十歲開始一份新工作，對她來說一定很不容易。
  4. Our new neighbours came from Syria last year. It must not have been easy for them to learn a new language.
    我們的新鄰居去年從敘利亞來。學一種新的語言對他們來說一定很不容易。
  5. The family moved house three times in two years. It must not have been easy for the children to change schools so often.
    那家人兩年搬了三次家。這麼常轉學對孩子們來說一定很不容易。
  6. Jack told his parents that he had crashed the car. It must not have been easy for him to tell the truth.
    Jack 告訴爸媽他把車撞壞了。說出真相對他來說一定很不容易。

② …, which must not have been easy for + 人

  1. My grandmother married a man with three little children, which must not have been easy for her.
    我奶奶嫁給一個有三個小孩的男人，這對她來說一定很不容易。
  2. My uncle worked two jobs to pay for college, which must not have been easy.
    我叔叔做兩份工作付大學學費，這一定很不容易。
  3. In the 1960s my grandparents lived in a tiny flat with five kids, which must not have been easy.
    一九六〇年代，我爺爺奶奶跟五個孩子住在一間很小的公寓，這一定很不容易。
  4. Our teacher looked after her sick mother for years, which must not have been easy.
    我們老師照顧生病的媽媽好幾年，這一定很不容易。
  5. At twenty, my father moved to Japan, where he knew nobody, which must not have been easy.
    我父親二十歲搬去日本，在那裡一個人都不認識，這一定很不容易。
  6. The Wilsons lost everything in the fire and had to start again, which must not have been easy.
    Wilson 一家在火災中失去了一切，必須從頭開始，這一定很不容易。

③ 主詞 + mustn't have been + more than：推論數量和時間

  1. The captain made a second announcement. It mustn't have been more than an hour after the first one.
    機長又做了一次廣播。離第一次一定不超過一個小時。
  2. My grandmother looks very young in this photo with my mum. She mustn't have been more than twenty when she had her first child.
    這張照片裡我奶奶抱著我媽媽，看起來好年輕。她生第一個孩子的時候，一定不超過二十歲。
  3. The room was almost empty when I arrived late. There mustn't have been more than ten people at the meeting.
    我遲到的時候，房間幾乎是空的。開會的人一定不超過十個。
  4. We got to the lake before lunch, and the children weren't tired. The walk mustn't have been more than two miles.
    我們午餐前就走到湖邊，孩子們也不累。那段路一定不超過兩英里。
  5. The paint on the shop sign still looks new. The shop mustn't have been open for more than a year.
    店招牌的油漆看起來還很新。那家店開業一定不超過一年。
  6. We got soaked on the way to the bus stop. The shower mustn't have been more than ten minutes long, though.
    我們走去公車站時全身濕透了。不過那陣雨一定不超過十分鐘。

④ 主詞 + must not have been + 形容詞：推論過去的情況

  1. In 1900 there were robbers on the mountain road. The road must not have been safe in those days.
    一九〇〇年那條山路上有強盜。那個年代那條路一定不安全。
  2. My grandfather lost ten kilos at the army camp. The food there must not have been very good.
    我爺爺在軍營瘦了十公斤。那裡的食物一定不怎麼好。
  3. The farmhouse had no heating and thin walls. Life on the farm must not have been comfortable in winter.
    農舍沒有暖氣，牆又薄。冬天在農場的生活一定不舒服。
  4. Many patients caught infections there in the 1800s. The old hospital must not have been very clean.
    十九世紀很多病人在那裡受到感染。那間老醫院一定不太乾淨。
  5. My parents drove across Spain in August in their old car. The journey must not have been pleasant without air conditioning.
    我爸媽八月開他們的舊車穿越西班牙。沒有冷氣，那趟旅程一定不舒服。
  6. The cottage had six rooms and only one fire. The house must not have been warm in winter.
    那間小屋有六個房間，卻只有一個火爐。那間房子冬天一定不暖和。

⑤ 主詞 + must not have + 過去分詞：推論過去沒發生

  1. I called Sam across the street. He must not have heard me, because he didn't turn round.
    我在街對面叫 Sam。他一定沒聽到我，因為他沒有轉過頭來。
  2. Everyone was at Emma's party except Lucy. She must not have known about it.
    除了 Lucy，大家都去了 Emma 的派對。她一定不知道派對的事。
  3. We wrote to the hotel a month ago, and they still haven't answered. They must not have received our letter.
    我們一個月前寫信給飯店，他們到現在還沒回。他們一定沒收到我們的信。
  4. The police have a clear video of the man's face. The thief must not have seen the camera.
    警察有那個人臉部的清楚影片。小偷一定沒看到攝影機。
  5. The car door was open when we came back. You must not have locked it.
    我們回來的時候車門是開的。你一定沒鎖車。
  6. Most of the soup was left in the bowls. The guests must not have liked it.
    碗裡的湯大部分都剩下來了。客人一定不喜歡那道湯。
  7. I woke up at ten and missed my train. I must not have set my alarm.
    我十點才醒，錯過了火車。我一定沒設鬧鐘。
