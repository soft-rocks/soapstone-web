I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

我當時想，也許有小偷闖進了我家。

could have 推⁠測過⁠去可⁠能發⁠生⁠的事

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

我當時想，也許有小偷闖進了我家。

說話者當時不知道有沒有小偷。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，或 been 加其⁠他成⁠分

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

過去分詞

He could have taken a taxi.

他可能搭了計程車。
been + 名詞

It could have been a mistake.

那可能是個誤會。
been + 動詞-⁠ing

She could have been sleeping.

她當時可能在睡覺。
been + 過去分詞
被動

The window could have been left open.

窗戶可能一直沒關。

疑⁠問⁠詞問⁠句⁠的 could 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

疑問詞 + could + 主詞 + have

Where could she have put the tickets?

她會把票放到哪裡去了？
主詞是疑問詞

Who could have done this?

這會是誰做的？

推⁠測過⁠去⁠的⁠事，could 後⁠面⁠接 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

A thief could entered the house.

A thief could have entered the house.

could 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞用原⁠形，寫 have

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

She could has forgotten.

She could have forgotten.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接過⁠去⁠式

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

Who could have took it?

Who could have taken it?

take 的過去式是 took，過去分詞是 taken。

could have 不⁠確⁠定，couldn't have 和 must have 確⁠定

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

could have
不確定

He could have taken a taxi.

他可能搭了計程車。
couldn't have
確定沒有發生

She couldn't have seen us.

她不可能看到我們。
must have
確定發生了

Someone must have opened the door.

一定有人開了門。

說⁠話者知⁠道沒⁠有發⁠生⁠時，could have 說⁠的⁠是沒⁠有發⁠生⁠的可⁠能

I thought perhaps a thief could have entered my house.

你本來可能會受傷的！

說話者知道對方沒有受傷。

例句

① could have + 過去分詞

  1. Someone could have taken your bag by mistake.
    可能有人不小心拿錯了你的包包。
  2. She could have missed the train.
    她可能沒趕上火車。
  3. The letter could have got lost in the post.
    那封信可能在郵寄途中弄丟了。
  4. He could have left the keys in the car.
    他可能把鑰匙留在車上了。
  5. They could have gone to the cinema without us.
    他們可能沒等我們就去看電影了。
  6. The cat could have come in through the window.
    貓可能是從窗戶進來的。
  7. I could have dropped my wallet on the bus.
    我可能把錢包掉在公車上了。
  8. Mia could have forgotten about our meeting.
    Mia 可能忘了我們要開會。
  9. The storm could have damaged the roof.
    暴風雨可能損壞了屋頂。
  10. A fox could have knocked over the bins.
    可能是狐狸把垃圾桶撞倒了。
  11. The kids could have eaten the last biscuits.
    孩子們可能把最後幾塊餅乾吃掉了。
  12. He could have heard the news from his sister.
    他可能是從他姊姊那裡聽到消息的。
  13. The water could have come from the flat upstairs.
    水可能是從樓上那戶流下來的。
  14. I thought perhaps someone could have followed me home.
    我當時想，可能有人跟蹤我回家。
  15. Your phone could have switched itself off.
    你的手機可能自己關機了。

② could have been + 形容詞、名詞或地點

  1. It could have been the wind.
    那可能是風聲。
  2. The man in the photo could have been her uncle.
    照片裡那個男人可能是她叔叔。
  3. She could have been ill that day.
    她那天可能生病了。
  4. They could have been at the beach when you called.
    你打電話的時候，他們可能在海邊。

③ could have been + 動詞-ing

  1. He could have been joking.
    他可能是在開玩笑。
  2. She could have been waiting at the other entrance.
    她可能一直在另一個入口等。
  3. They could have been talking about someone else.
    他們可能是在講別人。

④ could have been + 過去分詞（被動）

  1. The money could have been stolen while we were out.
    錢可能是我們出門時被偷的。
  2. The message could have been deleted by accident.
    訊息可能被不小心刪掉了。
  3. The painting could have been sold years ago.
    那幅畫可能好幾年前就被賣掉了。
  4. The match could have been cancelled because of the rain.
    比賽可能因為下雨取消了。

⑤ 疑問詞 + could have

  1. Who could have sent me these flowers?
    這些花會是誰送我的呢？
  2. Where could she have put the tickets?
    她會把票放到哪裡去了？
  3. What could have caused the accident?
    這場意外會是什麼造成的？
  4. How could the dog have got out?
    狗是怎麼跑出去的呢？
