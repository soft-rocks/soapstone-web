It's smaller than my old phone.

它比我的舊手機小。

than 後⁠面放比⁠較⁠的對⁠象

It's smaller than my old phone.

海水比游泳池的水溫暖。

than 後⁠面⁠的形⁠式

It's smaller than my old phone.

名詞片語

Paris is bigger than Rome.

巴黎比羅馬大。
受格代名詞

She is taller than him.

她比他高。
主格 + 助動詞

You are faster than I am.

你比我快。
所有格代名詞

Their kitchen is cleaner than ours.

他們的廚房比我們的乾淨。

比⁠較⁠級後⁠面⁠接 than，不⁠接 that

It's smaller than my old phone.

Paris is bigger that Rome.

Paris is bigger than Rome.

比⁠較用 than，不⁠用 then

It's smaller than my old phone.

My brother is older then me.

My brother is older than me.

then 是然後、那時候。

比⁠較⁠的東⁠西要對得上

It's smaller than my old phone.

My car is newer than Tom.

My car is newer than Tom's.

車跟車比。Tom's 是 Tom 的車。

例句

① than + 名詞片語

  1. The sea is warmer than the pool.
    海水比游泳池的水溫暖。
  2. Paris is bigger than Rome.
    巴黎比羅馬大。
  3. Mount Jade is higher than Mount Fuji.
    玉山比富士山高。
  4. Science is more interesting than history for me.
    對我來說，自然科比歷史有趣。
  5. The blue shirt is cheaper than the white one.
    藍色那件襯衫比白色那件便宜。
  6. Cats are quieter than dogs.
    貓比狗安靜。
  7. The train is faster than the bus.
    火車比公車快。
  8. A week in the mountains is better than a week at home.
    在山上待一個禮拜比在家待一個禮拜好。
  9. This year's festival was bigger than last year's festival.
    今年的節慶比去年的大。
  10. My bedroom is smaller than the living room.
    我的臥室比客廳小。

② than + 代名詞

  1. The twins are shyer than me.
    那對雙胞胎比我害羞。
  2. She is taller than him.
    她比他高。
  3. You are faster than I am.
    你比我快。
  4. Ann is stronger than he is.
    Ann 比他強壯。
  5. They are richer than us.
    他們比我們有錢。
  6. My parents are busier than I am.
    我爸媽比我忙。

③ than + 所有格代名詞

  1. Their kitchen is cleaner than ours.
    他們的廚房比我們的乾淨。
  2. Her answer was better than yours.
    她的答案比你的好。
  3. Their house is older than ours.
    他們的房子比我們的老。
  4. My car is newer than Tom's.
    我的車比 Tom 的新。
  5. Is your phone cheaper than hers?
    你的手機比她的便宜嗎？

④ 不同的時態和句型

  1. Last winter was colder than this one.
    去年冬天比今年冷。
  2. The movie will be longer than the book.
    電影會比書長。
  3. Is Tokyo more expensive than Taipei?
    東京比台北貴嗎？
  4. Nothing is faster than light.
    沒有東西比光快。
  5. The new library is more modern than the old one.
    新的圖書館比舊的現代。
  6. Breakfast here is cheaper than lunch.
    這裡的早餐比午餐便宜。
  7. Ken's cat is lazier than mine.
    Ken 的貓比我的懶。
  8. The red team was stronger than the blue team.
    紅隊比藍隊強。
  9. Was the trip more expensive than you thought?
    這趟旅行比你想的貴嗎？
