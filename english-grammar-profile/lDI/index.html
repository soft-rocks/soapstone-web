Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

你介意我再借住一次你的公寓嗎？

新⁠的主⁠詞是做動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 那⁠件⁠事⁠的人

你介意我再借住一次你的公寓嗎？

要住公寓的人是 me，不是 you。

新⁠的主⁠詞可⁠以是受格代⁠名⁠詞、名⁠詞或所⁠有⁠格

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

受格代名詞

I don't mind him coming with us.

我不介意他跟我們一起去。
名詞

I can't stand people shouting at me.

我受不了有人對我大吼。
所有格
正式

I hope you don't mind my writing to you again.

希望您不介意我再次寫信給您。

沒⁠有新⁠的主⁠詞⁠時，做⁠事⁠的人是句⁠子⁠的主⁠詞

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

沒有新的主詞

I don't mind waiting for the bus.

我不介意等公車。
有新的主詞

I don't mind you waiting in my office.

我不介意你在我辦公室等。

新⁠的主⁠詞是代⁠名⁠詞⁠時用受格

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

Would you mind I bring my dog to the picnic?

Would you mind me bringing my dog to the picnic?

新⁠的主⁠詞後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

I can't imagine my dad dance.

I can't imagine my dad dancing.

mind 後⁠面⁠的新⁠的主⁠詞不⁠接不⁠定⁠詞

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

She doesn't mind her son to play games.

She doesn't mind her son playing games.

說別⁠人做⁠的⁠事，要寫⁠出新⁠的主⁠詞

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

My boss doesn't mind leaving early on Fridays.

My boss doesn't mind me leaving early on Fridays.

想說老闆不介意我早走。

感⁠官⁠動⁠詞⁠的受⁠詞接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，說正⁠在進⁠行⁠的事

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

I saw my sister talking to the head teacher.

我看到我姊姊在跟校長說話。

want 後⁠面⁠的人接不⁠定⁠詞

Would you mind me staying in your flat again?

I want you to stay for dinner.

我要你留下來吃晚餐。

例句

① Would you mind / Do you mind + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing？

  1. I'm in Paris next week. Would you mind me sleeping on your sofa?
    我下星期在巴黎。你介意我睡你家沙發嗎？
  2. "Do you mind me opening the window? It's really hot." "No, go ahead."
    你介意我開窗嗎？好熱。不介意，開吧。
  3. Would you mind my friend joining us for dinner?
    你介意我朋友跟我們一起吃晚餐嗎？
  4. "Do you mind me asking how old you are?" "Not at all. I'm forty."
    你介意我問你幾歲嗎？不介意。我四十歲。

② mind + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. If your boss doesn't mind you leaving early, take Friday afternoon off.
    如果你老闆不介意你早走，星期五下午就休假吧。
  2. My parents don't mind me coming home late at weekends.
    我爸媽不介意我週末晚回家。
  3. I don't mind the kids watching TV after they finish their homework.
    孩子們做完功課後看電視，我不介意。
  4. Our neighbours don't seem to mind the dog barking.
    我們的鄰居好像不介意狗叫。

③ can't stand / hate / dislike + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. I couldn't be famous. I couldn't stand people taking pictures of me all day.
    我當不了名人。我受不了大家整天拍我。
  2. My dad hates people using their phones at dinner.
    我爸很討厭有人在吃飯的時候用手機。
  3. She can't stand her brother borrowing her things without asking.
    她受不了她弟弟沒問就借她的東西。
  4. Many teachers dislike students arriving late.
    很多老師不喜歡學生遲到。

④ like / love + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. "Why do you go to that café?" "I love the owner chatting with everyone."
    你為什麼去那家咖啡店？我很喜歡老闆跟每個人聊天。
  2. My grandmother likes us visiting her on Sundays.
    我奶奶喜歡我們星期天去看她。
  3. I don't like you driving so fast.
    我不喜歡你開那麼快。

⑤ imagine + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. "Mark is going to be a teacher." "Really? I can't imagine him standing in front of a class."
    Mark 要當老師了。真的嗎？我無法想像他站在全班面前。
  2. Can you imagine your parents dancing at a rock concert?
    你能想像你爸媽在搖滾演唱會上跳舞嗎？
  3. We imagined Iceland being a frozen land, but it was green in summer.
    我們以為冰島是一片冰天雪地，可是夏天的冰島是綠的。

⑥ remember / appreciate + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. I remember my grandfather telling me stories by the fire.
    我記得我爺爺在火爐旁講故事給我聽。
  2. "Do you remember us meeting for the first time?" "Of course. It was at Anna's party."
    你記得我們第一次見面嗎？當然記得。是在 Anna 的派對上。
  3. We really appreciate you coming all this way to see us.
    你大老遠來看我們，我們真的很感謝。
  4. I'd appreciate your sending the report before Friday.
    如果您能在星期五前寄報告，我會很感謝。

⑦ risk + 新的主詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. Don't leave the door open. You risk the cat getting out.
    別讓門開著。貓可能會跑出去。
  2. We can't risk the children getting lost in the crowd.
    我們不能冒險讓孩子們在人群中走失。
  3. If you park there, you risk your car being towed away.
    你把車停在那裡，可能會被拖吊。

⑧ 所有格 + 動詞-ing（正式）

  1. Thank you for your letter. I hope you will forgive my not replying sooner.
    謝謝您的來信。希望您原諒我沒有早點回信。
  2. The manager did not appreciate his leaving without notice.
    經理很不滿他沒有通知就走了。
  3. Please excuse my arriving late. The train was delayed.
    請原諒我遲到。火車誤點了。
  4. The headteacher did not like their using phones in class.
    校長不喜歡他們在課堂上用手機。

⑨ 新的主詞是名詞片語

  1. I can't stand the people upstairs playing loud music at night.
    我受不了樓上的人晚上放很大聲的音樂。
  2. Do you remember the old man next door singing every morning?
    你記得隔壁那位老先生每天早上唱歌嗎？
  3. My mum doesn't mind my friends staying over at weekends.
    我媽不介意我朋友週末來家裡過夜。
