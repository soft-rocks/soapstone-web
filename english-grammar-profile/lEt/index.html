My friend is a nurse.

我朋友是護士。

主⁠詞放⁠在最⁠前⁠面，後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞

My friend is a nurse.

我在夜校讀日文。

he、she、it 的一⁠般動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠s

My friend is a nurse.

I / you / we / they

My aunt and uncle are nurses. They work in a hospital.

我阿姨和姨丈是護理師。他們在醫院工作。
he / she / it

My brother is never home on weekends. He plays football every Saturday.

我哥哥週末從來不在家。他每個星期六踢足球。

主⁠詞和名⁠詞中⁠間要⁠有 be

My friend is a nurse.

Our new neighbor a famous chef.

Our new neighbor is a famous chef.

主⁠詞是 she，動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠s

My friend is a nurse.

She live in Taipei.

She lives in Taipei.

職⁠業⁠的單⁠數名⁠詞前⁠面加 a 或 an

My friend is a nurse.

Her uncle is architect in Tainan.

Her uncle is an architect in Tainan.

例句

① 主詞 + be + 名詞

  1. The woman in the red coat is our new manager.
    穿紅外套的那位女士是我們的新經理。
  2. That's Ken in the garden next door. Ken is my neighbor.
    隔壁花園裡那個是 Ken。Ken 是我的鄰居。
  3. We have rice fields in the country. Both of my grandparents are farmers.
    我們在鄉下有稻田。我的爺爺奶奶都是農夫。
  4. Mr Lee retired in June. Mrs. Wang is our new English teacher.
    李老師六月退休了。王老師是我們的新英文老師。
  5. Can I borrow books from this library? I'm a student at the university.
    我可以向這間圖書館借書嗎？我是那所大學的學生。
  6. My mother works at the hospital. She is a doctor.
    我媽媽在醫院工作。她是醫生。

② 主詞 + be + 形容詞 / 地方

  1. My cousin is very funny, but sometimes he is a little loud.
    我表哥很好笑，但有時候有點吵。
  2. We eat out a lot. The noodle shop on our street is very cheap.
    我們常常在外面吃。我們這條街上的麵店很便宜。
  3. My uncle is flying to Tokyo today. He is at the airport now.
    我叔叔今天要飛去東京。他現在在機場。
  4. Let's go to the beach. The sky is clear and blue this morning.
    我們去海邊吧。今天早上天空又藍又晴朗。
  5. I always drink coffee at two. I'm sleepy after lunch every day.
    我總是兩點喝咖啡。我每天吃完午餐都很睏。
  6. Mum, where are the car keys? The keys are on the table.
    媽，車鑰匙在哪裡？鑰匙在桌上。

③ 主詞 + 一般動詞

  1. My sister isn't at home this year. She studies law in Taichung.
    我姊姊今年不在家。她在台中讀法律。
  2. I ride my bike to my best friend's house. She lives near the river.
    我騎腳踏車去我最好的朋友家。她住在河邊。
  3. My brother is never home on weekends. He plays football every Saturday.
    我哥哥週末從來不在家。他每個星期六踢足球。
  4. Our flat is never quiet. We have two cats.
    我們的公寓從來不安靜。我們養了兩隻貓。
  5. My aunt and uncle are nurses. They work in a hospital.
    我阿姨和姨丈是護理師。他們在醫院工作。
  6. We never eat out on weekdays. My mother cooks dinner every night.
    我們平日從來不在外面吃。我媽媽每天晚上煮晚餐。

④ 主詞 + be + 動詞-ing

  1. My parents are working in Dubai. I'm living with my aunt this year.
    我爸媽在杜拜工作。我今年住在我阿姨家。
  2. Don't call Lucy tonight. She's studying for a test.
    今天晚上別打給露西。她在準備考試。
  3. Water came through the ceiling last night. My dad is fixing the roof today.
    昨天晚上水從天花板滴下來。我爸爸今天在修屋頂。
  4. It's a sunny Sunday. Dad is washing the car in front of the house.
    今天是晴朗的星期天。爸爸正在家門口洗車。

⑤ 兩個子句和不同的句型

  1. Go to the night market for souvenirs. The market is very cheap and you can find everything there.
    去夜市買紀念品吧。那個市場很便宜，什麼都買得到。
  2. I've worked at the bakery for a year. I like my job and my boss is nice.
    我在麵包店工作一年了。我喜歡我的工作，我的老闆人也很好。
  3. Only three thousand people live here. My town is small, but it has a big park.
    這裡只住了三千人。我住的小鎮很小，但是有一座大公園。
  4. I'm at work all day. You can call me after six.
    我整天都在上班。你可以在六點以後打給我。
  5. My school starts at nine. I leave home at half past eight.
    我的學校九點上課。我八點半出門。
  6. We don't need the lights on in the day. Our classroom has big windows.
    我們白天不用開燈。我們的教室有大窗戶。
  7. Ken's mother is French, and his father is Japanese. Ken speaks four languages.
    Ken 的媽媽是法國人，爸爸是日本人。Ken 會說四種語言。
  8. We can't go in yet. The museum opens at nine.
    我們還不能進去。博物館九點開門。
