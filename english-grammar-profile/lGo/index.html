I could have listened more to my team members.

我本來可以多聽聽團隊成員的意見的。

主⁠詞是說⁠話者自⁠己，could have 表⁠示後⁠悔

I could have listened more to my team members.

我本來可以多聽聽團隊成員的意見的。

說話者當時做得到，卻沒有多聽別人的意見。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，或 been 加形⁠容⁠詞

I could have listened more to my team members.

過去分詞

I could have stayed in touch with him.

我本來可以跟他保持聯絡的。
been + 形容詞

I could have been more patient.

我本來可以更有耐心一點的。

後⁠面⁠接 but，說⁠出當⁠時沒⁠做⁠的原⁠因

I could have listened more to my team members.

我本來可以問的，可是我太害羞了。

當⁠時想⁠做卻做不⁠到，用 wish 或 If only 加 could have

I could have listened more to my team members.

I wish I could have
想做，卻做不到

I wish I could have come to your party.

真希望我當時能去你的派對。
If only I could have

If only I could have spoken to him.

要是我當時能跟他說上話就好了。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，不⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I could have listened more to my team members.

I could have listen more.

I could have listened more.

wish 後⁠面用 could，不⁠用 can

I could have listened more to my team members.

I wish I can have stayed.

I wish I could have stayed.

can 後面不接 have 和過去分詞。

說過⁠去⁠的⁠事，wish I could 後⁠面⁠接 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I could have listened more to my team members.

I wish I could stayed longer.

I wish I could have stayed longer.

I wish I could stay longer 也對，說的是現在的希望。

should have 是該⁠做⁠的⁠事，could have 是更⁠好⁠的選⁠擇

I could have listened more to my team members.

主⁠詞換⁠成別⁠人，could have 就⁠是批⁠評

I could have listened more to my team members.

你本來可以跟我說的。

少⁠了 have，說⁠的就⁠是現⁠在或未⁠來⁠的事

I could have listened more to my team members.

我明天可以幫你。

說話者在提議。

例句

① I / we + could have + 過去分詞

  1. I could have revised more before the exam.
    考試前我本來可以多複習一點的。
  2. I could have called my grandmother more often.
    我本來可以多打電話給我奶奶的。
  3. We could have left home a bit earlier.
    我們本來可以早一點出門的。
  4. I could have asked for help sooner.
    我本來可以早點找人幫忙的。
  5. I could have taken that job in Tokyo.
    我本來可以接下東京那份工作的。
  6. We could have saved more money when we were younger.
    我們年輕的時候本來可以多存一點錢的。
  7. I could have told her how I felt.
    我本來可以告訴她我的感受的。
  8. I could have spent more time with my kids.
    我本來可以多陪陪孩子的。
  9. We could have booked a cheaper flight.
    我們本來可以訂便宜一點的機票。
  10. I could have checked the weather before we set off.
    出發前我本來可以先看一下天氣的。
  11. I could have rehearsed my speech one more time.
    我本來可以把演講再排練一次的。
  12. Looking back, I could have handled the argument better.
    回頭想想，那次爭吵我本來可以處理得更好。
  13. We could have bought that house for half the price ten years ago.
    十年前我們本來可以用一半的價錢買下那間房子。
  14. I could have bought the tickets when they were still cheap.
    票還便宜的時候，我本來可以先買的。
  15. I could have written to him while he was in hospital.
    他住院的時候，我本來可以寫信給他的。
  16. I could have listened to my parents.
    我本來可以聽我爸媽的話的。
  17. We could have taken the train instead of driving.
    我們本來可以搭火車，不用開車的。

② could have + 過去分詞, but ...

  1. I could have said no, but I didn't want to upset him.
    我本來可以拒絕的，可是我不想讓他不高興。
  2. I could have gone to university, but I started working at eighteen.
    我本來可以上大學的，可是我十八歲就開始工作了。
  3. We could have stayed another night, but we were in a hurry to get home.
    我們本來可以多住一晚，可是我們急著回家。

③ I / we + could have been + 形容詞或名詞

  1. I could have been kinder to my brother when we were kids.
    我們小時候，我本來可以對我弟好一點。
  2. I could have been a better friend to her.
    我本來可以當她更好的朋友。
  3. We could have been more patient with each other.
    我們本來可以對彼此多一點耐心。
  4. I could have been less offhand with the new intern.
    我本來可以對那個新來的實習生別那麼隨便。

④ I wish I could have + 過去分詞

  1. I wish I could have stayed longer.
    真希望我當時能待久一點。
  2. I wish I could have been there for your birthday.
    真希望你生日那天我能在場。
  3. We wish we could have helped more.
    真希望我們當時能多幫一點忙。
  4. I wish I could have met your father.
    真希望我有機會見到你爸爸。

⑤ If only I could have + 過去分詞

  1. If only I could have seen her one more time.
    要是我能再見她一面就好了。
  2. If only we could have stopped him.
    要是我們當時攔得住他就好了。
