It was a beautiful red dress with blue flowers on the back.

那是一件很漂亮的紅色洋裝，背面有藍色的花。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語放⁠在名⁠詞後⁠面，修⁠飾它前⁠面⁠的名⁠詞

那是一件很漂亮的紅色洋裝，背面有藍色的花。

不⁠同⁠的介⁠系⁠詞說⁠明特⁠徵、主⁠題、位⁠置或來⁠源

It was a beautiful red dress with blue flowers on the back.

with
特徵a girl with long hair
about
主題a film about Bavarian pupils
on
位置the shop on the corner
from
來源a letter from my aunt

動⁠詞跟⁠著主⁠要名⁠詞，不⁠跟⁠著介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語⁠裡⁠的名⁠詞

那箱老照片在閣樓。

介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語不⁠放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

It was a beautiful red dress with blue flowers on the back.

I met a with long hair girl.

I met a girl with long hair.

主⁠要名⁠詞是 key，動⁠詞用單⁠數

The key to the doors are on the table.

The key to the doors is on the table.

說⁠明東⁠西有什⁠麼特⁠徵，用 with

It was a beautiful red dress with blue flowers on the back.

I want a room of a sea view.

I want a room with a sea view.

例句

① 名詞 + with + 名詞片語

  1. She wore a beautiful red dress with blue flowers on the back.
    她穿了一件很漂亮的紅色洋裝，背面有藍色的花。
  2. Who's the man with the black hat?
    那個戴黑帽子的男人是誰？
  3. We'd like a room with a sea view, please.
    我們想要一間看得到海的房間。
  4. I bought a phone with a really good camera.
    我買了一支相機很好的手機。
  5. They live in a house with a big garden.
    他們住在一棟有大院子的房子裡。

② 名詞 + about + 名詞片語

  1. A TV company came to our school to make a film about Bavarian pupils.
    一家電視公司來我們學校拍一部關於巴伐利亞學生的影片。
  2. I'm reading a book about the history of Rome.
    我在看一本關於羅馬歷史的書。
  3. There's a programme about sharks on tonight.
    今晚有一個關於鯊魚的節目。
  4. She wrote a song about her grandmother.
    她寫了一首關於她奶奶的歌。

③ 名詞 + of + 名詞片語

  1. Can I see that picture of your family?
    我可以看看你那張全家福嗎？
  2. I'd like a cup of tea and a piece of cake.
    我想要一杯茶和一塊蛋糕。
  3. The end of the film was really sad.
    那部電影的結局好悲傷。
  4. We need a map of the city.
    我們需要一張這座城市的地圖。

④ 名詞 + in / on / at / across + 名詞片語

  1. The house across the road has been for sale for a year.
    對面那棟房子已經賣了一年了。
  2. The shop on the corner sells fresh bread.
    轉角那家店有賣新鮮麵包。
  3. The people in the flat upstairs are very noisy.
    樓上公寓的人很吵。
  4. Do you know the woman at the door?
    你認識門口那位女士嗎？
  5. The books on the top shelf are mine.
    最上層架子的書是我的。

⑤ 名詞 + from / for / to + 名詞片語

  1. There's a postcard from Grandma on the kitchen table for you.
    廚房桌上有一張奶奶寄給你的明信片。
  2. I bought a present for your mum. Do you think she'll like it?
    我買了一份禮物要送你媽媽，你覺得她會喜歡嗎？
  3. The train to London leaves at six.
    往倫敦的火車六點出發。
  4. Is there a bus from the airport to the city centre?
    有從機場到市中心的公車嗎？

⑥ 介系詞片語一個接一個

  1. I'm looking for a jacket with a hood for the winter.
    我在找一件冬天穿、有帽子的外套。
  2. There's a photo of my parents on their wedding day.
    有一張我爸媽結婚那天的照片。
  3. Can you get a box for the books on the floor?
    你可以拿一個箱子來裝地上的書嗎？

⑦ 名詞 + 介系詞片語當主詞

  1. The box of old photos is in the attic.
    那箱老照片在閣樓。
  2. The price of the tickets has gone up again.
    票價又漲了。
  3. The girl with the red bike lives next door.
    騎紅色腳踏車的那個女孩住在隔壁。
  4. A friend from school is coming to stay.
    一個學校的朋友要來住幾天。

⑧ 名詞 + 介系詞片語當受詞

  1. I lost the key to my bike.
    我把腳踏車的鑰匙弄丟了。
  2. Can you buy a bottle of milk on your way home?
    你回家的路上可以買一瓶牛奶嗎？
