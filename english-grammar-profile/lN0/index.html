例句

① not being + 過去分詞 + enough

  1. The new store has very few customers. In my opinion, the store is not being promoted enough.
    新店的客人很少。我覺得這家店的宣傳做得不夠。
  2. Our local library is closing on Mondays. It isn't being funded enough by the council.
    我們的地方圖書館星期一不開了。市議會給的經費不夠。
  3. Our team keeps losing because the young players aren't being trained enough.
    我們隊一直輸，因為年輕球員的訓練不夠。
  4. The museum is empty most days. It isn't being advertised enough.
    博物館大部分時間都沒人。它的廣告做得不夠。
  5. Science isn't being taught enough in primary schools.
    小學的自然課教得不夠。

② not being + 過去分詞 + properly

  1. Mobile phones are useful, but as long as they're not being used in a sensible way, they can harm children's health.
    手機很有用，可是只要沒被合理地使用，就會傷害孩子的健康。
  2. The old castle is falling down. It isn't being looked after properly.
    那座古堡快倒了。它沒有被好好維護。
  3. I'm worried about the new workers. They aren't being trained properly.
    我很擔心新來的員工。他們沒有受到好好的訓練。
  4. The recycling isn't being sorted properly, so most of it goes to waste.
    回收的東西沒有好好分類，所以大部分都浪費掉了。
  5. Customers' complaints aren't being handled properly. Nobody calls them back.
    客人的抱怨沒有被好好處理。沒有人回電給他們。

③ not being taken seriously

  1. Air pollution in our city is getting worse, but the problem isn't being taken seriously.
    我們城市的空氣汙染愈來愈嚴重，可是這個問題沒有被認真看待。
  2. I keep telling the doctor about my headaches, but I feel I'm not being taken seriously.
    我一直跟醫生說我頭痛，可是我覺得他沒把我的話當真。
  3. Young people's ideas aren't being taken seriously by the government.
    政府沒有認真看待年輕人的想法。
  4. Safety rules on building sites still aren't being taken seriously enough.
    工地的安全規定還是沒有被足夠重視。

④ not being seen as

  1. Things have changed. Women are not being seen as inferior to men and unable to work outside the home.
    情況已經變了。女性不再被看成比男性差、不能出外工作的人。
  2. These days, cooking isn't being seen as a woman's job any more.
    現在煮飯已經不再被看成女人的工作了。
  3. Video games aren't being seen only as a hobby for children now.
    電子遊戲現在不再只被看成小孩的嗜好。
  4. Working from home isn't being seen as strange any more.
    在家工作不再被看成奇怪的事了。

⑤ 報告和正式意見

  1. According to the survey, our website isn't being visited by young customers.
    根據調查，年輕的顧客沒有在逛我們的網站。
  2. The report shows that the new rules aren't being followed in most offices.
    報告顯示，大部分辦公室都沒有遵守新規定。
  3. Many talented students are not being given the chance to go to university.
    很多有才華的學生沒有得到上大學的機會。
  4. It is clear that the money is not being spent where it is needed most.
    很明顯，錢沒有花在最需要的地方。
  5. In many schools, students' mental health is not being discussed openly.
    在很多學校，學生的心理健康沒有被公開討論。

⑥ 不同的主詞

  1. I'm not being listened to at meetings, so I've stopped giving ideas.
    開會時沒人聽我說話，所以我不再提意見了。
  2. You aren't being paid enough for all the work you do.
    以你做的工作量來說，你拿的錢不夠。
  3. The children aren't being given enough time to play outside.
    孩子們沒有足夠的時間在外面玩。
  4. Our neighbourhood isn't being cleaned as often as it should be.
    我們社區打掃的次數沒有應有的那麼多。
  5. Older people aren't being shown how to use the new machines at the bank.
    沒有人教老人家怎麼用銀行的新機器。

⑦ 討論時

  1. "Why are sales so low?" "I think the product isn't being marketed to the right people."
    銷售量怎麼這麼低？我覺得產品沒有賣給對的客群。
  2. "Is the river any cleaner now?" "No, the factories still aren't being checked."
    河現在有比較乾淨嗎？沒有，工廠還是沒人檢查。
  3. "Is the new speed limit working?" "No. It isn't being enforced by the police at all."
    新的速限有用嗎？沒有。警察根本沒在執行。
