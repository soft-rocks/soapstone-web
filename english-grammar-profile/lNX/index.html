Suddenly, the window opened by itself and the room filled with cold air.

突然間，窗戶自己打開了，房間裡灌滿了冷空氣。

by itself 說東⁠西自⁠己動⁠起⁠來

突然間，窗戶自己打開了，房間裡灌滿了冷空氣。

on its own 意思一樣，可以互換。

by itself 說自⁠己動⁠起⁠來、單⁠獨，或不⁠需⁠要幫⁠忙

Suddenly, the window opened by itself and the room filled with cold air.

自己動起來

The heating comes on by itself at six every morning.

暖氣每天早上六點會自動打開。
單獨

Don't leave the puppy by itself all day.

別讓小狗整天自己待著。
不需要幫忙

A small cut like this will heal by itself.

像這樣的小傷口會自己好。

by 後⁠面用反⁠身代⁠名⁠詞 itself

Suddenly, the window opened by itself and the room filled with cold air.

The door opened by it.

The door opened by itself.

複⁠數名⁠詞用 by themselves

Suddenly, the window opened by itself and the room filled with cold air.

The windows opened by itself.

The windows opened by themselves.

指人用 himself 或 herself

My son walked to school by itself.

My son walked to school by himself.

說⁠法是 by itself 或 on its own

Suddenly, the window opened by itself and the room filled with cold air.

The machine works by its own.

The machine works by itself.

例句

① 自己動起來

  1. My laptop keeps turning off by itself.
    我的筆電一直自己關機。
  2. Did the alarm go off by itself, or did someone press it?
    警報器是自己響的，還是有人按了？
  3. The radio started playing by itself in the middle of the night.
    半夜收音機自己開始播放。
  4. The car rolled down the hill by itself because I forgot the handbrake.
    因為我忘了拉手煞車，車子自己滑下坡了。
  5. The app updates by itself, so you don't need to do anything.
    這個應用程式會自己更新，你不用做任何事。
  6. The kettle switches off by itself when the water boils.
    水燒開時，熱水壺會自己關掉。
  7. A book fell off the shelf by itself. How strange!
    一本書自己從架子上掉下來，好奇怪！
  8. The garage door opened by itself while we were on holiday.
    我們去度假的時候，車庫門自己打開了。
  9. Does the robot vacuum clean the whole flat by itself?
    掃地機器人會自己把整間公寓打掃乾淨嗎？
  10. The bread machine mixes and bakes the dough by itself.
    麵包機會自己揉麵和烤麵包。

② 單獨

  1. A rabbit kept by itself will get lonely.
    單獨養的兔子會寂寞。
  2. The house stands by itself at the end of the road.
    那棟房子孤零零地立在路的盡頭。
  3. This word can't be used by itself; it always needs a noun after it.
    這個字不能單獨用，後面一定要接名詞。
  4. One small tree grows by itself in the middle of the car park.
    一棵小樹獨自長在停車場中間。
  5. Our cat prefers to eat by itself in the kitchen.
    我們的貓喜歡自己在廚房吃飯。
  6. Is it safe to leave the bike by itself outside the station?
    把腳踏車單獨放在車站外面安全嗎？
  7. This sauce is too strong to eat by itself, so serve it with rice.
    這個醬單吃太重口味了，要配飯吃。
  8. The village sits by itself in the valley, miles from the nearest town.
    那個村子孤立在山谷裡，離最近的小鎮好幾英里。
  9. One photo by itself doesn't prove anything.
    單單一張照片證明不了什麼。
  10. The old horse spent the winter by itself in the field.
    那匹老馬獨自在田裡過冬。
  11. A single candle by itself gave enough light to read.
    光是一根蠟燭就有足夠的光線看書。

③ 不需要幫忙

  1. The problem won't go away by itself; we need to talk about it.
    問題不會自己消失，我們得談一談。
  2. The baby bird learned to fly by itself.
    那隻雛鳥自己學會了飛。
  3. The plant grew back by itself after the winter.
    冬天過後，那株植物自己又長回來了。
  4. Your cold should clear up by itself in a few days.
    你的感冒過幾天應該會自己好。
  5. The traffic jam cleared by itself after an hour.
    塞車一個小時後自己就紓解了。
  6. The business won't grow by itself. You need to advertise.
    生意不會自己變好，你得打廣告。
  7. Will this stain come out by itself in the wash?
    這塊污漬洗一洗會自己掉嗎？
  8. My hair usually dries by itself in about an hour.
    我的頭髮通常大概一個小時就會自己乾。
  9. The bruise faded by itself within a week.
    瘀青一個禮拜內就自己消了。
  10. Don't worry about the noise; the engine settles down by itself once it warms up.
    別擔心那個聲音，引擎熱了之後就會自己安靜下來。
