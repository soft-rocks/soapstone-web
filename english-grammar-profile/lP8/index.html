Music was her life, not only a hobby.

音樂是她的生命，不只是嗜好。

說一⁠般⁠的音⁠樂，不⁠加 the

音樂是她的生命，不只是嗜好。

特⁠定⁠的事⁠物才加 the

Music was her life, not only a hobby.

一般

Education is important.

教育很重要。
特定

The education in this country is free.

這個國家的教育是免費的。

抽⁠象不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞說藝⁠術、社⁠會、感⁠覺和價⁠值

Music was her life, not only a hobby.

藝術和媒體music, art, television, literature
社會education, health, crime, pollution
感覺和價值love, happiness, freedom, success

說一⁠般⁠的快⁠樂，不⁠加 the

Music was her life, not only a hobby.

The happiness is more important than money.

Happiness is more important than money.

art 是⁠不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞，不⁠加 a

She has a talent for an art.

She has a talent for art.

in this river 說⁠明是哪⁠裡⁠的汙⁠染，前⁠面加 the

Pollution in this river is terrible.

The pollution in this river is terrible.

例句

① 藝術和媒體

  1. She played the piano every day. Music was her life, not only a hobby.
    她每天彈鋼琴。音樂是她的生命，不只是嗜好。
  2. Television is more attractive because it has moving images.
    電視比較吸引人，因為它有動態影像。
  3. Art helps children express their feelings.
    藝術幫助孩子表達感受。
  4. My son finds literature boring, but he loves science.
    我兒子覺得文學很無聊，可是他很愛科學。

② 社會

  1. Education is important to prevent you from being a dog on a leash!
    教育很重要，可以讓你不必像被拴著的狗一樣！
  2. Crime has fallen in our area this year.
    我們這一區今年的犯罪率下降了。
  3. Unemployment is a big problem for young people.
    失業對年輕人來說是個大問題。
  4. Pollution is getting worse in big cities.
    大城市的汙染越來越嚴重。
  5. Doctors say that sleep is as important as food.
    醫生說睡眠跟食物一樣重要。

③ 感覺和價值

  1. My grandparents say love kept them together for sixty years.
    我爺爺奶奶說，是愛讓他們在一起六十年。
  2. Happiness doesn't come from expensive things.
    快樂不是來自昂貴的東西。
  3. Young people want freedom.
    年輕人想要自由。
  4. Success takes hard work and a bit of luck.
    成功需要努力和一點運氣。
  5. Come on, everyone. Time is money, and the client is waiting.
    大家快點，時間就是金錢，客戶在等了。

④ 抽象不可數名詞當受詞

  1. I love music from the sixties.
    我很愛六〇年代的音樂。
  2. She's studying art at university.
    她在大學讀藝術。
  3. We need peace, not war.
    我們需要和平，不要戰爭。
  4. Children need love and attention.
    孩子需要愛和關心。

⑤ 抽象不可數名詞在介系詞後面

  1. He's interested in politics.
    他對政治有興趣。
  2. She wrote a book about friendship.
    她寫了一本關於友誼的書。
  3. They fought for freedom.
    他們為自由而戰。
  4. My parents taught me about respect.
    我爸媽教我要懂得尊重。

⑥ 不加 the 和加 the 比較

  1. Coffee is my favourite drink in the morning.
    咖啡是我早上最愛的飲料。
  2. The coffee in this café is too strong for me.
    這家咖啡店的咖啡對我來說太濃了。
  3. Life in a big city is exciting.
    大城市的生活很刺激。
  4. The life of a farmer is hard.
    農夫的生活很辛苦。
  5. Food is expensive these days.
    最近食物很貴。
  6. The food in this restaurant is excellent.
    這家餐廳的食物很棒。

⑦ 抽象不可數名詞 + 形容詞

  1. Modern art is hard to understand.
    現代藝術很難懂。
  2. Classical music helps me study.
    古典音樂幫助我讀書。
  3. Good health is the best gift.
    健康是最好的禮物。
