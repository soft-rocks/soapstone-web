例句

① Not having been + 過去分詞

  1. Not having been given precise orders, he decided to work on his own initiative.
    因為沒有收到明確的指示，他決定自己看著辦。
  2. Not having been told about the change, I went to the wrong room.
    因為沒人告訴我改了，我走錯了教室。
  3. Not having been invited, she stayed at home that evening.
    她沒被邀請，所以那天晚上待在家裡。
  4. Not having been trained for the job, he made a lot of mistakes at first.
    他沒受過這份工作的訓練，所以一開始犯了很多錯。
  5. Not having been warned about the weather, the climbers were caught in the snow.
    登山客沒有收到天氣警告，結果被困在雪裡。

② Having been + 過去分詞，放在句首

  1. Having been taught the rights and the wrongs, we had an idea about everything.
    我們被教過什麼是對、什麼是錯，所以對每件事都有自己的看法。
  2. Having been chosen as captain, Tom felt he had to work harder.
    被選為隊長之後，Tom 覺得他得更努力。
  3. Having been delayed by the snow, the train arrived two hours late.
    火車因為下雪延誤，晚了兩個小時才到。
  4. Having been brought up by her grandparents, she is very close to them.
    她是爺爺奶奶帶大的，所以跟他們很親。
  5. Having been stopped by the police twice, I now drive very carefully.
    被警察攔下來兩次之後，我現在開車很小心。
  6. Having been left empty for years, the house needed a lot of repairs.
    這間房子空了好幾年，所以需要大量修繕。

③ having been + 過去分詞，放在主詞後面

  1. I myself, having been educated the hard way, would insist on my children getting the best possible education.
    我自己是吃過苦才受到教育的，所以我會堅持讓孩子得到最好的教育。
  2. The old bridge, having been damaged in the flood, was closed for a year.
    舊橋在水災中受損，封了一年。
  3. My grandfather, having been born in a small village, never liked big cities.
    我爺爺在小村子出生，從來不喜歡大城市。
  4. The letter, having been sent to the wrong address, arrived a week late.
    這封信寄到錯的地址，所以晚了一個星期才到。

④ 說明經驗和教育

  1. Having been given a second chance, she worked harder than anyone else.
    她得到第二次機會之後，比任何人都努力。
  2. Having been treated badly by his last boss, he didn't trust his new manager.
    他被前任老闆虧待過，所以不信任新經理。
  3. Having been raised on a farm, Ben knew how to look after animals.
    Ben 在農場長大，所以知道怎麼照顧動物。
  4. Having been trained in Paris, the chef cooks excellent French food.
    這位主廚在巴黎受訓過，所以做得一手好法國菜。

⑤ 說明原因

  1. Having been asked to speak at short notice, I hadn't prepared anything.
    我是臨時被叫上台講話的，所以什麼都沒準備。
  2. Having been accused of cheating, the player left the club.
    那個球員被指控作弊，所以離開了球隊。
  3. Having been promised a pay rise, the workers were angry when it didn't come.
    工人們被承諾會加薪，所以加薪沒來時他們很生氣。
  4. Having been rejected by three universities, she decided to take a year off.
    她被三所大學拒絕之後，決定休息一年。

⑥ 小說和故事

  1. Having been locked in the room all night, the prisoner was tired and hungry.
    犯人被鎖在房間裡一整晚，又累又餓。
  2. Having been left a fortune by his uncle, he gave up his job.
    他叔叔留給他一大筆遺產，所以他辭掉了工作。
  3. Having been seen near the house, the man was questioned by the police.
    那個男人被人看到在房子附近，所以被警察盤問。
  4. Having been bitten once, the boy was afraid of dogs for years.
    那個男孩被咬過一次，好幾年都很怕狗。

⑦ 不同的主詞

  1. Having been cleaned and painted, the room looked like new.
    房間打掃和油漆過之後，看起來像新的一樣。
  2. Having been fed, the baby went straight to sleep.
    寶寶喝完奶之後，馬上就睡著了。
  3. Not having been paid for two months, the staff went on strike.
    員工兩個月沒拿到薪水，所以罷工了。
  4. Having been checked by two teachers, the exam papers were sent back to the students.
    考卷經過兩位老師檢查之後，發還給學生。
