Will you send it for me?

你可以幫我把它寄出去嗎？

Will you + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 ...?

Will you、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Will you send it for me?

Will you請對方做某件事。主詞是 you。
原形動詞說話者請對方做的事。

回⁠答

例句

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Will you send it for me?

請⁠求用疑⁠問⁠句，will 放⁠在 you 前⁠面

例句

① Will you + 原形動詞 ...?

  1. Will you post this parcel for me?
    你可以幫我寄這個包裹嗎？
  2. Will you come to my house tomorrow at six?
    你明天六點可以來我家嗎？
  3. Will you pass me the salt?
    你可以把鹽遞給我嗎？
  4. Will you help me carry this box?
    你可以幫我搬這個箱子嗎？
  5. Will you turn the TV down?
    你可以把電視轉小聲嗎？
  6. Will you look after my cat this weekend?
    這個週末你可以幫我照顧貓嗎？
  7. Will you call me when you get there?
    你到了以後可以打給我嗎？
  8. Will you buy some milk on your way home?
    你回家路上可以買點牛奶嗎？
  9. Will you lend me your pen?
    你可以借我你的筆嗎？
  10. Will you check my homework?
    你可以幫我檢查功課嗎？
  11. Will you tell Tom I'm sorry?
    你可以跟 Tom 說我很抱歉嗎？
  12. Will you pick me up from the airport on Friday?
    你星期五可以來機場接我嗎？
  13. Will you water the plants while I'm away?
    我不在的時候，你可以幫我澆花嗎？
  14. Will you take a photo of us?
    你可以幫我們拍張照嗎？
  15. Will you keep this a secret?
    你可以保守這個祕密嗎？

② Will you + 原形動詞 ..., please?

  1. Will you hang your coat in the hall, please?
    可以請你把外套掛在玄關嗎？
  2. Will you sign the card for Grandma, please?
    可以請你在給奶奶的卡片上簽名嗎？
  3. Will you wait a moment, please?
    可以請你稍等一下嗎？
  4. Will you keep the noise down after ten, please?
    十點以後可以請你小聲一點嗎？
  5. Will you sit down, please?
    可以請你坐下嗎？
  6. Will you tidy your room before the guests come, please?
    客人來之前，可以請你整理房間嗎？
  7. Will you read the first question, please?
    可以請你唸第一題嗎？
  8. Will you show me your ticket, please?
    可以請你出示車票嗎？
  9. Will you write your address on the back of the envelope, please?
    可以請你把地址寫在信封背面嗎？
  10. Will you switch off your phones, please?
    可以請你們把手機關機嗎？
  11. Will you open your books at page ten, please?
    可以請你們把書翻到第十頁嗎？
  12. Will you send me the file, please?
    可以請你把檔案寄給我嗎？
  13. Will you put your bags under the seat, please?
    可以請你把包包放在座位底下嗎？
  14. Will you speak a bit louder, please?
    可以請你說大聲一點嗎？
  15. Will you wash your hands before dinner, please?
    晚餐前可以請你去洗手嗎？
  16. Will you leave the key at reception, please?
    可以請你把鑰匙留在櫃檯嗎？
