例句

① a bit + 形容詞-er

  1. This jacket is a bit cheaper than that one.
    這件外套比那件便宜一點。
  2. He's a bit shorter than his brother.
    他比他哥哥矮一點。
  3. The second lesson was a bit longer.
    第二堂課長一點。
  4. Is the blue bag a bit lighter?
    藍色那個包包有比較輕一點嗎？
  5. The water was a bit colder than yesterday.
    水比昨天冷一點。
  6. My new office is a bit quieter.
    我的新辦公室安靜一點。
  7. Their garden is a bit smaller than ours.
    他們的花園比我們的小一點。

② a little bit + 比較級

  1. My friend is a little bit younger than me.
    我朋友比我小一點。
  2. The soup is a little bit saltier today.
    今天的湯鹹一點。
  3. The music is a little bit louder in this room.
    這個房間的音樂大聲一點。
  4. My hair is a little bit darker now.
    我的頭髮現在深一點了。
  5. The new chairs are a little bit higher.
    新的椅子高一點。
  6. The test was a little bit easier than last time.
    這次考試比上次簡單一點。

③ a bit + more + 形容詞

  1. The hotel was a bit more expensive than we planned.
    旅館比我們預計的貴一點。
  2. Could you be a bit more careful with the plates?
    你拿盤子可以小心一點嗎？
  3. The second movie was a bit more exciting.
    第二部電影刺激一點。
  4. Ken is a little bit more reticent than his sister.
    Ken 比他姊姊話少一點。
  5. This road is a bit more dangerous at night.
    這條路晚上危險一點。

④ a little + 比較級

  1. The second room was a little larger.
    第二個房間大一點。
  2. Prices are a little higher this year.
    今年的價格高一點。
  3. The new design is a little simpler.
    新的設計簡單一點。
  4. The river was a little deeper than we thought.
    河比我們想的深一點。
  5. Our flight was a little later than planned.
    我們的班機比原本晚一點。

⑤ look / feel / get 後面

  1. I feel a bit better after some sleep.
    睡了一下之後，我覺得好一點了。
  2. You look a bit thinner than last year.
    你看起來比去年瘦一點。
  3. It's getting a bit warmer now.
    現在變得暖和一點了。
  4. Ann looks a little bit taller in those shoes.
    Ann 穿那雙鞋看起來高一點。
  5. The baby feels a bit hotter than usual.
    寶寶摸起來比平常燙一點。
  6. The roads got a bit busier after eight.
    八點以後路上車多了一點。
  7. My English is getting a little better.
    我的英文進步了一點。
