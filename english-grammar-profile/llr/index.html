On the one hand, going to a large school is better for making friends, but on the other hand, I think you can learn better in a small school.

一方面，念大型學校比較容易交到朋友；但另一方面，我覺得在小型學校可以學得更好。

前一⁠句說一⁠件⁠事，言談標⁠記引⁠出和它對⁠比⁠的事

這間公寓很新潮。不過，它離車站很遠。

言談標記放在句首時，後面加逗號。

on the one hand 和 on the other hand 比⁠較一⁠件⁠事⁠的兩⁠面

On the one hand, going to a large school is better for making friends, but on the other hand, I think you can learn better in a small school.

一方面，這份工作薪水很好；但另一方面，工時很長。

兩⁠個看⁠法也⁠可⁠以分⁠成兩⁠個句⁠子

兩個句子

On the one hand, the city has great museums. On the other hand, it's very expensive.

一方面，這座城市有很棒的博物館。另一方面，它的消費很高。
單獨用

I'd like to go to the party. On the other hand, I'm really tired.

我想去派對。可是話說回來，我真的很累。

on the other hand 前面不一定有 on the one hand。

however 可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首、句中或句⁠尾

句首

The test was difficult. However, most students passed.

考試很難。不過，大部分學生都過了。
主詞後面

My brother loves sport. My sister, however, prefers reading.

我哥哥很愛運動。不過我姊姊比較喜歡看書。
句尾

The hotel was lovely. The food was disappointing, however.

飯店很棒。不過，餐點令人失望。

放在句中時，前後加逗號。放在句尾時，前面加逗號。

in contrast 和 by contrast 比⁠較兩⁠個不⁠同⁠的⁠人、東⁠西或⁠時⁠期

in contrast

The north of the country is dry. In contrast, the south gets a lot of rain.

這個國家的北部很乾。相較之下，南部雨很多。
by contrast

Tom is very outgoing. By contrast, his brother is quite reserved.

Tom 很外向。相較之下，他弟弟相當拘謹。

這兩個說法多用在正式的書面文字，像報告和論說文。

on the contrary 表⁠示前一⁠句說⁠的不⁠對，事⁠實正⁠好相⁠反

否定句後面

The film wasn't boring. On the contrary, it was fascinating.

那部電影不無聊。正好相反，它很精彩。
反駁對方

"You must be tired." "On the contrary, I feel great."

「你一定累了。」「才不呢，我覺得精神很好。」

原句：比⁠較大⁠型學⁠校和小⁠型學⁠校，各說一⁠個好⁠處

On the one hand, going to a large school is better for making friends, but on the other hand, I think you can learn better in a small school.

一方面，念大型學校比較容易交到朋友；但另一方面，我覺得在小型學校可以學得更好。

固⁠定說⁠法是 on the other hand，介⁠系⁠詞用 on

On the one hand, going to a large school is better for making friends, but on the other hand, I think you can learn better in a small school.

The car is cheap. In the other hand, it uses a lot of fuel.

The car is cheap. On the other hand, it uses a lot of fuel.

比⁠較兩⁠個不⁠同⁠的⁠人，不⁠用 on the contrary

My old teacher was strict. On the contrary, my new teacher is very relaxed.

My old teacher was strict. In contrast, my new teacher is very relaxed.

however 是副⁠詞，兩⁠個子⁠句之⁠間用句⁠號或分號

The flat is nice, however it's too small for us.

The flat is nice. However, it's too small for us.

用逗號時，改用連接詞 but：The flat is nice, but it's too small for us.

in contrast to 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞片⁠語⁠時是介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

in contrast to
名詞片語

In contrast to her sister, Anna is very quiet.

和她姊姊相比，Anna 很安靜。
in contrast,
言談標記

Summers here are hot. In contrast, the winters are mild.

這裡的夏天很熱。相較之下，冬天很溫和。

however 後⁠面直⁠接接形⁠容⁠詞或副⁠詞⁠時，意⁠思是不⁠管多⁠麼

however hard
不管多麼

However hard he practises, he can't sing the high notes.

不管他怎麼練習，就是唱不上高音。
However,
言談標記

We waited for an hour. However, nobody came.

我們等了一個小時。不過，沒有人來。

例句

① on the one hand 和 on the other hand

  1. On the one hand, I'd like a bigger house, but on the other hand, I don't want a longer commute.
    一方面，我想要大一點的房子；但另一方面，我不想通勤更久。
  2. On the one hand, social media keeps us in touch. On the other hand, it wastes a lot of time.
    一方面，社群媒體讓我們保持聯絡。另一方面，它很浪費時間。
  3. On the one hand, the course was useful, but on the other hand, it was far too short.
    一方面，這門課很有用；但另一方面，它實在太短了。
  4. On the one hand, living alone gives you freedom. On the other hand, it can be lonely.
    一方面，一個人住很自由。另一方面，可能會很寂寞。

② on the other hand 單獨用

  1. The salary is a pittance. On the other hand, the work is interesting.
    薪水少得可憐。可是話說回來，工作很有意思。
  2. Trains are faster. On the other hand, buses are much cheaper.
    火車比較快。可是另一方面，公車便宜多了。
  3. He can be impulsive. On the other hand, he's always honest with us.
    他有時候很衝動。可是另一方面，他對我們一向很誠實。
  4. I could take the job in London. On the other hand, I could stay here and finish my degree.
    我可以接倫敦的工作。不過話說回來，我也可以留在這裡念完學位。

③ however 放在句首

  1. The plan sounds good. However, it isn't relevant to our problem.
    這個計畫聽起來不錯。不過，它跟我們的問題無關。
  2. We waited for an hour. However, nobody came.
    我們等了一個小時。不過，沒有人來。
  3. Most owls are nocturnal. However, some hunt during the day.
    大部分的貓頭鷹在夜間活動。不過，有些在白天獵食。
  4. I'd love to help. However, I'm busy all weekend.
    我很想幫忙。不過，我整個週末都很忙。

④ however 放在句中或句尾

  1. My parents liked the film. My friends, however, thought it was crummy.
    我爸媽喜歡那部電影。不過我朋友覺得它很爛。
  2. The first chapter is slow. The rest of the book, however, is excellent.
    第一章節奏很慢。不過這本書其他的部分很精彩。
  3. She agreed to come. She didn't stay long, however.
    她答應來了。不過她沒待多久。
  4. The room was small. It was very clean, however.
    房間很小。不過很乾淨。

⑤ in contrast 和 by contrast

  1. Summers here are hot. In contrast, the winters are mild.
    這裡的夏天很熱。相較之下，冬天很溫和。
  2. The old phone was heavy. In contrast, the new model weighs only 150 grams.
    舊手機很重。相較之下，新機型只有一百五十公克。
  3. Sales rose in Asia. By contrast, they fell sharply in Europe.
    亞洲的銷售額上升了。相較之下，歐洲的銷售額大幅下滑。
  4. My father is an inveterate talker. My mother, by contrast, rarely says a word.
    我爸爸是個改不了的話匣子。相較之下，我媽媽很少說話。

⑥ on the contrary

  1. The exam wasn't easy. On the contrary, it was the hardest one this year.
    這次考試不簡單。正好相反，它是今年最難的一次。
  2. She isn't covetous. On the contrary, she gives away most of her money.
    她不貪心。正好相反，她把大部分的錢都送人了。
  3. "Did you hate the trip?" "On the contrary, we had a wonderful time."
    你們不喜歡那趟旅行嗎？才不呢，我們玩得很開心。
  4. "I suppose the news upset you." "On the contrary, I was jubilant."
    我想這個消息讓你很難過吧。才不呢，我高興得不得了。

⑦ 對話

  1. "Should we buy the bigger car?" "On the one hand, it's safer. On the other hand, it's hard to park."
    我們要買大一點的車嗎？一方面，它比較安全。另一方面，它很難停車。
  2. "Is the new manager strict?" "On the contrary, she's very easy-going."
    新經理很嚴格嗎？才不呢，她很隨和。
  3. "Was the hotel nice?" "Yes. The beach was quite dirty, however."
    飯店好嗎？好。不過海灘蠻髒的。
  4. "Shall we eat out tonight?" "I'd love to. On the other hand, we've spent a lot this week."
    我們今晚要出去吃嗎？我很想。不過話說回來，我們這星期花了不少錢。

⑧ 論說文和報告

  1. Online courses are flexible. However, students often miss face-to-face contact.
    線上課程很有彈性。不過，學生常常想念面對面的互動。
  2. On the one hand, tourism brings money to the region. On the other hand, it damages the environment.
    一方面，觀光替這個地區帶來收入。另一方面，它破壞環境。
  3. In 2010, most people read the news in print. In contrast, most people today read it online.
    二〇一〇年，大部分的人看紙本新聞。相較之下，現在大部分的人在網路上看新聞。
  4. Private schools have small classes. By contrast, many state schools have over thirty students per class.
    私立學校的班級人數少。相較之下，很多公立學校一班超過三十個學生。
