After a few minutes the door opened and the man who had been following me came in.

過了幾分鐘，門開了，一直跟蹤我的那個男人走了進來。

名⁠詞 + who / which / that + had been + ⁠-⁠ing

who、which / that、had been + ⁠-⁠ing

who說人。
which / that說東西。
had been + -⁠ing在主要的事發生之前，一直在做的事。

介⁠紹故⁠事⁠的人⁠物

例句

There were two men who had been chasing the thief.

有兩個男人一直在追那個小偷。

關係子句常用來介紹故事裡的人物。

說歷⁠史人⁠物

例句

The guide told us about an old lord who had been living there in the 18th century.

導遊跟我們說了一個 18 世紀住在那裡的老貴族的故事。

導覽和介紹常用這個句型說以前住在某地的人。

been 前⁠面要⁠有 had

The man who had been following me came in.

After a few minutes the door opened and the man who had been following me came in.

After a few minutes the door opened and the man who had been following me came in.

The dog that had been bark all night was quiet.

The dog that had been barking all night was quiet.

例句

① 名詞 + who had been + -ing：故事裡的人物

  1. I hid in a café. After a few minutes the door opened, and the man who had been following me came in.
    我躲進一家咖啡館。幾分鐘後門開了，一直跟著我的那個男人走了進來。
  2. There were two men who had been chasing the thief through the market.
    有兩個男人一直在市場裡追那個小偷。
  3. The girl who had been crying in the corner suddenly smiled.
    一直在角落哭的那個女孩突然笑了。
  4. The boy who had been sitting next to me on the bus got off at the station.
    公車上一直坐在我旁邊的那個男孩在車站下車了。
  5. The woman who had been watching us from the window came out.
    一直在窗邊看著我們的那個女人走了出來。
  6. The children who had been playing in the snow came in for hot chocolate.
    一直在雪地裡玩的孩子們進來喝熱可可。

② 名詞 + who had been + -ing + 時間：歷史和導覽

  1. The guide told funny stories about an old lord who had been living in the castle in the 18th century.
    導遊講了幾個有趣的故事，關於一個 18 世紀住在城堡裡的老貴族。
  2. We met an old fisherman who had been working on the boats for sixty years.
    我們遇到一個在船上工作了六十年的老漁夫。
  3. The museum told the story of a family who had been making glass for five generations.
    博物館講了一個五代都在做玻璃的家族的故事。
  4. I spoke to a nurse who had been looking after patients there since the war.
    我跟一位護理師談話，她從戰爭時期就一直在那裡照顧病人。
  5. We visited a farmer who had been growing grapes on the hill all his life.
    我們拜訪了一位一輩子都在山坡上種葡萄的農夫。
  6. The book is about a teacher who had been teaching in the same village for forty years.
    這本書講的是一位在同一個村子教了四十年書的老師。

③ 名詞 + that / which had been + -ing：說東西

  1. The tap that had been dripping all night finally stopped.
    那個整晚都在滴水的水龍頭終於停了。
  2. The car which had been making a strange noise broke down on the motorway.
    那台一直發出奇怪聲音的車在高速公路上拋錨了。
  3. The rain that had been falling all day turned to snow.
    下了一整天的雨變成了雪。
  4. The dog that had been barking all night was finally quiet.
    那隻整晚都在叫的狗終於安靜了。
  5. The machine which had been working perfectly suddenly stopped.
    那台一直運作得很好的機器突然停了。
  6. The clock that had been ticking on the wall stopped at midnight.
    牆上一直滴答響的時鐘在半夜停了。

④ 主詞 + 過去式 + 名詞 + who had been + -ing：認識或遇到

  1. At the party I met a woman who had been living in Japan for ten years.
    在派對上我遇到一個在日本住了十年的女人。
  2. The police found the man who had been stealing bikes from the station.
    警察找到了那個一直在車站偷腳踏車的男人。
  3. I finally spoke to the neighbour who had been playing the piano every night.
    我終於跟那個每天晚上都在彈鋼琴的鄰居說話了。
  4. The school thanked the parents who had been helping in the library.
    學校謝謝那些一直在圖書館幫忙的家長。
  5. She married the man who had been writing her letters for years.
    她嫁給了那個寫信給她寫了好幾年的男人。
  6. We thanked the volunteers who had been cleaning the beach all morning.
    我們謝謝那些整個早上都在清理海灘的志工。

⑤ The + 名詞 + (that) + 主詞 + had been + -ing + 過去式：受詞的關係子句

  1. The book that I had been reading all week was finally finished.
    我讀了一整個星期的那本書終於讀完了。
  2. The house that they had been building for years was ready at last.
    他們蓋了好幾年的房子終於完工了。
  3. The job that she had been hoping for went to someone else.
    她一直希望得到的那份工作給了別人。
  4. The letter that I had been waiting for arrived on Monday.
    我一直在等的那封信星期一到了。
  5. The concert that we had been looking forward to was cancelled.
    我們一直很期待的那場演唱會取消了。（被動）
  6. The money that he had been saving went on a new car.
    他一直在存的錢花在一台新車上了。
  7. The puppy that the children had been asking for finally arrived.
    孩子們一直吵著要的那隻小狗終於來了。
