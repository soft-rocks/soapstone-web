例句

① as + 過去分詞 + above、earlier、previously

  1. As mentioned above, the hotel has free parking for guests.
    如上所述，這家飯店替住客提供免費停車。
  2. As mentioned earlier, the meeting room has moved to the third floor.
    如前所述，會議室已經搬到三樓。
  3. As stated above, members must pay the club fee by the end of March.
    如上所述，會員必須在三月底前繳清社費。
  4. As noted earlier, the new software will save each team about two hours a week.
    如前所述，新軟體每週能替每個團隊省下大約兩小時。
  5. As discussed above, the main problem with the old bus route is the traffic in the city centre.
    如上所討論，舊公車路線的主要問題是市中心的交通。
  6. As explained earlier, our refund policy does not cover sale items.
    如前所說明，我們的退款規定不包括特價商品。
  7. As mentioned previously, the library will close for repairs in August.
    如先前所提，圖書館八月會關閉整修。
  8. As shown above, most students prefer to study in the evening.
    如上所示，大部分學生比較喜歡晚上讀書。
  9. As stated previously, the company does not share customer details with anyone.
    如先前所述，本公司不會把顧客資料提供給任何人。
  10. As noted above, the figures in this report are not final yet.
    如上所述，這份報告裡的數字還不是最終的。

② as + 過去分詞 + below、later

  1. As shown below, the number of visitors doubled between 2019 and 2023.
    如下所示，參觀人數在 2019 到 2023 年之間增加了一倍。
  2. As explained below, you can pay online or at the front desk.
    如下所說明，您可以線上付款，也可以在櫃檯付款。
  3. As described below, each room has a small kitchen and a balcony.
    如下所述，每個房間都有一個小廚房和一個陽台。
  4. As discussed later in this report, the café could open on Sundays too.
    如本報告後面所討論，咖啡廳星期天也可以營業。
  5. As explained later in this guide, you can change your password at any time.
    如本指南後面所說明，您隨時可以更改密碼。

③ as + 過去分詞 + in + 文件的部分

  1. As explained in Section 3, the survey took place over two weeks.
    如第三節所說明，這份調查進行了兩週。
  2. As shown in the table, women spent more on books than men did.
    如表格所示，女性花在書上的錢比男性多。
  3. As noted in my last email, I can only start work in September.
    如我上一封信所提，我九月才能開始上班。
  4. As mentioned in the introduction, this essay compares life in the city and in the countryside.
    如導言所提，這篇文章比較城市和鄉下的生活。
  5. As stated in the contract, you must clean the flat before you move out.
    如合約所載明，你搬走之前必須把公寓打掃乾淨。

④ as follows 和 the following

  1. The opening hours are as follows: Monday to Friday, 9 to 6, and Saturday, 10 to 4.
    營業時間如下：星期一到星期五九點到六點，星期六十點到四點。
  2. My reasons for leaving the team are as follows: I have less free time, and I want to focus on my exams.
    我退出團隊的理由如下：我的空閒時間變少了，而且我想專心準備考試。
  3. The prizes for the competition are as follows: first prize, a bike; second prize, a camera.
    比賽的獎品如下：第一名是一台腳踏車，第二名是一台相機。
  4. Please answer the following questions before the interview.
    面試前請回答以下問題。
  5. The following students have not handed in their homework yet.
    以下這幾位同學還沒交作業。
  6. The following is a list of the books you will need for the course.
    以下是這門課需要的書單。
  7. The following is a summary of what we agreed with the landlord yesterday.
    以下是我們昨天跟房東談定的事項摘要。

⑤ see above 和 see below

  1. For the full price list, see below.
    完整的價目表請見下方。
  2. The training is on 12 June (see above for the address).
    訓練在六月十二日（地址請見上方）。
  3. Parking is free for guests, but the car park closes at midnight (see below).
    住客停車免費，但停車場半夜十二點關閉（請見下方）。

⑥ the above 和 the points above

  1. If you have any questions about the above, please call me.
    如果您對以上內容有任何疑問，請打電話給我。
  2. I hope the above explains why we cancelled the trip.
    希望以上內容說明了我們為什麼取消這趟旅行。
  3. Taking the points above into account, I recommend the smaller hotel.
    考慮到以上幾點，我推薦那家比較小的飯店。
  4. Please check the dates above before you book your flight.
    訂機票之前，請先確認上面的日期。
  5. We will discuss all of the above at the next staff meeting.
    以上所有內容，我們會在下次員工會議上討論。
