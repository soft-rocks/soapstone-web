例句

① 主詞 + may be + 動詞-ing

  1. Your boss may be calling at ten asking for that report.
    你老闆可能會在十點打電話來要那份報告。
  2. I may be working late tonight, so don't wait for me.
    我今晚可能要加班，所以別等我。
  3. The new products may be coming out in the spring.
    新產品可能會在春天推出。
  4. They may be selling the house next year.
    他們明年可能會賣掉房子。
  5. We may be getting a new manager soon.
    我們可能很快就會有新經理。
  6. Ann may be staying with us for a few days.
    Ann 可能會在我們家住幾天。
  7. The prices may be going up again in July.
    價格七月可能又要漲了。
  8. I may be travelling a lot for work next year.
    我明年可能會常常出差。
  9. You may be hearing from our lawyer.
    你可能會收到我們律師的消息。
  10. The band may be playing some new songs tonight.
    樂團今晚可能會唱一些新歌。
  11. She may be studying abroad next autumn.
    她明年秋天可能會出國念書。
  12. I may not be coming to class tomorrow.
    我明天可能不會來上課。
  13. The students may not be taking the exam this year.
    學生今年可能不會參加考試。

② 主詞 + might be + 動詞-ing

  1. We might be moving to Canada next year.
    我們明年可能會搬到加拿大。
  2. Tom might be joining us for dinner.
    Tom 也許會來跟我們一起吃晚餐。
  3. I might be changing jobs soon.
    我可能很快就要換工作了。
  4. They might be closing the factory at the end of the year.
    他們年底可能會關閉工廠。
  5. It might be raining when we get there.
    我們到那裡的時候可能正在下雨。
  6. She might be giving a talk at the conference.
    她可能會在研討會上演講。
  7. The kids might be sleeping when you arrive.
    你到的時候孩子們可能已經在睡了。
  8. He might be writing a book about his travels.
    他可能會寫一本關於他旅行的書。
  9. We might be needing some extra help on Saturday.
    我們星期六可能需要多一點人手。
  10. The team might be playing without their captain.
    球隊可能要在沒有隊長的情況下比賽。
  11. I might be seeing her at the weekend.
    我週末可能會見到她。
  12. The workers might be striking again next month.
    工人下個月可能又要罷工了。
  13. You might be waiting a long time for a bus at this hour.
    這個時間你可能要等公車等很久。
  14. Some people might be feeling nervous before the interview.
    有些人在面試前可能會覺得緊張。
  15. I might not be using the car tomorrow.
    我明天可能不會用到車。
  16. They might not be coming back until Sunday.
    他們可能要到星期天才會回來。
  17. The museum might not be opening on time.
    博物館可能不會準時開門。
  18. He might not be playing in the final.
    他可能不會上場打決賽。
