I do miss you.

我真的很想你。

do 放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面，表⁠示真⁠的、確⁠實

I do miss you.

我們真的很喜歡住在海邊。

he、she、it 用 does，過⁠去用 did

I do miss you.

does

Who made this? This soup does taste good.

這是誰做的？這湯真的很好喝。
did

I did call you, but you didn't answer.

我真的有打給你，可是你沒接。

用⁠來回⁠應別⁠人⁠的懷⁠疑

I do miss you.

A: You didn't study. B: I did study, but the test was hard.

A：你沒讀書。B：我真的有讀，只是考試很難。

does 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I do miss you.

She does likes chocolate.

She does like chocolate.

說過⁠去⁠的事用 did

I do miss you.

You do say that yesterday.

You did say that yesterday.

主⁠詞是 he，用 does

I do miss you.

He do work hard.

He does work hard.

例句

① do + 原形動詞

  1. My new school is fine, but I do miss my old school.
    我的新學校還不錯，可是我真的很想念我以前的學校。
  2. We stopped in front of a picture of the sea at the gallery. I do love the colors in this painting.
    我們在美術館一幅海的畫前面停下來。我真的很喜歡這幅畫的顏色。
  3. With three kids, we do need a bigger car.
    有三個孩子，我們真的需要一輛大一點的車。
  4. Yes, I do have a dog. He's at my parents' house.
    對，我確實有養狗，牠在我爸媽家。
  5. The new cleaners start at six every morning. They do work hard, and they never complain.
    新的清潔人員每天早上六點就開始工作。他們真的很努力，而且從不抱怨。
  6. I do hope the rain stops before the wedding.
    我真的希望婚禮前雨會停。

② does + 原形動詞

  1. She does like chocolate, but she's on a diet.
    她確實喜歡巧克力，只是在節食。
  2. Our new waiter is slow, but he does try his best.
    我們的新服務生動作很慢，不過他真的很盡力。
  3. Maybe we should go home. The sky over the hills does look dark.
    也許我們應該回家了。山那邊的天空看起來真的很暗。
  4. Mum says he's lazy, but my brother does help at home sometimes.
    媽媽說他很懶，可是我弟弟有時候確實會在家幫忙。
  5. Who made this? This soup does taste good.
    這是誰做的？這湯真的很好喝。

③ did + 原形動詞

  1. Don't say you're busy on Saturday. You did promise to help me move.
    別說你星期六很忙。你確實答應過要幫我搬家。
  2. I did call you, but you didn't answer.
    我真的有打給你，可是你沒接。
  3. Thanks for dinner. We did enjoy the fish.
    謝謝你的晚餐，我們真的很喜歡那道魚。
  4. Grandpa did take his pills this morning. I watched him.
    爺爺今天早上確實吃了藥，我親眼看到的。
  5. I missed Tom's party last week. He did tell me, but I forgot.
    我錯過了湯姆上星期的派對。他確實告訴過我，可是我忘了。

④ 回應別人的懷疑

  1. A: You never cook. B: I do cook! I made pasta last night.
    A：你從來不煮飯。B：我有煮啊！我昨晚才煮了義大利麵。
  2. A: Did you lock the door? B: Yes, I did lock it.
    A：你有鎖門嗎？B：有，我真的鎖了。
  3. A: Ken never reads. B: He does read. He reads comics.
    A：Ken 從來不看書。B：他有看啊，他看漫畫。
  4. A: You didn't study. B: I did study, but the test was hard.
    A：你沒讀書。B：我真的有讀，只是考試很難。

⑤ 客氣地道歉、感謝和不同的句型

  1. Thank you for your email of 3 May. I do apologize for the late reply.
    謝謝你五月三日的來信。我真的很抱歉這麼晚才回覆。
  2. Grandma's funeral was very sad. We do appreciate all the flowers you sent.
    奶奶的葬禮很令人難過。我們真的很感謝你送來的花。
  3. I lost my job last year too. I do understand how you feel.
    我去年也丟了工作。我真的懂你的感受。
  4. Let's eat out on Friday. I do want to try that new Thai place.
    我們星期五出去吃吧。我真的很想去試那家新的泰國餐廳。
  5. Your sister is still upset about the party. I do think you should talk to her.
    你姊姊還在為派對的事難過。我真的覺得你應該跟她談談。
  6. Listen to my cousin in the choir. She does sing beautifully.
    你聽合唱團裡我表妹的聲音。她唱歌真的很好聽。
  7. Everyone said our idea was crazy. The plan did work in the end.
    大家都說我們的想法很瘋狂。那個計畫最後確實成功了。
  8. That man waved at me in the shop. I do remember his face, but not his name.
    那個男人在店裡跟我揮手。我確實記得他的臉，可是不記得他的名字。
  9. The final ended two to one. Our team did win the cup, but only just.
    決賽以二比一結束。我們的隊伍確實贏了獎盃，只是險勝。
  10. You do look pale today. Did you skip breakfast?
    你今天看起來臉色真的很蒼白，你沒吃早餐嗎？
