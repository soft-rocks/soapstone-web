We are not any happier than you were.

我們一點也沒有比你們當時快樂。

not any 和 no 放⁠在比⁠較⁠級前⁠面，表⁠示差⁠距是零

We are not any happier than you were.

not any（口語）

The new phone isn't any faster than my old one.

新手機一點也沒有比我的舊手機快。
no（正式）

Taking a taxi at rush hour is no faster than walking.

尖峰時間搭計程車沒有比走路快。

no + 比⁠較⁠級 + than 表⁠示最⁠多只⁠有

We are not any happier than you were.

水最多只到我的膝蓋。

no better 說情⁠況沒⁠有改⁠善

We are not any happier than you were.

My cold is no better today.

我的感冒今天沒有好一點。

no 前⁠面不⁠再⁠加 not

We are not any happier than you were.

The bus is not no cheaper than the train.

The bus is no cheaper than the train.

any 後⁠面要⁠用比⁠較⁠級

We are not any happier than you were.

The new office isn't any big than the old one.

The new office isn't any bigger than the old one.

這⁠裡⁠的 any 要和 not 一⁠起用

We are not any happier than you were.

My coffee is any warmer now.

My coffee isn't any warmer now.

例句

① be not any + 比較級

  1. The new phone isn't any faster than my old one.
    新手機一點也沒有比我的舊手機快。
  2. The kids aren't any happier with the new teacher.
    孩子們換了新老師，一點也沒有比較開心。
  3. The hotel by the beach isn't any cheaper.
    海邊那家旅館一點也沒有比較便宜。
  4. The second test wasn't any easier than the first.
    第二次考試一點也沒有比第一次簡單。
  5. My brother isn't any taller than me now.
    我弟弟現在一點也沒有比我高。
  6. These shoes aren't any more comfortable than my old ones.
    這雙鞋一點也沒有比我的舊鞋舒服。
  7. The traffic wasn't any lighter on Sunday.
    星期天交通一點也沒有比較順。

② be no + 比較級

  1. Taking a taxi at rush hour is no faster than walking.
    尖峰時間搭計程車沒有比走路快。
  2. The bus is no cheaper than the train.
    公車沒有比火車便宜。
  3. His second book is no better than his first.
    他的第二本書沒有比第一本好。
  4. Living in the city is no more expensive than living here.
    住在城裡沒有比住在這裡貴。
  5. The new road is no safer than the old one.
    新的路沒有比舊的安全。
  6. Our house is no bigger than theirs.
    我們的房子沒有比他們的大。

③ no + 比較級 + than 表示最多只有

  1. The water was no higher than my knees.
    水最多只到我的膝蓋。
  2. The room is no bigger than a cupboard.
    那個房間差不多只有櫥櫃那麼大。
  3. The kitten was no bigger than my hand.
    那隻小貓頂多只有我的手那麼大。
  4. The path was no wider than a bicycle.
    那條小路頂多只有一台腳踏車寬。
  5. The shop is no further than the end of this street.
    那家店最遠就在這條街的盡頭。
  6. The scratch is no longer than a centimeter.
    這道刮痕頂多一公分長。

④ no better / not any better

  1. My cold is no better today.
    我的感冒今天沒有好一點。
  2. The weather isn't any better in the north.
    北部的天氣一點也沒有比較好。
  3. His English is no worse than mine.
    他的英文沒有比我的差。
  4. The food here isn't any worse than at home.
    這裡的食物一點也沒有比家裡的差。
  5. The pain isn't any better after the medicine.
    吃了藥以後，痛一點也沒有好。

⑤ 不同的時態和句型

  1. The prices won't be any lower next month.
    下個月價格一點也不會比較低。
  2. The second movie was no more exciting than the first.
    第二部電影沒有比第一部刺激。
  3. Her new job isn't any more interesting.
    她的新工作一點也沒有比較有趣。
  4. The streets were no quieter at midnight.
    到了半夜，街上也沒有比較安靜。
  5. I'm not any older than you, so don't tell me what to do.
    我又沒有比你大，別管我。
  6. The trip home was no shorter than the trip there.
    回家的路沒有比去的路短。
