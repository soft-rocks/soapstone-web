Yesterday was the most exciting day of my life!

昨天是我這輩子最興奮的一天！

最⁠高⁠級 + 名⁠詞後⁠面⁠接更⁠多種介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

Yesterday was the most exciting day of my life!

in our lives

Exercise is the most important thing in our lives.

運動是我們生活中最重要的事。
for + 對象

Sleep is the best medicine for a cold.

睡覺是治感冒最好的藥。
on / at + 地方

The fish is the most expensive dish on the menu.

魚是菜單上最貴的菜。

one of the + 最⁠高⁠級 + 複⁠數名⁠詞

Yesterday was the most exciting day of my life!

威尼斯是歐洲最美的城市之一。

one of the 後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞用複⁠數

Yesterday was the most exciting day of my life!

It's one of the best restaurant in town.

It's one of the best restaurants in town.

主⁠詞是 one，動⁠詞用單⁠數

Yesterday was the most exciting day of my life!

One of the oldest buildings are the library.

One of the oldest buildings is the library.

the most 前⁠面要加 the

Yesterday was the most exciting day of my life!

Getting the job was most exciting news of the year.

Getting the job was the most exciting news of the year.

例句

① the + 最高級 + 名詞 + of my life / of the year

  1. Graduation day was the proudest moment of my parents' lives.
    畢業典禮那天是我爸媽這輩子最驕傲的時刻。
  2. Leaving home was the hardest decision of my life.
    離家是我這輩子最難的決定。
  3. It was the coldest winter of the century.
    那是這個世紀最冷的冬天。
  4. Seeing my son walk for the first time made it the best day of my year.
    看到我兒子第一次走路，那天成了我今年最棒的一天。
  5. The typhoon last week brought the heaviest rain of the year.
    上禮拜的颱風帶來今年最大的雨。

② the + 最高級 + 名詞 + in our lives / in history

  1. Exercise is the most important thing in our lives.
    運動是我們生活中最重要的事。
  2. It was the worst earthquake in the country's history.
    那是這個國家史上最嚴重的地震。
  3. Family is the most precious thing in my life.
    家人是我生命中最珍貴的。
  4. She was the youngest winner in the history of the prize.
    她是這個獎項史上最年輕的得主。
  5. Some people say Bologna has the oldest university in the world.
    有人說波隆那有世界上最古老的大學。

③ the + 最高級 + 名詞 + for / on / at

  1. Sleep is the best medicine for a cold.
    睡覺是治感冒最好的藥。
  2. Vegetables are the healthiest food for children.
    蔬菜是對小孩最健康的食物。
  3. The fish is the most expensive dish on the menu.
    魚是菜單上最貴的菜。
  4. She was the most beautiful woman at the party.
    她是派對上最美的女人。
  5. This is the best seat on the plane.
    這是飛機上最好的位子。
  6. He was the oldest man at the meeting.
    他是會議上年紀最大的人。

④ one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞

  1. Venice is one of the most beautiful cities in Europe.
    威尼斯是歐洲最美的城市之一。
  2. He's one of the best players on the team.
    他是隊上最好的球員之一。
  3. It's one of the most popular shows on TV.
    那是電視上最受歡迎的節目之一。
  4. This is one of the oldest buildings in Taipei.
    這是台北最古老的建築之一。
  5. She's one of the kindest people I know.
    她是我認識最好心的人之一。
  6. Rice is one of the most important foods in Asia.
    米是亞洲最重要的食物之一。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. What's the most interesting place in your city?
    你們城市裡最有趣的地方是哪裡？
  2. Who was the best teacher at your school?
    你學校裡最好的老師是誰？
  3. What's the most useful app on your phone?
    你手機上最有用的 app 是什麼？
  4. One of the oldest trees in the park fell down last night.
    公園裡最老的樹之一昨晚倒了。
  5. Is this the cheapest flight on the website?
    這是網站上最便宜的機票嗎？
  6. What was the best thing about your holiday in Japan?
    你在日本的假期中最棒的是什麼？
  7. Is Mount Everest the highest mountain on Earth?
    聖母峰是地球上最高的山嗎？
  8. We stood in the longest line at the airport for two hours.
    我們在機場最長的隊伍裡站了兩個小時。
