We must insist that the agreement is binding and that the contract shall be fulfilled.

我們必須堅持這份協議具有約束力，而且合約必須履行。

主⁠詞 + shall + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

shall、shall be + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

shall必須、應當。用在法律和正式的規定。
shall be + 過去分詞被動，說事情必須被怎麼處理。

insist that … shall

We must insist that the agreement is binding and that the contract shall be fulfilled.

例句

We insist that the money shall be returned.

我們堅持這筆錢必須退還。

insist that 後面的子句常用 shall 加強語氣。

shall be 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

We must insist that the agreement is binding and that the contract shall be fulfilled.

The contract shall be fulfil.

The contract shall be fulfilled.

shall 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We must insist that the agreement is binding and that the contract shall be fulfilled.

The buyer shall to pay within thirty days.

The buyer shall pay within thirty days.

shall 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

We must insist that the agreement is binding and that the contract shall be fulfilled.

The company shalls provide training.

The company shall provide training.

例句

① insist that + 主詞 + shall be + 過去分詞：律師信

  1. The factory delivered only half the machines. We therefore need to insist that the agreement is binding and that the contract shall be fulfilled.
    工廠只交了一半的機器。因此我們必須堅持，這份協議有約束力，合約必須履行。（被動）
  2. Your company still owes us twelve thousand pounds. We insist that the full amount shall be paid by the end of the month.
    貴公司還欠我們一萬兩千英鎊。我們堅持全額必須在月底前付清。（被動）
  3. Half of the chairs arrived broken. Our client insists that the damaged goods shall be replaced.
    有一半的椅子送到時是壞的。我們的客戶堅持損壞的貨物必須更換。（被動）
  4. The new school must open in September. We must insist that the work shall be completed by June.
    新學校九月一定要開學。我們必須堅持工程要在六月前完成。（被動）
  5. Your lease ends next week. The owners insist that the building shall be returned in its original condition.
    你的租約下星期到期。屋主堅持建築物必須恢復原狀交還。（被動）
  6. We never agreed to join your mailing list. We insist that our names shall be removed from it.
    我們從來沒有同意加入你們的郵寄名單。我們堅持我們的名字必須從名單上移除。（被動）

② 主詞 + shall + 原形動詞：合約的條款

  1. Clause two of the lease says: "The tenant shall pay the rent on the first day of each month."
    租約第二條寫著：承租人應於每月一日支付租金。
  2. The sales contract is clear: "The seller shall deliver the goods within fourteen days."
    買賣合約寫得很清楚：賣方應於十四天內交貨。
  3. My new job contract says: "The employee shall work thirty-seven hours a week."
    我的新工作合約寫著：員工每週應工作三十七個小時。
  4. Under our rental agreement, the landlord shall repair any damage to the roof.
    根據我們的租賃協議，房東應修理屋頂的任何損壞。
  5. The last line of the agreement reads: "Both parties shall keep the terms of this agreement secret."
    協議的最後一行寫著：雙方應對本協議的條款保密。
  6. The new safety law has a clear rule: the company shall provide all staff with safety training.
    新的安全法規有一條清楚的規定：公司應為所有員工提供安全訓練。

③ 名詞 + shall be + 過去分詞：規定事情怎麼處理

  1. Our contract with the Spanish supplier says: "All payments shall be made in euros."
    我們和西班牙供應商的合約寫著：所有款項應以歐元支付。（被動）
  2. The lawyer added one more line: "Any changes to this contract shall be agreed in writing."
    律師又加了一行：本合約的任何變更應以書面同意。（被動）
  3. The rules of the parents' association say that the meeting shall be held every three months.
    家長會的章程規定，會議應每三個月舉行一次。（被動）
  4. The hotel's guest rules state: "Complaints shall be sent to the manager within seven days."
    飯店的住客規定寫著：投訴應於七天內送交經理。（被動）
  5. According to the school rules, the prize shall be given to the student with the highest mark.
    根據校規，獎項應頒給分數最高的學生。（被動）
  6. The builders received a notice from the council: "All rubbish shall be removed from the site before Friday."
    建商收到議會的通知：所有垃圾應於星期五前清出工地。（被動）

④ 主詞 + shall + 原形動詞：法律和章程

  1. The new constitution says that every citizen shall have the right to vote.
    新憲法規定，每個公民都應享有投票權。
  2. Under the constitution, the president shall serve for four years.
    根據憲法，總統任期應為四年。
  3. Article five of the society's rules says: "The committee shall meet at least once a month."
    學會章程第五條寫著：委員會應至少每月開會一次。
  4. The sign on the factory gate reads: "No person shall enter the building without a pass."
    工廠大門的牌子寫著：任何人沒有通行證不得進入大樓。
  5. The rules of the tennis club say: "Members shall pay their fees by the first of January."
    網球俱樂部的章程寫著：會員應於一月一日前繳交會費。
  6. The club's rules are clear on this point: the club shall elect a new chairperson every two years.
    俱樂部的章程對這一點寫得很清楚：俱樂部應每兩年選出新主席。

⑤ You shall + 原形動詞：很正式或文學的命令

  1. In the film, the old wizard stands on the bridge and shouts at the monster: "You shall not pass!"
    電影裡，老巫師站在橋上對怪物大喊：你不准過去！
  2. In the story, the fairy godmother says: "You shall go to the ball, Cinderella."
    故事裡，仙女教母說：仙杜瑞拉，你一定會去參加舞會。
  3. I'm so sorry about the mistake. You shall have your money back by Friday. I promise.
    這個錯誤我很抱歉。星期五前你一定會拿回你的錢。我保證。
  4. The king said to the young knight: "You shall stay here until I return."
    國王對年輕的騎士說：在我回來之前，你要待在這裡。
  5. You have damaged our property and refused to pay. You shall hear from our lawyer.
    你損壞了我們的財產又拒絕賠償。你會收到我們律師的消息。
  6. The old farmer told his sons: "If you work hard, you shall have a reward."
    老農夫告訴他的兒子們：如果你們努力工作，你們一定會得到獎賞。
  7. The headmistress in the old novel says to the girl: "You shall not leave this room without permission."
    那本老小說裡的女校長對女孩說：沒有允許，你不准離開這個房間。
