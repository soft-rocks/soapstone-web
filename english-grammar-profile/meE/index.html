We haven't decided where to go yet.

我們還沒決定要去哪裡。

否⁠定⁠句是主⁠詞加 haven't，再⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

We haven't decided where to go yet.

我們還沒決定要去哪裡。

他們討論了好幾個禮拜，還沒有結果。

主⁠詞可⁠以是代⁠名⁠詞或名⁠詞

We haven't decided where to go yet.

代名詞

My friends had a baby girl last week. They haven't chosen a name for her.

我朋友上禮拜生了一個女兒。他們還沒幫她取名字。
名詞

Business is slow at the moment. The company hasn't hired anyone new.

最近生意很差。公司沒有僱用新人。

否⁠定⁠句常加 yet、since、never

We haven't decided where to go yet.

yet

We haven't booked the flights yet.

我們還沒訂機票。
since

My uncle is still in shock. He hasn't spoken to anyone since the accident.

我叔叔還沒從驚嚇中恢復。他從那次意外以後就沒跟任何人說過話。
never

I've never ridden a motorbike.

我從來沒騎過機車。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式⁠的否⁠定不⁠用 didn't

We haven't decided where to go yet.

We didn't decided where to go yet.

We haven't decided where to go yet.

never 已⁠經是否⁠定，不⁠加 not

We haven't decided where to go yet.

My brother hasn't never eaten raw fish.

My brother has never eaten raw fish.

否⁠定⁠句⁠的 yet 放⁠在句⁠尾

We haven't decided where to go yet.

The council yet hasn't repaired the road.

The council hasn't repaired the road yet.

有過⁠去⁠的時⁠間，用 didn't 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We haven't decided where to go yet.

我爸爸工作很忙。我們去年夏天哪裡都沒去。

haven't 加名⁠詞⁠的意⁠思是沒⁠有，現⁠在很⁠少用

We haven't decided where to go yet.

我付不了計程車錢。我沒有錢。

still 強⁠調等了很⁠久

We haven't decided where to go yet.

我們談這趟旅行談了好幾個月。我們到現在還沒決定。

例句

① 主詞是代名詞：I / you / we / they + haven't

  1. My friend gave me a novel for my birthday. I haven't started the book yet.
    朋友送我一本小說當生日禮物。那本書我還沒開始看。
  2. Are you feeling ill? You haven't touched your soup.
    你不舒服嗎？你的湯一口都沒動。
  3. My aunt's family lives far away. We haven't seen our cousins since Christmas.
    我阿姨一家住得很遠。我們從聖誕節以後就沒見過表兄弟姊妹。
  4. My friends had a baby girl last week. They haven't chosen a name for her.
    我朋友上禮拜生了一個女兒。他們還沒幫她取名字。
  5. I really need a holiday. I haven't had a day off for weeks.
    我真的需要放假。我好幾個禮拜沒休假了。
  6. The prices go up every day. We haven't booked the flights yet.
    價格每天都在漲。我們還沒訂機票。

② 主詞是代名詞：he / she / it + hasn't

  1. My daughter feels lonely. She hasn't made many friends at her new school.
    我女兒覺得很孤單。她在新學校沒交到幾個朋友。
  2. Give Grandpa's old jacket away. He hasn't worn it for years.
    把爺爺那件舊外套送人吧。他好幾年沒穿了。
  3. Look at my phone screen. It hasn't worked properly since you dropped it.
    你看我的手機螢幕。你摔過以後，它就沒正常過。
  4. My mum takes the bus to work now. She hasn't driven since her accident.
    我媽媽現在搭公車上班。她出車禍以後就沒開過車。
  5. Dad got up late this morning. He hasn't shaved today.
    爸爸今天早上起晚了。他今天沒刮鬍子。

③ 主詞是名詞

  1. We miss them a lot. My parents haven't visited us this year.
    我們很想他們。我爸媽今年還沒來看過我們。
  2. Business is slow at the moment. The company hasn't hired anyone new.
    最近生意很差。公司沒有僱用新人。
  3. We took the exam two weeks ago. Our teacher hasn't marked our tests yet.
    我們兩個禮拜前考試。老師還沒改我們的考卷。
  4. It's time for bed, but the children haven't brushed their teeth.
    該睡覺了，可是孩子們還沒刷牙。
  5. I'm waiting for a call about the job. My phone hasn't rung all morning.
    我在等工作的電話。我的手機一整個早上都沒響。
  6. We always order the same dishes. The restaurant hasn't changed its menu in years.
    我們每次都點一樣的菜。那家餐廳好幾年沒換菜單了。

④ 更多的動詞

  1. This physics book is too hard for me. I haven't understood a word of the first chapter.
    這本物理書對我來說太難了。第一章我一個字都沒看懂。
  2. Don't look so sad. We haven't forgotten your birthday.
    別那麼難過。我們沒忘記你的生日。
  3. My friend said she would pay me back in May. She hasn't kept her promise.
    我朋友說她五月會還我錢。她沒有遵守承諾。
  4. It's been too dry. My grandparents haven't grown any vegetables this year.
    天氣太乾了。我爺爺奶奶今年沒種菜。
  5. My husband works every weekend. He hasn't spent much time with his family recently.
    我先生每個週末都在工作。他最近沒花多少時間陪家人。
  6. All your old toys are in the attic. I haven't thrown anything away.
    你所有的舊玩具都在閣樓。我什麼都沒丟。

⑤ never

  1. I'm a bit nervous about the trip. I've never ridden a motorbike.
    這趟旅行我有點緊張。我從來沒騎過機車。
  2. I'm teaching her to send emails. My grandmother has never used a computer.
    我在教她寄電子郵件。我奶奶從來沒用過電腦。
  3. My husband and I are excited about Japan. We've never travelled abroad together.
    我和我先生很期待去日本。我們從來沒一起出國過。
  4. Don't play chess with my uncle. He has never lost a game.
    不要跟我叔叔下西洋棋。他從來沒輸過。

⑥ 完整形式：have not / has not

  1. My car was stolen last month. The police have not found it.
    我的車上個月被偷了。警察還沒找到。
  2. Visitors must wait until spring. The museum has not opened its new wing.
    參觀的人得等到春天。博物館的新展區還沒開放。
  3. My dad had his operation this morning. We have not heard anything from the hospital.
    我爸爸今天早上動手術。我們還沒收到醫院的任何消息。
