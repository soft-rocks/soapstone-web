If you are interested in this house, please call me on my mobile phone.

如果您對這間房子有興趣，請打我的手機。

this 指前⁠面說⁠過⁠的東⁠西

If you are interested in this house, please call me on my mobile phone.

前一句

The house is in a quiet street near the park.

這間房子在公園附近一條安靜的街上。
後一句

If you are interested in this house, please call me on my mobile phone.

如果您對這間房子有興趣，請打我的手機。

第⁠一⁠次用 a，再提⁠到用 this

If you are interested in this house, please call me on my mobile phone.

第一次

There is a problem with my heating.

我家的暖氣有問題。
再提到

Could you send someone to check this problem?

可以請您派人來看看這個問題嗎？

我們對這個情況很不滿。

If you are interested in this house, please call me.

I found two old letters in the box. These letters are from my grandfather.

例句

① 廣告

  1. We have a flat for rent near the station. This flat has two bedrooms.
    我們有一間公寓要出租，在車站附近。這間公寓有兩間臥室。
  2. I'm selling my old bike. This bike is only two years old.
    我要賣我的舊腳踏車。這台腳踏車才騎兩年。
  3. Our school offers a summer course in art. This course is for children aged 8 to 12.
    我們學校開了一門暑期美術課。這門課給八到十二歲的小孩上。
  4. The museum has a new show about dinosaurs. This show ends in May.
    博物館有一個新的恐龍展。這個展覽五月結束。
  5. We are looking for a waiter for our café. This job is from Monday to Friday.
    我們咖啡館在找一個服務生。這份工作是星期一到星期五。

② 寫信抱怨問題

  1. The lift in our building doesn't work. This problem started last week.
    我們大樓的電梯壞了。這個問題上星期就開始了。
  2. My new phone keeps turning off. Can you do something about this problem?
    我的新手機一直自己關機。你們可以處理一下這個問題嗎？
  3. The shower in my room is always cold. Please fix this shower as soon as possible.
    我房間的淋浴一直都是冷水。請盡快修好這個淋浴。
  4. The internet in the hotel is very slow. I am not happy with this service.
    旅館的網路很慢。我對這個服務很不滿意。

③ 介紹禮物

  1. Many people gave me presents. My aunt gave me a watch. This watch is my favourite present.
    很多人送我禮物。我阿姨送我一支手錶。這支手錶是我最喜歡的禮物。
  2. My brother sent me a book from London. This book is about the history of the city.
    我哥哥從倫敦寄了一本書給我。這本書講的是那個城市的歷史。
  3. I got a camera for my birthday. I take this camera everywhere.
    我生日收到一台相機。我去哪裡都帶著這台相機。
  4. My friends gave me a plant. I keep this plant on my desk.
    朋友送我一盆植物。我把這盆植物放在書桌上。

④ 介紹地方

  1. Last summer we stayed at a small hotel by the sea. This hotel had a lovely garden.
    去年夏天我們住在海邊的一間小旅館。這間旅館有一個很漂亮的花園。
  2. There is a new café on my street. The cakes in this café are delicious.
    我住的街上開了一家新咖啡館。這家咖啡館的蛋糕很好吃。
  3. We visited a village in the mountains. People in this village make cheese.
    我們去了山上的一個村子。這個村子的人做起司。
  4. I go to a gym near my office. This gym opens at six in the morning.
    我去辦公室附近的一家健身房。這家健身房早上六點開門。

⑤ 說一個主意或計畫

  1. Let's have a picnic on Saturday. What do you think of this plan?
    我們星期六去野餐吧。你覺得這個計畫怎麼樣？
  2. Maybe we can share a taxi to the airport. This idea will save us money.
    也許我們可以一起搭計程車去機場。這個主意可以幫我們省錢。
  3. Tom wants to cycle across Europe. I think this plan is a bit crazy.
    Tom 想騎腳踏車橫越歐洲。我覺得這個計畫有點瘋狂。
  4. We could meet at the library to study. Do you like this idea?
    我們可以約在圖書館讀書。你喜歡這個主意嗎？

⑥ 說一個消息

  1. My sister is going to have a baby. This news made my parents very happy.
    我姊姊要生小孩了。這個消息讓我爸媽很高興。
  2. Our teacher is leaving the school. We were sad to hear this news.
    我們的老師要離開學校了。我們聽到這個消息很難過。
  3. The concert is cancelled because of the rain. Lots of fans are angry about this decision.
    演唱會因為下雨取消了。很多歌迷對這個決定很生氣。
  4. They are building a new road behind my house. I don't like this news at all.
    他們要在我家後面蓋一條新的路。我一點也不喜歡這個消息。

⑦ 說一個情況

  1. My boss wants me to work every weekend. I can't accept this situation.
    我老闆要我每個週末都上班。我沒辦法接受這個情況。
  2. There are no buses after ten at night. This problem makes it hard for me to get home.
    晚上十點以後就沒有公車了。這個問題讓我很難回家。
  3. Two of my classmates are always late. The teacher is angry about this situation.
    我有兩個同學總是遲到。老師對這個情況很生氣。
  4. The prices in the supermarket go up every month. Many people are worried about this problem.
    超市的價格每個月都在漲。很多人都很擔心這個問題。
  5. Our flight is delayed by five hours. Who can we talk to about this delay?
    我們的班機延誤了五個小時。這次延誤我們可以找誰說？
