If you need anything, please let me know.

如果你需要什麼，請跟我說。

動⁠詞 + 不⁠定代⁠名⁠詞

If you need anything, please let me know.

如果你需要什麼，請跟我說。

something、everything、anything

If you need anything, please let me know.

something

I want to buy something for my sister.

我想買個東西給我妹妹。
everything

He eats everything on his plate.

他把盤子裡的東西都吃光。
anything

Do you want anything?

你要什麼嗎？

if 句用 anything

If you need anything, please let me know.

如果你看到什麼奇怪的東西，打電話給我。

like 後⁠面加 to

I like eat everything.

I like to eat everything.

something 放⁠在動⁠詞後⁠面

I can cook for you something.

I can cook something for you.

anything 不⁠加 s

If you need anything, please let me know.

Do you need anythings from the shop?

Do you need anything from the shop?

請別⁠人吃⁠喝用 something

你要喝點什麼嗎？

已⁠經提⁠過⁠的東⁠西用 it

我的筆不見了，我找不到它。

例句

① something

  1. I want to eat something sweet.
    我想吃點甜的。
  2. She bought something at the market.
    她在市場買了東西。
  3. Let's cook something for dinner.
    我們煮點東西當晚餐吧。
  4. I found something under the bed.
    我在床底下找到一樣東西。
  5. He wrote something on the board.
    他在黑板上寫了些字。
  6. I've got something in my eye.
    我眼睛裡有東西。

② everything

  1. I like everything about this town.
    這個小鎮的一切我都喜歡。
  2. She tells her mother everything.
    她什麼事都跟媽媽說。
  3. We sold everything in the shop.
    我們把店裡的東西都賣光了。
  4. He checked everything before the test.
    考試前他把所有東西都檢查了一遍。
  5. I packed everything in one bag.
    我把所有東西都裝進一個包包裡。

③ anything 在問句

  1. Do you want anything from the kitchen?
    你要廚房裡的什麼東西嗎？
  2. Can you smell anything strange in here?
    你有聞到這裡有什麼怪味嗎？
  3. Did you get anything nice in the sales?
    你在特賣有買到什麼好東西嗎？
  4. Do you need anything for school?
    你上學需要什麼東西嗎？
  5. Did she say anything about me?
    她有說到我什麼嗎？

④ anything 在否定句

  1. I don't want anything for my birthday this year.
    今年生日我什麼都不要。
  2. She didn't buy anything at the shop.
    她在那家店什麼都沒買。
  3. We haven't planned anything for the weekend.
    這個週末我們什麼都還沒安排。
  4. He doesn't drink anything at night.
    他晚上什麼都不喝。
  5. I didn't find anything in the car.
    我在車上什麼都沒找到。

⑤ anything 在 if 句

  1. If you want anything, just ask.
    如果你想要什麼，問我就好。
  2. If you hear anything, tell me.
    如果你聽到什麼，跟我說。
  3. If you forget anything, I can bring it.
    如果你忘了帶什麼，我可以幫你拿。
  4. If you find anything, put it here.
    如果你找到什麼，就放在這裡。
  5. If you need anything for the room, ask at the desk.
    房間裡需要什麼的話，到櫃台問。

⑥ 請別人吃喝

  1. You look hungry. Would you like something from the fridge?
    你看起來餓了。要不要吃點冰箱裡的東西？
  2. Can I offer you something cold?
    要不要喝點冰的？
  3. Do you want something hot?
    你要吃點熱的嗎？
  4. I'm going to the bakery. Do you want something?
    我要去麵包店，你要買點什麼嗎？
  5. Shall I make you something before you go?
    你走之前要不要我幫你做點吃的？
