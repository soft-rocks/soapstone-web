It might not be very fashionable, but that's the way I like it.

這也許不太時髦，但我就是喜歡這樣。

主⁠詞 + might not + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

might not、mightn't、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

It might not be very fashionable, but that's the way I like it.

might not可能不。
mightn'tmight not 的縮寫，主要用在英式英文。

例句

① might not + 原形動詞, but + 句子：承認缺點

  1. My flat might not be very big, but it's close to the sea.
    我的公寓可能不太大，可是離海很近。
  2. My car might not be fast, but it's never broken down.
    我的車可能不快，可是從來沒拋錨過。
  3. The hotel might not have a pool, but the rooms are lovely.
    那家飯店可能沒有游泳池，可是房間很漂亮。
  4. My cooking might not look nice, but it tastes good.
    我做的菜可能不好看，可是很好吃。
  5. Our garden might not be big, but we grow all our own vegetables.
    我們的花園可能不大，可是我們自己種所有的蔬菜。
  6. The job might not pay much, but I love it.
    這份工作的薪水可能不多，可是我很喜歡。

② this / that + might not / mightn't + be：寫作的看法

  1. People watch too much TV these days, and in a way this mightn't be such a good thing.
    現在大家看太多電視了，從某方面來說，這可能不是什麼好事。
  2. Buying a bigger car might not be the best idea.
    買一台比較大的車可能不是最好的主意。
  3. That mightn't be the right answer.
    那可能不是正確的答案。
  4. Eating late at night might not be good for your sleep.
    晚上很晚吃東西可能對你的睡眠不好。
  5. This might not be the right time to talk about money.
    現在可能不是談錢的好時機。
  6. Working at weekends mightn't be a good idea for students.
    週末工作對學生來說可能不是好主意。

③ 主詞 + might not + 原形動詞：以後可能不會發生

  1. I might not come to the party. I'm very tired.
    我可能不會去派對。我很累。
  2. The shop might not open tomorrow because of the holiday.
    因為放假，那家店明天可能不會開。
  3. We might not have time to visit the castle.
    我們可能沒有時間去參觀城堡。
  4. She might not pass her driving test the first time.
    她第一次考駕照可能不會過。
  5. The bus might not stop here on Sundays.
    星期天公車可能不會停在這裡。
  6. They mightn't finish the road before winter.
    他們可能沒辦法在冬天前把路修好。

④ 主詞 + might not + 原形動詞：現在可能不是真的

  1. He might not know about the meeting. Let's tell him.
    他可能不知道開會的事。我們告訴他吧。
  2. The water might not be warm enough for swimming.
    水可能不夠溫暖，不能游泳。
  3. She mightn't like spicy food. Let's ask her.
    她可能不喜歡辣的食物。我們問問她吧。
  4. The machine might not work without batteries.
    那台機器沒有電池可能不能用。
  5. They might not speak English, so learn a few words of Greek.
    他們可能不會說英文，所以學幾個希臘文的字吧。
  6. This address might not be right.
    這個地址可能不對。

⑤ 主詞 + might not + 原形動詞, so + 句子

  1. The restaurant might not take cards, so bring some cash.
    那家餐廳可能不收卡，所以帶一些現金。
  2. I might not hear the phone, so send me a message.
    我可能聽不到電話，所以傳訊息給我。
  3. The trains might not run on time, so leave early.
    火車可能不會準時，所以早點出發。
  4. The children might not want to walk far, so take the car.
    孩子們可能不想走很遠，所以開車去。
  5. There might not be any food there, so bring sandwiches.
    那裡可能沒有吃的，所以帶三明治。
  6. The museum might not be open on Monday, so check the website.
    博物館星期一可能沒開，所以先看一下網站。
  7. You mightn't get a seat, so book a ticket now.
    你可能沒有位子，所以現在就訂票。
