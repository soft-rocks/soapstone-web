Unfortunately, my team lost.

可惜我們隊輸了。

unfortunately 放⁠在句⁠首，說⁠出壞消⁠息

可惜我們隊輸了。

actually 說⁠出真⁠正⁠的情⁠況，of course 說明⁠顯⁠的事

actually
其實

I actually prefer tea.

其實我比較喜歡茶。
of course
當然

Of course, my new watch is black.

我的新手錶當然是黑色的。

態⁠度副⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在 be、助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面或一⁠般動⁠詞前⁠面

be 後面

The hotel was actually very nice.

那家飯店其實很不錯。
助動詞後面

They will of course need a map.

他們當然需要一張地圖。
一般動詞前面

She actually lives next door to my aunt.

她其實就住在我阿姨家隔壁。

回⁠答問⁠題⁠時，of course 表⁠示同⁠意，actually 客⁠氣⁠地更⁠正

of course

Can I use your phone? Of course.

「我可以用你的電話嗎？」「當然可以。」
actually

Are you from Spain? Actually, I'm from Mexico.

「你是西班牙人嗎？」「其實我是墨西哥人。」

否⁠定⁠的回⁠答用 of course not

Is it raining? Of course no.

Is it raining? Of course not.

unfortunate 的 e 要留⁠著

Unfortunately, my team lost.

Unfortunatly, the shop was closed.

Unfortunately, the shop was closed.

of course 的 of 不⁠能省⁠略

Course, you can come with us.

Of course, you can come with us.

對⁠方說新消⁠息⁠時，不⁠回⁠答 of course

I passed my driving test! Of course.

I passed my driving test! Well done!

例句

① unfortunately

  1. Unfortunately, it rained all weekend.
    可惜整個週末都在下雨。
  2. Unfortunately, I can't come to your party.
    很可惜，我不能去你的派對。
  3. Unfortunately, the tickets were sold out.
    很可惜，票都賣完了。
  4. We wanted to see the castle, but unfortunately it was closed.
    我們想看那座城堡，可惜它沒開。
  5. My grandfather is unfortunately not very well.
    可惜我爺爺身體不太好。
  6. Unfortunately, she didn't get the job.
    可惜她沒得到那份工作。

② actually

  1. Actually, I don't like chocolate very much.
    其實我不太喜歡巧克力。
  2. He looks young, but he's actually forty.
    他看起來很年輕，其實已經四十歲了。
  3. I actually enjoyed the film.
    其實我挺喜歡那部電影的。
  4. Actually, I've changed my mind.
    其實我改變主意了。
  5. The new teacher was actually really friendly.
    新老師其實很友善。
  6. "Is this your car?" "Actually, it's my sister's."
    這是你的車嗎？其實是我姊姊的。
  7. Did you actually see the accident?
    你真的看到那場意外了嗎？

③ of course

  1. Of course, I'll help you with your homework.
    我當然會幫你寫功課。
  2. Of course, everyone wants to win.
    當然，每個人都想贏。
  3. "Do you remember me?" "Of course I do!"
    你記得我嗎？當然記得！
  4. "Can I sit here?" "Yes, of course."
    我可以坐這裡嗎？當然可以。
  5. We'll invite your parents, of course.
    我們當然會邀請你爸媽。
  6. Of course it's cold. It's January!
    當然冷啊。現在是一月！
  7. "Is the museum free?" "Of course not."
    博物館免費嗎？當然不是。

④ 放在 be、助動詞後面或一般動詞前面

  1. The hotel was actually very nice.
    那家飯店其實很不錯。
  2. I can't actually remember her name.
    我其實想不起她的名字。
  3. She actually lives next door to my aunt.
    她其實就住在我阿姨家隔壁。
  4. We have unfortunately lost the key.
    很可惜，我們把鑰匙弄丟了。
  5. They will of course need a map.
    他們當然需要一張地圖。

⑤ 放在句首

  1. Actually, we met last year.
    其實我們去年見過面。
  2. Unfortunately, the bus didn't come.
    可惜公車沒來。
  3. Of course, you can bring your dog.
    你當然可以帶你的狗來。
  4. Actually, I've never been to Paris.
    其實我從來沒去過巴黎。
  5. Unfortunately, my brother broke his leg.
    很不幸，我哥哥摔斷了腿。
  6. Of course, the food was delicious.
    當然，食物很好吃。
