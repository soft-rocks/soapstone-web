Do you have any furniture in this room?

你這個房間裡有家具嗎？

furniture 是總⁠稱，不⁠加 ⁠-⁠⁠s

你這個房間裡有家具嗎？

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞有食⁠物、抽⁠象⁠的東⁠西和總⁠稱

Do you have any furniture in this room?

食物和飲料water, milk, bread, rice, food
抽象的東西music, time, homework, help, fun
總稱furniture, money, luggage, weather

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞動⁠詞用單⁠數，問數⁠量用 how much

Do you have any furniture in this room?

單數動詞

The money is on the table.

錢在桌上。
how much

How much time do we have?

我們有多少時間？

help 是⁠不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞，不⁠加 a

I need a help with my homework.

I need some help with my homework.

furniture 沒⁠有複⁠數⁠形

Do you have any furniture in this room?

We bought some new furnitures.

We bought some new furniture.

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞問數⁠量用 how much

How many money do you have?

How much money do you have?

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞當主⁠詞，動⁠詞用單⁠數

The weather is cold today.

例句

① 食物和飲料

  1. Sorry, there's no milk left. Do you want your tea black?
    抱歉，牛奶沒了。你的茶要不加奶嗎？
  2. There's some bread in the cupboard if you're hungry.
    你餓的話，櫥櫃裡有一些麵包。
  3. We had rice and chicken for dinner last night.
    我們昨天晚餐吃了飯和雞肉。
  4. It's hot today, so take plenty of water on the walk.
    今天很熱，散步的時候多帶點水。
  5. The food at your party was delicious. Who made it?
    你派對上的食物好好吃，是誰做的？

② 抽象的東西

  1. In my free time I listen to music, watch TV and talk to my friends.
    我有空的時候聽音樂、看電視、跟朋友聊天。
  2. Come to my house at 4.30 pm, so we have time to get ready.
    四點半來我家，這樣我們才有時間準備。
  3. I can't come out tonight. I've got a lot of homework.
    我今晚不能出去，我有很多功課。
  4. The kids had great fun at the fair yesterday.
    孩子們昨天在遊樂會玩得很開心。
  5. Can you give me some information about the evening course?
    你可以給我一些關於夜間課程的資訊嗎？

③ 總稱

  1. The flat is empty. We have to buy all the furniture ourselves.
    公寓是空的，所有家具都得我們自己買。
  2. I haven't got any money with me. Can you pay?
    我身上沒帶錢，你可以付嗎？
  3. How much luggage are you taking to Spain?
    你要帶多少行李去西班牙？
  4. The weather there was cold and wet all week.
    那裡的天氣整個禮拜都又冷又濕。
  5. There's a lot of traffic in the city centre today.
    今天市中心車很多。

④ some / any + 不可數名詞

  1. I'm making some coffee. Do you want a cup?
    我在泡咖啡，你要一杯嗎？
  2. Is there any cheese in the fridge for the sandwiches?
    冰箱裡有起司可以做三明治嗎？
  3. We can carry the boxes ourselves. We don't need any help from the neighbours.
    我們可以自己搬箱子，不需要鄰居幫忙。
  4. Let's buy some fruit for the picnic on Sunday.
    我們買點水果，星期天去野餐吧。

⑤ how much + 不可數名詞

  1. How much time do we have before the train leaves?
    火車開之前我們還有多少時間？
  2. How much water should a baby drink in hot weather?
    天氣熱的時候，寶寶應該喝多少水？
  3. How much money did you spend in London?
    你在倫敦花了多少錢？

⑥ 不可數名詞 + 單數動詞

  1. Turn that down! The music is so loud I can't hear the phone.
    關小聲一點！音樂大聲到我聽不到電話。
  2. The money for the school trip is in the envelope.
    校外教學的錢在信封裡。
  3. The coffee in this café comes from Colombia.
    這家咖啡店的咖啡來自哥倫比亞。
  4. Kids, wash your hands. Dinner is on the table!
    孩子們，去洗手，晚餐上桌了！

⑦ 不可數名詞不加限定詞

  1. My doctor told me to eat less salt and more fruit.
    我的醫生叫我少吃鹽、多吃水果。
  2. My little girl would eat chocolate for breakfast if I let her.
    我女兒要是我准，她早餐會吃巧克力。
  3. Money isn't everything, but it helps.
    錢不是一切，不過有錢比較好辦事。
  4. We're out of bread and milk. I'll go to the shop.
    我們的麵包和牛奶都沒了，我去一趟商店。
  5. We sell fresh fish every morning at the harbour.
    我們每天早上在港口賣新鮮的魚。
