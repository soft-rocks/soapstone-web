I didn't tell anyone about this story at first.

一開始我沒跟任何人說這件事。

否⁠定⁠的動⁠詞後⁠面用 anyone

一開始我沒跟任何人說這件事。

不⁠否⁠定動⁠詞⁠時，用 no one、nothing、nowhere

I didn't tell anyone about this story at first.

any⁠-⁠ 接在否⁠定⁠的動⁠詞後⁠面，no⁠-⁠ 單⁠獨表⁠示否⁠定

I didn't tell anyone about this story at first.

人anyone、anybody ／ no one、nobody
東西、事情anything ／ nothing
地方anywhere ／ nowhere

no one 寫成兩個字，nobody 寫成一個字。

主⁠詞是否⁠定⁠的人或東⁠西⁠時，用 nobody 或 nothing

I didn't tell anyone about this story at first.

這間老房子什麼都壞了。

一⁠句⁠話只⁠用一⁠個否⁠定

I didn't tell anyone about this story at first.

The burglars didn't leave nothing in the safe.

The burglars didn't leave anything in the safe.

anyone 不⁠當否⁠定⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

I didn't tell anyone about this story at first.

Anybody didn't wake up when the alarm went off.

Nobody woke up when the alarm went off.

no one 寫⁠成兩⁠個字

Noone saw the accident.

No one saw the accident.

nobody 當主⁠詞⁠時，動⁠詞用單⁠數

Nobody was brave enough to jump.

例句

① 否定的動詞 + anyone / anybody

  1. I broke Mum's vase, and I didn't tell anyone for a week.
    我打破了媽媽的花瓶，一個星期都沒跟任何人說。
  2. It's my first day at this school, so I don't know anybody yet.
    今天是我在這所學校的第一天，所以我還一個人都不認識。
  3. We rang the doorbell twice, but there wasn't anyone at home.
    我們按了兩次門鈴，可是家裡沒有人。
  4. Please don't show anybody these photos. They're private.
    請不要把這些照片給任何人看。這是私人的。
  5. She was so upset that she didn't want to talk to anyone.
    她難過到不想跟任何人說話。

② 否定的動詞 + anything

  1. I haven't seen anything of the city yet. I only got here last night.
    我還沒看到這座城市的任何東西。我昨晚才到。
  2. The thief didn't take anything, but he broke the window.
    小偷什麼都沒拿，可是打破了窗戶。
  3. Don't say anything to Dad about the car. I'll tell him myself.
    車子的事什麼都別跟爸爸說。我自己跟他說。
  4. I can't see anything without my glasses.
    我不戴眼鏡什麼都看不到。
  5. We didn't have anything to eat on the long train journey.
    長途火車上我們什麼吃的都沒有。

③ 否定的動詞 + anywhere

  1. I've looked everywhere, but I can't find my passport anywhere.
    我每個地方都找了，可是哪裡都找不到我的護照。
  2. We didn't go anywhere during the holidays because Mum was ill.
    放假的時候我們哪裡都沒去，因為媽媽生病了。
  3. There isn't anywhere to sit in this waiting room.
    這間候診室裡沒有地方可以坐。

④ 肯定的動詞 + nothing

  1. I told the customs officer I had nothing to declare, but he didn't believe me.
    我跟海關說我沒有東西要申報，可是他不相信我。
  2. There's nothing in the fridge. Let's order a pizza.
    冰箱裡什麼都沒有。我們叫披薩吧。
  3. We asked about her new job, but she said nothing about it.
    我們問她新工作的事，可是她一句也沒提。
  4. The wedding is on Saturday, and I have nothing to wear.
    婚禮在星期六，我沒有衣服可以穿。

⑤ 肯定的動詞 + no one / nobody

  1. I knocked and waited, but I saw no one.
    我敲了門也等了，可是一個人都沒看到。
  2. My brother is very careful with his new car. He lends it to nobody.
    我哥哥對他的新車很小心。他誰都不借。
  3. The town was empty. We met nobody on the way.
    鎮上空蕩蕩的。我們一路上一個人都沒遇到。

⑥ 肯定的動詞 + nowhere

  1. There's nowhere to park near the hospital.
    醫院附近沒地方停車。
  2. After the hotel closed, the tourists had nowhere to stay.
    飯店關門以後，觀光客沒地方住。

⑦ no one / nobody / nothing 當主詞

  1. Nobody will ever know who that man was.
    永遠不會有人知道那個男人是誰。
  2. Nobody called while you were out.
    你出去的時候沒有人打電話來。
  3. Don't book the boat trip for us. No one in my family can swim.
    別幫我們訂搭船的行程。我家沒有人會游泳。
  4. Nothing happened. It was a quiet day.
    什麼事都沒發生。那是平靜的一天。
  5. Nothing is more important to her than her children.
    對她來說，沒有什麼比她的孩子更重要。
  6. The teacher asked a very hard question, and no one knew the answer.
    老師問了一個很難的問題，沒有人知道答案。

⑧ 簡答：Nothing / Nobody / Nowhere

  1. "What did you buy at the market?" "Nothing. It was all too expensive."
    你在市集買了什麼？什麼都沒買。東西都太貴了。
  2. "Who was at the door?" "Nobody. It was just the wind."
    門口是誰？沒有人。只是風。
  3. "Where are you going tonight?" "Nowhere. I'm staying in."
    你今晚要去哪裡？哪裡都不去。我待在家。
