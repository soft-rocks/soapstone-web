I have a piece of news.

我有一個消息。

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞用單⁠位詞來數

我有一個消息。

單⁠位詞說容⁠器、形⁠狀或重⁠量

I have a piece of news.

容器a bottle of water, a cup of tea
形狀和大小a slice of bread, a bar of chocolate
重量和量a kilo of rice, a litre of milk
a piece ofa piece of advice, a piece of furniture

例句

① a piece of + 名詞

  1. Sit down. I have a piece of news for you.
    坐下，我有一個消息要告訴你。
  2. You must bring two pencils, a rubber and a piece of paper.
    你得帶兩支鉛筆、一個橡皮擦和一張紙。
  3. Can I give you a piece of advice?
    我可以給你一個建議嗎？
  4. This old chair is my favourite piece of furniture.
    這張舊椅子是我最喜歡的家具。
  5. Would you like a piece of cake?
    你要吃一塊蛋糕嗎？

② 容器 + of + 名詞

  1. We have to bring a bottle of water, trainers and a T-shirt.
    我們要帶一瓶水、運動鞋和一件 T 恤。
  2. I'd love a cup of tea, please.
    我好想喝杯茶。
  3. Drink a glass of milk before bed.
    睡前喝一杯牛奶。
  4. He bought a box of chocolates for his mum.
    他買了一盒巧克力給他媽媽。
  5. Can you get me a can of lemonade from the fridge?
    你可以從冰箱幫我拿一罐檸檬汽水嗎？

③ 形狀和大小 + of + 名詞

  1. I had a slice of toast for breakfast.
    我早餐吃了一片吐司。
  2. The baker gave us a free loaf of bread because it was closing time.
    因為快打烊了，麵包師傅送了我們一條麵包。
  3. She ate a whole bar of chocolate.
    她吃了一整條巧克力。
  4. There's a sheet of paper on the printer.
    印表機上有一張紙。

④ 重量和量 + of + 名詞

  1. I need a kilo of rice and a litre of milk.
    我需要一公斤米和一公升牛奶。
  2. Add two spoons of sugar.
    加兩匙糖。
  3. We bought half a kilo of cheese.
    我們買了半公斤起司。

⑤ 數字 + 單位詞s + of + 名詞

  1. We need two bottles of water for the trip.
    我們這趟需要兩瓶水。
  2. I drink three cups of coffee a day.
    我一天喝三杯咖啡。
  3. Take some pieces of paper for your notes.
    拿幾張紙寫筆記。
  4. She ate two slices of pizza.
    她吃了兩片披薩。
  5. We need four loaves of bread for the party.
    派對需要四條麵包。

⑥ 點餐和購物

  1. Can I have a bowl of soup and a glass of water, please?
    請給我一碗湯和一杯水。
  2. Two cups of coffee and a piece of chocolate cake, please.
    請給我兩杯咖啡和一塊巧克力蛋糕。
  3. I'd like a bag of apples and a bunch of bananas.
    我要一袋蘋果和一串香蕉。
  4. How much is a bottle of olive oil?
    一瓶橄欖油多少錢？

⑦ 單位詞片語當主詞

  1. A cup of tea always makes me feel better.
    一杯茶總是讓我感覺好一點。
  2. A piece of glass cut my foot on the beach.
    海灘上一片玻璃割傷了我的腳。
  3. Two bottles of milk are enough for the weekend.
    兩瓶牛奶夠週末喝了。
  4. A slice of cake costs three pounds.
    一片蛋糕三英鎊。
  5. A tube of toothpaste lasts us about a month.
    一條牙膏我們大概可以用一個月。
