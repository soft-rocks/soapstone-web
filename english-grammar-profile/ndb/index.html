主⁠詞 + was / were + supposed to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 (+ but ...)

was / were supposed to原本安排好、原本應該。
原形動詞原本要做的事。
but ...後來發生的改變。

安⁠排改⁠變了

例句

I was supposed to arrive on 11th June, but I will arrive next month.

我原本應該六月十一日到，可是我會下個月才到。

說到未來的安排時，was supposed to 表示這個安排已經改了。

例句

① was / were supposed to ..., but ...：安排沒有照原樣進行

  1. We were supposed to go to the beach, but it was too windy.
    我們原本要去海邊，可是風太大了。
  2. I was supposed to work on Saturday, but my boss gave me the day off.
    我星期六原本要上班，可是老闆讓我放假。
  3. The parcel was supposed to arrive yesterday, but it's still not here.
    包裹原本應該昨天到，可是到現在還沒來。
  4. She was supposed to fly to Rome, but the airport was closed.
    她原本要飛去羅馬，可是機場關閉了。
  5. The workers were supposed to finish the roof last week, but it rained every day.
    工人原本應該上星期把屋頂做好，可是每天都在下雨。
  6. I was supposed to cook dinner, but I fell asleep on the sofa.
    我原本要煮晚餐，可是我在沙發上睡著了。
  7. We were supposed to have a test today, but the teacher was ill.
    我們今天原本要考試，可是老師生病了。
  8. The plumber was supposed to come at eight, but he turned up at noon.
    水電工原本應該八點來，可是他中午才出現。
  9. The shop was supposed to open at nine, but the door was still locked.
    那家店原本應該九點開門，可是門還鎖著。
  10. They were supposed to get married in May, but they postponed the wedding.
    他們原本要在五月結婚，可是他們把婚禮延後了。

② was / were supposed to + 原形動詞：未來的安排改變了

  1. I was supposed to arrive on 11th June, but I will arrive next month instead.
    我原本應該六月十一日到，可是我會改成下個月到。
  2. The new road was supposed to open next year, but it will be later.
    新路原本應該明年通車，可是會更晚。
  3. We were supposed to move house in March, but now we're moving in May.
    我們原本要三月搬家，可是現在改成五月。
  4. The concert was supposed to be next Saturday, but they've changed the date.
    演唱會原本應該在下星期六，可是他們改了日期。
  5. I was supposed to start my new job on Monday, but it's now Wednesday.
    我原本應該星期一開始新工作，可是現在改成星期三。
  6. The guests were supposed to stay for a week, but they're leaving on Friday.
    客人原本要待一個星期，可是他們星期五就要走了。
  7. My cousin was supposed to visit us at Easter, but she's coming in the summer.
    我表妹原本要在復活節來看我們，可是她改成夏天來。

③ 主詞 + was supposed to be + 時間 / 地點 / 名詞 / 形容詞

  1. The meeting? It was supposed to be on Tuesday at one.
    那個會議？原本應該是星期二一點。
  2. It was supposed to be a surprise, but my sister told him.
    這原本應該是個驚喜，可是我妹告訴他了。
  3. It was supposed to be a quiet weekend, but ten people came to stay.
    這原本應該是個安靜的週末，可是來了十個人住。
  4. The party was supposed to be at Tom's house, not at the pub.
    派對原本應該在 Tom 家，不是在酒吧。
  5. It was supposed to be sunny today, but look at the rain.
    今天原本應該是晴天，可是你看這場雨。
  6. The film was supposed to be funny, but nobody laughed.
    那部電影原本應該很好笑，可是沒有人笑。
  7. The trip was supposed to be cheap, but it cost us a fortune.
    這趟旅行原本應該很便宜，可是花了我們一大筆錢。

④ Weren't you supposed to ...? / 否定

  1. Weren't you supposed to be at work today?
    你今天不是應該在上班嗎？
  2. Wasn't the train supposed to leave at ten?
    火車不是應該十點開嗎？
  3. You weren't supposed to open the present until your birthday!
    你應該等到生日才能拆禮物的！
  4. I wasn't supposed to see the photos, but I found them on the table.
    我原本不應該看到那些照片，可是我在桌上發現了。
  5. Weren't we supposed to meet at the café?
    我們不是說好在咖啡店見面嗎？
  6. The kids weren't supposed to eat the cake before the party.
    孩子們原本不應該在派對前吃蛋糕。
  7. Wasn't your brother supposed to help us move the sofa?
    你哥不是說好要幫我們搬沙發嗎？
