It will probably be on TV next month!

它下個月大概會在電視上播！

肯⁠定⁠句⁠裡，probably 放⁠在 will 後⁠面

它下個月大概會在電視上播！

否⁠定⁠句⁠裡，probably 放⁠在 won't 前⁠面

It will probably be on TV next month!

我表哥大概不會來婚禮。

確⁠定⁠的程⁠度：definitely、certainly 最⁠高，possibly 最⁠低

It will probably be on TV next month!

definitely
很確定

I'll definitely give you the bike by half past five.

我五點半前一定會把腳踏車給你。
probably
相當確定

He's probably at the gym.

他大概在健身房。
possibly
不太確定

Because of the snow, the train from Leeds will possibly be late.

因為下雪，從里茲來的火車可能會誤點。

definitely 和 certainly 可⁠以單⁠獨回⁠答問⁠題

「你會去嗎？」「一定會。」

確⁠定程⁠度副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I'll meet definitely your parents at the station.

I'll definitely meet your parents at the station.

definitely 中⁠間是 ⁠-⁠ite⁠-⁠

I'll definately help you with the garden.

I'll definitely help you with the garden.

放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面要⁠用副⁠詞 probably

It will probably be on TV next month!

My aunt will probable stay at home this Christmas.

My aunt will probably stay at home this Christmas.

例句

① probably

  1. The shop will probably close early on Christmas Eve.
    那家店平安夜大概會提早關門。
  2. We'll probably move house next year.
    我們明年大概會搬家。
  3. He's probably at the gym.
    他大概在健身房。
  4. Your keys are probably in your other bag.
    你的鑰匙大概在你另一個包包裡。
  5. They probably took the wrong road.
    他們大概走錯路了。
  6. I'll probably see you at the weekend.
    我週末大概會見到你。

② probably + 否定

  1. She probably won't remember me.
    她大概不會記得我。
  2. It probably isn't true.
    那大概不是真的。
  3. My parents probably don't know about the party.
    我爸媽大概不知道派對的事。
  4. I probably won't finish before six.
    我大概六點前做不完。

③ definitely

  1. I'll definitely be at your sister's concert on Saturday.
    你姊姊星期六的音樂會我一定會到。
  2. This is definitely the creamiest ice cream I've ever tasted.
    這絕對是我吃過最濃郁的冰淇淋。
  3. With three dogs, we definitely need a bigger car for camping trips.
    有三隻狗，我們去露營絕對需要一輛大一點的車。
  4. She definitely said three o'clock.
    她確實說的是三點。
  5. That was definitely a mistake.
    那絕對是個錯誤。

④ certainly

  1. If I were you, I'd certainly say no.
    如果我是你，我一定會拒絕。
  2. The new manager is certainly very busy.
    新經理確實很忙。
  3. It's certainly colder today than it was last week.
    今天確實比上星期冷。
  4. They will certainly win the match.
    他們一定會贏得比賽。
  5. You certainly know a lot about cars.
    你確實很懂車。

⑤ possibly

  1. Because of the snow, the train from Leeds will possibly be late.
    因為下雪，從里茲來的火車可能會誤點。
  2. He's possibly the funniest uncle in our whole family.
    他可能是我們全家最好笑的叔叔。
  3. We could possibly meet on Friday.
    我們也許可以星期五碰面。
  4. It's possibly the most important decision of my life.
    這可能是我人生中最重要的決定。

⑥ 單獨回答問題

  1. "Can I borrow your pen?" "Certainly."
    我可以借你的筆嗎？當然可以。
  2. "Are you coming to the wedding?" "Definitely!"
    你會來婚禮嗎？一定會！
  3. "Will they be late?" "Probably."
    他們會遲到嗎？大概會。
  4. "Is she going to say yes?" "Possibly."
    她會答應嗎？有可能。

⑦ definitely not、probably not

  1. "Do you want to go camping?" "Definitely not."
    你想去露營嗎？絕對不要。
  2. "Will it snow tomorrow?" "Probably not."
    明天會下雪嗎？大概不會。
  3. "Are you going to tell him?" "Certainly not!"
    你要告訴他嗎？當然不要！
