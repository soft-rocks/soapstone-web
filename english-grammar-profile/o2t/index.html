How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

你能去看那個展覽，真是太幸運了！

How 加形⁠容⁠詞，後⁠面⁠接主⁠詞和動⁠詞

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

這片海灘好美！

後⁠面加 to 不⁠定⁠詞，說⁠明原⁠因

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

你能去看那個展覽，真是太幸運了！

How 加形⁠容⁠詞，直⁠接接 to 不⁠定⁠詞

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

真高興再見到你！

主⁠詞在動⁠詞前⁠面，不⁠倒裝

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

How beautiful is this beach!

How beautiful this beach is!

形⁠容⁠詞後⁠面⁠接主⁠詞，用 How

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

What lucky you are!

How lucky you are!

to 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

How lucky you are to have visited that exhibition!

How lovely to getting your letter!

How lovely to get your letter!

例句

① How + 形容詞 + 主詞 + be

  1. How beautiful this beach is!
    這片海灘好美！
  2. How lucky you are!
    你真幸運！
  3. How tall your son is now!
    你兒子現在長得好高！
  4. How quiet the house is without the kids!
    孩子們不在，家裡好安靜！
  5. How cold it is today!
    今天好冷！
  6. How expensive everything is here!
    這裡的東西都好貴！

② How + 形容詞 + 主詞 + 一般動詞 / 助動詞

  1. How big the children have grown!
    孩子們都長這麼大了！
  2. How proud your parents must be!
    你爸媽一定好驕傲！
  3. How short the holidays always seem!
    假期總是感覺好短！
  4. How good this soup smells!
    這湯聞起來好香！
  5. How kind you have been to us!
    你一直對我們好好！

③ How + 形容詞 + to 不定詞

  1. How lovely to hear from you after so long!
    這麼久之後收到你的消息真開心！
  2. How nice to see you again!
    真高興再見到你！
  3. How kind of you to help!
    你真好心，還來幫忙！
  4. How strange to see snow in April!
    四月看到雪真奇怪！
  5. How wonderful to be home again!
    又回到家真好！

④ How + 形容詞 + 主詞 + be + to 不定詞

  1. How lucky the kids were to see a whale on the trip!
    孩子們在旅行中看到鯨魚，真幸運！
  2. How lucky we were to find a table!
    我們能找到位子真幸運！
  3. How brave she was to say no!
    她敢拒絕，真勇敢！
  4. How silly I was to believe him!
    我竟然相信他，真傻！
  5. How clever you are to fix it so fast!
    你這麼快就修好了，真聰明！

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. How happy I am to hear that!
    聽到這個消息我好高興！
  2. How sad it is that they moved away!
    他們搬走了，好難過！
  3. How different the town looks now!
    這個小鎮現在看起來好不一樣！
  4. How tired you must be after the trip!
    旅行回來你一定好累！
  5. How wonderful it would be to live by the sea!
    住在海邊該有多好！
  6. How small the world is!
    世界真小！
  7. How lovely your garden looks in spring!
    你的花園春天看起來好漂亮！
  8. How easy it was to find the hotel!
    飯店好好找！
  9. How good it feels to sleep in my own bed!
    睡在自己的床上感覺真好！
