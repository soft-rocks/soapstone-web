What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

我們需要的是一個針對年輕人的強力宣傳活動。

What 子⁠句先說⁠出話⁠題，焦⁠點放⁠在句⁠尾

我們需要的是一個針對年輕人的強力宣傳活動。

原來的句子是 We need a strong publicity campaign.

焦⁠點可⁠以是名⁠詞片⁠語、to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞或 that 子⁠句

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

名詞片語

What I want is a quiet weekend.

我想要的是一個安靜的週末。
to + 原形動詞

What tourists want is to see the famous sights.

觀光客想要的是去看有名的景點。
原形動詞

What you should do is call her right now.

你該做的是馬上打電話給她。
that 子句

What I like about the book is that it's easy to follow.

我喜歡這本書的地方是它很好懂。

be 通⁠常用單⁠數，焦⁠點是複⁠數名⁠詞⁠時也⁠可⁠以用複⁠數

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

單數

What students learn is mostly facts and theories.

學生學到的大多是事實和理論。
複數

What everyone loved were the special effects.

大家喜歡的是特效。

be 的時⁠態跟⁠著 What 子⁠句

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

現在式

What worries me is the cost.

讓我擔心的是費用。
過去式

What surprised me was the size of the crowd.

讓我意外的是人潮的規模。

What 子⁠句不⁠是問⁠句，不⁠加 do

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

What do we need is more time.

What we need is more time.

What 子⁠句後⁠面要⁠有 be

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

What I want a quiet weekend.

What I want is a quiet weekend.

What 補上子⁠句缺⁠的受⁠詞，That 不⁠行

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

That I need is a holiday.

What I need is a holiday.

What 子⁠句用 do，焦⁠點用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

What you should do is calling the police.

What you should do is call the police.

What 已⁠經是受⁠詞，子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

What companies care about it is money.

What companies care about is money.

it 分⁠裂句⁠的焦⁠點在⁠前，what 準分⁠裂句⁠的焦⁠點在句⁠尾

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

it 分裂句

It's a strong publicity campaign that we need.

我們需要的是一個強力的宣傳活動。
what 準分裂句

What I want is a quiet weekend.

我想要的是一個安靜的週末。

焦⁠點也⁠可⁠以放⁠在句⁠首，what 子⁠句放⁠在 be 後⁠面

What we need is a strong publicity campaign directed at young people.

一個安靜的週末就是我想要的。

例句

① 焦點是名詞片語

  1. What I need right now is a cup of coffee.
    我現在需要的是一杯咖啡。
  2. What companies care about is profit.
    公司在乎的是利潤。
  3. What this town lacks is a decent cinema.
    這個小鎮缺的是一家像樣的電影院。
  4. What she really wanted was a bit of respect.
    她真正想要的是一點尊重。
  5. What I like most about my job is the people.
    我最喜歡我工作的一點是那裡的人。
  6. What the team needs is a good coach.
    這支球隊需要的是一個好教練。
  7. What worries the parents is the traffic outside the school.
    讓家長擔心的是學校外面的車流。

② 焦點是 to + 原形動詞

  1. What tourists want is to see the real city.
    觀光客想要的是看到真實的城市。
  2. What I'd love is to spend a year in Spain.
    我很想做的是在西班牙待一年。
  3. What we'd like is to finish the project by spring.
    我們想要的是在春天前完成這個計畫。
  4. What he wanted was to be left alone.
    他想要的是別人不要管他。

③ 焦點是原形動詞（What 子句用 do）

  1. What you should do is talk to your teacher.
    你該做的是去找你的老師談談。
  2. What I did was call the bank straight away.
    我做的就是馬上打電話給銀行。
  3. What we'll do is meet at the station at six.
    我們就這樣：六點在車站碰面。
  4. What she does is check every report twice.
    她的做法是每份報告都檢查兩遍。
  5. What they did was paint the whole house in a weekend.
    他們做的就是在一個週末內把整間房子漆完。

④ 焦點是 that 子句

  1. What I found fascinating was that the story still feels fresh.
    我覺得很迷人的是，這個故事到現在還是很新鮮。
  2. What surprised everyone was that he said yes.
    讓大家意外的是他答應了。
  3. What I didn't know was that the shop had closed.
    我不知道的是那家店已經關了。
  4. What matters is that nobody was hurt.
    重要的是沒有人受傷。

⑤ be 用複數

  1. What the guests liked best were the desserts.
    客人最喜歡的是甜點。
  2. What I miss most are my friends from school.
    我最想念的是學校的朋友。
  3. What I remember are the long summer evenings.
    我記得的是那些漫長的夏日傍晚。
  4. What the kids enjoyed most were the animals.
    孩子們最喜歡的是那些動物。

⑥ be 用過去式

  1. What upset her was the way he spoke to the waiter.
    讓她不高興的是他跟服務生說話的態度。
  2. What made the trip special was the food.
    讓這趟旅行特別的是食物。
  3. What annoyed him was the noise from upstairs.
    讓他心煩的是樓上傳來的噪音。
  4. What we didn't expect was such a warm welcome.
    我們沒想到的是會受到這麼熱情的歡迎。

⑦ 焦點放在句首

  1. A good night's sleep is what you need.
    你需要的就是好好睡一覺。
  2. Honesty is what I value most in a friend.
    誠實是我最看重朋友的一點。
  3. That's exactly what I was going to say.
    那正是我要說的。
