例句

① in front of / next to

  1. The film starts at half past seven. Let's meet in front of the cinema at seven.
    電影七點半開始。我們七點在電影院前面碰面吧。
  2. You'll find it easily. There's a big tree in front of my school.
    你很容易找到。我學校前面有一棵大樹。
  3. My little sister always wants to sit next to Grandpa.
    我妹妹總是想坐在爺爺旁邊。
  4. Can you buy some bread when you post the letter? The bakery is next to the post office.
    你寄信的時候可以順便買麵包嗎？麵包店在郵局旁邊。
  5. Did you hear that noise? A taxi stopped in front of our house at midnight.
    你有聽到那個聲音嗎？半夜有一輛計程車停在我們家前面。
  6. I like my new room. My desk is next to the window.
    我喜歡我的新房間。我的書桌在窗戶旁邊。

② near to / close to

  1. Our flat is small, but we live close to the station.
    我們的公寓很小，但是住得離車站很近。
  2. I'm going for a run at lunch. Is your office near to the river?
    我午餐時間要去跑步。你的辦公室在河邊附近嗎？
  3. Bring your swimsuit. The campsite is close to a waterfall.
    帶你的泳衣。營地離一座瀑布很近。
  4. My grandparents live near to us, so they often pick up the kids from school.
    我爺爺奶奶住在我們家附近，所以他們常去學校接孩子。
  5. I'm tired of the long drive. I want a flat close to my work.
    我受夠了每天開那麼久的車。我想要一間離公司近的公寓。

③ such as

  1. I hate the gym. I like outdoor sports such as hiking and cycling.
    我討厭健身房。我喜歡戶外運動，像是健行和騎腳踏車。
  2. You can buy snacks such as chips and cookies at the counter.
    你可以在櫃台買零食，像是洋芋片和餅乾。
  3. Our new guide is great. She speaks several languages, such as French and Japanese.
    我們的新導遊很棒。她會說好幾種語言，像是法文和日文。
  4. The doctor told me to eat more vegetables such as carrots and spinach.
    醫生叫我多吃一點蔬菜，像是紅蘿蔔和菠菜。
  5. On our trip to Japan, we visited big cities such as Tokyo and Osaka.
    我們去日本旅行的時候，去了一些大城市，像是東京和大阪。

④ because of / instead of

  1. Grandma will arrive after lunch. The flight is late because of the fog.
    奶奶午餐後才會到。班機因為起霧誤點了。
  2. We have to go the long way. The road is closed because of a fallen tree.
    我們得繞遠路。那條路因為有一棵樹倒了而封閉。
  3. I'm trying to drink less sugar. Can I have water instead of soda?
    我想少喝一點糖。我可以喝水，不要汽水嗎？
  4. It's a lovely day. Let's walk instead of taking the bus.
    天氣很好。我們走路吧，不要搭公車。
  5. Sorry we missed the start of the meeting. Because of the traffic, we arrived late.
    抱歉我們錯過了會議的開頭。因為塞車，我們遲到了。

⑤ out of 和不同的情境

  1. When I opened the door, the cat jumped out of the box.
    我一開門，貓就從箱子裡跳出來。
  2. He took his phone out of his pocket and showed me the photo.
    他從口袋裡拿出手機，給我看那張照片。
  3. You don't need a taxi. There's a bus stop right in front of the hotel.
    你不用搭計程車。飯店前面就有一個公車站。
  4. Guess what? I sat next to a famous actor on the plane.
    你猜怎麼樣？我在飛機上坐在一位有名的演員旁邊。
  5. I can't take him for a walk. My dog won't go out because of the thunder.
    我沒辦法帶牠去散步。我的狗因為打雷不肯出門。
  6. I had steak for lunch, so I'll have fish instead of meat tonight.
    我午餐吃了牛排，所以今晚要吃魚，不吃肉。
  7. The park is close to my house, so I go there every day.
    公園離我家很近，所以我每天都去。
  8. Our guide was very late. We waited in front of the museum for an hour.
    我們的導遊遲到很久。我們在博物館前面等了一個小時。
  9. You'll enjoy it here. Our school offers clubs such as chess and drama.
    你會喜歡這裡的。我們學校有一些社團，像是西洋棋社和戲劇社。
