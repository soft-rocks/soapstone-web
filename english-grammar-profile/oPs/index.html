Is this area in the city or in the countryside?

這一區是在市區還是在鄉下？

or 也⁠可⁠以連⁠接兩⁠個同⁠一⁠種⁠的片⁠語

片⁠語可⁠以是名⁠詞片⁠語、動⁠詞片⁠語或形⁠容⁠詞片⁠語

例句

① 介系詞片語 + or + 介系詞片語

  1. I saw your flat online. Is this area in the city or in the countryside?
    我在網路上看到你的公寓。這一區是在市區還是在鄉下？
  2. I need to book a dentist appointment. Is the meeting in the morning or in the afternoon?
    我得預約看牙醫。會議在早上還是下午？
  3. I can't find my keys. Did you leave them on the table or in the car?
    我找不到鑰匙。你把它們留在桌上還是車上？
  4. You've booked our holiday. Is the hotel near the beach or in the town centre?
    你訂好我們的假期了。飯店在海灘附近還是在市中心？
  5. I want to study with you this week. Do you usually study at home or at the library?
    我這星期想跟你一起念書。你通常在家念書還是在圖書館念書？
  6. Are we meeting at the station or outside the cinema?
    我們是在車站見面還是在電影院外面？
  7. You have an interesting accent. Did you grow up in the north or in the south?
    你的口音很特別。你是在北部還是南部長大的？

② 名詞片語 + or + 名詞片語

  1. Both jackets fit you well. Do you want the red jacket or the blue one?
    兩件外套你穿都很合身。你要紅色那件還是藍色那件？
  2. Do you feel more sympathy for your friends or your parents?
    你比較同情你的朋友，還是你的父母？
  3. Would you prefer a quiet weekend or a big party for your birthday?
    你生日想要一個安靜的週末，還是一個盛大的派對？
  4. Is your brother the tall man in the hat or the one by the door?
    你哥哥是戴帽子那個高個子，還是門邊那個？
  5. Should we take the early train or the last one?
    我們該搭早班的火車還是最後一班？
  6. I'm booking our flights now. Do you want a window seat or an aisle seat?
    我現在在訂機票。你要靠窗的位子還是靠走道的位子？
  7. Is the party for her birthday or her new job?
    那個派對是為了她的生日，還是她的新工作？

③ 動詞片語 + or + 動詞片語

  1. Do you want to eat out or cook at home tonight?
    你今晚想去外面吃還是在家煮？
  2. Shall we take a taxi or walk to the hotel?
    我們要搭計程車還是走路去飯店？
  3. Did you lose your phone or leave it at work?
    你的手機是弄丟了還是留在公司了？
  4. Do you prefer to read the news or watch it on TV?
    你比較喜歡讀新聞還是看電視新聞？
  5. Would you rather stay in a hotel or rent a flat?
    你比較想住飯店還是租公寓？
  6. Should I send the letter by post or bring it to your office?
    我該把信寄給你，還是拿去你的辦公室？
  7. Do you want to play tennis or go for a swim?
    你想打網球還是去游泳？

④ 形容詞片語 + or + 形容詞片語

  1. You haven't touched your bowl. Is the soup too hot or just right?
    你都沒動你那碗。湯太燙還是剛剛好？
  2. You're walking strangely. Are the shoes too big or too small?
    你走路怪怪的。鞋子太大還是太小？
  3. Was the exam easier than last year or more difficult?
    這次考試比去年簡單還是比較難？
  4. Is your new boss easy to work with or very strict?
    你的新老闆好相處還是很嚴格？

⑤ 時間片語 + or + 時間片語

  1. Are you leaving this weekend or next week?
    你這個週末走還是下星期走？
  2. The police want to know when you saw him. Did you see him last night or this morning?
    警察想知道你什麼時候看到他。你是昨晚還是今天早上看到他的？
  3. Do you want to meet before lunch or after work?
    你想在午餐前見面還是下班後？

⑥ 用 Which 開頭的選擇疑問句

  1. Which do you like better, the book or the film?
    你比較喜歡哪一個，書還是電影？
  2. Which bus goes to the airport, the number 5 or the number 12?
    哪一班公車到機場，五號還是十二號？
  3. Which room is yours, the one upstairs or the one next to the kitchen?
    哪一間是你的房間，樓上那間還是廚房旁邊那間？
