We'll meet at my house at 3 o'clock. Don't be late!

我們 3 點在我家碰面。不要遲到！

說⁠完安⁠排，再⁠加一⁠句 Don't 提⁠醒對⁠方

We'll meet at my house at 3 o'clock. Don't be late!

火車八點開，別錯過了！

常⁠見⁠的提⁠醒

We'll meet at my house at 3 o'clock. Don't be late!

Don't forget

It's her eightieth birthday today. Call your grandmother tonight. Don't forget.

今天是她八十歲生日。今晚打給你奶奶，別忘了。
Don't lose it

Here's the key to the flat. Don't lose it.

這是公寓的鑰匙，別弄丟了。
Don't tell anyone

I'm getting married in May. Don't tell anyone yet.

我五月要結婚了，先別告訴任何人。

否⁠定祈⁠使⁠句用 Don't 開⁠頭

We'll meet at my house at 3 o'clock. Don't be late!

Bring your passport. Not forget!

Bring your passport. Don't forget!

late 是形⁠容⁠詞，前⁠面要加 be

We'll meet at my house at 3 o'clock. Don't be late!

See you at six. Don't late!

See you at six. Don't be late!

Don't 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We'll meet at my house at 3 o'clock. Don't be late!

Keep the key safe. Don't losing it.

Keep the key safe. Don't lose it.

例句

① ... Don't be late!

  1. The film starts at seven. Don't be late!
    電影七點開始，不要遲到！
  2. Class begins at 8:30 sharp. Don't be late.
    八點半準時上課，不要遲到。
  3. The bus leaves at noon. Don't be late, or it'll go without you.
    公車中午開，別遲到，不然它不會等你。
  4. Dinner is at six tonight. Don't be late again!
    今晚六點吃晚餐，別再遲到了！
  5. We'll pick you up at nine. Don't be late.
    我們九點去接你，不要遲到。

② ... Don't forget.

  1. The café is behind the bank, near the bus stop. Don't forget.
    咖啡店在銀行後面，公車站附近，別忘了。
  2. You have PE first thing tomorrow. Pack your gym shoes tonight. Don't forget!
    你明天第一堂是體育課。今晚把運動鞋收進包包，別忘了！
  3. It's her eightieth birthday today. Call your grandmother tonight. Don't forget.
    今天是她八十歲生日。今晚打給你奶奶，別忘了。
  4. The Wi-Fi password is sunny123. Don't forget it.
    無線網路的密碼是 sunny123，別忘了。
  5. The doctor gave me pills for my ear. Take your medicine after lunch. Don't forget!
    醫生開了治耳朵的藥給我。午餐後吃藥，別忘了！

③ ... Don't lose / Don't miss

  1. Here's the key to the flat. Don't lose it.
    這是公寓的鑰匙，別弄丟了。
  2. You need it to get out of the station. Keep your ticket. Don't lose it!
    出站要用它。車票收好，別弄丟了！
  3. The last train is at midnight. Don't miss it.
    最後一班火車在午夜，別錯過了。
  4. The sale ends on Sunday. Don't miss it!
    特賣星期天結束，別錯過了！
  5. My phone is new, and you don't have the number. Write down my number. Don't lose it.
    我的手機是新的，你沒有號碼。把我的號碼記下來，別弄丟了。

④ ... Don't tell / Don't touch

  1. The party is a surprise. Don't tell Ann!
    那個派對是驚喜，別告訴 Ann！
  2. I'm getting married in May. Don't tell anyone yet.
    我五月要結婚了，先別告訴任何人。
  3. The paint is still wet. Don't touch it.
    油漆還沒乾，別碰。
  4. The cake is for tomorrow. Don't eat it!
    蛋糕是明天要吃的，別吃！
  5. This is my sister's laptop. Don't open it.
    這是我姊姊的筆電，別打開。

⑤ ... Don't worry. 和其他提醒

  1. I'll meet you at the station. Don't worry.
    我會去車站接你，別擔心。
  2. Thanks for feeding the cat this weekend. Lock the door when you leave. Don't leave it open.
    謝謝你這個週末幫忙餵貓。離開時把門鎖上，別開著。
  3. It's snowing outside. Wear a warm coat. Don't catch a cold.
    外面在下雪。穿件保暖的外套，別感冒了。
  4. The road to the hotel has many sharp bends. Drive slowly on the mountain road. Don't rush.
    去飯店的路有很多急彎。山路上開慢一點，別急。
  5. Turn off the oven at four. Don't let the cake burn.
    四點把烤箱關掉，別讓蛋糕烤焦。
  6. The museum is very big. Wait for me by the gate. Don't go in alone.
    博物館很大。在門口等我，別自己進去。
  7. You know the job well. Speak slowly in the interview. Don't panic.
    你很懂這份工作。面試時說慢一點，別慌。
  8. We'll be away for two weeks in August. Water the plants every day. Don't let them dry out.
    我們八月要出門兩個星期。每天澆花，別讓它們乾掉。
  9. The road by the school is busy. Hold my hand when we cross. Don't run.
    學校旁邊的路車很多。過馬路時牽著我的手，別跑。
  10. The teacher handed out the test. Answer all the questions. Don't leave any blank.
    老師發了考卷。每一題都要回答，別空著。
