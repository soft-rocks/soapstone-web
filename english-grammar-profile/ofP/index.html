Did you know that the next Harry Potter movie is being filmed in my school?

你知道下一部《哈利波特》電影正在我們學校拍嗎？

is 加 being，再⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，說正⁠在被做⁠的事

下一部《哈利波特》電影正在我們學校拍

正⁠在做用 is being，常⁠常發⁠生用 is

Did you know that the next Harry Potter movie is being filmed in my school?

正在做

The road is being repaired.

路正在修。
常常發生

The road is repaired every summer.

這條路每年夏天都會修。

正⁠在做⁠的事要⁠有 being

Did you know that the next Harry Potter movie is being filmed in my school?

The film is filmed in my school right now.

The film is being filmed in my school right now.

being 前⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

Did you know that the next Harry Potter movie is being filmed in my school?

The road being repaired.

The road is being repaired.

being 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Did you know that the next Harry Potter movie is being filmed in my school?

The house is being painting.

The house is being painted.

例句

① is being + 過去分詞

  1. Did you know that the next Harry Potter movie is being filmed in my school?
    你知道下一部《哈利波特》電影正在我們學校拍嗎？
  2. We can't use the gym this week. The floor is being cleaned.
    我們這星期不能用體育館。地板正在清洗。
  3. Let's stay at my house and visit Tokyo, where an interesting history exhibition is being held.
    我們住我家，去東京玩吧，那裡正在辦一個很有意思的歷史展覽。
  4. Sorry, you can't take the bus today. The bridge is being repaired.
    抱歉，你今天不能搭公車。橋正在修。
  5. Look at all those cranes. A new hospital is being built next to the station.
    你看那些起重機。車站旁邊正在蓋一間新醫院。
  6. My laptop is being fixed, so I'm using my brother's.
    我的筆電正在修，所以我在用我哥哥的。
  7. Don't sit there! That chair is being painted.
    不要坐那裡！那張椅子正在上漆。

② are being + 過去分詞

  1. The TV people are at our school today. They're filming a class where new sports clothes are being tested.
    電視台的人今天在我們學校。他們在拍一個班，那個班正在試穿新的運動服。
  2. The streets near my flat are being dug up for new pipes.
    我家附近的街道正在挖，要換新的水管。
  3. Come and see the baby birds! They're being fed by their mother.
    快來看小鳥！鳥媽媽正在餵牠們。
  4. All the old trees in the park are being cut down. It's so sad.
    公園裡的老樹正在被砍掉。好可惜。
  5. Our exams are being marked this week, so we'll get the results on Monday.
    我們的考卷這星期在改，所以星期一會拿到成績。
  6. The rooms upstairs are being painted, so we're sleeping in the living room.
    樓上的房間正在粉刷，所以我們睡客廳。

③ am being + 過去分詞

  1. I'm being followed by a strange dog! It's been behind me for ten minutes.
    有一隻奇怪的狗在跟著我！牠已經在我後面十分鐘了。
  2. My mum is calling, but I can't answer. I'm being interviewed for the school newspaper.
    我媽媽打來了，可是我不能接。學校報紙正在訪問我。
  3. Sorry, I can't talk now. I'm being driven to the station by my dad.
    抱歉，我現在不能講。我爸爸正開車載我去車站。

④ 主詞是人

  1. Grandpa had a fall, but he's fine. He's being looked after by the nurses now.
    爺爺跌倒了，不過他沒事。現在護士正在照顧他。
  2. The teachers are busy this morning. The new students are being shown around the school.
    老師們今天早上很忙。他們正帶新生參觀學校。
  3. My brother loves cooking. He's being trained as a chef in a hotel in Paris.
    我哥哥很愛做菜。他正在巴黎一家飯店受訓當廚師。
  4. We got to the stadium early. The players are being given their new shirts.
    我們很早就到球場。球員們正在領新球衣。

⑤ 新聞和公告

  1. Prices have gone up a lot this year. As a result, the country's economy is being watched closely by many experts.
    今年物價漲了很多。因此，這個國家的經濟正受到很多專家密切關注。
  2. Please wait. Your call is being transferred.
    請稍候。您的電話正在轉接。
  3. The plane has landed, but passengers are being asked to stay in their seats.
    飛機降落了，可是乘客正被要求留在座位上。
  4. There's too much traffic on the old road. A new road is being planned between the two towns.
    舊的路車太多了。兩個鎮之間正在規劃一條新路。
  5. The forest fire is still burning. It's being fought by over a hundred firefighters.
    森林大火還在燒。一百多名消防員正在救火。

⑥ 問答

  1. "Where's your car?" "It's being washed at the garage."
    你的車呢？在修車廠洗。
  2. "Why is the road closed?" "A film is being made there."
    那條路為什麼封起來了？那裡在拍電影。
  3. "Is your kitchen finished?" "No, the new cupboards are being put in this week."
    你家廚房弄好了嗎？還沒，新櫃子這星期在裝。
  4. "What's that noise?" "The house next door is being knocked down."
    那是什麼聲音？隔壁的房子在拆。
  5. "Is dinner ready?" "Almost. The pasta is being cooked now."
    晚餐好了嗎？快了。義大利麵正在煮。
  6. "Can I use the meeting room?" "Not yet. It's being cleaned."
    我可以用會議室嗎？還不行。正在打掃。
