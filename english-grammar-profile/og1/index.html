It's the best place that I have ever seen.

這是我看過最棒的地方。

that 子⁠句用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式，說⁠到目⁠前⁠為⁠止⁠的經⁠驗

It's the best place that I have ever seen.

搬到台南是我做過最好的決定。

that 當受⁠詞⁠時可⁠以省⁠略

It's the best place that I have ever seen.

These are the best cookies my mum has ever baked.

這是我媽媽烤過最好吃的餅乾。

省略 that 的說法比較口語。

that 也⁠可⁠以當子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

It's the best place that I have ever seen.

It's the best restaurant that has ever opened in this street.

那是這條街上開過最好的餐廳。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

It's the best place that I have ever seen.

Grandma makes the best soup that I've ever ate.

Grandma makes the best soup that I've ever eaten.

關⁠係⁠代⁠名⁠詞用 that，不⁠用 what

It's the best place that I have ever seen.

This is the best coffee what I've ever had in Taipei.

This is the best coffee that I've ever had in Taipei.

that 已⁠經是受⁠詞，不⁠再⁠加 it

It's the best place that I have ever seen.

That's the best song that the band has ever written it.

That's the best song that the band has ever written.

例句

① the best + 名詞 + that + have ever

  1. Going to France for a year was the best decision that I've ever made.
    去法國待一年是我做過最好的決定。
  2. We've been to Japan four times. Kyoto is the best city that we have ever visited.
    我們去過日本四次。京都是我們去過最棒的城市。
  3. My dad told me to save a little every month. This is the best advice that anyone has ever given me.
    我爸爸叫我每個月存一點錢。這是別人給過我最好的建議。
  4. Ms Lopez always listens to our ideas. She's the best boss that I've ever worked for.
    羅培茲女士總是聽我們的想法。她是我遇過最好的老闆。
  5. We spent three days at a farm in the hills. It was the best trip that our class has ever taken.
    我們在山上的農場待了三天。那是我們班參加過最棒的一次旅行。
  6. My brother took this at my graduation. That's the best photo that he's ever taken of me.
    我哥哥在我畢業典禮上拍了這張。那是他幫我拍過最好看的照片。

② the best + 名詞 + have ever（省略 that）

  1. These are the best cookies my mum has ever baked.
    這是我媽媽烤過最好吃的餅乾。
  2. We won the league in her first year. She's the best coach our team has ever had.
    她來的第一年我們就贏了聯賽。她是我們隊有過最好的教練。
  3. The sand is white, and the water is clear. This is the best beach we've ever been to.
    沙子是白的，海水很清澈。這是我們去過最棒的海灘。
  4. My son scored three goals on Saturday. It was the best match he's ever played.
    我兒子星期六進了三球。那是他打過最好的一場比賽。
  5. That was the best speech I've ever heard at a wedding.
    那是我在婚禮上聽過最精彩的致詞。

③ the best + 名詞 + that + has ever

  1. They make their own bread and cakes. It's the best café that has ever opened near our office.
    他們自己做麵包和蛋糕。那是我們辦公室附近開過最好的咖啡店。
  2. The fans built a statue of him outside the stadium. He's the best player that has ever played for the club.
    球迷在球場外面為他立了一座雕像。他是這個俱樂部有過最好的球員。
  3. It won five awards this year. It's the best film that has ever come out of this studio.
    它今年得了五個獎。那是這家製片廠拍過最好的電影。
  4. Lisa wants to sell our cakes online. This is the best idea that has ever come from our team.
    麗莎想在網路上賣我們的蛋糕。這是我們團隊想過最好的點子。

④ the worst / the most + 形容詞 + 名詞 + that

  1. The wind took the roof off the school. It was the worst storm that we've ever had here.
    風把學校的屋頂吹走了。那是我們這裡遇過最嚴重的暴風雨。
  2. Dr Kim answered all my questions for half an hour. He's the most patient doctor that I've ever met.
    金醫生花了半個小時回答我所有的問題。他是我遇過最有耐心的醫生。
  3. I was flying over my school on a bicycle. That was the strangest dream that I've ever had.
    我騎著腳踏車飛過我的學校。那是我做過最奇怪的夢。
  4. My aunt paid two thousand dollars for it. It's the most expensive watch that she has ever bought.
    我阿姨花了兩千塊買的。那是她買過最貴的手錶。
  5. The roller coaster went upside down three times. That was the scariest ride that the kids have ever been on.
    雲霄飛車倒轉了三次。那是孩子們坐過最可怕的遊樂設施。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. My present for Dad is a watch again. What's the best gift that you've ever received?
    我送爸爸的禮物又是手錶。你收過最好的禮物是什麼？
  2. We're writing about school memories in class. Who's the best teacher you've ever had?
    我們在課堂上寫學校的回憶。你遇過最好的老師是誰？
  3. We finally reached the top of the mountain. Is this the best view that you've ever seen?
    我們終於到了山頂。這是你看過最美的景色嗎？
  4. We swam in the lake every day in 2015. That was the best summer that I can remember.
    2015 年我們每天都在湖裡游泳。那是我記得最棒的夏天。
  5. Grandpa's operation went well. It's the best news that we've had all year.
    爺爺的手術很順利。這是我們今年聽到最好的消息。
  6. You've travelled to so many countries. Tell me about the best holiday you've ever had.
    你去過那麼多國家。跟我說說你度過最棒的假期。
  7. I can't stop laughing. That's the best joke that I've heard in a long time.
    我笑得停不下來。那是我很久以來聽過最好笑的笑話。
  8. They played for three hours in the rain. It was the best concert that the band has ever given.
    他們在雨中演奏了三個小時。那是那個樂團辦過最好的演唱會。
  9. Your family moved a lot when you were young. Which was the best school that you've ever studied at?
    你小時候常常搬家。你讀過最好的學校是哪一所？
  10. The teacher smiled at Tom. That's the best answer that anyone has given so far.
    老師對湯姆笑了笑。那是目前為止最好的答案。
