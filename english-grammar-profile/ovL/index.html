couldn't 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，說過⁠去做不⁠到⁠的事

I couldn't buy the T-shirt because it was too expensive.

我買不起那件 T 恤，因為它太貴了。

說話者當時想買，卻做不到。

couldn't 常接找⁠不⁠到、睡⁠不⁠著、去不⁠了這⁠類動⁠作

I couldn't buy the T-shirt because it was too expensive.

找不到

I couldn't find my keys this morning.

她昨晚睡不著。

例句

① 主詞 + couldn't + 原形動詞

  1. I couldn't get the lid off the jar.
    我轉不開罐子的蓋子。
  2. She couldn't remember where she had parked.
    她想不起來車停在哪裡。
  3. We couldn't see anything in the dark.
    天太黑，我們什麼都看不到。
  4. He couldn't swim when he was little.
    他小時候不會游泳。
  5. They couldn't hear the teacher at the back of the room.
    他們坐在教室後面，聽不到老師說話。
  6. I couldn't understand the question.
    我看不懂那個問題。
  7. My dad couldn't fix the car.
    我爸爸修不好那輛車。
  8. The baby couldn't stop crying.
    寶寶一直哭個不停。
  9. I couldn't work without the key to the office.
    沒有辦公室的鑰匙，我沒辦法工作。
  10. We couldn't get tickets for the concert.
    我們買不到演唱會的票。

② couldn't + 原形動詞 + because + 原因

  1. I couldn't call you because my phone was dead.
    我沒辦法打給你，因為我的手機沒電了。
  2. She couldn't come to the party because she was sick.
    她沒辦法來派對，因為她生病了。
  3. We couldn't finish the game because it got dark.
    我們沒辦法把比賽打完，因為天黑了。
  4. He couldn't eat the cake because he is allergic to nuts.
    他不能吃那個蛋糕，因為他對堅果過敏。
  5. I couldn't sleep because the neighbours were too noisy.
    我睡不著，因為鄰居太吵了。
  6. They couldn't buy the house because it cost too much.
    他們買不起那間房子，因為太貴了。
  7. I couldn't read the sign because it was too far away.
    我看不清那個標誌，因為太遠了。
  8. She couldn't answer the phone because she was driving.
    她沒辦法接電話，因為她在開車。

③ couldn't + 原形動詞 + because of + 名詞

  1. We couldn't fly home because of the snow.
    因為下雪，我們沒辦法搭飛機回家。
  2. I couldn't concentrate because of the noise.
    因為太吵，我沒辦法專心。
  3. They couldn't play football because of the rain.
    因為下雨，他們沒辦法踢足球。
  4. She couldn't walk far because of her bad knee.
    因為膝蓋不好，她沒辦法走太遠。
  5. He couldn't see the stage because of the tall man in front of him.
    因為前面那個高個子，他看不到舞台。

④ couldn't + 原形動詞，前後說出結果

  1. It was so hot that I couldn't sleep.
    天氣熱得我睡不著。
  2. The box was too heavy. I couldn't lift it.
    那個箱子太重了。我抬不起來。
  3. I couldn't find a taxi, so I walked home.
    我叫不到計程車，所以走路回家。
  4. The water was too cold. We couldn't stay in for long.
    水太冷了。我們沒辦法在水裡待太久。
  5. She couldn't decide, so she bought both dresses.
    她決定不了，所以兩件洋裝都買了。
  6. The shop was closed, so I couldn't buy any bread.
    店關了，所以我沒買到麵包。
  7. The film was so sad that he couldn't stop crying.
    那部電影太感人了，他哭個不停。
