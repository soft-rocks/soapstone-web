I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

我會形容自己是個隨和、心胸開放的人。

形⁠容⁠詞 + 名⁠詞⁠-⁠ed，說人⁠的特⁠質

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

我們的新醫生心地很善良。

名⁠詞 + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，多⁠半說事⁠情給⁠人⁠的感⁠覺

那是讓人看到下巴都掉下來的景色。

副⁠詞 / 名⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，有被⁠動⁠的意⁠思

我們終於去了期待已久的旅行。

形⁠容⁠詞 + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，說人⁠的個⁠性

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

你對吃的東西很隨和嗎？

self⁠-⁠ 和固⁠定⁠的片⁠語

self- + 形容詞或過去分詞

She's a self-confident speaker.

她是一個很有自信的演講者。
固定的片語

She grew up in a well-to-do family.

她在一個有錢的家庭長大。

說人⁠的特⁠質，名⁠詞要加 ⁠-⁠ed

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

She is an open-mind person.

She is an open-minded person.

說東⁠西給⁠人⁠的感⁠覺，用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

They made an eye-caught poster.

They made an eye-catching poster.

放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面要加連⁠字⁠號

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

She gave a well prepared talk.

She gave a well-prepared talk.

例句

① 形容詞 + 名詞-ed

  1. Dr Patel always has time for the old patients. Our new doctor is kind-hearted.
    Patel 醫生總是有時間陪老病人。我們的新醫生心地很善良。
  2. My grandparents don't like new ideas, but my parents are more open-minded.
    我爺爺奶奶不喜歡新的想法，不過我爸媽心胸比較開放。
  3. Lily never gives up when a puzzle is hard. She's a strong-willed girl.
    Lily 碰到難的謎題從不放棄。她是個意志堅強的女孩。
  4. Don't ask the boss for anything before ten. He's short-tempered in the morning.
    十點以前別跟老闆要求任何事。他早上脾氣很差。
  5. My father is absent-minded. He forgets his keys almost every day.
    我爸爸很健忘。他幾乎每天都忘了帶鑰匙。
  6. Sam's girlfriend moved to Canada. He was broken-hearted when she left.
    Sam 的女朋友搬去加拿大了。她離開的時候，他很心碎。
  7. The animal shelter needs warm-hearted volunteers at weekends.
    動物收容所週末需要熱心的志工。
  8. My aunt thinks girls shouldn't play football. She's a narrow-minded person.
    我阿姨覺得女生不該踢足球。她是個心胸狹窄的人。

② 名詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. We reached the top of the mountain at sunrise. It was a jaw-dropping view.
    我們在日出時爬到山頂。那是讓人看到下巴都掉下來的景色。
  2. Let's stop at the bakery on the corner. It sells mouth-watering cakes.
    我們在轉角的麵包店停一下吧。那家店賣讓人流口水的蛋糕。
  3. The students advertised the school play with a bright red poster. It was really eye-catching.
    學生們用一張鮮紅色的海報宣傳學校的話劇。那張海報很吸引人。
  4. We talked about the film all the way home. It was a thought-provoking story.
    我們回家的路上一直在談那部電影。那是一個讓人深思的故事。
  5. Our team lost the final in the last minute. It was heart-breaking.
    我們隊在最後一分鐘輸掉決賽。那真讓人心碎。
  6. Over ninety thousand people came to the match. It was a record-breaking crowd.
    有超過九萬人來看那場比賽。觀眾人數破了紀錄。
  7. A boy found my wallet and brought it to the police. It was heart-warming news.
    一個男孩撿到我的皮夾，送到警察局。那是溫暖人心的消息。

③ 副詞 / 名詞 + 過去分詞

  1. We saved for three years. Last summer we finally went on our long-awaited trip to Japan.
    我們存了三年的錢。去年夏天，我們終於去了期待已久的日本之旅。
  2. Before you use the pool, please read the above-mentioned rules.
    使用游泳池之前，請閱讀上面提到的規則。
  3. Clara always knows the news first. She's a well-informed reporter.
    Clara 總是第一個知道新聞。她是一個消息靈通的記者。
  4. Nobody lives in Elm Hollow now. It's a long-forgotten village in the hills.
    現在沒有人住在 Elm Hollow 了。那是山裡一個被遺忘很久的村子。
  5. The kids came home from camp and ate a home-cooked dinner.
    孩子們從營隊回家，吃了一頓家常晚餐。
  6. Grandma collects old plates. She has twenty hand-painted plates on the kitchen wall.
    奶奶收集舊盤子。她廚房的牆上有二十個手繪的盤子。

④ self- 和固定片語

  1. Ann won the speech contest last year. She's a self-confident speaker.
    Ann 去年得了演講比賽冠軍。她是一個很有自信的演講者。
  2. My uncle doesn't have a boss. He's self-employed.
    我叔叔沒有老闆。他自己當老闆。
  3. Mrs Hart had a pony when she was a child. She grew up in a well-to-do family.
    Hart 太太小時候有一匹小馬。她在一個有錢的家庭長大。
  4. Tom only talks about himself. He seems very self-centred.
    Tom 只會講他自己。他看起來很自我中心。
  5. The Smiths have a big house by the lake. They're well-to-do people.
    Smith 一家在湖邊有一棟大房子。他們是有錢人。

⑤ 不同的句子

  1. I'm cooking dinner for your visit. Are you easy-going about food?
    我要為你的來訪煮晚餐。你對吃的東西很隨和嗎？
  2. I didn't guess the killer in the book. The ending was truly jaw-dropping.
    我沒猜到書裡的兇手。結局真的讓人吃驚。
  3. Our café is growing fast. We want open-minded and hard-working staff.
    我們的咖啡館發展得很快。我們想要心胸開放又勤奮的員工。
  4. Everyone looked at Mia's red dress. It was the most eye-catching dress at the party.
    大家都在看 Mia 的紅洋裝。那是派對上最吸引人的洋裝。
