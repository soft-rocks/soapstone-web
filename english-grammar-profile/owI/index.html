I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

我會形容自己是個隨和、心胸開放的人。

形⁠容⁠詞 + 名⁠詞⁠-⁠ed，說人⁠的特⁠質

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

我們的新醫生心地很善良。

名⁠詞 + 動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，說事⁠情給⁠人⁠的感⁠覺

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

那是讓人看到下巴都掉下來的景色。

副⁠詞 / 形⁠容⁠詞 + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，說東⁠西被怎⁠麼⁠樣了

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

我們終於去了期待已久的旅行。

self⁠-⁠ 和固⁠定⁠的片⁠語

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

self- + 形容詞

She is very self-confident.

她很有自信。
固定的片語

They come from a well-to-do family.

他們出身有錢人家。

說人⁠的特⁠質，名⁠詞要加 ⁠-⁠ed

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

She is an open-mind person.

She is an open-minded person.

說東⁠西給⁠人⁠的感⁠覺，用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

They made an eye-caught poster.

They made an eye-catching poster.

放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面要加連⁠字⁠號

I would describe myself as an easy-going and open-minded person.

We went on a long awaited trip.

We went on a long-awaited trip.

例句

① 形容詞 + 名詞-ed

  1. Our new doctor is kind-hearted.
    我們的新醫生心地很善良。
  2. Young people are often open-minded.
    年輕人通常心胸比較開放。
  3. She is a strong-willed girl.
    她是個意志堅強的女孩。
  4. Our boss is short-tempered in the morning.
    我們老闆早上脾氣很差。
  5. My father is absent-minded and forgets his keys.
    我爸爸很健忘，常常忘了帶鑰匙。
  6. He was broken-hearted when she left.
    她離開的時候，他很心碎。
  7. They need warm-hearted volunteers.
    他們需要熱心的志工。
  8. She is a narrow-minded person.
    她是個心胸狹窄的人。

② 名詞 + 動詞-ing

  1. It was a jaw-dropping view.
    那是讓人看到下巴都掉下來的景色。
  2. The bakery sells mouth-watering cakes.
    那家麵包店賣讓人流口水的蛋糕。
  3. They made an eye-catching poster.
    他們做了一張很吸引人的海報。
  4. It was a thought-provoking movie.
    那是一部讓人深思的電影。
  5. Losing the final was heart-breaking.
    輸掉決賽讓人很心碎。
  6. The game had a record-breaking crowd.
    那場比賽的觀眾人數破了紀錄。
  7. We heard some heart-warming news.
    我們聽到一些溫暖人心的消息。

③ 副詞 / 形容詞 + 過去分詞

  1. We finally went on our long-awaited trip.
    我們終於去了期待已久的旅行。
  2. Please read the above-mentioned rules.
    請閱讀上面提到的規則。
  3. She is a well-informed reporter.
    她是一個消息靈通的記者。
  4. It is a long-forgotten village.
    那是一個被遺忘很久的村子。
  5. They ate a home-cooked dinner.
    他們吃了一頓家常晚餐。
  6. The museum has many hand-painted plates.
    那間博物館有很多手繪的盤子。

④ self- 和固定片語

  1. Ann is a self-confident speaker.
    Ann 是一個很有自信的演講者。
  2. My uncle is self-employed.
    我叔叔是自己當老闆。
  3. She grew up in a well-to-do family.
    她在一個有錢的家庭長大。
  4. He seems very self-centered.
    他看起來很自我中心。
  5. They are well-to-do people with a big house.
    他們是住大房子的有錢人。

⑤ 不同的句子

  1. Are you easy-going about food?
    你對吃的東西很隨和嗎？
  2. The ending was truly jaw-dropping.
    結局真的讓人吃驚。
  3. We want open-minded and hard-working staff.
    我們想要心胸開放又勤奮的員工。
  4. That was the most eye-catching dress at the party.
    那是派對上最吸引人的洋裝。
