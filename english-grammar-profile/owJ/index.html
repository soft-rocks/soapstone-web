例句

① this week / this month / this year

  1. I've worked late every night since Monday. It's been a long week.
    我從星期一起每天晚上都加班。這個禮拜好漫長。
  2. Good news from the sales team. We've sold more cars this month than last month.
    業務部有好消息。我們這個月賣的車比上個月多。
  3. I joined a book club in January. I've read four books this year.
    我一月參加了讀書會。我今年讀了四本書。
  4. The manager is angry with Tom. He's been late three times this week.
    經理在生湯姆的氣。他這個禮拜已經遲到三次了。
  5. We're top of the league. The team has played really well this season.
    我們在聯賽排第一。球隊這一季打得很好。
  6. A coffee costs four pounds now. Prices have gone up a lot this year.
    現在一杯咖啡要四英鎊。今年物價漲了很多。
  7. I've had a lot of night shifts. I've hardly seen my kids this week.
    我上了很多夜班。我這個禮拜幾乎沒見到孩子。

② today

  1. My watch counts my steps. I've walked ten thousand steps today.
    我的手錶會算步數。我今天走了一萬步。
  2. You look pale. Have you eaten anything today?
    你臉色很蒼白。你今天有吃東西嗎？
  3. My mum is worried about the party. She's called me five times today.
    我媽媽在擔心派對的事。她今天打了五次電話給我。
  4. I'm going to have a cold shower. It's been really hot today.
    我要去沖個冷水澡。今天好熱。
  5. I've been in meetings since nine. I haven't checked my email today.
    我從九點就一直在開會。我今天還沒看電子郵件。
  6. The café was full at lunch. We've had a lot of customers today.
    咖啡店中午客滿。我們今天客人很多。

③ so far / recently

  1. We're halfway through our trip to Spain. The holiday has been wonderful so far.
    我們的西班牙之旅過了一半。到目前為止，這趟假期很棒。
  2. The school is collecting money for a new library. We've raised two hundred pounds so far.
    學校在為新圖書館募款。到目前為止，我們募到兩百英鎊。
  3. We're going to visit him on Sunday. My grandfather hasn't been very well recently.
    我們星期天要去看他。我爺爺最近身體不太好。
  4. I want to go to the cinema tonight. Have you seen any good films recently?
    我今晚想去看電影。你最近有看什麼好電影嗎？
  5. Our busy season ended in March. Things have been quiet at work recently.
    我們的旺季三月就結束了。最近工作上很清閒。

④ 不說出時間：從情境知道

  1. Thank you for the wonderful dinner. This evening has been lovely.
    謝謝你這頓很棒的晚餐。今晚很愉快。
  2. Thanks for answering all my questions about the flat. You've been very helpful.
    謝謝你回答我關於公寓的所有問題。你幫了很大的忙。
  3. We're packing up the tent on Sunday afternoon. It's been a sunny, warm weekend.
    我們星期天下午在收帳篷。這個週末又晴朗又溫暖。
  4. Welcome home. The children have been so good, and they're asleep now.
    歡迎回家。孩子們一直都很乖，現在已經睡了。

⑤ 還沒結束和已經結束

  1. It's only eleven o'clock, and I've had three meetings this morning.
    現在才十一點，我今天早上已經開了三個會。
  2. It's four in the afternoon now. I missed the bus this morning, so I was late for work.
    現在是下午四點。我今天早上沒趕上公車，所以上班遲到了。
  3. It's only July, and we've had a lot of rain this summer.
    現在才七月，今年夏天雨已經下得很多了。
  4. We had to buy new plants in the spring. Our garden flooded twice last winter.
    我們春天得買新的植物。我們的花園去年冬天淹了兩次水。
  5. She's at the doctor's again. My sister has been ill a lot this year.
    她又去看醫生了。我妹妹今年常常生病。
  6. He's healthy now. My uncle was in hospital for a month last year.
    他現在很健康。我叔叔去年住院住了一個月。

⑥ 這段時間裡發生了很多次

  1. We have to start the report again. My boss has changed the plan three times today.
    我們得重寫報告。我老闆今天把計畫改了三次。
  2. The storms keep cutting the lines. We've had four power cuts this month.
    暴風雨一直弄斷電線。我們這個月停了四次電。
  3. I need to check my work more carefully. I've made the same mistake twice this week.
    我得更仔細檢查我的工作。這個禮拜我同一個錯犯了兩次。
