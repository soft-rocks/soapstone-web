We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

我們 6:30 會在 River's Cafe 等你。

正⁠式⁠的郵⁠件寫完⁠整⁠的 will

We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

我們 6:30 會在 River's Cafe 等你。

對方到的時候，等的動作已經開始。

主⁠詞是 I 和 we 時，will 可⁠以換⁠成 shall

We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

will 和 shall 都縮⁠寫成 'll

We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

我星期六會上班。

shall 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠的 be

We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

We shall to be waiting for you at the station.

We shall be waiting for you at the station.

shall 後⁠面先接 be，再⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

I shall attending class on Monday.

I shall be attending class on Monday.

shall 後⁠面⁠的 be 用原⁠形

We will be waiting for you at the River's Cafe at 6:30.

We shall are leaving at dawn.

We shall be leaving at dawn.

例句

① 主詞 + will be + 動詞-ing

  1. Our guide will be meeting you at the hotel entrance.
    我們的導遊會在飯店門口和你會合。
  2. The minister will be visiting the school next Tuesday.
    部長下星期二會來參觀學校。
  3. Passengers will be boarding through gate twelve.
    乘客會從十二號登機門登機。
  4. The board will be discussing the new budget all afternoon.
    董事會整個下午都會在討論新的預算。
  5. Your teacher will be marking the essays over the weekend.
    你們老師週末會在改作文。
  6. The museum will be closing early on Christmas Eve.
    博物館平安夜會提早關門。
  7. Two professors will be reviewing your dissertation.
    兩位教授會審查你的論文。
  8. The trains will be running every ten minutes during the festival.
    節慶期間火車每十分鐘一班。
  9. Our staff will be wearing green shirts, so you can find them easily.
    我們的工作人員會穿綠色上衣，所以你很容易找到他們。
  10. The new manager will be starting on the first of June.
    新經理六月一日會開始上班。
  11. Ann will be giving the opening speech.
    Ann 會發表開幕致詞。
  12. The doctors will be operating on him for about three hours.
    醫生會替他動大約三個小時的手術。
  13. You will be sharing a room with two other students.
    你會和另外兩個學生共住一間房。
  14. The builders will be furnishing the flats with new kitchens.
    建商會替這些公寓裝上新廚房。
  15. The company will be launching its new app in the autumn.
    公司秋天會推出新的應用程式。

② I / we + shall be + 動詞-ing

  1. I shall be attending class the day after tomorrow.
    我後天會去上課。
  2. We shall be travelling through France in May.
    我們五月會在法國各地旅行。
  3. I shall be thinking of you all on Friday.
    星期五我會想著你們大家。
  4. We shall be leaving at dawn, so pack your bags tonight.
    我們天一亮就要出發，所以今晚把行李收好。
  5. I shall be working at home for the rest of the week.
    這星期剩下的幾天我都會在家工作。
  6. We shall be holding the meeting in the main hall.
    我們會在大禮堂開會。
  7. I shall be writing to the director about this.
    這件事我會寫信給主任。
  8. We shall be staying in Edinburgh until the end of August.
    我們會在 Edinburgh 待到八月底。
  9. I shall be speaking to your parents this evening.
    今天晚上我會跟你父母談一談。
  10. We shall be celebrating our fiftieth anniversary in July.
    我們七月要慶祝結婚五十週年。

③ 主詞 + 'll be + 動詞-ing

  1. We'll be landing in about twenty minutes.
    我們大約二十分鐘後會降落。
  2. She'll be studying abroad next year.
    她明年會出國念書。
  3. They'll be playing at home on Saturday.
    他們星期六會在主場比賽。
  4. I'll be keeping an eye on the kids while you're out.
    你出去的時候，我會幫你看著孩子。
  5. He'll be joining us later for dessert.
    他晚一點會來跟我們一起吃甜點。
  6. You'll be hearing from our lawyer soon.
    你很快就會收到我們律師的消息。
