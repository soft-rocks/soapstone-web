Why don't you wear your black suit?

你何不穿你那套黑色西裝？

Why don't you 不⁠是問原⁠因，是給建⁠議

例句

① Why don't you ...?

  1. You look great in it. Why don't you wear your black suit?
    你穿那套很好看。你何不穿你那套黑色西裝？
  2. You're always tired. Why don't you go to bed earlier?
    你總是很累。你何不早點睡？
  3. Why don't you ask your teacher? She can explain it.
    你何不問問你的老師？她可以解釋給你聽。
  4. If the shoes hurt, why don't you take them back to the shop?
    如果鞋子會痛，你何不拿回店裡退？
  5. You love animals. Why don't you work at the zoo this summer?
    你很愛動物。你今年夏天何不去動物園打工？
  6. Why don't you call her? She's probably worried about you.
    你何不打給她？她可能在擔心你。
  7. Your room is so dark. Why don't you paint the walls white?
    你的房間好暗。你何不把牆漆成白色？
  8. You've worked all day. Why don't you sit down and have a cup of tea?
    你工作了一整天。你何不坐下來喝杯茶？

② Why don't we ...?

  1. The film ends at ten. Why don't we go to a disco after that?
    電影十點結束。之後我們何不去跳舞？
  2. It's a lovely day. Why don't we have lunch in the garden?
    天氣真好。我們何不在花園吃午餐？
  3. The bus is very slow. Why don't we take a taxi?
    公車好慢。我們何不搭計程車？
  4. Your office is near the station. Why don't we meet at the café there?
    你辦公室在車站附近。我們何不在那裡的咖啡店碰面？
  5. We're all free on Sunday. Why don't we visit Grandma?
    我們星期天都有空。我們何不去看奶奶？
  6. They don't know anyone here yet. Why don't we invite the new neighbours for dinner?
    他們在這裡還不認識任何人。我們何不邀請新鄰居來吃晚餐？
  7. I'm bored. Why don't we play a game?
    我好無聊。我們何不來玩個遊戲？

③ ..., if you want

  1. The film finishes at seven. We could have dinner after that, if you want.
    電影七點結束。你要的話，我們之後可以去吃晚餐。
  2. You look stuck. I can help you with your homework, if you want.
    你看起來卡住了。你要的話，我可以幫你寫功課。
  3. You can stay with us, if you want. We have a spare room.
    你要的話可以住我們家。我們有一間空房。
  4. The buses are so slow. If you want, I can lend you my bike.
    公車好慢。你要的話，我可以把腳踏車借你。
  5. It's going to be sunny. We can go to the beach tomorrow, if you want.
    明天會出太陽。你要的話，我們明天可以去海邊。

④ ..., if you like

  1. You've never seen the old castle? We can go there, if you like.
    你沒看過那座古堡嗎？你想的話，我們可以去那裡。
  2. Your flight lands at midnight. I can pick you up at the airport, if you like.
    你的班機半夜才到。你想的話，我可以去機場接你。
  3. Is your battery dead? You can use my phone, if you like.
    你的手機沒電了嗎？你想的話，可以用我的手機。
  4. It's raining too hard to go out. If you like, we can watch a film tonight.
    雨太大出不了門。你想的話，我們今晚可以看電影。
  5. It's your first visit. I can show you around the town, if you like.
    這是你第一次來。你想的話，我可以帶你在鎮上逛逛。

⑤ 回應建議

  1. "Why don't we go swimming?" "Good idea! Let's go."
    我們何不去游泳？好主意！走吧。
  2. "Why don't you try the soup?" "No, thanks. I'm not hungry."
    你何不嚐嚐這個湯？不用了，謝謝。我不餓。
  3. "I can carry that, if you like." "Thanks, that's very kind."
    你想的話我可以幫你拿。謝謝，你人真好。
  4. "Why don't we leave early?" "OK, let's go at six."
    我們何不早點走？好，六點走。
  5. "We can eat out tonight, if you want." "That sounds great."
    你要的話，我們今晚可以去外面吃。聽起來很棒。
