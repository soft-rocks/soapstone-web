How much money must I bring with me?

我得帶多少錢去？

Must + 主⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

How much money must I bring with me?

Must I我必須……嗎？
疑問詞what、when、how much 這類詞放在句首。
原形動詞問的那件事。

用 or 列⁠出兩⁠個選⁠擇

How much money must I bring with me?

例句

Can I change the date, or must I cancel?

我可以改日期嗎，還是我必須取消？

must 的問句常跟另一個問句用 or 連在一起。

must 的問⁠句不⁠用 do

How much money must I bring with me?

Do I must pay now?

Must I pay now?

問⁠句把 must 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

How much money must I bring with me?

How much money I must bring?

How much money must I bring?

must 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

How much money must I bring with me?

Must we to wear a tie at the company dinner?

Must we wear a tie at the company dinner?

例句

① 疑問詞 + must I + 原形動詞？：問規定

  1. Finally, how much money must I bring with me on the course?
    最後，上課期間我必須帶多少錢？
  2. Thank you for the plane ticket. What time must I be at the airport?
    謝謝你們的機票。我必須幾點到機場？
  3. I'd like to apply for the summer job at your hotel. Which form must I fill in?
    我想應徵你們飯店的暑期工作。我必須填哪一張表格？
  4. I'd like to book the room for the first week of July. When must I send the money?
    我想訂七月第一週的房間。我必須什麼時候匯錢？
  5. I'm driving from France to Switzerland next week. What documents must I show at the border?
    我下星期要從法國開車去瑞士。在邊境我必須出示什麼證件？
  6. I'm interested in the job at your café. How many hours must I work each week?
    我對你們咖啡店的工作有興趣。我每個星期必須工作幾個小時？

② …, or must I + 原形動詞？：問另一個選擇

  1. I'm ill and can't travel. Is it possible to use the prize next year, or must I cancel my trip to India?
    我生病了，沒辦法旅行。獎品可以明年用嗎，還是我必須取消去印度的旅行？
  2. I'd like to buy two tickets for the show. Can I pay by card, or must I pay in cash?
    我想買兩張表演的票。我可以刷卡嗎，還是我必須付現金？
  3. I don't eat meat or fish. Can I bring my own food, or must I eat in the hotel?
    我不吃肉也不吃魚。我可以自己帶食物嗎，還是我必須在飯店吃？
  4. I'm afraid of lifts. Can I choose my room, or must I take the one you give me?
    我很怕搭電梯。我可以選房間嗎，還是我必須住你們給我的那間？
  5. I live two hours from your school. Is the course online, or must I come to the school?
    我住的地方離你們學校兩個小時。這門課是線上的嗎，還是我必須去學校？
  6. I've finished the application. Can I send the form by email, or must I post it?
    我填好申請表了。我可以用電子郵件寄表格嗎，還是我必須郵寄？

③ Must I + 原形動詞？：問是不是必須

  1. I'm staying at your hostel in May. Must I bring my own towels, or do you provide them?
    我五月要住你們的青年旅館。我必須自己帶毛巾嗎，還是你們會提供？
  2. I'd like to visit the castle on Saturday. Must I book in advance?
    我想星期六去參觀城堡。我必須事先預約嗎？
  3. I'm joining the camping trip in August. Must I bring a sleeping bag, or are there beds?
    我八月要參加露營。我必須帶睡袋嗎，還是那裡有床？
  4. The language course costs eight hundred pounds. Must I pay the whole price now?
    語言課程要八百英鎊。我現在必須付全額嗎？
  5. I'm a university student. Must I show my student card to get the discount?
    我是大學生。我必須出示學生證才能拿到折扣嗎？
  6. I failed the driving test by two points. Must I take the whole test again?
    我差兩分沒通過駕照考試。我必須整個重考嗎？

④ Must we / you + 原形動詞？

  1. The party has only just started. Must we leave so early?
    派對才剛開始。我們一定要這麼早走嗎？
  2. It's midnight and the baby is asleep. Must you play your music so loud?
    現在半夜了，寶寶在睡覺。你一定要把音樂放這麼大聲嗎？
  3. Our flat is tiny. Must we invite all your cousins to the dinner?
    我們的公寓很小。晚餐一定要邀請你所有的表親嗎？
  4. The film started ten minutes ago. Must you always be late?
    電影十分鐘前就開始了。你一定要每次都遲到嗎？
  5. I'm so tired, and the report is long. Must we finish it today?
    我好累，報告又很長。我們一定要今天做完嗎？
  6. We're having dinner together. Must you check your phone every five minutes?
    我們在一起吃晚餐。你一定要每五分鐘就看一次手機嗎？

⑤ Why must + 主詞 + 原形動詞？：表示不滿

  1. I only asked about your day. Why must you always argue with me?
    我只是問你今天過得怎樣。你為什麼總是要跟我吵？
  2. I was late for work again today. Why must the trains be late every morning?
    我今天上班又遲到了。為什麼火車每天早上都要誤點？
  3. Ben and Sara never help in the kitchen. Why must I do all the washing-up?
    Ben 和 Sara 從來不幫忙廚房的事。為什麼所有的碗都要我洗？
  4. The forest is full of birds and deer. Why must they build a road through it?
    森林裡都是鳥和鹿。為什麼他們一定要在那裡蓋路？
  5. I planned a picnic for Saturday, and it's raining again. Why must it rain every weekend?
    我計畫星期六去野餐，結果又下雨了。為什麼每個週末都要下雨？
  6. I nearly fell over again. Why must you leave your shoes in the hall?
    我又差點跌倒了。你為什麼一定要把鞋子放在走廊？
  7. My appointment was at ten, and it's now eleven. Why must we wait so long at the doctor's?
    我約的是十點，現在已經十一點了。我們在診所為什麼要等這麼久？
