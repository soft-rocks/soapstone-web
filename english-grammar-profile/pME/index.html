The country's population is not likely to increase fast enough.

你不一定要請他們來家裡。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are + not + likely / certain / sure / obliged / bound + to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

not likely to不太可能。
not certain to / not sure to不一定會。
not obliged to沒有義務、不必。
not bound to不一定會。

not 的位⁠置

The country's population is not likely to increase fast enough.

例句

Its good effects are not certain to outweigh the bad ones.

它的好處不一定大過壞處。
例句

The bus isn't likely to come before nine.

公車不太可能在九點前來。

not 放在 be 動詞後面，形容詞前面。口語常用縮寫 isn't、aren't。

not 放⁠在 be 動⁠詞後⁠面

The country's population is not likely to increase fast enough.

Prices not are likely to fall.

Prices are not likely to fall.

likely 是形⁠容⁠詞，否⁠定用 be 動⁠詞加 not

The country's population is not likely to increase fast enough.

The plan doesn't likely to succeed.

The plan is not likely to succeed.

形⁠容⁠詞後⁠面要接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

The country's population is not likely to increase fast enough.

She isn't certain win the prize.

She isn't certain to win the prize.

例句

① 主詞 + be + not + likely + to + 原形動詞：不太可能

  1. The old factory is not likely to open again.
    那間舊工廠不太可能再開工了。
  2. The rain isn't likely to stop before the evening, so take an umbrella.
    雨在傍晚前不太可能停，帶把傘吧。
  3. My boss is not likely to give me a day off next week. We're too busy.
    我老闆下星期不太可能讓我休假。我們太忙了。
  4. The new road isn't likely to be ready before the summer.
    新的道路在夏天前不太可能完工。
  5. The doctor says my grandfather is not likely to need an operation.
    醫生說我爺爺不太可能需要開刀。
  6. The team aren't likely to score many goals without their best player.
    少了最好的球員，球隊不太可能進很多球。
  7. Our landlord isn't likely to lower the rent, but I'll ask him anyway.
    我們的房東不太可能降房租，可是我還是會問他。

② 主詞 + be + not + certain + to + 原形動詞：不一定會

  1. As for tourism, its positive effects are not certain to outweigh the negative ones.
    說到觀光業，它的正面影響不一定大過負面影響。
  2. A good degree is not certain to get you a good job these days.
    現在有好學歷不一定能找到好工作。
  3. The ferry isn't certain to run if the wind gets stronger.
    風再大一點，渡輪不一定會開。
  4. A famous actor is not certain to make the film a hit.
    有名演員不一定能讓電影賣座。
  5. Cheap flights aren't certain to save you money once you pay for bags.
    付了行李費以後，便宜機票不一定能幫你省錢。
  6. More police on the streets are not certain to reduce crime.
    街上多一點警察不一定能減少犯罪。
  7. Your old phone isn't certain to work with the new charger.
    你的舊手機不一定能用新的充電器。

③ 主詞 + be + not + obliged + to + 原形動詞：沒有義務

  1. You don't get on with your cousins, so you are not obliged to invite them over.
    你跟你的表親處不來，所以你不必邀請他們來家裡。
  2. Shoppers are not obliged to give their email address at the till.
    購物的人在收銀台不必留電子郵件地址。
  3. You aren't obliged to reply to every message from your manager at night.
    晚上你不必回覆經理傳來的每一則訊息。
  4. Volunteers are not obliged to work every weekend.
    志工不必每個週末都來工作。
  5. Hotel guests aren't obliged to leave their passports at the desk.
    飯店的客人不必把護照留在櫃台。
  6. As a visitor, you are not obliged to join in the prayers.
    你是訪客，不必跟著一起禱告。
  7. Readers aren't obliged to pay for the online version of the paper.
    讀者看報紙的網路版不必付錢。

④ 主詞 + be + not + bound + to + 原形動詞：沒有義務

  1. You are not bound to accept the first offer you get.
    你不一定要接受第一個報價。
  2. The company is not bound to give you a refund after thirty days.
    三十天後，公司沒有義務退款給你。
  3. Witnesses are not bound to answer questions from reporters.
    證人沒有義務回答記者的問題。
  4. Tenants aren't bound to stay for the whole year.
    房客不必住滿一整年。
  5. The new owner is not bound to keep the old staff.
    新老闆沒有義務留下原來的員工。

⑤ 主詞 + be + not + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞：作文和報告的判斷

  1. Higher taxes on cigarettes are not likely to make everyone stop smoking.
    提高香菸稅不太可能讓每個人都戒菸。
  2. New factories are not certain to bring jobs to the local people.
    新工廠不一定會為當地人帶來工作。
  3. Online classes aren't likely to replace teachers completely.
    線上課程不太可能完全取代老師。
  4. The ban on plastic bags is not certain to solve the problem of waste.
    禁用塑膠袋不一定能解決垃圾問題。
  5. Small companies are not obliged to publish their accounts every year.
    小公司不必每年公布帳目。
