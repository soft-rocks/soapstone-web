I personally think going to a large school is quite a good idea.

我個人覺得去讀大型學校是個滿好的主意。

quite 放⁠在 a 前⁠面

I personally think going to a large school is quite a good idea.

湖離這裡滿遠的。

a 或 an 看後⁠面形⁠容⁠詞⁠的發⁠音

I personally think going to a large school is quite a good idea.

quite a + 子音

She has quite a big family. There are six kids.

她家人口滿多的，有六個小孩。
quite an + 母音

We live in quite an old house near the river.

我們住在河邊一棟相當老的房子。

複⁠數和不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面沒⁠有 a

I personally think going to a large school is quite a good idea.

複數名詞

They're quite nice people.

他們是滿好的人。
不可數名詞

我們上禮拜天氣還算不錯。

quite a long time 說時⁠間相⁠當久

I personally think going to a large school is quite a good idea.

I've been waiting for quite a long time.

我已經等了滿久了。

quite 不⁠放⁠在 a 後⁠面

母⁠音開⁠頭⁠的形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面用 an

It's quite an interesting book.

複⁠數名⁠詞前⁠面不⁠加 a

例句

① quite a + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. Let's drive. The lake is quite a long way from here.
    開車吧，湖離這裡滿遠的。
  2. In summer, starting work early is quite a good idea.
    夏天提早開始工作是個滿好的主意。
  3. That's quite a difficult situation you're in.
    你現在的處境相當棘手。
  4. She has quite a big family. There are six kids.
    她家人口滿多的，有六個小孩。
  5. It was quite a hot day, so we went to the pool.
    那天滿熱的，所以我們去游泳池。
  6. He's quite a good cook for a ten-year-old.
    以十歲小孩來說，他算很會煮菜了。
  7. My brother drives quite a fast car.
    我哥哥開的車滿快的。

② quite an + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. It's quite an interesting book about bees.
    那是一本跟蜜蜂有關、相當有趣的書。
  2. We live in quite an old house near the river.
    我們住在河邊一棟相當老的房子。
  3. That was quite an easy test.
    那次考試還算簡單。
  4. It was quite an expensive dinner, but the food was great.
    那頓晚餐相當貴，不過菜很好吃。
  5. She gave quite an unusual answer.
    她給了一個相當特別的答案。

③ quite + 形容詞 + 複數 / 不可數名詞

  1. They're quite nice people.
    他們是滿好的人。
  2. We had quite good weather last week.
    我們上禮拜天氣還算不錯。
  3. You can find quite cheap furniture at the market on Sundays.
    星期天的市集可以買到滿便宜的家具。
  4. The Lins next door have been quite friendly neighbors to us.
    隔壁的林家一直是對我們相當友善的鄰居。
  5. My parents give me quite good advice.
    我爸媽給我的建議還滿好的。
  6. These are quite old photos of my grandmother.
    這些是我奶奶滿久以前的照片。

④ quite a long time / quite a lot

  1. I've been waiting for quite a long time.
    我已經等了滿久了。
  2. I've been thinking about your letter for quite a long time.
    我想你那封信想了滿久。
  3. We haven't seen Ken for quite a long time.
    我們滿久沒見到 Ken 了。
  4. She knows quite a lot about cars.
    她對車子懂得滿多的。
  5. It took quite a long time to fix the machine.
    修那台機器花了滿久的時間。
  6. We spent quite a lot on the holiday.
    這趟假期我們花了滿多錢的。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Is it quite a long drive to your parents' house?
    開車到你爸媽家滿遠的嗎？
  2. Thanks, everyone. That was quite a useful meeting.
    謝謝大家，這次會議還滿有用的。
  3. That's quite a nice jacket. Where did you get it?
    那件夾克還滿好看的，你在哪裡買的？
  4. Quite a big crowd came to the concert.
    滿多人來看那場演唱會。
  5. You have quite a good memory.
    你記性滿好的。
  6. It's going to be quite a busy week.
    這個禮拜會滿忙的。
