句中副⁠詞放⁠在 have 和 been 中⁠間

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

我也已經在合唱團唱了五年了。

說話者在志工報名表上加上第二項經驗。

常⁠用⁠的句中副⁠詞

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

附加also
時間just、recently、already、always
確定的程度probably、certainly、really
頻率often、constantly

否⁠定⁠句⁠的 probably 放⁠在 hasn't 前⁠面

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

haven't 後面

We lost every game this season. We haven't really been trying.

我們這一季每場都輸。我們沒有真的在努力。
hasn't 前面

Your sister looks very tired. She probably hasn't been sleeping well.

你姊姊看起來很累。她大概最近都睡不好。

問⁠句⁠的副⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

你還穿著昨天的衣服。你真的工作了一整夜嗎？

副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在 been 後⁠面

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

My mum has been also taking yoga classes.

My mum has also been taking yoga classes.

副⁠詞不⁠放⁠在 have 前⁠面

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

We just have been looking at your holiday photos.

We have just been looking at your holiday photos.

probably 放⁠在 hasn't 前⁠面

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

Your son hasn't probably been listening in class.

Your son probably hasn't been listening in class.

現⁠在完⁠成簡⁠單式說一⁠個經⁠驗

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

我會彈鋼琴，我也在合唱團唱過歌。

recently 也常放⁠在句⁠尾

I've also been singing in a choir for five years.

我需要放假。我最近一直覺得很累。

例句

① also

  1. I'm studying at university, and I've also been working as a waiter at weekends.
    我在念大學，我週末也一直在當服務生。
  2. Our class sold cakes for charity. We have also been collecting old toys for the children's hospital.
    我們班為慈善義賣蛋糕。我們也一直在幫兒童醫院收集舊玩具。
  3. My sister has a busy summer. She's also been taking driving lessons.
    我妹妹這個夏天很忙。她也一直在上駕訓課。
  4. My parents are saving money. They've also been planning a trip to Iceland.
    我爸媽在存錢。他們也一直在計畫去冰島旅行。
  5. He retired last year, but he's still busy. My father has also been helping at the food bank.
    他去年退休了，可是還是很忙。我爸爸也一直在食物銀行幫忙。

② just

  1. Dear Editor, I have just been reading an article in your newspaper about school meals.
    編輯您好，我剛剛在看你們報紙上一篇關於學校午餐的文章。
  2. Oh, hi, Ben! We've just been talking about you.
    噢，嗨，班！我們剛剛在聊你。
  3. My cousin is very excited. She's just been telling me about her new job.
    我表姊很興奮。她剛剛在跟我講她的新工作。
  4. Good news about your holiday. I've just been speaking to the manager.
    你的假有好消息。我剛剛在跟經理談。

③ recently / already / always

  1. Come for dinner on Friday. I've recently been learning to cook Thai food.
    星期五來吃晚餐吧。我最近一直在學煮泰國菜。
  2. My son is close to his grandmother. He has recently been spending a lot of time with her.
    我兒子跟他奶奶很親。他最近花很多時間陪她。
  3. We only got here ten minutes ago, and you've already been complaining about the food.
    我們十分鐘前才到，你就已經一直在抱怨食物了。
  4. Where is the waiter? We've already been waiting for forty minutes.
    服務生在哪裡？我們已經等了四十分鐘了。
  5. Water is coming through the ceiling again. My husband has always been saying he'll fix the roof.
    天花板又漏水了。我先生一直都說他會修屋頂。

④ probably / certainly / really

  1. There are bottles everywhere outside. The neighbours have probably been having a party.
    外面到處都是瓶子。鄰居剛剛大概在開派對。
  2. Your daughter got top marks. She's certainly been working hard this term.
    你女兒拿了最高分。她這學期的確很用功。
  3. I'm sad that the cooking class ends next week. I've really been enjoying it.
    烹飪課下禮拜就結束了，我很難過。這門課我真的上得很開心。
  4. I'm sorry, my phone was broken. You've probably been wondering why I didn't call.
    抱歉，我的手機壞了。你大概一直在想我為什麼沒打電話。
  5. Don't be angry with Tom. He has certainly been trying his best.
    不要生湯姆的氣。他的確一直盡力在做。

⑤ often / constantly

  1. We have a big project this month. I've often been staying late at the office.
    我們這個月有個大案子。我常常留在辦公室加班。
  2. We need a new one. The printer has constantly been breaking down.
    我們需要一台新的。印表機一直不斷地故障。
  3. My little brother has constantly been asking for a puppy since his birthday.
    我弟弟從生日以後就一直吵著要一隻小狗。

⑥ 否定句

  1. I failed the test. I haven't really been paying attention in class.
    我考試不及格。我上課其實都沒在專心。
  2. Don't believe everything Max says. He hasn't always been telling the truth.
    不要相信麥克斯說的每件事。他不是一直都說實話。
  3. I've sent Emma five texts. She probably hasn't been checking her messages.
    我傳了五則簡訊給艾瑪。她大概一直沒看訊息。
  4. My son's teacher called me today. He certainly hasn't been doing his homework.
    我兒子的老師今天打電話給我。他確實一直沒寫功課。
  5. My parents should stop paying for the gym. They haven't really been using it.
    我爸媽不應該再付健身房的錢了。他們其實都沒在用。

⑦ 問句

  1. Your brother has a cold. Have you also been feeling ill?
    你哥哥感冒了。你也一直覺得不舒服嗎？
  2. Her French is still quite weak. Has she really been living in Paris for ten years?
    她的法文還是很弱。她真的在巴黎住了十年嗎？
  3. You're out of breath. Have you just been running?
    你喘不過氣。你剛剛在跑步嗎？
  4. Your lease ends in June. Have you already been looking for a new flat?
    你的租約六月到期。你已經開始在找新公寓了嗎？
