I can cook some food for you.

我可以煮點東西給你吃。

介⁠系⁠詞放⁠在名⁠詞或代⁠名⁠詞前⁠面

I can cook some food for you.

我可以煮點東西給你吃。

for you 說明是給誰的。

常⁠用⁠的介⁠系⁠詞說地⁠點、方⁠向、對⁠象或一⁠起⁠的人

I can cook some food for you.

in
在……裡面in the box, in Tainan
at
在某個地點at home, at school
to
到、往go to school, send it to me
for
給、為了a present for you
with
和……一起play with my brother

月⁠份用 in，星⁠期用 on，幾⁠點用 at

onon Friday, on 3 May

介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面⁠的代⁠名⁠詞用受格

I can cook some food for you.

These cookies are for she.

These cookies are for her.

星⁠期前⁠面用 on

The shop is closed in Sunday mornings.

The shop is closed on Sunday mornings.

去某⁠個地⁠方用 go to

Does your son go school near here?

Does your son go to school near here?

home 是例外，說 go home。

例句

① 地點：in / on / at

  1. Look, there's a frog in the swimming pool!
    你看，游泳池裡有一隻青蛙！
  2. Can you hear that noise? There's a cat on the roof.
    你有聽到那個聲音嗎？屋頂上有一隻貓。
  3. Mum, I'm at the supermarket now. Do we need milk?
    媽，我現在在超市。家裡要買牛奶嗎？
  4. My uncle has a farm in the mountains. We visit him every summer.
    我叔叔在山上有一座農場。我們每年夏天都去找他。
  5. Don't worry, your phone is on the kitchen table.
    別擔心，你的手機在廚房的桌上。
  6. On our last day, we had lunch at a little café by the sea.
    最後一天，我們在海邊的一家小咖啡店吃午餐。

② 時間：in / on / at

  1. Bring an umbrella if you visit. The rainy season here starts in May.
    你要來的話，帶把傘。這裡的雨季五月開始。
  2. Don't forget to study. We have a spelling test on Wednesday.
    別忘了讀書。我們星期三有拼字小考。
  3. I can't meet before eight. My yoga class finishes at half past seven.
    我八點前沒辦法碰面。我的瑜伽課七點半結束。
  4. My grandmother waters her plants in the evening, when it's cooler.
    我奶奶傍晚天氣比較涼的時候幫她的植物澆水。
  5. I'm busy on Friday. Can you come on Saturday afternoon?
    我星期五很忙。你星期六下午可以來嗎？

③ 方向和來源：to / from

  1. I don't have a car. I walk to work every day.
    我沒有車。我每天走路上班。
  2. She's at the post office. She's sending a postcard to her friend in Japan.
    她在郵局。她要寄一張明信片給她在日本的朋友。
  3. You look worried. Is this email from your boss?
    你看起來很擔心。這封電子郵件是你老闆寄的嗎？
  4. My teacher is from Canada, so she speaks English and French.
    我的老師是加拿大人，所以她會說英文和法文。
  5. We're flying to Bangkok next week for my cousin's wedding.
    我們下禮拜要飛去曼谷參加我表哥的婚禮。

④ 對象和一起的人：for / with

  1. It's Mother's Day tomorrow. I made this card for my mom.
    明天是母親節。我做了這張卡片要給我媽媽。
  2. Do you want to go to the night market with us tonight?
    你今晚要跟我們一起去夜市嗎？
  3. Can you carry it? This bag is too heavy for me.
    你可以幫我拿嗎？這個包包對我來說太重了。
  4. I play tennis with my dad on Sundays.
    我星期天會跟我爸爸打網球。
  5. I just got back to the office. Is there a message for me?
    我剛回到辦公室。有給我的留言嗎？
  6. My aunt isn't married. She lives with two cats.
    我阿姨沒結婚。她跟兩隻貓一起住。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Please don't wear shoes in the house. Put your shoes in the cupboard by the door.
    在家裡請不要穿鞋。把你的鞋子放進門邊的櫃子裡。
  2. Let's meet at the bus stop outside the library.
    我們在圖書館外面的公車站碰面吧。
  3. You didn't have to buy me lunch. Thank you for the sandwich.
    你不用請我吃午餐的。謝謝你的三明治。
  4. Sorry, I'll miss our class this week. I'm going to the dentist on Thursday.
    抱歉，這星期的課我不能去。我星期四要去看牙醫。
  5. Can you hold my coffee for me while I tie my shoe?
    我綁鞋帶的時候，你可以幫我拿一下咖啡嗎？
  6. He isn't home today. He's shopping with his sister in Taichung.
    他今天不在家。他跟他姊姊在台中逛街。
  7. We don't go out much. We usually stay at home in the evening.
    我們不常出門。我們晚上通常待在家。
  8. I got a letter from the school today. My exam results are good!
    我今天收到學校寄來的信。我的考試成績很好！
