It's no problem if you want to leave at 4:30 p.m., as the match will have finished by that time.

你想下午 4:30 離開也沒問題，因為那時比賽已經結束了。

主⁠詞 + will have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞 + by + 未⁠來⁠的時⁠間點

will have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞、by + 未⁠來⁠的時⁠間點

It's no problem if you want to leave at 4:30 p.m., as the match will have finished by that time.

will have + 過去分詞預計在那個時間點之前完成的事。
by + 未來的時間點最晚在什麼時候完成，像 by that time、by the end of the month、by the time you arrive。

預⁠計完⁠成⁠的事

例句

If you cycle to work, you will have saved a lot of money by the end of the month.

如果你騎腳踏車上班，到月底你就省下很多錢了。

說話者根據計畫或常理，推算事情到那時已經完成。

預⁠計沒⁠完⁠成⁠的事

例句

You won't have spent anything on petrol.

你就不會花錢在汽油上了。

否定句說到那個時間點還沒完成的事。

by the time + 現⁠在簡⁠單式

It's no problem if you want to leave at 4:30 p.m., as the match will have finished by that time.

例句

By the time you wake up, I'll have left for the airport.

等你醒來的時候，我已經出發去機場了。

by the time 後面接子句時，子句用現在簡單式，不用 will。

by the time 子⁠句說未⁠來⁠的時⁠間，用現⁠在簡⁠單式

By the time you will arrive, we will have eaten.

By the time you arrive, we will have eaten.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

It's no problem if you want to leave at 4:30 p.m., as the match will have finished by that time.

The match will have finish by that time.

The match will have finished by that time.

說最⁠晚在什⁠麼⁠時⁠候完⁠成，用 by

It's no problem if you want to leave at 4:30 p.m., as the match will have finished by that time.

We will have finished in Friday.

We will have finished by Friday.

例句

① 預計完成的事

  1. The concert will have ended by eleven, so you can take the last train.
    音樂會十一點前就結束了，所以你趕得上末班車。
  2. If you walk every day, you will have lost five kilos by the summer.
    如果你每天走路，到夏天你就瘦了五公斤。
  3. The plane will have landed by the time you get to the airport.
    等你到機場的時候，飛機已經降落了。
  4. Come after eight. We'll have finished dinner by then.
    八點以後再來，那時候我們就吃完晚餐了。
  5. By the time the police arrive, the thief will have escaped.
    等警察到的時候，小偷已經逃走了。
  6. The tickets will have sold out by Monday, so book now.
    到星期一票就賣光了，所以現在就訂。
  7. By the weekend, the swelling will have gone down.
    到週末，腫就消了。
  8. I'll have finished the report by the time you need it.
    等你需要報告的時候，我就已經寫好了。
  9. In an hour, the cake will have cooled enough to cut.
    再過一個小時，蛋糕就涼到可以切了。
  10. By the time you read this letter, I'll have started my new job.
    等你讀到這封信的時候，我已經開始新工作了。
  11. The movers will have taken everything out by noon.
    到中午，搬家工人就把東西全部搬走了。
  12. By next year, the city will have planted ten thousand trees.
    到明年，這座城市就已經種了一萬棵樹。
  13. By the time the kids come home, I'll have tidied their room.
    等孩子們回家的時候，我就已經整理好他們的房間了。
  14. The shops will have closed by the time we get there.
    等我們到那裡的時候，商店都已經關門了。
  15. In three weeks, she'll have recovered completely.
    再過三個星期，她就完全康復了。
  16. By the time the guests arrive, the band will have set up.
    等客人到的時候，樂團就已經架好設備了。

② 預計沒完成的事

  1. By the end of the month, you won't have paid anything for parking.
    到月底，你就不會付任何停車費了。
  2. Don't come at nine. I won't have got dressed.
    九點別來，那時候我還沒換好衣服。
  3. By Friday the parcel won't have arrived.
    到星期五，包裹還不會送到。
  4. The paint won't have dried by the time the guests come.
    等客人來的時候，油漆還不會乾。
  5. The bakery won't have opened by six.
    到六點，麵包店還沒開門。
  6. By the time the film starts, they won't have found a parking space.
    等電影開始的時候，他們還沒找到停車位。
  7. We won't have decided on a name by the time the baby is born.
    等寶寶出生的時候，我們還沒決定好名字。
  8. The council won't have fixed the lights by Christmas.
    到聖誕節，市議會還沒把路燈修好。
  9. By the deadline, half the class won't have handed in their essays.
    到截止日，班上有一半的人還沒交作文。
  10. The medicine won't have started working by tonight.
    到今晚，藥效還不會開始發揮。
  11. She won't have heard about the party by then.
    到那時候，她還不會聽說派對的事。
  12. By the end of the day, the workers won't have cleared all the snow.
    到今天結束，工人還沒把雪全部清完。
  13. The new manager won't have met everyone by the end of the week.
    到週末，新經理還沒跟每個人見過面。
  14. By the summer the bridge won't have reopened.
    到夏天，橋還不會重新開放。
  15. I won't have seen the doctor by the time you call.
    等你打來的時候，我還沒看完醫生。
