You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

你應該正視這個問題，把真相告訴大家。

片⁠語介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞由動⁠詞、小⁠品⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞組⁠成

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

你應該正視這個問題，把真相告訴大家。

face up to 是正視難處理的事。

受⁠詞可⁠以是名⁠詞、代⁠名⁠詞或動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

名詞

We need to cut down on costs.

我們得減少開支。
代名詞

I always looked up to her.

我一直很尊敬她。
動詞⁠-⁠ing

I'm trying to cut down on eating sugar.

我正在試著少吃糖。

同⁠一⁠個動⁠詞，小⁠品⁠詞和介⁠系⁠詞不⁠同，意⁠思不⁠同

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

look up to
尊敬

The young players look up to their captain.

年輕球員很尊敬他們的隊長。
look down on
看不起

He looks down on people without a degree.

他看不起沒有學位的人。

cut down 表⁠示減⁠少⁠時，要加介⁠系⁠詞 on

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

We need to cut down sugar.

We need to cut down on sugar.

小⁠品⁠詞不⁠能省⁠略

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

I can't put with his jokes any more.

I can't put up with his jokes any more.

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞也放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

Everyone looks her up to.

Everyone looks up to her.

come up 的介⁠系⁠詞是 with

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

He came up of a plan.

He came up with a plan.

make up 是片⁠語動⁠詞，make up for 多了介⁠系⁠詞

You should face up to the problem and tell everybody the truth.

make up
編造

She made up a story.

她編了一個故事。
make up for
彌補

Let me make up for it with dinner.

讓我請你吃晚餐補償你。

例句

① face up to / own up to

  1. "I lost the client's file." "You have to face up to it and tell the boss."
    我把客戶的檔案弄丟了。你得面對這件事，去跟老闆說。
  2. He finally faced up to the fact that he needed help.
    他終於承認自己需要幫忙。
  3. Nobody owned up to breaking the window, so the whole class stayed late.
    沒有人承認打破了窗戶，所以全班都留下來。
  4. "Who ate the cake?" "Tom owned up to it in the end."
    誰吃了蛋糕？最後 Tom 承認了。

② look up to / look down on

  1. Kids look up to their older brothers and sisters.
    小孩子都很崇拜他們的哥哥姊姊。
  2. "Who's your hero?" "My grandad. I've always looked up to him."
    你最崇拜誰？我爺爺。我一直很尊敬他。
  3. In the old days, some people looked down on women who worked.
    以前有些人看不起出去工作的女人。
  4. Don't look down on him just because he's young.
    不要因為他年輕就看不起他。

③ come up with

  1. We need a name for the café. Can anyone come up with something?
    我們的咖啡店需要一個名字。有人想得出來嗎？
  2. The team came up with a plausible answer to the problem.
    團隊想出了一個合理的解決辦法。
  3. "How did you come up with that idea?" "I was in the shower."
    你是怎麼想到那個點子的？我在洗澡的時候想到的。

④ put up with

  1. My neighbours play loud music every night. I can't put up with it.
    我的鄰居每天晚上都大聲放音樂。我受不了了。
  2. "How do you put up with his jokes?" "I just don't listen."
    你怎麼受得了他的笑話？我就不聽啊。
  3. The workers put up with the cold office all winter.
    員工整個冬天都忍受著冰冷的辦公室。

⑤ cut down on

  1. My doctor says I should cut down on coffee.
    我的醫生說我應該少喝咖啡。
  2. The school is trying to cut down on paper, so homework is online now.
    學校在試著少用紙，所以現在作業都在網路上交。
  3. "Are you still smoking?" "Yes, but I've cut down on it."
    你還在抽菸嗎？還在，可是我抽得比較少了。

⑥ make up for / live up to

  1. I'm sorry I missed your birthday. Let me make up for it with dinner.
    抱歉我錯過了你的生日。讓我請你吃晚餐補償你。
  2. He worked late every night to make up for the lost time.
    他每天晚上都加班，把浪費掉的時間補回來。
  3. Everyone said the film was great, but it didn't live up to the hype.
    大家都說那部電影很棒，可是它沒有大家說的那麼好。
  4. Her parents expect a lot. She works hard to live up to their hopes.
    她爸媽期望很高。她很努力達到他們的期望。

⑦ stand up for / get away with

  1. When the other kids laughed at me, my friend stood up for me.
    其他小孩笑我的時候，我的朋友替我說話。
  2. You have to stand up for what you believe in.
    你要堅持你相信的事。
  3. He copied my homework and got away with it.
    他抄了我的作業，結果沒被抓到。
  4. "She was late again and the boss said nothing." "She always gets away with it."
    她又遲到了，老闆什麼都沒說。她每次都沒事。

⑧ drop out of / catch up on / keep up with

  1. He dropped out of university after one year to start a business.
    他念了一年大學就退學去創業。
  2. I'm staying home this weekend to catch up on some sleep.
    我這個週末要待在家補眠。
  3. The class goes so fast that I can't keep up with it.
    這堂課進度好快，我跟不上。
  4. "How was your holiday?" "Great, but now I have to catch up on my emails."
    你的假期怎麼樣？很棒，可是現在得處理積下來的電子郵件。

⑨ 受詞是動詞-ing

  1. "Why is Dad angry?" "My brother finally owned up to taking his car."
    爸為什麼生氣？我哥終於承認他開走了爸的車。
  2. She got away with leaving work early all week.
    她整個星期都提早下班，也沒被說。
  3. I want to cut down on spending money on clothes.
    我想少花點錢買衣服。
