Nowadays nothing is free, so you have to pay for everything.

現在沒有什麼東西是免費的，所以每樣東西你都得付錢。

不⁠定代⁠名⁠詞 + 單⁠數動⁠詞

Nowadays nothing is free, so you have to pay for everything.

現在沒有什麼東西是免費的。

指⁠人、指東⁠西、指地⁠方

指人

Somebody has taken my umbrella.

有人拿走了我的雨傘。
指東西

Something smells good in the kitchen.

廚房裡有東西好香。
指地方

Everywhere was full of tourists in August.

八月到處都是觀光客。

指很⁠多人也用單⁠數動⁠詞

everyone

Everyone in my class likes the new teacher.

我們班每個人都喜歡新老師。
everything

Everything in this shop costs one pound.

這家店每樣東西都賣一英鎊。

動⁠詞用單⁠數

Nowadays nothing is free, so you have to pay for everything.

Everybody know the answer.

Everybody knows the answer.

nobody 後⁠面⁠接肯⁠定動⁠詞

Nowadays nothing is free, so you have to pay for everything.

Nobody didn't bring a raincoat on the hike.

Nobody brought a raincoat on the hike.

否⁠定放⁠在主⁠詞：用 nobody

Anybody didn't answer the phone.

Nobody answered the phone.

everybody 連在⁠一⁠起寫

Every body enjoyed the concert.

Everybody enjoyed the concert.

all + 複⁠數名⁠詞，動⁠詞用複⁠數

我們公寓所有的窗戶都面海。

後⁠面用 they 指回 everyone

大家都做完了，對吧？

例句

① everyone / everybody

  1. Everyone wants a bigger flat these days.
    現在每個人都想住大一點的公寓。
  2. Everybody was asleep when I got home.
    我回到家的時候大家都睡了。
  3. Everyone in the team has a laptop.
    隊上每個人都有一台筆電。
  4. Everybody loves a long weekend.
    連假誰都喜歡。
  5. Everyone at the wedding was dancing.
    婚禮上每個人都在跳舞。

② someone / somebody / anyone / anybody

  1. Someone wants to speak to the manager.
    有人要找經理。
  2. Somebody has left the lights on.
    有人沒關燈。
  3. Does anyone have a spare pen?
    有人有多的筆嗎？
  4. Anybody can use the gym after six.
    六點以後誰都可以用健身房。
  5. Someone from the bank called this morning.
    今天早上銀行有人打電話來。

③ no one / nobody

  1. No one knows where he lives.
    沒有人知道他住哪裡。
  2. Nobody answers the phone at lunchtime.
    午餐時間沒有人接電話。
  3. No one has ever seen him angry.
    從來沒有人看過他生氣。
  4. Nobody likes waiting in the rain.
    沒有人喜歡在雨中等。
  5. No one in my family plays the piano.
    我家沒有人會彈鋼琴。

④ everything / something / anything

  1. Everything is more expensive than last year.
    每樣東西都比去年貴。
  2. Something is wrong with my phone.
    我的手機出了點問題。
  3. Everything was ready before the guests arrived.
    客人到之前一切都準備好了。
  4. Is anything missing from your bag?
    你的包包有少什麼東西嗎？
  5. Something has to change.
    一定得有所改變。
  6. Anything is better than staying at home all day.
    做什麼都比整天待在家好。

⑤ nothing

  1. Nothing is open on Christmas Day.
    聖誕節當天什麼店都沒開。
  2. Nothing has changed since I left.
    我離開以後什麼都沒變。
  3. Nothing was said about the money.
    錢的事一句都沒提。（被動）
  4. Nothing tastes better than fresh bread.
    沒有什麼比剛出爐的麵包更好吃。
  5. Nothing in the fridge looks fresh.
    冰箱裡的東西看起來都不新鮮了。

⑥ everywhere / somewhere / anywhere / nowhere

  1. Everywhere was quiet after midnight.
    過了半夜到處都很安靜。
  2. Somewhere warm sounds perfect for our holiday.
    找個溫暖的地方度假，聽起來很棒。
  3. Somewhere quiet would be better for the meeting.
    開會找個安靜的地方比較好。
  4. Anywhere is fine for dinner.
    晚餐在哪裡吃都可以。
  5. Nowhere in the town is open after ten.
    十點以後鎮上沒有一個地方開著。
