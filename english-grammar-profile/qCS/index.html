For people who can't afford a car, a bicycle is perhaps the cheapest way to travel in the city.

對買不起車的人來說，腳踏車大概是在市區移動最便宜的方式。

to 不⁠定⁠詞說⁠明是做⁠什⁠麼事最怎⁠麼⁠樣

For people who can't afford a car, a bicycle is perhaps the cheapest way to travel in the city.

去那裡最快的方法是搭火車。

名⁠詞也⁠可⁠以是 place、time、thing

For people who can't afford a car, a bicycle is perhaps the cheapest way to travel in the city.

place to

Don't carry your passport around the city. The safest place to keep it is the hotel safe.

別帶著護照在市區走。放護照最安全的地方是飯店的保險箱。
time to

Book your trip to Spain for the winter. The cheapest time to fly is in February.

你去西班牙的行程訂在冬天吧。二月是搭飛機最便宜的時候。
the most + thing to

The most important thing to remember is your passport.

最要記得帶的是你的護照。

way of 後⁠面不⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

For people who can't afford a car, a bicycle is perhaps the cheapest way to travel in the city.

The cheapest way of send money home is online.

The cheapest way to send money home is online.

to 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

That was the fastest way to go home.

For people who can't afford a car, a bicycle is perhaps the cheapest way to travel in the city.

Cycling is the cheapest way to travel.

例句

① the + 形容詞-est + way to

  1. The bridge has a toll, and taxis cost a lot. Taking the ferry is the cheapest way to cross the bay.
    過橋要收費，計程車又很貴。搭渡輪是過海灣最便宜的方式。
  2. The fastest way to reach the airport is the express train.
    去機場最快的方法是搭快線列車。
  3. Our shop still writes every sale in a notebook. Using a computer is the easiest way to keep records.
    我們店裡還是把每筆交易寫在筆記本上。用電腦是記錄資料最簡單的方法。
  4. I still type with two fingers. What's the fastest way to learn to type?
    我打字還是用兩根手指。學打字最快的方法是什麼？
  5. The simplest way to save money is to cook at home.
    省錢最簡單的方法是在家煮飯。
  6. We've walked for half an hour already. Is this the shortest way to walk to the beach?
    我們已經走了半個小時。這是走去海邊最近的路嗎？

② the + 形容詞-est + place / time to

  1. Don't carry your passport around the city. The safest place to keep it is the hotel safe.
    別帶著護照在市區走。放護照最安全的地方是飯店的保險箱。
  2. Book your trip to Spain for the winter. The cheapest time to fly is in February.
    你去西班牙的行程訂在冬天吧。二月是搭飛機最便宜的時候。
  3. The café is too noisy for me. The quietest place to study is the top floor of the library.
    咖啡館對我來說太吵了。讀書最安靜的地方是圖書館頂樓。
  4. It's thirty-five degrees in the afternoon here. Early morning is the coolest time to go for a run.
    這裡下午有三十五度。清晨是去跑步最涼快的時候。
  5. The supermarket is a long way away. The nearest place to buy bread is the shop on the corner.
    超市很遠。最近可以買麵包的地方是轉角那家店。

③ the most + 形容詞 + 名詞 + to

  1. Storms come fast in the mountains. The most important thing to check before a hike is the weather.
    山上的暴風雨來得很快。爬山前最要緊的是先看天氣。
  2. Book a table at Rosa's for Saturday. It's the most popular place to eat in town.
    星期六去羅莎餐廳訂位吧。那是鎮上最熱門的吃飯地點。
  3. I'm going to Osaka next week. What's the most useful word to learn in Japanese?
    我下星期要去大阪。日文裡最值得學的字是哪一個？
  4. The bus stops right outside our house. Taking it is the most convenient way to get to school.
    公車就停在我們家外面。搭公車是上學最方便的方式。
  5. Come to our town in August. The most interesting time to visit is during the festival.
    八月來我們鎮上吧。節慶期間是最有意思的造訪時間。

④ the worst / the hardest + 名詞 + to

  1. Let's go on Saturday morning instead. Friday afternoon is the worst time to drive into the city.
    我們改星期六早上去吧。星期五下午是開車進城最糟的時間。
  2. I loved my year in Spain. At the end, the hardest thing to do was say goodbye.
    我很愛在西班牙的那一年。到最後，最難做的事是說再見。
  3. You won't get a seat at eight o'clock. Rush hour is the worst time to take the subway.
    你八點鐘不會有位子坐。尖峰時間是搭地鐵最糟的時候。
  4. My Spanish course was fun. The hardest part to learn was the grammar.
    我的西班牙文課很好玩。最難學的部分是文法。
  5. Don't leave it under that tree. That's the worst place to park your car, because of the birds.
    別停在那棵樹下。那是停車最糟的地方，因為有鳥。

⑤ 問句和不同的句型

  1. I want to send these books to my son in Canada. Which is the cheapest way to send a parcel?
    我想把這些書寄給我在加拿大的兒子。寄包裹最便宜的方法是哪一種？
  2. The waves look big today. Where's the safest place to swim?
    今天浪看起來很大。在哪裡游泳最安全？
  3. I've never grown anything before. Which is the easiest plant to grow on a balcony?
    我從來沒種過東西。在陽台上種哪一種植物最容易？
  4. I don't like crowds. When's the quietest time to visit the museum?
    我不喜歡人多。什麼時候去博物館人最少？
  5. For students, the library is the cheapest place to work.
    對學生來說，圖書館是最省錢的讀書地方。
  6. When you're lost, the best thing to do is ask for help.
    迷路的時候，最好的做法是找人幫忙。
  7. Please leave your room by eleven. That's the latest time to check out.
    請在十一點前離開房間。那是最晚的退房時間。
  8. When a friend is sad, the kindest thing to do is listen.
    朋友難過的時候，最體貼的做法是聽他說。
  9. The flowers come out in April. Spring is perhaps the best time to walk in the hills.
    花在四月開。春天大概是去山上散步最好的時候。
