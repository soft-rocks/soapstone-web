Luckily enough, we have a garden where we can cook over an open fire.

幸好我們有個花園，可以在那裡生火煮東西。

副⁠詞 + enough 放⁠在句⁠首

Luckily enough, we have a garden where we can cook over an open fire.

幸好我們有個花園。

也⁠可⁠以插⁠在主⁠詞和動⁠詞中⁠間

Luckily enough, we have a garden where we can cook over an open fire.

說也奇怪，這個計畫進行得非常順利。

各⁠個組⁠合⁠的意⁠思

Luckily enough, we have a garden where we can cook over an open fire.

luckily enough很幸運的是
surprisingly enough出乎意料的是
sadly enough可惜的是，令人難過的是
strangely enough、oddly enough說也奇怪
funnily enough說來好笑，說來也巧
interestingly enough有意思的是

這⁠裡⁠的 enough 不⁠是足⁠夠

加強語氣

Surprisingly enough, he passed the test.

出乎意料的是，他考試過了。
足夠

He didn't study hard enough to pass the test.

他讀得不夠認真，考試沒過。

enough 放⁠在副⁠詞後⁠面

Luckily enough, we have a garden where we can cook over an open fire.

Enough surprisingly, nobody complained.

Surprisingly enough, nobody complained.

說⁠出看⁠法用副⁠詞 funnily

Funny enough, I was thinking about you this morning.

Funnily enough, I was thinking about you this morning.

後⁠面直⁠接接子⁠句，不⁠加 that

Luckily enough that we had an umbrella.

Luckily enough, we had an umbrella.

前⁠面不⁠再⁠加 very

Very sadly enough, the shop closed last year.

Sadly enough, the shop closed last year.

例句

① luckily enough

  1. Luckily enough, the restaurant still had one table free.
    很幸運的是，那家餐廳還有一張空桌。
  2. Luckily enough, my neighbour had a spare key.
    很幸運的是，我鄰居有一把備用鑰匙。
  3. Luckily enough, the weather stayed dry for the whole festival.
    很幸運的是，整個音樂節期間都沒下雨。
  4. Luckily enough, nobody was in the car when the tree fell.
    很幸運的是，樹倒下來的時候車裡沒人。
  5. Luckily enough, I'd saved a copy of the report on my laptop.
    很幸運的是，我在筆電裡存了一份報告。

② surprisingly enough

  1. Surprisingly enough, the cheapest wine tasted the best.
    出乎意料的是，最便宜的酒最好喝。
  2. Surprisingly enough, my grandfather loves video games.
    出乎意料的是，我爺爺很愛打電動。
  3. Surprisingly enough, the train arrived exactly on time.
    出乎意料的是，火車準時到站了。
  4. The children, surprisingly enough, ate all their vegetables.
    出乎意料的是，孩子們把蔬菜全吃光了。
  5. Surprisingly enough, very few people applied for the job.
    出乎意料的是，應徵這份工作的人很少。

③ sadly enough

  1. Sadly enough, I wasn't able to find any job offers.
    可惜的是，我沒找到任何工作機會。
  2. Sadly enough, the old theatre is going to be knocked down.
    令人難過的是，那間老劇院要被拆掉了。
  3. Sadly enough, many of these animals are now in danger.
    令人難過的是，這些動物很多現在都瀕臨絕種。
  4. Sadly enough, the band split up before their second album.
    可惜的是，那個樂團在出第二張專輯前就解散了。

④ strangely enough、oddly enough

  1. Strangely enough, I've never been to the beach near my house.
    說也奇怪，我從來沒去過我家附近的海灘。
  2. Oddly enough, the shop was empty on a Saturday afternoon.
    說也奇怪，星期六下午店裡竟然沒人。
  3. The dog, strangely enough, didn't bark at the stranger.
    說也奇怪，那隻狗沒有對陌生人叫。
  4. Oddly enough, I felt more relaxed during the exam than before it.
    說也奇怪，我考試的時候反而比考前放鬆。
  5. Strangely enough, we both chose the same dress for the party.
    說也奇怪，我們兩個為派對挑了同一件洋裝。

⑤ funnily enough

  1. Funnily enough, I met my wife on a train.
    說來也巧，我是在火車上認識我太太的。
  2. Funnily enough, my brother and I have the same birthday.
    說來也巧，我和我哥同一天生日。
  3. Funnily enough, I was just about to call you.
    說來也巧，我正要打給你。
  4. Funnily enough, the person sitting next to me was from my hometown.
    說來也巧，坐在我旁邊的人跟我同鄉。

⑥ interestingly enough

  1. Interestingly enough, the word "robot" comes from Czech.
    有意思的是，robot 這個字來自捷克文。
  2. Interestingly enough, women in the survey slept less than men.
    有意思的是，調查中的女性睡得比男性少。
  3. Interestingly enough, sales went up during the rainy months.
    有意思的是，下雨的那幾個月銷售量反而上升了。
  4. The book, interestingly enough, was written in only three weeks.
    有意思的是，這本書只花了三個禮拜就寫完了。

⑦ 插在主詞和動詞中間

  1. My sister, luckily enough, found her passport in the taxi.
    很幸運的是，我姊姊在計程車上找到了她的護照。
  2. The meeting, sadly enough, ended without any decision.
    可惜的是，會議最後沒有做出任何決定。
  3. Our old car, funnily enough, never broke down once.
    說來好笑，我們那輛舊車一次都沒壞過。
