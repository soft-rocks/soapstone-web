Wear an apron over your clothes.

在衣服外面穿上圍裙。

例句

① 交通和指路

  1. I'll pick you up after your class. Take the bus to the library, then call me.
    你下課後我去接你。搭公車到圖書館，然後打給我。
  2. The museum is easy to reach by underground. Take the yellow line to the river.
    搭地鐵去博物館很方便。搭黃線到河邊。
  3. The bus driver will tell you when we arrive. Get off at the third stop.
    公車司機到了會告訴你。在第三站下車。
  4. Our farm is hard to find. Turn right after the petrol station.
    我們的農場很難找。過了加油站右轉。
  5. You're nearly there. Walk a little farther, and you'll see the school.
    你快到了。再往前走一點，你就會看到學校。
  6. Here's how to get to work on your first day. Take the green bus outside the bank at 7:15.
    這是你第一天上班的交通方式。七點十五分在銀行外面搭綠色公車。

② 要帶的東西

  1. The bus ride to the zoo takes three hours. Bring some fruit and a drink.
    搭巴士去動物園要三個小時。帶些水果和飲料。
  2. It's going to rain on the school trip. Don't forget your raincoat and boots.
    校外教學那天會下雨。別忘了帶雨衣和雨靴。
  3. The school kitchen is closed for repairs. Bring your own lunch on Friday.
    學校廚房在維修。星期五自己帶午餐。
  4. Take a jacket. It gets cold at night.
    帶件夾克，晚上會變冷。
  5. We'll be on the beach all day. Pack sunscreen and a hat.
    我們整天都會在海灘上。帶防曬乳和帽子。

③ 做事的方法

  1. Put on an apron before you start cooking.
    開始煮飯前先穿上圍裙。
  2. This cleaner is very strong. Wear rubber gloves when you use it.
    這個清潔劑很強。用的時候要戴橡膠手套。
  3. The chef showed us how to make the sauce. Cut the onions into small pieces.
    主廚教我們怎麼做醬汁。把洋蔥切成小塊。
  4. The receptionist handed me a form. Fill in your name and address here.
    櫃台人員給了我一張表格。在這裡填你的姓名和地址。
  5. This is the new coffee machine. Press the green button to start the machine.
    這是新的咖啡機。按綠色的按鈕啟動機器。
  6. The pharmacist gave me some advice. Keep the medicine in a cool place.
    藥劑師給了我一些建議。把藥放在陰涼的地方。

④ 學校和工作的指示

  1. Good morning, class. Open your books at page 20.
    同學們早安。把書翻到第 20 頁。
  2. Pencil is not allowed in the exam. Write your answers in pen.
    考試不能用鉛筆。用原子筆寫答案。
  3. Find a partner. Work in pairs for this exercise.
    找一個夥伴。這個練習兩人一組。
  4. The teacher gave us homework for the weekend. Email me your answers by Monday.
    老師給我們週末的作業。星期一之前把答案寄給我。
  5. You have five minutes left. Check your spelling before you hand in the test.
    你們還有五分鐘。交考卷之前檢查拼字。

⑤ 提醒和安全

  1. Thanks for looking after our flat. Remember to water the plants on Sunday.
    謝謝你幫我們看公寓。星期天記得澆花。
  2. The steps are wet after the rain. Hold the handrail on the stairs.
    下過雨後台階是濕的。走樓梯時扶著扶手。
  3. You're the last one in the classroom. Turn off the lights when you leave.
    你是教室裡最後一個人。離開時把燈關掉。
  4. Children cross the road at three o'clock. Drive slowly near the school.
    孩子們三點過馬路。在學校附近開慢一點。
  5. Storms come quickly in the mountains. Check the weather before you go hiking.
    山上的暴風雨來得很快。去健行之前先查天氣。
  6. These apples came from the market. Wash the fruit before you eat it.
    這些蘋果是市場買的。吃水果之前先洗乾淨。
  7. Don't cross the road yet. Wait for the green light.
    先別過馬路。等綠燈。
  8. The inspector may check it on the train. Keep your ticket until the end of the trip.
    查票員可能會在火車上查票。車票留到旅程結束。
