You never remember anything!

你老是什麼都不記得！

never 本⁠身是否⁠定，受⁠詞用 anything

你老是什麼都不記得！

頻⁠率副⁠詞放⁠在 be 和助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

You never remember anything!

be 後面

The bus is usually on time.

公車通常很準時。
助動詞後面

I can never find a parking space here.

我在這裡從來都找不到停車位。

usually 是大⁠部⁠分⁠的時⁠候，often 是很⁠多次

You never remember anything!

usually

I usually take the train to work.

我通常搭火車上班。
often

She often forgets her keys.

她常忘記帶鑰匙。
never

She never eats breakfast.

她從不吃早餐。

weekly 這⁠類副⁠詞放⁠在句⁠尾

這本雜誌每週出刊。

never 已⁠經表⁠示否⁠定，不⁠再⁠加 don't

You never remember anything!

I don't never eat meat.

I never eat meat.

weekly 不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面

We weekly clean the house.

We clean the house weekly.

often 不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

I visit often my grandparents.

I often visit my grandparents.

usually 放⁠在 be 後⁠面

The shop usually is open until nine.

The shop is usually open until nine.

一⁠句只⁠用一⁠個否⁠定詞

You never remember anything!

He never says nothing.

He never says anything.

weekly 放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面⁠時是形⁠容⁠詞

形容詞

We have a weekly meeting.

我們每週開一次會。
副詞

We meet weekly to talk about the project.

我們每週開會討論這個專案。

問頻⁠率用 How often

你多久去一次健身房？

例句

① usually + 動詞

  1. People usually eat breakfast between seven and nine.
    大家通常七點到九點吃早餐。
  2. I usually take the train to work.
    我通常搭火車上班。
  3. My grandmother usually goes to bed at ten.
    我奶奶通常十點睡覺。
  4. Do you usually cook at home?
    你通常在家煮飯嗎？
  5. We usually didn't have much homework on Fridays.
    我們以前星期五通常沒什麼功課。
  6. Usually I walk the dog after dinner.
    我通常晚餐後遛狗。

② be + usually

  1. The library is usually quiet in the morning.
    圖書館早上通常很安靜。
  2. My brother is usually late for dinner.
    我哥哥吃晚餐通常會遲到。
  3. Are the shops usually open on Sundays?
    商店星期天通常有開嗎？

③ often + 動詞

  1. I often go to the history museum on Saturdays.
    我星期六常去歷史博物館。
  2. We often play football in the park.
    我們常在公園踢足球。
  3. My parents often call me on Sundays.
    我爸媽常在星期天打電話給我。
  4. Does it often snow here?
    這裡常下雪嗎？
  5. She often forgets her keys.
    她常忘記帶鑰匙。
  6. I don't often watch TV.
    我不常看電視。

④ be + often

  1. The trains are often full in the morning.
    早上的火車常常客滿。
  2. He is often tired after work.
    他下班後常常很累。
  3. The weather is often windy here.
    這裡常常颳風。

⑤ never + 動詞

  1. I never drink coffee at night.
    我晚上從不喝咖啡。
  2. My dad never says sorry.
    我爸從來不道歉。
  3. We never went abroad when I was a child.
    我小時候我們從沒出過國。
  4. You never listen to me!
    你從來都不聽我說！
  5. She never eats breakfast.
    她從不吃早餐。

⑥ be + never、助動詞 + never

  1. The bus is never on time.
    公車從來不準時。
  2. I can never find a parking space here.
    我在這裡從來都找不到停車位。
  3. He is never at home in the evening.
    他晚上從來不在家。
  4. I will never forget this day.
    我永遠不會忘記這一天。

⑦ weekly、monthly、daily 放在句尾

  1. Do I have to pay weekly or monthly?
    我要每週付還是每月付？
  2. The magazine comes out weekly.
    這本雜誌每週出刊。
  3. We meet weekly to talk about the project.
    我們每週開會討論這個專案。
  4. Take this medicine daily.
    這個藥每天吃。
  5. We pay the rent monthly.
    我們每個月付一次房租。
