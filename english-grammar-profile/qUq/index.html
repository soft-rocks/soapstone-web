Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

它們不但能幫你省下歸檔的時間，還能節省辦公空間。

Not only 放句⁠首，do 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

它們不但能幫你省下歸檔的時間，還能節省辦公空間。

單⁠數主⁠詞用 does

Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

do

Not only do they save time, but they also save space.

它們不但省時間，還能省空間。
does

Not only does she sing, but she also writes her own songs.

她不只會唱歌，還自己寫歌。

動⁠詞是 be 時，be 直⁠接移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

be

Not only is the hotel cheap, but it's also near the beach.

這家飯店不只便宜，還離海邊很近。

Not only 不⁠在句⁠首就⁠不⁠倒⁠裝

Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

一般

They not only save time but also save space.

它們不只省時間，還省空間。
倒裝

Not only do they save time, but they also save space.

它們不但省時間，還能省空間。

倒裝的句子比較正式。

Not only 放句⁠首⁠時要倒⁠裝

Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

Not only they save time, but they also save space.

Not only do they save time, but they also save space.

but 後⁠面用一⁠般語⁠序

Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

Not only does he cook, but also does he clean.

Not only does he cook, but he also cleans.

be 的句⁠子不⁠加 does

Not only do they save you filing time, but they also save office space.

Not only does the room is big, but it's also bright.

Not only is the room big, but it's also bright.

例句

① Not only do + 複數主詞

  1. Let's go digital. Not only do these files save you filing time, but they also save office space.
    我們改用電子檔吧。這些檔案不但能幫你省下歸檔的時間，還能節省辦公空間。
  2. Sell your car and get a bike. Not only do bikes save money, but they also keep you fit.
    把車賣了，買台腳踏車吧。腳踏車不只省錢，還能讓你保持健康。
  3. They're always tired. Not only do my parents work full-time, but they also look after my grandma.
    他們總是很累。我爸媽不只全職工作，還要照顧我奶奶。
  4. Not only do trees give us shade, but they also clean the air.
    樹不只給我們遮蔭，還能淨化空氣。
  5. Not only do I enjoy cooking, but I also love washing up.
    我不只喜歡煮飯，也很喜歡洗碗。
  6. Not only do the students study hard, but they also help in the community.
    這些學生不只用功讀書，還在社區幫忙。

② Not only does + 單數主詞

  1. Visit our town! Not only does it look nice, but it's also full of places to visit.
    來我們鎮上玩吧！這裡不只很漂亮，還有很多地方可以參觀。
  2. Ask Maria for help. Not only does she speak four languages, but she also teaches them.
    去找 Maria 幫忙。她不只會說四種語言，還會教。
  3. Not only does this phone take great photos, but it also lasts all day.
    這支手機不只拍照很好看，電池還能撐一整天。
  4. Come to his concert. Not only does my brother play the guitar, but he also writes songs.
    來聽他的演唱會吧。我哥哥不只會彈吉他，還會寫歌。
  5. Not only does the new café serve good coffee, but it also has free Wi-Fi.
    那家新咖啡店不只咖啡好喝，還有免費 Wi-Fi。
  6. Not only does the park have a lake, but it also has a small zoo.
    這座公園不只有湖，還有一座小動物園。
  7. Not only does exercise help your body, but it also improves your mood.
    運動不只對身體好，還能讓心情變好。

③ Not only is / are + 主詞

  1. Book this place. Not only is the flat cheap, but it's also close to the station.
    訂這間吧。這間公寓不只便宜，還離車站很近。
  2. You'll love this hotel. Not only are the staff friendly, but they also speak English.
    你會喜歡這家飯店的。員工不只很親切，還會說英文。
  3. Not only is this book interesting, but it's also easy to read.
    這本書不只有趣，還很好讀。
  4. Not only is our teacher kind, but she's also very funny.
    我們老師不只人好，還很好笑。
  5. Forget the final. Not only are the tickets expensive, but they're also hard to get.
    決賽就別想了。票不只很貴，還很難買到。

④ 說兩件不好的事

  1. I'm tired of my brother. Not only does he never call, but he also forgets my birthday.
    我受夠我哥了。他不只從來不打電話，還忘記我的生日。
  2. I hate the number 12. Not only does the bus come late, but it's also always full.
    我討厭十二號公車。它不只晚來，還總是擠滿了人。

⑤ 正式的文字

  1. Not only does the new law protect workers, but it also helps small companies.
    新的法律不只保護勞工，還幫助小公司。
  2. Not only do electric cars cut pollution, but they also cost less to run.
    電動車不只減少污染，用起來也比較省錢。
  3. Not only does the report show the problem, but it also offers a solution.
    這份報告不只指出問題，還提出解決辦法。
  4. Not only do online courses save time, but they also reach more students.
    線上課程不只省時間，還能接觸到更多學生。
  5. Not only does the museum attract tourists, but it also creates jobs.
    博物館不只吸引觀光客，還創造工作機會。

⑥ 一般語序：not only ... but also

  1. Buy the leather ones. These bags not only look good but also last for years.
    買皮的吧。這些包包不只好看，還能用很多年。
  2. Watch her in the show. She not only sings but also dances.
    去看她的表演。她不只會唱歌，還會跳舞。
  3. The plan is not only cheap but also quick.
    這個計畫不只便宜，還很快。
  4. My husband is great. He not only cooks but also cleans the kitchen afterwards.
    我老公很棒。他不只煮飯，煮完還會清理廚房。
  5. This app is not only free but also very easy to use.
    這個 app 不只免費，還很好用。
