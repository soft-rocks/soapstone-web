常⁠見⁠的說⁠法

I think it is better to stay at another hotel.

another day、another time改天、下次
another way別的方法
another place別的地方

單⁠數名⁠詞前⁠面用 another

I think it is better to stay at another hotel.

Let's go to other restaurant.

Let's go to another restaurant.

改⁠天說 another day

I can't go today. I'll go other day.

I can't go today. I'll go another day.

複⁠數名⁠詞前⁠面用 other

Do you have this shirt in another colours?

Do you have this shirt in other colours?

兩⁠個⁠裡⁠面剩⁠下⁠的用 the other

例句

① 換一個地方

  1. This café is too crowded. Let's find another café.
    這家咖啡館人太多了。我們找別家咖啡館吧。
  2. Personally, I think it's better to stay at another hotel, such as a Holiday Inn.
    我個人覺得住別家飯店比較好，像假日飯店。
  3. The family moved to another city for the father's job.
    那一家人因為爸爸的工作搬到別的城市。
  4. If the bank is closed, try another branch.
    如果這家銀行關了，去別家分行試試。

② 改天、下次

  1. I'm busy now. Can we meet another day?
    我現在很忙。我們可以改天見面嗎？
  2. Thanks, but I'm full. Maybe another time.
    謝謝，可是我吃飽了。下次吧。
  3. I'll miss class tomorrow, but I can come another day to make up the work.
    我明天會缺課，可是我可以改天來補。
  4. The match was cancelled because of the rain. They'll play it another weekend.
    比賽因為下雨取消了。他們會改到別的週末比。

③ 換一個東西

  1. This printer doesn't work. Use another one.
    這台印表機壞了。用別台。
  2. The shoes were too small, so I asked for another size.
    鞋子太小了，所以我要了別的尺寸。
  3. My pen ran out of ink. Can I borrow another pen?
    我的筆沒水了。我可以跟你借別支筆嗎？
  4. Please choose another password. This one is too short.
    請換一個密碼。這個太短了。

④ 換一個方法

  1. If this doesn't work, we'll try another way.
    如果這樣不行，我們就試別的方法。
  2. There must be another solution to the problem.
    這個問題一定有別的解決辦法。
  3. The bridge is closed, so the bus is taking another route.
    橋封了，所以公車改走別的路線。
  4. We need to find another way to save money.
    我們得找別的方法省錢。

⑤ 換一個人

  1. If Mr Ross is busy, can I speak to another teacher?
    如果 Ross 老師在忙，我可以跟別的老師談嗎？
  2. She left her husband for another man.
    她為了別的男人離開了她先生。
  3. The doctor is on holiday, so you'll see another doctor today.
    那位醫生在休假，所以你今天會看別的醫生。
  4. I'm sorry, you'll have to ask another person.
    抱歉，你得問別人。

⑥ 另一個國家、另一種語言

  1. It's exciting to live in another country.
    住在別的國家很刺激。
  2. Learning another language opens many doors.
    學另一種語言會帶來很多機會。
  3. In another life, I would like to be a musician.
    如果有來生，我想當音樂家。
  4. They come from another part of the world.
    他們來自世界的另一個地方。

⑦ 那是另一回事

  1. Winning one game is easy, but winning the whole season is another challenge.
    贏一場比賽很容易，可是贏下整個球季又是另一個挑戰。
  2. That's another story. I'll tell you later.
    那是另一回事。我晚點再告訴你。
  3. Talking about a plan is one thing, but doing it is another thing.
    說一個計畫是一回事，做起來又是另一回事。
  4. Whether he will agree is another question.
    他會不會同意又是另一個問題。
  5. The price is fine, but the size is another problem.
    價錢沒問題，可是尺寸又是另一個問題。
  6. That's another matter. Let's discuss it next week.
    那是另一件事。我們下星期再討論。
