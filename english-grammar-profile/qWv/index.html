Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

每個房間裡都聽不到一點聲音。

Not a single 加單⁠數名⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首，表⁠示連一⁠個都⁠沒⁠有

每個房間裡都聽不到一點聲音。

single 可⁠以省⁠略，加⁠上語⁠氣更強

Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

Not a single

Not a single guest arrived on time.

沒有一個客人準時到。
Not a

Not a word was said.

一句話都沒有人說。

後⁠面常接被⁠動，注⁠意⁠力放⁠在沒⁠有東⁠西上

Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

一句話都沒有人說。

a single 當受⁠詞⁠時，not 放⁠在動⁠詞上

Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

我們一整天連一條魚都沒釣到。

主⁠詞是單⁠數名⁠詞，動⁠詞用單⁠數

Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

Not a single parent were at the meeting.

Not a single parent was at the meeting.

a 後⁠面⁠接單⁠數名⁠詞

Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

Not a single students passed.

Not a single student passed.

這⁠個句⁠型用 not，不⁠用 no

Not a single noise could be heard from inside any of the rooms.

No a single cloud was in the sky.

Not a single cloud was in the sky.

例句

① Not a single + 名詞 + 被動

  1. The museum was closed. Not a single visitor could be seen.
    博物館關了。一個參觀的人都看不到。
  2. The couple had argued, and not a single word was exchanged in the car home.
    那對夫妻吵架了，回家的車上兩個人一句話都沒說。
  3. Not a single tree was left standing after the storm.
    暴風雨過後，一棵樹都沒有留下來。
  4. Not a single ticket was sold for the Monday show.
    星期一那場表演一張票都沒賣出去。
  5. The teacher checked every page, and not a single error was found.
    老師檢查了每一頁，一個錯誤都沒找到。
  6. Not a single light could be seen in the village.
    村子裡一盞燈都看不到。

② Not a single + 名詞 + 主動的動詞

  1. Not a single member of the group expected the coach to break down in the middle of nowhere.
    團裡沒有一個人料到遊覽車會在荒郊野外拋錨。
  2. Not a single student passed the final test.
    期末考一個學生都沒及格。
  3. Not a single person came to help us when the car broke down.
    車子拋錨的時候，沒有一個人來幫我們。
  4. Not a single cloud was in the sky, so we went to the beach.
    天上一朵雲都沒有，所以我們去了海邊。
  5. Not a single shop in the village sells fresh bread.
    村子裡沒有一家店賣新鮮麵包。
  6. Not a single player on the team scored in the match.
    隊上沒有一個球員在比賽中得分。

③ Not a + 名詞（省略 single）

  1. I listened at the door, but not a sound came from the kitchen.
    我在門口聽，可是廚房沒有傳出一點聲音。
  2. Not a word was said about the money.（被動）
    錢的事一句都沒提。
  3. Not a day goes by without my grandmother calling me.
    我奶奶沒有一天不打電話給我。
  4. Not a moment passed before the phone rang again.
    電話才停一下就又響了。
  5. Not a drop of rain has fallen here since April.
    從四月到現在，這裡一滴雨都沒下。

④ 否定的動詞 + a single + 名詞（受詞）

  1. I didn't see a single car on the road all night.
    整晚路上我連一輛車都沒看到。
  2. She didn't make a single mistake in the test.
    她考試一個錯都沒犯。
  3. We didn't get a single reply to our advertisement.
    我們的廣告連一個回覆都沒收到。
  4. He didn't say a single word during dinner.
    晚餐的時候他一句話都沒說。
  5. I haven't had a single day off this month.
    這個月我一天假都沒放。
  6. The shop was open all day, but it didn't sell a single umbrella.
    那家店開了一整天，可是一把傘都沒賣出去。

⑤ not a single + 名詞 + of + 名詞片語

  1. Not a single one of my friends remembered my birthday.
    我的朋友沒有一個記得我的生日。
  2. Not a single one of the windows was open.
    窗戶一扇都沒開。
  3. Not a single one of us knew the answer.
    我們沒有一個人知道答案。

⑥ 故事和寫作

  1. The streets were empty. Not a single person was out in the snow.
    街上空蕩蕩的。雪地裡一個人都沒有。
  2. We waited for hours, but not a single bus came.
    我們等了好幾個小時，可是一班公車都沒來。
  3. The exam room was silent. Not a single pen moved.
    考場一片安靜。沒有一支筆在動。
  4. She read every page of the letter, but not a single line explained why he left.
    她把信讀了每一頁，可是沒有一行說明他為什麼離開。
  5. Not a single star was visible through the thick clouds.
    厚厚的雲層後面一顆星星都看不到。
