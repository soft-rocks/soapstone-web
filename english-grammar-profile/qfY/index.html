例句

① all + 複數名詞

  1. This notice is for all students: we are going to have a concert at school.
    這份通知是給全體學生的：學校要辦一場音樂會。
  2. All students in Year 9 need to bring a packed lunch on Friday.
    九年級所有學生星期五都要帶便當。
  3. All rooms have free Wi-Fi.
    所有房間都有免費 Wi-Fi。
  4. All drivers in the race got a cup at the end.
    參加比賽的所有車手最後都拿到一個獎盃。
  5. I like all kinds of music, from rock to jazz.
    我各種音樂都喜歡，從搖滾到爵士。

② both + 複數名詞

  1. It was exciting, as both teams played well, but India won the match.
    比賽很刺激，因為兩隊都打得很好，不過最後是印度贏了。
  2. Hold the cup with both hands. It's hot.
    兩隻手捧著杯子。很燙。
  3. There are shops on both sides of the street.
    街道兩邊都有商店。
  4. Both girls want to be doctors.
    兩個女孩都想當醫生。
  5. I've read both books, and I prefer the first one.
    兩本書我都讀過，我比較喜歡第一本。

③ a few + 複數名詞

  1. Hi Tom! I'm just writing to say hello, and to tell you a few things.
    嗨，Tom！我只是寫信打個招呼，順便跟你說幾件事。
  2. We're staying at the beach for a few days.
    我們要在海邊待幾天。
  3. I'll be ready in a few minutes.
    我再幾分鐘就好了。
  4. A few people were waiting outside the shop.
    有幾個人在店外面等。
  5. I've got a few questions about the homework.
    我對功課有幾個問題。

④ 過去的事

  1. All the guests left before midnight.
    所有客人都在半夜以前走了。
  2. We stayed in a small hotel for a few nights.
    我們在一間小旅館住了幾晚。
  3. Both players were tired at the end of the game.
    比賽結束的時候，兩個選手都很累。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. All flights to Paris are cancelled because of the snow.
    因為下雪，所有飛往巴黎的班機都取消了。
  2. A few students stayed after class to talk to the teacher.
    有幾個學生下課後留下來跟老師說話。
  3. Both my grandmothers live in the country.
    我外婆和奶奶都住在鄉下。

⑥ 對話

  1. "Which one do you like?" "I like both colours."
    你喜歡哪一個？兩種顏色我都喜歡。
  2. "Have you got any stamps?" "Yes, a few stamps. How many do you need?"
    你有郵票嗎？有，有幾張。你要幾張？
  3. "Can everyone come to the meeting?" "Yes, all teachers will be there."
    大家都能來開會嗎？能，所有老師都會到。
