Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

要是當時有大眾運輸可以搭，我就會選那個，不開車了。

had 放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，取⁠代 if

Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

要是我早知道會塞車，我就早點出門了。

這個句型說過去沒有發生的事，用在正式的書面文字和演講。

been 加形⁠容⁠詞或名⁠詞

Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

been + 形容詞

Had he been more careful, he wouldn't have broken his leg.

要是他小心一點，就不會摔斷腿了。
been + 名詞

Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

要是當時有大眾運輸可以搭，我就會選那個，不開車了。

been 是 be 的過去分詞。

否⁠定：not 放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

Had + 主詞 + not

Had she not called me, I would have missed the flight.

要是她沒打電話給我，我就錯過班機了。
Had + 主詞 + not

Had we not booked a table, we would have waited for an hour.

要是我們沒有訂位，就得等一個小時了。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句也⁠可⁠以用 could have 或 might have

could have
當時可以做到的事

Had you asked me, I could have lent you the money.

要是你開口，我就可以借你錢了。
might have
當時可能發生的事

Had the ambulance come sooner, he might have survived.

要是救護車早點來，他也許就活下來了。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句用 would + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，說現⁠在⁠的結⁠果

現在的結果

Had I saved more money, I would own a car now.

要是我以前多存點錢，現在就有車了。
現在的結果

Had he not lost his passport, he would be in Tokyo by now.

要是他沒弄丟護照，現在已經在東京了。

原句：當⁠時沒⁠有大⁠眾運⁠輸可⁠以搭

Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

要是當時有大眾運輸可以搭，我就會選那個，不開車了。

說話的人當時開車。would have chosen 說過去沒有發生的選擇。

倒⁠裝已⁠經取⁠代 if

Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

If had I known about the meeting, I would have come.

Had I known about the meeting, I would have come.

保留 if 時，用 If I had known。

倒⁠裝⁠的句⁠型不⁠縮⁠寫

Hadn't she warned us, we would have taken the wrong road.

Had she not warned us, we would have taken the wrong road.

說過⁠去⁠的結⁠果，主⁠要⁠子⁠句用 would have

Had public transportation been an option, I would have chosen that instead of my car.

Had they left earlier, they would catch the train.

Had they left earlier, they would have caught the train.

那班火車已經開走了，結果也在過去。

If + 主⁠詞 + had 意⁠思一⁠樣，也用在口⁠語

If I had

If I had known about the party, I would have gone.

要是我知道有派對，我就會去了。
If we hadn't

If we hadn't taken a taxi, we would have been late.

要是我們沒搭計程車，就遲到了。

If 的句型可以縮寫成 hadn't。

Were 的倒⁠裝說未⁠來，Had 的倒⁠裝說過⁠去

Were ... to
未來假設的事

Were the train to arrive late, we would miss the concert.

萬一火車晚到，我們會錯過音樂會。
Had
過去沒有發生的事

Had the train arrived late, we would have missed the concert.

要是火車當時晚到，我們就錯過音樂會了。

例句

① Had + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. Had I known you were in town, I would have invited you to dinner.
    要是我知道你在城裡，就會請你吃晚餐了。
  2. Had we left five minutes earlier, we would have caught the ferry.
    要是我們早五分鐘出發，就趕上渡輪了。
  3. Had she listened to her lawyer, she would have avoided the fine.
    要是她聽律師的話，就不會被罰款了。
  4. Had the referee seen the foul, he would have sent the player off.
    要是裁判看到那次犯規，他早就把那名球員罰下場了。
  5. Had they checked the weather, they would have stayed at home.
    要是他們有看天氣預報，就會待在家裡了。
  6. Had you told me about the contretemps, I would have tried to placate the client.
    要是你跟我說了那場小風波，我就會想辦法安撫客戶。

② Had + 主詞 + been + 形容詞或名詞

  1. Had the hotel been cheaper, we would have stayed another week.
    要是飯店便宜一點，我們就多住一個星期了。
  2. Had I been in your position, I would have done the same.
    要是我是你，我也會這麼做。
  3. Had the instructions been clearer, nobody would have got lost.
    要是說明清楚一點，就不會有人迷路了。
  4. Had she been a little older, she would have understood.
    要是她大一點，她就會懂了。
  5. Had the task been less laborious, we would have finished by lunch.
    要是那份工作不那麼費力，我們中午前就做完了。

③ 否定

  1. Had we not booked a table, we would have waited for an hour.
    要是我們沒有訂位，就得等一個小時了。
  2. Had he not apologized, I would never have spoken to him again.
    要是他沒道歉，我就再也不跟他說話了。
  3. Had the firefighters not arrived so quickly, the whole building would have burned down.
    要是消防員沒有那麼快趕到，整棟大樓早就燒光了。
  4. Had I not missed the bus, I would never have met my wife.
    要是我沒錯過那班公車，就不會認識我太太了。
  5. Had the company not cut costs, it would have gone bankrupt.
    要是公司沒有削減成本，早就破產了。

④ could have 和 might have

  1. Had I studied harder, I could have got into medical school.
    要是我當時更用功，就能進醫學院了。
  2. Had the road been less icy, the accident might never have happened.
    要是路面沒那麼滑，那場意外可能就不會發生。
  3. Had you asked me, I could have lent you the money.
    要是你開口，我就可以借你錢了。
  4. Had the letter arrived on time, things might have turned out differently.
    要是那封信準時到，事情的結果可能就不一樣了。
  5. Had we invested in that company, we might have made a fortune.
    要是我們投資了那家公司，可能已經賺了一大筆錢。

⑤ 主要子句說現在的結果

  1. Had I taken that job in Paris, I would be living in France now.
    要是我接了巴黎那份工作，現在就住在法國了。
  2. Had she finished her degree, she would have a better job today.
    要是她把學位念完，今天會有更好的工作。
  3. Had we bought a house ten years ago, we wouldn't be paying rent now.
    要是我們十年前買了房子，現在就不用付房租了。
  4. Had he not lost his passport, he would be in Tokyo by now.
    要是他沒弄丟護照，現在已經在東京了。

⑥ 報告和論說文

  1. Had the government acted sooner, it could have saved thousands of lives.
    要是政府早點行動，就能救成千上萬條人命。
  2. Had the bank's managers been less venal, the crisis would never have happened.
    要是銀行經理們沒那麼貪財，那場危機根本不會發生。
  3. Had the council listened to residents, it would have kept the library open.
    要是市議會聽了居民的意見，就會讓圖書館繼續開放。
  4. Had the researchers repeated the experiment, they might have got different results.
    要是研究人員重做那個實驗，可能會得到不同的結果。
  5. Had the regime been less draconian, fewer people would have left the country.
    要是那個政權沒那麼嚴苛，離開那個國家的人會少一點。
  6. Had the inspectors done more than a perfunctory check, they would have found the cracks.
    要是檢查員不只是草草看一下，就會發現那些裂縫。
  7. Had early doctors understood germs, millions of patients would have survived.
    要是早期的醫生了解細菌，幾百萬個病人就能活下來。
