We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

我們喜歡一起彈鋼琴、唱歌。

enjoy 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

我們喜歡一起彈鋼琴、唱歌。

否⁠定和問⁠句⁠的 do 放⁠在 enjoy 前⁠面

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

否定

I don't enjoy driving at night.

我不喜歡晚上開車。
問句

Did you enjoy the film?

你喜歡那部電影嗎？

enjoy 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠用 to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

I enjoy to swim in the sea.

I enjoy swimming in the sea.

enjoy 後⁠面⁠的動⁠詞不⁠用原⁠形

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

My dad enjoys cook for us.

My dad enjoys cooking for us.

and 連⁠接⁠的兩⁠個動⁠詞都用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

We enjoy reading and watch films.

We enjoy reading and watching films.

enjoy 後⁠面要接名⁠詞或動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

I really enjoyed at the party.

I really enjoyed the party.

說玩得開心時用 enjoy myself。

like 兩⁠種形⁠式都⁠可⁠以⁠接，enjoy 只接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

like

I like swimming. I like to swim.

我喜歡游泳。
enjoy

I enjoy swimming in the sea.

我喜歡在海裡游泳。

enjoy yourself 的意⁠思是玩⁠得開⁠心

We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.

在派對上玩得開心！

例句

① enjoy + 動詞-ing

  1. My sister and I love music. We enjoy playing the piano and singing together.
    我姊姊和我很愛音樂。我們喜歡一起彈鋼琴、唱歌。
  2. You know I really enjoy listening to music, so the concert tickets are a perfect present.
    你知道我真的很喜歡聽音樂，所以演唱會的票是最棒的禮物。
  3. My grandmother enjoys working in her garden every morning.
    我奶奶喜歡每天早上在她的花園裡做事。
  4. "Why do you walk to work?" "I enjoy walking. It helps me think."
    你為什麼走路上班？我喜歡走路。走路能幫我想事情。
  5. Our son enjoys building things with his toy bricks.
    我們兒子喜歡用積木蓋東西。
  6. I enjoy cooking for my friends at the weekend.
    我週末喜歡煮飯給朋友吃。
  7. Most children enjoy being outside.
    大部分的小孩都喜歡待在戶外。

② enjoy + 兩個動詞-ing

  1. On holiday we enjoy swimming in the morning and reading in the afternoon.
    度假的時候，我們喜歡早上游泳、下午看書。
  2. My uncle enjoys fishing and camping by the lake.
    我叔叔喜歡在湖邊釣魚和露營。
  3. She enjoys drawing and painting, so she wants to study art.
    她喜歡素描和畫畫，所以她想念藝術。
  4. Now that they've retired, my parents enjoy travelling and trying new food.
    我爸媽退休以後，喜歡到處旅行、嚐嚐新的食物。

③ enjoyed：過去

  1. We really enjoyed staying at your house. Thank you!
    我們很喜歡住在你家。謝謝你！
  2. The kids enjoyed feeding the ducks at the park.
    孩子們在公園餵鴨子餵得很開心。
  3. I enjoyed working with you on this project.
    我很高興能和你一起做這個計畫。
  4. When I was young, I enjoyed climbing trees with my brother.
    我小時候喜歡跟我哥一起爬樹。

④ 否定

  1. I don't enjoy driving at night because the lights hurt my eyes.
    我不喜歡晚上開車，因為車燈讓我眼睛不舒服。
  2. My brother doesn't enjoy doing his homework, but he always finishes it.
    我弟弟不喜歡寫功課，可是他總是會寫完。
  3. She didn't enjoy living in a big city, so she moved back home.
    她不喜歡住在大城市，所以搬回家了。
  4. Nobody enjoys waiting in the rain for a bus.
    沒有人喜歡在雨中等公車。

⑤ 問句

  1. "Do you enjoy working at the hospital?" "Yes, but it's very tiring."
    你喜歡在醫院工作嗎？喜歡，可是很累。
  2. "Did you enjoy visiting your grandparents?" "Yes, we had a great time."
    你去看爺爺奶奶開心嗎？開心，我們玩得很愉快。
  3. "What do you enjoy doing in your free time?" "I enjoy taking photos."
    你空閒的時候喜歡做什麼？我喜歡拍照。
  4. "Does your dog enjoy swimming?" "He loves it!"
    你的狗喜歡游泳嗎？牠超愛的！

⑥ enjoy + 名詞

  1. "Did you enjoy the film?" "Yes, it was really funny."
    你喜歡那部電影嗎？喜歡，真的很好笑。
  2. Thank you for coming. We hope you enjoyed your stay.
    謝謝您的光臨。希望您住得愉快。
  3. Everyone enjoyed the food at the wedding.
    大家都很喜歡婚禮上的食物。
  4. "Here's your coffee. Enjoy your breakfast!" "Thank you."
    這是您的咖啡。早餐請慢用！謝謝。

⑦ enjoy + 反身代名詞

  1. Have a great trip and enjoy yourselves!
    旅途愉快，好好玩！
  2. "How was the party?" "Great! I really enjoyed myself."
    派對怎麼樣？很棒！我玩得很開心。
  3. The children enjoyed themselves at the beach all day.
    孩子們整天在海邊玩得很開心。
  4. "Did you enjoy yourself at the concert?" "Yes, the band was amazing."
    你在演唱會上玩得開心嗎？開心，那個樂團太棒了。
