I am not going to go to school tomorrow.

我明天不打算去上學。

主⁠詞 + am / is / are + not + going to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

not、going to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I am not going to go to school tomorrow.

not放在 am、is、are 後面。
going to + 原形動詞不會做或不會發生的事。

縮⁠寫

I am not going to go to school tomorrow.

I'm not going to

I'm not going to tell you.

我不會告訴你。
isn't going to / 's not going to

He isn't going to like it.

他不會喜歡的。
aren't going to / 're not going to

They aren't going to win.

他們不會贏。

口語常用縮寫。

there is not going to be

例句

There isn't going to be a party this year.

今年不會辦派對。

there is not going to be 加名詞，表示某樣東西不會存在。

going 後⁠面要⁠有 to，再⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I am not going to go to school tomorrow.

I'm not going go to the meeting.

I'm not going to go to the meeting.

be going to 的否⁠定用 be 動⁠詞加 not，不⁠用 don't

I am not going to go to school tomorrow.

I don't going to go.

I'm not going to go.

I am not 縮⁠寫成 I'm not

I am not going to go to school tomorrow.

I amn't going to help.

I'm not going to help.

例句

① 主詞 + am / is / are + not + going to + 原形動詞

  1. I am not going to apply for that job in London.
    我不打算應徵倫敦那份工作。
  2. She is not going to change her mind.
    她不打算改變主意。
  3. We are not going to buy a new car this year.
    我們今年不打算買新車。
  4. The shop is not going to open on Monday.
    那家店星期一不會開。
  5. They are not going to sell the farm.
    他們不打算賣掉農場。
  6. It is not going to rain this weekend.
    這個週末不會下雨。
  7. My parents are not going to let me go to the concert.
    我爸媽不會讓我去聽音樂會。
  8. The teacher is not going to give us homework today.
    老師今天不會出功課給我們。
  9. I am not going to eat any more chocolate.
    我不要再吃巧克力了。
  10. He is not going to finish the report on time.
    他沒辦法準時寫完報告。

② 縮寫：I'm not / isn't / aren't going to

  1. I'm not going to lend him any more money.
    我不打算再借他錢了。
  2. He isn't going to enjoy the long drive to the coast.
    他不會喜歡開那麼長的車去海邊。
  3. They aren't going to win the cup this year.
    他們今年不會贏得獎盃。
  4. I'm not going to wait any longer.
    我不要再等了。
  5. She's not going to come to the party.
    她不會來派對。
  6. We aren't going to have enough time.
    我們不會有足夠的時間。
  7. You're not going to believe this.
    你一定不會相信這件事。
  8. The bus isn't going to stop here.
    公車不會停在這裡。
  9. I'm not going to watch TV tonight.
    我今晚不打算看電視。
  10. They're not going to move house after all.
    他們最後不會搬家了。
  11. It isn't going to be easy.
    這不會很容易。
  12. My brother isn't going to study law.
    我哥不打算念法律。
  13. We're not going to stay long.
    我們不會待很久。

③ there is not going to be + 名詞

  1. There is not going to be an adult there to stop you.
    那裡不會有大人管你。
  2. There isn't going to be a school trip in the spring.
    春天不會有校外教學。
  3. There aren't going to be any buses after midnight.
    半夜以後不會有公車。
  4. There isn't going to be enough food for everyone.
    食物不會夠所有人吃。
  5. There's not going to be a test on Friday.
    星期五不會考試。
  6. There aren't going to be many people at the meeting.
    會議不會有很多人來。
  7. There isn't going to be time to see the museum.
    不會有時間去參觀博物館。
  8. There aren't going to be any classes next week.
    下星期不會上課。
