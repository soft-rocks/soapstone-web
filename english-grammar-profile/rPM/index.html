例句

① 疑問詞 + have / has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. Where have you put my glasses?
    你把我的眼鏡放到哪裡去了？
  2. Why has the price changed?
    價錢為什麼變了？
  3. How have you been since the operation?
    你開刀之後身體還好嗎？
  4. What have they done to the garden?
    他們把花園弄成什麼樣子了？
  5. Where has Emma gone?
    Emma 去哪裡了？
  6. How has the new manager settled in?
    新經理適應得怎麼樣？
  7. Why have the shops closed so early today?
    店家今天為什麼這麼早就關門了？
  8. What have you told your parents?
    你跟你爸媽說了什麼？

② how long / how many / how much

  1. How long has your sister been a nurse?
    你姊姊當護士多久了？
  2. How many countries have you visited?
    你去過幾個國家？
  3. How much money has she saved?
    她存了多少錢？
  4. How long has your grandfather lived in that village?
    你爺爺在那個村子住多久了？
  5. How many cups of coffee have you had today?
    你今天喝了幾杯咖啡？

③ Have / Has + 主詞 + 過去分詞

  1. Has your son ever ridden a horse?
    你兒子騎過馬嗎？
  2. Has the doctor called back?
    醫生回電了嗎？
  3. Have they fixed the lift yet?
    他們修好電梯了沒？
  4. Has your brother found a job?
    你哥找到工作了嗎？
  5. Have we met before?
    我們以前見過嗎？
  6. Has anyone seen my phone?
    有人看到我的手機嗎？
  7. Have you read the instructions?
    你看過說明了嗎？

④ 回答：Yes / No + 主詞 + have / has

  1. Has the train left? — Yes, it has.
    火車開走了嗎？開走了。
  2. Have you paid the bill? — No, I haven't.
    你付帳單了嗎？還沒。
  3. Has Tom finished the report? — No, he hasn't.
    Tom 報告寫完了嗎？還沒。
  4. Have the kids eaten? — Yes, they have.
    孩子們吃過了嗎？吃過了。

⑤ 疑問詞當主詞

  1. What has happened to your car?
    你的車怎麼了？
  2. Who has taken my umbrella?
    誰拿走了我的傘？
  3. Which team has won the most games?
    哪一隊贏最多場？
  4. Who has broken the window?
    誰把窗戶打破了？

⑥ 問候：How has / have ... been

  1. How has work been this week?
    這禮拜工作還好嗎？
  2. How have your parents been?
    你爸媽最近好嗎？
  3. How has the weather been in Scotland?
    蘇格蘭最近天氣怎麼樣？
