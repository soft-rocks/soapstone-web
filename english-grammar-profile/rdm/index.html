I spent about £35 on all those clothes.

我忘了跟你說明天晚上的一些細節。

主⁠詞 + 動⁠詞⁠的過⁠去⁠式

I spent about £35 on all those clothes.

規則動詞加 -⁠ed，像 liked、stayed、tried。
不規則動詞要一個一個記，像 spent、forgot、won。

spent + 錢 / 時⁠間 + on

I spent about £35 on all those clothes.

例句

The maths was very hard last night. I spent two hours on my homework.

昨晚的數學很難。我花了兩個小時寫功課。

spent 說花了多少錢或時間在某件事上。

spend 的過⁠去⁠式是 spent

I spent about £35 on all those clothes.

I spended all my pocket money at the fair.

I spent all my pocket money at the fair.

buy 的過⁠去⁠式是 bought

My mum buyed a new coat for the winter.

My mum bought a new coat for the winter.

win 的過⁠去⁠式是 won

Our team winned the match against the city school.

Our team won the match against the city school.

例句

① 主詞 + spent + 錢 / 時間 + on + 名詞

  1. The second-hand market was so cheap. I spent about £35 on all those clothes.
    二手市集好便宜。我買那些衣服總共花了大約三十五英鎊。
  2. It rained all day on Sunday. We spent the whole afternoon on a big jigsaw puzzle.
    星期天下了一整天的雨。我們整個下午都在拼一幅大拼圖。
  3. Look at that shiny red car outside. My dad spent a lot of money on it.
    你看外面那台閃亮的紅色車子。我爸爸在它上面花了很多錢。
  4. My sister got top marks. She spent three hours on her science project.
    我妹妹拿了最高分。她花了三個小時做她的自然科作業。
  5. I love reading. I spent my birthday money on books.
    我很愛看書。我把生日禮金花在買書上。
  6. My parents love Italy. They spent all their savings on a trip to Rome.
    我爸媽很愛義大利。他們把所有的積蓄都花在去羅馬的旅行上。

② 主詞 + forgot + to + 原形動詞 / 名詞

  1. I'm sorry, I forgot to tell you some details about tomorrow evening.
    抱歉，我忘了告訴你一些明天晚上的細節。
  2. My sister came home very wet. She forgot her umbrella on the bus.
    我姊姊全身濕透回到家。她把雨傘忘在公車上了。
  3. I forgot to switch off the oven before we went out.
    我們出門前我忘了關烤箱。
  4. My brother feels terrible. He forgot his mother's birthday.
    我哥哥覺得很糟。他忘了他媽媽的生日。
  5. Someone came into the garden last night. We forgot to lock the back door.
    昨晚有人進了花園。我們忘了鎖後門。
  6. We had a free weekend. The teacher forgot to give us our homework.
    我們這個週末很空閒。老師忘了給我們功課。

③ 主詞 + won / lost + 名詞：贏得和失去

  1. I liked the match very much because my favourite team won. It was great.
    我很喜歡那場比賽，因為我最喜歡的隊伍贏了。太棒了。
  2. Look at the cup in the hall. Our school won the football cup last year.
    你看大廳裡的獎盃。我們學校去年贏得了足球盃。
  3. My cousin is a great artist. She won first prize in the painting competition.
    我表妹畫畫很厲害。她在繪畫比賽中得了第一名。
  4. It was so close. We lost the final by one point.
    比分好接近。我們以一分之差輸了決賽。
  5. My uncle is very lucky. He won a holiday in a radio competition.
    我叔叔運氣很好。他在一個廣播比賽中贏得了一趟旅行。
  6. Don't call him tonight. My brother lost his phone at the concert.
    今晚別打給他。我哥哥在演唱會上弄丟了手機。

④ 主詞 + bought / took + 名詞：購物和旅行

  1. It's Mother's Day tomorrow. I bought a present for my mum at the market.
    明天是母親節。我在市場買了一個禮物給我媽媽。
  2. The trip was beautiful. We took a lot of photos at the waterfall.
    那趟旅行很美。我們在瀑布拍了很多照片。
  3. My friend worked until nine. She bought a ticket for the late train.
    我朋友工作到九點。她買了一張晚班火車的票。
  4. There was no bridge. They took the boat to the island.
    那裡沒有橋。他們搭船去島上。
  5. My aunt bought a second-hand piano for her daughter.
    我阿姨幫她女兒買了一台二手鋼琴。
  6. My parents were busy on Saturday. I took my little sister to the zoo.
    我爸媽星期六很忙。我帶我妹妹去動物園。

⑤ 主詞 + 規則動詞的過去式：更多日常的事

  1. Our holiday in Spain was great. We stayed at a lovely hotel near the beach.
    我們在西班牙的假期很棒。我們住在海灘附近一家很棒的飯店。
  2. I tried sushi for the first time in Japan.
    我在日本第一次吃壽司。
  3. My grandmother smiled when she saw the old photos.
    我奶奶看到那些舊照片時笑了。
  4. The taxi driver was very kind. He carried my bags to the car.
    計程車司機人很好。他幫我把行李提到車上。
  5. Mrs Lee was leaving the school. We planned a surprise party for her.
    李老師要離開學校了。我們為她計畫了一個驚喜派對。
  6. The children enjoyed the trip to the farm.
    孩子們很喜歡去農場的旅行。
  7. I'm so tired this morning. I studied all night for the exam.
    我今天早上好累。我為了考試讀了一整晚的書。
