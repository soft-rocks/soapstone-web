The city where I live is very nice and extremely charming.

我住的城市很棒，而且非常迷人。

where 接在地⁠點名⁠詞後⁠面，說⁠明是哪⁠一⁠個地⁠方

我住的城市很棒，而且非常迷人。

子⁠句⁠的動⁠詞不⁠接⁠受⁠詞用 where，要接⁠受⁠詞用 which

The city where I live is very nice and extremely charming.

where

The town where I grew up is very small.

我長大的那個小鎮很小。
which

The town which I visited last summer is very small.

我去年夏天去的那個小鎮很小。

子⁠句放⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面或句⁠尾

The city where I live is very nice and extremely charming.

主詞後面

The café where we first met has closed down.

我們第一次見面的那家咖啡店關了。
句尾

I'd like to find a park where my dog can run around.

我想找一個讓我的狗可以到處跑的公園。

where 已⁠經表⁠示在那⁠裡，不⁠再⁠加 there

The city where I live is very nice and extremely charming.

The village where my parents live there is beautiful.

The village where my parents live is beautiful.

visited 要接⁠受⁠詞，用 which

The museum where we visited was full of tourists.

The museum which we visited was full of tourists.

lived 不⁠接⁠受⁠詞，用 where

The city where I live is very nice and extremely charming.

That's the house which I lived when I was a child.

That's the house where I lived when I was a child.

也可以寫成 which I lived in。

例句

① 地點名詞在主詞的位置

  1. The beach where we spent our honeymoon is now a resort.
    我們度蜜月的海灘現在變成度假村了。
  2. The office where my mother works is on the tenth floor.
    我媽媽上班的辦公室在十樓。
  3. The school where I teach has only two hundred students.
    我教書的學校只有兩百個學生。
  4. The restaurant where they got engaged doesn't exist anymore.
    他們訂婚的那家餐廳已經不在了。
  5. The street where we parked the car was really dark.
    我們停車的那條街好暗。
  6. The village where my grandmother grew up has no supermarket.
    我奶奶長大的那個村子沒有超市。

② 地點名詞在受詞的位置

  1. We're looking for a hotel where we can bring our dog.
    我們在找一間可以帶狗入住的飯店。
  2. I remember the room where I used to do my homework.
    我記得我以前寫功課的那個房間。
  3. Let's go to a place where nobody knows us.
    我們去一個沒人認識我們的地方吧。
  4. Can you show me the spot where you found the wallet?
    你可以給我看你撿到皮夾的地方嗎？
  5. They moved to a town where houses are much cheaper.
    他們搬到一個房子便宜很多的小鎮。
  6. I need a quiet corner where I can study.
    我需要一個安靜的角落念書。

③ the place / somewhere / anywhere + where

  1. This is the place where the accident happened.
    這就是事故發生的地方。
  2. Is there somewhere where we can sit down?
    有沒有什麼地方我們可以坐下來？
  3. Home is the place where I feel most relaxed.
    家是我覺得最放鬆的地方。
  4. I'd like to live somewhere where it never snows.
    我想住在一個從來不下雪的地方。
  5. We couldn't find anywhere where they sold batteries.
    我們找不到有賣電池的地方。

④ 不同的時態

  1. That's the corner where I'll be waiting for you.
    那就是我會等你的那個轉角。
  2. This is the park where I learned to ride a bike.
    這是我學會騎腳踏車的公園。
  3. The airport where we've just landed is tiny.
    我們剛降落的機場好小。
  4. The gym where I was training last year has closed.
    我去年訓練的那家健身房關了。
  5. Do you know the bakery where they make those huge croissants?
    你知道做那種超大可頌的麵包店嗎？

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Taitung is the city where I feel most at home.
    台東是讓我最有家的感覺的城市。
  2. I've booked a table at the restaurant where we had our first date.
    我在我們第一次約會的那家餐廳訂了位子。
  3. Is this the shop where you bought your glasses?
    這是你買眼鏡的店嗎？
  4. The temple where we stayed had a beautiful garden.
    我們住過的那間寺廟有一座很美的花園。
  5. Show me the drawer where you keep the spare keys.
    給我看你放備用鑰匙的抽屜。
  6. The island where they filmed that movie is full of tourists now.
    他們拍那部電影的那座島現在到處都是觀光客。
  7. That's the bridge where my father proposed to my mother.
    那就是我爸爸跟我媽媽求婚的那座橋。
  8. We visited the factory where they make the chocolate.
    我們參觀了製作那種巧克力的工廠。
