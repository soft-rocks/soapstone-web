Wouldn't it be wonderful!

那該有多好啊！

助⁠動⁠詞加 n't 放⁠在句⁠首，表⁠示感⁠嘆

Wouldn't it be wonderful!

真美啊！

常⁠見⁠的說⁠法：想⁠像、讚⁠嘆、希⁠望對⁠方同⁠意、回⁠想

Wouldn't it be wonderful!

想像

Wouldn't it be lovely to live by the sea!

住在海邊該有多好！
讚嘆

Isn't that amazing!

希望對方同意

Wouldn't it be wonderful!

n't 跟⁠著助⁠動⁠詞或 be

例句

① Wouldn't it be + 形容詞

  1. Wouldn't it be wonderful to live by the sea!
    住在海邊該有多好啊！
  2. Wouldn't it be nice to have a whole week off!
    能放一整個禮拜的假該有多好！
  3. Wouldn't it be great if we all went together!
    如果我們大家一起去，那該有多棒！
  4. Wouldn't it be fun to learn to dance!
    學跳舞該有多好玩！
  5. Wouldn't it be lovely to see the northern lights!
    能看到北極光該有多好！

② Isn't it / Isn't that + 形容詞

  1. Look at the view! Isn't it beautiful!
    你看那景色！真美啊！
  2. Isn't that amazing! She's only six.
    太厲害了吧！她才六歲。
  3. Isn't this cake delicious!
    這蛋糕真好吃！
  4. Blue sky and no wind. Isn't it a perfect day for sailing!
    藍天又沒風，今天真適合出海！
  5. Aren't they cute!
    他們好可愛啊！

③ Doesn't that sound / look ...?

  1. A weekend in the mountains? Doesn't that sound perfect?
    去山上過週末？你不覺得聽起來很完美嗎？
  2. Doesn't she look happy!
    她看起來好開心啊！
  3. Doesn't this smell good!
    這聞起來好香啊！
  4. Doesn't the garden look lovely in spring!
    花園春天看起來好漂亮啊！
  5. Don't you just love this song!
    你不覺得這首歌超棒嗎！

④ Didn't we / Wasn't it ...!

  1. Didn't we have a great time in Paris!
    我們在巴黎玩得真開心啊！
  2. Wasn't that a fantastic concert!
    那場演唱會真是太精彩了！
  3. Wasn't the seafood at that little harbor restaurant amazing!
    港口那家小餐廳的海鮮真是太好吃了！
  4. Didn't the choir sound wonderful at the wedding!
    婚禮上的合唱團唱得真好聽啊！
  5. Weren't the kids good today!
    孩子們今天真乖啊！

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Wouldn't you love to visit Japan!
    你不會很想去日本嗎！
  2. Isn't it strange how quiet the town is today!
    今天鎮上這麼安靜，好奇怪啊！
  3. Hasn't she grown!
    她長這麼大了！
  4. Won't it be nice to see everyone again!
    能再見到大家該有多好！
  5. Isn't it lucky the shop was still open!
    還好那家店還開著！
  6. Doesn't time fly!
    時間過得真快啊！
  7. Top of the class! Haven't you worked hard this year!
    全班第一！你今年真的好用功！
  8. Isn't it a shame they can't come!
    他們不能來，真可惜！
  9. Wouldn't it be amazing to see the band play live!
    能看那個樂團現場演出該有多棒！
  10. Don't you look smart in your new uniform!
    你穿新制服真帥氣！
