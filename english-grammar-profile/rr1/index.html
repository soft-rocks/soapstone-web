Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

有些學生得到的專業協助，會比他們應該得到的少。

主⁠詞 + 動⁠詞 + …, as + 主⁠詞 + ought to.

Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

ought to句子在 to 結束，後面的動詞省略。
省略的動詞前面已經說過，聽的人知道是什麼。

to 不⁠能省⁠略

Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

例句

My doctor says I need more exercise. I don't exercise as often as I ought to.

醫生說我需要多運動。我運動得沒有我應該的那麼常。

省略的時候，to 要留下來。

回⁠答問⁠題

Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

例句

"Should I call him?" "Yes, I think you ought to."

「我該打給他嗎？」「對，我想你應該打。」

回答問題時也可以只說 ought to。

should 後⁠面不⁠接 to

Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

I'm always tired at work. I don't sleep as much as I should to.

I'm always tired at work. I don't sleep as much as I ought to.

動⁠詞省⁠略以⁠後，句⁠子在 to 結⁠束

Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

Dad does all the cleaning. My brother doesn't help, but he ought to it.

Dad does all the cleaning. My brother doesn't help, but he ought to.

ought 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.

Kate lives far from home now. She doesn't call her mum, but she oughts to.

Kate lives far from home now. She doesn't call her mum, but she ought to.

例句

① as + 主詞 + ought to：比較實際和應該

  1. With forty pupils in a class, some students would not get as much specialised help as they ought to.
    一班四十個學生，有些學生就得不到他們應該得到的專門協助。
  2. My jeans feel tight again. I don't go to the gym as often as I ought to.
    我的牛仔褲又變緊了。我上健身房沒有我應該的那麼常。
  3. My son's English teacher is worried. He doesn't read as much as he ought to.
    我兒子的英文老師很擔心。他讀的書沒有他應該讀的那麼多。
  4. Our bins are full of plastic every week. We don't recycle as much as we ought to.
    我們的垃圾桶每週都裝滿塑膠。我們回收的沒有我們應該回收的那麼多。
  5. The children are up until eleven playing games. They don't sleep as much as they ought to.
    孩子們打電動打到十一點。他們睡得沒有他們應該睡的那麼多。
  6. My grandparents live two hours away. I don't visit them as often as I ought to.
    我爺爺奶奶住在兩個小時車程外。我去看他們沒有我應該的那麼常。

② 主詞 + don't + 動詞, but + 主詞 + ought to

  1. I get headaches in the afternoon. I don't drink much water, but I ought to.
    我下午常頭痛。我不常喝水，可是我應該多喝。
  2. My sister rides her bike to school every day. She doesn't wear a helmet, but she ought to.
    我妹妹每天騎腳踏車上學。她沒戴安全帽，可是她應該戴。
  3. Our neighbours spend everything they earn. They don't save any money, but they ought to.
    我們的鄰居賺多少花多少。他們都不存錢，可是他們應該存。
  4. Grandma cooks for Uncle Bill every Sunday. He never says thank you, but he ought to.
    奶奶每個星期天都煮飯給 Bill 叔叔吃。他從來不說謝謝，可是他應該說。
  5. Everyone in my family leaves at different times. We don't eat breakfast together, but we ought to.
    我家每個人出門的時間都不一樣。我們不一起吃早餐，可是我們應該一起吃。
  6. Mum works full time. My brother doesn't help with the cooking, but he ought to.
    媽媽做全職工作。我哥哥不幫忙煮飯，可是他應該幫忙。

③ "Should + 主詞 + …？" "Yes, 主詞 + ought to."：回答

  1. "A boy in my class took my phone. Should I tell my teacher?" "Yes, I think you ought to."
    我班上一個男生拿了我的手機。我該告訴我的老師嗎？對，我想你應該說。
  2. "That Italian place is always busy on Saturdays. Do you think we should book?" "Yes, we ought to."
    那家義大利餐廳星期六總是客滿。你覺得我們應該訂位嗎？對，我們應該訂。
  3. "Amy broke Jo's favourite mug. Should she apologise?" "I think she ought to."
    Amy 打破了 Jo 最喜歡的杯子。她應該道歉嗎？我想她應該。
  4. "I've had this cough for three weeks. Should I go to the doctor?" "Yes, you really ought to."
    我咳了三個星期了。我該去看醫生嗎？對，你真的應該去。
  5. "Their cousin is in town on the day of the party. Should they invite her?" "I suppose they ought to."
    派對那天他們的表妹剛好在城裡。他們應該邀請她嗎？我想他們應該邀請。
  6. "Dad hasn't heard from me for a month. Do you think I should call him?" "Yes, you ought to."
    爸爸一個月沒有我的消息了。你覺得我應該打給他嗎？對，你應該打。

④ 主詞 + 動詞 + … + if + 主詞 + ought to

  1. You know more about the project than me, but I'll speak at the meeting if you think I ought to.
    你比我了解這個專案，可是如果你覺得我應該在會議上發言，我就發言。
  2. The shop gave you twenty pounds too much? Take the money back if you think you ought to.
    店家多找了你二十英鎊？如果你覺得應該還錢，就還吧。
  3. You know who broke Leo's bike. Tell him the truth if you feel you ought to.
    你知道是誰弄壞了 Leo 的腳踏車。如果你覺得應該告訴他真相，就說吧。
  4. Grandma seems lonely tonight. We'll stay longer if you think we ought to.
    奶奶今晚好像很寂寞。如果你覺得我們應該多待一會，我們就多待。
  5. The weather looks bad for Saturday. Change the plan if you think we ought to.
    星期六的天氣看起來很糟。如果你覺得我們應該改計畫，就改吧。
  6. There's a man in a car outside the school every day. Phone the police if you think you ought to.
    每天都有個男人坐在學校外面的車裡。如果你覺得應該報警，就打電話吧。

⑤ 主詞 + 動詞 + more / less than + 主詞 + ought to

  1. My brother buys new trainers every month. He spends more than he ought to.
    我哥哥每個月都買新球鞋。他花的錢比他應該花的多。
  2. I have biscuits with every cup of tea. I eat more sugar than I ought to.
    我每喝一杯茶就配餅乾。我吃的糖比我應該吃的多。
  3. My mum is a nurse and often works twelve-hour shifts. She works longer hours than she ought to.
    我媽媽是護理師，常常一班工作十二個小時。她工作的時間比她應該工作的長。
  4. My cousins have a TV in every room. They watch more TV than they ought to.
    我表弟妹每個房間都有電視。他們看的電視比他們應該看的多。
  5. Our accountant checked our forms. Every year we pay more tax than we ought to.
    我們的會計師檢查了我們的表格。我們每年繳的稅都比我們應該繳的多。
  6. It's the summer holidays, so the kids stay up later than they ought to.
    現在是暑假，所以孩子們熬夜熬得比他們應該的晚。
  7. I can't put it down, even at dinner. I check my phone more often than I ought to.
    我連吃晚餐都放不下手機。我看手機看得比我應該的還常。
