I asked Pat not to tell anyone, and she promised me she wouldn't.

我請 Pat 別跟任何人說，她答應我她不會說。

原話⁠的 Don't 轉⁠述成 not to 加原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

我請 Pat 別跟任何人說，她答應我她不會說。

原話是 "Please don't tell anyone."

ask 是請⁠求，tell 是命⁠令或警⁠告

I asked Pat not to tell anyone, and she promised me she wouldn't.

ask

She asked me not to call her after ten.

她請我十點以後別打給她。
tell

My mum told me not to touch the oven.

我媽叫我別碰烤箱。

not to 和 to 可⁠以用 and 連起⁠來

我爸叫我別走遠，待在他旁邊。

warn、remind、beg 也接人 + not to

I asked Pat not to tell anyone, and she promised me she wouldn't.

warn
警告

The guide warned us not to swim in the river.

導遊警告我們別在河裡游泳。
remind
提醒

She reminded me not to forget my keys.

她提醒我別忘了帶鑰匙。
beg
懇求

He begged her not to leave.

他求她別走。

原話⁠的 don't 轉⁠述⁠時換⁠成 not to

I asked Pat not to tell anyone, and she promised me she wouldn't.

I asked Pat don't tell anyone.

I asked Pat not to tell anyone.

tell 後⁠面要接被要⁠求⁠的人

I asked Pat not to tell anyone, and she promised me she wouldn't.

She told not to go near the edge.

She told us not to go near the edge.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I asked Pat not to tell anyone, and she promised me she wouldn't.

He told me not to went there alone.

He told me not to go there alone.

say 後⁠面不⁠接⁠人，要說⁠出被要⁠求⁠的人用 tell

The doctor said me not to eat too much salt.

The doctor told me not to eat too much salt.

not 的位⁠置不⁠同，意⁠思不⁠同

didn't tell
沒有下命令

I didn't tell him to leave.

我沒有叫他走。
not to
叫他不要做

I told him not to leave.

我叫他不要走。

asked 後⁠面⁠接 if 子⁠句，轉⁠述⁠的⁠是問⁠題

我問 Pat 她有沒有跟別人說。

例句

① asked + 人 + not to

  1. She asked me not to tell her parents.
    她請我別跟她爸媽說。
  2. We asked the neighbours not to park in front of our gate.
    我們請鄰居別把車停在我們家門口。
  3. The teacher asked the students not to use their phones in class.
    老師請學生上課時別用手機。
  4. I asked him not to be late again.
    我請他別再遲到了。
  5. They asked us not to take photos during the show.
    他們請我們表演期間別拍照。
  6. He asked me not to mention it to anyone at work.
    他請我別跟公司的任何人提這件事。

② told + 人 + not to

  1. She told me not to be scared.
    她叫我別害怕。
  2. I told him not to climb the wall, but he didn't listen.
    我叫他別爬那面牆，但他不聽。
  3. The police told everyone not to go near the building.
    警察叫大家別靠近那棟大樓。
  4. My boss told me not to worry about the mistake.
    我老闆叫我別擔心那個錯誤。
  5. Mum told us not to make a mess in the kitchen.
    媽媽叫我們別把廚房弄亂。
  6. The coach told the players not to give up.
    教練叫球員別放棄。

③ not to 和 to 一起用

  1. The guide told us not to touch anything and to stay on the path.
    導遊叫我們什麼都別碰，走在步道上。
  2. She asked me not to shout and to speak slowly.
    她請我別大吼，說慢一點。
  3. The nurse told him not to get up and to call if he needed anything.
    護士叫他別起來，需要什麼就叫人。
  4. They told us not to panic and to leave the building quietly.
    他們叫我們別慌張，安靜地離開大樓。
  5. He asked me not to wait for him and to start without him.
    他請我別等他，先開始。

④ warned / reminded / begged + 人 + not to

  1. The doctor warned me not to lift anything heavy.
    醫生警告我別提重物。
  2. My friend warned me not to eat the fish there.
    我朋友警告我別吃那裡的魚。
  3. Dad reminded us not to leave the lights on.
    爸爸提醒我們別讓燈開著。
  4. The email reminded customers not to reply to this address.
    那封信提醒客戶別回覆這個信箱。
  5. She begged him not to sell the house.
    她求他別把房子賣掉。

⑤ 後面說出對方的回應

  1. I asked her not to say anything, and she kept her promise.
    我請她什麼都別說，她也守住了承諾。
  2. He told me not to touch the dog, but I did anyway.
    他叫我別碰那隻狗，但我還是碰了。
  3. We asked them not to make so much noise, and they apologized.
    我們請他們別那麼吵，他們道了歉。
  4. She told the kids not to run, but they didn't hear her.
    她叫孩子們別跑，但他們沒聽到。
  5. I asked my brother not to borrow my car, and he agreed.
    我請我弟別借我的車，他答應了。

⑥ 被動：be told / be asked + not to

  1. We were told not to open the door to strangers.
    我們被告知不要給陌生人開門。
  2. I was asked not to discuss the case with the press.
    我被要求不要跟媒體談這個案子。
  3. Visitors are asked not to feed the animals.
    請遊客不要餵動物。
  4. The students were told not to bring bags into the exam room.
    學生被告知不能帶包包進考場。
