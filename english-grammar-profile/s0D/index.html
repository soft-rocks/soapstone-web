Will they be holding any activities at night?

他們晚上會舉辦什麼活動嗎？

是⁠非疑⁠問⁠句把 will 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Will they be holding any activities at night?

他們晚上會舉辦什麼活動嗎？

學生問夏令營期間晚上會不會有活動在進行。

簡答只⁠用 will 或 won't

Will they be holding any activities at night?

問

Will you be using the printer this afternoon?

你今天下午會用印表機嗎？
答

Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

會，我會用。／不會，我不會用。

wh⁠-⁠ 疑⁠問⁠詞放⁠在句⁠首，後⁠面⁠的順⁠序和是⁠非疑⁠問⁠句相⁠同

Will they be holding any activities at night?

派對上你會煮什麼？

who 當主⁠詞⁠時，句⁠子不⁠換位⁠置

誰會到車站接我們？

否⁠定疑⁠問⁠句確⁠認以⁠為⁠會發⁠生⁠的事

Will they be holding any activities at night?

你今晚不是又要加班嗎？

will 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠的 be

Will they be holding any activities at night?

Will you being coming to the meeting?

Will you be coming to the meeting?

只⁠有 will 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面，be 留⁠在主⁠詞後⁠面

Will they be holding any activities at night?

Will be they holding a party?

Will they be holding a party?

未⁠來進⁠行⁠式⁠的疑⁠問⁠句用 will，不⁠用 do

Will they be holding any activities at night?

What do you be doing next summer?

What will you be doing next summer?

簡答不⁠重⁠複 be

Yes, I will be.

Yes, I will.

例句

① Will + 主詞 + be + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. Will they be holding any activities at night?
    他們晚上會舉辦什麼活動嗎？
  2. Will you be using the car tomorrow?
    你明天會用車嗎？
  3. Will Ann be joining us for lunch?
    Ann 會來跟我們一起吃午餐嗎？
  4. Will the shops be opening on the holiday?
    假日那天商店會開嗎？
  5. Will we be stopping for a break on the way?
    我們路上會停下來休息嗎？
  6. Will your parents be staying for the weekend?
    你爸媽週末會留下來嗎？
  7. Will the teacher be checking our homework today?
    老師今天會檢查我們的作業嗎？
  8. Will you be paying by card or in cash?
    你會刷卡還是付現？
  9. Will he be bringing his dog?
    他會帶他的狗來嗎？
  10. Will the professor be reading every dissertation herself?
    教授會親自看每一篇論文嗎？
  11. Will the band be playing any old songs?
    樂團會唱一些老歌嗎？

② wh- 疑問詞 + will + 主詞 + be + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. What will you be doing in a few years?
    幾年後你會在做什麼？
  2. Where will you be staying in Rome?
    你在羅馬會住在哪裡？
  3. When will the new teacher be starting?
    新老師什麼時候開始上班？
  4. How long will you be waiting there?
    你會在那裡等多久？
  5. What time will the plane be landing?
    飛機幾點會降落？
  6. Which room will we be using for the meeting?
    我們會用哪一間房間開會？
  7. How will you be getting to the airport?
    你要怎麼去機場？
  8. Why will they be closing the road?
    他們為什麼要封路？
  9. What will she be wearing to the wedding?
    她會穿什麼去參加婚禮？
  10. Where will the kids be sleeping tonight?
    孩子們今晚會睡哪裡？
  11. How many people will you be inviting?
    你會邀請多少人？

③ Who + will be + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. Who will be driving the van?
    誰會開那輛廂型車？
  2. Who will be looking after the baby?
    誰會照顧寶寶？
  3. Who will be giving the speech at the ceremony?
    典禮上誰會致詞？
  4. Who will be taking notes at the meeting?
    會議上誰會做紀錄？

④ Won't + 主詞 + be + 動詞-ing ...?

  1. Won't you be needing a coat?
    你不會需要外套嗎？
  2. Won't they be expecting us at six?
    他們不是六點等我們嗎？
  3. Won't she be missing her friends?
    她不會想念她的朋友嗎？
  4. Won't you be feeling tired after the flight?
    坐完飛機你不會累嗎？
  5. Won't he be finishing work soon?
    他不是快下班了嗎？
