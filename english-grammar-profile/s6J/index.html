這些就是我能告訴你的主要內容。

these + 複⁠數動⁠詞

These are the main things that I can tell you.

這些就是我能告訴你的主要內容。

this 和 these

These are the main things that I can tell you.

單數

This is my new address.

這是我的新地址。
複數

These are my new trainers.

這是我的新運動鞋。

these 在句⁠子⁠裡⁠的位⁠置

These are the main things that I can tell you.

主詞

These are my cousins from Canada.

這幾位是我從加拿大來的表兄弟姊妹。
受詞

I made these for you.

這些是我做給你的。
介系詞後面

Put your coat with these.

把你的外套和這些放在一起。

複⁠數用 these

These are the main things that I can tell you.

I've got two tickets for the show. This are for you and me.

I've got two tickets for the show. These are for you and me.

後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞用複⁠數

These are the main things that I can tell you.

These are my best friend, Tom and Sam.

These are my best friends, Tom and Sam.

拼⁠法是 these

These are the main things that I can tell you.

Theses are the photos from my holiday.

These are the photos from my holiday.

these + 名⁠詞

最近我都很早起。

these 和 those

這些是我的，那邊那些是你的。

例句

① these + are

  1. These are my parents, Anna and Peter.
    這兩位是我的爸媽，安娜和彼得。
  2. These are the best seats in the theatre.
    這些是劇院裡最好的位子。
  3. These are much cheaper than the ones online.
    這些比網路上的便宜多了。
  4. These are the rules of the game.
    這些是遊戲規則。
  5. Are these your gloves? I found them in the car.
    這是你的手套嗎？我在車上找到的。

② these + were

  1. These were my grandfather's tools.
    這些是我爺爺的工具。
  2. These were the happiest years of my life.
    這幾年是我人生中最快樂的時光。
  3. These were all on sale, so I bought three.
    這些都在特價，所以我買了三個。
  4. These were the only shoes left in my size.
    這些是唯一剩下有我尺寸的鞋子。
  5. These were taken on our first day in London.
    這些是我們在倫敦第一天拍的。

③ these 當受詞

  1. Can you hold these for a moment?
    你可以幫我拿一下這些嗎？
  2. Try these. They're really sweet.
    吃吃看這些，很甜。
  3. I need to post these today.
    我今天得把這些寄出去。
  4. Please don't throw these away.
    請不要把這些丟掉。
  5. My dad gave me these for my birthday.
    這些是我爸爸送我的生日禮物。

④ 介系詞 + these

  1. What do you think of these?
    你覺得這些怎麼樣？
  2. I can't decide between these.
    我在這些裡面拿不定主意。
  3. Write your name on each of these.
    在這些上面每一個都寫上你的名字。
  4. You can do a lot with these.
    這些可以拿來做很多事。
  5. Be careful with these. They're made of glass.
    小心這些，是玻璃做的。

⑤ 寫信介紹事情

  1. These are the most important years for us as teenagers, and they have to be the best years!
    這幾年是我們青少年最重要的時期，一定要是最美好的時光！
  2. These are some photos of my new house.
    這些是我新家的照片。
  3. These are the places I want to show you when you come.
    這些是你來的時候我想帶你去的地方。
  4. I've attached three files. These are the reports you asked for.
    我附上了三個檔案，這些是你要的報告。
  5. These are just a few ideas for your trip.
    這些只是你旅行的一些點子。

⑥ 信的結尾

  1. I think these are the most important things to remember.
    我覺得這些是最需要記住的事。
  2. These are the questions I still have. Write soon!
    這些是我還有的問題，快點回信！
  3. I hope these are useful for your project.
    希望這些對你的報告有幫助。
  4. These are my plans for the summer. What are yours?
    這些是我暑假的計畫，你的呢？
  5. Here are my answers. I hope these help.
    這是我的回答，希望這些對你有幫助。
  6. These are only my opinions, of course.
    當然，這些只是我的意見。
