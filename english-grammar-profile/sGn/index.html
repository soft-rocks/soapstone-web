Never have I seen such a realistic and impressive film before.

我從來沒看過這麼逼真、這麼令人印象深刻的電影。

never 放⁠在句⁠首，助⁠動⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Never have I seen such a realistic and impressive film before.

我從來沒看過這樣的電影。

一⁠般⁠的語⁠序和倒⁠裝⁠句

Never have I seen such a realistic and impressive film before.

一般的語序

I have never seen such a beautiful beach.

我從來沒看過這麼美的海灘。
倒裝句，語氣比較強

Never have I seen such a beautiful beach.

我從來就沒看過這麼美的海灘。

原⁠本有助⁠動⁠詞，就移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Never have I seen such a realistic and impressive film before.

現在完成式Never have I + 過去分詞
過去完成式Never had I + 過去分詞
未來式Never will I + 原形動詞
情態助動詞Never could I、Never would I + 原形動詞

原⁠本沒⁠有助⁠動⁠詞，就加⁠上 do、does、did

我從來就沒想過會贏。

助⁠動⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

Never have I seen such a realistic and impressive film before.

Never I have eaten such delicious food.

Never have I eaten such delicious food.

過⁠去簡⁠單式加⁠上 did

Never I imagined that I would live abroad.

Never did I imagine that I would live abroad.

did 後⁠面用原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Never did I thought she would leave.

Never did I think she would leave.

never 和 not 不⁠一⁠起用

Never haven't I felt so tired.

Never have I felt so tired.

例句

① Never have I + 過去分詞

  1. Never have I met such a kind and generous person.
    我從來沒遇過這麼善良、這麼大方的人。
  2. Never have I felt so welcome in a foreign country.
    我在國外從來沒有這麼受歡迎的感覺。
  3. Never have I heard such a beautiful voice.
    我從來沒聽過這麼美的聲音。
  4. Never have we had so much rain in one month.
    我們從來沒有一個月下過這麼多雨。
  5. Never has the city looked so beautiful.
    這座城市從來沒有這麼美過。
  6. Never have I been so embarrassed in my whole life.
    我這輩子從來沒有這麼丟臉過。

② Never had I + 過去分詞

  1. Never had I seen so many people in one place.
    我從來沒在同一個地方看過這麼多人。
  2. Never had she travelled alone before that trip.
    在那趟旅行之前，她從來沒有自己一個人旅行過。
  3. Never had I worked so hard for an exam.
    我從來沒有為一場考試這麼用功過。
  4. Never had they experienced such cold weather.
    他們從來沒碰過這麼冷的天氣。
  5. Never had I imagined that the job would be so stressful.
    我從來沒想到那份工作壓力會這麼大。

③ Never did I + 原形動詞

  1. Never did I expect to see him again.
    我從沒想過會再見到他。
  2. Never did we think the hotel would be so crowded.
    我們從沒想過旅館會這麼擠。
  3. Never did she complain about her difficult job.
    她從來沒有抱怨過她辛苦的工作。
  4. Never did I believe that I could run a marathon.
    我從來不相信自己能跑完馬拉松。
  5. Never did my grandfather miss a football match on TV.
    我爺爺從來沒錯過電視上的任何一場足球賽。
  6. Never did I dream that I would win the competition.
    我做夢也沒想到我會贏得比賽。

④ Never will I + 原形動詞

  1. Never will I forget the day my daughter was born.
    我永遠不會忘記我女兒出生的那一天。
  2. Never will I stay in that hotel again.
    我再也不會住那間旅館了。
  3. Never will I lend him money again.
    我再也不會借錢給他了。
  4. Never will we forget your kindness.
    我們永遠不會忘記你的好意。
  5. Never will I trust that airline again after what happened.
    發生那件事以後，我再也不會相信那家航空公司了。

⑤ Never could I、Never would I + 原形動詞

  1. Never could I live in such a noisy city.
    我絕對沒辦法住在這麼吵的城市。
  2. Never would I lie to my best friend.
    我絕對不會對我最好的朋友說謊。
  3. Never could we afford a house in this part of town.
    我們絕對買不起這一區的房子。
  4. Never would she leave her children alone at home.
    她絕對不會把孩子單獨留在家裡。

⑥ Never do I + 原形動詞

  1. Never do I feel as relaxed as I do by the sea.
    我從來沒有像在海邊時那麼放鬆。
  2. Never does my brother say thank you.
    我弟弟從來不說謝謝。

⑦ Never + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 主要動詞 + before

  1. Never had I spoken in front of so many people before.
    我以前從來沒在這麼多人面前說過話。
  2. Never have I tasted such fresh fish before.
    我以前從來沒吃過這麼新鮮的魚。
  3. Never had the team won a championship before.
    這支隊伍以前從來沒拿過冠軍。
