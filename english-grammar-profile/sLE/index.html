主⁠詞 + would + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

would、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

would以前常常。說過去重複的動作。
原形動詞過去的習慣。

用在故⁠事⁠裡

In my opinion I worked very hard. I would spend a lot of hours at work.

例句

At night they would go to the beach and light a fire.

晚上他們常常會去海灘生火。

故事常用 would 說人物過去的習慣。

說過⁠去⁠的狀⁠態用 used to，不⁠用 would

In my opinion I worked very hard. I would spend a lot of hours at work.

When I was young, I would have long hair.

When I was young, I used to have long hair.

would 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

In my opinion I worked very hard. I would spend a lot of hours at work.

We would went to the park every Sunday.

We would go to the park every Sunday.

would 後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

In my opinion I worked very hard. I would spend a lot of hours at work.

My dad would to read us stories.

My dad would read us stories.

例句

① 主詞 + would + 原形動詞：過去工作的習慣

  1. Last year I worked in a busy restaurant. In my opinion I worked very hard. I would spend a lot of hours at work.
    去年我在一家很忙的餐廳工作。我認為我工作得很努力。我常常在工作上花很多時間。
  2. I would start at six in the morning and finish late at night.
    我常常早上六點開始，晚上很晚才結束。
  3. My boss would check every plate before it left the kitchen.
    我老闆常常會在每一盤菜離開廚房前檢查。
  4. We would have a short break at three o'clock.
    我們常常在三點的時候休息一下。
  5. I would take the last bus home after work.
    下班後我常常搭最後一班公車回家。
  6. On Fridays the restaurant would be full until midnight.
    星期五餐廳常常客滿到半夜。

② 主詞 + would + 原形動詞：故事裡人物的習慣

  1. The pirates were clever. At night they would go to the rocky seaside and light a fire to trick the boats.
    那些海盜很聰明。晚上他們常常會去岩石海岸生火，欺騙船隻。
  2. Every morning the old fisherman would sit by the harbour and mend his nets.
    每天早上那個老漁夫都會坐在港口邊補他的網。
  3. The girl would climb the tree and watch the road for her father.
    那個女孩常常會爬上樹，看著路等她爸爸回來。
  4. The king would walk around the city at night in old clothes.
    國王常常晚上穿著舊衣服在城裡走來走去。
  5. The wolf would come to the village when the snow was deep.
    雪很深的時候，狼常常會來村子裡。
  6. Every evening the children would gather around the fire for a story.
    每天傍晚孩子們都會圍在火邊聽故事。

③ When + 主詞 + was …, + 主詞 + would + 原形動詞：童年的回憶

  1. When I was a child, my grandmother would bake bread every Saturday.
    我小時候，我奶奶每個星期六都會烤麵包。
  2. When we were young, we would play in the woods until dark.
    我們小時候，常常在樹林裡玩到天黑。
  3. When I was at school, my mum would walk me to the gate every day.
    我上學的時候，我媽媽每天都會陪我走到校門口。
  4. When my dad was a boy, he would swim in the river every summer.
    我爸爸小時候，每年夏天都會在河裡游泳。
  5. When we lived in Italy, we would eat outside every evening.
    我們住在義大利的時候，每天傍晚都在外面吃飯。
  6. When I was small, my brother would tell me scary stories at night.
    我小時候，我哥哥晚上常常會講恐怖故事給我聽。

④ 主詞 + would often / always + 原形動詞：說多常

  1. My grandfather would often fall asleep in his armchair after lunch.
    我爺爺午餐後常常會在扶手椅上睡著。
  2. We would always go to the same beach every summer.
    我們每年夏天總是會去同一個海灘。
  3. My aunt would often send us parcels from Canada.
    我阿姨常常會從加拿大寄包裹給我們。
  4. Our teacher would always start the lesson with a song.
    我們老師總是會用一首歌開始上課。
  5. My friends and I would often meet at the café after school.
    我和朋友常常放學後在咖啡館碰面。
  6. The dog would always wait for me at the door.
    那隻狗總是會在門口等我。

⑤ 主詞 + would + 原形動詞, and then + 主詞 + would + 原形動詞：一連串的習慣

  1. Every Sunday we would visit my grandparents, and then we would have lunch together.
    每個星期天我們都會去看我的祖父母，然後一起吃午餐。
  2. After school I would do my homework, and then I would go to football practice.
    放學後我會先寫功課，然後去練足球。
  3. In summer we would get up early, and then we would cycle to the lake.
    夏天我們會早起，然後騎腳踏車去湖邊。
  4. My mum would cook dinner, and then my dad would wash up.
    我媽媽會煮晚餐，然後我爸爸會洗碗。
  5. Every morning she would feed the chickens, and then she would collect the eggs.
    每天早上她會餵雞，然後撿雞蛋。
  6. At Christmas we would decorate the tree, and then we would sing carols.
    聖誕節我們會裝飾聖誕樹，然後唱聖誕歌。
  7. On rainy days we would build a tent in the living room, and then we would read inside it.
    下雨天我們會在客廳搭帳篷，然後在裡面讀書。
