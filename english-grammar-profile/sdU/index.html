Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

請記得帶你的睡衣和牙刷。

remember to 說記⁠得要去做⁠的事

請記得帶你的睡衣和牙刷。

帶東西這件事還沒發生。

提⁠醒別⁠人做⁠事，用 remember to 或 don't forget to

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

remember to

Please remember to lock the door.

請記得鎖門。
don't forget to

Don't forget to come.

別忘了要來。

not 放⁠在 to 前⁠面，否⁠定不⁠定⁠詞⁠的動⁠作

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

decide not to

I decided not to go.

我決定不去。
promise not to

She promised not to tell anyone.

她答應不告訴任何人。

decide 後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠時，動⁠詞前⁠面加 to

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

We decided stay at home.

We decided to stay at home.

promise 後⁠面⁠接 to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞，不⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

He promised calling me.

He promised to call me.

時⁠態只⁠加在⁠前⁠面⁠的動⁠詞⁠上，to 後⁠面用原⁠形

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

I forgot to brought my keys.

I forgot to bring my keys.

否⁠定不⁠定⁠詞⁠時，not 後⁠面⁠的 to 不⁠能省⁠略

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

I planned not visit them.

I planned not to visit them.

remember 接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，說記⁠得以⁠前做過⁠的事

Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.

remember to
要去做

Please remember to lock the door.

請記得鎖門。
remember + 動詞⁠-⁠ing
以前做過

I remember meeting her at the party.

我記得在派對上見過她。

例句

① remember to / don't forget to

  1. Hi Leo, you're staying with us on Saturday. Please remember to bring your pajamas and your toothbrush.
    嗨，Leo，你星期六要來我們家住。請記得帶你的睡衣和牙刷。
  2. The party starts at seven. Don't forget to come!
    派對七點開始。別忘了要來！
  3. Remember to turn off the lights when you leave the office.
    離開辦公室的時候記得關燈。
  4. "Did you remember to call the dentist?" "Oh no, I forgot."
    你有記得打給牙醫嗎？糟了，我忘了。
  5. Don't forget to water the plants while we're away.
    我們不在的時候，別忘了幫植物澆水。

② forget to

  1. I forgot to buy milk, so we can't have tea.
    我忘了買牛奶，所以我們不能喝奶茶了。
  2. My brother always forgets to close the fridge.
    我弟弟總是忘了關冰箱。
  3. Sorry I'm late. I forgot to set my alarm.
    抱歉我遲到了。我忘了設鬧鐘。

③ hope to / plan to

  1. I bought warm clothes because I hope to go to England this year.
    我買了保暖的衣服，因為我希望今年能去英國。
  2. "Are you going home for the holidays?" "No, we plan to stay in Taipei this year."
    你們放假要回家嗎？不，我們今年打算待在台北。
  3. "What are you doing when you finish school?" "I'm planning to study medicine."
    你畢業後要做什麼？我打算讀醫學。
  4. My parents hope to retire by the sea.
    我爸媽希望退休後住在海邊。

④ decide to

  1. It was raining, so we decided to stay at home and watch a film.
    在下雨，所以我們決定待在家看電影。
  2. After ten years in the bank, she decided to open a café.
    在銀行工作十年之後，她決定開一家咖啡廳。
  3. "Which phone did you buy?" "I decided to keep my old one."
    你買了哪一支手機？我決定繼續用舊的。

⑤ promise to / agree to

  1. Dad promised to take us to the zoo on Sunday.
    爸爸答應星期天帶我們去動物園。
  2. "Will you call me when you arrive?" "I promise to call."
    你到了會打給我嗎？我答應會打。
  3. "Can we keep the puppy?" "OK. Mum has agreed to let him stay."
    我們可以養這隻小狗嗎？好。媽媽同意讓牠留下來。
  4. The neighbours agreed to turn the music down after ten.
    鄰居同意十點以後把音樂關小聲。

⑥ offer to / refuse to

  1. When my car broke down, a kind man offered to help.
    我的車拋錨的時候，有個好心的男人主動說要幫忙。
  2. My sister offered to drive me to the airport.
    我姊主動說要開車送我去機場。
  3. The taxi driver refused to take us because our dog was too big.
    那個計程車司機不肯載我們，因為我們的狗太大了。
  4. My old laptop is so slow. It refuses to open more than two programs.
    我的舊筆電好慢。它開不了兩個以上的程式。

⑦ 不定詞的否定：not to

  1. It was very late, so I decided not to call her.
    已經很晚了，所以我決定不打給她。
  2. She promised not to tell anyone about the surprise party.
    她答應不把驚喜派對的事告訴任何人。
  3. Please try not to make any noise. The baby is asleep.
    請盡量不要發出聲音。寶寶睡著了。
  4. We agreed not to spend too much on presents this year.
    我們說好今年不在禮物上花太多錢。

⑧ 問句和不同的時態

  1. "When did you decide to learn Korean?" "After my trip to Seoul."
    你什麼時候決定學韓文的？去首爾玩了以後。
  2. "Have you remembered to pack the tickets?" "Yes, they're in my bag."
    你記得把票放進行李了嗎？記得，在我包包裡。
  3. "Are you planning to come to the meeting tomorrow?" "Yes, but I'll be a bit late."
    你明天打算來開會嗎？會，可是我會晚一點到。
  4. I'll try to finish the report by Friday, but I can't promise.
    我會盡量在星期五前把報告寫完，可是我不能保證。
