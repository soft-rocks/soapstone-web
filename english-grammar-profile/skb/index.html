例句

① for having been + 過去分詞

  1. In some countries, wedding guests are expected to give the couple expensive presents as a reward for having been invited.
    在一些國家，婚禮的客人受到邀請，照理要送新人貴重的禮物當作回報。
  2. The restaurant was very busy. We got free drinks for having been kept waiting so long.
    那家餐廳很忙。我們等了那麼久，所以拿到免費的飲料。
  3. He was sent home for having been rude to a teacher.
    他因為對老師不禮貌被送回家。
  4. I'm very grateful for having been given this chance to study abroad.
    我很感激得到這個出國唸書的機會。
  5. The company apologised to customers for having been charged twice.
    那家公司向被重複收費的客人道歉。

② without having been + 過去分詞

  1. Miss Kenton knocks on his door and comes in without having been asked to do so.
    Kenton 小姐敲了敲他的門，沒等他允許就進來了。
  2. He left the company without having been paid for his last month.
    他離開公司的時候，最後一個月的薪水還沒拿到。
  3. You can't start the job without having been trained first.
    你沒先受過訓練就不能開始做這份工作。
  4. The money was spent without having been approved by the manager.
    這筆錢沒有經過經理同意就花掉了。
  5. She signed the contract without having been told about the extra costs.
    她簽了合約，可是沒人告訴她有額外的費用。

③ of having been + 過去分詞

  1. We caught the bus with the feeling of having been given one of the funniest evenings of our lives.
    我們搭上公車，覺得自己度過了人生中最好笑的一個晚上。
  2. I can't get rid of the feeling of having been cheated.
    我一直覺得自己被騙了。
  3. She's proud of having been chosen to represent her school.
    她很自豪被選為學校的代表。
  4. He has no memory of having been taken to hospital.
    他不記得自己被送去醫院。

④ after having been + 過去分詞

  1. After having been delayed for six hours, the plane finally took off.
    延誤了六個小時之後，飛機終於起飛了。
  2. After having been closed for two years, the museum opened again in May.
    博物館關了兩年之後，五月重新開放。
  3. After having been rejected twice, his book was finally published.
    被拒絕兩次之後，他的書終於出版了。
  4. After having been lost for a week, the dog came home on its own.
    狗走失了一個星期之後，自己回家了。

⑤ about / at having been + 過去分詞

  1. The customers complained about having been kept waiting for an hour.
    客人抱怨被晾在那裡等了一個小時。
  2. She was upset about having been left out of the team photo.
    她很難過團體照裡沒有她。
  3. He was angry at having been treated like a child.
    他很生氣被當成小孩對待。
  4. They laughed about having been fooled by such an easy trick.
    他們笑自己被這麼簡單的把戲騙了。

⑥ 其他介系詞

  1. Despite having been warned many times, he still drove too fast.
    雖然被警告過很多次，他開車還是太快。
  2. As well as having been robbed, we missed our flight home.
    我們除了被搶，還錯過了回家的班機。
  3. Apart from having been bitten by a few insects, we had a great holiday.
    除了被幾隻蟲咬了之外，我們的假期很棒。
  4. He got the job thanks to having been recommended by his old boss.
    他能得到這份工作，是因為前老闆推薦了他。

⑦ 不同的語氣

  1. We would like to apologise to all passengers for having been kept waiting.
    我們要向所有久候的乘客致歉。
  2. "What did he say about the interview?" "He talked about having been asked some very strange questions."
    他說面試怎麼樣？他說被問了一些很奇怪的問題。
  3. "Why is she angry?" "She's angry about not having been told about the party."
    她為什麼生氣？她氣沒人跟她說派對的事。
  4. I still remember the shock of having been called into the head teacher's office.
    我還記得被叫進校長室的那種震驚。
  5. After all these years, he's still bitter about not having been promoted.
    這麼多年了，他對沒被升職還是很不甘心。
