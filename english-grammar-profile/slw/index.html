Either you get something from your country, or I get something from their wedding list.

要嘛你從你們國家帶點東西來，要嘛我從他們的結婚禮物清單上買一樣。

Either + 子⁠句 + , or + 子⁠句

Either you get something from your country, or I get something from their wedding list.

要嘛你從你們國家帶點東西來，要嘛我從他們的結婚禮物清單上買一樣。

用冒⁠號帶⁠出兩⁠個選⁠項

You have two options: either you take the train tonight, or you wait for the first bus tomorrow.

你有兩個選擇：要嘛今晚搭火車，要嘛等明天第一班公車。

選⁠項⁠裡⁠面再⁠接子⁠句

which補充說明前面的事
because說明理由
and接著說下一步

Either we meet at the station, which is easier for you, or I pick you up at home and we drive there together.

要嘛我們在車站碰面，這樣對你比較方便，要嘛我去你家接你，我們一起開車過去。

either 和 or 搭⁠配

Either you get something from your country, or I get something from their wedding list.

Either you call the hotel, or I send them an email.

You have two choices: either you stay, or you go.

例句

① Either + 子句 + , or + 子句

  1. Either you finish the report tonight, or you explain to the manager why it is late.
    要嘛你今晚寫完報告，要嘛你跟經理解釋為什麼遲交。
  2. Either the meeting starts on time, or we postpone it until next week.
    要嘛會議準時開始，要嘛我們延到下星期。
  3. Either she has forgotten our appointment, or her train is delayed.
    不是她忘了我們的約，就是她的火車誤點了。
  4. Either we fix the roof this summer, or we will have serious problems in the winter.
    要嘛我們今年夏天修屋頂，要嘛冬天會有大麻煩。
  5. Either the shop has sold out, or they never had the book in stock.
    不是書店賣完了，就是他們根本沒進那本書。

② 用冒號帶出兩個選項

  1. You have two choices: either you accept the offer, or you look for another job.
    你有兩個選擇：要嘛接受這個工作，要嘛另外找工作。
  2. There are only two possibilities: either the letter got lost, or someone took it by mistake.
    只有兩個可能：不是信寄丟了，就是有人拿錯了。
  3. We have two options for the party: either we book a restaurant, or we cook at home.
    派對我們有兩個選擇：要嘛訂餐廳，要嘛在家裡煮。
  4. The plan is simple: either the government lowers taxes, or businesses move abroad.
    道理很簡單：要嘛政府降稅，要嘛企業搬到國外。
  5. Here is the deal: either you clean your room, or you stay home this weekend.
    就這樣說定：要嘛你把房間整理乾淨，要嘛這個週末待在家。

③ 選項後面接 which 子句

  1. Either we take the coast road, which is longer but more beautiful, or we use the motorway.
    要嘛我們走海岸公路，那條比較遠但風景比較美，要嘛走高速公路。
  2. Either you stay in the city centre, which is expensive, or you find a room near the university.
    要嘛你住市中心，那裡很貴，要嘛在大學附近找個房間。
  3. Either we buy the blue sofa, which matches the curtains, or we keep the old one for another year.
    要嘛我們買那張藍色沙發，它和窗簾很配，要嘛舊的再用一年。
  4. Either the company opens a new branch, which would create jobs, or it closes the old factory.
    要嘛公司開一家新分店，這樣會創造工作機會，要嘛關掉舊工廠。

④ 選項後面接 because 子句

  1. Either he missed the bus because he always leaves late, or he is ill again.
    不是他沒趕上公車，因為他每次都很晚出門，就是他又生病了。
  2. Either they cancelled the concert because of the storm, or the website is wrong.
    不是他們因為暴風雨取消了音樂會，就是網站寫錯了。
  3. Either you give her flowers because she loves gardening, or you take her out for dinner.
    要嘛你送她花，因為她很愛園藝，要嘛你帶她出去吃晚餐。
  4. Either we leave the car at home because parking is difficult, or we arrive very early.
    要嘛我們把車留在家裡，因為停車很難，要嘛我們很早就到。

⑤ 選項裡面用 and 接下一步

  1. Either you send me the files today, or I come to your office and copy them myself.
    要嘛你今天把檔案寄給我，要嘛我去你辦公室自己複製。
  2. Either we rent a van and move everything ourselves, or we pay a removal company.
    要嘛我們租一輛廂型車自己搬，要嘛付錢請搬家公司。
  3. Either you call the landlord and complain, or I write to him and ask for a meeting.
    要嘛你打給房東抱怨，要嘛我寫信給他，約他見個面。
  4. Either we book the flights now and save money, or we wait and hope for a last-minute deal.
    要嘛我們現在訂機票省點錢，要嘛等等看有沒有最後一刻的特價。

⑥ 書信和報告裡的長句

  1. Either the committee approves the budget at the next meeting, or the project will be delayed by at least six months.
    要嘛委員會在下次會議通過預算，要嘛這個計畫至少延後六個月。
  2. Either you come to the airport, which I would really appreciate, or I take a taxi and meet you at the hotel.
    要嘛你來機場接我，我會很感謝，要嘛我搭計程車到飯店跟你碰面。
  3. Either the school buys new computers this year, or the students continue to share the old ones in the library.
    要嘛學校今年買新電腦，要嘛學生繼續在圖書館共用舊的。
  4. Either we reduce our energy use at home, or we accept much higher bills next winter.
    要嘛我們在家少用點電，要嘛明年冬天接受貴很多的帳單。
  5. Either the airline refunds the full price, or I will take the matter to a lawyer.
    要嘛航空公司全額退費，要嘛我會找律師處理這件事。

⑦ 兩個選項的主詞不同

  1. Either your brother picks up the children, or I leave work early.
    要嘛你哥去接孩子，要嘛我提早下班。
  2. Either the guests bring their own drinks, or we order some from the shop.
    要嘛客人自己帶飲料，要嘛我們跟店家訂一些。
  3. Either the teacher explains the task again, or the students ask their classmates for help.
    要嘛老師再講一次作業怎麼做，要嘛學生問同學。
  4. Either my parents visit us in June, or we fly to see them at Christmas.
    要嘛我爸媽六月來看我們，要嘛我們聖誕節飛回去看他們。
