Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

你仔細想一想，再告訴我你決定怎麼做。

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞和小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間

Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

你仔細想一想，再告訴我你決定怎麼做。

think over 的意思是仔細考慮。

名⁠詞受⁠詞放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞前⁠後都⁠可⁠以，代⁠名⁠詞只⁠能放⁠在中⁠間

Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

代名詞

I heard about the club, and I wanted to check it out.

我聽說了那個社團，想去看看。
名詞在中間

I want to check the club out.

我想去看看那個社團。
名詞在後面

I want to check out the club.

我想去看看那個社團。

代⁠名⁠詞不⁠放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

I like the jacket. Can I try on it?

I like the jacket. Can I try it on?

put off 要⁠有受⁠詞

Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

The meeting is on Friday, but we have to put off.

The meeting is on Friday, but we have to put it off.

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞用受格

Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

Please call she back later.

Please call her back later.

仔⁠細考⁠慮是 think over，不⁠是 think out

Think it over and tell me what you decide to do.

Think it out and tell me your answer.

Think it over and tell me your answer.

介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞和片⁠語介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞⁠的代⁠名⁠詞放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

介系詞動詞

I'll look for it in the dictionary.

我會在字典裡找。
片語介系詞動詞

I can't put up with it.

我受不了了。

例句

① think over / check out

  1. "We'd like to offer you the job." "Thank you. Can I think it over this weekend?"
    我們想給你這份工作。謝謝。我可以這個週末想一想嗎？
  2. It's a big decision, so think it over carefully.
    這是個重大的決定，所以好好想一想。
  3. I saw an ad for a new gym on TV, and I wanted to check it out.
    我在電視上看到一家新健身房的廣告，想去看看。
  4. "Have you seen that new band?" "No, but I'll check them out on Saturday."
    你看過那個新樂團嗎？沒有，可是我星期六會去看看。

② try on

  1. I love shopping for clothes, but I hate trying them on.
    我很愛買衣服，可是很討厭試穿。
  2. "These shoes look nice." "Try them on. The mirror is over there."
    這雙鞋很好看。試穿看看。鏡子在那邊。
  3. She took the dress to the changing room and tried it on.
    她把洋裝拿進試衣間試穿。

③ sort out / look up

  1. "My computer isn't working." "Don't worry, I'll sort it out."
    我的電腦壞了。別擔心，我來處理。
  2. There's a problem with your bill. The manager will sort it out.
    您的帳單有點問題。經理會處理。
  3. "What's the capital of Peru?" "I don't know. Let's look it up."
    秘魯的首都是哪裡？不知道。我們查一下吧。
  4. I didn't know his phone number, so I looked it up online.
    我不知道他的電話號碼，所以上網查了。

④ put off

  1. The match was going to be on Sunday, but the rain made them put it off.
    比賽本來是星期天，可是下雨讓他們延期了。
  2. "Have you been to the dentist?" "No, I keep putting it off."
    你去看牙醫了嗎？還沒，我一直拖。
  3. We can't put the meeting off again. Let's have it tomorrow.
    我們不能再延後這個會議了。明天開吧。

⑤ call back / let down

  1. "Mr Lee is in a meeting." "OK. Can you ask him to call me back?"
    李先生在開會。好。你可以請他回我電話嗎？
  2. I missed your call. I'll call you back after lunch.
    我錯過了你的電話。午餐後回電給你。
  3. My team needs me on Saturday. I can't let them down.
    我的隊伍星期六需要我。我不能讓他們失望。
  4. "You promised to come!" "I know. I'm sorry I let you down."
    你答應過要來的！我知道。抱歉讓你失望了。

⑥ turn down / hand in

  1. They offered him a job in Paris, but he turned it down.
    他們給了他巴黎的工作，可是他拒絕了。
  2. "Can you hear the baby? Your radio is very loud." "Oh, sorry. I'll turn it down now."
    你聽得到寶寶的聲音嗎？你的收音機很大聲。喔，抱歉。我現在就調小聲。
  3. "Where's your homework?" "I handed it in yesterday."
    你的作業呢？我昨天交了。
  4. Write your answers on the paper and hand it in at the end of the exam.
    把答案寫在紙上，考試結束時交上來。

⑦ give back / take back / bring back

  1. "Can I borrow your bike?" "Sure, but give it back by six."
    我可以借你的腳踏車嗎？好啊，可是六點前要還我。
  2. The shirt was too small, so I took it back to the shop.
    那件襯衫太小了，所以我拿回店裡退。
  3. I borrowed Tom's camera, and I have to bring it back tomorrow.
    我借了 Tom 的相機，明天得拿回去還他。

⑧ 受詞是名詞

  1. Can I try this jacket on?
    我可以試穿這件外套嗎？
  2. Please hand your phones in before the exam.
    考試前請把手機交出來。
  3. Let's put off the trip until next month.
    我們把旅行延到下個月吧。
  4. She turned down the offer because the pay was too low.
    她拒絕了那個工作機會，因為薪水太低。
  5. I'll look up the train times for you.
    我幫你查火車時刻。
  6. Can you sort out the problem with the printer?
    你可以處理印表機的問題嗎？
