We used to play together every single day!

我們以前每天都玩在一起！

used to 說過⁠去常⁠做、現⁠在不⁠做⁠的事

我們以前每天都玩在一起！

used to 說過⁠去⁠的習⁠慣，也說過⁠去⁠的狀⁠態

We used to play together every single day!

過去的習慣

I used to walk to school.

我以前都走路上學。
過去的狀態

There used to be a cinema here.

這裡以前有一家電影院。

否⁠定和問⁠句用 did，後⁠面寫 use to

We used to play together every single day!

否定

I didn't use to like coffee.

我以前不喜歡咖啡。
問句

Did you use to live here?

你以前住在這裡嗎？

ought to 表⁠示應⁠該，否⁠定是 ought not to

ought to

I think you ought to join a gym.

我覺得你應該去健身房。
ought not to

You ought not to drive so fast.

你不應該開這麼快。

used to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We used to play together every single day!

I used to played the piano.

I used to play the piano.

did 後⁠面寫 use to，不⁠寫 used to

We used to play together every single day!

She didn't used to eat fish.

She didn't use to eat fish.

used to 只⁠說過⁠去，現⁠在⁠的習⁠慣用現⁠在簡⁠單式

We used to play together every single day!

I use to get up at seven every day.

I usually get up at seven every day.

ought 後⁠面要⁠有 to

You ought tell her the truth.

You ought to tell her the truth.

be used to 的意⁠思是習⁠慣⁠了，後⁠面⁠接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

We used to play together every single day!

used to
以前常做

I used to walk to school.

我以前都走路上學。
be used to
習慣了

I'm used to getting up early.

我習慣早起了。

例句

① used to：過去的習慣

  1. "Do you remember me? We used to play together every single day!"
    你還記得我嗎？我們以前每天都玩在一起！
  2. When I was a student, I used to stay up until three in the morning.
    我當學生的時候，常常熬到凌晨三點。
  3. Before the new road, my mum used to drive two hours to work.
    新路開通以前，我媽都要開兩個小時的車去上班。
  4. "Do you still play tennis?" "No, but I used to play every weekend."
    你還打網球嗎？沒有了，可是我以前每個週末都打。
  5. We used to spend every summer at my grandparents' farm.
    我們以前每年夏天都在我爺爺奶奶的農場過。

② used to：過去的狀態

  1. There used to be a small bookshop on this corner. Now it's a bank.
    這個轉角以前有一家小書店。現在變成銀行了。
  2. My brother used to have long hair, but he cut it last year.
    我哥以前留長髮，可是他去年剪掉了。
  3. "Is this your old house?" "Yes, we used to live on the second floor."
    這是你以前的家嗎？對，我們以前住在二樓。
  4. This town used to be very quiet before the factory opened.
    工廠開之前，這個小鎮以前很安靜。
  5. "You're so good at maths!" "Thanks, but I used to be terrible at it."
    你數學好好！謝謝，可是我以前數學很爛。

③ used to：否定

  1. "You run every morning?" "Yes, but I didn't use to. I started last year."
    你每天早上都跑步？對，可是我以前沒有。我去年才開始。
  2. My sister didn't use to be so shy. She was very talkative as a child.
    我妹以前沒這麼害羞。她小時候話很多。
  3. We didn't use to have a car, so we went everywhere by bus.
    我們以前沒有車，所以去哪裡都搭公車。

④ used to：問句

  1. "Did you use to live near here?" "Yes, in the blue building over there."
    你以前住在這附近嗎？對，就是那邊那棟藍色的大樓。
  2. "Did your mum use to work in a hospital?" "Yes, she was a nurse."
    你媽媽以前在醫院工作嗎？對，她以前是護理師。
  3. "What did you use to do on Sunday afternoons?" "We went to the park."
    你們以前星期天下午都做什麼？我們去公園。

⑤ ought to：建議

  1. You look tired. I think you ought to join a gym and walk more.
    你看起來很累。我覺得你應該去健身房，多走路。
  2. "My tooth really hurts." "You ought to see a dentist."
    我的牙齒好痛。你應該去看牙醫。
  3. It's a long drive. We ought to leave before seven.
    要開很久的車。我們應該七點以前出發。
  4. You ought to call your grandmother. She misses you.
    你應該打給你奶奶。她很想你。
  5. Before the interview, you ought to read about the company.
    面試前你應該先了解一下那家公司。

⑥ ought to：說對的事

  1. He broke your phone, so he ought to pay for it.
    他弄壞了你的手機，所以他應該賠錢。
  2. Children ought to be in bed by nine on school nights.
    上學日的晚上，孩子九點以前應該要上床。
  3. "Mrs Wu helped me with my bags." "You ought to thank her with a card."
    吳太太幫我提了袋子。你應該寫張卡片謝謝她。
  4. Everyone ought to learn how to swim.
    每個人都應該學游泳。

⑦ ought not to：否定

  1. You ought not to drive so fast in the rain.
    下雨天你不應該開這麼快。
  2. You ought not to eat sweets before bed. They're bad for your teeth.
    你睡前不應該吃糖果。對牙齒不好。
  3. "Should I tell him about the party?" "No, you ought not to. It's a surprise."
    我應該跟他說派對的事嗎？不，你不應該說。那是驚喜。

⑧ used to 和 ought to 在同一段話裡

  1. I used to eat a lot of junk food. I know I ought to eat better now.
    我以前吃很多垃圾食物。我知道我現在應該吃得健康一點。
  2. My grandpa used to smoke a lot. Now he tells everyone they ought to stop.
    我爺爺以前抽很多菸。現在他跟每個人說他們應該戒菸。
  3. We used to meet every week. We ought to do that again.
    我們以前每個星期都見面。我們應該再像以前那樣見面。
