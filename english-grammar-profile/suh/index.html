They will certainly have a banquet that day, and you oughtn't miss it for the world!

你說什麼都不該錯過！

主⁠詞 + oughtn't + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

oughtn't、省⁠略 to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

oughtn'tought not 的縮寫。不應該。
省略 to口語和非正式的信件常省略。
原形動詞不應該做的事。

正⁠式⁠的寫⁠作比⁠較常保⁠留 oughtn't 後⁠面⁠的 to

比較正式

The doctor says it's only a cold. You oughtn't to worry.

醫生說只是感冒。你不應該擔心。

兩種說法都有人用。正式的寫作比較常保留 to。

not … for the world 表⁠示無⁠論⁠如⁠何都⁠不

例句

My best friend is getting married in June. I wouldn't miss it for the world.

我最好的朋友六月要結婚。我無論如何都不會錯過。

not … for the world 表示「無論如何都不」，加強語氣。

撇號放⁠在 n 和 t 中⁠間：oughtn't

They will certainly have a banquet that day, and you oughtn't miss it for the world!

The parade is on Saturday. You ought'nt miss it.

The parade is on Saturday. You oughtn't miss it.

oughtn't 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

They will certainly have a banquet that day, and you oughtn't miss it for the world!

The band only plays here once a year. You oughtn't missing the show.

The band only plays here once a year. You oughtn't miss the show.

ought 的否⁠定是 oughtn't，不⁠用 don't

They will certainly have a banquet that day, and you oughtn't miss it for the world!

You'll feel sick on the boat. You don't ought eat so much.

You'll feel sick on the boat. You oughtn't eat so much.

例句

① You oughtn't + miss / forget：不該錯過

  1. There will certainly be a banquet that day, and you oughtn't miss it for the world!
    那天一定會辦一場盛宴，你無論如何都不該錯過！
  2. You're in Sydney for New Year? The fireworks start at midnight. You oughtn't miss them.
    你新年在雪梨？煙火半夜開始。你不該錯過。
  3. Mum was very upset last year. You oughtn't forget her birthday again.
    媽媽去年很難過。你不該再忘記她的生日。
  4. Everything in the bookshop is half price, and the sale ends tomorrow. You oughtn't miss it.
    書店全部半價，特價明天結束。你不該錯過。
  5. We're flying to Spain at six in the morning. You oughtn't forget to bring your passport.
    我們早上六點要飛西班牙。你不該忘記帶護照。
  6. It's the last concert of the tour, and it's in our town. You oughtn't miss it.
    這是巡迴演唱的最後一場，而且就在我們鎮上。你不該錯過。

② You oughtn't + 原形動詞：給建議

  1. You've studied every day for a month. You oughtn't worry so much about the exam.
    你一個月來每天都在讀書。你不該那麼擔心考試。
  2. You keep waking up with a stomach ache. You oughtn't eat so late at night.
    你一直肚子痛醒。你不該那麼晚還吃東西。
  3. You've been awake since five this morning. You oughtn't drive when you're this tired.
    你從今天早上五點就醒著。你這麼累的時候不該開車。
  4. You can't pay your rent this month. You oughtn't spend all your money on clothes.
    你這個月付不出房租。你不該把錢都花在衣服上。
  5. Three bikes were stolen on our street last week. You oughtn't leave yours outside without a lock.
    上星期我們這條街有三台腳踏車被偷。你不該把你的腳踏車放在外面不上鎖。
  6. That story about the moon isn't true. You oughtn't believe everything you read online.
    那個關於月亮的故事不是真的。你不該相信你在網路上讀到的每一件事。

③ I / We oughtn't + 原形動詞：說自己

  1. I've already had two slices. I oughtn't eat any more cake, but it's so good.
    我已經吃了兩片了。我不該再吃蛋糕了，可是它太好吃了。
  2. We oughtn't stay out too late. We have school tomorrow.
    我們不該在外面待太晚。我們明天要上學。
  3. I oughtn't say this, but I don't like Sara's new haircut.
    我不該這麼說，可是我不喜歡 Sara 的新髮型。
  4. Half the vegetables in our fridge go in the bin every week. We oughtn't waste so much food.
    我們冰箱裡一半的蔬菜每週都被丟掉。我們不該浪費這麼多食物。
  5. My friends lost their jobs this year. I oughtn't complain. My job is quite good.
    我朋友今年都失業了。我不該抱怨。我的工作還不錯。
  6. Our gas bill was huge last month. We oughtn't keep the heating on when nobody's at home.
    我們上個月的瓦斯費很高。沒人在家的時候，我們不該開著暖氣。

④ 主詞 + oughtn't to + 原形動詞：保留 to

  1. You're at the office until ten every night. You oughtn't to work so hard.
    你每天晚上都在辦公室待到十點。你不該這麼拚命工作。
  2. The report says children watch four hours of TV a day. Children oughtn't to watch so much.
    報告說孩子一天看四個小時的電視。孩子們不該看這麼多。
  3. Tom shouted at his mum in the supermarket. He oughtn't to speak to her like that.
    Tom 在超市對他媽媽大吼。他不該那樣跟她說話。
  4. The river floods every few winters. They oughtn't to build houses so close to it.
    那條河每隔幾個冬天就淹水。他們不該把房子蓋得離河這麼近。
  5. The park has no lights after nine. Lucy oughtn't to walk home alone at night.
    公園九點以後沒有燈。Lucy 晚上不該一個人走路回家。
  6. The guests are already at the door. We oughtn't to keep them waiting.
    客人已經到門口了。我們不該讓他們等。

⑤ 主詞 + oughtn't + 原形動詞：說別人或機構

  1. The shirt was ten pounds and the postage was eight. The shop oughtn't charge so much for delivery.
    襯衫十英鎊，運費卻要八英鎊。那家店的運費不該收這麼貴。
  2. My boss shouts at us every day. He oughtn't treat his staff so badly.
    我老闆每天都對我們大吼。他不該這樣虧待他的員工。
  3. Hundreds of people use the library every week. The council oughtn't close it.
    每週有好幾百人用圖書館。市議會不該關閉它。
  4. Our neighbours' visitors block our drive every weekend. They oughtn't park in front of our gate.
    我們鄰居的客人每個週末都擋住我們的車道。他們不該把車停在我們大門前。
  5. The cars go very fast on this street. The children oughtn't play so near the road.
    這條街的車開得很快。孩子們不該在離馬路這麼近的地方玩。
  6. Emma isn't sure about the job in London. She oughtn't take it if she doesn't want it.
    Emma 對倫敦的工作不確定。如果她不想要，她不該接受。
  7. Bus fares went up in January and again in June. The bus company oughtn't raise them again.
    公車票價一月漲過，六月又漲了。公車公司不該再漲價。
