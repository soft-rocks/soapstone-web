例句

① don't / doesn't + anything

  1. I don't know anything about cars.
    我對車子一竅不通。
  2. My grandfather doesn't throw anything away.
    我爺爺什麼東西都不丟。
  3. We don't need anything else, thanks.
    我們不需要別的了，謝謝。
  4. She doesn't tell me anything about her work.
    她工作上的事什麼都不跟我說。
  5. They don't sell anything vegetarian here.
    這裡沒有賣任何素食。
  6. He doesn't do anything at the weekend.
    他週末什麼事都不做。

② didn't + anything

  1. I didn't understand anything in that lesson.
    那堂課我一點都沒聽懂。
  2. She didn't say anything when I came in.
    我進來的時候她一句話都沒說。
  3. We didn't win anything at the fair.
    我們在園遊會什麼獎都沒抽到。
  4. The thieves didn't take anything.
    小偷什麼都沒拿。
  5. He didn't eat anything at the party.
    他在派對上什麼都沒吃。

③ can't / couldn't + anything

  1. I can't remember anything about the accident.
    那場車禍的事我什麼都不記得了。
  2. We couldn't do anything to help him.
    我們什麼忙都幫不上。
  3. It's so noisy. I can't hear anything.
    太吵了，我什麼都聽不到。
  4. The doctor couldn't find anything wrong with me.
    醫生查不出我有什麼毛病。
  5. I'm sorry, but I can't promise you anything.
    抱歉，我什麼都不能跟你保證。

④ haven't / hasn't + anything

  1. I haven't bought anything for the party yet.
    派對的東西我都還沒買。
  2. He hasn't sent me anything yet.
    他還沒寄任何東西給我。
  3. We haven't heard anything from the school.
    我們還沒收到學校的任何消息。
  4. She hasn't touched anything on her plate.
    她盤子裡的東西一口都沒動。
  5. Nobody has told me. I haven't heard anything.
    沒人跟我說，我什麼都沒聽說。

⑤ won't / isn't / wasn't + anything

  1. This old radio won't play anything any more.
    這台舊收音機已經什麼都放不出來了。
  2. There isn't anything in the box.
    箱子裡什麼都沒有。
  3. There wasn't anything on the news about it.
    新聞上沒有提到這件事。
  4. My computer won't open anything this morning.
    我的電腦今天早上什麼檔案都打不開。
  5. It isn't anything serious, just a headache.
    沒什麼嚴重的，只是頭痛。

⑥ Don't + anything

  1. Please don't move anything on my desk.
    請不要動我桌上的東西。
  2. Don't say anything to Mum yet.
    先別跟媽媽說。
  3. Don't buy anything for me. I'm fine.
    不用幫我買什麼，我不用。
  4. Don't sign anything before you read it.
    看清楚之前什麼都不要簽。
  5. Don't eat anything before the blood test.
    抽血前什麼都不要吃。
