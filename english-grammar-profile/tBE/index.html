My friend is called Jessica.

我朋友叫 Jessica。

例句

① 人

  1. My friend is called Jessica. She lives next door.
    我朋友叫 Jessica，她住在隔壁。
  2. My mother is a teacher at my school.
    我媽媽是我學校的老師。
  3. The doctor is very kind to children.
    那位醫生對小朋友很好。
  4. My brother is a nurse, so he often works at night.
    我哥哥是護理師，所以他常上夜班。
  5. Our neighbour has a big dog.
    我們的鄰居養了一隻大狗。

② 地方

  1. My house is in Ilford, near the station.
    我家在伊爾福德，靠近車站。
  2. The school is at the end of this road.
    學校在這條路的盡頭。
  3. Excuse me, is there a bank on this street?
    不好意思，這條街上有銀行嗎？
  4. We play in the park after school.
    我們放學後在公園玩。
  5. London is a big city.
    倫敦是一個大城市。

③ 東西

  1. Where is my phone? I can't find it.
    我的手機呢？我找不到。
  2. You can borrow this book. I finished it last night.
    這本書你可以借去，我昨晚看完了。
  3. Please leave your umbrella by the door.
    請把你的雨傘放在門邊。
  4. My dad has a red car.
    我爸爸有一台紅色的車。
  5. Mum, there's a spider in the bath!
    媽，浴缸裡有一隻蜘蛛！

④ 時間

  1. I visit my grandma every week after work.
    我每個禮拜下班後去看我奶奶。
  2. I get up at seven in the morning.
    我早上七點起床。
  3. We have English three times a week.
    我們一個禮拜上三次英文課。
  4. My birthday is next month.
    我的生日在下個月。

⑤ 活動

  1. In my free time I go shopping, and I like swimming.
    我有空的時候會去逛街，而且我喜歡游泳。
  2. My sister loves dancing.
    我妹妹很愛跳舞。
  3. Football is my favourite sport.
    足球是我最喜歡的運動。
  4. I do my homework after dinner.
    我吃完晚餐後寫功課。

⑥ 句首的普通名詞大寫

  1. Dogs are my favourite animals.
    狗是我最喜歡的動物。
  2. Breakfast is at eight.
    早餐八點開始。
  3. Teachers work very hard.
    老師們工作很辛苦。
  4. Coffee keeps me awake.
    咖啡讓我保持清醒。

⑦ 普通名詞和人名、地名

  1. My teacher is called Mr Smith.
    我的老師是 Smith 先生。
  2. This summer I'm visiting my cousin in Paris.
    今年夏天我要去巴黎找我表姊。
  3. My cat is called Tiger.
    我的貓叫 Tiger。
  4. My sister works in a hotel in Rome.
    我姊姊在羅馬的一家飯店工作。
