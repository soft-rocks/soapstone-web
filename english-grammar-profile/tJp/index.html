He was actually acting like a ghost.

他還在紅綠燈那裡等。

主⁠詞 + was / were + 副⁠詞 + ⁠-⁠ing

still、actually、already

still還在。
actually其實、真的。
already已經。
probably大概。

still 說還⁠沒⁠停

例句

He was still waiting at the traffic light.

他還在紅綠燈前等著。

was still + -⁠ing 說過去某個時間，事情還在進行。

actually 說真⁠正⁠的情⁠況

例句

She was actually laughing, not crying.

她其實是在笑，不是在哭。

was actually + -⁠ing 說跟別人想的不一樣的真實情況。

副⁠詞放⁠在 was 和 ⁠-⁠ing 中⁠間

He was actually acting like a ghost.

He was waiting still at the light.

He was still waiting at the light.

放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面要⁠用副⁠詞

She was actual crying.

She was actually crying.

主⁠詞是 they，用 were

They was already eating when I arrived.

They were already eating when I arrived.

例句

① 主詞 + was / were + still + -ing：還在

  1. The light had turned green, but the old man was still waiting at the crossing.
    燈已經變綠了，可是那個老人還在斑馬線前等著。
  2. When I woke up at noon, my brother was still sleeping.
    我中午醒來的時候，我哥哥還在睡覺。
  3. The guests were still arriving when dinner started.
    晚餐開始的時候客人還在陸續到來。
  4. At ten o'clock she was still working on her essay.
    十點的時候她還在寫作文。
  5. The workers were still building the bridge a year later.
    一年以後工人還在蓋那座橋。
  6. The band was still playing when we left the party.
    我們離開派對的時候樂團還在演奏。

② 主詞 + was / were + actually + -ing：其實是

  1. On Halloween my brother hid in the garden under a white sheet. He was actually acting like a ghost.
    萬聖節晚上我哥哥披著白床單躲在花園裡。他其實是在扮鬼。
  2. I thought she was angry, but she was actually laughing.
    我以為她在生氣，可是她其實是在笑。
  3. The dog looked asleep, but it was actually watching us.
    那隻狗看起來在睡覺，可是牠其實在看著我們。
  4. We thought they were arguing, but they were actually practising a play.
    我們以為他們在吵架，可是他們其實是在排練一齣戲。
  5. He looked busy, but he was actually playing games on his computer.
    他看起來很忙，可是他其實在電腦上打電動。
  6. I was actually enjoying the rain until I fell in a puddle.
    我其實很享受那場雨，直到我掉進一個水坑。

③ 主詞 + was / were + already + -ing：已經在

  1. When we got to the cinema, the film was already starting.
    我們到電影院的時候，電影已經開始了。
  2. By six o'clock, people were already queuing outside the shop.
    六點的時候，人們已經在店外排隊了。
  3. When I arrived, my friends were already eating.
    我到的時候，我的朋友們已經在吃了。
  4. By March, the farmers were already planting their crops.
    到三月的時候，農夫們已經在種作物了。
  5. When the head teacher walked into the hall, the choir was already singing.
    校長走進禮堂的時候，合唱團已經在唱了。
  6. By lunchtime, the snow was already melting.
    到午餐的時候，雪已經在融化了。

④ 主詞 + was / were + probably + -ing：大概在

  1. I didn't answer because I was probably cycling home.
    我沒接，因為我大概在騎腳踏車回家。
  2. When you called, they were probably having dinner.
    你打來的時候，他們大概在吃晚餐。
  3. She didn't see you because she was probably reading.
    她沒看到你，因為她大概在看書。
  4. At that time, my dad was probably driving to work.
    那時候，我爸爸大概在開車上班。
  5. The children were probably playing in the park.
    孩子們大概在公園裡玩。
  6. He was probably sleeping when the storm started.
    暴風雨開始的時候他大概在睡覺。

⑤ 主詞 + was / were + 副詞 + -ing：其他常用的副詞

  1. The baby was finally sleeping after hours of crying.
    哭了好幾個小時以後，寶寶終於在睡覺了。
  2. The sun was slowly going down behind the hills.
    太陽正慢慢地落到山丘後面。
  3. We were happily walking along the beach when it started to rain.
    我們正開心地沿著海灘散步，這時開始下雨了。
  4. He was secretly planning a surprise party for his wife.
    他正偷偷地為他太太計畫一個驚喜派對。
  5. The children were quietly drawing at the kitchen table.
    孩子們正安靜地在廚房的桌子上畫畫。
  6. She was nervously waiting for her exam results.
    她正緊張地等著她的考試結果。
  7. They were patiently queuing for the museum to open.
    他們正耐心地排隊等博物館開門。
