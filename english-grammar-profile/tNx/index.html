I think it is an experience not to be missed.

我覺得這是一次不容錯過的體驗。

名⁠詞後⁠面⁠接 not to be 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

不容錯過的經驗

前⁠面可⁠以接動⁠詞、形⁠容⁠詞或名⁠詞

I think it is an experience not to be missed.

動詞

She deserves to be told the truth.

她應該知道真相。
be supposed to

Some training is supposed to be given.

應該會有一些訓練。
形容詞

My essay was ready to be printed.

我的作文可以印了。
名詞

It's an experience not to be missed.

這是不容錯過的經驗。

情⁠態動⁠詞後⁠面⁠接 be，不⁠加 to

I think it is an experience not to be missed.

新房子可能不會用磚頭蓋。

否⁠定不⁠定⁠詞，和否⁠定前⁠面⁠的動⁠詞

否定不定詞

我說不要打擾我。
否定前面的動詞

I didn't ask to be woken up.

我沒有說要叫醒我。

It is an experience not be missed.

It is an experience not to be missed.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 be

Some training is supposed to be given.

I think it is an experience not to be missed.

The houses might not be built.

例句

① 名詞加 (not) to be + 過去分詞

  1. I'd like to stay in a tent. I've never done it, and I think it's an experience not to be missed.
    我想住帳篷。我從來沒住過，我覺得這是不容錯過的經驗。
  2. You must see the fireworks on New Year's Eve. They're a sight not to be missed.
    你一定要去看跨年煙火。那是不能錯過的景象。
  3. There's a pile of letters on my desk. They're the letters to be posted today.
    我桌上有一疊信。那些是今天要寄的信。
  4. Let's make a list of the jobs to be done before the guests arrive.
    我們把客人來之前要做的事列出來。
  5. Have you read the rules? There's a long list of things not to be brought into the exam room.
    你看過規定了嗎？有一長串不准帶進考場的東西。

② 形容詞加 to be + 過去分詞

  1. My composition was ready to be printed, so I looked for some paper.
    我的作文可以印了，所以我去找紙。
  2. The cake is still hot. It isn't ready to be cut yet.
    蛋糕還很燙。還不能切。
  3. Grandpa's watch is very old. He'd be happy to be given a new one for his birthday.
    爺爺的錶很舊了。生日收到一支新的，他會很高興。
  4. My sister sang at their wedding. She was so proud to be asked.
    我姊姊在他們的婚禮上唱歌。被邀請唱歌讓她很驕傲。
  5. I was lucky to be chosen for the team. Lots of people tried out.
    我很幸運被選進隊裡。很多人去試了。

③ 動詞加 to be + 過去分詞

  1. She worked so hard this year. She deserves to be given a prize.
    她今年很努力。她應該得獎。
  2. I'm going to hospital on Monday. I expect to be kept in for two nights.
    我星期一要去住院。我想會要住兩個晚上。
  3. My little brother hates to be told what to do.
    我弟弟討厭別人叫他做事。
  4. The new manager seems to be liked by everyone in the office.
    辦公室裡的人好像都很喜歡新經理。
  5. We hope to be invited to their wedding next summer.
    我們希望明年夏天能受邀參加他們的婚禮。

④ be supposed to be + 過去分詞

  1. According to your advert, some training is supposed to be given. When does it start?
    照你們的廣告，應該會有一些訓練。什麼時候開始？
  2. Don't touch those boxes. They're supposed to be checked by the manager first.
    別碰那些箱子。應該要先讓經理檢查。
  3. The essays were supposed to be handed in yesterday, but half the class forgot.
    作文應該昨天交，可是班上一半的人忘了。
  4. This door is supposed to be locked at night. Who left it open?
    這扇門晚上應該要鎖。誰沒關？

⑤ not to be + 過去分詞

  1. I took part in your research to help young people not to be addicted to computer games.
    我參加你們的研究，是想幫助年輕人不要沉迷電腦遊戲。
  2. Please put a sign on the door. I asked not to be disturbed.
    請在門上掛個牌子。我說過不要打擾我。
  3. Read the label first. This medicine is not to be taken with alcohol.
    先看標籤。這個藥不能跟酒一起吃。
  4. She's very shy. She prefers not to be noticed at parties.
    她很害羞。在派對上她寧願別人不要注意到她。
  5. The box said "Not to be opened until Christmas", so I put it under the tree.
    盒子上寫著聖誕節前不可打開，所以我把它放在樹下。

⑥ 情態動詞加 (not) be + 過去分詞

  1. In the future, homes might not be built of bricks at all.
    未來的房子可能完全不用磚頭蓋。
  2. "Can this dress be washed in the machine?" "No, it should be washed by hand."
    這件洋裝可以用洗衣機洗嗎？不行，要手洗。
  3. The tickets must be paid for before Friday, or you'll lose your seat.
    票一定要在星期五前付錢，不然你的位子就沒了。
  4. These old photos shouldn't be thrown away. They're our family history.
    這些舊照片不該丟。它們是我們家的歷史。
  5. The work could be finished by tomorrow if we start now.
    我們現在開始的話，明天就可以完成。

⑦ 問句

  1. "Is this soup supposed to be eaten cold?" "Yes, it's a summer soup."
    這碗湯是要冷的吃嗎？對，這是夏天的湯。
  2. "Do you expect to be paid for the extra work?" "Of course I do!"
    你期待加班有錢拿嗎？當然！
