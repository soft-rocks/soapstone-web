The survey might not have asked an accurate sample of people.

這份調查訪問的對象可能不具代表性。

主⁠詞 + might not have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

might not have、過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

might not have過去可能沒有。
過去分詞可能沒發生或沒做好的事。

質⁠疑報⁠告和調⁠查

例句

The figures might not have included part-time workers.

這些數字可能沒有包括兼職員工。

寫信給報社或主管時，常用這個句型小心地質疑資料。

安⁠慰和解⁠釋

例句

Even though things might not have gone as you expected, we thank you for your help.

雖然事情可能沒有照您預期的發展，我們還是謝謝您的幫忙。

Even though … might not have … 先承認事情不如預期，再說另一個重點。

might not 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 have

The survey might not have asked an accurate sample of people.

The report might not has shown everything.

The report might not have shown everything.

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The survey might not have asked an accurate sample of people.

They might not have ask enough people.

They might not have asked enough people.

even though 和 but 不⁠一⁠起用

Even though it might not have worked, but we learned a lot.

Even though it might not have worked, we learned a lot.

例句

① 主詞 + might not have + 過去分詞：質疑調查和報告

  1. The questionnaire might not have reached people without the internet.
    這份問卷可能沒有送到沒網路的人手上。
  2. The figures might not have included part-time workers.
    這些數字可能沒有包括兼職員工。
  3. The researchers might not have spoken to enough young people.
    研究人員可能沒有訪問夠多年輕人。
  4. The report might not have mentioned all the costs.
    這份報告可能沒有提到所有的費用。
  5. The study might not have lasted long enough to show real results.
    這項研究可能進行得不夠久，看不出真正的結果。
  6. The journalist might not have checked the facts.
    那位記者可能沒有查證事實。

② Even though + 主詞 + might not have + 過去分詞, + 句子

  1. Even though things might not have gone as you expected, please remember that this is a charity.
    雖然事情可能沒有照您預期的發展，請記得這是一個慈善活動。
  2. Even though the weather might not have been perfect, the festival was a success.
    雖然天氣可能不完美，節慶還是很成功。
  3. Even though you might not have won, you played really well.
    雖然你可能沒有贏，你踢得真的很好。
  4. Even though the food might not have been hot, the guests loved it.
    雖然食物可能不夠熱，客人還是很喜歡。
  5. Even though we might not have raised much money, everyone had a good time.
    雖然我們可能沒募到很多錢，大家都玩得很開心。
  6. Even though the trip might not have been cheap, it was worth it.
    雖然那趟旅行可能不便宜，可是很值得。

③ 主詞 + might not have + 過去分詞, but + 句子：安慰

  1. The charity run might not have attracted many runners, but the whole town came to watch.
    慈善路跑可能沒吸引很多跑者，可是全鎮的人都來看了。
  2. The charity sale might not have made much money, but it brought the neighbours together.
    慈善義賣可能沒賺很多錢，可是讓鄰居們聚在一起。
  3. Your first concert might not have been perfect, but the audience loved it.
    你的第一場音樂會可能不完美，可是觀眾很喜歡。
  4. The new shop might not have made a profit yet, but it's getting busier.
    那家新店可能還沒賺錢，可是生意越來越好了。
  5. Your speech might not have been very long, but it was clear.
    你的演講可能不長，可是很清楚。
  6. The garden might not have won a prize, but it looks lovely.
    那座花園可能沒得獎，可是看起來很漂亮。

④ 主詞 + might not have + 過去分詞：解釋別人為什麼沒做

  1. Don't be angry with him. He might not have known the meeting was today.
    別生他的氣。他可能不知道今天要開會。
  2. The children might not have understood the rules of the game.
    孩子們可能沒聽懂遊戲規則。
  3. She might not have had time to answer your letter.
    她可能沒時間回你的信。
  4. The new staff might not have been told about the fire drill.
    新員工可能沒被告知消防演習的事。（被動）
  5. Your friend might not have seen you wave.
    你朋友可能沒看到你揮手。
  6. The waiter might not have heard you ask for water.
    服務生可能沒聽到你要水。

⑤ 主詞 + might not have + 過去分詞：說過去的情況

  1. People in the village might not have travelled far in those days.
    那個年代，村子裡的人可能沒去過很遠的地方。
  2. The early cars might not have gone faster than a horse.
    早期的汽車可能沒有比馬快。
  3. My grandparents might not have had a television until the 1960s.
    我祖父母可能到 1960 年代才有電視。
  4. The first settlers might not have planned to stay.
    最早的移民可能沒打算留下來。
  5. Children in the past might not have gone to school after the age of twelve.
    以前的孩子可能十二歲以後就不上學了。
  6. The workers might not have earned enough to feed their families.
    工人賺的錢可能不夠養家。
  7. The town might not have had clean water until the new pipes came.
    在新的水管來之前，這個鎮可能沒有乾淨的水。
