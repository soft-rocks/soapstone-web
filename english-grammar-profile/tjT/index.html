例句

① 名詞 + involved

  1. Thank you for coming to the interview. We'll pay all the travel costs involved.
    謝謝你來面試。我們會負擔所有相關的交通費用。
  2. Everyone involved in the accident spoke to the police.
    事故相關的每個人都跟警方談過了。
  3. The rafting trip was really dangerous. Nobody told us about the risks involved.
    泛舟那趟真的很危險。沒人告訴我們其中的風險。
  4. The school concert raised £2,000. Thanks to everyone involved.
    學校音樂會募到兩千英鎊。謝謝所有參與的人。
  5. We painted the whole house ourselves. The work involved was more than we expected.
    我們自己把整間房子漆好。需要做的工作比我們預期的多。

② 名詞 + required / needed

  1. Our hotel meets all the safety standards required.
    我們的飯店符合所有要求的安全標準。
  2. You need a visa for this trip. Fill in all the forms required.
    這趟旅行你需要簽證。請填好所有要求的表格。
  3. Our old car keeps breaking down, but we don't have the money needed for a new one.
    我們的舊車一直壞，可是我們沒有買新車需要的錢。
  4. We have to be in Leeds by noon. Can you check the time needed for the trip?
    我們中午以前要到里茲。你可以查一下這趟路需要的時間嗎？
  5. We are looking for a new receptionist. The skills required are listed below.
    我們在找一位新的櫃台人員。需要的技能列在下面。
  6. My sister phoned the bank about her loan. She had all the information needed.
    我姊姊打電話給銀行問她的貸款。她有所有需要的資料。

③ 名詞 + shown / mentioned / given

  1. The dates shown in red are holidays.
    用紅色標示的日期是假日。
  2. You won't pay more at the till. The prices shown include tax.
    你在收銀台不會多付錢。標示的價格含稅。
  3. The problems mentioned in your letter are now fixed.
    你信裡提到的問題現在已經解決了。
  4. Here's how to set up your new printer. Follow the steps given on this page.
    這是你的新印表機的安裝方法。照這一頁給的步驟做。
  5. Our guidebook is five years old. The hotel mentioned in it is closed now.
    我們的旅遊指南是五年前的。裡面提到的那家飯店現在關了。
  6. Our office has moved. Please use the address given below.
    我們的辦公室搬家了。請使用下面提供的地址。

④ 名詞 + used / chosen / paid

  1. My son is only seven, but he can read this story alone. The words used in it are easy.
    我兒子才七歲，可是他可以自己讀這個故事。裡面用的字很簡單。
  2. The scientists cleaned the river in a year. The method used was very simple.
    科學家一年就把那條河清乾淨了。用的方法很簡單。
  3. The school picked six students for the science prize. The students chosen will travel to London.
    學校挑了六個學生參加科學獎。被選上的學生會去倫敦。
  4. We painted the baby's room last week. The colors chosen for it are calm.
    我們上星期粉刷了寶寶的房間。房間挑的顏色很柔和。
  5. The school disco has finished. Please count the money collected at the door.
    學校的舞會結束了。請點一下在門口收到的錢。
  6. We spent £500 on my son's birthday. The money spent on the party was too much.
    我們為我兒子的生日花了五百英鎊。花在派對上的錢太多了。

⑤ the people concerned 和其他

  1. The school bus will not run next week. The families concerned have all been told.
    校車下星期停駛。相關的家庭都已經被通知了。
  2. Some exam papers went missing. The head teacher spoke to the teachers concerned.
    有一些考卷不見了。校長跟相關的老師談過了。
  3. We asked our neighbours about a street party. Most of the people asked said yes.
    我們問鄰居要不要辦街頭派對。被問到的人大多說好。
  4. We cooked too much for the wedding. The food left after the party went to a shelter.
    我們為婚禮煮了太多菜。派對剩下的食物送到了收容所。
  5. Welcome to the hotel. The rooms booked for the conference are on the second floor.
    歡迎來到本飯店。為會議訂的房間在二樓。
  6. The shop is busier now. The changes made last year helped a lot.
    那家店現在客人比較多了。去年做的改變幫助很大。
  7. We had too much cake at the launch. Ten of the people invited didn't come.
    發表會的蛋糕太多了。受邀的人有十個沒來。
