例句

① Are + 主詞

  1. Dad's waiting in the car. Are you ready?
    爸爸在車上等了。你準備好了嗎？
  2. You're holding your stomach. Are you in pain?
    你一直按著肚子。你會痛嗎？
  3. You've been out all day. Are you thirsty? There's juice in the fridge.
    你在外面待了一整天。你渴嗎？冰箱裡有果汁。
  4. I'd like to say thank you for dinner. Are your parents at home this weekend?
    我想謝謝他們請我吃晚餐。你爸媽這個週末在家嗎？
  5. Are these your keys? I found them on the floor.
    這些是你的鑰匙嗎？我在地上找到的。
  6. The car park is almost empty. Are we too early for the show?
    停車場幾乎是空的。我們太早來看表演了嗎？

② Is + 主詞

  1. I heard your dog was ill. Is it OK now?
    我聽說你的狗生病了。牠現在還好嗎？
  2. Is your sister at home? I need to talk to her.
    你姊姊在家嗎？我得跟她說話。
  3. Excuse me, is this your umbrella? You left it on the bus.
    不好意思，這是你的傘嗎？你把它忘在公車上了。
  4. It's a public holiday. Is the museum open today?
    今天是國定假日。博物館今天有開嗎？
  5. We don't have a car this week. Is it far to the beach from here?
    我們這星期沒有車。從這裡到海灘遠嗎？
  6. Is he your new teacher? He looks very young.
    他是你的新老師嗎？他看起來好年輕。

③ Am I

  1. Am I late? I'm sorry, the bus was slow.
    我遲到了嗎？抱歉，公車很慢。
  2. Sorry, it's my first day here. Am I in the right room for the English class?
    抱歉，我今天第一天來。我是在英文課的教室嗎？
  3. I'm fifty, and I've never played an instrument. Am I too old to learn the piano?
    我五十歲了，從來沒彈過樂器。我學鋼琴會不會太老了？

④ Was / Were + 主詞

  1. Was the film good? I want to see it.
    那部電影好看嗎？我想去看。
  2. Were you at home last night? I called you three times, but nobody answered.
    你昨晚在家嗎？我打了三次給你，都沒人接。
  3. Welcome back! You were away for two weeks. Was it cold in Scotland?
    歡迎回來！你去了兩個星期。蘇格蘭冷嗎？
  4. Your team lost the final. Were the players very upset?
    你們隊決賽輸了。球員們很難過嗎？
  5. Your mum and your aunt are both teachers. Was your grandfather a teacher too?
    你媽媽和你阿姨都是老師。你爺爺也是老師嗎？
  6. Your holiday photos are lovely. Was the hotel near the sea?
    你的度假照片很漂亮。那家飯店靠近海嗎？

⑤ 回答

  1. "Are you tired?" "Yes, I am. I didn't sleep well."
    你累了嗎？累。我沒睡好。
  2. "Is it your birthday today?" "No, it isn't. It's tomorrow."
    今天是你的生日嗎？不是。是明天。
  3. "Were you at the party?" "Yes, I was. It was great."
    你有去派對嗎？有。很好玩。
  4. "Is the shop open?" "No, it's not. It's Sunday."
    店有開嗎？沒有。今天是星期天。
  5. "The answer is twelve. Am I right?" "Yes, you are."
    答案是十二。我說對了嗎？對，你說對了。

⑥ Is there / Are there

  1. I need to send this parcel. Is there a post office in this village?
    我得寄這個包裹。這個村子有郵局嗎？
  2. I want to make pancakes. Are there any eggs in the fridge?
    我想做鬆餅。冰箱裡有蛋嗎？
  3. You're two hours late. Was there a lot of traffic?
    你晚了兩個小時。路上車多嗎？
  4. I saw the photos online. Were there many people at the concert?
    我在網路上看到照片了。演唱會人多嗎？

⑦ 確認聽到的事

  1. You're moving to Canada? Is that true?
    你要搬去加拿大？是真的嗎？
