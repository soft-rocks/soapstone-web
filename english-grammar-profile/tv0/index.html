The one that impressed me most was the Chinese New Year Carnival in Hong Kong.

讓我印象最深的，是香港的農曆新年嘉年華。

The one that + 子⁠句當主⁠詞

The one that impressed me most was the Chinese New Year Carnival in Hong Kong.

讓我印象最深的，是香港的農曆新年嘉年華。

that 當主⁠詞不⁠能省⁠略，當受⁠詞可⁠以省⁠略

The one that impressed me most was the Chinese New Year Carnival in Hong Kong.

當主詞

The one that won the prize was a small French film.

得獎的是一部法國小片。
當受詞

The one I really wanted was the red one.

我真正想要的是紅色那個。

the one 用單⁠數，the ones 用複⁠數

The one that impressed me most was the Chinese New Year Carnival in Hong Kong.

the one

The one that I remember best is our first holiday together.

我記得最清楚的是我們第一次一起去度假。
the ones

The ones that sold out first were the cheapest seats.

最先賣光的是最便宜的位子。

the ones 用複⁠數動⁠詞

The ones that I liked best was the little shops by the harbour.

The ones that I liked best were the little shops by the harbour.

當主⁠詞⁠的 that 不⁠能省⁠略

The one that impressed me most was the Chinese New Year Carnival in Hong Kong.

The one surprised me most was the price.

The one that surprised me most was the price.

子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

The one that I enjoyed it most was the boat trip.

The one that I enjoyed most was the boat trip.

指人用 who，不⁠用 what

The one what helped me most was my teacher.

The one who helped me most was my teacher.

what 開⁠頭⁠的句⁠子

讓我印象最深的是音樂。

it 分⁠裂句：重⁠點放⁠在⁠前⁠面

讓我印象最深的是那個嘉年華。

例句

① The one that + 動詞

  1. The one that broke down was my brother's car.
    拋錨的是我哥的車。
  2. The one that got away was the biggest fish of the day.
    跑掉的那條是當天最大的魚。
  3. The one that costs the most isn't always the best.
    最貴的不一定最好。
  4. The one that caught my eye was a little blue teapot.
    吸引我目光的是一個藍色小茶壺。
  5. The one that worries me is the last question.
    我擔心的是最後一題。
  6. The one that arrived late was the parcel from Japan.
    晚到的是從日本寄來的包裹。

② The one (that) + 主詞 + 動詞

  1. The one I chose in the end was the cheaper flat.
    我最後選的是比較便宜的那間公寓。
  2. The one we stayed in was a small cottage by the lake.
    我們住的是湖邊的一間小屋。
  3. The one she recommended is closed on Mondays.
    她推薦的那家星期一沒開。
  4. The one that I read last summer was much better.
    我去年夏天讀的那本好看多了。
  5. The one you need is on the top shelf.
    你要的那個在最上層。

③ The ones that

  1. The ones that grew fastest were the tomatoes by the wall.
    長得最快的是牆邊的番茄。
  2. The ones that we bought at the airport were twice the price.
    我們在機場買的那些貴了一倍。
  3. The ones that sell best are the small cakes.
    賣得最好的是小蛋糕。
  4. The ones I really miss are my old school friends.
    我真正想念的是我的老同學。
  5. The ones that arrived first got the best seats.
    先到的人拿到了最好的位子。

④ the one who / the ones who

  1. The one who called you was my manager.
    打給你的是我的主管。
  2. The ones who suffer most are the children.
    受害最深的是孩子。
  3. The one who taught me to swim was my uncle.
    教我游泳的是我叔叔。
  4. The ones who stayed until the end were rewarded.
    待到最後的人都得到了回報。
  5. The one who usually complains is always the first to leave.
    最常抱怨的人總是最先走。

⑤ 最高級和 most

  1. The one that impressed the judges most was the youngest singer.
    最讓評審印象深刻的是年紀最小的歌手。
  2. The one that frightened me most was the ghost train.
    最讓我害怕的是鬼屋列車。
  3. The one I enjoyed least was the history museum.
    我最不喜歡的是歷史博物館。
  4. The ones that lasted longest were the cheapest boots.
    最耐穿的反而是最便宜的靴子。
  5. The one that made me laugh most was the final scene.
    讓我笑得最厲害的是最後一幕。

⑥ 談經驗和看法

  1. The one that has changed most is the price of housing.
    改變最大的是房價。
  2. The one that will make the biggest difference is better public transport.
    影響最大的會是更好的大眾運輸。
  3. The ones that really matter are the long-term effects.
    真正重要的是長期的影響。
  4. The one that I think will last is the friendship we built.
    我覺得會長久的，是我們建立起來的友誼。
  5. The ones that he loved most were the stories about the sea.
    他最愛的是關於大海的故事。
