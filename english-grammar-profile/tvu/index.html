例句

① the best possible + 名詞

  1. Most of our clients work near the river. Central London is the best possible location for the office.
    我們大部分的客戶都在河邊附近工作。倫敦市中心是辦公室最理想的地點。
  2. My parents are selling their house this spring. They want the best possible price for it.
    我爸媽今年春天要賣房子。他們想用最好的價格賣掉。
  3. My grandmother was in hospital for a month. The doctors gave her the best possible care.
    我奶奶住院住了一個月。醫生給了她最好的照顧。
  4. I'm sorry your order arrived late. We'll find the best possible solution.
    很抱歉你的訂單晚到了。我們會找出最好的解決辦法。
  5. The final is on Sunday. Our team wants to get the best possible result.
    決賽在星期天。我們隊想拿到最好的成績。

② the + 形容詞-est + possible + 名詞

  1. The manager spoke about next year's plans. We want to raise sales to the highest possible level.
    經理談到明年的計畫。我們想把銷售量提高到最高的水準。
  2. We'll repair the roof in three stages. This isn't the quickest possible plan, but it's the cheapest.
    我們會分三個階段修屋頂。這不是最快的計畫，不過是最便宜的。
  3. All our staff are busy right now. We'll call you back at the earliest possible moment.
    我們所有的員工現在都在忙。我們會盡快回電給你。
  4. The client moved the deadline to Friday. We need to finish in the shortest possible time.
    客戶把截止日改到星期五。我們要在最短的時間內完成。
  5. The theatre wanted students to come. They sold the tickets at the lowest possible price.
    劇院希望學生來看。他們用最低的價格賣票。
  6. The salesman said the car was very safe. It was built with the strongest possible steel.
    業務員說這輛車很安全。它是用最堅固的鋼材打造的。

③ the most + 形容詞 + possible + 名詞

  1. My dad paid extra for a bed seat. This is the most comfortable possible seat on the plane.
    我爸爸多付錢買了可以躺平的座位。這是飛機上最舒服的座位。
  2. We didn't want to change planes with a baby. We chose the most direct possible flight.
    我們不想帶著寶寶轉機。我們選了最直接的航班。
  3. My grandmother doesn't understand computers. Explain it in the most basic possible terms.
    我奶奶不懂電腦。用最基本的說法解釋。
  4. The interviewer asked about her weaknesses. She gave the most honest possible answer.
    面試官問她的缺點。她給了最誠實的回答。
  5. The old bridge might fall down. The engineers took the most careful possible approach.
    那座舊橋可能會倒。工程師採取了最謹慎的做法。

④ the worst / smallest / lowest + possible + 名詞

  1. My car broke down on the way to my wedding. It happened at the worst possible time.
    我的車在去婚禮的路上拋錨了。這件事發生在最糟的時候。
  2. We were halfway up the mountain. The rain came at the worst possible moment.
    我們爬到半山腰。雨在最不巧的時候下了。
  3. Dad has a problem with his heart. Use the smallest possible amount of salt.
    爸爸心臟有問題。鹽盡量少放。
  4. The neighbours have a new baby. We kept the noise to the lowest possible level.
    鄰居剛生了寶寶。我們盡量把噪音降到最低。
  5. The airline charges for big suitcases. We packed the smallest possible bags for the flight.
    航空公司對大行李箱收費。我們為了搭飛機，帶了最小的行李。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. The client wants to sign the contract soon. What's the earliest possible date for the meeting?
    客戶想盡快簽約。會議最早可以排在哪一天？
  2. Three hundred pounds for a phone repair? Is this the cheapest possible option?
    修手機要三百英鎊？這是最便宜的選擇嗎？
  3. Your new passport is ready. We'll send it by the fastest possible route.
    你的新護照好了。我們會用最快的路線寄出。
  4. I got a new job on 2 January. That was the best possible start to the year.
    我一月二日找到新工作。那是今年最好的開始。
  5. Our host family met us at the airport with flowers. They gave us the warmest possible welcome.
    我們的寄宿家庭帶著花到機場接我們。他們給了我們最熱情的歡迎。
  6. The party will be boring at the start. Let's leave at the latest possible time.
    派對一開始會很無聊。我們盡量晚一點出發。
  7. My sister met her boyfriend's parents last week. She tried to make the best possible impression.
    我姊姊上星期見了她男朋友的爸媽。她努力留下最好的印象。
  8. We wanted to talk about the plans. We asked for the quietest possible table in the café.
    我們想談那些計畫。我們在咖啡店要了最安靜的位子。
  9. There are six of us, with the grandparents. We booked the largest possible room for the family.
    加上爺爺奶奶我們有六個人。我們幫全家訂了最大的房間。
