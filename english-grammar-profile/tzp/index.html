例句

① neither of us / neither of them

  1. Neither of us had been to Rome before.
    我們兩個都沒去過羅馬。
  2. I spoke to both doctors, but neither of them could help.
    我跟兩位醫生都談過，可是他們兩個都幫不上忙。
  3. Neither of them wanted to wash the dishes.
    他們兩個都不想洗碗。
  4. We both tried, but neither of us passed the test.
    我們兩個都試了，可是都沒通過考試。
  5. She has two cats, and neither of them likes water.
    她有兩隻貓，兩隻都不喜歡水。

② either of us / either of them

  1. Have either of you seen my glasses?
    你們兩個有誰看到我的眼鏡嗎？
  2. I don't think either of them will come.
    我覺得他們兩個都不會來。
  3. If either of you needs help, just call me.
    你們兩個誰需要幫忙，就打給我。
  4. Either of them would be a good choice.
    兩個之中選哪一個都不錯。
  5. I didn't like either of them very much.
    他們兩個我都不太喜歡。

③ none of us

  1. None of us expected the test to be so hard.
    我們沒有一個人料到考試這麼難。
  2. None of us had a map, so we got lost.
    我們沒有一個人帶地圖，所以迷路了。
  3. The bus was late, and none of us had any money for a taxi.
    公車誤點，我們沒有一個人有錢搭計程車。
  4. None of us knew how to fix the car.
    我們沒有一個人會修車。
  5. We waited for an hour, but none of us complained.
    我們等了一個小時，可是沒有一個人抱怨。

④ none of them

  1. I bought five lottery tickets, but none of them won.
    我買了五張彩券，可是一張也沒中。
  2. I tried on lots of shoes, but none of them fitted.
    我試穿了很多雙鞋，可是沒有一雙合腳。
  3. He sent several emails, but none of them got a reply.
    他寄了好幾封電子郵件，可是沒有一封有回音。
  4. The children were tired, and none of them wanted to walk.
    孩子們都累了，沒有一個想走路。
  5. I've read his books, but I didn't enjoy any of them. None of them had a good ending.
    我讀過他的書，可是一本也不喜歡。沒有一本的結局是好的。

⑤ none of you

  1. None of you need to stay late tonight.
    你們今晚都不用留下來加班。
  2. I know none of you want to hear this.
    我知道你們都不想聽這個。
  3. None of you were at the meeting, so I'll explain again.
    你們都沒來開會，所以我再說明一次。
  4. Is it true that none of you has a car?
    你們真的沒有一個人有車嗎？
  5. If none of you can come, we'll cancel the trip.
    如果你們都不能來，我們就取消這次旅行。

⑥ 在故事和信裡

  1. We had two maps, but neither of them showed the village.
    我們有兩張地圖，可是兩張都沒標出那個村子。
  2. The waiter spoke quickly, and none of us understood him.
    服務生說話很快，我們沒有一個人聽得懂。
  3. There are skiing and rafting trips. If you're interested in either of them, sign up today.
    有滑雪和泛舟的行程。你對其中任何一個有興趣，今天就報名。
  4. My two sisters live abroad, and I rarely see either of them.
    我的兩個姊姊住在國外，我很少見到她們。
  5. When the lights went out, none of us moved.
    燈熄掉的時候，我們沒有一個人動。
  6. My grandparents lived in the country, but neither of them ever learned to drive.
    我的祖父母住在鄉下，可是兩個都沒學過開車。
