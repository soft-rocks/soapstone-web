My house is number 8, and it's the one with silver gates.

我家是 8 號，就是有銀色大門的那一間。

the one + 後置修⁠飾⁠語

My house is number 8, and it's the one with silver gates.

我家是 8 號，就是有銀色大門的那一間。

後置修⁠飾⁠語⁠的種⁠類

My house is number 8, and it's the one with silver gates.

介系詞片語

Which bag is yours? The one on the chair?

哪個包包是你的？椅子上那個嗎？
關係子句

Do you remember the café? The one that sold those huge cakes?

你記得那家咖啡店嗎？賣超大蛋糕的那一家？
省略關係代名詞

All my friends gave me presents, but the one I liked best was a book.

朋友都送了我禮物，我最喜歡的是一本書。

the + 修⁠飾⁠語 + one

My house is number 8, and it's the one with silver gates.

序數

My house is the first one on the left.

我家是左邊第一間。
other

I bought a new jacket because the other one that I had was too small.

我買了一件新外套，因為原本那件太小了。
形容詞

I want the blue one in the window.

我要櫥窗裡那個藍色的。

one 前⁠面加 the

My house is number 8, and it's the one with silver gates.

My bike is one with the red seat.

My bike is the one with the red seat.

it 後⁠面不⁠接修⁠飾⁠語

Which is your car? It with the roof box?

Which is your car? The one with the roof box?

當主⁠詞⁠的 that 不⁠省⁠略

My house is the one has a red door.

My house is the one that has a red door.

子⁠句⁠裡不⁠再⁠加 it

The one I bought it last week is already broken.

The one I bought last week is already broken.

複⁠數用 the ones

我的鞋子是門邊那雙。

指得出⁠來用 that one

我要那個。

例句

① the one + 介系詞片語

  1. My sister is the one in the green dress.
    我姊姊是穿綠色洋裝的那個。
  2. Our flat is the one above the bakery.
    我們家是麵包店樓上那間。
  3. I'd like a cake, please. The one with strawberries.
    我想要一個蛋糕，要有草莓的那個。
  4. Which key opens the garage? The one with the red tag.
    哪一把鑰匙開車庫？掛紅色標籤的那把。
  5. My desk is the one by the window.
    我的桌子是窗邊那張。
  6. We need the bus to the airport, not the one to the city centre.
    我們要搭往機場的公車，不是往市中心的那班。

② the one + 關係子句

  1. Is this the restaurant? The one that your brother told us about?
    是這家餐廳嗎？你哥跟我們說的那家？
  2. I lost my umbrella, the one that my grandma gave me.
    我把傘弄丟了，就是奶奶送我的那把。
  3. She's the one who helped me at the station.
    她就是在車站幫我的那個人。
  4. Do you remember the film? The one that made us cry?
    你記得那部電影嗎？讓我們哭的那部？
  5. He's the one who always sits at the back.
    他就是總坐在後面的那個人。

③ the one + 省略關係代名詞的子句

  1. This is the one I told you about.
    這就是我跟你說的那個。
  2. The dress was nice, but the one I tried first fitted better.
    那件洋裝不錯，可是我先試的那件比較合身。
  3. That's the one we saw last week.
    那就是我們上週看到的那個。
  4. The one you ordered will arrive on Friday.
    你訂的那個星期五會到。
  5. Here's the one my teacher recommended.
    這就是我老師推薦的那本。

④ the + 序數 + one

  1. Her office is the first one at the top of the stairs.
    她的辦公室是樓梯上去第一間。
  2. Their shop is the second one after the bank.
    他們的店是銀行後面第二間。
  3. The last one in the row is mine.
    那一排最後一個是我的。
  4. Our classroom is the third one along the corridor.
    我們的教室是走廊上第三間。
  5. Get off at the next stop, not the one after it.
    在下一站下車，不是再下一站。

⑤ the other one + 後置修飾語

  1. I like this phone more than the other one I had.
    我喜歡這支手機勝過原本那支。
  2. Let's look at the other one with the bigger garden.
    我們去看另一間花園比較大的吧。
  3. This hotel is cheaper than the other one near the beach.
    這家飯店比海邊那家便宜。
  4. The other one that we looked at was too small.
    我們看過的另一間太小了。
  5. Where's the other one with the long handle?
    另一把長柄的在哪裡？

⑥ the + 形容詞 + one + 後置修飾語

  1. I want the big one on the top shelf.
    我要最上層那個大的。
  2. The black one with the white stripes is mine.
    黑底白條紋的那個是我的。
  3. The small one that you bought is perfect.
    你買的那個小的剛剛好。
  4. Can you pass me the red one next to you?
    可以把你旁邊那個紅色的遞給我嗎？
  5. The old one in the garage still works.
    車庫裡那台舊的還能用。
