例句

① some：肯定句

  1. These shoes were cheap, so I bought some.
    這些鞋子很便宜，所以我買了幾雙。
  2. We need more paper. I'll bring some tomorrow.
    我們需要更多紙，我明天會帶一些來。
  3. I've got lots of stickers. You can have some.
    我有很多貼紙，你可以拿一些。
  4. There were nice T-shirts in the shop, and some were very cheap.
    店裡有好看的 T 恤，有些很便宜。
  5. My grandma grows tomatoes and always gives us some.
    我奶奶種番茄，每次都會給我們一些。

② any：否定句

  1. I wanted some chocolate, but there wasn't any.
    我想吃巧克力，可是一點也沒有。
  2. He asked for money, but I didn't have any.
    他跟我要錢，可是我沒有。
  3. We looked for blackberries, but we didn't find any.
    我們找了黑莓，可是一顆也沒找到。
  4. I like his songs, but I don't know any by heart.
    我喜歡他的歌，可是一首也背不起來。
  5. I looked for empty seats on the bus, but there weren't any.
    我在公車上找空位，可是一個也沒有。

③ any：問句

  1. I need some coins for the machine. Do you have any?
    我需要一些投幣機用的硬幣，你有嗎？
  2. We're out of bread. Is there any in the freezer?
    麵包吃完了，冷凍庫裡還有嗎？
  3. I'm looking for good films. Can you recommend any?
    我在找好看的電影，你可以推薦幾部嗎？
  4. There were lots of fish in the lake. Did you catch any?
    湖裡有很多魚，你有釣到嗎？
  5. I forgot my pencils. Have you brought any?
    我忘了帶鉛筆，你有帶嗎？

④ some 和 any 在同一句

  1. I bought some apples because we didn't have any.
    我買了一些蘋果，因為家裡一顆也沒有。
  2. Please bring some sandwiches. We haven't got any.
    請帶一些三明治來，我們這裡沒有。
  3. She has some old photos, but I don't have any.
    她有一些舊照片，可是我一張也沒有。
  4. Tom has some free time, but I haven't got any this week.
    湯姆有一些空閒時間，可是我這個星期一點也沒有。
  5. Our neighbours had lots of cherries. They gave us some, but they didn't keep any for themselves.
    我們的鄰居有很多櫻桃，他們分了一些給我們，可是自己一顆也沒留。

⑤ some ... some

  1. We tried on lots of clothes. Some were cheap, but some were expensive.
    我們試穿了很多衣服，有些很便宜，有些很貴。
  2. I read all the books. Some were good, and some were boring.
    那些書我全都讀了，有些很好看，有些很無聊。
  3. The children were busy. Some were drawing, and some were singing.
    孩子們都很忙，有些在畫畫，有些在唱歌。
  4. We saw many animals at the zoo. Some were asleep.
    我們在動物園看到很多動物，有些在睡覺。
  5. I made lots of cookies. Some were burnt, but most were fine.
    我做了很多餅乾，有些烤焦了，不過大部分都還好。

⑥ 提議和請求

  1. I've made coffee. Do you want some?
    我煮了咖啡，你要喝嗎？
  2. These grapes are lovely. Have some.
    這些葡萄很好吃，吃一點吧。
  3. That bread smells good. Can I try some?
    那個麵包好香，我可以吃一點嗎？
  4. If you need more paper, there's some in my desk.
    你還需要紙的話，我的書桌裡有一些。
  5. There's soup on the stove. Help yourself to some.
    爐子上有湯，自己盛來喝。
  6. I've got plenty of pens. Would you like some?
    我有很多筆，你要幾支嗎？
