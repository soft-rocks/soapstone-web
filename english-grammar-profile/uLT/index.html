Being invited by your boss to an unexpected lunch makes you feel noticed.

老闆突然請你吃午餐，會讓你覺得自己被注意到了。

being 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞⁠的子⁠句當主⁠詞

老闆請你吃午餐，會讓你覺得自己被注意到了。

當主⁠詞，或放⁠在句⁠首交⁠代原⁠因

Being invited by your boss to an unexpected lunch makes you feel noticed.

當主詞

Being chosen for the team was a great feeling.

被選進隊裡的感覺很棒。
交代原因

Being born in Mexico, she knows the country well.

她在墨西哥出生，所以很了解這個國家。
省略 being

Born in Mexico, she knows the country well.

她在墨西哥出生，所以很了解這個國家。

主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞要⁠是被做這⁠件⁠事⁠的人

Being chosen for the team, my parents were very proud.

Being chosen for the team, I felt very proud.

Being given so many presents make me feel shy.

Being given so many presents makes me feel shy.

被做⁠的事用 being 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Inviting by your boss makes you feel noticed.

Being invited by your boss makes you feel noticed.

例句

① Being + 過去分詞當主詞

  1. Small things matter at work. Being invited by your boss to an unexpected lunch makes you feel noticed.
    工作上小事很重要。老闆突然請你吃午餐，會讓你覺得自己被注意到了。
  2. Being chosen for the national team was the best moment of my life.
    被選進國家隊是我人生中最棒的時刻。
  3. Nobody enjoys being told what to do all the time.
    沒有人喜歡一直被別人指揮。
  4. Being stopped by the police can be frightening, even if you've done nothing wrong.
    被警察攔下來可能很可怕，就算你什麼也沒做錯。
  5. Being left alone in a strange city was a new experience for me.
    一個人被留在陌生的城市，對我來說是新的經驗。
  6. My little sister hates being laughed at.
    我妹妹很討厭被人取笑。

② 介系詞後面的 being + 過去分詞

  1. I'm tired of being treated like a child.
    我受夠了被當成小孩。
  2. She left the party early to avoid being asked about her exam results.
    她提早離開派對，以免被問到考試成績。
  3. He's afraid of being seen without his glasses.
    他怕被人看到沒戴眼鏡的樣子。
  4. I don't mind being asked for help. Just send me a message.
    我不介意別人找我幫忙。傳個訊息給我就好。

③ Being + 過去分詞交代原因

  1. Being born and raised in Mexico, she is this country's best representative to the world.
    她在墨西哥出生長大，是這個國家面向世界最好的代表。
  2. Being overworked and badly paid, many nurses are leaving the job.
    因為工作過量又薪水低，很多護理師正在離職。
  3. Being given a second chance, he worked harder than ever.
    得到第二次機會，他比以前更努力。
  4. Being asked to give a speech at short notice, I was very nervous.
    臨時被要求致詞，我很緊張。
  5. Being trained as a nurse, my mum knew exactly what to do after the accident.
    我媽媽受過護理訓練，所以意外發生後她很清楚該怎麼做。

④ 省略 being

  1. Built in 1890, the station is the oldest building in town.
    車站建於 1890 年，是鎮上最古老的建築。
  2. Painted by a local artist, the mural shows the history of our town.
    這幅壁畫是當地一位畫家畫的，畫的是我們鎮的歷史。
  3. Surrounded by mountains, the village is very quiet in winter.
    這個村子被群山環繞，冬天很安靜。
  4. Shocked by the news, she couldn't say a word.
    聽到這個消息，她嚇得說不出話來。

⑤ 在作文裡說明看法

  1. Being protected by law, these animals cannot be hunted.
    這些動物受法律保護，不能獵捕。
  2. Being surrounded by computers all day, young people find it hard to switch off.
    整天被電腦包圍，年輕人很難放鬆下來。
  3. Being paid less than men for the same work is still a problem for many women.
    同樣的工作拿得比男人少，對很多女性來說仍然是個問題。
  4. Being criticised in public can make people lose confidence.
    在大家面前被批評，會讓人失去信心。
  5. Being forced to wear a uniform doesn't help students learn.
    被強迫穿制服並不會幫助學生學習。

⑥ 不同的時間

  1. Being woken up at five every morning by the birds, I soon got used to early starts.
    每天早上五點被鳥吵醒，我很快就習慣早起了。
  2. Being praised by her teacher, Lucy felt proud all day.
    被老師稱讚之後，Lucy 整天都很得意。
  3. Being kept waiting for an hour, the customers started to complain.
    被晾在那裡等了一個小時，客人開始抱怨了。
  4. Being followed by photographers is part of a famous person's life.
    被攝影師跟拍是名人生活的一部分。

⑦ 對話

  1. "Why did you leave the job?" "Being ignored by my manager every day got too much."
    你為什麼辭職？每天被經理忽視，我受不了了。
  2. "How did it feel to win?" "Being given the prize in front of my family was amazing."
    贏的感覺怎樣？在家人面前領獎真的很棒。
  3. "Do you like your new school?" "Yes. Being welcomed by everyone made it easy."
    你喜歡新學校嗎？喜歡。大家都很歡迎我，所以很容易適應。
