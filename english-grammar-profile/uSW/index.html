I will pick you up and arrange everything.

我會去接你，也會把一切安排好。

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞和小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間

I will pick you up and arrange everything.

我會去接你，也會把一切安排好。

pick up 的意思是開車去接。

名⁠詞受⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間或後⁠面

I will pick you up and arrange everything.

名詞在中間

Can you turn the TV off?

你可以把電視關掉嗎？
名詞在後面

Can you turn off the TV?

你可以把電視關掉嗎？

代⁠名⁠詞不⁠放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I will pick you up and arrange everything.

I'll pick up you at the station.

I'll pick you up at the station.

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞用受格

I will pick you up and arrange everything.

Can you wake I up at seven?

Can you wake me up at seven?

丟⁠掉是 throw away，away 不⁠能省⁠略

I will pick you up and arrange everything.

This shirt is dirty. Throw it.

This shirt is dirty. Throw it away.

pick up 要⁠有受⁠詞

I will pick you up and arrange everything.

Your coat is on the floor. Pick up.

Your coat is on the floor. Pick it up.

介⁠系⁠詞動⁠詞⁠的代⁠名⁠詞放⁠在介⁠系⁠詞後⁠面

I lost my keys, and I'm looking for them.

我的鑰匙不見了，我在找。

get up 說自⁠己起⁠床⁠時沒⁠有受⁠詞

I got up at six.

我六點起床。

例句

① pick up

  1. "What time does your train arrive?" "At five." "OK, I'll pick you up."
    你的火車幾點到？五點。好，我去接你。
  2. My dad picks me up from school every day.
    我爸每天去學校接我。
  3. Your bag is on the floor. Pick it up, please.
    你的包包在地上。請撿起來。
  4. Can you pick us up after the party?
    派對結束後你可以來接我們嗎？

② ring up / help out

  1. Ring me up or send me an email if you have any questions.
    有問題的話打電話給我或寄電子郵件給我。
  2. I don't have Tom's number. Can you ring him up?
    我沒有 Tom 的號碼。你可以打給他嗎？
  3. Could you come here by nine o'clock to help me out?
    你可以九點前過來幫我一下嗎？
  4. My neighbours are moving tomorrow, so we're going to help them out.
    我的鄰居明天要搬家，所以我們要去幫忙。

③ throw away / give back

  1. These old shoes are too small. You can throw them away.
    這雙舊鞋太小了。你可以丟掉。
  2. "Is this milk OK?" "No, it's old. Throw it away."
    這牛奶還能喝嗎？不行，過期了。丟掉吧。
  3. That's my pen! Give it back!
    那是我的筆！還給我！
  4. I borrowed two books from the library, and I have to give them back on Friday.
    我從圖書館借了兩本書，星期五要還。

④ put on / take off / try on

  1. "Here's your coat. It's cold outside." "Thanks, Mum. I'll put it on."
    你的外套在這裡。外面很冷。謝謝媽。我會穿上。
  2. "Can I try these jeans on?" "Of course. You can try them on over there."
    我可以試穿這條牛仔褲嗎？當然。你可以在那邊試穿。
  3. Your shoes are wet. Take them off before you come in.
    你的鞋子濕了。進來前先脫掉。
  4. I like this hat. Can I try it on?
    我喜歡這頂帽子。可以試戴嗎？

⑤ turn on / turn off

  1. The heater is broken. I can't turn it on.
    暖氣壞了。我打不開。
  2. "The TV is too loud." "Sorry, I'll turn it off."
    電視太大聲了。抱歉，我把它關掉。
  3. Your phone is ringing. Turn it off, please. We're in a meeting.
    你的手機在響。請關掉。我們在開會。

⑥ wake up

  1. "What time do you want breakfast?" "Wake me up at eight, please."
    你想幾點吃早餐？請八點叫我起床。
  2. The baby is sleeping. Please don't wake her up.
    寶寶在睡覺。請不要吵醒她。
  3. The dog barks every morning and wakes us up.
    那隻狗每天早上都叫，把我們吵醒。

⑦ 受詞是名詞，放在小品詞後面

  1. "It's so hot in here." "Can you turn on the fan?"
    這裡好熱。你可以開電扇嗎？
  2. I'll pick up the children from school today.
    今天我去學校接孩子。
  3. Don't throw away those boxes. We need them for the move.
    不要丟掉那些箱子。搬家要用。
  4. She put on her best dress for the party.
    她穿上最好的洋裝去參加派對。

⑧ 受詞是名詞，放在動詞和小品詞中間

  1. Can you turn the music down? I'm on the phone.
    你可以把音樂調小聲嗎？我在講電話。
  2. Tom picked his sister up at the airport.
    Tom 去機場接他妹妹。
  3. Take your shoes off, please. The floor is clean.
    請把鞋子脫掉。地板是乾淨的。
  4. My brother threw his old guitar away last week.
    我哥哥上星期把他的舊吉他丟掉了。
  5. Two waiters are ill today. Could you help the kitchen staff out?
    今天有兩個服務生生病了。你可以幫廚房的員工一下嗎？
