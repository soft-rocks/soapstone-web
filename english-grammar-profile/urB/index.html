We must all act immediately because the future of humanity may be in danger.

我們所有人都必須馬上行動。

主⁠詞 + must + 副⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

must all、must never、must strongly

We must all act immediately because the future of humanity may be in danger.

must all全都必須。主詞是複數，像 we、you、they。
must never絕對不可以。
must strongly強烈地。常接 recommend、advise、suggest。
must first必須先。

must all

We must all act immediately because the future of humanity may be in danger.

例句

You must all sign the form.

你們每個人都必須簽這張表格。

all 放在 must 後面，指主詞裡的每一個人。

must strongly recommend

例句

I must strongly advise you to see a lawyer.

我必須強烈建議你去找律師。

all 放⁠在 must 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞中⁠間

We must all act immediately because the future of humanity may be in danger.

The river is rising fast. We must go all to the hills tonight.

The river is rising fast. We must all go to the hills tonight.

副⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I must strongly to recommend the boat trip to the islands.

I must strongly recommend the boat trip to the islands.

must all 的主⁠詞要⁠是複⁠數

We must all act immediately because the future of humanity may be in danger.

Our new teacher is strict. He must all arrive on time.

Our new teacher is strict. We must all arrive on time.

例句

① 主詞 + must all + 原形動詞：每個人都必須

  1. The ice is melting and the seas are rising. We must all act immediately because the future of humanity may be in danger.
    冰在融化，海在上升。我們全都必須馬上行動，因為人類的未來可能有危險。
  2. Listen, class. You must all hand in your phones before the exam.
    同學們聽好。考試前你們每個人都必須交出手機。
  3. There's plastic everywhere on the sand. We must all help to keep the beach clean.
    沙灘上到處都是塑膠。我們全都必須幫忙保持海灘乾淨。
  4. Two hundred people are coming to the conference. They must all wear a name badge.
    會議有兩百人要來。他們全都必須戴名牌。
  5. The river is very low after the dry spring. We must all try to use less water this summer.
    春天很乾，河水很低。這個夏天我們全都必須盡量少用水。
  6. You have two hours to look around the town. You must all be back on the bus by four o'clock.
    你們有兩個小時逛小鎮。你們全都必須在四點前回到巴士上。

② 主詞 + must never + 原形動詞：絕對不可以

  1. There is one thing we must never forget: we have to be ourselves.
    有一件事我們絕對不可以忘記：我們必須做自己。
  2. The rescue team hasn't found the climbers yet, but we must never give up hope.
    救難隊還沒找到登山客，可是我們絕對不可以放棄希望。
  3. Two of my friends had a car crash last year. You must never drive after drinking.
    我有兩個朋友去年出了車禍。你喝酒以後絕對不可以開車。
  4. Someone stole money from the till last night. Staff must never leave the shop without locking it.
    昨晚有人偷了收銀機的錢。員工離開商店時絕對不可以不鎖門。
  5. A child nearly drowned in the pool today. We must never let this happen again.
    今天有個孩子差點在泳池溺水。我們絕對不可以讓這種事再發生。
  6. The medical students learn this in their first week: doctors must never share their patients' secrets.
    醫學生第一週就學到：醫生絕對不可以洩露病人的秘密。

③ I must strongly + recommend / advise / suggest

  1. Your guidebook is very useful. In addition, I must strongly recommend that you add something about nightlife.
    你的旅遊書很有用。另外，我必須強烈建議你加一些夜生活的介紹。
  2. Some parts of this contract aren't clear. I must strongly advise you not to sign it yet.
    這份合約有些地方不清楚。我必須強烈建議你先不要簽。
  3. You're visiting our town in June? I must strongly recommend the fish restaurant by the harbour.
    你六月要來我們鎮上？我必須強烈推薦港口邊那家海鮮餐廳。
  4. A hospital visit abroad can cost thousands. I must strongly suggest that you take out travel insurance.
    在國外看一次醫院可能要好幾千元。我必須強烈建議你保旅遊險。
  5. There have been some thefts in the hotel. We must strongly advise all guests to lock their rooms.
    飯店裡發生了幾起竊案。我們必須強烈建議所有客人鎖好房門。
  6. Your son is clearly very tired. I must strongly recommend a few days of rest.
    你兒子顯然很累。我必須強烈建議他休息幾天。

④ 主詞 + must first + 原形動詞：必須先

  1. To get a visa for this country, you must first apply online.
    要拿到這個國家的簽證，你必須先在網路上申請。
  2. Before we discuss the budget, we must first agree on the plan.
    討論預算之前，我們必須先就計畫達成共識。
  3. Welcome to the climbing centre. New members must first attend a safety talk.
    歡迎來到攀岩中心。新會員必須先參加安全說明會。
  4. If you want to rent a flat in London, you must first show proof of income.
    如果你想在倫敦租公寓，你必須先出示收入證明。
  5. Sales of our new phone are low. The company must first find out what customers want.
    我們新手機的銷量很低。公司必須先了解顧客想要什麼。
  6. You want to grow carrots? Before planting, you must first dig the soil.
    你想種紅蘿蔔？種之前，你必須先翻土。

⑤ 主詞 + must certainly / also / therefore + 原形動詞

  1. Customers are leaving because of the long queues. The new manager must certainly deal with this problem.
    顧客因為排隊太久都走了。新經理一定要處理這個問題。
  2. Our supplier has doubled its prices. We must therefore find a new one.
    我們的供應商把價格提高了一倍。因此我們必須找一家新的。
  3. Many young people get into debt with credit cards. Schools must also teach children about money.
    很多年輕人用信用卡欠了債。學校也必須教孩子關於錢的知識。
  4. Visitors can never find a space near the market. The town must therefore build a new car park.
    遊客在市場附近總是找不到車位。因此這個鎮必須蓋一座新的停車場。
  5. The art is amazing in this city. You must certainly visit the museum while you're here.
    這個城市的藝術很精彩。你在這裡的時候一定要去參觀博物館。
  6. The new laws affect students the most. The government must also listen to young people.
    新法律對學生的影響最大。政府也必須傾聽年輕人的聲音。
  7. The theatre lost money again this year. We must therefore raise the price of tickets.
    劇院今年又虧錢了。因此我們必須提高票價。
