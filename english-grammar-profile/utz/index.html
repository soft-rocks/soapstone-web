The teachers are very nice and friendly.

老師們人都很好，也很友善。

and 放⁠在兩⁠個形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間

The teachers are very nice and friendly.

老師們人都很好，也很友善。

兩個形容詞都形容主詞。

兩⁠個形⁠容⁠詞也⁠可⁠以放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面或 look 後⁠面

The teachers are very nice and friendly.

be 後面

My room is small and quiet.

我的房間又小又安靜。
名詞前面

It was a long and difficult day.

那是漫長又辛苦的一天。
look / feel 後面

You look tired and hungry.

你看起來又累又餓。

very 放⁠在第⁠一⁠個形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

The teachers are very nice and friendly.

那片海灘非常乾淨又漂亮。

兩⁠個形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間要加 and

The teachers are very nice and friendly.

The hotel was clean cheap.

The hotel was clean and cheap.

and 後⁠面要接形⁠容⁠詞，不⁠接動⁠詞

The teachers are very nice and friendly.

The soup is hot and taste.

The soup is hot and tasty.

taste 是動詞，形容詞是 tasty。

主⁠詞和形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間要⁠有 be

The teachers are very nice and friendly.

The rooms clean and quiet.

The rooms are clean and quiet.

例句

① 主詞 + be + 形容詞 + and + 形容詞

  1. My room is small and quiet.
    我的房間又小又安靜。
  2. The weather was sunny and warm.
    天氣晴朗又溫暖。
  3. Is the water clean and safe?
    這水乾淨又安全嗎？
  4. Our new car is fast and comfortable.
    我們的新車又快又舒服。
  5. The test was long and difficult.
    那次考試又長又難。
  6. My grandmother is old, but she is strong and healthy.
    我奶奶年紀大了，可是身體強壯又健康。
  7. The cake was soft and sweet.
    那個蛋糕又軟又甜。
  8. These shoes are cheap and light.
    這雙鞋又便宜又輕。
  9. Ken was very kind and patient with me.
    Ken 對我很好，也很有耐心。
  10. The streets were dark and empty.
    街上又暗又沒有人。
  11. Our teacher is strict and fair.
    我們老師很嚴格，也很公平。
  12. The park is big and green.
    那座公園又大又綠。
  13. Her answer was short and clear.
    她的回答簡短又清楚。

② 形容詞 + and + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. It was a long and difficult day.
    那是漫長又辛苦的一天。
  2. She has a warm and friendly smile.
    她的笑容溫暖又親切。
  3. We stayed in a small and quiet hotel.
    我們住在一間小而安靜的旅館。
  4. He gave a short and clear answer.
    他給了一個簡短又清楚的回答。
  5. It's a cheap and easy dinner.
    這是一道便宜又簡單的晚餐。
  6. They live in a large and modern house.
    他們住在一棟又大又現代的房子。

③ 主詞 + look / feel + 形容詞 + and + 形容詞

  1. You look tired and hungry.
    你看起來又累又餓。
  2. I feel happy and relaxed today.
    我今天覺得開心又放鬆。
  3. The room looks clean and bright.
    這個房間看起來乾淨又明亮。
  4. After the run, I felt hot and thirsty.
    跑完步以後，我覺得又熱又渴。
  5. The bread smells fresh and warm.
    這麵包聞起來很新鮮，還熱熱的。
  6. Ann looked surprised and pleased.
    Ann 看起來又驚訝又開心。

④ very / really + 形容詞 + and + 形容詞

  1. The people here are really kind and helpful.
    這裡的人真的很好，也很樂於幫忙。
  2. The beach was very clean and beautiful.
    那片海灘非常乾淨又漂亮。
  3. My new phone is really small and fast.
    我的新手機真的又小又快。
  4. The children were very quiet and polite.
    孩子們很安靜，也很有禮貌。
  5. The museum is really big and interesting.
    那間博物館真的很大，也很有趣。
