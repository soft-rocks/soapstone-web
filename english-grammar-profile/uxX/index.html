例句

① 單數名詞 + is + 過去分詞

  1. Welcome to the team! Our office is situated near the airport, so you can take the train.
    歡迎加入團隊！我們的辦公室在機場附近，你可以搭火車來。
  2. You'll love this hotel. It's situated right next to the beach.
    你會喜歡這間旅館。它就在海灘旁邊。
  3. Our school is very old. The main building is made of stone.
    我們學校很老。主建築是石頭蓋的。
  4. My sister lives in Montreal. French is spoken there, so she's learning it.
    我姊姊住在蒙特婁。那裡說法語，所以她在學。
  5. Don't worry about the bill. Breakfast is included in the price.
    別擔心帳單。早餐含在價錢裡。
  6. My dad has a stressful job, but he's paid very well.
    我爸爸的工作壓力很大，可是薪水很高。

② 複數名詞 + are + 過去分詞

  1. My bedroom is my favourite room. The walls are painted dark blue, and the floor is wood.
    我的臥室是我最喜歡的房間。牆壁漆成深藍色，地板是木頭的。
  2. Don't put the rubbish out tonight. The bins in our street are emptied on Tuesdays.
    今晚不要把垃圾拿出去。我們這條街的垃圾桶星期二才清。
  3. These cakes are delicious. They're made with fresh fruit from our garden.
    這些蛋糕很好吃。它們是用我們院子裡的新鮮水果做的。
  4. You can't miss the shop. The windows are decorated with lights all year.
    你不會錯過那家店。它的窗戶一年到頭都掛著燈。
  5. You don't need to buy a computer. Students at our school are given a laptop in their first year.
    你不用買電腦。我們學校的學生一年級都會拿到一台筆電。
  6. Calculators are allowed in the maths exam, so bring yours tomorrow.
    數學考試可以帶計算機，明天記得帶你的來。

③ I / you / we + am / are + 過去分詞

  1. I'm a waiter at a café. I'm paid every Friday.
    我在咖啡店當服務生。我每個星期五領薪水。
  2. Our grandparents live far away, but we're invited to their house every summer.
    我們的爺爺奶奶住得很遠，可是每年夏天都邀我們去他們家。
  3. I'm not good at sport, so I'm always chosen last for the team.
    我運動不好，所以分隊的時候我總是最後一個被選。
  4. In this job, you're expected to start at eight.
    這份工作要八點上班。
  5. On the course, you're given lunch and a cup of coffee in the afternoon.
    上課的時候，中午有午餐，下午有一杯咖啡。

④ he / she / it / they + is / are + 過去分詞

  1. Our cat is very old now. She's fed three times a day.
    我們的貓現在很老了。她一天吃三餐。
  2. My uncle is a famous chef. He's known all over the country.
    我叔叔是一位有名的廚師。全國的人都知道他。
  3. I love these shoes. They're made in Italy.
    我很喜歡這雙鞋。是義大利做的。
  4. The library is great. It's used by students from all over the city.
    那間圖書館很棒。全市的學生都會去用。
  5. My little brothers are twins. They're often mixed up by their teachers.
    我的弟弟們是雙胞胎。老師常常把他們搞混。

⑤ 規定和安排

  1. We can't go into the zoo yet. The gates are opened at nine.
    我們還不能進動物園。大門九點才開。
  2. I like swimming at my gym. The pool is cleaned every night.
    我喜歡在我的健身房游泳。游泳池每天晚上都會清洗。
  3. There's a sign at the gallery entrance. Visitors are asked to leave their bags at the door.
    美術館入口有一個告示。訪客要把包包留在門口。
  4. Welcome to the online course. Your homework is checked by a teacher every week.
    歡迎參加線上課程。你的功課每個星期都會有老師檢查。

⑥ 問答

  1. "Where's your new flat?" "It's situated above a bakery in the old town."
    你的新公寓在哪裡？在老城區一家麵包店樓上。
  2. "Is service included?" "Yes, it's added to the bill."
    有含服務費嗎？有，加在帳單裡了。
  3. "What's this soup made with?" "It's made with pumpkin and a little cream."
    這個湯是用什麼做的？是南瓜加一點鮮奶油做的。
  4. "How often are the windows cleaned?" "They're cleaned once a month."
    窗戶多久擦一次？一個月一次。
  5. "Do you have to pay for the bus?" "No, students are given a free pass."
    你搭公車要付錢嗎？不用，學生有免費的乘車證。
