My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco.

我的朋友來自波蘭、匈牙利、利比亞和摩洛哥。

主⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面，說句⁠子講⁠的是⁠誰

我的朋友來自波蘭、匈牙利、利比亞和摩洛哥。

主⁠詞可⁠以是人⁠名、名⁠詞片⁠語或複⁠數名⁠詞

My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco.

人名

Anna is my sister.

Anna 是我姊姊。
限定詞 + 名詞

His name is Stanislav.

他的名字是 Stanislav。
+ 形容詞

The new teacher is very nice.

新老師人很好。
複數名詞

Cats sleep a lot.

貓很會睡。

主⁠詞⁠的單複⁠數決⁠定動⁠詞⁠的形⁠式

My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco.

單數主詞

My brother lives in London.

我哥哥住在倫敦。
複數主詞

My parents live in Spain.

我爸媽住在西班牙。

英⁠文⁠的句⁠子要⁠有主⁠詞，說天⁠氣用 it

My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco.

Is very cold today.

It is very cold today.

主⁠詞是單⁠數，現⁠在⁠式動⁠詞加 ⁠-⁠⁠s

My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco.

My sister live in Paris.

My sister lives in Paris.

名⁠詞已⁠經是主⁠詞，不⁠再⁠加 he

My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco.

My father he is a doctor.

My father is a doctor.

例句

① 限定詞 + 名詞

  1. My friends are from Poland, Hungary, Libya and Morocco. We met at language school.
    我的朋友來自波蘭、匈牙利、利比亞和摩洛哥，我們是在語言學校認識的。
  2. His name is Stanislav, and he's from Prague.
    他的名字是 Stanislav，他來自布拉格。
  3. Our flat has a small balcony with lots of plants.
    我們的公寓有一個種滿植物的小陽台。
  4. Our English teacher plays the guitar in a band at weekends.
    我們的英文老師週末會在樂團彈吉他。
  5. This road leads to the old castle on the hill.
    這條路通往山丘上的老城堡。

② 人名

  1. Anna is my best friend.
    Anna 是我最好的朋友。
  2. Tom and Lucy live next door.
    Tom 和 Lucy 住在隔壁。
  3. Mr Brown teaches us maths.
    Brown 老師教我們數學。
  4. Maria has two brothers.
    Maria 有兩個兄弟。

③ 限定詞 + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. The new receptionist speaks four languages.
    新來的櫃台人員會說四種語言。
  2. My little sister goes to school next year.
    我妹妹明年要上學。
  3. A big dog lives in that house.
    那間房子裡住著一隻大狗。
  4. The old church is very beautiful.
    那座老教堂很漂亮。

④ 複數名詞

  1. Cats sleep a lot during the day.
    貓白天睡很多。
  2. My parents live in Spain.
    我爸媽住在西班牙。
  3. The twins next door play the violin every evening.
    隔壁的雙胞胎每天晚上都拉小提琴。
  4. Our neighbours have a swimming pool.
    我們的鄰居有一座游泳池。

⑤ 單數主詞 + 動詞-s

  1. My brother studies medicine, so he's always busy.
    我哥哥讀醫學，所以他總是很忙。
  2. The bakery on the corner sells the best croissants in town.
    轉角那家麵包店賣城裡最好吃的可頌。
  3. My mum works in a hospital.
    我媽媽在醫院工作。
  4. The last bus to the village leaves at half past eleven.
    開往村子的末班車十一點半發車。

⑥ 名詞片語主詞 + be

  1. My favourite colour is green.
    我最喜歡的顏色是綠色。
  2. The water in the lake is too cold for swimming.
    湖水太冷了，不能游泳。
  3. My dad's car is red.
    我爸爸的車是紅色的。
  4. Your new glasses are the same colour as mine.
    你的新眼鏡跟我的顏色一樣。

⑦ 名詞片語主詞 + 過去式

  1. The party was great.
    派對很棒。
  2. My grandparents were farmers.
    我爺爺奶奶以前是農夫。
  3. The train left at six.
    火車六點開走了。
  4. Her brother came to the wedding.
    她哥哥來參加了婚禮。

⑧ 疑問句裡的主詞

  1. Is your sister a student?
    你姊姊是學生嗎？
  2. Excuse me, does this train stop at Oxford?
    不好意思，這班火車有停牛津嗎？
