It would be quite a formal meeting.

那會是一場相當正式的會議。

quite 放⁠在 a 前⁠面，修⁠飾整⁠個名⁠詞片⁠語

那會是一場相當正式的會議。

quite 後⁠面也⁠可⁠以只接 a 加名⁠詞

It would be quite a formal meeting.

得獎真是一大驚喜。

rather 放⁠在 a 前⁠面或後⁠面都⁠可⁠以

a 前面

I live rather a long way from work.

我住的地方離公司滿遠的。
a 後面

She gave a rather short speech.

她的致詞滿短的。

almost、nearly 加名⁠詞片⁠語，表⁠示差⁠一⁠點就⁠是

almost

After all, he is almost a human.

畢竟牠幾乎就是個人了。
nearly

The baby is nearly a year old now.

寶寶現在快一歲了。

quite 放⁠在 a 前⁠面，不⁠放⁠在 a 後⁠面

It would be quite a formal meeting.

It was a quite good film.

It was quite a good film.

very 放⁠在 a 後⁠面，修⁠飾形⁠容⁠詞

It was very a hot day.

It was a very hot day.

單⁠數可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面要⁠有 a

We live rather long way from the station.

We live rather a long way from the station.

表⁠示差⁠一⁠點用 almost，不⁠用 most

It took most an hour.

It took almost an hour.

例句

① quite + a + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. That was quite a difficult exam.
    那次考試相當難。
  2. My aunt runs quite a successful bakery.
    我阿姨開了一家相當成功的麵包店。
  3. We had quite a long wait at the airport.
    我們在機場等了滿久。
  4. It's quite a big house for two people.
    兩個人住，那間房子算滿大的。

② quite + a + 名詞

  1. Winning the prize was quite a surprise.
    得獎真是一大驚喜。
  2. Your little boy is quite a character.
    你家小男孩真是個有個性的孩子。
  3. Moving abroad was quite a challenge for us.
    搬到國外對我們來說是不小的挑戰。
  4. There was quite a crowd outside the shop.
    那家店外面圍了一大群人。

③ rather + a + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. It was rather a strange question.
    那是個滿奇怪的問題。
  2. The plumber left rather a big mess in the kitchen.
    水電工在廚房留下一大堆髒亂。
  3. We had rather a cold summer this year.
    今年夏天滿冷的。
  4. That's rather an expensive restaurant.
    那家餐廳滿貴的。

④ a + rather + 形容詞 + 名詞

  1. She gave a rather short speech.
    她的致詞滿短的。
  2. It was a rather boring party.
    那個派對滿無聊的。
  3. My uncle drives a rather old car.
    我叔叔開一輛滿舊的車。
  4. The bride wore a rather simple dress.
    新娘穿了一件滿簡單的禮服。

⑤ almost、nearly + 名詞片語

  1. I waited almost an hour for the bus.
    我等公車等了快一個小時。
  2. The baby is nearly a year old now.
    寶寶現在快一歲了。
  3. My grandad was almost a professional footballer.
    我爺爺差一點就成了職業足球員。
  4. We've been friends for nearly a decade.
    我們當朋友快十年了。
  5. He drank almost a whole bottle of wine.
    他差不多喝了一整瓶紅酒。
  6. The flight was nearly an hour late.
    班機晚了快一個小時。
  7. Our dog is almost a member of the family.
    我們的狗差不多就是家裡的一份子。
  8. The repairs cost nearly a month's salary.
    修理費差不多是一個月的薪水。

⑥ 疑問句

  1. Isn't it rather a long way to walk?
    走路去不會滿遠的嗎？
  2. Wasn't that quite a risk?
    那樣不是滿冒險的嗎？
  3. Has it been almost a year since we last met?
    我們上次見面已經快一年了嗎？
  4. Isn't that rather a high price for a used bike?
    一台二手腳踏車賣這個價錢不會太高嗎？
  5. Was it quite a big wedding?
    婚禮辦得滿盛大的嗎？
  6. Did the job take almost a week?
    那份工作花了快一個星期嗎？
  7. Wasn't it quite a shock to see him again?
    再見到他不是滿震驚的嗎？
  8. Isn't your son almost a teenager now?
    你兒子不是快十三歲了嗎？
