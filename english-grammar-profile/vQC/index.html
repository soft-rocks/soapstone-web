That is the best game I've ever played.

那是我玩過最棒的一場比賽。

最⁠高⁠級加現⁠在完⁠成⁠式，說⁠到現⁠在最特⁠別⁠的經⁠驗

That is the best game I've ever played.

那是我玩過最棒的一場比賽。

說話者拿這場和這輩子打過的每一場比。

ever 放⁠在 have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞中⁠間

That is the best game I've ever played.

That is the best game I've ever played.

名⁠詞後⁠面可⁠以加 that，意⁠思不⁠變

That is the best game I've ever played.

我看了二十分鐘就走出來了。那是我看過最爛的電影。

the first、the only 後⁠面也常接現⁠在完⁠成⁠式

That is the best game I've ever played.

我在這架飛機上有點緊張。這是我第一次搭飛機。

比⁠較到現⁠在⁠的經⁠驗，用現⁠在完⁠成⁠式

That is the best game I've ever played.

That was the most boring museum my class ever visit.

That was the most boring museum my class has ever visited.

ever 不⁠放⁠在句⁠尾

That is the best game I've ever played.

This is the longest bridge we've driven across ever.

This is the longest bridge we've ever driven across.

最⁠高⁠級加名⁠詞，前⁠面要加 the

That is the best game I've ever played.

That's tallest tree my son has ever climbed.

That's the tallest tree my son has ever climbed.

比⁠較⁠的範⁠圍已⁠經結⁠束，用過⁠去簡⁠單式

That is the best game I've ever played.

我們在蘇格蘭每天都打高爾夫。那是我那趟旅行打得最好的一場。

never 加比⁠較⁠級，意⁠思和最⁠高⁠級加 ever 一⁠樣

That is the best game I've ever played.

我們在最後一秒贏了決賽。我從來沒打過更好的比賽。

in 或 of 說比⁠較⁠的範⁠圍是一⁠個群⁠體

That is the best game I've ever played.

把球傳給里歐。他是隊上最好的球員。

例句

① the + 最高級 + 名詞 + I've ever + 過去分詞

  1. Look at the white sand and the clear water. This is the most beautiful beach I've ever seen.
    你看這白沙和清澈的海水。這是我看過最美的海灘。
  2. The fish was cold and the rice was hard. That was the worst meal I've ever eaten.
    魚是冷的，飯是硬的。那是我吃過最難吃的一餐。
  3. I laughed all the way through it. It's the funniest book I've ever read.
    我從頭笑到尾。這是我讀過最好笑的書。
  4. Your new neighbour brought us soup when we were ill. She's the kindest person I've ever met.
    你的新鄰居在我們生病時送湯來。她是我遇過最善良的人。
  5. I only answered half of the questions. That was the hardest exam I've ever taken.
    我只答了一半的題目。那是我考過最難的考試。
  6. I slept for ten hours at this hotel. This is the most comfortable bed I've ever slept in.
    我在這間飯店睡了十個小時。這是我睡過最舒服的床。
  7. I dreamed that my cat could drive a bus. That's the strangest dream I've ever had.
    我夢到我的貓會開公車。那是我做過最奇怪的夢。

② 主詞是 he / she：has ever

  1. Look at the photo from my dad's trip. It's the biggest fish he's ever caught.
    你看我爸爸這次出遊的照片。這是他釣過最大的魚。
  2. My mum walked thirty kilometres for charity. It was the longest walk she has ever done.
    我媽媽為了慈善活動走了三十公里。那是她走過最長的一段路。
  3. My brother paid two thousand pounds for that camera. This is the most expensive thing he's ever bought.
    我哥哥花了兩千英鎊買那台相機。這是他買過最貴的東西。
  4. My son hasn't put down the new bike all day. That's the best present he has ever had.
    我兒子一整天都離不開那台新腳踏車。那是他收過最好的禮物。
  5. The lake froze for three months. It's the coldest winter my grandmother has ever known.
    湖結冰了三個月。這是我奶奶經歷過最冷的冬天。

③ you / we / they：have ever

  1. We're on the eightieth floor now. Is this the tallest building you've ever been in?
    我們現在在八十樓。這是你進過最高的大樓嗎？
  2. We asked the chef for the recipe. That was the most delicious soup we've ever tasted.
    我們跟主廚要了食譜。那是我們喝過最好喝的湯。
  3. The two teams scored six goals each. It's the most exciting match they've ever played.
    兩隊各進了六球。這是他們踢過最精采的比賽。
  4. We're choosing a film for Halloween. What's the scariest film you've ever watched?
    我們在挑萬聖節要看的電影。你看過最恐怖的電影是哪一部？
  5. We sold three hundred cakes on Saturday. That was the busiest day we've ever had in the shop.
    我們星期六賣了三百個蛋糕。那是我們店裡最忙的一天。

④ 加 that

  1. The cheese on this one is amazing. It's the best pizza that I've ever had.
    這一片的起司超好吃。這是我吃過最好吃的披薩。
  2. Mr Brown explained the problem five times without getting angry. He's the most patient teacher that we've ever had.
    布朗老師把那道題講了五次，一點都沒生氣。他是我們遇過最有耐心的老師。
  3. My sister took a photo from the top of the mountain. That's the most amazing view that she's ever seen.
    我姊姊在山頂拍了一張照片。那是她看過最壯觀的景色。
  4. The wind blew the roof off the school. It was the worst storm that our town has ever had.
    風把學校的屋頂吹掉了。那是我們鎮上遇過最嚴重的暴風雨。

⑤ the first / the only + 現在完成式

  1. I'm so excited to see Tokyo. It's the first time I've been to Japan.
    我好期待看到東京。這是我第一次去日本。
  2. Please be kind about the taste. This is the only cake I've ever baked.
    味道請多包涵。這是我唯一烤過的蛋糕。
  3. We lost our first five games. Today is the first time we've won a game this season.
    我們前五場都輸了。今天是我們這一季第一次贏球。
  4. My aunt plays chess every day. She's the only person who has ever beaten me at chess.
    我阿姨每天下西洋棋。她是唯一下西洋棋贏過我的人。

⑥ never + 比較級

  1. That dog is as big as a horse. I've never seen a bigger dog.
    那隻狗跟馬一樣大。我從來沒看過更大的狗。
  2. The sun was out every day in Greece. We've never had a better holiday.
    我們在希臘每天都是晴天。我們從來沒有過更棒的假期。
  3. The student said a bird ate his homework. The teacher has never heard a worse excuse.
    那個學生說一隻鳥吃了他的作業。老師從來沒聽過更爛的藉口。
  4. Your father paid for everyone's dinner again. I've never met anyone more generous than him.
    你爸爸又請大家吃晚餐了。我從來沒遇過比他更大方的人。
  5. My parents had a sea view and free breakfast. They've never stayed in a nicer hotel.
    我爸媽的房間看得到海，早餐還免費。他們從來沒住過更好的飯店。
