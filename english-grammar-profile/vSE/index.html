In my opinion, our town must clearly use this area to create a park and a playground for children.

這個問題一定還有別的解決辦法。

主⁠詞 + must + 副⁠詞 + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

must certainly / definitely、must clearly、must completely

must certainly / definitely一定必須、一定。
must clearly顯然必須。
must completely完全。常接 disagree、agree、change。
must seriously認真地。常接 think、consider。

推⁠論和義⁠務都⁠可⁠以加副⁠詞

In my opinion, our town must clearly use this area to create a park and a playground for children.

義務

We must seriously consider the cost.

我們必須認真考慮費用。
推論

There must surely be another way.

一定還有別的方法。

must 說義務和推論的時候，都可以加副詞。

副⁠詞放⁠在 must 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞中⁠間，不⁠放⁠在動⁠詞和受⁠詞中⁠間

In my opinion, our town must clearly use this area to create a park and a playground for children.

We must consider seriously the cost.

We must seriously consider the cost.

放⁠在動⁠詞前⁠面要⁠用副⁠詞 clearly

In my opinion, our town must clearly use this area to create a park and a playground for children.

The town must clear use this area.

The town must clearly use this area.

副⁠詞後⁠面直⁠接接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

I must completely to disagree.

I must completely disagree.

例句

① 主詞 + must + clearly / definitely + 原形動詞：意見和建議

  1. Recommendation: in my opinion, our town must clearly use this area to create a park and a playground for children.
    建議：我認為，我們鎮顯然必須用這塊地蓋一座公園和兒童遊樂場。
  2. Three parents complained about bullying this month. The school must clearly do more to stop it.
    這個月有三位家長投訴霸凌。學校顯然必須做更多事來阻止。
  3. The concert is selling fast. We must definitely book the tickets this week.
    音樂會的票賣得很快。我們這個星期一定要訂票。
  4. I waited forty minutes on the phone again. The company must clearly improve its customer service.
    我又在電話上等了四十分鐘。這家公司顯然必須改善客戶服務。
  5. You've had that pain for a month now. You must definitely see a doctor about your knee.
    你膝蓋痛了一個月了。你的膝蓋一定要去看醫生。
  6. Nobody asked us about the new road. The council must clearly listen to local people.
    沒有人問過我們新馬路的事。市議會顯然必須聽聽當地人的意見。

② There + must + certainly / surely + be + 名詞：推論一定有

  1. Building on the park is not the answer. There must certainly be other solutions to the problem of space for new houses.
    在公園上蓋房子不是答案。新房子用地的問題一定還有其他解決辦法。
  2. The train to Edinburgh costs two hundred pounds. There must surely be a cheaper way to travel.
    去愛丁堡的火車要兩百英鎊。一定有比較便宜的旅行方式。
  3. Our car has broken down in this small village. There must certainly be someone who can help us.
    我們的車在這個小村子裡拋錨了。一定有人能幫我們。
  4. We only had two coffees, and they charged us thirty pounds. There must surely be a mistake in the bill.
    我們只喝了兩杯咖啡，他們卻收我們三十英鎊。帳單一定有錯。
  5. Dan hasn't spoken to us for a week. There must certainly be a reason for his silence.
    Dan 一個星期沒跟我們說話了。他不說話一定有原因。
  6. Everyone has to cross the busy road to get on the bus. There must surely be a better place for the bus stop.
    每個人都要穿過車很多的馬路才能搭公車。公車站一定有更好的位置。

③ I must + completely / strongly / partly + agree / disagree

  1. The article says that only doctors and lawyers earn a lot of money. I must completely disagree with this statement.
    那篇文章說只有醫生和律師才賺得到很多錢。我必須完全反對這個說法。
  2. The letter in today's paper asks for more cycle paths. I must completely agree with the writer.
    今天報紙上的投書要求更多自行車道。我必須完全同意寫信的人。
  3. Tom says that homework is a waste of time. I must partly agree with him, but not completely.
    Tom 說回家作業是浪費時間。我必須部分同意他，可是不是完全同意。
  4. The council wants to close our only library. I must strongly disagree with this decision.
    議會想關掉我們唯一的圖書館。我必須強烈反對這個決定。
  5. People often say that teenagers don't work hard. I must completely reject the idea that young people are lazy.
    大家常說青少年不努力。我必須完全否定年輕人很懶的說法。
  6. I didn't check the plans before the building work started. I must partly accept the blame for what happened.
    施工開始前我沒有檢查設計圖。我必須承擔這件事的部分責任。

④ 主詞 + must + seriously / carefully + 原形動詞

  1. Our children have left home, and the bills are high. We must seriously consider moving to a smaller house.
    孩子們都離家了，帳單又很高。我們必須認真考慮搬到小一點的房子。
  2. Many families can't pay their heating bills this winter. The government must seriously think about the cost of energy.
    很多家庭這個冬天付不起暖氣費。政府必須認真考慮能源的費用。
  3. This is a two-year rental agreement. You must carefully read the contract before you sign it.
    這是兩年的租約。你簽約前必須仔細讀合約。
  4. Some teenagers spend eight hours a day on their phones. Parents must seriously think about how much time their children spend online.
    有些青少年一天花八個小時用手機。父母必須認真想一想孩子花多少時間上網。
  5. The bank will read our report on Monday. We must carefully check every figure in it.
    銀行星期一會看我們的報告。我們必須仔細檢查報告裡的每一個數字。
  6. Our chess club has only six members left. The club must seriously look for new members.
    我們的西洋棋社只剩六個會員了。社團必須認真找新會員。

⑤ 主詞 + must + surely / certainly + 原形動詞：推論

  1. Ask Mrs Cole about the grammar question. She must surely know the answer. She's a teacher.
    去問 Cole 太太那個文法問題。她一定知道答案。她是老師。
  2. Mum and Dad left the party two hours ago. They must certainly be home by now.
    爸媽兩個小時前就離開派對了。他們現在一定已經到家了。
  3. You've been at the hospital since six this morning. You must surely be tired.
    你從今天早上六點就在醫院了。你一定很累。
  4. Everybody laughed at the new design. The manager must certainly feel embarrassed now.
    大家都在笑新的設計。經理現在一定很尷尬。
  5. Ben shouted at his best friend in front of everyone. He must surely regret what he said.
    Ben 在大家面前對他最好的朋友大吼。他一定後悔他說的話。
  6. Look at all the people in the queue for the new phone. It must certainly be popular.
    你看排隊買新手機的人。它一定很受歡迎。
  7. Its trunk is four metres wide. This must surely be the oldest tree in the park.
    它的樹幹有四公尺寬。這一定是公園裡最老的樹。
