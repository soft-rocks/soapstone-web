Eat healthy foods rather than fatty foods.

要吃健康的食物，不要吃油膩的食物。

選 A，不⁠選 B，A 和 B 是同⁠一⁠種成⁠分

Eat healthy foods rather than fatty foods.

走樓梯，不要搭電梯。

A 和 B 也⁠可⁠以是介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

Eat healthy foods rather than fatty foods.

I'd like to sit by the window rather than near the door.

我想坐窗邊，不想坐門旁邊。

rather 後⁠面要接 than

Eat healthy foods rather than fatty foods.

Drink water rather juice.

Drink water rather than juice.

Eat healthy foods rather than fatty foods.

We chose the train rather of the plane.

We chose the train rather than the plane.

A 是介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語，B 也⁠要是介⁠系⁠詞⁠片⁠語

Eat healthy foods rather than fatty foods.

Go by train rather than the car.

Go by train rather than by car.

例句

① 名詞片語 + rather than + 名詞片語

  1. The school nurse talked to the parents about lunch boxes. Choose healthy snacks rather than chips.
    學校護理師跟家長談午餐盒的事。選健康的點心，不要選洋芋片。
  2. I ate here last week. Order the soup rather than the salad.
    我上星期在這裡吃過。點湯，別點沙拉。
  3. Petrol is very expensive now. Use your bike rather than the car for short trips.
    現在汽油很貴。短程就騎腳踏車，不要開車。
  4. We wanted to be near the lake. We booked a cabin rather than a hotel room.
    我們想住在湖邊。我們訂了小木屋，沒訂飯店房間。
  5. The dentist looked at my son's teeth. Drink water rather than soda with your meals, she said.
    牙醫看了我兒子的牙齒。她說：吃飯配水，不要配汽水。
  6. My sister wanted to save money. She bought a used car rather than a new one.
    我姊姊想省錢。她買了中古車，沒買新車。

② 說喜好和選擇

  1. Big schools have more clubs and sports. Some parents think it's better to go to a large school rather than a small one.
    大學校有比較多社團和運動。有些家長認為讀規模大的學校比讀小的學校好。
  2. Coffee keeps me awake at night. I'd prefer tea rather than coffee, thanks.
    咖啡會讓我晚上睡不著。我想喝茶，不喝咖啡，謝謝。
  3. My parents are going to Bali for a rest. They want a quiet hotel rather than a big one.
    我爸媽要去峇里島休息。他們想要安靜的飯店，不要大飯店。
  4. It's a lovely sunny day. Let's have a picnic rather than a restaurant meal.
    今天天氣很好。我們去野餐吧，不要上館子。
  5. My girlfriend is on a diet. On Valentine's Day I gave her flowers rather than chocolates.
    我女朋友在節食。情人節我送她花，沒送巧克力。

③ 介系詞片語 + rather than + 介系詞片語

  1. The room is small. Put the sofa against the wall rather than in the middle.
    房間很小。把沙發靠牆放，不要放在中間。
  2. The station is always crowded on Fridays. Let's meet at the café rather than at the station.
    車站星期五總是很擠。我們在咖啡店碰面，不要在車站。
  3. It's too hot inside today. We'll eat in the garden rather than in the kitchen.
    今天屋子裡太熱了。我們在花園吃，不在廚房吃。
  4. The cat keeps eating the leaves. Put the plant on the table rather than on the floor.
    貓一直吃葉子。把植物放在桌上，不要放在地上。
  5. My grandparents keep chickens and grow vegetables. They live in the country rather than in the city.
    我爺爺奶奶養雞，也種菜。他們住在鄉下，不住城市。

④ 時間和方式

  1. I go to bed at nine these days. Call me in the morning rather than at night.
    我最近九點就睡了。早上打給我，不要晚上打。
  2. The taxi driver has no change. Pay by card rather than in cash.
    計程車司機沒有零錢。用卡付，不要付現金。
  3. Japan is very hot in August. Go in spring rather than in summer.
    日本八月很熱。春天去，不要夏天去。
  4. The form must arrive by Friday. Send it by email rather than by post.
    表格星期五前要送到。用電子郵件寄，不要用郵寄。
  5. Your homework will take an hour. Do it now rather than later.
    你的作業要花一個小時。現在就做，不要等以後。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. The airport bus is only five dollars. Why not take the bus rather than a taxi?
    機場巴士只要五塊。何不搭公車，別搭計程車？
  2. We can finish the new kitchen in a week. The problem is the cost rather than the time.
    我們一個星期就可以做好新廚房。問題在於費用，而不是時間。
  3. I'll be fine after the flood, but I'm worried about my mother rather than myself.
    淹水以後我會沒事，可是我擔心的是我媽媽，而不是我自己。
  4. The children tell Miss Wu all their secrets. She's a friend rather than a teacher to them.
    孩子們把所有秘密都告訴吳老師。對他們來說，她比較像朋友，而不是老師。
  5. Everything is low and you can touch it all. The museum is for children rather than adults.
    每樣東西都放得很低，全部都可以摸。那間博物館是給小孩的，不是給大人的。
  6. The subway is closed for repairs today. Should we take the bus rather than the tram?
    地鐵今天停駛維修。我們要搭公車，不搭電車嗎？
  7. Our Chinese partners joined the call. Everyone spoke English rather than Spanish at the meeting.
    我們的中國合作夥伴也加入了通話。大家在會議上說英文，不說西班牙文。
  8. I usually read the news online rather than in the paper.
    我通常在網路上看新聞，不看報紙。
  9. You can't sleep after your evening run. Try running in the morning rather than in the evening.
    你晚上跑完步就睡不著。試試早上跑步，不要晚上跑。
