Do let me know your views on this.

請一定要讓我知道你對這件事的看法。

祈⁠使⁠句前⁠面加 Do，語⁠氣熱⁠情或強⁠調

Do let me know your views on this.

快進來躲雨，把身體擦乾。

邀⁠請、請對⁠方聯⁠絡、強⁠調指⁠示

Do let me know your views on this.

例句

① 邀請和歡迎

  1. Do come and see the garden while the roses are out.
    趁玫瑰開著，一定要來看看花園。
  2. Welcome to our home. Do help yourself to the fruit on the table.
    歡迎來我們家。桌上的水果請自己拿。
  3. Do take a seat. The manager will be with you soon.
    請坐，經理馬上就來。
  4. Do stay the night. The last train has gone.
    留下來過夜吧，最後一班火車已經開走了。
  5. Grandma waved goodbye at the door. Do come again soon!
    奶奶在門口揮手說再見。一定要再來喔！
  6. We loved having you for dinner. Do bring your sister next time.
    很高興你來吃晚餐。下次一定要帶你妹妹來。

② 請對方聯絡

  1. You start in Tokyo next week. Do write and tell me about your new job.
    你下星期在東京開始上班。一定要寫信告訴我你新工作的事。
  2. The new timetable is attached. Do give us your opinion before Friday.
    新的時間表在附件裡。請一定要在星期五前告訴我們你的意見。
  3. Do ring us once you've landed in Sydney.
    你在雪梨降落後，一定要打電話給我們。
  4. Congratulations on your little girl! Do send me some photos of the baby.
    恭喜你生了女兒！一定要傳幾張寶寶的照片給我。
  5. It was lovely to meet you on the course. Do keep in touch.
    很高興在課程上認識你。一定要保持聯絡。

③ 強調指示

  1. This bread is tricky. Do follow the recipe exactly the first time.
    這種麵包很難做。第一次一定要完全照著食譜做。
  2. The landlord wants your answer today. Do read the contract before you sign it.
    房東今天要你的答覆。簽約前一定要先讀合約。
  3. The roads are busy in the city. Do wear a helmet on your bike.
    市區的路車很多。騎腳踏車一定要戴安全帽。
  4. There are two streets with that name. Do check the address before you leave.
    有兩條街是那個名字。出發前一定要確認地址。
  5. There were two break-ins on our street last month. Do lock the door at night.
    上個月我們這條街有兩起闖空門。晚上一定要鎖門。

④ Do be + 形容詞

  1. The path to the bus stop is frozen. Do be careful on the ice.
    去公車站的路結冰了。在冰上一定要小心。
  2. Do be quiet in the library this week. Students have exams.
    這個禮拜在圖書館一定要安靜，學生在考試。
  3. Do be patient with him. He's new.
    對他一定要有耐心，他是新來的。
  4. Do be gentle with the kitten. She's only two weeks old.
    對小貓一定要溫柔，牠才兩個禮拜大。

⑤ 正式的信件和不同的句型

  1. The bank sent me a letter about my new card. Do contact us if you have any questions.
    銀行寄給我一封關於新卡的信。如有任何問題，請務必與我們聯絡。
  2. The receptionist gave me my room key. Do enjoy your stay at our hotel.
    櫃台人員給了我房間鑰匙。祝您在本飯店住得愉快。
  3. Do try the fish. It's delicious.
    一定要嚐嚐這道魚，很好吃。
  4. Call me tonight. Do tell me how the job interview went.
    今晚打給我。一定要告訴我面試的結果。
  5. Edinburgh has a lot to see. Do visit the castle if you have time.
    愛丁堡有很多東西可以看。如果有時間，一定要去參觀那座城堡。
  6. Our chef has changed the dishes for spring. Do have a look at the new menu.
    我們的主廚換了春季的菜。請一定要看看新的菜單。
  7. Do remember to water my plants while I'm away.
    我不在的時候，一定要記得幫我澆花。
  8. Our wedding is on 12 June. Do let us know if you can come.
    我們的婚禮在六月十二日。請一定要告訴我們你能不能來。
  9. It's your first week here. Do feel free to ask for help.
    這是你在這裡的第一個星期。有需要請儘管開口。
  10. I've been in hospital for two weeks. Do forgive me for the late reply.
    我住院了兩個星期。請一定原諒我這麼晚才回覆。
