have been 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing，說反⁠覆到現⁠在⁠的活⁠動

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

我最近很常看電視，尤其是體育節目。

說話者扭傷了腳，在家休息兩個禮拜。

常加⁠的頻⁠率和時⁠間⁠的說⁠法

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

次數a lot、often、every day、twice a week
一段時間for two weeks、for months
起點since January、since I moved here
最近lately、recently

從動⁠詞看⁠出活⁠動反⁠覆發⁠生

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

我哥哥想練好身材。他上健身房上了兩個禮拜了。

since 持⁠續到現⁠在，不⁠用現⁠在進⁠行⁠式

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

My dad is playing golf every weekend since May.

My dad has been playing golf every weekend since May.

說⁠出確⁠切次⁠數，用現⁠在完⁠成簡⁠單式

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

I've been calling the bank three times today.

I've called the bank three times today.

been 不⁠能漏⁠掉

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

My daughter has going to swimming lessons since March.

My daughter has been going to swimming lessons since March.

反⁠覆⁠的活⁠動和單⁠一持⁠續⁠的活⁠動

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

很多次

I feel much fitter. I've been swimming every morning this month.

我覺得身體好多了。這個月我每天早上都去游泳。
一次持續

My arms are tired. I've been swimming for an hour.

我的手臂好累。我游了一個小時了。

現⁠在簡⁠單式只⁠說現⁠在⁠的習⁠慣

I've been watching TV a lot, especially the sports programs.

現在完成進行式

My legs are getting stronger. I've been cycling to work since the train strike started.

我的腿越來越有力了。從火車罷工以後，我就一直騎腳踏車上班。
現在簡單式

I don't need a parking space. I cycle to work.

我不需要停車位。我騎腳踏車上班。

例句

① 加次數的說法

  1. My commute is an hour long. I've been listening to a lot of podcasts on the way to work.
    我通勤要一個小時。我最近上班路上常聽 podcast。
  2. We've been eating out a lot because the kitchen is being repaired.
    廚房在整修，所以我們最近常在外面吃。
  3. My grandmother is lonely. She's been calling me every night.
    我奶奶很寂寞。她每天晚上都打電話給我。
  4. I can't sleep at night. I've been drinking too much coffee.
    我晚上睡不著。我最近咖啡喝太多了。
  5. We can't sleep on Saturdays. The neighbours have been having parties every weekend.
    我們星期六都睡不著。鄰居每個週末都在開派對。
  6. My boyfriend is in Japan this week. He's been sending me messages all day.
    我男朋友這個禮拜在日本。他一整天都一直傳訊息給我。

② 加 for + 一段時間

  1. My cousin looks much stronger. He has been lifting weights for a year.
    我表哥看起來壯多了。他練舉重練了一年了。
  2. My back feels much better. I've been taking yoga classes for six months.
    我的背好多了。我上瑜伽課上了半年了。
  3. My cousins look tired. They've been visiting their grandmother in hospital for weeks.
    我的表弟妹看起來很累。他們去醫院看奶奶看了好幾個禮拜了。
  4. My husband and I love the club. We've been playing tennis on Sundays for years.
    我和我先生很愛那個俱樂部。我們每個星期天打網球，打了好幾年了。
  5. My sister is going to see a doctor. She's been having headaches for a month.
    我姊姊要去看醫生。她頭痛一個月了，常常發作。

③ 加 since + 起點

  1. I've lost five kilos. I've been walking to school since the bus route changed.
    我瘦了五公斤。從公車路線改了以後，我就一直走路上學。
  2. He hardly talks to us now. My son has been playing his new game since Christmas.
    他現在幾乎不跟我們說話。我兒子從聖誕節以後就一直在玩他的新遊戲。
  3. My mum has a new hobby. She's been going to dance classes since March.
    我媽媽有了新嗜好。她從三月開始上舞蹈課。
  4. Nobody says anything on the line. We've been getting strange phone calls since Monday.
    電話那頭都沒人說話。我們從星期一開始一直接到奇怪的電話。
  5. My fingers hurt. I've been practising the guitar every day since I bought it.
    我的手指好痛。我買了吉他以後每天都在練。

④ 加 lately / recently

  1. You look very tired. Have you been sleeping badly lately?
    你看起來很累。你最近常睡不好嗎？
  2. We have a big project at the office. I've been working late a lot recently.
    我們公司有個大案子。我最近常常加班。
  3. The teacher is worried. Tom's been missing classes lately.
    老師很擔心。湯姆最近常常翹課。
  4. Yes, Jo and I are friends again. We've been seeing a lot of each other recently.
    對，我和喬又和好了。我們最近常見面。
  5. Leave home early. The trains have been running late lately.
    早點出門。火車最近常常誤點。

⑤ 從動詞看出活動反覆發生

  1. I'm going to put my name on it. Someone has been taking my lunch from the office fridge.
    我要在上面寫名字。有人一直拿走我放在辦公室冰箱的午餐。
  2. The postman is scared of our house. My dog has been barking at him.
    郵差很怕我們家。我的狗一直對他叫。
  3. I know that journalist's name. I've been reading his articles in the local paper.
    我知道那個記者的名字。我一直有在看他登在地方報紙上的文章。
  4. I'm angry with my sister. She's been borrowing my car without asking.
    我在生我妹妹的氣。她一直沒問我就借走我的車。
  5. The election is next week. The candidates have been knocking on doors to ask for votes.
    選舉在下禮拜。候選人挨家挨戶敲門拉票。

⑥ 問句

  1. You look so relaxed. What have you been doing in the evenings?
    你看起來好放鬆。你最近晚上都在做什麼？
  2. You look fitter. Have you been running every morning?
    你看起來更健康了。你每天早上都有去跑步嗎？
  3. Your grandson is so kind. How often has he been visiting you?
    你孫子真貼心。他多常來看你？
  4. You look exhausted. Has the baby been waking up at night?
    你看起來累壞了。寶寶晚上常常醒來嗎？
  5. These tomatoes taste great. Where have you been buying your vegetables?
    這些番茄好好吃。你最近都在哪裡買菜？
