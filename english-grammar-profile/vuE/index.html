What others say doesn't matter in the least.

別人說什麼一點都不重要。

in the least 放⁠在否⁠定⁠句⁠裡，表⁠示一⁠點⁠也⁠不

別人說什麼一點都不重要。

放⁠在句⁠尾，或放⁠在 not 和形⁠容⁠詞中⁠間

What others say doesn't matter in the least.

句尾

The kids weren't scared in the least.

孩子們一點也不害怕。
not 和形容詞中間

The kids weren't in the least scared.

孩子們一點都不害怕。

Not in the least 可⁠以單⁠獨回⁠答問⁠題

What others say doesn't matter in the least.

「你介意我坐這裡嗎？」「一點也不介意。」

in the least 只⁠用在否⁠定⁠句

What others say doesn't matter in the least.

My parents were happy in the least about my plan.

My parents weren't happy in the least about my plan.

in the least 要⁠有 the

What others say doesn't matter in the least.

The delay doesn't matter in least.

The delay doesn't matter in the least.

加⁠強否⁠定用 in the least，不⁠用 at the least

What others say doesn't matter in the least.

I wasn't surprised at the least.

I wasn't surprised in the least.

at least 的意思是至少。

例句

① 否定的動詞 + in the least（句尾）

  1. My friends think my haircut is strange, but it doesn't bother me in the least.
    朋友覺得我的髮型很怪，可是我一點也不在意。
  2. She failed her driving test, but that didn't discourage her in the least.
    她駕照沒考過，可是這一點也沒讓她灰心。
  3. The long wait didn't bother me in the least. I had a good book.
    等了那麼久，我一點也不在意。我有一本好書可以看。
  4. Dad was in a bad mood, but it didn't affect the party in the least.
    爸爸心情不好，可是一點也沒影響派對。
  5. It rained all afternoon, but that didn't spoil our day in the least.
    下了一整個下午的雨，可是一點也沒破壞我們這一天。
  6. I don't mind waiting in the least. Take your time.
    我一點也不介意等。慢慢來。

② be + not + in the least + 形容詞

  1. Many television bosses are not in the least interested in quality. They only care about viewers.
    很多電視台的主管一點也不在乎品質。他們只在乎收視率。
  2. I'm not in the least tired. Let's keep walking.
    我一點也不累。我們繼續走吧。
  3. He wasn't in the least surprised when she said no.
    她拒絕的時候，他一點也不意外。
  4. The neighbours' dog is huge, but the children were not in the least afraid of it.
    鄰居的狗很大，可是孩子們一點也不怕牠。
  5. Don't worry about next year. The exam wasn't in the least difficult.
    別擔心明年的事。考試一點也不難。
  6. I'm not in the least worried about the interview.
    我一點也不擔心面試。

③ be + not + 形容詞 + in the least

  1. She wasn't worried in the least about the trip.
    她對這趟旅行一點也不擔心。
  2. We asked for directions, but the hotel staff weren't helpful in the least.
    我們問路，可是飯店的員工一點也不幫忙。
  3. I'm not angry in the least. I just want to know what happened.
    我一點也不生氣。我只是想知道發生了什麼事。
  4. We paid a lot for it, but the new sofa isn't comfortable in the least.
    我們花了很多錢，可是新沙發一點也不舒服。

④ Not in the least：簡答

  1. "Do you mind if I bring my dog?" "Not in the least."
    你介意我帶狗來嗎？一點也不介意。
  2. "Were you nervous before the speech?" "Not in the least."
    你演講前會緊張嗎？一點也不會。
  3. "Am I disturbing you?" "Not in the least. Come in."
    我打擾到你了嗎？一點也沒有。進來吧。
  4. "Are you disappointed with the result?" "Not in the least. We did our best."
    你對結果失望嗎？一點也不。我們盡力了。

⑤ 否定的動詞 + 受詞 + in the least：對人沒有影響

  1. I live on a busy road, but the noise doesn't disturb me in the least.
    我住在大馬路邊，可是噪音一點也不會吵到我。
  2. She criticised his cooking, but her comments didn't upset him in the least.
    她批評他煮的菜，可是她的評論一點也沒讓他難過。
  3. The price doesn't interest me in the least. I just want good quality.
    我一點也不在乎價錢。我只要品質好。
  4. It was below zero, but the cold didn't stop the runners in the least.
    氣溫在零度以下，可是寒冷一點也沒擋住跑者。

⑥ 正式的寫作

  1. The new rules do not in the least change the rights of employees.
    新規定一點也不會改變員工的權利。
  2. Sales fell after the price rise. The results were not in the least surprising.
    漲價後銷量下降了。這些結果一點也不令人意外。
  3. Hundreds of customers wrote in, but the company was not in the least concerned about the complaints.
    好幾百位顧客寫信來，可是那家公司一點也不在意這些投訴。
  4. This does not in the least mean that the project has failed.
    這一點也不表示這個計畫失敗了。

⑦ 其他動詞 + in the least

  1. The train was an hour late, but my boss didn't care in the least.
    火車晚了一個小時，可是我老闆一點也不在意。
  2. I don't care in the least what he thinks.
    他怎麼想，我一點也不在乎。
  3. My grandmother didn't seem in the least tired after the long flight.
    長途飛行之後，我奶奶看起來一點也不累。
