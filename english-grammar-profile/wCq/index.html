I look forward to hearing from you.

期待你的回信。

look forward to 後⁠面⁠接主⁠詞期⁠待⁠的事

期待你的回信。

例句

① 信和電子郵件的結尾：look forward to + 動詞-ing

  1. Thank you for your application. We look forward to meeting you on Friday.
    謝謝您的申請。我們期待星期五和您見面。
  2. Please send me the price list. I look forward to hearing from you.
    請把價目表寄給我。期待您的回覆。
  3. Thanks for inviting me to speak. I look forward to seeing you all in May.
    謝謝你們邀請我演講。期待五月和大家見面。
  4. The hotel wrote: "We look forward to welcoming you next week."
    飯店寫道：我們期待下週歡迎您光臨。
  5. Dear Ms Lee, I have attached my CV. I look forward to discussing the job with you.
    李小姐您好，我附上了我的履歷。期待和您談談這份工作。
  6. I hope my answers are pertinent to your study. I look forward to reading the report.
    希望我的回答對您的研究有用。期待讀到那份報告。
  7. Thanks again for your help. I look forward to working with you.
    再次謝謝您的幫忙。期待和您合作。

② 信和電子郵件的結尾：look forward to + 名詞

  1. The meeting is at ten in Room 4. I look forward to our talk.
    會議十點在四號會議室。期待我們的談話。
  2. Thank you for the offer. I look forward to your reply.
    謝謝您提供這個機會。期待您的回覆。
  3. Your order will arrive on Monday. We look forward to your next visit.
    您的訂單星期一會送到。期待您下次光臨。
  4. The tickets are in this email. We look forward to the show.
    票在這封信裡。我們期待這場表演。

③ 跟朋友說話：be looking forward to + 名詞

  1. "Are you going to Tom's party?" "Yes, I'm really looking forward to it."
    你要去 Tom 的派對嗎？要啊，我很期待。
  2. My kids are looking forward to the summer holidays.
    我的孩子們很期待暑假。
  3. We've booked the flights, and we're looking forward to the trip.
    我們訂好機票了，很期待這趟旅行。
  4. "How's work?" "Busy. I'm looking forward to the weekend."
    工作怎麼樣？很忙。我很期待週末。
  5. My grandma is looking forward to her birthday dinner.
    我奶奶很期待她的生日晚餐。

④ 跟朋友說話：be looking forward to + 動詞-ing

  1. "See you on Saturday!" "Great, I'm looking forward to seeing you!"
    星期六見！太好了，好期待見到你！
  2. She's looking forward to starting her new job.
    她很期待開始新工作。
  3. We're looking forward to trying that new Thai restaurant.
    我們很期待去吃那家新開的泰國餐廳。
  4. My brother is looking forward to having his own room.
    我弟弟很期待有自己的房間。
  5. "Is your sister coming home?" "Yes, and we're looking forward to spending Christmas together."
    你姊姊要回家嗎？要，我們很期待一起過聖誕節。

⑤ 過去：was looking forward to

  1. I was looking forward to the match, but it rained all day.
    我本來很期待那場比賽，可是下了一整天的雨。
  2. The children were looking forward to going to the zoo, so they were sad when it closed early.
    孩子們本來很期待去動物園，所以動物園提早關門讓他們很難過。
  3. He was looking forward to meeting her parents, but they cancelled the dinner.
    他本來很期待見她的父母，可是他們取消了晚餐。

⑥ 否定和問句

  1. I'm not looking forward to the exam on Monday.
    我一點都不期待星期一的考試。
  2. "Do you have to go to the dentist?" "Yes, and I'm not looking forward to it."
    你得去看牙醫嗎？對，我一點都不期待。
  3. He isn't looking forward to telling his boss about the mistake.
    他一點都不想跟老闆說那個錯誤。
  4. "Are you looking forward to the wedding?" "Yes, very much!"
    你期待那場婚禮嗎？很期待！
  5. "What are you looking forward to most this year?" "Moving to a bigger flat."
    你今年最期待什麼？搬到大一點的公寓。

⑦ 第三人稱單數：looks forward to

  1. My dad looks forward to his morning coffee every day.
    我爸每天都很期待早上那杯咖啡。
  2. Our teacher says she looks forward to reading our stories.
    我們老師說她很期待讀我們寫的故事。
  3. Every year Anna looks forward to the music festival.
    Anna 每年都很期待那個音樂節。
