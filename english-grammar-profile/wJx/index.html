We would suggest that our management takes a closer look.

我們建議我們的管理階層再仔細看一看。

主⁠詞 + suggest / recommend / insist / ask / propose + that + 主⁠詞 + 現⁠在簡⁠單式

suggest / recommend / propose、insist / ask / demand、現⁠在簡⁠單式

suggest / recommend / propose建議。
insist / ask / demand要求。
現在簡單式英式英文常用。主詞是 he、she、it 時，動詞加 -⁠s。
原形動詞正式的寫法。不論主詞是什麼，都用原形動詞，叫做假設語氣。

現⁠在簡⁠單式和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We would suggest that our management takes a closer look.

現在簡單式

I suggest that he takes the train.

我建議他搭火車。
原形動詞

I suggest that he take the train.

我建議他改搭火車。

兩種寫法意思一樣。主詞是 I、we、you、they 時，兩種寫法看起來一樣。

would suggest / would recommend

We would suggest that our management takes a closer look.

例句

We would recommend that you book early.

我們建議您提早訂位。

would 讓建議聽起來比較客氣。

suggest 後⁠面不⁠接「受⁠詞 + to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞」

We would suggest that our management takes a closer look.

I suggest you to take the train.

I suggest that you take the train.

recommend 後⁠面不⁠直⁠接接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

We would suggest that our management takes a closer look.

I recommend to go to the theatre.

I recommend that we go to the theatre.

子⁠句⁠裡要⁠有完⁠整⁠的動⁠詞，主⁠詞後⁠面不⁠直⁠接接動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I recommend that we booking the tickets today.

I recommend that we book the tickets today.

例句

① suggest + that + 主詞 + 現在簡單式

  1. We suggest that the company reviews its prices.
    我們建議公司重新檢討價格。
  2. I suggest that she talks to her teacher about it.
    我建議她跟老師談談這件事。
  3. The report suggests that the council builds more cycle lanes.
    報告建議市議會建更多自行車道。
  4. I'd suggest that he finds a cheaper flat.
    我會建議他找一間便宜一點的公寓。
  5. We would suggest that the hotel improves its breakfast.
    我們建議飯店改善早餐。
  6. The doctor suggests that my father walks for half an hour every day.
    醫生建議我爸每天走路半小時。
  7. I suggest that we meet again next week.
    我建議我們下星期再見面。

② recommend / propose + that + 主詞 + 現在簡單式

  1. I recommend that we go to the theatre in Ginza where Miss Saigon is on.
    我建議我們去銀座那家正在演《西貢小姐》的劇院。
  2. We would recommend that every student reads the guide before the trip.
    我們建議每個學生在旅行前讀一下手冊。
  3. The committee proposes that the club opens on Sundays.
    委員會提議俱樂部星期天也開放。
  4. I recommend that you check the timetable before you leave.
    我建議你出發前先查時刻表。
  5. The bank recommends that customers change their passwords regularly.
    銀行建議客戶定期更改密碼。
  6. I propose that we vote on it now.
    我提議我們現在就投票。
  7. The manager recommends that the team takes a short break.
    經理建議團隊休息一下。

③ insist / ask / demand + that + 主詞 + 現在簡單式

  1. We insist that the agreement between you is legally binding.
    我們堅持你們之間的協議要有法律效力。
  2. My mother insists that I phone her every Sunday.
    我媽堅持要我每個星期天打電話給她。
  3. The landlord asks that tenants keep the stairs clear.
    房東要求房客保持樓梯通暢。
  4. The workers demand that the factory pays them for the extra hours.
    工人要求工廠支付加班費。
  5. She insists that everyone takes their shoes off at the door.
    她堅持每個人都要在門口脫鞋。
  6. The school asks that parents collect their children by four.
    學校要求家長四點前接孩子。
  7. The customers demand that the airline gives them a full refund.
    顧客要求航空公司全額退款。
  8. He insists that his son studies law.
    他堅持要他兒子念法律。

④ 正式的寫法：that + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. The committee recommends that the plan be accepted.
    委員會建議接受這個計畫。（被動）
  2. I suggest that he take a few days off.
    我建議他休幾天假。
  3. The law requires that every driver wear a seat belt.
    法律規定每個駕駛都要繫安全帶。
  4. We propose that the meeting start at nine.
    我們提議會議九點開始。
  5. She insisted that her name be removed from the list.
    她堅持要把她的名字從名單上刪掉。（被動）
  6. The judge ordered that the man pay a fine.
    法官命令那個男人繳罰款。
  7. It is essential that every child have a place at school.
    每個孩子都必須有學校可以念。
  8. The doctor advised that she not eat any sugar.
    醫生建議她不要吃糖。
  9. They demanded that the government act at once.
    他們要求政府馬上行動。
