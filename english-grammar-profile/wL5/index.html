例句

① biggest

  1. Elephants are the biggest animals on land.
    大象是陸地上最大的動物。
  2. We booked the biggest room in the hotel for the family.
    我們幫全家訂了飯店裡最大的房間。
  3. What's the biggest city in Australia?
    澳洲最大的城市是哪一個？
  4. This is the biggest pizza I've ever seen!
    這是我看過最大的披薩！
  5. Their biggest problem is money.
    他們最大的問題是錢。
  6. The biggest fish was about a meter long.
    最大的那條魚大約一公尺長。

② hottest

  1. July is the hottest month here.
    七月是這裡最熱的月份。
  2. The news says tomorrow will be the hottest day so far.
    新聞說明天會是到目前為止最熱的一天。
  3. Be careful. That's the hottest pan.
    小心，那是最燙的鍋子。
  4. Kaohsiung is one of the hottest cities in Taiwan.
    高雄是台灣最熱的城市之一。
  5. Sit in the shade. This is the hottest spot in the garden.
    坐在陰影下吧，這裡是花園裡最熱的地方。

③ thinnest / fattest

  1. This is the thinnest phone in the shop.
    這是店裡最薄的手機。
  2. Cut the bread into the thinnest slices you can.
    把麵包切成你能切的最薄片。
  3. He was the thinnest boy in my class.
    他是我們班上最瘦的男生。
  4. Our cat is the fattest cat on the street.
    我們家的貓是這條街上最胖的貓。
  5. The fattest pig won a prize at the fair.
    最肥的那隻豬在市集上得了獎。

④ wettest / saddest

  1. November is the wettest month in Keelung.
    十一月是基隆最多雨的月份。
  2. That was the wettest summer in years.
    那是好幾年來最多雨的夏天。
  3. It's the saddest movie I've ever watched.
    那是我看過最悲傷的電影。
  4. The day my dog died was the saddest day of my life.
    我的狗死掉的那天是我這輩子最難過的一天。
  5. That was the saddest song on the album.
    那是這張專輯裡最悲傷的歌。

⑤ 兩個最高級和不同的句型

  1. Rome has some of the biggest and oldest churches in Europe.
    羅馬有一些歐洲最大也最古老的教堂。
  2. This is the hottest and driest part of the country.
    這是這個國家最熱也最乾的地區。
  3. Who is the fittest player on the team?
    隊上體能最好的球員是誰？
  4. That's the reddest apple in the box.
    那是箱子裡最紅的蘋果。
  5. We sat in the hottest part of the stadium.
    我們坐在體育場最熱的區域。
  6. Is this the biggest size you have?
    這是你們最大的尺寸嗎？
  7. It was the wettest and coldest week of the trip.
    那是這趟旅行最濕最冷的一週。
  8. The fattest book on the shelf is a dictionary.
    架子上最厚的書是一本字典。
  9. This is the flattest road for cycling.
    這是最平、最適合騎腳踏車的路。
