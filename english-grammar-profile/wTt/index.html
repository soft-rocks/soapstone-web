I want to see you on Saturday and Sunday.

我想在星期六和星期天見你。

星⁠期⁠的名⁠字在句中⁠也大⁠寫開⁠頭

我想在星期六和星期天見你。

人⁠名、地⁠名、星⁠期、月⁠份、語⁠言和節⁠日都大⁠寫

I want to see you on Saturday and Sunday.

人名Jessica, Mr Smith, Dr Lee
地名Leicester, Japan, Hamilton
星期和月份Monday, Friday, March, December
語言和國籍English, French, Chinese
節日Christmas, New Year

城⁠市和月⁠份前⁠面通⁠常不⁠加 a 或 the

I want to see you on Saturday and Sunday.

城市

I live in Leicester.

我住在萊斯特。
月份

My birthday is in May.

我的生日在五月。

有些名字本身帶 the，像 the British Museum。

星⁠期和月⁠份⁠的名⁠字大⁠寫開⁠頭

See you on monday.

See you on Monday.

She speaks english and french.

She speaks English and French.

季⁠節不⁠是專⁠有⁠名⁠詞，小⁠寫開⁠頭

I love Summer.

I love summer.

例句

① 星期

  1. I'm busy on Friday, but I want to see you on Saturday and Sunday.
    我星期五很忙，可是我想在星期六和星期天見你。
  2. The art classes after school are on Tuesdays.
    放學後的美術課在星期二。
  3. Sorry, the dentist can't see you until Thursday afternoon.
    抱歉，牙醫要到星期四下午才能幫你看診。
  4. Don't go to the baker's tomorrow. It never opens on Wednesdays.
    明天別去麵包店，它星期三都不開。

② 月份和節日

  1. I'm having a party on Friday 25 December.
    我十二月二十五日星期五要開派對。
  2. We always go skiing in February.
    我們每年二月都去滑雪。
  3. What are you doing for Christmas this year?
    你今年聖誕節要做什麼？
  4. School finishes in July.
    學校七月放假。

③ 人名

  1. In my class there's a girl called Maria.
    我們班有一個叫 Maria 的女生。
  2. Mr Brown is our new maths teacher.
    Brown 先生是我們的新數學老師。
  3. Please give this letter to Dr Patel.
    請把這封信交給 Patel 醫生。
  4. Have you met Ahmed? He's from Egypt.
    你見過 Ahmed 嗎？他是埃及人。

④ 城市、國家和地區

  1. In Leicester I live in an area called Hamilton.
    我在萊斯特住在一個叫漢彌爾頓的地區。
  2. My parents are from Brazil, but I grew up in Canada.
    我爸媽是巴西人，可是我在加拿大長大。
  3. The kids are excited because we're flying to Italy next week.
    孩子們好興奮，因為我們下禮拜要飛去義大利。
  4. Is Manchester far from London?
    曼徹斯特離倫敦遠嗎？

⑤ 語言和國籍

  1. She speaks English, Spanish and a little Japanese.
    她會說英文、西班牙文和一點日文。
  2. My husband is Scottish, so we spend New Year in Edinburgh.
    我先生是蘇格蘭人，所以我們都在愛丁堡過新年。
  3. I'm learning French at evening classes.
    我在上法文夜間課。
  4. There's a new Chinese restaurant opposite the station.
    車站對面新開了一家中國餐廳。

⑥ 學校、公司和建築

  1. This place is Gloscat College in Gloucester.
    這裡是格洛斯特的 Gloscat 學院。
  2. After the interview, my brother got a job at Google.
    面試完以後，我哥哥拿到了 Google 的工作。
  3. We visited the British Museum on Sunday.
    我們星期天去了大英博物館。
  4. Meet me outside the Odeon cinema at seven.
    七點在 Odeon 電影院外面跟我碰面。

⑦ 專有名詞和普通名詞

  1. My favourite season is summer, but my favourite month is June.
    我最喜歡的季節是夏天，可是我最喜歡的月份是六月。
  2. My uncle lives in a small town in Wales.
    我舅舅住在威爾斯的一個小鎮。
  3. Our teacher, Mrs Hill, comes from Ireland.
    我們的老師 Hill 女士來自愛爾蘭。
  4. There's a famous river in Paris called the Seine.
    巴黎有一條有名的河叫塞納河。

⑧ 專有名詞前面不加 the

  1. My grandparents flew to Portugal for their anniversary.
    我爺爺奶奶飛去葡萄牙慶祝結婚紀念日。
  2. After university, my sister got a job in Tokyo.
    大學畢業後，我姊姊在東京找到工作。
  3. We arrived in Berlin on Thursday.
    我們星期四抵達柏林。
