As you may have noticed, there has been a delay setting up the new computer system.

我想，那次意外的事他可能已經原諒我了。

主⁠詞 + may have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

may have、過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

As you may have noticed, there has been a delay setting up the new computer system.

may have可能已經。
過去分詞過去可能發生的事，像 noticed、seen、forgotten、left。

否⁠定：may not have

例句

She may not have heard you.

她可能沒聽到你說話。

may not have 加過去分詞，說過去某件事可能沒有發生。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞，go 的過⁠去⁠分⁠詞是 gone

As you may have noticed, there has been a delay setting up the new computer system.

He may have went home.

He may have gone home.

may 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 have

As you may have noticed, there has been a delay setting up the new computer system.

She may has forgotten.

She may have forgotten.

說過⁠去⁠的可⁠能用 may have 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

As you may have noticed, there has been a delay setting up the new computer system.

They may missed the train.

They may have missed the train.

例句

① as you may have + 過去分詞：對方可能已經知道

  1. As you may have noticed, the lift is out of order again.
    大家可能已經注意到，電梯又故障了。
  2. As you may have heard, our manager is leaving at the end of the month.
    大家可能已經聽說了，我們經理月底要離職。
  3. As you may have seen in the news, the bridge will be closed for a week.
    你可能已經在新聞上看到了，那座橋會封閉一個星期。（被動）
  4. As you may have guessed, the party is a surprise for Mum.
    你可能已經猜到了，這個派對是給媽媽的驚喜。
  5. As you may have read, the school is going to build a new gym.
    你可能已經讀到了，學校要蓋一座新的體育館。

② 主詞 + may have + 過去分詞：猜過去發生的事

  1. He smiled at me today. I think he may have forgiven me for that accident!
    他今天對我笑了。我想他可能已經原諒我那次出的意外了！
  2. I can't find my keys. I may have left them at the office.
    我找不到鑰匙。我可能把它們留在辦公室了。
  3. She isn't answering. She may have gone to bed.
    她沒接電話。她可能已經睡了。
  4. The road is wet. It may have rained in the night.
    路是濕的。夜裡可能下過雨。
  5. They're late. They may have missed the train.
    他們遲到了。他們可能錯過了火車。
  6. He looks happy. He may have got the job.
    他看起來很高興。他可能拿到那份工作了。
  7. I may have made a mistake in the last question.
    我最後一題可能寫錯了。

③ you may have + 過去分詞：說可能的壞處

  1. If the parcel looks too small, you may have ordered the wrong size.
    如果包裹看起來太小，你可能訂錯尺寸了。
  2. If you didn't read the contract, you may have agreed to pay extra.
    如果你沒讀合約，你可能已經同意多付錢了。
  3. You may have paid too much if you bought the ticket at the station.
    如果你在車站買票，你可能付太多錢了。
  4. You may have given your password to a stranger without knowing it.
    你可能在不知情的情況下把密碼給了陌生人。
  5. By the time you arrive, the best seats may have gone.
    等你到了，最好的位子可能已經沒了。
  6. You may have lost your data if you didn't save it.
    如果你沒存檔，你的資料可能已經不見了。

④ you may have + 過去分詞：承認自己做的事

  1. You may have seen that your beautiful blue vase is not in your room any more. I'm afraid I broke it!
    你可能已經發現你那個漂亮的藍色花瓶不在房間裡了。恐怕是我打破的！
  2. You may have noticed that your chocolate has gone. Sorry, I ate it.
    你可能已經發現你的巧克力不見了。抱歉，我吃掉了。
  3. You may have wondered why I didn't call. My phone was stolen.
    你可能在想為什麼我沒打電話。我的手機被偷了。（被動）
  4. You may have found my shoes in your car. I left them there last week.
    你可能已經在你車上找到我的鞋子。我上星期留在那裡的。
  5. You may have heard a loud noise last night. That was me falling over.
    你昨晚可能聽到一聲巨響。那是我跌倒了。
  6. You may have realised that I forgot your birthday. I'm so sorry.
    你可能已經發現我忘了你的生日。真的很抱歉。

⑤ may not have + 過去分詞：可能沒有發生

  1. Call her again. She may not have heard the phone.
    再打給她一次。她可能沒聽到電話響。
  2. He may not have got my message.
    他可能沒收到我的訊息。
  3. They may not have known about the change of plan.
    他們可能不知道計畫改了。
  4. I may not have explained it very clearly.
    我可能沒解釋得很清楚。
  5. My cousin may not have read the invitation before she booked her holiday.
    我表妹訂假期之前，可能沒看到邀請函。
  6. The waiter may not have understood our order.
    服務生可能沒聽懂我們點的菜。
  7. You may not have noticed, but I've had my hair cut.
    你可能沒注意到，可是我剪頭髮了。
