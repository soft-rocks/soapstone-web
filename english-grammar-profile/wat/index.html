I will let you know as soon as I get the exact date.

我一拿到確切日期就通知你。

主⁠詞 + will + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + as soon as + 主⁠詞 + 現⁠在簡⁠單式

as soon as、as soon as 子⁠句、另⁠一半⁠的句⁠子

I will let you know as soon as I get the exact date.

as soon as一……就……。前一件事發生後，後一件事馬上發生。
as soon as 子句說未來的事，動詞用現在簡單式。
另一半的句子用 will、be going to、祈使句這類說未來的形式。

現⁠在完⁠成⁠式

I will let you know as soon as I get the exact date.

例句

I'll send it as soon as I've finished it.

我一寫完就寄給你。

as soon as 後面也可以用現在完成式。這樣寫強調前一件事已經做完。

as soon as 說未⁠來⁠的時⁠間⁠時，後⁠面用現⁠在簡⁠單式

I will let you know as soon as I get the exact date.

I'll let you know as soon as I will get the date.

I'll let you know as soon as I get the date.

這⁠個連⁠接⁠詞是 as soon as，前⁠面⁠的 as 不⁠能省⁠略

I will let you know as soon as I get the exact date.

I'll phone you soon as I land.

I'll phone you as soon as I land.

as soon as 子⁠句以⁠外⁠的那一⁠半說未⁠來，要⁠用 will

I will let you know as soon as I get the exact date.

I tell you as soon as I hear anything.

I'll tell you as soon as I hear anything.

例句

① 主詞 + will + 原形動詞 + as soon as + 現在簡單式

  1. I'll call you as soon as the plane lands.
    飛機一降落我就打給你。
  2. She'll start work as soon as she finishes university.
    她一大學畢業就會開始工作。
  3. We'll leave as soon as the taxi comes.
    計程車一到我們就走。
  4. I'll pay you back as soon as I get my salary.
    我一領到薪水就還你錢。
  5. He'll reply as soon as he reads your message.
    他一看到你的訊息就會回覆。
  6. The shop will reopen as soon as the builders finish the repairs.
    工人一修好，那家店就會重新營業。
  7. I'll send you the photos as soon as I get home.
    我一到家就把照片寄給你。
  8. They'll serve dinner as soon as everyone sits down.
    大家一坐好，他們就會上晚餐。
  9. We'll start the meeting as soon as the manager arrives.
    經理一到，我們就開始開會。
  10. I'll make a cup of tea as soon as the water boils.
    水一開我就泡杯茶。

② As soon as + 現在簡單式, 主詞 + will + 原形動詞

  1. As soon as I hear any news, I'll let you know.
    我一聽到任何消息就會通知你。
  2. As soon as the rain stops, we'll go for a walk.
    雨一停我們就去散步。
  3. As soon as she gets her results, she'll apply for the course.
    她一拿到成績就會申請那門課程。
  4. As soon as the film finishes, I'll go straight to bed.
    電影一結束我就直接去睡覺。
  5. As soon as you're ready, we'll leave.
    你一準備好我們就出發。
  6. As soon as the baby falls asleep, I'll start cooking.
    寶寶一睡著，我就開始煮飯。
  7. As soon as the tickets go on sale, I'll buy two.
    票一開賣我就買兩張。

③ be going to / be planning to + 原形動詞 + as soon as

  1. I'm planning to move as soon as I finish college.
    我打算一讀完大學就搬家。
  2. We're going to buy a house as soon as we save enough money.
    我們一存夠錢就要買房子。
  3. He's going to learn to drive as soon as he turns seventeen.
    他一滿十七歲就要學開車。
  4. I'm going to have a long bath as soon as I get home.
    我一到家就要好好泡個澡。
  5. She's planning to travel as soon as the exams are over.
    她打算一考完試就去旅行。

④ 祈使句 + as soon as + 現在簡單式

  1. Call me as soon as you arrive at the hotel.
    你一到飯店就打給我。
  2. Please send the form back as soon as you can.
    請盡快把表格寄回來。
  3. Take the cake out of the oven as soon as it turns brown.
    蛋糕一變成褐色就從烤箱拿出來。
  4. Let me know as soon as the parcel arrives.
    包裹一到就通知我。
  5. Text me as soon as the match ends.
    比賽一結束就傳訊息給我。

⑤ as soon as + 現在完成式

  1. I'll email you the report as soon as I've checked it.
    我一檢查完報告就用電子郵件寄給你。
  2. We'll go out as soon as I've had a shower.
    我一洗完澡我們就出門。
  3. You can go out as soon as you've done your homework.
    你一寫完功課就可以出去。
  4. I'll give the book back as soon as I've read it.
    我一讀完這本書就還你。
