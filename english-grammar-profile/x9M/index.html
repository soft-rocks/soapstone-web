I'm working today.

我今天要上班。

助⁠動⁠詞 be 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 組⁠成現⁠在進⁠行⁠式

我今天要上班。

'm 是 am 的縮寫。

助⁠動⁠詞 be 照主⁠詞變⁠化

I'm working today.

Iam、I'm
he、she、itis、he's、she's、it's
you、we、theyare、you're、we're、they're

字尾是 ⁠-⁠e 的去⁠掉 e，單⁠音⁠節⁠的重⁠複最⁠後⁠的子⁠音

I'm working today.

加 -⁠ingwork → working
去掉 ewrite → writing
重複子音sit → sitting、run → running

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式說正⁠在做⁠的⁠事、這⁠段時⁠間⁠的事和安⁠排好⁠的計⁠畫

I'm working today.

正在做

He's cooking dinner.

他在煮晚餐。
這段時間

I'm reading a great book this week.

我這星期在看一本很好看的書。
安排好的計畫

We are going to the park on Sunday.

我們星期天要去公園。

否⁠定加 not，問⁠句把助⁠動⁠詞 be 放⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

I'm working today.

否定

I'm not working today.

我今天不用上班。
問句

Are you working today?

你今天要上班嗎？

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面要⁠有 am、is 或 are

I'm working today.

I working today.

I'm working today.

助⁠動⁠詞 be 後⁠面⁠的一⁠般動⁠詞用動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing

I'm working today.

She is cook dinner now.

She is cooking dinner now.

主⁠詞是複⁠數名⁠詞，助⁠動⁠詞 be 用 are

I'm working today.

My friends is waiting for me.

My friends are waiting for me.

sit 要重⁠複 t 再⁠加 ⁠-⁠ing

I'm working today.

We are siting in the garden.

We are sitting in the garden.

現⁠在簡⁠單式說習⁠慣，現⁠在進⁠行⁠式只⁠說今⁠天

I'm working today.

現在簡單式

I work on Saturdays.

我星期六都要上班。
現在進行式

I'm working today.

我今天要上班。

be 動⁠詞後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞⁠時是連⁠綴⁠動⁠詞，不⁠是助⁠動⁠詞

I'm working today.

我今天很累。

例句

① 說話的時候正在做的事

  1. "Can I call you back? I'm driving."
    我晚點再打給你好嗎？我在開車。
  2. "Where's Mum?" "She's sleeping. She had a long day."
    媽媽在哪裡？她在睡覺。她今天很累。
  3. "Let's walk to the shop." "Wait, it's raining. Let's take the car."
    我們走路去商店吧。等一下，在下雨。我們開車去吧。
  4. "What are the kids doing?" "They're playing in the garden."
    孩子們在做什麼？他們在院子裡玩。
  5. Shh! The baby is sleeping in the next room.
    噓！寶寶在隔壁房間睡覺。
  6. I can't talk now. We're having dinner.
    我現在不能講電話。我們在吃晚餐。

② 這段時間在做的事

  1. My brother is studying medicine in Taichung.
    我哥哥在台中讀醫學系。
  2. "How's your English?" "Better. I'm taking classes on Tuesday evenings."
    你的英文怎麼樣？進步了。我現在每週二晚上都去上課。
  3. Dear Ben, I am writing to tell you about my first week at college.
    親愛的 Ben，我寫信來跟你說我在大學第一週的事。
  4. We're living with my parents until our new flat is ready.
    在我們的新公寓弄好之前，我們都跟我爸媽住。
  5. She's learning to swim this summer.
    她這個夏天在學游泳。

③ 已經安排好的計畫

  1. "Do you want to have lunch?" "Sorry, I'm working today."
    要一起吃午餐嗎？抱歉，我今天要上班。
  2. We are going to the park on Sunday. Do you want to come?
    我們星期天要去公園。你要一起來嗎？
  3. My parents are flying to Japan tomorrow morning.
    我爸媽明天早上要飛日本。
  4. "What are you doing this weekend?" "I'm visiting my grandma."
    你這個週末要做什麼？我要去看我奶奶。
  5. Tom is having a party on Saturday, and he's inviting everyone in our class.
    Tom 星期六要辦派對，他要邀請我們班上的每個人。

④ 否定

  1. "Are you coming with us?" "No, I'm not coming. I've got a cold."
    你要跟我們一起去嗎？不，我不去。我感冒了。
  2. "Can you print this for me?" "Sorry, the printer in our office isn't working."
    你可以幫我印這個嗎？抱歉，我們辦公室的印表機壞了。
  3. Turn off the TV. You aren't watching it.
    把電視關掉。你又沒在看。
  4. My sister isn't living at home this year. She's in London.
    我妹妹今年沒住在家裡。她在倫敦。

⑤ 問句

  1. "Are you working today?" "No, it's my day off."
    你今天要上班嗎？不用，今天我休假。
  2. "Is the bus coming?" "Yes, I can see it at the corner."
    公車來了嗎？來了，我看到它在轉角。
  3. "Why are you laughing?" "Look at the dog. He's wearing your hat!"
    你在笑什麼？你看那隻狗。牠戴著你的帽子！
  4. "Who is your sister talking to?" "That's her new boss."
    你姊姊在跟誰講話？那是她的新老闆。

⑥ 動詞-ing 的拼法

  1. Please be quiet. I'm writing an important email.
    請安靜一點。我在寫一封很重要的電子郵件。
  2. "Where's Ann?" "She's sitting by the window."
    Ann 在哪裡？她坐在窗邊。
  3. My dad is running in the city marathon on Sunday.
    我爸星期天要跑城市馬拉松。
  4. We're making a cake for Grandpa's birthday.
    我們在為爺爺的生日做蛋糕。
  5. "Mum, the dog is swimming in the lake!" "Oh no, get him out!"
    媽，狗在湖裡游泳！糟了，快把牠弄上來！
  6. "Are you leaving already?" "Yes, my bus comes in five minutes."
    你已經要走了嗎？對，我的公車五分鐘後就來了。
