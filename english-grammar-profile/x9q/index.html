The announcer said that the police had arrested a man who had stolen five paintings.

播報員說，警方逮捕了一名男子，他偷了五幅昂貴的畫。

主⁠詞 + said / told + 人 + (that) + 主⁠詞 + had + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

said / told轉述的動詞，用過去式。
had + 過去分詞轉述的內容裡更早發生的事。

直⁠接引⁠述和間⁠接引⁠述

直接

My flatmate called me from outside. "I have lost my keys," she said.

我室友從外面打給我。她說：「我把鑰匙弄丟了。」
間接

My flatmate called me from outside. She said she had lost her keys.

我室友從外面打給我。她說她把鑰匙弄丟了。

what had happened

例句

The little girl was crying at the bus stop. She tried to explain what had happened.

那個小女孩在公車站哭。她試著解釋發生了什麼事。

what had happened 常跟 explain、know、find out 一起用。

轉⁠述過⁠去更早⁠的⁠事，用 had 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The announcer said that the police had arrested a man who had stolen five paintings.

My aunt said that she has lost her passport the day before.

My aunt said that she had lost her passport the day before.

told 後⁠面要接人

My brother told that he had finished the painting.

My brother told me that he had finished the painting.

had 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

The announcer said that the police had arrested a man who had stolen five paintings.

My friends said they had saw the film twice.

My friends said they had seen the film twice.

例句

① 主詞 + said + that + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞：轉述新聞

  1. One morning I was listening to the radio, when the announcer said that the police had arrested a man who had stolen five paintings.
    有一天早上我在聽收音機，播報員說警察逮捕了一個偷了五幅畫的男人。
  2. The newspaper said that a storm had destroyed the old pier.
    報紙說一場暴風雨摧毀了那座老碼頭。
  3. We landed twenty minutes early. The pilot said that a strong wind had helped us.
    我們提早二十分鐘降落。機長說一陣強風幫了我們。
  4. The weatherman said that it had been the hottest day of the year.
    氣象播報員說那是一年中最熱的一天。
  5. The report said that prices had gone up by ten per cent.
    報告說物價上漲了百分之十。
  6. The head teacher said that our school had won the national prize.
    校長說我們學校贏得了全國獎。

② 主詞 + tried to explain / didn't know + what had happened

  1. She was so upset that when she tried to explain what had happened, she couldn't.
    她太難過了，所以她試著解釋發生了什麼事的時候，說不出來。
  2. I looked at the address and I knew what had happened to my sister's postcard.
    我看了地址，就知道我姊姊的明信片怎麼了。
  3. Nobody could explain what had happened to the missing dog.
    沒有人能解釋那隻走失的狗怎麼了。
  4. He didn't understand what had happened until he read the letter.
    他讀了那封信才明白發生了什麼事。
  5. The children told their parents what had happened at school.
    孩子們告訴他們爸媽在學校發生了什麼事。
  6. Finally, the police found out what had happened that night.
    最後，警察查出了那天晚上發生了什麼事。

③ 主詞 + told + 人 + that + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞

  1. My sister told me that she had passed her driving test.
    我姊姊告訴我她通過了駕照考試。
  2. The doctor told us that the operation had gone well.
    醫生告訴我們手術很順利。
  3. My new classmate speaks Japanese. He told me that he had lived in Japan as a child.
    我的新同學會說日文。他告訴我他小時候住過日本。
  4. The waiter told us that they had run out of fish.
    服務生告訴我們魚已經賣完了。
  5. My friend told me that she had found a new job.
    我朋友告訴我她找到了一份新工作。
  6. The teacher told the class that someone had taken her pen.
    老師告訴全班有人拿走了她的筆。

④ 主詞 + explained / admitted + that + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞

  1. Tom came to class at ten. He explained that he had missed the bus.
    湯姆十點才來上課。他解釋說他錯過了公車。
  2. My daughter looked at the floor. She admitted that she had broken the vase.
    我女兒低頭看著地板。她承認是她打破了花瓶。
  3. The manager explained that the delivery had been delayed.
    經理解釋說送貨延誤了。
  4. My brother admitted that he had borrowed my bike.
    我哥哥承認他借了我的腳踏車。
  5. The hotel explained that they had given our room to someone else.
    飯店解釋說他們把我們的房間給了別人。
  6. My friend talked about the novel for an hour. Then he admitted that he had never read the book.
    我朋友聊那本小說聊了一個小時。後來他承認他從來沒讀過那本書。

⑤ 主詞 + said + 主詞 + hadn't + 過去分詞

  1. My uncle said he hadn't been on a train since he was a boy.
    我叔叔說他從小時候就沒坐過火車了。
  2. The shop said they hadn't received my order.
    那家店說他們沒收到我的訂單。
  3. My dad looked terrible after the night shift. He said he hadn't slept for two days.
    我爸爸值完夜班看起來很糟。他說他兩天沒睡了。
  4. My mum said she hadn't heard from my aunt for weeks.
    我媽媽說她好幾個星期沒有我阿姨的消息了。
  5. The students said they hadn't understood the question.
    學生們說他們沒聽懂那個問題。
  6. The driver said he hadn't seen the red light.
    司機說他沒看到紅燈。
  7. Our neighbour said she hadn't heard anything in the night.
    我們的鄰居說她夜裡什麼都沒聽到。
