I bought a new pullover because my old one is too small.

我買了一件新的套頭毛衣，因為我舊的那件太小了。

one 代⁠替單⁠數名⁠詞

I bought a new pullover because my old one is too small.

……新的套頭毛衣，因為我舊的那件……

說⁠過⁠的名⁠詞、看⁠得⁠出⁠來⁠的名⁠詞

I bought a new pullover because my old one is too small.

說過的

This cup is dirty. Can I have a clean one?

這個杯子髒了，可以給我一個乾淨的嗎？
看得出來的

I'll take the black one with the big screen.

（在手機店）我要黑色、大螢幕的那支。

單⁠獨用 one

I bought a new pullover because my old one is too small.

單獨用

I need an umbrella. Have you got one?

我需要一把傘，你有嗎？
加形容詞

I need an umbrella. Have you got a big one?

我需要一把傘，你有大的嗎？

不⁠特⁠定⁠的一⁠個用 one

I bought a new pullover because my old one is too small.

I don't have a pen. Can you lend me it?

I don't have a pen. Can you lend me one?

單⁠獨用⁠的 one 不⁠加 a

I bought a new pullover because my old one is too small.

I'd like a sandwich. Can you make me a one?

I'd like a sandwich. Can you make me one?

不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞不⁠用 one

I need some advice. Can you give me a good one?

I need some advice. Can you give me some good advice?

複⁠數用 ones

我的鞋子舊了，我需要新的。

同⁠一⁠個東⁠西用 it

我買了一盞新檯燈，它很亮。

例句

① 限定詞 + 修飾語 + one

  1. My bag is broken, so I need a new one.
    我的包包壞了，所以我需要一個新的。
  2. This room is too small. Have you got a bigger one?
    這個房間太小了，有大一點的嗎？
  3. I don't like the red dress. I prefer the green one.
    我不喜歡那件紅洋裝，我比較喜歡綠色那件。
  4. Our new kitchen is twice the size of the old one.
    我們的新廚房是舊的兩倍大。
  5. We sold our car and bought a cheaper one.
    我們把車賣了，買了一台比較便宜的。
  6. That's a nice coat, but I want a warmer one.
    那件外套不錯，可是我想要暖一點的。

② 單獨用 one

  1. I need a pencil. Have you got one?
    我需要一支鉛筆，你有嗎？
  2. If you want a ticket, I can get you one.
    你想要票的話，我可以幫你拿一張。
  3. There's a bank near here. I saw one on the corner.
    這附近有銀行，我在轉角看到一間。
  4. We don't have a garden, but we'd love one.
    我們沒有花園，可是很想要一個。
  5. I've never had a dog, but I really want one.
    我從來沒養過狗，可是我真的很想養一隻。

③ the one

  1. Which bike is yours? The one by the gate?
    哪一台腳踏車是你的？門邊那台嗎？
  2. I like this song more than the one on the radio.
    我比較喜歡這首歌，勝過廣播裡那首。
  3. Is this the right bus? No, you need the one after it.
    這是對的公車嗎？不是，你要搭下一班。
  4. That's the one I wanted.
    那就是我想要的那個。
  5. The one on the top shelf is cheaper.
    最上層那個比較便宜。

④ this one、that one、which one

  1. I don't like that cake. Can I have this one?
    我不喜歡那個蛋糕，我可以拿這個嗎？
  2. Which one do you want, the big one or the small one?
    你要哪一個，大的還是小的？
  3. That one looks better than this one.
    那個看起來比這個好。
  4. This chair is broken. Sit on that one.
    這張椅子壞了，坐那張吧。
  5. There are two pens here. Which one is yours?
    這裡有兩支筆，哪一支是你的？

⑤ 在店裡

  1. Could you wrap the gold one for me, please?
    可以幫我把金色那個包起來嗎？
  2. This one's too tight. Is there a looser one?
    這件太緊了，有寬鬆一點的嗎？
  3. I'll take the cheap one.
    我要便宜的那個。
  4. Is the blue one on sale?
    藍色的有特價嗎？
  5. Have you got one in black?
    有黑色的嗎？

⑥ another、the other、the next + one

  1. This apple is very good. Can I have another one?
    這顆蘋果很好吃，我可以再吃一顆嗎？
  2. One glove is here. Where's the other one?
    一隻手套在這裡，另一隻在哪裡？
  3. We bought one lamp for the bedroom and another one for the hall.
    我們買了一盞燈放臥室，另一盞放走廊。
  4. This lesson was easy. The next one will be harder.
    這一課很簡單，下一課會比較難。
  5. I finished my coffee and ordered another one.
    我喝完咖啡，又點了一杯。
