肯⁠定⁠句是主⁠詞加 could，再⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

You could come by bus, and we could meet at the bus station.

你可以搭公車來，我們可以在公車站碰面。

肯⁠定⁠句⁠的 could 常⁠用來建⁠議、說過⁠去⁠的能⁠力和可⁠能

You could come by bus, and we could meet at the bus station.

建議

We could go to the museum near Cibeles.

我們可以去 Cibeles 附近的博物館。
過去的能力

My brother could ride a horse at six.

我哥六歲就會騎馬了。
可能

That could be our bus.

那可能就是我們要搭的公車。

例句

① 建議：主詞 + could + 原形動詞

  1. You could take a taxi from the airport.
    你可以從機場搭計程車。
  2. We could visit the royal palace.
    我們可以去參觀皇宮。
  3. You could stay at my flat.
    你可以住我家。
  4. We could have lunch in the old market.
    我們可以在舊市場吃午餐。
  5. You could bring your guitar.
    你可以帶你的吉他來。
  6. We could walk around the park in the afternoon.
    我們下午可以在公園裡散步。
  7. You could call me when you arrive.
    你到了可以打給我。
  8. We could watch a football match on Sunday.
    我們星期天可以去看足球賽。

② 兩個 could，用 and 連起來

  1. You could come by train, and I could pick you up.
    你可以搭火車來，我可以去接你。
  2. We could cook dinner, and you could choose the film.
    我們可以煮晚餐，你可以挑電影。
  3. I could buy the drinks, and you could bring the snacks.
    我可以買飲料，你可以帶零食。
  4. You could fly to Madrid, and we could drive to the coast together.
    你可以飛到馬德里，我們再一起開車去海邊。
  5. She could wash the dishes, and he could dry them.
    她可以洗碗，他可以擦乾。

③ 過去的能力：主詞 + could + 原形動詞

  1. My grandmother could knit very quickly.
    我奶奶織毛線織得很快。
  2. I could run very fast at school.
    我上學的時候跑得很快。
  3. She could speak French when she was a child.
    她小時候會說法文。
  4. We could see the sea from our old house.
    我們以前的房子看得到海。
  5. He could sing really well.
    他以前唱歌很好聽。
  6. My cat could open the kitchen door.
    我的貓會開廚房的門。
  7. They could play the piano and the violin.
    他們會彈鋼琴，也會拉小提琴。

④ 可能：主詞 + could + 原形動詞

  1. That could be the wrong address.
    那可能是錯的地址。
  2. It could be cold in the evening.
    晚上可能會冷。
  3. The tickets could sell out quickly.
    票可能很快就會賣完。
  4. You could be right.
    你可能是對的。
  5. The trip could take four hours.
    這趟路可能要四個小時。

⑤ 所有主詞都用 could

  1. I could help you with your bags.
    我可以幫你拿行李。
  2. He could show you the city.
    他可以帶你逛逛這座城市。
  3. It could rain tomorrow.
    明天可能會下雨。
  4. They could meet us at the hotel.
    他們可以在飯店跟我們碰面。
  5. She could lend you a map.
    她可以借你一張地圖。
