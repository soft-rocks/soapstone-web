Firstly, I advise you to visit both schools.

首先，我建議你兩間學校都去看看。

firstly 放⁠在句⁠首，列⁠出第一⁠點

首先，我建議你兩間學校都去看看。

secondly 接⁠下一⁠點，finally 接最⁠後一⁠點

Firstly, I advise you to visit both schools.

secondly

Secondly, talk to some students there.

第二，跟那裡的學生聊聊。
finally

Finally, compare the prices.

最後，比較一下價錢。

what's more 補⁠充一⁠點，actually 帶⁠出新⁠的資⁠訊

what's more

The topic is useful. What's more, it's very interesting.

這個主題很實用。而且很有趣。
actually

I said Tuesday, but actually the meeting is on Wednesday.

我說星期二，不過會議其實是星期三。

firstly 只⁠用在列⁠點，說先⁠後⁠順⁠序用 first

Firstly, I advise you to visit both schools.

Firstly I fed the cat, and then I made breakfast.

First I fed the cat, and then I made breakfast.

列⁠出最⁠後一⁠點用 finally，不⁠用 at last

At last, I want to thank everyone for coming.

Finally, I want to thank everyone for coming.

what's more 的 's 不⁠能省⁠略

The flat is cheap. What more, it has a garden.

The flat is cheap. What's more, it has a garden.

finally 有兩⁠個 l

Finaly, we need a bigger room for the class.

Finally, we need a bigger room for the class.

例句

① firstly

  1. Firstly, the classes are too big.
    首先，班級人數太多了。
  2. Firstly, thank you for your letter.
    首先，謝謝你的來信。
  3. Firstly, the hotel is too far from the centre.
    首先，飯店離市中心太遠了。
  4. There are two reasons. Firstly, I don't have time.
    有兩個原因。第一，我沒有時間。
  5. Firstly, make a list of what you need.
    首先，把你需要的東西列出來。

② secondly、thirdly

  1. Secondly, we need better books.
    第二，我們需要更好的課本。
  2. Secondly, the rooms were dirty.
    第二，房間很髒。
  3. Secondly, it's too expensive for students.
    第二，這對學生來說太貴了。
  4. Thirdly, the staff were not very helpful.
    第三，員工不太幫得上忙。
  5. Thirdly, you should practise every day.
    第三，你應該每天練習。

③ finally

  1. Finally, I think the speaker did an excellent job.
    最後，我覺得講者講得很好。
  2. Finally, the trip will cost less than last year.
    最後，這次旅行的費用會比去年少。
  3. Finally, I would like to ask about the price.
    最後，我想問一下價錢。
  4. Finally, don't forget to switch off the lights.
    最後，別忘了關燈。

④ what's more

  1. The café has great coffee. What's more, it's cheap.
    那家咖啡館的咖啡很好喝。而且很便宜。
  2. He's late every day. What's more, he never says sorry.
    他每天都遲到。而且從來不道歉。
  3. The course is online. What's more, you can start any time.
    這門課在線上。而且隨時都可以開始。
  4. It's a lovely town. What's more, the people are very friendly.
    那是個很可愛的小鎮。而且人都很友善。
  5. The bike is light. What's more, it folds up.
    這台腳踏車很輕。而且可以折起來。

⑤ actually

  1. My boss will talk about selling abroad. Actually, our company wants to open a shop in Spain.
    我老闆會談到海外銷售。其實我們公司想在西班牙開店。
  2. I said Tuesday, but actually the meeting is on Wednesday.
    我說星期二，不過會議其實是星期三。
  3. You think I'm angry, but actually I'm just tired.
    你以為我在生氣，可是我其實只是累了。
  4. We thought it was a holiday. Actually, the school was open.
    我們以為那天放假。其實學校有上課。

⑥ 在一段話裡列點

  1. There are three things to remember. Firstly, be on time. Secondly, bring a pen. Finally, switch off your phone.
    有三件事要記住。第一，準時。第二，帶筆。最後，手機關機。
  2. I chose this school for two reasons. Firstly, it's near my home. Secondly, the teachers are excellent.
    我選這間學校有兩個原因。第一，離我家近。第二，老師很棒。
  3. Firstly, I want to say sorry. Secondly, I want to explain.
    首先，我想道歉。第二，我想解釋一下。
  4. First, open the app. Second, choose your seat. Finally, pay.
    首先，打開應用程式。第二，選座位。最後，付款。
  5. The plan has two problems. Firstly, it costs too much. Secondly, it takes too long.
    這個計畫有兩個問題。第一，花費太高。第二，時間太長。
  6. Firstly, the food was cold. What's more, the waiter was rude.
    首先，食物是冷的。而且服務生很沒禮貌。
  7. Finally, I'd like to thank my family.
    最後，我想謝謝我的家人。
