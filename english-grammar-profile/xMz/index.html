代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞放⁠在動⁠詞和小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

我盡量把它清乾淨了。

clean up 的意思是打掃乾淨。

名⁠詞受⁠詞可⁠以放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞中⁠間或後⁠面

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

名詞在中間

I cleaned the kitchen up.

我把廚房打掃乾淨了。
名詞在後面

I cleaned up the kitchen.

我把廚房打掃乾淨了。

代⁠名⁠詞受⁠詞常指前⁠面說⁠過⁠的東⁠西

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

我聽說那個新樂團很好，就去聽聽看。

代⁠名⁠詞不⁠放⁠在小⁠品⁠詞後⁠面

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

The problem is hard, but we'll figure out it.

The problem is hard, but we'll figure it out.

laugh off 要⁠有受⁠詞

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

He made a mistake, but he laughed off.

He made a mistake, but he laughed it off.

淡⁠化是 play down，play up 是誇⁠大

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

The news was bad, but the minister played it up.

The news was bad, but the minister played it down.

put up with 的代⁠名⁠詞放⁠在 with 後⁠面

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

I can't put up with it any more.

我再也受不了了。

talk it over 的 it 可⁠以指整⁠件⁠事

I tried to clean it up as much as possible.

Let's talk it over after dinner.

我們晚餐後好好談一談。

例句

① clean up / fix up

  1. "Wasn't there a party here last night?" "Yes. The living room was a mess, but I tried to clean it up as much as possible."
    昨晚這裡不是辦過派對嗎？對。客廳很亂，可是我盡量清乾淨了。
  2. The kids spilled juice on the floor, and their dad cleaned it up.
    孩子們把果汁灑在地上，爸爸把它擦乾淨了。
  3. We bought an old house and fixed it up ourselves.
    我們買了一間舊房子，自己整修。
  4. The old bike was rusty, so my grandad fixed it up for me.
    那台舊腳踏車生鏽了，所以我爺爺幫我修好了。

② check out / figure out

  1. I'd heard a lot about the band, so I went to check them out last week.
    我聽說了很多那個樂團的事，所以上星期去聽了聽。
  2. "There's a new café on the corner." "Let's check it out after work."
    轉角開了一家新咖啡店。下班後去看看吧。
  3. The instructions were in Japanese, but I figured them out.
    說明書是日文的，可是我弄懂了。
  4. "How did you open the box?" "It took a while, but I figured it out."
    你是怎麼打開這個盒子的？花了點時間，可是我想出來了。

③ talk over

  1. "Should we buy the flat?" "Let's talk it over with your parents first."
    我們該買那間公寓嗎？我們先跟你爸媽好好談談。
  2. They had a problem with the plan, so they talked it over at lunch.
    他們對計畫有意見，所以午餐時好好討論了一下。
  3. Before you quit, talk it over with your manager.
    辭職之前，先跟你的經理好好談談。

④ play down / laugh off / brush off

  1. The company had a big loss, but the boss played it down.
    公司虧了一大筆錢，可是老闆輕描淡寫地帶過。
  2. "Was she upset about the mistake?" "No, she just laughed it off."
    她對那個錯誤很難過嗎？沒有，她一笑置之。
  3. People made jokes about his accent, but he laughed them off.
    大家拿他的口音開玩笑，可是他一笑置之。
  4. I told him he looked ill, but he brushed it off.
    我跟他說他看起來氣色不好，可是他不當一回事。
  5. The doctor warned her about her heart, but she brushed the warning off.
    醫生警告她心臟有問題，可是她不當一回事。

⑤ back up

  1. "Did you lose all your photos?" "No, luckily I'd backed them up."
    你的照片都不見了嗎？沒有，還好我有備份。
  2. Save your work and back it up every evening.
    把你的工作存檔，每天晚上備份。
  3. I told the teacher what happened, and my friends backed me up.
    我把發生的事告訴老師，我的朋友都支持我的說法。

⑥ write off / hand over

  1. "Can the car be repaired?" "No, the insurance company has written it off."
    車子還能修嗎？不能，保險公司認定報廢了。
  2. After the first year, many people wrote the business off, but it did well.
    第一年過後很多人都不看好這家公司，可是它做得很好。
  3. The guard asked for my passport, so I handed it over.
    警衛要看我的護照，所以我把它交出去了。
  4. "Where are the keys?" "I handed them over to the new tenants."
    鑰匙在哪裡？我交給新房客了。

⑦ pin down

  1. I asked him when he'd pay, but I couldn't pin him down.
    我問他什麼時候付錢，可是沒辦法逼他給個確切的答覆。
  2. "When is the meeting?" "I don't know. Try to pin her down."
    會議是什麼時候？不知道。你逼她給個確定的時間吧。

⑧ 受詞是名詞

  1. I cleaned up the kitchen before my parents came home.
    我爸媽回家前，我把廚房打掃乾淨了。
  2. We need to figure out a way to get there on time.
    我們得想個辦法準時到那裡。
  3. The government tried to play down the problem.
    政府試圖淡化這個問題。
  4. Always back up your files before you update your computer.
    更新電腦之前，一定要先備份檔案。
  5. The thief handed over the bag when the police arrived.
    警察到的時候，小偷交出了包包。
  6. She laughed off the rumours about her new job.
    她對關於她新工作的謠言一笑置之。
