What had he been doing that morning?

那天早上他一直在做什麼？

Had + 主⁠詞 + been + ⁠-⁠ing + …？

What had he been doing that morning?

Had放在主詞前面。
been + -⁠ing一直在做的事。
疑問詞what、why、how long、where 放在句首。

How long had …？

例句

How long had you been waiting?

你等了多久？

How long had … been + -⁠ing 問一件事持續了多久。

正⁠式和文⁠學⁠的用⁠法

例句

Why had she been waiting for so long?

她為什麼等了那麼久？

這種問句不常出現在日常對話裡，常用在小說、報導和調查。

問⁠句把 had 放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面

What had he been doing that morning?

What he had been doing?

What had he been doing?

過⁠去完⁠成進⁠行⁠式⁠的問⁠句用 had

What had he been doing that morning?

Why did she been waiting so long?

Why had she been waiting so long?

had 和 ⁠-⁠ing 中⁠間要⁠有 been

What had he been doing that morning?

How long had you waiting?

How long had you been waiting?

例句

① What had + 主詞 + been doing？：問之前在做什麼

  1. The old man was found in his study at noon. The detective asked, "What had he been doing that morning?"
    那個老人中午在書房被發現。偵探問：那天早上他一直在做什麼？（被動）
  2. What had the children been doing before the fire started?
    火災開始之前，孩子們一直在做什麼？
  3. What had you been doing all day when I called?
    我打給你的時候，你那一整天都在做什麼？
  4. What had the driver been doing just before the accident?
    意外發生前，司機一直在做什麼？
  5. What had the students been doing while the teacher was out?
    老師不在的時候，學生們一直在做什麼？
  6. What had she been doing in the garden for so long?
    她在花園裡待了那麼久，一直在做什麼？

② Why had + 主詞 + been + -ing？：問原因

  1. She was still at the station at midnight. So why had she been waiting for such a long time?
    她半夜還在車站。那她為什麼等了那麼久？
  2. The neighbours complained in the morning. Why had our parrot been screaming since dawn?
    鄰居早上來抱怨。我們的鸚鵡為什麼從天亮就一直在尖叫？
  3. Why had he been hiding the letters in his desk?
    他為什麼一直把那些信藏在書桌裡？
  4. Why had they been arguing before the party?
    派對前他們為什麼一直在吵架？
  5. Why had you been crying when I found you?
    我找到你的時候，你為什麼一直在哭？
  6. Why had the shop been losing money for years?
    那家店為什麼好幾年來一直在虧錢？

③ How long had + 主詞 + been + -ing？：問持續多久

  1. Your face was blue with cold. How long had you been standing outside the station?
    你的臉凍得發青。你在車站外面站了多久？
  2. How long had they been living in Spain before they came back?
    他們回來之前在西班牙住了多久？
  3. How long had she been working there when she left?
    她離開的時候，在那裡工作了多久？
  4. How long had it been raining when you left the house?
    你出門的時候，雨已經下了多久？
  5. How long had the patient been feeling ill?
    病人不舒服多久了？
  6. How long had the two of them been seeing each other?
    他們兩個交往了多久？

④ Had + 主詞 + been + -ing？：問是不是一直在做

  1. Had you been drinking before you drove home?
    你開車回家之前有喝酒嗎？
  2. Had she been sleeping well before the exam?
    考試前她一直睡得好嗎？
  3. Had they been living there long?
    他們在那裡住很久了嗎？
  4. Had it been snowing all night?
    整晚都在下雪嗎？
  5. Had he been taking his medicine every day?
    他有每天吃藥嗎？
  6. Had you been expecting the news?
    你一直在等這個消息嗎？

⑤ Where / Who + had + 主詞 + been + -ing？

  1. Where had he been staying before he moved here?
    他搬來這裡之前一直住在哪裡？
  2. Who had she been talking to on the phone all evening?
    她整個傍晚在電話上一直跟誰說話？
  3. Where had the cat been hiding for three days?
    那隻貓這三天一直躲在哪裡？
  4. Who had they been working for before the company closed?
    公司倒閉前他們一直在幫誰工作？
  5. Where had you been going every Saturday morning?
    你每個星期六早上都去哪裡？
  6. Who had been sending her flowers every week?
    是誰每個星期都送花給她？
  7. Where had the money been coming from all those years?
    那些年來錢一直是從哪裡來的？
