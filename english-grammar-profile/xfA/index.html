She doesn't travel as much as she used to.

她現在不像以前那麼常旅行了。

used to 後⁠面省⁠略前⁠面說⁠過⁠的動⁠詞

She doesn't travel as much as she used to.

她旅行沒有以前那麼多了。

比⁠較⁠級 + than 的句⁠子也⁠在 used to 結⁠束

She doesn't travel as much as she used to.

比較級 + than

He reads much faster than he used to.

他讀得比以前快多了。

any more, but 後⁠面也⁠在 used to 結⁠束

She doesn't travel as much as she used to.

any more, but

I don't smoke any more, but I used to.

我現在不抽菸了，可是我以前抽。

回⁠答問⁠題⁠時，答句在 used to 結⁠束

She doesn't travel as much as she used to.

問句

Do you play the piano?

你彈鋼琴嗎？
答句

Not now, but I used to.

現在不彈了，可是我以前彈。

省⁠略動⁠詞⁠的時⁠候，to 要留⁠下⁠來

She doesn't travel as much as she used.

She doesn't travel as much as she used to.

I don't sing in the shower as much as I used to.

例句

① 主詞 + don't / doesn't + 動詞 + as much as + 主詞 + used to

  1. My grandmother's knees are bad now. She doesn't travel as much as she used to.
    我奶奶的膝蓋現在不好。她旅行沒有以前那麼多了。
  2. My dentist told me to cut down on sweets. I don't eat as much chocolate as I used to.
    我的牙醫叫我少吃甜食。我吃巧克力沒有以前那麼多了。
  3. My dad is sixty-five now. He doesn't work as hard as he used to.
    我爸爸現在六十五歲了。他工作沒有以前那麼辛苦了。
  4. We don't go out as much as we used to since the baby was born.
    寶寶出生以後，我們出門沒有以前那麼多了。
  5. My brother started a new job in London. He doesn't play the guitar as much as he used to.
    我哥哥在倫敦開始了新工作。他彈吉他沒有以前那麼多了。
  6. Now that I work from home, I don't spend as much money on lunch as I used to.
    我現在在家工作，午餐花的錢沒有以前那麼多了。

② 主詞 + 動詞 + 比較級 + than + 主詞 + used to

  1. We saw my brother's band play again last night. I must admit they sounded a lot better than they used to.
    我們昨晚又去看我哥哥的樂團表演。我必須承認，他們聽起來比以前好多了。
  2. Since the new coach arrived, our team plays much better than it used to.
    新教練來了以後，我們隊踢得比以前好多了。
  3. My little brother loves his new library card. He reads much faster than he used to.
    我弟弟很喜歡他新的借書證。他讀得比以前快多了。
  4. The railway added new trains this year. The trains to London run more often than they used to.
    鐵路公司今年加開了新列車。往倫敦的火車班次比以前多。
  5. My sister spent the summer in Lyon. She speaks French better than she used to.
    我姊姊暑假待在里昂。她的法文說得比以前好。
  6. My grandma checked her receipt from the baker's. Bread costs more than it used to.
    我奶奶看了麵包店的收據。麵包比以前貴。

③ 主詞 + don't / doesn't + 動詞 + as often as + 主詞 + used to

  1. Our cousins moved to the north. We don't see them as often as we used to.
    我們的表親搬到北部了。我們見到他們的次數沒有以前那麼多了。
  2. The pool is a long bus ride from my new flat. I don't go swimming as often as I used to.
    游泳池離我的新公寓要坐很久的公車。我去游泳的次數沒有以前那麼多了。
  3. My friend is busy with her new baby. She doesn't call her mother as often as she used to.
    我朋友忙著照顧剛出生的寶寶。她打電話給她媽媽沒有以前那麼常了。
  4. Our old neighbours live across town now. They don't come to our parties as often as they used to.
    我們以前的鄰居現在住在城的另一頭。他們來我們派對的次數沒有以前那麼多了。
  5. My grandfather's back hurts in the cold. He doesn't go fishing as often as he used to.
    我爺爺的背在冷天會痛。他去釣魚沒有以前那麼常了。
  6. We're saving for a house. We don't eat out as often as we used to.
    我們在存錢買房子。我們出去吃飯沒有以前那麼常了。

④ 主詞 + don't / doesn't + 動詞 + any more, but + 主詞 + used to

  1. My doctor asked about my habits. I don't smoke any more, but I used to.
    我的醫生問我的生活習慣。我現在不抽菸了，可是我以前抽。
  2. A letter came for Maria, the woman before us in this flat. She doesn't live here now, but she used to.
    有一封信寄給瑪麗亞，她是在我們之前住這間公寓的人。她現在不住這裡，可是她以前住過。
  3. My daughter found an old dog lead in the garage. We don't have a dog now, but we used to.
    我女兒在車庫找到一條舊狗繩。我們現在沒有養狗，可是以前有。
  4. My uncle has a broken nose from his school days. He doesn't play rugby any more, but he used to.
    我叔叔的鼻子是學生時代斷的。他現在不打橄欖球了，可是他以前打。
  5. I asked for a paper at the corner shop. They don't sell newspapers any more, but they used to.
    我在轉角的商店要買報紙。他們現在不賣報紙了，可是以前有賣。
  6. My little brother is scared of the dark. I don't believe in ghosts now, but I used to.
    我弟弟怕黑。我現在不相信鬼，可是我以前相信。

⑤ "Do you …？" "Not now, but I used to."：回答問題

  1. My friend saw the piano in our hall. "Do you play the piano?" "Not now, but I used to."
    我朋友看到我們走廊上的鋼琴。你彈鋼琴嗎？現在不彈了，可是我以前彈。
  2. I mentioned my trip to France. "Does your brother still live in Paris?" "No, but he used to."
    我提到我要去法國。你哥哥還住在巴黎嗎？沒有，可是他以前住過。
  3. My colleague saw a helmet in my office. "Do you ride a motorbike?" "Not any more, but I used to."
    我同事在我辦公室看到一頂安全帽。你騎摩托車嗎？現在不騎了，可是我以前騎。
  4. My friend has a new boss called Mr Davies. "Do you know Mr Davies?" "Not really, but my mum used to."
    我朋友的新老闆叫戴維斯先生。你認識戴維斯先生嗎？不算認識，可是我媽媽以前認識。
  5. My aunt asked about my cousin. "Does she still sing in the choir?" "No, but she used to."
    我阿姨問起我表妹。她還在合唱團唱歌嗎？沒有，可是她以前有。
  6. Our new neighbours have ski boots by their door. "Do you go skiing?" "Not these days, but we used to."
    我們的新鄰居門邊放著滑雪靴。你們去滑雪嗎？最近沒有，可是我們以前會去。
  7. My friend showed me his old coins. "Do you collect anything?" "Not now, but I used to."
    我朋友給我看他的舊硬幣。你有收集什麼東西嗎？現在沒有，可是我以前有。
