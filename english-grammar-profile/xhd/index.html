例句

① 主詞 + have been + 動詞-ing + for ...

  1. I'm in my last year at Leeds University. I've been studying international business there for four years.
    我在里茲大學念最後一年。我在那裡念國際企業念了四年。
  2. Where is it? We've been waiting for the bus for half an hour.
    公車在哪裡？我們等公車等了半個小時。
  3. The road is still closed. They've been building that bridge for three years.
    那條路還是封著。那座橋他們蓋了三年。
  4. Where were you? I've been trying to call you for an hour.
    你去哪裡了？我打電話給你打了一個小時了。
  5. Everyone in town knows them. My parents have been running the shop for over twenty years.
    鎮上每個人都認識他們。我爸媽開這家店開了二十多年。
  6. You've been sitting at that computer for hours. Take a break.
    你在電腦前面坐了好幾個小時了，休息一下吧。

② 主詞 + has been + 動詞-ing + for ...

  1. Anna knows all the customers. She has been working at the bank for ten years as a cashier.
    安娜認識所有的客人。她在那家銀行當出納十年了。
  2. The river is very high. It's been raining for three days.
    河水漲得很高。雨已經下了三天了。
  3. My brother is getting worried. He has been looking for a job for months.
    我哥開始擔心了。他找工作找了好幾個月了。
  4. I think she's hungry. The baby has been crying for twenty minutes.
    我想她餓了。寶寶哭了二十分鐘了。
  5. Jake knows all the songs. He has been playing in the band for a long time.
    傑克每首歌都會。他在那個樂團演奏很久了。
  6. Everyone in the village trusts her. Dr Lee has been treating patients at this clinic for fifteen years.
    村裡每個人都信任她。李醫師在這家診所看診十五年了。

③ since + 起點

  1. I know this street well. I've been living here since last spring.
    我很熟這條街。我從去年春天開始就住在這裡。
  2. Ask Mrs Chen about the school. She has been teaching there since 2018.
    學校的事去問陳老師。她從 2018 年就在那所學校教書。
  3. Tom and I are very happy. We've been going out together since the summer.
    我和湯姆很幸福。我們從夏天開始交往。
  4. Can you call the repair man? The printer has been making a strange noise since Monday.
    你可以打給修理人員嗎？印表機從星期一開始就一直發出怪聲。
  5. I need to lie down. I've been feeling sick since breakfast.
    我得躺一下。我從吃完早餐就一直覺得不舒服。
  6. My dad never buys new things. He's been using the same phone since 2015.
    我爸從來不買新東西。他從 2015 年就一直用同一支手機。

④ since + 子句

  1. My sister loves big cities. She has been living in Tokyo since she finished university.
    我姊姊很愛大城市。她大學畢業以後就一直住在東京。
  2. I want to buy a flat next year. I've been saving money since I started my first job.
    我明年想買一間公寓。我從第一份工作開始就一直在存錢。
  3. The kids are so noisy. They've been arguing since they got in the car.
    孩子們好吵。他們從上車就一直在吵架。
  4. Look at all these pictures. My grandfather has been painting since he retired.
    你看這些畫。我爺爺退休以後就一直在畫畫。
  5. My brother never calls us. We've been worrying about him since he moved abroad.
    我哥從不打電話給我們。他搬到國外以後，我們就一直擔心他。

⑤ How long 問句

  1. Your Japanese is excellent. How long have you been studying it?
    你的日文很好。你學日文多久了？
  2. Your sister looks very tired. How long has she been working as a nurse?
    你姊姊看起來好累。她當護理師多久了？
  3. The waiting room is very full. How long have you been waiting for the doctor?
    候診室好多人。你等醫生等多久了？
  4. I've never met the new neighbours. How long have they been living next door?
    我從來沒見過新鄰居。他們住在隔壁多久了？

⑥ 沒有 for 或 since，從上下文看出活動還在繼續

  1. Sorry I'm late. Have you been waiting long?
    抱歉我遲到了，你等很久了嗎？
  2. I've been reading a great book about the history of tea.
    我最近在看一本講茶葉歷史的好書。
  3. She's been preparing for her exams, so she hasn't had much free time.
    她在準備考試，所以沒什麼空。
  4. Our flat is too small for us. We've been thinking of moving to the countryside.
    我們的公寓對我們來說太小了。我們一直在考慮搬到鄉下。
