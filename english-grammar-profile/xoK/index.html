What could have happened?

會是出了什麼事呢？

疑⁠問⁠詞當主⁠詞，後⁠面直⁠接接 could have 和過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

What could have happened?

會是出了什麼事呢？

說話者不知道發生了什麼事。

肯⁠定⁠句和否⁠定⁠句，could 後⁠面⁠接 have

What could have happened?

肯定句

The keys could have dropped out of my pocket.

鑰匙可能從我口袋掉出去了。
否定句

The cat couldn't have climbed that high.

貓不可能爬那麼高。

問⁠句只⁠把 could 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Where could they have gone?

肯⁠定⁠句⁠裡，might have 和 could have 都⁠能推⁠測過⁠去

What could have happened?

might have

He might have missed the train.

他可能沒趕上火車。

否定的 might not have 表示可能沒有發生，couldn't have 表示不可能發生。

例句

① 主詞 + could have + 過去分詞

  1. In fact, it could have been dangerous.
    其實那本來可能很危險。
  2. The thief could have climbed in through the kitchen window.
    小偷可能是從廚房窗戶爬進來的。
  3. We could have stayed at a cheaper hotel.
    我們本來可以住便宜一點的旅館。
  4. She could've called a taxi from the station.
    她本來可以在車站叫計程車的。
  5. Your umbrella could have fallen behind the sofa.
    你的傘可能掉到沙發後面了。
  6. The game could have ended in a draw.
    那場比賽本來可能踢成平手。
  7. They could have sold the car for more money.
    他們本來可以把車賣得貴一點。

② 主詞 + couldn't have + 過去分詞

  1. Our teacher couldn't have picked anyone better to do it.
    我們老師不可能挑到更適合做這件事的人了。
  2. He couldn't have driven home. His car was at the garage.
    他不可能開車回家。他的車在修車廠。
  3. I couldn't have asked for a better birthday.
    這是我過得最好的一次生日。
  4. You couldn't have chosen a worse day for a picnic.
    你挑了一個最不適合野餐的日子。
  5. The dog couldn't have opened the fridge by itself.
    狗不可能自己打開冰箱。
  6. They couldn't have finished the job without a ladder.
    沒有梯子，他們不可能完成那份工作。

③ 疑問詞當主詞 + could have + 過去分詞？

  1. What could have woken the baby up?
    會是什麼把寶寶吵醒了？
  2. Who could have left the gate open?
    大門會是誰沒關的？
  3. What could have made her so angry?
    是什麼事讓她那麼生氣呢？
  4. Who could have written this note?
    這張紙條會是誰寫的？
  5. What could have gone wrong with the plan?
    那個計畫會是哪裡出了錯呢？

④ 疑問詞 + could + 主詞 + have + 過去分詞？

  1. Where could the children have hidden?
    孩子們會躲到哪裡去了？
  2. What could he have meant by that?
    他那句話會是什麼意思呢？
  3. How could she have known my name?
    她怎麼會知道我的名字？
  4. Which train could she have caught?
    她會是搭了哪一班火車？
  5. Where could I have left my glasses?
    我會把眼鏡放到哪裡去了？
  6. How could they have missed such a big sign?
    那麼大的牌子，他們怎麼會沒看到？

⑤ Could + 主詞 + have + 過去分詞？

  1. Could the battery have died?
    會不會是電池沒電了？
  2. Could the shop have closed early?
    那家店會不會提早關門了？
  3. Could you have done anything differently?
    你那時有沒有可能換個做法？
  4. Could she have taken the wrong bus?
    她會不會是搭錯公車了？
  5. Could the parcel have been delivered to our neighbour?
    包裹會不會送到我們鄰居家了？
  6. Could anyone have seen the accident?
    會不會有人看到了那場車禍？
