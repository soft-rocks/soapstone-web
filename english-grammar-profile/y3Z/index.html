The music is going to be loud, since it's rap.

音樂會很大聲，因為是饒舌樂。

說特⁠定⁠的東⁠西，不⁠可⁠數⁠名⁠詞前⁠面加 the

音樂會很大聲，因為是饒舌樂。

說的是那場派對上的音樂。

一⁠般⁠的東⁠西不⁠加 the，特⁠定⁠的東⁠西加 the

The music is going to be loud, since it's rap.

一般

I love music.

我很愛音樂。
特定

The music at the party was great.

派對上的音樂很棒。

有說⁠明、前⁠面提⁠過或雙⁠方都知⁠道，就⁠是特⁠定⁠的東⁠西

The music is going to be loud, since it's rap.

例句

① the + 不可數名詞（雙方都知道）

  1. Bring earplugs to the party. The music is going to be loud, since it's rap.
    帶耳塞去派對吧，音樂會很大聲，因為是饒舌樂。
  2. We went to the beach, and the weather was absolutely fine.
    我們去了海邊，天氣好極了。
  3. Don't drink the water from the tap in this hotel.
    這家飯店的自來水別喝。
  4. The traffic was terrible on the way to the airport.
    去機場的路上交通很糟。
  5. How was the food at your sister's wedding?
    你姊姊婚禮上的食物怎麼樣？

② the + 不可數名詞 + 片語

  1. If you can't find the information about the new art class, please call me.
    如果你找不到新美術課的資料，請打電話給我。
  2. The money in my wallet is for the bus.
    我錢包裡的錢是要搭公車用的。
  3. The coffee at the station café is awful.
    車站咖啡店的咖啡很難喝。
  4. I love the furniture in your living room.
    我很喜歡你客廳的家具。
  5. The advice from your teacher was really useful.
    你老師給的建議很有用。

③ the + 不可數名詞 + 子句

  1. You still haven't paid back the money I lent you last month.
    我上個月借你的錢你還沒還。
  2. The homework we got today is really hard.
    我們今天拿到的功課很難。
  3. The bread you bought yesterday is already dry.
    你昨天買的麵包已經乾了。
  4. The milk I bought this morning smells funny.
    我今天早上買的牛奶聞起來怪怪的。

④ 前面提過，再提時加 the

  1. I bought some cheese and some bread. The cheese was from France.
    我買了一些起司和一些麵包，起司是法國來的。
  2. They gave us some information, but the information was out of date.
    他們給了我們一些資料，可是那些資料過時了。
  3. We ordered rice and fish. The rice was cold.
    我們點了飯和魚，飯是冷的。
  4. My aunt sent me some money. I spent the money on books.
    我阿姨寄了一些錢給我，我把那筆錢拿去買書了。

⑤ 不加 the 和加 the 比較

  1. My dentist says chocolate is bad for my teeth.
    我的牙醫說巧克力對我的牙齒不好。
  2. The chocolate in this box is from Belgium.
    這盒巧克力是比利時的。
  3. Plants need water and light to grow.
    植物需要水和光才能生長。
  4. The water in this lake is very clean.
    這個湖的水很乾淨。

⑥ the + 不可數名詞當受詞

  1. Can you turn down the music? I'm trying to sleep.
    你可以把音樂關小聲一點嗎？我想睡覺。
  2. Please put the luggage in the car.
    請把行李放上車。
  3. Did you check the weather for tomorrow?
    你看過明天的天氣了嗎？
  4. Thanks for the help with my homework.
    謝謝你幫我寫功課。

⑦ the + 不可數名詞在介系詞後面

  1. The kids are swimming in the water near the rocks.
    孩子們在岩石附近的水裡游泳。
  2. There's a hair in the soup!
    湯裡有一根頭髮！
  3. I'm worried about the traffic tonight.
    我擔心今晚的交通。
  4. We waited two weeks for the furniture from the online shop.
    網路商店的家具我們等了兩個禮拜。
  5. We sat in the sunshine all afternoon.
    我們整個下午都坐在陽光下。
