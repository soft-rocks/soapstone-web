When you read a letter, you can almost feel the writer's emotions. In the same way, when you read a book, you can feel the author's emotions.

讀信的時候，你幾乎能感受到寫信人的情緒。同樣地，讀書的時候，你也能感受到作者的情緒。

前一⁠句寫一⁠件⁠事，言談標⁠記引⁠出相⁠似⁠的另一⁠件⁠事

When you read a letter, you can almost feel the writer's emotions. In the same way, when you read a book, you can feel the author's emotions.

茶在中國很受歡迎。同樣地，咖啡在義大利很受歡迎。

言談標記放在句首時，後面加逗號。

常⁠見⁠的表⁠示比⁠較⁠的言談標⁠記

similarly

Boys need sport lessons. Similarly, girls need them.

男孩需要上體育課。同樣地，女孩也需要。
likewise

The staff were friendly. Likewise, the guide was helpful.

員工很友善。同樣地，導遊也很熱心。
in the same way

A plant needs water. In the same way, a child needs love.

植物需要水。同樣地，孩子需要愛。
in a similar way

Birds fly south in winter. In a similar way, some fish swim to warmer seas.

鳥在冬天往南飛。同樣地，有些魚會游到比較溫暖的海域。

言談標⁠記也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞、be 動⁠詞或助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

be 動詞後面

The first test was easy. The second test was similarly easy.

第一次考試很簡單。第二次考試同樣很簡單。
助動詞後面

My parents never watch TV. My grandparents have likewise stopped watching it.

我爸媽從來不看電視。我祖父母同樣也不看了。
主詞後面

Anna uses French at work. Her brother likewise uses French in his job.

Anna 工作時用法語。她弟弟工作時同樣用法語。

放在句中時，similarly 和 likewise 前後可以不加逗號。

分號後⁠面⁠的言談標⁠記，後⁠面加逗⁠號

物價在五月上漲了；同樣地，六月又漲了一次。

原句：in the same way 引⁠出讀⁠書

When you read a letter, you can almost feel the writer's emotions. In the same way, when you read a book, you can feel the author's emotions.

讀信的時候，你幾乎能感受到寫信人的情緒。同樣地，讀書的時候，你也能感受到作者的情緒。

言談標⁠記用副⁠詞 similarly，不⁠用形⁠容⁠詞 similar

Our school has a library. Similar, the town has a big library.

Our school has a library. Similarly, the town has a big library.

in the same way 的 same 前⁠面加 the

When you read a letter, you can almost feel the writer's emotions. In the same way, when you read a book, you can feel the author's emotions.

Cats sleep a lot. In same way, dogs sleep most of the day.

Cats sleep a lot. In the same way, dogs sleep most of the day.

likewise 是副⁠詞，兩⁠個子⁠句之⁠間用分號或句⁠號

The bus was late, likewise the train was late.

The bus was late; likewise, the train was late.

兩⁠件⁠事相⁠反⁠時，用 in contrast

My sister loves cats. Likewise, my brother hates them.

My sister loves cats. In contrast, my brother hates them.

表示比較的言談標記引出相似的事。也可以用 on the other hand。

like 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞片⁠語，Likewise. 可⁠以單⁠獨回話

When you read a letter, you can almost feel the writer's emotions. In the same way, when you read a book, you can feel the author's emotions.

like
介系詞

Like Japan, Korea has four seasons.

韓國跟日本一樣有四季。
Likewise.
回話

"It was nice to meet you." "Likewise."

「很高興認識你。」「我也是。」

例句

① similarly

  1. The company assigned Tom to the sales team. Similarly, it moved Anna to marketing.
    公司把 Tom 派到業務組。同樣地，公司把 Anna 調到行銷部。
  2. Most teachers concur that homework helps students. Similarly, many parents think it is useful.
    大部分老師都同意功課對學生有幫助。同樣地，很多家長也認為功課有用。
  3. The old factory is now defunct. Similarly, the mine near the river closed years ago.
    那座舊工廠現在已經停業了。同樣地，河邊那座礦場好幾年前就關了。
  4. You need a passport to enter the country. Similarly, you need one to leave.
    入境這個國家要護照。同樣地，出境也要護照。
  5. I don't eat meat. Similarly, my wife avoids fish and eggs.
    我不吃肉。同樣地，我太太也不吃魚和蛋。
  6. The number of farmers fell in the 1990s; similarly, fewer people worked in factories.
    1990 年代農民人數減少了；同樣地，在工廠工作的人也變少了。

② likewise

  1. In Nepal, New Year comes in April. Likewise, Thailand celebrates New Year in April.
    尼泊爾的新年在四月。同樣地，泰國也在四月過新年。
  2. The nurses were very compassionate. Likewise, the doctor took time to talk to us.
    護理師都很有同情心。同樣地，醫生也花時間跟我們說明。
  3. Children must wear helmets on bikes. Likewise, adults should wear them on scooters.
    孩子騎腳踏車必須戴安全帽。同樣地，大人騎機車也應該戴。
  4. My brother advocates cycling to work. Likewise, his friends all ride bikes.
    我哥哥主張騎腳踏車上班。同樣地，他的朋友也都騎腳踏車。
  5. The first chapter is quite discursive; likewise, the second chapter moves slowly.
    第一章寫得相當散漫；同樣地，第二章的步調也很慢。
  6. Visitors must not touch the paintings. Likewise, they may not use flash cameras.
    參觀者不可以觸摸畫作。同樣地，也不可以用閃光燈拍照。

③ in the same way

  1. Exercise keeps your body strong. In the same way, reading keeps your mind active.
    運動讓身體強壯。同樣地，閱讀讓頭腦保持活躍。
  2. You wouldn't go into someone's house without asking. In the same way, you shouldn't read their messages without asking.
    你不會沒問過就走進別人家。同樣地，你也不應該沒問過就看別人的訊息。
  3. A tyrant controls people with fear. In the same way, a bad boss can control the staff.
    暴君用恐懼控制人民。同樣地，糟糕的老闆也可能用恐懼控制員工。
  4. Music can make a long drive feel shorter. In the same way, a good book makes a flight pass quickly.
    音樂可以讓長途開車感覺短一點。同樣地，一本好書能讓飛行時間過得很快。
  5. We learn our first language by listening. In the same way, we can learn a second one.
    我們靠聽來學母語。同樣地，我們也可以這樣學第二語言。

④ in a similar way

  1. Bees work together to build a hive. In a similar way, ants build their nests as a team.
    蜜蜂合力建蜂巢。同樣地，螞蟻也一起築巢。
  2. The first film was a big success. In a similar way, the second one sold out in many cinemas.
    第一部電影很成功。同樣地，第二部在很多電影院都賣光了票。
  3. Some people relax by cooking. In a similar way, I relax by working in the garden.
    有些人靠做菜放鬆。同樣地，我靠整理花園放鬆。

⑤ 放在句中

  1. The lake was frozen. The river was similarly covered in ice.
    湖結冰了。河面同樣也結了一層冰。
  2. The first plan failed. The second plan will likewise fail without more money.
    第一個計畫失敗了。沒有更多經費的話，第二個計畫同樣會失敗。
  3. Our team lost last week. We have similarly lost two games this month.
    我們隊上週輸了。這個月我們同樣也輸了兩場。
  4. The manager condemned the decision. The staff likewise refused to accept it.
    經理譴責這個決定。員工同樣拒絕接受。
  5. Older people use the internet more than before. Young children are similarly spending more time online.
    老年人比以前更常上網。小孩子同樣也花更多時間上網。

⑥ 分號後面

  1. The kitchen needs a new floor; similarly, the bathroom needs new tiles.
    廚房需要換新地板；同樣地，浴室也需要換新磁磚。
  2. Students can borrow ten books; likewise, teachers can borrow ten.
    學生可以借十本書；同樣地，老師也可以借十本。
  3. The fans rallied outside the stadium; in the same way, people gathered in the town square.
    球迷聚集在球場外；同樣地，民眾也聚集在鎮上的廣場。
  4. The old system often crashed; in a similar way, the new one stops working twice a week.
    舊系統常常當機；同樣地，新系統一個星期也會停擺兩次。

⑦ 論說文和報告

  1. The number of workers is expected to fall by 2050. Similarly, the share of children is likely to drop to 11.5%.
    到 2050 年，勞動人口預計會減少。同樣地，兒童的比例可能會降到 11.5%。
  2. Vietnam celebrates New Year according to the lunar calendar. Likewise, Korea uses the lunar calendar for its New Year.
    越南依照農曆過新年。同樣地，韓國也依照農曆過新年。
  3. A good teacher listens to students. In the same way, a good manager listens to the staff.
    好老師會傾聽學生的話。同樣地，好主管也會傾聽員工的話。
