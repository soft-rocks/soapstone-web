How will you get here?

情⁠態動⁠詞放⁠在主⁠詞前⁠面，後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

你要怎麼過來這裡？

不⁠同⁠的情⁠態動⁠詞問不⁠同⁠的事

例句

① 疑問詞 + will + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. There's no bus to my village. How will you get here on Saturday?
    沒有公車到我們村子。你星期六要怎麼過來？
  2. We're having a picnic. What will you bring?
    我們要去野餐。你會帶什麼？
  3. You've sent your application. When will they tell you their decision?
    你已經寄出申請了。他們什麼時候會告訴你結果？
  4. You don't know anyone in Glasgow. Where will you sleep?
    你在格拉斯哥一個人都不認識。你要睡哪裡？
  5. Who will look after the dog while you're away?
    你不在的時候，誰會照顧狗？

② 疑問詞 + can + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. I'm stuck on this question. What can I do?
    我卡在這一題。我可以怎麼辦？
  2. "Hello, City Bank. How can I help you?" "I've lost my card."
    您好，城市銀行。有什麼需要幫忙的嗎？我的卡弄丟了。
  3. I'd love to see your new baby. When can I visit you?
    我很想看看你的新寶寶。我什麼時候可以去看你？
  4. I'm free most of next week. Which afternoon can you come round?
    我下星期大部分時間都有空。你哪天下午可以過來？
  5. My phone doesn't work in this country. Where can I get a local SIM?
    我的手機在這個國家不能用。我可以在哪裡買到當地的 SIM 卡？
  6. The new sofa has arrived, and it's heavy. Who can help me move it?
    新沙發送到了，很重。誰可以幫我搬？

③ 疑問詞 + should + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. It's my first job interview. What should I wear?
    這是我第一次面試。我應該穿什麼？
  2. The living room is already full. Where should we put the new bookcase?
    客廳已經滿了。新書架應該放在哪裡？
  3. I've never made rice before. How long should I cook it?
    我從來沒煮過飯。要煮多久？
  4. Who should I talk to about my bill?
    我的帳單問題應該找誰？
  5. When should we leave for the airport?
    我們應該什麼時候出發去機場？

④ 疑問詞 + shall + I / we + 原形動詞

  1. We've got the whole evening free. What shall we do?
    我們整個晚上都有空。我們做什麼好？
  2. Where shall we meet, at the station or the café?
    我們在哪裡見面，車站還是咖啡店？
  3. I'd love to help you paint the kitchen. What time shall I arrive on Sunday?
    我很樂意幫你漆廚房。我星期天幾點到好？
  4. How shall we pay, cash or card?
    我們要怎麼付，現金還是刷卡？

⑤ 疑問詞 + could / would + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Excuse me. Where could I find a taxi?
    不好意思。我可以在哪裡叫到計程車？
  2. Welcome! Come in and sit down. What would you like to drink?
    歡迎！進來坐。你想喝什麼？
  3. Which day would suit you best?
    哪一天對你最方便？
  4. How could I get to the museum from here?
    我從這裡要怎麼去博物館？
  5. Why would anyone want to live there?
    誰會想住在那裡？

⑥ Why + can't / won't + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. Why can't I go to the party? Everyone else is going.
    為什麼我不能去派對？其他人都要去。
  2. Why won't the car start? I filled it with petrol yesterday.
    車子為什麼發不動？我昨天才加滿油。

⑦ How much / How many / How long + 情態動詞

  1. My car needs new tyres. How much will that set me back?
    我的車要換新輪胎。那要花我多少錢？
  2. We're expecting a big crowd. How many people can the hall hold?
    我們預計會來很多人。這個大廳可以容納多少人？
  3. I have a train at four. How long will your talk last?
    我四點要搭火車。你的演講會講多久？
  4. How far can you swim without stopping?
    你不停下來可以游多遠？
