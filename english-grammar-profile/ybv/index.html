主⁠詞 + must have + 過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

must have、過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

must have過去一定。說話者很確定。
過去分詞過去一定發生的事。

過⁠去⁠的推⁠論

I must have fallen about fifty times.

例句

I realised he must have forgotten.

我發現他一定是忘了。

I realised 或 I thought 加 must have，說當時推論出的事。

have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

推⁠論過⁠去用 must have 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

I must have fallen about fifty times.

He hasn't called me back. He must forgot my number.

He hasn't called me back. He must have forgotten my number.

例句

① 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞：推論過去一定發生了

  1. Skiing was hard at the beginning. I must have fallen about fifty times.
    一開始滑雪很難。我一定跌了大概五十次。
  2. The grass is wet this morning. It must have rained last night.
    今天早上草是濕的。昨晚一定下過雨。
  3. Anna's coat isn't on the hook. She must have left early.
    Anna 的外套不在掛鉤上。她一定提早走了。
  4. You look really tired. You must have worked very hard this week.
    你看起來好累。你這個星期一定工作得很辛苦。
  5. The back door is open and the cat is outside. Someone must have forgotten to close it.
    後門開著，貓跑到外面了。一定有人忘了關門。
  6. Tom answered every question about the story. He must have read the book.
    Tom 答出了故事的每一個問題。他一定讀過那本書。

② I realised / thought + 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞

  1. He didn't come to the café. When I realised he must have forgotten, I called him up.
    他沒來咖啡館。我發現他一定是忘了，就打電話給他。
  2. My keys weren't in my bag when I got home. I thought I must have left them at work.
    我回家時鑰匙不在包包裡。我以為我一定把鑰匙留在公司了。
  3. When the lights went out, we realised the storm must have damaged the power line.
    燈熄的時候，我們發現暴風雨一定弄壞了電線。
  4. The bus went past the airport. I realised I must have taken the wrong one.
    公車開過了機場。我發現我一定搭錯車了。
  5. Her grandson never got her birthday card. She thought it must have got lost in the post.
    她孫子一直沒收到她寄的生日卡。她以為卡片一定是寄丟了。
  6. After twenty minutes we were still on the motorway. We realised we must have missed the turning.
    二十分鐘後我們還在高速公路上。我們發現一定是錯過了那個轉彎。

③ 主詞 + must have been + 形容詞：推論過去的感覺

  1. I heard you won the singing competition. You must have been so happy!
    聽說你贏了歌唱比賽。你一定很高興！
  2. A big dog jumped at my little sister in the park. She must have been scared.
    公園裡有一隻大狗撲向我妹妹。她一定很害怕。
  3. You camped at the top of Ben Nevis in March? It must have been cold on the mountain.
    你三月在 Ben Nevis 山頂露營？山上一定很冷。
  4. My grandparents drove from London to Edinburgh in one day. They must have been tired.
    我爺爺奶奶一天之內從倫敦開車到愛丁堡。他們一定很累。
  5. The test must have been difficult. Nobody in the class passed.
    那次考試一定很難。班上沒有人及格。
  6. You saw the Northern Lights in Norway? It must have been wonderful.
    你在挪威看到了北極光？那一定很美好。

④ 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞：推論數量

  1. My feet really hurt after the city tour. I must have walked twenty kilometres today.
    城市導覽之後，我的腳好痛。我今天一定走了二十公里。
  2. Look at all those plates in front of Jack. He must have eaten six pieces of cake.
    你看 Jack 面前那些盤子。他一定吃了六塊蛋糕。
  3. The buses were on strike, and we didn't know. We must have waited two hours at the bus stop.
    公車在罷工，我們卻不知道。我們一定在公車站等了兩個小時。
  4. My daughter knows every line of Harry Potter. She must have read it ten times.
    我女兒會背《哈利波特》的每一句話。她一定讀了十遍。
  5. Where were you? I must have called you a hundred times!
    你去哪裡了？我一定打給你一百次了！
  6. The wedding was in a castle, with three hundred guests. They must have spent a lot of money.
    婚禮在一座城堡裡辦，有三百位客人。他們一定花了很多錢。

⑤ 主詞 + must have + 過去分詞：推論原因

  1. I was away for two weeks, and now the plants are dead. You must have forgotten to water them.
    我出門兩個星期，現在植物都死了。你一定忘了澆水。
  2. My stomach hurts after lunch. I must have eaten something bad.
    午餐後我肚子痛。我一定吃了不乾淨的東西。
  3. The car won't start this morning. The battery must have died in the cold.
    車子今天早上發不動。電瓶一定是在冷天裡沒電了。
  4. Mia didn't say a word at dinner. Something must have happened at school.
    Mia 晚餐時一句話都沒說。學校裡一定發生了什麼事。
  5. The town centre is empty this morning. Everyone must have gone to the sale at the new mall.
    今天早上市中心沒有人。大家一定都去新購物中心搶特價了。
  6. There's sand all over the back seat. The kids must have been to the beach.
    後座到處都是沙子。孩子們一定去過海灘。
  7. The kitchen floor is wet near the dog's bowl. The dog must have knocked over its water.
    廚房地板在狗碗旁邊濕了。狗一定打翻了牠的水。
