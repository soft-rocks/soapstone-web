Is it possible to use the prize next year, or must I cancel my trip?

這個獎品可以留到明年再用嗎？還是我得取消去印度的旅行？

Must + I / we + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞 + …？

must I / must we我／我們必須……嗎？
疑問詞問細節，像 when、where、how many。

寫⁠信詢⁠問規⁠定

Is it possible to use the prize next year, or must I cancel my trip?

例句

By what date must I reply?

我必須在哪一天前回覆？

正式的信件常用 must I 問規定、條件和截止日期。

must 放⁠在句⁠首，不⁠用 do

Is it possible to use the prize next year, or must I cancel my trip?

I'm applying for a library card. Do I must bring a photo?

I'm applying for a library card. Must I bring a photo?

疑⁠問⁠詞後⁠面先放 must，再放主⁠詞

Is it possible to use the prize next year, or must I cancel my trip?

Where I must sign?

Where must I sign?

mustn't 是「不⁠可⁠以」

"Must I pay now?" "No, you mustn't pay now."

"Must I pay now?" "No, you don't have to."

例句

① …, or must I + 原形動詞？：問有沒有其他選擇

  1. My sister is getting married that week. Is it possible to use the prize next year, or must I cancel my trip?
    我妹妹那個星期結婚。可以明年再用這個獎嗎，還是我必須取消這趟旅行？
  2. I've broken my leg and can't fly to the conference. Can my colleague come instead, or must I go myself?
    我摔斷了腿，不能飛去開會。可以讓我同事代替我去嗎，還是我必須自己去？
  3. The blue sofa I ordered from your shop looks grey in my room. Can I change the colour, or must I keep this one?
    我在你們店裡訂的藍色沙發放在我房間看起來是灰色的。我可以換顏色嗎，還是我必須留著這張？
  4. My meeting in York ends at nine in the evening. Is there a later train, or must I stay the night?
    我在 York 的會議晚上九點才結束。有晚一點的火車嗎，還是我必須住一晚？
  5. I missed the March exam because I was ill. Can I take it in June, or must I wait until next year?
    我三月生病，錯過了考試。我可以六月考嗎，還是我必須等到明年？
  6. We're booking a holiday at your hotel. Can the children share our room, or must we book two?
    我們要訂你們飯店的假期。孩子們可以跟我們住同一間嗎，還是我們必須訂兩間？

② how much / how many + must I + 原形動詞？

  1. Meals are not included in the price. How much money must I bring for food each day?
    價格不包含餐費。我每天必須帶多少錢吃飯？
  2. I'm applying for a student visa. How many photos must I send with the application?
    我在申請學生簽證。申請時我必須寄幾張照片？
  3. I'd like to join the school orchestra. How many hours a week must I practise?
    我想加入學校管弦樂團。我一個星期必須練習幾個小時？
  4. I'd like to rent the flat from the first of May. How much must I pay as a deposit?
    我想從五月一日開始租這間公寓。我必須付多少訂金？
  5. Our literature course starts in September. How many books must we read before then?
    我們的文學課九月開始。在那之前我們必須讀幾本書？
  6. Thank you for explaining the knee operation, doctor. How long must I stay in hospital after it?
    醫生，謝謝您說明膝蓋手術的事。手術後我必須住院多久？

③ when / where / what + must I + 原形動詞？

  1. Thank you for your letter about the summer course. By what date must I send back the form?
    謝謝您寄來暑期課程的信。我必須在哪一天前寄回表格？
  2. I bought two tickets for the concert online. Where must I go to collect them?
    我在網路上買了兩張音樂會的票。我必須去哪裡拿票？
  3. I've just opened an account with your bank. What must I do if I lose my card?
    我剛在你們銀行開了帳戶。如果我的卡掉了，我必須怎麼做？
  4. Our flight home is at eight in the evening. When must we leave the room on the last day?
    我們回程的班機是晚上八點。最後一天我們必須什麼時候退房？
  5. The shoes I bought from your website are too small. Who must I speak to about a refund?
    我在你們網站買的鞋子太小了。退款的事我必須跟誰談？
  6. I'm getting my degree next month. What must I wear for the ceremony?
    我下個月拿學位。參加典禮我必須穿什麼？

④ Must I / we + 原形動詞？：問是不是必須

  1. I'm planning a holiday in Vietnam. Must I have a visa to visit your country?
    我計畫去越南度假。去你們國家我必須有簽證嗎？
  2. We've booked a cottage for a week in August. Must we bring our own sheets and towels?
    我們訂了八月一週的小屋。我們必須自己帶床單和毛巾嗎？
  3. I'm staying at the hotel for a week. Must I be a member to use the pool?
    我要在飯店住一個星期。我必須是會員才能用游泳池嗎？
  4. The museum costs ten pounds for adults. Must we pay for the children too?
    博物館大人要十英鎊。小孩我們也必須付錢嗎？
  5. I'm going away on Friday. Must I return the library book in person?
    我星期五要出門。我必須親自還圖書館的書嗎？
  6. The wedding starts at ten. Must we arrive before nine?
    婚禮十點開始。我們必須九點前到嗎？

⑤ Must I really + 原形動詞？：不想做的時候

  1. Must I really go to Ben's party tonight? I'm so tired.
    我今晚真的一定要去 Ben 的派對嗎？我好累。
  2. Must we really leave now? The film isn't over.
    我們真的一定要現在走嗎？電影還沒演完。
  3. I'm full, Mum. Must I really eat all these vegetables?
    媽，我吃飽了。我真的一定要吃完這些蔬菜嗎？
  4. Everyone at school wears new jackets. Must I really wear this old one?
    學校的每個人都穿新外套。我真的一定要穿這件舊的嗎？
  5. We saw Aunt Mary last weekend. Must we really visit her again this weekend?
    我們上個週末才去看過瑪麗阿姨。這個週末我們真的又要去看她嗎？
  6. My friends are waiting for me at the park. Must I really tidy my room today?
    我朋友在公園等我。我今天真的一定要整理房間嗎？
  7. The cable car to the top is closed. Must we really walk all the way up the hill?
    上山的纜車停駛了。我們真的一定要一路走上山嗎？
