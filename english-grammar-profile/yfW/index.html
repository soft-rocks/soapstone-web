When I was a child, I used to go to a small school and I enjoyed it very much.

我小時候念的是一所規模很小的學校。

主⁠詞 + used to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

used to、didn't use to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

used to過去常常，現在不再。
didn't use to過去不會，現在會。
原形動詞過去重複的動作或狀態。

和 since 一⁠起說改⁠變

例句

I didn't use to laugh much, but since we met, I'm always happy.

我以前不太笑，可是自從我們認識以後，我總是很快樂。

didn't use to … but since … 說從某個時候起，情況改變了。

只發⁠生一⁠次⁠的事用過⁠去⁠式，不⁠用 used to

When I was a child, I used to go to a small school and I enjoyed it very much.

Yesterday I used to go to the park.

Yesterday I went to the park.

現⁠在⁠的習⁠慣用現⁠在⁠式

When I was a child, I used to go to a small school and I enjoyed it very much.

I used to go to the gym every day now.

I go to the gym every day now.

used to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

When I was a child, I used to go to a small school and I enjoyed it very much.

She used to came to my house.

She used to come to my house.

例句

① When I was a child, I used to + 原形動詞：童年的回憶

  1. When I was a child, I used to go to a small village school, and I enjoyed it very much.
    我小時候念一所鄉下的小學校，而且我非常喜歡。
  2. When I was a child, I used to spend every summer at my grandparents' farm.
    我小時候每年夏天都在我祖父母的農場過。
  3. When I was a child, I used to climb the big tree in our garden.
    我小時候常常爬我們花園裡那棵大樹。
  4. When I was a child, I used to believe that the moon followed our car.
    我小時候以為月亮會跟著我們的車子走。
  5. When I was a child, I used to hide under the bed during storms.
    我小時候暴風雨的時候都躲在床底下。
  6. When I was a child, I used to draw pictures on the walls.
    我小時候常在牆上畫畫。

② 主詞 + used to + come / visit + …：以前常來往的人

  1. My best friend used to come to my house and play computer games with me every Saturday.
    我最好的朋友以前每個星期六都來我家跟我打電動。
  2. My uncle used to visit us every Christmas with a bag full of presents.
    我叔叔以前每年聖誕節都帶著一袋禮物來看我們。
  3. Our neighbour used to bring us fresh eggs from her chickens.
    我們的鄰居以前會帶她的雞生的新鮮雞蛋給我們。
  4. My grandmother used to stay with us for a month every summer.
    我奶奶以前每年夏天都來我們家住一個月。
  5. The postman used to stop and chat with my mother.
    郵差以前會停下來跟我媽媽聊天。
  6. My cousins used to come over every Sunday for lunch.
    我的表親以前每個星期天都來吃午餐。

③ 主詞 + didn't use to + 原形動詞, but since + 子句：自從某時改變了

  1. I didn't use to laugh much, but since the day we met, I'm always happy.
    我以前不太笑，可是自從我們認識那天起，我總是很快樂。
  2. I didn't use to sleep well, but since I started yoga, I sleep like a baby.
    我以前睡不好，可是自從我開始做瑜伽，我睡得像嬰兒一樣。
  3. She didn't use to go out much, but since she got a dog, she walks every day.
    她以前不常出門，可是自從她養了狗，她每天都去散步。
  4. We didn't use to eat together, but since Dad changed jobs, we have dinner as a family.
    我們以前不一起吃飯，可是自從爸爸換了工作，我們全家一起吃晚餐。
  5. He didn't use to like school, but since he joined the band, he loves it.
    他以前不喜歡學校，可是自從他加入樂團，他很喜歡。
  6. I didn't use to cook, but since I moved out, I cook every night.
    我以前不煮飯，可是自從我搬出來住，我每天晚上都煮飯。

④ 主詞 + used to + 原形動詞：過去的工作和生活

  1. My mother used to work as a nurse before I was born.
    我媽媽在我出生前是護理師。
  2. People used to wash their clothes by hand in the river.
    人們以前在河裡手洗衣服。
  3. My grandfather used to walk five miles to work every day.
    我爺爺以前每天走五英里去上班。
  4. Children used to leave school at fourteen.
    孩子們以前十四歲就離開學校了。
  5. We used to keep chickens in the back garden.
    我們以前在後院養雞。
  6. My parents used to run a small hotel by the sea.
    我爸媽以前在海邊經營一家小旅館。

⑤ 主詞 + used to + 原形動詞 + 頻率：以前多常做

  1. I used to go swimming three times a week.
    我以前一個星期去游泳三次。
  2. We used to visit the seaside every August.
    我們以前每年八月都去海邊。
  3. She used to phone her mother every evening at seven.
    她以前每天傍晚七點都打電話給她媽媽。
  4. He used to play tennis twice a week with his brother.
    他以前一個星期跟他哥哥打兩次網球。
  5. They used to have a party every New Year's Eve.
    他們以前每年除夕夜都辦派對。
  6. I used to read a book a week when I was at school.
    我上學的時候以前一個星期讀一本書。
  7. My dad used to take us fishing every Sunday morning.
    我爸爸以前每個星期天早上都帶我們去釣魚。
