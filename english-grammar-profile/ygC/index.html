I'm doing a lot of great leisure activities.

我最近參加了很多很棒的休閒活動。

進⁠行⁠式說最⁠近反⁠覆⁠做、只維⁠持一⁠段時⁠間⁠的事

我最近參加了很多很棒的休閒活動。

暫⁠時⁠的習⁠慣用進⁠行⁠式，一⁠直都⁠有⁠的習⁠慣用現⁠在簡⁠單式

I'm doing a lot of great leisure activities.

一直都有

I go swimming every Saturday.

我每個星期六都去游泳。
暫時

I'm going swimming every Saturday this summer.

今年夏天我每個星期六都去游泳。

暫⁠時反⁠覆⁠的動⁠作用現⁠在進⁠行⁠式

I'm doing a lot of great leisure activities.

These days I take the train to work because my car is broken.

These days I'm taking the train to work because my car is broken.

長⁠久⁠的事⁠實用現⁠在簡⁠單式

I'm doing a lot of great leisure activities.

The earth is going round the sun once a year.

The earth goes round the sun once a year.

現⁠在進⁠行⁠式要⁠有 am、is 或 are

I'm doing a lot of great leisure activities.

She running every morning this month.

She's running every morning this month.

例句

① 最近常做的活動

  1. Hi Max! Life here is great. I'm doing a lot of great leisure activities.
    嗨 Max！這裡的生活很棒。我最近參加了很多很棒的休閒活動。
  2. I'm playing this exciting game called Diner Dash. It's about running a restaurant.
    我最近在玩一個很刺激的遊戲，叫 Diner Dash。它是在講經營一家餐廳。
  3. I'm going to yoga twice a week at the moment. I feel so much better.
    我最近一個星期去上兩次瑜伽。我覺得舒服多了。
  4. Builders are redoing our kitchen, so we're eating out a lot these days.
    工人在重新裝修我們家廚房，所以我們最近常在外面吃。
  5. My dad is cycling to work this month to get fit.
    我爸這個月騎腳踏車上班，想把身體練好。
  6. I'm a bit worried. The kids are watching too much TV during the holidays.
    我有點擔心。放假的時候孩子們看太多電視了。
  7. I'm reading a chapter of my book every night before bed. It helps me sleep.
    我最近每晚睡前都看一章書。這樣比較好睡。
  8. My sister is practising the piano for three hours a day before her concert.
    我姊姊音樂會前每天練三小時鋼琴。

② 暫時的通勤和工作安排

  1. My car is broken, so I'm taking the train to work these days.
    我的車壞了，所以我最近搭火車上班。
  2. She's working late most evenings because of the big project.
    因為那個大案子，她最近大部分的晚上都加班。
  3. I'm walking the dog for my neighbour while she's in hospital.
    我鄰居住院的這段時間，我幫她遛狗。
  4. Our teacher is giving us a test every Friday this term.
    我們老師這學期每個星期五都考試。
  5. He's driving his daughter to school every day until her ankle heals.
    他女兒的腳踝好之前，他每天開車送她上學。
  6. We're meeting every Monday to plan the school fair.
    我們每個星期一都開會籌備園遊會。

③ 最近反覆發生的問題

  1. I have to see the doctor because I'm having some problems with my back.
    我得去看醫生，因為我的背最近有點問題。
  2. I'm getting a lot of headaches these days. Maybe I need glasses.
    我最近常頭痛。我可能需要戴眼鏡了。
  3. My laptop is crashing every time I open a big file.
    我的筆電最近每次打開大檔案就當機。
  4. The baby is waking up three or four times a night at the moment.
    寶寶最近一晚會醒來三四次。
  5. I'm so tired. Our neighbours are having a lot of parties this summer.
    我好累。我們鄰居今年夏天常開派對。
  6. I'm not sleeping well these days. I keep thinking about work.
    我最近睡不好。我一直在想工作的事。

④ 新的習慣

  1. I'm trying to drink more water, so I'm carrying a bottle everywhere.
    我想多喝水，所以最近走到哪都帶著水壺。
  2. She's cooking at home more often to save money.
    她為了省錢，最近比較常在家煮飯。
  3. Mum is taking dance classes every Thursday. She loves them.
    媽媽最近每個星期四都去上舞蹈課。她很喜歡。
  4. My grandparents live nearby now, so I'm spending more time with them.
    我爺爺奶奶現在住在附近，所以我最近常花時間陪他們。
  5. He's going to bed earlier because he starts work at six now.
    他現在六點就要上班，所以最近比較早睡。
  6. We're speaking only English at dinner to help the kids practise.
    為了讓孩子練習，我們最近吃晚餐的時候只講英文。

⑤ 疑問句

  1. I haven't seen you there. Are you still going to the gym every morning?
    我都沒在那裡看到你。你最近還是每天早上去健身房嗎？
  2. You look so relaxed. What are you doing in your free time these days?
    你看起來好放鬆。你最近空閒的時候都在做什麼？
  3. I saw his boots in the car. Is your son still playing football on Saturdays?
    我在車上看到他的球鞋。你兒子最近星期六還在踢足球嗎？
  4. I see your light on at five. Why are you getting up so early these days?
    我看到你五點就開燈了。你最近為什麼起那麼早？
