Besides, you're 18 years old, and you aren't a baby.

再說，你都 18 歲了，又不是小嬰兒。

前一⁠句說一⁠件⁠事，言談標⁠記補上另一⁠件⁠事

Besides, you're 18 years old, and you aren't a baby.

這家飯店離海灘很近。此外，它還有一座大泳池。

言談標記放在句首時，後面加逗號。

moreover、furthermore 和 in addition 多⁠用在正⁠式⁠的書⁠面文⁠字

moreover

The plan is too expensive. Moreover, it would take years.

這個計畫太貴了。而且要花好幾年。
furthermore

The course is free. Furthermore, students get a certificate.

這門課免費。此外，學生還能拿到證書。
in addition

The flat has two bedrooms. In addition, there is a small garden.

這間公寓有兩間臥室。此外，還有一個小花園。

besides 和 what is more 也引⁠出補⁠充⁠的資⁠訊

besides

I don't want to go out. Besides, it's raining.

我不想出門。再說，外面在下雨。
what is more

The town is beautiful. What is more, the people are very friendly.

這個小鎮很漂亮。而且，那裡的人很友善。
what's more

Cycling is good for your health. What's more, it's free.

騎腳踏車對身體好。而且還不用錢。

besides 多用在口語，常引出更重要的理由。what is more 在口語裡常縮寫成 what's more。

分號後⁠面⁠的言談標⁠記，後⁠面加逗⁠號

房間很髒；而且員工很沒禮貌。

moreover 和 furthermore 可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞或 be 動⁠詞後⁠面

be 動詞後面

The new system is, furthermore, much easier to use.

此外，新系統用起來也簡單得多。
主詞後面

The museum, moreover, is free on Sundays.

而且，那間博物館星期天免費。

放在句中時，前後加逗號。

原句：Besides 補上更重⁠要⁠的理⁠由

Besides, you're 18 years old, and you aren't a baby.

再說，你都 18 歲了，又不是小嬰兒。

說話的人先說了一個理由，再用 Besides 補上更重要的理由。

言談標⁠記是 besides，不⁠是介⁠系⁠詞 beside

Besides, you're 18 years old, and you aren't a baby.

I'm too tired to cook. Beside, there's no food in the fridge.

I'm too tired to cook. Besides, there's no food in the fridge.

in addition to 後⁠面要接名⁠詞片⁠語或動⁠名⁠詞

The job is well paid. In addition to, it's close to my home.

The job is well paid. In addition, it's close to my home.

what is more 縮⁠寫成 what's more，不⁠能拿⁠掉 is

The phone is cheap. What more, it has a great camera.

The phone is cheap. What's more, it has a great camera.

moreover 是副⁠詞，兩⁠個子⁠句之⁠間用分號或句⁠號

The flat is cheap, moreover it is near the station.

The flat is cheap; moreover, it is near the station.

besides 和 in addition to 後⁠面⁠接名⁠詞片⁠語⁠時是介⁠系⁠詞

besides
介系詞

Besides English, she speaks French and Italian.

除了英文，她還會說法文和義大利文。
in addition to
名詞片語

In addition to the main course, we ordered two desserts.

除了主菜，我們還點了兩份甜點。
in addition to
動名詞

In addition to working full-time, he looks after his mother.

除了全職工作，他還要照顧他媽媽。

例句

① moreover

  1. The new law is arbitrary. Moreover, it will be hard to enforce.
    這條新法律很武斷。而且很難執行。
  2. Exercise improves your mood. Moreover, it helps you sleep better.
    運動能改善心情。而且能幫助你睡得更好。
  3. The evidence is tenuous. Moreover, the main witness has changed her story twice.
    證據很薄弱。而且主要證人已經改了兩次說法。
  4. The company failed to pay its staff; moreover, it lied to the bank.
    那家公司沒付員工薪水；而且還對銀行說謊。

② furthermore

  1. Online banking is fast. Furthermore, you can use it at any time of day.
    網路銀行很快。此外，一天當中任何時間都能用。
  2. The town needs a new hospital. Furthermore, the old one is too far from the centre.
    這個鎮需要一間新醫院。此外，舊醫院離市中心太遠了。
  3. The report is superficial. Furthermore, it contains several mistakes.
    這份報告很膚淺。此外，裡面還有好幾個錯誤。
  4. We have enough money for the project; furthermore, the council has offered to help.
    我們有足夠的經費做這個計畫；此外，市議會也表示願意幫忙。

③ in addition

  1. The course is cheap. In addition, the teachers are very experienced.
    這門課很便宜。此外，老師都很有經驗。
  2. You will need a passport. In addition, you must bring two photos.
    你需要護照。此外，你還必須帶兩張照片。
  3. The hotel offers free breakfast. In addition, guests can use the gym.
    飯店提供免費早餐。此外，房客還可以用健身房。
  4. Our new manager is friendly. In addition, she's very good at facilitating meetings.
    我們的新經理很友善。此外，她很會主持會議。

④ besides

  1. I don't want to see that film. Besides, I've got work to do.
    我不想看那部電影。再說，我還有工作要做。
  2. We can't afford a new car. Besides, the old one still works.
    我們買不起新車。再說，舊的還能開。
  3. Don't worry about the test. Besides, it's only a practice one.
    別擔心考試。再說，那只是練習考。
  4. I'm not lending him money again. Besides, he never paid me back last time.
    我不會再借他錢了。再說，他上次也沒還我。
  5. It's too late to call her now. Besides, she's probably asleep.
    現在打給她太晚了。再說，她大概已經睡了。

⑤ what is more 和 what's more

  1. The food was delicious. What is more, the prices were very reasonable.
    菜很好吃。而且，價格很合理。
  2. She speaks four languages. What is more, she learned them all as an adult.
    她會說四種語言。而且，她全都是長大以後才學的。
  3. The tent is easy to put up. What's more, it only weighs two kilos.
    這頂帳篷很好搭。而且，只有兩公斤重。
  4. He was late again. What's more, he didn't even say sorry.
    他又遲到了。而且，他連對不起都沒說。
  5. The flat is in the centre; what's more, it's quite cheap.
    那間公寓在市中心；而且還滿便宜的。

⑥ 放在句中

  1. The new plan is quicker. It is, moreover, much cheaper than the old one.
    新計畫比較快。而且，它比舊的便宜得多。
  2. The office is on the fifth floor. The building, furthermore, has no lift.
    辦公室在五樓。此外，那棟大樓沒有電梯。
  3. The new drug works quickly. It is, furthermore, safe for children.
    這種新藥見效很快。此外，它對兒童也很安全。
  4. Life there is easy for older people. The village, moreover, has its own doctor.
    那裡的生活對老人家很方便。而且，那個村子有自己的醫生。

⑦ 論說文和正式信件

  1. Public transport reduces traffic. Moreover, it is better for the environment.
    大眾運輸能減少車流。而且對環境比較好。
  2. I have five years of experience in sales. In addition, I speak fluent Spanish.
    我有五年的業務經驗。此外，我的西班牙語很流利。
  3. Many students cannot afford the fees. Furthermore, there are not enough places at the university.
    很多學生付不起學費。此外，大學的名額也不夠。
  4. The library is too small. In addition, it closes too early for working people.
    圖書館太小了。此外，它關門的時間太早，上班的人來不及去。
  5. Volunteering helps others. What is more, it teaches young people new skills.
    當志工能幫助別人。而且，能讓年輕人學到新技能。
  6. Children who read every day write better. Furthermore, they tend to do better at school.
    每天閱讀的孩子寫作比較好。此外，他們在學校的表現往往也比較好。
