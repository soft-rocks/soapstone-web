We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

我們即將在公司導入新的識別證系統。

be about to 說即⁠將發⁠生⁠的事

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

我們即將在公司導入新的識別證系統。

準備已經做完，新系統很快就會開始。

just 放⁠在 be 和 about to 中⁠間，時⁠間更⁠近

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

about to
即將

The winter sales are about to start.

冬季特賣即將開始。
just about to
下一刻就要

We are just about to launch a new snack for children.

我們正要推出一款兒童點心。

公⁠司和團⁠體常⁠用 be about to 宣⁠布即⁠將推⁠出⁠的措⁠施

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

Our school is about to celebrate its hundredth birthday.

我們學校即將慶祝創校一百週年。

The bank is about to raise its interest rates.

銀行即將調高利率。

just about to 常⁠用來說⁠明現⁠在不⁠能做別⁠的事

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

我正要去洗澡，我等一下回電給你好嗎？

just 放⁠在 be 後⁠面、about to 前⁠面

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

We're just about to leave.

about 後⁠面要⁠有 to

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

The game is about to start.

about to 前⁠面用 be，不⁠用 will

We're about to introduce a new identity card system in our company.

We will about to launch a new product.

We are about to launch a new product.

例句

① 主詞 + am / is / are + about to + 原形動詞

  1. Our club is about to elect a new chairman.
    我們社團即將選出新的主席。
  2. The hospital is about to open a new children's ward.
    醫院即將開設新的兒童病房。
  3. The airline is about to start flights to Vietnam.
    這家航空公司即將開航飛越南的航線。
  4. Our neighbours are about to have their third child.
    我們的鄰居快要生第三個孩子了。
  5. The city is about to open its first underground line.
    這座城市即將開通第一條地鐵線。
  6. My sister is about to become a doctor.
    我姊姊快要當上醫生了。
  7. The government is about to change the tax rules.
    政府即將修改稅務規定。
  8. The factory is about to close for good.
    這間工廠即將永久關閉。
  9. You're about to meet the most important people in the company.
    你即將見到公司裡最重要的人。
  10. A new chapter of my life is about to begin.
    我人生的新篇章即將開始。
  11. The professor is about to publish her treatise on Chinese poetry.
    教授即將出版她那部關於中國詩的專論。
  12. Prices are about to go up again.
    物價又要漲了。
  13. The two companies are about to join together.
    這兩家公司即將合併。
  14. We're about to move into a bigger office.
    我們即將搬進更大的辦公室。
  15. The winter sales are about to start.
    冬季特賣即將開始。
  16. The two sides are about to sign a peace agreement.
    雙方即將簽署和平協議。

② 主詞 + am / is / are + just about to + 原形動詞

  1. We are just about to launch a new snack for children.
    我們正要推出一款兒童點心。
  2. I'm just about to start a meeting. Can I text you later?
    我正要開會，我晚一點傳訊息給你好嗎？
  3. The doctor is just about to see you.
    醫生馬上就要幫你看診了。
  4. She's just about to leave for the airport.
    她正要出發去機場。
  5. We're just about to sit down to dinner.
    我們正要坐下來吃晚餐。
  6. Hold on, I'm just about to find out.
    等一下，我馬上就知道了。
  7. The show is just about to begin, so turn off your phone.
    節目馬上就要開始了，把手機關掉。
  8. The kids are just about to go to bed.
    孩子們正要上床睡覺。
  9. I'm just about to finish this chapter.
    我這一章馬上就讀完了。
  10. He's just about to call you back.
    他正要回你電話。
  11. The plane is just about to touch down.
    飛機正要著陸。
  12. The guests are just about to arrive.
    客人馬上就要到了。
  13. I'm just about to lock up the shop.
    我正要鎖店門。
  14. The runners are just about to cross the finish line.
    跑者們馬上就要衝過終點線了。
  15. We're just about to board, so I have to go.
    我們正要登機，我得走了。
