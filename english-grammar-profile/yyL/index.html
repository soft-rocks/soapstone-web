I had no clothes to wear to John's party, so I went shopping yesterday.

我沒有衣服可以穿去 John 的派對，所以我昨天去逛街了。

no 放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面，動⁠詞用肯⁠定⁠的形⁠式

我沒有衣服可以穿去 John 的派對，所以我昨天去逛街了。

no + 名⁠詞和 not any + 名⁠詞⁠的意⁠思一⁠樣

I had no clothes to wear to John's party, so I went shopping yesterday.

no

Our street has no trees.

我們這條街沒有樹。
not any
說話時比較常用

Our street doesn't have any trees.

我們這條街一棵樹都沒有。

there is 和 there are 後⁠面常接 no + 名⁠詞

I had no clothes to wear to John's party, so I went shopping yesterday.

這棟大樓沒有電梯。

no 已⁠經表⁠示否⁠定，動⁠詞不⁠加 not

I had no clothes to wear to John's party, so I went shopping yesterday.

We don't have no bread for breakfast.

We have no bread for breakfast.

動詞加 not 時，名詞前面用 any。

名⁠詞前⁠面用 no，不⁠用 not

I had no clothes to wear to John's party, so I went shopping yesterday.

There is not cinema in our town.

There is no cinema in our town.

no 後⁠面⁠的名⁠詞不⁠加 a

I had no clothes to wear to John's party, so I went shopping yesterday.

We have no a garden.

We have no garden.

例句

① 主詞 + 動詞 + no + 複數名詞

  1. I had no shoes for the wedding, so I borrowed my sister's.
    我沒有鞋子可以穿去婚禮，所以借了我姊姊的。
  2. The new flat is lovely, but it has no curtains yet.
    新公寓很漂亮，可是還沒有窗簾。
  3. We've got no eggs, so I can't make pancakes.
    我們沒有蛋了，所以我沒辦法做鬆餅。
  4. I have no plans for the weekend. Do you want to do something?
    我週末沒有計畫。你想做點什麼嗎？
  5. My grandfather had no brothers or sisters.
    我爺爺沒有兄弟姊妹。
  6. The teacher gave us no instructions, so we didn't know what to do.
    老師沒給我們任何說明，所以我們不知道要做什麼。

② 主詞 + 動詞 + no + 不可數名詞

  1. I bought new shoes, and now I have no money until Friday.
    我買了新鞋，現在到星期五之前都沒錢了。
  2. Sorry, I've got no time to talk today. I'm late for work.
    抱歉，我今天沒時間講話。我上班要遲到了。
  3. Sorry about the cold shower. The hotel has no hot water this morning.
    冷水澡真抱歉。飯店今天早上沒有熱水。
  4. He had no idea where his keys were.
    他完全不知道鑰匙在哪裡。
  5. We had no electricity for two days after the storm.
    暴風雨過後，我們有兩天沒電。
  6. My grandma takes her tea with no sugar.
    我奶奶喝茶不加糖。

③ 主詞 + 動詞 + no + 單數名詞

  1. My brother has no car, so he takes the bus everywhere.
    我哥哥沒有車，所以去哪裡都搭公車。
  2. This room has no window. It's very dark.
    這個房間沒有窗戶。很暗。
  3. She has no job at the moment, but she's looking.
    她目前沒有工作，可是在找。

④ there is / there are + no + 名詞

  1. There's no milk in the fridge. Can you buy some?
    冰箱裡沒有牛奶了。你可以去買一點嗎？
  2. There are no buses on Sunday mornings in our village.
    我們村子星期天早上沒有公車。
  3. There was no answer when I called the office.
    我打電話到辦公室，沒有人接。
  4. There were no seats left, so we stood at the back.
    沒有位子了，所以我們站在後面。
  5. There's no room in the car for another bag.
    車上沒有空間再放一個袋子了。
  6. There is no swimming pool in the hotel, but there's a beach nearby.
    飯店沒有游泳池，可是附近有海灘。

⑤ no + 名詞當主詞

  1. No students came to class on the day of the storm.
    暴風雨那天，沒有學生來上課。
  2. No buses run on Christmas Day, so we'll have to walk.
    聖誕節那天沒有公車，所以我們得走路。
  3. No tickets are left for Saturday's game.
    星期六的比賽已經沒有票了。

⑥ 告示裡的 No + 名詞 / 動詞-ing

  1. No cycling on the footpath.
    人行道上禁止騎腳踏車。
  2. No smoking anywhere in the station.
    車站內全面禁菸。
  3. No entry. Staff only.
    禁止進入。限員工。

⑦ no 和 not any 對比

  1. I have no brothers. I don't have any sisters either.
    我沒有哥哥弟弟。我也沒有姊姊妹妹。
  2. There's no bread left, and there isn't any rice.
    麵包吃完了，米也沒有了。
  3. We had no rain in July, and we didn't have any in August.
    七月沒下雨，八月也沒有。
  4. She made no mistakes in the test, but I didn't get any right.
    她考試一個錯都沒有，可是我一題都沒答對。
