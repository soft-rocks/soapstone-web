Not knowing North London very well, I took quite a long time to get there.

我對北倫敦不太熟，所以花了好一段時間才到那裡。

Not 加動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 放⁠在句⁠首，說⁠明原⁠因

我對北倫敦不太熟，所以花了好一段時間才到那裡。

I 也是不熟悉北倫敦的人。

Not 常接 knowing、wanting、being sure 這⁠類動⁠詞

Not knowing North London very well, I took quite a long time to get there.

wanting

Not wanting to be late, I took a taxi.

我不想遲到，所以搭了計程車。
being sure

Not being sure of the answer, she said nothing.

她不確定答案，所以什麼都沒說。

之⁠前沒⁠做⁠的⁠事，用 Not having 加過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

Not knowing North London very well, I took quite a long time to get there.

Not having

Not having eaten all day, I was starving by dinner time.

我一整天都沒吃東西，到晚餐時間已經餓壞了。

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 子⁠句⁠的否⁠定用 Not，不⁠用 Don't

Not knowing North London very well, I took quite a long time to get there.

Don't knowing what to do, I called my sister.

Not knowing what to do, I called my sister.

Not 放⁠在動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 前⁠面

Not knowing North London very well, I took quite a long time to get there.

Knowing not the way, we got lost.

Not knowing the way, we got lost.

動⁠詞⁠-⁠ing 的動⁠作者要⁠是主⁠要⁠子⁠句⁠的主⁠詞

Not knowing North London very well, I took quite a long time to get there.

Not knowing the area, the streets confused me.

Not knowing the area, I soon got lost.

不熟悉這一區的是 I。

例句

① Not knowing

  1. Not knowing anyone in the city, I felt very lonely at first.
    我在這個城市誰都不認識，一開始覺得很孤單。
  2. Not knowing the rules, he made a lot of mistakes.
    他不懂規則，所以犯了很多錯。
  3. Not knowing what to say, she just smiled.
    她不知道該說什麼，就只是笑了笑。
  4. Not knowing the language, we used a translation app.
    我們不會說當地的語言，就用翻譯應用程式。
  5. Not knowing how long the meeting would last, I cancelled my dinner plans.
    我不知道會議會開多久，就取消了晚餐的計畫。
  6. Not knowing his phone number, I sent him an email.
    我不知道他的電話，就寄了電子郵件給他。

② Not wanting

  1. Not wanting to wake my parents, I took off my shoes at the door.
    我不想吵醒我爸媽，就在門口脫了鞋。
  2. Not wanting to waste food, she took the leftovers home.
    她不想浪費食物，就把剩菜帶回家。
  3. Not wanting to seem rude, he ate everything on his plate.
    他不想顯得沒禮貌，就把盤子裡的東西全吃了。
  4. Not wanting to miss the start, we ran all the way to the cinema.
    我們不想錯過開頭，就一路跑到電影院。
  5. Not wanting any trouble, the shop owner gave him a refund.
    店老闆不想惹麻煩，就退錢給他了。

③ Not being / Not understanding

  1. Not being a morning person, I always set three alarms.
    我不是早起的人，所以都會設三個鬧鐘。
  2. Not understanding the question, he asked the teacher again.
    他聽不懂問題，又問了老師一次。
  3. Not being very tall, she sat in the front row.
    她個子不高，所以坐在第一排。
  4. Not understanding the instructions, we built the shelf upside down.
    我們看不懂說明，把架子裝反了。
  5. Not being able to drive, my grandmother takes the bus everywhere.
    我奶奶不會開車，到哪裡都搭公車。

④ Not having + 過去分詞

  1. Not having slept well, I found it hard to concentrate.
    我沒睡好，很難專心。
  2. Not having read the book, I couldn't join the discussion.
    我沒讀過那本書，沒辦法加入討論。
  3. Not having seen her for years, I didn't recognize her.
    我好幾年沒見到她，認不出她來。
  4. Not having booked a table, we had to wait an hour.
    我們沒訂位，只好等了一個小時。
  5. Not having heard the alarm, she missed her first class.
    她沒聽到鬧鐘，錯過了第一堂課。

⑤ 不同的情境

  1. Not having any cash, I paid by card.
    我身上沒現金，就刷卡付了。
  2. Not liking crowds, we went to the museum on a weekday.
    我們不喜歡人多，就挑平日去博物館。
  3. Not feeling well, he left the party early.
    他不太舒服，就提早離開了派對。
  4. Not expecting visitors, I was still in my pajamas.
    我沒料到會有客人，還穿著睡衣。
  5. Not trusting the weather, she packed an umbrella.
    她不太相信天氣會好，就帶了一把傘。
  6. Not needing the car that day, I lent it to my neighbor.
    我那天不需要車，就借給了鄰居。
  7. Not being gregarious by nature, he found parties exhausting.
    他天生不愛交際，覺得參加派對很累。
  8. Not realizing the time, we talked until midnight.
    我們沒注意到時間，聊到了半夜。
  9. Not wishing to disturb you, I left a note on your desk.
    我不想打擾你，就在你桌上留了張字條。
