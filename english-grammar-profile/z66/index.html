例句

① 聽到好消息

  1. "I've got a new job." "That's fantastic!"
    我找到新工作了。太好了！
  2. "We're going to have a baby." "That's wonderful news!"
    我們要有寶寶了。真是好消息！
  3. "My sister is coming to visit." "That's nice."
    我姊姊要來看我。真好。
  4. "I got an A in my English exam." "That's brilliant!"
    我英文考試拿了 A。太厲害了！
  5. "The rain has stopped." "That's good. Let's go out."
    雨停了。很好，我們出去吧。

② 聽到壞消息

  1. "I lost my wallet on the bus." "Oh, that's terrible!"
    我的錢包在公車上掉了。喔，真糟糕！
  2. "The concert was cancelled." "That's a shame."
    演唱會取消了。真可惜。
  3. "I failed my driving test again." "Oh no, that's too bad."
    我駕照又沒考過。喔不，真遺憾。
  4. "My grandmother is in hospital." "I'm sorry. That's awful."
    我奶奶住院了。我很遺憾，真不好受。
  5. "We missed the train by a minute." "That's so annoying!"
    我們差一分鐘沒趕上火車。真氣人！

③ 回應提議

  1. "Why don't we eat outside?" "That's a great idea!"
    我們何不在外面吃？好主意！
  2. "Let's ask Mr Brown for help." "Yes, that's a good plan."
    我們去請布朗老師幫忙吧。對，這個計畫不錯。
  3. "We could go to the beach on Saturday." "That's perfect! I'm free then."
    我們星期六可以去海邊。太好了！我那天有空。
  4. "Maybe we should leave early." "That's true. The roads will be busy."
    也許我們應該早點出發。沒錯，路上會很塞。
  5. "Shall I bring some drinks?" "That's very kind of you."
    要我帶點飲料嗎？你真好。

④ 表示驚訝

  1. "He's only fourteen and he's at university." "That's amazing!"
    他才十四歲就上大學了。太驚人了！
  2. "The shop gave me the shoes for free." "Really? That's strange."
    店家把鞋子免費送我。真的嗎？好奇怪。
  3. "Tickets are two hundred pounds each." "That's crazy!"
    票一張兩百英鎊。太誇張了！
  4. "She swam across the lake in winter." "That's incredible."
    她冬天游過了那座湖。真不可思議。
  5. "I saw a fox in the garden." "That's unusual."
    我在花園裡看到一隻狐狸。這很少見。

⑤ 回應別人的幫忙

  1. "I've washed the dishes for you." "Oh, that's lovely. Thanks."
    我幫你洗好碗了。喔，你真好，謝謝。
  2. "I'll drive you to the airport." "That's really helpful."
    我開車送你去機場。這真的幫了大忙。
  3. "Here's your umbrella. You left it in class." "That's great, thank you!"
    這是你的傘，你忘在教室了。太好了，謝謝！
  4. "I'm sorry I'm late." "That's OK. We haven't started yet."
    抱歉我遲到了。沒關係，我們還沒開始。
  5. "Do you need any more help?" "No, that's fine, thanks."
    你還需要幫忙嗎？不用了，這樣就好，謝謝。

⑥ 朋友間的對話

  1. "I'm going to Italy next month." "That's exciting! Where are you going?"
    我下個月要去義大利。真令人興奮！你要去哪裡？
  2. "I've started doing yoga." "That's interesting. Do you like it?"
    我開始做瑜伽了。真有意思，你喜歡嗎？
  3. "I bought this jacket for ten pounds." "That's cheap!"
    這件夾克我十英鎊買的。真便宜！
  4. "I have to work all weekend." "That's not fair!"
    我整個週末都要工作。這不公平！
  5. "My brother can speak five languages." "That's cool!"
    我哥哥會說五種語言。好酷！
  6. "Let's meet at the café at six." "OK, that's fine with me."
    我們六點在咖啡店見。好，我可以。
