It is almost certain that we'll have fights in the future, but we will probably overcome them.

我們將來幾乎一定會吵架，不過我們大概都能克服。

It + is + 程⁠度副⁠詞 + 形⁠容⁠詞 + that + 子⁠句

程⁠度副⁠詞、形⁠容⁠詞

It is almost certain that we'll have fights in the future, but we will probably overcome them.

程度副詞放在形容詞前面，調整程度。
形容詞表示可能性或把握，像 certain、probable、possible、likely、true。

放⁠在括⁠號或補⁠充說⁠明⁠裡

例句

If there is a strike (which is quite probable), you will have to take a taxi.

如果有罷工（這相當有可能），你就得搭計程車。

程度副詞加形容詞常放在補充說明裡。

程⁠度副⁠詞放⁠在形⁠容⁠詞前⁠面

It is certain almost that he will come.

It is almost certain that he will come.

修⁠飾形⁠容⁠詞要⁠用副⁠詞 fairly

It is fair likely that prices will rise.

It is fairly likely that prices will rise.

is 後⁠面⁠接形⁠容⁠詞 certain

It is almost certain that we'll have fights in the future, but we will probably overcome them.

It is almost certainly that we'll win.

It is almost certain that we'll win.

例句

① It is + almost / nearly + 形容詞 + that + 子句

  1. It is almost certain that the price of petrol will go up.
    油價幾乎一定會上漲。
  2. It is nearly certain that the shop will raise its prices.
    那家店幾乎一定會漲價。
  3. It is almost certain that the meeting will run over time.
    會議幾乎一定會超過時間。
  4. It's almost impossible that nobody saw the accident.
    幾乎不可能沒有人看到那場意外。
  5. It is nearly certain that the team will go down this season.
    這支隊伍這一季幾乎一定會降級。
  6. It's almost certain that she'll get the job.
    她幾乎一定會得到那份工作。
  7. It's nearly certain that our flight will leave late tonight.
    我們今晚的班機幾乎一定會晚起飛。

② It is + quite / fairly + 形容詞 + that + 子句

  1. It is quite probable that the flight will be delayed.
    班機相當有可能會延誤。（被動）
  2. It's fairly likely that we'll need more chairs.
    我們還算有可能需要更多椅子。
  3. It is quite possible that he forgot to send the email.
    他相當有可能忘了寄那封電子郵件。
  4. It's fairly certain that the shop will close next year.
    那家店明年會關門，這一點還算確定。
  5. It is quite likely that the rain will stop by noon.
    雨中午前停的可能性相當高。
  6. It is fairly clear that the plan needs more work.
    這個計畫還需要再改，這點還算清楚。
  7. It's quite possible that I'm wrong about this.
    這件事我相當有可能弄錯了。
  8. It's reasonably certain that the bridge will open in May.
    那座橋五月會通車，這一點還算確定。

③ 主詞 + is + 程度副詞 + 形容詞

  1. If the shop is closed, which is fairly likely on a Sunday, we'll go to the market.
    如果那家店沒開（星期天還算有可能），我們就去市場。
  2. The results, which are fairly predictable, will come out next week.
    結果相當容易預測，下星期會公布。
  3. A small delay is quite likely, so leave early.
    有一點延誤相當有可能，所以早點出發。
  4. His story is fairly believable.
    他的說法還算可信。
  5. A change in the timetable is quite possible.
    時刻表相當有可能會改。
  6. Her answer was rather surprising.
    她的回答有點令人意外。
  7. The weather forecast is fairly accurate this week.
    這週的天氣預報還算準。

④ It is + not entirely / not completely + 形容詞 + that + 子句

  1. It is not entirely clear that the new system is better.
    新系統比較好這一點並不完全清楚。
  2. It's not completely certain that the concert will go ahead.
    音樂會不完全確定會照常舉行。
  3. It is not entirely true that all teenagers are lazy.
    所有青少年都很懶，這並不完全正確。
  4. It's not entirely impossible that they will change their minds.
    他們改變主意並不是完全不可能。
  5. It is not completely clear who will pay for the repairs.
    誰要付修理費並不完全清楚。
  6. It is not entirely certain that the company made a profit.
    那家公司有沒有獲利並不完全確定。

⑤ 程度副詞加形容詞加 probably：更委婉

  1. It's almost certain that we'll disagree, but we will probably find a solution.
    我們幾乎一定會意見不合，不過我們大概會找到解決辦法。
  2. It is quite likely that the train will be late, so we'll probably take a taxi.
    火車相當有可能會誤點，所以我們大概會搭計程車。
  3. It is fairly certain that it will rain, so the party will probably move indoors.
    會下雨這一點還算確定，所以派對大概會移到室內。
