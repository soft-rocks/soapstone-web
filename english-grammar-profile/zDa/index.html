主⁠詞 + has to / have to + be …

It's got to be incredible to see a castle rising from the sea.

has to be / has got to be現在一定是。
had to be過去一定是。用在故事和敘述。
had to have + 過去分詞一定已經發生了。
be推論常用 be，也可以用其他動詞。

和 must 比⁠較

must

You must be tired.

你一定累了。
have got to

You've got to be joking!

你一定是在開玩笑！

have to 和 have got to 推論的意思跟 must 差不多，語氣更強，比較口語。

過⁠去⁠的推⁠論

例句

It had to have been Peter.

那一定是彼得。

說過去的推論，可以用 had to be 或 had to have 加過去分詞。

has got 後⁠面要接 to 和原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

It's got to be incredible to see a castle rising from the sea.

It's got be the wrong key.

It's got to be the wrong key.

had to have 後⁠面⁠接過⁠去⁠分⁠詞

She had to have leave already.

She had to have left already.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形 be

It's got to be incredible to see a castle rising from the sea.

There has to is a mistake.

There has to be a mistake.

例句

① has / have got to be：現在一定是

  1. Look at the queue. That new café has got to be good.
    你看那個隊伍。那家新咖啡店一定很好。
  2. You've got to be joking! You paid two hundred pounds for those shoes?
    你一定是在開玩笑！你花兩百英鎊買那雙鞋？
  3. This has got to be the best pizza in town.
    這一定是城裡最好吃的披薩。
  4. She's got to be over ninety, but she still walks every day.
    她一定超過九十歲了，可是她還是每天散步。
  5. There's got to be a better way to do this.
    一定有比較好的方法做這件事。
  6. That's got to be his brother. They look exactly the same.
    那一定是他哥哥。他們長得一模一樣。

② has / have to be：現在一定是

  1. The keys aren't in my bag, so they have to be in the car.
    鑰匙不在我包包裡，所以一定在車上。
  2. There has to be a mistake. I didn't order three pizzas.
    一定弄錯了。我沒有點三個披薩。
  3. He works seven days a week. He has to be exhausted.
    他一星期工作七天。他一定累壞了。
  4. With all those lights on, they have to be at home.
    燈都開著，他們一定在家。
  5. This has to be the right street. There's the church on the corner.
    這一定是對的街。轉角那裡就是教堂。
  6. The answer has to be in the second chapter.
    答案一定在第二章。

③ had to be：過去一定是

  1. My mother never visits without phoning first. There had to be something else going on.
    我媽媽從來不會沒打電話就來。一定有別的事。
  2. The door was open when we got home. Someone had to be inside.
    我們回家的時候門開著。一定有人在裡面。
  3. The test was easy for everyone, so the answers had to be on the internet.
    那次考試大家都覺得很簡單，所以答案一定在網路上。
  4. He knew all about the party. Somebody had to be telling him our plans.
    他知道派對的一切。一定有人把我們的計畫告訴他。
  5. It was so quiet in the house. The children had to be asleep.
    屋子裡好安靜。孩子們一定睡著了。
  6. She spoke perfect French, so she had to be from Paris.
    她的法文說得很好，所以她一定是巴黎人。

④ had to have + 過去分詞：一定已經發生了

  1. I saw a tall man with red hair at the window. Yes, I was sure. It had to have been Peter.
    我看到窗邊有一個紅頭髮的高個子。對，我很確定。那一定是彼得。
  2. The cake was gone. The dog had to have eaten it.
    蛋糕不見了。一定是狗吃掉的。
  3. Her bike wasn't outside, so she had to have left already.
    她的腳踏車不在外面，所以她一定已經走了。
  4. The road was wet. It had to have rained in the night.
    路是濕的。夜裡一定下過雨。
  5. Only two people had the key, so one of them had to have opened the safe.
    只有兩個人有鑰匙，所以一定是其中一個人打開了保險箱。
  6. He knew the answer before I told him. He had to have seen the list.
    我還沒告訴他，他就知道答案了。他一定看過那份名單。

⑤ has to have + 過去分詞：現在推論過去

  1. The letter isn't here. It has to have got lost in the post.
    信不在這裡。一定是郵寄的時候弄丟了。
  2. She speaks Japanese very well. She has to have lived in Japan.
    她的日文說得很好。她一定在日本住過。
  3. The window is broken. Someone has to have thrown a stone.
    窗戶破了。一定有人丟了石頭。
  4. They aren't answering. They have to have gone out.
    他們沒接電話。他們一定出去了。
  5. The milk smells bad. It has to have been in the fridge for weeks.
    牛奶有臭味。它一定在冰箱裡放了好幾個星期。
  6. He looks so happy. He has to have passed his exam.
    他看起來好高興。他一定通過考試了。
  7. My phone isn't in my pocket. I have to have left it at the cafe.
    我的手機不在口袋裡。我一定把它留在咖啡館了。
