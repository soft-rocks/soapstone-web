Not only 放⁠在句⁠首，have 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

我不但從十歲起就一直在它的街道上閒逛，

說話者應徵導遊。

but ... also 加⁠上第⁠二件事

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

也慢慢了解了它的歷史。

not only 不⁠在句⁠首，不⁠倒裝

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

I have not only been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

我不但從十歲起就一直在它的街道上閒逛，也慢慢了解了它的歷史。

第⁠二件事⁠的時⁠態不⁠用跟第一⁠件⁠事一⁠樣

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

My wife has decided to go back to university. Not only have I been expecting it, but I will also support her.

Not only 在句⁠首，要倒⁠裝

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

Not only I have been working here for ten years, but I have also trained new staff.

Not only have I been working here for ten years, but I have also trained new staff.

只⁠有 have 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

Not only have I been studying French, but I have also lived in Paris.

but 後⁠面⁠的子⁠句不⁠倒裝

Not only has she been teaching for years, but has she also written two books.

Not only has she been teaching for years, but she has also written two books.

現⁠在完⁠成簡⁠單式說做⁠完⁠的事

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

連⁠接兩⁠個詞⁠時不⁠在句⁠首，不⁠倒裝

Not only have I been wandering its streets since I was ten, but I have also come to know its history.

我阿姨在羅馬當導遊。她不但會說法文，也會說義大利文。

例句

① Not only have I been + 動詞-ing

  1. I'd like to be the new shop manager. Not only have I been working here for twelve years, but I have also trained most of the new staff.
    我想當新的店長。我不但在這裡工作了十二年，大部分的新員工也是我訓練的。
  2. I'm a good choice for the Madrid office. Not only have I been studying Spanish for three years, but I have also lived in Madrid.
    我很適合去馬德里的辦公室。我不但學了三年西班牙文，也在馬德里住過。
  3. Ask me anything about the band. Not only have I been playing in it since school, but I also write most of our songs.
    樂團的事儘管問我。我不但從學生時代就一直在裡面演奏，我們大部分的歌也是我寫的。
  4. Sorry I missed your party. Not only have I been looking after my father, but I have also been running the shop.
    抱歉我沒去你的派對。我不但一直在照顧我爸爸，也一直在顧店。
  5. I'm ready to leave home. Not only have I been saving for a year, but I have also found a cheaper flat.
    我準備好搬出去住了。我不但存了一年的錢，也找到一間比較便宜的公寓。
  6. Thank you for the job offer. Not only have I been expecting this offer for months, but I am also ready to start next week.
    謝謝您給我這份工作。我不但等這個工作機會等了好幾個月，下禮拜也可以馬上開始。

② Not only have we / they been + 動詞-ing

  1. We'd like to speak to the manager. Not only have we been waiting for an hour, but nobody has told us why.
    我們想跟經理談談。我們不但等了一個小時，也沒有人跟我們說原因。
  2. The village is angry with the builders. Not only have they been building flats on the field, but they have also closed the footpath.
    村民很氣那些建商。他們不但一直在那塊空地上蓋公寓，也把步道封起來了。
  3. Our club had a busy year. Not only have we been raising money for the hospital, but we have also organised visits for the patients.
    我們社團今年很忙。我們不但一直在幫醫院募款，也安排了探望病人的活動。
  4. We've called the council. Not only have our neighbours been playing loud music every night, but they have also blocked our drive.
    我們打電話給市政府了。我們的鄰居不但每天晚上大聲放音樂，也擋住了我們的車道。
  5. The new fees have made people angry. Not only have the students been protesting all week, but they have also written to the minister.
    新的學費讓大家很生氣。學生們不但抗議了一整個禮拜，也寫信給部長了。

③ Not only has he / she / it been + 動詞-ing

  1. Mrs Clark is the best person for the prize. Not only has she been teaching for twenty years, but she has also written three textbooks.
    克拉克老師是最適合得獎的人。她不但教了二十年的書，也寫了三本教科書。
  2. I need to talk to you about Mark. Not only has he been arriving late, but he has also missed two meetings.
    我得跟你談談馬克的事。他不但一直遲到，也錯過了兩次會議。
  3. My son loves animals. Not only has he been helping at the animal shelter, but he has also adopted a dog from it.
    我兒子很愛動物。他不但一直在動物收容所幫忙，也從那裡領養了一隻狗。
  4. Please send someone to the flat. Not only has the heating been making a strange noise, but it has also stopped working twice.
    請派人來公寓一趟。暖氣不但一直發出奇怪的聲音，也壞了兩次。
  5. The workers are worried about their jobs. Not only has the company been losing money, but it has also lost its best managers.
    員工很擔心工作不保。這家公司不但一直在虧錢，也失去了最好的幾位經理。
  6. My wife's exam is next week. Not only has she been feeling nervous, but she has also been sleeping badly.
    我太太下禮拜要考試。她不但一直很緊張，也一直睡不好。

④ 正式的信：應徵和推薦

  1. Not only have I been volunteering at the festival for five years, but I have also managed the ticket office.
    我不但在這個音樂節當了五年志工，也管理過售票處。
  2. Not only has Ms Lee been working in our team since 2019, but she has also led two major projects.
    李小姐不但從二〇一九年起就在我們的團隊工作，也帶過兩個大型專案。
  3. Not only have I been coaching children's football, but I have also completed a first aid course.
    我不但一直在教小朋友踢足球，也上完了急救課程。
  4. Not only has he been running the club's website, but he has also organised all our events.
    他不但一直在管社團的網站，也籌辦了我們所有的活動。
  5. Not only have I been reading your magazine for years, but I have also visited most of the places in it.
    我不但看你們的雜誌看了好幾年，雜誌裡的地方我也大多去過。

⑤ 正式的信：投訴

  1. Not only have your workers been leaving rubbish in my garden, but they have also damaged my fence.
    你們的工人不但一直把垃圾丟在我的花園，也弄壞了我的圍籬。
  2. Not only have I been waiting for my refund for six weeks, but I have also had no reply to my emails.
    我不但等退款等了六個禮拜，我寄的電子郵件也一直沒有回覆。
  3. Not only has the noise been keeping us awake, but it has also upset our baby.
    那個噪音不但吵得我們睡不著，也把我們的寶寶吵得很不安。
  4. Not only have the trains been running late, but the prices have also gone up.
    火車不但一直誤點，票價也漲了。

⑥ 演講和報告

  1. Not only have we been reducing our energy use, but we have also planted two thousand trees.
    我們不但一直在減少用電，也種了兩千棵樹。
  2. Not only has the town been growing fast, but it has also become more expensive.
    這個小鎮不但發展得很快，也變得比較貴了。
  3. Not only have scientists been studying this problem for decades, but they have also found some answers.
    科學家不但研究這個問題研究了幾十年，也找到了一些答案。
  4. Not only have prices been rising, but wages have also fallen.
    物價不但一直在漲，薪水也降了。
  5. Not only has our team been training harder, but it has also won its last five games.
    我們隊不但練得比以前更勤，最近五場比賽也都贏了。
