I think we are all very concerned about the present situation.

我想我們都很擔心現在的情況。

present、future、former 只放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

I think we are all very concerned about the present situation.

present（現在的）

We've hired five new people this year. Our present office is too small.

我們今年請了五個新人。我們現在的辦公室太小了。
future（未來的）

What are your future plans?

你未來有什麼計畫？
former（以前的）

He's a former student of mine.

他是我以前的學生。

present 放⁠在 be 後⁠面，意⁠思是⁠在場

I think we are all very concerned about the present situation.

名詞前面

The present manager is very kind.

現任經理人很好。
be 後面

All the parents were present at the meeting.

所有的家長都出席了會議。

說現⁠在⁠的用 present，不⁠用 now

I think we are all very concerned about the present situation.

We are worried about the now situation.

We are worried about the present situation.

former 放⁠在名⁠詞前⁠面

I think we are all very concerned about the present situation.

We visited our school former last week.

We visited our former school last week.

future 加單⁠數名⁠詞，前⁠面要⁠有 my 這⁠類字

I think we are all very concerned about the present situation.

I want to meet future wife soon.

I want to meet my future wife soon.

例句

① present + 名詞

  1. Orders have dropped by half this year. The present situation at the factory is not good.
    今年的訂單少了一半。工廠目前的情況不太好。
  2. The old library burned down in 1985. The present building was finished in 1990.
    舊的圖書館在 1985 年燒掉了。現在的建築是 1990 年完工的。
  3. My grandparents sold that house long ago. Who is the present owner of the house?
    我爺爺奶奶很久以前就賣掉那棟房子了。這棟房子現在的屋主是誰？
  4. I'm sorry, sir. At the present time, we have no free rooms.
    先生，很抱歉。目前我們沒有空房。
  5. Why change the way we book holidays? The present system works well.
    為什麼要改我們排休假的方式？目前的制度運作得很好。
  6. We've hired five new people this year. Our present office is too small.
    我們今年請了五個新人。我們現在的辦公室太小了。

② future + 名詞

  1. Ken talked about his future plans at dinner.
    晚餐時 Ken 聊到他未來的計畫。
  2. Thank you for the talk about your new book. We'd like to hear about your future projects.
    謝謝你介紹你的新書。我們想聽聽你未來的計畫。
  3. This is my future husband, Tom.
    這是我未來的老公，Tom。
  4. Don't spend all your first salary. Save some money for future needs.
    別把第一份薪水全部花掉。存點錢以備將來之需。
  5. The company wants to open shops in Japan. It is thinking about its future growth.
    公司想在日本開店。它在考慮未來的發展。
  6. Let's plant trees along the river. Future generations will thank us.
    我們沿著河邊種樹吧。後代會感謝我們。

③ former + 名詞

  1. Mrs. Lin is a former colleague of my father's.
    林太太是我爸爸以前的同事。
  2. The guide showed us the big room upstairs. In former times, people held dances in this hall.
    導遊帶我們看樓上的大房間。以前人們會在這個大廳辦舞會。
  3. Our hotel has classrooms for bedrooms. It's in a former school.
    我們的飯店用教室當臥室。它開在一所以前的學校裡。
  4. Guess who I saw this morning? I met my former boss at the station.
    猜猜我今天早上看到誰？我在車站遇到我以前的老闆。
  5. My friends moved out of the village in 2015. Their former house is now a café.
    我朋友們 2015 年搬出那個村子。他們以前住的房子現在是一家咖啡店。
  6. Our university opened a new hall on Monday. The former president gave a speech.
    我們大學星期一啟用了一棟新大樓。前任總統發表了演說。

④ the present + 名詞 和 be present

  1. Mr Brown retired two years ago. The present head teacher started last year.
    布朗先生兩年前退休了。現任校長是去年上任的。
  2. Nobody was ill this morning. Everyone in the class was present today.
    今天早上沒有人生病。今天班上每個人都到了。
  3. The director's name isn't in the notes. Was she present at the meeting?
    筆記裡沒有主任的名字。她有出席會議嗎？
  4. Let's wait until the sales to buy a sofa. The present prices are too high.
    我們等到打折再買沙發吧。現在的價格太高了。
  5. The trains were late that morning. Only ten people were present when the meeting started.
    那天早上火車誤點。會議開始的時候，只有十個人在場。

⑤ 不同的句型

  1. Anna got married again last year. Do you know her former husband?
    安娜去年又結婚了。你認識她的前夫嗎？
  2. I've changed jobs. Please send future emails to my new address.
    我換工作了。以後的電子郵件請寄到我的新地址。
  3. They stopped making cars in our town in 2000. The former factory is now a museum.
    我們鎮上 2000 年就不再生產汽車了。以前的工廠現在是博物館。
  4. The house is cheap, but the roof is old. We need to think about future costs.
    那棟房子很便宜，可是屋頂很舊。我們要考慮將來的費用。
  5. You didn't like your last job at the bank. Is your present job interesting?
    你不喜歡你上一份在銀行的工作。你現在的工作有趣嗎？
  6. I've got three wedding invitations this month. My former classmates are getting married.
    我這個月收到三張喜帖。我以前的同學都要結婚了。
  7. My sister and her fiancé are building a house. Their future home will be near the sea.
    我姊姊和她的未婚夫在蓋房子。他們未來的家會在海邊。
