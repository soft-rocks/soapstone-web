Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

因此，參觀動物園可以幫助人們認識大自然。

前一⁠句寫原⁠因，言談標⁠記引⁠出結⁠論

Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

動物園教孩子認識野生動物。因此，參觀動物園可以幫助人們認識大自然。

言談標記放在句首時，後面加逗號。

therefore、thus、hence 和 as a result 也引⁠出結⁠論

therefore

The hall is small. Therefore, we can only invite fifty guests.

會場很小。因此，我們只能邀請五十位客人。
thus

The new road is shorter. Thus, the trip takes twenty minutes less.

新的路比較短。因此，路程少了二十分鐘。
hence

The software is free. Hence, many schools use it.

這套軟體是免費的。因此，很多學校都用它。
as a result

The factory closed. As a result, two hundred people lost their jobs.

工廠關了。結果，兩百個人失業。

言談標⁠記也⁠可⁠以放⁠在主⁠詞或助⁠動⁠詞後⁠面

Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

主詞後面

The results, therefore, are not reliable.

因此，這些結果並不可靠。
助動詞後面

We have therefore decided to change the date.

因此，我們決定改日期。

放在句中時，言談標記前後可以加逗號，也可以不加。

分號後⁠面⁠的言談標⁠記，後⁠面加逗⁠號

Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

票一小時就賣完了；因此，很多球迷進不了場。

只⁠有 hence 後⁠面可⁠以直⁠接接名⁠詞片⁠語

這家飯店就在海邊，所以價格才這麼高。

therefore 是副⁠詞，兩⁠個子⁠句之⁠間用分號或句⁠號

The train was late, therefore we missed the meeting.

The train was late; therefore, we missed the meeting.

consequently 後⁠面不⁠接 of，名⁠詞片⁠語前⁠面用 as a result of

Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

Consequently of the strike, the trains stopped.

As a result of the strike, the trains stopped.

as a result 的 result 前⁠面加 a

As result, we cancelled the meeting.

As a result, we cancelled the meeting.

so 是連⁠接⁠詞，可⁠以用逗⁠號連⁠接兩⁠個子⁠句

Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

so
連接詞

It started to rain, so we finished our picnic in the car.

開始下雨了，所以我們在車上把野餐吃完。
therefore
副詞

The train was late; therefore, we missed the meeting.

火車誤點；因此，我們錯過了會議。

as a result of 後⁠面⁠接原⁠因，as a result 引⁠出結⁠果

Consequently, visiting zoos can help people learn about nature.

as a result of
原因

As a result of the storm, the school closed for two days.

因為暴風雨，學校停課兩天。
as a result
結果

The factory closed. As a result, two hundred people lost their jobs.

工廠關了。結果，兩百個人失業。

例句

① therefore

  1. The bridge is closed for repairs. Therefore, all buses will use the north road.
    橋在整修，不能通行。因此，所有公車都會改走北邊的路。
  2. You have missed three classes. Therefore, you cannot take the final exam.
    你缺了三堂課。因此，你不能參加期末考。
  3. Our flight leaves at six. Therefore, we should be at the airport by four.
    我們的班機六點起飛。因此，我們四點前應該到機場。
  4. The second plan costs less and takes less time. I therefore recommend it.
    第二個方案花費比較少，時間也比較短。因此，我建議採用這個方案。
  5. The data was collected in only one city; therefore, the results may not apply to the whole country.
    資料只在一個城市收集；因此，這個結果不一定適用於全國。

② thus

  1. The new machines work faster. Thus, the factory can produce more cars each day.
    新機器運作得比較快。因此，工廠每天可以生產更多車子。
  2. Few people replied to the survey. The findings are thus limited.
    回覆問卷的人很少。因此，這些研究結果有限。
  3. The volunteers cleaned the beach every weekend. Thus, the town saved a lot of money.
    志工每個週末都去清理海灘。因此，鎮上省下了很多錢。
  4. Students who read every day learn new words quickly; thus, reading helps them write better.
    每天閱讀的學生學新單字學得很快；因此，閱讀能幫助他們寫得更好。

③ consequently

  1. Our main supplier went out of business. Consequently, we had to find a new one.
    我們的主要供應商倒閉了。因此，我們必須另外找一家。
  2. He did not read the instructions. Consequently, he broke the printer.
    他沒有看說明書。結果，他把印表機弄壞了。
  3. The heating broke down in January; consequently, the school closed for a week.
    暖氣在一月壞掉了；因此，學校停課一週。
  4. Many young people move to the cities. The villages are consequently getting smaller.
    很多年輕人搬到城市。因此，村子越來越小。
  5. The museum has no lift. Consequently, visitors in wheelchairs cannot reach the top floor.
    博物館沒有電梯。因此，坐輪椅的參觀者到不了頂樓。

④ hence

  1. The hotel is far from the station. Hence, most guests come by taxi.
    飯店離車站很遠。因此，大部分客人都搭計程車來。
  2. The old system was slow. Hence, the company decided to replace it.
    舊系統很慢。因此，公司決定換掉它。
  3. Fresh fruit is expensive in winter; hence, many families buy frozen fruit.
    冬天新鮮水果很貴；因此，很多家庭買冷凍水果。

⑤ hence + 名詞片語

  1. The film is three hours long, hence the break in the middle.
    這部電影長達三小時，所以中間才有休息時間。
  2. My sister has a new job in Tokyo, hence her move to Japan.
    我姊姊在東京找到了新工作，所以她才搬去日本。
  3. The town grew up around a market, hence its name, Market Hill.
    這個城鎮是在市場周圍發展起來的，所以才叫 Market Hill。

⑥ as a result

  1. The company cut its prices. As a result, sales went up by 30%.
    公司降價了。結果，銷售額增加了百分之三十。
  2. I trained every morning for six months. As a result, I finished the marathon in under four hours.
    我每天早上訓練，練了六個月。結果，我四小時內就跑完了馬拉松。
  3. The river flooded last spring. As a result, many farmers lost their crops.
    那條河去年春天氾濫。結果，很多農民的作物都沒了。
  4. The café started selling breakfast; as a result, it now opens at seven.
    那家咖啡廳開始賣早餐；因此，現在七點就開門。

⑦ 放在句中

  1. The report, therefore, needs more examples.
    因此，這份報告需要更多例子。
  2. We have therefore decided to move the meeting to Friday.
    因此，我們決定把會議改到星期五。
  3. The two schools are very different, and parents should thus visit both before they choose.
    這兩間學校很不一樣，因此家長在選擇之前應該兩間都去看看。
  4. The rent is high. Most students, consequently, share a flat.
    房租很高。因此，大部分學生都跟別人合租公寓。
  5. Our team had less time than the others and was therefore unable to finish the project.
    我們這組的時間比其他組少，因此沒辦法完成這個專題。
  6. The roads were icy, and the police therefore asked drivers to stay at home.
    路面結冰，因此警方要駕駛人待在家裡。
  7. Cars cause most of the pollution in the city. The council should therefore improve public transport.
    城裡大部分的污染來自汽車。因此，市議會應該改善大眾運輸。
