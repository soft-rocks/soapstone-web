Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling and I ought to go.

我覺得你應該跟他們兩個一起去度假。

主⁠詞 + ought to + 原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

ought to、原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

ought to應該。意思和 should 差不多。
原形動詞應該做的事。

ought 後⁠面一⁠定要⁠有 to

Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling and I ought to go.

ought to

You've had a long week. You ought to rest.

你這個星期很累。你應該休息。

ought 後面一定要接 to，這點和 should 不同。

所⁠有主⁠詞都用 ought to

Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling and I ought to go.

例句

Her cough is getting worse. She ought to see a doctor.

她咳得越來越嚴重。她應該去看醫生。

主詞是 he、she、it 時，ought 也不加 -⁠s。

ought 後⁠面要接 to

Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling and I ought to go.

You look really pale. You ought go home and lie down.

You look really pale. You ought to go home and lie down.

ought 不⁠加 ⁠-⁠s

Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling and I ought to go.

Lily hasn't phoned home all week. She oughts to call her mum.

Lily hasn't phoned home all week. She ought to call her mum.

to 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling and I ought to go.

The last bus is at eleven. We ought to leaving soon.

The last bus is at eleven. We ought to leave soon.

例句

① I ought to + 原形動詞：自己應該做的事

  1. Oh, I'm sorry, my mum's calling me for dinner and I ought to go.
    噢，抱歉，我媽媽叫我去吃晚飯，我應該走了。
  2. It's nearly midnight, and I have school tomorrow. I ought to go to bed.
    快半夜了，我明天還要上學。我應該去睡覺了。
  3. I ought to call my grandmother. It's her birthday today.
    我應該打電話給我奶奶。今天是她的生日。
  4. My maths test is on Friday. I ought to study tonight, but I'm so tired.
    我的數學考試在星期五。我今天晚上應該讀書，可是我好累。
  5. I ought to return these library books. They're a week late.
    我應該還這些圖書館的書。已經晚了一個星期。
  6. My aunt sent me a lovely sweater for Christmas. I ought to write her a thank-you card.
    我阿姨聖誕節寄給我一件很漂亮的毛衣。我應該寫一張謝卡給她。

② You ought to + 原形動詞：給建議

  1. Your parents are both lonely. I think you ought to go on holiday with both of them.
    你爸媽都很寂寞。我想你應該跟他們兩個一起去度假。
  2. You ought to wear a coat. It's below zero outside.
    你應該穿外套。外面零度以下。
  3. This restaurant is famous for its seafood. You ought to try the fish here.
    這家餐廳的海鮮很有名。你應該試試這裡的魚。
  4. Your back has hurt for weeks. You ought to get it checked.
    你的背痛了好幾個星期。你應該去檢查一下。
  5. Some boys in your class keep taking your lunch? You ought to tell your teacher.
    班上有些男生一直拿你的午餐？你應該告訴你的老師。
  6. You've been at your desk since eight. You ought to take a break.
    你從八點就坐在桌子前。你應該休息一下。

③ We ought to + 原形動詞：大家應該做的事

  1. It's twenty to five. We ought to leave now or we'll miss the train.
    四點四十了。我們應該現在走，不然會錯過火車。
  2. Mother's Day is on Sunday. We ought to buy Mum a present.
    母親節在星期天。我們應該買禮物給媽媽。
  3. Grandpa lives alone since Grandma died. We ought to visit him more often.
    奶奶過世後，爺爺一個人住。我們應該更常去看他。
  4. Mrs Kay next door has a broken arm. We ought to help her with her shopping.
    隔壁的 Kay 太太手臂斷了。我們應該幫她買東西。
  5. Our bin is full of bottles and paper again. We ought to recycle more of our rubbish.
    我們的垃圾桶又裝滿了瓶子和紙。我們應該回收更多垃圾。
  6. We ought to book the hotel in Cornwall soon. It gets busy in summer.
    我們應該早點訂 Cornwall 的飯店。夏天很多人。

④ He / She / They + ought to + 原形動詞

  1. Emma said something unkind to Lucy at school. She ought to apologise to her.
    Emma 在學校對 Lucy 說了不好聽的話。她應該跟她道歉。
  2. My uncle had two accidents this year. He ought to drive more carefully.
    我叔叔今年出了兩次車禍。他應該開車小心一點。
  3. Our guests left at six. They ought to be here by now.
    我們的客人六點出發。他們現在應該到了。
  4. My brother left university a year ago and still lives at home. He ought to get a job.
    我哥哥大學畢業一年了，還住在家裡。他應該找一份工作。
  5. It's after ten on a school night. The children ought to be in bed.
    明天要上學，現在已經十點多了。孩子們應該在床上了。
  6. Tom only eats pizza and chips. He ought to eat more vegetables.
    Tom 只吃披薩和薯條。他應該多吃蔬菜。

⑤ I think + 主詞 + ought to + 原形動詞

  1. You broke Sam's phone. I think you ought to say sorry to him.
    你弄壞了 Sam 的手機。我想你應該跟他說對不起。
  2. We've been lost for an hour. I think we ought to ask for help.
    我們迷路一個小時了。我想我們應該請人幫忙。
  3. My sister got a job offer in Paris. I think she ought to take it.
    我姊姊收到巴黎的工作機會。我想她應該接受。
  4. The twins want to leave school at sixteen. I think they ought to listen to their parents.
    那對雙胞胎想十六歲就離開學校。我想他們應該聽他們爸媽的話。
  5. Dad has a bad cold. I think he ought to rest for a few days.
    爸爸重感冒。我想他應該休息幾天。
  6. Our old pool closed last year. I think the town ought to build a new one.
    我們的舊游泳池去年關了。我想這個鎮應該蓋一座新的。
  7. The forest path has no lights. I think we ought to go home before it gets dark.
    森林小路沒有燈。我想我們應該在天黑前回家。
