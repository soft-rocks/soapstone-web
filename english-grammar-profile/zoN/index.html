It's a bit strange, don't you think?

有點怪，你不覺得嗎？

don't you think 加在看⁠法後⁠面，希⁠望對⁠方同⁠意

有點怪，你不覺得嗎？

don't you think 可⁠以放⁠在句⁠尾或句⁠首

It's a bit strange, don't you think?

句尾

This soup needs more salt, don't you think?

這湯要再加點鹽，你不覺得嗎？
句首

Don't you think this soup needs more salt?

你不覺得這湯要再加點鹽嗎？
單獨

Don't you think so?

你不覺得嗎？

don't you agree 比⁠較正⁠式

It's a bit strange, don't you think?

agree

I think it's the best way, don't you agree?

我覺得這是最好的方法，你不同意嗎？

常用在討論和寫作裡。

徵⁠求同⁠意用否⁠定⁠的 don't

It's a bit strange, don't you think?

It's a great idea, do you think?

It's a great idea, don't you think?

放⁠在句⁠首⁠時，後⁠面⁠的子⁠句用直⁠述⁠句⁠的語⁠序

It's a bit strange, don't you think?

Don't you think is this film too long?

Don't you think this film is too long?

問⁠句⁠的主⁠詞是 you，用 don't

It's a bit strange, don't you think?

The food is great here, doesn't you think?

The food is great here, don't you think?

例句

① 看法, don't you think?

  1. The ending was so sudden. It's a bit strange, don't you think?
    結局好突然。有點怪，你不覺得嗎？
  2. Look at the sea. It's beautiful, don't you think?
    你看那片海。好美，你不覺得嗎？
  3. Let's try another shop. This jacket is too expensive, don't you think?
    我們去別家店看看吧。這件外套太貴了，你不覺得嗎？
  4. We should leave now, don't you think? The traffic will be terrible later.
    我們應該現在走，你不覺得嗎？晚一點交通會很糟。
  5. Look at your son next to his father. He's taller than his dad now, don't you think?
    你看你兒子站在他爸旁邊。他現在比他爸高了，你不覺得嗎？
  6. That's a lot of money for a sandwich, don't you think?
    一個三明治那麼貴，你不覺得嗎？
  7. Listen to the girl on stage. She sings really well, don't you think?
    你聽臺上那個女生。她唱得真好，你不覺得嗎？
  8. We painted it last week. The room looks bigger with the white walls, don't you think?
    我們上星期油漆了。牆漆成白色以後房間看起來比較大，你不覺得嗎？

② Don't you think + 看法?

  1. Look at the clouds. Don't you think it's too cold to swim today?
    你看那些雲。你不覺得今天太冷不能游泳嗎？
  2. She might be busy. Don't you think we should call her first?
    她可能在忙。你不覺得我們應該先打給她嗎？
  3. Look at Mum's old photos. Don't you think that fashion always looks funny when it's not today's fashion?
    你看媽媽以前的照片。你不覺得不是現在流行的打扮看起來都很好笑嗎？
  4. We've walked for three hours. Don't you think the kids need a break?
    我們走了三個小時了。你不覺得孩子們需要休息一下嗎？
  5. You've been working for hours. Don't you think you should stop?
    你已經工作好幾個小時了。你不覺得你該停下來了嗎？

③ Don't you think so?

  1. I want to tell her later. The surprise will be even bigger if I wait. Don't you think so?
    我想晚點再跟她說。我等一下，驚喜會更大。你不覺得嗎？
  2. This is the best pizza in town. Don't you think so?
    這是鎮上最好吃的披薩。你不覺得嗎？
  3. She helped me after class. The new teacher is really nice. Don't you think so?
    她下課後幫了我。新老師人真好。你不覺得嗎？
  4. It's much quieter here in the morning. Don't you think so?
    這裡早上安靜多了。你不覺得嗎？

④ 看法, don't you agree?

  1. We've tried everything else. I think it's the best way, don't you agree?
    其他方法我們都試過了。我覺得這是最好的方法，你不同意嗎？
  2. Students have so little money. Public transport should be free for them, don't you agree?
    學生的錢很少。他們搭大眾運輸應該免費，你不同意嗎？
  3. We don't need two meetings. This plan saves us a lot of time, don't you agree?
    我們不用開兩次會。這個計畫幫我們省了很多時間，你不同意嗎？
  4. They're on screens all day. Children need more time outdoors, don't you agree?
    他們整天都在看螢幕。孩子需要多一點時間待在戶外，你不同意嗎？
  5. Nobody can focus after two hours. The meeting is too long, don't you agree?
    兩個小時以後大家都無法專心。這個會開太久了，你不同意嗎？

⑤ Don't you agree (that) + 看法?

  1. There's nowhere for kids to play. Don't you agree that the city needs more parks?
    孩子沒地方玩。你不同意這個城市需要更多公園嗎？
  2. He said sorry. Don't you agree that everyone deserves a second chance?
    他道歉了。你不同意每個人都值得第二次機會嗎？
  3. Only the girls have to wear skirts. Don't you agree that the rules are unfair?
    只有女生要穿裙子。你不同意這些規定不公平嗎？

⑥ 回答

  1. "It's a bit strange, don't you think?" "Yes, I think so too."
    有點怪，你不覺得嗎？對，我也這麼覺得。
  2. "This film is great, don't you think?" "Not really. It's too long for me."
    這部電影很棒，你不覺得嗎？還好。我覺得太長了。
  3. "We should go by train, don't you agree?" "Yes, I agree."
    我們應該搭火車去，你不同意嗎？對，我同意。
  4. "Don't you think she looks tired?" "Yes, she does. She's working too hard."
    你不覺得她看起來很累嗎？對啊。她工作太拚了。
  5. "The food here is amazing, don't you think?" "Absolutely. Let's come back next week."
    這裡的食物超好吃，你不覺得嗎？完全同意。我們下星期再來吧。
