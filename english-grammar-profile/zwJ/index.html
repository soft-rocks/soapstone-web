It is not possible to have a conversation on the veranda without shouting. Nor is it possible to watch television in peace.

在陽台上不用大吼就沒辦法講話，也沒辦法安靜地看電視。

Nor 開⁠頭再⁠加一⁠件否⁠定⁠的⁠事，be 移⁠到主⁠詞前⁠面

My brother didn't come to the wedding. Neither did he send a card.

He never said sorry, and nor did he pay for the damage.

It is not possible to have a conversation on the veranda without shouting. Nor is it possible to watch television in peace.

「我不喜歡恐怖片。」「我也不喜歡。」

Nor 放⁠在句⁠首⁠時要倒⁠裝

It is not possible to have a conversation on the veranda without shouting. Nor is it possible to watch television in peace.

I didn't call him. Nor I sent an email.

Nor 本⁠身是否⁠定，助⁠動⁠詞不⁠加 not

It is not possible to have a conversation on the veranda without shouting. Nor is it possible to watch television in peace.

He can't swim. Nor can't he ride a bike.

He can't swim. Nor can he ride a bike.

前一⁠句也⁠要是否⁠定⁠句

It is not possible to have a conversation on the veranda without shouting. Nor is it possible to watch television in peace.

did 後⁠面⁠接原⁠形⁠動⁠詞

She didn't reply. Neither did she call.

例句

① Nor + be + 主詞

  1. The library isn't open on Sundays. Nor is it open in the evenings.
    圖書館星期天不開，晚上也不開。
  2. The flat wasn't big. Nor was it close to the station.
    那間公寓不大，離車站也不近。
  3. The instructions weren't clear. Nor were they in English.
    說明不清楚，而且也不是英文的。
  4. I'm not a morning person. Nor am I very good at waking up early.
    我不是早起的人，也不太擅長早起。

② Nor + do / does / did + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. I didn't like the food at the hotel. Nor did I like the noisy bar.
    我不喜歡飯店的菜，也不喜歡吵鬧的酒吧。
  2. The company doesn't pay well. Nor does it offer training.
    那家公司薪水不高，也不提供訓練。
  3. We didn't see any wildlife. Nor did we see the famous waterfall.
    我們沒看到任何野生動物，也沒看到那個有名的瀑布。
  4. My parents don't own a car. Nor do they want one.
    我爸媽沒有車，也不想要車。
  5. The landlord never fixed the heating. Nor did he answer our emails.
    房東從來沒修暖氣，也沒回我們的電子郵件。

③ Neither + do / does / did + 主詞 + 原形動詞

  1. At first, I didn't pay attention to all her magazines. Neither did I object to her buying new clothes every month.
    一開始我沒注意她訂了那麼多雜誌，也沒反對她每個月買新衣服。
  2. He didn't apologise. Neither did he explain why he was late.
    他沒道歉，也沒解釋為什麼遲到。
  3. The school doesn't have a gym. Neither does it have a playing field.
    那所學校沒有體育館，也沒有運動場。
  4. I don't enjoy cooking. Neither do I enjoy washing up.
    我不喜歡煮飯，也不喜歡洗碗。

④ Nor / Neither + 情態動詞 + 主詞

  1. We can't afford a new car. Nor can we afford to fix the old one.
    我們買不起新車，也付不起修舊車的錢。
  2. I won't lend him money. Neither will I give him a job.
    我不會借他錢，也不會給他工作。
  3. Children under twelve cannot use the pool alone. Nor can they use the gym.
    十二歲以下的兒童不能單獨使用泳池，也不能使用健身房。
  4. You shouldn't drive when you're tired. Nor should you drive after drinking.
    累的時候不應該開車，喝了酒也不應該開車。

⑤ Nor / Neither + have / has / had + 主詞

  1. I haven't finished the report. Nor have I started the presentation.
    我還沒寫完報告，簡報也還沒開始做。
  2. The council hasn't repaired the road. Neither has it put up any warning signs.
    市議會沒修那條路，也沒立任何警告標誌。
  3. We hadn't booked a hotel. Nor had we checked the weather.
    我們沒訂飯店，也沒查天氣。

⑥ and nor / but nor

  1. She never wrote to us, and nor did she call.
    她從來沒寫信給我們，也沒打電話。
  2. I don't like big cities, and nor does my wife.
    我不喜歡大城市，我太太也不喜歡。
  3. The old bridge isn't safe for lorries, and nor is the road to the farm.
    那座舊橋卡車走不安全，通往農場的路也不安全。

⑦ 簡短的回應：Neither / Nor + 助動詞 + 主詞

  1. "I don't eat meat." "Neither do I."
    我不吃肉。我也不吃。
  2. "I can't swim." "Nor can my brother."
    我不會游泳。我哥哥也不會。
  3. "I wasn't at the party on Saturday." "Neither was I. I was ill."
    我星期六沒去派對。我也沒去。我生病了。
  4. "We haven't been to Japan." "Neither have we."
    我們沒去過日本。我們也沒有。
  5. "I didn't understand the question." "Nor did I."
    我沒聽懂那個問題。我也沒有。
  6. "Tom won't be at the meeting." "Neither will Anna."
    Tom 不會來開會。Anna 也不會。

⑧ 正式的寫作

  1. The government has not explained the new tax. Nor has it said how the money will be used.
    政府沒有說明新的稅，也沒說錢會怎麼用。
  2. The study did not include older adults. Nor did it look at people with heart disease.
    這項研究沒有納入老年人，也沒有研究心臟病患者。
